DEEPAKFERT: Q1 FY27 Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Mining Chemicals (TAN): Q1 FY27 sales volumes fell 12% YoY and 21% QoQ to 130 KT, attributed to production and dispatch disruptions arising from changes to the PESO ANRS portal. The company also flagged that a monsoon-driven slowdown in mining and infrastructure activity may weigh on Q2 demand.

Industrial Chemicals (IPA): IPA sales volumes dropped 76% YoY and 71% QoQ to 4 KT, caused by curtailment of the RGP quota by the Government of India linked to the US–Iran conflict. IPA capacity utilisation fell to 29% due to a shutdown on this account. Total Nitric Acid volumes were flat YoY but down 7% QoQ.

Crop Nutrition: The segment faced a challenging environment from delayed monsoon progression, elevated raw material costs and inadequate subsidy support. Specialty Fertilizer volumes were hit by elevated sulphur prices, lower Bensulf utilisation and regulatory restrictions. Bulk manufacturing capacity utilisation in Q1 FY27 was 78% for ANP and 63% for NPK.

Broader disruption: The company described the quarter as marked by volatility and supply chain disruptions arising from the US–Iran conflict, which caused temporary volume disruptions in both TAN and IPA.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Record profitability: Consolidated operating revenue rose 22% YoY and 8% QoQ to ₹3,256 Cr. Operating EBITDA reached an all-time high of ₹845 Cr, up 65% YoY and 139% QoQ, with margins at 26.0%. Net profit doubled YoY to ₹490 Cr (up 252% QoQ), with PAT margin at 15.0%.

Realisation-led gains: Revenue growth was driven by stronger realisations across Ammonia, Mining Chemicals and Industrial Chemicals. TAN revenue rose 37% YoY despite lower volumes, aided by improved realisation linked to the US–Iran conflict and elevated FGAN prices. Nitric Acid profitability benefited from stronger realisations, and IPA contribution per tonne improved on strong pharma-grade demand.

LNG integration: Commencement of Equinor LNG supplies supported margin expansion, alongside the integrated gas-to-ammonia platform and long-term LNG sourcing strategy.

Premiumisation and B2C: Specialty and Croptek products contributed 43% of Crop Nutrition revenues. B2C revenue in Mining Chemicals grew 42% YoY to ₹151 Cr, or 17% of segment revenues. NPK manufactured sales reached 133 KT, up 4% YoY and 11% QoQ.

Capex projects nearing completion: The Gopalpur TAN project (376 KTPA, capex ₹2,675 Cr) is ~96% complete, and the Dahej Nitric Acid project (WNA 300 KTPA and CNA 150 KTPA, capex ₹1,983 Cr) is ~93% complete. Commercial operations are expected towards the end of Q2 FY27, with profitability contribution expected from Q3 onwards. Post-expansion, total AN capacity would be ~1.0 MMTPA and WNA capacity ~1.2 MMTPA. 65% of CNA capacity is tied up through a 20-year long-term contract.

Balance sheet: Net debt reduced to ₹4,719 Cr from ₹4,824 Cr in March 2026 despite ₹515 Cr of Q1 capex. Net Debt/EBITDA improved to 1.40x from 2.86x, and Net Debt/Equity to 0.60x from 0.65x.

Outlook: Nitric Acid demand is expected to be stable with limited import supply. Improved propylene availability is expected to support recovery in IPA operations, though a correction in domestic IPA prices is anticipated. Crop Nutrition demand conditions remain broadly supportive with widespread rainfall.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory and portal-related disruption: PESO ANRS portal changes disrupted TAN production and dispatch; RGP quota curtailment by the Government of India shut down IPA operations.
  • Monsoon and demand seasonality: Monsoon-related moderation in mining and infrastructure activity may impact Q2 TAN demand; delayed monsoon progression affected Crop Nutrition.
  • Subsidy dependence: A stable Crop Nutrition outlook depends on the Government timely aligning nutrient subsidy rates with elevated input costs.
  • Commodity and input price exposure: Elevated raw material costs, elevated sulphur prices and lower Bensulf utilisation pressured the specialty fertiliser portfolio.
  • Price correction risk: A correction in domestic IPA prices is expected as RGP quota relaxation improves supply availability.
  • Tax litigation: Mahadhan Agritech Limited has a disputed tax liability of ₹1,046 Lakhs for AY 2015-16 pending before the CIT(A), with a consequential penalty appeal of ₹9,604 Lakhs also pending. Separately, the Income Tax Department has appealed before the High Court against a favourable Appellate Tribunal order under Section 153A; the appeal is yet to be admitted. Management remains confident of a favourable outcome based on merits and legal advice.
  • Project execution and commercialisation: The Gopalpur and Dahej projects are nearing completion, with execution and commercialisation risk described as significantly mitigated but commissioning still underway.

4. Management Guidance vs Observed Performance

Project timelines: Management stated the Gopalpur TAN and Dahej Nitric Acid projects are expected to begin contributing to profitability from Q3 onwards, with commercial operations expected towards the end of Q2 FY27. Observed progress: Gopalpur ~96% complete and Dahej ~93% complete, with commissioning activities underway and total capex within the approved limit.

IPA recovery: Management expects improved propylene availability to support recovery in IPA operations, while also expecting a correction in domestic IPA prices. Observed Q1 performance: IPA volumes fell 76% YoY and 71% QoQ, with capacity utilisation at 29% due to the RGP-driven shutdown.

TAN margins: Management expects elevated FGAN prices, favourable import parity and value-based pricing to support margins, while flagging monsoon-related demand moderation. Observed Q1 performance: TAN volumes fell 12% YoY, but revenue rose 37% YoY on stronger realisations.

Crop Nutrition: Management expects demand conditions to remain broadly supportive given widespread rainfall, contingent on timely subsidy alignment. Observed Q1 performance: NPK manufactured sales grew 4% YoY and 11% QoQ, while the segment faced delayed monsoon, elevated raw material costs and inadequate subsidy support.

Balance sheet discipline: Management highlighted strong cash generation and balance sheet discipline alongside strategic capex. Observed: net debt reduced to ₹4,719 Cr and Net Debt/EBITDA improved to 1.40x despite ₹515 Cr of Q1 capex.

Corporate actions: The Board approved the re-appointment of M/s P G Bhagwat LLP as Tax Auditors for FY 2026-27 on 30 July 2026. Deepak Mining Solutions Limited completed the acquisition of Chardham Chemicals Private Limited, an explosives manufacturer, on 6 May 2026 for a total consideration of ₹2,145 Lakhs. The 46th AGM is scheduled for Tuesday, 1 September 2026 at 11:00 A.M. IST via video conference.
