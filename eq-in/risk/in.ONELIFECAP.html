ONELIFECAP: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The company has disclosed a malware/cybersecurity incident affecting certain IT systems, which resulted in corruption of primary and backup electronic data. This represents an operational disruption with potential implications for data integrity and system reliability.

Open litigation and contingent liabilities weigh on the company. These include a Cenvat credit tax demand of ₹171.92 Lakhs, an income tax demand of ₹652.14 Lakhs for assessment year 2012-13, and historical SEBI related-party disclosure penalties. Regulatory non-compliances and penalties have also been imposed in past periods.

On the standalone financials, employee benefits expense rose to ₹50.11 Lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹40.03 Lakhs in the same quarter a year earlier, while finance costs increased to ₹36.62 Lakhs from ₹4.21 Lakhs. Other expenses also rose to ₹33.16 Lakhs from ₹19.37 Lakhs. On a consolidated basis, finance costs rose to ₹64.78 Lakhs from ₹17.58 Lakhs year-on-year, and other expenses rose to ₹245.08 Lakhs from ₹111.69 Lakhs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company completed a ₹36.00 Crore Rights Issue (Letter of Offer dated February 12, 2025; listed on BSE and NSE on March 24, 2026). As of March 31, 2026, 75% of proceeds had been deployed; the remaining 25% was fully utilized during the quarter ended June 30, 2026 for the stated object, which included meeting margin requirements of Dealmoney Commodities Private Limited. This boosted equity share capital to ₹3,736.00 Lakhs (from ₹1,336.00 Lakhs a year earlier) and supported institutional capital adequacy.

The company is rolling out and scaling its proprietary Super App ecosystem, “Onelifetouch,” integrating stock broking, credit syndication, healthcare logistics, real estate, and e-commerce, which is intended to drive organic lead generation and lower customer acquisition costs.

During the quarter, the company converted 16,16,162 convertible warrants in Swojas Foods Limited into an equivalent number of equity shares, remitting ₹2,00,00,004.75 as the balance 75% subscription amount; 67,83,838 warrants remain outstanding for potential future conversion. The company also increased its stake in subsidiary Dealmoney Real Estate Private Limited by subscribing to 42,100 equity shares at ₹357 per share (total investment ₹1,50,29,700), raising its holding from 1,00,000 to 1,42,100 equity shares.

The Board approved the “Onelife Capital Advisors Limited Employee Stock Option Plan 2026” on May 30, 2026, for up to 18,68,000 stock options; shareholders approved it via Postal Ballot on July 10, 2026 (results declared July 13, 2026). No options had been granted as of June 30, 2026, so no Ind AS 102 expense was recognized in the quarter.

3. Key Risks

  • The cybersecurity incident and corruption of primary and backup data pose risks to operational continuity and data recovery.
  • Contingent tax liabilities (Cenvat credit demand of ₹171.92 Lakhs and AY 2012-13 income tax demand of ₹652.14 Lakhs) and historical SEBI related-party disclosure penalties represent financial and regulatory exposure.
  • Past regulatory non-compliances and penalties indicate compliance risk.
  • The company holds 67,83,838 outstanding convertible warrants in Swojas Foods Limited, which carry conversion and valuation uncertainty.
  • The ESOP Plan 2026 could result in future dilution and accounting expense once options are granted, though no impact was recorded in the June 2026 quarter.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management stated it is actively exploring subsequent corporate funding initiatives and institutional capital placement avenues to fund rapid scaling of high-growth business segments without impacting existing reserves. It also expressed a strong probability of success in ongoing tax appeals and regulatory proceedings.

Observed performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 shows improvement against the prior-year quarter. On a standalone basis, total income was ₹296.64 Lakhs versus ₹2.01 Lakhs a year earlier, and profit for the period was ₹176.49 Lakhs versus a loss of ₹61.64 Lakhs. On a consolidated basis, total income was ₹870.49 Lakhs versus ₹207.04 Lakhs, and profit for the period was ₹389.34 Lakhs versus a loss of ₹52.51 Lakhs. Consolidated EPS was ₹1.01 versus a loss of ₹0.39 per share a year earlier.

Sequentially, however, performance moderated: standalone total income fell to ₹296.64 Lakhs from ₹331.59 Lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and standalone profit fell to ₹176.49 Lakhs from ₹198.14 Lakhs. Consolidated total income fell to ₹870.49 Lakhs from ₹2,106.78 Lakhs, and consolidated profit fell to ₹389.34 Lakhs from ₹1,269.48 Lakhs, largely reflecting lower other income (₹150.53 Lakhs versus ₹1,716.68 Lakhs in the prior quarter).

For FY 2025-26, standalone total income was ₹679.61 Lakhs with net profit after tax of ₹208.16 Lakhs; consolidated total income was ₹3,051.55 Lakhs with consolidated net profit after tax of ₹550.50 Lakhs. The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹0.01 per equity share (0.10%) for FY 2025-26, subject to member approval at the 19th AGM.

The 19th AGM is scheduled for Tuesday, September 29, 2026. The Register of Members and Share Transfer Books will be closed from September 23, 2026 to September 29, 2026, with September 22, 2026 as the record date for the final dividend. M/s. Mukesh Siroya and Co. has been appointed as Scrutinizer for e-voting, and Mr. Pandoo Naig is proposed for re-appointment as Director.
