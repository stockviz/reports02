Headwinds and Challenges

Margin compression despite revenue growth. In Q1 FY27, GCL Consolidated revenue grew 31% YoY to ₹974 crore, but EBITDA declined 1% to ₹56 crore and EBITDA margin fell from 7.6% to 5.8%. Core Businesses revenue grew 16% YoY to ₹710 crore, yet EBITDA fell 2% to ₹90 crore with margin declining from 14.9% to 12.6%. GCL Standalone EBITDA declined 7% to ₹71 crore with margin down from 14.0% to 11.3%. The company attributes this to higher commodity costs during the quarter alongside deliberate investments in capabilities and growth initiatives.

Industrial Solutions decline. Industrial Solutions revenue fell 4% YoY to ₹80 crore in Q1 FY27 (9% growth post portfolio rationalization).

Prior exceptional charges. In the quarter ended March 31, 2026, an impairment provision of ₹15.98 crore was recognized on tangible and intangible assets under development, following an impairment indicator from significant adverse changes in expected project execution and customer demand. A ₹15.75 crore provision was also accrued in FY26 related to the four Labour Codes notified on November 21, 2025, with finalization of Central/State Rules still being monitored.

GEML IPO withdrawal. On July 31, 2026, GEML informed SEBI and the stock exchanges that it would not avail the one-time relaxation (SEBI circular dated April 7, 2026) permitting extension of validity of observation letters expiring between April 1 and September 30, 2026. The SEBI Final Observations dated May 8, 2025 lapsed, and GEML stated it will not proceed with the IPO, citing the proposed rights issue and restrictions under SEBI ICDR Regulations on further issuances.

Subsidiary losses. The consolidated review report notes that one subsidiary’s consolidated results and two subsidiaries’ standalone results reflect total revenues of ₹296.38 crore and total net loss after tax of ₹53.68 crore for Q1 FY27. Segment results show Electric Mobility & Other Vehicles reported a loss of ₹45.77 crore in Q1 FY27.

Retail supply constraints. The Retail business demonstrated operational resilience “despite supply constraints,” with portfolio pruning now completed.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong consolidated and core revenue growth. Consolidated revenue grew 31% YoY in Q1 FY27, with growth across both Core and investee businesses. Core businesses grew 16% YoY (19% post portfolio rationalization).

Energy Solutions momentum. Energy Solutions revenue grew 21% YoY to ₹203 crore. The domestic MHP (58.5–500 KVA) genset business grew 32% YoY. The company deployed its first 1250 kVA Megaseries genset in SAARC, commissioned a BESS pilot, and continues focus on export markets in Africa, SAARC, and the Middle East.

Mobility Solutions growth. Mobility Solutions revenue grew 18% YoY to ₹427 crore, with strong revenue growth of 36% YoY in the Auto segment supported by domestic demand and steady exports. Share of non-3W diesel engines OE business reached 60% in Q1 FY27. Engineered Components grew 14% YoY, securing new OEM orders from CAT (UK) and TAFE.

GEML EV performance. E2W volumes grew 101% YoY in Q1 FY27, with market share expanding from 4.3% in FY26 to 5.6% in June 2026. GEML achieved a milestone of 4 lakh electric scooters sold and market leadership in Bihar with 19%+ market share. L5 Vahan volumes grew 39% YoY. GEML raised ₹530 crore through a fully subscribed rights issue, with GCL fully subscribing to its entitlement of approximately ₹331 crore.

GFL expansion. Greaves Finance Limited’s total managed AUM rose to ₹560 crore from ₹521 crore in March 2026, expanded to 86 locations (12 more added), and raised ₹50 crore from GCL, increasing net worth to ₹153 crore.

International expansion. International business revenue share is at 13% of Core business sales. A wholly owned subsidiary, Greaves International Trading FZE, was incorporated in Dubai, UAE, as a gateway to the Middle East and Africa.

Strategic initiatives. The company appointed a CTO to lead R&D and New Product Development, commissioned a Robotic Gantry Cell at the CSN manufacturing facility, and initiated delivery of FM-UL compliant fire-fighting engines for export customers while executing a defence order for direct supply to the Armed Force.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Guidance provided (Greaves.Next): - Energy Solutions share to increase to become a dominant part of portfolio - Organic growth engine at 16–20% CAGR - EBITDA margins of 13–15% - Prudent investment plan of ₹500–700 crore - Expanding beyond India with focus on increasing international business share - Priority on execution discipline, portfolio mix optimization, and capital efficiency

Observed performance (Q1 FY27): - Core Businesses revenue grew 16% YoY, at the lower end of the 16–20% CAGR guidance range - Core Businesses EBITDA margin was 12.6%, below the 13–15% guided range - GCL Consolidated EBITDA margin was 5.8%, well below the guided range - International business revenue share stood at 13% of Core business sales, described as “on track” - Capex plans for Core businesses described as “on track”

The Q1 FY27 performance shows revenue growth within but at the lower bound of the guided organic growth range, while EBITDA margins remain below the guided 13–15% range for Core Businesses, with the company citing commodity costs and deliberate growth investments as factors.
