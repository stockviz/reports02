OnMobile Global — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue contraction and losses. Consolidated revenue from operations for Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) was INR 1,227.55 million, down from INR 1,276.73 million in the March 31, 2026 quarter and INR 1,253.30 million in the June 30, 2025 quarter. The company reported a consolidated loss for the quarter of INR 289.23 million, with a loss attributable to owners of INR 288.33 million. Standalone results showed a loss of INR 72.35 million for the quarter, compared with a profit of INR 218.92 million in the June 30, 2025 quarter.

Restructuring and severance costs. Exceptional items of INR 141.44 million (consolidated) and INR 96.09 million (standalone) were recorded for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, relating to head count restructuring and optimization costs.

Prior impairment of receivables. Impairment losses on financial assets and contract assets for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 included INR 468.40 million receivable from a specific party whose revenue contract was terminated during the previous year. This drove a standalone loss of INR 399.33 million in the March 31, 2026 quarter.

Decline in India revenue. Consolidated revenue from India fell to INR 123.01 million in Q1 FY2026-27 from INR 176.49 million in the prior quarter and INR 273.33 million a year earlier.

EBITDA compression. EBITDA declined to INR 15 million in Q1 FY27 from INR 65 million in Q4 FY26, attributed to ONMO+ launch expenses.

Investigation into allegations. The Audit Committee has appointed an independent law firm to investigate unsubstantiated allegations received from a former consultant regarding potential anomalies in operations. The investigation is underway with no conclusions reached, and the company has stated it will make further disclosures as required based on the outcome and any material developments.

Labour Code impact. The Government of India notified four Labour Codes on November 21, 2025. The company recorded an incremental impact of INR 4.61 million for the year ended March 31, 2026, primarily gratuity-related due to a change in wage definition, and continues to monitor finalization of Central/State Rules.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

ONMO+ Smart Console launch. The company launched the ONMO+ Smart Console through a Flipkart partnership on June 26, 2026, and is in advanced discussions with retail partner groups. The company has invested over $35 million in technology and reached 226 cities across India with the Smart Console.

Gaming revenue growth. Q1 FY27 gaming revenue was INR 394 million, up 2.4% QoQ. The global gaming industry is projected to reach $400 billion by 2029 with an expected CAGR of 10.5%.

Distribution expansion beyond telcos. The Smart Console expansion opens new distribution channels including e-commerce, retail stores, broadband/ISPs, and smart TVs and set-top boxes.

Geographic expansion plans. Management plans market expansion for the ONMO+ Smart Console in Spain in December 2026 and other geographies in 2027.

Africa revenue growth. Consolidated revenue from Africa rose to INR 227.09 million in Q1 FY2026-27 from INR 204.50 million in the prior quarter and INR 195.35 million a year earlier.

Cash position. Closing cash was INR 1,355 million as of Q1 FY27.

3. Key Risks

  • The outcome of the independent law firm investigation into allegations from a former consultant regarding potential operational anomalies, which may have legal or compliance implications.
  • Continued revenue decline and losses at both standalone and consolidated levels.
  • Dependence on the ONMO+ Smart Console and retail partnerships for the growth trajectory, with launch expenses already weighing on EBITDA.
  • Impairment risk on receivables, as evidenced by the INR 468.40 million impairment related to a terminated revenue contract.
  • Regulatory risk from the Labour Codes, with final Central/State Rules and clarifications still pending.
  • Foreign exchange and geographic concentration considerations, given significant revenue from Europe (INR 542.89 million in Q1 FY2026-27) and other international markets.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Guidance: Management targets reaching $3 million Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), expects gaming revenues to grow by 50% in FY 2027, and plans ONMO+ Smart Console market expansion in Spain in December 2026 and other geographies in 2027. Management remains focused on converting the pipeline, driving sustainable growth in the coming quarters, improving profitability, achieving sustainable EBITDA growth, stronger cash generation, and creating long-term value for shareholders.

Observed performance: Q1 FY27 revenue was INR 1,241 million, down 3.8% QoQ. Gaming revenue was INR 394 million, up 2.4% QoQ. EBITDA was INR 15 million (1.2% margin), down from INR 65 million in Q4 FY26 due to ONMO+ launch expenses. PAT was a loss of INR 289 million, including severance costs of INR 141 million. The ONMO+ Smart Console was launched on Flipkart on June 26, 2026. The company has invested over $35 million in technology and reached 226 cities across India.

AGM proceedings (August 11, 2026): The 26th Annual General Meeting was held on August 11, 2026, at 4:00 P.M. IST and concluded at 4:53 P.M. IST through video conferencing. Resolutions transacted included adoption of audited financial statements for FY ended March 31, 2026, appointment of François-Charles Sirois as a Director liable to retire by rotation, appointment of Leo Olebe as an Independent Director, approval of bonus for FY 2026 to François-Charles Sirois, revision of remuneration payable to François-Charles Sirois and Radhika Venugopal, re-appointment of Radhika Venugopal as Whole-time Director & CFO, payment of remuneration to non-executive directors, and material related party transactions with ONMO INC and between subsidiaries.
