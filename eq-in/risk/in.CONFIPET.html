CONFIPET: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Geopolitical disruption in West Asia: Ongoing conflict in the Middle East caused supply uncertainties and volatility in international and domestic LPG prices. Sourcing cargoes became considerably more challenging, and India was particularly affected due to its high dependence on imported LPG (Note 5 to the Q1 FY26-27 results; management commentary).
  • Subdued Auto LPG performance: The company’s strategic prioritisation of Bulk supply (higher margins) and PCD supply under government mandate (household cooking gas availability) meant the Auto LPG division underperformed, hit by higher prices and lower availability. Higher LPG prices also created a temporary industry-wide slowdown in Auto LPG demand.
  • GST input tax credit mismatch: The holding company’s Input Tax Credit balance per the GSTN portal (including Electronic Credit and Cash Ledgers) exceeded the amount recorded in its books by Rs. 1,288 Lakhs. Reconciliation is ongoing; management believes there will be no material impact on the financial position. The auditors drew attention to this via an Emphasis of Matter, without modifying their conclusion.
  • Income-tax search: Income-tax authorities conducted search activity in October 2025 at certain premises, sites, and residences of some employees. The company cooperated and provided details. As of the date of the financial results, no written communication on the outcome had been received, and consequently no accounting provision was made.
  • Auditor scope limitations: The consolidated review relied on other auditors for 19 subsidiaries (total revenue Rs. 52,335 Lakhs; PAT Rs. 105 Lakhs), on management-certified accounts for 3 subsidiaries (revenue Rs. 452 Lakhs; PAT Rs. 68 Lakhs), and on unaudited management information for 1 associate and 10 step-down joint ventures (net loss after tax Rs. 1 Lakh). The prior-year corresponding quarter had a modified conclusion from the then joint auditors.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Structural LPG demand growth: India is the world’s third-largest LPG consumer, with consumption expected to expand significantly on rising household penetration, industrial demand, and adoption in mobility and commercial applications. The Indian LPG market is projected to reach approximately 40–42 million metric tons by FY 2030.
  • Market position: CPIL holds a 33% share within the private marketing segment of India’s LPG market.
  • Network expansion: The GoGas packed LPG customer base in the HoReCa segment grew to more than 3,400 accounts, and the GoGas dealer network increased to over 3,150 dealers. Operations include more than 315 Auto LPG dispensing stations, a growing CNG network of over 50 stations, 82 CNG road vehicles, 15 cylinder manufacturing plants, and 3 high-pressure cylinder manufacturing plants.
  • Related party transactions for growth: The company plans material related party transactions with Sneha Petroleum, Gaspoint Petroleum (India) Limited, Halol Cylinders Private Limited, Uma Gaspoint Bottling Private Limited, and Taraa LPG Bottling Private Limited, to support business growth, operational synergies, cost optimisation, and economies of scale.
  • Auto LPG station target: The company is targeting completion of 500 Auto LPG stations, adding 75 in 2026-2027 and 100 in 2027-2028.
  • Group structure changes: BW Confidence Enterprises Pvt Ltd and GBC LPG Private Limited transitioned from joint ventures to subsidiary/step-down subsidiary status with effect from 08/04/26.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued Middle East conflict and LPG price/supply volatility, which affects sourcing economics and Auto LPG demand.
  • The unresolved GST input tax credit reconciliation (Rs. 1,288 Lakhs difference) and any adjustments that may arise on completion.
  • The income-tax search outcome remains unknown; no provision has been made, leaving potential for future financial impact if the department communicates findings.
  • Reliance on unaudited or management-certified financial information for a portion of the group (3 subsidiaries, 1 associate, 10 step-down joint ventures) and on other auditors for 19 subsidiaries.
  • Execution risk on the Auto LPG station expansion targets and related party transaction benefits.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management targets quarter-on-quarter revenue and PAT growth of 10% to 15% in coming quarters, along with EBITDA growth. For Auto LPG stations, the target is 500 stations, with 75 additions in 2026-2027 and 100 in 2027-2028.
  • Observed performance (Q1 FY26-27, quarter ended 30 June 2026): Consolidated revenue from operations was approximately INR 2,408 crores, up about 117% year-on-year from INR 1,112 crores in Q1 FY26. EBITDA was approximately INR 147 crores, up roughly 64% year-on-year. PAT more than tripled year-on-year to approximately INR 62.60 crores from INR 20.5 crores. Consolidated basic EPS was 1.86.
  • Standalone comparison: Standalone revenue from operations for Q1 FY26-27 was Rs. 2,34,877 Lakhs versus Rs. 1,06,351 Lakhs in Q1 FY26-25; standalone PAT was Rs. 6,079 Lakhs versus Rs. 1,940 Lakhs; standalone basic EPS was 1.83 versus 0.58.
  • Sequential context: Consolidated revenue rose from Rs. 2,40,853 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026 to Rs. 2,40,853 Lakhs… (the Q1 FY26-27 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 2,40,853 Lakhs, compared with Rs. 1,21,569 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2025 and Rs. 1,11,199 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026). Consolidated PAT for Q1 FY26-27 was Rs. 6,255 Lakhs versus Rs. 3,439 Lakhs in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 2,045 Lakhs in Q4 FY26.
  • Dividend: The Board recommended a final dividend of Rs. 0.10/- (10%) per equity share for FY 2025-26.
  • AGM and record date: The 32nd AGM is scheduled for September 30, 2026, at 01:00 PM via Video Conferencing/OAVM. The record date for the final dividend and e-voting cut-off is September 23, 2026, with book closure from September 24 to September 30, 2026.
  • Auditor appointments: M/s. Katariya and Munot are proposed for re-appointment as joint statutory auditors for five years at a remuneration of Rs. 4.00 Lakhs for FY 2026-27; M/s. Narendra Peshne & Associates are appointed as cost auditors.
  • Investor engagement: An analysts/institutional investors group meeting and site visit was scheduled for Friday, 17th July 2026, in Nagpur. A conference call was held on August 21, 2026, at 3:30 PM IST, with the audio recording made available.
