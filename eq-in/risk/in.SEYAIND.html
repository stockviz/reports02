SEYAIND — Recent Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Ongoing insolvency process. Seya Industries Ltd has been admitted into Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, by order of the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench, dated November 2, 2023. As a consequence, the powers of the Board of Directors stand suspended and are being exercised by the Interim Resolution Professional (IRP).
  • No operating revenue. Revenue from Operations (Net) is nil for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, as it was for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, the quarter ended June 30, 2025, and the year ended March 31, 2026. Total income for the June 2026 quarter consists entirely of Other Income of Rs. 383.72 lakhs.
  • Persistent losses. The company reported a loss from ordinary activities before exceptional items and tax of Rs. 125.64 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 119.12 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 136.52 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the loss before exceptional items and tax was Rs. 512.93 lakhs.
  • Cost base exceeds income. Total expenses of Rs. 509.36 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter exceeded total income of Rs. 383.72 lakhs. Depreciation and amortisation alone was Rs. 440.07 lakhs, the largest expense item.
  • Sequential worsening in loss after tax. Loss after tax was Rs. 71.49 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter versus Rs. 70.58 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, and Rs. 198.75 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Governance and oversight constraints. The financial statements were approved by the IRP based on the recommendation of an independent committee constituted by the IRP, rather than by the Board of Directors. The statutory auditor’s review report is addressed to the IRP.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Other income provides a stable income base. Other income was Rs. 383.72 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, Rs. 380.75 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, and Rs. 522.00 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, totalling Rs. 1,664.75 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Narrowing year-on-year loss. The loss from ordinary activities before exceptional items and tax narrowed to Rs. 125.64 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 136.52 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Loss after tax narrowed to Rs. 71.49 lakhs from Rs. 198.75 lakhs over the same comparison.
  • Improved earnings per share year-on-year. Basic and diluted EPS (before and after extraordinary items, not annualised) improved to Rs. (0.27) in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. (0.75) in the June 2025 quarter, and from Rs. (1.08) for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Single focused reporting segment. The company operates in only one reportable segment, ‘Speciality Chemical Intermediates’.
  • Lower total expenses year-on-year. Total expenses declined to Rs. 509.36 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 658.52 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, with employee benefit expense falling to Rs. 39.39 lakhs from Rs. 49.74 lakhs and depreciation and amortisation falling to Rs. 440.07 lakhs from Rs. 578.62 lakhs.

3. Key Risks

  • CIRP-related uncertainty. The company remains under CIRP initiated by NCLT order dated November 2, 2023, with Board powers suspended and exercised by the IRP. The resolution outcome and timeline are not disclosed in the supplied material.
  • Dependence on non-operating income. With no revenue from operations across all periods shown, the company’s income depends entirely on other income, exposing results to variability in that source (Rs. 522.00 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter versus Rs. 383.72 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter).
  • Sustained cash operating losses before depreciation. Losses before exceptional items and tax persisted in every period presented, including a full-year loss of Rs. 512.93 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Tax expense despite pre-tax losses. Tax expense of Rs. 54.14 lakhs was recorded in the June 2026 quarter, Rs. 48.55 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, Rs. 74.29 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, and Rs. 225.52 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026, reducing losses otherwise available to shareholders.
  • Limited assurance. The financial results are unaudited and have been subject to a limited review, which the auditor notes provides less assurance than an audit and would not necessarily identify all significant matters.
  • No exceptional item support in the latest quarter. An exceptional item of Rs. 136.52 lakhs appeared in the June 2025 quarter but was nil in the June 2026 and March 2026 quarters and for the year ended March 31, 2026.
