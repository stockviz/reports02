Headwinds and Challenges

  • Auditor qualification on employee benefits: The statutory auditor’s limited review report for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, states that the company has not provided for post-employment benefits and other long-term employee benefits under defined benefit plans on an accrual basis, instead paying them as and when they fall due. The auditor notes this deviates from Ind AS 19 and that the quantum of the deviation cannot be ascertained because no actuarial report or calculation basis is available. The auditor further states that had Ind AS 19 been followed, employee benefit expense would have increased and profit for the period would have reduced.
  • Board’s position on the same matter: The Board’s notes to the results state that the liability is negligible and will not materially affect the financial position, and that given the size of the company no provision has been made. The Board’s note also refers to the provision being determined on the basis of actuarial valuation methods under Ind AS, which sits alongside the auditor’s statement that no actuarial report or calculation basis is available.
  • Key managerial personnel turnover: Ms. Zinal Modi resigned as Company Secretary and Compliance Officer with effect from the close of business hours on August 12, 2026, citing personal reasons and the pursuit of professional growth opportunities. Mr. Jatin Kharwa was appointed as Company Secretary and Compliance Officer with effect from the same date.
  • Single reportable segment: Management has concluded, under Ind AS 108, that the company has only one reportable segment — Textile — so segment-wise disclosures are not applicable. This concentrates the business in a single industry.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Cost optimisation and solar power: The company states that profitability continues to be supported by strategic cost-optimisation measures, including increased reliance on solar power generation, which has contributed to savings in overall energy costs. Solar power generation showed improvement during the quarter, resulting in better utilisation of installed capacity. Management expects continued focus on optimising energy consumption and improving operational efficiencies to support profitability going forward.
  • Associate company holdings: The financial results include the company’s associates — Mohit Overseas Limited (49.25%) and Mohit Yarns Limited (49.48%) — whose share of profit/loss is included in the consolidated results.
  • Proposed related party borrowings: The AGM notice seeks approval for material related party transactions involving borrowings of up to Rs. 1500 lakhs each from promoters Mr. Narayan Sitaram Saboo, Mr. Manish Narayan Saboo, Mr. Mohit Narayan Saboo, and Mr. Naresh Sitaram Saboo. The stated rationale is that the proposed transactions and arrangements will help strengthen business operations and in turn improve financial performance.

Key Risks

  • Accounting compliance risk: The unresolved Ind AS 19 deviation, with the quantum of impact unascertainable, means the reported profit figures carry an unquantified downward adjustment risk if the accounting treatment were changed.
  • Governance and continuity risk: The resignation and simultaneous replacement of the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer, effective August 12, 2026, is a change in a Key Managerial Personnel role.
  • Concentration risk: Operations are confined to a single reportable segment (Textile), with no segment diversification disclosed.
  • Related party dependence: The proposed borrowings of up to Rs. 1500 lakhs each from four promoter individuals indicate reliance on promoter funding, subject to shareholder approval at the AGM.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management states that profitability continues to be supported by cost-optimisation measures including solar power, that solar generation improved during the quarter with better utilisation of installed capacity, and that these measures are expected to provide continued support to profitability going forward. Management also states that the proposed related party borrowing arrangements will strengthen business operations and improve financial performance.
  • Observed performance: The standalone results disclose revenue from operations of 3752.21 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and the results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board on August 12, 2026. The auditor’s limited review conclusion is qualified only by the Ind AS 19 employee benefits matter; subject to that exception, nothing came to the auditor’s attention indicating the statements are not prepared in accordance with applicable accounting standards. The Board’s note on the employee benefits qualification asserts the liability is negligible and will not impact the financial position, while the auditor states the impact cannot be ascertained — the two positions are not reconciled in the disclosed material.
