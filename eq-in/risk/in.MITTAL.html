Mittal Life Style Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sharp sequential decline in standalone revenue. Standalone revenue from operations fell from Rs. 2,100.77 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to Rs. 1,576.71 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026. On a year-on-year basis, revenue was also lower than the Rs. 2,118.06 lakh recorded in the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

Profitability compression. Standalone profit before tax dropped to Rs. 16.58 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from Rs. 28.53 lakh in the preceding quarter and Rs. 9.32 lakh in the year-ago quarter. Standalone profit after tax was Rs. 10.00 lakh, compared with Rs. 4.22 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26 and Rs. 7.01 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26. Full-year standalone PAT for FY2025-26 was Rs. 109.89 lakh on revenue of Rs. 8,453.59 lakh.

Consolidated performance also weakened sequentially. Consolidated revenue from operations declined to Rs. 1,934.81 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from Rs. 2,407.15 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26. Consolidated profit after tax fell to Rs. 69.47 lakh from Rs. 45.74 lakh in the prior quarter, and was well below the Rs. 229.08 lakh reported for the full year FY2025-26.

Regulatory query on financial results. The Company received an email from NSE dated June 25, 2026 regarding its financial results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, concerning the standalone Statement of Profit and Loss and discrepancies between the standalone PDF and XBRL figures. The Company clarified on June 26, 2026 that the differences were due to rounding-off (Rs. 0.01 lakh to Rs. 0.04 lakh, approximately Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 4,000) and that there was no substantive discrepancy.

Investor voting dissent, albeit minimal. At the 21st AGM held on July 28, 2026, the ordinary resolution to re-appoint Mr. Brijeshkumar Mittal as a director retiring by rotation received 1 vote against (105 votes) and 1 invalid/abstain vote representing 2,64,72,593 votes. The special resolution for consolidation of equity shares received 1 vote against (34 votes), and the special resolution for alteration of the Capital Clause of the Memorandum of Association received 3 votes against (60 votes).

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Subsidiary acquisition and consolidation. The Company acquired the entire share capital of JK Infrasol Private Limited on June 2, 2025, making it a wholly owned subsidiary with effect from that date. JK Infrasol contributed total revenue of Rs. 392.50 lakh and net profit after tax of Rs. 59.47 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, as noted in the limited review report on the consolidated results.

Preferential allotment completed. During the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Company allotted 99,225 equity shares of face value Re. 1 each at an issue price of Rs. 1.80 per share (including a securities premium of Rs. 0.80 per share) on a preferential basis on May 6, 2026.

Capital restructuring approved. The 21st AGM approved a special resolution for consolidation of equity shares from face value of Re. 1 each to face value of Rs. 10 each, and a special resolution for alteration of the Capital Clause of the Memorandum of Association. Both resolutions were passed with overwhelming majority (99.9999% in favour).

Other income supported by treasury and subsidiary operations. Other income for Q1 FY2026-27 included Rs. 37.29 lakh being proceeds of profit from the sales of investments in mutual funds and interest on loan given to the subsidiary company.

Internal auditor appointed. Ms. Shilpa Khimaram Parmar was appointed as Internal Auditor of the Company with effect from May 26, 2026 for FY2026-27. She is a B.Com graduate with 3.5 years of experience in accounts and audit.

Unmodified audit opinion. The Statutory Auditors issued an unmodified opinion on both the standalone and consolidated audited financial results for the quarter and financial year ended March 31, 2026.

3. Key Risks

Revenue concentration and volatility. The Company operates in a single reportable segment, and segmental reporting under Ind AS 108 is not applicable. This limits diversification across business lines.

Dependence on subsidiary performance. The consolidated results include JK Infrasol Private Limited, whose financial results were reviewed by the auditors. Any deterioration in the subsidiary’s performance would directly affect consolidated numbers.

Related-party exposure. The Company has given loans to its subsidiary, with interest income from such loans forming part of other income. The standalone balance sheet shows loans and advances of Rs. 1,572.50 lakh as at March 31, 2026.

Working capital intensity. Trade receivables stood at Rs. 2,514.44 lakh (standalone) and Rs. 2,677.35 lakh (consolidated) as at March 31, 2026. Trade payables increased substantially to Rs. 2,544.97 lakh (consolidated) from Rs. 475.48 lakh in the prior year.

Regulatory compliance and disclosure scrutiny. The NSE query regarding the standalone Statement of Profit and Loss and XBRL discrepancies indicates heightened regulatory attention on disclosure accuracy.

Dependence on mutual fund gains. Other income includes gains from sale of mutual fund investments, which are market-linked and may not be recurring.
