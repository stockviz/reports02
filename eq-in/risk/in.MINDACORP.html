Minda Corporation — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Input cost pressure: The quarter was described as challenging from an input cost perspective, with higher prices of key raw materials, supply chain and logistics disruptions, manpower-related disruptions, and wage increases pressuring margins across the automotive component industry.
  • Margin drag: EBITDA margin was partially impacted by higher commodity prices, rising labour costs, and increased freight expenses. A large portion of these headwinds was offset by operational efficiency and operating leverage.
  • Sequential margin dip: EBITDA margin of 11.5% in Q1 FY27 was 47 bps lower than the 11.9% reported in the preceding quarter, though 19 bps higher year-on-year.
  • Associate/JV earnings compression: Share of profit from associates and JVs fell to about INR 18 crores in Q1 FY27 from about INR 31 crores in Q4 FY26. This was attributed to excess depreciation at Flash Electronics (PAT contribution falling from about INR 25 crores to INR 18–19 crores) and a sharp decline at Furukawa (from about INR 5 crores to about INR 80 lakhs), the latter linked to commodity and labour cost escalations.
  • Flash Electronics margin dip: Flash’s EBITDA margin of 15.4% was marginally lower than the previous quarter, again attributed to higher commodity prices and labour costs. Management noted a back-to-back pass-through arrangement with customers, with a possible lag of a quarter or two.
  • Minda VAST margin dilution: The consolidation of Minda VAST (a passenger vehicle-focused business) pulled overall margins down, as its margin profile (8.4% in Q1 FY27) remains below the company average.
  • Industry cyclicality: QoQ industry growth was described as flattish at about 0.3%, indicating sequential demand moderation even as YoY comparisons remained strong.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record quarterly performance: Highest-ever consolidated revenue of INR 1,846 crores, up 33.2% YoY and 8.4% QoQ; highest-ever quarterly EBITDA of INR 212 crores (up 35.4% YoY); PAT of INR 206 crores (up 215.8% YoY, including an exceptional gain of INR 106 crores net of tax from the Minda VAST consolidation).
  • Strong industry backdrop: Indian automotive production grew about 22% YoY in Q1 FY27, the highest-ever first-quarter production volumes. Two-wheelers grew ~23%, passenger vehicles ~17%, three-wheelers ~39%, and commercial vehicles and tractors ~15% each.
  • EV momentum: Electric two-wheeler registrations crossed 5 lakh units for the first time in a single quarter; EV penetration reached ~10.6% in two-wheelers and ~7.5% in passenger vehicles. At Minda Corporation, EV revenue is close to 10% (up 40% YoY); at Flash Electronics, EV revenue is about 30% (up ~90% YoY); at group level, close to 14%.
  • Order book: Lifetime order book of approximately INR 2,500 crores added during the quarter, spread across vehicle access, castings, wiring harnesses, instrument clusters, and new energy/electronics, across ICE and EV, passenger vehicles, two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, and domestic and export markets.
  • Segment performance: Mechatronics and aftermarket grew 33% YoY; information and connected systems grew 34% YoY, led by wiring harness and instrument clusters. Wiring harness grew over 30% YoY and instrument clusters over 35% YoY.
  • Minda VAST consolidation: Effective from Q1 FY27, strengthening passenger vehicle presence (share of passenger vehicles in revenue rose from 15% to 19%) and expanding the vehicle access portfolio (locksets, latches, handles, immobilizers, passive entry, power access). Kit value is currently INR 8,000–13,000, with scope to rise as new products are developed with the JV partner.
  • New businesses and investments: INR 63 crores invested in group companies — Spark Minda Green Mobility Solutions, Spark Minda HCMF (sunroofs), and Spark Minda Toyo Denso (switches). Sunroof SOP is on track with customer trials approved; Turntide motor controller SOPs expected from October–November.
  • Switches order ramp-up: Lifetime order book above INR 1,000 crores, with SOP expected in Q1 FY28, first-year revenue of about INR 150 crores, and peak in FY29.
  • Exports and cross-selling: Exports constitute about 8–10% of the total order book; Flash’s exports are about 12–15% of its revenue. Cross-selling from Minda to Flash (die castings already started, wiring harnesses under testing) is ramping up.
  • R&D and IP: 335 patents filed, of which 150 granted; seven patents filed in the quarter.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Vision 2030 revenue target: Management reaffirmed the INR 17,500 crores revenue target by FY30, built on six pillars — growth in existing businesses, premiumization, exports, new product launches, JVs/technical agreements, and R&D investment. An analyst questioned a perceived INR 3,000–3,500 crore gap; management disputed the calculation and cited about INR 4,600 crores of opportunity from new products, organic growth of about 30%, and continued pursuit of inorganic opportunities.
  • Margin guidance: Longer-term target of 12.5% EBITDA margin by 2030. FY26 actual was 11.7%; Q1 FY27 came in at 11.5%. Management indicated that maintaining 11.5–12% for the rest of the year would be satisfactory. This compares with the 11.5% delivered in Q1 FY27.
  • Flash Electronics growth: Management targets 20–24% growth (not 30%, citing capacity build needs) and a longer-term EBITDA margin of 16–17%. Q1 FY27 actual: revenue INR 533 crores (up 42% YoY), EBITDA margin 15.4%, PAT margin 6.6% — growth ahead of the guided range, margin below the longer-term target.
  • Minda VAST margin: Management aims to bring Minda VAST’s margin at par with Minda Corporation’s overall level. Q1 FY27 actual margin was 8.4%, up from 6.5% in Q1 FY26 and 6.5–7% in Q4 FY26 — showing improvement but still below company average.
  • Capex: FY27 capex guidance of about INR 400 crores, spread across business verticals and divisions, without specific allocation between EV and ICE.
  • Capacity utilization: Group average cited at about 77–80%, varying by plant and product line.
  • Switches ramp-up: SOP expected Q1 FY28, about INR 150 crores revenue in FY28, peak in FY29 — consistent with the stated lifetime order book above INR 1,000 crores.
  • Sunroof SOP: Guided for Q2; management confirmed customer trials were approved on the first attempt and the timeline is on track.
  • Turntide: SOPs guided for October–November; production facilities are in place and lines are being tested.
  • Cost pass-through: Management stated most indexed commodities have back-to-back arrangements with customers, with possible lag of a quarter or two, and expressed confidence that Flash will be back on track in the next one or two quarters.

Broker Narrative

The narrative evolved from a cautious ‘Accumulate’ in 2023, focused on near-term headwinds like weak 2W volume and geopolitical export risks, to a confident ‘Buy’ in 2026 driven by sustained premiumisation, EV-led content growth, and structural demand drivers. Persisting themes include premiumisation, EV expansion, and geopolitical/macro uncertainties, while the emphasis shifted from easing input cost pressures to renewed commodity and supply chain worries, and from export stagnation fears to a more nuanced view of regional export softness.
