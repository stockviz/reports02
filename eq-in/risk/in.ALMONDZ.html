Almondz Global Securities — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Financial Services. The Financial Services vertical (Wealth Advisory & Broking / Corporate Advisory / Merchant Banking) reported 1Q FY 2026-27 revenue of Rs. 15.97 crore with profit of Rs. 6.49 crore, down from revenue of Rs. 20.80 crore and a loss of Rs. (0.51) crore in 4Q FY 2025-26. The company attributes the sequential decline primarily to a one-time significant capital profit on the sale of a partial strategic investment in a subsidiary to a group company that had boosted 4Q FY 2025-26. On a year-on-year basis, however, the vertical improved: 1Q FY 2026-27 revenue of Rs. 15.97 crore with profit of Rs. 6.49 crore compares with revenue of Rs. 9.20 crore and profit of Rs. 2.52 crore in 1Q FY 2025-26 (the release also cites Rs. 17.17 crore revenue and Rs. 7.69 crore profit for the YoY comparison).

Green Fuel. PGIPL’s Odisha plant has completed all planned construction, installation and commissioning and is fully ready for commercial operations, but full-scale commercial production has not commenced because the procurement agreement with the Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) is still awaited. Procurement will be finalized through a tendering process that the OMCs have not yet floated. The previous tender cycle could not be participated in because the plant was not ready for production at that time. PGIPL’s 1Q FY 2026-27 revenue of Rs. 179.33 crore and profit of Rs. 11.41 crore were marginally below 4Q FY 2025-26 (revenue Rs. 179.35 crore, profit Rs. 12.15 crore) and below 1Q FY 2025-26 revenue of Rs. 192.25 crore, though profit improved year-on-year from Rs. 10.55 crore.

Infrastructure Advisory. Revenue fell to Rs. 28.53 crore in 1Q FY 2026-27 from Rs. 51.39 crore in 4Q FY 2025-26, which the company attributes mainly to the year-end effect in March 2026 as per market practice in the infrastructure advisory industry. Profit was broadly stable at Rs. 2.49 crore versus Rs. 2.31 crore sequentially, and improved year-on-year from revenue of Rs. 22.43 crore and profit of Rs. 1.88 crore in 1Q FY 2025-26. The business is characterized by long gestation periods, government and multilateral agency interactions, technical teams, and sector-specific expertise.

Regulatory/compliance. The company received an NSE email dated 18 June 2026 citing deficiencies in filed results: the Declaration of Unmodified Opinion had not been submitted, and the Limited Review/Independent Auditor’s Report was not in the SEBI-prescribed format because the same UDIN appeared in both the Standalone and Consolidated Auditor’s Reports. The company stated the Unmodified Opinion Report had already been filed in XBRL, that the PDF declaration was inadvertently omitted, and that a clerical error caused the Consolidated UDIN to be mentioned in the Standalone report. Revised documents were submitted on 22 June 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Infrastructure Advisory guidance. Management expects revenue and profitability growth of 20–22% in the current financial year compared with the previous financial year, and states the company is hopeful of maintaining a growth trend in revenue and profitability over the previous year. The company has won new business of Rs. 97.32 crore during the current financial year.

Green Fuel. Once the OMC tender is floated and PGIPL successfully enters a procurement contract, commercial production will commence immediately, with the Odisha plant fully ready.

Demerger. Almondz Global Securities is demerging its Infrastructure Advisory Business Undertaking into Almondz Global Infra – Consultant Limited. The share exchange ratio is 666 equity shares of the Resulting Company (face value Rs. 10 each) for every 10,000 equity shares of the Demerged Company (face value Rs. 1 each), and listing will be sought for the Resulting Company. The Board believes the Scheme is in the best interest of all stakeholders and will unlock value for the Infrastructure Advisory Business by providing clear visibility into the operations, financial performance, and growth prospects of each entity independently. The segregation is expected to enable better resource allocation, streamlined decision-making, sharper focus on innovation, strategic clarity, and operational independence, with both businesses seen as carrying significant potential for growth and profitability.

Broader environment. The company cites strong domestic macroeconomic fundamentals, increasing financialization of household savings, rising retail participation through SIPs, government capital expenditure, infrastructure-led growth, and India’s clean energy transition favoring bioenergy and ethanol manufacturing as supportive factors.

3. Key Risks

  • Macroeconomic uncertainties including geopolitical conflicts, volatile crude oil prices, inflationary pressures, and fluctuations in interest rates.
  • Financial services risks such as fluctuations in capital market activity, investor sentiment, and liquidity conditions.
  • Infrastructure risks including project delays, cost escalations, and land acquisition challenges.
  • Bioenergy feedstock availability and pricing.
  • Cybersecurity threats.
  • Dependence of the Green Fuel vertical’s commercial production on the timing of the OMC tender and the successful conclusion of a procurement contract.
  • Long gestation periods and dependence on government and multilateral agency interactions in the Infrastructure Advisory Business.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Infrastructure Advisory. Management guides to 20–22% growth in revenue and profitability for the current financial year versus the previous year, and to maintaining a growth trend. Observed 1Q FY 2026-27 performance shows revenue of Rs. 28.53 crore and profit of Rs. 2.49 crore, up year-on-year from Rs. 22.43 crore and Rs. 1.88 crore in 1Q FY 2025-26, but down sequentially from Rs. 51.39 crore and Rs. 2.31 crore in 4Q FY 2025-26, with the sequential revenue decline attributed to the March year-end effect. The company has won new business of Rs. 97.32 crore in the current financial year.

Green Fuel. Management states commercial production will commence immediately once the tender is floated and a procurement contract is entered. Observed status is that the Odisha plant is fully ready but production has not commenced on full scale, and the OMC tender has not yet been floated. PGIPL’s 1Q FY 2026-27 revenue of Rs. 179.33 crore and profit of Rs. 11.41 crore were roughly flat sequentially and lower in revenue year-on-year, with profit higher year-on-year.

Financial Services. No specific forward guidance is provided for this vertical. Observed 1Q FY 2026-27 revenue of Rs. 15.97 crore and profit of Rs. 6.49 crore declined sequentially from Rs. 20.80 crore and a loss of Rs. (0.51) crore in 4Q FY 2025-26, which management attributes to the one-time capital profit in the prior quarter, while year-on-year performance improved.

Overall. Consolidated 1Q FY 2026-27 revenue was Rs. 44.96 crore with profit of Rs. 12.55 crore, versus revenue of Rs. 72.55 crore and profit of Rs. 5.37 crore in 4Q FY 2025-26, and versus revenue of Rs. 32.28 crore and profit of Rs. 6.93 crore in 1Q FY 2025-26. Management expresses confidence that strategic focus on business diversification, technology adoption, operational excellence, prudent capital allocation, and strong corporate governance will enable delivery of sustainable long-term value to shareholders. For FY 2025-26, consolidated total income reached Rs. 19,162.82 lakhs with profit after tax of Rs. 2,873.02 lakhs, and the Board did not recommend any dividend for the year to conserve resources. The 32nd Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, 30 September 2026 at 11:00 a.m. IST through video conference or other audio-visual means.
