HAVISHA — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • The company carries accumulated losses of about Rs 100 crores from the past.
  • Its private LPG business was badly affected when the Government of India could not implement its commitment to remove the LPG subsidy over three years.
  • It recorded a Diluted Earnings Per Share of Rs. -0.12 per equity share.
  • It reported a net loss after tax of INR 182.32 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The company turned profitable following its merger with Shri Shakti Resorts & Hotels Ltd, as per NCLT Order dated November 16, 2021.
  • Business growth and future prospects are substantially enhanced by the renewal of the land lease at Begumpet Airport for 30 years starting January 1, 2023, for constructing and operating an airport hotel and related business activities.
  • The company became debt-free in FY 2007-08 after the Chairman and Managing Director cleared bank loans of about Rs 106 crores under a One Time Settlement.

3. Key Risks

  • The historical accumulated losses of about Rs 100 crores and the past disruption in the private LPG business illustrate exposure to policy-dependent segments.
  • The most recent reported performance — a net loss after tax of INR 182.32 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 — indicates continued pressure on profitability.
  • The proposals before shareholders involve related-party elements: revalidation and approval for the issue of 1,50,00,000 sweat equity shares of Rs. 2 each for non-cash consideration to Mr. Venkat Manohar Dontamsetti, Chairman and Managing Director, and approval for material related party transactions such as lending or borrowing transactions up to an aggregate value not exceeding Rs. 9 Crores or 100% of turnover, whichever is higher.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • No forward-looking guidance or projections are provided in the supplied material.
  • The observable performance record shows a turnaround to profitability following the merger with Shri Shakti Resorts & Hotels Ltd (NCLT Order dated November 16, 2021), supported by the 30-year Begumpet Airport land lease renewal effective January 1, 2023.
  • Against that backdrop, the quarter ended June 30, 2026 recorded a net loss after tax of INR 182.32 lakhs, and the company’s historical position includes accumulated losses of about Rs 100 crores and a Diluted EPS of Rs. -0.12 per equity share.
