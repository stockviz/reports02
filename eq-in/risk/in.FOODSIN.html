FOODSIN — Q1 FY27 Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Export logistics disruption: Sales volumes in the export segment were significantly hit by export freight and container availability issues attributed to a continuing war situation. Approximately 1,800 MT of finished goods remained overdue for shipment.
  • Realization pressure: Average realizations declined by ~18.5% YoY, reflecting sales of inventory manufactured during the 2025 crop season at substantially lower raw material costs.
  • Mango crop pricing: The fruit price of the Mango crop was lower in the current procurement season compared to last year, continuing low realization. Management notes this will continue through the current year.
  • Tomato execution shortfall: The company was unable to execute canned Tomato products against confirmed orders due to poor raw material quality.
  • Overall contraction: Total income for Q1FY27 was Rs 160 Cr, down 33% YoY from Rs 239 Cr. PAT was Rs 4 Cr, down 46% YoY from Rs 7 Cr. Total tonnage fell to 20,836 MT from 29,964 MT in Q1FY26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Spray dried powders: Volumes grew 22% in the quarter.
  • Frozen food: Volumes up ~10% and value up ~19.5% YoY in Q1 FY27, supported by improved realizations from higher contributions of value-added products and growing demand from the US market.
  • Spices: The Kusum Spices business grew 14.7% YoY in Q1 FY27.
  • Tetra Recart expansion: The company is continuing to expand its international presence in Tetra Recart and evaluating avenues to enhance capacity utilization, supported by repeat business from existing customers.
  • Sustainable farming: The soil regeneration pilot scaled from Konkan in 2024 to Chittoor in 2026. Over the next five years, the company aims to cover and certify 1,500+ additional farmers across over 2,000+ hectares under the SAI/Rainforest Alliance platform.
  • Pectin project: A JV has been set up to convert fruit waste into Pectin, Oils, and Butter, with India currently importing 95% of its pectin.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued export freight and container availability constraints tied to the war situation, with ~1,800 MT of finished goods awaiting shipment.
  • Persistence of low mango realizations into the current year.
  • Raw material quality issues preventing execution of confirmed Tomato orders.
  • Pricing remains a pure pass-through of raw material movements, leaving limited insulation from input cost swings.
  • Total financial indebtedness (short-term and long-term) stood at Rs 46,916.60 Lakhs as of June 30, 2026, with zero default on loans and revolving facilities from banks and financial institutions.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Low fruit prices for the Mango crop will continue to drive low realizations in the current year. Observed: Q1FY27 total income fell 33% YoY to Rs 160 Cr and PAT fell 46% YoY to Rs 4 Cr, with average realizations down ~18.5% YoY — consistent with the stated realization pressure.
  • Guidance: Pricing remains a pure pass-through of raw material movements. Observed: The realization decline is attributed to sales of inventory manufactured during the 2025 crop season at substantially lower raw material costs, aligning with the pass-through characterization.
  • Guidance: Continued expansion of international presence in Tetra Recart and evaluation of avenues to enhance capacity utilization. Observed: Repeat business from existing customers is cited as demonstrating growing acceptance in international markets; frozen food value grew ~19.5% YoY on higher value-added contributions and US demand.
  • Guidance: Target to certify 1,500+ additional farmers across 2,000+ hectares under SAI/Rainforest Alliance over five years. Observed: The soil regeneration pilot has scaled from Konkan (2024) to Chittoor (2026).
