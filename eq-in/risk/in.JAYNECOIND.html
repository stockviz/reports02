Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory and legal overhang from the coal block matter. The Directorate of Enforcement (ED) had, in earlier years, provisionally attached properties, plant and machinery at Dagori and Siltara worth Rs. 30,758 Lakhs over alleged misuse of coal from the Gare Palma IV/4 coal block. The PMLA Appellate Tribunal allowed the Company’s appeals and set aside the provisional attachments by order dated 28th November 2024, and the Court of Special Judge, New Delhi (CBI Court) discharged the Company, Mr Arvind Jayaswal and Mr Ramesh Jayaswal under Sections 3 and 4 of the PMLA by order dated 19th March 2024, holding there was no offence of money laundering absent any charge of cheating in securing the coal block allocation. The ED has challenged the CBI Court order in the Supreme Court via a Special Leave Petition. While hearing the ED’s SLP, the Supreme Court gave oral directions to the Company not to press its application before the CBI Court for release of the attached properties, and the Company gave an oral undertaking not to press for early adjudication. The matter therefore remains unresolved, with the attached properties not yet released.
  • Finance costs. Finance costs rose to Rs. 6,553 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30th June 2026 from Rs. 6,895 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31st March 2026 and Rs. 11,938 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30th June 2025. For the year ended 31st March 2026, finance costs were Rs. 42,622 Lakhs.
  • Unallocated expenditure. Other un-allocable expenditure was Rs. 1,813 Lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 49 Lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 717 Lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Media speculation risk. Recent media coverage and unverified information, speculation or market rumours concerning DATASEL S.R.L., Italy, prompted the Company to clarify that DATASEL S.R.L. is a subsidiary of Neco Defence Munitions Private Limited and is not an associate, joint venture or subsidiary of Jayaswal Neco Industries Limited (JNIL). Both entities are owned by the same promoters group, and Shri Anand Jayaswal is the only common director. JNIL has no direct relation or transactions with DATASEL S.R.L. and Neco Defence Munitions Private Limited, and any consequences arising from DATASEL S.R.L. will have no financial impact on JNIL.
  • Volume movement query. The Company stated there is no undisclosed information or announcement that needs to be informed to the stock exchanges, and believes the significant increase in equity share volume is possibly market driven.

Key Risks

  • Coal block / PMLA proceedings. The ED’s Special Leave Petition before the Supreme Court against the CBI Court’s discharge order remains pending. The Supreme Court’s oral direction and the Company’s oral undertaking not to press for release of the attached properties mean the properties attached to the extent of Rs. 30,758 Lakhs remain subject to the ongoing proceedings. The Company’s conclusion in the limited review report is not modified in respect of this matter.
  • Segment concentration. The Steel segment accounted for the overwhelming majority of revenue and segment results, with segment revenue of Rs. 199,224 Lakhs out of total turnover of Rs. 211,736 Lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, and segment results of Rs. 34,085 Lakhs out of total segment results of Rs. 34,169 Lakhs.
  • Associate company structure. The Company jointly holds the equity share capital of Maa Usha Urja Private Limited, an associate company, without having any beneficiary interest in those shares. Accordingly, the Company is not required to prepare Consolidated Financial Statements.
  • Related-party perception risk. The DATASEL S.R.L. matter illustrates the risk of market rumours linking the Company to entities in the same promoters group, even where JNIL has no direct relation or transactions and no financial impact is expected.
