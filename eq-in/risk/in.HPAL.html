HP Adhesives Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Fire incident at Unit I (17 January 2026). A fire at the Company’s manufacturing Plant Unit-I at Survey No. 7, Hissa 14, Village Narangi, Taluka Khalapur, District Raigad (Maharashtra) damaged property, plant and equipment, inventories and other assets, and disrupted operations. The Company recognised an estimated loss of ₹2,498.50 lakhs in an earlier period (quarter ended 31 March 2026) and a further ₹89.84 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, comprising a write-off of PPE of ₹63.45 lakhs and debris removal and related expenses of ₹26.38 lakhs, with corresponding increases in an insurance claim receivable. The Company’s own disclosure states the incident caused disruption to certain manufacturing operations and impacted financial performance, resulting in inadequate profits in the preceding financial year 2025-26.

Weak quarterly performance. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, consolidated revenue from operations was ₹6,341.65 lakhs, lower than ₹6,610.49 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Consolidated loss before exceptional items and tax was ₹135.40 lakhs, versus a profit before tax of ₹557.19 lakhs in the prior-year quarter. Consolidated loss after tax was ₹90.55 lakhs, compared with a profit after tax of ₹419.38 lakhs a year earlier. Basic and diluted EPS moved from ₹0.46 to negative ₹0.10. Standalone results followed the same pattern: revenue of ₹6,333.60 lakhs, loss before tax of ₹126.04 lakhs and loss after tax of ₹83.64 lakhs, against a profit after tax of ₹422.33 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

Cost pressure. Total consolidated expenses rose to ₹6,583.47 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹6,163.05 lakhs a year earlier, with cost of material consumed at ₹3,623.16 lakhs (versus ₹2,877.25 lakhs), purchases of stock-in-trade at ₹1,539.57 lakhs (versus ₹906.68 lakhs) and other expenses at ₹1,186.39 lakhs (versus ₹773.95 lakhs). Finance costs increased to ₹80.52 lakhs from ₹64.49 lakhs.

Prior-year exceptional charges. Exceptional items for the year ended 31 March 2026 included ₹32.34 lakhs paid towards the accepted portion of a GST demand of ₹311.01 lakhs raised pursuant to a GST Audit for FY 2019-20 to 2023-24, and ₹6.58 lakhs paid pursuant to a Final Order of the Hon’ble CESTAT, Mumbai. The year also included an incremental gratuity impact of ₹42.06 lakhs following notification of the four Labour Codes on 21 November 2025.

Subsidiary drag. The wholly owned subsidiary, Unitybond Solutions Private Limited, reported total revenue of ₹34.05 lakhs and a net loss after tax of ₹6.95 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with total assets of ₹283.43 lakhs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management’s stated improvement measures. Management expects that measures undertaken towards operational efficiency, cost optimisation, business expansion, product development and market penetration will contribute to improved productivity and profitability, supported by the continued involvement of experienced managerial personnel.

Sequential revenue recovery. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to ₹6,341.65 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from ₹5,782.07 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, and the consolidated loss before exceptional items and tax narrowed to ₹135.40 lakhs from ₹523.61 lakhs in that preceding quarter. Standalone revenue similarly improved to ₹6,333.60 lakhs from ₹5,770.26 lakhs, with the pre-exceptional loss narrowing to ₹126.04 lakhs from ₹525.35 lakhs.

Insurance coverage. The Company holds insurance coverage for reinstatement value of damaged assets and business interruption losses, and has recognised a corresponding insurance claim receivable, pending final assessment and settlement.

Product and capacity track record. The re-appointment disclosure notes that under Ms. Nidhi Motwani’s leadership the organisation experienced a significant increase in output capacity and consistent production efficiency, and that she was instrumental in launching and growing the SR/SH and Silicone sealant products.

Balance sheet and capital position. Paid-up equity share capital stood at ₹1,837.49 lakhs (face value ₹2 per share) as at 30 June 2026. Other equity was ₹16,937.04 lakhs on a consolidated basis and ₹16,887.24 lakhs on a standalone basis as at 31 March 2026. Net IPO proceeds of ₹9,669.12 lakhs raised in FY2021-22 were fully utilised as on 31 March 2026, with no unspent proceeds; amounts in the Monitoring Agency Account and related bank FD represent surplus funds.

Dividend. The Board declared a dividend of 20% (₹0.40 per equity share of ₹2 each) for the financial year ended 31 March 2026.

3. Key Risks

Insurance claim uncertainty. The auditor’s review reports draw attention to inherent uncertainty in the measurement of the insurance claim receivable, noting that the final outcome may differ from the amount currently recognised, pending completion of claim assessment and settlement. The final impact of the fire, if any, will depend on completion of the claim assessment and settlement.

Earnings volatility. The results show a swing from profit to loss on both a consolidated and standalone basis between the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and the quarter ended 30 June 2026, alongside a loss in the quarter ended 31 March 2026.

Operational concentration. The Group operates primarily in a single reportable business segment, adhesives and sealants products, with no separate segment disclosures under Ind AS 108.

Regulatory and tax exposures. The prior-year GST audit demand and CESTAT order illustrate exposure to tax and regulatory proceedings; the Labour Codes notified on 21 November 2025 required an incremental gratuity liability assessment of ₹42.06 lakhs.

Subsidiary losses. Unitybond Solutions Private Limited recorded a net loss after tax of ₹6.95 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management’s forward-looking statement is that operational efficiency, cost optimisation, business expansion, product development and market penetration measures will contribute to improved productivity and profitability, supported by experienced managerial personnel.

Observed performance provides mixed evidence against this guidance. On a sequential basis, consolidated revenue improved from ₹5,782.07 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 to ₹6,341.65 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and the pre-exceptional loss narrowed from ₹523.61 lakhs to ₹135.40 lakhs, consistent with a direction of improvement. However, on a year-on-year basis, revenue declined from ₹6,610.49 lakhs to ₹6,341.65 lakhs, and the Company moved from a profit before tax of ₹557.19 lakhs and profit after tax of ₹419.38 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 to a loss before tax of ₹135.40 lakhs and loss after tax of ₹90.55 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Total expenses also remained higher year-on-year. The Company’s own disclosure attributes the inadequate profits in FY2025-26 to the fire incident’s disruption of manufacturing operations. The additional ₹89.84 lakhs fire-related loss recognised in the June 2026 quarter, and the pending insurance settlement, indicate that the full financial effect of the incident had not been finalised as at the date of the results.
