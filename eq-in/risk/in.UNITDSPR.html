Headwinds and Challenges

  • Maharashtra policy impact: The company states that growth in Q1FY27 was “partially offset by the continuing impact of the adverse policy in Maharashtra.” This affected both segments — it is cited as a driver of the 17.5% decline in Popular segment NSV and also weighed on the overall P&A growth picture.
  • Karnataka excise slab changes: Popular segment realisations were “adversely impacted” by recent policy changes in Karnataka owing to excise slab changes, alongside the Maharashtra impact.
  • Popular segment decline: Popular segment NSV fell 17.5% to INR206 Cr and volume fell 14.1% to 2,070 thousand cases. Its share of net sales dropped 2.2 ppt year-on-year to 7.6%.
  • Overall volume decline: Total volume declined 3.4% to 14,474 thousand cases, even as P&A NSV grew 10.1%; P&A volume itself was down 1.3%.
  • West Asia crisis: Cited as an adverse impact partially offsetting gross margin gains and as a factor impacting EBITDA in the quarter.
  • Margin contraction: EBITDA margin contracted 30 bps to 16.0%, mainly on account of higher A&P investments (marketing spends rose to 11.5% of net sales from 9.3%).
  • Regulatory action on product labels: FSSAI issued an order dated 29 July 2026 in relation to identified whisky products produced at a third-party manufacturing unit in Madhya Pradesh, on the ground that product labels were not in conformance with FSSA provisions. This order was revoked by a further FSSAI order dated 20 August 2026. The company stated there was no material operational or financial impact from either the original order or the revocation.
  • Historical/legal matters: The results notes describe continuing uncertainties from the Initial Inquiry and Additional Inquiry (including actual and potential diversion of funds), regulatory notices from SEBI, the Directorate of Enforcement, authorised dealer banks and other agencies, the subsidiary rationalisation process, litigation with IDBI Bank (including pledged shares held by USL Benefit Trust and INR 46 crores deposited and carried as recoverable), and the UBHL loan dispute. The company wrote off outstanding loans of INR 1,238 crores during the year ended March 31, 2026, while continuing to pursue legal remedies. Management states it is currently unable to estimate the financial impact of potential non-compliances, if established.
  • Exceptional items: Q1FY27 included INR 81 crores of exceptional items, comprising INR 26 crores of severance costs relating to a closed unit under the Supply Agility Programme and INR 55 crores of employee severance costs.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Prestige & Above momentum: P&A NSV grew 10.1% to INR2,478 Cr, with P&A saliency at 91.7% of net sales, up 3.4 ppt year-on-year. The company attributes this to Smirnoff local flavour innovation and broad-based portfolio growth.
  • Overall NSV growth: Net sales rose 6.0% to INR2,703 Cr.
  • Gross margin expansion: Gross profit grew 11.2% with gross margin at 46.1%, up 212 bps, attributed to sustained revenue growth, management interventions, better mix and productivity flow-through, partially offset by the West Asia impact.
  • Profit growth: PAT rose 51.6% to INR391 Cr, helped by higher other income (INR222 Cr versus INR61 Cr) and lower finance costs.
  • Premium craft beverage investment: On 22 July 2026 the Board approved an investment in Nuvola Spirits Private Limited (NSPL) — 17,350 CCPS and 10 equity shares for INR 2.69 crore, equal to 10.08% of NSPL’s issued and paid-up capital on a fully diluted basis. NSPL owns craft liqueur brands Mikiamo and Seoulmate (Soju, Limoncello, Meloncello, Amara Rosso). The company states this aligns with its strategy of backing innovative founders and capitalising on emerging consumer trends in the premium craft beverage segment. NSPL’s sales were nil in FY23-24, INR 0.38 crore in FY24-25 and INR 3.50 crore (unaudited) in FY25-26, all from India. The SSHA provides the company with the right to appoint one director and an observer, tag-along and drag-along rights, and an option to acquire remaining shares upon NSPL achieving pre-agreed milestones within a defined time period. Completion is expected on or before 21 September 2026.
  • Sports segment divestment: The Board approved the sale of 100% of Royal Challengers Sports Private Limited on 24 March 2026. Competition Commission of India approval has been received and a BCCI application has been made; the transaction is expected to be completed within 12 months of the original announcement. Sports is presented as discontinued operations.
  • CEO commentary: Mr. Praveen Someshwar stated the company commenced fiscal 2027 “on a strong note with double-digit growth in the Prestige & Above segment,” that consumer-centric interventions give confidence to “increase growth further as the year progresses,” and that the company continues to “future-proof our portfolio.”

Key Risks

  • Adverse state-level policy and excise changes (Maharashtra, Karnataka) affecting realisations and segment performance.
  • Regulatory action by FSSAI on product labelling conformance, as evidenced by the 29 July 2026 order (subsequently revoked on 20 August 2026).
  • Geopolitical disruption, as illustrated by the West Asia crisis impact on gross margin and EBITDA.
  • Dependence on continued premiumisation, given the declining Popular segment and overall volume contraction.
  • Legal and regulatory exposure from historical matters: the Additional Inquiry, recovery suits, the UBHL loan dispute and Official Liquidator claims, the IDBI Bank litigation over pledged assets and shares, and the subsidiary rationalisation process, where the financial impact of potential non-compliances cannot currently be estimated.
  • Execution risk on strategic transactions, including the NSPL investment (subject to milestone-based option rights) and the RCSPL sale (pending BCCI approval).

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Management stated it had “commenced fiscal 2027 on a strong note with double-digit growth in the Prestige & Above segment” and expressed confidence to “increase growth further as the year progresses.” Observed Q1FY27 performance shows P&A NSV growth of 10.1%, consistent with the double-digit description, while total NSV grew 6.0% and total volume declined 3.4%.
  • Management’s stated strategy of “backing innovative founders and capitalizing on emerging consumer trends within the premium craft beverage segment” is reflected in the NSPL investment approved on 22 July 2026, with NSPL’s unaudited FY25-26 sales of INR 3.50 crore against INR 0.38 crore in FY24-25.
  • The company’s disclosure on the FSSAI matter stated no material operational or financial implications from the 29 July 2026 order; the subsequent 20 August 2026 revocation order confirmed there is no material operational or financial impact on the company.
  • The company disclosed that no unpublished price sensitive information is proposed to be shared during the investor conferences scheduled from 21 to 22 September 2026, and that the schedule is subject to change due to exigencies on the part of analysts, investors or the company.
