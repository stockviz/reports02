FIVESTAR — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Asset quality stress remains elevated. Gross Stage 3 assets rose to 3.46% in Q1FY27 from 3.37% in Q4FY26 and 2.46% in Q1FY26. Net Stage 3 assets increased to 2.10% from 2.00% sequentially and 1.25% a year earlier. The 30+ DPD book stood at 12.38% in Q1FY27, improved from 12.69% in Q4FY26 but still above the 11.31% recorded in Q1FY26. The 1+ DPD book was 18.33% in Q1FY27 versus 16.70% in Q1FY26.

Credit costs remain materially above historical levels. Credit cost to average AUM was 1.85% in Q1FY27, compared with 1.58% in Q1FY26 and 0.83% for FY2025. Loan losses and provisions for FY2026 were ₹2,163 Mn against ₹890 Mn in FY2025, a 143% increase.

Profitability has compressed. Return on Equity declined to 14.46% in Q1FY27 from 16.57% in Q1FY26 and 18.68% in FY2025. Return on Average AUM fell to 8.11% from 8.80% a year earlier. Net Interest Margin was 19.97% in Q1FY27 versus 19.99% in Q1FY26 and 20.46% for FY2025.

Operating cost pressure. Operating expenses rose 21% year-on-year in Q1FY27, with the operating cost-to-income ratio at 36.62% versus 33.45% in Q1FY26. Total cost-to-income ratio was 45.86% in Q1FY27 against 41.34% in Q1FY26.

Yield compression. Interest income as a percentage of average loan portfolio declined to 22.46% in Q1FY27 from 23.51% in Q1FY26 and 24.03% in FY2025.

Liquidity conditions. The company noted it borrowed ₹4,500 Mn at an all-inclusive cost of 8.33% “despite not so favourable liquidity conditions.”

Seasonality. Q1 was described as the “seasonally weakest quarter” for collections.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Record disbursements. Q1FY27 disbursements reached ₹14,964 Mn, described as a historical best, growing 23% quarter-on-quarter and 16% year-on-year.

Portfolio growth. AUM reached ₹137,218 Mn, up 4% sequentially and 10% year-on-year. The five-year AUM CAGR was approximately 24%.

Improving collections. Unique customer collection efficiency (excluding NPA loans) was 97.9% in Q1FY27 versus 98.1% in Q4FY26; x-bucket collections were 99.2% versus 99.3%. The current book improved to 83.30% from 82.69% sequentially.

Cost of funds reduction. Book cost of funds fell to 8.80% in Q1FY27 from 8.95% in Q4FY26 and 9.54% in Q1FY26 — about 75 bps reduction over four quarters and about 130 bps over five years. Incremental borrowing cost was 8.33%.

Branch expansion. 856 branches across 11 states/UTs, with 12 branches opened during the quarter.

Liquidity buffer. Total liquidity of ₹22,972 Mn (₹18,472 Mn unencumbered cash and cash equivalents plus ₹4,500 Mn unavailed bank/FI sanctions). LCR was 296% at June 30, 2026 versus 258% at March 31, 2026. No cumulative ALM mismatch in any time bucket.

Capital strength. CRAR was 51.25% at June 30, 2026; debt-equity ratio was 1.03.

Credit ratings. ICRA AA- Stable, CARE AA- Positive, India Ratings AA- Positive; short-term CARE A1+.

Digital and technology leverage. Digital collections were 76% in Q1FY27. Turnaround time improved to 8 days in Q1FY27 from 12 days in FY23.

Fundraising capacity. The 42nd AGM on August 31, 2026 will consider issuance limits for NCDs aggregating up to ₹5,000 Crores through private placement.

Governance and audit. Limited review by Deloitte Haskins & Sells carried an unmodified opinion. No auditor qualifications; multiple RBI inspections with nil divergences. Appointment of Sreeram Ranganathan Iyer as Additional Director (Non-Executive Independent Director) effective July 25, 2026; extension of Chief Compliance Officer tenure by three years from October 1, 2026; proposed appointment of M/s Suri & Co as Joint Statutory Auditors for three years from the 42nd AGM.

3. Key Risks

Credit and asset quality risk. Gross Stage 3 at 3.46% and 30+ DPD at 12.38% indicate continued portfolio stress. Stage 3 ECL provision coverage was 40.14% at June 30, 2026, down from 41.40% at March 31, 2026 and 50.02% at June 30, 2025.

Collateral and concentration risk. All loans are secured against borrower property, usually self-occupied residential property. Portfolio is concentrated in southern India — Andhra Pradesh 36%, Tamil Nadu 28%, Telangana 19% — with the top three states accounting for 83%.

Funding and liquidity risk. The company flagged unfavourable liquidity conditions. Projected debt repayments are ₹5,901 Mn in Q2FY27, ₹7,640 Mn in Q3FY27, ₹5,641 Mn in Q4FY27 and ₹6,731 Mn in Q1FY28.

Interest rate and yield risk. Portfolio yield declined to 22.46% in Q1FY27 from 23.51% in Q1FY26; spreads depend on continued cost-of-funds management.

Operational and technology risk. The model relies on proprietary underwriting, ecosystem checks, digital collections and cloud infrastructure, with a 24x7 Security Operations Center monitoring for threats.

Regulatory risk. Operations are subject to RBI directions and SEBI LODR requirements; the company is an NBFC-ND-SI.

Forward-looking statement risk. The presentation notes risks including fluctuations in earnings, ability to manage growth, competition, economic growth, ability to attract and retain skilled professionals, government policies, and interest and fiscal costs.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Growth guidance. Management stated the company is “back on the growth trajectory” and that at the current pace of quarterly growth, “which will only improve in the coming quarters,” it “should be able to achieve our full year growth guidance very comfortably.” Observed: Q1FY27 disbursements of ₹14,964 Mn were a historical best, up 23% QoQ and 16% YoY; AUM grew 4% QoQ and 10% YoY. FY2026 disbursements of ₹46,757 Mn were lower than FY2025’s ₹49,697 Mn and FY2024’s ₹48,814 Mn.

Asset quality guidance. Management stated: “With our strong collections, we should see asset quality metrics consistently improving in the quarters to come.” Observed: 30+ DPD improved to 12.38% from 12.69% QoQ, and the current book improved to 83.30% from 82.69%. However, Gross NPA rose to 3.46% from 3.37% QoQ and Net NPA rose to 2.10% from 2.00%. Slippages were flat at 0.70%. Credit cost dropped to 1.85% from 1.88% QoQ.

Cost of funds. Management noted the ₹4,500 Mn borrowing at 8.33% “helped bring down our COF on the book from 8.95% for Q4FY26 to 8.80% for Q1FY27” and that this ensured spreads “remain intact (despite drop in yields).” Observed: book COF fell to 8.80%; NIM was 19.97% versus 20.07% in Q4FY26.

Quarter characterization. Management stated the quarter “panned out in line with our expectations, which vindicated the actions we undertook to overcome the challenges we faced.” Observed: PAT of ₹2,714 Mn was up 1% QoQ and 2% YoY; PBT of ₹3,620 Mn was up 1% QoQ and 2% YoY.

Historical context. The 10-year snapshot shows FY2026 gross Stage 3 at 3.37% versus 1.79% in FY2025, and FY2026 credit cost to income at 8.60% versus 4.04% in FY2025 — indicating the asset quality challenge intensified in FY2026 relative to prior years.

Broker Narrative

The narrative began as a high-growth formal-credit story with a proven underwriting model, strong pricing power, and branch-led sourcing. By the last report, the broker still had a Buy but acknowledged slower AUM growth, elevated opex, NIM compression, rising bad loans, and higher credit costs. The emphasis shifted from large structural tailwinds and low credit costs to defending growth through disbursement momentum, lower funding costs, and improved early delinquencies, while the optimistic tone remained despite growing asset-quality and cost pressures.

Fears that came true

  • The flagged deceleration in AUM growth materialised: AUM grew only 10% YoY in the last report versus the earlier >25% expectation, coinciding with many DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes after 2023.
  • The flagged risk of stress unfolding higher than anticipated materialised: GS3/NS3 rose to 3.45%/2.1%, Stage 3 PCR fell to ~40%, and write-offs remained elevated.
  • The flagged cost/asset-ratio increase materialised: opex rose 21% YoY due to salary revisions and incentives, with meaningful operating leverage unlikely in FY27.

Optimism that failed

  • The expectation of >25% AUM growth failed to materialise, as the last report guided only ~20% AUM growth for FY27 and actual AUM rose just 10% YoY.
  • The proven-underwriting/low-credit-cost optimism failed: credit costs were guided at 1.7-1.9% and Stage 3 rose, versus the historical <2.5% GNPL and <1% credit cost.
  • The strong pricing-power/spread-improvement optimism failed: NIM contracted ~10bp QoQ and yields were expected to moderate a further 10-15bp.
