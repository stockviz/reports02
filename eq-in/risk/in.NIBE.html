NIBE Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sharp swing to consolidated losses in Q1 FY27. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Group reported a loss before tax of ₹(1,523.23) lakhs and a net loss after tax of ₹(1,199.74) lakhs, compared with a profit before tax of ₹178.68 lakhs and net profit of ₹104.26 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Total comprehensive loss for the quarter was ₹(1,201.14) lakhs. Basic and diluted earnings per share turned negative at ₹(7.34), against ₹0.72 a year earlier.

Segment-level weakness. The Defence segment, the Group’s principal business, recorded a segment loss before tax of ₹(1,496.85) lakhs for Q1 FY27, versus a profit of ₹388.18 lakhs in Q1 FY26. The Aeronautics segment also recorded a loss before tax of ₹(26.38) lakhs.

Revenue decline. Consolidated revenue from operations fell to ₹6,301.42 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from ₹8,249.92 lakhs in Q1 FY26. Standalone revenue declined to ₹5,701.72 lakhs from ₹8,119.01 lakhs over the same comparison. Sequentially, consolidated revenue dropped sharply from ₹25,950.40 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

Standalone loss. On a standalone basis, Nibe Limited reported a net loss after tax of ₹(866.68) lakhs for Q1 FY27, compared with net profit of ₹389.03 lakhs in Q1 FY26, with basic and diluted EPS of ₹(5.99).

Subsidiary losses and unaudited component. The auditor’s review report notes that one subsidiary’s interim results — reflecting total revenues of ₹88.67 lakhs and a net loss after tax of ₹20.02 lakhs for the period April 1, 2026 to April 9, 2026 — were not reviewed and were furnished by management. Nibe Aeronautics Limited ceased to be a step-down subsidiary as of April 9, 2026.

Warrant subscription pending. As of June 30, 2026, 75% of the warrant subscription remained pending for 1,242,000 warrants, indicating that a portion of the preferential allotment proceeds had not yet been received.

Governance and compliance reminders. Shareholders holding physical shares were reminded to update KYC details and nomination choices in line with SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/POD-1/P/CIR/2024/37.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Major Indian Army contract. On August 14, 2026, NIBE signed a contract with the Indian Army for supply of the ‘Vayuastra’ loitering munition system under the Fast Track Procurement (FTP) route. NIBE was awarded the L2 vendor share, valued at approximately ₹563.35 crore. Deliveries are to commence in line with the contractual schedule agreed with the Indian Army. The company describes the order as expected to contribute meaningfully to its defence order book.

New MSEDCL order through subsidiary. On August 18, 2026, Nibe Space Private Limited, a 76%-owned subsidiary, received a letter of award worth ₹12,14,85,476 (exclusive of GST) from Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd (MSEDCL), a Government of Maharashtra undertaking. The scope covers GIS-based survey, mapping and digital data preparation of MSEDCL’s distribution network from 33 KV substations to end consumer service points, including overhead and underground infrastructure in the Pune and Kokan region. Execution is to occur in tranches within 42 months.

Defence portfolio breadth. The company’s defence portfolio spans loitering munitions, rocket and missile subsystems, unmanned aerial systems (UAS), counter-UAS (C-UAS) solutions, and precision-engineered components for the Indian Armed Forces, positioned alongside the Government of India’s Atmanirbhar Bharat and Make in India initiatives.

Expansion and funding plans. The company plans to acquire stakes in other corporates for growth and expansion, render financial assistance by way of investment or loans to its subsidiaries, and extend corporate guarantees for loans obtained by subsidiaries. The AGM notice includes special business for borrowing up to ₹1,000 crores in excess of limits under Section 180(1)(c), creating securities on properties, and approving material related party transactions with various subsidiaries and promoter group companies.

Preferential allotment proceeds deployed. Funds raised via preferential allotment (₹13,466.89 lakhs total, raised March 5, 2026 and June 8, 2026) were substantially utilized — ₹13,464.36 lakhs as of June 30, 2026 — across capex (₹2,400.00 lakhs), repayment of existing borrowing (₹7,500.00 lakhs), working capital (₹2,311.00 lakhs), and general corporate purposes (₹1,253.36 lakhs). No deviation or variation in use of funds was reported, with Crisil Ratings Limited as monitoring agency.

Dividend proposed. A dividend of ₹1.30 per equity share (13%) was proposed for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Earnings volatility: The swing from profit to loss between Q1 FY26 and Q1 FY27, and the sharp sequential revenue decline from Q4 FY26, indicate significant variability in business performance.
  • Segment concentration: The Group primarily operates in the defence sector, and the Defence segment’s loss before tax of ₹(1,496.85) lakhs in Q1 FY27 drove the overall loss.
  • Execution timelines: The MSEDCL order is to be executed in tranches over 42 months, and the Vayuastra contract deliveries are scheduled per the contractual timeline with the Indian Army, exposing the company to execution and delivery risk over extended periods.
  • Contract position: NIBE holds the L2 (second lowest) vendor share on the Vayuastra contract, not the lead position.
  • Related party exposure: Proposed material related party transactions with subsidiaries and promoter group companies, and corporate guarantees for subsidiary loans, concentrate financial exposure within the group.
  • Leverage and security creation: Proposed borrowing up to ₹1,000 crores in excess of Section 180(1)(c) limits and creation of securities on properties would increase financial obligations.
  • Pending warrant proceeds: 75% of warrant subscription for 1,242,000 warrants remained pending as of June 30, 2026.
  • Unreviewed subsidiary results: A portion of consolidated results rests on unaudited, management-furnished interim financials for one subsidiary.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Order book guidance versus reported results. Management states the Vayuastra order is “expected to contribute meaningfully to NIBE’s defence order book over [FY/delivery timeline], with deliveries to commence in line with the contractual schedule agreed with the Indian Army.” The press release leaves the FY/delivery timeline as a placeholder. Observed performance in the quarter in which the contract was signed (Q1 FY27, ended June 30, 2026) shows the Defence segment recording a loss before tax of ₹(1,496.85) lakhs and consolidated revenue declining year-on-year, so the anticipated contribution had not yet manifested in reported results.

Fund utilization guidance versus observed deployment. The preferential allotment objects allocated ₹12,685 lakhs to capex, ₹7,500 lakhs to repayment of existing borrowing, ₹2,500 lakhs to working capital, and ₹2,500 lakhs to general corporate purposes. Observed utilization as of June 30, 2026 was ₹2,400.00 lakhs for capex, ₹7,500.00 lakhs for borrowing repayment, ₹2,311.00 lakhs for working capital, and ₹1,253.36 lakhs for GCP — with no reported deviation or variation, and ₹2.53 lakhs remaining unutilized in a separate bank account.

Growth and expansion plans. Management’s stated plans to acquire stakes in other corporates, provide financial assistance to subsidiaries, and extend corporate guarantees are forward-looking proposals subject to shareholder approval at the AGM on September 29, 2026. No financial figures or completion timelines are provided for these plans in the supplied material.

Dividend proposal. A dividend of ₹1.30 per equity share (13%) for FY26 was proposed, despite the Q1 FY27 standalone and consolidated losses reported subsequently.
