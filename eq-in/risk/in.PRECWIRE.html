Headwinds and Challenges

  • The proposed acquisition of land carries a regulatory risk: if the land is agricultural, the requisite non-agricultural (NA) permission/conversion may not be obtained, or the company may otherwise not be legally permitted to continue holding such agricultural land. In that event, the company would need to take necessary steps to dispose of or sell the land in accordance with applicable laws.
  • The preferential issue of CCDs is conditional upon and subject to receipt of shareholder approval by special resolution at the EGM, in-principle approval of the National Stock Exchange of India Limited, and such other approvals, consents, permissions, and no-objections as may be required under applicable law.
  • The CCDs are unsecured, unrated, and unlisted, and carry a 12% per annum coupon payable in three pro-rata instalments after 180 days, 360 days, and for the balance residual period up to conversion.
  • The CCDs do not by themselves give the holder any rights of an equity shareholder of the company.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The company is raising an aggregate amount of ₹150 crore through the issue of Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) to support its working capital requirements and capital expenditure.
  • The objects of the preferential issue are to meet the funding requirement for new, ongoing, and future expansion and modernization projects, capital expenditure, and any other projects related to the business of the company, including long-term business investments, and to meet working capital needs.
  • The company proposes to utilise an amount of up to ₹10 crore out of the proceeds of the preferential issue towards the purchase/acquisition of suitable land, subject to identification of suitable land, completion of requisite legal and technical due diligence, applicable approvals and permissions, and compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.
  • The Silvassa Expansion and Modernization Project approved in the Board Meeting held on 14 February 2026 has been revised to 4620 MT/PA instead of the originally approved capacity of 3920 MT/PA, at a project cost of ₹38 crores. After completion of the project, net effective installed capacity will be 69,200 MT/PA by end of FY 2028.
  • An unsecured working capital facility of ₹200 crores from IDFC First Bank Limited was approved.
  • The company held an Extra-Ordinary General Meeting (EGM) on September 05, 2026, where members approved the issuance of 37,50,000 unsecured, unrated, and unlisted 12% Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) having a face value of ₹400 per CCD on a preferential basis to non-promoters, raising an aggregate amount of ₹150 crore. The special resolution was passed with 99.999% votes in favour.

Key Risks

  • The land acquisition risk described above, including the possibility that NA permission/conversion may not be obtained or that the company may not be legally permitted to continue holding agricultural land, requiring disposal/sale of the land in accordance with applicable laws.
  • The preferential issue is subject to statutory and regulatory approvals, including shareholder approval by special resolution at the EGM, in-principle approval of NSE, and other approvals, consents, permissions, and no-objections as may be required under applicable law.
  • The CCDs are unsecured, unrated, and unlisted.
  • The CCDs carry a 12% per annum coupon on face value, payable in three instalments calculated on a pro-rata basis after 180 days, 360 days, and for the balance residual period up to conversion into equity shares.
  • The CCDs do not give the holder any rights of an equity shareholder of the company.
  • The equity shares to be allotted upon conversion of CCDs will rank pari passu with the then existing paid up equity capital of the company.
  • The CCDs and the equity shares arising upon conversion are subject to lock-in requirements as prescribed under Chapter V of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, 2018, with effect from the date of allotment of the CCDs/equity shares, as applicable.
  • Each CCD shall be compulsorily converted into equity shares at any time after 12 months but not later than 18 months from the date of allotment, at the option of the CCD holder.
