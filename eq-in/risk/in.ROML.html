Headwinds and Challenges

  • Unresolved payments to unsecured operational creditors and public fixed deposit holders. The outstanding amount was Rs. 52.98 lakh as at June 30, 2026, compared with Rs. 57.73 lakh as at March 31, 2026. The statutory auditor drew attention to this in its Emphasis of Matter paragraph in the limited review report dated August 13, 2026, while stating that its opinion was not qualified in respect of the matter.
  • Application pending before the NCLT. The Company filed an application with the Hon’ble NCLT dated September 30, 2022 seeking directions for payments relating to the outstanding amounts owed to non-traceable unsecured operational creditors and public fixed deposit holders.
  • Sequential revenue decline. Revenue from operations was Rs. 3,555.73 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, lower than Rs. 4,163.46 lakh for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, though higher than Rs. 3,359.70 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Sequential profit decline. Profit before tax fell to Rs. 152.40 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 from Rs. 78.73 lakh for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and profit after tax fell to Rs. 111.30 lakh from Rs. 50.98 lakh over the same comparison. Total comprehensive income also declined to Rs. 111.30 lakh from Rs. 1,498.87 lakh.
  • Rising finance costs. Finance cost increased to Rs. 243.89 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 from Rs. 221.85 lakh for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 202.37 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
