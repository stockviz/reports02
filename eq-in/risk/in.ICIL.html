Indo Count Industries (ICIL) — Q1 FY27 Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Container availability constrained volume throughput in Q1 FY27, with volumes flat year-on-year at 23 Mn Mtrs despite demand recovery.
  • Product mix change within the Core business impacted realizations during the quarter.
  • Rising employee costs, attributed to commencement of operations at the Greenfield facility and scale-up across other facilities.
  • Bhilad (Gujarat) manufacturing facility operations have been temporarily affected since July 23, 2026 due to unprecedented rainfall and flooding. The surveyor’s assessment of damages is ongoing, and the insurance claim will be filed after that assessment. As a non-adjusting event, no effect was given in the Q1 FY27 results.
  • Finance cost drag: FY26 finance costs included interest of Rs. 1,281.81 lakhs paid on delayed refund of IGST to Maharashtra State GST authorities following their search and inspection of records in March 2026.
  • Managerial remuneration: FY26 remuneration was paid/provided in line with Section 197 approvals except for Rs. 296.43 lakhs, for which shareholder approval via special resolution is being sought at the ensuing AGM.
  • Labour Codes transition: the four Labour Codes notified on November 21, 2025 created an incremental past service cost (gratuity of Rs. 881.92 lakhs and compensated absences of Rs. 78.78 lakhs) recognised in FY26; the company continues to monitor finalisation of rules and clarifications.
  • Near-term global uncertainties remain, per management commentary.
  • General risk factors cited: fluctuations in earnings, managing growth, domestic and international competition, economic growth in India and abroad, ability to attract and retain skilled professionals, time and cost overruns on contracts, managing international operations, government policies and regulations, interest and other fiscal costs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Trade agreements and sourcing shifts: the global textile industry is entering a new phase with evolving trade agreements and changing sourcing patterns, creating a favourable environment for Indian exporters. The India-UK Free Trade Agreement implementation, along with progress on US and EU trade negotiations, is expected to strengthen India’s competitiveness.
  • Normalization of the US tariff scenario supported demand recovery and order flow, with QoQ volumes up ~12%.
  • New Business scaling: contributed one-third of total revenue in Q1 FY27, with contribution nearly tripling YoY; described as on track to double next year, with ~Rs. 1,500 Crs turnover.
  • Core Business described as reaching new levels, with ~Rs. 4,000 Crs turnover.
  • US Utility Bedding facilities reached 60%–65% utilization despite new facility ramp-up.
  • Indo Count 2.0 strategy: strengthening global presence, expanding value-added businesses, and creating sustainable value.
  • Brand and market position: among the top three global bed linen manufacturers in the US; owner of the 175+ year-old ‘Wamsutta’ brand; licensed brands added across Fashion, Utility, and Institutional Bedding in the US; investments in US utility bedding manufacturing.
  • ESG recognition: S&P Global ESG Score of 78 for 2025, ranked among the top 3% globally within Textile, Apparel and Luxury Goods peers; three CITI Textile Sustainability Awards 2026 for energy-efficient manufacturing, ESG integration, and responsible cotton sourcing.
  • Credit profile: ICRA AA- (Stable) / A1+ and CARE AA- (Stable) / A1+ ratings.
  • E-commerce sales penetration cited as a growth avenue.

3. Key Risks

  • Earnings volatility and growth-management risk.
  • Domestic and international competition.
  • Dependence on economic growth in India and abroad.
  • Talent attraction and retention.
  • Time and cost overruns on contracts.
  • Risks inherent in managing international operations (the group includes subsidiaries in the US, UK, Shanghai, and DMCC).
  • Government policy and regulatory changes, including the recently notified Labour Codes and GST-related matters.
  • Interest and other fiscal costs.
  • Container availability affecting volume throughput.
  • Near-term global uncertainties.
  • Operational disruption risk illustrated by the Bhilad flood event, with insurance recovery subject to surveyor assessment.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - FY27 revenue target of ~Rs. 5,500 Crores with ~13% EBITDA margin, implying 30%+ revenue growth and an incremental revenue addition of ~Rs. 1,300 Crores. - FY27 volume guidance of 105–110 Mn Mtrs (vs. 94.1 Mn Mtrs in FY26, +31%). - FY27e EBITDA margin of ~13% (vs. 11% in FY26, +200bps). - Aspiration to double revenue by 2028 over the FY25 base. - New Businesses targeted to double next year to ~Rs. 1,500 Crs turnover; Core Business at ~Rs. 4,000 Crs.

Observed performance in Q1 FY27: - Total Income of Rs. 1,224 Crs, up 26.5% YoY and 12.5% QoQ — an all-time high consolidated quarterly revenue. - EBITDA of Rs. 160 Crs with margin at 13.1%, up 74bps YoY and 241bps QoQ, already at the guided ~13% level. - PAT of Rs. 63 Crs, up 62.0% YoY; EPS of Rs. 3.19. - Volumes of 23 Mn Mtrs, flat YoY (container availability cited) but up ~12% QoQ. - New Business contributed one-third of total revenue, nearly tripling YoY. - US Utility Bedding utilization at 60%–65%. - Management characterised Q1 FY27 as a steady start toward the FY27 goal, with EBITDA margin recovery attributed to operating leverage from New Business scale-up and stronger Core Business volumes.

Other developments: - At the 37th AGM held August 25, 2026, all six resolutions passed with requisite majority, including adoption of financial statements, declaration of a final dividend of Rs. 1.50 per equity share, re-appointment of directors, and approval of waivers of recovery for excess managerial remuneration. - Officials will attend the Ashwamedh – Elara India Dialogue 2026 investor conference in Mumbai on September 2, 2026, with discussions based strictly on publicly available information and no UPSI intended to be discussed. - Q1 FY26 comparatives were restated following finalisation of purchase price allocations for the Fluvitex USA (81% stake, acquired September 2024) and Modern Home Textile (100% stake, acquired October 2024) acquisitions; restated Q1 FY26 PAT was Rs. 3,901.98 lakhs (vs. Rs. 3,779.49 lakhs reported) and EPS 1.97 (vs. 1.91).

Broker Narrative

The early narrative was recovery-driven, focused on inventory normalisation, cotton-price stability, FTA/export-policy support, and margin recovery via operating leverage. By the last report, the story shifted to new-business scale-up (up 198% YoY, 32% of revenue) as the main growth engine, with FY27 revenue/volume guidance and a 15-16% EBITDA margin target reiterated. Persistent themes were India’s sourcing-share gains, festive-demand momentum, and margin expansion; the changed elements were logistics/geopolitical disruptions, flood-related plant shutdowns, and a greater emphasis on diversification beyond core bed linen.

Fears that came true

  • The early risk of not maintaining optimum utilisation materialised when the Bhilad facility was temporarily shut due floods, and the latest actual return of +12.3% fell short of the +22.4%/+40.7% predicted on the two August 2026 calls.

Optimism that failed

  • The early expectation of cotton-price stabilisation failed, as raw material prices increased after geopolitical tensions.
  • The expected bed-linen volume recovery from inventory correction was undermined by container unavailability, which reduced bed linen volumes by 3% YoY and persisted into 2Q.
