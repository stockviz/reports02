GANESHHOU: Q1 FY27 Announcements Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin compression from land monetisation. The One 91 Thaltej land sale, completed during Q1 FY27, carried a higher acquisition cost because the land entered the company through a 2020 amalgamation. This depressed EBITDA margin to 39.3% in Q1 FY27, against 80.7% in Q4 FY26 and 84.8% in Q1 FY26. Management attributed the lower margin to the higher book value at which the amalgamated land was carried.

One-time tax burden. The same amalgamation history meant tax on the land sale was computed on the amalgamated company’s original acquisition cost, producing a higher one-time tax charge. PAT fell 32% sequentially and 55% year-on-year to Rs. 420 million, even as PBT rose 10% sequentially. Management described the elevated tax rate as specific to this quarter rather than an ongoing rate.

Revenue recognition timing. Malabar Retreat, at approximately 83% construction completion, could not have revenue recognised in Q1 FY27 because the project is incomplete under the applicable accounting standard. Q1 FY27 revenue was therefore largely land sale plus inventory from older completed projects, not the flagship ongoing residential project.

Execution and sales risk on large commercial development. Management explained that developing One 91 Thaltej as a 30-plus-floor commercial tower would have spread roughly Rs. 2,000–2,100 crore of value over about five years (three years of construction plus two years of sales), and chose upfront monetisation over that risk.

Leasing not yet complete. Around 43% of Million Minds leasable area is documented through executed LoIs (about 2.64 lakh sq ft), with a further 15–20% under negotiation. Portions of the SEZ space remain unleased, and full leasing is expected only over the next two to three months.

Godhavi timing uncertainty. Management declined to pinpoint a quarter for Godhavi monetisation, citing fluid developments in and around the area and a preference to observe for more quarters.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Ahmedabad market environment. The presentation cites Ahmedabad as Gujarat’s real estate nucleus with about 42% of state investments, an EMI-to-income ratio of roughly 18% in 2025, Metro Phase II, proximity to GIFT City, and selection as a host city for the 2030 Commonwealth Games. Property values are expected to appreciate 10–15% annually.

Million Minds commercial annuity. Phase I is in final completion stages, with fit-outs progressing and lease rentals guided to commence in Q4 FY27. Demand is coming from GCCs, technology companies, and managed co-working spaces. Management indicated FY28 could see a full year of rentals and that the figure could exceed Rs. 75 crore, above the Rs. 70 crore referenced in the call. The project is IGBC Platinum-rated, designed by RSP Architects, managed by Tishman Speyer, and spread across 65 acres with over 15 million sq ft of planned development.

Residential pipeline. Malabar Retreat is about 83% complete with 73 units booked (roughly 45% of total units) for a sale value of about Rs. 183 crore, also about 45% of total sale value. Phase II of Million Minds is guided for launch in Q3 FY27 and Phase I of the Million Minds residential project in Q4 FY27.

Land bank scale. Approximately 510–518 acres of fully paid land reserves, including 411 acres at Godhavi Township and 65 acres at Million Minds, with additional parcels under negotiation or acquisition not yet included in that figure and partial payments already made.

Capital allocation flexibility. Management framed land monetisation as part of capital allocation strategy rather than a response to liquidity needs, noting the balance sheet is almost debt-free and that cash is being held to strike land acquisition opportunities.

Long-term development target. The presentation cites a target of developing approximately 31 msf of infrastructure and special projects over the next few years with a total sales value of approximately INR 225,000 million.

3. Key Risks

  • Project mix and IndAS 115 timing. Management stated quarterly earnings are significantly influenced by project mix, stage of completion, and revenue recognition timing, and that quarterly margins should not be viewed in isolation.
  • Commercial leasing execution. Full leasing of Million Minds depends on concluding the remaining negotiations, including SEZ portions, within the stated two-to-three-month window.
  • Concentration of development in Million Minds. The rationale for selling One 91 Thaltej shifts development concentration toward the 65-acre Million Minds ecosystem, where multiple phases are planned.
  • Land acquisition deployment. Cash retained from monetisation is intended for land purchases described as under discussion; outcomes depend on striking deals at opportune moments.
  • Godhavi development fluidity. Plans for the 411-acre Godhavi parcel, described as a combination of township development, plotted development, and land sales, remain unquantified pending further observation.
  • Scheme of arrangement pending. The Scheme of Arrangement between Gatil Properties Private Limited and Ganesh Housing Limited received a no-adverse-observation letter from stock exchanges on July 06, 2026, and is under process of filing before the NCLT Ahmedabad bench, subject to regulatory and other approvals. No impact was given in the Q1 FY27 standalone results.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

FY27 guidance. Management guided FY27 revenue of approximately Rs. 1,000–1,200 crore and PAT of Rs. 300–325 crore. Revenue could rise substantially while PAT could rise about 10% or be maintained, attributed to the exceptional one-time tax hit in Q1 FY27.

Q1 FY27 actuals versus guidance context. Q1 FY27 revenue was Rs. 2,802 million (Rs. 280 crore), up 130% sequentially and 86% year-on-year. EBITDA was Rs. 1,100 million, PBT Rs. 1,049 million, and PAT Rs. 420 million. Against the full-year PAT guidance of Rs. 300–325 crore, Q1 PAT of Rs. 42 crore represents a modest start, consistent with management’s note that the one-time tax affected the quarter.

Cash flow versus revenue. Management stated that barring what might happen in the last quarter, most of the FY27 revenue would be realised in cash, with cash flows close to the Rs. 1,000 crore or Rs. 700 crore level for the year. Management also committed to publishing GDV and cash flow metrics going forward, emphasising cash flows over reported revenue.

Leasing timeline. Management guided full rentals from Q4 FY27, with FY28 expected to deliver a full year of rentals potentially exceeding Rs. 75 crore. This follows the Q1 FY27 position of 43% documented LoIs and 15–20% under negotiation.

Launch timeline. Phase II of Million Minds is guided for Q3 FY27 and Phase I of the Million Minds residential project for Q4 FY27.

FY28 guidance deferred. Management declined to provide FY28 guidance, preferring year-on-year guidance and indicating FY28 guidance may come in Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28, with a possible update within six weeks if traction develops.

Land sale disclosure constraint. Management could not disclose the One 91 Thaltej realisation amount because the buyer is also a listed entity and both companies must receive clearance before disclosing. Payment status was described as most payments normally coming in along with the sale, with details to follow once both companies are cleared to speak.
