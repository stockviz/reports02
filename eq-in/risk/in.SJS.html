SJS Enterprises — Recent Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • The company’s forward-looking statements flag potential risks from changes in economic conditions, demand and supply dynamics, competition, input cost variations, regulatory changes, geopolitical events, currency fluctuations, technological developments, or force majeure events.
  • The business is tied to automotive industry production cycles. In Q1 FY2027, automotive industry (2W + PV) production growth was 21.7% YoY, and SJS’s automotive business grew 32.4% YoY — meaning the company’s outperformance depends on continuing to outpace underlying industry growth.
  • The company is pursuing a registered office shift from Karnataka to Maharashtra, which requires member approval (obtained via postal ballot, deemed passed September 06, 2026 with 99.9984% in favour) and further regulatory approvals under the Companies Act, 2013.
  • The acquisition of the remaining 9.9% stake in Walter Pack Automotive Products India Private Limited (WPI) qualifies as a Related Party Transaction because Mr. Roy Mathew is a Whole-Time Director of WPI, though it is on an arm’s length basis.
  • WPI’s turnover declined from Rs. 1,746.52 Mn in FY 2024-25 to Rs. 1,668.96 Mn in FY 2025-26, indicating a year-on-year contraction at that subsidiary.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Q1 FY2027 delivered the highest ever quarterly revenue and profitability since IPO, with revenue of Rs. 2,610.0 Mn (up 24.5% YoY), EBITDA of Rs. 799.6 Mn (up 36.2% YoY, 30.0% margin), and PAT of Rs. 744.2 Mn (up 115.0% YoY).
  • This marked the 27th consecutive quarter of outperforming the automotive industry.
  • Growth was driven by the PV segment (up 45.4% YoY) and strong export momentum (exports up 83.2% YoY, contributing 9.8% of total revenue).
  • Key growth prospects cited include premiumization, building mega OEM accounts, expanding exports, new business breakthroughs with large OEMs and locations, and inorganic growth through acquisitions in adjacent categories like IMD/IML, IME, and plastic injection moulding.
  • Multiple new business wins were secured from leading OEMs including Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors, TVS Motors, Autoliv, Royal Enfield, Škoda, John Deere, Hero MotoCorp, and Maruti Suzuki, across domestic and international markets.
  • SDPL’s new manufacturing facility in Pune commenced commercial operations in August 2026, enhancing capacity.
  • The Board approved incorporation of a wholly owned subsidiary for the Cover Glass & Display business, with investment of up to INR 10,00,00,000 (Rs. 10 Crore), to engage in manufacturing, assembling, testing and sale of displays for automotive, consumer and other applications.
  • The in-house R&D Centre received recognition from the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR).
  • SJS achieved a CareEdge ESG Rating of 75.6.
  • The company completed acquisition of the balance 34,661 equity shares (9.90%) in WPI for Rs. 199.22 Mn, making WPI a wholly owned subsidiary, enabling closer operational alignment and cross-selling (SDPL won new programmes with Tata Motors; WPI won new business with M&M).
  • The company remains debt-free with a net cash position of Rs. 3,287.7 Mn as of June 30, 2026, and generated free cash flow of Rs. 837.5 Mn in the quarter. Consolidated ROE and ROCE stood at 20.3% and 37.2% respectively.

3. Key Risks

  • Forward-looking statements are subject to risks including significant changes in the economic environment in India and overseas, tax laws, inflation, and litigation.
  • Dependence on the automotive and consumer appliance industries, and on OEM relationships, exposes the company to demand cycles in those end markets.
  • The company’s stated risks include competition, input cost variations, regulatory changes, geopolitical events, currency fluctuations, technological developments, and force majeure events.
  • The registered office relocation and the WoS incorporation require regulatory approvals, introducing execution and timing risk.
  • The WPI acquisition is a related party transaction, though conducted at arm’s length pursuant to the SPA.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Management expects SJS to continue its strong financial performance trajectory, outperform underlying industry growth by 1.5 to 2x, and maintain robust margin profiles.
  • Management notes that over 88% of forecasted FY27 revenue is covered by the current order book.
  • Observed performance in Q1 FY2027: automotive business revenue grew 32.4% YoY versus automotive industry (2W + PV) production growth of 21.7% YoY — an outperformance of roughly 1.5x, consistent with the stated 1.5–2x guidance range.
  • Normalised PAT grew 45.2% YoY to Rs. 502.5 Mn, with margin expanding 274 bps YoY to 19.3% — the highest quarterly margin since listing, consistent with the guidance to maintain robust margin profiles.
  • Management stated it remains focused on expanding the premium portfolio, strengthening OEM partnerships, and investing in technology and capabilities, with a strong order book and diversified business model positioning the company for sustainable growth.
  • The company will participate in the ‘Anand Rathi G-200 Summit 2026’ on 21st September, 2026 in Mumbai for 1x1 and Group Meetings with investors and analysts; no unpublished price sensitive information (UPSI) is intended to be discussed.
  • The Board approved allotment of 2,22,875 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each under the SJS Enterprises - Employee Stock Option Plan 2021, increasing paid-up equity share capital from Rs. 32,02,66,540/- (3,20,26,654 shares) to Rs. 32,24,95,290/- (3,22,49,529 shares).
