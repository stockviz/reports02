Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential revenue decline: Revenue from operations fell to ₹1,091.20 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹1,279.91 crore in Q4 FY26, a drop of roughly 15% quarter-on-quarter.
  • Steel Pipes & Tubes segment weakness: Segment revenue declined to ₹1,069.32 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹1,258.77 crore in Q4 FY26, and segment results fell to ₹138.75 crore from ₹191.26 crore over the same period.
  • Rig segment near break-even: The Rig segment contributed only ₹0.30 crore in Q1 FY27, down from ₹1.34 crore in Q4 FY26, and recorded a negative result of ₹9.13 crore for the full year FY26.
  • Associate/JV losses: Share of loss from associates and joint ventures was ₹3.72 crore in Q1 FY27, compared with ₹3.51 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹3.19 crore in Q1 FY25, and ₹13.60 crore for FY26.
  • Tax expense volatility: Deferred tax moved from a credit of ₹30.30 crore in Q4 FY26 to a charge of ₹7.23 crore in Q1 FY27, contributing to a higher total tax expense of ₹56.01 crore versus ₹48.30 crore in the prior quarter.
  • Governance transition: The company appointed a new Whole-time Director (Mr. Shiv Kumar Singhal) and a new Independent Director (Dr. Raj Kamal Agarwal) effective 7 August 2026, both subject to shareholder approval.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year improvement: Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of ₹1,091.20 crore was lower than Q1 FY25’s ₹1,142.86 crore, but net profit rose to ₹270.78 crore from ₹233.86 crore, and EPS increased to ₹20.21 from ₹17.45.
  • Other income boost: Other income was ₹175.12 crore in Q1 FY27, compared with ₹159.71 crore in Q1 FY25 and a negative ₹47.99 crore in Q4 FY26.
  • Power segment stability: Power – Electricity revenue was ₹20.58 crore in Q1 FY27, broadly stable versus ₹20.51 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹20.84 crore in Q1 FY25, with segment results of ₹14.01 crore.
  • Rig segment year-on-year growth: Rig revenue rose to ₹15.10 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹9.06 crore in Q1 FY25.
  • Strong balance sheet: Total assets stood at ₹8,065.83 crore (standalone) and ₹8,086.10 crore (consolidated) as of 30 June 2026, with reserves and surplus of ₹7,061.86 crore (standalone) and ₹7,076.02 crore (consolidated).
  • Dividend and governance continuity: The 38th AGM on 15 September 2026 approved all seven resolutions, including adoption of financial statements, dividend declaration, director appointments/re-appointments, director remuneration, and ratification of cost auditors’ remuneration.
  • Subsidiary and associate footprint: The consolidated results include three subsidiaries, one joint venture (Gondkhari Coal Mining Ltd), and one associate (Jindal Pipe (Singapore) Pte Ltd), alongside MSL Seamless Tubes Limited, United Seamless Limited, Maharashtra Seamless Finance Limited, Maharashtra Seamless (Singapore) Pte Ltd, and Jindal Premium Connections Pvt Ltd.

  • Sequential earnings volatility: Net profit after tax fell to ₹107.53 crore in Q4 FY26 from ₹270.78 crore in Q1 FY27, illustrating quarter-to-quarter swings.
  • Unreviewed subsidiary and associate financials: The consolidated results include three subsidiaries whose interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors (total revenue ₹0.16 crore, net profit ₹0.12 crore, total comprehensive income ₹0.10 crore for Q1 FY27), and one associate whose interim financial information was not reviewed by its auditors (share of loss ₹3.72 crore).
  • Joint venture information gap: Gondkhari Coal Mining Ltd reported nil profit/loss for Q1 FY27, and its interim financial results were not available for review; management stated these are not material to the Group.
  • Foreign operations exposure: Exchange differences on translating foreign operations were negative ₹0.03 crore in Q1 FY27, compared with positive ₹46.39 crore in Q4 FY26.
