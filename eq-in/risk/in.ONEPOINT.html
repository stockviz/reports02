ONEPOINT: Recent Corporate Developments, Growth Drivers and Risks

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty. The company operates in an environment exposed to macroeconomic volatility, shifts in global trade and economic policy, geopolitical tensions, fluctuating crude oil and commodity prices, and supply chain disruptions. Its forward-looking statements explicitly flag uncertainties arising from the behaviour of the financial industry, from future integration of businesses, and from numerous other matters of national, regional and global scale, including political, economic, business, competitive and regulatory factors.

Competitive intensity and shifting customer expectations. The company faces intense competition from global BPM companies and digital consulting firms, alongside changing customer expectations toward AI-enabled solutions. Meeting these expectations requires continuous investment in AI and automation capability.

Integration and execution demands. The company has pursued a rapid series of acquisitions — ITCube Solutions (SPA executed 22 February 2024; final tranche completed and announced 16 July 2026), a 73.68% equity interest in Itnity Pte. Ltd. by One Point One Singapore Pte. Ltd. during FY2025-26, and control over the Netcom Group by One Point One Solutions MENA Holdings Ltd. with effect from 28 February 2026 (agreement dated 19 December 2025). Each of these brings integration risk, and the company itself cites uncertainties from future integration of businesses.

Operational, financial and compliance risks. Identified risks include currency exchange rate fluctuations, credit risks, potential liquidity pressures, cybersecurity threats, talent acquisition and retention challenges in AI and automation, regulatory compliance changes, and technology infrastructure risks.

Accounting and regulatory transition. The Government of India, vide Notification dated 21 November 2025, notified the Labour Codes (Code on Wages 2019, Industrial Relations Code 2020, Code on Social Security 2020, and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020). Under Ind AS 19, the company recognised past service costs of Rs. 37.67 lakh (standalone) and Rs. 147.06 lakh (consolidated) as an exceptional item for the year ended 31 March 2026. The company continues to monitor finalisation of rules by central and state governments, including clarifications or additional guidance, and will assess accounting implications as developments occur.

Disclosure compliance lapse. The exchange sought clarification (email dated 22 June 2026) regarding non-submission of the declaration for unmodified opinion in the financial results filed on 27 May 2026. The company confirmed the Statutory Auditors (M/s. SIGMAC and Co.) had issued an unmodified opinion on standalone and consolidated results for the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026, regretted the inadvertent omission, and submitted the required declaration on the NEAPS portal under the Result Adequacy Accuracy Module (reply dated 23 June 2026).

No dividend. The Board did not recommend a dividend for FY 2025-26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Dual growth engines: global delivery and Agentic AI. Management describes growth as driven by the dual engines of global delivery and Agentic AI (ResolX), with full integration of Netcom operations expanding the global delivery footprint and nearshore presence in LATAM (Costa Rica, Colombia, Panama). Management states that Q1FY27 marks a defining phase in the company’s transformation as strategic investments translate into business momentum, positioning 1Point1 Solutions to address a significantly larger market opportunity through global delivery, intelligent automation and measurable business outcomes.

Rapid global adoption of Generative AI. Opportunities cited include the rapid global adoption of GenAI, Agentic AI platforms (ResolX) offering Resolution-as-a-Service, and increasing demand for technology-led BPM services with outcome-linked commercial models.

ITCube acquisition completed. The acquisition of 100% of ITCube Solutions Private Limited was completed with the final tranche (24%) announced on 16 July 2026, following the first tranche (76%) and the SPA dated 22 February 2024. ITCube is engaged in IT and ITES, specialising in Business Process Management services and global software consulting and development, with expertise in Microsoft technologies (SharePoint, D365, Power Platform, .NET, React, Python), ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 27001 accreditations, and Microsoft Gold Partner status. The company states the acquisition strengthens its IT and ITES capabilities, provides a resilient revenue base, an experienced workforce, and a diversified client portfolio across multiple geographies, and would result in a better financial and operating structure by expanding service offerings and enhancing technological capabilities. ITCube’s reported turnover was INR 5,128.03 lakh in FY 2025-26, INR 4,867.69 lakh in FY 2024-25, and INR 5,389.97 lakh in FY 2023-24. The total transaction value was INR 84,00,04,632, entirely in cash, comprising first tranche consideration of INR 58,44,85,636 for 76%, second tranche consideration of INR 15,00,00,600 for 24%, and buyback proceeds payable by ITCube to the Sellers in connection with the pre-closing buyback of 905 shares. No governmental or regulatory approvals were required, and the acquisition is not a related party transaction.

Netcom and LATAM expansion. The Netcom Group acquisition expands operations in Latin America, adding nearshore presence in Costa Rica, Colombia and Panama.

Domestic and structural tailwinds. Opportunities include robust domestic consumption, government focus on digital public infrastructure, growth in global capability centres in India, and cross-selling digital transformation services to a diversified client portfolio across BFSI, healthcare, telecom, retail and utilities. Management expects to scale in healthcare (especially US RCM), BFSI and telecom, pursue international expansion through new delivery capabilities and acquisitions, and maintain focus on operational efficiency, standardisation and margin enhancement.

Fundraise. The Board approved raising up to Rs. 9,00,00,000 via a preferential issue of up to 15,00,000 warrants convertible into equity shares to non-promoter investor Raavi Enterprise at Rs. 60 per warrant. Proposals also include increasing borrowing limits up to Rs. 500 crores.

Recognition. The company was honoured with the ‘Best Use of AI in Customer Service’ award at the India CX Strategy & Summit 2026, validating its AI-led solutions.

Scale and footprint. The company describes itself as a global AI-powered customer experience and enterprise operations company with dual listings on NSE and BSE, over 8,000 professionals, 10 global delivery centres across 4 continents, 17 years of delivery expertise, 100+ clients, and ISO 42001 certification for its ResolX AI platform. Earlier material referenced nine global delivery centres and more than 6,000 professionals serving clients across the US, Europe, Asia and India.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Q1FY27 performance. Management reports that in Q1FY27 total income reached INR 161.9 crores (62.4% QoQ, 117.3% YoY growth), revenue from operations reached INR 158.3 crores (64.6% QoQ, 129.4% YoY growth), EBITDA reached INR 39.38 crores (56.0% QoQ, 91.5% YoY growth), and PAT reached INR 16.31 crores (58.7% QoQ, 72.8% YoY growth). Revenue growth is attributed to full integration of Netcom operations and growing demand for ResolX. Management states Q1FY27 marks a defining phase as strategic investments translate into business momentum.

FY2025-26 consolidated performance. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 31,338.08 lakh (up 22.24% YoY) and consolidated PAT was Rs. 3,820.54 lakh (up 15.38% YoY). Standalone revenue from operations was Rs. 22,794.91 lakh for the year ended 31 March 2026, against Rs. 20,143.99 lakh for the year ended 31 March 2025. Standalone profit for the year was Rs. 3,018.87 lakh, against Rs. 2,760.92 lakh in the prior year. Consolidated profit for the year was Rs. 3,820.54 lakh, against Rs. 3,315.87 lakh in the prior year. Consolidated total income was Rs. 33,103.19 lakh, against Rs. 27,016.89 lakh in the prior year.

Segment mix. Standalone revenue from rendering of services was Rs. 19,489.26 lakh in India and Rs. 3,305.65 lakh outside India. Consolidated revenue from rendering of services was Rs. 19,562.51 lakh in India and Rs. 11,775.57 lakh outside India.

Balance sheet expansion. Consolidated total assets rose to Rs. 83,681.51 lakh as at 31 March 2026 from Rs. 50,085.43 lakh as at 31 March 2025, with goodwill on consolidation rising to Rs. 31,596.24 lakh from Rs. 3,524.40 lakh, and non-current borrowings rising to Rs. 14,122.94 lakh from Rs. 148.85 lakh. Consolidated cash and cash equivalents rose to Rs. 5,501.44 lakh from Rs. 910.89 lakh.

Guidance themes. Management expects to continue leveraging ResolX and outcome-based operating models, utilise Netcom’s capabilities to grow the business, expand operational capacity with new delivery infrastructure, scale in healthcare (especially US RCM), BFSI and telecom, pursue international expansion through new delivery capabilities and acquisitions, and maintain focus on operational efficiency, standardisation and margin enhancement. Management notes that forward-looking statements address expected future business and financial performance but does not provide specific numerical financial guidance and explicitly states it does not undertake to update forward-looking statements.

Governance calendar. The 18th AGM is scheduled for Friday, 25 September 2026, through video conferencing, with a cut-off date of Friday, 18 September 2026 for remote e-voting.
