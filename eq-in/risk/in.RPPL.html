Rajshree Polypack Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Geopolitical uncertainty: Export revenue growth of 30.1% to ₹70.08 crore in FY26 was achieved “despite geopolitical uncertainties,” and management refers to “temporary geopolitical disruptions” as a factor in the operating environment.
  • Joint venture losses: The company’s share of loss in Olive Ecopak Private Limited amounted to ₹86.95 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Because accumulated share of losses exceeded the company’s interest in the JV during Q3 FY2024-25, no further share of losses has been recognised since the carrying amount of the investment was reduced to Nil, in compliance with Ind AS 28.
  • Regulatory scrutiny of filings: On June 25, 2026, the NSE sought clarification on the audited financial results, including a machine-readable/legible copy not being submitted and the results not appearing to follow Schedule III/Ind AS format because standalone and consolidated figures were identical. The company responded on June 29, 2026, explaining that the only difference between standalone and consolidated results relates to the JV share of profit or loss, and that identical figures arose because no further JV losses were recognised after the investment carrying amount reached Nil. The company noted the Exchange had raised similar clarifications for Q4 FY2024-25 and Q1–Q3 FY2025-26, each time accepting the same explanation.
  • Margin compression versus the preceding quarter: While Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin of 16.05% rose year-on-year from 14.64%, it was lower than Q4 FY26’s 17.14%. PAT margin of 7.04% was also below Q4 FY26’s 6.96% on a comparable basis but below the Q4 FY26 level of 6.96%… (Q1 FY27 PAT margin 7.04% vs Q4 FY26 6.96% — a sequential improvement, though EBITDA margin declined sequentially).
  • Finance costs: Finance costs of ₹243.66 lakh in Q1 FY27 remained elevated relative to the year-ago quarter’s ₹285.28 lakh, though lower sequentially from ₹242.26 lakh in Q4 FY26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record quarterly revenue: Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was the highest ever at ₹102.91 crore, up 24.71% year-on-year from ₹82.51 crore in Q1 FY26.
  • Profit growth: Q1 FY27 PAT was ₹7.25 crore, up 76.83% year-on-year from ₹4.10 crore, with PAT margin expanding to 7.04% from 4.97%.
  • EBITDA growth: Q1 FY27 EBITDA was ₹16.52 crore, up 36.75% year-on-year, with margin at 16.05% versus 14.64%.
  • Capacity expansion:
    • Injection moulding capacity reached a new milestone of 5,800 MT per annum following a 1,000 MT addition since FY23.
    • Sleeving capacity increased from 1,275 lakh units per annum to 1,675 lakh units per annum.
  • Export momentum: Export revenue grew 30.1% to ₹70.08 crore in FY26, with demand sustained across international markets.
  • JV performance: Olive Ecopack reported an improved EBITDA margin of 26.77% in Q1 FY27, strengthening the sustainable packaging platform in paper-based food packaging.
  • Renewable energy initiative: A group captive wind-solar arrangement of approximately 1.9 MW is scheduled to be commissioned by October 2026, expected to meet nearly 30% of energy requirements through renewable sources and generate annual savings of around ₹1.75 crore.
  • Industry outlook: India’s packaging industry is expected to reach US$92 billion by FY30, driven by demand from packaged food, dairy, quick service restaurants, and organised retail, as well as sustainability, premiumisation, and convenience packaging trends.
  • New order: On July 23, 2026, the company announced a repeat order from an existing customer — a large packaging manufacturer listed on an Indian stock exchange, majorly owned by a Finland-based packaging giant — for supply of plastic rigid sheets, valued at approximately ₹1.03 crore, to be fulfilled by August 2026 or as mutually agreed.
  • Product and market expansion: Management cites continued scale-up of the injection moulding business, new product development, expanding customer applications, and deepening presence across domestic and export markets.

3. Key Risks

  • Geopolitical disruptions affecting export markets.
  • Joint venture exposure: Continued losses at Olive Ecopak, with the company’s share of losses no longer being recognised after the investment carrying amount was reduced to Nil; any future recovery or additional funding requirements are not quantified in the material.
  • Regulatory/compliance risk: Repeated Exchange clarifications regarding the format and content of financial results submissions, including identical standalone and consolidated figures.
  • Margin volatility: Sequential decline in EBITDA margin from Q4 FY26 (17.14%) to Q1 FY27 (16.05%).
  • Dependence on customer concentration: The repeat order disclosure indicates reliance on existing customers for incremental business.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The company notes that non-historical statements are based on current assumptions and expectations, and actual results may differ materially due to risks, uncertainties, and external factors.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Renewable energy: Management guides that the ~1.9 MW group captive wind-solar arrangement is expected to become operational by October 2026, meeting nearly 30% of energy requirements and generating annual savings of around ₹1.75 crore. As of the Q1 FY27 press release (August 5, 2026), the project was not yet commissioned.
  • Capacity: Management reports injection moulding capacity reached 5,800 MT per annum following a 1,000 MT addition since FY23, and sleeving capacity increased to 1,675 lakh units per annum — both presented as achieved milestones.
  • Growth outlook: Management states it remains confident of delivering sustainable long-term value, supported by an integrated platform, diversified product portfolio, and disciplined execution, with focus on strengthening customer relationships, expanding product offerings, and investing in innovation, sustainability, and manufacturing capabilities.
  • Observed performance versus guidance: Q1 FY27 delivered the highest-ever quarterly revenue (₹102.91 crore), 24.71% YoY growth, EBITDA up 36.75% YoY, and PAT up 76.83% YoY, consistent with management’s stated focus on operational efficiency, product mix improvement, and capacity utilisation. Export revenue growth of 30.1% in FY26 and the July 2026 repeat order support the stated export and customer-relationship focus.
  • Investor communications: The company scheduled an earnings conference call for August 6, 2026, and a Non-Deal Roadshow for investors/brokers on September 4, 2026, at Awfis BKC, Mumbai, organized by Churchgate Partners, where officials stated they will refer only to information already in the public domain and will not share unpublished price-sensitive information.
  • AGM: The 15th Annual General Meeting was held on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, with all eight resolutions passed with requisite majority through remote e-voting and e-voting during the meeting; the notice was dated August 5, 2026, and the cut-off date for voting eligibility was September 8, 2026.
