Headwinds and Challenges

  • Continued operational losses. The standalone results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 show a loss before exceptional items and tax of INR (539.40) lakhs, following a loss of INR (994.48) lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and INR (2,798.41) lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. The consolidated loss before exceptional items and tax was INR (1,000.07) lakhs for the June 2026 quarter.
  • Suboptimal capacity utilisation and weak market conditions. The auditors’ Emphasis of Matter refers to continued operational losses, suboptimal utilisation of production capacity, and prevailing market conditions affecting liquidity.
  • Liquidity pressure. The Emphasis of Matter notes prevailing market conditions affecting liquidity, and management’s stated objectives include reduction of debt and strengthening liquidity.
  • Discontinued operations remain loss-making. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, discontinued operations recorded revenue of INR 5.89 lakhs against expenses of INR 123.93 lakhs, a net loss of INR (118.04) lakhs. The year ended March 31, 2026 saw a discontinued-operations loss of INR (2,332.03) lakhs.
  • Prior-year inventory impairment. Exceptional items for the previous year included an impairment of inventories of INR (3,646.60) lakhs, attributed to tariff-related uncertainties impacting demand and geopolitical developments, which lowered the net realisable value of certain inventories below carrying cost.
  • New Labour Code impact. Exceptional items for the previous year included INR (107) lakhs relating to a gratuity obligation arising from the change in actuarial computation of the defined benefit obligation following the broadened wage definition under the New Labour Code.
  • Joint venture reporting gap. The consolidated results include the Group’s share of net profit of Gruppo P&P Loyal SPA, a foreign joint venture, based on financial information not subjected to review or audit. The auditors note non-compliance with Regulation 33(3)(h) of the SEBI LODR because the joint venture’s results became material to the Group after the holding company reported a net loss for the quarter. The company states it is taking steps to have the joint venture’s financial results reviewed at the earliest date.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Asset monetisation progress. The company states it has made considerable progress in monetising underutilised and non-core assets and has identified further assets for monetisation, including assets classified as “Held for Sale,” with the objective of reducing debt, strengthening liquidity, and improving cost efficiencies.
  • Profit on sale of assets. Exceptional items for the current quarter include a profit on sale of assets of INR 233.76 lakhs from disposal of idle plant and machinery identified as surplus to operating requirements. The previous year included a profit on sale of assets of INR 3,381.64 lakhs from disposal of land, wind mills, and idle plant and machinery.
  • Operational realignment. The company states it continues efforts towards realignment and rationalisation of manufacturing operations to improve capacity utilisation, enhance operational efficiencies, and optimise costs to achieve sustainable profitability at the operational level.
  • Sequential improvement in standalone results. The standalone loss before exceptional items and tax narrowed from INR (994.48) lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to INR (539.40) lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Standalone loss after tax narrowed from INR (1,727.71) lakhs to INR (333.68) lakhs over the same comparison.
  • Joint venture contribution. The consolidated results include a share of profit from the joint venture of INR 644.70 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a share of loss of INR (684.15) lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026.
  • Lower finance costs. Standalone finance costs declined to INR 501.30 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from INR 674.29 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and INR 949.34 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

Key Risks

  • Dependence on successful implementation of initiatives. The auditors state that the company’s ability to achieve operational profits is completely dependent on the successful implementation of the operational rationalisation, cost optimisation, and asset monetisation measures.
  • Material unreviewed joint venture information. The Group’s share of net profit after tax and total comprehensive income of INR 644.70 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 in respect of the foreign joint venture is based on interim financial information furnished by management that has not been reviewed by auditors, and this information is material to the Group.
  • Regulatory non-compliance. The consolidated results could not comply with Regulation 33(3)(h) of the SEBI LODR in respect of the overseas joint venture.
  • Sustained losses. Losses have continued across the standalone and consolidated results for the periods presented, including a standalone loss after tax of INR (6,621.76) lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Inventory valuation sensitivity. The prior-year impairment of INR (3,646.60) lakhs shows exposure of inventory carrying values to tariff-related uncertainties, geopolitical developments, and demand conditions.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance. Management states it is confident of achieving sustainable operational profitability in the near future, citing progress achieved so far in operational improvement and liquidity enhancement. The company also states it continues efforts towards realignment and rationalisation of manufacturing operations, and has identified certain assets for monetisation, including assets classified as “Held for Sale,” to reduce debt, strengthen liquidity, and improve cost efficiencies.
  • Observed performance. The quarter ended June 30, 2026 still recorded a standalone loss before exceptional items and tax of INR (539.40) lakhs and a consolidated loss before exceptional items and tax of INR (1,000.07) lakhs. The standalone loss after tax was INR (333.68) lakhs and the consolidated loss after tax was INR (149.65) lakhs for the quarter. The profit on sale of assets of INR 233.76 lakhs is reported as an exceptional item rather than as operational profitability. The auditors note that the company’s ability to achieve operational profits is completely dependent on the successful implementation of the stated initiatives.

  • The 80th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, September 22, 2026, through video conferencing or other audio-visual means.
