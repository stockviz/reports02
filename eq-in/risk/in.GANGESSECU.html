Ganges Securities Limited — Q1 FY2026-27 Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak sequential performance in the standalone investing business. On a standalone basis, total income for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was ₹62.93 lakhs, marginally lower than ₹67.67 lakhs in the same quarter a year earlier (June 30, 2025). Interest income rose to ₹56.67 lakhs from ₹55.72 lakhs, but the company recorded no dividend income in the quarter, compared with the prior-year quarter when dividend income was also absent at the standalone level. Standalone net profit fell to ₹8.08 lakhs from ₹12.89 lakhs year-on-year.

Sharp sequential decline in standalone profitability. Compared with the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (which showed a loss of ₹33.73 lakhs), the June 2026 quarter returned to profit, but the March quarter itself had been weak, with total expenses of ₹93.89 lakhs against income of ₹59.90 lakhs.

Tea business losses at the segment level in the prior quarter. In the consolidated segment results, the Tea Business reported a segment loss of ₹393.97 lakhs for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, against a profit of ₹60.12 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the Tea Business segment recorded a loss of ₹128.29 lakhs.

Volatile other comprehensive income. Other comprehensive income swung sharply, from ₹2,140.82 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025 to ₹6,032.33 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026 on a standalone basis, and from a negative ₹8,380.95 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. This volatility reflects fair-value movements in the investment portfolio rather than operating performance.

Dependence on a single subsidiary for consolidated results. The consolidated results include Cinnatolliah Tea Limited, whose interim financials were reviewed by another auditor and not by the principal auditor. That subsidiary contributed total revenue of ₹712.45 lakhs, net profit of ₹114.18 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of ₹123.42 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Consolidated revenue growth. Consolidated total revenue from operations rose to ₹770.45 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹635.63 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. This was driven by tea sales of ₹712.45 lakhs, up from ₹565.39 lakhs a year earlier.

Consolidated profitability improved. Consolidated profit before tax was ₹145.96 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with ₹131.77 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated net profit after tax rose to ₹122.27 lakhs from ₹103.18 lakhs. Consolidated earnings per share improved to ₹1.22 from ₹1.03.

Investing business segment remains profitable. The Investing Business segment reported a profit of ₹93.47 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, up from ₹70.10 lakhs in the same quarter a year earlier, and higher than the ₹32.73 lakhs reported in the March 2026 quarter.

Tea business returned to profit. After the segment loss of ₹393.97 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, the Tea Business reported a segment profit of ₹56.57 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, close to the ₹60.12 lakhs recorded in the June 2025 quarter.

Proposed loan to Morton Foods Limited. The Board approved, subject to shareholder approval, an unsecured loan of up to ₹15,00,00,000 (Rupees fifteen crores) to Morton Foods Limited, extended in the ordinary course of business and on an arm’s length basis. This reflects deployment of the company’s investment-oriented balance sheet.

Large investment asset base. Segment assets for the Investing Business stood at ₹61,388.97 lakhs as of June 30, 2026, up from ₹55,641.54 lakhs as of March 31, 2026, indicating growth in the investment portfolio.

3. Key Risks

Concentration in investment income. The standalone business is entirely an Investing Business, with revenue dominated by interest income and, in some periods, dividend income. Standalone dividend income was nil in the June 2026 quarter, while it was ₹567.91 lakhs for the full year ended March 31, 2026, showing the variability of this income stream.

Exposure to tea operations through the subsidiary. The consolidated entity depends on Cinnatolliah Tea Limited, whose results are subject to tea price, cost, and inventory dynamics. Consolidated expenses include cost of materials consumed of ₹232.40 lakhs, change in inventories of finished goods of negative ₹378.98 lakhs, and employee benefits expense of ₹598.65 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter.

Counterparty exposure from the proposed loan. The proposed unsecured loan of up to ₹15 crores to Morton Foods Limited is subject to shareholder approval and is unsecured, creating credit exposure.

Reliance on other auditors for subsidiary review. The principal auditor did not review the subsidiary’s interim financial statements and based its conclusion solely on the report of another auditor, which limits the scope of assurance over consolidated figures.

Volatility in comprehensive income. Total comprehensive income on a standalone basis was ₹6,040.41 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with negative ₹8,414.68 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹2,153.71 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, reflecting significant swings in items that will not be reclassified to profit or loss.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The source material does not contain explicit forward-looking management guidance or projections. The observable performance against the company’s stated positioning is as follows:

  • The company describes itself as having only one business segment, the Investing Business, at the standalone level. Observed standalone performance shows interest income of ₹56.67 lakhs and no dividend income in the June 2026 quarter, with net profit of ₹8.08 lakhs, compared with ₹12.89 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.
  • The consolidated results include the Tea Business through Cinnatolliah Tea Limited. The Tea Business moved from a segment loss of ₹393.97 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter to a segment profit of ₹56.57 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, while for the full year ended March 31, 2026 it recorded a segment loss of ₹128.29 lakhs.
  • The Board’s approval of the ₹15 crore unsecured loan to Morton Foods Limited, subject to shareholder approval, indicates a decision to deploy capital in the ordinary course of business on an arm’s length basis. No guidance on expected returns from this loan is provided in the source material.
  • The auditors’ limited review reports for both standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 state that nothing came to their attention indicating material misstatement, and the Board approved the results at its meeting on August 13, 2026.
