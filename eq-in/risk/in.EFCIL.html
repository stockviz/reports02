EFC (I) Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin compression. While Q1 FY27 revenue grew 29% year-on-year to ₹2,828.79 million, EBITDA margin declined to 43.5% from 46.5% in Q1 FY26, and EBITDA fell 14% quarter-on-quarter from ₹1,435.7 million in Q4 FY26. PAT margin improved year-on-year to 25.1% but remained below the FY26 full-year trajectory in the sequential comparison.

Sequential revenue decline. Revenue contracted 3% quarter-on-quarter (₹2,928.8 million in Q4 FY26 versus ₹2,828.79 million in Q1 FY27), indicating a pause in the sequential growth trajectory even as annual comparisons remain strong.

Rising leverage and lease obligations. Non-current lease liabilities rose to ₹8,362.1 million in FY26 from ₹4,856.9 million in FY25, and non-current borrowings increased to ₹2,379.1 million from ₹2,108.9 million. Finance costs rose to ₹562.1 million in FY26 from ₹456.8 million in FY25. The balance sheet expanded sharply, with total assets at ₹26,750.9 million versus ₹16,991.7 million a year earlier.

Working capital build-up. Trade receivables increased to ₹2,446.5 million in FY26 from ₹983.2 million in FY25, and other financial assets (non-current) rose to ₹11,597.1 million from ₹4,590.2 million, reflecting significant capital deployment.

Cash flow pressure. Net cash from operating activities was ₹2,085.4 million in FY26, but cash flow from financing activities was negative ₹9,907.9 million, and cash and cash equivalents declined to ₹105.0 million from ₹171.1 million.

Industry-level restraints. The institutional interior fit-out market faces rising input/material costs and fragmented procurement with long public tender cycles.

Execution and integration demands. The company is simultaneously integrating the Ultrafresh acquisition, adding a large Pune managed-office facility, and shifting its registered office — a combination that places demands on management bandwidth and operational infrastructure.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong annual growth. FY26 revenue rose 58% year-on-year to ₹10,366.8 million, EBITDA grew 43% to ₹4,683.2 million, and PAT grew 67% to ₹2,346.5 million. Q1 FY27 continued the momentum with 29% revenue growth and 52% PAT growth year-on-year.

Enterprise demand and client stickiness. Enterprise clients contributed 65% of revenue, with an average enterprise client tenure of 52 months and the top 10 clients accounting for 25% of revenue. The company served over 780 clients across 25 cities in Q1 FY27.

GCC-led office demand. GCC leasing reached approximately 10.3 million sq ft in Q2 2026, with H1 2026 absorption of approximately 19.6 million sq ft, up 17% year-on-year. GCCs drove 62% of Fortune 500 leasing and 53% of transactions above 100,000 sq ft.

Flex and managed space mainstreaming. Flexible workspace accounted for 27% of Q2 2026 office leasing — the second-largest occupier segment after GCCs — and 24% of H1 2026 leasing across the top eight cities, up from 21% in H1 2025. Flex operators drove 38% of Pune’s Q2 leasing.

Pune expansion. On August 17, 2026, EFC announced a fresh five-year lease of an entire A+ category building at Koregaon Park Annex comprising 95,897 sq ft, with revenue potential exceeding ₹70 crores and adding 2,000+ seats. Full-building control is expected to support branding, customer experience, space utilisation, and margin optimisation.

Ultrafresh acquisition. On August 18, 2026, the Board approved the acquisition of 100% of Ultrafresh Modular Solutions Limited (10,44,783 equity shares) for ₹53,99,98,920, discharged through the issuance of up to 19,99,996 equity shares at ₹270 per share on a preferential basis. Ultrafresh is a 51% subsidiary of TTK Prestige, operates in modular kitchens, wardrobes, and customised home interiors, owns a manufacturing plant at Nalagarh, Himachal Pradesh, and reported turnover of ₹36.32 crores (FY 2025-26), ₹32.49 crores (FY 2024-25), and ₹31.20 crores (FY 2023-24). The acquisition is intended to strengthen EFC’s furniture manufacturing and Design & Build verticals.

Order book visibility. Design & Build carries an order book of ₹2,280 million with 85+ designers and engineers, 52+ clients, and 5.91 million sq ft designed across 20+ locations. Furniture manufacturing carries an order book of ₹53+ crores.

Asset ownership model. The company owns 279,460 sq ft across four properties (Sprint, Wakdewadi; Sprint Towers; Marisoft IT Park; Konark Alpha). A case study cites gross rental income of approximately ₹5.25 crores at an 18% gross rental yield in the first 12 revenue months, capital appreciation of approximately ₹10 crores (39% over 16 months), and an annualised IRR of approximately 30%.

Industry drivers. The India furniture market is supported by urban migration (+2.1% CAGR impact), government housing push under PMAY (+2.3%), omnichannel and AR visualisation (+1.8%), commercial and hospitality expansion (+1.6%), and rising disposable income (+1.4%).

Infrastructure-led micro-markets. Pune crossed the 100-million-sq-ft office market mark, and CBRE expects occupiers to selectively pursue tier-II city expansion through 2026.

3. Key Risks

Integration risk. The Ultrafresh acquisition, the new Koregaon Park facility, and the registered office shift are concurrent initiatives requiring execution across three verticals.

Approval dependency. The preferential issue of up to 19,99,996 equity shares is subject to shareholder approval via postal ballot and applicable stock exchange and regulatory approvals. The acquisition is expected to complete on or before October 31, 2026, with allotment within 15 days of shareholder resolution or receipt of last approval.

Dilution. The issuance of up to 19,99,996 equity shares at ₹270 per share will expand the equity base; allottees include TTK Prestige Limited and eight other non-promoter entities.

Concentration and cyclicality. Top 10 clients contribute 25% of revenue, and the business is exposed to commercial real estate cycles, enterprise capex decisions, and occupancy ramp-up timing.

Input cost and procurement risk. Rising input/material costs and fragmented procurement with long public tender cycles affect the institutional fit-out market.

Leverage and refinancing. Lease liabilities and borrowings have grown materially, with finance costs rising year-on-year.

Forward-looking statement risk. The investor presentation notes that forward-looking statements are subject to known and unknown risks, uncertainties, and assumptions, and actual results could differ materially.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Management commentary (Q1 FY27). Chairman & MD Umesh Sahay stated that FY27 began on a positive note with continued momentum, driven by disciplined execution, sustained enterprise demand, and focus on profitable, capital-efficient growth. Priorities stated were deepening enterprise relationships, improving asset productivity, maintaining discipline in expansion, and driving sustainable profitability. Management expressed confidence in delivering consistent growth based on a strong operating platform, improving scale benefits, and a healthy business pipeline.

Observed performance. Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹2,828.79 million represented 29% year-on-year growth, consistent with the stated momentum, but declined 3% sequentially. EBITDA grew 20% year-on-year but declined 14% sequentially, and EBITDA margin contracted both year-on-year (43.5% vs 46.5%) and sequentially (vs 49.0% in Q4 FY26). PAT grew 52% year-on-year and 3% sequentially, with PAT margin at 25.1% versus 21.3% in Q1 FY26 and 23.5% in Q4 FY26. The stated emphasis on “profitable, capital-efficient growth” is set against a quarter of margin compression and a sequential revenue dip.

Expansion commentary. On the Koregaon Park lease, management stated that complete control over the premises would enable consistent workplace experience, operational efficiency, margin optimisation, and flexibility in configuring space, and that all three core verticals — Managed Office Spaces, Design & Build, and Furniture Manufacturing — are scaling together. The revenue potential of more than ₹70 crores over the five-year lease and the addition of 2,000+ seats are stated expectations; realised occupancy and revenue ramp-up are not yet reported.

Registered office shift. Management noted that upon receipt of member approval, the Board will determine the effective date of the shift having regard to completion of necessary infrastructure and facilities at the proposed premises to ensure smooth and effective operations. The shift is expected to facilitate operational efficiency and optimisation of administrative costs.

Investor engagement. Officials will interact with analysts and investors at the Anand Rathi Annual Flagship Conference G-200 Summit 2026 in Mumbai on September 22, 2026, at 10:00 AM, with no unpublished price sensitive information to be shared. The 42nd AGM is scheduled for September 30, 2026, at 12:00 P.M. IST through video conferencing, with remote e-voting from September 27 to September 29, 2026, and a cut-off date of September 23, 2026. Agenda items include adoption of standalone and consolidated audited financial statements for FY 2025-26, re-appointment of Mr. Abhishek Narbaria as a Director retiring by rotation, and a special resolution to shift the registered office from Shivajinagar to Hinjewadi Phase-I, Pune.
