Aditya Birla Money — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential earnings decline. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, Profit Before Tax fell to ₹1,540.18 lakh from ₹2,571.53 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and Profit After Tax fell to ₹1,112.74 lakh from ₹1,873.24 lakh. Total Income also declined sequentially to ₹13,145.75 lakh from ₹13,362.93 lakh.
  • Broking segment weakness. Broking segment results dropped sharply to ₹101.13 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹875.32 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹569.29 lakh in the June 2025 quarter, even as broking revenue was broadly flat sequentially (₹9,814.20 lakh versus ₹9,880.72 lakh).
  • Rising finance costs. Finance costs rose to ₹4,367.05 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹4,023.74 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹3,159.22 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Higher employee and depreciation costs. Employee benefits expense increased to ₹3,173.59 lakh from ₹2,619.51 lakh sequentially, and depreciation and amortisation rose to ₹358.51 lakh from ₹295.52 lakh.
  • Margin compression. Operating margin declined to 11.78% in the June 2026 quarter from 19.81% in the March 2026 quarter and 18.25% in the June 2025 quarter. Net profit margin declined to 8.51% from 14.43% and 13.64% respectively.
  • Elevated leverage. Outstanding debt rose to ₹2,30,628.04 lakh as at June 30, 2026 from ₹2,17,928.54 lakh as at March 31, 2026 and ₹1,65,839.99 lakh as at June 30, 2025. The debt-equity ratio rose to 7.36 times from 7.26 times sequentially and 6.58 times a year earlier. Total debts to total assets rose to 68.38% from 62.87% as at March 31, 2026.
  • Weaker interest coverage. The interest service coverage ratio declined to 1.44 times in the June 2026 quarter from 1.72 times in the March 2026 quarter and 1.75 times in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Low retail participation in voting. Of 42,755 shareholders on the record date of July 23, 2026, only 102 public shareholders and 1 promoter/promoter group shareholder attended the 30th AGM through video conferencing, with no shareholders present in person or by proxy. Public non-institution votes polled represented 0.2984% of their outstanding shares, and public institutions polled no votes.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year revenue growth. Total Revenue from Operations for the June 2026 quarter was ₹13,077.22 lakh, up from ₹11,270.79 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Interest income rose to ₹7,074.70 lakh from ₹5,537.60 lakh, and fees and commission income rose to ₹4,620.01 lakh from ₹4,421.12 lakh.
  • Wholesale debt market stability. Wholesale debt market segment revenue was ₹3,195.04 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, up from ₹3,034.01 lakh sequentially and ₹3,064.48 lakh year-on-year, with segment results of ₹1,383.90 lakh.
  • Net worth accretion. Net worth increased to ₹31,333.63 lakh as at June 30, 2026 from ₹30,025.27 lakh as at March 31, 2026 and ₹25,207.22 lakh as at June 30, 2025. Reserves excluding revaluation reserve/other equity rose to ₹30,768.54 lakh from ₹29,460.18 lakh sequentially.
  • Proposed capital increase. Shareholders approved, at the 30th AGM on July 30, 2026, an increase in the Authorised Share Capital of the Company and consequent alteration of the Memorandum of Association, passed as an Ordinary Resolution with 99.9986% of votes polled in favour.
  • Board continuity. Mr. Gopi Krishna Tulsian was re-appointed as Non-Executive Director, retiring by rotation, via a Special Resolution passed with 99.9986% of votes polled in favour.
  • Full-year profitability base. For the year ended March 31, 2026, Total Income was ₹47,333.13 lakh, Profit Before Tax was ₹7,870.92 lakh, Profit After Tax was ₹5,847.59 lakh, and Earnings Per Share was ₹10.35 (annualised).

3. Key Risks

  • Leverage and coverage risk. With the debt-equity ratio at 7.36 times and interest service coverage at 1.44 times as at June 30, 2026, debt servicing capacity has narrowed relative to prior periods.
  • Concentration in broking. Broking accounted for ₹9,814.20 lakh of the ₹13,077.22 lakh total segment revenue in the June 2026 quarter, and its segment result fell to ₹101.13 lakh, making overall profitability sensitive to broking performance.
  • Cost inflation. Finance costs, employee benefits, and depreciation all increased sequentially, compressing margins.
  • Regulatory cost impact. The Government of India implemented four new Labour Codes, including the Code on Wages, 2019, with effect from November 21, 2025. The Company recorded an exceptional item for the year ended March 31, 2026 of ₹312.65 lakh, comprising gratuity of ₹2.78 crore and compensated absences of ₹0.34 crore, arising primarily from a change in wage definition.
  • Impairment charges. Impairment on financial instruments was ₹28.36 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, up from ₹2.43 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Low shareholder engagement. Voting participation by public non-institutions was 0.2984% of their outstanding shares, and public institutions cast no votes on any resolution.

  • Director re-appointment. The Special Resolution to re-appoint Mr. Gopi Krishna Tulsian as Non-Executive Director passed with 41,594,021 votes in favour and 603 against, representing 99.9986% of votes polled in favour.
  • Financial statement adoption. The Ordinary Resolution to adopt the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended March 31, 2026 passed with 41,594,054 votes in favour and 570 against.

The statutory auditor, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, issued a Limited Review Report dated July 14, 2026 on the June 2026 quarterly results, stating that nothing came to their attention causing them to believe the results contained any material misstatement or failed to disclose required information.
  • Labour Code impact assessment.
