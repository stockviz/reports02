PALASH SECURITIES LIMITED — Analysis of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Loss-making standalone operations. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone total income was ₹12.45 lakhs against total expenses of ₹16.51 lakhs, producing a loss before tax of ₹4.06 lakhs and a net loss after tax of ₹5.47 lakhs. This continues the pattern seen in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 (net loss of ₹5.76 lakhs) and the quarter ended 31 March 2026 (net loss of ₹29.49 lakhs).

Sharp decline in revenue scale. Standalone revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹3.70 lakhs, compared with ₹4.26 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and ₹1.46 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, standalone total income was ₹408.03 lakhs, supported by dividend income of ₹358.65 lakhs and a gain on sale of investments of ₹13.69 lakhs — neither of which recurred in the June 2026 quarter.

Loss of control over Morton Foods Limited (MFL). During the previous year ended 31 March 2026, a rights issue by MFL in which the Holding Company did not participate reduced the Group’s aggregate shareholding (including subsidiaries and nominee shareholders) from 51.37% to 44.95%. Consequently, the Group ceased to have control over MFL with effect from 18 November 2025, and MFL ceased to be a subsidiary from that date. MFL is now an associate accounted for under Ind AS 28.

MFL’s going-concern uncertainty. The associate, MFL, incurred a net loss of ₹352.66 lakhs during the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and its net worth has substantially eroded as of that date. MFL’s auditors, in a review report dated 6 August 2026, reported that these conditions indicate a material uncertainty regarding MFL’s ability to continue as a going concern. MFL’s financial information has nonetheless been prepared on a going-concern basis, based on management’s positive outlook towards its growth and ability to continue.

Discontinued food processing operations. Following the loss of control over MFL, the business operations of MFL — which constituted the only separately identifiable operating segment of the Group — ceased to be consolidated. The Group’s food processing segment revenue, which was ₹1,343.58 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025, was nil in the quarter ended 30 June 2026. The Group now has a single reportable segment (Investing Business), and separate segment reporting is no longer applicable after that date.

Consolidated losses. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, consolidated total income was ₹39.87 lakhs against total expenses of ₹24.28 lakhs, but the share of loss of the associate (₹158.52 lakhs) drove a loss before tax of ₹142.93 lakhs and a net loss after tax of ₹146.54 lakhs. This compares with a net loss after tax of ₹302.23 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and ₹143.2 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026.

Volatile other comprehensive income. Consolidated other comprehensive income swung from negative ₹8,375.57 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 to positive ₹4,528.59 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, reflecting fair value movements on the investment portfolio. Standalone other comprehensive income was negative ₹2.45 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, versus positive ₹24.90 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

Fee and commission expense volatility. Standalone fees and commission expenses rose to ₹6.83 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from ₹0.09 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, though they were broadly comparable to ₹6.40 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Investment portfolio scale. Consolidated segment assets for the Investing Business stood at ₹56,861.12 lakhs as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹52,285.10 lakhs as of 31 March 2026. Standalone other equity was ₹2,852.49 lakhs as of 31 March 2026.

Recovery in fair value gains. The consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 include a net gain on fair value changes of ₹19.95 lakhs, compared with a net loss on fair value changes of ₹22.25 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Consolidated other comprehensive income attributable to owners was positive ₹4,528.59 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter.

Rental income stability. Standalone rental income was ₹8.75 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, consistent with ₹8.75 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and ₹9.44 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, rental income was ₹35.69 lakhs.

Prior-year gain on loss of control. The Group, upon loss of control over MFL, derecognised MFL’s assets and liabilities, derecognised non-controlling interest, and recognised the retained investment at fair value as of the date of loss of control under Ind AS 110. This resulted in a net gain of ₹2,413.64 lakhs recognised under “Other Income” in the consolidated financial results for the previous year ended 31 March 2026.

Retained associate stake. The Group retains a 44.95% equity interest in MFL as an associate, accounted for under Ind AS 28, with management expressing a positive outlook on MFL’s growth and ability to continue as a going concern.

Subsidiary structure. The Group comprises the Holding Company and four wholly-owned subsidiaries — Champaran Marketing Company Limited, Hargaon Investment & Trading Company Limited, Hargaon Properties Limited, and OSM Investment & Trading Company Limited — each held at 100.00% as of 30 June 2026, 31 March 2026, and 30 June 2025.

Full-year profitability in FY2025-26. Despite quarterly losses, the standalone results for the year ended 31 March 2026 showed a profit before tax of ₹350.12 lakhs and a net profit after tax of ₹236.08 lakhs, with basic and diluted earnings per share of ₹2.36. Consolidated net profit after tax attributable to owners for the year ended 31 March 2026 was ₹1,606.97 lakhs.

3. Key Risks

Going-concern risk at the associate level. MFL’s auditors have flagged a material uncertainty regarding MFL’s ability to continue as a going concern, citing a net loss of ₹352.66 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and substantial erosion of net worth. MFL’s financial information has been prepared on a going-concern basis based on management’s positive outlook.

Concentration in a single segment. Following the loss of control over MFL, the Group’s operations now represent a single reportable segment (Investing Business). The Group no longer has more than one reportable segment, and separate segment reporting is not applicable after 18 November 2025.

Dependence on investment income. Standalone revenue is driven by interest income, dividend income, and gains on sale of investments. Dividend income of ₹358.65 lakhs in FY2025-26 and gain on sale of investments of ₹13.69 lakhs did not recur in the June 2026 quarter, contributing to the quarter’s loss.

Fair value volatility. The consolidated results show significant swings in other comprehensive income and fair value gains/losses across quarters, reflecting mark-to-market movements on the investment portfolio.

Tax expense volatility. Standalone tax expense was ₹1.41 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with ₹24.38 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 (which included ₹17.85 lakhs related to earlier years) and nil in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Consolidated tax expense was ₹3.64 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, versus a credit of ₹55.24 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.

Reliance on other auditors. The auditors did not review the interim financial results of four subsidiaries (total revenues before consolidation adjustments of ₹27.41 lakhs, total net profit after tax of ₹17.45 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of ₹4,549.45 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026) or of one associate (Group’s share of net loss after tax of ₹158.52 lakhs and total comprehensive loss of ₹159.48 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026). The auditors’ conclusion relies solely on the reports of other auditors for these entities.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

MFL going-concern outlook. Management has expressed a positive outlook towards MFL’s growth and its ability to continue as a going concern in the foreseeable future, and MFL’s financial information has been prepared on a going-concern basis on that basis. Observed performance shows MFL incurred a net loss of ₹352.66 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with substantially eroded net worth, and MFL’s auditors reported a material uncertainty on its ability to continue as a going concern in their review report dated 6 August 2026.

Segment reporting. Management’s consolidated results note that, following the loss of control over MFL effective 18 November 2025, the Group no longer has more than one reportable segment, and separate segment reporting is not applicable for periods after that date. Observed segment data confirms the Food Processing Business segment revenue was nil in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, versus ₹1,343.58 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

Quarterly performance versus prior-year profitability. The standalone results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 show a net loss after tax of ₹5.47 lakhs, compared with a full-year net profit after tax of ₹236.08 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026. The consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 show a net loss after tax of ₹146.54 lakhs, compared with consolidated net profit after tax attributable to owners of ₹1,606.97 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026.

Auditor conclusions. The statutory auditors expressed an unmodified conclusion on both the standalone and consolidated unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with the consolidated conclusion not modified in respect of the MFL going-concern matter or the reliance on other auditors.

Annual General Meeting. The 12th Annual General Meeting of Palash Securities Limited was held on 5 August 2026, where all three ordinary resolutions — adopting the financial statements, re-appointing director Ms. Shalini Nopany, and appointing/fixing the remuneration of the Statutory Auditors — were passed with the requisite majority based on the scrutinizer’s report.
