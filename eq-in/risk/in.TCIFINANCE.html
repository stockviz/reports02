TCI Finance Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Regulatory action on NBFC registration. TCI Finance received a notice from the Reserve Bank of India directing it to surrender its Certificate of Registration for voluntary deregistration as an NBFC, on the grounds of non-maintenance of the minimum Net Owned Funds. The company filed a writ petition before the Hon’ble High Court of Telangana seeking to quash the notice and stay further proceedings. On 07.07.2025 the Court passed an interim order directing the RBI not to take coercive action until the next hearing and to file its reply by 04.08.2025. The matter was listed for multiple hearings; the RBI filed its reply and the interim stay continued. The matter was kept for final hearing on 28.01.2026, with the stay extended and the matter listed for final hearing on 10.06.2026. On 10 June 2026 the RBI took two weeks’ time to engage senior counsel, and the matter was listed for 9 July, with the interim stay extended until then. On 9 July senior counsel was engaged by the RBI, and the matter is now listed for final hearing.

Corporate guarantee exposures and claims. The company had given corporate guarantees to lenders of Amrit Jal Ventures Private Limited (AJVPL) and its wholly owned subsidiary, Gati Bhasmey Limited, aggregating to Rs. 25,619.80 Lakhs. During 2019-2020, certain lenders invoked the guarantees and raised claims on the company aggregating to Rs. 17,820.89 Lakhs. The company made a provision of Rs. 7,798.91 Lakhs on an estimated basis, considering the disputed nature of the claim and what it describes as unlawful invocation of the corporate guarantee. The balance liability of Rs. 17,820.89 Lakhs has been treated as a contingent liability.

Going concern uncertainty. Due to adverse developments in the entities to which the company has advanced loans, given guarantees, or made investments, the company had substantial exposures. These exposures adversely affect future incomes and have substantially affected operations, indicating uncertainties relating to the going concern status of the company.

Weak operating performance. Total revenue from operations was nil for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, nil for the quarter ended 30 June 2025, and nil for the year ended 31 March 2026. Total income for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was nil, compared with Rs. 0.11 Lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and Rs. 48.37 Lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026. The company reported a loss of Rs. 34.62 Lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, a loss of Rs. 41.54 Lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025, and a loss of Rs. 178.35 Lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026. Other equity stood at a negative Rs. 9,104.37 Lakhs as at 31 March 2026.

Guarantor-related liabilities. The company had availed borrowings from certain lenders and, by amendment to the loan agreement, arranged shares held by Mahendra Kumar Agarwal & Sons HUF and Manish Agarwal Benefit Trust (Guarantors) as additional security. Due to defaults by the company, the lenders sold these shares and recovered their dues fully. As a result, the company recognised a liability in favour of the guarantors of Rs. 364.25 Lakhs, being the amount of loan extinguished by the lenders out of the sale proceeds of the shares. Of this, Rs. 206.37 Lakhs has been paid, and Rs. 157.88 Lakhs remained outstanding as at 30 June 2026. During the period ended 30 June 2026, the lenders invoked shares given as guarantee by Mr. Mahendra Kumar Agarwal and realised Rs. 38 Lakhs; the company recognised a liability of Rs. 38 Lakhs in favour of Mr. Mahendra Kumar Agarwal in place of the original lenders.

Modified audit opinion. The statutory auditors qualified their opinion with respect to the matters in paragraphs 3 and 6 of the notes and emphasised the matter in paragraph 7. The limited review report states that, in the absence of adequate basis for recognition of partial liability, the company ought to have recognised the full liability in its books; had it done so, the loss for the quarter and other equity (negative balance) would have been higher by Rs. 17,820.89 Lakhs. The auditors also stated that, in the absence of sufficient and appropriate evidence and given the liabilities devolved on the company upon invocation of guarantees by lenders of other entities, preparation of financial statements on a going concern basis is not appropriate, and they were unable to comment on the effect on carrying values of assets and liabilities had the statements not been prepared as a going concern.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management’s revival efforts. Management stated it is in the process of identifying various alternatives and new areas to venture into for reviving the company. On this basis, the financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.

Interim judicial protection. The Telangana High Court’s interim order directing the RBI not to take coercive action remains in force, with the stay extended on multiple occasions, including until the 9 July listing and thereafter as the matter is listed for final hearing.

Investment holding. The company’s investment in Allcargo Gati Limited (earlier known as GATI Limited) underwent a stock split, pursuant to which 10 equity shares held by the company were subdivided into 63 equity shares. The total number of shares held increased from 284,838 to 1,794,479, without any change in the overall cost of investment, and the cost per share was adjusted proportionately. The stock split did not result in any change in the total carrying value of the investment as at the reporting date.

Write-backs. During an earlier year, the balance of interest payable previously recognised in the books was written back by Rs. 49.96 Lakhs, and such interest liability is no longer carried in the financial statements. Sundry creditors and sundry advances payable and receivable were written back and written off to the extent of Rs. 0.84 Lakhs and Rs. 2.68 Lakhs respectively, which are no longer payable and receivable.

Other comprehensive income. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, other comprehensive income was Rs. 3.87 Lakhs, comprising remeasurement gain on investments of Rs. 4.43 Lakhs and deferred tax on remeasurement gain on investments of Rs. 0.55 Lakhs. Total comprehensive income for the quarter was a loss of Rs. 30.74 Lakhs.

Shareholder support. At the 52nd Annual General Meeting held on 27 August 2026, shareholders passed two ordinary resolutions with 100% of valid votes cast in favour: adopting the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, along with the Director’s and Auditor’s reports, and reappointing Mr. I M Usman Sheriff as Director.

3. Key Risks

  • Uncertainty relating to the going concern status of the company, as stated in the notes and reiterated by the auditors.
  • The RBI notice directing surrender of the Certificate of Registration for voluntary deregistration as an NBFC on grounds of non-maintenance of minimum Net Owned Funds, with the matter pending final hearing.
  • Contingent liability of Rs. 17,820.89 Lakhs arising from invocation of corporate guarantees, which the auditors state ought to have been recognised in the books; recognition would increase the loss for the quarter and the negative other equity by that amount.
  • The auditors’ inability to comment on the effect on the loss for the current quarter and retained earnings had the matters in the basis for qualified conclusion been given effect in the financial results.
  • The auditors’ inability to comment on the effect on carrying values of assets and liabilities had the financial statements not been prepared as a going concern.
  • Nil revenue from operations across the reported periods and continued losses.
  • Negative other equity of Rs. 9,104.37 Lakhs as at 31 March 2026.
  • Outstanding liability of Rs. 157.88 Lakhs to guarantors as at 30 June 2026, plus the Rs. 38 Lakhs liability recognised in favour of Mr. Mahendra Kumar Agarwal during the quarter.
  • Adverse developments in the entities to which the company has advanced loans, given guarantees, or made investments, which adversely affect future incomes.
