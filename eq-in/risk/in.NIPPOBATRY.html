Headwinds and Challenges

The company’s FY 2025-26 performance reflects a loss before tax of Rs. 996.71 lakhs, with the restated figures showing a loss after tax of Rs. 1,030.71 lakhs for the year. The restated total comprehensive loss for FY 2025-26 stood at Rs. 1,023.96 lakhs.

Material headwinds cited include a steep rise in raw material costs, the conversion of battery-operated torches to rechargeable batteries, and changes in sales volume mix. These factors weighed on the business through the year.

The Q1 FY 2026-27 standalone results (quarter ended June 30, 2026) show a loss before tax of Rs. 294.03 lakhs and a loss after tax of Rs. 228.97 lakhs, indicating that profitability pressure persisted into the first quarter of the new financial year, even as demand conditions improved.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The Q1 FY 2026-27 results showed considerable recovery in demand and business results, with further progressive recovery expected in the remaining quarters of 2026-27.

The company completed the amalgamation of Helios Strategic Systems Limited (erstwhile wholly owned subsidiary) into the parent, approved by the NCLT Chennai Bench on March 10, 2026, with an appointed date of April 1, 2024, and effective from April 2, 2026. The amalgamation has been accounted for under the pooling of interests method, with prior period figures restated under Ind AS 103.

The consolidated entity includes subsidiaries Nippo Green Energy Private Limited and Medcuore Medical Solutions Private Limited. The consolidated Q1 FY 2026-27 results show revenue from operations of Rs. 11,614.05 lakhs, with a loss before tax of Rs. 322.00 lakhs and a loss after tax of Rs. 201.00 lakhs.

Segment reporting shows the Airpurifier segment contributed Rs. 29.48 lakhs in revenue for Q1 FY 2026-27, with a segment loss before tax and interest of Rs. 27.97 lakhs.

Key Risks

CCI penalty matter: In a suo moto case relating to cartelisation of zinc carbon dry cell batteries, the CCI imposed a penalty of Rs. 4,226 lakhs against the company. The company appealed before the NCLAT in 2018 against the CCI’s order dated April 2019. The NCLAT stayed the penalty with a direction to deposit 10% (Rs. 422 lakhs) with the NCLAT Registry, which the company deposited through an FD within the stipulated time. Based on legal opinion, no provision has been made for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022: The company is required to fulfil Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations from FY 2025-26 for its non-rechargeable portable batteries. Operational and implementation aspects, including the EPR compliance framework through the CPCB Portal and the pricing mechanism for EPR Credits, remain under deliberation with the MOEFCC and CPCB. Pending regulatory clarity and stabilisation of the implementation framework, the company is currently unable to reliably estimate the further financial impact, if any, which will be accounted for once a reliable estimate is established.

Raw material cost pressure: The steep rise in raw material costs remains a material risk to margins, as reflected in the FY 2025-26 loss.

Product transition risk: The conversion of battery-operated torches to rechargeable batteries and changes in sales volume mix pose ongoing challenges to the product portfolio and revenue composition.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management expects further progressive recovery of demand and underlying business results for the remaining quarters of 2026-27, following the improvement seen in Q1.

Observed performance provides partial support for this guidance: Q1 FY 2026-27 standalone revenue from operations was Rs. 11,584.57 lakhs, higher than the restated Q4 FY 2025-26 figure of Rs. 10,638.73 lakhs, though below the restated Q1 FY 2025-26 figure of Rs. 11,944.02 lakhs. The standalone loss before tax narrowed to Rs. 294.03 lakhs in Q1 FY 2026-27 from a restated loss of Rs. 2,020.47 lakhs in Q4 FY 2025-26, but the company remained in loss, and the Q1 FY 2025-26 comparative had shown a profit before tax of Rs. 118.80 lakhs.

The consolidated Q1 FY 2026-27 loss before tax of Rs. 322.00 lakhs compares with a restated loss of Rs. 2,049.10 lakhs in Q4 FY 2025-26 and a restated profit before tax of Rs. 118.80 lakhs in Q1 FY 2025-26.

The 53rd Annual General Meeting is scheduled for September 28, 2026, through video conferencing, with book closure from September 22 to September 28, 2026, and a record date of September 21, 2026. A dividend of Rs. 3.75 per equity share has been recommended for FY 2025-26, subject to member approval. Ms. Suneeta Reddy is standing for re-appointment as director.
