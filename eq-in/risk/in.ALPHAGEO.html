Headwinds and Challenges

  • Weak sequential profitability. Standalone revenue from operations fell to Rs. 4,084.28 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 from Rs. 4,683.56 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, and standalone profit before tax from continuing operations dropped to Rs. 105.97 lakhs from Rs. 1,701.37 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026. On a consolidated basis, revenue from operations was Rs. 4,084.28 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 versus Rs. 4,928.96 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, with a consolidated loss before tax of Rs. 423.56 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.
  • Full-year losses. For the year ended 31 March 2026, standalone loss before tax from continuing operations was Rs. 1,910.67 lakhs and loss after tax from continuing operations was Rs. 1,429.49 lakhs. Consolidated loss before tax was Rs. 1,881.69 lakhs and loss after tax was Rs. 1,394.63 lakhs.
  • Tax dispute exposure. A demand of Rs. 601.53 lakhs was raised in FY 2021-22 over disallowance of excess depreciation claimed on imported machinery. In FY 2024-25, the Managing Director received a separate demand of Rs. 1,644.78 lakhs in his personal capacity on the same matter, indemnified by the Company and recognised as a non-current income tax asset. Aggregate exposure of Rs. 2,246.31 lakhs is disclosed as a contingent liability, with no provision recognised.
  • FEMA proceedings and frozen deposits. Fixed deposits of Rs. 1,601.08 lakhs were provisionally seized in FY 2022-23 by the Directorate of Enforcement over alleged FEMA contraventions. A show cause notice dated 29 January 2026 was received from the adjudicating authority, and the matter remains under adjudication. No provision has been recognised; the matter is disclosed as a contingent liability.
  • Loss of subsidiary status. Alphageo Offshore Services Private Limited ceased to be a subsidiary of Alphageo (India) Limited effective 18 September 2026 after AGIL’s shareholding fell below 51% following renunciation of its entitlement in AOSPL’s rights issue.
  • Auditor emphasis of matter. The statutory auditors drew attention to both the FEMA proceedings and the tax demand matters in their review reports, though the opinion was not modified.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong year-on-year improvement. Standalone revenue from operations rose to Rs. 4,084.28 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 from Rs. 4,084.28 lakhs in the prior-year comparative, with standalone profit before tax of Rs. 105.97 lakhs versus a loss position in the March 2026 quarter. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 4,084.28 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.
  • Subsidiary contributions. The consolidated results include an Indian subsidiary whose interim results reflected total income of Rs. 3,126.47 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 199.73 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and a foreign subsidiary with total income of Rs. 24.93 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 12.45 lakhs.
  • Dividend. A dividend of Rs. 5/- per equity share was approved by members at the 39th Annual General Meeting held on 18 September 2026, with 11 September 2026 fixed as the record date.
  • Leadership continuity. Mr. Dinesh Alla was re-appointed as Chairman and Managing Director for five years effective 21 August 2026, with remuneration approved for three years. Mr. Sashank Alla’s remuneration as Whole-time Director was approved for the remaining two years of his tenure effective 29 September 2026.
  • Single-segment focus. The group operates in one reportable segment—geophysical data acquisition, processing and interpretation services—under Ind AS 108.

Key Risks

  • Contingent liabilities. Aggregate tax-related exposure of Rs. 2,246.31 lakhs and FEMA-related frozen deposits of Rs. 1,601.08 lakhs remain unresolved, with no provisions recognised based on management and external expert assessments.
  • Dependence on key personnel. Mr. Dinesh Alla has served as Chairman and Managing Director since 2017 and is associated with the Company since 1991; he is the spouse of Joint Managing Director Mrs. Savita Alla and father of Whole-time Director Mr. Sashank Alla.
  • Concentration and cyclicality. The business depends on seismic survey contracts across diverse terrains in India, with revenue concentrated in a single reportable segment.
  • Governance and related-party considerations. The re-appointment and remuneration resolutions for Mr. Dinesh Alla and Mr. Sashank Alla were subject to member approval at the 39th AGM, and both are related to each other and to Mrs. Savita Alla.
  • Subsidiary deconsolidation. The cessation of AOSPL as a subsidiary effective 18 September 2026 changes the group’s consolidation scope.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management assessed the tax matter as defensible on merits with likelihood of outflow not probable, and the FEMA matter as having a strong case on merits with likelihood of material outflow not probable at this stage. No provisions were recognised for either matter.
  • The Company recognised the Managing Director’s personal tax demand of Rs. 1,644.78 lakhs as a non-current income tax asset on the basis that it is indemnified by the Company.
  • Observed performance shows standalone loss before tax from continuing operations of Rs. 1,910.67 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026 and consolidated loss before tax of Rs. 1,881.69 lakhs, alongside a return to standalone profit before tax of Rs. 105.97 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.
  • The statutory auditors expressed unmodified opinions on both standalone and consolidated results, with emphasis of matter paragraphs on the FEMA and tax matters.
  • The Board approved the 39th AGM for 18 September 2026 via video conferencing, with 11 September 2026 as cut-off and record date, and all five resolutions were passed with the requisite majority.
