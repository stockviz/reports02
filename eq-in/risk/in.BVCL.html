Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates in an environment shaped by external pressures that can affect costs and demand. These include fluctuations in fuel and power costs, volatility in raw material prices, and rising logistics and freight expenses. It also faces intense market competition, changes in government policies and regulations, seasonal factors, adverse weather conditions, and supply chain disruptions.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management points to a positive long-term demand outlook for the cement industry, supported by rapid urbanisation, increasing per capita cement consumption, and government infrastructure investments. Specific drivers cited include increased investments in infrastructure, housing, commercial construction, and industrial development, as well as government expenditure on highways, railways, airports, irrigation projects, urban infrastructure, and affordable housing. The North-Eastern region’s lower per capita cement consumption is also identified as an opportunity. Government initiatives such as the National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP), PM Gati Shakti, Smart Cities Mission, and PMAY are cited as supporting demand.

Key Risks

The material risks identified are the external factors noted above: fuel and power cost fluctuations, raw material price volatility, increasing logistics and freight expenses, intense market competition, changes in government policies and regulations, seasonal factors, adverse weather conditions, and supply chain disruptions.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management’s forward-looking view is that the long-term demand outlook for the cement industry remains positive, supported by urbanisation, rising per capita cement consumption, government infrastructure investments, and initiatives such as NIP, PM Gati Shakti, Smart Cities Mission, and PMAY.

Against this, the reported financial performance for the year ended March 31, 2026 shows gross turnover increased by 2.27% to Rs. 21,170.59 Lakhs, while profit after tax decreased to Rs. 495.72 Lakhs. The Board did not recommend any dividend for the financial year 2025-26.

For the first quarter ended June 30, 2026, the standalone results show revenue from operations of Rs. 502.043 Lakhs (as printed in the statement), total income of Rs. 502.908 Lakhs, and net profit after tax of Rs. 301.60 Lakhs. The consolidated results for the same quarter show total income of Rs. 5,008.80 Lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 306.25 Lakhs. The statutory auditors issued an unmodified limited review report on both the standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

The Board also approved several governance and compliance matters at its meeting on August 13, 2026, including the re-appointment of Mr. Kamakhya Chamaria as Managing Director (subject to shareholder approval), the re-appointment of Mr. Nishant Garodia as a director retiring by rotation, the re-appointment of M/s RKKV & Associates as Cost Auditor for FY 2026-27, and the appointment of Mr. Deepak Virmani as Internal Auditor for FY 2026-27. The 27th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, September 29, 2026, at 03:00 P.M. through video conferencing or other audio-visual means.
