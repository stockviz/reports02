Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material cost inflation: Steep increases in feed raw material prices — fish meal, soybean meal and wheat flour — are cited as a material headwind, along with supply-side constraints. Higher input costs are linked to lower profit before tax and compressed margins.
  • Margin compression: In Q1 FY27, consolidated revenues grew 18.3% year on year to Rs 18,999 Mn on shrimp feed volume growth, but profit after tax fell 37.4% year on year to Rs 1,163 Mn, with margins dropping to 6.1% and EPS at 7.58. Consolidated PBT was INR 157 crores, down 15% quarter on quarter and 37% year on year, even as gross income rose to INR 1,966 crores (up 30% QoQ and 19% YoY).
  • Shrimp processing weakness: A drop in shrimp processing revenue and sales volume is identified as a challenge.
  • External and regulatory uncertainty: Climatic changes and diseases affecting aquaculture activity, withdrawal of reciprocal tariffs, geopolitical situations, uncertainties in the Indian and international economies, competition, and potential withdrawal of governmental fiscal incentives are all flagged as risks.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Feed volume momentum: Shrimp feed sales rose to 1,93,852 MTs in Q1 FY27, with strong volume growth in the shrimp feed business described as the driver of consolidated revenue growth.
  • Monsoon and weather: A favorable monsoon across India and easing of El Nino effects are cited as tailwinds.
  • Raw material outlook: Anticipated price stabilization of raw materials upon arrival of fresh crops.
  • Pet food segment: Growth in the pet food segment under the Avant Furst brand, with an estimated project investment of around INR 175 crores.
  • Exports and realizations: Potential export growth, favorable foreign exchange movements, improved average selling price realizations, and higher contributions from other income.

Key Risks

  • Cost-side pressure from fish meal, soybean meal and wheat flour prices, compounded by supply-side constraints.
  • Aquaculture production risk from climatic changes and disease.
  • Trade and policy risk from withdrawal of reciprocal tariffs, geopolitical situations, and possible withdrawal of governmental fiscal incentives.
  • Market and competitive risk from economic uncertainty in India and internationally, and competition.
  • Earnings sensitivity: the Q1 FY27 results show revenue growth coexisting with a sharp year-on-year decline in profitability, indicating margin vulnerability to input costs.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Feed sales: Management estimates FY27 feed sales of around 5,85,000 MTs. Observed Q1 FY27 feed sales were 1,93,852 MTs.
  • Shrimp exports: Management estimates FY27 shrimp exports of around 19,000 MTs. The Q1 FY27 disclosures note a drop in shrimp processing revenue and sales volume, which is the segment relevant to this guidance.
  • Pet food investment: Management estimates total investment for the pet food project at around INR 175 crores; the project is associated with the Avant Furst brand.
  • Profitability versus revenue: While revenue and feed volumes grew in Q1 2026, PBT and PAT declined year on year and margins fell to 6.1%, reflecting the raw material cost pressure management identifies as a headwind.
  • Forward-looking caveat: Management states that forward-looking statements on market opportunities and business prospects are subject to known and unknown risks, uncertainties and assumptions and are not guarantees of future performance.
