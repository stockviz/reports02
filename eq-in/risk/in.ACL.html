Andhra Cements Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sustained losses. The company reported a net loss of ₹3,593 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a net loss of ₹2,962 lakh in the same quarter a year earlier. Loss before tax widened to ₹4,777 lakh from ₹2,962 lakh year-on-year. Total expenses of ₹19,113 lakh exceeded total income of ₹14,336 lakh in the June 2026 quarter.

Sequential deterioration. Compared with the quarter ended March 31, 2026, income fell from ₹16,254 lakh to ₹14,336 lakh, while loss before tax increased from ₹4,080 lakh to ₹4,777 lakh. The March 2026 quarter had shown a net profit of ₹4,852 lakh, which turned into a net loss in the June 2026 quarter.

Full-year loss. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the company recorded a loss before tax of ₹15,648 lakh and a net loss of ₹6,716 lakh on total income of ₹45,239 lakh.

Cost pressure. Total expenses for the year ended March 31, 2026 were ₹60,887 lakh against total income of ₹45,239 lakh, indicating that costs remained well above revenue for the full year.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Deferred tax recognition. The company recognised deferred tax assets based on projected future taxable income, stating there is convincing evidence that sufficient taxable profits will be available to utilise carried-forward business losses, unabsorbed depreciation, and unused tax credits. In the previous year the company opted to be taxed under Section 115BAA of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and deferred tax assets and liabilities were remeasured using expected future tax rates. A tax credit of ₹1,184 lakh was recognised in the June 2026 quarter, reducing the net loss to ₹3,593 lakh from a loss before tax of ₹4,777 lakh.

Parent group support and related-party arrangements. The members approved material related party transactions with Sagar Cements Limited (the holding company) for an aggregate value up to ₹500 crores for one year, and with RV Consulting Services Private Limited for an aggregate value up to ₹100 crores for one year, both in the ordinary course of business and at arm’s length.

Proposed amalgamation. The Board on June 05, 2026 approved a proposed Scheme of Amalgamation under which Andhra Cements Limited would merge into Sagar Cements Limited with effect from April 01, 2026 (the appointed date), subject to approvals from stock exchanges, SEBI, shareholders, creditors, the National Company Law Tribunal, and other regulatory authorities.

Improved year-on-year income. Total income for the June 2026 quarter (₹14,336 lakh) was higher than the June 2025 quarter (₹10,058 lakh), even though the company remained loss-making.

3. Key Risks

Regulatory and approval risk on the merger. The Scheme of Amalgamation is subject to approval of stock exchanges, SEBI, shareholders and creditors of the respective companies, the National Company Law Tribunal, and other regulatory and governmental authorities. Completion is therefore contingent on multiple external approvals.

Dependence on projected profitability for deferred tax. Recognition of deferred tax assets rests on projected future taxable income. If those projections are not achieved, the basis for recognising the assets could be affected. The company also noted that any reversal of deferred tax liabilities is expected to be substantially offset against the reversal of deferred tax assets.

Continued loss-making operations. The company has reported losses at both the quarterly and full-year levels, with expenses exceeding income, which represents an ongoing operational risk.

Related-party concentration. The company has sought member approval for significant related-party transactions with its holding company and with RV Consulting Services Private Limited, reflecting reliance on group entities for business arrangements.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Deferred tax guidance versus reported results. The company’s stated basis for recognising deferred tax assets is projected future taxable income providing convincing evidence of sufficient taxable profits to utilise carried-forward losses, unabsorbed depreciation, and unused tax credits. Observed performance shows a net loss of ₹3,593 lakh for the June 2026 quarter and a net loss of ₹6,716 lakh for the year ended March 31, 2026, with a loss before tax of ₹15,648 lakh for the full year. The tax credit of ₹1,184 lakh in the June 2026 quarter reduced the reported net loss relative to the loss before tax.

Merger timeline. The Board approved the Scheme of Amalgamation on June 05, 2026 with an appointed date of April 01, 2026. The scheme remains subject to the approvals listed above, and no completion has been reported in the supplied material.

Minimum public shareholding. The parent company conducted an Offer for Sale of 1,38,25,821 equity shares on January 09, 2026, January 12, 2026, March 17, 2026, and March 18, 2026, reducing its shareholding in ACL from 90.00% to 75.00% to meet Minimum Public Shareholding requirements.

Shareholder approvals. At the 87th Annual General Meeting held on June 25, 2026, members approved the audited financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, the re-appointment of Mr. S. Sreekanth Reddy as a director retiring by rotation, ratification of remuneration of ₹5,00,000 plus reimbursement of applicable taxes and expenses to M/s. Narasimha Murthy & Co, Cost Accountants, for the financial year ending March 31, 2027, and the two material related-party transactions described above.
