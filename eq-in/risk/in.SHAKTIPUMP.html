Shakti Pumps (India) Limited — Recent Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Input cost pressures and lower realizations on select orders. Management stated that despite these pressures, EBITDA margins were held broadly stable sequentially, reflecting the resilience of the operating model and continued focus on profitable growth.
  • Margin compression visible in reported results. Consolidated EBITDA margin fell to 9.6% in Q1 FY27 from 23.1% in Q1 FY26, and PAT margin fell to 6.0% from 15.6%. For FY26, EBITDA margin was 15.6% versus 24.0% in FY25, and PAT margin was 9.5% versus 16.2%.
  • Global uncertainties affecting exports. Management noted the Export business sustained healthy momentum despite global uncertainties.
  • Margin realization in Magel Tyala flagged as an area to monitor. The company identified ongoing opportunities in Maharashtra under Magel Tyala, with margin realization an area to monitor.
  • Long approval and validation cycles in the EV business. The automotive sector’s typical long approval and validation cycles mean EV motors and controllers are described as a medium-term optionality rather than a near-term revenue driver.
  • Working capital absorption. Changes in working capital were negative in each year from FY23 to FY26, including ₹(1,727) Mn in FY26. Working capital secured loans rose to ₹4,457 Mn at Mar-26 from ₹1,324 Mn at Mar-25.
  • Forward-looking statement risk factors. The company notes its forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties including government actions, local, political, or economic developments, and industry risks.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Policy-backed market opportunity. A ₹5.5 Trillion total potential market for solar irrigation, comprising 12–13.5 Mn additional solar pump demand and 8–9 Mn diesel pump replacement demand. PM-KUSUM 2.0 is described as on the horizon, with central and state allocations creating recurring tender visibility.
  • Market leadership under PM-KUSUM. Estimated ~25% market share under PM-KUSUM, with 86,086 solar pumps installed in FY26 (+20% YoY) and 2.58 lakh+ cumulative solar pumps installed till date. Execution scoreboard (PM-KUSUM Portal, 30 Jun 2026): 1,90,698 pumps installed by SPIL under KUSUM and 66,926 under non-KUSUM.
  • Order book. ₹10,000 Mn order book as of 22 July 2026 (inclusive of GST), including Magel Tyala (₹5,220 Mn), Karnataka REDL (₹2,350 Mn), MP Urja Vikas (₹1,670 Mn), and other domestic and export business (₹550 Mn).
  • New order win. A Letter of Empanelment from MSEDCL for 10,000 Off-Grid SPWPS pumps (3 HP, 5 HP, 7.5 HP) for Maharashtra under Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana, valued at ~₹235.92 Crores inclusive of GST (₹216.64 Crores excluding GST).
  • Capacity expansion. A ~₹17,000 Mn phased capex programme to double core capacity (pumps, motors, VFDs, solar structures), with new capacities expected online from Q3 FY27. A 0.5 GW DCR module facility is expected to commission by September 2026, and a 2.2 GW solar DCR cell and module greenfield expansion at Pithampur is expected by FY28. Backward integration is expected to secure solar cell supply, improve cost optimisation, and support long-term profitability.
  • Diversification beyond core solar pumping. Solar Rooftop (Shakti Energy Solutions Ltd.) targets the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana opportunity, with 90+ dealers onboarded and 10% better generation versus competing inverters per early customer/partner feedback. EV motors and controllers (Shakti EV Mobility Pvt. Ltd.) have cumulative investment of ₹700 Mn till July 2026 out of a planned ₹1,140 Mn capex, with products at vehicle-level trials with auto OEMs.
  • Retail/cash sales channel. ₹770 Mn revenue from cash sales in FY26, a non-tender, retail/distributor-led channel that diversifies revenue away from pure government dependence and improves working capital efficiency through faster payment collection.
  • Exports. Presence in 100+ countries; executed a contract worth USD 35.30 million from the Government of Uganda for solar-powered water pumping. SPIL is part of the International Solar Alliance, which has aggregated demand for more than 2,70,000 solar pumps across 22 countries, more than 1 GW of solar rooftop across 11 countries, and more than 10 GW of solar mini-grids across 9 countries.
  • Geographic expansion. Entry into Karnataka, alongside established presence across Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, UP and MP, supporting repeat tender wins.
  • ESG rating upgrade. ICRA ESG Rating Limited upgraded the overall ESG Rating from [ICRA ESG] Impact Rating 75, Good to [ICRA ESG] Impact Rating 78, Good on August 27, 2026. Environment score 81 (Outstanding), Social score 80 (Outstanding), Governance score 71 (Good).
  • Credit rating. IND AA-/Stable long-term credit rating from India Ratings.

3. Key Risks

  • Government policy and tender dependence. Revenue is significantly tied to government-led programmes (PM-KUSUM, Magel Tyala, state schemes). The opportunity pipeline depends on policy momentum, including expected PM-KUSUM 2.0 inflows.
  • Execution risk at scale. PM-KUSUM 2.0 commentary notes that states need vendors that can install, commission and service thousands of pumps across dispersed villages, making execution the moat.
  • Margin risk on select orders. Lower realizations on select orders and Magel Tyala margin realization are explicitly flagged.
  • Working capital and balance sheet risk. Rising working capital secured loans and negative working capital changes in recent years; management states it prioritizes balance sheet discipline over near-term growth.
  • Export and geopolitical risk. Global uncertainties cited as a factor affecting the export business.
  • EV business timing risk. Long automotive approval and validation cycles mean the EV segment is a medium-term optionality rather than a near-term revenue driver.
  • Forward-looking statement risk factors. Government actions, local, political, or economic developments, and industry risks.
  • Subsidiary review scope. The consolidated results include one subsidiary whose interim financial information was not reviewed by its auditors (immaterial to the Group per management), and two overseas subsidiaries whose information was prepared under foreign GAAP and converted to Indian GAAP by management.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Order book and revenue visibility. Management cited a healthy order book of around ₹10,000 Mn and growing visibility around PM-KUSUM 2.0 and other state-led solar irrigation programs, and expressed confidence in capturing the opportunity ahead. The order book as of 22 July 2026 was ₹10,000 Mn (inclusive of GST), consistent with this statement.
  • Margin stability. Management stated that despite input cost pressures and lower realizations on select orders, EBITDA margins were held broadly stable sequentially. Observed: consolidated EBITDA margin was 9.6% in Q1 FY27 versus 9.7% in Q4 FY26, consistent with broadly stable sequential margins; however, this compares with 23.1% in Q1 FY26.
  • Growth expectations. Management stated it expects growth to be supported by a stronger execution pipeline, an improving order mix, and continued operational efficiencies. Observed: Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was ₹8,587 Mn versus ₹6,225 Mn in Q1 FY26, and solar pump installations grew 58% YoY in Q1 FY27.
  • Export momentum. Management stated the Export business sustained healthy momentum despite global uncertainties, with significant traction in the dealer and distributor export business. Observed: overseas subsidiaries segment revenue was ₹34.95 crores in Q1 FY27 versus ₹31.09 crores in Q1 FY26.
  • Capacity and project timelines. Management stated the 0.5 GW DCR module facility and 2.2 GW integrated DCR Cell & Module project remain on track. The presentation specifies expected commissioning by September 2026 for the 0.5 GW module and by FY28 for the 2.2 GW cell and module capacity, with new core capacities expected online from Q3 FY27.
  • New business progress. Management stated the Solar Cash/Retail business is gaining encouraging traction, the Solar Rooftop business is progressing well, and the EV business has reached a milestone with customer validations and trial orders underway. Observed: ₹770 Mn FY26 cash sales revenue, 90+ rooftop dealers onboarded, and EV products at vehicle-level trials with auto OEMs.
  • Order execution guidance. The MSEDCL empanelment order for 10,000 pumps is to be executed within 60 days from the date of issuance of Work Order/Notice to Proceed (NTP).
  • Sustainability commitment. Management remains committed to advancing its sustainability agenda through innovation, continuous improvement, and meaningful stakeholder engagement, following the ESG rating upgrade to 78, Good.
  • Investor engagement. Management is participating in the Anand Rathi Annual Flagship Conference – G200 Summit 2026 on 21st September 2026 in Mumbai, with the Investor Presentation available on the company website.
