GVPIL: 34th AGM Voting Outcome and Related Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

The 34th Annual General Meeting of GE Power India Limited was held on Friday, 14 August 2026 at 11:30 A.M. (IST) through Video Conference / Other Audio Visual Means, transacting business set out in the AGM Notice dated 19 June 2026. The meeting was conducted entirely in virtual mode, with no shareholders present in person or through proxy; attendance was limited to one promoter/promoter group shareholder and 45 public shareholders joining via video conferencing, against 72,829 shareholders on record as of the record date of Friday, 07 August 2026.

Shareholder participation in the resolutions was concentrated rather than broad-based. Across the resolutions, total votes polled represented 70.4034% to 70.4573% of outstanding shares, but this was driven overwhelmingly by the promoter and promoter group, which holds 4,61,02,083 shares and voted 100% of its holding through e-voting on every resolution. Public institutions voted only 34.2995% to 36.8301% of their holdings, and public non-institutions voted just 3.6972% to 3.7459% of their holdings. This pattern indicates that the outcome of every resolution was effectively determined by the promoter block, with limited participation from public shareholders.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

All eight resolutions placed before shareholders were passed. The resolutions covered adoption of the audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for the financial year ended 31 March 2026, declaration of a final dividend of ₹7/- per equity share of face value of ₹10/- each for that year, reappointment of Mr. Craig Martin Richards (DIN: 11141735) as a Director retiring by rotation, reappointment of M/s. Deloitte Haskins & Sells, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No.: 015125N) as Statutory Auditors for a second term of five consecutive years, ratification of remuneration payable to Cost Auditors for the financial year 2026-27, reappointment of Ms. Shukla Wassan (DIN: 02770898) as a Non-Executive Independent Director, appointment of Mr. Shrikar Thakur (DIN: 11789178) as a Whole-time Director, and approval of enhanced overall limits for inter-corporate loan/guarantee/security/investment under Section 186 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The dividend declaration and the approval of enhanced Section 186 limits point to continued shareholder returns alongside expanded financial flexibility for inter-corporate transactions. The reappointment of the statutory auditors for a second five-year term and the ratification of cost auditor remuneration provide continuity in the audit framework. The board changes—reappointment of an independent director and appointment of a whole-time director—indicate continuity and renewal in leadership.

Key Risks

The voting results show dissent on specific resolutions, concentrated among public institutions. On the reappointment of Mr. Craig Martin Richards (Resolution 3), public institutions cast 49,944 votes against out of 5,26,788 votes polled, representing 9.4809% opposition within that category. On the appointment of Mr. Shrikar Thakur as Whole-time Director (Resolution 7), public institutions again cast 49,944 votes against out of 5,26,788 votes polled, also 9.4809% opposition. On the reappointment of Ms. Shukla Wassan as Non-Executive Independent Director (Resolution 6), public institutions cast 8,040 votes against out of 5,26,788 votes polled, or 1.5262% opposition. Public non-institutions cast 301 to 467 votes against on several resolutions, with the highest count of 467 against on Resolutions 6 and 7. These dissent levels, while small relative to total votes polled, indicate that a segment of institutional shareholders did not support certain board appointments.
