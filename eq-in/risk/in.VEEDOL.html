Veedol Corporation — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weaker standalone profitability. On a standalone basis, revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹392.30 crore, down from ₹427.66 crore in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and up only modestly from ₹372.77 crore in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Profit before tax fell to ₹30.08 crore from ₹44.09 crore sequentially and from ₹19.70 crore a year earlier, while profit for the period declined to ₹26.18 crore from ₹48.84 crore sequentially. Standalone basic and diluted EPS dropped to ₹15.40 from ₹28.74 in the preceding quarter.

Cost pressure at the standalone level. Cost of materials consumed rose to ₹235.95 crore from ₹226.07 crore sequentially, purchases of stock-in-trade increased to ₹33.78 crore from ₹18.21 crore, and franchisee fees rose to ₹73.84 crore from ₹68.94 crore. Other expenses also increased to ₹40.89 crore from ₹53.68 crore a year earlier but were higher than the ₹53.68 crore comparative in the prior-year quarter on a like-for-like basis.

New Labour Code transition. The Government of India on 21 November 2025 consolidated 29 existing labour legislations into four Labour Codes. The company restructured office staff salaries effective 23 March 2026 and recognised an incremental employee benefit obligation of ₹2.60 crore as past service cost. The company continues to monitor finalisation of State Rules and government clarifications and may need to consider further accounting as those develop.

Subsidiary dissolution. Veedol Deutschland GmbH, a wholly owned subsidiary, is in the process of dissolution, subject to applicable compliances and clearances. Its financial results are not significant to the consolidated results.

Dependence on a single segment. The group operates in a single reportable operating segment — Lubricants — with no separate segment information disclosed.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Consolidated revenue scale and momentum. Consolidated revenue from operations was ₹608.57 crore for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with ₹606.98 crore in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and ₹514.28 crore in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Full-year consolidated revenue for the year ended 31 March 2026 was ₹2,168.54 crore.

Strong consolidated profitability. Consolidated profit before tax was ₹101.79 crore for the June 2026 quarter, up from ₹63.90 crore sequentially and ₹63.72 crore a year earlier. Consolidated profit for the period was ₹77.92 crore, and consolidated EPS was ₹45.85, compared with ₹33.81 sequentially and ₹29.23 a year earlier. Consolidated total comprehensive income was ₹76.94 crore.

Joint venture contribution. The group’s share of profit of a joint venture (Eneos VCL India Private Limited, formerly Eneos Tide Water Lubricants India Private Limited) accounted for using the equity method was ₹3.22 crore for the quarter, with the group’s share of net profit after tax of ₹8.22 crore and total comprehensive income of ₹8.24 crore attributable to the joint venture as reported by the auditors.

International footprint. The consolidated results include Veedol International Limited, Veedol International FZCO (formerly Veedol International DMCC), Veedol UK Limited (including step-down subsidiaries Granville Oil & Chemicals Limited and Veedol Ireland Limited), and Veedol Deutschland GmbH (under dissolution). Six subsidiaries (including two step-down subsidiaries) contributed total revenues of ₹218.00 crore and total net profit after tax of ₹57.60 crore for the quarter.

Promoter group consolidation. Standard Greases and Specialities Private Limited (SGSPL), part of the promoter and promoter group, will acquire 2,95,000 equity shares (1.69% of paid-up equity capital) from Janus Consolidated Finance Private Limited on 14 August 2026 via an off-market inter-se transfer, pursuant to a Composite Scheme of Arrangement involving the amalgamation of Janus into SGSPL. The aggregate promoter and promoter group holding remains unchanged at 40.28% before and after the transaction. The acquirer’s holding rises from 38.59% to 40.28%, while the seller’s 1.69% is extinguished.

ESG recognition. Crisil ESG Ratings & Analytics Ltd. voluntarily assigned Veedol Corporation Limited an ESG Rating of ‘Crisil ESG 61’ (Rating Category: Strong) for FY 2025-26, prepared independently from publicly available data without being engaged by the company.

3. Key Risks

Earnings volatility. Standalone profit for the quarter fell sharply both sequentially and year-on-year, with EPS declining from ₹28.74 to ₹15.40 sequentially, indicating sensitivity to cost and revenue movements.

Regulatory and compliance risk. The transition to the new Labour Codes carries uncertainty around State Rules and government clarifications, with potential for further accounting impacts. The Veedol Deutschland GmbH dissolution remains subject to compliances and clearances under applicable laws.

Concentration risk. The group operates in a single reportable segment (Lubricants), concentrating exposure to that industry’s dynamics.

Related-party and promoter group transactions. The proposed inter-se transfer of 1.69% of equity shares between promoter group entities relies on the exemption under Regulation 10(1) of the SEBI SAST Regulations; the transaction is off-market and does not change aggregate promoter holding.

Auditor reliance on other auditors. The consolidated review relies on reports of other auditors for six subsidiaries (including two step-down subsidiaries) and one joint venture; the conclusion is not modified in respect of these matters.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Labour Code accounting. Management stated that the company continues to monitor finalisation of State Rules and clarifications from the Government on other aspects of the Labour Code and would consider appropriate accounting on the basis of such developments. Observed performance: the company has already restructured office staff salaries effective 23 March 2026 and recognised ₹2.60 crore as past service cost in employee benefit expenses, with the incremental impact reflected in the statement of profit and loss.

Veedol Deutschland GmbH dissolution. Management decided on 18 May 2024 to dissolve the subsidiary, subject to compliances and clearances. Observed performance: the entity remains in process of dissolution, and its financial results are not significant to the consolidated results.

Name change. Pursuant to board and shareholder approvals dated 23 July 2024 and 29 August 2024 respectively, and a certificate of incorporation from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs dated 20 September 2024, the company’s name changed from Tide Water Oil Co. (India) Limited to Veedol Corporation Limited effective 20 September 2024. Observed performance: the change is reflected in the current financial statements and disclosures.

Promoter group reorganisation. The proposed acquisition by SGSPL of 2,95,000 equity shares from Janus is being undertaken pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement involving the amalgamation of Janus into SGSPL, with the acquisition date stated as 14 August 2026. Observed performance: the transaction is an off-market inter-se transfer amongst promoter and promoter group entities, with aggregate promoter holding unchanged at 40.28%.

Annual General Meeting. Veedol Corporation Limited held its 103rd Annual General Meeting on 24 August 2026, where shareholders passed all eight resolutions, including adoption of financial statements, dividend declarations, appointment and re-appointment of directors and managing directors, related party transactions, and cost auditor remuneration.
