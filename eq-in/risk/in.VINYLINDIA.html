VINYLINDIA: Recent Corporate Announcements Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The first quarter ended 30 June 2026 shows a sharp sequential decline in revenue from operations, falling to Rs. 9,964 lakhs from Rs. 17,984 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Revenue was also lower than the Rs. 15,123 lakhs recorded in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Total income followed the same trend, at Rs. 10,592 lakhs for Q1 FY2027 versus Rs. 18,319 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 and Rs. 15,532 lakhs in Q1 FY2026.

The expense structure reflects the trading nature of the business. Purchase of traded goods was Rs. 16,575 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, higher than the Rs. 14,099 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 and lower than the Rs. 17,259 lakhs in Q1 FY2026. The company recorded a negative inventory movement of Rs. 7,631 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, compared with a positive Rs. 3,126 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 and a negative Rs. 2,918 lakhs in Q1 FY2026, indicating a drawdown of traded goods inventory during the quarter.

Foreign exchange difference expense remained a recurring cost, at Rs. 472 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, Rs. 383 lakhs in Q4 FY2026, Rs. 387 lakhs in Q1 FY2026, and Rs. 1,007 lakhs for the full year ended 31 March 2026. This suggests continued exposure to currency movements.

Employee benefit expenses rose to Rs. 211 lakhs in Q1 FY2027 from Rs. 38 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 and Rs. 143 lakhs in Q1 FY2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Despite lower revenue, profitability improved. Profit before tax rose to Rs. 889 lakhs in Q1 FY2027 from Rs. 613 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 and Rs. 602 lakhs in Q1 FY2026. Profit for the period was Rs. 662 lakhs, up from Rs. 465 lakhs and Rs. 445 lakhs in the comparable periods. Total comprehensive income was Rs. 660 lakhs in Q1 FY2027.

Earnings per share improved to Rs. 3.61 in Q1 FY2027 from Rs. 2.53 in Q4 FY2026 and Rs. 2.42 in Q1 FY2026. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, EPS was Rs. 8.99.

The company continues to operate as a single reportable segment, Trading in Chemicals, with no subsidiaries, associates, or joint ventures.

At the 40th Annual General Meeting held on 5 June 2026, shareholders approved a dividend of Rs. 7 per equity share of Re. 1 each on 1,83,37,111 equity shares, absorbing Rs. 12,83,59,777 out of the current year’s profit, payable to shareholders on the register as of the record date of 22 May 2026.

Shareholders also approved material related party transactions with Pidilite Industries Limited, the promoter company, for sale of the company’s goods/products/materials on an ongoing and continuous basis, for an aggregate value not exceeding Rs. 1,300 Crores, for the period from 1 April 2026 until the conclusion of the AGM for the financial year ending 31 March 2027, on an arm’s length basis and in the ordinary course of business. This resolution passed with 99.06% votes in favour and 0.94% against.

The AGM also approved the re-appointment of Shri M. B. Parekh as Managing Director for a further five years with effect from 1 April 2027, and the re-appointment of Shri P. D. Shah as an Independent Director for a second term of five consecutive years from 6 October 2026 to 5 October 2031. Approval was granted for payment of commission to non-executive directors of up to 1% per annum of net profits for five years commencing 1 April 2027.

3. Key Risks

The company’s business is concentrated in a single segment, Trading in Chemicals, and it has no subsidiaries, associates, or joint ventures, which concentrates operational and financial risk.

A significant portion of the company’s sales is directed to Pidilite Industries Limited, its promoter company, under the approved related party transaction framework of up to Rs. 1,300 Crores for FY2027. This creates dependence on a related party for revenue.

Foreign exchange difference expense is a recurring item, amounting to Rs. 472 lakhs in Q1 FY2027 and Rs. 1,007 lakhs for the full year FY2026, exposing the company to currency fluctuation risk.

The company relies on purchases of traded goods, which were Rs. 16,575 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, and inventory movements can be volatile, as seen in the swing from a positive Rs. 3,126 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 to a negative Rs. 7,631 lakhs in Q1 FY2027.
