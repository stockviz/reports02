Digitide Solutions: Recent Corporate Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

Profitability pressure at the consolidated level. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, consolidated revenue from operations was INR 7,750.72 million, down from INR 7,999.52 million in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, though higher than INR 7,357.37 million in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Consolidated profit before exceptional items and tax fell to INR 109.19 million in the June 2026 quarter, compared with INR 120.29 million in the March 2026 quarter and INR 288.58 million in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated profit for the period was INR 29.33 million, versus a loss of INR 50.05 million in the March 2026 quarter and profit of INR 96.93 million in the June 2025 quarter.

Loss attributable to owners. Profit/(loss) attributable to owners of the Company was a loss of INR 18.91 million in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a loss of INR 126.96 million in the March 2026 quarter and profit of INR 57.32 million in the June 2025 quarter. Total comprehensive income attributable to owners was a loss of INR 25.59 million in the June 2026 quarter.

Standalone loss. At the standalone level, the Company reported a loss before exceptional items and tax of INR 95.45 million for the June 2026 quarter, compared with profit of INR 253.14 million in the March 2026 quarter and INR 132.81 million in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone loss for the period was INR 105.81 million, versus profit of INR 68.53 million in the March 2026 quarter and INR 35.93 million in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone basic and diluted loss per share was INR 0.71 for the June 2026 quarter.

Segment performance decline. Business Process Management segment revenue was INR 5,377.17 million in the June 2026 quarter, down from INR 5,507.62 million in the March 2026 quarter and INR 5,387.10 million in the June 2025 quarter. Segment results for BPM fell to INR 760.00 million from INR 897.13 million in the March 2026 quarter and INR 914.08 million in the June 2025 quarter. Tech and Digital segment revenue was INR 2,373.55 million, down from INR 2,491.90 million in the March 2026 quarter, while segment results fell to INR 189.32 million from INR 300.56 million.

Exceptional items and one-time costs. The Group recognised past service costs related to the New Labour Codes (effective November 21, 2025) of INR 158.48 million for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and INR 412.23 million for the year ended March 31, 2026, presented as exceptional items. Demerger expenses, professional services, and stamp duty of INR 2.70 million, INR 88.65 million, and INR 235.36 million were incurred in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, quarter ended June 30, 2025, and year ended March 31, 2026, respectively, also classified as exceptional items. At the standalone level, exceptional items were INR 123.20 million for the March 2026 quarter and INR 88.65 million for the June 2025 quarter.

Finance costs and depreciation. Consolidated finance costs rose to INR 151.22 million in the June 2026 quarter from INR 112.19 million in the June 2025 quarter. Depreciation and amortisation was INR 551.76 million in the June 2026 quarter, compared with INR 460.75 million in the June 2025 quarter.

Unallocated corporate expenses. Unallocated corporate expenses were INR 180.42 million in the June 2026 quarter, INR 318.71 million in the March 2026 quarter, and INR 281.36 million in the June 2025 quarter.

Unreviewed subsidiaries. The consolidated results include four subsidiaries whose interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors, reflecting nil revenue, total loss after tax of INR 2.26 million, and total comprehensive loss of INR 2.05 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Management stated these are not material to the Group.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Leadership strengthening. On August 20, 2026, Digitide announced two senior leadership appointments. Harigovind Krishnasamy was appointed Chief Financial Officer (Designate) with immediate effect and will assume the office of CFO effective October 1, 2026. He brings over 21 years of finance leadership experience, including as CFO of Matrimony.com Limited and Indiavidual Learning Limited (Embibe, the edtech business within the Reliance Jio group), and senior finance roles at Vedanta, DEN Networks, and Jio Platforms. Suraj Prasad, who served as CFO since the company’s demerger and listing, transitioned to Chief Strategy and Corporate Development Officer effective October 1, 2026, with a mandate spanning portfolio transformation, M&A, strategic investments, and investor relations.

Stated strategic direction. The company described the appointments as part of a deliberate, profitability-led strategy built on sharper focus, disciplined capital allocation, and a conviction that sustainable value comes from doing the right things well. The company stated it is strengthening its core, accelerating AI and Digital capabilities, deepening its international presence, and pursuing selective build, partner, and acquire opportunities.

Tech and Digital growth. Tech and Digital segment revenue was INR 2,373.55 million in the June 2026 quarter, up from INR 1,970.27 million in the June 2025 quarter, though down from INR 2,491.90 million in the March 2026 quarter. For the year ended March 31, 2026, Tech and Digital revenue was INR 9,101.70 million.

International presence. The Group operates through subsidiaries including Brainhunter Systems Ltd., Mindwire Systems Limited, MFXchange Holdings, Inc., MFXchange US, Inc., Alldigi Tech Inc. (USA), Allsectech Manila Inc. (Philippines), Quess Corp (USA) Inc., Quess GTS Canada Holding Inc., and Digitide IT Solutions L.L.C S.O.C.

ESOP Trust establishment. The Company incorporated the Digitide ESOP Trust on April 18, 2026 to administer the Employee Stock Option Scheme 2026 (ESOS 2026) and future employee benefit plans. The Trust did not undertake any operations or transactions during the period.

Investor engagement. The Company participated in the Emkay Confluence 2026 Conference on August 14, 2026 in Mumbai as a group meeting in person.

AGM resolutions passed. All five resolutions set out in the Notice of the 2nd AGM held on August 28, 2026 were passed with the requisite majority. These included adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, reappointment of director Mr. Anish Thurthi, approval of commission for Independent Directors, and appointment of Mr. Sameer Ahluwalia as Whole-time Director, Executive Director, and CEO.

Key Risks

Profitability and loss risk. The Company reported consolidated loss attributable to owners of INR 18.91 million and standalone loss of INR 105.81 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Consolidated profit for the year ended March 31, 2026 was INR 55.45 million, with loss attributable to owners of INR 163.50 million.

Dependence on BPM segment. Business Process Management contributed INR 5,377.17 million of the INR 7,750.72 million consolidated revenue in the June 2026 quarter, representing the majority of revenue. Segment results for BPM declined year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter.

Exceptional items recurrence. The Company has incurred exceptional items related to New Labour Codes and demerger expenses. The New Labour Codes became effective November 21, 2025, and the Group estimated employee benefit liabilities resulting in past service costs.

Unreviewed subsidiary financials. Four subsidiaries’ interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors, though management stated these are not material to the Group.

Foreign exchange and hedging. The consolidated results include foreign exchange differences on translating financial information of foreign operations of negative INR 10.18 million for the June 2026 quarter, and net change in fair value of forward contracts designated as cash flow hedges of INR 26.03 million.

Finance cost increase. Consolidated finance costs increased to INR 151.22 million in the June 2026 quarter from INR 112.19 million in the June 2025 quarter.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Profitability-led strategy. Management stated the appointments mark a deliberate step in Digitide’s next chapter built on sharper focus, disciplined capital allocation, and a conviction that sustainable value comes from doing the right things well, not simply doing more. The company stated it is strengthening its core, accelerating AI and Digital capabilities, deepening international presence, and pursuing selective build, partner, and acquire opportunities. Observed performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 shows consolidated revenue declined sequentially from INR 7,999.52 million to INR 7,750.72 million, and profit before exceptional items and tax declined from INR 120.29 million to INR 109.19 million.

Capital allocation and performance measurement. Sameer Ahluwalia, CEO and Executive Director, stated that Harigovind Krishnasamy brings financial rigour to sharpen how the company allocates capital and measures performance, and Suraj Prasad brings strategic depth to decide where the company competes and where it does not. Observed performance shows the Company reported a standalone loss of INR 105.81 million and consolidated loss attributable to owners of INR 18.91 million for the June 2026 quarter.

Portfolio transformation and M&A. Suraj Prasad stated the natural next step is applying financial rigour to where the company takes Digitide next, sharpening the portfolio, deepening partnerships, and making deliberate choices about where to build, partner, and acquire. No specific M&A transactions or portfolio changes were disclosed in the supplied material.

Strong foundation and runway. Harigovind Krishnasamy stated Digitide has a strong foundation, a differentiated set of AI-led platforms, and a clear runway in a market that is expanding quickly. Observed performance shows Tech and Digital segment revenue grew year-on-year from INR 1,970.27 million to INR 2,373.55 million in the June 2026 quarter, while BPM segment revenue was broadly flat year-on-year at INR 5,377.17 million versus INR 5,387.10 million.

Financial discipline since listing. Suraj Prasad stated that since Digitide became an independent, listed company, the company built the financial discipline needed to stand on its own. Observed performance shows the Company reported standalone loss of INR 105.81 million for the June 2026 quarter and consolidated profit for the year ended March 31, 2026 of INR 55.45 million.

AGM outcomes. All five resolutions set out in the Notice of the 2nd AGM held on August 28, 2026 were passed with the requisite majority, including adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, reappointment of director Mr. Anish Thurthi, approval of commission for Independent Directors, and appointment of Mr. Sameer Ahluwalia as Whole-time Director, Executive Director, and CEO.
