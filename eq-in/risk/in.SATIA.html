Satia Industries — Q1 FY27 Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Planned PM3 shutdown: Paper Machine 3 was shut down from June 1, 2026 for a major modernization and upgradation program. The refurbishment is expected to continue for 4–5 months. Volumes during Q1 FY27 were impacted by this shutdown, even as pricing improved.
  • Revenue decline: Revenue from operations fell to INR 3,618 Mn in Q1 FY27, down 2% YoY from INR 3,709 Mn in Q1 FY26 and 7% QoQ from INR 3,896 Mn in Q4 FY26.
  • EBITDA decline YoY: EBITDA stood at INR 508 Mn in Q1 FY27 versus INR 632 Mn in Q1 FY26, a 20% decline. EBITDA margin contracted 300 bps YoY to 14.0% from 17.0%.
  • Net loss: The company reported a net loss of INR 171 Mn in Q1 FY27, compared with PAT of INR 316 Mn in Q1 FY26 and INR 58 Mn in Q4 FY26. This was attributed to a one-time, non-cash deferred tax charge arising from the transition to the concessional tax regime, which does not represent a cash outflow or a deterioration in operating performance.
  • Tax regime transition: The company opted for the concessional tax regime from FY27, discontinuing deductions under Section 80-IA. Consequently, the Cogeneration Division is no longer reported as a separate segment, and the company lost eligibility to claim Section 80-IA deductions for that division.
  • External operating environment: Geopolitical developments continued to influence global trade flows and logistics, including container availability, keeping the operating environment dynamic. Management noted continued monitoring of input costs, trade flows, and market conditions.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Improved pricing and gross margins: Gross margins improved sequentially to 50.9% in Q1 FY27 from 48.0% in Q4 FY26, driven by better pricing realizations. Pricing witnessed an uptick during the quarter.
  • Reduced dumping: Demand remained steady due to lower dumping activity, and moderation in import dumping supported realizations.
  • Strong sequential EBITDA recovery: EBITDA rose 116% QoQ from INR 236 Mn in Q4 FY26 to INR 508 Mn in Q1 FY27, with EBITDA margin improving 799 bps sequentially (800 bps as described in the release) from 6.0% to 14.0%.
  • PM3 modernization benefits: Upon completion, the refurbishment is expected to enable higher machine speed, enhance production capacity, improve product quality, and strengthen operating efficiencies.
  • Concessional tax regime: Opting for the concessional tax regime under Section 200 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 carries an effective tax rate of 25.168% including surcharge and cess.
  • Cutlery/tableware business: The moulded cutlery business continued to operate during the quarter as part of a broader sustainable packaging portfolio. All old machines are operating at 100% capacity, inventory has been scaled up to meet anticipated demand, and new machinery for moulding cups is expected to start production from Q2 FY27. Five more cutlery machines were added in 2025-26, taking the total to 14.
  • Integrated manufacturing platform: SIL has 4 paper machines, 100% in-house power generation, chemical recovery plant, integrated pulping, and power self-sufficiency, with total installed capacity exceeding 2,00,000 MTPA and approximately 500 acres of eucalyptus plantations.
  • Distribution network: Pan-India network of 100+ dealers and three branch offices (Delhi, Chandigarh, Jaipur), with over 2,600 employees.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution risk on PM3: The refurbishment is expected to continue for 4–5 months; timely completion is a stated management focus, and FY27 is described as an important year from an execution standpoint.
  • Volume impact from shutdown: The PM3 shutdown weighed on Q1 FY27 volumes, and the extended shutdown period may continue to affect volumes.
  • External/geopolitical factors: Global trade flows, logistics, container availability, input costs, and market conditions remain influenced by global developments.
  • Tax-related impact: The transition to the concessional tax regime resulted in a one-time, non-cash deferred tax charge and the loss of Section 80-IA deductions for the Cogeneration Division.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The company’s disclaimer notes that forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties including government actions, local political or economic developments, and technological risks that could cause actual results to differ materially.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management stated the PM3 refurbishment work is expected to continue for 4–5 months, and upon completion is expected to enhance production capacity, improve product quality, and strengthen operating efficiencies. Management said FY27 will remain an important year from an execution standpoint, with focus on timely completion of key projects, disciplined execution, maintaining customer service levels, and ensuring a smooth transition through the investment phase. New cutlery cup-moulding machinery is expected to start production from Q2 FY27.
  • Observed performance: In Q1 FY27, revenue declined 2% YoY and 7% QoQ, with volumes impacted by the PM3 shutdown from June 1, 2026. EBITDA margin improved sequentially by ~800 bps to 14.0% on higher realizations, though EBITDA declined 20% YoY. PAT was a loss of INR 171 Mn due to the one-time deferred tax charge. Gross margins improved sequentially to 50.9%. The cutlery business continued to operate, with all old machines at 100% capacity and inventory scaled up ahead of anticipated demand.
