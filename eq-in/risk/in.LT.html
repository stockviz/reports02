Headwinds and Challenges

  • The Infrastructure & Utilities segment recorded a 3% year-on-year decline in customer revenues for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, attributed to execution challenges in the Water & Effluent Treatment business. Segment EBITDA margin contracted to 5.1% from 5.5%, driven by a change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables.
  • The Energy – Conventional segment saw order inflow decline 90% year-on-year to ₹3,053 crore, reflecting deferment of certain anticipated orders and a high base effect from an ultra-mega order secured in the CarbonLite Solutions business in the prior-year quarter.
  • The Energy – Green segment recorded an 11% year-on-year decline in customer revenues, largely due to supply chain disruptions arising from the West Asia conflict in the Solar business.
  • The Manufacturing & Products segment EBITDA margin fell to 15.2% from 17.5%, reflecting a change in sales mix.
  • The Technology, Platforms & Services segment EBITDA margin declined marginally to 19.2% from 19.5%, due to higher manpower cost and forex variation adversely impacting hedged portfolio positions.
  • The Realty segment EBITDA margin declined to 36.9% from 48.8%, primarily due to a change in sales composition.
  • Group EBITDA declined 3% year-on-year to ₹6,116 crore, with EBITDA margin contracting to 9.0% from 9.9%.
  • Management noted that the financial year commenced against a backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties. The extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt growth momentum. Inflation is expected to trend higher from relatively benign levels, driven by higher food and energy prices, weather-related uncertainties, and currency depreciation. The resurgence of geopolitical tensions in West Asia and continued uncertainty around global trade policies remain key risks.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Group order inflows rose 14% year-on-year to ₹108,014 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with international orders at ₹60,702 crore contributing 56%. The consolidated order book stood at ₹778,954 crore as on June 30, 2026, up 5% over March 2026, with international orders constituting 52%.
  • The Infrastructure & Utilities segment secured order inflow of ₹44,357 crore, more than 100% growth year-on-year, with orders for Residential & Commercial buildings and Ferrous Metal projects. Its order book stood at ₹350,506 crore.
  • The Energy – Green segment secured orders of ₹33,042 crore, up 58% year-on-year, driven by ultra-mega orders in the Offshore Wind business. Its order book stood at ₹146,700 crore.
  • The Manufacturing & Products segment reported order inflows of ₹5,535 crore, up 74%, attributable to multiple refinery equipment package orders in the Heavy Engineering business.
  • The Financial Services segment recorded income from operations of ₹5,042 crore, up 27%, driven by focused and higher disbursements in the retail business. The total loan book stood at ₹129,634 crore, up 6% from March 2026, with the retail loan book constituting 98%. Profit before tax rose to ₹1,236 crore from ₹943 crore.
  • The Realty segment reported order inflows of ₹1,299 crore, up 32%, and customer revenues of ₹1,009 crore, more than 100% growth, driven by higher handover of residential apartments. The segment order book stood at ₹17,330 crore.
  • The Technology, Platforms & Services segment recorded customer revenues of ₹14,627 crore, up 15%, with international billing contributing 92%.
  • L&T Realty has an estimated development potential of approximately 71 million square feet across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Bengaluru, NCR and Chennai. During FY 2025-26, the Realty Business Unit achieved pre-sales of approximately ₹10,000 crore and contributed about 0.59% of consolidated revenue, and is targeting accelerated pre-sales growth over the next five years.
  • L&T Energy Hydrocarbon Offshore secured a large-class order (valued between ₹2,500 to 5,000 crore) from ONGC for the Additional Development of Ratna-I (ADR I) and NLM-14 project off India’s west coast, covering EPCIC of three new well-head platforms, one riser platform, subsea pipelines and cables, and brownfield modifications, supporting ONGC’s long-term production objectives.
  • L&T became the first private sector corporate in India to raise ₹500 crore through tokenised bonds with a 3-year tenure under SEBI’s blockchain-based tokenisation framework, using DLT-based infrastructure and CBDC-based settlement.
  • The Group realigned its portfolio effective April 1, 2026 under the Lakshya 2031 strategic plan, creating a separate Energy – Green segment and classifying Realty as a separate reportable segment, to enhance strategic focus, capital allocation efficiency and operational agility.
  • Management stated the Company remains committed to delivering sustainable shareholder value by leveraging sector-specific technology, investing in AI, and adopting digital solutions, while ensuring efficient execution of its order book and judicious capital allocation.

Key Risks

  • Execution challenges in the Water & Effluent Treatment business and expected delays in collection of receivables, which increased credit provisions in the Infrastructure & Utilities segment.
  • Deferment of anticipated orders in the Energy – Conventional segment.
  • Supply chain disruptions arising from the West Asia conflict affecting the Solar business.
  • Higher manpower cost and forex variation adversely impacting hedged portfolio positions in the Technology, Platforms & Services segment.
  • Elevated energy prices, higher inflation from food and energy prices, weather-related uncertainties, and currency depreciation.
  • Geopolitical tensions in West Asia and uncertainty around global trade policies.
  • Divergent monetary and fiscal policy frameworks globally, trade realignment, AI-driven transformation, and climate-related challenges reshaping the global economy.
