Headwinds and Challenges

  • Competition is a primary threat, driven by aggressive pricing from new entrants and changes in technology and markets.
  • The company faces supply chain disruptions, non-availability of project fronts, increased input costs, and high attrition rates among engineering professionals in the EPC sector.
  • Regulatory requirements are tightening, shrinking the productive life of resources.
  • The company was subject to penalties for minor delays in regulatory disclosures.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The Flue Gas Desulphurization (FGD) segment has revived due to stringent MoEFCC environmental norms and mandated installations in thermal power plants.
  • Opportunities exist in expanding into video security surveillance through artificial intelligence, green hydrogen production and transportation, and aerospace engineering.
  • The company reports robust customer inquiries and repeat orders from large corporates.
  • National demand for aluminum is projected to scale toward 8.5 million tonnes by 2030, and the power industry is expected to add approximately 33,000 MW of coal-fired thermal power capacity in the coming years.
  • Management foresees high-margin opportunities across both the thermal energy and non-ferrous metals sectors over the next 5 years, and sees Ducon as strategically placed to capitalize on regional investments in Odisha, India’s aluminium capital.
  • Ducon secured an end-to-end contract for the supply and service of a Forced Cooling Network (FCN) System for the potlines at one of the largest single Aluminium Smelter Plants in India.

Key Risks

  • Aggressive pricing by new entrants and technology/market changes could pressure the company’s competitive position.
  • Supply chain disruptions, non-availability of project fronts, and increased input costs could affect project execution and margins.
  • High attrition rates in the EPC sector for engineering professionals pose an operational risk.
  • Tightening regulatory requirements and penalties for disclosure delays represent compliance and reputational risks.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Management expects repeat orders to move upwards and aims to book more projects in FGD and bulk material handling systems in the immediate future.
  • The company plans to further reduce its fund-based bank liabilities (cash credit) and expand the volume of its rural and urban electrification project segments.
  • Management foresees tremendous, high-margin opportunities across the thermal energy and non-ferrous metals sectors over the next 5 years.

  • The Board recommended no dividend for the year to plough back profits.
