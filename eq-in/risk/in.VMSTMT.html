VMS TMT Limited — Q1 FY27 Announcements and AGM Materials

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material cost pressure: Scrap prices rose due to the recent Iran conflict, and 50% to 60% of scrap is imported. Unfavourable forex movements and a sudden rise in the US dollar affected average cost.
  • Margin compression: Despite 16.16% year-on-year revenue growth in Q1 FY27, EBITDA fell to ₹1,218.93 lakhs from ₹2,061.76 lakhs in Q1 FY26, and net profit fell to ₹446.75 lakhs from ₹857.64 lakhs. Earnings per share declined to ₹0.90 from ₹2.48.
  • Monsoon impact: A minor monsoon impact during the June quarter caused lower sales while production continued.
  • Competitive intensity: High competition in the infrastructure and builder lobby sector.
  • Rising power costs: Power costs increased after the 30-ton furnace setup.
  • General business risks: Performance of the Indian economy and international markets, industry performance, competition, ability to implement strategy, future growth and expansion levels, technological implementation and changes, fluctuations in revenue, income or cash flows, market preferences, and exposure to market risks.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Backward integration: Commissioning of the billet manufacturing facility strengthened raw material security and cost efficiency, with in-house billet production from scrap. Integrated capacity stands at 2,00,000 MT of TMT Bars and 2,16,000 MT of Billets, supporting a seamless billet-to-TMT process. Backward integration was completed in September 2024 with the 2,16,000 MT billet facility.
  • Renewable energy: A 15 MW captive solar power plant is under development, with 12 MW operational as of August 7, expected to yield savings of approximately ₹10 crores annually and support long-term energy cost optimisation.
  • Amalgamation: The proposed amalgamation of Aditya Ultra Steel Limited with VMS TMT Limited was approved, subject to regulatory approvals, aimed at strengthening manufacturing capabilities and market presence, and expanding the dealer network across Gujarat including Saurashtra and Kutch.
  • Distribution strength: Products are marketed under the Kamdhenu brand through 3 distributors and 227 dealers, under an exclusive retail licence in Gujarat (excluding Saurashtra and Kutch).
  • Demand environment: Strong demand in tier 2, tier 3, and rural markets; approximately 10% CAGR in industry production and consumption of TMT bars; rising demand with infrastructure growth and seismic safety needs.
  • Diversified portfolio: TMT Bars, Billets, Binding Wire and Scrap, with continued focus on quality and BIS compliance, plus a dedicated transport network.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material price volatility linked to imported scrap and geopolitical developments such as the Iran conflict.
  • Foreign exchange fluctuations affecting input costs.
  • Monsoon-related sales seasonality.
  • Intense competition in the infrastructure and builder segment.
  • Rising power costs following the 30-ton furnace setup.
  • Execution and regulatory risk on the Aditya Ultra Steel amalgamation, which remains subject to approvals.
  • Broader economic, market, technological and cash-flow risks cited in the company’s forward-looking statement disclaimer.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Sales growth projected on average around 10% to 15%, with corresponding improvements in EBITDA and PAT; annual dealer network growth targeted at 10% to 15%; inventory expected to normalise and come below ₹200 crores after Diwali; top line targeted to reach ₹2,000 crores plus over the next 2 to 3 years.
  • Observed performance: Q1 FY27 total income of ₹24,787.89 lakhs represented 16.16% year-on-year growth over ₹21,339.35 lakhs in Q1 FY26, exceeding the guided sales growth range. However, EBITDA and PAT declined year on year, so the guided improvement in profitability was not reflected in the quarter. On a sequential basis, performance improved: total income rose from ₹24,135.49 lakhs in Q4 FY26, EBITDA from ₹1,218.93 lakhs versus ₹1,218.93 lakhs reported for Q1 FY27 against ₹2,061.76 lakhs in Q1 FY26, and net profit rose to ₹446.75 lakhs from ₹228.99 lakhs, with EPS improving to ₹0.90 from ₹0.46.
  • Other stated expectations: Management expects inventory to normalise after Diwali, continues to focus on operational excellence, strengthening market presence, creating sustainable value for stakeholders, leveraging integrated manufacturing models, and benefiting from long-term growth in infrastructure and construction demand. The company also cites a ~10% CAGR in industry TMT bar production and consumption as a demand backdrop.
