AI Summary

asof: 2026-09-17

Headwinds and Challenges

  • Khavda solar evacuation risk: The remaining capacity of the Khavda solar project faces potential power evacuation issues and delays.
  • Stranded gas-based assets: Gas-based assets are not expected to be revived due to geopolitical situations and high gas prices.
  • Regulatory conditionality on BESS: The 20 MW/120 MWh Battery Energy Storage System contract awarded to Bondada Engineering Limited is subject to GUVNL consent.
  • Board composition change: Shri Kanyo Sadhuram Badlani ceased to be a Nominee Director with effect from 31/08/2026 following nomination withdrawal by M/s. Gujarat State Fertilizers & Chemicals Limited (GSFC). Resolution No. 03 concerning his re-appointment at the 41st AGM has consequently been dropped, leaving only Resolutions Nos. 1, 2, 4, and 5 before members.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • BESS expansion at Baroda: The Board approved awarding a contract worth Rs. 239.25 Crores to M/s. Bondada Engineering Limited, Hyderabad, for design, engineering, supply & procurement, civil & structural works, erection including testing, commissioning including O&M of ten years, for a BESS rated 20 MW/120 MWh at the 132 kV GIPCL substation, Vadodara. The EPC contract value is Rs. 219.87 Crores (inclusive GST @18%) and the total comprehensive O&M contract value is Rs. 19.37 Crores (inclusive GST @18%) for 10 years. The time period is 18 months from issuance of the Letter of Intent.
  • Thermal expansion: A 750 MW thermal lignite-based power station expansion is planned, targeting commissioning by 2031–2033.
  • Khavda solar completion: Commissioning of the remaining 500 MW Khavda solar project phases is pending.
  • Demand environment: Strong power demand in South Gujarat with top merit order dispatch.
  • Khavda solar performance: The 600 MW Khavda solar project achieved full allocation and is generating a CUF of 33%, with anticipated annual revenue of around INR 420 crores and EBITDA of INR 350–360 crores.

Key Risks

  • Power evacuation constraints and delays on the Khavda solar project’s remaining capacity.
  • Gas-based assets remaining stranded, with no expected revival given geopolitical conditions and high gas prices.
  • Execution and consent risk on the BESS contract, which is subject to GUVNL consent and has an 18-month timeline from Letter of Intent.
  • Financing scale risk associated with the 750 MW thermal expansion, with peak debt estimated around INR 4,500 to INR 6,500 crores.
  • Governance continuity risk from the withdrawal of the GSFC nominee director and the dropping of the related AGM resolution.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: For FY26-27, management expects total EBITDA around INR 950 crores, interest cost of INR 250 to 260 crores, and depreciation of INR 425 to 450 crores. Post-commissioning of the 500 MW solar plant, depreciation is projected at INR 550 to 600 crores and interest cost around INR 400 to 450 crores.
  • Observed performance: The 600 MW Khavda solar project has achieved full allocation and is generating a CUF of 33%, with anticipated annual revenue of around INR 420 crores and EBITDA of INR 350–360 crores. The remaining 500 MW Khavda phases are yet to be commissioned, and the 750 MW thermal expansion is targeted for commissioning by 2031–2033, so the post-commissioning depreciation and interest guidance remains forward-looking relative to current operations.
  • Reporting context: The Board approved the Standalone Unaudited Financial Results for Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026) at its 342nd meeting on 11 August 2026, following Audit Committee review on 10 August 2026 and limited review by the Statutory Auditors, M/s. K C Mehta & Co. LLP. The company has a single reportable segment, “Power Generation,” and no subsidiary, associate, or joint venture as on 30 June 2026.
