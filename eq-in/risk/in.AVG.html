AVG Logistics — Q1 FY2026-27 Announcements Review

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Loss of the Indian Railways parcel train lease: The company had a lease agreement with Indian Railways on the Yesvantpur–Patel Nagar–Yesvantpur route. Indian Railways terminated the lease, citing an objection by South Western Railways on account of loss of revenue from uneconomical operation. At termination, AVG recognised a gain of ₹2,119.41 lakhs (net right-of-use assets of ₹4,321.94 lakhs less lease balance of ₹6,441.35 lakhs) under Ind AS 116, disclosed as a separate line item. This removed a rail route from the network and the associated revenue stream.
  • Sequential revenue decline: Consolidated revenue from operations fell from ₹15,527.79 lakhs in Q4 FY26 to ₹13,247.68 lakhs in Q1 FY27. Freight income declined from ₹14,833.68 lakhs to ₹12,599.09 lakhs, and warehousing income declined from ₹583.15 lakhs to ₹535.98 lakhs.
  • Warehousing income also lower year-on-year: Warehousing income of ₹535.98 lakhs in Q1 FY27 was below ₹686.41 lakhs in Q1 FY26.
  • Competitive pressure acknowledged: The Managing Director’s statement refers to “an increasingly competitive logistics environment” in which the company must serve evolving customer needs.
  • XBRL filing discrepancy: On 25 June 2026, the company responded to an NSE query regarding consolidated EPS in its XBRL filing not matching the PDF version of financial results submitted on 23 June 2026. The company attributed this to a technical issue in the XBRL Excel Utility, where certain figures were inadvertently omitted during validation and XML generation. The company stated it rectified the discrepancy and filed revised XBRL, with NSE acknowledgement dated 25 June 2026.
  • Subsidiaries not audited for the quarter: The auditor’s review report notes that the interim financial information of two subsidiaries (Galaxy Packers and Movers Private Limited and Kaizen Logistics) was not reviewed by their auditors and was furnished by management. These subsidiaries reflect total revenue of ₹192.49 lakhs, net profit after tax of ₹12.18 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of ₹12.18 lakhs for the quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Rights Issue completed: During Q1 FY27, the company raised ₹52.93 crore through a rights issue. The issue opened on 1 June 2026 and closed on 9 June 2026, with 36,50,356 equity shares allotted at ₹145 per share, increasing paid-up equity share capital from ₹1,505.77 lakhs to ₹1,870.81 lakhs. Net proceeds of ₹5,203.95 lakhs (after issue expenses of ₹89.06 lakhs) are earmarked for working capital and general corporate purposes. Utilisation up to 30 June 2026: ₹2,918.29 lakhs toward working capital, ₹678.89 lakhs toward general corporate purpose, with ₹1,606.77 lakhs unutilised.
  • Haldiram Nagpur contract: AVG secured an order for deployment of 100 dedicated vehicles from Haldiram, Nagpur (sweets, namkeen). AVG will provide logistics services across the Western and Southern regions, as well as Odisha, Bihar, and Jharkhand.
  • Joint venture with Baidyanath Group: The company incorporated a joint venture aimed at accelerating adoption of LNG-powered transportation across steel and metals, FMCG, cement, and other high-volume logistics segments. AVG will leverage its logistics network and customer relationships; Baidyanath LNG will provide LNG fleets, fueling infrastructure, and technical expertise.
  • Technology-led operating model: The company is using technology to track and monitor vehicles across routes, and has implemented in-house developed software for flexible, technology-driven solutions. Real-time vehicle tracking gives customers visibility and operational control. The investor presentation describes an in-house Fleet Tracking & Command Center providing real-time fleet visibility, smart fleet allocation, and reduced empty miles.
  • Vision 2030 roadmap (from investor presentation): Targets include ₹1,250 crore revenue by 2030, scaling liquid logistics (ISO tank operations) with ₹24 crore+ annual revenue starting FY26-27, scaling industrial logistics (steel and cement) from ₹12 crore to ₹100 crore+ annually, adding 2 lakh+ sq. ft. of warehousing (~₹12 crore revenue next year), and a projected PAT of ₹120 crore. The presentation also cites ₹100 crore+ capex in FY25-26 and further ₹50 crore per annum.
  • New business wins cited in the presentation: PepsiCo India 3PL operations at Kosi Kalan, Uttar Pradesh (over a million cases per month); a ₹105 crore Indian Railways contract; six Indian Railways tenders worth ₹5,100 million for six leased parcel trains; UPSRTC tender; acquisition of 99% stake in Kaizen Logistics; and 4 acres acquired at Khordha, Odisha.
  • Green fleet positioning: First Indian company to acquire a fleet of five high-tonnage (55 MT) electric vehicles, with handover in Jamshedpur in March 2025; first LNG fleet launched; LNG/EV/CNG vehicles aligned with India’s 2030 freight rail target.
  • Investor re-entry: The presentation cites Sixth Sense Ventures re-entering AVG Logistics with approximately 17% stake, referencing the firm’s earlier partnership with the promoters from 2018.

3. Key Risks

  • Contract concentration and termination risk: The Indian Railways lease termination on the Yesvantpur–Patel Nagar route illustrates exposure to counterparty decisions on long-term contracts. The termination was attributed to uneconomical operation and loss of revenue.
  • Regulatory and listing compliance risk: The XBRL discrepancy required a clarification to NSE and a revised filing, highlighting disclosure and filing-process risk.
  • Dependence on joint venture partners and new verticals: The LNG joint venture with Baidyanath depends on the partner providing LNG fleets, fueling infrastructure, and technical expertise. The liquid logistics, industrial logistics, and warehousing expansion plans are at varying stages of execution.
  • Execution risk on Vision 2030 targets: The ₹1,250 crore revenue and ₹120 crore PAT targets by 2030 are forward-looking statements subject to risks and uncertainties noted in the company’s disclaimer, including government actions, political or economic developments, and technological risks.
  • Subsidiary oversight: Two subsidiaries’ interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors for Q1 FY27, and the auditor’s conclusion relies on management-prepared unaudited results for those entities.
  • Capital structure changes: Increase in authorised share capital from ₹21 crore to ₹25 crore, an Employee Stock Option Scheme 2026 with a pool of 9,50,000 options (exercise price equal to face value), and the rights issue all affect the equity base. The ESOP scheme is subject to shareholder approval at the AGM on 25 September 2026.
  • Governance and leadership transition: Continuation of Mr. Susheel Kumar Tyagi as Independent Director post age 75 and appointment of Mr. Sumit Garg as Whole-Time Director for five years from 14 August 2026 are subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing AGM.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Q1 FY27 reported performance (consolidated): Revenue from operations ₹132.48 crore, up 5.97% year-on-year from ₹125.02 crore. PBT ₹8.71 crore, up 24.42%, with PBT margin at 6.58% versus 5.60%. PAT ₹6.46 crore, up 29.98%, with PAT margin at 4.87% versus 3.98%. The company described Q1 FY27 as “a promising start to the fiscal year.”
  • Sequential comparison: Consolidated revenue declined from ₹15,527.79 lakhs in Q4 FY26 to ₹13,247.68 lakhs in Q1 FY27. Q4 FY26 included the one-time lease termination gain of ₹2,119.41 lakhs, which is not present in Q1 FY27.
  • FY26 full-year baseline (from press release and presentation): Total income ₹582.48 crore, EBITDA ₹112.45 crore, PAT ₹26.17 crore. The Vision 2030 target of ₹1,250 crore revenue implies more than doubling from the FY26 base over the plan period.
  • Rights issue proceeds utilisation: Of the ₹5,203.95 lakhs net proceeds, ₹2,918.29 lakhs (working capital) and ₹678.89 lakhs (general corporate purpose) were utilised by 30 June 2026, with ₹1,606.77 lakhs unutilised. The stated objects were augmenting working capital and general corporate purposes.
  • Dividend recommendation: The Board recommended a final dividend of Re. 1.2 per equity share for FY2025-26, subject to shareholder approval at the 17th AGM on 25 September 2026.
  • Auditor conclusion: The statutory auditors issued an unmodified limited review report on both standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with the qualification that two subsidiaries’ interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors and was management-prepared.
  • Segment performance: Freight income ₹12,599.09 lakhs (versus ₹11,764.82 lakhs in Q1 FY26); warehousing income ₹535.98 lakhs (versus ₹686.41 lakhs in Q1 FY26); other operating income ₹112.61 lakhs (versus ₹50.92 lakhs in Q1 FY26). All revenue was within India; no revenue from outside India.
  • Standalone versus consolidated: Standalone revenue from operations was ₹13,078.22 lakhs versus consolidated ₹13,247.68 lakhs. Standalone PAT was ₹601.45 lakhs versus consolidated ₹645.52 lakhs. The difference reflects the two subsidiaries.
