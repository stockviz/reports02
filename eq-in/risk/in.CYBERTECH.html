CYBERTECH: Recent Corporate Announcements — Key Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Rising cost base: Total expenses on a consolidated basis rose to ₹6,401.75 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹5,441.00 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26. Outsourced service costs increased to ₹1,726.00 lakhs from ₹1,397.33 lakhs, employee benefits rose to ₹3,733.58 lakhs from ₹3,364.93 lakhs, and other expenses climbed to ₹813.26 lakhs from ₹563.82 lakhs.
  • Finance costs increased: Consolidated finance costs rose to ₹41.31 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹37.80 lakhs a year earlier and ₹16.09 lakhs in the preceding quarter.
  • Tax burden: Consolidated current tax rose to ₹258.35 lakhs from ₹246.29 lakhs year-on-year, with an additional tax adjustment for earlier years of ₹7.41 lakhs in the quarter.
  • Currency translation drag: The consolidated foreign currency translation reserve moved to a loss of ₹24.96 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with a gain of ₹22.67 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and a gain of ₹455.55 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26.
  • Sequential profit decline: Consolidated profit for the period fell to ₹947.72 lakhs from ₹706.74 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26 on a comparable basis, though it rose from ₹817.34 lakhs year-on-year. Standalone profit declined sequentially to ₹594.30 lakhs from ₹665.20 lakhs.
  • Capital reduction from buyback: Paid-up equity share capital declined to ₹3,028.06 lakhs as at June 30, 2026 from ₹3,113.06 lakhs, reflecting the buyback and extinguishment of 8,50,000 equity shares.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Revenue growth: Consolidated revenue from operations rose to ₹6,844.73 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹5,819.41 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and ₹6,233.37 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26. Standalone revenue rose to ₹4,402.28 lakhs from ₹3,962.24 lakhs year-on-year.
  • Other income support: Consolidated other income increased to ₹818.55 lakhs from ₹704.59 lakhs year-on-year.
  • Profit growth year-on-year: Consolidated profit for the period rose to ₹947.72 lakhs from ₹817.34 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26. Standalone profit rose to ₹594.30 lakhs from ₹481.40 lakhs.
  • Earnings per share improvement: Consolidated basic EPS rose to ₹3.06 from ₹2.63 year-on-year; standalone basic EPS rose to ₹1.92 from ₹1.55.
  • Shareholder returns: The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹4/- per equity share of face value ₹10/- for FY2025-26.
  • Capital return via buyback: The company completed a buyback of 8,50,000 equity shares at ₹170 per share, totaling ₹1,445.00 lakhs (excluding transaction costs of ₹53.00 lakhs), concluded on June 18, 2026, funded from free reserves.
  • Single reportable segment: The Group operates as a single reportable business segment, ‘Information Technology Services’, under IND AS 108.

3. Key Risks

  • Cost inflation risk: The pace of growth in outsourced service costs, employee benefits, and other expenses outpacing revenue growth in some periods could pressure margins.
  • Currency risk: Foreign currency translation movements have swung between gains and losses across quarters, affecting total comprehensive income.
  • Tax risk: Deferred tax and current tax charges, along with adjustments for earlier years, add variability to net profit.
  • Buyback execution risk: The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹1,445.00 lakhs and creation of a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹85.00 lakhs, reducing free reserves.
  • Dependence on subsidiaries: The consolidated results include wholly owned subsidiaries CyberTech Systems and Software Inc. USA, Spatialitics LLC USA, and CyberTech Systems and Software Canada Inc., exposing the Group to cross-border operational and regulatory risks.
