LPDC — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Karnal township exposure and settlement. Advances originally given to Ansal Landmark Townships Pvt. Ltd. (ALTPL) were transferred to Eterna Living Pvt. Ltd. (ELPL), the special purpose entity for the Karnal township project, under a Business Transfer Agreement dated 2 April 2012. Under an agreement dated 16 May 2013 (amended from time to time), ELPL was to allot plots/flats in group housing, row housing and commercial property, with the timeline revised up to 31 March 2027. During FY 2025-26, regulatory constraints, expiry of approvals and uncertainty over allotment within the agreed timeframe led the Company and ELPL to mutually cancel the remaining space booking arrangement. Shareholders approved the cancellation at an Extra Ordinary General Meeting held 12 March 2026, and a Refund and Settlement Agreement was executed on 27 March 2026.

Recoverability of the Karnal refund. ELPL irrevocably agreed to refund Rs.3,526.15 lakhs by 26 September 2026. The Company received Rs.15.00 lakhs during the quarter ended 31 March 2026, adjusted against the outstanding balance, leaving Rs.3,511.15 lakhs, reclassified from “Advance against acquisition of property to related party” to “Refund receivable against advance paid for acquisition of property” under other current financial assets. A provision of Rs.1,600.00 lakhs recognised in earlier years was retained as at 30 June 2026, considering the latest audited financial position of ELPL, leaving a net carrying amount of Rs.1,911.15 lakhs. The auditors drew attention to this recoverability matter in the Limited Review Report (Note 4), without modifying their opinion.

Mining demands. A demand notice of Rs.105.90 lakhs was received during FY 2021-22 from the Office of the Mining Officer, Government of Odisha, for alleged excess extraction/production of minerals at Talabasta Fireclay Mines beyond permitted quantities during 2000-01 to 2010-11, under Section 21(5) of the Mines & Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957. The Company’s position is that royalty and other dues were paid and minerals were used captively in its refractory unit. An appeal was filed on 9 March 2022 before the Director of Mines, Bhubaneswar; the hearing concluded on 22 June 2022 and the order remains reserved. Separately, a letter dated 8 November 2023 from the Deputy Director of Mines, Cuttack Circle sought Rs.13.05 lakhs as differential dead rent for July 2010 to December 2014; the Company responded on 5 December 2023 that nothing was payable since the lease expired on 6 January 2005. A Writ Petition was filed in the High Court of Orissa, Cuttack, and a stay on the demand was received subsequent to the balance sheet date, valid till 29 July 2025. The matter was not listed on 29 July 2025 and remains pending.

Repayment timeline extension. Based on a request received from ELPL, the Board at its meeting on 12 August 2026 agreed to extend the repayment timeline for the outstanding balance from 26 September 2026 to 31 March 2027, subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing Annual General Meeting.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Recovery of legacy balances and ECL reversal. During the quarter ended 30 June 2026, pursuant to settlement agreements with Saya Buildcon Consortium Private Limited, the Company recovered capital advances of Rs.400.00 lakhs, an inter-corporate loan of Rs.367.07 lakhs and accrued interest of Rs.9.76 lakhs. This resulted in a reversal of the corresponding expected credit loss provision aggregating Rs.776.83 lakhs, which had been recognised against these balances in earlier years. This reversal is the principal driver of the quarter’s reported profitability.

Quarterly performance. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, total income was Rs.932.28 lakhs (including the Rs.776.83 lakhs ECL reversal), profit before tax was Rs.806.24 lakhs, and profit after tax was Rs.696.43 lakhs, with basic and diluted EPS of Rs.0.52. This compares with total income of Rs.16.62 lakhs, a loss before tax of Rs.0.92 lakhs and a loss after tax of Rs.0.68 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

Single operating segment. The Company operates primarily in real estate development, which is its only operating segment under Ind AS-108. It had no subsidiary, associate or joint venture as at 30 June 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Counterparty and refund risk on the Karnal exposure: the net receivable of Rs.1,911.15 lakhs from ELPL remains subject to recovery, with the timeline now proposed to be extended to 31 March 2027, and the retained provision reflects prudence over ELPL’s latest audited financial position.
  • Contingent mining liabilities: the Rs.105.90 lakhs Odisha demand (appeal pending, order reserved) and the Rs.13.05 lakhs dead rent demand (writ pending, interim stay granted till 29 July 2025, matter not listed thereafter).
  • Dependence on non-operating income: the June 2026 quarter’s profit is attributable to the one-time ECL reversal rather than to revenue from operations, which was Rs.123.19 lakhs for the quarter.
  • Approval risk: the extended ELPL repayment timeline requires shareholder approval at the ensuing Annual General Meeting.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Karnal refund timeline. Management’s stated position, via the 27 March 2026 Refund and Settlement Agreement, was that ELPL would irrevocably refund Rs.3,526.15 lakhs on or before 26 September 2026. Observed performance: only Rs.15.00 lakhs was received during the quarter ended 31 March 2026, and on 12 August 2026 the Board agreed to extend the deadline to 31 March 2027, subject to shareholder approval — indicating the original schedule has not been met and the recovery is being pushed out.

Revenue trajectory. Revenue from operations was Rs.123.19 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with Rs.576.26 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and Rs.734.86 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026. The June 2025 quarter showed no revenue from operations. The most recent quarter’s revenue is therefore well below the immediately preceding quarter and the prior full year.

Profit quality. The quarter ended 30 June 2026 reported profit after tax of Rs.696.43 lakhs against a loss after tax of Rs.0.68 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. The swing is driven by the Rs.776.83 lakhs ECL reversal and Rs.32.26 lakhs of other income, rather than by operating revenue, which remains modest.

Auditor position. The statutory auditors issued an unmodified conclusion on the June 2026 results, with an Emphasis of Matter on the recoverability of the ELPL space booking advances, and noted that the results were approved by the Board on 12 August 2026 (Board meeting convened at 3:50 p.m. and concluded at 4:10 p.m.).

Governance calendar. The 50th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Monday, 21 September 2026 at 11:30 a.m. via video conferencing/OAVM. The e-voting cut-off date is 14 September 2026, e-voting runs from 9:00 a.m. on 18 September 2026 to 5:00 p.m. on 20 September 2026, and Mr. Rahul Dhupar, Practicing Company Secretary, has been appointed Scrutinizer.
