Headwinds and Challenges

  • External operating environment: Management describes an “uncertain external environment” marked by global trade realignments, currency volatility, and elevated input costs, while stating the company continues to adapt effectively.
  • Industrial Systems margin pressure: Segment PBIT fell to INR 148 Cr (8.8% of sales) in Q1FY27 from INR 172 Cr (10.9% of sales) in Q1FY26. The margin deviation is largely attributed to a one-off provision of INR 20 Cr in the Railways business. Sales growth in the segment was modest at 6% YoY.
  • Semiconductor drag on consolidated profitability: Continued investment in the talent pool for semiconductor businesses had a total segment impact of INR 43 Cr (132 bps) in Q1FY27. The consolidated semiconductor segment reported a loss of INR 49.99 Cr for the quarter, versus a loss of INR 8.70 Cr in Q1FY26.
  • Consolidated growth trailing standalone: Consolidated sales grew 14% YoY and consolidated PAT 16% YoY, below the standalone growth rates of 16% and 27% respectively, with margin gains from standalone performance partially offset by semiconductor investment.
  • Capacity constraints in EHV GIS: The existing EHV GIS manufacturing facility is operating at full capacity, with constraints in both production throughput and physical space; the shop floor, assembly bays, testing areas, and material storage are fully utilized, and the plant cannot accommodate additional equipment or production lines without disrupting ongoing operations. Existing capacity utilization was 91% in FY26.
  • Auditor transition: S R B C & CO LLP intended to resign as statutory auditors effective close of business hours on August 14, 2026, consequent to the holding company’s (Tube Investments of India Limited) auditor rotation. The company sought member approval via Postal Ballot to appoint M/s. Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP as Statutory Auditors to fill the casual vacancy, for the period from 19th August 2026 until the conclusion of the 90th AGM in 2027, at an aggregate annual remuneration of Rs. 1.58 crores (excluding taxes and out-of-pocket expenses).
  • Tax litigation: The company is involved in certain ongoing direct tax litigations pending before various forums; management is confident of a favourable outcome based on prevailing jurisprudence, past rulings, and legal opinions.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong order momentum: Standalone order intake for Q1FY27 was INR 4,692 Cr, with unexecuted order backlog of INR 17,333 Cr as of 30th June 2026, up 45% YoY, offering multi-quarter revenue visibility. Consolidated order intake was INR 5,211 Cr, with backlog up 45% YoY at INR 18,965 Cr.
  • Power Systems outperformance: Segment sales rose 31% YoY to INR 1,402 Cr, with PBIT up 44% to INR 324 Cr (23.1% of sales). Margin expanded 209 bps, attributed to disciplined execution and operating leverage. Segment order backlog was INR 14,434 Cr, up 59% YoY, offering revenue visibility spanning several future quarters.
  • Motors growth: Industrial Systems saw strong double-digit growth in Motors sales and double-digit YoY growth in Motors orders.
  • EHV switchgear capacity expansion: On 4th June 2026, the company commissioned the S3 Unit-II EHV switchgear facility at Pimpalgaon Garudeshwar, Nashik, Maharashtra, manufacturing EHV circuit breakers in the 33 kV to 245 kV range. This expands EHV circuit breaker manufacturing capacity by 80% (incremental 7,200 units annually vs. existing 9,000 units annually), with 500 kV and 350 kV high-voltage testing laboratories, designed to meet growing demand for power transmission equipment in domestic and international markets.
  • Further GIS capacity expansion: The Board approved a brownfield expansion for EHV Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS) at Vilholi, Nashik, with total estimated project cost of approximately Rs. 35.17 Crores (net of taxes), expected completion within 4–6 months. Proposed capacity addition is 600 Eq Units (FY30 peak capacity) against existing capacity of 228 Eq Units, financed through internal accruals/equity. It is expected that 87% of the brownfield project assets will be transferred to the Greenfield facility upon its commissioning.
  • Semiconductor commercialization: CG Semi Private Limited announced commencement of commercial production at its G1 Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test facility in Sanand on 4th July 2026.
  • Demand drivers: Management cites intact and diversified demand drivers spanning grid modernisation, renewable integration, rail modernisation, and electronics manufacturing in India, supported by improvement in manufacturing activity and private capex.
  • Acquisition completion: Axiro Semiconductor Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary, completed the acquisition of 100% of Tosil Systems Private Limited on 20th August 2026, following execution of a Securities Purchase Agreement disclosed on 17th August 2026, for a consideration of Rs. 16.44 Crores. Tosil has become a wholly owned subsidiary of Axiro.
  • Investor engagement: A one-to-one meeting with Premji Invest was scheduled for Wednesday, 12th August 2026.

Key Risks

  • Global trade realignments, currency volatility, and elevated input costs persisting in the operating environment.
  • Concentration of margin pressure in Industrial Systems from the Railways one-off provision, with segment PBIT down 14% YoY.
  • Continued losses and investment requirements in the semiconductor segment, including talent pool investment, weighing on consolidated margins.
  • Capacity constraints at the existing EHV GIS facility limiting ability to meet higher order intake without compromising delivery timelines and quality standards.
  • Ongoing direct tax litigations pending before various forums.
  • Execution and completion risk on the approved brownfield GIS expansion (4–6 month timeline) and the transfer of 87% of brownfield project assets to the Greenfield facility upon commissioning.
  • Transition risk associated with the statutory auditor change from S R B C & CO LLP to Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP, subject to member approval via Postal Ballot.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management states it will “stay the course on capacity investment,” believing the medium-term opportunity is stronger than near-term headwinds, with priority on “building capability today.” Observed actions consistent with this include the commissioning of the S3 Unit-II EHV switchgear facility on 4th June 2026, Board approval of the Vilholi GIS brownfield expansion (Rs. 35.17 Crores, 4–6 months, 600 Eq Units peak capacity by FY30), and commencement of commercial production at the CG Semi OSAT facility in Sanand on 4th July 2026.
  • Management describes “steady, disciplined execution” and states demand drivers remain intact. Observed Q1FY27 performance: standalone sales up 16% YoY, PBT up 27% YoY with 140 bps margin expansion; consolidated sales up 14% YoY and PAT up 16% YoY; standalone ROCE at 23% and consolidated ROCE at 20%; order backlog up 45% YoY on both standalone and consolidated bases.
  • Management notes margin gains from strong standalone performance were partially offset by continued semiconductor talent pool investment (INR 43 Cr impact, 132 bps). Observed consolidated EBITDA margin of 14.7% versus standalone 16.9% in Q1FY27 is consistent with this offset.
  • Management attributes Industrial Systems margin deviation largely to a one-off INR 20 Cr Railways provision; observed segment PBIT margin of 8.8% versus 10.9% in Q1FY26.
  • Management states the existing EHV GIS facility is at full capacity and the current and projected order pipeline necessitates doubling of existing manufacturing capacity; observed FY26 capacity utilization of 91% and proposed addition of 600 Eq Units against existing 228 Eq Units.
  • Management states the auditor alignment is intended to enable better coordination with the Holding Company’s auditors and bring efficiency and synergy to the audit process, given CG together with its subsidiaries represents more than 50% of the Holding Company’s consolidated financial statements; observed action is the proposed appointment of Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP via Postal Ballot for the period from 19th August 2026 until the conclusion of the 90th AGM in 2027.

Broker Narrative

Narrative shifted from strong growth optimism (27% revenue growth, 97% power systems orders, 85,000 MVA expansion) to cautious outlook with multiple headwinds (T&D capex slowdown, commodity inflation, weak motor demand, railway weakness). Transformer capacity target was raised to 120,000 MVA and order book remained robust at INR144b, but margin pressure and demand softness dominated. Railway flipped from Kavach-driven growth to weakness, while semiconductor/OSAT became a new theme.

Fears that came true

  • T&D capex slowdown materialized, directly hitting the power transformer business that was expected to benefit from rising solar capacity.
  • Commodity price increases materialized, pressuring margins as flagged in the earlier EBITDA contraction concern.
  • Railway segment weakness materialized, reversing the earlier optimism around the Kavach TCAS order and railway business growth.
  • Weak motor demand materialized, contradicting the earlier optimism about IE5/IE5+ and EV motors/controllers development.
  • Limited OSAT experience emerged as a real constraint on semiconductor business scaling plans.

Optimism that failed

  • EV motor and controller development failed to generate demand as motor demand turned weak industry-wide.
  • Export capability enhancement faced limitations due to limited OSAT experience in semiconductor operations.
  • Margin expansion optimism failed as estimates were revised down by 60-110 bps and commodity prices increased.
