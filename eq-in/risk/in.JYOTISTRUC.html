Jyoti Structures Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Erosion of subsidiary net worth: The net worth of certain overseas subsidiaries and one Indian subsidiary has been fully eroded on account of accumulated losses. No operations were conducted during the year in the overseas subsidiaries.
  • Receivables provisioning and reconciliation: A provision of Rs. 15.25 Cr has been made for estimated credit loss on trade receivables, and a reconciliation process regarding trade receivables is ongoing.
  • Unresolved reconciliations and unreviewed accounts: There are unresolved reconciliations with trade receivables and uncertainties regarding the impact of unreviewed management accounts of certain overseas branches and subsidiaries.
  • Internal auditor removal: The Board, at its meeting on August 04, 2026, approved the removal of M/s. Bhushan Khot & Co., Chartered Accountants, as one of the Internal Auditors with immediate effect from that date, based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee. M/s. KNBJ & Co. continues as Internal Auditor.
  • Shareholder dissent on certain resolutions: At the 51st AGM held on August 13, 2026, the resolution to increase authorised share capital (Item 3) saw 7.69% of valid votes cast against it, and the branch auditor appointment (Item 4) and cost auditor remuneration ratification (Item 5) each saw approximately 4.98%–5.05% against. Adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements (Items 1 and 2) saw only 0.06% against.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Fundraising evaluation: The Board considered and evaluated raising funds up to INR 250 crores by way of issuance of equity shares or other instruments/securities through qualified institutions placement, private placement, further public issue, preferential issue, rights issue, or any combination thereof. Instruments considered include convertible debentures or warrants.
  • Strong Q1 FY 2026-27 performance: For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the company reported standalone total income of ₹255.18 Cr versus ₹162.40 Cr in Q1 FY 2025-26 (up 57.1%), EBITDA of ₹24.97 Cr versus ₹13.14 Cr (up 90.0%), PBT of ₹19.27 Cr versus ₹10.24 Cr (up 88.2%), and net profit of ₹19.46 Cr versus ₹11.16 Cr (up 74.4%). EBITDA margin improved to 9.79% from 8.09%, and PBT margin to 7.55% from 6.31%.
  • Order pipeline and sector opportunity: Management cited a robust order pipeline and continued opportunities in the transmission and distribution (T&D) sector, with focus on disciplined execution and sustainable growth.
  • Authorised share capital increase: Shareholders approved an increase in authorised share capital and consequential amendment to the Memorandum of Association (Item 3), carried with 92.31% of valid votes in favour.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued losses at overseas and one Indian subsidiary, with fully eroded net worth and no overseas operations during the year.
  • Credit loss exposure on trade receivables, evidenced by the Rs. 15.25 Cr provision, with reconciliation still ongoing.
  • Uncertainty from unreviewed management accounts of certain overseas branches and subsidiaries.
  • Governance and control attention arising from the removal of one internal auditor mid-year.
  • Execution and delivery risk inherent in the EPC and T&D project portfolio, which management identifies as the basis for its growth outlook.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management stated the company remains focused on disciplined execution and sustainable growth, citing a robust order pipeline and T&D opportunities. Observed Q1 FY 2026-27 performance is consistent with this: total income rose 57.1% year-on-year to ₹255.18 Cr, EBITDA rose 90.0% to ₹24.97 Cr, and net profit rose 74.4% to ₹19.46 Cr, with margin expansion of 169 BPS at EBITDA level and 125 BPS at PBT level.
  • The Board evaluated fundraising of up to INR 250 crores, and shareholders subsequently approved an increase in authorised share capital at the 51st AGM on August 13, 2026, with 92.31% of valid votes in favour — a step consistent with enabling future fund-raising.
  • The statutory auditors issued an unmodified review report for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • No specific forward-looking financial guidance figures were provided in the supplied material beyond the qualitative statements on order pipeline, T&D opportunities, and focus on disciplined execution and sustainable growth.
