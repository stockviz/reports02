Shiv Aum Steels Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory scrutiny over financial results signing. On July 01, 2026, the company responded to communications from the National Stock Exchange regarding deficiencies observed in its financial results for the period ended March 31, 2026 (approved by the Board on May 23, 2026). The company clarified that the results were duly signed by Mr. Krishna Nagin Mehta, Whole-time Director, who is authorized to sign financial results under Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and asserted there was no deficiency. This indicates the exchange raised a compliance query on the signing of the annual results.

  • Subsidiary profitability drag. The consolidated review report discloses that Shivoham Ventures Private Limited (100% subsidiary) recorded total revenue of ₹908.61 lakhs (gross of eliminations ₹205.30 lakhs) as at June 30, 2026, but total net profit of only ₹10.81 lakhs — a very thin margin relative to revenue.

  • Limited review, not audit. The standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 are unaudited and subject to limited review, which provides only moderate assurance and is substantially less in scope than an audit.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Proposed BSE main board listing. On August 11, 2026, the Board approved making an application for direct listing/trading of the company’s equity shares on the Main Board of the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). This was subsequently approved by shareholders at the 7th AGM (Post IPO) held on September 09, 2026, with 100% of valid votes in favour. A main board listing could broaden the investor base and improve liquidity.

  • Stable auditor continuity. The Board approved the re-appointment of M/s Agarwal Jain & Gupta, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 013538C), as Statutory Auditor for FY 2026-27 and 2027-28, subject to member approval. The firm’s profile includes Audit & Assurance, Taxation, Corporate Advisory, RBI/FEMA compliances, and IPO/SEBI-related certifications, with experience across private, government, and listed entities.

  • Consolidated structure with a wholly-owned subsidiary. The consolidated results include Shivoham Ventures Private Limited (100% holding), indicating an operating group structure beyond the standalone steel business.

3. Key Risks

  • Compliance and disclosure risk. The exchange’s identification of deficiencies in the March 31, 2026 financial results, even though the company disputed them, highlights exposure to regulatory review of filings.

  • Dependence on limited review assurance. Interim results carry only moderate assurance; material misstatements may not be detected that an audit would identify.

  • Subsidiary performance concentration. The subsidiary’s net profit of ₹10.81 lakhs on revenue of ₹908.61 lakhs indicates low profitability, which could weigh on consolidated earnings.

  • Execution risk on the BSE listing application. The direct listing on BSE’s main board remains an application; approval and timing are not assured by the documents.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance/forward-looking items: The Board approved CSR expenditure for FY 2026-27, the Draft Board’s Report and annexures for FY 2025-26, the Management Discussion and Analysis Report for FY 2025-26, and the notice for the 7th AGM (Post IPO). The company also stated its intention to apply for direct listing/trading on the BSE Main Board. The statutory auditor term covers FY 2026-27 and 2027-28.

  • Observed performance: For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the company reported unaudited standalone and consolidated results under Ind AS, accompanied by limited review reports with no material misstatements noted. The subsidiary Shivoham Ventures reported revenue of ₹908.61 lakhs (gross of eliminations ₹205.30 lakhs) and net profit of ₹10.81 lakhs as at June 30, 2026.

  • Shareholder validation: All eight resolutions at the 7th AGM (Post IPO) on September 09, 2026 were passed with the requisite majority and 100% of valid votes in favour, including adoption of audited financial statements, director appointments/continuation, statutory auditor re-appointment, and the BSE main board listing application.
