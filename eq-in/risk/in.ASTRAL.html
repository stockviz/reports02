Astral Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

Headwinds and Challenges

Weak piping demand environment. Management states that overall demand in the plastic pipe industry was weak in Q1 FY-27, primarily due to volatility in polymer prices, which were on a downward trend. Industry demand was down by approximately 10% during the quarter.

Flat volumes in the core plumbing business. Despite gaining market share, Astral closed the quarter with flat volume growth. Plumbing sales in M.T. were 56,146 in Q1 FY-27 versus 56,074 in Q1 FY-25-26, a change of 0.1%. Value growth of 10.1% was driven by better realisation rather than volume.

Margin pressure in Adhesives. The Adhesive India Business delivered 24.9% sales growth with a 12.2% EBIDTA margin, but management notes pressure on margin due to higher-priced raw material in stock and price increases taken with a gap. Management expresses confidence this will be overcome in the next quarter.

Lower segment margin in Paints and Adhesives. Segment EBIDTA margin for Paints and Adhesives declined to 8.7% in Q1 FY-27 from 9.2% in Q1 FY-25-26, even as revenue grew 29.5%.

Paint business at break-even. The Paint Business grew 48.7% in sales, described as the first time in history, but delivered EBIDTA break-even, indicating it is not yet contributing profit.

Gross profit trend. Consolidated gross profit declined across the periods shown, from 40.5% in Q1 FY-22 to 31.1% in Q1 FY-27.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Market share gains in piping. Management states the company is continuously gaining market share in the piping industry, with growth highest among all leading players in the plastic pipe industry, and achieved the highest EBIDTA of 18.9% among all industry players.

Realisation and pricing support. Realisation was better during the quarter, producing 10.1% value growth against flat volume. Management expects this to continue in the coming quarter because PVC prices are inching up in Q2 and implementation of MIP (Minimum Import Price) will help stabilise PVC prices, particularly protecting the bottom price of PVC.

Capacity expansion. Pipes and Fittings production capacity increased from 4,17,645 M.T. to 4,21,497 M.T. during Q1 FY-27.

CPVC Resin plant. The new 40,000 M.T. CPVC Resin plant Phase I is progressing as per schedule, expected ready by December with trial runs in Q4 FY-27. Management expects this plant to help gain market share in CPVC Pipe and Fitting and improve margins, with full benefit available from FY-28 onwards.

Bathware growth. Bathware business grew 18.1% in sales during Q1 FY-27.

Adhesives momentum. New Bharat accelerated rural expansion, adding 8,000+ towns in Q1 and taking direct dealers beyond 1,500, including 500+ additions during the quarter. Joinery established presence across 8 states. Sales through Modern Trade and digital commerce expanded, maintaining best-seller leadership on such platforms. Construction Chemicals growth was driven by the Trubuild relaunch, sharper category focus, and accelerated PAN-India expansion in Tile Adhesives.

International Adhesives recovery. Adhesive Overseas Business grew 26% in sales with positive EBIDTA margin of 4.9%, against prior guidance of 10%+ revenue growth.

Specialty Chemicals entry. Astral Chemie Limited entered definitive agreements dated June 11, 2026 to acquire a 60% partnership interest with effect from April 1, 2026 in Differentiated & Sustainable Solutions LLP (DSS), with an upfront payment of Rs. 391 million. DSS contributed sales revenue of Rs. 67 million and EBIDTA of Rs. 9 million on a standalone basis in its first quarter, opening a new avenue for higher-value growth.

Paints acceleration. Paint Business grew 48.7% in sales with all six operating states contributing to momentum. Extura Vivid, a new exterior emulsion, strengthened the portfolio with differentiated segment-first features.

Key Risks

Demand sensitivity to polymer prices. Industry demand weakness was attributed to volatility and downward trends in polymer prices, indicating the business is exposed to pricing cycles in raw materials.

Margin sustainability in Adhesives. Raw material cost dynamics and timing gaps in price increases created margin pressure, with recovery dependent on future pricing actions.

Execution risk on capacity projects. The CPVC Resin plant timeline (ready by December, trial runs in Q4 FY-27, full benefit from FY-28) carries execution and commissioning risk.

Paint business profitability. Despite strong sales growth, the Paint Business is at EBIDTA break-even, with profitability yet to be demonstrated.

Governance disclosure correction. Astral Limited submitted revised voting results for its 30th Annual General Meeting held on August 24, 2026. The correction was made under Resolution No. 4 regarding the re-appointment of Mr. Sandeep Engineer as Managing Director, where the field “Whether promoter/promoter group are interested in the agenda/resolution?” was incorrectly stated as “No” instead of “Yes”.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

International Adhesives. Management had guided 10%+ revenue growth; the business delivered 26% growth in Q1 FY-27, which management describes as excellent recovery against guidance.

Adhesives margin recovery. Management states confidence that margin pressure from higher-priced raw material in stock and gapped price increases will be overcome in the next quarter.

Piping realisation. Management expects better realisation to continue in the coming quarter due to PVC prices inching up in Q2 and implementation of MIP stabilising PVC prices.

CPVC Resin plant. Management states the plant is progressing as per schedule, will be ready by December, trial runs in Q4 FY-27, and full benefit available from FY-28 onwards.

Consolidated performance versus prior year. Q1 FY-27 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 15,780 million versus Rs. 13,612 million in Q1 FY-25-26, a 15.9% increase. EBIDTA was Rs. 2,440 million (15.5% of net sales) versus Rs. 1,940 million (14.3%), a 25.8% increase. PBT was Rs. 1,628 million (10.3%) versus Rs. 1,098 million (8.1%), a 48.3% increase. PAT before OCI was Rs. 1,202 million (7.6%) versus Rs. 792 million (5.8%), a 51.8% increase. Cash profit was Rs. 1,956 million (12.4%) versus Rs. 1,511 million (11.1%), a 29.5% increase. Basic/diluted EPS was Rs. 4.47 versus Rs. 3.02, a 48.0% increase.

Plumbing segment. Revenue was Rs. 10,505 million versus Rs. 9,539 million, a 10.1% increase. Segment EBIDTA was Rs. 1,983 million (18.9%) versus Rs. 1,565 million (16.4%), a 26.7% increase. Sales in M.T. were 56,146 versus 56,074, a 0.1% increase.

Paints and Adhesives segment. Revenue was Rs. 5,275 million versus Rs. 4,073 million, a 29.5% increase. Segment EBIDTA was Rs. 457 million (8.7%) versus Rs. 375 million (9.2%), a 21.9% increase.

Balance sheet. Consolidated cash including cash equivalents and bank balances as at June 30, 2026 was Rs. 4,666 million.

Dividend/TDS communication. A shareholders communication on tax deducted at source on payment of final dividend for Financial Year 2025-26 was issued on July 31, 2026.

Earnings call transcript. The transcript of the earnings call held on August 12, 2026 was uploaded on the investor relations section of the company website, as communicated on August 18, 2026.

Broker Narrative

The narrative evolved from 2023 optimism on domestic plumbing volumes, margin expansion, and paints growth to 2026 concerns over resin volatility, channel destocking, and Chinese import competition, while raw material cost pressures persisted (high-cost inventory → resin volatility) and capacity ambitions remained (Hyderabad plant → capacity additions). The stock’s severe 2023-2024 corrections validated early valuation and cost fears, but the 2026 Buy call pivoted to import protection and Wavin integration as new catalysts.

Fears that came true

  • Rich Valuation (50-60x): Materialized as the stock suffered severe corrections (-18% to -36%) in 2023-2024, indicating the premium price was unsustainable.
  • High Cost Inventory: Delayed raw material benefits persisted as resin price volatility and elevated PVC prices continued to pressure margins and offtake through 2026.

Optimism that failed

  • Strong Plumbing Volume Growth: Failed as weak agricultural demand and elevated PVC prices significantly weighed on offtake by 2026.
  • Significant Margin Expansion: Failed as sharp resin price volatility and channel destocking reversed margin gains.
  • Strong Business Guidance (15-20% volume, 17-18% margins): Failed to materialize amid ongoing destocking and demand softness despite capacity additions.
