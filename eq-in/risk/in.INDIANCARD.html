Headwinds and Challenges

  • The Carding business continues to face “incessant headwinds” from the Indian Textile Industry, attributed to global geopolitical disturbances and a global economic slowdown. This is stated in both the standalone and consolidated results notes for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • Standalone revenue from operations declined to Rs. 853.64 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from Rs. 902.08 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26, though it rose sequentially from Rs. 822.53 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26.
  • Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 977.94 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with Rs. 965.35 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and Rs. 1,123.54 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26.
  • The consolidated Card Clothing segment reported a segment loss before tax and interest of Rs. 312.25 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with a loss of Rs. 295.72 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and a loss of Rs. 130.52 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26.
  • The New Labour Codes, effective November 21, 2025, resulted in an incremental impact of Rs. 23.19 lakhs in the quarter ended December 31, 2025, Rs. 88.10 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and Rs. 111.29 lakhs for the year, disclosed as an exceptional item. The impact primarily arises from a change in the definition of wages and recognition of past service costs. The government is still in the process of notifying related rules, and any further impact will be evaluated and accounted for in the period in which they are notified.
  • Mr. Sanjeevkumar Karkamkar resigned as Executive Director & Chief Financial Officer effective September 1, 2026, citing advancing age and difficulty in contributing effectively. He will continue as a Non-Independent Non-Executive Director. The Board approved this on August 14, 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The Board approved a further investment in the equity share capital of Garnett Wire Ltd., UK, a wholly-owned subsidiary since 1997, engaged in manufacturing metallic card clothing and providing carding solutions to the global textile industry. The object is to expand business for the Company’s products abroad, given Garnett Wire’s presence in the UK and neighboring region.
  • The proposed investment is up to £250,000 for additional Ordinary Shares of £1 each, in cash, at a cost mutually agreed between the parties or as determined by an Independent Valuer/Chartered Accountant. Completion is expected within approximately 60 working days, subject to customary closing conditions. No prior RBI approval is required as it falls under the automatic route for Overseas Direct Investment.
  • Garnett Wire Ltd.’s turnover has grown over the last three years: £738,701 in FY2023-24, £781,435 in FY2024-25, and £826,760 in FY2025-26, with a net profit of £27,497 in FY2025-26.
  • The Company completed the sale of its commercial property at Amar Business Zone in July 2026 for a total consideration of Rs. 1,320 lakhs, following Board approval on February 11, 2026.
  • The Realty segment reported a segment profit before tax and interest of Rs. 911.01 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with Rs. 614.43 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26.
  • Standalone other income rose to Rs. 1,005.11 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from Rs. 715.19 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and Rs. 233.80 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26.

Key Risks

  • Continued weakness in the Indian Textile Industry could prolong pressure on the Carding business, which remains loss-making at the segment level.
  • The full impact of the New Labour Codes remains uncertain pending notification of related rules, and further costs may be recognized in future periods.
  • The Garnett Wire investment is a related party transaction because Mr. Mehul K. Trivedi, Deputy Chairman of the Company, is also a Director on the Board of Garnett Wire Ltd., UK. The valuation is proposed to be carried out by an Independent Valuer/Chartered Accountant to ensure the transaction is at arm’s length.
  • The investment in Garnett Wire is subject to customary closing conditions and may be completed within approximately 60 working days or such other extended timeline as mutually agreed.
  • The resignation of the Executive Director & CFO creates a leadership transition, with Mr. Karkamkar continuing as a Non-Independent Non-Executive Director.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management expects recovery of the Textile industry by the end of FY2026-27. In the observed quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Carding business continued to suffer headwinds, with the consolidated Card Clothing segment reporting a loss before tax and interest of Rs. 312.25 lakhs, wider than the Rs. 295.72 lakhs loss in Q1 FY2025-26.
  • The Company’s standalone net profit for Q1 FY2026-27 was Rs. 490.59 lakhs, compared with Rs. 292.54 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and a loss of Rs. 361.66 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26. Consolidated net profit attributable to equity holders was Rs. 456.42 lakhs, compared with Rs. 229.44 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26 and a loss of Rs. 338.15 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26.
  • Standalone earnings per share was Rs. 8.26 in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with Rs. 4.92 in Q1 FY2025-26 and a loss of Rs. 6.09 in Q4 FY2025-26. Consolidated earnings per share was Rs. 7.68, compared with Rs. 3.86 in Q1 FY2025-26 and a loss of Rs. 5.69 in Q4 FY2025-26.
  • The 72nd Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 24, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. IST via video conferencing or other audio visual means.
