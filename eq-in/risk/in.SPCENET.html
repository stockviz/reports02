SPCENET — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Standalone versus consolidated divergence. Standalone revenue and profitability declined in FY 2025-26, while consolidated revenue and profitability increased on the back of growth in the service-income segment. This indicates that the standalone trading entity is not the driver of group performance.
  • Trading segment weakness in the June 2026 quarter. On a consolidated basis, the Trading of Goods segment recorded a segment result of negative ₹136.41 lakh for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with positive ₹235.89 lakh in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and positive ₹25.50 lakh in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Net revenue from operations for the quarter (₹4,887.78 lakh) was also lower than the year-ago quarter (₹5,338.96 lakh).
  • Rising finance costs. Consolidated finance costs rose to ₹98.85 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹38.67 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹2.40 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone finance costs show the same pattern (₹98.85 lakh versus ₹38.67 lakh and ₹2.40 lakh).
  • Commodity and market volatility, counterparty credit and settlement risk, liquidity and financing risk, foreign-exchange risk, regulatory and statutory compliance risk, technology and cybersecurity risk, investment and execution risk, supply-chain and geopolitical risk, and human-capital risk are identified as material headwinds and challenges.
  • Governance transition items. The Chairperson position had fallen vacant and was filled on 14 August 2026. Mr. Prathipati Parthasarathi’s first term as Independent Director ended on 29 April 2026, with no continuity of office proposed between that expiry and the effective date of any fresh appointment, which itself requires Member approval by Special Resolution.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Service-income growth. Consolidated service income rose to ₹3,302.20 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹2,944.65 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹2,693.30 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. For FY 2025-26, service income was ₹10,142.14 lakh against trading of goods at ₹9,329.01 lakh.
  • Other income uplift. Consolidated other income was ₹1,985.34 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, compared with ₹46.58 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹9.54 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone other income was ₹1,662.47 lakh for the June 2026 quarter.
  • Growth in domestic consumption and infrastructure, expansion of service-oriented business income, ongoing digitalisation of trading and business processes, improved working-capital management, and evaluation of new business opportunities through newly established LLPs and expanded object clauses are identified as material tailwinds and growth prospects.
  • Fundraise capacity. The Board approved an enabling proposal to raise up to ₹500 crore in one or more tranches through permissible modes, subject to Member and regulatory approvals, with instrument, route, timing, pricing and commercial terms to be finalised separately.
  • Board strengthening. Mr. Srikanth Tatipaka was appointed as an Additional Director (Non-Executive) effective 14 August 2026, bringing experience in operations management, business development, sales strategy and executive leadership across hospitality and EdTech. Mr. Prathipati Parthasarathi was recommended for a fresh second term as Non-Executive Independent Director for five consecutive years, subject to Member approval by Special Resolution.
  • Audit Committee reconstitution. Effective 12 August 2026, the Audit Committee was reconstituted with Mr. Deenadayal Tripurasetty as Chairperson and Ms. Anima Rajmohan Nair and Mr. Sarat Kumar Mallik as members, all Independent Directors, to strengthen corporate governance and ensure continued compliance.

3. Key Risks

  • Commodity-price risk and market volatility.
  • Counterparty credit and settlement risk.
  • Liquidity and financing risk, reflected in the sharp quarter-on-quarter rise in finance costs.
  • Foreign-exchange risk, relevant given the group’s Hong Kong and Dubai subsidiaries and the exchange differences on translation of foreign operations recognised in other comprehensive income (negative ₹6.45 lakh in the June 2026 quarter versus positive ₹325.69 lakh in the March 2026 quarter).
  • Regulatory and statutory compliance risk.
  • Technology and cybersecurity risk.
  • Investment and execution risk, including the stated intent to pursue expansion only after satisfactory due diligence and requisite approvals.
  • Supply-chain and geopolitical risk.
  • Human-capital risk, including attracting and retaining skilled personnel.
  • Segment concentration and margin risk, given the negative trading segment result in the June 2026 quarter and the reliance on service income and other income for consolidated profitability.
  • Dependence on subsidiary performance. The consolidated statement includes six subsidiaries whose unaudited interim results were furnished by management; the auditor’s conclusion on those amounts is based solely on management reports and review procedures. Those subsidiaries contributed total revenue of ₹3,131.20 lakh, total net profit of ₹134.83 lakh and total comprehensive profit of ₹128.38 lakh for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
  • Related-party transaction exposure, with material related-party transactions placed before Members at the 16th AGM.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: For FY 2026-27, management intends to maintain focus on established trading operations while strengthening the performance and governance of its service-led businesses. Principal priorities include improving trading margins and transaction quality, strengthening customer and supplier relationships, managing receivables, liquidity and debt prudently, improving returns on investments, and pursuing expansion only after satisfactory due diligence and requisite approvals.
  • Observed performance: The June 2026 quarter shows the trading segment result turning negative at ₹136.41 lakh on a consolidated basis, while service income grew sequentially and year-on-year. Consolidated net revenue from operations declined year-on-year (₹4,887.78 lakh versus ₹5,338.96 lakh) but rose sequentially (versus ₹4,526.24 lakh). Consolidated profit before tax rose sharply to ₹1,636.62 lakh from ₹464.45 lakh sequentially and ₹391.02 lakh year-on-year, supported by other income of ₹1,985.34 lakh. Consolidated profit for the period was ₹1,376.19 lakh, with ₹1,278.12 lakh attributable to equity holders of the parent and ₹98.07 lakh to non-controlling interest. Basic and diluted EPS were ₹0.23 for the quarter, against ₹0.09 in the March 2026 quarter and ₹0.07 in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Standalone performance: Standalone revenue from operations was ₹1,756.58 lakh for the June 2026 quarter, against ₹1,443.63 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹2,693.33 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone profit before tax was ₹1,457.36 lakh, supported by other income of ₹1,662.47 lakh. Standalone profit for the period was ₹1,241.35 lakh, with basic and diluted EPS of ₹0.22.
  • Governance actions taken: The Board approved the Annual Report for FY 2025-26, appointed a Scrutinizer for e-voting, and convened the 16th AGM for 29 September 2026 at 2:00 P.M. IST through video conferencing or other audio-visual means. The statutory auditors issued an unmodified opinion. The company did not meet Section 135 criteria for CSR applicability in FY 2025-26.
  • Employee stock activity: The Nomination and Remuneration Committee approved the re-grant of 6,02,500 Employee Stock Options at an exercise price of Re. 1/- per option and the allotment of 6,04,500 fully paid-up Equity Shares of face value Re. 1/- each pursuant to the exercise of vested options under the Spacenet Employee Stock Option Scheme 2021.
  • Reporting correction: A mismatch in the Standalone EPS reported in the XBRL filing occurred due to an inadvertent typographical/data entry error, which was purely clerical in nature and did not change the financial results approved by the Board of Directors and submitted in PDF format.
