Wockhardt Limited — Summary of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Regulatory and customs penalty. The company received an Adjudication Order from the Commissioner of Customs imposing a penalty of ₹5,00,00,000 and a redemption fine of ₹10,00,00,000 in lieu of confiscation of mis-declared goods (API), along with an additional ₹1,00,00,000 penalty levied on an officer of the company. The order relates to mis-classification of an Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient. The company is challenging the order through an appeal and states that it has no material impact on its financials or operations aside from the monetary penalties.

Antimicrobial resistance burden. The company’s press release notes that around 10 lakh patients annually die in India due to multi-drug-resistant infections — more than four times the number of Indian lives lost annually during the COVID-19 pandemic. Despite more than 300 antibiotics introduced since penicillin, AMR has continued to emerge and accelerate, posing one of the greatest threats to global public healthcare.

Shareholder dissent on certain resolutions. Voting results from the 27th AGM show notable opposition on specific items: - Resolution 9 (appointment of Ms. Zahabiya Khorakiwala as Executive Co-chairperson of Wockhardt Bio AG and her remuneration of USD 250,000 per annum): 36.9133% of votes polled were against, with public institutions voting 44.5828% against. - Resolution 2 (re-appointment of Ms. Zahabiya Khorakiwala as Director retiring by rotation): 8.7043% of votes polled were against, with public institutions voting 40.8395% against. - Resolution 4 (revision in remuneration payable to Dr. Habil Khorakiwala, Executive Chairman): 8.9750% of votes polled were against, with public institutions voting 42.1108% against. - Resolution 8 (raising additional capital up to ₹3,000 Crore including via QIP): 1.0467% of votes polled were against, with public institutions voting 4.9046% against.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Wockhardt Antibiotic Academy. The company launched the Wockhardt Antibiotic Academy, described as an open scientific learning platform accessible to the registered medical fraternity across India. It aims to facilitate continuous medical education through exchange of knowledge, scientific discussions, and sharing of real-world clinical experience among healthcare professionals. The initiative is positioned as the first step in building a nationwide scientific movement, with the company hoping to collaborate with clinicians across the country to strengthen the fight against antimicrobial resistance.

New antibiotic pipeline. The press release references Zaynich as a new antibiotic and notes an urgent need for both new antibiotics and a science-based platform for constructive dialogue on AMR. Dr. Habil Khorakiwala stated that it is essential to use the right antibiotic for the right patient at the right time.

QIDP status and global footprint. Wockhardt is described as a research-based global pharmaceutical and biotech company whose new drug programme has focused on unmet need for anti-bacterial drugs effective against untreatable superbugs. The company states it is the only company in the world where USFDA has given QIDP Status (Qualified Infectious Disease Product) for 6 of its anti-bacterial discovery programmes — 3 Gram Negative and 3 Gram Positive effective against untreatable superbugs. It employs around 3,200 people across 27 nationalities with presence in UK, Ireland, Switzerland, France, Mexico, Russia and many other countries, with manufacturing and research facilities in India and UK and a manufacturing facility in Ireland. 78% of global revenues come from international businesses.

Capital raising approval. Shareholders approved a special resolution for raising additional capital not exceeding ₹3,000 Crore by way of one or more public or private offerings including through a Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP) to eligible investors through issuance of equity shares or other eligible securities in one or more tranches. A separate special resolution for modification in the objects of the QIP was also approved.

Related party transaction approvals. Shareholders approved material related party transactions with Wockhardt Bio AG (up to ₹1,000 Crore), among Wockhardt Bio AG and CP Pharmaceuticals Limited (up to ₹550 Crore), and among Wockhardt Bio AG and Wockhardt UK Limited (up to ₹550 Crore).

3. Key Risks

Customs penalty and appeal outcome. The Adjudication Order from the Commissioner of Customs imposes monetary penalties totaling ₹16,00,00,000 (₹5,00,00,000 penalty + ₹10,00,00,000 redemption fine + ₹1,00,00,000 on an officer). The company is challenging the order by way of appeal before the appropriate authority. The outcome of the appeal is uncertain.

Concentration of promoter voting power. Promoter and Promoter Group held 79,758,848 shares out of 162,492,169 total shares (approximately 49.1% of outstanding shares). On resolutions 5, 6, 7, and 9, promoter votes were not polled (0 votes), meaning outcomes depended entirely on public shareholders. On resolution 9, only 63.0867% of votes polled were in favour, indicating significant reliance on non-institutional public shareholders for approval.

Dependence on public institutional shareholders. Public institutions held 27,054,080 shares and voted 84.5980% of their holdings on most resolutions. Their dissent on resolutions 2, 4, and 9 (40.8395%, 42.1108%, and 44.5828% against respectively) indicates potential governance or remuneration concerns among institutional investors.

AMR as a systemic challenge. The press release frames antimicrobial resistance as an accelerating threat, noting that resistance is not solved by guidelines alone but at the patient’s bedside. The company acknowledges that the science belongs to the community that advances it and to clinicians who translate it into better outcomes.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Customs matter. Management stated it is confident of the merits of its case and is taking necessary steps to challenge the order by way of an appeal before the appropriate authority. Management also stated that the order has no material impact on its financials or operations, aside from the monetary penalties. No financial figures beyond the penalties were provided.

Financial results. The Board of Directors approved the Un-Audited Financial Results (Standalone & Consolidated) for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, which were reviewed by MSKC & Associates, LLP, Statutory Auditors. No specific financial figures or guidance were disclosed in the supplied material.

AGM proceedings. The Chairman briefed members about strategic focus and way forward for the company. The Chairman noted there was no qualification, observation, comment or emphasis of matter in the Independent Auditor’s Reports or the Secretarial Auditors Report. All 10 resolutions were passed. The AGM was attended by 187 members as per the records of Attendance Register, with 14 promoters/promoter group and 173 public shareholders attending through video conferencing.

Antibiotic Academy. Management described the Academy as the first step in building a nationwide scientific movement, with the true measure of success being the number of patients whose lives are saved through better-informed clinical decisions. Ms. Annapurna Das, President, India & NCE, Emerging Markets, stated that every breakthrough antibiotic is only the beginning and its true value emerges through robust evidence and clinician judgement.
