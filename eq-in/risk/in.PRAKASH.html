Prakash Industries: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Tax regime change: The company has opted for the new tax regime under the Income Tax Act, 2025 with effect from 1st April, 2026. As a result, the tax exemption under section 80-IA is no longer available. This has led to higher tax expenses and lower profit after tax compared with the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Coal mining capacity expansion: The Bhaskarpara Coal Mine production capacity has been increased from 1.0 MTPA to 1.2 MTPA. This follows Environmental Clearance from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change on 12th June 2026 and a Consent to Operate (CTO) granted by the Chhattisgarh Environment Conservation Board (CECB) on 10th September 2026.
  • Coal extraction target: The company extracted approximately 3.3 lac MT of coal from the Bhaskarpara Coal Mine and is targeting coal extraction of 1.2 Mn tonnes in the current financial year, in line with the increased Environmental Clearance capacity of 1.2 MTPA.
  • Improved quarterly profitability metrics: EBITDA for the quarter increased to 154 Crores, with a higher EBITDA margin of 14.9%.

3. Key Risks

  • Loss of tax exemption: The unavailability of the section 80-IA exemption under the new tax regime represents a material risk to profitability, as reflected in higher tax expenses and lower profit after tax relative to the prior-year comparable quarter.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management is targeting coal extraction of 1.2 Mn tonnes in the current financial year, consistent with the increased Environmental Clearance capacity of 1.2 MTPA.
  • Observed performance: For the quarter ended 30th June 2026, the company reported Net Sales of 1032 Crores, EBITDA of 154 Crores, and Profit after Tax of 71 Crores (7,147 lakhs). Coal extraction of approximately 3.3 lac MT was achieved from the Bhaskarpara Coal Mine.

Additional Investor-Relevant Facts

  • Dividend and AGM: The 45th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, 30th September, 2026, at 12.30 p.m. via Video Conferencing / OAVM. The Record Date for the final dividend of Rs. 1.80 per equity share (18%) for the financial year 2025-26 is Wednesday, 16th September, 2026. The cut-off date for remote e-voting is Wednesday, 23rd September, 2026.
  • Governance changes: M/s. SGAJ & Associates were appointed as new Statutory Auditors for a 5-year term, and ten officials were included in the category of Senior Management Personnel.
