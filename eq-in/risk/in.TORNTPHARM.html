Torrent Pharmaceuticals — Recent Corporate Announcements

Headwinds and Challenges

Germany: Growth continued to be impacted by supply disruption at a third-party supplier and lower tender offtake during the quarter. Germany revenues were Rs. 318 crores, up 3%, while constant currency revenues at EUR 29 million were down 9%.

Brazil: The Company undertook a one-time channel inventory reduction in response to channel requests for extended credit periods amid rising interest costs. Brazil constant currency revenues at R$ 147 million were up only 3%, even as reported revenues rose 27%.

Others segment: The “Others” category declined 24% year-on-year, with revenues of Rs. 112 crores.

JB Pharma integration costs: Exceptional items included regulatory and statutory fees and other related costs associated with JB Pharma’s acquisition and merger — Rs. 2 crores for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and Rs. 47 crores and Rs. 70 crores for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 respectively. Severance compensation of Rs. 19 crores was incurred for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 on account of restructuring of JB Pharma’s distribution network.

Fire incident: An inventory write-off of Rs. 19 crores for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 followed a fire incident at one of the erstwhile JB Pharma warehouses. No effect has been given to the related insurance claim, which is under assessment.

Pending stamp duty: The financial results do not include the effect of stamp duty payable in respect of the Scheme of Amalgamation, pending adjudication by the appropriate authority.

Leverage and coverage: Paid-up debt capital stood at Rs. 11,390 crores as at June 30, 2026, versus Rs. 643 crores as at June 30, 2025. On a consolidated basis, the debt-equity ratio was 0.82 times (versus 0.33 times a year earlier), interest service coverage ratio was 4.60 times (versus 14.20 times), and net profit margin was 11.5% (versus 17.2%). The listed non-convertible debentures aggregating Rs. 10,990 crores are secured by first ranking exclusive charge over designated account assets and specified trademarks, with security cover exceeding 110% of principal and accrued interest.

Margin dilution: Consolidated PAT rose only 3% year-on-year to Rs. 566 crores despite revenue growth of 55%, with PAT margin at 11.5% versus 17.2%.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

India: India revenues at Rs. 2,157 crores were up 19% versus IPM growth of 12% (AIOCD PharmaTrac). Torrent is now ranked 1st in the IPM cardiac market. Gx Semaglutide recorded a Q1 FY27 market share of 36% (oral and injectable combined).

United States: US revenues at Rs. 418 crores were up 36%; constant currency revenues at US$ 44 million were up 23%, driven by new launches achieving targeted market share and certain one-time opportunities.

Brazil pipeline: As per IQVIA, Torrent grew 21% versus market growth of 5%, aided by top brands and recent launches. Torrent has 58 products under ANVISA review.

JB Business: Revenues at Rs. 1,201 crores were up 10%; gross profit at Rs. 859 crores (71.5% margin) up 15%; Op. EBITDA at Rs. 424 crores (35.3% margin) up 34%. India Rx business (ex trade generics and contrast media) at Rs. 657 crores grew 13%; international business at Rs. 468 crores grew 12%; CDMO business at Rs. 145 crores grew 27%.

Base business: Revenues at Rs. 3,720 crores were up 17%; Op. EBITDA at Rs. 1,240 crores grew 20%, with margins of 33.3%.

Overall scale: Consolidated revenue at Rs. 4,921 crores was up 55% year-on-year; Op. EBITDA before exceptional items at Rs. 1,664 crores was up 61%, with margin at 33.8% and gross margin at 76.4%. R&D spend was Rs. 172 crores, up 10%.

ESG recognition: NSE Sustainability Ratings and Analytics Limited independently assigned an ESG Rating of 70 to the Company (intimated August 11, 2026).

Portfolio positioning: The Company describes itself as specialty-focused with ~75% of India revenues from chronic and sub-chronic therapies, present in 50+ countries, ranked No. 1 amongst Indian pharma companies in Brazil and Germany, with 16 manufacturing facilities of which 8 are USFDA approved, and approximately 900+ scientists in R&D.

  • Exposure to tender-driven demand volatility in Germany.
  • Channel financing conditions and interest cost pressures affecting trade channels, as seen in Brazil.
  • Integration and restructuring risk associated with the JB Pharma acquisition and amalgamation, including distribution network restructuring and severance costs.
  • Uninsured or under-assessment losses, as with the warehouse fire where the insurance claim remains under assessment.
  • Contingent cost from stamp duty on the Scheme of Amalgamation, pending adjudication.
  • Elevated leverage following the JB Pharma transaction, with debt-equity at 0.82 times and interest service coverage at 4.60 times on a consolidated basis.
  • Security cover on listed non-convertible debentures of Rs. 10,990 crores tied to designated account assets and specified trademarks.

Broker Narrative

The rating moved from a cautious Neutral to a Buy as the investment case shifted from organic US/domestic/LatAm growth to the JBCP acquisition and its synergies. Gross margin expansion and domestic formulation growth remained persistent positives, but Germany reversed from a tender-win growth opportunity into tender-competition and supply-disruption risk. The early US profitability/approval worry also stayed unresolved until FY27 guidance, while new concerns centred on JBCP integration, debt, and temporary supply shocks.

Fears that came true

  • The US base-business pricing/approval headwind persisted: US profitability was still only guided for FY27, and the 2026-07-31 reports’ actual return was -3.0%, with Prabhudas’ Buy marked DISAPPOINTMENT.
  • The early higher-cost concern was echoed in the last report’s JBCP sales-force integration, higher depreciation/interest, and forex losses, which were flagged as temporary financial drags around the same negative-return window.

Optimism that failed

  • The early expectation that German tender wins would improve growth failed: the last report cited aggressive tender competition pressuring market share and a third-party supplier disruption affecting 10-15% of Germany revenues.
