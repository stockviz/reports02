Headwinds and Challenges

Operational transition to BSNL. Under a Service Level Agreement (SLA) dated 22.11.2024, which superseded an earlier agreement of 18.08.2021, the entire telecom operations of MTNL in Delhi and Mumbai are being run by BSNL with effect from 01.01.2025. BSNL is responsible for CAPEX and OPEX and is to ensure EBITDA-neutral operations. Certain customers in Delhi and Mumbai have been migrated to BSNL from 01.01.2025, with billing and collection handled by BSNL. MTNL recognised revenue of Rs. 34.39 crore during the quarter on an agreed revenue-sharing basis, based on information provided by BSNL.

Losses and negative net worth. For the quarter ended 30.06.2026, MTNL reported a standalone loss after tax of Rs. 841.07 crore (against Rs. 941.03 crore in the quarter ended 30.06.2025 and Rs. 3,102.94 crore for the year ended 31.03.2026). Net worth was negative at Rs. 30,801.34 crore as at 30.06.2026. The company has been classified as an “Incipient Sick CPSE” by the Department of Public Enterprises.

Debt default and NPA status. Due to default in repayment of bank instalments/interest amounting to Rs. 3,119.49 crore, all loan accounts from banks became non-performing assets as on 30.06.2026. The company has initiated discussions with lender banks for possible resolution and settlement. The auditor’s emphasis of matter notes defaults in repayment of term loan instalments of Rs. 2,196 crore and interest on term loan of Rs. 1,701 crore, with interest on bonds funded by the Government of India.

Weak coverage and liquidity ratios. Debt Service Coverage Ratio and Interest Service Coverage Ratio stood at 0.06 times for the quarter. The current ratio was 0.36 times (standalone). Operating margin was negative 84.01% and net profit margin negative 420.36% for the quarter.

Audit qualifications and unresolved reconciliations. The statutory auditors issued a qualified conclusion, citing matters including: revenue recognition based on BSNL-provided data that could not be independently verified; net recoverable from BSNL of Rs. 3,961.05 crore subject to reconciliation and confirmation; net payable to DoT of Rs. 428.28 crore subject to reconciliation; unlinked credits of Rs. 40.45 crore; deferred capitalisation of property, plant and equipment; a contingent liability of Rs. 455.15 crore on 2G spectrum one-time charges; GST not paid on electricity charges recovered from tenants; ECL provisioning practices not fully compliant with Ind AS 109; non-compliance with Ind AS 116 on leases; manual/average billing in certain Delhi and Mumbai units due to software downtime; no impairment assessment on the 3G spectrum licence carrying value of Rs. 690.27 crore; unreconciled bank accounts; penal guarantee fees of Rs. 352.30 crore disclosed as a contingent liability rather than provided for; non-recognition of Rs. 11.67 crore received from the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports; and non-recognition of rental income on a Mumbai property let to BSNL (Rs. 7.48 crore for the quarter, Rs. 67.35 crore cumulative).

Trust deficits. Deficits in MTNL trusts as on 30.06.2026: Gratuity Trust Rs. 76.81 crore (since 2016), Leave Encashment Rs. 14.45 crore (since 2024), and GPF Trust Rs. 123.00 crore (since 2019).

Regulatory penalties. MTNL received orders dated 14.09.2026 (received 15.09.2026) from TRAI imposing financial disincentives of Rs. 11,00,000 for contravention of regulations 9 and 10, and Rs. 5,70,000 for contravention of regulation 13 of the Standards of Quality of Service of Access and Broadband Service Regulations, 2024. Management stated there is no material impact on the financial, operational, or other activities of the company.

Pending arbitration. In the Canara Bank/Canfina bond settlement matter, the arbitrator published an award on 03.03.2022 against MTNL for Rs. 160 crore with simple interest at 6% p.a. from 21.10.1993, plus Rs. 0.93 crore in costs. MTNL filed a petition before the Delhi High Court to set aside the award and submitted a title deed as security per an order dated 10.05.2024. The next hearing is proposed for 05.11.2026.

Tax disputes. MTNL claimed benefits under section 80IA of the Income Tax Act for FY 1996-97 to 2005-06; appellate authorities allowed 75% of the claim. MTNL has retained a provision of Rs. 375.96 crore for assessment years 1998-99, 1999-00 and 2000-2001, and shown Rs. 243.22 crore as a contingent reserve for assessment years 2001-02 to 2006-07.

Governance. The auditor noted that the company does not have the requisite number of independent directors as required under Section 149 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Government support. The going-concern assessment cites continued Government of India support: funding of the ex-gratia cost of the Voluntary Retirement Scheme; approval of sovereign guarantee backed bonds of Rs. 17,571 crore (fully raised to date); soft loans aggregating Rs. 3,657.14 crore towards servicing of interest thereon; and sanctioned budgetary support of Rs. 1,851 crore for network upgradation.

EBITDA-neutral BSNL arrangement. The Revenue Sharing Policy approved by the Board provides for telecom operations at Delhi and Mumbai to be run by BSNL under the service agreement dated 22 November 2024 on an EBITDA-neutral basis.

Asset monetisation. Ongoing monetisation of land and buildings with the assistance of DIPAM yielded Rs. 418.76 crore during FY 2025-26, with balance properties under process.

Pending government decisions. The recommendations of the Committee of Secretaries examining asset monetisation, AGR dues, debt restructuring and the proposed merger with BSNL are awaited, and further support towards working capital is under consideration of the Government; no amount in respect thereof has been recognised.

Key Risks

  • Going concern. The auditor’s emphasis of matter states that negative net worth, continuing cash losses, current liabilities substantially exceeding current assets, Incipient Sick CPSE classification, loan defaults and government-funded bond interest cast significant doubt over the company’s ability to continue as a going concern. The results have nevertheless been prepared on a going concern basis, citing the majority Government of India stake.
  • Unreconciled balances. Net recoverable from BSNL of Rs. 3,961.05 crore and net payable to DoT of Rs. 428.28 crore are subject to reconciliation and confirmation, with pending disputes over claims and counter claims.
  • Unquantified audit qualifications. The auditors state they are unable to quantify the impact of numerous qualification items on the financial results.
  • Contingent liabilities. These include the 2G spectrum demand (Rs. 455.15 crore disclosed as contingent liability), penal guarantee fees (Rs. 352.30 crore), and income tax demands (Rs. 243.22 crore contingent reserve).
  • Regulatory risk. TRAI imposed financial disincentives in September 2026 for quality-of-service regulation contraventions.
  • Billing and accounting system weaknesses. Manual/average billing in certain units, unreconciled bank accounts, and non-compliance with Ind AS 116 and Ind AS 109 requirements.
  • Litigation risk. The Canara Bank/Canfina arbitration award of Rs. 160 crore plus interest and costs remains under challenge, with the next hearing on 05.11.2026.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance/forward-looking elements:

  • Management assessed that the going concern basis remains appropriate, citing government support, the EBITDA-neutral BSNL arrangement, and ongoing asset monetisation. No adjustments have been made to carrying amounts or classifications of assets and liabilities that might be necessary if the company could not continue as a going concern.
  • The recommendations of the Committee of Secretaries on asset monetisation, AGR dues, debt restructuring and the proposed BSNL merger are awaited; further working capital support is under consideration of the Government, with no amount recognised.
  • Management stated that the TRAI financial disincentives have no material impact on the financial, operational, or other activities of the company.
  • The 40th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 11:30 A.M. through video conferencing or other audio-visual means. The register of members and share transfer books will be closed from September 24 to September 30, 2026, and the cut-off date for voting and remote e-voting is September 23, 2026. Business items include adoption of financial statements, re-appointment of Dr. Kalyan Sagar Nippani, fixing statutory auditor remuneration, appointing Shri Shyamal Misra and Shri K. Balaji as Government Nominee Directors, and ratifying cost auditors’ remuneration for FY 2026-27.

Observed performance against these positions:

  • Despite the EBITDA-neutral objective of the BSNL arrangement, MTNL reported a standalone loss after tax of Rs. 841.07 crore for the quarter ended 30.06.2026, with negative operating margin of 84.01%.
  • Revenue from operations for the quarter was Rs. 200.08 crore (standalone), against Rs. 158.14 crore in the quarter ended 30.06.2025 and Rs. 887.27 crore for the year ended 31.03.2026.
  • The revenue share recognised from BSNL during the quarter was Rs. 34.39 crore, and the auditors noted the underlying data could not be independently verified.
  • Asset monetisation yielded Rs. 418.76 crore during FY 2025-26, while the balance properties remain under process.
  • The company remains in default on bank loans (Rs. 3,119.49 crore), and all bank loan accounts are NPAs as on 30.06.2026, indicating that the anticipated support and restructuring outcomes had not yet been reflected in the reported position.
