Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Smart metering expansion: AESL is ramping up its smart metering business and is on course to become India’s leading smart metering integrator. The company has identified a new “Smart Meter” segment effective Q2 FY2025-26, reflecting the growing scale of this business.
  • Acquisition of IntelliSmart Infrastructure: AESL executed a binding securities purchase and subscription agreement to acquire 100% equity stake in IntelliSmart Infrastructure Private Limited, a leading smart metering JV between the National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF) and Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL). The total consideration is ₹3,050 crore plus 4.7 crore meters, subject to customary and regulatory approvals. The acquisition is expected to strengthen AESL’s position as India’s largest smart metering platform.
  • Power supply portfolio expansion: Powerpulse Trading Solutions Limited (PTSL), a wholly-owned subsidiary of AESL, entered into an agreement with Adani Power Limited on 8 September 2026, under which APL assigned all rights and obligations of a Letter of Award from MSEDCL to PTSL for supplying 2,500 MW Round-the-Clock power over 25 years. PTSL subsequently executed a Power Purchase Agreement with MSEDCL on 8 September 2026. The transaction is expected to further strengthen PTSL’s power supply portfolio and reinforce its position in the energy solutions business.
  • Distribution network expansion: AESL is expanding its distribution network through parallel licenses and competitive retail solutions, and increasing its share of green power.
  • ESG leadership: AESL achieved the distinction of being the highest ESG-scored company globally in the Utilities sector as assessed by S&P Global, securing an ESG Score of 90 out of 100 compared to an industry average of 41, driven by scores of 93 in Environmental, 89 in Social, and 84 in Governance & Economic.
  • Capital raising approval: The company received shareholder approval via a special resolution at the Extra-ordinary General Meeting held on 25 July 2026 to raise capital from eligible investors through an issuance of equity shares and/or other eligible securities. The resolution was passed with 99.9995% of votes in favour.
  • Segment performance: For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the Transmission segment reported revenue of ₹3,335.26 crore and segment results of ₹1,327.46 crore. The Distribution segment reported revenue of ₹3,520.43 crore and segment results of ₹356.92 crore. The Energy Solutions Platform segment reported revenue of ₹1,906.83 crore and segment results of ₹590.27 crore.
