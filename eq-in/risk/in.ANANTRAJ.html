Anant Raj Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • The company’s own disclosures state that forward-looking statements and business prospects are subject to known and unknown risks, uncertainties, and assumptions that are difficult to predict. These include the performance of the Indian and international economies, industry performance, competition, the company’s ability to successfully implement strategy, future levels of growth and expansion, technological changes and advancements, changes in revenue, income or cashflows, market preferences, and exposure to market risks.
  • The proposed composite scheme of arrangement is subject to receipt of all necessary statutory, regulatory and judicial approvals, including approvals from the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), SEBI, the stock exchanges, shareholders, creditors and other applicable authorities. Completion of the demerger and the independent listing of Ashok Cloud Private Limited therefore depends on these approvals and exemptions.
  • The data centre and cloud business being carved out is at an early stage of monetisation. Ashok Cloud Private Limited’s turnover was Nil for FY 2023-24, FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26, and its net worth as on March 31, 2026 was Rs. 4.39 lakh with paid-up capital of Rs. 5,00,000.
  • The restructuring involves related party transactions: the subscription to ACPL shares pursuant to the rights issue constitutes a related party transaction, though it is exempt under Regulation 23(5) of the SEBI Listing Regulations.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strategic demerger: On July 21, 2026, the Board approved a Composite Scheme of Arrangement to create two focused listed companies — Anant Raj Limited (real estate and infrastructure) and Ashok Cloud Private Limited (data centre and cloud services, including AI workloads). The scheme is intended to enable focused growth strategies, improve operational efficiency, unlock shareholder value, provide direct shareholder participation in the digital infrastructure business, simplify the corporate structure, and give the cloud business flexibility to attract sector-focused investors, partnerships, acquisitions and growth capital.
  • Data centre capacity expansion: The company targets scaling Data Center IT load capacity to 117 MW to commence by FY28 and reaching 357 MW by FY32. It plans to operationalise an incremental 35 MW IT load at Manesar and Rai in the current financial year, along with cloud services.
  • Cloud and AI services: Ashok Cloud is positioned to provide advanced data centres, co-location, sovereign public cloud, AI-ready cloud infrastructure, and DC & DR services including cloud migration and data backup.
  • International expansion: Anant Raj Cloud Singapore Pte. Ltd. was incorporated on June 15, 2026 to provide co-location and cloud services, including AI services, to overseas customers.
  • Real estate pipeline: Development of the ~220-acre flagship Anant Raj Estate and residential projects in Sector 63A Gurugram (including The Estate One offering 0.90 million sq. ft. of saleable area), unlocking high-value Delhi land reserves (83.43 acres of identified marquee land and 100 acres of fully paid freehold land), and expansion of commercial annuity properties including Anant Raj Center 1, Anant Raj Center 2 and Ashok Tower. Management targets incremental annual rental of Rs. 210 Cr p.a. from these commercial expansions, and plans to launch The Estate Apartments 2 and Luxury Group Housing phases.
  • Funding capacity: Unutilised QIP proceeds of Rs. 689.99 crores as at June 30, 2026 are available for deployment. The company also increased its shareholding in Romano Projects Private Limited to 100%, making it a wholly owned subsidiary.
  • Balance sheet: Net debt-free status as of March 31, 2026, and full discharge of the outstanding NCD liability of Rs. 6.50 crores through financing arrangements with the State Bank of India.

3. Key Risks

  • Dependence on NCLT, SEBI, stock exchange, shareholder, creditor and other approvals for the composite scheme; the demerger and listing of Ashok Cloud may not complete as proposed.
  • Execution risk in the data centre build-out, given the scale of the targeted ramp from 28 MW operational capacity to 357 MW by FY2032 and the early-stage revenue profile of the carved-out business.
  • General economic, industry, competitive, technological, market-preference and market-risk exposures flagged in the company’s own forward-looking statement caveats.
  • Related party transaction exposure in the funding of ACPL, notwithstanding the exemption relied upon.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Data Center IT load capacity to reach 117 MW to commence by FY28 and 357 MW by FY32; incremental 35 MW IT load at Manesar and Rai to be operationalised in the current financial year along with cloud services; launches of The Estate Apartments 2 and Luxury Group Housing phases; incremental annual rental of Rs. 210 Cr p.a. from Anant Raj Center 1 & 2 and Ashok Tower expansions; listing of Ashok Cloud on NSE and BSE subject to approvals.
  • Observed performance: As of the disclosures, 28 MW of data center capacity is operational, against the 357 MW FY2032 target. Q1 FY27 consolidated total income was Rs. 650.75 crores with net profit of Rs. 149.19 crores; standalone total income was Rs. 414.68 crores with profit after tax of Rs. 79.10 crores. Revenue from operations for the quarter was Rs. 631.40 Cr, EBITDA Rs. 202.74 Cr and PAT Rs. 149.19 Cr. The company has a 5-decade real estate legacy with 22.34 msf of residential and commercial projects and ~320 acres of fully paid ready-to-develop NCR land bank. The NCD liability of Rs. 6.50 crores was fully discharged, and net debt-free status was reported as of March 31, 2026.
  • Capital actions supporting guidance: On July 20, 2026, the Finance and Investment Committee approved an additional investment in ACPL of Rs. 74,86,45,106 by acquiring 37,43,22,553 fully paid-up equity shares at Rs. 2 each via a right issue, raising ACPL’s paid-up equity share capital from Rs. 5,00,000 to Rs. 74,91,45,106, with Anant Raj’s shareholding remaining at 100%. The stated object was to meet funding requirements for development of ACPL’s data centres and cloud business.
  • Shareholder approvals: At the 41st AGM held on August 07, 2026, all nine resolutions — including adoption of audited financial statements for FY ended March 31, 2026, declaration of final dividend, reappointment of Sh. Aman Sarin, appointment of Sh. Anish Sarin as Director and Whole-time Director, and related items — were approved with requisite majority. Total votes polled were 249,223,159 (69.2523%) on the dividend resolution, with 99.9999% in favour.

Broker Narrative

The first report was a defensive Buy: it flagged delayed cloud/data-center capacity, internally funded DC capex, higher other costs, estimate cuts and rising leverage. By the last report the narrative had flipped to an unhedged Buy with no headwinds, recasting the data-center business as a scaled-up, high-margin growth engine supported by AI demand, cloud economics, the ACPL demerger and a strong real-estate pipeline.

Fears that came true

  • The flagged deferral of the 14MW cloud IT load, with only 6MW commissioning by 1HFY27 and 8MW kept vacant, was followed by a -4.0% actual return and a DISAPPOINTMENT outcome on the 2025-08-06 call.
