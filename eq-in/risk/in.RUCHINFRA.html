RUCHINFRA: Recent Corporate Developments and Performance Signals

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sequential profitability reversal at the standalone level. Standalone profit before tax fell from Rs. 788 lacs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 to Rs. 303 lacs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and from Rs. 703 lacs for the full year ended 31 March 2026. The immediately preceding quarter (ended 31 March 2026) had recorded a loss before tax of Rs. 312 lacs, so the June 2026 quarter represents a recovery on a sequential basis but a sharp decline year-on-year.

Windpower drag. The standalone windpower segment recorded a segment loss of Rs. 19 lacs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a loss of Rs. 123 lacs in the March 2026 quarter and a loss of Rs. 8 lacs in the June 2025 quarter. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, the windpower segment loss was Rs. 165 lacs. At the consolidated level, windpower segment results were positive at Rs. 313 lacs in June 2026, but this compares with a loss of Rs. 473 lacs in the March 2026 quarter.

Unallocable losses. Standalone unallocable segment results were a loss of Rs. 165 lacs in June 2026, Rs. 541 lacs in March 2026, Rs. 333 lacs in June 2025, and Rs. 635 lacs for the full year ended 31 March 2026. Consolidated unallocable results were a loss of Rs. 183 lacs in June 2026, Rs. 283 lacs in March 2026, and Rs. 453 lacs in June 2025.

Preference share liability restructuring. The preference and equity shareholders consented to revise the terms of 54,60,613 6% non-convertible, cumulative, redeemable preference shares (including redemption thereof) under Section 48 of the Companies Act, 2013 during the year ended 31 March 2026. Consequently, the current financial liability as at 31 March 2025 included redemption under the earlier terms, and the entire preference share amount is now included under current and non-current financial liabilities as at 31 March 2026.

Regulatory process dependency for the composite scheme. The draft Composite Scheme of Amalgamation approved by the Board on 28 May 2026 remains subject to receipt of requisite statutory and regulatory approvals. Applications seeking No Objection/Observation Letters were made to BSE and NSE on 18 June 2026 under Regulation 37 of SEBI LODR. The company received clarifications from the exchanges, prompting an Addendum dated 20 July 2026 to the Valuation Report dated 21 May 2026 and an Addendum dated 21 July 2026 to the Fairness Opinion dated 22 May 2026, along with amendments advised by the stock exchanges to the draft scheme.

Infrastructure segment remains the core earnings engine. Standalone infrastructure segment revenue was Rs. 1,009 lacs in June 2026, Rs. 990 lacs in March 2026, Rs. 995 lacs in June 2025, and Rs. 3,959 lacs for the full year ended 31 March 2026. Standalone infrastructure segment results (profit before tax and interest) were Rs. 458 lacs in June 2026, Rs. 342 lacs in March 2026, Rs. 475 lacs in June 2025, and Rs. 1,566 lacs for the full year. Consolidated total income rose to Rs. 1,844 lacs from Rs. 2,324 lacs in June 2025, though the prior-year quarter included higher other income of Rs. 671 lacs versus Rs. 78 lacs in June 2026.

Consolidated profitability improvement. Consolidated profit before tax was Rs. 545 lacs in June 2026, compared with a loss of Rs. 66 lacs in March 2026 and profit of Rs. 916 lacs in June 2025. Windpower revenue scale-up at consolidated level.
