Kridhan Infra Limited — Summary of Recent Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • SEBI show cause notice and adjudication proceedings. On August 12, 2026, SEBI issued a Show Cause Notice to the company, and adjudication proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, with the appointment of an Adjudicating Officer. The alleged violations relate to:
    • Non-recognition of interest on loans from banks in the preparation and presentation of standalone and consolidated financial statements and financial results for FY2023, resulting in alleged non-compliance with Ind AS 1, 23, 32 and 109.
    • Alleged failure to make disclosures in financial statements regarding departures from accounting standards, in violation of Ind AS 1.
    • Non-filling of the position of Managing Director from October 10, 2022 to February 12, 2024, and Chief Financial Officer from July 1, 2022 to February 12, 2024, for a period exceeding three months, allegedly resulting in non-compliance with Regulations 17(1E), 26A(1) and 26A(2) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
    • The company states that the SCN does not quantify any monetary penalty and that the financial implications, if any, will depend on the outcome of the adjudication proceedings; at present the impact cannot be ascertained.
  • Eroded net worth and accumulated losses. The audit qualification statements for FY2024-25 record negative net worth on both a consolidated basis (₹-28,317.35 lakhs) and a standalone basis (₹-28,146.29 lakhs), with accumulated losses and fully eroded net worth. The company attributes the erosion primarily to payment of borrowing costs and SOP fines.
  • Defaults in repayment of working capital borrowings. The auditors’ qualification notes defaults in repayment of working capital borrowings.
  • Going concern qualification. The auditors issued a qualified opinion in relation to the going concern assumption for FY2024-25, on both standalone and consolidated financial statements.
  • Unascertainable impact of the qualification. Management states the impact of the audit qualification is unascertainable, and that it has sought balance confirmation from the lender but had not received confirmation as on March 31, 2025. The auditor did not provide comments on management’s clarifications.
  • One Time Settlement still in process (consolidated). On a consolidated basis, the company had received in-principle approval for a One Time payment of certain amounts over a period of time, which was still under process; the corresponding liability would be derecognized post payments and final approval of the lenders.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Completed One Time Settlement (standalone). On a standalone basis, the company states it has successfully completed the One Time Settlement in respect of debt due to one of its lenders, with the required payments made over time and duly accounted for in the books.
  • Proposed diversification and expansion. The company plans proposed diversification and expansion of operations.
  • Preferential allotment to augment equity. Recent preferential allotment of equity shares and/or warrants is expected to augment the equity base and provide additional financial resources to support expansion of business activities. The standalone audit qualification statement notes these efforts to increase the equity base will support growth of business activities.
  • Cost reduction measures. Management cites cost reduction measures, alongside future business prospects, as supporting continued operations.
  • Proposed increase in borrowing and investment limits. The 20th AGM agenda includes an increase in borrowing limits up to ₹100 Crores under Section 180(1)(c) and enhancement of investment/loan/guarantee limits up to ₹200 Crores under Section 186 of the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Board and auditor refresh. The AGM seeks approval for the appointment of M/s U B Lakhani & Company as Statutory Auditors (to fill a casual vacancy from August 31, 2026, and for a five-year term from the 20th to the 25th AGM), the appointment of Mr. Badatala Sreenivasa Rao as an Independent Director, and the appointments of Mr. Krishnaprasad Sunder Rao and Mr. Mithlesh Jaiswal as Executive/Whole-time Directors.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory and penalty risk. The SEBI adjudication proceedings could result in penalties under Sections 15A(b) and 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992; the amount is not quantified and the impact cannot currently be ascertained.
  • Going concern risk. The company’s ability to continue as a going concern depends on the success of One Time Settlement proposals, cost reduction measures, and future business prospects, as noted in the audit qualifications.
  • Balance confirmation risk. No balance confirmation from the lender as on March 31, 2025 had been received, leaving the impact of the audit qualification unascertainable.
  • Financial position risk. Negative net worth, accumulated losses, and defaults in repayment of working capital borrowings are recorded in the FY2024-25 statements.
  • Execution risk on settlements. On a consolidated basis, the One Time Settlement remained subject to payments and final lender approval, with derecognition of the corresponding liability contingent on completion.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management states that the increase in productivity or profits cannot be forecasted accurately in measurable terms, but with the steps taken for improvement, the outlook is expected to improve. Management remains confident of the company’s ability to continue its operations on a going concern basis.
  • Guidance: Management views the One Time Settlement proposals submitted to lenders, cost reduction measures, future business prospects, and the preferential allotment of equity/warrants as supporting continued operations and growth.
  • Observed performance (FY2024-25, per the audit qualification statements):
    • Consolidated: Turnover/Total income ₹304.10 lakhs; Total expenditure ₹285.05 lakhs; Net Profit/(Loss) ₹7,229.20 lakhs; EPS ₹7.63; Total assets ₹2,643.58 lakhs; Total liabilities ₹30,960.93 lakhs; Net worth ₹-28,317.35 lakhs.
    • Standalone: Turnover/Total income ₹303.78 lakhs; Total expenditure ₹279.30 lakhs; Net Profit/(Loss) ₹7,231.81 lakhs; EPS ₹7.63; Total assets ₹2,514.16 lakhs; Total liabilities ₹30,660.45 lakhs; Net worth ₹-28,146.29 lakhs.
    • The reported figures were unchanged after adjusting for the audit qualifications, as the impact of the qualification was not quantified by the auditor.
  • Observed events versus guidance: The standalone statement records completion of the One Time Settlement with one lender, while the consolidated statement records the in-principle approval as still under process. The going concern qualification and unascertainable impact of the qualification stand alongside management’s stated confidence in continued operations.
