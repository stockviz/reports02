Indian Overseas Bank — Q1 FY2027 Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Corporate book contraction: The corporate/others segment declined 10.09% quarter-on-quarter (from ₹49,390 crore in March 2026 to ₹44,405 crore in June 2026) and 13.69% year-on-year. Management attributed this to exiting a single large account of around ₹10,000 crore in April 2026 because pricing did not match; roughly 40% of that exposure was replaced during Q1.
  • Pricing discipline versus volume: Management stated it is unwilling to grow the corporate book at sub-7% rates merely to increase volumes, which constrains growth in that segment.
  • Elevated operating expenses: Operating expenses rose 68.81% year-on-year to ₹3,155 crore, with staff expenses up 80.45% to ₹2,104 crore, compressing the gap between operating income growth and operating profit growth (operating profit rose only 14.21% versus 38.35% operating income growth).
  • SMA-2 uptick: SMA-2 increased by about ₹500 crore over March 2026, even as total SMA declined by about ₹2,200 crore to around 4%. Management said most of this was regularized.
  • Overseas asset quality: Overseas advances carried an NPA ratio of 8.68% in June 2026 (down from 8.79% in March 2026 but still far above domestic levels).
  • Disputed tax demands: Disputed tax demands relating to income tax of ₹4,63,156.15 lakh, Service Tax of ₹27,780.48 lakh, and Goods and Service Tax of ₹1,60,776.33 lakh.
  • External uncertainty: Management repeatedly flagged the West Asia situation as a live risk, while noting no sector- or product-level stress had yet been observed from it.
  • MCLR revision: The bank raised MCLR by 5 bps across tenors from three months to three years effective 15.08.2026 (three-month to 8.45%, six-month to 8.70%, one-year and two-year to 8.85%, three-year to 8.90%), while overnight and one-month MCLR were unchanged at 7.90% and 8.25%.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record profitability: Net profit reached an all-time quarterly high of ₹1,659 crore, up 49.32% year-on-year; operating profit rose 14.21% to ₹2,693 crore; NII rose 34.30% to ₹3,688 crore.
  • Margin expansion: Global NIM rose to 3.37% (from 3.25% in March 2026) and domestic NIM to 3.48% (from 3.35%). Management attributed this to both higher interest income and lower interest expenses, with all term deposit repricing completed about six months prior.
  • RAM-led credit growth: Total advances grew 22.75% year-on-year to ₹3,22,132 crore, driven by RAM (retail, agriculture, MSME) growth of 35.82%; retail rose 36.49%, agriculture 46.84%, and MSME 16.15%. RAM share of advances reached 81.21%.
  • Deposit franchise: Total deposits rose 13.72% to ₹3,76,193 crore; savings bank deposits rose 18.14% and retail term deposits 15.81%, with domestic CASA at 41.45%.
  • Asset quality improvement: Gross NPA fell to 1.33% and net NPA to 0.18%; slippage ratio hit a historic low of 0.06%; PCR rose to 97.67%; total recovery of ₹654 crore was 3.35 times quarterly slippages.
  • Capital strength: CRAR stood at 19.36% (Tier I 16.88%) against a regulatory requirement of 11.50%.
  • Capital raise approved: The Board approved raising up to ₹5,000 crore in equity, with plans to approach the market in more than one tranche in Q3/Q4 FY27; the AGM on July 7, 2026 passed the enabling resolution. A ₹1,000 crore Tier 2 bond raise was also referenced.
  • ECLGS disbursement pipeline: Of an eligible universe of about ₹4,400 crore, ₹2,600 crore had been disbursed, with ₹1,800 crore expected over the following one to one-and-a-half months and 95–100% completion targeted by end-August or early September 2026.
  • ECL provisioning buffer: Against an internal estimate of about ₹3,000 crore additional requirement, the bank had provided ₹2,150 crore (₹1,700 crore up to March plus ₹400 crore added in Q1).
  • GIFT City / IFSCA: Approval received about three to four months prior; operations expected to commence in about two months, with a target book of around $500 million by end-FY27.
  • FCNR(B) deposits: $300 million mobilized, with a target to double to $600–650 million by September 2026.
  • Digital and distribution expansion: Domestic branches increased to 3,522 (28 added during the quarter), ATMs/CRs to 3,691, and business correspondents to 13,401. Around 96% of transactions are digital, about 75% of onboarding is digital, and the bank is among the top two PSBs by loan disbursements via the Unified Lending Interface (2.62 lakh+ accounts, ₹5,700+ crore disbursed).
  • Corporate pipeline: Around ₹14,000 crore sanctioned and at various stages of disbursement, with corporate book growth of 12–13% expected for the year.

3. Key Risks

  • Concentration and pricing risk in corporate lending, illustrated by the exit from the ~₹10,000 crore account.
  • SMA-2 build-up requiring monitoring, though management linked the low slippage ratio (0.06%) to regularization.
  • Overseas asset quality, with the overseas NPA ratio at 8.68%.
  • Dependence on non-core income: Q1 profit was materially supported by PSLC commission of ₹863 crore (up 333.67% year-on-year) and recovery from technically written-off accounts of ₹487 crore; management acknowledged these may not recur at the same level.
  • External geopolitical uncertainty, specifically the West Asia situation.
  • Disputed tax demands across income tax, service tax, and GST as noted above.
  • Execution risk on the equity raise, which is subject to market conditions and statutory approvals, with Government of India holding 92.44% and SEBI minimum public shareholding considerations in play.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Credit growth: Guidance is 13–14% as a minimum, with management noting the bank has grown around 22% over the last three years. Observed: 22.75% year-on-year in Q1 FY27, above guidance.
  • NIM: Guidance is to maintain 3.30–3.40%. Observed: global NIM 3.37% and domestic NIM 3.48% in Q1 FY27, within/above the guided band.
  • ROA: Guidance is to stay above 1.20% internally, with around 1.46% targeted by end-FY27 and 1.4–1.5% over the next two to three quarters. Observed: 1.41% in Q1 FY27, up 27 bps year-on-year.
  • Credit cost: Full-year guidance of 0.35–0.40%. Observed: 0.14% for the June quarter.
  • Corporate book: Guidance of 12–13% growth for the year, against a 10.09% Q-o-Q decline in Q1.
  • ECLGS: Guidance of 95–100% disbursement by end-August/early September 2026, against ₹2,600 crore of ₹4,400 crore disbursed so far.
  • Capital raise: Guidance to approach the market in Q3/Q4 FY27 in one or more tranches, subject to market conditions.
  • FCNR(B): Guidance to double mobilization to $600–650 million by September 2026, from $300 million already raised.
  • GIFT City: Guidance to commence operations in about two months with a ~$500 million book by end-FY27.
  • Consistency: Management characterized consistency as the bank’s hallmark, citing 11 consecutive quarters of positive trends in targeted metrics, and stated accumulated losses have been made nil through internal accruals and netting off against share premium.
