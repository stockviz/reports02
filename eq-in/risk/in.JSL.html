Broker Narrative

From the first to the last report, the core bullish thesis stayed intact: capacity expansion, backward integration/margin improvement, and demand from railways/metro/infrastructure and green energy remained recurring positives, and both calls were Buy. The risk narrative, however, shifted from early worries about unprotected Indonesian imports, macro weakness, and global slowdown to concrete 2026 shocks: Middle East-linked industrial gas/propane shortages, cost inflation, and EU quotas/CBAM. The later reports also replaced policy-support optimism with external-trade-policy caution, while maintaining volume targets and adding export diversification as a new tailwind.

Fears that came true

  • The early high-power-cost risk under ‘global slowdown’ materialized in the 2026 report: propane/LPG prices were nearly 3x pre-war and sharply raised power & fuel costs, a pressure tied to weak operating leverage.
  • The early macroeconomic vulnerability was borne out by the 2024-07 and 2024-08 DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes, where predicted +29.8% and +13.6% returns turned into -8.1% and -1.8% actual returns.
