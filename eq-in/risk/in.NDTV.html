NDTV: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak quarterly performance. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, NDTV reported revenue from operations of ₹7,158 lakh on a standalone basis, down from ₹12,039 lakh in the preceding quarter (ended March 31, 2026) and up only modestly from ₹5,424 lakh in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. Total income was ₹7,496 lakh, against ₹12,303 lakh in the preceding quarter. The company recorded a net loss of ₹7,647 lakh for the quarter, compared with a loss of ₹8,243 lakh in the preceding quarter and a loss of ₹7,171 lakh in the year-ago quarter. Total expenses of ₹15,143 lakh exceeded total income, with marketing, distribution and promotional expenses and production expenses being the largest cost items.

Persistent losses. The audited figures for the year ended March 31, 2026 show a net loss of ₹31,060 lakh, following a loss of ₹9,857 lakh in the prior year. Earnings per share for the June 2026 quarter was negative at ₹(6.78) basic and diluted.

Regulatory and legal overhang. Several matters remain unresolved: - SEBI has filed a Special Leave Petition before the Supreme Court against the Bombay High Court’s September 4, 2019 judgment concerning rejected settlement applications; no stay has been granted and the next hearing date is yet to be notified. - A SEBI demand notice dated November 22, 2019 for ₹307 lakh (plus interest) relating to a penalty of ₹200 lakh imposed for alleged non-disclosure of a disputed tax demand of ₹450 crore remains contested. SEBI has filed an SLP against the Bombay High Court judgment, and while the Solicitor General orally undertook that no coercive action would be taken, the matter is pending. - Income tax reassessment for Assessment Year 2008-09 has produced a revised demand of ₹42,036 lakh (₹420.36 crore) as of March 2026, up from ₹35,807 lakh (₹358.07 crore) earlier, despite a reduction in assessed income, due to statutory interest. A complete stay of demand was granted on April 28, 2026, and an appeal is before the CIT(A). - A CBI FIR dated August 19, 2019 against the company and certain former officials alleging that funds invested in subsidiaries during 2004–2010 were proceeds of unknown public servants remains under investigation.

Joint venture issues. Financial statements for two joint ventures (Indianroots Retail Private Limited and Indianroots Shopping Limited) were not received for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Their investments were fully impaired in earlier years, and the MCA portal reports their status as inactive. Three other joint ventures’ financial information was furnished by management and not reviewed by auditors.

Labour code transition. The Government of India consolidated 29 labour legislations into 4 Labour Codes effective November 21, 2025. Supporting rules are yet to be notified, and the financial implications were recognised in the previous year ended March 31, 2026 based on best estimates.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

GoodTimes channel acquisition. NDTV entered into a binding Term Sheet dated September 19, 2025 with Lifestyle & Media Broadcasting Limited for acquisition of the “GoodTimes” channel and associated intellectual property rights. On August 17, 2026, the company disclosed entering into an Asset Purchase Agreement with the same seller for acquisition of the business undertaking comprising the GoodTimes channel. The acquisition is intended to strengthen NDTV’s strategic positioning, diversify and expand operational capabilities, and enhance long-term stakeholder value. The transaction is on an arm’s length basis, supported by a valuation report from a registered valuer. Consideration is cash plus television advertising inventory, with a lump sum of up to ₹18 crore on a cash-free, debt-free basis, subject to adjustments. Completion is expected within approximately three months, subject to MIB approval and other regulatory/statutory approvals.

Related party context. The seller is a joint venture of NDTV and therefore a related party. The promoter, promoter group and group companies have no interest in the seller except to the extent of indirect shareholding, if any.

Amalgamation completed. Pursuant to a Regional Director (Northern Region) order dated September 12, 2025, the amalgamation of NDTV Networks Limited, NDTV Worldwide Limited, NDTV Media Limited, and NDTV Labs Limited with NDTV became effective on October 1, 2025 upon filing of Form INC-28. All assets, liabilities and obligations of the transferor companies vested in NDTV. The authorised share capital was revised to ₹2,36,77,00,000 divided into 59,19,25,000 equity shares of ₹4 each. Comparative financial information has been restated under Ind AS 103 as if the amalgamation had taken place from the beginning of the earliest period presented.

Legal relief obtained. In connection with the CBI FIR dated June 2, 2017, the CBI filed a closure report accepted by the Rouse Avenue Court, holding that no criminality or violation was found. The Delhi High Court, via orders dated July 28, 2025 and August 18, 2025, disposed of related writ petitions and quashed the ECIR registered by the Directorate of Enforcement, subject to liberty to revive if the CBI closure report is set aside by a superior court.

Tax stay secured. A complete stay of demand was granted on April 28, 2026 for the AY 2008-09 reassessment demand, providing relief from immediate recovery.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory approval risk: Completion of the GoodTimes acquisition depends on MIB approval and other regulatory/statutory approvals, which may not be obtained on the expected timeline or at all.
  • Litigation risk: Pending SEBI SLPs, the income tax appeal before the CIT(A), and the CBI investigation could result in adverse outcomes, penalties, or recovery actions if stays are vacated or appeals fail.
  • Financial performance risk: Continued losses and negative operating cash flows could pressure liquidity and limit investment capacity.
  • Joint venture risk: Inactive joint ventures and unreceived financial statements create uncertainty, though management represents them as not material.
  • Integration risk: The amalgamation and the GoodTimes acquisition require integration of operations, personnel, and systems, which may not achieve intended synergies.
  • Regulatory change risk: The Labour Codes’ supporting rules are yet to be notified, and the full financial impact may differ from current estimates.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance on GoodTimes acquisition timeline. Management stated the transaction is expected to be completed within approximately three months from the August 17, 2026 disclosure. As of the supplied material, completion remains subject to closing conditions including MIB approval; no completion has been reported.

Guidance on acquisition rationale. Management stated the acquisition will strengthen strategic positioning, diversify and expand operational capabilities, and enhance long-term stakeholder value. Observed performance for the June 2026 quarter shows continued losses and lower revenue compared to the preceding quarter, so the anticipated benefits have not yet manifested in reported results.

Guidance on joint ventures. Management represented that the two joint ventures with unreceived financial statements are not material to consolidated results and that there is no foreseeable financial impact. Auditors noted the investments were fully impaired in earlier years and the entities are reported as inactive on the MCA portal.

Guidance on labour codes. Management recognised financial implications of the Labour Codes in the previous year ended March 31, 2026 based on best information and estimates, with supporting rules yet to be notified.

Observed performance versus prior periods. The June 2026 quarter loss of ₹7,647 lakh was narrower than the preceding quarter’s loss of ₹8,243 lakh but wider than the year-ago quarter’s loss of ₹7,171 lakh. Revenue from operations of ₹7,158 lakh was higher than the year-ago quarter’s ₹5,424 lakh but significantly lower than the preceding quarter’s ₹12,039 lakh. The company remains loss-making at both the standalone and consolidated levels.
