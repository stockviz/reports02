Network18: Recent Corporate Announcements — Key Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Advertising environment split between government and corporate spend. In Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), demand for government advertising — Centre and State campaigns, political party spends, and PSUs — rose sharply for the industry, driven by state elections in West Bengal and Tamil Nadu. At the same time, non-government advertising inventory declined by over 10% year-on-year. Corporate spends were impacted by the West Asia conflict and the forecast of a weak monsoon. Network18’s own non-government inventory consumption grew ~2%, which the company describes as better than the industry.

Cost inflation from the annual increments cycle. Growth in operating costs was primarily due to the annual increments cycle being completed in the first quarter of this year, compared to the second quarter last year. Other operating costs grew moderately, and EBITDA remained flattish mainly due to the impact of the increase in employee costs not present in the base.

Suspension of television viewership ratings. Citing concerns over coverage related to the West Asia conflict, the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) initially directed the Broadcast Audience Research Council (BARC) to pause weekly viewership data for news channels for a four-week period starting March. This pause was extended multiple times as the conflict dragged on and continues to be in force. Earlier in July 2026, the MIB directed BARC to stop publishing television ratings across both news and non-news genres until its licence is renewed under the Television Ratings Policy, 2026. The directive pauses BARC’s ratings operations until the ratings body secures licence renewal and is certified as fully compliant with the new regulatory framework. The new policy introduces expanded sample sizes, enhanced board governance norms, stricter audit requirements, compliance with data protection provisions, greater disclosure of methodologies, and periodic inspections by the ministry. The company notes that revenue momentum was maintained during a period when viewership ratings have been unavailable, and the Chairman observed that government interventions on viewership ratings have been negative for industry sentiment.

Reported losses. On a standalone basis, Network18 reported a loss before exceptional items and tax of ₹77.92 crore for Q1 FY27, versus a loss of ₹70.84 crore in Q1 FY26 and ₹60.76 crore in Q4 FY26. On a consolidated basis, loss before share of associates/JVs, exceptional items and tax was ₹76.10 crore, versus ₹72.11 crore in Q1 FY26 and ₹54.68 crore in Q4 FY26. Consolidated loss before exceptional items and tax was ₹38.36 crore, compared with ₹1.79 crore in Q1 FY26 and ₹17.45 crore in Q4 FY26. Consolidated net loss attributable to owners was ₹38.71 crore, versus a profit of ₹148.03 crore in Q1 FY26 (which included an exceptional gain) and a loss of ₹30.52 crore in Q4 FY26.

Sequential decline. Consolidated operating revenue declined 16.2% quarter-on-quarter to ₹516 crore from ₹616 crore, and consolidated operating EBITDA declined 75.3% QoQ to ₹8 crore from ₹30 crore.

Leverage and coverage ratios. The consolidated debt-equity ratio stood at 0.63 in Q1 FY27, versus 0.59 in Q1 FY26. The consolidated debt service coverage ratio and interest service coverage ratio were 0.28 in Q1 FY27, versus 0.96 in Q1 FY26 and 0.65 in Q4 FY26. The consolidated current ratio was 0.23, versus 0.25 in Q1 FY26 and 0.26 in Q4 FY26.

Comparability effects. News18 Marathi became a wholly owned subsidiary on October 29, 2025, following acquisition of shares held by Lokmat Media, so figures for the corresponding previous period are not comparable. Eenadu Television Private Limited ceased to be an associate with effect from July 7, 2025, following assignment of voting rights on the 24.5% equity holding to its promoter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Election-driven advertising. The advertising business delivered strong performance during multiple state elections, garnering a significant share of advertising spends. Network18’s ad inventory consumption grew 10% YoY compared to 3% for the industry. Consolidated operating revenue grew 10.3% YoY to ₹516 crore, and consolidated operating EBITDA grew 80.3% YoY to ₹8 crore, with the operating EBITDA margin at 1.5% versus 0.9% in Q1 FY26.

Digital leadership. Network18 continued to be the #1 digital news/information network in India with a monthly reach of 350mn+ (Comscore Total Digital Population Report, May ’26), described elsewhere in the release as ~360mn monthly users, representing 76% reach in the segment. It maintained clear dominance as the biggest news network on YouTube with 1.5x+ video views of the nearest competitor (public sources — Playboard, Social Blade — for April–June 2026). It recorded over 6 billion video views on YouTube in June, ~2x the nearest competitor. Total views across social platforms crossed 32 billion during the quarter, up 31% QoQ, and social footprint crossed 472 million followers. Fifteen Network18 channels ranked #1 in their respective categories in June 2026, including CNBC Awaaz, Moneycontrol, and multiple Hindi and regional news brands.

Connected TV monetisation. Network18 saw a strong uptick in monetisation on Connected TV screens, which is emerging as an important revenue stream, with reach and engagement on Connected TVs continuing to grow exponentially.

Moneycontrol. Moneycontrol continued as India’s leading financial intelligence platform with more than 3x time-spent and 2x page views compared to the nearest competitor. Moneycontrol Pro maintained its status as India’s largest digital news subscription platform with more than 1 million paid subscribers. It added ‘Portfolio X Ray’ for in-depth portfolio analysis, and Super Pro — an AI-powered premium subscription service offering access to SEBI-registered Research Analysts — continued to see good subscriber addition and renewal rates above industry benchmarks. The fintech business maintained strong momentum, driven by advanced data modelling that improved lead quality, approval rates, and click-through rates; the partner network for secured lending (Gold Loans, LAMF) expanded to include Muthoot Finance, Muthoot Fincorp, Rupeek, and DSP Finance.

News18.com and Firstpost. News18.com recorded an 8% sequential increase in unique users, with average session length up over 10%. A revamped Election Analytics Centre led to a 2.5x engagement time on the homepage. A FIFA World Cup microsite was launched, Tech2 was relaunched with an AI-powered experience, and hyperlocal coverage from Local18 contributed nearly a quarter of overall traffic. Firstpost’s YouTube subscriber count neared 10 million, and it delivered over 270 million video views on YouTube during the quarter, of which more than 50% came from outside India. Firstpost expanded its original content slate with new shows including The Link, The Blueprint, The Champion Code, The Spend, and Sports Explainers.

Pre-suspension TV leadership. Prior to the suspension of ratings data, Network18 was India’s #1 TV news network, with a portfolio of 20 channels — the largest in India — leading in all-India viewership share (13.8%) and reach (230 million people a month, 35% higher than the nearest competitor). It led key markets: CNBC-TV18 (#1 Business channel, 63.3%+ share), News18 India (#1 Hindi news channel, 12.8% share), and CNN-News18 (#1 English news channel, 36.0% share), with strong regional positions in Marathi (28.0%), Kannada (22.5%), West Bengal (21.2%), and leadership in several Hindi-speaking regional markets.

Associate/JV contribution. Share of profit of associates and joint ventures was ₹37.74 crore in Q1 FY27, versus ₹70.32 crore in Q1 FY26 and ₹37.23 crore in Q4 FY26.

3. Key Risks

  • Macro and geopolitical risk: The West Asia conflict and weak monsoon forecast dampened the macroeconomic mood and corporate advertising spends.
  • Regulatory risk: The MIB’s directives pausing BARC ratings — first for news channels and then across news and non-news genres until licence renewal under the Television Ratings Policy, 2026 — create an extended period without viewership data. The new policy imposes expanded sample sizes, enhanced board governance norms, stricter audit requirements, data protection compliance, greater methodology disclosure, and periodic ministry inspections.
  • Advertising concentration risk: Sharp growth in government advertising coincided with a decline of over 10% YoY in non-government advertising inventory, indicating dependence on a narrow, potentially volatile spend category.
  • Cost risk: The annual increments cycle and higher employee benefit expenses weighed on EBITDA, which remained flattish despite revenue growth.
  • Leverage and coverage risk: Consolidated debt-equity rose to 0.63 from 0.59 a year earlier, while debt service and interest service coverage ratios fell to 0.28 from 0.96.
  • Comparability risk: The News18 Marathi acquisition and the ETPL de-association affect period-to-period comparability of reported figures.
  • Loss-making operations: Both standalone and consolidated results show losses at the profit-before-tax level for the quarter.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The supplied material contains limited explicit forward guidance. The clearest forward-looking statement is from Mr. Adil Zainulbhai, Chairman of Network18: the quarter was “a mixed one, for us as well as the industry”; while state elections gave a boost to advertising revenue, the ongoing geopolitical conflict and weak monsoon forecast were dampeners for the macroeconomic mood, and government interventions on viewership ratings were negative for industry sentiment. He stated that despite these developments, the company is “fully focused on making our products better by ensuring that they serve their consumers effectively, so that when the macro environment improves, we are in the right position to benefit from it.”

Observed performance against this framing:

  • Election advertising boost — observed. Consolidated operating revenue grew 10.3% YoY to ₹516 crore, with the release attributing this to strong advertising revenue growth during elections in West Bengal and Tamil Nadu. Ad inventory consumption grew 10% YoY versus 3% for the industry.
  • Macro dampeners — observed. Non-government advertising inventory declined over 10% YoY, with corporate spends impacted by the West Asia conflict and weak monsoon forecast. Consolidated operating EBITDA fell 75.3% QoQ to ₹8 crore, and consolidated operating revenue fell 16.2% QoQ.
  • Ratings suspension — observed. The release confirms the pause was extended multiple times and remains in force, and that the MIB subsequently directed BARC to stop publishing ratings across news and non-news genres until licence renewal. The company states it maintained revenue momentum “even during a period when viewership ratings have been unavailable.”
  • Product focus — observed. The release documents multiple product initiatives during the quarter: Portfolio X Ray and Super Pro at Moneycontrol, the Election Analytics Centre and FIFA World Cup microsite at News18.com, the AI-powered relaunch of Tech2, and new Firstpost shows.
  • Cost pressure — observed. Employee benefits expense rose to ₹208.34 crore consolidated in Q1 FY27 from ₹185.61 crore in Q1 FY26, and the release attributes flattish EBITDA to the increase in employee costs not present in the base.

The material also records that the 31st Annual General Meeting was scheduled for Wednesday, September 16, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. (IST) through video conferencing/other audio-visual means, with the cut-off date for e-voting fixed as Wednesday, September 9, 2026.
