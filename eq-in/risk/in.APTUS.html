Broker Narrative

Initially, the broker narrative was highly optimistic, emphasizing strong asset quality, high yields, aggressive branch expansion, and stable management under the founder. By the last report, the tone became more guarded, acknowledging moderate disbursements, NIM pressure from assignments and leverage, rising credit costs, and slower-than-expected geographical expansion, though still retaining a Buy rating on operational efficiency and sequential asset quality improvement. The core themes of asset quality and expansion persisted, but the confidence in growth shifted to caution.

Optimism that failed

  • The expectation of aggressive branch expansion driving strong disbursement and AUM growth failed, as the last report cites moderate disbursements and AUM growth.
  • The confidence in a steady ~17% yield from the AUM mix was undermined by NIM decline from assignments and higher leverage.
  • The tight control on asset quality was contradicted by a sharp increase in credit costs, despite sequential improvement.
  • The contiguous expansion strategy with deep presence in existing states and leadership position failed to prevent a protracted geographical expansion.
