AI Summary

asof: 2026-09-16

Headwinds and Challenges

  • Geopolitical challenges and cost inflation were cited by management as factors faced during Q1FY27, even as the company delivered double-digit growth across segments.
  • Auto ICE mix moderation: While Auto ICE continued to grow (20.8% YoY), its contribution to overall sales moderated to 65% (from 72.7% in Q1FY26), reflecting faster traction in Non-Auto rather than a decline in ICE.
  • Sweden operations underperformance relative to other geographies: Europe exports grew 32.1% YoY, but excluding Sweden operations, growth was 48.8% YoY, indicating Sweden grew at a slower 11.6% YoY.
  • Litigation settlement: An exceptional item of INR 169 million was recognised in Q1FY27 towards settlement of a litigation matter before the U.S. District Court (disclosed in the 2021 prospectus). The matter was settled without admission of liability, and the settlement amount is expected to be substantially covered by insurance. Excluding the post-tax impact of INR 127 million, normalised PAT stood at INR 1,000 million.
  • FY26 exceptional item: Increase in gratuity liability arising out of past service cost and increase in leave liability on account of changes in Labour laws.
  • Customer concentration: Top 5 customer share was 44.5% in FY26, though this has been declining over time (59.2% in FY21), reflecting diversification.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Highest-ever quarterly revenue: Q1FY27 revenue of INR 10,213 million, crossing the INR 10,000 million mark with 33.3% YoY growth.
  • Non-Auto segment momentum: Highest-ever quarterly sales, growing 129.9% YoY, with contribution rising to 20.8% of overall sales (against a long-term vision of 20%). The ADS business was the key growth driver, with sales increasing more than 3x YoY.
  • ADS order backlog: Total unexecuted order backlog for the ADS business stood at INR 44,368 million as on June 30, 2026, executable over approximately 5 years. FY27 ADS revenue guidance is INR 5,500–6,000 million.
  • Auto-Tech Agnostic and xEV: Highest-ever quarterly sales, growing 22.2% YoY, with xEV growing at nearly twice the pace of tech-agnostic.
  • Geographic outperformance: International business grew 71.4% YoY; exports to other foreign countries increased more than 3x YoY (driven by Semiconductor business); exports to USA nearly doubled (Aerospace and Passenger Vehicles); exports to Europe grew 32.1% YoY (48.8% excluding Sweden).
  • Segment-level growth: Passenger Vehicles +38.2% YoY, Commercial Vehicles +18.5% YoY, 2W +14.6% YoY, Off-Road +50.1% YoY.
  • Order book (excluding ADS): Peak annual revenues for new business stood at INR 18,493 million as on June 30, 2026, expected to reach peak annual revenue in the next 3 years. Domestic 45%, Global 55%.
  • Capacity expansion: Capex of INR 5,911 million in FY26 and INR 1,342 million in Q1FY27. Upcoming projects include Pantnagar (Plant 6) forging capacity for 2W, Manesar (Plant 4) 80k sq. ft. for PV and 2W, Nichidai Sansera JV for cold and warm forged precision components, Bengaluru (Plant 2) machined capacity for Auto Tech-agnostic and xEV, an 80k sq. ft. hangar expansion, a new defence plant (45k sq. ft.), and a brownfield surface coating facility.
  • JV with Nichidai Corporation: 60:40 JV (Sansera 60%, Nichidai 40%) to manufacture precision forged and machined parts in aluminium and steel for differential assemblies, compressors, driveline and other advanced automotive components not currently manufactured by Sansera. Sansera will invest INR 500 million in one or more tranches.
  • MMRFIC partnership: Strategic investment in advanced radar technologies with rights to increase stake to 51% at a predefined valuation formula. Further investment of INR 100 million made during the year.
  • Diversification vision: Long-term vision to increase Non-Auto to 20% and Auto Tech-agnostic & xEV to 20% of sales, while reducing Auto ICE to 60%.
  • Awards: JIPM TPM Excellence Consistency Award, Royal Enfield 2026 Reliable Partner (Quality & Delivery), General Motors Supplier Quality Excellence Award 2025, Bajaj Auto Platinum, and others.

Key Risks

  • Forward-looking statement risks (as disclosed): Fluctuations in earnings, ability to manage growth, competition (domestic and international), economic growth in India and abroad, ability to attract and retain highly skilled professionals, time and cost overruns on contracts, ability to manage international operations, government policies and actions, regulations, and interest and other fiscal costs prevailing in the economy.
  • Geopolitical challenges referenced by management as a factor in the operating environment.
  • Cost inflation referenced by management.
  • Execution risk on capacity expansion: The company is building capacity ahead of demand, both for existing components and new qualifications.
  • Order book conversion risk: The order book represents peak annual revenues for new business based on LOIs/POs for which production has started beyond April 1, 2026 or is yet to start, expected to reach peak annual revenue in the next 3 years.
  • ADS backlog execution: The ADS order backlog of INR 44,368 million is executable over approximately 5 years.
  • Litigation: The U.S. District Court litigation was settled in Q1FY27 without admission of liability, with the settlement amount expected to be substantially covered by insurance.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • FY27 topline guidance: Management expects to end FY27 with high-teens topline growth, with a continued focus on improving margin profile. Q1FY27 delivered 33.3% YoY revenue growth, above the high-teens full-year guidance.
  • ADS revenue guidance: FY27 ADS revenue guidance of INR 5,500–6,000 million. Q1FY27 ADS segment revenue was INR 1,454 million (Q1FY26: INR 545 million).
  • Margin profile: Management stated a continued focus on improving margin profile. Q1FY27 EBITDA margin was 19.2% (Q1FY26: 17.2%; FY26: 18.1%), and PAT margin was 8.6% (Q1FY26: 8.2%; FY26: 9.3%). Normalised PAT (excluding exceptional item post-tax impact) was INR 1,000 million, a 59% YoY growth.
  • Non-Auto contribution target: Management’s long-term vision of 20% Non-Auto contribution was achieved in Q1FY27 at 20.8%.
  • Order book guidance: Order book as on March 31, 2026 represents orders which moved to mass production as on April 1, 2026; peak value of these orders shall reflect in the topline once they reach maturity. The order book is expected to reach peak annual revenue in the next 3 years.
  • Capacity building: Management stated commitment to building capacity ahead of strong demand, both for growth in existing components and for new qualifications, underpinned by existing qualifications across critical components and strong OEM relationships.
  • Long-term vision: Auto ICE to 60%, Auto Tech-agnostic & xEV to 20%, Non-Auto to 20% of sales. Q1FY27 actuals: Auto ICE 65.4%, Auto Tech-agnostic & xEV 13.7%, Non-Auto 20.8%.

Broker Narrative

Broker narrative stayed consistently bullish, evolving from an early recovery-and-diversification thesis focused on ICE concentration, low utilization, and export/aerospace growth into a broader ADS/non-auto/defence-led growth story with margin recovery. Persistent themes included revenue acceleration, high-margin product mix, exports, and improving profitability. The changed emphasis was a shift from fearing ICE dependence to treating ADS, non-auto, and defence as the main growth engines, while adding geopolitical execution delays as a new risk but retaining a Buy stance.
