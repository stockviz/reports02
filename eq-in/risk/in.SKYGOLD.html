Sky Gold and Diamonds: Recent Developments and Outlook

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Gold price volatility affects working capital requirements and demand measured by weight.
  • Customer concentration, with reliance on major retail partners, remains a structural challenge.
  • Working capital intensity is a persistent feature of the business model.
  • Currency fluctuations pose ongoing exposure.
  • Regulatory and compliance changes, such as expanding hallmarking requirements, add cost and operational complexity.
  • Talent and key-person risk regarding skilled artisans.
  • General forward-looking risks cited by the company include the performance of the Indian and international economies, industry performance in India and worldwide, competition, ability to successfully implement strategy, technological advancements, changes in revenue, income or cash flows, market preferences, and market risk exposure.
  • Operational disruption: the Investor’s Meet scheduled for Wednesday, 9th September 2026 was cancelled due to unavoidable exigencies, pursuant to Regulation 30(6) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Structural shift from unorganised to organised hallmarked retail jewellery in India. The organised retail segment is projected to grow from USD 19.2 billion in FY 2020 to USD 82.65 billion by FY 2028.
  • Rising demand for lightweight and value-added jewellery, including 9kt/18kt and diamond-studded pieces.
  • Export expansion into GCC markets via the Dubai office (Sky Souk Jewellery Trading LLC, UAE).
  • Increasing share of the advance gold model and strategic acquisitions.
  • Expansion of the Indian jewellery retail market, projected to reach USD 145 billion by FY 2028, supported by rising disposable incomes and strong cultural affinity for gold.
  • Growth in alternative categories such as gemstone and fashion jewellery.
  • Scaling the lab-grown diamond segment and increasing the export mix.
  • MSCI India Domestic Small Cap Index inclusion (announced 17 August 2026), which broadens visibility within the institutional investment community.
  • Operating cash flow turned positive during the current quarter, supporting working capital discipline.
  • Governance and management strengthening, including a structured leadership approach, strengthened financial and governance processes, and engagement with a large global accounting firm.
  • Asset-light manufacturing model supporting capacity scaling with capital efficiency.
  • Credit rating upgrade to IND A/Stable/IND A1 and a working capital cycle reduced to under 60 days.
  • ‘Sky Gold 3.0’ initiative focused on asset-light manufacturing and positive cash generation.

3. Key Risks

  • Gold price volatility, customer concentration, working capital intensity, currency fluctuations, regulatory and compliance changes (including hallmarking), and talent/key-person risk.
  • Forward-looking statement risks: Indian and international economic performance, industry performance, competition, strategy execution, technological advancements, changes in revenue/income/cash flows, market preferences, and market risk exposure.
  • The cancellation of the Investor’s Meet on 9th September 2026 due to unavoidable exigencies represents a near-term engagement disruption.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

Observed performance:

  • FY 2025-26 (consolidated): Revenue from operations reached Rs. 6,29,488.68 lakhs, showing 77.4% growth; PAT reached Rs. 28,183.08 lakhs.
  • Q1 FY27: Revenue from operations was Rs 2,012.8 crore (+77.9% Y-o-Y); Gross Profit was Rs 187.5 crore (+105.3% Y-o-Y); EBITDA was Rs 156.7 crore (+119.6% Y-o-Y); PAT was Rs 104.9 crore (+140.7% Y-o-Y).
  • Q1 FY27 (subsidiaries not reviewed by the principal auditor): Two subsidiaries contributed total revenue of Rs. 37,481.69 lakhs, total net profit after tax of Rs. 2,244.11 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 2,244.11 lakhs.
  • Operational milestones: Credit rating upgrade to IND A/Stable/IND A1; working capital cycle reduced to under 60 days; operating cash flow turned positive during the current quarter.
  • Governance actions: Promoters will not draw salaries from FY27; all promoter payouts will be solely through dividends generated from operating cash flows. The Board approved the appointment of M/s M S K A & Associates LLP as Statutory Auditor for five consecutive years from the conclusion of the 18th AGM until the conclusion of the 23rd AGM (FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31), subject to shareholder approval.
  • Auditor review: The Statutory Auditors issued Limited Review Reports with an unmodified opinion on the unaudited standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

Management guidance:

  • FY27 guidance (revised upwards): Revenue at ~Rs 8,100 crore; EBITDA margin in the range of ~7.0-7.5%; PAT margin guided at 4.5%-4.75%.
  • FY30 guidance: Revenue expected at ~Rs 18,000-19,000 crore; PAT margin projected at ~5.25%+ aggregating to Rs 945 crore; ROCE of 27%+; CFO/PAT of ~20%+.
  • Long-term strategic targets: Net debt-free status by FY 2030; working capital cycle reduced to 50-55 days by 2030; export contributions scaled toward 20% of total volumes; advance gold model expanded to approximately 30% of total volumes by FY30.
  • Other forward-looking statements: The company will intimate the stock exchange of any further updates regarding the cancelled Investor’s Meet.
