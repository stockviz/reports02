Global Education Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Weak revenue base at OwnPrep Private Limited. The entity acquired on August 11, 2026 reported revenue from operations of ₹9.66 lakh in FY 2025-26, down from ₹19.19 lakh in FY 2024-25 (and ₹10.46 lakh in FY 2023-24). The acquired business is small and its most recent full-year revenue declined year over year.
  • Low shareholder participation in governance. At the Fifteenth AGM (July 31, 2026), 16,889 shareholders were on record, but only 53 members voted through remote e-voting and e-voting at the AGM, and only 35 members attended via VC/OAVM. Public-institutional holding recorded zero votes on all resolutions.
  • Shareholder communication gaps. Of the 16,242 registered email addresses to which the AGM notice was sent on July 8, 2026, 559 were classified invalid and 514 bounced (1,073 records in aggregate), and 349 shareholder records in the BENPOS had no email ID available.
  • Dissent on specific resolutions. On Resolution 4 (continuation of Mr. Gururaj Vasantrao Karajagi’s directorship beyond age 75) and Resolution 5 (re-appointment of Mr. Inder Krishen Bhat as Independent Director), 550 public votes were cast against, with 99.8857% in favour and 0.1143% against among public-others; both passed as Special Resolutions.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Full ownership of OwnPrep Private Limited. On August 11, 2026, the Board approved acquisition of 4,900 equity shares (49%) of OwnPrep for ₹49,000, plus beneficial interest in one remaining share for ₹10 — total cash consideration ₹49,010. OwnPrep became a wholly owned subsidiary, with the Company directly holding 9,999 shares (99.99%) and beneficially owning the remaining share through a nominee. The stated objects are to consolidate ownership and control, enable closer strategic and operational integration, simplify governance, and strengthen presence in technology-enabled learning and digital education.
  • Order win in interactive display panels. A confirmed Purchase Order from 3C IT Solutions & Telecoms (India) Limited covers supply of 130 units of Teachmint 75” Pro X2075PB14 interactive digital display panels, valued at ₹1,61,85,000 (excluding GST) and ₹1,90,98,300 (including GST), to be executed within 15 days.
  • Strategic stake via demerger. Rishiraj Infravision Private Limited allotted 24,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹1 each to Global Education Limited under an NCLT-sanctioned Scheme of Demerger, with no cash consideration. Post-allotment, Global Education holds 24,28,230 equity shares, approximately 28.24% of Rishiraj Infravision’s post-allotment equity share capital.
  • Dividend continuity. The AGM confirmed the interim dividend and declared a final dividend of 25%, i.e., ₹0.50 per equity share, for FY 2025-26.
  • Revised Dividend Distribution Policy. The Board approved a revised Dividend Distribution Policy under Regulation 43A of the SEBI Listing Regulations, effective August 11, 2026, subject to further modification and periodic review.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution risk on the purchase order. The 3C IT Solutions order is to be executed within 15 days in accordance with the agreed terms of the Purchase Order.
  • Scale and integration risk on OwnPrep. The wholly owned subsidiary operates in ed-tech, digital education services and technology-enabled educational solutions, with the most recent full-year revenue at ₹9.66 lakh.
  • Governance concentration. Voting outcomes show near-total reliance on promoter votes: 37,019,951 promoter and promoter-group votes were cast in favour on every resolution, versus 481,087–481,212 public-others votes, with zero public-institutional votes recorded.
  • No reported defaults or deviations. The Integrated Filing (Financial) for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 states “Not Applicable as no default” for outstanding default on loans and debt securities, and “Not Applicable” for deviation or variation in proceeds of public/rights/preferential/QIP issues.
  • Related-party safeguards on the OwnPrep transaction. Based on declarations placed before the Audit Committee and Board, the selling shareholders are not related parties, the transaction does not constitute a related party transaction, and save for the Company’s existing shareholding and control in OwnPrep, the promoter/promoter group/group companies have no interest in OwnPrep, the selling shareholders, or the transaction. No specific governmental or regulatory approval was required; post-closing filings and compliances are to be undertaken within prescribed timelines.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on the OwnPrep acquisition timeline. The company indicated the acquisition was expected to complete on August 11, 2026, subject to statutory, contractual and customary closing formalities. Observed outcome: the acquisition was completed on August 11, 2026, and OwnPrep became a wholly owned subsidiary.
  • Guidance on the 3C IT Solutions order. Management stated the order is to be executed within 15 days in accordance with the agreed terms, and that the Company shall keep the Stock Exchange informed of any further material development. No completion or delivery update is provided in the supplied material.
  • Guidance on the revised Dividend Distribution Policy. The policy takes effect from August 11, 2026 and remains subject to further modifications and periodic reviews as required under applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
  • Financial results review status. The Board approved unaudited standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with Limited Review Reports from M/s Patel Shah & Joshi, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 107768W), dated August 11, 2026. The review reports state that a review is substantially less in scope than an audit and no audit opinion is expressed; no financial figures from the results are included in the supplied material.
