DEEDEV: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Deferred revenue from Middle East disruption. In Q1 FY27, revenue recognition of approximately ₹25 crore from certain orders was deferred due to temporary geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and customer-related issues, despite materials being fully manufactured, packed, and ready for dispatch. Dispatches have since normalized, and the deferred revenue is expected to be recognized in the coming quarter.

Power division tariff disputes. The company’s power business faces ongoing legal challenges. For the 8 MW Abohar biomass plant, PSERC reduced the tariff to ₹5.26 per unit retrospectively from 1 January 2024 (order dated 15 May 2025), creating a payable of ₹1,682.87 lakhs for excess revenue billed from 1 January 2024 to 30 April 2025. A review petition revised the tariff to ₹5.88 per unit (order dated 20 August 2025), reducing the payable to ₹1,384.96 lakh, with recovery capped at 50% of monthly bills (order dated 4 September 2025). PSPCL challenged the 20 August 2025 order before APTEL, and the company filed a writ petition before the Punjab & Haryana High Court, which stayed the PSERC orders via interim order dated 23 September 2025. No adjustments have been made in the financial results for excess revenue billed.

Malwa Power PPA expiry and tariff uncertainty. Malwa Power Private Limited’s 20-year PPA with PSPCL expired on 27 April 2025. PSPCL offered ₹3.50 per kWh against the existing ₹8.59 per kWh. PSERC’s final order dated 27 March 2026 determined the extended-period tariff at ₹5.224 per kWh with 5% annual escalation on the variable component, subject to the outcome of PSPCL’s appeal before APTEL. The auditor noted that, in the absence of sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the carrying value of Malwa Power’s assets (₹5,082.67 lakhs as at 30 June 2026), it was unable to determine whether any impairment is required. Management stated it is unable to assess the consequential impact of these uncertainties pending the APTEL outcome and evaluation of strategic alternatives.

Non-core business losses. The power division reported a segment loss before interest and tax of ₹68.87 lakhs in Q1 FY27 (standalone) and ₹120.86 lakhs (consolidated). The biomass pellet facility became operational midway through Q1 FY27, only partially offsetting non-core business losses.

Labour Codes impact. The Government of India notified the Labour Codes on 21 November 2025. The company recognized a past service cost charge of ₹227.41 lakhs under exceptional items in the quarter ended 31 March 2026.

Other risks cited. Potential risks include the occurrence of an event of default regarding credit facilities, inadequacy or loss of profits affecting executive remuneration minimum limits, technical glitches during remote e-voting or virtual meeting participation, and general risks involving growth, competition, acquisitions, domestic and international economic conditions, changes in government regulations, laws, judicial pronouncements, tax regimes, and the ability to attract and retain high-quality human resources.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong Q1 FY27 performance. Revenue from operations stood at ₹294.5 crore, up 31.6% YoY, driven by healthy execution momentum in the Piping & Fittings segment and strong supplies to the Power sector. Operating EBITDA rose to ₹49.7 crore (up 38.7% YoY) with a margin of 16.9%. PAT grew to ₹16.1 crore (up 22.4% YoY). Diluted EPS was ₹2.32 versus ₹1.90.

Order book. Closing order book stood at ₹2,428 crore as on 30 June 2026, a growth of 92.5% YoY, with an additional L1 position of ₹12 crore. Monthly execution data for 1–31 August 2026 showed an opening order book of ₹2,428.20 crore, order inflows of ₹95.59 crore, execution of ₹70.26 crore, and a closing order book of ₹2,454.12 crore. Cumulative order inflows for FY 2026-27 were ₹972.62 crore.

New contract win. On 20 August 2026, the company announced securing contracts aggregating ₹36 crore (including GST) on a job work basis from Reliance Industries Ltd. for pipe shop fabrication work, to be completed within 9 months. The order is domestic and not a related party transaction.

Backward integration and capacity expansion. The seamless pipe manufacturing facility, commissioned in March 2026, is progressing through its ramp-up phase and is expected to contribute meaningfully as utilization improves. The Anjar pipe fabrication facility added 30,000 MT of fabrication capacity during FY26 and continues to scale up.

Balance sheet strengthening. The company completed a ₹300 crore preferential issue during Q1 FY27, allotting 59,76,096 equity shares at ₹502 per share on 8 July 2026. Proceeds earmarked towards debt repayment are expected to materially reduce leverage and finance costs. Approximately ₹225 crore of debt reduction was cited via preferential issue proceeds.

Non-core business improvement. Following the tariff revision for the 6 MW Muktsar biomass power plant, the applicable tariff increased from ₹3.50 per kWh to ₹5.224 per kWh in FY26, with annual 5% escalation on the variable component. The 72,000 MTPA biomass pellet facility, with estimated revenue generation of ₹80 crore in FY27, commenced commercial operations approximately halfway through Q1 FY27, with full benefit expected from Q2 FY27.

Sector outlook. Management cited India’s ongoing capital expenditure cycle, sustained investments across power, oil & gas, chemical, and process industries, a global capex upcycle, expansion in power and nuclear sectors, hydrogen ecosystem optionality, modular skids and ancillary revenues, and emerging high-growth segments like data centers and semiconductors.

Authorised share capital increase. The Board approved reclassification of existing authorised share capital and an increase from ₹85 crore (8,50,00,000 equity shares) to ₹95 crore (9,50,00,000 equity shares), subject to member approval, to meet future fund-raising requirements and growth plans.

3. Key Risks

  • Tariff and legal risk: Ongoing APTEL appeal and High Court proceedings regarding power division tariffs, with no adjustments made in financial results for excess revenue billed; adverse outcomes could affect profitability and cash flows.
  • Impairment uncertainty: The auditor was unable to determine whether impairment is required for Malwa Power’s assets (₹5,082.67 lakhs) due to uncertainty over future cash flows.
  • Event of default risk: The company has availed a ₹2,000 crore loan facility from Bank of India (Lead Bank) and a consortium of lenders, under which lenders have an option, in the event of default, to convert the whole or part of the outstanding loan into equity shares. The Board approved seeking shareholder approval under Section 62(3) as an enabling provision in line with sanction letters governing existing working capital facilities approved in May 2025; the proposal does not relate to any new borrowing.
  • Execution and geopolitical risk: Temporary disruptions in the Middle East and customer-related issues deferred approximately ₹25 crore of revenue recognition in Q1 FY27.
  • Regulatory and macroeconomic risk: Changes in government regulations, laws, judicial pronouncements, tax regimes, and domestic/international economic conditions affecting demand, supply, and prices.
  • Human capital risk: Ability to attract and retain high-quality human resources.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance provided: - Unlocking ₹2,500 crore revenue with 20% margins by FY30. - Revenue CAGR from FY26–30E of ~22%. - Operating EBITDA margin guidance of 19–20% by FY30. - Operating EBITDA CAGR from FY26–30 of ~26%. - PAT drivers at 9–10% of revenue in FY30. - PAT CAGR from FY26–30 of ~30%. - FY27 revenue from power and biomass pellets estimated at ~₹80 crore. - Projected revenue for FY27 of approximately ₹47.71 crore (including ₹23.4 crore from the pellet plant) for Malwa Power. - Biomass pellet facility estimated revenue generation of ₹80 crore in FY27. - Full benefit from the pellet facility expected from Q2 FY27. - Deferred revenue of ~₹25 crore expected to be recognized in the coming quarter.

Observed performance: - Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹294.5 crore (up 31.6% YoY) and operating EBITDA margin of 16.9% — below the FY30 margin guidance of 19–20%, but management stated the seamless pipe facility is still in ramp-up and expected to contribute meaningfully as utilization improves. - Closing order book of ₹2,428 crore as on 30 June 2026 (up 92.5% YoY), with an additional L1 position of ₹12 crore, providing revenue visibility. - The biomass pellet facility commenced commercial operations midway through Q1 FY27, resulting in only partial financial contribution in Q1, consistent with guidance that full benefit is expected from Q2 FY27. - The power division continued to report segment losses in Q1 FY27, though the pellet facility partially offset non-core business losses. - The ₹300 crore preferential issue was successfully completed, with allotment on 8 July 2026, and proceeds earmarked for debt repayment are expected to reduce leverage and finance costs. - The company maintained a final dividend recommendation of Re. 1/- per equity share for FY 2025-26, and the 37th AGM is scheduled for 23 September 2026.
