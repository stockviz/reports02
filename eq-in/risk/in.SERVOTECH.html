Servotech Renewable Power System Limited — Q1 FY27 Announcements Review

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • EV charger demand described as stagnant. In response to a question on DC and AC chargers, management stated there is “not much growth in that right now” and that the market is “a little bit stagnant,” with growth expected only in the future.
  • Capacity constraints in battery packs. Management stated the installed battery pack production capacity “has been fulfilled” and the company is “not able to meet the demand,” which is driving the next phase of capacity expansion.
  • Order book visibility. Management explained that confirmed government procurement orders are getting “smaller and smaller,” and that in the channel-partner-led model “you do not have such a sound order book on a paper.” Execution is instead driven by production pipelines and sales team efforts.
  • Subsidiary drag. Servotech Sports & Entertainment Pvt. Ltd. was confirmed to be not profitable in the quarter. Management attributed this to ongoing business building in event management, cricket team franchise ownership, and ownership of the Dream League of India, with profitability expected only in the long term.
  • Seasonality. Management reiterated that H2 is always better than H1, based on the record of the last five to ten years.
  • Share price volatility. An investor noted significant share price fluctuation over the last three quarters despite strong results. Management declined to comment on market sentiment, stating it has no control over or focus on the stock market.
  • Potential absence or inadequacy of profits. The AGM material identifies the potential absence or inadequacy of profits in financial years as a material headwind or risk.
  • Rising scale and complexity. The AGM material also flags the increased scale and complexity of operations as a challenge.
  • Capital intensity in BESS. Management noted that capital consumption in battery energy storage is “very high,” though it framed this alongside good returns and contained investment levels.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong Q1 FY27 performance. Standalone total revenue grew 66.31% to ₹208.1 crore from ₹125.14 crore in Q1 FY26; EBITDA grew 62.85% to ₹47 crore (from ₹23.18 crore); PAT stood at ₹11.10 crore. Consolidated revenue grew 57.69% to ₹216.29 crore from ₹137.17 crore; EBITDA grew 93.35% to ₹20.94 crore; PAT grew 74.51% to ₹7.95 crore.
  • Order wins during the quarter. A major rooftop solar order from South Central Railway; a 900 kW BESS order from Uttar Pradesh (UPSRLM, Rural Development Department, for a 900 kW solar rooftop project across 12 locations, to be executed within six months, announced July 16, 2026); and a BEE 5-star rating for 60 kW and 120 kW DC chargers, with 240 kW in process.
  • Haryana manufacturing expansion. An MoU with the Government of Haryana, described in the presentation as a ₹400 crore MoU to expand manufacturing capacity in the state. Management said a new plant is coming soon and building/structure work has started.
  • BESS capacity roadmap. Management stated battery capacity will double within 6 months, triple by March 21, 2027, and reach 10X in the next 2 years. Management clarified this refers to production capacity, not capital outlay.
  • Channel distribution expansion. 800+ distributors across metros and cities, 6,000+ retailers covering more than 370 towns across India, and partner retention above 70%. Management said the company is penetrating South India, North India, and North East India.
  • Government scheme tailwinds. Management said channel distribution is being expanded specifically to cater to PM Surya Ghar Yojana demand. On PM E-drive, Servotech expects to supply chargers and to expand the Servotech EV Infra charging network across states.
  • EV truck and e-rickshaw chargers. Management stated chargers for EV trucks are already being made and installed, and that products for the e-rickshaw segment are being sold.
  • Related party opportunity. The AGM material cites material related party transactions with Rhine Solar Limited (up to INR 500.00 crore for FY 2026-27) as expected to contribute positively to revenues and profitability, ensure supply chain continuity, optimise procurement costs, improve operational efficiencies, strengthen business synergies, and maximise utilisation of manufacturing capacities.
  • Strategic initiatives. The AGM material cites continued business growth, increased scale, strategic initiatives, and exploration of new technologies as tailwinds.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution timing risk on new orders. Management stated that execution of any new order does not happen in a month or two, and that revenue in the quarter came from previous orders; new order contribution will only be visible in coming quarters.
  • Capacity-demand mismatch. Management stated the company is “not able to make as much material as there are orders,” which is the stated reason for capacity expansion.
  • Competition in EV charging. An investor asked whether competition is growing amid large e-bus rollouts and multiple key players. Management responded that Servotech is on the list of major players and will contribute, without quantifying competitive pressure.
  • Debt trajectory. Management stated debt can increase as the company grows, with an intention to maintain the debt-equity ratio in range and to avoid equity issuance given market conditions.
  • Disclosure limitations. Management declined to disclose current production capacity, stating that giving such an idea in the public domain is “very dangerous” and that it will only share what it can state precisely. Management also declined to comment on the share price.
  • Subsidiary profitability risk. Servotech Sports & Entertainment is not profitable in the quarter, with profitability dependent on long-term business building.
  • Forward-looking statement risk. The press release disclaimer notes uncertainties arising from the behaviour of the renewable energy, electronics, and EV infrastructure industries; from integration and performance of subsidiaries and joint ventures; and from political, economic, business, competitive, or regulatory matters, which may cause actual results to differ materially.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • FY27 outlook. Asked for FY27 revenue and margin guidance, management said the effort is to “continue at this level” and that Q1 is self-explanatory, without providing a numeric range. Management attributed momentum to distribution channel expansion, new product lines, manufacturing, and prior-year capital investment in machinery and plant.
  • Margins. Management stated margin is “not very important” and is a by-product of doing the right work and delivering what the customer needs, pointing to the last 5-6 years of financials as evidence. No specific margin guidance was given.
  • Order book. Management did not provide a numeric order book, explaining that confirmed government order sizes are shrinking and the channel-partner model does not produce a large paper order book. Management said the company will reach its target for the year based on its production and pipeline setup.
  • Seasonality guidance. Management guided that H2 is always better than H1, consistent with the last 5-10 year record.
  • Capacity guidance. Management guided to doubling battery capacity in 6 months, tripling by March 21, 2027, and 10X in 2 years, clarifying this is production capacity, not capital.
  • BESS revenue share. Management declined to state the expected percentage of revenue from BESS, saying only that performance will be “much better than before.”
  • Debt guidance. Management guided that debt may increase but will remain in range, with efforts to grow profits and the network to keep the debt-equity ratio balanced.
  • Long-term trajectory. Asked where the company will be in 2-3 years, management referenced the transformation over the past 5 years and said it thinks the same way for the next 2-3 years. Management corrected an investor’s characterization, stating the company was not even at ₹300 crore five years ago.
  • AGM guidance. The AGM material states the company expects improved sales and profitability figures in the next financial years, and that the Rhine Solar transactions are expected to ensure supply chain continuity, optimise procurement costs, improve operational efficiencies, strengthen business synergies, and maximise utilisation of manufacturing capacities.
  • Observed versus guided. The Q1 FY27 results show standalone revenue growth of 66.31% and consolidated revenue growth of 57.69%, with management describing the quarter as a strong beginning and the first quarter as self-explanatory relative to its stated intent to sustain momentum. Management’s statement that current-quarter revenue reflects execution of previous orders, not new orders, is consistent with its guidance that new order contribution will appear in coming quarters.
