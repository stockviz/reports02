Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Growing demand for naturally derived and sustainable pine-based chemicals across pharmaceutical, personal care, home care, fragrance, adhesive and specialty chemical industries.
  • Increasing consumer preference for bio-based and environmentally responsible products.
  • Rising demand for high-performance resins and terpene derivatives.
  • Expansion of the consumer products business through Mangalam Brands Private Limited.
  • Increasing export opportunities driven by global supply chain diversification.
  • During FY 2025-26, consolidated Net Sales crossed Rs. 623 crore, and Profit After Tax stood at Rs. 25.38 crore.
  • Camphor production capacity increased up to 12,000 MT per annum.
  • The company operates in a single reportable segment of chemicals, with 96% revenue from B2B terpene operations and 4% from synthetic resin operations.
  • Consolidated revenue from operations for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was Rs. 17,909.24 lakh, higher than Rs. 14,655.39 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025 and Rs. 15,361.80 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026.
