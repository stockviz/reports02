NELCAST — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Q1FY27 profitability pressure. Nelcast’s Q1FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026) results, approved by the Board on 27 July 2026, show revenue growth but a sharp profitability decline. Total revenue rose 2.8% year-on-year to ₹345.4 crore (from ₹336.0 crore in Q1FY26), but EBITDA fell 37.9% to ₹20.1 crore, with EBITDA margin down to 5.8% from 9.6% (a decline of 381 bps). EBITDA/kg fell 35.5% to ₹9.5 from ₹14.7. PAT fell 58.9% to ₹5.1 crore, with PAT margin at 1.5% versus 3.7%.

Causes cited. The company attributes the profitability impact to elevated raw material costs arising from macroeconomic and geopolitical developments, and to labour availability constraints across the industry. The investor presentation describes the impact as temporary, with underlying business momentum remaining encouraging.

Export softness in the prior year. The investor presentation notes that in FY26, exports were impacted by a slowdown in the U.S. economy amid geopolitical headwinds. Around 30–35% of overall revenues is generated from export markets.

Industry-level operational challenges. M&HCV demand is described as robust despite industry-wide operational challenges.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Demand environment. Q1FY27 revenue growth was supported by healthy demand across key end markets. The tractor segment’s contribution increased to 27.1% of revenues from 23.5% in Q1FY26, supported by healthy rural sentiment and stable customer schedules. M&HCV remained the largest revenue contributor, supported by freight movement and infrastructure activity.

Exports recovering. Export revenues improved sequentially to ₹113.3 crore, growing 8.2% quarter-on-quarter, with customer activity strengthening across key export markets. Export recovery is expected to continue, supported by normalization of customer schedules, favourable demand trends and emission-related demand tailwinds.

New products. Multiple new products are entering production from Q2FY27, with initial contribution beginning in Q2 and a gradual ramp-up expected through the year. New product programs are expected to ramp up progressively through Q2 and Q3, with meaningful contribution to volumes and revenues by H2FY27.

Margin improvement levers. EBITDA/kg is expected to improve with higher utilisation, product mix enrichment, new product ramp-up and realization of customer price revisions. The company has initiated price revision discussions with customers, with benefits expected to be realized gradually over the coming quarters.

Customer expansion. The company reports steady progress in expanding customer relationships across Europe and has secured new business opportunities that strengthen its long-term growth pipeline.

Industry outlook. The tractor industry outlook remains positive, supported by rural demand, favourable monsoon conditions and policy support; tractor sales are projected at ~12 lakh units in FY27 (per Crisil ratings), after FY26 recorded highest-ever annual sales of 11.6 lakh units (up 23.5% YoY). M&HCV industry volume is entering a period of modest growth of 5–7% in FY27, with replacement demand expected to be driven by higher freight rates, better affordability from GST rationalisation, and an ageing truck fleet of around 10 years.

Capacity headroom. Installed capacity stands at 160,000 metric tons per year, and can be further enhanced by 50,000 MT within existing plants with minimal investment.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material and macro/geopolitical exposure: Elevated input costs arising from macroeconomic and geopolitical headwinds weighed on Q1FY27 profitability; the company notes these as temporary factors.
  • Labour availability: Constraints across the industry affected the quarter’s performance.
  • Export market dependence: Exports constitute around 30–35% of revenues, and FY26 exports were impacted by a U.S. economic slowdown amid geopolitical headwinds.
  • Customer concentration/positioning: The company notes that ~80% of export products and 50–60% of domestic products are single-sourced, alongside a customer base of more than 40 OEM and Tier-1 customers.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The press release’s safe harbor notes that several risks, uncertainties and unknown factors — including general market, macro-economic, governmental and regulatory trends, currency and interest rate movements, competitive pressures, technological developments, changes in financial conditions of third parties, and legislative developments — could cause actual results to differ materially from expectations.
  • Subsidiary not yet operating: The wholly-owned subsidiary NC Energy Limited has not commenced commercial operations; its interim results reflect nil revenue, nil profit after tax and nil comprehensive income for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and are not material to the Group.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Q1FY27 outturn versus stated outlook. Management described Q1FY27 as a steady performance with revenue growth supported by healthy demand, while profitability was temporarily impacted by elevated raw material costs and labour availability challenges. The FY27 outlook anticipates that new product launches, improving capacity utilisation and cost pass-through initiatives will support stronger operational performance over the coming quarters.

EBITDA/kg. Q1FY27 EBITDA/kg stood at ₹9.5, impacted by elevated raw material costs and other macro headwinds. Guidance states EBITDA/kg is expected to improve with higher utilisation, product mix enrichment, new product ramp-up and realization of customer price revisions.

New product timing. Guidance indicates new products enter production from Q2FY27, with initial contribution in Q2, gradual ramp-up through the year, and meaningful contribution to volumes and revenues by H2FY27.

Exports. Q1FY27 exports of ₹113.3 crore grew 8.2% sequentially; guidance expects export recovery to continue, supported by normalization of customer schedules, favourable demand trends and emission-related demand tailwinds.

Segment outlook versus Q1FY27 performance. Tractor segment contribution rose to 27.1% of revenues in Q1FY27 from 23.5% in Q1FY26, against a positive FY27 tractor industry outlook. M&HCV remained the largest revenue contributor amid robust demand, against an expected 5–7% industry volume growth in FY27.

Managing Director’s framing. Mr. Deepak Reddy Ponnavolu described Q1FY27 as steady, noted the temporary nature of profitability pressures, cited initiated price revision discussions with expected gradual realization, highlighted commencement of new product production in Q2FY27, expected capacity utilisation improvement, and progress in expanding European customer relationships. He characterized the company as being at an important inflection point, with foundations being laid for the next phase of growth.

Annual context. FY26 (year ended 31 March 2026) consolidated figures show revenue from operations of ₹1,328.4 crore, EBITDA of ₹124.5 crore, and PAT of ₹48.4 crore, compared with FY25 revenue of ₹1,251.7 crore, EBITDA of ₹105.6 crore, and PAT of ₹37.3 crore.
