Headwinds and Challenges

  • Going-concern pressure: For the financial year ended March 31, 2026, the company reported standalone revenue from operations of Rs. 534.53 lakhs and a net loss after tax of Rs. 482.35 lakhs. The auditors issued a qualified opinion flagging material uncertainty over the company’s ability to continue as a going concern, citing eroded net worth and current liabilities exceeding current assets.
  • Structural content and market pressures: The company faces content exploitation and IP retention limitations, audience fragmentation and changing viewership algorithms, digital piracy and illicit AI replication, macroeconomic pressures on advertising and streaming subscriptions, and an evolving regulatory and legal compliance environment.
  • Regulatory non-compliance: A delay in compliance with Regulation 6(1) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 drew observations from the NSE and a notice for non-compliance with a total fine payable of Rs. 35,400 (inclusive of GST).
  • Tax demand: The company received an Income Tax/TDS demand notice u/s 156 based on orders u/s 201(1)/201(1A) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for FY 2023-24 amounting to Rs. 1,67,81,895/- inclusive of interest, of which Rs. 66,43,825/- is under dispute; the balance is undisputed and duly accounted for in books.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • CENVAT credit relief: The entire demand of CENVAT credit of Rs. 5,32,43,696/-, along with applicable interest and penalties, stands completely set aside with consequential relief, confirming the company’s entitlement to CENVAT credit on telecast fees. CESTAT, Chennai allowed the company’s appeals and set aside the Order-in-Original passed by the Commissioner of Service Tax-II, Chennai, upholding telecast fees as eligible input services under Rule 2(l) of the CENVAT Credit Rules, 2004.
  • Growth avenues: Expansion into FAST channels and global licensing, leveraging generative AI for cost efficiency, hyper-localized and regional market expansion, short-form and micro-drama monetization, and direct-to-consumer and brand integration.
  • Governance and leadership actions: The Board on August 28, 2026 approved reconstitution of the Nomination & Remuneration Committee, the appointment of Mr. Rahhul Sarath as Additional Director (Executive) effective September 1, 2026, the appointment of Mrs. M. Uma as Additional Director (Independent) effective September 1, 2026, and the updated draft Annual Report for FY 2026-27. Full compliance regarding the Company Secretary was achieved on June 13, 2026.

Key Risks

  • Material uncertainty relating to going-concern status due to eroded net worth and current liabilities exceeding current assets.
  • Content exploitation and IP retention limitations, alongside digital piracy and illicit AI replication.
  • Audience fragmentation and changing viewership algorithms affecting reach and monetization.
  • Macroeconomic pressures on advertising and streaming subscriptions.
  • Evolving regulatory and legal compliance environment, evidenced by the NSE non-compliance notice and fine of Rs. 35,400 (inclusive of GST).
  • Tax exposure: the disputed portion of Rs. 66,43,825/- within the Rs. 1,67,81,895/- TDS demand for FY 2023-24.

  • Management expresses confidence in driving a business turnaround, resolving current volume and liquidity constraints, and clearing all statutory liabilities in full compliance with regulations.
