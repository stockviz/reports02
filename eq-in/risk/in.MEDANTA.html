Headwinds and Challenges

  • Noida ramp-up drag: Noida generated total income of INR 855 million in Q1 FY27 but an EBITDA loss of INR 49 million. This was an improvement from an EBITDA loss of INR 236 million in Q4 FY26, but the unit remains loss-making at the EBITDA level.
  • Margin compression: Reported EBITDA margin declined to 23.8% in Q1 FY27 from 24.3% in Q1 FY26 (a 51 bps decline). EBITDA ex-Noida margin was 25.8% versus 24.6% a year earlier, but the consolidated margin was pulled down by Noida.
  • PAT decline: Profit after tax fell 1.1% year-on-year to INR 1,573 million, with PAT margin at 11.9% versus 15.1% in Q1 FY26. Q1 FY26 had a non-recurring exceptional income of INR 196 million from reversal of potential interest liability on EPCG following transfer of the EPCG license from MHPL to GHL.
  • Rising costs: Employee benefits expense rose 33.3% year-on-year to INR 5,136 million, outpacing total income growth of 26.2%. Finance costs rose 93.5% year-on-year to INR 267 million, and depreciation and amortisation rose 52.9% to INR 690 million.
  • Occupancy dip: Occupancy was 62.6% in Q1 FY27 versus 63.2% in Q1 FY26 (a 53 bps decline), reflecting the addition of new bed capacity. Excluding Noida, occupancy stood at 66%.
  • Regulatory and approval dependencies: The Mumbai project awaits MHADA and Environmental Clearance. The Pitampura project’s building plan is under preparation. The Varanasi project’s building plans are under preparation. The Indore cancer unit is to be operationalised in Q2/Q3 FY27.
  • Labour Codes: The Government of India notified four Labour Codes on 21 November 2025, consolidating 29 existing labour laws. The company continues to monitor finalisation of State Rules and further government clarifications.
  • KYC/compliance requirements: SEBI mandates KYC and nomination updates for physical securities holders; effective April 1, 2024, RTA will attend to service requests only after KYC updation. Dividend payments to physical shareholders without updated KYC will be made only in electronic mode.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong revenue growth: Highest ever quarterly total income of INR 13,262 million in Q1 FY27, up 26.2% year-on-year. Revenue from operations rose 26.5% to INR 13,041 million.
  • EBITDA growth: EBITDA ex-Noida grew 23.9% year-on-year to INR 3,201 million with margins of 25.8%. Reported EBITDA including Noida was INR 3,153 million, up 23.5% year-on-year.
  • Volume growth: In-patient count rose 27.7% year-on-year to 60,214; out-patient count rose 34.5% to 1,100,928. Occupied bed days increased 21.1% year-on-year.
  • ARPOB improvement: ARPOB stood at INR 70,224, up 5.5% year-on-year, driven by improvement in ALOS (from 3.03 days in Q1 FY26 to 2.87 days in Q1 FY27) and change in case mix.
  • International patients: International patients revenue increased 23.0% year-on-year to INR 782 million.
  • OPD Pharmacy growth: OPD Pharmacy (Hospital & Retail) revenue increased 50.9% year-on-year to INR 609 million.
  • Capacity expansion: 72 new beds added in Q1 FY27 (51 in Noida, 21 in Lucknow). Total installed beds at 3,737 as of June 30, 2026. Noida reached 433 operational beds with 550 planned. Lucknow reached 778 beds (950 planned). Patna has 601 beds (650 planned).
  • Noida progress: LINAC and Brachytherapy operationalised; all major insurance and PSU empanelment completed; 5,000+ procedures performed in Q1 FY27; 250+ experienced doctors, 80+ senior clinicians, 720+ nurses and paramedics.
  • Upcoming projects: Five upcoming hospitals in Mumbai, Delhi (Pitampura, South Delhi), Guwahati and Varanasi. Guwahati scaled up from 400 to 650 beds at total capex of INR 9,700 million. South Delhi: 400 beds in partnership with DLF. Pitampura: ~750-bed hospital under O&M agreement. Varanasi: 400-bed project under built-to-suit and lease arrangement. Indore Cancer Unit: 80-bed cancer hospital under business transfer agreement, announced April 2026, to be operationalised in Q2/Q3 FY27.
  • Capex plan: Total capex of ~INR 48,500 million planned for next 5 years, to be funded by a combination of debt funding and internal accruals. INR 1,610 million of capex incurred in Q1 FY2027. Maintenance capex estimated at ~INR 6,000 million over next three years.
  • Clinical achievements: VV-ECMO-supported therapeutic bronchoscopy at Gurugram; robotic-assisted paraesophageal hernia repair at Noida; Deep Brain Stimulation for DYT1 dystonia at Gurugram; first private healthcare institution in Uttar Pradesh to perform Whole Lung Lavage at Lucknow; 1,000+ joint replacement surgeries using robotic technology at Lucknow within one year; first Transarterial Chemoembolization at Patna; CRRT with bilirubin adsorption system at Patna.
  • Talent addition: 70+ doctors on-boarded in Q1 FY27, including 50+ senior clinicians across Cardiac, Cancer, Neurosciences, Urology and Orthopaedics.
  • Continuity of care: 825+ active touch points and 325+ collection centers across 100+ cities supported by 12 labs and 4 HLMs/RLMs. 1.56 lakh patient bookings, a growth of 21% year-on-year. 22 operational pharmacies (7 hospital, 2 mediclinics, 13 retail).
  • Dividend: Board recommended final dividend of INR 0.50 per equity share for FY 2025-26, subject to shareholder approval at the 22nd AGM on September 16, 2026. Record date is August 14, 2026.

Key Risks

  • Execution risk on expansion: Multiple greenfield and brownfield projects (Mumbai, Pitampura, South Delhi, Guwahati, Varanasi, Indore cancer unit) are at various stages of approval and construction. Delays in regulatory approvals (MHADA, Environmental Clearance) or construction could affect timelines.
  • Noida profitability timeline: While Noida EBITDA losses narrowed significantly (from INR 236 million in Q4 FY26 to INR 49 million in Q1 FY27), the unit has not yet turned EBITDA-positive.
  • Cost inflation: Employee benefits expense growing at 33.3% year-on-year, faster than revenue growth of 26.2%, could pressure margins if the trend continues.
  • Finance cost escalation: Finance costs rose 93.5% year-on-year, and with planned debt funding for the ~INR 48,500 million capex programme, finance costs could continue to rise.
  • Regulatory changes: The Labour Codes notified on 21 November 2025 could have financial implications; the company continues to monitor finalisation of State Rules. The company had presented an incremental impact of INR 352.00 million (standalone) / INR 365.98 million (consolidated) under exceptional items for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Dependence on clinical talent: The model relies on attracting and retaining high-quality doctors and clinicians. 70+ doctors were on-boarded in Q1 FY27, but competition for talent is a risk.
  • Concentration in North and East India: The network is concentrated in North and East India, though expansion into Mumbai and Guwahati is planned.
  • Dividend withholding tax compliance: Non-resident shareholders must provide specific documentation (TRC, Form 41, self-declaration) by August 30, 2026 to claim treaty benefits; otherwise tax may be deducted at 20% plus surcharge and cess.
  • KYC non-compliance: Physical shareholders who have not updated KYC details may face dividend payment issues and cannot lodge grievances or avail service requests from RTA.

  • Capex plan: Management stated total capex of ~INR 48,500 million planned for next 5 years, funded by a combination of debt funding and internal accruals. Observed: INR 1,610 million of capex incurred in Q1 FY2027.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative evolved from early concerns over LINAC installation disruptions and delayed new centre turnarounds to later optimism around greenfield ramp-ups and operating leverage, though new unit start-up losses persisted as a recurring headwind. While early tailwinds centered on occupancy peaks and margin restoration by Q4FY24, later optimism pivoted to volume-led growth and capacity additions at proven units. The persistent theme of new centre operational drag shifted from oncology competition to specific geographic issues like Lucknow and Noida.

  • The contraction of gross and EBITDA margins to 16.1% due to higher overheads and clinical talent onboarding materialized, correlating with the DISAPPOINTMENT outcome of -11.4% on 2024-05-21 as profitability remained depressed.

Optimism that failed

  • The optimism around EBITDA margin restoration by Q4FY24 failed to materialize, correlating with the DISAPPOINTMENT outcome of -11.4% on 2024-05-21 when margins remained depressed.
  • The expectation of EBITDA margin expansion to ~18.4% by FY25E failed to fully materialize, correlating with the FLAT outcome of -0.2% on 2026-08-10 as start-up losses at new units like Noida continued to drag overall profitability.
