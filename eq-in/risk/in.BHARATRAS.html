BHARATRAS: Q1 FY2026-27 Board Meeting and Financial Results Summary

Headwinds and Challenges

  • Revenue declined year-on-year. Standalone revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹33,816 lakh, compared with ₹37,740 lakh in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Consolidated revenue was also ₹33,816 lakh for the quarter, against ₹37,740 lakh a year earlier.
  • Profitability weakened. Standalone profit before exceptional items and tax fell to ₹4,831 lakh from ₹7,270 lakh in the prior-year quarter. Standalone profit for the period was ₹3,664 lakh, down from ₹4,366 lakh. Consolidated profit for the period was ₹3,723 lakh, compared with ₹3,966 lakh a year earlier.
  • Margins compressed. Standalone operating margin declined to 14.73% from 19.76% in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Net profit margin fell to 10.94% from 11.68%. Consolidated operating margin was 14.91% versus 18.69%, and net profit margin was 11.12% versus 10.61%.
  • Inventory turnover slowed. Standalone inventory turnover ratio (annualised) was 3.67 times, compared with 5.13 times in the prior-year quarter. Consolidated inventory turnover was also 3.67 times versus 5.13 times.
  • Environmental damage compensation matter concluded. Following the fire incident at the Dahej Unit-II on 17 May 2022, the National Green Tribunal (NGT), Pune, passed an order dated 5 April 2024 (pronounced 29 May 2024). The Company deposited ₹11.50 crore towards Environmental Damage Compensation with GPCB under protest. The Supreme Court disposed of the Company’s appeal and upheld the NGT order vide its order dated 7 August 2026, concluding the matter at the appellate level.
  • Insurance claims still pending. The insurance claim for material damage to Block-D of the Dahej Plant remains under process, with the final claim bill submitted to the Insurance Company/Surveyor. The Loss of Profit (FLOP) insurance claim is also under process and will be recognised on receipt basis.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Proposed restructuring. The Board considered a proposal for restructuring involving the Company, BRL Finlease Limited, Centum Finance Limited and their wholly owned subsidiaries. The restructuring is aimed at rationalising the existing corporate structure, enabling focused management of distinct business verticals, improving operational and capital efficiencies and facilitating potential value creation for the Company and its stakeholders. The Board in-principally agreed to the proposal and authorised the management to undertake further evaluation, appoint advisors and take the proposal forward. Detailed terms and modalities are under consideration and will be finalised after requisite evaluation and discussions with stakeholders and advisors.
  • Credit ratings maintained. The Company continues to maintain CARE AA- (AA Minus) for Long Term Facilities and CARE A1+ (A One Plus) for Short Term Facilities.
  • Joint venture performance. Nissan Bharat Rasayan Private Limited, the Company’s joint venture accounted for using the equity method under Ind AS, reported total revenue of ₹58.49 crore and profit after tax of ₹1.97 crore for the period ended 30 June 2026. The profit after tax includes a foreign exchange gain of ₹6.64 crore. The Company’s share of the joint venture’s net profit, amounting to ₹0.59 crore, has been recognised in the consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.
  • Strong balance sheet indicators. Standalone net worth stood at ₹1,28,507.88 lakh as at 30 June 2026, up from ₹1,15,031.01 lakh as at 30 June 2025. Consolidated net worth was ₹1,31,169.36 lakh, compared with ₹1,16,968.15 lakh a year earlier. Standalone debt-equity ratio was 0.06 times, and consolidated debt-equity ratio was also 0.06 times.
  • No defaults. There is no default on outstanding loans and debt securities as on 30 June 2026.
  • Dividend proposal. The Board fixed Thursday, 17 September 2026 as the Record Date for determining entitlement of shareholders to payment of Final Dividend @10% or ₹0.50 per equity share (if declared by members in the forthcoming AGM) for the financial year ended 31 March 2026.
  • Annual General Meeting and e-voting. The 37th AGM is scheduled for Thursday, 24 September 2026. The Register of Members and Transfer Books will be closed from Friday, 18 September 2026 to Thursday, 24 September 2026 (both days inclusive). The Cut-Off date for determining eligible shareholders to cast votes electronically is Thursday, 17 September 2026, with e-voting from Monday, 21 September 2026 (9:30 A.M. IST) to Wednesday, 23 September 2026 (5:00 P.M. IST).

Key Risks

  • Pending insurance claims. The material damage and Loss of Profit insurance claims relating to the 17 May 2022 fire incident remain under process, with recognition of the FLOP claim dependent on receipt.
  • Regulatory and environmental compliance. The NGT order and Supreme Court judgment concluding the environmental damage compensation matter require continued strengthening of environmental, safety and process-control systems.
  • Restructuring execution. The proposed restructuring is at an in-principle stage; detailed terms and modalities are yet to be finalised, and the detailed proposal, including any Scheme of Arrangement, will be placed before the Board for consideration.
  • Margin and turnover pressure. Operating margins and inventory turnover have declined year-on-year, indicating pressure on operational efficiency.
  • Dependence on joint venture performance. Consolidated results include the joint venture’s performance, which in the current period was supported by a foreign exchange gain of ₹6.64 crore within its profit after tax.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Restructuring guidance. The Board has in-principally agreed to the restructuring proposal and authorised management to undertake further evaluation and appoint advisors. The detailed terms and modalities are presently under consideration and shall be finalised after requisite evaluation and discussions with relevant stakeholders and advisors. The detailed proposal, including the Scheme of Arrangement, if any, shall thereafter be placed before the Board. No financial figures or timelines for the restructuring have been provided.
  • Insurance claims guidance. The Company states the material damage claim is under process with the final claim bill submitted, and the FLOP claim will be recognised on receipt basis. No amount or timeline has been indicated.
  • Dividend guidance. The final dividend of ₹0.50 per equity share (10%) is subject to declaration by members at the forthcoming AGM. The Record Date is 17 September 2026.
  • Observed performance versus prior periods. Revenue, profit before tax, profit for the period, operating margin and inventory turnover all declined in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 compared with the quarter ended 30 June 2025, while net worth and debt-equity ratios remained stable or improved. The Company has not provided forward financial guidance in the supplied material.
  • Share capital changes. During the quarter ended 31 December 2025, shareholders approved sub-division of face value from ₹10 to ₹5 per equity share and bonus shares in the ratio 1:1, with 83,10,536 bonus shares allotted on 15 December 2025. Paid-up equity share capital increased from ₹4,15,52,680 to ₹8,31,05,360, divided into 1,66,21,072 equity shares of ₹5 each. EPS has been restated for all comparative periods.
