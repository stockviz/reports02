Xelpmoc Design and Tech — Q1 FY27 Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak operating revenue and persistent losses. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs 9.6 million in Q1 FY27, down 10.9% quarter-on-quarter from Rs 10.8 million in Q4 FY26, though up 22.5% year-on-year from Rs 7.8 million in Q1 FY26. Adjusted Operating EBITDA remained negative at Rs (14.9) million, and PAT was Rs (22.0) million, wider than both Q4 FY26 (Rs (17.8) million) and Q1 FY26 (Rs (18.9) million). The company remains loss-making at both the operating and net levels.

Going-concern uncertainties in group entities. The auditors’ review report draws attention to Note 9 of the consolidated results, which states that Soultrax Studios Private Limited has accumulated losses and fully eroded net worth, indicating a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt on its ability to continue as a going concern. Note 10 similarly flags that certain indicators exist which may cast significant doubt on Signal Analytics Private Limited’s ability to continue as a going concern, with management continuously evaluating operational and financial support requirements.

Restricted consolidation and non-recognition of losses. During the quarter, the company did not consolidate its step-down subsidiary and did not account for its share of losses of associates, as investments in these entities were fully impaired in prior periods and no legal or constructive obligation to provide additional financial support exists. The company also discontinued recognition of further losses relating to Xperience India Private Limited under Ind AS 28.

Funding constraints and long enterprise sales cycles. The company cites a cautious start-up funding climate and persistent funding constraints, long sales and adoption cycles for enterprise AI solutions, foreign exchange volatility, and general macroeconomic, geopolitical, and regulatory uncertainties as material headwinds.

Portfolio concentration and valuation dependence. The fair value of investments stood at approximately Rs 726.8 million as of June 30, 2026, against a total cost of investment of Rs 79.8 million. A substantial portion of this fair value is concentrated in Mihup Communication Private Limited (Rs 453.6 million) and Woovly India Private Limited (Rs 179.4 million). Portfolio fair value movements materially affect reported comprehensive income.

Dependence on a small revenue base. Consolidated operating revenue for FY26 was Rs 37.4 million, with 100% coming from the corporate segment. The company maintains a financial runway of approximately 10 to 12 months.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Enterprise AI and document intelligence adoption. DocuXray, the AI-powered document intelligence and compliance automation platform, continued to demonstrate strong technical capability during the quarter, with features spanning structured and unstructured data extraction, workflow automation, and a rules-based intelligent validation engine. Management cites growing enterprise adoption of AI, document intelligence, and compliance automation as a tailwind.

RELY commercial launch in AgeTech. RELY, the flagship Agetech platform for Assisted and Senior Living ecosystems, went live during the quarter with three customers successfully onboarded. The platform targets senior living operations, care, CRM, billing, F&B, and inventory, and is positioned to capitalize on emerging digital opportunities in Agetech.

Portfolio company momentum. Several investee companies reported progress: - Mihup: Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs 88.9 million, with Rs 1,000 million+ contracted value; onboarded HDFC onto its platform; signed a multi-million dollar contract with TML. - Woovly: Current ARR of USD 4.3 million with 245+ brands on platform; operates two verticals including Live2.ai SaaS, with enterprise clients including Shiseido, L’Oréal, Samsonite, Titan Group, Decathlon, Unilever, Schwarzkopf, and Diageo. - Pencil: Signed a USD 950K service deal with Alphabet/Google, with revenue spread across 36 months; Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs 433 lakhs; AI-enabled products scheduled to go live by October 2026. - The Star In Me: Q1 FY27 gross revenues booked of Rs 1.75 crores; new clients include AXA, 7-Eleven, Cognizant, Hexaware, JCPenney, Salesforce, and Virtusa. - Kids Stop Press: Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs 27 lakhs. - Biome: Announced first late co-founding investment in Zoop.Money; Zoop surpassed INR 300 Cr inventory value across 70+ projects and completed a core-banking integration with ICICI Bank; raising INR 8 Cr at ~INR 80 Cr valuation (4.4x uplift). Biome increased investment in OsteoForge to INR 4 Cr for a 10% combined stake, with OsteoForge valuation up 2.5x to INR 100 Cr within five months.

Portfolio fair value appreciation. Total portfolio fair value rose to Rs 726.8 million as of June 30, 2026, from Rs 531.8 million as of June 30, 2025, reflecting embedded value in the start-up investment portfolio.

Product-led recurring revenue. Management cites increasing product-led recurring revenue as an opportunity, alongside expansion into AgeTech with RELY.

3. Key Risks

  • Going-concern risk at Soultrax Studios and Signal Analytics, as flagged in the auditors’ review report.
  • Continued operating losses with Adjusted Operating EBITDA negative in each of the last five quarters (Q1 FY26 through Q1 FY27).
  • Revenue concentration in the corporate segment (100% of FY26 operating revenue).
  • Limited financial runway of approximately 10 to 12 months.
  • Dependence on portfolio fair value for comprehensive income, with concentration in Mihup and Woovly.
  • Enterprise sales cycle risk for AI solutions, which management identifies as long.
  • Foreign exchange volatility and general macroeconomic, geopolitical, and regulatory uncertainties.
  • Start-up funding climate remaining cautious, affecting both investee companies and the company’s equity-linked model.
  • Restricted consolidation of step-down subsidiary and non-recognition of associate losses, which limits visibility into those entities’ performance.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance provided: - Management’s immediate priorities for FY27 are to widen enterprise adoption of DocuXray and RELY, convert the existing pipeline into commercial deployments, maintain a disciplined cost structure, and progress towards EBITDA profitability and operating leverage. - The CEO stated the primary goal is to achieve profitability at the earliest by leveraging proprietary products and services, with focus on revenue generation from the corporate sector. - Pencil’s AI-generated audio (enhancement), AI-led assessment tool, and AI-generated pitch bible are scheduled to go live within 180 days, i.e., by October 2026. - RELY is positioned to capitalize on emerging digital opportunities across Agetech and enterprise automation.

Observed performance: - RELY went live during Q1 FY27 with three customers onboarded, consistent with the commercialization guidance. - DocuXray continued to demonstrate technical capability during the quarter, though no specific revenue contribution was disclosed. - Adjusted Operating EBITDA improved marginally year-on-year (Rs (14.9) million in Q1 FY27 vs. Rs (16.4) million in Q1 FY26) but deteriorated quarter-on-quarter (vs. Rs (15.6) million in Q4 FY26), indicating that progress towards EBITDA profitability remains gradual. - Revenue declined 10.9% quarter-on-quarter, which is inconsistent with a near-term inflection towards profitability unless offset by cost reductions or product revenue in subsequent quarters. - The company maintains a financial runway of approximately 10 to 12 months, which management will need to address alongside its profitability objectives.

Key facts for investors: - The Audit Committee and Board approved a proposal for the sale or disposal of its investment in Equity Shares of Woovly India Private Limited at a total consideration of Rs. 14,58,36,810/-, based on a valuation report from a registered valuer. - Consolidated operating revenue for FY26 was Rs 37.4 million, 100% from the corporate segment; net loss for the year was Rs 75.9 million. - Fair value of investments rose to Rs 726.8 million. - The Board approved the appointment of Mr. Arvind Khandelwal as Chief Business Officer effective August 21, 2026. - The Board approved the ‘Xelpmoc Design and Tech Limited - Employee Stock Option Scheme 2026’ covering up to 7,41,116 equity shares, subject to shareholder and stock exchange approvals. - During Q1 FY27, 40,000 equity shares were allotted on conversion of stock options under the 2019 ESOP scheme, increasing paid-up capital to Rs 14,79,94,930 comprising 1,47,99,493 equity shares of face value Rs 10 each; a further 135,681 options were granted to eligible employees under the 2019 scheme. - No dividend was recommended.
