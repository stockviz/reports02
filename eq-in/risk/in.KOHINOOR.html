Kohinoor Foods Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Legacy debt and litigation overhang. The company’s lenders filed a petition before DRT Delhi under Section 19(4) of the Act. On 28.10.2025, DRT passed an order against the company directing it to pay banks INR 926.13 crores, less amounts received from sale/release of mortgage properties, along with costs and interest at 11% per annum from the date of filing of the TA until realisation, failing which the amount was to be recovered from sale of hypothecated/charged stock, tangible movable and available mortgage properties within 30 days. The company is contesting the matter and has filed an appeal before DRAT Delhi against the 28.10.2025 order (diary no. 407/2026). The next hearing date before DRT Delhi is 17.08.2026.

One Time Settlement (OTS) not fully closed out. The OTS resolution plan submitted by the company was sanctioned by consortium lenders during FY 2024-2025, and the company states it has fully paid the OTS amount along with interest during the last year. However, NOCs have been obtained from only 5 out of 6 lenders; the remaining lender NOC and the consolidated NOC from the Lead Bank are still in process.

Earlier DRT restraint. An ex-parte interim order dated 25-06-2020 from DRT-III, Delhi in the matter of ICICI Bank vs. Kohinoor Foods Limited restrained the company from transferring, alienating, dealing with, disposing of, encumbering or creating third-party interest in hypothecated assets/immovable properties. The loan was subsequently taken over by Prudential ARC; following full payment of the agreed OTS amount with interest, Prudential ARC withdrew the case and issued an NOC.

Vendor execution petition. A vendor filed an execution petition before the Faridabad District & Session Judge. The company approached the High Courts of Chandigarh and Delhi for a stay, and also filed a petition before the Apex Court contesting proceedings before the Faridabad District & Session Court. The Apex Court allowed the company’s petition, granting relief against the impugned order and directing the company to approach the High Court for further relief. The company has deposited the impugned order amount in compliance with the court’s order and is contesting the matter. Hearings: High Court Chandigarh on 17.08.2026; High Court Delhi on 23.09.2026.

Registered office premises notice. The Municipal Corporation issued a notice to the Builder of Pinnacle Tower to vacate the premises at Surajkund, Faridabad. The Builder obtained interim stay from the High Court of Punjab and Haryana; the company also filed a petition before the civil judge, senior division, Faridabad. The Faridabad Court stated that since interim stay had already been granted by the High Court, no ground was made out to grant the relief prayed for at this stage. Next hearing: 05.10.2026.

Non-provision of interest on revoked corporate guarantee. The company has not provided interest towards the revoked corporate guarantee of its wholly owned subsidiary in USA, to the extent of Rs 86.61 lacs for the quarter and Rs. 1614.89 lacs up to 30.06.2026 from the date of revocation, in its books of account. The auditors note that no provision has been made towards penal interest, penalties, or any other debit or credit as may be done by lenders, and that in the absence of reconciliation and complete statements of account from banks, the aggregate amount not provided is not ascertainable with accuracy.

Qualified audit conclusion. The statutory auditors (N.C. Raj & Associates) issued a qualified conclusion on both standalone and consolidated unaudited results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, on the basis of the non-provision of interest on the revoked corporate guarantee and the going-concern assessment.

Other auditor emphasis matters. The auditors also flagged that the company has not made provision for demands raised by various authorities (such as Income Tax, GST) pending before appellate forums, and has not made provision for interest on late payments to creditors covered under the MSMED Act; the possible impact of non-provision is not commented upon. Balances of some debtors and creditors are subject to confirmation. The company has not accounted for deferred tax expense/income during the quarter, with the deferred tax asset/liability to be reviewed at year end.

SEBI show cause notice. A Show Cause Notice under Rule 4 of SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995 has been issued in the name of the Directors and the Company. The Directors and Company have applied for a settlement application with SEBI; the SEBI Settlement Committee has granted a hearing, and the Director/representative has submitted the reply to SEBI.

Exchange clarification on results format. In response to an NSE email dated September 11, 2025 seeking explanation on financial results submitted for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 — noting that the results were not as per the format prescribed under Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013 or Indian Accounting Standards, and that standalone and consolidated figures were the same — the company clarified on September 12, 2025 that its subsidiaries are not operational and there is impact of statutory and other nominal expenses incurred in those subsidiaries, hence the standalone and consolidated financials appear identical.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

OTS sanctioned and paid. The OTS resolution plan submitted by the company was sanctioned by consortium lenders during FY 2024-2025, and the company has fully paid the OTS amount along with interest during the last year. NOCs have been obtained from 5 out of 6 lenders, with the remaining lender NOC and consolidated NOC from the Lead Bank in process.

Prudential ARC case withdrawn. Following full payment of the agreed OTS amount with interest, Prudential ARC withdrew its case and issued an NOC to the company.

Proposed rights issue. The Board at its meeting held on 13 August 2026 approved raising funds through issuance and allotment of equity shares for an aggregate amount of up to Rs. 1,05,00,00,000 (Rupees One Hundred Five Crores Only) on a rights issue basis, on terms and conditions to be decided by the Board, to eligible equity shareholders as on a record date to be determined and notified subsequently, subject to receipt of applicable regulatory and statutory approvals under SEBI ICDR Regulations, 2018, SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015, and the Companies Act, 2013. The equity shares proposed to be issued have a face value of Rs. 10 each; the number of shares will be determined after receiving in-principle approval from the stock exchange.

Going concern assessment. As per the assessment of management, the company continues to be a going concern, based on the sanction of revised/revival of the One Time Settlement by the lead bank, which has been fully paid by the company.

No impairment assessment. As per management’s assessment, the recoverable amount of the assets is higher than carrying value, and hence no impairment of assets needs to be recorded in the financial statements.

Single-segment food business. The company is primarily engaged in the business of manufacturing, trading and marketing of food products, which is a single segment as per Ind AS 108.

  • The DRT Delhi order dated 28.10.2025 directing payment of INR 926.13 crores (less amounts received from sale/release of mortgage properties) with costs and 11% per annum interest, and the recovery mechanism against hypothecated/charged stock, tangible movable and available mortgage properties within 30 days of the order, which the company is contesting before DRAT Delhi.
  • Outstanding consolidated NOC from the Lead Bank and NOC from one remaining lender under the OTS.
  • The vendor execution petition before Faridabad District & Session Judge and related proceedings before the High Courts and the Apex Court.
  • The Municipal Corporation notice to the Builder of Pinnacle Tower for vacating the registered/corporate office premises at Surajkund, Faridabad.
  • Non-provision of interest on the revoked corporate guarantee of the US wholly owned subsidiary (Rs 86.61 lacs for the quarter; Rs. 1614.89 lacs up to 30.06.2026), with the aggregate amount not ascertainable with accuracy per the auditors.
  • Non-provision for demands raised by various authorities (Income Tax, GST etc.) pending before appellate forums, and non-provision of interest on late payments to creditors covered under the MSMED Act.
  • SEBI Show Cause Notice under Rule 4 of the SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995 issued to the Directors and the Company, currently in the settlement process.
  • Debtor and creditor balances subject to confirmation.
  • Deferred tax expense/income not accounted for during the quarter, pending year-end review.
  • The rights issue is subject to receipt of applicable regulatory and statutory approvals, and the number of shares to be issued will be determined only after in-principle approval from the stock exchange.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Going concern. Management assesses that the company continues to be a going concern based on the sanction of revised/revival of the One Time Settlement by the lead bank, which has been fully paid. The auditors reference this assessment in the basis of their qualified conclusion, noting the company continues to be a going concern on that basis.

Asset recoverability. Management assesses that the recoverable amount of assets is higher than carrying value, and hence no impairment is required. The auditors include this as an emphasis of matter.

OTS completion. Management states the OTS amount along with interest has been fully paid and that NOCs have been obtained from 5 out of 6 lenders, with the remaining NOC and consolidated NOC from the Lead Bank in process. The auditors’ emphasis of matter repeats this position.

Subsidiary operations. Management clarified to the exchange (September 12, 2025) that the company’s subsidiaries are not operational and that statutory and other nominal expenses incurred in those subsidiaries cause standalone and consolidated financials to appear identical.

Auditor qualifications versus management positions. The auditors’ qualified conclusion on both standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 is based on the non-provision of interest on the revoked corporate guarantee and the going-concern assessment. The auditors also state that, with limited information, the aggregate amount not provided in the books of account is not ascertainable with accuracy, and that they are unable to comment upon the possible impact of non-provision for authority demands and MSMED interest.

Corporate actions. The Board approved the unaudited standalone and consolidated financial results for the first quarter ended 30 June 2026 at its meeting on 13 August 2026, and approved the rights issue of up to Rs. 105 crores. The 37th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for 11:30 A.M. on Wednesday, 30 September 2026, with the Register of Members and Share Transfer Books closed from Thursday, 24 September 2026 to Wednesday, 30 September 2026 (both days inclusive). The Board recommended the appointment of M/s. Arora & Choudhary, Chartered Accountants (FRN: 003870N), as Statutory Auditors for a term of 5 consecutive years from the conclusion of the 37th AGM until the conclusion of the 42nd AGM, subject to shareholder approval, consequent upon completion of the tenure of the existing Statutory Auditor, M/s. N C Raj & Associates, Chartered Accountants.
