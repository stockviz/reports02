Headwinds and Challenges

Persistent losses at the standalone level. Ravinder Heights Limited reported a standalone net loss of ₹10.82 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with ₹13.25 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and ₹17.11 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the standalone loss was ₹59.22 lakh. Standalone revenue from operations was flat at ₹35.28 lakh across all three comparative quarters, indicating no top-line growth at the parent level.

Consolidated losses widened sequentially. On a consolidated basis, the group reported a net loss after tax of ₹168.72 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a loss of ₹294.02 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹51.97 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated revenue from operations was only ₹1.34 lakh for the June 2026 quarter, against ₹12.72 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.

Cost base remains heavy relative to revenue. Consolidated employee benefits expense rose to ₹137.87 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹77.29 lakh a year earlier. Depreciation and amortisation rose to ₹78.44 lakh from ₹53.07 lakh. Other expenditure was ₹162.53 lakh, up from ₹132.36 lakh. Total consolidated expenses were ₹381.71 lakh against total income of ₹203.29 lakh.

Dependence on non-operating income. Consolidated other income of ₹201.95 lakh was the dominant contributor to total income of ₹203.29 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, far exceeding revenue from operations of ₹1.34 lakh.

Audit scope limitations. The auditor did not review the interim financial results of five subsidiaries, whose results reflected total revenues of ₹1.56 lakh, a net loss after tax of ₹160.93 lakh and a comprehensive loss of ₹160.93 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. A further subsidiary’s interim results, reflecting total revenue of ₹Nil, net profit after tax of ₹2.59 lakh and comprehensive profit of ₹2.59 lakh, were not reviewed by its auditor. Management stated these were not material to the group.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Security deposits received under the collaboration arrangement. Under a Second Addendum executed on September 14, 2025 to the original Collaboration Agreement dated December 10, 2019, M/s Radhika Heights Limited and its wholly owned subsidiaries (the “Owners”) and M/s Bestech India Pvt. Ltd. (the “Developer”) agreed to additional non-refundable security deposits. The Developer paid an additional ₹3,000.00 lakh on September 19, 2025 and a final tranche of ₹2,000.00 lakh on November 19, 2025, on top of the original ₹2,500.00 lakh deposit. The Owners recognised revenue of ₹7,500.00 lakh for the year ended March 31, 2026 under Ind AS 115, with ₹Nil recognised in each of the quarters ended June 30, 2026, March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2025.

Single-segment real estate focus. The group is engaged in real estate development, specifically construction of residential/commercial properties and renting of property, and operates as a single segment under Ind AS 108.

Corporate governance and compliance actions. The Board approved the re-appointment of M/s SNVA & Co., Chartered Accountants, as Internal Auditors for FY 2026-27, and appointed Mr. Girish Madan as Scrutinizer for the e-voting process at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The Board also approved the Annual Report along with the Director’s Report and annexures for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

Key Risks

Revenue recognition concentrated in a single prior period. The ₹7,500.00 lakh revenue recognised in FY 2025-26 arose from non-refundable security deposits under the collaboration agreement, with no comparable revenue recognised in the quarters ended June 30, 2026, March 31, 2026 or June 30, 2025. This concentration means reported consolidated income is not recurring in nature.

Subsidiary-level losses. The five subsidiaries not reviewed by the auditor recorded a combined net loss after tax of ₹160.93 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, which is a substantial portion of the group’s consolidated loss of ₹168.72 lakh for the same period.

Reliance on the Developer counterparty. The collaboration arrangement with M/s Bestech India Pvt. Ltd. is central to the group’s revenue recognition, and the deposits are described as non-refundable.

Continued negative earnings per share. Consolidated basic and diluted EPS was negative ₹0.28 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, negative ₹0.48 for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and negative ₹0.08 for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Standalone EPS was negative ₹0.02 for the June 2026 quarter.
