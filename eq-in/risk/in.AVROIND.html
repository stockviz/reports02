AVROIND: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates in an environment marked by significant cost pressures arising from increases in prices of polymers and raw materials linked to global crude oil markets. Volatile crude oil prices, inflationary pressures, heightened geopolitical tensions, and global supply chain disruptions are cited as material challenges. The business also faces competition from other players.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management points to strong domestic demand, rising urbanization, improving consumer aspirations, increasing housing demand, and a growing dealer and distribution ecosystem as supportive factors. Market expansion initiatives and growth in the Indian plastic furniture market are also identified as opportunities. The company has incorporated M/s Avro Recycling Limited as a wholly owned subsidiary for exclusive focus on recycling plastic scrap, with an investment of Rs. 39.76 Crore, positioning it to leverage recycling and circular economy opportunities.

3. Key Risks

The identified risks centre on input cost volatility tied to crude oil-linked polymers and raw materials, inflationary pressures, geopolitical tensions, and supply chain disruptions. Competitive pressure from other players in the plastic furniture market is also a risk factor.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

Management states it remains focused on strengthening market penetration, enhancing operational efficiencies, expanding manufacturing and distribution capabilities, product innovation, and leveraging recycling and circular economy opportunities.

Observed performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 shows standalone total income of Rs. 2,526.81 lakhs and profit for the period of Rs. 68.72 lakhs, compared with standalone total income of Rs. 2,325.35 lakhs and profit of Rs. 105.46 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. On a consolidated basis, total income was Rs. 2,741.34 lakhs with profit of Rs. 69.32 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, against Rs. 2,325.35 lakhs total income and Rs. 105.19 lakhs profit in the corresponding quarter of the prior year. Sequentially, standalone profit declined from Rs. 86.34 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to Rs. 68.72 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, while consolidated profit declined from Rs. 98.13 lakhs to Rs. 69.32 lakhs over the same period.

For the full year ended March 31, 2026, standalone total income was Rs. 9,020.76 lakhs with profit of Rs. 420.71 lakhs, and consolidated total income was Rs. 10,368.14 lakhs with profit of Rs. 459.73 lakhs. The prior year (FY 25) standalone figures cited were total income of Rs. 82.93 Crore and net profit of Rs. 3.04 Crore, against FY 26 total income of Rs. 99.30 Crore and net profit of Rs. 4.21 Crore.

Other developments during the period include the sale of certain Property, Plant and Equipment (including CWIP) amounting to Rs. 1261.98 lakhs to the wholly owned subsidiary AVRO Recycling Limited during the June 2026 quarter. The Board approved a split of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10 each to Rs. 1 each, with the record date of May 05, 2026 and a new ISIN effective from the ex-date. The Board also allotted 1,06,090 equity shares on conversion of warrants upon receipt of the balance 75% of the issue price, while 4,24,361 warrants lapsed due to non-receipt of the balance 75%, with the 25% amount received earlier forfeited, aggregating to Rs. 135.00 lakhs. No investor complaints were received or pending during the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

The 30th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 01:00 PM via video conferencing or other audio-visual means.
