FSL: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Exceptional charges in Q1FY27. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, exceptional items included a charge of ₹716.86 million (₹563.14 million net of tax), comprising: - ₹356.73 million estimated to be non-recoverable following contract termination by a client; - ₹283.74 million for indemnification of a regulatory penalty to a customer arising from non-fulfilment of contractual performance obligations; - ₹76.39 million of fair value adjustment on contingent consideration.

Insider-trading code violation. On June 29, 2026, the company disclosed an inadvertent trading violation by a designated person — Mr. Dinesh Jain, President and Chief Financial Officer. The violations observed were (1) trading in the company’s securities during the trading window closure period and (2) contra trades executed (a sell of 7,211 shares at ₹242 and a buy of 7,211 shares at ₹238.68 on NSE, both dated June 29, 2026). Actions taken: a cautionary notice was issued; the DP is prohibited from entering into any transaction (buy or sell) in company shares for the next six months following the prior transaction; and the matter will be placed before the upcoming Audit Committee meeting. The company stated the deviation is not material relative to total paid-up equity share capital, that this was the first instance of unintentional non-compliance (procedural) by the DP, and that the DP proactively informed the Compliance Officer beforehand. No amount was collected for the violation, and there were no previous instances since the last financial year.

Risk factors disclosed. Risks and uncertainties cited include: fluctuations in earnings; ability to manage growth; intense competition in the BPS market affecting cost advantages; wage increases; ability to attract and retain highly skilled professionals; time and cost overruns on client contracts; client concentration; managing ramp-ups and growth; international operations; reduced demand in key focus verticals; disruptions in telecom infrastructure and technology; completing and integrating potential acquisitions; liability for damages on service contracts; subsidiary performance; withdrawal of government fiscal incentives; political instability; legal restrictions on raising capital and acquiring companies outside India; unauthorized use of intellectual property; and general economic conditions.

Attrition. Voluntary attrition stood at 27.5% for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Addressable market expansion. Management cites a services-as-software opportunity: a global services-as-software market opportunity of US$1.5 trillion by 2035, compared with a US$220 billion global BPO services market growing at 4–5% CAGR — described as a ~7x larger addressable market. Growth prospects also include discontinuities caused by macro and technology shifts, disrupting traditional business models with the “Intelligence That Operates” playbook, and leveraging a right-scale advantage alongside long-standing client relationships and domain expertise.

Q1FY27 performance drivers. Revenue grew 22.9% YoY, the ninth straight quarter of double-digit YoY revenue growth. Key drivers cited: broad-based progress across verticals with new logo additions; four large deals signed in Q1FY27; strong demand for AI-led transformation, collections, and AI-enabled solutions; and expanding addressable markets through innovation.

AI partnership with Cresta (August 11, 2026). Firstsource announced a strategic partnership with Cresta, a customer experience AI platform, to deliver an integrated AI technology solution for enterprise CX transformation. The partnership combines Cresta’s unified AI platform (AI agents, agentic AI augmentation for human agents, conversation intelligence) with Firstsource’s domain-driven systems integration, outcome measurement frameworks, and operational AI expertise. Firstsource has established a dedicated AI Center of Excellence as the integration engine for Cresta + Firstsource deployments. The partnership targets enterprises in the US, UK, Australia, and the Middle East. Management framed this as co-engineering a production-ready AI solution, with Ashish Chawla (Head, CX and Consulting) describing Kairos as “our operating system for AI-native operations” and positioning the company as a partner that “owns the outcome, not just implements the technology.”

Broker Narrative

The narrative started with an Accumulate call that acknowledged healthcare/CMT weakness and high attrition but leaned on margin expansion, new clients, and high-TAM opportunities. It then moved through a prolonged Reduce phase during 2023–2025 as actual returns turned negative and repeatedly produced DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes, with brokers emphasizing downside risks. The final report flipped to Buy, highlighting consistent margin expansion, a $1bn+ deal pipeline, and large-deal wins while treating healthcare/CMT volatility as timing-related rather than structural. Persistent themes were healthcare/CMT revenue volatility, attrition, and margin expansion; the call stance shifted from Accumulate to Reduce to Buy.

Fears that came true

  • Healthcare/provider weakness materialized: the early PHE transition and provider segment pressures were followed by a terminated healthcare BPaaS contract, an exceptional provision, and a 2% QoQ Healthcare decline in Q1 FY27.
  • CMT weakness materialized: the early CMT sequential weakness persisted, with CMT growth still facing project timing volatility in the last report.
  • High attrition proved to be an ongoing risk: attrition rose ~330 bps QoQ to 33.1% in Q1 FY27, consistent with the stock’s negative/DISAPPOINTMENT phase.

Optimism that failed

  • The expected Healthcare Provider revival from Medicaid Redetermination wins did not materialize: Healthcare declined 2% QoQ in Q1 FY27 and one BPaaS engagement was wound down.
  • The early expectation that robust client additions would ramp revenue did not offset core vertical weakness: despite repeated new logo wins, Healthcare and CMT continued to show declines/timing volatility.
