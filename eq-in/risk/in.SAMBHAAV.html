Headwinds and Challenges

  • Standalone profitability turned negative in Q1 FY2027. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, standalone revenue from operations was ₹852.90 lakhs (up from ₹814.65 lakhs in Q1 FY2026), but total expenses rose to ₹990.62 lakhs, producing a loss before tax of ₹97.58 lakhs and a loss for the period of ₹100.74 lakhs. This compares with a profit before tax of ₹4.90 lakhs and profit of ₹0.70 lakhs in Q1 FY2026.
  • The Media and Allied segment recorded a segment loss. Standalone Media and Allied Business segment result was a loss of ₹60.51 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, versus a profit of ₹30.50 lakhs in Q1 FY2026 and ₹65.78 lakhs in Q4 FY2026. Segment revenue for Media and Allied Business was ₹647.58 lakhs in Q1 FY2027.
  • One-time rebranding expenditure weighed on the quarter. The company completed a strategic rebranding of its radio channel from TOP FM to LFM during the quarter, incurring ₹82.04 lakhs, described as a one-time strategic expenditure that is not a recurring operational cost. This was included in Other Expenditure. Other expenses rose to ₹466.98 lakhs standalone in Q1 FY2027 from ₹319.77 lakhs in Q1 FY2026.
  • Consolidated losses were larger. Consolidated loss before tax was ₹214.78 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, including a share of loss of associate of ₹90.14 lakhs. Consolidated loss for the period was ₹211.04 lakhs, with loss attributable to owners of ₹202.94 lakhs and to non-controlling interest of ₹8.10 lakhs.
  • Full-year FY2026 standalone profitability was modest relative to revenue. For the year ended March 31, 2026, standalone revenue from operations was ₹3,867.30 lakhs with profit before tax of ₹150.71 lakhs and profit for the year of ₹44.40 lakhs. Consolidated full-year FY2026 revenue from operations was ₹4,310.08 lakhs with profit before tax of ₹98.96 lakhs and profit for the year of ₹8.19 lakhs.

  • Revenue growth at the standalone level. Standalone revenue from operations increased to ₹852.90 lakhs in Q1 FY2027 from ₹814.65 lakhs in Q1 FY2026. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to ₹961.72 lakhs from ₹922.53 lakhs over the same comparison.
  • Technology and Allied Business expanded. Standalone Technology and Allied Business segment revenue was ₹205.32 lakhs in Q1 FY2027 versus ₹193.09 lakhs in Q1 FY2026, with a segment result of ₹74.18 lakhs (versus ₹75.24 lakhs). On a consolidated basis, Technology and Allied Business revenue was ₹314.14 lakhs versus ₹300.97 lakhs, with a segment result of ₹53.35 lakhs versus ₹45.76 lakhs.
  • Two reportable operating segments. The company operates in Media and Allied Services and Technology and Allied Services, with segment disclosure under Ind AS 108.
  • Group structure. The company has one subsidiary, Ved Techno Serve Private Limited, and one associate, Gujarat News Broadcaster Private Limited, and prepares consolidated financial statements accordingly.
  • Rebranding positioned as a strategic rollout. The TOP FM to LFM rebranding included launch and promotional campaigns, brand identity development, creative and marketing activities, on-ground launch events, advertising and publicity, digital promotions, outdoor media, and associated branding initiatives.
