Fino Payments Bank (FINOPB) — Q1 FY’27 Update

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Revenue and profitability decline: Revenue fell 32% YoY to ₹306.9 crore in Q1 FY’27, and net revenue declined 14% YoY (4% QoQ) to ₹131.2 crore. PBT turned negative at ₹(13.7) crore, and PAT was ₹(13.7) crore, against ₹17.8 crore in Q1’26. EBITDA declined 30% YoY and 23% QoQ to ₹43.1 crore.
  • Recalibration of B2B UPI P2M: The decline in revenue and profitability is attributed primarily to the recalibration of the Digital Payments Services – B2B UPI P2M vertical and the cash-driven transaction business. Digital Payments Services remained non-operational in Q1 FY’27, with relaunch expected only in Q4 FY’27. Digital Payment Services revenue was ₹0.5 crore, down 99% QoQ and 100% YoY.
  • UPI pressure on transaction business: UPI continued to impact Remittance, MATM and AePS, with revenue in that transaction business declining 50% YoY and throughput decreasing 44% YoY. DMT revenue fell 55% YoY and MicroATM + AEPS revenue fell 45% YoY.
  • CMS competition: Although CMS throughput improved 26% sequentially to ₹18,092 crore, competition from banks and fintechs continues to dominate pricing. CMS revenue declined 37% YoY.
  • Rising costs: Operating cost rose 2% YoY and 10% QoQ to ₹88.1 crore; other financial cost and depreciation rose 53% YoY and 12% QoQ to ₹56.8 crore. Cost-to-income rose to 28.7% from 19.9% in Q1’26 and 23.5% in Q4’26.
  • Lower average CASA balance and renewal income growth moderation: Average CASA balance per account declined to ₹1,280 from ₹1,317 in Q1’26 and ₹1,299 in Q4’26. Renewal income growth slowed to 7% YoY in Q1’27 from 42% YoY in Q1’26.
  • Digital throughput moderation: Digital throughput declined 13% YoY in Q4’26 and 7% YoY in Q1’27 to ₹63,390 crore; digital transaction count grew only 1% YoY. Fino’s UPI contribution slipped to 1.27% in Q1’27 from 1.52% in Q1’26.
  • August 2026 operating update: Deposit accounts opened fell 22% YoY to 2,43,216; renewal income declined 4% YoY to ₹22.8 crore; transaction business throughput declined 19% YoY to ₹3,770 crore; B2B UPI P2M throughput was nil, down 100% YoY.
  • Leadership continuity: The Board approved an extension of Mr. Ketan Merchant’s tenure as Interim CEO for a period not exceeding 3 months effective from August 27, 2026, subject to RBI approval.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Highest quarterly net revenue margin: Net revenue margin expanded 925 bps YoY and 275 bps QoQ to 42.8% in Q1 FY’27, led by higher contribution from the CASA business segment, which rose to 54% of revenue.
  • Deposit franchise growth: Average total deposits increased 12% YoY to ₹2,772 crore. CASA accounts grew 22% YoY to 1.83 crore, with 8.4 lakh new accounts opened in Q1’27 (9,281 accounts per day). Cost of funds was stable at 1.4%.
  • Retail digital expansion: Digitally active customers grew 22% YoY to 64.6 lakh. FinoPay users grew 38% YoY to 8.4 lakh. Mobile banking user ranking improved to 5 from 6. Technical decline improved to 0.02% from 0.10% in Q1’26.
  • Loan referral momentum: Referral loan disbursals surged 214% YoY to ₹628 crore in Q1’27, nearly 50% of total referral loans disbursed in FY’26. The August 2026 update showed loan referral disbursals at ₹243 crore, up 367% YoY.
  • CMS recovery: CMS throughput improved 26% sequentially to ₹18,092 crore; the August 2026 update showed CMS throughput at ₹6,669 crore, up 17% YoY.
  • UPI throughput growth: UPI throughput grew 14% YoY and 2% QoQ to ₹60.1k crore in Q1 FY’27. Total throughput was ₹1.11 lakh crore, down 10% YoY but up 3% QoQ, with sequential recovery driven by a diversified business mix. The Bank processed 94.7 crore transactions worth ₹1.11 lakh crore in Q1 FY’27.
  • SFB transition: The Bank received ‘In-principle’ approval from the RBI to convert into a Small Finance Bank on 5th December 2025, making it the first Payments Bank to achieve this distinction. SFB preparation is progressing on track, with LOS and LMS identified, a consultant partnered for transition, senior credit professionals hired, and key technology providers onboarded for lending operations, LMS and credit underwriting.
  • Fino 2.0 strategy: The Bank is adopting a three-pronged DDD (Data, Distribution and Digital) strategy for accelerating growth-oriented strategies.
  • UPI P2M MDR visibility: Currently limited revenue-generating throughput exists; UPI P2M MDR visibility is expected to contribute to revenue.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued impact of UPI on transaction business (Remittance, MATM and AePS), with revenue down 50% YoY and throughput down 44% YoY.
  • Non-operational Digital Payments Services until an expected relaunch in Q4 FY’27, leaving a revenue gap in the interim.
  • Pricing pressure in CMS from banks and fintechs.
  • Negative PBT and PAT in Q1 FY’27, with rising cost-to-income at 28.7%.
  • Dependence on timely SFB transition milestones and required conditions within the stipulated timeline.
  • Interim CEO tenure extension is subject to RBI approval, creating leadership continuity uncertainty.
  • Declining average CASA balance per account and moderating renewal income growth.
  • August 2026 update showed deposit accounts opened down 22% YoY and transaction business throughput down 19% YoY.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management stated Fino remains firmly on track to meet the required conditions for SFB conversion within the stipulated timeline. Observed: SFB preparation and implementation are progressing on track with LOS and LMS identified, a consultant partnered for SFB transition, and senior credit professionals and key technology providers onboarded.
  • Guidance: Digital Payments Services relaunch expected in Q4 FY’27. Observed: The segment remained non-operational in Q1 FY’27, with revenue at ₹0.5 crore, down 99% QoQ and 100% YoY.
  • Guidance: Management noted that sustained deposit growth and healthy momentum in referral loan disbursals continue to reinforce the Bank’s preparedness for its proposed transition to a Small Finance Bank. Observed: Average total deposits grew 12% YoY to ₹2,772 crore; referral loan disbursals surged 214% YoY to ₹628 crore in Q1’27, nearly 50% of FY’26 total disbursals; the August 2026 update showed average total deposits at ₹2,821 crore (up 12% YoY) and loan referral disbursals at ₹243 crore (up 367% YoY).
  • Guidance: Management described Q1 FY’27 as the beginning of a consolidation phase focused on retail business, building the small finance bank, and enhancing low-cost liabilities through aggressive customer acquisition and referral lending. Observed: CASA customer base grew 22% YoY to 1.83 crore with 8.4 lakh new accounts; digitally active customers grew 22% YoY to 64.6 lakh; FinoPay users grew 38% YoY to 8.4 lakh.
  • Guidance: Management stated focus on disciplined investments in technology, governance and developing SFB capabilities to scale the business while maintaining an asset-light and capital-efficient model. Observed: Operating cost rose 2% YoY and 10% QoQ; other financial cost and depreciation rose 53% YoY; cost-to-income rose to 28.7%.
  • Guidance: Management expressed commitment to building a differentiated, AI-driven, technology-led liability-first banking franchise leading to long-term value creation. Observed: Net revenue margin reached its highest quarterly level of 42.8%, with CASA contributing 54% of revenue; EBITDA margin remained steady at 14.0% compared to 13.6% in Q1’26.
