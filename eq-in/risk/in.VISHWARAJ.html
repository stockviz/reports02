1. Headwinds and Challenges

The quarter ended June 30, 2026 was a loss-making period. Total income fell to ₹11,022.41 lakh from ₹13,492.86 lakh in the same quarter a year earlier, and from ₹11,252.91 lakh in the immediately preceding quarter. The company reported a net loss of ₹2,583.57 lakh for the quarter, wider than the ₹1,638.66 lakh loss in the June 2025 quarter and reversing the ₹1,092.86 lakh profit recorded in the March 2026 quarter. Basic and diluted EPS for the quarter stood at ₹(1.19), compared with ₹(0.75) a year earlier and ₹0.53 in the prior quarter.

Revenue softness was visible across most segments. Sugar revenue declined to ₹6,106.57 lakh from ₹8,040.65 lakh a year earlier. Co-generation revenue fell sharply to ₹222.63 lakh from ₹392.01 lakh. Distillery revenue eased to ₹4,289.60 lakh from ₹4,555.70 lakh. Vinegar revenue declined to ₹216.12 lakh from ₹316.68 lakh.

Cost pressures were pronounced. Finance costs rose to ₹1,201.43 lakh from ₹667.30 lakh in the June 2025 quarter, and depreciation increased to ₹612.81 lakh from ₹405.65 lakh. Changes in inventories contributed ₹10,321.51 lakh to expenses during the quarter, reflecting the seasonal build-up of sugar stock. The company recorded a book loss, and note 6 to the results states that no provision for current tax liability was made for the quarter for this reason.

Segment results were negative in the core businesses: Sugar at ₹(627.80) lakh, Co-generation at ₹(554.60) lakh, and Distillery at ₹(584.67) lakh. Only the IML segment (₹61.29 lakh) and Vinegar Unit (₹364.46 lakh) reported positive segment results.

The balance sheet contracted, with total assets falling to ₹73,227.57 lakh as at June 30, 2026 from ₹82,775.47 lakh as at March 31, 2026. Other equity declined to ₹18,355.33 lakh from ₹20,938.90 lakh, reflecting the quarterly loss. Inventories dropped to ₹14,702.14 lakh from ₹25,061.60 lakh, and trade payables fell to ₹3,014.18 lakh from ₹10,177.77 lakh, indicating a significant unwinding of the prior year-end working capital position.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The Distillery segment remains the largest revenue contributor at ₹4,289.60 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, ahead of Sugar at ₹6,106.57 lakh on a comparable basis and far above Co-generation and Vinegar. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the Distillery segment generated ₹9,409.95 lakh in revenue and ₹11,203.75 lakh in segment results, the strongest segment performance for the year.

The Vinegar Unit has shown improving momentum, with segment results rising to ₹364.46 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹212.96 lakh a year earlier and ₹109.02 lakh in the March 2026 quarter. The IML segment also improved to ₹61.29 lakh from ₹36.47 lakh a year earlier.

Other income increased to ₹315.80 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹186.98 lakh a year earlier, providing some support to total income.

The company continued to invest in capital work in progress, which rose to ₹2,343.13 lakh as at June 30, 2026 from ₹2,165.19 lakh as at March 31, 2026, and incurred ₹280.85 lakh on purchase of fixed assets (including CWIP) during the quarter. Cash and cash equivalents improved to ₹683.99 lakh from ₹244.86 lakh at the start of the period, and net cash from operating activities was positive at ₹2,014.22 lakh for the quarter, helped by inventory liquidation of ₹10,359.46 lakh.

The company maintained a clean investor grievance record, stating that no complaints were received from investors or shareholders during April 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026.

  • Finance cost burden: Finance costs of ₹1,201.43 lakh in the June 2026 quarter exceeded the year-ago figure by a wide margin and represented a significant drag on profitability, alongside depreciation of ₹612.81 lakh.
  • Borrowing profile: Non-current borrowings rose to ₹17,029.50 lakh as at June 30, 2026 from ₹13,134.18 lakh as at March 31, 2026, while current borrowings stood at ₹24,935.37 lakh. Total borrowings remain substantial relative to the equity base of ₹4,356.55 lakh in share capital and ₹18,355.33 lakh in other equity.
