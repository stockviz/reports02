RKEC Projects Limited — Analysis of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sharp revenue contraction and deepening losses. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, revenue from contracts was ₹1,732.00 lacs, compared with ₹7,607.37 lacs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and ₹4,041.48 lacs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Total income for the June 2026 quarter was ₹1,876.01 lacs against ₹7,655.76 lacs a year earlier. The company reported a loss before exceptional items and tax of ₹2,376.38 lacs for the June 2026 quarter, versus a profit before tax of ₹455.72 lacs in the June 2025 quarter. Loss after tax for the June 2026 quarter was ₹1,729.81 lacs, compared with a profit of ₹335.19 lacs in the June 2025 quarter. For the year ended 31 March 2026, the loss before tax was ₹4,741.00 lacs and the loss after tax was ₹4,148.43 lacs.

Operating expenses exceeding revenue. In the June 2026 quarter, total expenses of ₹4,252.39 lacs exceeded total income of ₹1,876.01 lacs. Operating expenses alone were ₹3,208.83 lacs. This indicates a cost base that is not being covered by current revenue levels.

Termination of the Maharashtra Maritime Board (MMB) contract. Note 9 to the financial statements states that the contract awarded by MMB for the Radio Jetty Project, with an original contract value of ₹186.68 crore, was terminated by MMB on 22 April 2026 on grounds of alleged non-performance. As at the date of termination, the balance contract value was approximately ₹183.19 crore. MMB invoked and encashed bank guarantees amounting to ₹29.87 crore, comprising ₹9.34 crore towards Performance Guarantee and ₹20.53 crore towards Mobilisation Advance. The company has filed a Statement of Claims amounting to ₹36.18 crore and has recognised the ₹29.87 crore as recoverable from MMB under “Loans and Advances,” pending the outcome of the claims proceedings.

Debt Recovery Tribunal and other legal proceedings. Note 7 states that during the current financial year, various banks and financial institutions have initiated legal proceedings against the company before the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) and other judicial and regulatory forums in relation to certain outstanding dues. The matters are pending adjudication.

Income tax assessment orders and contingent liability. Note 6 states that the company has received income tax assessment orders for Assessment Years 2019-20, 2021-22, and 2023-24. The company has preferred appeals before the appropriate appellate authorities. A contingent liability of ₹20 crore has been provided in the financial statements as a matter of prudence and in accordance with expert advice.

Unpaid statutory liabilities. Note 10 refers to outstanding statutory liabilities relating to TDS and GST liability which remained unpaid.

Negative operating cash flows. Net cash from operating activities was negative ₹3,203.59 lacs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, following negative ₹3,020.75 lacs for the year ended 31 March 2026. Cash and cash equivalents declined to ₹148.80 lacs as at 30 June 2026 from ₹606.36 lacs as at 31 March 2026.

Rising short-term borrowings. Current borrowings increased to ₹23,516.23 lacs as at 30 June 2026 from ₹20,941.77 lacs as at 31 March 2026, while long-term borrowings decreased to ₹2,512.78 lacs from ₹2,765.31 lacs.

No internal audit report furnished. The limited review report notes that, as of 30 June 2026, no internal audit report pertaining to the period under review was furnished to the auditors for verification.

Decline in net worth. Other equity declined to ₹12,563.87 lacs as at 30 June 2026 from ₹14,293.59 lacs as at 31 March 2026, reflecting the loss for the quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Deferred tax asset recognition on tax losses. Note 8 states that the company has recognised a Deferred Tax Asset (DTA) on eligible tax losses in accordance with Ind AS 12, based on management’s assessment and expectation of sufficient future taxable profits against which the tax losses are expected to be utilised. The deferred tax asset increased to ₹1,743.61 lacs as at 30 June 2026 from ₹1,097.03 lacs as at 31 March 2026.

Reduction in trade receivables. Trade receivables decreased to ₹8,774.60 lacs as at 30 June 2026 from ₹12,910.07 lacs as at 31 March 2026, releasing ₹4,135.48 lacs of cash during the quarter.

Claims filed against MMB. The company has filed a Statement of Claims amounting to ₹36.18 crore in respect of the MMB matter and, based on management’s assessment, is confident of realising the aforesaid claims. The ₹29.87 crore of encashed bank guarantees has been recognised as recoverable from MMB.

Tax regime election. Note 3 states that the company elected to exercise the option permitted under section 115BAA of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and has recognised provision for income tax and remeasured its deferred tax assets and liabilities based on the rate prescribed in the said section.

Single reportable segment. Note 4 states that a common CODM assesses the internal reports of all projects, and no separate reportable segments are identified by management for reporting purposes.

Board meeting scheduled for 7 August 2026. A Board Meeting of RKEC Projects Limited is scheduled to be held on Friday, August 07, 2026, to consider and approve the Audited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results for the Quarter and Year ended March 31, 2026, and to consider the appointment of Mrs. Jeevan Jyothi Volla as an Additional Director.

3. Key Risks

  • Contract termination risk: The MMB Radio Jetty Project termination on 22 April 2026, with encashment of bank guarantees of ₹29.87 crore, illustrates exposure to contract termination and guarantee invocation. The recovery of the ₹29.87 crore is contingent on the outcome of claims proceedings.
  • Litigation and regulatory risk: Pending DRT and other judicial/regulatory proceedings initiated by banks and financial institutions in relation to outstanding dues.
  • Tax litigation risk: Income tax assessment orders for Assessment Years 2019-20, 2021-22, and 2023-24, with appeals pending; a contingent liability of ₹20 crore has been provided.
  • Liquidity risk: Negative operating cash flows, declining cash and cash equivalents (₹148.80 lacs as at 30 June 2026), rising short-term borrowings (₹23,516.23 lacs), and unpaid statutory liabilities relating to TDS and GST.
  • Revenue concentration and cost overrun risk: Total expenses exceeding total income in the June 2026 quarter, with operating expenses of ₹3,208.83 lacs against revenue from contracts of ₹1,732.00 lacs.
  • Going concern and DTA realisation risk: The recognition of DTA on tax losses depends on management’s expectation of sufficient future taxable profits; if these do not materialise, the DTA may need to be reassessed.
  • Internal audit gap: No internal audit report pertaining to the period under review was furnished to the auditors for verification as of 30 June 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance on DTA realisation. Note 8 states that the DTA has been recognised based on management’s assessment and expectation of sufficient future taxable profits against which the tax losses are expected to be utilised. Observed performance shows a loss of ₹2,376.38 lacs before tax for the June 2026 quarter and a loss of ₹4,741.00 lacs before tax for the year ended 31 March 2026, indicating that the expected future taxable profits have not yet materialised in the reported periods.

Guidance on MMB claims recovery. Note 9 states that, based on management’s assessment, the company is confident of realising the claims amounting to ₹36.18 crore. The ₹29.87 crore of encashed bank guarantees has been recognised as recoverable from MMB and classified under “Loans and Advances.” The outcome of the claims proceedings remains pending.

Guidance on tax appeals. Note 6 states that the company believes it has strong grounds in its favour and expects a favourable outcome at the appellate level, and that there is no material impact on the financial position, operations, or other activities of the company. A contingent liability of ₹20 crore has nonetheless been provided in the financial statements.

Guidance on legal proceedings. Note 7 states that the company is taking appropriate legal and other necessary steps in respect of the DRT and other proceedings and is pursuing its interests before the relevant forums. The matters remain pending adjudication.

Observed performance versus prior periods. The June 2026 quarter loss after tax of ₹1,729.81 lacs compares with a profit after tax of ₹335.19 lacs in the June 2025 quarter. For the year ended 31 March 2026, the loss after tax was ₹4,148.43 lacs. Earnings per share for the June 2026 quarter was negative ₹6.74 basic and diluted, compared with positive ₹1.40 basic and diluted in the June 2025 quarter, and negative ₹16.17 basic and diluted for the year ended 31 March 2026.
