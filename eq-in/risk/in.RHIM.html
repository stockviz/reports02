RHI Magnesita India — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Operating environment pressures: Management describes a challenging operating environment characterised by pricing pressures, cost inflation, and competitive intensity (Q1 FY27 press release, 11 August 2026).
  • Global and domestic headwinds: The MD & CEO notes challenges emanating from both global headwinds and domestic challenges.
  • Industry-specific pressures: The refractory industry faces pricing pressure and rising competition from global and regional entrants undertaking brownfield and greenfield expansion, alongside rising raw material, energy, and logistics costs.
  • Macro volatility: Global volatility, persistent uncertainty from volatile geopolitics, supply chain disruptions, inflationary pressures, fluctuating demand across steel and industrial markets, low-cost import competition and steel dumping in India, raw material cost volatility, foreign exchange volatility, and pricing pressure from excess industry capacity.
  • FY 2025-26 impairment: FY 2025-26 results included an exceptional goodwill impairment charge of INR 556 crore.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • India macro and policy support: India’s strong macroeconomic resilience and status as a fast-growing economy; government capital expenditure and infrastructure spending (National Infrastructure Pipeline, PM Gati Shakti); PLI schemes for manufacturing; safeguard duties on steel.
  • End-market demand: Growing demand in steel, cement, and emerging industrial sectors; ongoing capacity expansions in the steel and cement industries.
  • Market growth forecasts: India is the highest growth major market for refractories globally, with a 6–8% CAGR forecast. Management also anticipates the iron ore market will reach ~US$ 29.1 billion by 2030 with a CAGR of ~4.7% between 2025–2030.
  • Strategy and positioning: Adoption of the 4PRO solutions strategy, scaling 4PRO contracts, market share gains in flow control and non-cement industrial segments, and backward integration. The company holds a 30%+ market share in India.
  • Structural drivers: Infrastructure growth, urbanisation, and rising steel and cement demand in India.

3. Key Risks

  • Pricing pressure from excess industry capacity and competition from new entrants.
  • Raw material, energy, and logistics cost inflation.
  • Geopolitical volatility, supply chain disruptions, and foreign exchange volatility.
  • Low-cost import competition and steel dumping in India.
  • Fluctuating demand across steel and industrial markets.
  • Goodwill impairment risk, as evidenced by the INR 556 crore exceptional charge in FY 2025-26.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • FY 2025-26 performance: Revenue from operations reached INR 4,020 crore (9% year-on-year growth); adjusted EBITDA was INR 477 crore; adjusted profit after tax was INR 180 crore. The company recorded a net cash-positive position with a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of -0.1x and generated operating cash flow of INR 409 crore, alongside the INR 556 crore goodwill impairment charge.
  • Q1 FY27 performance (quarter ended 30 June 2026): Revenue from operations was ₹1,014 crore (+6% YoY, +9% QoQ); operating EBITDA was ₹147 crore (+42% YoY, +30% QoQ) at a 14.5% margin (+3.7 pp YoY); PAT was ₹65 crore (+83% YoY); shipment volumes were 122 KT; net cash/EBITDA ratio was 0.27x (improved to 0.3x).
  • Guidance stance: Management remains cautiously optimistic, expecting margin recovery to be supported by raw material cost normalisation, pricing actions, and strategic capital expenditure. Structural drivers such as infrastructure growth, urbanisation, and rising steel and cement demand in India continue to provide a strong foundation for future growth. Management projects long-term refractory market growth in India at a 6–8% CAGR.
  • Leadership transition: Q1 FY27 marked a leadership transition, with Mr. Pankaj Malhan appointed Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, while Mr. Parmod Sagar continues to serve as Chairman.

5. Other Corporate Developments

  • Statutory auditor change (14 August 2026): The Board noted the resignation of M/s Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP as Statutory Auditor of the Company and of RHI Magnesita India Refractories Limited (material wholly owned subsidiary), with immediate effect, to align the Statutory Auditor with the Ultimate Holding Company, RHI Magnesita N.V. The outgoing auditor confirmed there are no concerns, issues, or reasons for resignation other than those stated. The Board approved the appointment of M/s B S R & Co. LLP as Statutory Auditor to fill the casual vacancy, subject to member approval, and recommended a five-year term from the conclusion of the 16th AGM (calendar year 2026) until the conclusion of the 21st AGM (calendar year 2031). A parallel recommendation applies to RHIMIRL from its 8th AGM (2026) to its 13th AGM (2031).
  • Investor roadshow: Management will participate in an exclusive physical investor roadshow in Mumbai on 16–17 September 2026, 9:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., with no unpublished price sensitive information to be shared.
  • 16th AGM and dividend: The 16th AGM is scheduled for Tuesday, 29 September 2026 at 11:00 A.M. (IST) via video conferencing or other audio-visual means. The record date for the final dividend is Monday, 14 September 2026, with payment on or before Monday, 5 October 2026.
