Headwinds and Challenges

  • Climate and weather risk: Climate change and extreme weather events are disrupting crop cycles.
  • Cost pressures: The company faces escalating labour expenses, mandatory social welfare and infrastructure investments, and inflationary energy and raw material costs.
  • Labour availability: Acute seasonal labour deficits are a recurring operational challenge.
  • Competitive pressure: The unorganized sector accounts for 52% of the market, creating competitive pressure.
  • Market volatility: Volatile global markets affect the company’s key commodities.
  • Land litigations: Ongoing land litigations involve thousands of hectares.
  • Balance sheet position: As at 30 June 2026, the company’s current liabilities exceeded its current assets. Management states that, based on ageing and expected realisation of financial assets, payment dates of financial liabilities, expected future cash flows, sanctioned/unutilized credit facilities from bankers, and its business plans, the company is capable of meeting its financial obligations falling due within one year from the balance sheet date.
  • Tax position: The company has unabsorbed depreciation and carry-forward losses of earlier years and certain exempt income; tax expense was nil in the current and previous years.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Digital and AI integration: Integration of digital intelligence and Artificial Intelligence (AI) is expected to drive productivity and efficiency.
  • Plantation tourism: Expansion of plantation tourism, including bungalow and cave tourism, is a growth avenue.
  • Niche rubber products: High-demand niche value products in rubber, such as deproteinised and medical-grade rubber, present opportunities.
  • Yield improvements: Drip irrigation pilots are expected to support yield improvements.
  • Demand environment: Strong domestic and international demand for quality tea and natural rubber.
  • Rubber segment performance: The rubber segment contributed segment revenue of ₹6,348.16 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 and segment results of ₹571.10 lakhs, with income including sale of rubber trees.
  • Tea segment recovery: Tea segment results improved to ₹86.12 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹43.80 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26 and a negative ₹69.49 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026.
  • Management outlook: Management anticipates stabilization in global rubber prices and projected volume growth in both tea and rubber yields from existing and newly integrated operations, positioning the company for strengthened financial performance. The company remains committed to building a future-ready and climate-smart agricultural model combining sustainable business practices with innovation and long-term value creation.

Key Risks

  • Commodity and market risk: Volatile global markets and dependence on tea and natural rubber prices.
  • Operational risk: Climate change, extreme weather, seasonal labour deficits, and cost inflation.
  • Regulatory and social cost risk: Mandatory social welfare and infrastructure investments.
  • Competitive risk: The unorganized sector’s 52% market share.
  • Legal risk: Land litigations involving thousands of hectares.
  • Liquidity risk: Current liabilities exceeding current assets as at 30 June 2026, mitigated in management’s assessment by expected cash flows, credit facilities, and business plans.
  • Segment concentration: Operations are conducted through plantation estates and factories based in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, with business segments in tea, rubber, and others.
