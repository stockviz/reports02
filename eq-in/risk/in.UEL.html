UEL — Recent Corporate Announcements: Headwinds, Tailwinds, Risks, and Guidance vs. Performance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak operating revenue in the June 2026 quarter. Revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was Rs. 303.29 lakhs, down from Rs. 564.43 lakhs in the preceding quarter ended 31 March 2026, though higher than Rs. 264.89 lakhs in the corresponding quarter ended 30 June 2025. The sequential decline was driven by the solar power plant operation segment, whose revenue fell to Rs. 315.38 lakhs from Rs. 605.40 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.

Solar power plant operation segment loss. The solar power plant operation segment reported a segment loss before tax and finance cost of Rs. 92.53 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a profit of Rs. 162.47 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and a loss of Rs. 114.03 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Segment assets for this business also declined to Rs. 3,178.47 lakhs as at 30 June 2026 from Rs. 3,264.00 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 and Rs. 4,070.39 lakhs as at 30 June 2025.

EV segment continues to lose money. The EV segment reported a segment loss of Rs. 9.35 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, following a loss of Rs. 25.53 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 10.31 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. EV segment revenue was only Rs. 0.71 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, and EV segment assets fell sharply to Rs. 255.55 lakhs as at 30 June 2026 from Rs. 920.57 lakhs as at 30 June 2025.

Qualified review conclusion on Axis Bank interest income. The statutory auditors, Ashok Khasgiwala & Co. LLP, issued a qualified conclusion. Accrued interest income on fixed deposits with Axis Bank amounting to Rs. 4.06 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (Rs. 6.70 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025, Rs. 4.31 lakhs for the quarter ended 31 March 2026, and Rs. 17.49 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026) has not been credited by the bank, creating a difference between the books of account and the bank’s balance confirmation. The auditors noted this qualification is repetitive.

Dependence on non-operating income. Other income of Rs. 718.85 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter exceeded revenue from operations of Rs. 303.29 lakhs. This included Rs. 409.38 lakhs of bad debts recovered and Rs. 257.67 lakhs of interest income on an arbitration award for a 15 MW solar power plant from Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, Nashik. Total revenue for the quarter was Rs. 1,022.14 lakhs, of which the majority was non-operating.

Unallocable segment loss. The unallocable segment reported a loss of Rs. 29.75 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a profit of Rs. 31.82 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Year-on-year improvement in profitability. Profit before tax for the June 2026 quarter was Rs. 425.81 lakhs, up from Rs. 317.01 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 52.27 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. Profit for the period was Rs. 325.07 lakhs, compared with Rs. 248.60 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 42.46 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.

Manufacturing and sale of solar power systems segment strength. This segment reported revenue of Rs. 667.47 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, up from Rs. 568.00 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 0.15 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. Segment result before tax and finance cost was a profit of Rs. 559.23 lakhs, compared with Rs. 414.25 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and a loss of Rs. 60.44 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. Segment assets stood at Rs. 1,520.76 lakhs as at 30 June 2026.

Proposed preferential issue. The Board, at its meeting on 3 November 2025, approved a preferential issue and allotment of 1,275.70 lakhs equity shares of Rs. 1 each at Rs. 11 per share (including a premium of Rs. 10 per share) to non-promoter proposed investors for cash consideration aggregating up to Rs. 14,032.70 lakhs. Shareholders approved this at an extraordinary general meeting held on 1 December 2025, and the company has filed in-principal approval before the stock exchanges.

Resolution plan-related share issuance. The Board also approved an issue of 2,400 lakhs equity shares of Rs. 1 each to SVA Family Welfare Trust, the Resolution Applicant, against the convertible RA loan as per the approved resolution plan and the undertaking provided to the stock exchanges.

Shareholder approvals at the 27th AGM. At the AGM held on 14 July 2026, shareholders passed all six resolutions, including adoption of the audited standalone financial statements for FY2025-26, reappointment of Mr. Vikalp Mundra as director, appointment of Mrs. Geeta Mundra as Non-Executive Director (Non-Independent) and Chairman, approval under Section 185 for loans/guarantees/security, approval under Section 186 for investments/loans/guarantees, and borrowing limits under Section 180(1)(c) not exceeding Rs. 1,000 crores.

Committee reconstitution. Effective 30 July 2026, the Board reconstituted the CSR Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, inducting Ms. Geeta Mundra as an additional member of both committees.

3. Key Risks

Audit qualification on bank confirmation. The difference between accrued interest income on Axis Bank fixed deposits and the bank’s confirmation remains unresolved and has been flagged as a repetitive qualification. This raises a risk around the reliability of recorded interest income and the reconciliation of bank balances.

Concentration in non-operating income. A substantial portion of quarterly profitability depends on bad debt recoveries and arbitration award interest rather than recurring operations. The sustainability of such income is uncertain.

Segment losses in solar power plant operation and EV. Both segments reported losses in the June 2026 quarter. The solar power plant operation segment has now reported losses in two of the last three reported quarters, and the EV segment has consistently reported losses.

Dilution from proposed share issuances. The proposed preferential issue of 1,275.70 lakhs equity shares and the issue of 2,400 lakhs equity shares to SVA Family Welfare Trust would significantly increase the equity base. Paid-up equity share capital was Rs. 1,333.98 lakhs as at 30 June 2026, and diluted EPS of Rs. 0.09 was below basic EPS of Rs. 0.24, reflecting the dilutive effect of existing convertible instruments.

Dependence on regulatory and shareholder approvals. The preferential issue and the RA loan conversion remain subject to in-principal approval from stock exchanges and completion of formalities.

Borrowing capacity increase. Shareholders approved borrowing limits under Section 180(1)(c) not exceeding Rs. 1,000 crores, which represents a substantial potential increase in leverage relative to the current balance sheet, where total segment liabilities were Rs. 2,981.82 lakhs as at 30 June 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The source material does not contain explicit forward-looking management guidance on revenue, profitability, or segment performance. The available evidence of management intent and its observed outcomes is as follows:

Preferential issue timeline. The Board approved the preferential issue on 3 November 2025, shareholders approved it on 1 December 2025, and the company filed for in-principal approval before the stock exchanges. The June 2026 results note states the company has filed in-principal approval, indicating the process remains pending as of 30 July 2026.

Resolution plan commitment. The Board approved the issue of 2,400 lakhs equity shares to SVA Family Welfare Trust against the convertible RA loan as per the approved resolution plan and the undertaking provided to the stock exchanges. The June 2026 results note records this as approved.

Committee reconstitution. The Board approved the reconstitution of the CSR Committee and NRC effective 30 July 2026, inducting Ms. Geeta Mundra. This followed her appointment as Non-Executive Director (Non-Independent) and Chairman, which was approved by shareholders at the AGM on 14 July 2026.

Observed performance against prior periods. The June 2026 quarter showed year-on-year improvement in total revenue (Rs. 1,022.14 lakhs vs. Rs. 920.99 lakhs), profit before tax (Rs. 425.81 lakhs vs. Rs. 317.01 lakhs), and profit for the period (Rs. 325.07 lakhs vs. Rs. 248.60 lakhs). However, the sequential comparison against the March 2026 quarter shows a decline in revenue from operations (Rs. 303.29 lakhs vs. Rs. 564.43 lakhs) even as total revenue rose (Rs. 1,022.14 lakhs vs. Rs. 691.52 lakhs) due to higher other income. The manufacturing and sale of solar power systems segment drove the year-on-year improvement, while the solar power plant operation segment remained loss-making.
