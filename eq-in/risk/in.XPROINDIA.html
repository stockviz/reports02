Xpro India Limited — Q1 FY27 Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Geopolitical disruption: The conflict in West Asia pushed polymer prices higher across the value chain. The same conflict disrupted shipping schedules for the UAE project (Xpro Dielectric Films FZ-LLC), delaying arrival of certain utility equipment and raw material, which is expected to cause minor delays while remaining within the overall project timeline. The situation in the Middle East remains fluid and is being monitored closely.
  • COEX Cast Films weakness: The segment recorded lower volumes during the quarter, reflecting prevailing market conditions.
  • Cost build-up ahead of revenue: Organization and other costs reflected in aggregate expenses have built up ahead of capacity increases coming online.
  • Margin compression: EBITDA margin fell to 8.8% in Q1 FY27 from 9.9% in Q1 FY26 and 10.6% in Q4 FY26. PAT margin fell to 5.6% from 3.0% a year earlier but was down from 8.6% in Q4 FY26.
  • Sequential decline: Despite strong year-on-year growth, revenue, PBT and PAT were all lower than Q4 FY26 (PBT down 14.4%, PAT down 15.5%).
  • Full-year FY26 decline: FY26 revenue fell 5.6% to Rs. 505.5 Cr, EBITDA fell 7.6% to Rs. 49.2 Cr, PBT fell 28.8% to Rs. 41.3 Cr and PAT fell 30.3% to Rs. 30.5 Cr versus FY25.
  • Foreign exchange exposure: Euro-denominated export credit insurance-backed supplier credits for capital goods, repayable in instalments over ten years, produced an exchange fluctuation loss of INR 1,140.53 lacs in FY26 (standalone), partly offset by a gain of INR 15.58 lacs in Q1 FY27. The company notes these fluctuations may not be permanent and could reverse over the long tenure of the credits.
  • Subsidiary losses: The consolidated results include a subsidiary with total revenues of Nil and a net loss after tax of Rs. 210.15 lacs for the quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Dielectric films expansion: A new dielectric film line was commissioned at Barjora on March 27, 2026, doubling India nameplate capacity from 4,000 to 8,000 MT annually. Initial supplies to customers in India and international markets received encouraging feedback, and development of the full product range on the new line is in progress.
  • Dielectric films market position: The business sustained its strong market position, with continued onboarding of new customers and qualification for new applications, supporting ramp-up of the recently expanded capacity.
  • UAE project: Mechanical installation of key production equipment is almost complete and installation of ancillary equipment is progressing.
  • Market share gains: Against industry refrigerator production growth of 4% in Q1, the company achieved a 9% increase in sales volumes, reflecting market share gains supported by strong customer relationships and commercial execution.
  • Pricing: Average selling prices increased during the quarter, driven by both raw material passthrough and a favorable product mix.
  • Year-on-year growth: Q1 FY27 revenue rose 20.4% to Rs. 174.4 Cr, EBITDA rose 7.0% to Rs. 15.3 Cr, PBT rose 128.3% to Rs. 13.7 Cr and PAT rose 127.9% to Rs. 9.8 Cr versus Q1 FY26.
  • Balance sheet: As of June 30, 2026, an unutilised portion of INR 7,326.39 lacs (standalone) / INR 7,326.33 lacs (consolidated) of net proceeds from earlier issue and conversion of warrants into equity shares was held in bank deposits, pending final utilisation.
  • Dividend: A dividend of INR 2 per equity share for FY26 was approved by shareholders at the AGM held on July 20, 2026 and paid on July 28, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Industry downtrends, changes in the political and economic environment, tax laws, litigation, labour relations, exchange rate fluctuations, technological changes, and investment, cash flow or interest costs are cited as factors that could cause actual results to differ from forward-looking statements.
  • The Middle East conflict remains fluid, with potential for further disruption to the UAE project and to shipping, utility equipment and raw material availability.
  • Foreign exchange movements on long-tenure Euro-denominated supplier credits could produce further gains or losses.
  • The company does not undertake any obligation to update forward-looking statements to reflect events after the date thereof.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Management states its focus remains on volume and margin improvements. Observed performance shows a 9% volume increase in Q1 FY27 against 4% industry refrigerator production growth, but EBITDA margin declined to 8.8% from 9.9% a year earlier, indicating margin improvement has not yet been achieved.
  • The new Barjora dielectric film line was commissioned on March 27, 2026 with the aim of building a globally scaled dielectric films platform. Observed performance shows the business onboarding new customers and qualifying for new applications, supporting capacity ramp-up, though the company notes organization and other costs have built up ahead of capacity increases coming online.
  • The UAE project was expected to remain within the overall project timeline despite shipping disruptions; management continues to monitor the fluid Middle East situation closely.
  • The company describes operations and results as reflecting market and product-mix dynamics, usual seasonal cycles and global geo-political uncertainties.
