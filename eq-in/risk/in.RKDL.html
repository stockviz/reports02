Headwinds and Challenges

  • Qualified auditor review. The independent auditor’s review report on the standalone unaudited results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, dated 14 August 2026, is qualified. The same matters also qualified the audit report for the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026 (dated 25 May 2026).
  • Disputed recoverables. “Amounts recoverable under Dispute” of Rs. 2,900.25 lakhs are classified as Other Non-Current Assets. The company has filed cases and initiated recovery action, and SEBI’s order dated 12-03-2019 directed the parties to repay amounts to the company. The auditor states it is unable to comment on the reliability/recoverability of these debts, and no provision for Expected Credit Loss has been made.
  • Liquors India Limited / Lemonade dispute. Investment in Liquors India Limited of Rs. 825.71 lakhs and amounts received from Lemonade Shares & Securities Private Limited are treated as disputed, with no adjustment for the sale incorporated in the financial statements. The sale agreement for the entire undertaking has been challenged before NCLT; the company petition was ordered “Non Maintainable,” the company has appealed to NCLAT, filed a civil suit before the IInd Additional District Judge, Ranga Reddy District, L B Nagar, Hyderabad (seeking to rescind the agreement as void and restore parties to the pre-MOU position of 05-09-2012), and filed an SLP in the Supreme Court, along with complaints to Police, SEBI, and the Enforcement Directorate. The auditor notes the impact on loss and investments, if any, is unascertainable.
  • Unconfirmed balances and credit risk. Confirmations were not obtained as of 30 June 2026 for certain financial assets including Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors, and Tie Up Parties, and no allowance for expected credit loss was recognized on these assets even though indications of increased credit risks were observed. The consequential impact on financial results has not been ascertained.
  • Pending statutory dues. Statutory dues amounting to Rs. 291.15 lakhs are pending deposit with government authorities, and no provision for interest on these dues has been made; the overall interest liability and financial impact are not ascertainable.
  • Weak profitability. Profit before tax was Rs. 6.83 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Rs. 7.48 lakhs for the quarter ended 31 March 2026, and Rs. 2.78 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Profit for the year/period was Rs. 13.68 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026. EPS (basic and diluted, before and after extraordinary items) was Rs. 0.03 for the June 2026 quarter, Rs. 0.02 for the March 2026 quarter, Rs. 0.01 for the June 2025 quarter, and Rs. 0.06 for the year ended 31 March 2026.
  • Revenue decline sequentially. Revenue from operations was Rs. 1,247.49 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, down from Rs. 1,352.51 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, though above Rs. 811.36 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.
  • Rising finance costs. Finance cost rose to Rs. 8.69 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 2.87 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 0.78 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year revenue growth. Revenue from operations of Rs. 1,247.49 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 exceeded Rs. 811.36 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025.
  • Other income support. Other income was Rs. 124.18 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, including a write-back of a sundry creditors balance of Rs. 240.00 lakhs that had been outstanding for a substantial period and was considered no longer payable, recognized under “Other Income.”
  • SEBI order in the company’s favour. SEBI’s order dated 12-03-2019 directed Mr. Anil Agrawal and his associates to return Rs. 33.83 crores along with interest at 12% p.a. with effect from 01.04.2011. Management expects to get the amounts back in due course.
  • Legal progress in the Liquors India matter. The Supreme Court of India has directed the investigating agencies to take steps as advised in accordance with law, and the police authorities have filed a charge sheet against Anil Agrawal and 81 others.
  • Single reportable segment. The company operates in one reportable segment: manufacturing of Indian Made Foreign Liquor (IMFL).
  • Current investment valuation. The company has valued its current investment in shares of S V Distilleries Private Limited at cost.

Key Risks

  • Recoverability of the Rs. 2,900.25 lakhs of disputed amounts and the Rs. 825.71 lakhs investment in Liquors India Limited, with no Expected Credit Loss or diminution provision made.
  • Adverse outcomes in the NCLT/NCLAT/Supreme Court proceedings, civil suit, and recovery suits relating to the Lemonade Shares & Securities agreement.
  • Unascertained impact from unconfirmed debtor/creditor/tie-up balances and unrecognized expected credit losses.
  • Unascertained interest liability on Rs. 291.15 lakhs of pending statutory dues.
  • Continued auditor qualification on the financial results.
  • Thin margins: total income of Rs. 1,371.67 lakhs against total expenses of Rs. 1,364.84 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Recoverability expectation. Management expects to recover the disputed amounts in due course, citing the SEBI order dated 12-03-2019 directing Mr. Anil Agrawal and associates to return Rs. 33.83 crores with 12% p.a. interest from 01.04.2011. No provision for Expected Credit Loss has been made on these amounts. The auditor states it is unable to comment on reliability/recoverability.
  • No liability anticipated on the Liquors India matter. Management does not anticipate any liability on this account and has not provided for diminution in value of investments or Expected Credit Loss on the loan to Liquor India Limited. The auditor notes the matter is sub-judice and the impact is unascertainable.
  • Sundry creditors write-back. Management assessed that a Rs. 240.00 lakhs sundry creditors balance was no longer payable in the absence of any claim from the party, and wrote it back to Other Income during the quarter. This is reflected in the June 2026 quarter’s other income of Rs. 124.18 lakhs.
  • Observed performance context. Against these positions, reported profitability remains modest (PBT of Rs. 6.83 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter; Rs. 13.68 lakhs profit for the year ended 31 March 2026), and the auditor’s qualifications on the same matters recurred in the 31 March 2026 audit report and the 30 June 2026 review report.
