KCP: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak quarterly profitability. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone profit before tax was Rs 0.64 crore, down sharply from Rs 21.10 crore in the same quarter a year earlier (30 June 2025) and from Rs 58.63 crore in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Standalone profit for the period was Rs 0.48 crore, compared with Rs 14.28 crore a year earlier. Consolidated profit after tax attributable to owners was Rs 36.83 crore, against Rs 63.49 crore in the June 2025 quarter.

Cement segment pressure. Standalone cement revenue was Rs 357.98 crore in the June 2026 quarter, marginally below Rs 358.98 crore a year earlier, but segment EBIT collapsed to Rs 2.24 crore from Rs 23.51 crore in the June 2025 quarter. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, cement EBIT was Rs 65.93 crore on revenue of Rs 1,400.52 crore. This segment is the dominant contributor to standalone revenue and assets (cement assets of Rs 1,283.95 crore out of total standalone assets of Rs 1,627.98 crore as at 30 June 2026).

Heavy Engineering remains marginal. Standalone heavy engineering revenue was Rs 20.45 crore in the June 2026 quarter with EBIT of Rs 0.12 crore. For FY 2025-26, the segment recorded negative EBIT of Rs 4.66 crore on revenue of Rs 111.56 crore.

Seasonality and comparability. The consolidated results include KCP Vietnam Industries Limited, whose sugar business is seasonal, with the season normally running January–May in Vietnam. The company notes that financials from quarter to quarter may therefore not be comparable.

Cost and finance burden. Standalone finance cost was Rs 6.68 crore in the June 2026 quarter (Rs 5.47 crore a year earlier), and depreciation was Rs 17.43 crore (Rs 15.04 crore a year earlier). Consolidated finance cost was Rs 9.97 crore.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

New lines of business approved. On 3 August 2026, the Board approved proposals to engage in (i) manufacturing of Colour Paints and other Building Materials, and (ii) Builders and Developers, each as a new line of business.

Waste Heat Recovery System commissioned. The company announced successful commissioning of a 15.80 MW Waste Heat Recovery System with a turbine capacity of 16.58 MW at its cement manufacturing unit at Muktyala, Andhra Pradesh. This is a cement-segment efficiency and captive-power initiative.

Dividend continuity. The Board declared an interim dividend of 50% (Re 0.50 per equity share of face value Re 1) for FY 2026-27. Shareholders also approved a dividend for the year ended 31 March 2026 at the 85th AGM.

Sugar segment scale. Consolidated sugar revenue was Rs 390.67 crore in the June 2026 quarter, up from Rs 288.48 crore a year earlier, with segment EBIT of Rs 38.31 crore (versus Rs 66.33 crore a year earlier). For FY 2025-26, sugar revenue was Rs 1,021.47 crore with EBIT of Rs 190.56 crore.

Hotel segment stability. Hotel revenue was Rs 9.94 crore with EBIT of Rs 2.15 crore in the June 2026 quarter, versus Rs 9.32 crore revenue and Rs 1.76 crore EBIT a year earlier.

3. Key Risks

  • Cement margin volatility: the segment’s EBIT fell to Rs 2.24 crore in the June 2026 quarter from Rs 23.51 crore a year earlier despite broadly flat revenue, indicating sensitivity of profitability to cost and price movements.
  • Heavy engineering losses: the segment recorded negative full-year EBIT of Rs 4.66 crore for FY 2025-26 and only marginal positive EBIT in the June 2026 quarter.
  • Seasonal and external-market exposure: the Vietnam sugar subsidiary’s results depend on a January–May season, and the group’s consolidated performance includes a joint venture (Fives Cail KCP Limited) whose interim financial statements were reviewed by other auditors, not the group’s statutory auditor.
  • Execution risk in new businesses: colour paints/building materials and builders/developers are newly approved lines with no reported operating track record in the supplied material.
  • Finance and depreciation drag: rising finance cost and depreciation at the standalone level weigh on profitability.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

The supplied material contains no forward financial guidance from management. What can be compared is board-approved capital/strategic actions against reported results:

  • The Board approved entry into colour paints/building materials and builders/developers on 3 August 2026; no revenue or profit contribution from these lines is reported in the June 2026 quarter results.
  • The WHRS at Muktyala was commissioned (15.80 MW, turbine capacity 16.58 MW); the June 2026 quarter standalone power and fuel cost was Rs 119.13 crore, versus Rs 127.61 crore in the June 2025 quarter, while cement revenue was broadly flat — the results do not isolate the WHRS effect.
  • The Board declared a 50% interim dividend for FY 2026-27 on 3 August 2026, alongside standalone quarterly profit of Rs 0.48 crore and consolidated profit attributable to owners of Rs 36.83 crore.
  • All resolutions at the 85th AGM (held 3 August 2026, with remote e-voting from 31 July to 2 August 2026 and cut-off date 27 July 2026) were passed with requisite majority; the special resolutions on appointing Sri Parthapratim Brahma as Non-Executive Independent Director and on remuneration by way of commission to Independent and Non-Executive Directors each received 99.9997% votes in favour, with 164 votes against.
