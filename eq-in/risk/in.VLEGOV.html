Headwinds and Challenges

  • Revenue from operations declined to ₹64.90 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹118.54 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26, a fall of 45.25%. Total income declined 45.07% over the same period.
  • The company continued to report operating losses. EBITDA was negative at ₹54.24 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with negative ₹54.65 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26. Profit before tax was negative ₹56.82 lakh, and profit after tax was negative ₹57.36 lakh.
  • Total expenses of ₹124.47 lakh exceeded total income of ₹67.65 lakh in the quarter.
  • Other equity was negative at ₹6,388.52 lakh as of Q1 FY2026-27, against negative ₹6,275.47 lakh in the prior quarter.
  • The company’s business falls within a single segment, “E-Governance & IT/ITES Business,” so results depend on this single line of activity.
  • In the land records opportunity, only 30% of land parcels nationwide have been assigned ULPINs, leaving 70% unassigned, with significant gaps in states with incomplete land digitization and integration. Aadhaar integration with land records has been completed for only 2.16% of villages, with Aadhaar linking still needed for the remaining 6.38 lakh villages.
  • The company describes the need to address technical challenges in integration and validation for ULPIN, and to train state officials on Aadhaar integration workflows.
  • The company states it has revamped its business and strengthened its leadership team to align with its growth strategy and enhance eligibility to bid for large-scale government and private projects.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The company remains debt-free, with no long-term or short-term debt, which it describes as providing a robust financial foundation to support expansion plans and long-term sustainability.
  • In May 2025, the company signed an MoU with Ekansh Concepts under which VL E-Governance acts as EPC Partner for the Sankalp Industrial Smart City Project. The company states its role as EPC Partner has commenced and started generating revenue from this engagement, and that as the project scales up it anticipates a significant increase in both revenue and profitability in the coming quarters.
  • The Sankalp Industrial Smart City Project is located at the intersection of the Mumbai–Nagpur Samruddhi Mahamarg and the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway, covering 494 acres in Phase I, with an estimated EPC contract value of approximately ₹800 crores. Phased expansion includes Phase II of 565 acres and Phase III of 901 acres. Focus areas include renewable energy solutions, rooftop solar installations, open access and captive power infrastructure, power distribution franchisee, data centers, large-scale warehousing hubs, and a residential township with smart infrastructure.
  • The company is exploring new business opportunities in the Government’s e-Governance initiatives, which it expects to drive significant revenue growth in upcoming quarters.
  • The company plans to bid for new upcoming large-scale Government projects, citing over three decades of system integration capabilities and experience executing large-scale field-level projects.
  • Government programs the company is evaluating and planning to participate in include ULPIN, Aadhaar Integration with Land Records, the National Geospatial Knowledge-Based Land Survey of Urban Habitations (NAKSHA) under the land record digitalization mission (DILRMP), PM e-Drive EV Charging Station Operator (CPO), and Solar Power Project Implementation.
  • The company has executed land record digitization for the entire country of Philippines and for six districts in Uttar Pradesh, and states it is one of the few companies with the resources, experience, and bandwidth to undertake nationwide land record digitization programs.
  • The Philippines Land Titling Computerization project involved dematerializing 20 million land deeds and introducing paperless digital record keeping for 168 Registrar of Deeds offices.
  • The company has signed a Binding Term Sheet to acquire a 40% equity stake in HAL-Edgewood Technologies Private Limited (HETL), with the transaction at an advanced stage. HETL has developed patented, Make-in-India mission computers for military aircraft.
  • The company is evaluating business interests in infrastructure, renewable energy, electronics, software, hardware, and aeronautical systems for Aviation, Aerospace, Defense, and Satellite projects, either through acquisition or JV opportunity.
  • For PM e-Drive, the scheme was introduced by the Ministry of Heavy Industries in December 2024 with an outlay of ₹10,900 crores, focusing on public EV charging stations across cities and highways with up to 80% subsidies for upstream infrastructure.
  • For solar, the company aims to enter the solar power sector by engaging with solar power OEMs/EPCs through JVs or Consortiums.
  • The company plans to leverage the last-mile execution capabilities of Vakrangee Ltd through its Master franchisee and kendra network.
  • The company’s project experience includes computerization of the Election Commission (generated 100 million voters’ identification cards and managed over 100,000 citizen facilitation centers), UIDAI Aadhaar enrolment (more than 5 crore residents enrolled, around 4,000 enrolment centers), National Population Register digitization (data digitization of 145 million resident records across 27 zones and 1 UT), MCA-21, Passport Seva Kendra (77 PSKs), RSBY, smart-card based PDS in Haryana, and IGRS.
