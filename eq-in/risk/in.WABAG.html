WABAG: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • GST demand order (Uttar Pradesh): A demand order dated March 06, 2026 from the Office of the Deputy Commissioner, GST Commissionerate, Uttar Pradesh alleged an excess ITC claim for FY 2019-20 amounting to INR 43,73,666 under Section 74 of the CGST Act. A rectification order dated June 19, 2026 quashed the original demand and reduced it to INR 1,97,880, which the Company stated it would discharge.
  • Selective bidding approach: Management stated that the Company “persisted with our selective approach to bidding for projects,” indicating deliberate restraint in order intake rather than pursuing all available opportunities.
  • Forex volatility: Consolidated results for Q1 FY27 show a forex gain of INR 368 Mn, compared with a forex loss of INR 136 Mn in Q1 FY26 — a swing that flattered reported profitability relative to the prior-year quarter. Standalone figures show a similar pattern (forex gain of INR 289 Mn versus a loss of INR 103 Mn).
  • O&M share below medium-term target: O&M contributed 18% of revenues in Q1 FY27 against a medium-term outlook of 20%.
  • RoCE below target: RoCE stood at 19.6% in Q1 FY27 against a medium-term outlook of greater than 20%.
  • Invalid votes at the 31st AGM: Resolution 4 (remuneration payable to Mr. Rohan Mittal, a Related Party holding office or place of profit) recorded 2 invalid votes totalling 9,709,406 shares, all in the Promoter and Promoter Group category. The resolution nonetheless passed with 99.7629% of valid votes in favour.
  • Dissent on director appointment: Resolution 3 (appointment of Mr. S. Varadarajan as a Director liable to retire by rotation) saw 5.8584% of valid votes against, with Public Institutions voting 15.0902% against — the highest opposition of the six resolutions.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record order book: Order book reached approximately INR 194 Bn (INR 193,942 Mn) as of Q1 FY27, described as an all-time high and providing revenue visibility exceeding 4x revenue. Order intake in Q1 FY27 was INR 34,313 Mn.
  • Strong Q1 FY27 financial performance: Consolidated revenue from operations rose 20.8% YoY to INR 8,868 Mn; EBITDA rose 21.7% to INR 1,163 Mn; PAT rose 36.9% to INR 901 Mn. Standalone revenue rose 15.5% to INR 7,393 Mn; EBITDA rose 21.7% to INR 1,050 Mn; PAT rose 30.2% to INR 793 Mn.
  • Geographic expansion: The Company forayed into Kuwait with a “Mega” 60 MIGD (~272 MLD) SWRO order from MEWRE, and entered the UAE market with a “Large” 60 MLD order from Ajman Sewerage. In Europe, it secured a “Large” order for expansion of the Donauinsel Water Works in Vienna, Austria. In India, it won new orders with BWSSB (two energy-efficient WWTPs in Bengaluru) and DJB (17 MGD WWTP at Mitraon, Delhi).
  • Net cash position: Net cash (excluding HAM) stood at INR 9,649 Mn, extending the positive net cash streak to the 14th consecutive quarter. Gross cash position was INR 10,823 Mn.
  • Segment growth: EPC order backlog grew 38% YoY; O&M order backlog grew 26% YoY. EPC revenue grew 27% YoY; O&M revenue grew 7% YoY.
  • New business verticals: The Company is pursuing opportunities in PV Solar, semiconductors, data centres and green hydrogen, positioning itself as a complete water solution provider from raw water treatment to UPW to ETP/ZLD with long-term O&M. It secured a mega desalination order from the PV Solar sector and a breakthrough order for UPW, ETP and ZLD solutions for a solar cell manufacturing facility.
  • Bio-gas/CBG initiative: A strategic tie-up with Peak Sustainability Ventures to establish 100 CBG plants; a Bio-Gas Upgradation order for CBG production on a BOT model has been received and mobilisation has commenced.
  • Digitalisation: The Koyambedu TTRO Plant is powered with an operational intelligence platform (AI); an AI/ML-based NRW reduction solution is being piloted in GNN TTRO; an AI-based Operations & Decision Support System has been piloted in the AMAS Plant in Bahrain.
  • Blue Seed Initiative: First investment committed to Bengaluru-based Nimble Vision, an IoT water-infrastructure solutions provider; other pilot investment opportunities are being explored.
  • Awards and recognition: Global Water Awards 2026 (Wastewater Project of the Year for the Digha & Kankarbagh Integrated Wastewater Project in Patna); Asia Business Leader Awards 2026; National CSR & ESG Summit 2026; CII–SR Climate Action Competition Awards; Most Preferred Workplace 2026–27 for the fourth consecutive year.
  • Credit rating: AA-/Stable and A1+.

Broker Narrative

The narrative remained consistently bullish on WABAG’s order-book visibility and international expansion, with the order book scaling from Rs120bn/4x FY23 to a record Rs19,400cr/4.8x FY26 and Middle East exposure rising to ~35-40%. Execution delays were flagged in the first report and still appear in the last, but the later narrative added receivables/working-capital and margin pressure as live concerns. The margin story inverted: early reports expected ~170bps improvement over two years, while Q1FY27 showed gross/EBITDA margin contraction, with the buy case relying on management’s FY27 margin recovery guidance. Overall, optimism shifted from domestic municipal/desalination demand drivers toward international, O&M-led recurring revenue and balance-sheet strength.

Fears that came true

  • Execution delays materialized as a persistent risk: they were flagged in 2023, still listed in the 2026 report, and coincided with the 2025-02-11 DISAPPOINTMENT (-6.9% actual) and later margin pressure.
  • The flagged exposure to high operating costs materialized as cost inflation: Q1FY27 material cost rose to 76.2% of revenue and gross margin contracted 490 bps YoY, while forex-neutral EBITDA margin fell to 9.0% from 14.9%.

Optimism that failed

  • The expected ~170bps margin expansion over two years failed to materialize; instead, Q1FY27 gross margin contracted 490bps YoY and EBITDA margin declined to 9.0%.
  • The assumption that reduced commodity prices would support margins failed to hold, as material costs rose to 76.2% of revenue in Q1FY27.
