TATACONSUM — Q1 FY27 Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

India Tea: Volumes grew only 2% in the quarter, and revenue declined 4% as the benefit of lower tea costs was passed on to consumers. An unusually strong summer impacted category growth.

International (UK): The UK business declined 2% in constant currency, impacted by an intense summer that drove a slowdown in the everyday black tea category. Canada revenue was flat.

Non-Branded Business: Revenue declined 7% (10% in constant currency) as a sharp fall in global coffee prices impacted realisations. Average Robusta prices were down 26%. Solubles revenue fell 12% and plantations revenue fell 8%. Segment results for the non-branded business declined 24%, with segment margin falling to 9.9% from 12.0%.

Cost Pressures: Elevated coffee cost in the US, inflationary pressure on key input costs, and higher investments behind brands weighed on operating performance. In salt, calibrated price increases were actioned to offset input cost inflation.

International Margin: International Business segment margin declined to 13.0% from 13.5% year-on-year.

Investor Meeting Schedule: The schedule for investor meetings may change due to unforeseen circumstances involving the Analysts, Investors, or the Company.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Consolidated Performance: Revenue grew 12% to ₹5,349 crore, with India branded business delivering 13% underlying volume growth. Consolidated EBITDA grew 19% to ₹730 crore, with margins expanding 70 bps to 13.6%. Group Net Profit grew 29% to ₹427 crore.

Growth Businesses: ‘Growth businesses’ grew 47% year-on-year and scaled to 36% of the India business. This includes Tata Sampann (+58%), RTD (+41%), and Capital Foods and Organic India (+35% combined).

Tata Sampann: Revenue grew 58%, broad-based across categories, led by dry fruits, cold-pressed oils, and core pulses and spices. The brand strengthened its spices presence by building a complete whole spices portfolio. Innovation-to-sales was 8%.

Ready-to-Drink: Revenue grew 41%, driven by 35% volume growth, with broad-based portfolio growth and continued premiumisation. Two variants were introduced under Kombucha Zero (Cranberry and Pineapple) to strengthen the zero-sugar beverages portfolio.

Capital Foods and Organic India: Delivered growth of 40% and 27% respectively, with both domestic and export businesses performing well. Health & wellness and convenience-focused launches continue to expand the addressable market. GTM restructuring initiatives are showing encouraging early results.

Salt: Volumes and revenue grew 7%. Value-added salts delivered volume growth of 13%.

Coffee: India coffee grew 24% during the quarter.

International: Revenue grew 16% (3% in constant currency), with the US business delivering 7% constant currency growth. Teapigs and Good Earth continued to gain share in Specialty and Fruit & Herbal segments. Canada value share improved across regular and specialty.

Tata Starbucks: Revenue grew 11% with strong same-store sales growth and margin expansion driven by operating leverage. Four new cafés were opened, including two Reserve stores in Kolkata and New Delhi. The quarter ended with 498 stores. Starbucks Rewards was relaunched to drive engagement and visit frequency.

Innovation: 14 new products were launched during the quarter, with a strong innovation pipeline in place for the rest of the year.

Sustainability: Tata Consumer Products earned a place on the Dow Jones Sustainability World Index.

Capital Foods Stake Acquisition: The company acquired the residual stake held by Wildflower Private Trust in Capital Foods Private Limited on September 1, 2026, representing 5% of its total share capital. The company now holds 27,95,533 equity shares of ₹10 each, representing 80% of the paid-up equity share capital of Capital Foods.

Guidance/Forward-Looking Statements:

  • A strong innovation pipeline is in place for the rest of the year, with a roadmap to fuel the growth agenda.
  • The company intends to participate in investor meetings including the 33rd CITIC CLSA Investors’ Forum 2026 on September 21, 2026, and the BofA Asia Pacific Conference 2026 on September 22, 2026, in Hong Kong, with no Unpublished Price Sensitive Information proposed to be shared.

Observed Performance vs. Prior Period:

  • Consolidated EBITDA grew 19% with margins expanding 70 bps to 13.6%.
  • Group Net Profit grew 29%.
  • Growth businesses scaled to 36% of the India business, up 47% year-on-year.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative evolved from early optimism about distribution-driven scalability and high-growth category expansion (NourishCo, Tata Sampann, Tata Soulfull) toward concerns about coffee price volatility and regulatory tightening. International business margin pressure persisted as a theme but inverted—from expected recovery in FY23 to renewed damage from CY25 coffee price spikes—while core tea/coffee market share challenges remained unresolved. Distribution and innovation tailwinds were reframed around GTM overhaul and health/wellness focus in the last report, but the fundamental growth concerns in commoditized categories never fully dissipated.

Fears that came true

  • International Business Margin Pressure materialized in the last report as Arabica/Robusta prices surged 51%/10% in CY25, directly impacting the U.S. business and correlating with repeated DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes from 2024 onward.
  • Subdued Growth in Core Tea persisted as the broader pattern of broker DISAPPOINTMENT across 2024–2026 suggests value and volume growth in tea never recovered to long-term averages.
  • Commoditized Categories concern worsened as non-branded business revenue declined reflecting lower global coffee prices, confirming the difficulty of premiumization in core platforms.

Optimism that failed

  • Margin Recovery Potential failed as commodity prices did not sustainably soften—instead spiking sharply in CY25—directly contradicting the expected sequential margin improvements in the international business.
  • Strong Traction in High-Growth Categories (NourishCo ~50% CAGR, Tata Sampann 30% CAGR) did not translate into sustained stock outperformance given the overwhelming DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes across 53 reports.
  • Balance Sheet Strength & Restructuring value creation from the Tata Coffee merger did not materialize as a catalyst, as the stock consistently missed broker targets throughout the coverage period.
  • Starbucks Store Expansion, touted as a growth accelerator, failed to meaningfully offset persistent headwinds given the deep negative actual returns relative to predictions.
