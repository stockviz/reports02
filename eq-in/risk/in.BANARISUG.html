Headwinds and Challenges

The quarter ended 30 June 2026 was a loss-making period across most businesses. Total income fell to ₹17,841.99 lakh from ₹42,250.94 lakh in the quarter ended 30 June 2025, and from ₹30,927.40 lakh in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. The company reported a net loss of ₹1,091.54 lakh for the quarter, compared with net profit of ₹1,524.83 lakh a year earlier and ₹4,157.51 lakh in the preceding quarter. Loss per share was ₹8.70, against earnings per share of ₹12.16 in the June 2025 quarter and ₹33.15 in the March 2026 quarter.

Segment performance shows weakness across the portfolio. Sugar segment revenue dropped to ₹15,015.17 lakh from ₹37,694.77 lakh a year earlier, and the segment swung to a loss before tax and finance costs of ₹567.66 lakh from a profit of ₹2,606.34 lakh. Power segment revenue fell sharply to ₹239.09 lakh from ₹1,724.09 lakh, with a segment loss of ₹902.37 lakh against a loss of ₹278.40 lakh a year earlier. Distillery revenue declined to ₹2,022.21 lakh from ₹4,710.57 lakh, turning to a segment loss of ₹430.57 lakh from a profit of ₹108.83 lakh. Granite Products revenue fell to ₹222.77 lakh from ₹600.61 lakh, with the segment loss widening to ₹88.25 lakh from ₹40.40 lakh.

The cost structure amplified the revenue decline. Changes in inventories of finished goods, work-in-progress and stock-in-trade contributed ₹11,028.90 lakh to expenses in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a negative contribution of ₹1,034.79 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹24,986.05 lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Total expenses of ₹19,288.06 lakh exceeded total income, producing a loss before tax of ₹1,446.07 lakh.

A cyber security incident occurred at the Distillery Unit in Tamil Nadu on 27 June 2026 at around 4.40 P.M. IST. The company stated the attack was addressed immediately and there was no impact on routine operations of the unit. The details were received from CERT-In under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company is seeking to alter its object clauses in the Memorandum of Association to expand and align with modern business activities. The proposed scope includes manufacturing and processing of various sugar products, molasses, power plants, fertilizers, granites, mining, storage facilities, and agricultural development. This indicates a broadening of the business perimeter beyond current operations.

The company remains a single-entity structure with no subsidiary, associate, or joint venture for the three months ended 30 June 2026, which simplifies consolidation and governance.

The 42nd Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, 23 September 2026 at 4.15 P.M. through Video Conferencing or Other Audio Visual Means. The cut-off date for payment of dividend and voting is 16 September 2026, and remote e-voting is available from 19 September 2026 to 22 September 2026. The presence of a dividend item on the agenda indicates a distribution to shareholders is being proposed.

Key Risks

Revenue concentration in sugar is significant. Sugar segment revenue of ₹15,015.17 lakh accounted for the bulk of total segment revenue of ₹17,499.24 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, and the segment’s swing to a loss drove the overall loss before tax. Power and distillery, which are typically downstream diversification segments, also recorded losses, reducing the cushion from non-sugar activities.

The sharp quarter-on-quarter and year-on-year volatility in revenue and profitability is a material risk. Revenue from operations fell from ₹41,862.05 lakh in the June 2025 quarter to ₹17,244.99 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, and from ₹28,229.24 lakh in the March 2026 quarter. The changes in inventory line moved from a negative ₹1,034.79 lakh in March 2026 to a positive ₹11,028.90 lakh in June 2026, indicating significant swings in stock movement that affect reported results.

The cyber security incident at the Distillery Unit on 27 June 2026 represents an operational and information technology risk. Although the company stated there was no impact on routine operations, the incident was reported to CERT-In and disclosed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

Segment assets declined in sugar, power, and distillery compared with the June 2025 quarter, while unallocated assets rose to ₹31,382.88 lakh from ₹4,840.50 lakh. Total segment liabilities fell to ₹20,567.48 lakh from ₹25,915.61 lakh a year earlier, with the largest decline in unallocated liabilities.
