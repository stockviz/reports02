Headwinds and Challenges

  • Government and institutional tender dependence: Cancellation, delays, or lack of conversion of government and institutional tenders is the central headwind, and it has already translated into a significant revenue decline from ₹7,742.54 lakhs to ₹4,789.32 lakhs and a Profit After Tax decline from ₹483.10 lakhs to ₹106.06 lakhs.
  • Competitive pressure: The company faces competition from both organized and unorganized manufacturers.
  • Non-compliant and low-cost products: The availability of low-cost and non-compliant products in the market adds pricing and compliance pressure.
  • Input cost volatility: Raw material and component price volatility affects the business.
  • Servicing and calibration requirements: Products require regular servicing and calibration, adding ongoing operational obligations.
  • Technological change: Rapid technological changes require continuous adaptation.
  • Statutory and secretarial non-compliances: Specific statutory and secretarial non-compliances have resulted in penalties.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • New tender orders: Three tender orders aggregating approximately ₹15 crore were received in March and April 2026.
  • Drone manufacturing vertical: The DGCA Type Certificate received on 9 June 2026 for the ‘NADR10’ agricultural spraying drone opens a new manufacturing vertical.
  • GATC approval: Approval as a Government Approved Test Centre (GATC) under the Legal Metrology Act is a growth enabler.
  • Financial strength: A net-debt-free balance sheet with robust cash flows supports the business.
  • Sector expansion: Expanding presence in retail, healthcare, jewellery, and industrial sectors broadens the addressable market.
  • Policy support: Government initiatives such as Make in India, Atmanirbhar Bharat, and Digital India provide a supportive backdrop.

Key Risks

  • Revenue and profitability remain exposed to the timing and conversion of government and institutional tenders, as demonstrated by the FY2025-26 decline.
  • Competitive intensity from organized and unorganized manufacturers, combined with low-cost and non-compliant products, could pressure margins and market share.
  • Raw material and component price volatility could affect cost stability.
  • Rapid technological changes could render existing offerings less competitive if the company does not keep pace.
  • The need for regular servicing and calibration creates recurring operational and cost requirements.
  • Statutory and secretarial non-compliances have already resulted in penalties and represent a compliance risk.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management remains cautiously optimistic for FY27, citing the three tender orders worth approximately ₹15 crore received in March and April 2026 and other tenders at advanced stages.
  • This forward-looking optimism follows an FY2025-26 performance in which total revenue was ₹4,789.32 lakhs and Profit After Tax was ₹106.06 lakhs, both lower than the previous year due to subdued government tender business.
  • The company is prioritizing commercialization and marketing of the NADR10 agricultural drone, expansion of its sales and service network, enhancement of its core electronic weighing scale and security offerings, and sustainable long-term value creation.
  • The balance sheet remains net-debt-free with a net worth of ₹82.33 crore and cash equivalents of ₹3.31 crore, and 100% of equity shares are dematerialized.
  • The Board approved the Annual Report for FY2025-26 on September 4, 2026, and scheduled the 27th Annual General Meeting for September 28, 2026, at 1.00 PM via video conferencing/audio-visual means, with the register of members and share transfer books closed from September 22, 2026, to September 28, 2026, and CA Yash Goyal appointed as scrutinizer.
