PREMIERPOL — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • GST litigation overhang. As at June 30, 2026, the company has ongoing disputes with GST authorities regarding tax classification and applicable rates for certain products. The total demand stood at ₹183 lakhs as of March 31, 2025, and was contested before the Appellate Authority. Following partial relief, the revised demand stands at ₹98.58 lakhs. The company filed an appeal dated May 26, 2026 with the GST Appellate Tribunal to contest the balance demand and seek total relief; the appeal remains pending as of the date of the results.
  • Cost pressure visible in the quarterly numbers. Total expenses for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 were ₹8,851.00 lakhs against ₹8,073.00 lakhs in the preceding quarter, with cost of materials consumed rising to ₹4,961.00 lakhs from ₹4,186.00 lakhs. Profit before tax was ₹1,214.00 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter versus ₹1,167.00 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, indicating that the sequential increase in profit was modest relative to the increase in costs.
  • Finance costs. Finance costs were ₹118.00 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, unchanged from ₹118.00 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and marginally higher than ₹117.00 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year growth in income and profit. Total income for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was ₹10,065.00 lakhs compared with ₹7,497.00 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Net profit for the period was ₹908.00 lakhs versus ₹600.00 lakhs a year earlier. Earnings per share (basic and diluted, continuing and discontinued operations) was ₹0.87 for the June 2026 quarter against ₹0.57 for the June 2025 quarter.
  • Sequential improvement. Net profit rose to ₹908.00 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹858.00 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, and total income rose to ₹10,065.00 lakhs from ₹9,240.00 lakhs.
  • Full-year FY2025-26 performance. For the year ended March 31, 2026, total income was ₹34,126.00 lakhs, profit before tax was ₹4,280.00 lakhs, net profit was ₹3,188.00 lakhs, and EPS was ₹3.04.
  • Dividend proposal. A dividend for the financial year 2025-2026 is proposed to be considered at the AGM, with the record date fixed as Thursday, September 17, 2026 to determine eligible shareholders. The AGM notice covers a 15% dividend declaration (₹0.15 per share).
  • Strengthened balance sheet indicators. Reserves excluding revaluation reserves rose to ₹13,661.00 lakhs as at June 30, 2026 from ₹10,728.00 lakhs as at March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2025. The debt-equity ratio improved to 0.10 as at June 30, 2026 from 15.35 as at March 31, 2026 and 3.70 as at June 30, 2025. Interest service coverage ratio improved to 61.65 from 51.64 (March 2026) and 35.02 (June 2025).
  • Single-segment focus. The company is mainly engaged in manufacturing and sale of Flexible PVC Flooring, Film and Sheets, and the operations are considered a single business product, so segment reporting is not applicable.
