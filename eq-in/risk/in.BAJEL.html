Bajel Projects: Recent Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

Bajel Projects is carrying a meaningful receivables dispute burden. Arbitration proceedings are ongoing or have been invoked against three customers, covering recovery of outstanding balances plus interest on delayed payments and losses suffered. The total outstanding balance tied to these proceedings stood at INR 8,595.50 lakhs as at June 30, 2026. This represents capital locked in litigation rather than in operations.

The company’s growth model also depends on related-party execution. Its participation in the Lakadia transmission scheme runs through AnantGrid Projects One Private Limited (AGPOPL), in which Bajel holds only a 26% stake while NIIF holds 74%. The associated EPC opportunity is being routed through a materially related party transaction requiring member approval via postal ballot, with proposed transactions with AGPOPL and/or its wholly-owned subsidiary Lakadia A Power Transmission Limited totaling up to ₹1,535 Crores. This exceeds the annual materiality threshold of 10% of consolidated turnover, meaning the company’s forward order pipeline is concentrated in a counterparty that requires shareholder sanction and is not wholly owned.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The clearest tailwind is the scale of opportunity in Indian power transmission. The company points to increased project opportunities in the sector, and its collaboration with National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF) and AnantGrid Private Limited is positioned to pursue Tariff Based Competitive Bidding (TBCB) projects, build a high-quality order book, and support India’s target of 500 GW of renewable energy capacity by 2030.

That collaboration was formalized in March 2026, when NIIF signed an agreement with Bajel and AnantGrid Private Limited to jointly pursue power transmission opportunities through the TBCB route. The arrangement combines NIIF’s investment and asset management capabilities, AnantGrid’s project development and management expertise, and Bajel’s engineering, procurement and construction strengths.

The collaboration has already produced a concrete win. On 20 August 2026, AGPOPL received a Letter of Intent from PFC Consulting Limited declaring it successful bidder for the interstate transmission scheme covering evacuation of power from Lakadia (Phase-II: 7.5GW), Jam Khambhaliya (Phase-II: 5.5GW) and Jamnagar (Phase-I: 1GW) – Part-A. AGPOPL had emerged L1 after an e-reverse auction held on 6–7 August 2026. The project involves approximately 270 Km of 765 KV double-circuit transmission lines from Halvad to Vadodara in Gujarat, plus additional works at three substations: Halvad, Vadodara and South Olpad.

Separately, on 12 August 2026, Bajel was awarded an Ultra Mega EPC order by PowerGrid Corporation of India Limited, placed on behalf of its SPV WR ER Part A Power Transmission Limited. The scope covers Transmission Line Package TL05, including bypassing of the Raigarh (Tamnar) – Dharamjaygarh (Sec-B) 765kV D/C line and the Raigarh (Kotra) – Raigarh (Tamnar) 765kV D/C line at Raigarh (Tamnar) substation to form the Raigarh (Kotra) – Dharamjaygarh (Sec-B) 765kV D/C line; LILO of the Dharamjaygarh (Sec-B) – Jharsuguda (Sec-A) 765kV D/C line at Raigarh (Kotra)-II substation; and the Raigarh (Tamnar) – Raigarh (Kotra)-II substation 765kV D/C line. This is associated with the WR-ER Inter-Regional Network Expansion Scheme-Part A under the TBCB route. The contract is domestic, on an EPC basis, with a timeline of 33 months from issuance of the Notification of Award. Neither promoter/promoter group nor group companies have any interest in the awarding entity, and the order does not fall within related party transactions.

The company also deepened its equity link to the transmission platform, acquiring a 26% equity stake of Rs. 7.03 lakhs in Anant Grid Projects One Pvt. Ltd. during the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Key Risks
  • Counterparty and receivable risk: Arbitration against three customers over INR 8,595.50 lakhs of outstanding balances as at June 30, 2026 signals collection risk and potential earnings volatility from delayed payments.
  • Related-party concentration: The proposed transactions with AGPOPL and Lakadia A Power Transmission Limited, totaling up to ₹1,535 Crores, exceed the 10% consolidated turnover materiality threshold and require member approval through postal ballot with remote e-voting. Execution depends on this approval and on the counterparty’s project progress.
  • Minority position in the transmission vehicle: Bajel holds 26% of AGPOPL while NIIF holds 74%, so the Lakadia award accrues to an associate rather than a wholly-owned entity, with EPC work flowing back to Bajel through a related-party route.
  • Project execution risk: The PGCIL order carries a 33-month timeline from Notification of Award, and the Lakadia project involves roughly 270 Km of 765 KV double-circuit lines plus works at three substations — large, schedule-sensitive infrastructure scopes.
  • Dependence on policy-driven demand: The growth thesis rests on TBCB-route opportunities and India’s 500 GW renewable target, both of which are policy and bidding-cycle dependent.
Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Management’s forward-looking statements center on the NIIF-AnantGrid collaboration and the expectation that proposed related-party transactions with AGPOPL and/or Lakadia A Power Transmission Limited, totaling up to ₹1,535 Crores, will exceed the applicable materiality threshold. These transactions are intended to be undertaken in one or more tranches, on an arm’s length basis, and in the ordinary course of business.

Observed performance against that guidance is mixed but directionally supportive. The collaboration signed in March 2026 has already translated into a successful bid — AGPOPL’s LoI for the Lakadia Part-A scheme on 20 August 2026, following the 6–7 August 2026 e-reverse auction — which is tangible evidence that the partnership is converting into pipeline. The standalone PGCIL Ultra Mega order of 12 August 2026 shows the core EPC business continues to win work independently of the NIIF relationship.

On financial performance, for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the company reported total income of Rs. 57,195.43 lakhs and a net profit of Rs. 454.78 lakhs on a standalone basis. Against that income base, the ₹1,535 Crores related-party proposal is materially significant, consistent with management’s statement that it exceeds the 10% consolidated turnover threshold.

On governance and disclosure cadence, the company has scheduled an analyst and institutional investor meeting with DMZ Partner Investment Management LLP on September 16, 2026, in Mumbai as a virtual/one-on-one meeting, with no unpublished price sensitive information to be shared. Management notes the schedule is subject to change due to exigencies. Separately, the Nomination & Remuneration Committee approved the grant of 2,84,000 stock options under the ESOP 2024 scheme to 7 eligible employees at an exercise price of ₹116.07 per option.
