PAR Drugs and Chemicals — AGM 2026 Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates in a competitive environment for Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and Fine Chemicals, and its disclosures identify several pressures that can affect operating margins and profitability from time to time:

  • Pricing pressures in its product markets.
  • Fluctuations in input costs.
  • Changing customer requirements.
  • Regulatory requirements.
  • Prevailing market conditions.

The financial indicators disclosed in the Schedule V statements show a year-on-year softening in profitability for FY 2025-26 versus FY 2024-25: revenue from operations rose to ₹10,365.56 lakh from ₹10,097.35 lakh, but profit before tax declined to ₹1,730.16 lakh from ₹1,792.29 lakh, profit after tax declined to ₹1,310.86 lakh from ₹1,335.79 lakh, and EPS declined to ₹10.65 from ₹10.86. The company also notes that in any year of loss or inadequate profits, remuneration to its managing director, CEO and independent director would be payable as minimum remuneration under Schedule V, with requisite approvals.

On the shareholder-servicing side, the company has flagged unclaimed dividend and IEPF-related consequences: shares of shareholders who have not encashed the interim dividend declared for FY 2019-20 for seven consecutive years are liable to be transferred to the IEPF Authority if not claimed, with the company stating it has sent individual intimations. It also notes that transfers of securities in physical form will not be processed, requiring dematerialisation.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company’s stated improvement measures centre on:

  • Strengthening sales and marketing initiatives.
  • Expanding customer relationships and improving market penetration to increase sales and operating performance.
  • Cost optimisation, operational efficiency, prudent working-capital management and effective utilisation of resources.

Management expects these measures to support sustainable improvement in margins and profitability, and to contribute to improvement in productivity and profitability over the medium to long term, while stating that the precise impact cannot be determined with certainty given external factors.

The board has also revisited its investment approach: after reviewing the investment decision taken at the board meeting dated 14.05.2026, it decided on 22.08.2026 that the company can appoint any portfolio manager for investment purposes when in the best interest of the company, and authorised Mr. Falgun V. Savani (Chairman & Managing Director) or Mr. Jignesh Vallabhbhai Savani (CEO & Director) to decide where to make investments and to appoint a portfolio manager at any point of time.

Governance and compliance actions during the year include receipt and approval of the Secretarial Audit Report for the year ended March 31, 2026, approval of the Directors’ Report for FY 2025-26, approval of the cost audit report for FY 2025-26 with authorisation to file the required form with the Registrar of Companies, and authorisation to certify and file e-Form AOC-4 XBRL and MGT-7 for FY 2025-26.

3. Key Risks

  • Profitability sensitivity: The company attributes potential margin and profitability impact to competitive intensity, market conditions, input-cost fluctuations, customer requirements and regulatory developments, and states the precise impact of its improvement measures cannot be determined with certainty.
  • Remuneration approvals: The increases in remuneration for the Managing Director and CEO (₹64,00,000 per annum each for two years from 26.11.2026 to 25.11.2028) and the independent director remuneration (₹24,00,000 for one year from 01.10.2026 to 30.09.2027) require shareholder approval by special resolution; the independent director’s remuneration exceeds 50% of total remuneration payable to all non-executive directors, triggering Regulation 17(6)(ca) approval. In loss or inadequate-profit years, minimum remuneration under Schedule V would apply, subject to Central Government approval if required.
  • Related-party concentration in resolutions: Mr. Falgun Vallabhbhai Savani (21.50% shareholding) and Mr. Jignesh Vallabhbhai Savani (21.88% shareholding) are brothers and are deemed interested in each other’s remuneration resolutions; Mr. Pravin Manjibhai Bhayani’s spouse holds 6,000 equity shares.
  • Investment discretion: Authority delegated to two executives to decide investments and appoint portfolio managers at any point of time carries execution and oversight risk.
  • Shareholder compliance risk: Non-claiming of dividends and non-dematerialisation of physical shares can result in transfer of shares to the IEPF and inability to transfer physical securities.

4. Management Guidance vs Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management expects continued focus on sales growth, operational efficiency, cost optimisation and prudent resource utilisation to contribute to improvement in productivity and profitability over the medium to long term. It explicitly states the precise impact cannot be determined with certainty.
  • Observed performance (FY 2025-26 vs FY 2024-25): Revenue from operations increased (₹10,365.56 lakh vs ₹10,097.35 lakh), but profit before tax, profit after tax and EPS all declined (PBT ₹1,730.16 lakh vs ₹1,792.29 lakh; PAT ₹1,310.86 lakh vs ₹1,335.79 lakh; EPS ₹10.65 vs ₹10.86). The improvement in profitability that management expects over the medium to long term had not yet shown up in the FY 2025-26 indicators disclosed.
  • Remuneration vs performance: Past remuneration disclosures show Mr. Falgun Savani and Mr. Jignesh Savani each received ₹51.50 lakh in each of 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26, with sitting fees of ₹0.60/₹0.70/₹0.50 lakh and ₹0.35/₹0.40/₹0.30 lakh respectively. The proposed increase to ₹64 lakh per annum each follows a year in which revenue grew but profits and EPS declined. Mr. Pravin Bhayani received remuneration of ₹12.00 lakh (for the period 01.10.2023 to 31.03.2024) and ₹24.00 lakh in each of 2024-25 and 2025-26, with sitting fees of ₹0.90/₹1.00/₹0.75 lakh; the proposed ₹24 lakh for 01.10.2026–30.09.2027 is unchanged from the two preceding years.
  • Board attendance: Mr. Jignesh Vallabhbhai Savani attended 5 of 5 board meetings during the year, per the Annexure-A disclosure.
