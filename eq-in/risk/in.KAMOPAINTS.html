KAMOPAINTS: Recent Corporate Developments and Business Performance

Headwinds and Challenges

The company’s most recent quarterly performance reveals significant pressure on profitability. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30th June, 2026), revenue from operations declined 8% year-on-year to Rs. 46.3 crores from Rs. 50.2 crores in Q1 FY26. Gross profit fell 22% to Rs. 18.8 crores, with gross profit margin contracting to 40.5% from 47.6%. The company reported negative EBITDA of Rs. 2.1 crores (negative 4.4% margin) compared to positive EBITDA of Rs. 3.3 crores (6.6% margin) in the prior year period. Profit after tax was negative Rs. 4.4 crores versus positive Rs. 0.9 crores a year earlier.

The subsidiary, Kamdhenu Colour and Coatings Limited, showed similar trends with revenue declining 10% to Rs. 45.4 crores and negative EBITDA of Rs. 1.9 crores.

On a full-year basis, FY26 revenue of Rs. 245.3 crores declined from Rs. 266.1 crores in FY25 and Rs. 291.7 crores in FY24. EBITDA margin compressed to 3.9% in FY26 from 6.3% in FY25 and 7.7% in FY24. Profit after tax fell to Rs. 0.2 crores in FY26 from Rs. 6.7 crores in FY25 and Rs. 13.9 crores in FY24.

The balance sheet shows trade receivables of Rs. 147.0 crores as of March 2026, up from Rs. 137.5 crores a year earlier, representing a significant portion of total assets of Rs. 286.6 crores. Inventories stood at Rs. 56.0 crores.

The company also experienced a change in its Company Secretary and Compliance Officer, with Mr. Rohit resigning effective from close of business on 13th August, 2026, due to personal exigencies. Mr. Ankit was appointed as the new Company Secretary and Compliance Officer effective 14th August, 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company operates in India’s decorative paints segment under the Kamdhenu Paints brand, with a product portfolio spanning exterior and interior emulsions, primers, wood finishes, textured and designer paints, and construction chemicals across 40+ SKUs.

The company’s dealer network currently stands at 4,460+ dealers out of an estimated 50,000+ dealers in India. Management indicates that penetration can be tripled in five years through new dealer additions across regions. The current dealer proportion in regions outside North and East India is higher than the revenue contribution from those regions, which the company views as providing scope for volume expansion on existing investments.

Growth strategies outlined include product premiumization, brand equity investments, increased capacity, increased dealer density, targeting urban markets through institutional sales, increased penetration in South India through tie-up with contract manufacturers, expanding presence pan-India, and robust hiring plans with ERP tools and IT-aided supply chain.

The company has made investments in its subsidiary, Kamdhenu Colour and Coatings Limited, through a rights issue during the quarter ended 30th June, 2026, subscribing to 3,614 equity shares at Rs. 36,000 per share, aggregating to Rs. 21,301.04 lakhs.

Key Risks

The financial results show a business that has experienced declining revenues over the past three fiscal years and a sharp deterioration in profitability in the most recent quarter. The company reported negative EBITDA, negative EBIT, and negative profit before tax in Q1 FY27.

Trade receivables represent a substantial portion of the balance sheet at Rs. 147.0 crores against total assets of Rs. 286.6 crores as of March 2026. The company’s cash flow statement shows net cash from operating activities of Rs. 10.9 crores in FY26 compared to Rs. 1.0 crores in FY25.

The company has outstanding convertible warrants: 1,50,00,000 warrants remained outstanding as at 30th June, 2026, against which Rs. 2,255.00 lakhs had been received. No further conversion was made during the quarter ended 30th June, 2026.

The company’s operations are concentrated in North and East India, with sales more concentrated in these regions owing to a denser older dealer base.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

The investor presentation outlines growth strategies including tripling dealer penetration in five years, expanding pan-India presence, and targeting urban markets through institutional sales. The company also states it is “primed to capitalise on key growth drivers with a clear and focused strategy.”

However, the observed business performance shows revenue declining from Rs. 291.7 crores in FY24 to Rs. 266.1 crores in FY25 to Rs. 245.3 crores in FY26. The Q1 FY27 results show further year-on-year revenue decline of 8% and a swing to negative EBITDA and negative profit after tax.

The company’s stated focus on premiumization is reflected in its product portfolio, but the gross margin contraction from 47.6% in Q1 FY26 to 40.5% in Q1 FY27 indicates pricing or cost pressures.

The company has scheduled its 7th Annual General Meeting for Friday, 25th September, 2026, at 3:30 P.M. (IST) through Video Conferencing/Other Audio-Visual Means. Agenda items include adopting audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for the financial year ended 31st March, 2026, re-appointing Shri Sunil Kumar Agarwal who retires by rotation, and re-appointing Shri Ramesh Chand Surana and Shri Madhusudan Agarwal as Non-Executive Independent Directors for a second term of five consecutive years.
