CSL Finance — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • SME disbursements remained slow, reflecting a tough operating environment and internal productivity challenges. The broader SME lending space also remained slow.
  • Tightened credit policy and additional checks kept SME disbursements muted.
  • Net new branch additions have been muted, due to closing non-performing branches and reshuffling or relocating others.
  • AUM growth was accompanied by a shift in mix: the portfolio mix moved to 70:30 (Wholesale:SME) as at June 2026 from 69:31 as at March 2026, indicating relative softness in the SME side of the book.
  • Profit growth lagged revenue growth: for Q1FY27, Net Interest Income rose 12% YoY while PAT rose only 4% YoY.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • AUM growth: AUM grew 15% YoY to approximately ₹1,510 crore (including DA of ₹34 crore) as at June 2026, compared with approximately ₹1,310 crore (including DA of ₹50 crore) as at June 2025. A separate update cites AUM of ₹1,505 crore, up 16% YoY.
  • Funding momentum: a fresh sanction of ₹50 crore from an existing lender, plus ₹60 crore raised through Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). A term sheet was executed for NCDs aggregating ₹150 crore to be raised in multiple tranches, of which ₹60 crore has already been raised and ₹90 crore is proposed in subsequent tranches.
  • Liquidity and capital strength: liquidity surplus of approximately ₹175 crore (including undrawn sanctions of ₹90 crore of NCD) as at June 2026, and a capital adequacy ratio (CAR) of approximately 44%.
  • Repeat sanctions from existing lenders, a credit rating reaffirmed at A- Stable, and a diversified lender base now standing at 35.
  • Segment outlook: the Wholesale segment remains robust and is expected to be the key growth driver in FY27; SME Retail is expected to return to growth over the coming year.
  • Stated opportunities in retail lending and SME lending segments, driven by structured lending models, expansion into niche markets, and strategic long-term growth objectives.
  • Distribution and team: 37 operational branches and 25 spoke branches, with a team strength of 455 employees.
  • Capital return: the board recommended a final dividend of ₹10 per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, with a record date of September 12, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Asset quality: Gross NPA was 0.95%, Net NPA was 0.60%, and the Provision Coverage Ratio stood at 123.20% for Q1FY27; collection efficiency held at 98%.
  • Concentration in the wholesale book: with the Wholesale segment positioned as the key FY27 growth driver and the mix at 70:30, growth depends materially on that segment’s continued robustness.
  • Execution risk in SME Retail: the anticipated return to growth in SME Retail depends on resolving the productivity and credit-policy constraints that muted disbursements.
  • Branch network execution: muted net branch additions from closures and relocations affect distribution capacity.
  • Related party exposure: approval is sought for material related party transactions with CSL Capital Private Limited up to ₹250 crores.
  • Governance and capital structure changes: alteration of the Articles of Association to allow debenture trustees to appoint a nominee director, alongside the re-appointment of Mr. Rohit Gupta as Managing Director for five years effective August 10, 2027 to August 09, 2032, and the re-appointment of Ms. Rachita Gupta as Whole-Time Director.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Guidance: the Wholesale segment remains healthy and is expected to be the key growth driver in FY27; SME Retail is expected to return to growth over the coming year.
  • Observed performance: AUM reached ₹1,505 crore in Q1FY27, up 16% YoY; Net Interest Income was ₹45.0 crore, up 12% YoY; PAT was ₹22.1 crore, up 4% YoY; collection efficiency was 98%; Gross NPA 0.95%; Net NPA 0.60%; Provision Coverage Ratio 123.20%; AUM mix 30:70 for SME Retail versus Wholesale.
  • Contrast: the guidance for SME Retail to return to growth sits against the reported slow SME disbursements, tightened credit policy, and muted net branch additions. The Wholesale-led growth guidance is consistent with the reported robust wholesale segment and the 70:30 mix as at June 2026.
  • Funding guidance in practice: the ₹150 crore NCD term sheet with ₹60 crore already raised and ₹90 crore proposed in subsequent tranches is being executed in line with the stated multi-tranche plan; the June 2026 update notes liquidity of approximately ₹175 crore including undrawn NCD sanctions of ₹90 crore.
  • Provisional status: the June 2026 update is stated to be provisional and subject to limited review by the statutory auditors.
