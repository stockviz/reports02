KPI Green Energy — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Profitability pressure despite top-line growth. In Q1 FY27, Total Income rose 16% year-on-year to ₹710 crore and EBITDA rose 21% to ₹262 crore, but PAT declined 14% to ₹95 crore. The company attributes this to higher depreciation (up 70% to ₹51 crore) and finance costs (up 111% to ₹80 crore) on a growing asset base, with full earnings contribution expected to materialise during the year. Basic EPS fell 18% to ₹4.34.
  • Margin dilution. EBITDA margin slipped from 35% to 37% band commentary aside, PAT margin fell from 18% to 13% year-on-year.
  • Execution constraints in the sector. Land aggregation and evacuation approvals often delay renewable execution; the company is internalising both through a larger land bank and grid access.
  • Risk of inadequate profits. The annual report notes potential risks related to absence or inadequacy of profits in any financial year, though the company does not envisage any loss or inadequate profits.
  • Capital intensity. The IPP model is capital-intensive, and the CPP model is described as lower capital intensity; the combined model is positioned to avoid continuous equity dilution.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Policy-led visibility. The 500 GW by 2030 target, a ~40 GW per annum trajectory, ISTS charge waivers for projects commissioned before 2028, GST cut on RE equipment from 12% to 5% (September 2025), must-run status for RE, and rising RPO/RCO mandates (35.95% in FY27 to ~43.33% by FY30) underpin demand.
  • Demand drivers. Record peak demand of 271 GW in May 2026, projected to reach ~345 GW by 2030; data centres driving round-the-clock renewable demand; green hydrogen mission targeting 5 MMT/yr by 2030 needing ~125 GW of dedicated RE; low per-capita consumption of ~1,460 kWh.
  • Capacity scale-up. Total portfolio rose to 6.94 GW (up 71% YoY) from 4.06 GW, with 1.87 GW installed, 5.07 GW work-in-progress, 5.10 GW power evacuation capacity, and an 8,657-acre land bank. The group targets 10+ GW by 2030.
  • IPP ramp-up. IPP portfolio at 2.57 GWp with estimated generation of 390+ crore kWh/year; Q1 FY27 generation exceeded 65% of the FY26 total and was up 4x YoY. Revenue mix shifted toward IPP at 17% versus 10% in Q1 FY26.
  • New platforms. BESS (565 MW / 1,130 MWh BESPA signed, financial closure under progress), floating solar (142 MW EPC order at Kadana Dam), and energy trading (CERC + GERC licences secured, pilot trading underway).
  • Geographic and international expansion. Footprint expanded to Rajasthan in Q1 FY27; 133 total sites; MOU with Botswana for 5 GW renewable facilities (500 MW Phase 1 planning underway); UAE alliance with Fabtech group and F Plus Healthcare Technologies for data centre, pharma, biotech and life sciences solutions.
  • Order momentum. 2.88 GW of fresh orders booked in the year, with 0.85 GW commissioned.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution risk on the 5.07 GW work-in-progress pipeline, including hybrid, solar, wind, and BESS projects at various stages of PPA signing and financial closure.
  • Financing risk, evidenced by the 111% rise in interest cost and financial closure still under process for BESS projects.
  • Counterparty and offtake concentration, with GUVNL as offtaker for most utility-scale projects and SJVN backing the 300 MW wind project.
  • Regulatory and policy dependence on ISTS waivers, RPO mandates, and viability-gap funding continuing as expected.
  • Profitability risk if depreciation and finance costs continue to outpace revenue growth.
  • Forward-looking statement risk, as the presentation itself notes known and unknown risks, uncertainties, and factors that may cause actual results to differ materially.

4. Management Guidance vs Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management is confident of achieving sustained revenue growth and has adopted focused business strategies to improve sales and profitability. It has chalked out ambitious growth plans to scale operations and profitability, supported by renewable generation presence, power evacuation infrastructure, and multi-state expansion. The group targets 10+ GW by 2030.
  • Observed performance: Q1 FY27 delivered 16% Total Income growth and 21% EBITDA growth, consistent with the growth narrative, but PAT fell 14% and EPS fell 18%, indicating that profitability improvement has not yet materialised at the bottom line. The company states full earnings contribution from the growing asset base is expected to materialise during the year.
  • Capacity trajectory: Portfolio grew from 4.06 GW to 6.94 GW in a single year, with 2.88 GW of fresh orders and 0.85 GW commissioned, supporting the scale-up guidance.
  • Generation trajectory: Q1 FY27 IPP generation exceeded 65% of the entire FY26 total, up 4x YoY, supporting the annuity earnings build-out.
  • Dividend: A final dividend of ₹0.40 per equity share, including a special dividend of ₹0.15 per equity share, was recommended for FY 2025-26, alongside proposed appointment of M/s. M S K C & Associates LLP as statutory auditors.
  • Governance: The Board approved re-appointment of Mr. Sharadchandra Patil as Non-Executive Independent Director for a second five-year term from October 06, 2026 to October 05, 2031, and amendments to the Code of Practices and Procedures for Fair Disclosure of UPSI under Regulation 8 of PIT Regulations.
  • Investor engagement: Management will participate in the DAM Capital Renewable Energy Conference on August 27, 2026 in Mumbai, referring only to publicly available documents with no UPSI intended to be discussed. The 18th AGM is scheduled for September 29, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. IST via video conferencing, with remote e-voting from September 26 to September 28, 2026 and a cut-off date of September 22, 2026.
