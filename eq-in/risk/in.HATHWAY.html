Hathway Cable and Datacom — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Cable Television segment losses. The Cable Television Business reported a segment loss of ₹16.75 crores for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, widening from ₹9.20 crores in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Including the share of profit of joint ventures and associate (net), the total Cable Television Business loss was ₹18.59 crores for the June 2026 quarter, compared with ₹16.45 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹17.59 crores in the June 2025 quarter. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the total Cable Television Business loss was ₹51.72 crores.

Rising pay channel costs. Pay Channel Cost rose to ₹319.46 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹300.76 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹268.94 crores in the June 2025 quarter. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, Pay Channel Cost was ₹1,137.17 crores.

Broadband revenue decline. Broadband Business revenue fell to ₹141.81 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹143.15 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹147.27 crores in the June 2025 quarter. Full-year Broadband revenue for FY ended March 31, 2026 was ₹581.98 crores.

Profitability pressure. Consolidated Profit Before Tax declined to ₹32.64 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹40.28 crores in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated Net Profit fell to ₹24.56 crores from ₹31.03 crores over the same comparison. Standalone Net Profit declined to ₹18.37 crores from ₹21.43 crores. Consolidated EPS (basic and diluted) declined to ₹0.14 from ₹0.18.

Depreciation and amortisation burden. Depreciation, Amortisation and Impairment was ₹75.38 crores in the June 2026 quarter, though lower than ₹84.80 crores in the June 2025 quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Cable Television revenue growth. Despite segment losses, Cable Television Business revenue grew to ₹405.51 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹391.61 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹361.83 crores in the June 2025 quarter. Full-year Cable Television revenue for FY ended March 31, 2026 was ₹1,509.48 crores.

Overall revenue growth. Consolidated Revenue from Operations rose to ₹565.10 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹545.85 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹530.50 crores in the June 2025 quarter. Full-year consolidated revenue was ₹2,149.58 crores.

Dealing in securities segment. This segment contributed ₹17.78 crores in revenue and ₹17.78 crores in segment result for the June 2026 quarter, up from ₹11.09 crores in the March 2026 quarter, though below ₹21.40 crores in the June 2025 quarter.

Broadband segment turned positive. The Broadband Business segment result was ₹0.64 crores in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a loss of ₹0.75 crores in the June 2025 quarter. Including share of profit of associate, total Broadband Business was ₹1.45 crores, up from ₹2.29 crores in the June 2025 quarter.

Improved sequential profitability. Consolidated Profit Before Tax improved to ₹32.64 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹17.08 crores in the March 2026 quarter. Consolidated Net Profit rose to ₹24.56 crores from ₹11.25 crores sequentially.

Share of joint ventures and associate returned to profit. Share of net profit of Joint Ventures and Associate was ₹0.50 crores in the June 2026 quarter, recovering from a loss of ₹5.33 crores in the March 2026 quarter, though below ₹4.00 crores in the June 2025 quarter.

Auditor transition. Deloitte Haskins & Sells Chartered Accountants LLP was appointed as Auditors for five consecutive years commencing from the conclusion of the 66th AGM until the conclusion of the 71st AGM. Deloitte will act as Joint Auditors alongside Nayan Parikh & Co. until the conclusion of the 67th AGM, then continue as sole Auditors.

3. Key Risks

Department of Telecommunications demand notices. The Group received Show Cause cum Demand notices from the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India, demanding license fees amounting to ₹3,201.93 crores (consistent at March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2025). The demand includes interest and penalty computed up to the date of respective notices. The Group has made representations to DOT contesting the basis of such demands. Based on the opinion of a legal expert, the Group is confident it has strong grounds on merit to defend itself, and no provision has been made.

At the standalone level, the Company received SCNs for financial years 2005-06 to 2020-21 demanding license fees of ₹3,160.63 crores (consistent at March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2025). The Company has made representations to DOT contesting the basis of such demands. Based on the opinion of a legal expert, the Company is confident it has strong grounds on merit to defend itself, and no provision has been made.

Segment concentration in loss-making cable business. The Cable Television Business remains loss-making at the segment level despite revenue growth, representing a continuing drag on overall profitability.

Dependence on joint ventures and associate performance. The share of profit from joint ventures and associate fluctuated significantly, from ₹4.00 crores in June 2025 to a loss of ₹5.33 crores in March 2026 to ₹0.50 crores in June 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Auditor appointment and transition. The members at the AGM held on August 19, 2026 appointed Deloitte Haskins & Sells Chartered Accountants LLP as Auditors for five consecutive years. To ensure a smooth transition and orderly handover, Deloitte shall act as Joint Auditors along with Nayan Parikh & Co. until the conclusion of the 67th AGM, thereafter continuing as Auditors until the conclusion of the 71st AGM. This transition plan was disclosed alongside the AGM results.

Cost auditor remuneration ratification. The members ratified the remuneration of Cost Auditors for the financial year ending March 31, 2027.

Director retirement by rotation. Ms. Geeta Fulwadaya (DIN: 03341926) was appointed as Director, retiring by rotation.

AGM voting outcomes. All resolutions at the 66th AGM held on August 19, 2026 were passed with requisite majority. The total number of shareholders on the record date (August 12, 2026) was 4,81,613. Promoter and Promoter Group held 1,32,75,78,375 shares (100% of outstanding shares held by them). Public Institutions held 62,50,994 shares, of which 72.5815% of votes were polled. Public Non-Institutions held 43,62,75,131 shares, of which 0.1917% of votes were polled. Invalid votes were nil across all categories.

Financial results approval. The consolidated and standalone unaudited financial results for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026 were reviewed and recommended by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at their respective meetings held on July 16, 2026. The Statutory Auditors carried out a limited review of the results.
