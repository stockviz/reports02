Headwinds and Challenges

Flood disruption at the Silvassa unit. On 23 July 2026, operations at one of the Company’s plastic processing units located at Vasona, Silvassa (DNH & DD) were stopped due to a flood. The unit resumed normal operations on 11 August 2026. The Company states the unit is adequately covered by insurance and that it is in the process of ascertaining the extent of production loss and property damage caused by the disruption. The plastic processing segment is the Company’s principal revenue-generating segment, so this event falls on its core operations.

Qualified conclusion on consolidated results. The Limited Review Report on the unaudited consolidated financial results contains a qualified conclusion, while the standalone report carries an unmodified opinion. The qualification rests on two matters:

  • The consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 do not include quarterly financial results or financial information for one associate, Urban Infrastructure Holdings Private Limited, because that information was not available to the Company.
  • Interest accrued and due of Rs. 2,147 Lakh on inter-corporate deposits given by one of the Company’s subsidiaries in earlier years, overdue for a substantial period, against which the subsidiary has initiated legal proceedings. Management of the subsidiary considers the receivables good for recovery based on the value of the parties’ assets and commitments from their promoters, and no impairment provision has been considered necessary. The auditors of the subsidiary were unable to express an opinion on the ability of the subsidiary to recover the outstanding amount and possible impacts on the subsidiary’s and the Group’s consolidated financial results. This qualification is noted as coming from earlier years.

Frozen investments at a subsidiary. During the quarter, investments in shares and mutual funds of Urban Infrastructure Venture Capital Limited, a non-material wholly-owned subsidiary, continued to remain frozen by the Directorate of Enforcement vide Order dated 19 December 2025, while investigating a case for an alleged offence of money laundering. That company is barred from transferring or otherwise dealing in these investments. The auditors included this as an Emphasis of Matter, with their conclusion not modified in respect of it.

Steel segment losses. The steel segment reported a loss at the segment result level for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, continuing a loss position in the comparable prior periods presented.

Discontinued spinning operations. The Board approved discontinuation of the Spinning Division’s operations in a phased manner at its meeting on 29 June 2020. The division continues to report small losses and is treated as discontinuing operations under Ind AS 105. Management does not envisage any material financial impact from the discontinuation.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Plastic processing remains the profit engine. The plastic processing segment generated segment revenue of Rs. 15,464 Lakh and segment results of Rs. 2,701 Lakh in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, against revenue of Rs. 13,046 Lakh and results of Rs. 1,637 Lakh in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. This segment accounts for effectively all standalone operating revenue.

Real estate segment contribution at the consolidated level. The consolidated real estate segment reported revenue of Rs. 294 Lakh and segment results of Rs. 98 Lakh for the quarter, compared with revenue of Rs. 79 Lakh and a negative result of Rs. 12 Lakh in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

Other unallocable income. Standalone other unallocable income net of unallocable expenditure was Rs. 1,164 Lakh for the quarter, supporting profit before tax. The consolidated figures show a similar contribution.

Insurance cover on the flood-affected unit. The Company states the Silvassa unit is adequately covered by insurance, which is relevant to recovery of production loss and property damage once the extent is ascertained.

Dividend returns to shareholders. The 41st Annual General Meeting agenda includes confirming a special interim dividend of 500% (Rs. 5.00 per share) and declaring a final dividend of 50% (Rs. 0.50 per share).

Key Risks

  • Uncertainty over the outcome of legal proceedings relating to the Rs. 2,147 Lakh overdue inter-corporate deposit receivables, and the absence of any impairment provision against them.
  • The inability to obtain quarterly financial information from associate Urban Infrastructure Holdings Private Limited, which limits the completeness of consolidated reporting.
  • The continuing freeze on investments of Urban Infrastructure Venture Capital Limited under the Directorate of Enforcement order dated 19 December 2025, and the associated money laundering investigation.
  • The unresolved quantum of production loss and property damage from the 23 July 2026 flood at the Silvassa plastic processing unit, notwithstanding insurance cover.
  • Continued losses in the steel segment and the phased wind-down of the spinning division.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Management’s stated position on the spinning division discontinuation is that it does not envisage any material financial impact on the Company’s or the Group’s operations. The reported discontinuing operations results are consistent with a small, contained loss: a loss before tax of Rs. 7 Lakh for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Rs. 11 Lakh for the quarter ended 31 March 2026, Rs. 7 Lakh for the quarter ended 30 June 2025, and Rs. 35 Lakh for the year ended 31 March 2026.

On the flood disruption, management has not quantified the impact; it states the unit is adequately covered by insurance and that the Company is in the process of ascertaining the extent of production loss and property damage. The unit restarted normal operations on 11 August 2026, before the 13 August 2026 board meeting at which the results were approved.

On the overdue inter-corporate deposits, management of the subsidiary maintains the receivables are good for recovery and that no impairment provision is required, citing the value of the parties’ assets and commitments from their promoters, and states the subsidiary continues its efforts to recover these receivables. The auditors were unable to express an opinion on the recoverability and possible impacts.

The Register of Members and Share Transfer Books will be closed from Tuesday, 22 September 2026 to Monday, 28 September 2026. The cut-off date for voting and participation is Monday, 21 September 2026. Remote e-voting is available from 25 September 2026 (9:00 a.m.) to 27 September 2026 (5:00 p.m.).

Business items at the AGM include adopting the financial statements, confirming a special interim dividend of 500% (Rs. 5.00 per share) and declaring a final dividend of 50% (Rs. 0.50 per share), re-appointing Mr. Virendra Jain by rotation, re-appointing Mr. Dinesh Deokinandan Paliwal as Whole-time Director (Director - Works), and ratifying the cost auditor’s remuneration.

The standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board at meetings held on 13 August 2026. The standalone limited review report carries an unmodified opinion; the consolidated report carries a qualified conclusion. The consolidated results cover 20 listed subsidiaries and two associates, Searock Developers FZC and Urban Infrastructure Holdings Private Limited. The auditors did not review the quarterly financial results of 20 subsidiaries included in the consolidated results, which reflect total revenue of Rs. 643.42 Lakh, total net profit after tax of Rs. 158.39 Lakh, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 781.87 Lakh for the quarter, these having been reviewed by other auditors.
