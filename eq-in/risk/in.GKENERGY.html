GK Energy: Recent Developments, Risks, and Guidance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Margin compression in Q1 FY27. Standalone EBITDA margin fell to 17.05% in Q1 FY27 from 19.74% in Q1 FY26 and 20.54% in Q4 FY26. PAT margin declined to 11.81% from 12.51% (Q1 FY26) and 14.11% (Q4 FY26). Cost of goods sold rose 102.91% year-on-year against revenue growth of 71.10%, and installation and project admin charges rose 39.12% year-on-year.
  • Working capital intensity. Standalone net working capital days stood at 112 in FY 2026, with debtor days of 140, described as consistent with the nature of government-linked project receivables. Consolidated net working capital days were 101 in FY 2026 versus 90 in FY 2025, with debtor days at 125 versus 120.
  • Execution timelines on new orders. The rooftop solar empanelment requires projects to be completed within 60 days from issuance of respective work orders, including five years of O&M — a compressed delivery window across 100,000 households.
  • Dependence on government schemes and policy. The business is tied to programmes such as PM KUSUM, PM Surya Ghar Yojana, and state schemes; the disclosed risk factors include government policies and actions, regulations, and fiscal conditions.
  • Other disclosed risks. Fluctuations in earnings, ability to manage growth, competition (domestic and international), economic growth in India and abroad, ability to attract and retain skilled professionals, time and cost overruns on contracts, and interest and other fiscal costs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Q1 FY27 operational momentum. Standalone revenue from operations of ₹5,051.92 million, up 71.10% year-on-year and 20.69% quarter-on-quarter; EBITDA of ₹861.14 million; PAT of ₹596.73 million, up 61.55% year-on-year. Systems installed rose 122.76% year-on-year to 24,118.
  • Order book. ₹5,410 million (₹541 crore) as at 30 June 2026 including orders received post June 2026; ₹7,100 million (₹710 crore) as at 31 March 2026 including orders received post April 2026.
  • New empanelment. A Letter of Empanelment from a leading State Government-owned power distribution utility for 1,00,000 grid-connected rooftop solar projects of 1 kW each (100 MW), at ₹45,450 per kW including GST, aggregating approximately ₹454.50 crore including GST, with design, engineering, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and five-year O&M. Since 1 April 2026, allocations/empanelments exceed ₹1,092 crore including GST.
  • Policy-driven demand. PM Surya Ghar Yojana (₹75,000 crore outlay; subsidies of ₹30,000/kW up to 2 kW, ₹18,000/kW for larger systems, ₹18,000/kW for society rooftop plants up to 500 kW) and PM KUSUM support rooftop and pumping demand. India’s solar capacity grew from 2.8 GW (FY14) to 162.1 GW (June 2026); rooftop solar reached 30.1 GW and off-grid solar pumping 6.4 GW by June 2026.
  • Addressable market. 78 million farmers lack pumping solutions; less than 30% of farmers have irrigation access and about 2% of pumps are solar-powered.
  • Asset-light model. OEM/ODM-based, low-capex structure with 40+ owned vehicles, 1,500+ workforce (1,200+ technicians), 7,500+ villages covered, and 726 MW cumulative capacity installed.
  • New verticals. BESS and hybrid energy, plus utility-scale MW EPC, cited as future business opportunities.
  • Balance sheet strength. FY 2026 standalone total equity of ₹8,846 million (FY25: ₹2,091 million), cash and cash equivalents of ₹3,354.77 million, total borrowings of ₹2,026.62 million, and a surplus cash position of ₹2,406.15 million versus net debt of ₹1,550.66 million in FY 2025. Net cash from operating activities turned positive at ₹528.97 million in FY 2026 versus negative ₹986.13 million in FY 2025.

3. Key Risks

  • Earnings volatility and growth-management risk as scale increases.
  • Competition from domestic and international players.
  • Concentration in government-linked receivables (debtor days of 140 standalone in FY 2026).
  • Contract execution risk, including time and cost overruns, particularly against the 60-day completion requirement for the new rooftop empanelment.
  • Policy, regulatory, and fiscal risk given reliance on subsidy-backed schemes.
  • Talent attraction and retention risk.
  • Interest rate and other fiscal cost risk.
  • Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties, and actual results could materially differ.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Vision 2030: USD 1 billion business by 2030 across decentralized renewable energy, BESS, utility-scale MW EPC, and future-ready RE products; 1 million installed systems by 2030 (a 7x expansion from approximately 140,000 cumulative systems), with willingness to invest organically or pursue strategic acquisitions.
  • Observed performance against trajectory: FY 2026 standalone revenue of ₹15,325.41 million (+39.98% year-on-year), EBITDA of ₹3,131.85 million (+53.49%), PAT of ₹2,012.70 million (+51.08%), and 61,085 systems installed (+33.80%). Five-year standalone CAGR: revenue 116.0%, EBITDA 178.6%, PAT 234.9%, shareholders’ equity 214.0%.
  • Q1 FY27 versus prior guidance context: Management cited “highest ever quarterly revenue” of ₹5,052 million, EBITDA of ₹861 million, and PAT of ₹597 million, with continued scaling of rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar Yojana and advancement of the OEM/ODM ecosystem. However, EBITDA and PAT margins contracted year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter, indicating revenue growth outpaced profitability in the quarter.
  • Execution guidance: Management stated focus remains on timely execution, service quality, and expanding capabilities across decentralized renewable energy solutions, while noting forward-looking statements involve inherent risks and uncertainties.
  • Capital return: The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹0.50 per equity share (25% on face value of ₹2) for FY 2025-26, approved by shareholders at the 18th AGM on 31 August 2026, with a record date of 24 August 2026.
  • Governance actions: Board reconstituted the Stakeholders’ Relationship, Nomination and Remuneration, Risk Management, and Corporate Social Responsibility Committees effective 7 August 2026; CS Avanti Rajwade appointed Secretarial Auditor for FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31; Board’s Report, Corporate Governance Report, and MD&A for FY 2025-26 approved.
