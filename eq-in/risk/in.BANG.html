  • Year-on-year profitability also weakened. Standalone profit for the June 2026 quarter (Rs. 6.26 lakhs) was below the Rs. 115.02 lakhs reported for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Consolidated profit of Rs. 33.81 lakhs was below the Rs. 169.20 lakhs reported a year earlier.
  • Cost pressure from purchases of stock-in-trade. Standalone purchases of stock-in-trade rose to Rs. 3,812.45 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 2,267.15 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 4,032.03 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated purchases were Rs. 3,766.69 lakhs, up from Rs. 1,688.22 lakhs sequentially.

  • Related party transaction approvals sought. The AGM agenda includes approval of material related party transactions with Thomas Scott (India) Limited for up to Rs. 200 Crores by the company and up to Rs. 100 Crores by its wholly-owned subsidiary, Vedanta Creations Limited.
