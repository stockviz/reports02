AstraZeneca Pharma India — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Pricing and regulatory disputes. On July 27, 2026, the company received a demand notice from the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) alleging overcharging under the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013. The notice concerned Betaloc-50 (Metoprolol 50 mg tablets), which NPPA alleged was sold at an MRP higher than the ceiling price notified under DPCO, 2013, with the revised ceiling price not implemented on market stocks from the notifications dated March 2, 2016 and June 2, 2016. The alleged overcharging period was June to July 2016, and the demand was for Rs. 79,97,457 inclusive of interest calculated up to August 10, 2026, payable within 30 days of receipt. The company stated it was reviewing the notice and would take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

A separate NPPA order required the company to deposit Rs 1,486.58 Million (including interest) alleging overcharging of a combination respiratory drug sold between May 2016 and November 2025.

Litigation posture. The Hon’ble High Court of Delhi stayed the operation of the NPPA demand notice until the next hearing on February 10, 2027, subject to AstraZeneca Pharma India Limited depositing 15% of the amount mentioned in the demand notice with the Registrar General of the Court.

Manufacturing footprint. The company announced its intent to exit the manufacturing site in Bangalore.

Leadership transition. Dr. Sandeep Arora, Director – Medical Affairs, Biopharmaceutical Business Unit and Rare Disease Business Unit, resigned effective close of business hours on September 15, 2026, to pursue a career outside the organization. The company stated it is looking for a suitable replacement.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Revenue momentum. Total revenue from operations grew 30% to INR 6,828 Mn in Q1 FY2026–27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026). Growth was driven by core therapy areas: Oncology at INR 4,649 Mn (up 26%), Biopharmaceuticals (CVRM, R&I and V&I) at INR 1,619 Mn (up 36%), and Rare Diseases at INR 144 Mn (up 35x).

Regulatory approvals. The company received approval for acalabrutinib in combination with venetoclax with or without obinutuzumab for previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL)/small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL); approval for acalabrutinib in combination with bendamustine and rituximab for previously untreated mantle cell lymphoma (MCL) in patients not eligible for autologous stem cell transplant; and approval for trastuzumab deruxtecan in combination with pertuzumab for first-line treatment of unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer.

Strategic initiatives. The company signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Government of Telangana to introduce AI-enabled lung cancer screening across public healthcare facilities, deploying Qure.ai’s AI-powered chest X-ray solution across 20 public health facilities, with training and upskilling of healthcare professionals. It launched K+ Connect, a pan-India multidisciplinary initiative for hyperkalaemia management in heart failure and chronic kidney disease, and launched India Coronary Conquest (ICC) 2026 with STEMI India for cardiovascular capability building.

Recognitions. The company received the OPPI India Awards 2026 Medical Excellence Award for Rare Disease and was runner-up in Marketing Excellence for its Hyperkalaemia & Kidney Care initiative. It also received the Gold Award at the ETHRWorld Employee Experience Awards 2026.

3. Key Risks
  • The NPPA demand notices allege overcharging under DPCO, 2013, with recovery sought plus applicable interest; the Betaloc-50 matter remains subject to a Delhi High Court stay until the February 10, 2027 hearing, conditional on depositing 15% of the demanded amount.
  • The separate NPPA order requiring deposit of Rs 1,486.58 Million (including interest) relating to a combination respiratory drug sold between May 2016 and November 2025.
  • The intended exit from the Bangalore manufacturing site.
  • The resignation of Dr. Sandeep Arora effective September 15, 2026, with a replacement still being sought.
4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management stated its focus remains on investing strategically in opportunities that support sustainable long-term value creation, pushing the boundaries of science to deliver life-changing medicines, and expanding access to serve more patients in India. The CFO noted the 30% first-quarter growth to INR 6,828 Mn builds on a strong track record of consistent double-digit growth over the last five years.

Observed performance for Q1 FY2026–27: total revenue from operations of INR 6,828 Mn; profit before exceptional item and tax of INR 510 Mn; profit after exceptional item and tax of INR 507 Mn. The company clarified that the phrase “Profit after exceptional item and tax” in the financial performance summary is to be read as “Profit after exceptional item and before tax” (i.e. profit before tax).

Other corporate actions. All five resolutions proposed at the 47th Annual General Meeting held on August 10, 2026 were duly passed with the requisite majority, including adoption of financial statements, a dividend of Rs. 36 per share, appointment of a director, appointment of statutory auditors, and re-appointment of an independent director. Mr. Arun Krishna was appointed as an Additional Director (Non-Executive) effective August 17, 2026, and Ms. Bhavana Agrawal’s designation changed from Executive Director to Non-Executive Director effective September 1, 2026. An interaction with an institutional investor is scheduled for August 26, 2026 as a virtual one-on-one meeting, with dates subject to change.
