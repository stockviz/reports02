MOBIKWIK (One MobiKwik Systems Ltd.) Analysis

Stock Overview: Listed on NSE (MOBIKWIK) / BSE (544305). Digital payments (wallet, UPI, bill payments), financial services (credit distribution, wealth), and gateway (Zaakpay). Q2FY26 results show operational momentum but persistent losses. Post-IPO (Dec 2024), focus on scaling via ₹5.3 Bn proceeds.

Tailwinds (Positive Drivers)

  • Robust User/Merchant Scale: 183.5 Mn users (+10% YoY), 4.71 Mn merchants (+7% YoY). #1 Wallet by GTV; top-3 fastest-growing UPI app (38% QoQ txn growth, 28% GMV growth).
  • Payments Momentum: GMV ₹432 Bn (+53% YoY, 13% QoQ record high). Gross margins at 29.4% (up YoY); Net Payments Margin stable ~14 bps.
  • Financial Services Recovery: Digital Credit GMV ₹8.1 Bn (+16% QoQ); Gross margins 41.8% (231% QoQ gross profit jump). Lending expenses down 29% QoQ.
  • Cost Discipline: EBITDA swing +₹248 Mn QoQ to -₹64 Mn; Contribution Margin 34.4% (+690 bps QoQ). Path to breakeven evident.
  • Strategic Wins: SEBI nod for stock broking; RBI-partnered Digital Rupee/FX Retail launches; AI tools (Lens.ai, voice collections/payments); Zaakpay scaling for enterprises (IRCTC, Uber).

Headwinds (Challenges)

  • Persistent Losses: Q2FY26 Net Loss ₹286 Mn (QoQ better from ₹419 Mn, but YoY wider). Half-year loss ₹705 Mn. Negative EBITDA (-2.3% margin).
  • Fraud Hit: ₹118 Mn exceptional loss from merchant bug exploit (Nuh/Mewat); ₹219 Mn recovered, ₹66 Mn secured, balance pursued (ongoing FIR).
  • High Costs: Payment processing (₹1.34 Bn), employee expenses (₹353 Mn), lending ops (₹128 Mn). Cash burn: Ops cash flow -₹1.8 Bn half-year.
  • Revenue Pressure: Total Income flat QoQ (₹2.79 Bn); FS take-rate dipped to 7.6%. Waiver with lending partner (per RBI guidelines) impacted prior periods.
  • Macro/Competition: UPI dominance by giants; slowing user adds (3.3 Mn new Q2FY26 vs. 3.8 Mn prior).

Broker Narrative

The narrative began with an aggressive Buy and expectations of broad digital-finance tailwinds: 45%/61%/64% Revenue/EBITDA/PAT CAGRs, wallet market-share gains, and improved financial-services margins. By the last report, the broker still maintained Buy but acknowledged Q1FY26 revenue missed estimates, financial-services revenue fell 65.9% YoY, BNPL/ZIP remained weak, IPO-proceeds deployment lagged, and EBITDA break-even was pushed out. Persistent themes were wallet share gains, payment GMV growth, and eventual QoQ recovery; the changed emphasis was from high growth and margin expansion to a stretched break-even target and cost/gateway optimization.

Fears that came true

  • Intense payments competition materialized as payment-services revenue rose only 0.7% QoQ and the take rate fell from 0.64% to 0.56%.
  • Dependence on lending-partner relationships for credit products was borne out by financial-services revenue collapsing 65.9% YoY.
  • Regulatory pressure on unsecured lending/BNPL materialized as ZIP EMI GMV declined 21% YoY despite a QoQ recovery.
  • The risk of material BNPL moderation due to tighter unsecured-lending norms came true, with BNPL described as remaining under pressure.

Optimism that failed

  • The projected 45%/61%/64% Revenue/EBITDA/PAT CAGRs over FY24-FY27E failed to materialize, as Q1FY26 revenue was down 20.7% YoY with EBITDA and APAT losses of Rs415mn and Rs419mn.
  • The expected ~40% CAGR in digital lending disbursals did not materialize, with financial-services revenue down 65.9% YoY and ZIP EMI GMV down 21% YoY.
  • The anticipated benefit from IPO proceeds earmarked for growth failed to show through, as deployment was slower than expected and the company remained loss-making.
  • The early confidence in financial-services gross margin improvement to 45.7% proved misleading, as the later reported financial-services gross margin was only 13.3%.
