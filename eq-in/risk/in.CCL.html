CCL Products (India) Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sharp sequential decline in standalone profitability. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, standalone profit before tax was Rs. 3,674.97 lakhs, down from Rs. 11,200.62 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and below the Rs. 5,147.89 lakhs recorded in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Standalone net profit for the quarter was Rs. 2,239.95 lakhs, compared with Rs. 10,731.35 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 3,136.18 lakhs a year earlier. Standalone basic earnings per share fell to Rs. 1.68 from Rs. 8.06 quarter-on-quarter and Rs. 2.36 year-on-year.

The prior quarter and prior year were flattered by subsidiary dividend income. The standalone results note discloses that other income for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 included Rs. 9,241.90 lakhs, aggregating to Rs. 16,284.02 lakhs for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, pertaining to dividend income received from the wholly owned overseas subsidiary Ngon Coffee Company Limited, Vietnam. Standalone other income fell to Rs. 568.02 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 9,292.35 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.

Rising input costs. Standalone cost of materials consumed rose to Rs. 41,382.52 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 38,468.35 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 29,147.04 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. On a consolidated basis, cost of materials consumed was Rs. 81,395.19 lakhs, compared with Rs. 84,328.10 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 64,730.41 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

Consolidated revenue softened sequentially. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 1,20,044.62 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 1,22,444.38 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, though higher than Rs. 1,05,563.89 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

Finance costs remain a burden. Standalone finance costs were Rs. 1,502.70 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, and consolidated finance costs were Rs. 2,866.62 lakhs.

Scope limitations in the consolidated review. The auditor’s consolidated review report notes that the financial results of one subsidiary (total revenues of Rs. 57,559.62 lakhs, net profit after tax of Rs. 9,169.48 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026) were reviewed by other auditors, and that four subsidiaries (total revenue of Rs. 21,638.31 lakhs, net profit after tax of Rs. 761.21 lakhs) and one associate (net profit after tax of Rs. 1.33 lakhs) were not reviewed by their auditors, with management representing these as not material to the Group.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Year-on-year consolidated growth. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to Rs. 1,20,044.62 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 1,05,563.89 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated profit after tax and share of associates rose to Rs. 11,688.05 lakhs from Rs. 7,244.86 lakhs a year earlier, and consolidated basic EPS rose to Rs. 8.77 from Rs. 5.45.

Sequential consolidated profit improvement. Consolidated profit before tax increased to Rs. 12,901.75 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 12,314.40 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. Consolidated total comprehensive income rose to Rs. 14,265.57 lakhs from Rs. 8,206.74 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.

Positive other comprehensive income from overseas operations. Consolidated other comprehensive income was Rs. 2,577.52 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 961.88 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, reflecting items that will be reclassified to profit or loss.

Multi-country operating footprint. The Group comprises the parent plus five subsidiaries — CCL Food and Beverages Private Limited, Continental Coffee Private Limited, Ngon Coffee Company Limited (Vietnam), Continental Coffee SA (Switzerland), and Jayanti Pte Limited (Singapore) — and an associate, Mukkonda Renewables Private Limited.

Continued investor engagement. The company announced participation in Nirmal Bang Institutional Equities’ Semi-Annual Investor Conference in Mumbai on Monday, August 10, 2026, involving group and one-on-one meetings, with discussions based on publicly available information.

Shareholder returns. The Board fixed September 01, 2026 as the record date for payment of a final dividend of Rs. 3/- per equity share (nominal value Rs. 2/- each), and the 65th AGM held on September 08, 2026 passed the resolution declaring this dividend.

3. Key Risks

Earnings volatility from non-operating income. The disclosed dependence of prior-period standalone results on dividend income from the Vietnam subsidiary indicates that standalone profitability can swing materially based on subsidiary dividend decisions rather than core operations.

Concentration in a single reportable segment. Both the standalone and consolidated operations relate to one reportable segment, and segmental reporting under Ind AS 108 is not applicable, meaning results are not diversified across disclosed business lines.

Exposure to overseas operations and currency translation. The consolidated results include subsidiaries in Vietnam, Switzerland, and Singapore, and other comprehensive income includes items that will be reclassified to profit or loss, exposing reported comprehensive income to foreign exchange movements.

Inventory and working capital movements. Changes in inventories were negative Rs. 4,803.99 lakhs standalone and negative Rs. 3,551.77 lakhs consolidated in the June 2026 quarter, compared with negative Rs. 2,801.80 lakhs and negative Rs. 4,922.86 lakhs respectively in the March 2026 quarter.

Reliance on subsidiary audits. As noted in the review report, certain subsidiary and associate results were not reviewed by their auditors, and one material overseas subsidiary’s financials were reviewed by M/s. NSVR & Associates LLP rather than the parent’s statutory auditors.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The announcements do not contain explicit management guidance or forward-looking financial projections. The investor meet intimation of August 05, 2026 states that discussions would be based on publicly available information and that no Unpublished Price Sensitive Information was proposed to be shared. The AGM-related disclosures confirm that the 65th AGM was held on September 08, 2026, that five ordinary resolutions were proposed, considered, and passed with the requisite majorities — covering adoption of financial statements, declaration of a final dividend of Rs. 3.00/- per equity share, re-appointment of directors Smt. Challa Shantha Prasad and Sri B. Mohan Krishna, and ratification of remuneration to Cost Auditors for the financial year 2026-27 — and that the video recording of the meeting was uploaded to the company’s website at www.cclproducts.com.

Observed performance against the prior periods shows a mixed picture: standalone revenue from operations grew year-on-year (Rs. 57,675.02 lakhs versus Rs. 53,481.08 lakhs) and sequentially (versus Rs. 55,776.60 lakhs), but standalone net profit declined both year-on-year and sequentially, largely reflecting the absence of the subsidiary dividend income recorded in prior periods. Consolidated revenue and profit, by contrast, improved year-on-year, with consolidated profit after tax and share of associates rising to Rs. 11,688.05 lakhs from Rs. 7,244.86 lakhs.

Broker Narrative

The narrative remained firmly bullish from the first to the last report, with both maintaining Buy calls and emphasising volume-led growth, capacity expansion, and strong demand. The first report framed higher debt, rising interest costs, and margin compression as costs of growth, while the last report shifted to celebrating balance-sheet deleveraging and margin relief from stabilising/declining coffee prices. The key risk also changed from company-specific financial stress to an exogenous headwind: elevated input costs from the West Asia conflict.
