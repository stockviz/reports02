Headwinds and Challenges

  • Management described the operating environment as “dynamic and often challenging,” citing a “substantial increase in input cost” during the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • The company’s own results notes flag that mobilization and demobilization of resources, including rigs, can affect revenue and profitability from period to period.
  • The wholly owned subsidiary South West Resources Private Limited (formerly Pilot Pipelines Private Limited) incurred losses in earlier years, with accumulated losses of INR 273.52 lakhs as at June 30, 2026 that have fully eroded its net worth. Its current liabilities exceeded current assets as at that date. The auditor’s limited review report on that subsidiary’s standalone results notes these conditions indicate a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt on its ability to continue as a going concern; the accounts were prepared on a going concern basis after a letter of financial support from the holding company. The subsidiary did report a profit of INR 41.36 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • The consolidated statement includes the group’s share of profit after tax of INR 132.24 lakhs in respect of three joint ventures whose financial statements were not audited by the company’s auditors; that information was furnished by the Board and is unaudited.
  • The coal business segment (Jharkhand block) reported no revenue and no segment result for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the quarter ended June 30, 2025, and the year ended March 31, 2026, while carrying segment assets of INR 2,127.52 lakhs as at June 30, 2026. Segment liabilities for the coal business were nil as at June 30, 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Order book stood at an all-time high of INR 7,613 Mn (INR 761 crores), with segment-wise composition: Survey and Exploration of Mineral INR 4,506 Mn; CBM Production INR 2,222 Mn; Aquifer Mapping INR 575 Mn; Seismic and Coal Drilling INR 238 Mn; Seismic Exploration Services using Passive Seismic Tomography INR 72 Mn.
  • The company commenced operations to execute its single largest order, valued at INR 3,070 Mn (INR 307 crores), in Rajasthan, secured from a subsidiary of Hindustan Zinc Ltd. for core drilling.
  • It won an extension of its CBM production contract from Reliance Industries (RIL) valued at over INR 1,660 Mn (over INR 166 crores).
  • It was empanelled by Oil India Limited for providing 2D/3D Seismic Data Acquisition Services across OIL’s onshore areas.
  • It received a Letter of Intimation dated August 13, 2026 from Odisha Lift Irrigation Corporation Limited (OLIC), a Government of Odisha undertaking, for installation, electrification, energisation and commissioning of 500 deep borewells in Odisha, with an aggregate work value of approximately INR 18.71 crores including GST, to be executed within 180 days, with an 18-month guarantee period from completion and commissioning and an initial security deposit of 2% due within 7 days.
  • Jharkhand coal block exploration has been completed and the definitive Geological Report (GR) is under preparation, expected to be submitted by end of July 2026, after which the mining plan is to be fast-tracked alongside environmental clearances. The block spans 2.66 sq. km with estimated geological reserves of 84 million tonnes of W-IV grade coal, with a target to commence coal production by FY 2027-28.
  • The company was notified as an accredited prospecting agency by the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, vide notification dated November 26, 2025 (published in the Gazette on November 27, 2025), for coal and lignite prospecting.
  • In Oman, the first JV (Alara Resources LLC, formed 2018 with Alara Resources of Australia) was awarded an 11-year copper mining contract in 2021-22 with a total contract value of USD 125 million; mining operations commenced in February 2022. A second JV formed in 2024 was awarded Exploration and Mining Block 22-B in January 2025, containing geological reserves of copper, gold, silver, chromite and basalt; an airborne geological survey contract has been completed and data analysis and report preparation is underway.
  • The company made a strategic investment of AUD 0.5 million in Alara Resources Ltd, Australia (listed on the ASX), which holds 51% in the Oman JV, and holds a 17.50% share in Al Hadeetha Mining LLC (AHML), the second Oman JV. It is participating in ARL’s ongoing rights issue.
  • It entered the underground drilling domain, secured an order from Hindustan Copper Ltd., and commissioned 4 advanced rigs now operational.
  • It secured Aquifer Mapping contracts worth over INR 1,000 Mn from CGWB and has 6 ongoing aquifer mapping projects.
  • It raised capital through a preferential issue of equity/warrants in February 2025; the balance 75% amount of warrants issued on preferential basis was received and converted into equity shares.
  • Industry tailwinds cited: global mineral and energy exploration entering a supercycle; the Mineral Exploration Services Market projected to grow from USD 10.31 billion in 2024 to USD 18 billion by 2035 at ~5.2% CAGR; India’s coal production at a record 1,047.52 MT in FY2024-25 with government targets of ~1,404 MT by 2027 and ~1,577 MT by 2030; over 500 mineral blocks up for auction; 100% FDI in upstream oil and gas; and government impetus for rare earth and critical minerals exploration.
  • The company has 20 ongoing projects across 8 states, 43 drilling rigs (capabilities up to 2,500 meters), 15 geoscientists, and 3.2+ million meters drilled with zero Lost Time Injuries since inception.

Key Risks

  • The going concern uncertainty at South West Resources Private Limited, dependent on holding company financial support.
  • Reliance on unaudited financial information for three joint ventures in the consolidated results.
  • Execution risk on the OLIC order: 180-day execution timeline, 2% initial security deposit within 7 days, and an 18-month guarantee period.
  • The coal business remains pre-revenue with capital deployed (segment assets of INR 2,127.52 lakhs) and production targeted only by FY 2027-28, subject to GR submission, mining plan approval, and environmental clearances.
  • Input cost inflation, as flagged by management.
  • Periodic revenue and profitability variability from rig mobilization and demobilization.
  • Overseas JV exposure in Oman (copper/gold mining and exploration blocks) and the investment in ASX-listed Alara Resources Ltd., including participation in its rights issue.
  • The company’s forward-looking statements disclaimer notes known and unknown risks, uncertainties and factors that could cause actual results to differ materially, including ability to implement strategy, growth and expansion plans, regulatory changes, technological changes, investment income, and cash flow projections.
