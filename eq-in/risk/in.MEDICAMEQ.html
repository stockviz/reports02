MEDICAMEQ: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Regulatory non-compliance and exchange fines. The company received notices from both BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited dated May 27, 2026, regarding non-compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 under the applicable Master Circular on penal action. The specific issue flagged was non-compliance with Regulation 19(1)/19(2) relating to the constitution of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee — specifically, the inclusion of an Executive Member instead of a Non-Executive Member. Both exchanges levied a basic fine of ₹94,000 plus GST of ₹16,920, aggregating to ₹1,10,920 each. The payment was made to both exchanges on June 02, 2026.

The BSE notice also referenced a broader set of regulations for the quarter ended March 2026, including Regulation 17(1) (board composition/woman director), Regulation 17(1A) (non-executive director aged over seventy-five), Regulation 17(2) (number of board meetings), Regulation 17(2A) (quorum), Regulation 18(1) (audit committee constitution), Regulation 20(2)/(2A) (stakeholder relationship committee), Regulation 21(2) (risk management committee), and Regulation 27(2) (corporate governance report). However, fines were levied only in respect of Regulation 19(1)/19(2), with the other line items showing nil amounts.

The exchanges warned that failure to pay within 15 days could trigger freezing of the entire shareholding of promoters and, in the event of a second consecutive quarter of non-compliance for Regulations 17(1), 18(1), and 27(2), transfer of the company to the Z group and potential suspension of trading. The Board took note of these communications and directed officials to take corrective and compliance measures within stipulated timelines.

Sequential decline in standalone revenue. Standalone net sales/income from operations declined from ₹5,468.08 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (audited) to ₹4,385.27 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (unaudited). Consolidated net sales also declined from ₹6,064.66 lakh to ₹4,789.69 lakh over the same period.

Subsidiary losses. The limited review report on consolidated results notes that the subsidiaries included in the statement — Opal Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd and Medicamen Life Sciences Private Limited — reflected total revenues of ₹404.42 lakh and a total net loss after tax of ₹(68.90) lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Consolidated profit decline. Consolidated profit from continuing operations declined from ₹444.70 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (audited) to ₹184.60 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (unaudited). Consolidated total comprehensive income declined from ₹444.70 lakh to ₹184.60 lakh over the same period.
