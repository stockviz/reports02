Headwinds and Challenges

  • Loss-making on an Ind AS basis. The unaudited results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 show a loss before tax of ₹305.10 million, a loss after tax of ₹238.82 million, and a total comprehensive loss of ₹236.51 million. Basic and diluted EPS for the quarter were negative at ₹(1.13). For the year ended March 31, 2026, the loss after tax was ₹1,063.42 million and total comprehensive loss was ₹1,054.18 million.
  • Finance costs and depreciation weigh on reported results. For the June 2026 quarter, finance costs were ₹236.42 million and depreciation and amortisation was ₹1,272.19 million. The company explains that accounting losses under Ind AS 116 are largely due to non-cash depreciation on Right-of-Use assets and interest on lease liabilities, and that it is PAT positive and consistently pays income tax on a cash basis.
  • Employee benefit expense rose sequentially. Employee benefits expense increased to ₹276.61 million in the June 2026 quarter from ₹256.31 million in the March 2026 quarter and ₹199.71 million in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Other expenses increased year-on-year. Other expenses were ₹1,131.47 million in the June 2026 quarter, up from ₹911.49 million in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Forward-looking statement risk factors. The company notes that forward-looking statements involve known and unknown risks, uncertainties and other factors that may cause actual results, financial condition, performance or achievements to differ materially, including future changes or developments in its business, competitive environment, and political, economic, legal and social conditions.
  • Disclosure and reporting errors. A corrigendum was issued regarding a typographical error in the September 11, 2026 intimation of Analyst/Institutional Investor Meetings, correcting the Ahmedabad meeting date from Friday, September 17, 2026 to Friday, September 18, 2026. Separately, an inadvertent error caused a discrepancy in the EPS figure reported in the XBRL filing compared to the PDF version of the financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026; this was limited solely to the EPS disclosure, had no impact on the board-approved financial results or other disclosures, and was rectified with a revised XBRL filing.
  • Shareholder dissent on one resolution. At the 12th AGM held on August 12, 2026, Resolution No. 2 (re-appointment of Mr. Anshuman Das as a director retiring by rotation) passed with 96.17012% of valid votes in favour and 3.82988% against. Public Institutions voted 74.54200% in favour and 25.45800% against on this resolution.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Highest ever quarterly revenue. The company reported its highest ever quarterly revenue of ₹428 crore in Q1 FY27, representing 37% year-on-year growth. Revenue from operations for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was ₹4,226.85 million, compared with ₹3,092.93 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Profitability metrics improved. EBITDA increased 34% to ₹87 crore, EBIT grew 59% to ₹55 crore, and PAT grew 91% to ₹35 crore. EBITDA margin was 20%, EBIT margin 13%, and PAT margin 8%.
  • Occupancy strength. Steady-state occupancy strengthened to 90%, and overall occupancy improved to 86%.
  • Value-Added Services (VAS) momentum. VAS revenue reached ₹72 crore. Management notes that one-time VAS projects are expected to remain a recurring feature of the revenue mix given the ongoing nature and pipeline of project-based engagements.
  • Growing network and capacity. The company operates 137 centers across 17 cities, with 10.61 Mn Sq. ft. of Area Under Management (AUM) and 236K seats. There is an occupancy headroom of 3.87 Mn Sq. ft.
  • Strong balance sheet. Net debt stands at ₹(66) crore, net worth at ₹1,194 crore, and the debt-to-equity ratio is 0.05.
  • Large workspace deal signed. On August 19, 2026, IndiQube announced a workspace expansion deal with a leading global audio series platform for nearly 1,300 seats at its premium tech park facility on the Outer Ring Road in Bengaluru, valued at approximately ₹68 crore. The client initially scaled operations from IndiQube’s Koramangala facility and is transitioning to the new tech-enabled environment.
  • Startup and unicorn ecosystem as a growth engine. Startups and unicorns constitute nearly a quarter of overall occupancy, and more than 50 unicorns have chosen IndiQube as their workspace partner. Management positions the company as a long-term Total Workspace Outsourcing partner supporting clients from their 1-to-10 phase through 10-to-100 and beyond, over a 5-to-10-year horizon.
  • IPO proceeds deployment. The company completed its IPO of 29,542,340 equity shares at ₹237 per equity share, listed on NSE and BSE on July 30, 2025. Of total planned utilisation of ₹6,044.59 million, ₹2,690.37 million was utilised as at June 30, 2026, leaving ₹3,354.22 million unutilised, temporarily held in deposits/accounts with scheduled banks. Objects include funding capital expenditure for new centers, fit-out and interior in non-IndiQube properties, renewable power infrastructure, security deposits, and strategic commercial real estate opportunities.
  • Variation in IPO object utilisation approved. The Board approved seeking shareholder approval by postal ballot on May 20, 2026 for change and variation in the objects of utilisation of IPO proceeds; the special resolution was approved by shareholders with the requisite majority through postal ballot on June 24, 2026. Following repayment of borrowings, ₹16.95 million remained unutilised from the repayment object and was reallocated to General corporate purposes.

  • Guidance on IPO proceeds utilisation.
