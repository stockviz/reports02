JISLJALEQS — Q1 FY27 Announcements Review

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Muted Q1 FY27 performance (quarter ended June 30, 2026). Consolidated revenue was about ₹1,508 crores, roughly 2.4% lower year-on-year. Standalone revenue fell about 23.9% to ₹699 crores. Consolidated EBITDA declined about 18.7% to ₹164 crores, with margins at 10.9% versus 13.1% a year earlier. Reported PAT was negative at ₹17.8 crores consolidated, while adjusted PAT (before non-cash NCD interest unwinding) remained positive at ₹3.1 crores, down from ₹30.5 crores.

Segment-specific pressures: - Hi-Tech Agri: Revenue declined 22% (MIS business down 23.7%, Tissue Culture down 10.7%). EBITDA margin contracted 220 bps to 14.4%, attributed to lower fixed-cost absorption on reduced volumes. - Plastic: Domestic retail pipe demand was hit by the delayed monsoon and purchase postponement; India business revenue fell sharply on a standalone basis. - Agro Processing: Margins fell 3% (to 7.6%) on higher freight and power costs, despite 19.7% revenue growth.

Causes cited by management: - Volatility in polymer prices (up ~50%), which led customers to postpone purchase decisions. - Delayed onset of monsoon, which delayed farmer sowing and irrigation purchases; June rainfall deficit. - The West Asia crisis and related geopolitical uncertainty. - Strategic reduction in project/EPC billing (project revenue down 63% within Hi-Tech), with the company choosing cash-flow-positive business over subsidy-linked revenue with long receivables. - Limited solar pump billing in Q1, expected to pick up from September.

Balance sheet and debt: Approximately ₹690 crores of NCDs fall due in FY27 — about ₹230 crores in September 2026 and the remainder in March 2027. Consolidated borrowings stood at ₹3,891 crores as of June 30, 2026.

Legacy receivables: About ₹1,100 crores of receivables are over 180 days, of which ₹800–900 crores are government receivables and ~₹300 crores relate to farmer orders placed through state governments. Total net receivables were ₹1,975 crores in June, with ~55% linked to these issues.

Food business IPO delay: The expected IPO (around ₹1,000 crores including PE exit and primary raise) has been deferred due to weak market valuations; merchant bankers advised waiting. The Tamil Nadu land monetization was also delayed, partly due to elections in April–May.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Improving momentum: Management stated business improved progressively through May and June, with July registering positive revenue growth versus April–June, and August–September looking good.

Monsoon and water availability: Good July rainfall provided adequate soil moisture; major dams are ~67% filled, with expectations of reaching 85% by end of monsoon — supportive for Rabi and next summer irrigation demand.

Segment strengths: - Plastic: Overseas plastic business grew over 45% in revenue; EBITDA margin improved 110 bps to 11.3%. - Agro Processing: Revenue grew 19.7%, supported by the new beverage business (~₹60 crores in Q1), fruit pulp, and improved overseas European operations. - Solar pumps: Expected significant growth from September onwards; Q1 was seasonally light. - MSEDCL empanelment: A Letter of Empanelment for 5,000 Off-Grid DC Solar Photovoltaic Water Pumping Systems valued at ₹117.96 crore (inclusive of GST) under the Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana, scheduled for completion within 60 days.

New initiatives: - Biochar facility: Commissioned June 2, 2026 in Jalgaon — described as among the world’s largest single-unit biochar reactors, processing ~20,000 tonnes/annum of agricultural and fruit residue, with carbon credit potential (6–9 months to commence). - Coffee business: First orders received from the Coffee Board; revenue has started. - Order book: Consolidated order book of ₹1,700 crores (Hi-Tech Agri ₹135 crores, Plastic ₹276 crores, Food/Agri ₹1,289 crores); India order book ₹602 crores.

Working capital improvement: Consolidated net working capital cycle improved to 183 days (June 30, 2026) from 210 days a year earlier and 186 days in March 2026 — a 27-day YoY improvement. Standalone improved to 283 days from 296 days. Cash from operations was 78% of EBITDA consolidated.

Debt reduction track record: Management stated ~₹1,300 crores has been repaid to the banking system over the last three-and-a-half to four years, with debt reduced from ~₹7,000 crores to under ₹4,000 crores.

3. Key Risks

  • Debt repayment execution: ₹690 crores of NCDs due in FY27 (September and March). Management cites internal accruals, legacy receivable collections, asset monetization (surplus land), and refinancing as options, with term sheets in hand, but the obligations remain a key risk.
  • Receivable recovery: Target of ₹422 crores for FY27 from legacy receivables, of which ₹60 crores received in Q1; ₹380 crores targeted over the remaining nine months. Government payment delays are described as industry-wide.
  • Raw material and geopolitical volatility: Polymer price swings and West Asia crisis uncertainty could continue to affect demand timing and margins.
  • Monsoon dependence: Agricultural demand remains sensitive to rainfall timing and distribution.
  • Project business wind-down: Remaining billing of ~₹40–50 crores across projects (excluding one Pune water supply project with triple-digit billing remaining); 10–12 projects have O&M components over two to three years.
  • IPO/monetization timing: Food business IPO and Tamil Nadu land monetization face market- and process-related delays.
  • Write-off risk: Management stated no material write-offs are anticipated on project receivables, with provisions made historically.
