DIGJAMLMTD — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Discontinued manufacturing at the sole plant: Operations at the Company’s only manufacturing facility at Jamnagar were discontinued effective March 31, 2025. The related assets were reclassified as “Non-Current Assets Held for Sale” under Ind AS 105, with a carrying value of ₹5,318.53 lakhs as of June 30, 2026. Textile operations are now conducted from Bhiwandi, Maharashtra.
  • Negative working capital position: As of June 30, 2026, the Company’s current liabilities exceeded current assets by ₹2,107.75 lakhs. The annual disclosure material notes a figure of ₹2,191.48 lakhs for FY 2025-26 and describes this as creating a material uncertainty regarding going concern, though the financial statements were prepared on a going concern basis.
  • Losses at the net level: For FY 2025-26, the Company reported a net loss after tax of ₹(97.90) lakh (total comprehensive loss), compared with a net loss of ₹(1,044.09) lakh in the previous year. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the discontinued operations segment recorded a loss before tax of ₹(5.00) lakh.
  • Input cost and availability pressures: Variations in the availability and cost of key textile inputs such as synthetic fibres and specialised yarns, along with raw material and price volatility for wool, polyester, and viscose.
  • Difficult export environment: A challenging international business environment with uneven demand, aggressive pricing by overseas suppliers, tariff disadvantages, and competition from established and emerging textile-producing nations.
  • Demand and macroeconomic sensitivity: Fluctuations in consumer demand influenced by disposable income, inflation, and economic sentiment; exposure to global and domestic growth, interest rates, and currency movements.
  • Operational and compliance pressures: Evolving regulatory frameworks across taxation, product standards, and compliance; increasing environmental considerations and costs for responsible sourcing, traceability, and green manufacturing; supply chain and logistics disruptions; inventory accumulation and price erosion risks; and trade and geopolitical risks such as tariffs and trade restrictions.
  • Working capital strain: Working capital requirements affected by order volumes, inventory holding periods, and collection delays.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Value-added and MMF demand: Growing demand for value-added fabrics, technical textiles, and man-made fabrics (MMF) creating opportunities in premium and niche segments. The Company specialises in PV (Polyester Viscose) and PW (Pure Wool) fabrics.
  • Distribution expansion: Market penetration and distribution networks expanding through organised retail, e-commerce, and omnichannel platforms into Tier 2/3 cities.
  • Government policy support: Policies such as the PLI Scheme for Textiles, PM MITRA Parks, and import duty exemptions on raw materials and synthetic/MMF inputs.
  • Global sourcing shift: “China Plus One” diversification and international brands seeking alternative suppliers due to geopolitical tensions.
  • Trade agreements: Free Trade Agreements such as the India-UK FTA accelerating export opportunities.
  • Domestic macro resilience: India’s resilient domestic economy, expanding trade relationships, and continued policy support.
  • Proposed demerger: A Scheme of Arrangement involving Digjam and Reid & Taylor International Private Limited (RTIL) provides for the demerger of RTIL’s Textile undertaking into Digjam, with RTIL shareholders to be allotted Digjam shares. The appointed date is July 1, 2025, and the Scheme is subject to approvals and conditions. No effect of the Scheme has been considered in the financial results pending it coming into force.

3. Key Risks

  • Going concern uncertainty: The excess of current liabilities over current assets, combined with the discontinued Jamnagar operations, is described as a material uncertainty regarding going concern. Preparation on a going concern basis reflects management’s confidence in implementing operational and financial strategies, including realisation of assets held for sale, restructuring of the cost base, and the proposed scheme of arrangement.
  • Execution risk on asset monetisation: The Company’s plans depend on realisation of the Non-Current Assets Held for Sale (carrying value ₹5,318.53 lakhs as of June 30, 2026).
  • Scheme approval risk: The Scheme of Arrangement requires receipt of requisite approvals and fulfilment of conditions; it will take effect only upon such approvals. The equity shareholders’ meeting is scheduled for August 16, 2026, and the Scheme will be subject to subsequent sanction of the Tribunal and other regulatory approvals.
  • Sector and market risks: Raw material and price volatility, supply chain and logistics disruptions, inventory accumulation and price erosion, and trade and geopolitical risks such as tariffs and trade restrictions.
  • Regulatory and environmental risk: Evolving regulatory frameworks and increasing environmental compliance costs.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance — FY 2026-27 focus areas: Management’s stated key focus areas include strengthening the business by continuing to build presence in PV and PW fabrics and other commercially viable value-added textile categories; product and market diversification; strengthening long-term customer and supplier relationships; maintaining disciplined working capital and inventory management to improve liquidity and operational efficiency; continuing cost optimisation and efficient business processes; and evaluating market opportunities arising from India’s expanding trade relationships, global supply-chain diversification, and increasing demand for man-made and specialised textiles.
  • Observed performance — continuing operations: For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, revenue from operations was ₹745.79 lakh, total income was ₹759.50 lakh, and profit before tax from continuing operations was ₹87.43 lakh. For the year ended March 31, 2026, revenue from operations was ₹3,327.94 lakh, total income was ₹3,350.79 lakh, and profit before tax from continuing operations was ₹232.55 lakh.
  • Observed performance — discontinued operations: For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, loss before tax from discontinued operations was ₹(5.00) lakh. For the year ended March 31, 2026, loss before tax from discontinued operations was ₹(332.35) lakh.
  • Observed performance — net result: Net profit for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was ₹82.43 lakh, with total comprehensive income of ₹82.91 lakh. For the year ended March 31, 2026, net loss was ₹(99.80) lakh, with total comprehensive loss of ₹(97.90) lakh.
  • Observed performance — earnings per share: Basic and diluted EPS from continuing operations was ₹0.44 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, versus ₹(0.33) for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Basic and diluted EPS from discontinued operations was ₹(0.03) for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, versus ₹0.81 for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Combined basic and diluted EPS was ₹0.41 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, versus ₹0.48 for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Observed performance — balance sheet: Paid-up equity share capital stood at ₹2,000.00 lakh (face value ₹10 per share) as of June 30, 2026. Other equity was ₹(1,558.30) lakh as of March 31, 2026. The Company has outstanding preference share capital of ₹2,700 lakhs, and Finquest Financial Solutions Private Limited holds a 75% promoter stake.
  • Alignment observation: The quarterly results for the June 30, 2026 period show a return to profit at the continuing-operations level and a positive total comprehensive income for the quarter, which is consistent with management’s stated focus on cost optimisation, working capital discipline, and value-added categories. However, the Company continues to carry a negative working capital position and a loss from discontinued operations, and the FY 2025-26 full-year result remained a net loss, indicating that the turnaround described in management’s guidance is at an early stage relative to observed full-year performance.
