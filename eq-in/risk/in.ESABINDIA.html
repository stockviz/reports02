Headwinds and Challenges

  • Income tax penalty proceedings have escalated. The company had earlier disclosed receipt of two orders from the Income Tax Assessing Officer for AY 2017-18 and AY 2018-19, imposing penalties of Rs. 1,23,10,580 and Rs. 21,86,636 respectively, and had decided to appeal. On 20 August 2026, the Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) dismissed both appeals and upheld the Assessing Officer’s orders, confirming penalties under section 270A of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The orders relate to allegations that the company underreported total income. The company has stated it will prefer a further appeal against the CIT(A) orders.
  • Regulatory and disclosure burden. The penalty developments required disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and the company continues to face the cost and management attention associated with pursuing appellate remedies.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Renewable energy procurement at the Nagpur plant. The company proposes to procure an additional 1 MW of renewable energy (solar power) for its Nagpur plant through an equity investment in a Special Purpose Vehicle.
  • Investment in solar SPV. The company approved an investment of 26% equity, for a value up to Rs. 40,00,000, in a Special Purpose Vehicle to be incorporated by Sunsure Energy Private Limited for renewable energy supply.
  • Shareholder support for governance and capital actions. At the 39th Annual General Meeting held on 29 July 2026, all six resolutions were passed, including adoption of the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, declaration of a final dividend of Rs. 25 per equity share (250% of face value of Rs. 10 each), re-appointment of Mr. B Mohan as a director retiring by rotation, appointment of Mr. Curtis Evan Jewell as Non-Executive Nominee Director with effect from 1 May 2026, ratification of cost auditor remuneration of Rs. 5,50,000 for the financial year ending 31 March 2027, and approval of commission to Independent Directors not exceeding 1% of net profits for five financial years from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031.

Key Risks

  • Tax penalty exposure. The confirmed penalties total Rs. 1,45,97,216 across the two assessment years. The company is in the process of preferring an appeal against the CIT(A) orders, so the outcome remains subject to further appellate proceedings.
  • Concentration of voting power. The promoter and promoter group held 1,13,47,960 shares and voted in favour of all resolutions. On the director re-appointment resolution, 12,418 votes were cast against, and on the appointment of the Non-Executive Nominee Director, 13,065 votes were cast against, with public institutions accounting for the bulk of the against votes on both.
  • Execution risk on the renewable energy investment. The solar power procurement depends on incorporation of the Special Purpose Vehicle by Sunsure Energy Private Limited and the company’s 26% equity participation.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Dividend. The company declared a final dividend of Rs. 25 per equity share (250%) for the financial year ended 31 March 2026, which shareholders approved at the AGM on 29 July 2026.
  • Cost auditor remuneration. The Board, on the recommendation of the Audit Committee at its meeting held on 27 May 2026, approved remuneration of Rs. 5,50,000 payable to M/s. Geeyes & Co., Practicing Cost Accountants, for the financial year ending 31 March 2027; shareholders ratified this at the AGM.
  • Independent Director commission. Shareholders approved payment of commission not exceeding 1% of net profits in any financial year for five financial years commencing 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031.
  • Observed performance. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the company reported total income from operations of Rs. 42,290 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 5,614 lakhs.
  • Forward-looking statements. No management guidance or forward-looking statements are provided in the supplied material beyond the proposed renewable energy procurement and equity investment, and the stated intention to appeal the CIT(A) orders.
