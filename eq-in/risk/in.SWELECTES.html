Headwinds and Challenges

  • Consolidated performance in Q1 FY27 was affected by uncertainty around the implementation of ALMM 2 between May 2026 and July 2026, during which customers deferred placing orders.
  • Policy uncertainty and the pricing gap between DCR and NDCR modules led to a slowdown in the module market.
  • Consolidated results declined year-on-year: Total Income fell 25.5% to INR 14,071.59 lakhs, Revenue from Operations fell 26.2% to INR 13,077.21 lakhs, EBITDA fell 28.1% to INR 3,836.37 lakhs, PBT fell 62.7% to INR 1,039.38 lakhs, and PAT fell 63.8% to INR 765.33 lakhs.
  • The company recognized a provision of Rs. 8 crore for contingencies under other expenses in the consolidated financial statement.
  • The company has had to seek shareholder approval for material related party transactions, including with its subsidiary USolar Assetco Four Private Limited, and transactions involving SWELECT SolarKraft Private Limited with Gridnex Solar Power Private Limited, and between the company and Gridnex Solar Power Private Limited.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Standalone results improved sharply year-on-year: Total Income rose 71.2% to INR 13,085.86 lakhs, Revenue from Operations rose 82.0% to INR 11,948.55 lakhs, EBITDA rose 120.0% to INR 3,667.45 lakhs, PBT rose 341.0% to INR 2,225.52 lakhs, and PAT rose 362.5% to INR 1,867.81 lakhs.
  • The company is in advanced discussions for two large EPC orders, which if secured could significantly strengthen the order book and provide greater visibility for future growth.
  • SWELECT has tie-ups for domestic cells for up to 1 GW per year.
  • The module market situation is showing signs of correction, with demand picking up.
  • During Q1, the company announced investments in 110 MW of IPP projects and is in the process of completing the acquisition of two solar parks in Rajasthan, which can aggregate to 140 MW.
  • Projected IPP growth remains on track and is expected to contribute to better utilization of module manufacturing capacity.
  • The company is launching new solar kits focused on enabling effective solar energy harvesting even when the grid is down during the daytime, which it believes will help gain traction in the PM Surya Ghar market.
  • The additional subsidy announced by the Tamil Nadu government should provide further push to the solar market in the state.
