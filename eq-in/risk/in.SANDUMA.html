SANDUMA: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Customs penalty on a material subsidiary: A penalty order of ₹12 lakh was imposed under Section 114 of the Customs Act, 1962, on Royal Sandur Metals Private Limited (formerly Arjas Steel Private Limited), a material subsidiary. The penalty relates to non-payment of export duty by Sundaram Fasteners Limited (an SEZ unit) on export goods procured from the subsidiary during 22 May 2022 to 18 November 2022 — a period prior to SMIORE’s acquisition of the subsidiary. The order was received from the Office of the Principal Commissioner of Customs, Chennai-III, on 8 September 2026.
  • Quarter-on-quarter volume softness across segments: In Q1FY27, sales volumes declined sequentially across manganese ore (down 19% QoQ), iron ore (down 36% QoQ), ferroalloys (down 11% QoQ), and coke (down 4% QoQ). Manganese ore sales were also down 59% QoQ in absolute terms, attributed to volumes under conversion contracts.
  • Coke capacity underutilization: Only 2 of the 4 Coke Oven batteries were operating at optimum capacity utilization, with steady volumes under a contract manufacturing agreement. Management noted it has initiated the process to reignite the other 2 batteries.
  • Steel segment profitability pressure: While steel EBITDA margins improved, the segment’s performance remains tied to market conditions and internal efficiency initiatives, including energy cost optimizations per unit of production.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record mining volumes in FY26: Manganese ore achieved highest-ever production (+15% YoY) and sales (+93% YoY) volumes. Iron ore achieved highest-ever production (+14% YoY) and sales (+2% YoY) volumes.
  • Capacity expansions completed: Manganese ore capacity enhanced from 0.28 MTPA to 0.599 MTPA; iron ore capacity from 1.60 MTPA to 4.45 MTPA. Permissible Annual Production limit for iron ore enhanced to 4.36 MTPA, with approval for handling incidental iron ore of 0.327 Million Tonnes within 31 August 2026.
  • Downhill Conveyor System (DCS) completed: The 1.2 km Downhill Pipe Conveyor System at the mines stands completed and capitalized. Commercial operations are expected to commence by Q2FY27. SMIORE is the first private mine in Ballari and Vijayanagara districts to set up such a system, enabling environment-friendly ore transportation directly to the railway siding.
  • Beneficiation plant at mines: A beneficiation project has commenced, with committed capital expenditure of ₹285 crore, expected to be commissioned in Q2FY28. The project aims to improve ore quality and optimize average sales realizations of iron ore.
  • Ferroalloys recovery: The segment marked a healthy recovery in Q1FY27, with a 5% sequential improvement in realizations following a similar increase in Q4FY26. Market conditions for the broader iron and steel industry have been improving over the last few quarters.
  • Steel segment integration and efficiency: Arjas Steel production and sales volumes improved 10% and 9% YoY respectively in Q1FY27. Sales realizations improved 6% sequentially. Internal operational efficiency initiatives, including energy cost optimizations, led to improved EBITDA margins.
  • Renewable energy transition: 42.9 MW SMIORE Hybrid Renewable Capacity (SPV), 32 MW SMIORE WHRB Capacity, 20.4 MW Arjas Solar Capacity (SPV), and 21 MW Arjas Gas-Recovery Based Capacity.
  • New brand architecture and diversification: The Company adopted a unified Group identity, ‘Royal Sandur’. Two wholly owned subsidiaries are being incorporated: Royal Sandur Hospitality Private Limited (hospitality sector) and Royal Sandur Academy Private Limited (academy sector). The Company is also evaluating an opportunity in medical devices, consumables, and manufacturing.
  • Strong credit profile: A+ rated by CRISIL and ICRA for term loans and cash credit. Early payment of NCDs worth ₹423 crore through internal accruals, prior to maturity date.
  • Healthy liquidity: Standalone cash and cash equivalents of ₹534 crore; consolidated cash and cash equivalents of ₹565 crore as of Q1FY27.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory and legal risks: The customs penalty order on Royal Sandur Metals Private Limited illustrates exposure to regulatory proceedings, including those relating to periods prior to acquisition. The subsidiary is reviewing the order and assessing further legal recourse.
  • Market price volatility: Realizations across mining, ferroalloys, and steel segments are subject to domestic and international benchmark price movements. Management noted that Q1FY27 realizations improved in line with domestic benchmark improvements highlighted at the close of FY26.
  • Operational concentration: Mining operations are conducted from two mining leases located in Sandur, Karnataka, with leases valid up to 2033.
  • Integration risk: The strategic business acquisition of Arjas Steel (now Royal Sandur Metals) and its integration into SMIORE’s operations, including the steel segment consolidated with effect from 11 November 2024.
  • Dependence on contract manufacturing: Coke operations rely on a contract manufacturing agreement with a customer, with 2 of 4 batteries operating under this arrangement.
  • Execution risk on expansion projects: The beneficiation plant (₹285 crore capex, commissioning Q2FY28) and the DCS project (commercial operations expected Q2FY27) are subject to timely execution.
