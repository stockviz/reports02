Capillary Technologies: Recent Developments and Performance

Headwinds and Challenges

Cyber-enabled banking fraud at Czech subsidiary. A cyber-enabled banking fraud occurred at a step-down subsidiary incorporated in the Czech Republic (Session M Czech Republic s.r.o., acquired with Session M Inc. effective 1 May 2026). The fraud involved unauthorised bank transfers using advanced deep-fake methodologies including voice cloning, signature forging, and social engineering to impersonate Key Managerial Personnel. The company disclosed the incident on 6 July 2026, noting the incident occurred just prior to that weekend. The consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 recognised an exceptional expense of approximately ₹333.86 million (€3.04 million) related to these unauthorised transfers. Beneficiary accounts for approximately ₹46.69 million (€0.43 million) were frozen by banking authorities subsequent to 30 June 2026. The company notified its insurer and initiated a claim under its crime insurance policy, reported the matter to law-enforcement authorities, and initiated an independent forensic investigation, which remained ongoing as of the results date. The company also acknowledged a delay in making the disclosure, citing the time required to ascertain material facts and the immediate priority of protecting funds.

IT dependencies hindering marketing agility. The CustomerGlu acquisition announcement describes a core challenge of IT dependencies that hinder marketing agility for enterprise brands, which the acquisition is intended to address.

Labour Codes transition. The Government of India notified four Labour Codes on 21 November 2025, consolidating 29 existing labour laws. The company considered restructured employee compensation effective 1 April 2026 and assessed the impact. It recorded ₹Nil for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and ₹2.10 million and ₹18.24 million in the standalone statement of profit and loss for the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026 respectively.

Standalone profitability decline. On a standalone basis, profit after tax for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹77.65 million, compared with ₹98.95 million in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and a loss of ₹51.21 million in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Standalone revenue from operations was ₹642.87 million for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with ₹650.23 million in the prior quarter and ₹423.25 million a year earlier.

Consolidated loss for the quarter. The consolidated loss after tax for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹95.52 million, compared with profit after tax of ₹433.64 million in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and ₹7.45 million in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. This was driven by the exceptional expense of ₹333.86 million.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

CustomerGlu acquisition. On 4 August 2026, Capillary, through Capillary Pte Ltd, acquired the CustomerGlu software platform and associated intellectual property from Marax AI, Inc. (a Delaware-based gamification company) via an asset purchase agreement. CustomerGlu leverages GenAI to trigger in-app gamified actions such as challenges and streaks, allowing enterprise brands to bypass heavy IT dependencies and launch interactive UI experiences in near real-time. The acquisition is intended to unite an interactive engagement layer with Capillary’s AI-led personalization. CustomerGlu was founded by Prateek Gupta, Raman Shrivastava, and Sumant Subrahmanya, and the acquisition followed an eight-month AI-led transformation spearheaded by CEO Prateek Gupta.

Session M acquisition. On 24 February 2026, Capillary Technologies LLC entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement with Mastercard International Inc. to acquire 100% equity interest in Session M Inc. (Delaware, USA) and Session M Czech Republic s.r.o. The business combination was effective 1 May 2026. The company is completing its determination of working capital balances and has carried out a preliminary purchase price allocation between goodwill, intangible assets, deferred tax, and other working capital balances, to be finalised within twelve months as allowed under Ind AS.

Kognitiv acquisition. On 31 March 2025, Capillary Technologies LLC entered into a Purchase and Sale Agreement with Loyalty Solutions Holdings US Inc. to acquire 100% equity interest in Kognitiv Solutions Inc. (Ontario, Canada) and to purchase intellectual property rights, plus a separate Asset Purchase Agreement with Kognitiv US LLC for customer contracts, customer relationships, and associated receivables. The business combination was effective 1 May 2025 for a consideration of Canadian dollar 23.44 million (net of net working capital of Canadian dollar 0.56 million). This was expected to enhance loyalty business operations in the United States and establish a presence in Canada.

Revenue growth. Consolidated revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹2,566.44 million, up from ₹1,798.93 million in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and ₹1,913.46 million in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Total income was ₹2,623.52 million for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

Operating profit improvement. Consolidated profit before finance costs, depreciation, amortization and tax expense was ₹460.06 million for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with ₹189.94 million a year earlier and ₹381.05 million in the prior quarter.

IPO proceeds and utilisation. In FY 25-26, the company completed its IPO comprising a fresh issuance of 5,982,635 equity shares and an Offer for Sale of 9,228,796 equity shares at ₹549–577 per share, raising a total of ₹8,775.01 million (including ₹3,450.00 million from fresh issuance). Shares listed on BSE and NSE effective 21 November 2025. Net proceeds received were ₹3,229.08 million (net of IPO expenses of ₹220.92 million). As of 30 June 2026, ₹1,196.91 million had been utilised, with ₹2,032.17 million unutilised. Of the amount earmarked for inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and general corporate purposes (₹979.85 million), ₹850.13 million had been utilised as of 30 June 2026.

Scale and reach. Capillary supports 100+ loyalty programs in over 46 countries, delivers loyalty experiences for 1.9 billion+ consumers, and was founded in 2012.

Key Risks

  • Cyber and fraud risk. The Czech subsidiary fraud demonstrates exposure to advanced social engineering and deep-fake methodologies. The net financial impact remains uncertain pending the forensic investigation, recovery proceedings, and insurance claim outcome. The company stated it will recognise any resulting adjustments or provide additional disclosures in future periods.
  • Acquisition integration and purchase price allocation risk. The Session M acquisition’s working capital determination and purchase price allocation are preliminary and subject to finalisation within twelve months under Ind AS.
  • Customer churn risk in acquired businesses. The Kognitiv acquisition included a Churn Indemnity Clause; the seller failed to adhere to this commitment as customer contract terminations exceeded the agreed threshold, constituting a breach. The company received compensation of ₹249.60 million (CAD 3.88 million) from the seller during the quarter ended 31 March 2026, recognised as exceptional income.
  • Subsidiary financial information not reviewed. The consolidated statement includes interim financial information of 3 subsidiaries not reviewed by their auditors, reflecting total revenues of ₹24.18 million, net profit after tax of ₹0.57 million, and total comprehensive loss of ₹3.50 million for the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Management stated these are not material to the Group.
  • Foreign exchange translation exposure. Other comprehensive income includes exchange differences on translating foreign operations of ₹28.93 million for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with ₹296.65 million in the prior quarter and ₹59.73 million a year earlier.
  • Regulatory transition risk. The Labour Codes transition required restructured compensation and assessment of financial impact based on draft rules, FAQs, and legal opinion.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management stated that the business is on track to deliver its earlier FY27 guidance of ₹10,650 million in revenue and ₹1,720 million in Adjusted EBITDA. Observed performance for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 includes revenue from operations of ₹2,566.4 million (up 43% year-on-year), Adjusted EBITDA of ₹440.2 million (up 132% year-on-year), a reported PAT of ₹(95.5) million including the exceptional cyber fraud losses, and a normalized PAT of ₹254.4 million. The company also cited robust NRR expansion (111% organic, 113% overall), a 75% year-on-year increase in TTM New ACV, and expanding ARR reaching ₹10,266 million. The Session M acquisition was completed on 1 May 2026.

On the cyber fraud, management stated that based on information currently available there is no evidence of any compromise of customer data, employee data, or the company’s technology infrastructure, and that business operations continue without material disruption. Management stated it did not see the incident requiring modification or change to the company’s annual or other long-term goals. The company recovered EUR 0.45 million and noted additional funds were traced and accounts placed on hold, with the exact amount on hold yet to be determined.

The AGM held on 28 August 2026 approved all three ordinary resolutions—adopting the audited standalone financial statements, adopting the audited consolidated financial statements, and appointing Mr. Aneesh Reddy Boddu as a Director—with the requisite majority. The company is scheduled to participate in the Anand Rathi Annual Flagship Conference G-200 Summit 2026 on Monday, September 21, 2026, in Mumbai, in a physical format, with no Unpublished Price Sensitive Information intended to be discussed.
