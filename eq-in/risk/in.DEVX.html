Dev Accelerator Limited (DEVX) — Recent Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue contraction. Consolidated revenue from operations declined to Rs. 53.8 Cr in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 55.6 Cr in Q1 FY26, a fall of 3.3% year on year, and down 9.3% from Rs. 59.3 Cr in Q4 FY26. Standalone revenue grew 7.8% year on year to Rs. 42.0 Cr but declined 10.6% quarter on quarter.

Profitability compression at the operating level. Consolidated EBITDA fell 6.9% quarter on quarter to Rs. 30.3 Cr (Ind AS), and EBIT declined 29.2% quarter on quarter to Rs. 15.6 Cr. PBT dropped 85.0% quarter on quarter to Rs. 1.6 Cr, and PAT fell 81.1% quarter on quarter to Rs. 1.5 Cr.

Rising finance costs. Consolidated finance costs rose 14.1% year on year and 36.1% quarter on quarter to Rs. 14.0 Cr (Ind AS). Interest on borrowings under IGAAP rose 65.8% quarter on quarter to Rs. 5.8 Cr.

Rising depreciation and employee costs. Depreciation and amortisation increased 21.9% year on year to Rs. 17.7 Cr. Employee benefit expenses rose 32.4% year on year and 29.6% quarter on quarter to Rs. 6.1 Cr.

Mature center occupancy below portfolio average. Mature center occupancy stood at 69.8% in Q1 FY27, against overall occupancy of 91.9%, indicating under-utilisation at centers operating at full occupancy classification.

Center count reduction. Managed office centers declined to 27 in Q1 FY27 from 28 in both Q1 FY26 and Q4 FY26.

Leverage and debt profile. Gross debt stood at Rs. 135 Cr at Q1 FY27, with net debt of Rs. 81 Cr. Gross debt-to-equity was 0.66x and net debt-to-equity 0.40x. Net debt/EBITDA (IGAAP) was 1.04x in Q1 FY27, improving from 2.10x in FY26 and 5.90x in FY25.

Risks cited in the annual report material. Lease-commitment mismatch arising from long landlord leases of 5–9 years against flexible client contracts; client concentration where a significant share of revenue is derived from top clients; potential headcount growth moderation in IT/ITeS and GCC occupiers due to AI and automation; supply chain and labour cost volatility affecting fit-out costs and delivery timelines; a fragmented market with intensive price competition; and general geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainties affecting occupier hiring.

Forward-looking statement risk. Statements relating to future status, events, or circumstances are subject to numerous risks and uncertainties and are not necessarily predictive of future results.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Enterprise and GCC demand. Enterprise clients accounted for 70% of revenue from operations in Q1 FY27, up from 52% in Q1 FY26 and 55% in Q4 FY26. GCCs occupy approximately 34% of Grade-A office stock (~245 Mn sq. ft.) and are expected to exceed 2,350 units and 300+ Mn sq. ft. in three years.

Portfolio expansion. Total super built-up area rose 31.4% year on year to 1.13 Mn sq. ft. Operational seats increased to 17,294 from 14,144 in Q1 FY26. Occupancy improved to 91.9% from 88.6% year on year.

Pipeline. Management is focused on adding 2.31 Mn sq. ft. in the pipeline plus 0.19 Mn sq. ft. under fit-out, taking the total to 3.63 Mn sq. ft. by FY29, across 40 centers and 52,014 seats. Ten new centers (~2.38 Mn sq. ft.) are planned under the straight lease model, with 95% pre-leased before going operational.

Large contract and capex plans. India’s largest single managed office contract — 800,000 sq. ft. in Ahmedabad. A Rs. 100 Cr fit-out furniture capex over four years for 8,500 seats, targeting Rs. 120 Cr annual revenue.

New market entry. Additional centers planned in Bangalore, Pune, Gurgaon and Ahmedabad. Operations have commenced as lessee of the JUPL/AEPL property under the OpCo-PropCo model. Tokenisation process has started, with law firm and consultants onboarded and entity incorporation begun.

Technology and partnership initiatives. The AI Infrastructure Launchpad invites AI and PropTech innovators. A partnership with a leading pan-India media organisation provides access to a wider innovation network. The investment in Eezily provides access to broker networks, real estate market intelligence and demand insights.

Capital raising. Rs. 100 crore raised through non-convertible debt, diversifying capital sources. Upon conversion of recently issued preferential warrants, promoter shareholding is expected to increase from 36.81% to approximately 37.29%.

Sector tailwinds cited in annual report material. Rapid urbanisation and growth of Tier-II cities as cost-effective talent hubs; supportive government reforms (RERA, GST, REITs); infrastructure development (National Infrastructure Pipeline and PM GatiShakti); the China+1 supply chain shift; a large skilled STEM talent pool; and rising GCC investments driving flexible workspace demand.

Asset-light models. Development Management model targets higher returns with lower costs across 860,000 sq. ft., with landowners retaining ownership while the developer manages execution. The OpCo-PropCo model separates operational management from property ownership.

3. Key Risks

  • Lease-commitment mismatch: long landlord leases of 5–9 years against flexible client contracts.
  • Client concentration: a significant share of revenue derived from top clients.
  • Headcount growth moderation in IT/ITeS and GCC occupiers due to AI and automation.
  • Supply chain and labour cost volatility affecting fit-out costs and delivery timelines.
  • Fragmented market with intensive price competition.
  • Geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainties affecting occupier hiring.

  • Rising finance costs and depreciation weighing on net profitability despite EBITDA growth.
  • Mature center occupancy at 69.8%, below the 91.9% portfolio average.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Revenue guidance. Management is guiding consolidated revenue of Rs. 280 to Rs. 300 Crores for FY 2026-27. Observed Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 53.8 Cr, a 3.3% year-on-year decline and 9.3% quarter-on-quarter decline. FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs. 226 Cr, representing 42.2% YoY growth.

Portfolio guidance. Management targets expanding the operational portfolio to 12 Lacs Sq. Ft. by FY 2027-28 and adding 5 to 7 Lacs Sq. Ft. under the Development Management platform across Indore, Jaipur, Surat, and Vadodara. Observed operational SBA was 1.13 Mn sq. ft. in Q1 FY27, with 0.19 Mn sq. ft. under fit-out and 2.31 Mn sq. ft. in signed pipeline.

Margin guidance. Long-term operating disciplines target a cash EBIT margin of 20.0% to 22.0%. Observed Q1 FY27 consolidated EBIT margin was 27.4% (Ind AS) and 22.7% (IGAAP); standalone EBIT margin was 32.2% (Ind AS) and 26.4% (IGAAP).

Revenue-to-rent ratio guidance. Management targets a revenue-to-rent ratio above 2.2x. Observed Q1 FY27 ratio was 2.36x, against 2.38x in Q1 FY26 and 2.28x in Q4 FY26.

Debt-to-equity guidance. Management targets a debt-to-equity ratio below 1.0x. Observed Q1 FY27 gross debt-to-equity was 0.66x and net debt-to-equity 0.40x.

FY29 pipeline guidance. Management remains focused on adding 2.31 Mn sq. ft. in the pipeline, in addition to the 0.19 Mn sq. ft. under fit-out, taking the total to 3.63 Mn sq. ft. by FY29.

Chairman’s commentary versus observed results. The Chairman’s message describes “a steady performance during the quarter, supported by continued demand for managed office spaces from enterprise clients in our core markets,” and notes EBITDA growth of 14.7% YoY and PAT growth of 15x YoY. Observed consolidated EBITDA rose 14.7% year on year to Rs. 30.3 Cr, and PAT rose to Rs. 1.5 Cr from Rs. 0.1 Cr. However, on a sequential basis, EBITDA fell 6.9%, EBIT fell 29.2%, PBT fell 85.0%, and PAT fell 81.1%.
