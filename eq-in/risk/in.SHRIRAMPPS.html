Shriram Properties (SHRIRAMPPS): Q1 FY27 Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Modest Q1 FY27 performance. Revenue growth was muted because Q1 followed an aggressive handover quarter in Q4 FY26 and because the Q1 handover mix was skewed toward lower-ticket Kolkata units. Q1 FY27 revenues were ₹271 Crs, up 4% YoY, with EBITDA at ₹42 Crs and Net Profit at ₹11 Crs.
  • Margin pressure from product mix. Gross margin came in at 25% and EBITDA margin at 15%, impacted by the higher contribution of Kolkata-led handovers. Management characterised this as a product mix issue rather than a structural one, with margins expected to recover in H2 as the handover project mix improves.
  • JV losses. A JV loss of ₹4 Crs in Q1 FY27 reflected higher sales and administrative expenses at the 122 West project, partly offset by continued handovers from recently completed JV projects.
  • Back-ended launch and completion calendar. Q1 launches occurred late in the quarter (the Chennai premium launch was at end-June), limiting their Q1 contribution. Several FY27 launches are scheduled for H2, and some approvals are still awaited (e.g., Manjari in Pune moved from Q2 to Q3; Gateway C Zone in Chennai awaits approvals).
  • Regulatory/investigative overhang. A search operation was carried out by the Enforcement Directorate at the Company’s business premises in October 2024. As of the issuance of the financial results, the Company has not received formal communication regarding the findings of the investigation.
  • Negative Section 198 net profit. The net profit computed under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013, based on the Audited Standalone Financials for FY26, is negative.
  • Execution dependencies. Project delivery and revenue recognition depend on scheduled completions, approvals, and construction progress; the Company notes proactive planning and e-Khata buffers to mitigate slippage risk.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong operational start to FY27. Three launches were completed in Q1 FY27 — two new projects (Kolkata and Chennai) and one new phase launch (Chennai) — totalling 0.9 msf. Highest-ever Q1 sales were recorded at ₹484 Crs (0.85 msf volume), with collections up 8% YoY to ₹365 Crs and 690 units handed over.
  • Premiumisation and portfolio diversification. Entry into Chennai’s premium residential segment (Shriram Stellar, Koyambedu, ~₹320 Crs GDV) and launch of plots as a new product segment (Shriram Southbrook, Kolkata, ~₹130 Crs GDV) expand reach. The Kolkata plotted launch saw ~55% of inventory sold within 30 days; the Chennai premium launch saw ~20% of the project sold during launch week.
  • Kolkata value unlock. The FY26 amicable resolution continues to unlock monetisation opportunities, supported by favourable policy initiatives, improving sentiment, and land value appreciation. Expanded product offerings across plotted developments, villas, and commercial are driving faster and improved value realisation.
  • Business development momentum. One new project (~0.7 msf, estimated GDV potential of ₹650 Crs) was added in Q1. Projects aggregating over 7 msf are progressing toward closure, with addition likely in the next 3–6 months. The Company signed seven new land acquisition agreements with an estimated GDV of approximately ₹3,500 crore. Over 20 msf is under active evaluation.
  • Pipeline scale. Current pipeline stands at 33.7 msf (16.0 msf ongoing, 17.7 msf upcoming), with total GDV potential of ₹13,530 Crs (₹1,970 Crs unsold ongoing plus ₹11,560 Crs upcoming). Management aims to nearly double the upcoming project pipeline in 18–24 months.
  • Promoter confidence. Mr. Murali, CMD, completed acquisition of Holdco shares via inter-se transfer among promoters, raising effective holding from 7.42% (Sep’24) to 27.67%, making him the largest shareholder.
  • Balance sheet strength. Net debt of ₹432 Crs, Net Debt/Equity of 0.29x, cash and equivalents of ₹219 Crs, and a CRISIL A- (Positive) rating. Cost of debt is ~11+% and remains competitive, with benefit from rate reductions. Q1 FY27 cash flow from operations was ₹54 Crs (vs ₹24 Crs in Q1 FY26), and net free cash flow was ₹47 Crs (vs negative ₹133 Crs in Q1 FY26).
  • FY28 mission. Management targets FY28 sales value of ₹5,000+ Crs, revenues of ₹2,500+ Crs, and PBT of ₹250+ Crs, with ~₹14,000 Crs of revenue to be recognised over 5–7 years.

3. Key Risks

  • Enforcement Directorate search (October 2024). No formal communication on findings has been received as of the financial results issuance; the outcome remains uncertain.
  • Approval and launch timing risk. Multiple FY27 launches depend on pending approvals (Chennai Gateway C Zone, Pune Manjari, Bangalore villas/apartments, Pune Hinjewadi), with some already revised (Manjari shifted Q2 to Q3). Delays could affect FY27 sales and pipeline addition targets.
  • Handover concentration risk. Revenue recognition is skewed toward H2 FY27, with Q2 OC projects (5+ projects, 410+ units, ₹400+ Crs potential), H2 OC projects (2 projects, 400+ units, ₹160+ Crs), and remainder-of-year potential of 2,900+ units and ₹1,560+ Crs. Slippage in completions would materially affect full-year outcomes.
  • Margin mix risk. Continued higher contribution from lower-ticket Kolkata units could keep margins muted if the handover mix does not shift as expected.
  • JV performance risk. JV losses (₹4 Crs in Q1 FY27) could recur depending on project-level sales and administrative expenses.
  • Section 198 negative net profit. The negative net profit under Section 198 based on FY26 audited standalone financials is a compliance-related consideration.
  • Interest cost sensitivity. Finance costs were ₹21.2 Crs in Q1 FY27, flat YoY; cost of debt is ~11+%, and debt levels (gross external debt ₹651 Crs as of Jun’26) remain a fixed-cost burden.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

FY27 guidance (unchanged): - Sales volume: 5.0–5.5 msf (20%–33% YoY growth) - Sales value: ₹3,300–3,500 Crs (40%–49% YoY growth) - Collections: ₹2,100–2,200 Crs (26%–32% YoY growth) - Handovers: 3,750–3,800 units (8%–10% YoY growth) - Completions: 7–8 projects, 4.0–4.5 msf delivery - Pipeline addition: 7.0–8.0 msf - GDV addition: ₹5,000–6,000 Crs

Observed Q1 FY27 performance against guidance: - Sales value of ₹484 Crs (0.85 msf) represents roughly 14%–15% of the full-year sales value guidance midpoint and ~16% of volume guidance midpoint, consistent with management’s statement that the year is skewed toward H2. - Collections of ₹365 Crs represent ~17% of the full-year collections guidance midpoint. - Handovers of 690 units represent ~18% of the full-year handover guidance midpoint. - Q1 revenue of ₹271 Crs and net profit of ₹11 Crs reflect the modest start management anticipated, with revenue momentum expected to build through H2 on the back of the handover pipeline (2,900+ units, ₹1,560+ Crs revenue potential for the remainder of the year). - Management stated Q1 performance “remained modest” and that the “full year outcome to remain robust with skewed H2 launches and completions,” and reaffirmed FY27 guidance remains “firmly on track.”

Broker Narrative

The broker kept a Buy rating throughout and consistently cited the large unsold GDV, Kolkata land monetisation, and business-development pipeline as core growth drivers. The narrative shifted from an immediate value-unlock story (Kolkata dispute resolution worth roughly the market cap) to a more tempered multi-year monetisation and FY27 pre-sales guidance view, while acknowledging higher finance costs and soft revenue from legacy projects. Interest-rate and project-execution fears moved from hypothetical risks to explicit current headwinds, but optimism about pre-sales growth remained intact.

Fears that came true

  • The flagged interest-rate/finance-cost risk materialized: Q1FY27 PAT was hit by higher finance costs, a disappointment followed by negative/flat actual returns.
  • The flagged business-development/execution risk materialized: the last report still lists inability to acquire new projects and delays in under-construction residential projects as key risks, while actual returns were far below the Buy calls.
