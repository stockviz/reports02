Soma Textiles & Industries Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory filing delay: The financial results for the quarter ended 31 March 2026, submitted to the exchange on 30 May 2026, were filed after the stipulated three-hour window from the conclusion of the Board Meeting (which ended at 5:40 P.M.). The company attributed this to technical issues on the exchange portal and stated it communicated the issue to the exchange by email within the timeline, enclosing a signed copy of the results and a screenshot of the upload error.
  • Associate’s accumulated losses: The group’s 40% associate, Soma Textile FZC (incorporated outside India), has accumulated losses exceeding the carrying amount of the group’s investment. The investment has been reduced to Nil and recognition of further losses has been discontinued under the applicable accounting standards. Recognition of the group’s share of profits will resume only after such profits equal the cumulative unrecognised share of losses.
  • Low shareholder participation: At the 88th AGM held on 31 July 2026, total votes polled represented 24.4953% of outstanding shares. No promoter/promoter group shareholders attended the meeting through video conferencing, and public institutions polled no votes across all resolutions.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Dividend declaration: A final dividend at 5% per equity share of ₹10 each for the financial year ended 31 March 2026 was placed before shareholders and passed as an ordinary resolution with 99.9995% of votes polled in favour.
  • Strong shareholder approval across resolutions: All three agenda items — adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY2025-26, reappointment of Shri Shrikant Bhairaveshwar Bhat as a rotating director, and the dividend declaration — were approved with over 99.99% of votes polled in favour.
  • Timely quarterly reporting: Unaudited standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board on 31 July 2026, accompanied by a Limited Review Report from the statutory auditors, M/s. Pipara & Co., Chartered Accountants, Ahmedabad, in line with Regulation 33 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

3. Key Risks

  • Associate performance risk: Because the group’s share of losses in Soma Textile FZC has exceeded its interest, future profits from the associate will not be recognised until previously unrecognised losses are fully recouped. This limits any near-term earnings contribution from the associate.
  • Compliance and disclosure risk: The delayed filing of the 30 May 2026 results, even if attributable to portal issues, highlights exposure to SEBI (LODR) timelines. The company has stated it will take steps to avoid such occurrences.
  • Governance concentration: Voting outcomes show that promoter and promoter group votes (7,921,228 shares, or 29.7507% of outstanding shares) were cast entirely in favour of all resolutions, while public institution participation was nil. Public non-institution shareholders polled only 170,304 votes (2.6631% of outstanding shares) across resolutions.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Filing timelines: Management stated it attempted to file the 30 May 2026 results within the stipulated three hours and attributed the delay to portal technical issues, which it reported to the exchange with supporting evidence. The observed outcome was a delayed submission, which the exchange queried by email on 25 June 2026.
  • Standalone vs. consolidated parity: In response to the exchange’s query about identical figures in the standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended 31 March 2026, management clarified that the consolidated results include only the associate, Soma Textile FZC, accounted for under the equity method. Because the group’s share of accumulated losses exceeds the carrying amount of the investment, the investment has been reduced to Nil and further losses are not recognised, so there is no difference between standalone and consolidated figures. Management noted that any future profits of the associate will be recognised only after previously unrecognised losses are fully recouped.
  • Dividend: Management’s proposal of a 5% final dividend for FY2025-26 was endorsed by shareholders with 99.9995% of votes polled in favour, with only 40 votes against.
