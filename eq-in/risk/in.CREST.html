Crest Ventures Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak sequential and year-on-year earnings momentum (Q1 FY2026-27). On a standalone basis, total income fell to ₹1,919.03 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹2,199.51 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and ₹4,906.80 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Standalone net profit after tax declined to ₹354.74 lakh from ₹653.52 lakh sequentially and ₹2,210.30 lakh year-on-year. Consolidated net profit after tax was ₹1,146.61 lakh, against ₹854.96 lakh in the prior quarter and ₹2,574.02 lakh a year earlier. Consolidated total income declined to ₹3,028.21 lakh from ₹3,205.11 lakh sequentially and ₹6,310.83 lakh year-on-year.

Real estate segment losses. In the consolidated segment results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Real Estate & Related Activities segment recorded a loss of ₹(271.11) lakh, compared with a profit of ₹1,882.35 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Segment revenue for real estate fell to ₹593.76 lakh from ₹3,359.64 lakh a year earlier. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the segment result was ₹865.18 lakh.

Recoverability of deposits under auditor emphasis. Deposits aggregating ₹15,529.75 lakh placed with certain counterparties during FY2022-23 and FY2023-24 in connection with proposed joint development arrangements are the subject of an Emphasis of Matter in both the standalone and consolidated limited review reports. The auditors state they have relied on management explanation and judgement on this matter, and note that attention has been drawn to it since the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025 and in the audit report dated May 22, 2026. Management’s position is that security (original title documents, share pledges, and a mortgage charge created during the quarter ended December 31, 2025), a joint venture agreement over part of the land parcel, and an independent valuation confirming adequate security cover mean the carrying value is fully recoverable and adjustable against future development obligations, with no additional impairment provision necessary.

Scheme of Arrangement awaiting regulatory approval. The Board approved a Scheme of Arrangement on December 18, 2025 between Crest Ventures Limited (Demerged Company) and Crest Capital and Investment Limited (Resulting Company). NSE and BSE conveyed observation letters dated July 20, 2026 with no adverse observation, enabling filing with the National Company Law Tribunal. However, the observation letters remain conditional upon and effective only upon receipt of Reserve Bank of India approval as sectoral regulator; the Company’s RBI application is presently under consideration. The Scheme has not received all requisite regulatory approvals and has not been sanctioned by the NCLT as of the date of approval of the results, and therefore has no impact on the standalone or consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Declining capital adequacy and net profit margin. Standalone capital adequacy ratio fell to 62.26% at June 30, 2026 from 64.40% at March 31, 2026 and 78.62% at June 30, 2025. Standalone net profit margin declined to 18.49% from 29.71% sequentially and 45.05% year-on-year. Consolidated net profit margin was 37.86%, against 26.67% sequentially and 40.79% year-on-year.

Rising debt-equity and total debts to total assets. Consolidated debt-equity ratio rose to 0.21 times at June 30, 2026 from 0.13 times at June 30, 2025. Consolidated total debts to total assets rose to 0.14 from 0.10 a year earlier. Standalone debt-equity was 0.17 times, against 0.14 times a year earlier.

Dependence on unaudited or unreviewed component results. The consolidated review notes that the statement includes unaudited interim financial results of seven subsidiaries not reviewed by their auditors (total income ₹1.66 lakh, net loss ₹(19.81) lakh for the quarter), and the Group’s share of net loss of ₹(76.71) lakh in respect of two associates whose interim results were not reviewed by their auditors. Management states these are not material to the Group. The statement also includes results of seven subsidiaries reviewed by other independent auditors and three associates reviewed by their respective auditors.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Cluster redevelopment initiative in Dadar East. On June 29, 2026, the Company announced that its wholly owned subsidiary Sutlej Housing Pvt. Ltd. (SHPL) registered a Development Agreement with Prafulla Co-operative Housing Society, one of the constituent societies within a large-scale cluster redevelopment project in Dadar, Mumbai. The project has an estimated overall Gross Development Value of approximately ₹2,200 Crore. The Company describes Dadar as Mumbai’s principal multimodal transit and commercial hub with access to Western, Central, and Harbour railway networks and proximity to major central business districts.

Expansion of the real estate development platform. On August 17, 2026, Crest EZY Living Private Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary of CVL) incorporated EZY Living Nest Private Limited, and on August 18, 2026, EZY Living Spaces Private Limited. Both are step-down wholly owned subsidiaries with authorized capital of ₹15,00,000 and subscribed capital of ₹10,00,000 each. Their stated objects are real estate development and allied activities, including acquisition, development, leasing, and operation of residential, commercial, and purpose-built rental housing assets such as BTR (build-to-rent), co-living, and student housing. The Company states this proposed business is aligned with and forms part of its existing real estate business verticals and is intended to further its business objectives. Neither entity has commenced business operations.

Growing real estate segment asset base. Consolidated segment assets for Real Estate & Related Activities rose to ₹1,02,084.58 lakh at June 30, 2026 from ₹99,066.13 lakh at March 31, 2026 and ₹76,921.73 lakh at June 30, 2025. Segment liabilities for the same segment rose to ₹41,582.11 lakh from ₹35,362.29 lakh sequentially and ₹16,784.02 lakh a year earlier.

Financial & Related Services reported as a separate segment. The Group identified and disclosed ‘Financial & Related Services’ as a separate reportable segment, with prior period/year figures restated. This segment contributed revenue of ₹855.67 lakh and segment results of ₹252.48 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Share of profits from associates. The consolidated results include a share of profit of associates of ₹565.78 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a share of loss of ₹(188.32) lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025 and a profit of ₹129.58 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026.

Investing & Credit Activities segment scale. Segment assets for Investing & Credit Activities rose to ₹88,138.69 lakh at June 30, 2026 from ₹77,580.73 lakh at March 31, 2026 and ₹76,481.89 lakh at June 30, 2025.

Capital deployment into subsidiary. During the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Company invested ₹1,026.18 lakh in the rights issue of its wholly owned subsidiary Crest Capital and Investment Limited.

Full utilisation of NCD proceeds. The Company stated that it has fully utilised the proceeds of its Non-convertible Debentures for the purposes for which they were raised, so disclosures under Regulations 52(7) and 52(7A) regarding utilisation of issue proceeds and material deviation or variation are not applicable for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Strengthened insider trading framework. The Board on August 13, 2026 approved an amendment to the Code of Conduct for Prevention of Insider Trading (Version 3.0), including a structured digital database requirement, expanded UPSI categories, and revised definitions of designated persons.

3. Key Risks

Deposit recoverability risk. The ₹15,529.75 lakh of deposits with counterparties remain the principal item of auditor emphasis. Recovery depends on the mortgage charge, the joint venture agreement, an independent valuation report, and pledged shares. Management expects full adjustment or recovery in the ordinary course of the development cycle, but the auditors have relied on management explanation and judgement.

Regulatory approval risk on the demerger. The Scheme of Arrangement is conditional on RBI approval, which remains under consideration, and on NCLT sanction. Until these are received, the Scheme has no impact on reported results.

Execution risk in redevelopment. The Dadar cluster redevelopment project is described as having an estimated overall GDV of approximately ₹2,200 Crore, with Prafulla Co-operative Housing Society being one of the constituent societies forming part of the larger cluster redevelopment initiative. The project is at the stage of a registered Development Agreement.

New subsidiary entities with no operating history. EZY Living Nest and EZY Living Spaces were incorporated on August 17 and August 18, 2026 respectively, are yet to commence business operations, and have no turnover history. Their authorized and subscribed capital are ₹15,00,000 and ₹10,00,000 respectively.

Segment concentration and volatility. The Real Estate & Related Activities segment swung from a profit of ₹1,882.35 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025 to a loss of ₹(271.11) lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, indicating earnings volatility in this segment.

Reliance on unaudited components. Certain subsidiary and associate results included in the consolidated statement were not reviewed by their auditors, though management states they are not material.

Interest rate and finance cost sensitivity. Standalone finance costs were ₹528.46 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, against ₹431.78 lakh a year earlier. Consolidated finance costs were ₹533.73 lakh, against ₹438.33 lakh a year earlier.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Deposit recovery guidance. Management states it is firmly of the opinion that the carrying value of the ₹15,529.75 lakh deposits is fully recoverable and adjustable against future development obligations under the joint venture arrangements, that no additional provision for impairment is necessary, and that it expects the deposits to be fully adjusted or recovered in the ordinary course of the development cycle. Observed performance: the deposits remain outstanding and are the subject of an Emphasis of Matter in the limited review reports for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with attention drawn since the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025 and in the audit report dated May 22, 2026. No impairment has been recognised.

Demerger guidance. Management states the Scheme has no impact on the standalone or consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 because it has not received all requisite regulatory approvals and has not been sanctioned by the NCLT. Observed performance: the Scheme was approved by the Board on December 18, 2025; NSE and BSE observation letters dated July 20, 2026 conveyed no adverse observation; RBI approval remains under consideration.

Dadar project guidance. Mr. Vijay Choraria, Managing Director, stated that Dadar represents a rare combination of legacy value, strategic centrality and sustained residential demand, and that the Company aims to contribute to urban renewal through thoughtfully planned spaces, with an approach centred on disciplined growth, design excellence and high quality developments. Observed performance: the Development Agreement with Prafulla Co-operative Housing Society was registered, and the project is described as having an estimated overall GDV of approximately ₹2,200 Crore, with Prafulla Co-operative Housing Society being one of the constituent societies forming part of the larger cluster redevelopment initiative.

New subsidiary guidance. The Company states the proposed business of EZY Living Nest and EZY Living Spaces is aligned with and forms part of its existing real estate business verticals and is intended to further its business objectives. Observed performance: both entities are yet to commence business operations and have no turnover history.

Segment reporting guidance. The Group identified and disclosed ‘Financial & Related Services’ as a separate reportable segment and restated previous period/year figures accordingly. Observed performance: the segment reported revenue of ₹855.67 lakh and segment results of ₹252.48 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

NCD utilisation guidance. The Company stated it has fully utilised the proceeds of the NCDs for the purposes for which they were raised, so Regulations 52(7) and 52(7A) disclosures are not applicable for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Observed performance: no deviation or variation statement was required for the quarter.

AGM outcomes. The Company held its 44th Annual General Meeting on August 22, 2026. Shareholders approved all five proposed resolutions, which included the adoption of financial statements for FY 2025-26, declaration of a final dividend of Re. 1 per fully paid up equity share (10%), re-appointment of Ms. Sheetal Kapadia as Director, and approval of material related party transactions for both the company and its subsidiaries.
