Headwinds and Challenges

  • OAD and Cardiac momentum lagging: The two largest therapies, OAD and Cardiac, together representing 35% of the portfolio, have yet to regain full momentum. OAD grew 1.9% in Q1 FY27 against an IPM growth of 11.5%, and Cardiac grew 11.1% against an IPM growth of 16.9%.
  • Insulin represented market declining: The represented insulin market (RHI + Glargine, ₹2,778 cr) is declining at -1.2% YoY. Eris’ growth in this segment is a share-gain story rather than a market-growth story.
  • Swiss Parenterals margin pressure: There is a possibility of margin contraction in FY27 for Swiss Parenterals. Greater clarity on the impact is expected only after Q2 FY27.
  • EU CAPA actions pending: Execution of EU CAPA actions is underway, with sites targeted to be audit ready by December 2026. The base business currently has no contribution from the EU.
  • Gross margin decline: Consolidated gross margin fell to 72.6% in Q1 FY27 from 76.1% in Q1 FY26, attributed to evolving product mix with increasing contribution of Biologics, including Insulins and GLP-1.
  • EBITDA growth trailing revenue growth: Consolidated EBITDA grew 7.1% YoY against revenue growth of 13.0%, with EBITDA margin contracting to 33.9% from 35.8%.
  • “Others” therapy decline: The Others segment declined 4.5% in Q1 FY27 against IPM growth of 10.9%.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Broad-based portfolio momentum: 85% of the portfolio is in chronic and sub-chronic therapies, with most therapies clocking double-digit growth rates in Q1 FY27.
  • Insulin market share gains: Eris’ market share in RHI and Glargine increased from 9% to 16% since the Biocon acquisition, with significant headroom ahead. Eris grew 26.4% YoY in this segment with sales of ₹412 cr.
  • Insulin Analogs opportunity: The non-represented Insulin Analogs market is sized at ₹2,309 cr, growing 9.4% YoY, with Eris holding 0% share today. A pipeline of 5 products is set to launch starting FY28.
  • GLP-1 leadership: Eris has established early leadership in the GLP-1 (Semaglutide) market. In the first full quarter, the Gx market was ₹88 cr with Sundae at ₹12 cr. Sundae leads the market in sale units and Rx count, with unit share (~20%) higher than value share (~14%) due to more accessible pricing.
  • Onco+Nephro and VMN outperformance: Onco+Nephro grew 31.3% against IPM growth of 20.8%, and VMN grew 19.9% against IPM growth of 16.3%.
  • Insulin and GLP-1 outperformance: Insulin grew 27.7% against IPM growth of 4.5%, and GLP-1 grew 165.5% against IPM growth of 178%.
  • Swiss Parenterals long-term thesis intact: EU-CDMO project pipeline is intact, all development projects are on track, and Unit-3 commissioning is targeted in FY28 as per plan.
  • Eris Bionxt commercialization: A key priority for the next two quarters, aimed at control on supplies, margin improvement, and leveraging fiscal benefits.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative started with a Buy on acquisition-led growth, derma synergies, and gross margin expansion, and ended with another Buy, but the later case leaned on specific product catalysts (Semaglutide, Wegovy, Bhopal) and a promise of margin normalization rather than broad-based organic strength. Early worries about margin pressure and supply-chain disruptions persisted and became more central, as the last report again showed falling gross/EBITDA margins, muted domestic branded growth, and flattish international business. The rating stayed bullish at the endpoints, but the justification shifted from confident portfolio synergies to defensive hopes that launches and ramp-ups would fix the same underlying issues.

Fears that came true

  • EBITDA margin contraction flagged in 4QFY23 recurred in the last report with a 188 bps YoY decline, and the stock subsequently delivered -1.8% versus the +19.3% Buy target.
  • The early supply-chain warning in the insulin franchise resurfaced in international operations, with freight/supply-chain disruptions and RHI cartridge stock-outs coinciding with flattish growth expectations and DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes.
  • The initial concern about cost/expense pressure on margins materialized as gross margin fell 351 bps YoY in the last report due to product-mix change and higher solvent/raw material costs, alongside negative actual returns.

Optimism that failed

  • The early optimism on gross margin expansion from a superior product mix failed, as the last report recorded a 351 bps YoY gross margin decline on adverse mix and higher input costs.
  • The expectation of continued strong revenue growth from the base business and acquisitions failed to hold, with domestic branded formulation growth muted at 6% YoY and international business expected flattish/low-single-digit.
  • The assumption that the enlarged portfolio and field-force synergies would keep driving outperformance was contradicted by a series of Buy calls in 2025-2026 that returned negative actuals (e.g., -1.8% vs +19.3%).
