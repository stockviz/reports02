Mphasis Q1 FY27: Headwinds, Tailwinds, Risks, and Guidance Versus Performance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Margin pressure. Gross margin for the quarter was 26.9%, down 140 bps QoQ and 200 bps YoY. Operating margin was 14.8%, down 60 bps QoQ and 50 bps YoY. Net margin declined 80 bps QoQ and 60 bps YoY to 11.2%. The company states it delivered margins within the stated band “after absorbing the acquisition costs.”
  • Acquisition-related drag. The TAP acquisition (control obtained 21 April 2026) contributed ₹9.04 million to Group revenue for the quarter but had a loss impact of ₹149.50 million. Contingent consideration linked to continuing employment is being accounted for as a post-combination expense.
  • Softness in specific verticals. Logistics and Transportation segment revenue declined to ₹1,879.11 million in Q1 FY27 from ₹2,181.53 million in Q4 FY26 and ₹2,170.93 million in Q1 FY26. Insurance segment revenue also declined sequentially to ₹6,659.29 million from ₹6,791.01 million, though it grew YoY from ₹5,105.00 million.
  • Client pyramid attrition at the top. On a YoY basis, the Direct client pyramid showed a decline of one client in the $10Mn+ band and a decline of one client in the $150Mn+ band QoQ. No YoY additions were recorded in the $200Mn+, $150Mn+, $100Mn+, $75Mn+, or $50Mn+ bands.
  • Uncertain macro environment. Management explicitly references delivering growth “despite the uncertain macro environment.”
  • Currency and hedge volatility. Unallocated hedge impact was negative ₹832.95 million in Q1 FY27, compared with negative ₹641.40 million in Q4 FY26 and negative ₹16.54 million in Q1 FY26. Other comprehensive income included a negative ₹177.67 million exchange difference on translation of foreign operations, and a net change in fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges of positive ₹1,470.29 million (versus negative ₹2,123.85 million in Q4 FY26), illustrating significant swings.
  • Finance costs. Finance costs were ₹485.17 million in Q1 FY27, up from ₹418.23 million in Q1 FY26, though down from ₹773.38 million in Q4 FY26.
  • Shareholder dissent on certain resolutions. At the 35th AGM held 23 July 2026, the re-appointment of Mr. Nitin Rakesh as CEO & Managing Director for five years w.e.f. 1 October 2026 passed with 94.10% in favour and 5.90% against. The re-appointment of Ms. Maureen Anne Erasmus as Independent Director passed with 97.39% in favour and 2.61% against. The appointments of Mr. Kabir Mathur and Mr. Pankaj Sood as Directors passed with approximately 96.73% and 96.72% in favour respectively.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Revenue growth. Revenue grew 3.3% QoQ and 17.5% YoY on a reported basis. In USD terms, revenue grew 1.8% QoQ and 7.7% YoY; in constant currency, 2.1% QoQ and 8.3% YoY.
  • Direct business momentum. Direct revenue grew 1.9% QoQ and 9.2% YoY in USD terms, and 2.2% QoQ and 9.9% YoY in constant currency. Direct ROW grew 6.1% sequentially, led by GCCs. Americas and ROW showed strong sequential growth.
  • Vertical performance. BFS Direct grew 0.8% QoQ and 9.4% YoY, with 3.5%+ CQGR over the past eight quarters, driven by wallet share gains and growth in new wins/accounts. Direct Insurance grew 17.8% YoY. TMT Direct grew 16.4% QoQ and 13.6% YoY, driven by ramp-ups in new deal wins. Direct Others grew 5.2% sequentially, driven by new deal wins in the Retail segment. BPO QoQ growth was driven by a strong pipeline and new deal wins. APPS YoY growth was driven by AI-led modernization deals.
  • Deal wins. Net new TCV for the quarter was USD 461 million, with 63% AI-led. TTM TCV was over USD 1.8 billion. Three large deals were signed in Q1 FY27, including one USD 100 million+ deal. Backlog strength and shorter cycle deals are supporting revenue growth.
  • Pipeline. Pipeline reached record levels, growing 8% QoQ and 28% YoY.
  • Mphasis TRIA platform. Launched seven weeks before the call, TRIA is described as changing client conversations, with strong appetite for a “deployment ready” AI stack, multiple deals in closure and moving to execution, ARR and Managed Services opportunities being bundled, significant large deal activity influenced by TRIA, new addressable penetration at existing clients, and expansion from pilots into business adoption at multiple new opportunities.
  • Client additions. YoY additions in the Direct client pyramid included five clients in the $20Mn+ band and two in the $5Mn+ band. QoQ, one client was added in the $10Mn+ band.
  • Strategic acquisitions. Aokah Inc. (26% stake acquired 3 July 2025) is expected to support generating customer opportunities with prospective GCC customers. Locate Software Inc.’s digital transformation management business (control obtained 1 September 2025) adds digital transformation management capabilities. OKIN Process, Inc.’s BPO business (control obtained 1 April 2026) contributed ₹379.33 million to Group revenues in Q1 FY27. TAP (control obtained 21 April 2026) brings Continuum AI, a Decision Intelligence platform expected to be a key catalyst for Mphasis NeoIP. Red Oak Tech, Inc. (definitive agreement signed 1 July 2026) involves acquiring a customer contract for consulting services, with consideration up to ₹2,650.55 million (USD 28 million), subject to closing conditions.
  • Analyst recognition. Mphasis was featured in Forrester’s BPO Services Landscape Q2 2026, mentioned in Gartner’s Market Guide for BPO for Healthcare Organizations, recognised as a Major Contender in Everest Group’s Healthcare Payer Digital Services and Agentic Process Automation PEAK Matrix assessments 2026, recognised as an Aspirant in Everest Group’s Google Cloud Services PEAK Matrix 2026, and positioned in Horizon 1 by HFS in Data Modernization and AI 2026, Financial Crime Compliance 2026, and SAP S/4HANA Transformation Services 2026. It also won the Silver Award at the ETHR Future Skills Awards for the People Manager Certification program.
  • Dividend. A final dividend of ₹62 per equity share for FY26 was approved by shareholders at the AGM on 23 July 2026.
  • Segment result strength. TMT segment result rose to ₹2,111.63 million in Q1 FY27 from ₹1,168.80 million in Q4 FY26 and ₹1,304.54 million in Q1 FY26. Others segment result rose to ₹1,802.78 million from ₹1,428.49 million QoQ and ₹1,271.49 million YoY.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative shifted from broad-based macro and sector headwinds (weak US/Europe tech spending, cautious banks) to a narrower focus on AI-infrastructure bottlenecks (limited pilot-to-production conversion, power/supply-chain constraints). Persistent themes included cautious enterprise IT spending, while automotive/aerospace strength gave way to AI capex acceleration as the dominant tailwind.

Fears that came true

  • Persistent weak macro environment in the US and Europe leading to cautious near-term tech spending at large banks, which correlated with the prolonged stretch of DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes from late 2024 through 2025 as the stock posted negative actual returns.
  • Continued pressure on near-term tech spending at large enterprises along with cost-saving mandates, which materialized as multiple DISAPPOINTMENT calls during 2024-2025 when the stock fell short of predicted upside.
  • Medical devices space moderation due to changes in EU MDR adoption requirements, which aligned with the DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes as the stock underperformed during the period these sectoral pressures weighed.

Optimism that failed

  • Very strong demand in the automotive vertical for electrification and autonomous spending failed to translate into sustained upside, as the stock logged DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes through 2024-2025 despite this tailwind.
  • Aerospace strong spending driven by high travel demand did not prevent the stock’s prolonged drawdown, with multiple DISAPPOINTMENT calls from late 2024 onward.
