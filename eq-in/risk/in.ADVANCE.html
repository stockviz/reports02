ADVANCE — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory and registration environment: Stringent regulatory frameworks and lengthy registration cycles are cited as material challenges, alongside environmental sustainability requirements.
  • Raw material dependence: The business depends on imported technicals and intermediates.
  • Climate and seasonality: Climate volatility affects sowing cycles, and the business is seasonal in nature — quarterly results and performance can be impacted by weather conditions and cropping patterns.
  • Pricing pressure: External pricing pressures arise from high global capacities and Chinese exports.
  • Macro backdrop: A challenging global environment marked by geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions.
  • General risk factors: Regulatory changes, technological changes, market risks, general economic and political conditions in India, monetary and fiscal policies, inflation, deflation, unanticipated turbulence in interest rates, foreign exchange rates, equity prices or other rates, performance of financial markets, changes in domestic laws, natural calamities, violence, regulations, taxes, and changes in competition.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Demand and policy support: Growing global and domestic demand for agrochemicals, supportive government initiatives and domestic manufacturing policies.
  • Supply chain shift: The global shift towards diversified supply chains away from China, and the ‘China Plus One’ strategy.
  • Trade tailwinds: Zero tariffs on Indian agricultural products in the US.
  • Pricing umbrella: Government-imposed anti-dumping duties on Pretilachlor and PEDA imported from China.
  • Diversification: Opportunities in performance chemicals, fuel additives, and intermediates.
  • Capacity expansion: A 4x scale-up in 2,4-D herbicides; Unit IV at Gidhani/Gidani and proposed Unit V at Dahej (Dahej II GIDC Industrial Estate, Bharuch, Gujarat) to manufacture technical grade pesticides and enhance production capacity; a 3.75 MW solar power plant.
  • Export ambition: Aiming to increase export share from 2% to 20% of revenue by FY29.
  • Financial strength: Working capital strengthened through IPO proceeds; rising market demand.

3. Key Risks

  • Seasonality and weather/cropping-pattern dependence affecting quarterly performance.
  • Import dependence for technicals and intermediates.
  • External pricing pressure from high global capacities and Chinese exports.
  • Regulatory, technological, market, macroeconomic, monetary/fiscal, inflation/deflation, interest rate, foreign exchange, equity price, financial market, legal, natural calamity, violence, tax, and competitive risks.
  • Geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions in the global environment.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

Guidance and forward-looking statements: - Commence operations at the new Unit-4 technical manufacturing facility at Gidani (Raj.) by Q3 FY27. - Expects the upcoming Unit-5 facility at Bharuch, Gujarat to significantly enhance production capacity and support long-term growth strategy. - Target export revenue share of 20% by FY29. - Anticipates EBITDA margins of 12+%. - Priorities: expanding technical manufacturing capabilities, strengthening backward integration, enhancing product registrations, deepening customer relationships, pursuing export opportunities, and driving operational excellence. - Remains confident about future opportunities and the rest of FY2027.

Observed performance: - FY 2025-26: Revenue from operations of ₹6,377.8 million (up 27% Y-o-Y), EBITDA of ₹636.9 million, PAT of ₹352.8 million. - Q1 FY2027: Highest ever quarterly performance — revenue from operations of ₹3,304 million (up 96% Y-o-Y and 167% Q-o-Q); EBITDA of ₹351 million (up 105% Y-o-Y and 162% Q-o-Q) with a steady EBITDA margin of 10.6%; PAT of ₹225.5 million (up 152% Y-o-Y and 202% Q-o-Q); diluted EPS of ₹3.50. - Q1 FY27 board-approved standalone results (quarter ended June 30, 2026): Revenue from operations of ₹3,304.04 million and net profit of ₹254.6 million. - Margin comparison: The Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin of 10.6% is below the stated 12+% EBITDA margin anticipation. - Export share: Current export share is 2%, against the FY29 target of 20%. - Unit-4 timeline: Guidance is for commencement by Q3 FY27; the facility is described as a new Unit-4 technical manufacturing facility at Gidani, with construction of Unit-5 at Dahej II GIDC Industrial Estate, Bharuch, Gujarat commenced.

Other key facts: - IPO and listing on BSE and NSE completed on October 8, 2025, raising ₹192.85 crore at an issue price of ₹95 to ₹100 per equity share. - Installed capacity of nearly 90,000 MTPA across three integrated manufacturing units in Jaipur; over 410 CIB & RC registrations (over 400 product registrations across herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, and plant growth regulators). - Board approved appointment of Mr. Brijmohan Sharma as Non-Executive Independent Director, re-designation of Mr. Narendra Choudhary as Whole-Time Director, and scheduled the 24th Annual General Meeting for Friday, September 18, 2026. - A virtual one-on-one investor/analyst meeting with Equirus Capital Limited is scheduled for Tuesday, September 01, 2026, at 12:00 Noon, with no UPSI proposed to be shared.
