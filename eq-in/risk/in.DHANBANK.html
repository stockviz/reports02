Headwinds and Challenges

  • Macroeconomic and geopolitical strain: The Bank points to macroeconomic challenges, escalating geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East, volatile energy markets, and supply chain frictions as external pressures.
  • Interest rate and bond market pressure: Hardening of bond yields and government bond yields globally and in India is flagged, with potential mark-to-market losses across government securities portfolios.
  • Fiscal and trade concerns: Rising public debt and fiscal pressures, along with trade fragmentation and protectionist tariff policies, are identified as challenges.
  • Deposit and credit dynamics: Deposit pricing and rising interest expenses are cited as headwinds, alongside credit risks related to loan portfolios.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Macro opportunity: India maintaining its position as the fastest-growing major economy, resilient domestic consumption, robust manufacturing performance, and aggressive infrastructure investment and capital formation.
  • Business segment growth: Growth in retail, MSME, CASA deposits, gold loans, and forex businesses.
  • Digital and distribution expansion: Acceleration of digital innovation and technology-driven operations, and expanding branch networks especially in South India.
  • Fee income: Increasing fee-based income from insurance and mutual fund distribution.
  • Employee alignment: The Board approved the formulation of the ‘Dhanlaxmi Bank Limited Employee Stock Option Plan - 2025’ (ESOP 2025) to grant not exceeding 27,628,919 employee stock options to eligible employees, subject to member approval. No grants are being made currently as the plan is subject to shareholder approval.

Key Risks

  • Potential mark-to-market losses on government securities portfolios due to hardening bond yields.
  • Credit risks related to loan portfolios.
  • Deposit pricing pressure and rising interest expenses.
  • Broader macroeconomic, geopolitical, energy market, supply chain, fiscal, and trade policy uncertainties.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management aspires to sustain the business growth achieved in FY 2025-26, expand branch presence across key markets, strengthen the retail franchise, accelerate digital innovation, and continue investing in people. The focus remains on sustainable growth, financial resilience, and long-term value creation as the Bank approaches its historic centenary in 2027.
  • Observed performance: Total business grew by 19.67% to Rs. 33,771.57 Crore as on March 31, 2026. Deposits reached Rs. 18,642.88 Crore and gross advances reached Rs. 15,128.69 Crore. Net profit for FY 2025-26 increased to Rs. 102.75 Crore compared to Rs. 66.64 Crore in the previous year. Operating profit rose to Rs. 216.28 Crore. Gross NPA ratio improved to 1.89% and Net NPA to 0.51%. CRAR stood at 18.92%, with Core CRAR of 16.11%. The Board did not recommend any dividend for FY 2025-2026.
  • Governance and shareholder engagement: The Bank’s 99th Annual General Meeting will be held on Wednesday, September 23, 2026, at 11:00 AM IST through video conferencing/OAVM. Electronic dispatch of the Notice and Annual Report for FY 2025-26 was completed on August 31, 2026. Physical copies will not be sent. The cut-off date for voting is Thursday, September 17, 2026; remote e-voting runs from September 20, 2026, to September 22, 2026. The Register of Members and Share Transfer Books will remain closed from September 18, 2026, to September 23, 2026.
