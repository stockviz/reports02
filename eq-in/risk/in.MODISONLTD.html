Modison Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements: Key Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Fire incident at the factory (7 February 2026). A fire at the company’s factory destroyed items of property, plant and equipment (PPE) and inventory. The company charged off the written-down value of PPE (Rs. 153.58 Lakhs), cost of inventory (Rs. 867.46 Lakhs) and GST on inventories (Rs. 42.42 Lakhs), totalling Rs. 1,063.46 Lakhs, shown under “Exceptional Items” for the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026. The company lodged an insurance claim of Rs. 1,801.95 Lakhs and received preliminary confirmation for part payment of Rs. 170.00 Lakhs towards loss of PPE, accounted as other income in the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026. Claims for loss of inventory, the balance claim towards PPE, and GST on inventory remained in process with the insurer.

Silver hedging and mark-to-market losses. Exceptional items in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 were a net loss of Rs. 27.70 Lakhs, comprising a loss on hedging of silver in stock of Rs. 38.96 Lakhs and a profit on mark-to-market of forward contracts of Rs. 11.26 Lakhs. In the quarter ended 31 March 2026, exceptional items were a net loss of Rs. 1,992.45 Lakhs, including a silver hedging loss of Rs. 950.52 Lakhs and the fire loss of Rs. 1,063.46 Lakhs. For the year ended 31 March 2026, exceptional items totalled a net loss of Rs. 830.86 Lakhs.

Labour Codes impact. On 21 November 2025, the Government of India notified four Labour Codes, effective immediately, replacing 29 existing labour laws. Under Ind AS 19, the company recognised an increase in gratuity liability from past service cost and leave encashment totalling Rs. 98.95 Lakhs in the financial results for the year ended 31 March 2026. The company continues to monitor developments and will evaluate any further impact on employee benefit liability measurement.

Sequential decline in profitability. Standalone profit before tax fell from Rs. 4,780.15 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 to Rs. 4,582.76 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Profit for the period declined from Rs. 3,603.35 Lakhs to Rs. 3,384.35 Lakhs over the same period. Revenue from operations also declined sequentially from Rs. 28,731.89 Lakhs to Rs. 27,046.67 Lakhs.

Rising finance costs. Standalone finance cost increased to Rs. 431.34 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with Rs. 290.80 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and Rs. 207.00 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong year-on-year growth. Standalone revenue from operations rose to Rs. 27,046.67 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from Rs. 13,413.57 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Standalone profit before tax rose to Rs. 4,582.76 Lakhs from Rs. 641.14 Lakhs, and profit for the period rose to Rs. 3,384.35 Lakhs from Rs. 480.06 Lakhs over the same comparison. Basic/diluted EPS on a consolidated basis was Rs. 10.43 for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with Rs. 1.48 for the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

Divestment of wholly owned subsidiary. The Board approved divestment of 100% equity stake in Modison HV Private Limited (MHVPL), a wholly owned and non-material subsidiary. The estimated consideration is Rs. 27,68,000, based on a valuation undertaken for the transaction. The buyers are Mr. Girdhari Lal Modi and Mr. Kumar Jay Modi, who are part of the Promoter/Promoter Group; the transaction is a related party transaction proposed on an arm’s length basis. The company is to enter into a Share Sale Agreement on or before 12 September 2026 and expects to complete the sale/disposal on or before that date, subject to execution of the agreement and completion of statutory and regulatory formalities. MHVPL contributed Nil total income and had a net worth of Rs. 28,25,790.00 in the last financial year (ended 31 March 2026).

Prior divestment of Modison Hitech Private Limited. The consolidated results note that the company divested its entire stake in wholly owned subsidiary Modison Hitech Private Limited effective 9 June 2025, with no material impact on the financials.

Shareholder approvals at the 43rd AGM (21 July 2026). All seven resolutions were passed with the requisite majority, including adoption of audited financial statements, confirmation of interim dividend and declaration of final dividend, re-appointment of a director liable to retire by rotation, ratification of cost auditors’ remuneration for FY 2026-27, approval of a material related party transaction with Modison Copper Private Limited, approval of overall borrowing limits under Section 180(1)(c), and approval for creation of mortgage or charge on assets under Section 180(1)(a). The borrowing-limit and mortgage/charge approvals provide headroom for financing.

Insurance recovery in progress. The company has lodged an insurance claim of Rs. 1,801.95 Lakhs, of which Rs. 170.00 Lakhs has been confirmed for part payment, with the balance claims for inventory, PPE and GST still in process with the insurer.

3. Key Risks

  • Commodity price and hedging risk: Silver price movements affect inventory values and hedging outcomes, as reflected in recurring exceptional items for silver hedging and mark-to-market of forward contracts across the quarters presented.
  • Operational disruption risk: The February 2026 fire demonstrates exposure of plant, equipment and inventory to physical damage, with a portion of the insurance claim still unresolved.
  • Regulatory and employee-benefit risk: The newly notified Labour Codes required immediate recognition of past service cost, and the company continues to assess any further impact on employee benefit liability measurement.
  • Related party concentration: The proposed sale of MHVPL is to promoter-group buyers and is a related party transaction, and the AGM approved a material related party transaction with Modison Copper Private Limited.
  • Single-segment concentration: The business falls within a single primary segment, “Manufacturing of Electrical Contacts,” so segment reporting under Ind AS 108 is not applicable.
  • Finance cost escalation: The sequential and year-on-year rise in finance costs increases the burden on profitability.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The source material contains limited forward-looking management guidance. The principal forward-looking statements are:

  • Divestment timeline: The company stated it shall enter into a Share Sale Agreement with the proposed buyers on or before 12 September 2026 and expects to complete the sale/disposal on or before 12 September 2026, subject to execution of the agreement and completion of applicable statutory and regulatory formalities. As of the 13 August 2026 board meeting, the transaction had been approved by the Board but the agreement had not yet been entered into.
  • Labour Codes monitoring: The company stated it continues to monitor developments pertaining to the Labour Codes and will evaluate any impact on the measurement of employee benefits liability. The observed performance to date is the recognition of Rs. 98.95 Lakhs in past service cost and leave encashment in the year ended 31 March 2026.
  • Insurance claims: The company stated the other claim towards loss of inventory, balance claim towards PPE and GST for inventory is in process with the insurer. The observed position is that only Rs. 170.00 Lakhs of the Rs. 1,801.95 Lakhs claim has been confirmed for part payment.

On business performance, the observed results show a year-on-year improvement in revenue and profitability for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 relative to the quarter ended 30 June 2025, but a sequential decline relative to the quarter ended 31 March 2026. The prior-year comparative quarter (30 June 2025) had a much lower base, with profit for the period of Rs. 480.06 Lakhs (standalone) versus Rs. 3,384.35 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026. The quarter ended 31 March 2026 figures are balancing figures between the audited full-year figures and the published unaudited year-to-date figures up to the third quarter ended 31 December 2025.
