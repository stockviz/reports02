Jagran Prakashan Limited – Recent Corporate Announcements

Headwinds and Challenges

Profitability pressure despite revenue growth. In Q1FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), standalone operating revenues rose 11.1% to Rs 442.27 crores and advertisement revenues rose 14.5% to Rs 289.23 crores, but operating profit slipped to Rs 61.52 crores from Rs 64.70 crores. Standalone PBT fell to Rs 70.16 crores from Rs 94.73 crores and PAT to Rs 53.50 crores from Rs 71.35 crores. The prior-year quarter included Rs 23.85 crores received from maturity proceeds of a Keyman policy, which flattered the Q1FY26 comparison.

Radio weakness. Consolidated radio revenues were Rs 44.31 crores against Rs 49.06 crores a year earlier. Music Broadcast Limited’s operating revenue fell to Rs 44.54 crores in Q1FY27 from Rs 49.32 crores in Q1FY26, though its operating profit improved to Rs 8.92 crores from Rs 0.94 crores and PAT turned positive at Rs 9.22 crores versus a loss of Rs 2.17 crores. In Q4FY26, MBL recorded an impairment of non-current assets of Rs 49.00 crores and a loss after tax of Rs 47.96 crores.

Loss-making publications and digital drag. Other publications (Midday, Naidunia, I-Next, Punjabi Jagran, Inquilab-North and Sakhi) reported an operating loss of Rs 2.04 crores in Q1FY27 (margin -3.75%), versus a loss of Rs 2.36 crores in Q1FY26. Digital (print) reported an operating loss of Rs 3.07 crores (margin -12.49%), an improvement from a loss of Rs 4.69 crores a year earlier. Midday Infomedia Limited reported an operating loss of Rs 1.15 crores and a net loss of Rs 1.52 crores in Q1FY27; in Q4FY26 it wrote off deferred tax assets of Rs 11.40 crores recognised in earlier years, contributing to a Q4FY26 loss after tax of Rs 13.62 crores.

Circulation stagnation. Standalone circulation revenue was broadly flat at Rs 82.42 crores against Rs 82.30 crores; consolidated circulation revenue was Rs 84.78 crores against Rs 84.85 crores.

Legal proceedings. Company petitions before the National Company Law Tribunal, Allahabad — Mahendra Mohan Gupta and Ors. v. Devendra Mohan Gupta and Ors. (C.P. No. 64 of 2023) and Shailendra Mohan Gupta and Ors. v. Jagran Media Network Investment Private Limited (C.P. No. 57 of 2025) — were listed on 1 September 2026, with further arguments directed to continue on 7 October 2026 at 2:30 P.M.

Governance and approval requirements around promoter remuneration. The explanatory statement notes that remuneration payable to the Whole-time Directors who are promoters or members of the promoter group exceeds 5% of the Net Profits of the Company for FY2025-26, requiring shareholder approval by special resolution under Regulation 17(6)(e) of the Listing Regulations. Several directors are over 70 (Mr. Dhirendra Mohan Gupta, 82; Mr. Mahendra Mohan Gupta, 85; Mr. Devendra Mohan Gupta, 76), requiring special resolutions or approvals for their continuation.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Advertising recovery. Standalone advertisement revenues grew 14.5% year-on-year to Rs 289.23 crores; consolidated advertisement revenues (print and digital) grew 14% to Rs 299.25 crores.

Digital momentum. Standalone digital revenue rose 32.6% to Rs 23.38 crores from Rs 17.64 crores. Consolidated digital revenue (print) rose 30.8% to Rs 24.58 crores from Rs 18.80 crores, with the digital operating loss narrowing to Rs 3.07 crores from Rs 4.69 crores. Jagran New Media remained among the top 15 in the news and information category with around 48 million unique visitors; Jagran.com had ~36 million total unique visitors and Jagranjosh.com ~7 million (Comscore MMX Multi-Platform: June ’26).

Radio turnaround. MBL’s operating profit margin improved to 20.03% in Q1FY27 from 1.90% in Q1FY26 and 3.70% in Q4FY26, with PAT of Rs 9.22 crores versus a loss of Rs 2.17 crores a year earlier.

Outdoor and events growth. The outdoor and event segment grew operating revenue to Rs 62.95 crores from Rs 56.11 crores, with operating profit of Rs 5.64 crores (margin 8.96%) versus Rs 5.98 crores (10.66%) a year earlier.

Flagship franchise strength. Dainik Jagran operating revenue rose to Rs 314.15 crores from Rs 286.35 crores, with operating profit of Rs 59.84 crores (margin 19.05%) versus Rs 63.14 crores (22.05%).

Recognition. The Group received 25 awards during the quarter across Dainik Jagran (16), Jagran New Media (7) and the Jagran IT team (2).

Credit quality. CRISIL reaffirmed AA+Stable long/medium term and A1+ short term for the Company; A1+ short term and AA/Stable long term for Music Broadcast Limited; and AA(-)/stable long term for Midday Infomedia Limited.

Leadership continuity. The Board approved re-appointments of four Whole-time Directors (Mr. Dhirendra Mohan Gupta, Mr. Sunil Gupta, Mr. Sanjay Gupta, Mr. Shailesh Gupta) for five years from 1 October 2026, and the continuation of Mr. Mahendra Mohan Gupta as Non-Executive Director for five years from 1 October 2026 up to and including 30 September 2031.

Key Risks

  • NCLT proceedings: The two company petitions remain before the NCLT, Allahabad, with hearings listed on 1 September 2026 and continued arguments on 7 October 2026. Management states it will provide a disclosure or update when there is a material development in the proceedings.
  • Segment concentration and loss-making units: Other publications and digital (print) continue to operate at a loss, and Midday Infomedia remains loss-making.
  • Radio revenue volatility: MBL’s revenue declined year-on-year even as profitability improved, and it recorded a significant impairment in Q4FY26.
  • Dependence on advertisement revenue: The standalone business derives the largest share of operating revenue from advertisements, which grew strongly in Q1FY27 but remains sensitive to the advertising cycle.
  • Related-party and promoter remuneration approvals: The re-appointments and remuneration of promoter Whole-time Directors require special resolutions, and the aggregate promoter executive remuneration exceeds 5% of FY2025-26 net profits.
  • Age-related board approvals: Continuation of directors above 75 years of age requires shareholder approval under Regulation 17(1A).
