Orient Ceratech Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material dependence and import concentration: The business relies on imported critical raw materials including fused alumina, calcined alumina, low ferric bauxite, magnesia, microsilica, and graphite, with China as the dominant global supplier.
  • Input and operating cost volatility: Raw material prices, energy costs, freight rates, and foreign exchange movements are all cited as sources of volatility.
  • Competitive pricing pressure: The company faces pricing pressure from international competitors.
  • Supply chain and geopolitical uncertainty: Supply chain disruptions and geopolitical uncertainties are identified as challenges.
  • Regulatory and environmental compliance: Potential changes in regulatory and environmental compliance requirements present an ongoing challenge.
  • Discontinued Power Division: During the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the company completed the disposal of its Power Division (seven windmills). The Power Division has been classified as a discontinued operation under Ind AS 105, and comparative figures have been reclassified. The results of continuing operations now comprise only the remaining business segments. The disposal was executed via a slump sale on a going-concern basis with a cut-off date of 30 June 2026, following the agreement entered into on 30 June 2026 and an earlier disclosure dated 18 June 2026. Consideration was revised upwards to Rs. 16.45 Crore following subsequent negotiations.
  • Discontinued operations drag on reported earnings: For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the company reported a loss from discontinued operations (net of tax) of Rs. 198.63 Lakhs on a standalone basis and Rs. 198.63 Lakhs on a consolidated basis, alongside a loss before tax from discontinued operations of Rs. 460.67 Lakhs (standalone and consolidated) and an exceptional loss of Rs. 516.44 Lakhs (standalone and consolidated) related to discontinued operations.
  • Segment reporting change: From the current quarter, the company has only one reportable segment — “Manufacturing & Trading of Ceramic and related products” — under Ind AS 108, so separate segment information is no longer presented.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Steel industry expansion: Continued expansion of India’s steelmaking capacity and robust domestic demand support the core business.
  • Modern steelmaking technologies: Growing adoption of modern steelmaking technologies and a focus on furnace efficiency benefit demand for the company’s products.
  • Localisation and import substitution: Rising emphasis on localisation, import substitution, and mineral value addition creates opportunities.
  • Upstream oil and gas investment: Increasing upstream oil and gas investments support demand for ceramic proppants.
  • Export market penetration: Export market penetration is identified as a growth prospect.
  • Subsidiary contribution: The company has two wholly owned subsidiaries — Orient Advanced Materials Private Limited and Orient Advanced Materials FZE. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Orient Advanced Materials FZE (UAE) recorded total revenues of Rs. 1,536.14 Lakhs, net profit of Rs. 34.93 Lakhs, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 34.81 Lakhs, as reviewed by other auditors.
  • Dividend: The Board recommended a dividend of Rs. 0.35 per equity share (35%) for FY 2025-2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material supply concentration risk: Heavy reliance on imported critical raw materials with China as the dominant global supplier exposes the company to supply disruptions and price shocks.
  • Foreign exchange and commodity risk: Volatility in raw material prices, energy costs, freight rates, and foreign exchange movements can affect margins.
  • Competitive risk: Pricing pressure from international competitors.
  • Geopolitical and supply chain risk: Supply chain disruptions and geopolitical uncertainties.
  • Regulatory and environmental risk: Potential changes in regulatory and environmental compliance requirements.
  • Execution and transition risk from the Power Division disposal: The disposal of the Power Division means the company’s continuing operations now depend solely on the remaining business segments. The disposal does not affect the company’s ability to continue its remaining operations, per the company’s statement.
  • Investor grievance risk (minimal): For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, one investor/shareholder complaint was received and disposed of, with nil closing balance.
