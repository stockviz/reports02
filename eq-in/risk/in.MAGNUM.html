Magnum Ventures — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak quarterly profitability. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone total income was Rs. 12,374.16 lacs against total expenses of Rs. 12,773.37 lacs, producing a loss before tax of Rs. 399.21 lacs and a net loss of Rs. 827.70 lacs. Consolidated figures were similar (loss before tax Rs. 399.38 lacs; net loss Rs. 827.87 lacs). This compares with a profit before tax of Rs. 297.91 lacs and net profit of Rs. 532.49 lacs in the immediately preceding quarter ended 31 March 2026.

Segment divergence. In the June 2026 quarter, the Paper segment reported revenue of Rs. 10,226.96 lacs and segment results of Rs. 375.63 lacs, while the Hotel segment reported revenue of Rs. 2,147.20 lacs and results of Rs. 145.30 lacs. In the March 2026 quarter, the Hotel segment had contributed Rs. 3,996.83 lacs of revenue and Rs. 1,877.20 lacs of results, so the sharp sequential decline in overall profitability coincided with a much smaller Hotel contribution.

Finance cost burden. Finance cost of Rs. 920.14 lacs in the June 2026 quarter exceeded the combined segment results of Rs. 520.93 lacs, which is what drove the overall loss at the PBT level.

Leverage and coverage ratios. The debt-equity ratio rose to 0.40 in the June 2026 quarter from 0.34 in March 2026 and 0.27 in June 2025. Debt service coverage ratio fell to 0.23 from 2.06 in March 2026 and 0.21 in June 2025. Interest service coverage ratio was 0.61 versus 1.38 in March 2026. Outstanding debt excluding lease liabilities rose to Rs. 27,450.32 lacs from Rs. 23,432.84 lacs at 31 March 2026.

SEBI penalty and litigation. A SEBI Executive Director order dated 31 May 2023 imposed a penalty of Rs. 12,00,000 on the company and Rs. 54,00,000 collectively on directors and KMPs, with a one-year market-access restraint. The company appealed to SAT; SAT’s order dated 13 July 2023 gave no interim relief and directed deposit of the penalty, which the company made. The matter was listed for 11 August 2026 and has been adjourned to 15 October 2026 (per the standalone review report; the consolidated review report states 5 October 2026). A provision of Rs. 12,00,000 is in the books.

Auditor observations. The limited review reports state the auditors were unable to comment on physical verification of inventory and of Property, Plant & Equipment, and relied on management-certified values. Debtor, creditor and advance balances as at 30 June 2026 are subject to confirmation and reconciliation with unascertained consequential effect. Trade receivables of Rs. 5,753.85 lacs include Rs. 196.44 lacs outstanding more than six months, of which Rs. 46.04 lacs are under litigation.

Bank of Baroda deposits. The company made deposits of Rs. 300.00 lacs with Bank of Baroda, stated to cover expenses the bank would incur to withdraw cases filed against the company at various forums.

No dividend. No dividend on equity shares has been recommended for the year ended 31 March 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Fundraise via NCDs. The company issued and allotted 18% listed secured non-convertible debentures of Rs. 50 crores on 16 June 2026 to NEO Special Credit Opportunities Fund. Of this, Rs. 29,35,77,950 had been utilised as at the reporting date, with no deviation reported: Rs. 28,81,01,400 towards working capital and Rs. 54,76,550 towards transaction expenses, against original allocations of Rs. 49.20 crores and Rs. 80 lacs respectively.

Partial redemption. The company partially redeemed NCDs amounting to Rs. 1,63,43,698 on 30 June 2026 pursuant to the NCD terms. The security-cover certificate notes the company partly redeemed the debentures and the trustee has reduced the assets noted to hold charge.

Scheme of Arrangement. The company received observation letters from BSE and NSE for a proposed Scheme of Arrangement among Magnum Ventures Limited and Magnum Paperz Limited and their respective shareholders and creditors, under Sections 230 to 232 read with Section 66 of the Companies Act, 2013. The company is in the process of filing the necessary application with the National Company Law Tribunal.

Security cover. The auditor’s certificate states the security cover for secured listed NCDs as at 30 June 2026 is adequate per the debenture trust deeds, and nothing came to attention indicating non-compliance with general and financial covenants.

Year-on-year revenue comparison. June 2026 quarter revenue from operations of Rs. 12,340.63 lacs was higher than the Rs. 11,577.15 lacs reported in the June 2025 quarter, though the company still recorded a net loss in both periods.

Capital work in progress conversion. Capital work in progress of Rs. 9,918.28 lacs at 31 March 2026 is nil at 30 June 2026, with Property, Plant and Equipment rising from Rs. 87,286.33 lacs to Rs. 97,251.60 lacs, indicating assets moved into operation.

3. Key Risks
  • Continued losses at the PBT and net level, with the June 2026 quarter reversing the March 2026 profit.
  • Finance costs exceeding segment operating results, and a debt service coverage ratio of 0.23.
  • Rising debt levels (outstanding debt excluding lease liabilities up to Rs. 27,450.32 lacs) and a debt-equity ratio of 0.40.
  • Pending SEBI/SAT matter, next listed 15 October 2026 (standalone report) / 5 October 2026 (consolidated report), with the penalty deposited subject to appeal outcome.
  • Auditor inability to comment on physical verification of inventory and fixed assets, and unconfirmed debtor/creditor/advance balances.
  • Receivables risk: Rs. 196.44 lacs outstanding over six months, including Rs. 46.04 lacs under litigation.
  • Execution risk on the NCLT filing for the Scheme of Arrangement with Magnum Paperz Limited.
  • No dividend for FY ended 31 March 2026.
4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

The documents contain limited explicit forward-looking guidance. The observable forward-looking items are:

  • Scheme of Arrangement: management states the company is in the process of filing the necessary application with the NCLT after receiving BSE and NSE observation letters. No completion timeline is given.
  • NCD proceeds utilisation: the stated objects were working capital (Rs. 49.20 crores) and transaction expenses (Rs. 80 lacs). Observed utilisation as at 30 June 2026 was Rs. 28,81,01,400 and Rs. 54,76,550 respectively, with no deviation reported.
  • SEBI appeal: the matter has been adjourned to October 2026; no guidance on outcome is provided.

On observed performance versus prior periods: the June 2026 quarter produced a net loss of Rs. 827.70 lacs (standalone) against a net profit of Rs. 532.49 lacs in the March 2026 quarter, and against a net loss of Rs. 1,815.89 lacs in the June 2025 quarter. The full year ended 31 March 2026 showed a net loss of Rs. 1,137.39 lacs (standalone). The June 2026 quarter loss is narrower than the prior-year June quarter loss but represents a sequential deterioration from the March 2026 quarter.
