UNIECOM: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin compression from planned investments. Adjusted EBITDA declined to ₹8.1 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹9.5 crore in Q1 FY26, with Adjusted EBITDA margin falling to 15.8% from 21.1%. EBITDA fell to ₹5.5 crore from ₹8.4 crore, and PBT declined to ₹4.6 crore from ₹5.2 crore. Gross margin contracted 234 bps YoY to 51.9%. Total expenses rose 18.8% YoY to ₹48.2 crore, outpacing revenue growth of 14.3%, driven largely by employee benefits expense increasing to ₹20.61 crore from ₹14.56 crore.

Revenue stagnation on a sequential basis. Revenue of ₹51.4 crore was marginally lower than Q4 FY26’s ₹51.6 crore (down 0.5% QoQ), which management attributes to the seasonally stronger Q4 sale period, consistent with historical trends. Annual Recurring Revenue also declined 0.5% QoQ to ₹205.5 crore.

Loss of a significant client. Reported Uniware growth of 12.8% YoY in Q1 FY27 understates underlying growth, which exceeded 15% excluding the impact of a former top-ten client that discontinued operations last year.

Inadequate profits under statute. The company holds inadequate profits as per the calculation under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013 — relevant to the managerial remuneration and commission resolutions put to shareholders at the AGM.

Obsolescence of traditional delivery models. Management states that traditional delivery models are no longer sufficient to maintain competitive advantage, operational efficiency, or client retention.

Investment drag concentrated in H1. A large part of the growth investment is directed towards Shipway and funded through a portion of ongoing earnings. Most planned investment is front-loaded into H1 FY27, with Adjusted EBITDA moderation expected for the year.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Accelerating platform momentum. Uniware delivered its fourth consecutive quarter of improving growth, at 12.8% YoY (underlying growth above 15%). Shipway grew 16.8% YoY. Consolidated revenue grew 14.3% YoY to ₹51.4 crore from ₹44.9 crore.

Enterprise client additions. 115 enterprise customers were onboarded on Uniware in Q1 FY27, up 30.7% from 88 in Q1 FY26. Total enterprise clients rose 25.4% YoY to 1,228 and 9.1% QoQ. Marquee additions included Amul, Haldiram’s, STUDDS, Pigeon, Mahindra Logistics, and The Sleep Company.

New product monetisation. Continued adoption of Quick Commerce, B2B, UniReco and UniCapture modules, with usage growing among clients that adopted these capabilities in recent quarters. Partner-led offerings in adjacent categories are being tested to extend the value proposition and create additional revenue streams.

AI-led product evolution. The company is progressing from a System of Records (pre-FY22, no AI) to a System of Intelligence (FY23–26, AI-assisted) toward an Agentic System of Actions (FY27 onwards, AI-led execution). Beta testing of next-generation AI capabilities is in progress with select clients. Products include UniBot AI Assistant, Catalyst AI voice agent, and ShipSense AI courier allocation.

Shipway scale opportunity. Shipway is described as being at an early stage in a large market with low share today, a transaction-led model offering a materially larger revenue opportunity at scale, and the potential over the next few years to become a larger business than Uniware.

International expansion. Urban Company’s Middle East arm expanded its partnership with Unicommerce to power e-commerce operations in Saudi Arabia and the UAE using the Uniware platform. The company cites 360+ plug-and-play integrations and 8,000+ clients across India, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.

New AI-related corporate objects and revenue streams. Proposed amendments to the Memorandum of Association would incorporate Artificial Intelligence objects, alongside aims to commercialise operational data and intellectual property and diversify into AI-powered consulting and integration.

Balance sheet strength. Cash balance increased to ₹92.6 crore at the end of Q1 FY27 from ₹53.8 crore at the end of Q1 FY26. PAT grew to ₹4.7 crore from ₹3.9 crore YoY (aided by tax benefits recognised in the quarter) and rose 37.5% QoQ. Uniware standalone Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 35.3% from 32.9% YoY. Revenue per employee improved 20.4% YoY. Client concentration fell, with the top-10 client share of revenue at 11.9% versus 15.2% a year earlier.

Recognition and track record. Unicommerce was named a Major Player in IDC MarketScape 2026 for Worldwide AI-Enabled Order Orchestration and Fulfillment Applications, and recognised as SaaS Supply Chain Enabler of the Year 2026 by ISCM. The company reports 5X revenue growth over five years and FY26 Adjusted EBITDA larger than FY21 revenue.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution risk on growth investments: the benefits of AI, talent, and go-to-market spending are expected to build progressively, with individual initiatives maturing at different rates.
  • Margin trajectory risk: Adjusted EBITDA moderation is expected for FY27 as a whole, contingent on investment phasing and revenue contribution building.
  • Client discontinuation risk: demonstrated by the former top-ten client whose exit depressed reported Uniware growth.
  • Competitive and retention risk: traditional delivery models are no longer sufficient to maintain competitive advantage, operational efficiency, or client retention.
  • Regulatory/corporate governance items: inadequate profits under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013, and shareholder resolutions relating to managerial remuneration, waiver of recovery of additional benefits/perquisites up to ₹35 crores tied to stock option exercises, and a 1% profit-sharing commission for Non-Executive Independent Directors.
  • Dilution risk: proposed increase of the ESOS 2019 pool by up to 31,76,471 equity shares.
  • M&A risk: inorganic opportunities will be assessed on valuation, integration synergies, and whether a target is already profitable or has a credible path to profitability within Unicommerce.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: the investor presentation notes known and unknown risks and uncertainties including industry performance in India and worldwide, competition, strategy implementation, growth and expansion levels, technological change, and changes in revenue, income or cash flows.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - 15%+ growth in Uniware, with Shipway accelerating towards 20%+ by Q4 FY27. - Adjusted EBITDA to progressively improve in H2 FY27, with most investment front-loaded into H1 FY27. - Profitability improvement in H2 FY27 as investments yield results and operating leverage increases. - Shipway expected to exit FY27 with Q4 growth above 20% YoY. - Continued cash generation alongside investments. - Persistent efforts to increase productivity and profits in the near future, with hope of attaining expected results through continued efforts.

Observed performance against guidance: - Q1 FY27 Uniware growth of 12.8% YoY is below the 15%+ full-year aspiration on a reported basis; management states underlying growth exceeded 15% excluding the discontinued former top-ten client. - Shipway Q1 FY27 growth of 16.8% YoY is below the 20%+ Q4 FY27 exit target, consistent with the stated expectation of acceleration through the year. - Adjusted EBITDA margin of 15.8% in Q1 FY27 is below Q1 FY26’s 21.1% and FY26’s 21.5%, consistent with the stated expectation of moderation during the investment phase. - Uniware standalone Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 35.3% in Q1 FY27 from 32.9% in Q1 FY26, consistent with the claim that the Uniware business continues to demonstrate operating leverage. - Cash balance rose to ₹92.6 crore from ₹53.8 crore a year earlier, consistent with the stated continued cash generation. - Management states that over the two years since listing, both revenue and Adjusted EBITDA have doubled.
