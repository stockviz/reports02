Valor Estate Limited (formerly D B Realty Limited) — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak standalone profitability. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone revenue from operations was nil, with total income of Rs. 228.99 lakhs (entirely other income) against total expenses of Rs. 2,373.01 lakhs, producing a loss before tax of Rs. 2,144.02 lakhs and a loss for the period of Rs. 2,168.63 lakhs. This compares with a loss of Rs. 621.35 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and a loss of Rs. 7,730.77 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Standalone basic and diluted EPS was Rs. (0.40) for the June 2026 quarter.

Elevated finance costs. Standalone finance costs rose to Rs. 1,586.58 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, from Rs. 542.34 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 240.49 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated finance costs (net) were Rs. 3,046.77 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter.

Consolidated results near break-even. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 11,078.72 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, down sharply from Rs. 84,032.51 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated loss before tax was Rs. 118.65 lakhs and loss for the period was Rs. 132.87 lakhs, versus a profit of Rs. 1,371.36 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated EPS was Rs. (0.02).

Impairment and credit-loss recognition. Standalone results include a net reversal of impairment/expected credit loss of Rs. 502.55 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, following a charge of Rs. 3,859.75 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 17,749.99 lakhs for the year ended 31 March 2026. Consolidated impairment/expected credit loss was a net reversal of Rs. 502.55 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, against a charge of Rs. 17,233.53 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 31,302.20 lakhs for FY2026.

Auditor emphasis of matter — valuation estimates. The statutory auditors drew attention to reliance on independent valuer reports and internal assessments based on management estimates, including assumptions on development potential, expected sales price realisation and cost optimisation, applied to carrying values of inventories, security deposits, project/land advances and investments in subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates. This emphasis has been repeated since the quarter and nine months ended 31 December 2023.

Auditor emphasis of matter — pending litigation. The auditors also drew attention to uncertainty over the outcome of pending litigations or regulatory actions, with no further adjustments made pending final outcome. This emphasis has been repeated since the quarter and six months ended 30 September 2021.

Specific legal and regulatory matters. - An application by Neelkamal Realtors Suburban Private Limited for approval of revised plans under UDCPR 2020 was not accepted by the Bombay High Court; a writ petition filed with the Supreme Court has been admitted. - MIG (Bandra) Realtors & Builders Private Limited received a MHADA demand for development charges of Rs. 6,044.93 lakhs plus interest of Rs. 5,250.21 lakhs. The development charges have been provided for and paid as directed by the Supreme Court; interest has not been recognised pending final adjudication. - The Salt Department filed a Special Leave Petition against the Bombay High Court judgment dated 30 April 2026 (which had upheld Miraland Developers Private Limited’s rights over approximately 240 acres at Bhayander, Thane); the SLP is yet to be listed for admission.

Deferred tax assets not recognised. Deferred tax assets on unabsorbed depreciation and carry-forward losses (including capital losses) have not been recognised on prudence grounds due to lack of reasonable certainty of realisation in the foreseeable future.

Pending lender consent on acquisition. The acquisition of 9,89,800 Class A equity shares of Bamboo Hotels and Global Centre (Delhi) Private Limited for Rs. 59,670.49 lakhs, along with the existing outstanding loan of Rs. 1,05,889 lakhs from Advent Hotels International Limited (approved by shareholders on 19 April 2026 as a related party transaction), remains subject to lender consent; shares are yet to be transferred.

Unreviewed components in consolidated results. The consolidated results include interim financial results of twenty subsidiaries (including four step-down subsidiaries) and fourteen joint ventures/associates not reviewed by their auditors, and of four subsidiaries and two joint ventures reviewed by other auditors.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Goa International Convention Centre project. Following the Letter of Award dated 18 March 2026 from the Government of Goa (Department of Public Private Partnership), the project is being implemented through Blue Crest Properties Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary incorporated as the SPV. The SPV deposited a Performance Bank Guarantee of Rs. 57,90,00,000 (Rs. 57.90 crore) with the Authority and, on 28 July 2026, executed the Concession Agreement with the Director, Department of Public Private Partnership, Government of Goa, for development on a Design, Build, Finance, Operate and Transfer (DBFOT) basis. The company described the project as material in nature and expected to create significant long-term value for the Company and its shareholders. Rights and obligations, including development timelines, revenue rights and concession terms, are governed by the Concession Agreement read with the Letter of Award.

Bhayander land litigation outcome. The Bombay High Court, by judgment dated 30 April 2026, dismissed the Salt Department’s first appeal, upheld the trial court’s judgment that the Salt Department failed to establish title over approximately 240 acres at Bhayander, Thane, upheld Miraland Developers Private Limited’s rights to the land, and rejected the Salt Department’s request to continue an interim status quo order.

Acquisition pipeline. MIG (Bandra) Realtors & Builders Private Limited entered into an agreement with Adani Goodhomes Private Limited to acquire 95.22% equity shares of Radius Estates and Developers Private Limited for Rs. 37,283.00 lakhs, with completion expected by December 2027 subject to conditions precedent; Rs. 2,804.17 lakhs was paid as advance as at 30 June 2026.

Consolidated revenue base. Consolidated revenue from operations for the year ended 31 March 2026 was Rs. 1,59,326.93 lakhs, with profit for the year of Rs. 2,701.47 lakhs and basic/diluted EPS of Rs. 0.50, indicating an operating scale in FY2026 relative to the June 2026 quarter.

Subsidiary performance. The consolidated results include interim results of four subsidiaries reflecting total income of Rs. 5,209.72 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 2,277.76 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

Project management consultancy income. Consolidated other expenses for the June 2026 quarter include project management consultancy expense of Rs. 1,500.00 lakhs, an item exceeding 10% of total expenses.

  • Carrying values of inventories, security deposits, project/land advances and investments depend on management estimates and valuer reports covering development potential, sales price realisation and cost optimisation.
  • Continued losses at the standalone level and thin consolidated profitability, with finance costs rising year-on-year.
  • Deferred tax assets on unabsorbed depreciation and carry-forward losses remain unrecognised.
  • Completion of the Bamboo Hotels and Global Centre (Delhi) acquisition is contingent on lender consent; the Radius Estates and Developers acquisition is subject to conditions precedent and expected completion by December 2027.
  • The Salt Department’s Special Leave Petition against the 30 April 2026 Bombay High Court judgment is yet to be listed for admission.
  • The MHADA interest demand of Rs. 5,250.21 lakhs remains contested, with no provision recognised pending final adjudication.
  • Components of the consolidated results are based on financial information not reviewed by auditors, or reviewed by other auditors.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance/forward-looking statements: - Management believes the company has a strong case on merits in the Neelkamal Realtors Suburban Private Limited writ petition admitted by the Supreme Court. - Management expects the MHADA matter to be settled in favour of developers and does not anticipate further liability. - Management does not foresee an adverse impact on financial condition, results of operations or cash flows from legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business, and noted no material developments in pending litigations during the quarter. - Management believes amounts invested or advanced are recoverable and carrying values of related assets are appropriate. - The Goa convention centre project is expected to create significant long-term value. - Completion of the Radius Estates and Developers acquisition is expected by December 2027.

Observed performance against these positions: - Standalone loss for the June 2026 quarter (Rs. 2,168.63 lakhs) widened versus the June 2025 quarter (Rs. 621.35 lakhs), and standalone revenue from operations was nil in the June 2026 quarter. - Consolidated profit for the June 2026 quarter (loss of Rs. 132.87 lakhs) declined versus the June 2025 quarter (profit of Rs. 1,371.36 lakhs), while FY2026 consolidated profit was Rs. 2,701.47 lakhs. - The auditors have continued to draw attention to the litigation uncertainty since the quarter and six months ended 30 September 2021, and to valuation-estimate reliance since the quarter and nine months ended 31 December 2023, without modifying their conclusion. - The Salt Department filed a Special Leave Petition after the 30 April 2026 Bombay High Court judgment, so the Bhayander land matter remains subject to further proceedings. - The Bamboo Hotels and Global Centre (Delhi) share transfer had not been completed as at 30 June 2026, pending lender consent. - The Goa project progressed from Letter of Award (18 March 2026) to SPV incorporation, Performance Bank Guarantee deposit and Concession Agreement execution (28 July 2026).
