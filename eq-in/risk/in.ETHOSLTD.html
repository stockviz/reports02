Broker Narrative

The narrative stayed Buy throughout, but evolved from a pure growth story—luxury market 5x expansion, >15% SSSG, premiumisation, CPO/lifestyle diversification and store rollout—to a more defensive tone acknowledging currency hits, new-store operating leverage, associate/JV losses and negative FCF. Persisting themes were robust revenue/same-store growth, continued store expansion, CPO/lifestyle momentum, and eventual margin recovery via EFTA-related duty cuts; the change was that currency and expansion costs moved from flagged risks to explicit P&L drags, with net cash becoming the key comfort factor.

Fears that came true

  • The early currency-risk warning materialised as continued CHF-INR depreciation and a Rs44mn forex hit in Q4FY26, compressing gross margin and accompanying the negative actual returns on the 2025 Emkay Buy calls.
  • The early geographic/expansion risk materialised through negative operating leverage from nascent new stores, higher employee/other expenses, and Rs46mn of associate/JV ramp-up losses, dragging EBITDA margin and keeping FY26 FCF negative.

Optimism that failed

  • The >15% same-store growth cited at the start did not sustain through the last report, with FY26 same-store growth at 14.2%.
