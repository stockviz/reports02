AXISCADES Technologies — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Transition-quarter distortion of reported results. Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026) is described as “the first quarter of the transition.” The profit and loss statement now splits into discontinued operations and continuing/retained operations, with like-for-like carve-outs for comparative periods. Reported EBITDA fell to Rs 27.9 crore (8.1% margin) from Rs 34.1 crore (14.0%) in Q1 FY26, and reported PBT was Rs (11.9) crore against Rs 28.0 crore a year earlier. Reported PAT was Rs (14.8) crore versus Rs 20.9 crore.

One-off and exceptional charges. The quarter absorbed Rs 9.62 crore of one-time receivable provisions (largely on an aged defence transaction), Rs 3.50 crore of hedge unwinding, and Rs 21.81 crore of deal costs taken as an exceptional item. Of the estimated Rs 45 crore total transaction costs, Rs 21.81 crore sat in discontinued operations. Normalised, EBITDA was Rs 41.0 crore at an 11.8% margin, normalised PBT Rs 23.1 crore and normalised PAT Rs 20.2 crore.

Retained business profitability does not yet cover fixed charges. EBITDA of the retained business was Rs 8.70 crore, which does not yet cover finance and depreciation. The retained business also carries a build-up of strategic and corporate costs, including mid- and senior-level hires for the manufacturing pivot and ramp-up in Aerospace and Defence. Management states the immediate priority is closing the divestment and deploying proceeds.

Add Solutions drag. Add Solutions remains loss-making, with an EBITDA loss of Rs 4.84 crore and a PAT loss of Rs 6.72 crore, impacting reported EBITDA and PAT. It is the last remaining non-core/services business; its exit is under way and an action plan is in place in FY27.

Aerospace build-phase losses. Aerospace contributed Rs 6.1 crore of revenue against an EBITDA of Rs (6.2) crore (segment table shows Rs (5.4) crore). A thirty-member aerospace leadership team and capacity are in place ahead of the acquisitions and revenue, with aerospace qualification cycles described as running “years, not quarters.”

Defence margin pressure from provisions. Defence revenue more than doubled year on year to Rs 125.0 crore, but reported EBITDA of Rs 2.6 crore (2.1% margin) absorbs Rs 9.6 crore of one-time provisions; underlying EBITDA margin was 9.7%.

Divestment execution and balance-sheet unwind. The divestment transaction requires the Company to unwind certain balance sheet positions and incur costs with resultant impact on profitability. Closing conditions for the US$237 million engineering services divestment to Akkodis are in progress, with closing expected by November 2026.

Financing cost and dilution risk on growth funding. The company is raising up to Rs 200 crore through unrated, unlisted, secured, redeemable non-convertible debentures on a private placement basis, with a 12.50% p.a. coupon payable quarterly and tenure up to 60 months, to be utilised towards the acquisition of Cloud Wave Technologies. Separately, the AGM notice proposes increasing the borrowing limit and creation of charge on assets up to Rs 750 crore.

Statutory and governance matters. The AGM notice notes potential risks and situations including the possibility of loss or inadequate profits, though it states the company currently has adequate profits under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013. Proposals include paying commission to Non-Executive Directors up to 2% of standalone net profits, with a minimum of Rs 5,00,000 per annum in case of inadequate profits.

Forward-looking statement risk. The Q1 FY27 presentation and the Cloud Wave press release both state that forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Record revenue and rapid retained-business growth. Q1 FY27 revenue was Rs 346.7 crore, up 42.2% YoY and 27.0% QoQ — the highest in the Company’s history. Retained revenue grew ~94% YoY like-for-like, to Rs 183.4 crore from Rs 94 crore, and was up 68.4% QoQ, representing 52.9% of group revenue. Continuing revenue excluding Add Solutions was Rs 180.7 crore, up ~100% YoY.

Defence scale-up and visibility. Defence is the largest stream at Rs 125.0 crore, up ~111% YoY. New sole-source design wins in the quarter and after the balance-sheet date added Rs 332 crore to Assured Forecast Visibility (AFV), taking it to Rs 4,557 crore (up 4.8%), after Rs 125 crore of AFV executed in Q1. Wins include a 30 KW Laser DEW technical selection (ToT), DRDO CHESS development and production, anti-tank missile on-board computers, missile sub-system purchase orders, a missile programme PCM encoder, a mobile firing platform land system order, an Uttam radar antenna beam control order (Rs 6.9 crore development order with repeat potential), a 30m mobile mast system, and a marine helicopter Exciter Receiver Processor. Management expects Defence to deliver 75% year-on-year growth in FY27.

XiDA platform and marquee customers. XiDA (formerly ESAI) is the best-performing stream: revenue Rs 49.5 crore, up ~62% YoY, EBITDA Rs 14.7 crore at a 29.7% margin. The business acquired by transfer contributed from its first quarter at a 46.2% margin. Through a US business partnership, XiDA acquired two new customers — the world’s largest semiconductor equipment manufacturer and one of the world’s largest AI and hyperscale technology companies. Three of the world’s top five technology and hyperscale companies, and the largest semiconductor equipment manufacturer, are now customers. The business is being acquired by way of a business transfer agreement rather than share purchase, held through a wholly owned US holding company with an India mirror architecture; the agreement completes in Q2 FY27. Management is confident of more than 100% year-on-year growth in XiDA starting FY27.

Aerospace manufacturing pivot. Aerospace services has been fully divested and manufacturing is being built in its place. The Cloud Wave Technologies acquisition — an AS9100D-certified, Bengaluru-based precision engineering and manufacturing company founded in 2014, operating seven manufacturing units and serving Aerospace & Defence and Semiconductor sectors — was approved by the Board at an enterprise valuation of approximately Rs 260 crore, with a majority stake being acquired. It is expected to close in Q2, with annualised FY27 revenue of Rs 180 crore and EBITDA of Rs 39 crore (22% margin). It becomes the metallic manufacturing backbone for Defence and Electronics. A second transaction is in evaluation, and organic growth is also planned.

Center for Advanced Manufacturing (CAM). A 240,000 sq ft quad-use facility (Aerospace, Defence, Space and Electronics) on a 20-acre campus at Devanahalli, six kilometres from DAC, with land allocation in process. It will house current and future acquisitions. With the acquisition, the second transaction and organic growth, the platform targets annualised revenue of over Rs 375 crore and EBITDA of Rs 84 crore by Q4 FY27, building toward Rs 1,000 crore by FY2029.

New Space division. Satellite manufacturing, assembly, integration and test (MAIT) facility under construction at DAC. Technology transfer collaborations are under way, to be announced at the Bengaluru Space Expo and World Space Business Week in Paris, both in September 2026.

Divestment proceeds and extraordinary gain. Divestments of the Engineering services business and the Aerospace services business were announced in May and June respectively, for a minimum consideration of Rs 1,685 crore and a total consideration of Rs 2,256 crore. On closing, the Company will record an extraordinary gain of Rs 1,255 crore, more than compensating for transaction costs. Proceeds fund the move into aerospace manufacturing, defence systems, spacetech and XiDA, including acquisitions, with no equity dilution. Sale realisation is targeted in FY27: Phase 1 in Q2 with ~Rs 192 crore and Phase 2 in Q3 with ~Rs 718 crore net proceeds.

Missile Atmanirbhar Complex (MAC). Land acquisition done at TSIIC Hardware Park, Hyderabad, with an estimated project cost of Rs 500.25 crore. Ground Breaking Ceremony in July 2026, construction commencing; it will support missile sub-system and strategic electronics programmes.

Manufacturing infrastructure progress. DAL (Devanahalli AeroLand) is completed and commissioned on schedule, supporting aerospace and defence delivery. DAC (Devanahalli Atmanirbhar Complex) Phase 1 construction is under way, operational by FY27, and now also hosts the satellite MAIT facility. The radar hangar facility at DAC will be phased into operation by Q1 FY28.

Related-party scale-up. The AGM notice proposes approving material related party transactions with Mistral Solutions Private Limited up to Rs 4,77,00,00,000.

Cost efficiency. Group employee cost fell from 53.0% to 44.1% of revenue.

3. Key Risks

  • Reported profitability is currently negative at PBT and PAT level, driven by exceptional deal costs, one-off provisions and hedge unwinding; the retained business’s EBITDA does not yet cover finance and depreciation.
  • Add Solutions remains loss-making and is a drag on reported EBITDA and PAT until its exit is completed.
  • The divestment to Akkodis remains subject to closing conditions, with closing expected by November 2026; the Rs 1,255 crore extraordinary gain is contingent on closure.
  • Acquisitions (Cloud Wave, the XiDA business transfer, and further pipeline transactions) are subject to binding documentation, due diligence, Board approval and regulatory clearances; counterparties are referred to by project name pending execution and exchange disclosure.
  • Proforma annualised figures assume completion of the described acquisitions.
  • Aerospace qualification cycles run years, not quarters, and the segment is currently incurring losses by design while building ahead of revenue.
  • The Rs 200 crore NCD issuance carries a 12.50% p.a. coupon, and the proposed borrowing-limit increase to Rs 750 crore expands financial obligations.
  • AFV values are customer-communicated programme requirements subject to procurement timelines and executable through FY30.
  • The AGM notice flags the possibility of loss or inadequate profits, and Non-Executive Director commission includes a minimum payable even in case of inadequate profits.
  • Forward-looking statements in the Q1 FY27 presentation and Cloud Wave press release are subject to risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.

4. Management Guidance vs Observed Business Performance

Guidance restated on a proforma annualised basis. Management states the previously stated guidance on revenue, EBITDA and earnings per share stands and will be delivered at proforma annualised level. The commitment: “We divested Rs 74 crore of aerospace EBITDA. We will regain it in full, on a proforma annualised basis, in FY27 — and grow from there.” The replacement is targeted at 25% cost: Rs 74 crore of divested aerospace EBITDA replaced by the Rs 39 crore+ annualised FY27 aerospace acquisition, the XiDA business transfer, and other acquisitions in progress. Proforma annualised FY27 revenue guidance is Rs 1,377 crore on continuing operations, with EBITDA recovering to Rs 270 crore on a proforma annualised basis, and margin normalising as revenue reaches the retained cost base by Q4. EPS growth guidance is to be delivered at annualised level once acquisitions are consolidated.

Guidance milestones. Close the divestment; recover deferred revenue; conclude the acquisitions; realise the Add Solutions exit; announce Space partnerships; Defence scale-up. Two more acquisitions are in the pipeline beyond the current ones, with the third expected to close by Q3 and the fourth by Q4.

Observed performance against guidance. Retained revenue grew 94% YoY like-for-like in Q1 FY27 (Rs 183.4 crore vs Rs 94 crore), and continuing revenue excluding Add Solutions grew ~100% YoY — consistent with the stated growth trajectory. Defence revenue more than doubled YoY (~111%), against management’s FY27 guidance of 75% YoY growth. XiDA revenue grew ~62% YoY with a 29.7% margin, against management’s confidence of more than 100% YoY growth in XiDA starting FY27. Aerospace, guided to be rebuilt through manufacturing, contributed Rs 6.1 crore of revenue at an EBITDA loss, reflecting the stated build phase ahead of scale. The Cloud Wave acquisition, guided to close in Q2 with annualised FY27 revenue of Rs 180 crore and 22% EBITDA, had received Board approval at an enterprise valuation of approximately Rs 260 crore. The divestment, guided to close by November 2026, had shareholder approval for the US$237 million engineering services sale to Akkodis, with closing conditions in progress. Normalised EBITDA of Rs 41.0 crore at 11.8% margin, normalised PBT of Rs 23.1 crore and normalised PAT of Rs 20.2 crore were reported against reported figures of Rs 27.9 crore EBITDA, Rs (11.9) crore PBT and Rs (14.8) crore PAT.
