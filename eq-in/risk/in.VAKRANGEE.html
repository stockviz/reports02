Vakrangee Limited: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue pressure from strategic portfolio changes. Consolidated total income declined 1.6% quarter-on-quarter to ₹5,790.2 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 (from ₹5,886.0 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26). Management attributes this to a strategic reduction in low-margin business and the elimination of inter-company sales. On a year-on-year basis the decline is sharper: total income fell from ₹7,034.7 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26.

ATM segment losses. The consolidated segment information shows the “Sale of Automated Teller Machines Products/Services” segment recorded a loss of ₹105.7 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, following a loss of ₹172.6 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26 and a full-year FY2025-26 segment loss of ₹104.8 lakhs. Segment revenue for ATMs fell to ₹923.1 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹1,111.2 lakhs in Q4 FY2025-26 and ₹2,113.7 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26.

Structural shift away from cash-based services. The company acknowledges that with the rapid pace of digital payment penetration, cash transactions such as ATM and banking transactions are expected to slow down. It notes that standalone business models dependent solely on cash transactions—such as only White Label ATMs, only Banking (AEPS) providers, or only Money Transfer providers—face profitability and viability challenges.

Subsidiary performance drag. Vortex Engineering recorded revenue of ₹908.6 lakhs in Q1 FY2027. The consolidated results note that two subsidiaries (not reviewed by the principal auditors) reflected a total net loss after tax of ₹106.67 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Tax expense volatility. Consolidated tax expenses rose 146.2% quarter-on-quarter to ₹141.1 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, even as profit before tax increased.

Pending corporate action. The company filed a petition before the NCLT Mumbai on October 28, 2025, seeking consolidation of 1,08,31,91,807 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹1 each into 10,83,19,180 fully paid-up equity shares. The matter was listed for further consideration before the NCLT on August 6, 2026.

Lapsed warrants. Warrants that remained unexercised within the stipulated 18-month period lapsed in accordance with the terms of issue and SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018. The application money received against such warrants was forfeited and transferred to Capital Reserve in December 2025.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong quarterly profitability improvement. Consolidated profit after tax increased 39.3% quarter-on-quarter to ₹225.6 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27. EBITDA grew 24.9% quarter-on-quarter to ₹861.9 lakhs, with margins expanding to 15.4% from 12.2%. PAT margins improved by approximately 110 basis points to 4.0%. Cash profit rose 16.0% to ₹680.0 lakhs.

Debt-free balance sheet. Vakrangee Limited, along with its subsidiary companies, is debt-free. The consolidated balance sheet shows nil total debt, long-term debt, and short-term debt for both FY2025-26 and FY2024-25. Net worth stood at ₹22,335.4 lakhs as of FY2025-26.

Large addressable ATM market. India’s installed ATM capacity is approximately 250,000+ machines. The company cites banking sector branch expansion of 20,000+ new ATMs, a global ATM replacement cycle involving the switch from Windows 7 to Windows 10, a banking refresh cycle of 80,000+ ATMs over the next 18-24 months, and migration to Windows 11 with hardware upgrades from 9th to 12th Gen motherboards.

New order wins. On August 7, 2026, Vortex Engineering received a purchase order from one of India’s largest Public Sector Banks for the supply, installation, and maintenance of 540 ATMs and 1 KVA UPS, with a contract value of ₹37.79 crore (including GST). The order is to be executed over a period of 7 years, with the order received on August 6, 2026. This is described as a repeat order.

Vortex scale and capabilities. Vortex has supplied over 15,500 ATM machines to date, with an annual manufacturing capacity of 12,000 units. It holds 9 patents/IP in the field of ATMs and cash dispensers. Its Perfo® platform manages multi-vendor ATM terminals across multiple locations, with 11,000+ terminals referenced. Vortex serves clients in over 50 countries.

Kendra network expansion. Master Franchisees have been appointed in 553 districts across 31 states/UTs as of June 30, 2026, with a plan to achieve 100% district coverage. The store network comprises 23,276 outlets. More than 1,525 new ATM machines have been deployed since April 2025.

Strategic alliances. Twelve strategic alliances and ATM shipment mandates were secured during April 2025 to June 2026: 7 insurance partners, 5 lending partners, WLA license renewal, and 3 major ATM shipment mandates for Vortex. The RBI WLA authorization was renewed until March 31, 2027.

Focus on non-cash banking offerings. The company is strategically focusing on non-cash based banking offerings such as account opening, loan products, insurance services, fixed deposits, mutual funds, and NPA recovery. It has operationalized new partner banks and maintains more than 15,000 Banking BC points enabling AePS-based biometric services, with 30+ million PMJDY accounts opened.

Renewable energy expansion proposal. The company proposes to expand into the renewable energy sector, specifically manufacturing, contracting, assembling, and trading items such as lithium-ion battery packs, rooftop solar panels, hybrid inverters, battery energy storage systems, solar wafers, solar cells, solar modules, and fuel cells. This is driven by growing deployment in semi-urban and rural areas and push from Central and State governments. Management stated this expansion will provide the company with necessary flexibility to pursue new business opportunities, diversify its revenue streams, leverage its existing distribution network, and create long-term value for stakeholders.

New product launches. Vortex recently launched new products including Android ATM, MINI ATM, and Recycler ATM. The company also launched a Private & White Label apparel range and plans to diversify into new consumer and retail categories.

ESG recognition. Vakrangee earned an ‘S&P Global Bronze Class’ spot in the sustainability yearbook and scored 60 ESG Score in the Corporate Sustainability Assessment survey.

3. Key Risks

Segment concentration risk in ATM business. The ATM segment continues to report losses at the segment level, with segment results of negative ₹105.7 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27. Segment assets for ATMs stood at ₹5,784.03 lakhs, while segment liabilities were ₹2,368.80 lakhs.

Dependence on public sector bank orders. The recent ₹37.79 crore order is from a single public sector bank whose name is withheld due to confidentiality obligations. The order is to be executed over 7 years, indicating long gestation.

Technology transition risk. The company references migration to Windows 11 and hardware upgrades from 9th to 12th Gen motherboards. Failure to keep pace with these transitions could affect competitiveness.

Cash transaction decline. The company explicitly notes that cash transactions such as ATM and banking transactions are expected to slow down with digital payment penetration, creating profitability and viability challenges for standalone business models dependent on cash transactions.

Regulatory and compliance requirements. The company operates under RBI WLA authorization (renewed until March 31, 2027), SEBI Listing Regulations, and other regulatory frameworks. The NCLT petition for share consolidation remains pending.

Related party transactions. The company is seeking approval for material related party transactions with VL E-Governance & IT Solutions Limited, Vortex Engineering Private Limited, and Vakrangee Finserve Limited.

Foreign subsidiary review limitations. The consolidated results include one subsidiary located outside India whose interim financial statements were not reviewed by their auditors and were certified by management. Two subsidiaries were reviewed by other auditors, not the principal auditors.

Warrant forfeiture. The lapse of unexercised warrants within the stipulated 18-month period resulted in forfeiture of application money, which was transferred to Capital Reserve.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance on margin expansion. Management stated a long-term aim to enhance margin expansion and sustainable growth through strategic focus on high-margin business and phasing out low-margin business. Observed performance: EBITDA margin expanded to 15.4% in Q1 FY2026-27 from 12.2% in Q4 FY2025-26, and PAT margins improved by approximately 110 basis points to 4.0%. EBITDA grew 24.9% quarter-on-quarter.

Guidance on revenue trajectory. Management attributed the Q1 FY2026-27 revenue decline to strategic reduction in low-margin business and inter-company sales elimination. Observed performance: Total income declined 1.6% quarter-on-quarter to ₹5,790.2 lakhs, but profit before tax increased 67.2% quarter-on-quarter to ₹366.8 lakhs and PAT increased 39.3% to ₹225.6 lakhs.

Guidance on ATM business visibility. Management stated there is strong revenue and profitability visibility in FY27 driven by robust ATM machine orders, and that the annuity nature of the AMC business derives value from the growing scale of the installed base of ATMs. Observed performance: 139 ATMs were shipped in Q1 FY27, and 15,574 ATM machines have been shipped since inception. The ATM segment, however, reported a loss of ₹105.7 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27.

Guidance on Vortex growth. Management highlighted strong growth and profitability for the IP Product Software Business (Perfo). Observed performance: Vortex Engineering recorded revenue of ₹908.6 lakhs in Q1 FY27. The recent ₹37.79 crore order for 540 ATMs is to be executed over 7 years.

Guidance on Kendra network expansion. Management plans to achieve 100% district coverage to fast-track Kendra network expansion. Observed performance: Master Franchisees appointed in 553 districts across 31 states/UTs as of June 30, 2026, with 23,276 store outlets.

Guidance on renewable energy expansion. Management stated the expansion into renewable energy will provide flexibility to pursue new business opportunities, diversify revenue streams, leverage the existing distribution network, and create long-term value for stakeholders. Guidance on financial position. Management stated the company is well positioned to drive scalable, profitable growth with a debt-free balance sheet and cash profit rising 16.0% to ₹6.8 crore. Observed performance: Consolidated cash profit was ₹680.0 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27, up 16.0% quarter-on-quarter from ₹586.1 lakhs. Total debt remains nil.
