Headwinds and Challenges

  • Soft sequential growth. USD revenue grew just 0.1% QoQ (0.3% in constant currency) in Q1 FY27, a sharp deceleration from 1.2% QoQ in Q4 FY26 and 2.4% in Q3 FY26. INR revenue growth of 2.8% QoQ was aided by currency.
  • Financial Services weakness. The Financial Services segment declined 2.6% YoY in USD (2.5% in constant currency) and its share of revenue fell from 37.0% in Q1 FY26 to 34.0% in Q1 FY27.
  • Production segment decline. Production revenue fell 6.0% QoQ in USD (5.7% in constant currency), with segment share dropping to 19.4% from 20.7% in Q4 FY26.
  • Rest of the World softness. Rest of the World revenue declined 10.0% QoQ in USD (9.4% in constant currency), and Europe declined 2.4% QoQ (1.6% in constant currency).
  • Client count erosion. Active clients fell to 740 from 751 in Q4 FY26, and $1Mn+ clients declined to 407 from 411 a quarter earlier.
  • Margin pressure from forex. Forex losses widened to ₹2,664 Mn in Q1 FY27 from ₹786 Mn in Q4 FY26, weighing on profitability below the EBIT line.
  • Utilization below year-ago levels. Utilization (excluding trainees) at 86.4% was down 170 bps YoY, though up 70 bps QoQ.
  • Sub-contracting cost inflation. Sub-contracting expenses rose to ₹11,134 Mn from ₹9,333 Mn in Q4 FY26 and ₹6,254 Mn in Q1 FY26.
  • Pending acquisition execution risk. The proposed acquisition of Randstad Entities’ subsidiaries in the Netherlands, Australia, France and step-down subsidiaries in Belgium, Luxembourg, Portugal, Romania and Germany (enterprise valuation up to EUR 160 million, cash-free/debt-free) remains subject to execution of definitive agreements and requisite regulatory approvals; no impact was recognised as at June 30, 2026.
  • Open-source security challenge. As AI accelerates discovery of software vulnerabilities, organizations face security challenges across increasingly complex open-source ecosystems, with demand for speed and patch delivery beyond the means of any single vendor.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • AI-centric strategy traction. Management states the AI pivot is producing tangible proof points, visible in outcomes created and the size and nature of engagements won.
  • Strong order inflow. Order inflow was $1.68 Bn in Q1 FY27, up 3.1% YoY, following $1.69 Bn in each of Q3 and Q4 FY26.
  • Large-client expansion. $5Mn+ clients rose to 170 (up 11 YoY), $10Mn+ to 104 (up 14 YoY), $20Mn+ to 52 (up 11 YoY), and $50Mn+ to 15 (up 1 YoY).
  • Consumer and Technology & Services momentum. Consumer grew 17.3% YoY in USD (18.2% in constant currency) and Technology & Services grew 9.7% YoY (10.0% in constant currency).
  • North America recovery. North America grew 2.4% QoQ and 5.4% YoY in USD, with its revenue share rising to 73.9% from 72.3% in Q4 FY26.
  • Margin expansion. EBIT margin expanded to 15.5%, up 120 bps YoY and 40 bps QoQ; EBIT rose 27.9% YoY to ₹17,993 Mn.
  • Profit growth. Net profit rose 17.1% YoY and 9.5% QoQ to ₹14,686 Mn; EPS (basic) rose to ₹49.46 from ₹42.33 a year earlier.
  • Deal wins across verticals. Wins include a US-based insurance company (AI-led infrastructure modernization), a global travel management company (integrated iRun model), a US-based multinational (strategic vendor consolidation via iRun, iTransform and Business AI LoB — the large deal referenced at Investor Day), a global automotive manufacturer, a climate and energy solutions manufacturer (unified enterprise data platform), a US financial payments company, a European consumer company (AI-powered data and decision intelligence), a global industrial conglomerate (AI-driven proposal management), a US leisure travel company and a global financial administration company (BlueVerse Voicing), a Middle East telecom (managed services), and a multinational conglomerate (multi-year SAP S/4HANA engagement).
  • Partner ecosystem expansion. LTM is an early partner in AWS’s Agentic Process Transformation program; a Microsoft Agent 365 launch partner and among the fastest-growing GSIs in Azure ACR growth; part of the Salesforce FDE Program; and holds Google Cloud and Databricks Partner of the Year Awards, a Rubrik Global GSI Delivery Excellence award, and a Snowflake Partner Legacy Award.
  • Open-source security opportunity. Collaboration with IBM and Red Hat on Lightwell to help enterprises safeguard the open-source software supply chain through AI-driven vulnerability remediation, leveraging LTM’s IBM Platinum Partner status and expertise in application modernization, cloud, cybersecurity and DevSecOps.
  • Analyst recognition. Leader positions in ISG Provider Lens Databricks Ecosystem Partners 2026 (Global) and SAP Ecosystem 2026 (U.S.), and Avasant’s Oracle Cloud ERP Services 2026 RadarView; Major Contender in multiple Everest Group PEAK Matrix assessments; Enterprise Innovator in HFS Horizons reports.
  • Capital efficiency. Return on Capital Employed Equity rose to 29.8% from 29.2% in Q4 FY26; Return on Equity at 22.0%.
  • Cash generation. Free cash flow of ₹9,218 Mn in Q1 FY27; cash and investments of ₹1,50,213 Mn.

Key Risks

  • Currency exposure. 78.6% of revenue is USD-denominated; the USD/INR period average rate moved from 85.33 in Q1 FY26 to 94.87 in Q1 FY27. Forex loss of ₹2,664 Mn in Q1 FY27 versus a gain of ₹1,291 Mn in Q1 FY26. Hedges outstanding rose to $5,031 Mn from $4,282 Mn in Q4 FY26.
  • Client concentration. Top 5 clients contributed 23.5% of revenue and Top 10 contributed 32.0% in Q1 FY27, both up QoQ.
  • Segment concentration. Financial Services remains the largest segment at 34.0% of revenue and is contracting YoY.
  • Acquisition execution and integration. The Randstad transaction is subject to definitive agreements and regulatory approvals, with no impact recognised as at June 30, 2026.
  • Fair value gains in other income. A fair value gain of ₹1,978 million was recognised in Other Income on convertible instruments held in Voicing.AI, Inc., which were converted into equity instruments during the quarter — a non-recurring contributor to consolidated other income of ₹2,554 Mn.
  • Regulatory/labour cost impact. The New Labour Codes, effective November 21, 2025, resulted in an incremental past-service-cost expense presented as an exceptional item (₹5,281 Mn for the year ended March 31, 2026; ₹622 Mn in Q4 FY26).
  • Promoter group reclassification. Nabha Power Limited sought reclassification from ‘Promoter Group’ to ‘Public’; the Board approved the request on July 11, 2026, subject to requisite approvals. NSE and BSE No-Objection Letters were dated September 4, 2026.
  • Attrition and talent. TTM attrition at 13.3%, flat QoQ but down 110 bps YoY; total employees declined by 64 QoQ to 87,886, with Sales & Support down 245 QoQ.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • CEO and MD Venu Lambu stated: “With a strong order book and healthy pipeline across our industry segments, we are confident that our growth momentum will continue to build through the year.” Observed Q1 FY27 performance: USD revenue growth of 0.1% QoQ and 6.1% YoY; constant-currency growth of 0.3% QoQ and 6.4% YoY; order inflow of $1.68 Bn, down 0.3% QoQ but up 3.1% YoY.
  • Management characterised Q1 FY27 as reflecting “continued profitable growth” and progress on the AI-centric strategy. Observed: EBIT margin of 15.5% (up 120 bps YoY), PAT up 17.1% YoY, and EPS (basic) up to ₹49.46 from ₹42.33.
  • On the Lightwell collaboration with IBM and Red Hat, LTM plans to offer a comprehensive portfolio of services spanning remediation strategy, dependency analysis, risk-based prioritization, remediation program management, DevSecOps integration, testing and validation, and large-scale deployment support. No revenue or booking contribution from this offering is disclosed in the supplied material.
  • The Randstad acquisition (up to EUR 160 million enterprise valuation) is described as proposed and subject to definitive agreements and regulatory approvals; no impact has been recognised in the consolidated results as at June 30, 2026.
  • Investor engagement: LTM is scheduled to participate in the J.P. Morgan C-Suite Fireside Chat Virtual Series on September 9, 2026, and the UBS India Summit 2026 in Mumbai on September 11, 2026.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative shifted from broad structural concerns about slow pilot scaling and deflationary pressure to a sharper focus on acute operational bottlenecks like power constraints and chip innovation risks. Persistent optimism centered on the essential integration role of IT vendors and expanding monetization through new delivery models. However, the focus shifted from surging deal pipelines to accelerating cloud growth and multi-year contracts as the primary near-term drivers.

Fears that came true

  • The slow pilot-to-production scaling flagged early materialized as limited conversion rates, leading to disappointing actual returns relative to predictions in mid-2026.
  • The modest near-term ROI concern proved accurate, as actual returns fell significantly short of predictions despite growing AI adoption.
  • Budget realignment toward hyperscaler infrastructure resulted in near-term power constraints and shortages, causing actual returns to disappoint relative to predictions.
