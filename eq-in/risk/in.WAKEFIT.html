WAKEFIT — Summary of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material price volatility: Significant volatility in raw material prices following the Middle East situation, particularly across key mattress category inputs such as Polyol and TDI. This also impacted supply chain costs across other categories.
  • Input cost pressure on margins: The commodity volatility affected the mattress category directly and raised supply chain costs in other categories, creating cost pressure even as revenue grew.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Mattress business momentum: The mattress business grew 27.3% YoY, outpacing overall revenue growth of 16.6% YoY in Q1 FY27.
  • Cross-selling opportunity: Significant opportunity to drive cross-selling across mattresses, furniture, and furnishings through an expanding offline footprint.
  • Category mix shift: Furniture and furnishing are expected to contribute meaningfully over the next three to four years.
  • Retail expansion: The company added 27 COCO stores in Q1 FY27, reaching 165 active COCO stores across 100 cities in 22 states and 2 UTs, alongside 2,250 MBO stores.
  • Repeat customer base: Repeat customers contributed 36.7% of revenue.
  • IPO proceeds deployment: Future projected taxable profits and the use of IPO proceeds towards strategic initiatives, including planned investments towards expansion of the retail network.
  • Capital expenditure programme: Planned FY27 capex of around INR 1,000–1,200 mn, with approximately 80% allocated towards retail expansion (particularly the jumbo store format) and 20% towards manufacturing automation and other regular business upgrades.

3. Key Risks

  • Commodity and supply chain risk: Volatility in Polyol and TDI prices and related supply chain costs across categories.
  • Execution risk on expansion: The growth plan depends on adding nearly 80 COCO stores during FY27 and deploying capex on schedule.
  • Dependence on offline footprint for cross-selling: The cross-selling opportunity is tied to the expanding offline retail network.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Store additions: Management remains on track to add nearly 80 COCO stores during FY27. Observed performance: 27 COCO stores were added in Q1 FY27, bringing the active network to 165 stores.
  • Capex: Planned FY27 capex of around INR 1,000–1,200 mn remains on track, with approximately 80% towards retail expansion and 20% towards manufacturing automation and other regular business upgrades. No capex spend figure for the quarter was disclosed in the material.
  • Growth trajectory: Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was INR 4,049.1 mn, up 16.6% YoY; gross profit was INR 2,310.9 mn at a 57.1% margin; reported EBITDA was INR 564.0 mn; profit after tax was INR 233.8 mn.
