Reliance Power Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • CBI search and seizure operation: On July 18, 2026, Reliance Power disclosed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations that the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) conducted a search and seizure operation at the Company’s registered office premises. The operation was stated to have concluded, and the Company and its officials cooperated with the investigating authority. The disclosure linked the action to transactions involving Reliance Commercial Finance Limited and Reliance Home Finance Limited. The Company stated there was no impact on business operations, which continued in the normal course.
  • Criminal proceedings: The Company has been named as an accused in the first supplementary chargesheet filed in the Reliance Commercial Finance Limited matter, as learnt from a Press Release issued by the CBI.
  • Institutional shareholder dissent at the AGM: At the 32nd Annual General Meeting held on August 14, 2026, voting results showed significant opposition from Public Institutions on certain resolutions:
    • Resolution 1 (adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY ended March 31, 2026): Public Institutions voted 43.7983% in favour and 56.2017% against on votes polled.
    • Resolution 6 (Special Resolution — Issue of securities through Qualified Institutions Placement): Public Institutions voted 53.3019% in favour and 46.6981% against.
    • Resolution 7 (Special Resolution — Issue of Non-Convertible Debentures): Public Institutions voted 78.2828% in favour and 21.7172% against.
  • Low public non-institution participation: Public Non-Institutions holding 2,349,801,868 shares polled only 1.9957% of votes on Resolution 1, indicating limited retail participation in the voting process.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Fundraising approvals: The AGM passed Special Resolutions enabling the Company to issue securities through Qualified Institutions Placement (Resolution 6) and to issue Non-Convertible Debentures (Resolution 7). These approvals provide the Company with options to raise capital.
  • Board continuity and governance: The AGM passed ordinary resolutions for the re-appointment of Shri Sachin Mohapatra as a Director retiring by rotation (Resolution 2), appointment of Statutory Auditors and fixation of their remuneration (Resolution 3), and remuneration to Cost Auditors (Resolution 4). A Special Resolution for the appointment of Dr. Avinash Gupta (DIN: 02784546) as an Independent Director was also passed (Resolution 5).
  • Financial results disclosure: The Board Meeting held on August 6, 2026 approved the unaudited financial results (both Consolidated and Standalone) for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 of FY 2026-27, along with Limited Review Reports by the Statutory Auditors.

3. Key Risks

  • Legal and regulatory risk: The Company faces criminal proceedings as an accused in the CBI’s supplementary chargesheet related to Reliance Commercial Finance Limited. The Company has stated it intends to take appropriate legal steps to safeguard its interests and those of its stakeholders.
  • Reputational and market perception risk: The CBI search and seizure operation at the registered office, along with the chargesheet, may affect stakeholder confidence.
  • Shareholder approval risk: The significant institutional dissent on Resolution 1 (56.2017% of institutional votes against) and Resolution 6 (46.6981% of institutional votes against) indicates potential governance or strategic concerns among institutional investors.
  • Capital raising execution risk: While approvals for QIP and NCD issuance were obtained, the actual execution and market reception of these instruments remain subject to market conditions and investor appetite.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on legal matters: Management stated that the Company shall take all appropriate steps to safeguard its interest as well as the interests of all its shareholders and other stakeholders, as may be legally advised. This was in response to the CBI chargesheet naming the Company as an accused.
  • Guidance on operational impact: In the July 18, 2026 disclosure, the Company stated there is no impact on the business operations and that it continues to operate its business in the normal course. The Company also confirmed full cooperation with the investigating authority.
  • Observed performance: The Company disclosed its unaudited financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 on August 6, 2026, indicating continued financial reporting and operational activity. The AGM on August 14, 2026 proceeded with all resolutions passed, including adoption of financial statements for FY ended March 31, 2026, suggesting the Company maintained its corporate calendar and reporting obligations despite the disclosed legal developments.
