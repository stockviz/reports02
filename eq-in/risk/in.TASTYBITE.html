Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory compliance lapse: The Company delayed prior intimation of a Board Meeting held on 06 August 2025 for approval of financial results, resulting in non-compliance with Regulation 29(1) of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. NSE and BSE each levied a fine of INR 10,000. The delay was attributed to technical issues in XBRL filing validation.
  • Elevated employee turnover: Turnover rate for permanent employees rose to 17.47% in FY 2025-26 from 10.95% in FY 2024-25 and 7.76% in FY 2023-24, with male turnover at 16.87% in FY 2025-26.
  • Rising shareholder and customer grievances: Shareholder complaints increased to 34 in FY 2025-26 from 29 in FY 2024-25; customer complaints rose to 200 from 111; community complaints rose to 2 from nil.
  • Water and energy intensity: Water consumption and energy efficiency are identified as material issues, with the Company noting its manufacturing processes are water-intensive and that it seeks to reduce freshwater withdrawal.
  • Sustainable packaging pressure: Plastic packaging is identified as a material risk, with the Company noting the need to develop sustainable packaging solutions and manage post-consumer plastic waste through EPR arrangements.
  • Related-party concentration: Sales to related parties were 43.30% of total sales in FY 2025-26 (down from 51.10% in FY 2024-25), indicating continued dependence on group entities for a significant share of revenue.
  • No formal LCA yet: The Company has not formally conducted a Life Cycle Assessment for its products during the reporting period, though it is evaluating scope and methodology for a forthcoming period.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Diversified product mix: Ready-to-eat contributed 64.10% of turnover and formed frozen products 35.90%, both under NIC code 107.
  • Export orientation: Exports accounted for 68.80% of total turnover; the Company serves 18 states in India and 22 countries internationally.
  • Broad customer base: The Company serves QSRs, cloud kitchens, and the HoReCa segment, positioning itself as a “brand behind the brands” with customized solutions and efficient turnaround times.
  • Sustainability initiatives: The Company commenced water recycling in FY 2025-26, recycling more than 108 million liters, with no deviations from set targets. It operates an in-house biogas plant processing a significant portion of food waste, has implemented variable frequency drives (VFDs) to optimize energy consumption, and uses solar power and biomass alongside MSEDCL supply.
  • Sustainable sourcing: 100% of fresh vegetables are sourced from local farmers and farmer groups, supported by a Supplier Code of Conduct.
  • Certifications and accreditations: ISO 14001, ISO 45001, FSSC 22000 (Lloyd’s Register Quality Assurance), DSIR accreditation for the R&D department valid till March 2028, and valid FSSAI food manufacturing licenses.
  • EPR compliance: The Company holds valid CPCB registration and has partnered with an authorized agency for collection and recycling of plastic waste across India.
  • Community engagement: CSR initiatives focus on farmer development, skill enhancement for women, natural resource management, and rural school development; a significant portion of the workforce is recruited locally.
  • Governance framework: All nine NGRBC principles are covered by Board-approved policies, with senior management regularly reviewing performance and independent audits by external parties.

Key Risks

  • Regulatory and compliance risk: Demonstrated by the SEBI (LODR) Regulation 29(1) penalty of INR 10,000 each from NSE and BSE for delayed Board Meeting intimation.
  • Water availability and consumption risk: Identified as a material risk given the water-intensive nature of operations; mitigated through the 4R framework (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Recover), advanced ETPs, and zero liquid discharge technologies.
  • Energy efficiency risk: Identified as a material risk; mitigated through solar power, biomass, VFDs, and variable speed drives.
  • Sustainable packaging risk: Identified as a material risk; mitigated through developing sustainable packaging, reducing laminate thickness, minimizing paper usage, and EPR arrangements with authorized recyclers.
  • Community support risk: Identified as a material opportunity; the Company maintains proximity to local communities and engages through continuous collaboration, local employment, and CSR initiatives.
  • Related-party transaction risk: Material RPTs with Preferred Brands International, Inc., Mars Food UK Limited, and Mars Food US LLC for FY 2027-28 were approved at the AGM, with sales to related parties at 43.30% of total sales in FY 2025-26.
  • Human capital risk: Permanent employee turnover increased to 17.47% in FY 2025-26; no differently abled employees or workers are currently employed, though the Company states it is enhancing accessibility features.
  • Grievance volume risk: Customer complaints increased to 200 in FY 2025-26 from 111 in FY 2024-25; shareholder complaints rose to 34 from 29.
