Raj Rayon Industries Limited — Recent Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential revenue decline: Total income from operations fell to ₹20,546.28 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹29,588.09 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Year-on-year, it was also lower than the ₹26,039.96 lakhs recorded in the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Profitability pressure: Net profit after tax declined to ₹685.88 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from ₹1,403.21 lakhs in the preceding quarter (Q4 FY26), though it was higher than ₹607.27 lakhs in Q1 FY26. Basic EPS fell to ₹0.12 from ₹0.25 quarter-on-quarter.
  • Modified audit conclusion: The limited review by the statutory auditors for the June 30, 2026 results was completed with a modified conclusion.
  • Legacy inoperative bank accounts: The management is in the process of obtaining details of three inoperative bank accounts continuing in the name of the Company from prior to the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process. Letters with board resolutions have been sent to these bankers for closure, and responses are awaited.
  • Raw material volatility: The de-bottlenecking exercise was timed to coincide with a period of raw material volatility arising from the West Asia geopolitical crisis, which the Company used to minimise loss of productive days.
  • Execution and funding risks on expansion: The Phase II expansion, its funding, timelines and expected revenue and cost outcomes are subject to receipt of statutory, regulatory and corporate approvals, availability of financing on acceptable terms, and prevailing market conditions.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • ₹650 crore Phase II expansion: The Board approved a Phase II expansion at the existing Silvassa facility, adding 300 TPD of polyester yarn capacity and 50 TPD of recycled polyester yarn capacity, with full capacity commissioning targeted in CY2028. The Board also approved arranging requisite funds through available means and sources.
  • Revenue potential: At peak utilisation, the Company targets annual revenue potential of approximately ₹2,700–3,000 crore, with FY29 expected to be the first full year of commercialisation.
  • Value-added mix: At peak utilisation, value-added, specialty and sustainable products are expected to account for approximately 80% of revenue.
  • Downstream entry into fabrics: Fabric production of approximately 10 TPD is targeted to commence in Q3 FY27, marking entry into the fabrics segment.
  • Doubling of DDY capacity: Value-Added Dope-Dyed Yarn capacity is targeted to double, with commencement also expected in Q3 FY27.
  • Recycled polyester opportunity: Global brands are raising recycled content, with some targeting 100% recycled composition; recycled polyester yarns command a 30–40% premium over conventional virgin yarns. The proposed 50 TPD recycled facility is intended to internalise a requirement presently met by promoter group company SVG Fashions Private Limited through outsourced conversion, completing backward integration.
  • SVG Group credentials: Through SVG, the Company draws on approximately two decades of recycled textile manufacturing experience, around 300 million PET bottles recycled, GRS and OEKO-TEX certifications, an Intertek-certified testing laboratory, and supply to leading global sportswear and footwear brands.
  • Technical textiles and specialty yarns: Expansion into Industrial Denier Yarn for geogrids, seat belts, agritech and industrial packaging; bi-component yarns for premium suede-like fabrics; and high-performance functional yarns with antibacterial, antimicrobial and permanent moisture-wicking properties.
  • Capacity already enhanced: De-bottlenecking and system upgradation completed in May 2026 increased continuous polymerisation capacity from 350 TPD to 400 TPD, delivering finer process control and lower specific fuel consumption.
  • Cost and ESG initiatives: A ₹25 crore biomass heating system is to be commissioned by December 2026, eliminating approximately 23,000 kg per day of furnace oil consumption and lowering cost of production by approximately 1–2%. With furnace oil eliminated, electricity becomes the sole energy input, and the Company intends to meet close to 50% of this through solar generation, expected to deliver a further approximately 1–2% reduction in cost of production.
  • Related party transactions: The Company and related parties have been engaged in textile business, supply of fabrics and spinning yarn, and finance business; the proposed related party transactions are expected to aid growth. Shareholder approval is sought for related party transactions and limits with entities such as SVG Fashions Private Limited up to ₹900 crore.
  • Balance sheet position: Management stated the Company remained cash surplus throughout the de-bottlenecking exercise and that leverage remains low, which it believes positions the Company to fund Phase II without straining the balance sheet.

3. Key Risks

  • Audit qualification: The modified conclusion in the auditor’s limited review of the June 30, 2026 results.
  • Regulatory and approval risk: The Phase II expansion, its funding, timelines and expected revenue and cost outcomes are subject to statutory, regulatory and corporate approvals.
  • Financing risk: Availability of financing on acceptable terms is a condition for the expansion.
  • Market risk: Prevailing market conditions may affect outcomes.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: Statements about future status, events or circumstances are based on estimates and anticipated effects of future events and are subject to numerous risks and uncertainties; actual results may differ materially.
  • Legacy account risk: Inoperative bank accounts from the pre-CIRP period remain open, with closure pending banker response.
  • Raw material volatility: Volatility arising from the West Asia geopolitical crisis affected the operating environment during the de-bottlenecking period.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance — Phase II expansion: Board approved up to ₹650 crore capital expenditure for expansion of business and related infrastructure, including acquisition/installation of plant, machinery, equipment, facilities and other assets, with funds to be arranged through available means and sources. Full capacity commissioning is targeted for CY2028, with FY29 expected to be the first full year of commercialisation and peak-utilisation revenue potential of approximately ₹2,700–3,000 crore.
  • Observed performance — Q1 FY27: Total income from operations of ₹20,546.28 lakhs and profit after tax of ₹685.88 lakhs, with basic EPS of ₹0.12. This compares with ₹29,588.09 lakhs income and ₹1,403.21 lakhs PAT in Q4 FY26, and ₹26,039.96 lakhs income and ₹607.27 lakhs PAT in Q1 FY26.
  • Guidance — downstream and value-added initiatives: Fabric production of approximately 10 TPD and doubling of DDY capacity are both targeted to commence in Q3 FY27.
  • Guidance — de-bottlenecking: Completed in May 2026, raising continuous polymerisation capacity from 350 TPD to 400 TPD; the Company states this delivered higher throughput, finer process control and lower specific fuel consumption.
  • Guidance — biomass and solar: Biomass heating system targeted for commissioning by December 2026 at ₹25 crore capital outlay, eliminating approximately 23,000 kg per day of furnace oil consumption and lowering cost of production by approximately 1–2%; solar is intended to meet close to 50% of electricity requirements with a further approximately 1–2% cost reduction.
  • Guidance — balance sheet: Management stated the Company remained cash surplus during de-bottlenecking and leverage remains low, positioning it to fund Phase II without straining the balance sheet.
  • Guidance — related party growth: The Company states that proposed related party transactions will aid growth of the Company’s business, with shareholder approval sought for limits up to ₹900 crore with SVG Fashions Private Limited.
  • Governance timeline: The 33rd Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 12:00 Noon through Video Conferencing/Other Audio-Visual Means, with agenda items including adoption of audited financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, re-appointment of Ms. Sapna Rajkumar Agarwal retiring by rotation, ratification of M/s. C. Sahoo & Co. as Cost Auditors for ₹1,00,000 plus out-of-cost expenses and taxes, and approval of related party transactions and limits.
