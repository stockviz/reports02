IRIS RegTech Solutions — Recent Corporate Announcements

Headwinds and Challenges

  • Q1FY27 profitability weakened. Consolidated EBITDA for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 stood at INR 0.8 crore, against INR 1.4 crore in Q1FY26. The company attributed the moderation to a deliberate step-up in sales and marketing investment as it expands its footprint in international markets.
  • Consolidated loss for the quarter. The consolidated statement shows a net loss of ₹97.12 lakh for Q1FY27, compared with a profit of ₹22.43 lakh in Q1FY26 and ₹414.92 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Loss before exceptional items and tax was ₹95.36 lakh, versus ₹78.70 lakh profit a year earlier.
  • RegTech segment reported a loss at segment level. While RegTech revenue grew 51% YoY, its segment result was negative ₹138.74 lakh in Q1FY27 (against negative ₹128.56 lakh in Q1FY26), reflecting continued investment in the disclosure management business.
  • DataTech segment loss widened to ₹122.04 lakh in Q1FY27 from a profit of ₹11.44 lakh in Q1FY26.
  • Cost base rose sharply. Employee benefits expense increased to ₹2,051.96 lakh from ₹1,441.19 lakh YoY; depreciation and amortisation rose to ₹143.66 lakh from ₹49.63 lakh; finance costs rose to ₹34.04 lakh from ₹15.69 lakh.
  • Q1 is seasonally soft. Management noted that the first quarter is traditionally a softer quarter for the business.
  • Two subsidiaries were not reviewed by their auditors for the quarter — IRIS Business Services (Asia) Pte. Ltd. and one other — whose financial information reflected total revenue of ₹132.99 lakh and net profit after tax of ₹4.40 lakh before consolidation adjustments. Management stated this information is not material to the Group.
  • Low shareholder participation in AGM voting. Only 39.0221% of outstanding shares were voted on the resolutions at the 26th AGM, with public institutions voting just 1.7066% of their holdings.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong topline growth. Q1FY27 total revenue was ₹35.2 crore, up 33% YoY, with operating revenues up 30% YoY.
  • Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) momentum. ARR grew 7% during Q1FY27 and was up 11% as of July 31, 2026 compared with March 31, 2026.
  • RegTech was the fastest-growing segment, with revenue up 51% YoY, led by expansion of the IRIS CARBON disclosure management platform.
  • SupTech grew 20% YoY with higher profitability, led by large-scale regulatory digital-reporting implementations across Africa and the Middle East.
  • IRIS Carbon new-customer traction. ARR from the new disclosure management solution surpassed US$1 million. Management cited several significant new customers and strategic partnerships, including collaboration with several Big4 accounting firms across Europe.
  • South Africa contract extension. On July 28, 2026, the company confirmed extension of its engagement with Xpert Decisions Systems (Pty) Ltd for Phase 4 of the CODI deposit insurance solution (technology development, implementation and support services). This follows prior disclosures dated March 3, 2023 and December 11, 2025.
  • New product modules launched across SupTech and Banking RegTech platforms, strengthening end-to-end reporting capabilities.
  • Leadership strengthening. Mr. Shailesh Gupta was appointed Business Head – SupTech & Bank-RegTech and designated Senior Management Personnel effective August 20, 2026. He brings over 25 years of enterprise technology and RegTech experience and was instrumental in the initial development of IRIS iFile, IRIS CARBON and IRIS iDeal.
  • Global regulator footprint. The IRIS SupTech suite is used by 30+ regulators worldwide, including business registries, central banks and stock exchanges/securities commissions.
  • ESOP grant. On August 7, 2026, the NRC approved grant of 1,21,000 stock options under IRIS ESOP 2023 at an exercise price of ₹10, with vesting after disclosure of FY2028-29 financial results subject to performance criteria.

Key Risks

  • Profitability pressure from growth investment. The step-up in sales and marketing spend, together with rising employee, depreciation and finance costs, has compressed EBITDA and pushed the consolidated result into a loss for Q1FY27.
  • Segment concentration and mix. SupTech remains the largest revenue and profit contributor (₹1,949.92 lakh revenue and ₹627.09 lakh segment result in Q1FY27); weakness in this segment would materially affect overall performance.
  • RegTech and DataTech losses. Both segments reported negative segment results in Q1FY27, and their path to profitability is not quantified in the disclosures.
  • International execution and currency exposure. Operations span multiple geographies (Africa, Middle East, Europe, South Africa). Exchange differences on translation of foreign operations were negative ₹1.72 lakh in Q1FY27, and fair value changes on cash flow hedges were positive ₹36.07 lakh.
  • Contract confidentiality. Commercial terms and timelines for the CODI Phase 4 extension are confidential and subject to a formal agreement proposed to be executed between the parties.
  • Dependence on partners. The South Africa engagement is routed through Xpert Decisions Systems (Pty) Ltd as partner.
  • Forward-looking statement risk. The company’s safe harbor statement notes that statements about future status, events or circumstances are subject to numerous risks and uncertainties and are not necessarily predictive of future results.
  • Governance continuity. Resolutions 2, 3 and 4 at the 26th AGM related to the re-appointment of Mr. Balachandran Krishnan as director and Whole Time Director, and Ms. Deepta Rangarajan as Whole Time Director — both promoter-interested resolutions.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Growth commentary vs. reported results. Management stated its focus remains on growing recurring revenue through accelerated investments in sales, marketing and product innovation. Observed: revenue grew 33% YoY to ₹35.2 crore, but EBITDA fell to ₹0.8 crore from ₹1.4 crore and the consolidated result was a loss of ₹97.12 lakh — consistent with the stated investment posture but showing near-term margin cost.
  • ARR trajectory. Management highlighted ARR growth of 7% in Q1FY27 and 11% as of July 31, 2026 versus March 31, 2026. This is the only quantified recurring-revenue metric provided.
  • SupTech performance. Management stated SupTech delivered 20% YoY growth with healthy operating margins. Observed: SupTech revenue rose to ₹1,949.92 lakh from ₹1,621.04 lakh YoY, and segment result rose to ₹627.09 lakh from ₹474.76 lakh — consistent with the statement.
  • RegTech performance. Management described RegTech as the fastest-growing segment, up 51% YoY. Observed: RegTech revenue rose to ₹1,153.47 lakh from ₹762.41 lakh YoY, though the segment result remained negative at ₹138.74 lakh.
  • IRIS Carbon. Management stated ARR from the new disclosure management solution surpassed US$1 million and cited marquee customer wins and Big4 partnerships in Europe. No separate revenue or profit figure for IRIS Carbon is disclosed.
  • South Africa contract. Management stated a key contract in South Africa was successfully extended. This is corroborated by the July 28, 2026 disclosure of the CODI Phase 4 extension with XDS.
  • Leadership. Management stated it strengthened the leadership team with Mr. Shailesh Gupta’s appointment. This is corroborated by the August 7, 2026 board approval, with effect from August 20, 2026.
  • AGM outcomes. All four resolutions (adoption of FY26 audited financial statements; re-appointment of Mr. Balachandran Krishnan as rotating director; his re-appointment as Whole Time Director; and re-appointment of Ms. Deepta Rangarajan as Whole Time Director) were passed with the requisite majority on August 14, 2026. Total votes in favour were 80,26,124 against 30 votes against — 99.9996% in favour on votes polled.
