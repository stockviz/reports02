Shanthi Gears Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sharp year-on-year decline in Q1 FY27 performance. Revenue for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was ₹115.49 crores, compared with ₹134.89 crores in Q1 FY26. Profit before tax fell to ₹14.17 crores from ₹30.62 crores in the same quarter a year earlier. Profit after tax was ₹9.85 crores in Q1 FY27 versus ₹12.69 crores in Q1 FY26.
  • Sequential softening. Compared with the quarter ended 31 March 2026, revenue declined from ₹135.0 crores to ₹115.9 crores, and profit before tax fell from ₹25.03 crores to ₹14.17 crores.
  • Statutory impact of the new Labour Codes. On 21 November 2025, the Government of India notified four Labour Codes consolidating 29 existing labour laws. The Company recognised an incremental impact on its defined benefit obligation of ₹3.22 crores for the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and ₹4.78 crores in the audited financial results for the year ended 31 March 2026, presented as “Statutory impact of new labour code” under exceptional items. The Company continues to monitor finalisation of Central/State Rules and government clarifications on other aspects of the Labour Code.
  • Senior management departure. Mr. Malliraj S stepped down as Head – Strategic Sourcing effective closing hours of 15 September 2026, citing personal reasons.
  • Dissent on related-party remuneration. At the 53rd AGM held on 29 July 2026, the special resolution on payment of commission to Mr. M A M Arunachalam for FY 2025-26 passed with 96.3167% of votes polled in favour and 3.6833% against. Public institutions voted overwhelmingly against (99.9924% of their votes polled), while public non-institutions voted 99.7136% in favour.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Order book visibility. The unexecuted order book as on 30 June 2026 stood at ₹378 crores.
  • Cash generation. The Company generated free cash flow of ₹16.2 crores during Q1 FY27.
  • Return on capital. Return on average invested capital (ROIC) was 17% in Q1 FY27.
  • Stated strategic priorities. The Company continues to focus on four key priorities: Revenue, Profitability, Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) and Free Cash Flow (FCF).
  • Other income support. Other income rose to ₹4.36 crores in Q1 FY27 from ₹3.71 crores in Q1 FY26, partly cushioning the earnings decline.
  • Governance continuity. All seven resolutions transacted at the 53rd AGM were passed with the requisite majority, including adoption of FY26 audited financial statements, declaration of dividend, re-appointment of Mr. Mukesh Ahuja as Director retiring by rotation, commission payable to Non-Executive Directors for FY 2025-26 to FY 2029-2030, appointment of Mr. K Ilango as Non-Executive & Independent Director, commission to Mr. M A M Arunachalam for FY 2025-26, and ratification of Cost Auditor remuneration.

3. Key Risks

  • Earnings volatility. The steep year-on-year and sequential decline in revenue and profit indicates sensitivity to demand conditions in the gearbox and gear products business.
  • Regulatory cost uncertainty. The finalisation of Central/State Rules and government clarifications on the Labour Codes could result in further accounting effects beyond the amounts already recognised.
  • Key personnel risk. The departure of the Head – Strategic Sourcing effective 15 September 2026 removes a senior management position.
  • Shareholder dissent. The significant institutional opposition to the commission payable to Mr. M A M Arunachalam for FY 2025-26 signals governance-related scrutiny on related-party remuneration.
  • Single-segment concentration. The Company’s main business is manufacture of gearboxes and gear products, with no separate reportable segments under Ind AS 108, and no subsidiary, associate or joint venture.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • The Company states it continues to focus on four key priorities — Revenue, Profitability, ROIC and FCF. Against this stated focus, observed Q1 FY27 performance shows revenue of ₹115.49 crores (down from ₹134.89 crores in Q1 FY26), profit before tax of ₹14.17 crores (down from ₹30.62 crores), ROIC of 17%, and free cash flow of ₹16.2 crores.
  • The Company has disclosed that it continues to monitor the finalisation of Central/State Rules and government clarifications on other aspects of the Labour Code and would provide appropriate accounting effect on the basis of such developments as needed. The amounts recognised to date are ₹3.22 crores for the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and ₹4.78 crores for the year ended 31 March 2026.
  • The unexecuted order book of ₹378 crores as on 30 June 2026 provides forward visibility, while the Q1 FY27 results reflect the revenue and profit decline actually recorded in the quarter.
