Music Broadcast Limited (RADIOCITY) — Q1 FY27 Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Year-on-year revenue decline: Revenue for Q1 FY27 stood at INR44.5 crores compared to INR49.3 crores in Q1 FY26, a decline of 10% year-on-year.
  • Soft start to Q2: Management stated that Q2 is historically a weaker quarter and “it has begun on a softer note.”
  • Subdued pure radio advertising: Management noted that “pure radio advertising is subdued,” with advertiser spend mix shifting toward the Radio Plus (creative) business.
  • Regulatory constraints on radio: The industry has been lobbying the government for relief on allowing news and current affairs on radio; this has not yet materialized. Management said that until it happens, the business has been engineered to stay profitable through cost measures.
  • Government spend unpredictability: Government remains an important contributor to radio, but management said it is difficult to predict government spends because they depend on elections and topicality of government activity.
  • Seasonality: H1 typically contributes about 45% of the business and H2 about 55%.
  • Prior-year impairment context: An impairment of INR49 crores was taken in the year ended March 31, 2026, driven by lower revenue performance and a sharp fall in share price (to INR4.5 per share). The share price had recovered to INR6.4 as of the call, but remains below the net book value of the company.
  • Legal/promoter disputes: A petition under sections 241, 242 and 244 of the Companies Act, 2013 was filed with the NCLT (Allahabad Bench) on July 10, 2023 by Mr. Mahendra Mohan Gupta and Mr. Shailesh Gupta against other promoters and promoter group members of the Holding Company. An EGM of the Holding Company on May 29, 2026 passed resolutions for removal of seven Independent Directors and one Whole-time Director, but the NCLAT directed that implementation remain in abeyance pending adjudication by the NCLT. Management does not anticipate any material adverse impact on the Company’s financial position.
  • PPL music licensing litigation: In the matter of the Company vs Phonographic Performance Limited (PPL), the Madras High Court partly allowed PPL’s appeal providing a “minimum floor rate” of INR660 per needle hour for use of sound recordings for 2010–2020. The Company filed a Special Leave Petition before the Supreme Court. A contempt petition led to an order dated July 31, 2024 directing deposit of 50% of the projected amount (INR1,550 lakhs); the Division Bench of the Madras High Court, via common judgement dated December 10, 2025, allowed the Company’s LPA and set aside that order, and the contempt petition was disposed of on December 12, 2025. The SLP remains pending. Based on external legal counsel opinion and internal assessment, the Company does not expect outflow of economic resources.
  • MAT credit derecognition: During the year ended March 31, 2026, following the Finance Act, 2026, the Company derecognised MAT credit entitlement of INR1,032 lakhs to the extent considered no longer recoverable.
  • Cost savings largely exhausted: Management indicated most cost savings have been done and current cost levels are optimal; no further meaningful cost reduction is expected.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Sequential revenue growth: Revenue increased 9% sequentially over Q4 FY26 (INR40.8 crores to INR44.5 crores), with core radio rising to INR35.5 crores from INR34 crores and creative business rebounding strongly to INR9.8 crores.
  • Improved advertiser engagement: Share of top 25 radio spenders increased to 21.8% from 15.6% in Q4 FY26.
  • Significant profitability improvement: Operating EBITDA improved to INR8.9 crores in Q1 FY27 from INR0.9 crores in Q1 FY26; EBITDA margin expanded to 20% from 1.9%. Operating PBT was INR4.1 crores versus an operating loss of INR6.9 crores a year earlier. Reported PBT was INR12.3 crores (including other income of INR8.2 crores), and PAT was INR9.2 crores versus a loss of INR2.2 crores in Q1 FY26, with PAT margin of 20.7%.
  • Structural cost optimization: Total operating expenses declined 26% year-on-year to INR35.6 crores, aided by shifting studios to a hub-and-spoke model, moving to co-working spaces, and controlled marketing expenses.
  • Solution selling / Radio Plus strategy: The Company is moving into solution selling, with a team generating ideas for clients, selling the Radio Plus piece better, and conducting on-ground activations and events to shore up the creative business.
  • Recurring advertiser base: About 80% of advertisers are recurring, with roughly 20% new business each quarter (new = not advertised in the last year).
  • Digital presence: Digital contributes 4% of revenue. Social media ranks include Rank 1 on X (323K followers), Rank 2 on Facebook (4.6M), Rank 3 on Instagram (476K) and LinkedIn (61K), and Rank 4 on YouTube (1.56M). Digital client solutions cited include Muthoot Finance (2M views), Indian Bank (74K views), Council of Architecture (1.3M views), PNB Bank (195K views), and MG Comet (184K views).
  • Market position: Market share of 25% in the quarter; FCT/Non-FCT split of 78/22; 29% share of new clients coming to the radio business.
  • IPs and activations: Business Titans 5, Family Pro League, Cricket Ka Blockbuster, City Cine Awards, and Kabab are cited as impactful IPs.
  • Strong balance sheet: Net cash of INR270 crores as on June 2026.
  • NCRPS redemption completed: All outstanding Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares were redeemed on January 19, 2026; no listed non-convertible securities were outstanding as on June 30, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Revenue concentration on advertising cyclicality: Performance depends on advertiser participation, government spend timing, and seasonal patterns (H1 ~45%, H2 ~55%).
  • Regulatory risk: Continued absence of news and current affairs permission on radio limits industry growth prospects.
  • Legal contingencies: The PPL matter before the Supreme Court remains pending; an adverse outcome could result in outflow, though management does not currently expect one. The NCLT/NCLAT promoter disputes at the Holding Company level remain pending, with implementation of EGM resolutions held in abeyance.
  • Impairment risk: Further impairment depends on share price relative to net book value and year-end performance; management said if current satisfactory performance continues, no further impairment is expected, but this will be checked at year-end. Even with share price recovery, the share price remains below net book value, so no impairment gain is expected.
  • Cost optimization ceiling: With most cost savings already executed, future margin expansion depends more on revenue growth and operating leverage.
  • Q2 softness: Management flagged a measured start to Q2, with cautious optimism.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Cost savings: Management stated most cost savings are done and current quarterly cost levels are optimal. Observed: total operating expenses fell 26% year-on-year to INR35.6 crores in Q1 FY27, with employee expenses at INR12.3 crores (vs INR18.2 crores in Q1 FY26) and other expenses at INR18.5 crores (vs INR25.2 crores). Management attributed savings to hub-and-spoke studios, co-working spaces, and controlled marketing.
  • Revenue growth priority: Management stated revenue growth remains the foremost priority. Observed: revenue grew 9% sequentially but declined 10% year-on-year; management acknowledged Q2 has begun softer.
  • Margin trajectory: Management pointed to structural cost optimization supporting margin expansion. Observed: EBITDA margin expanded to 20.03% in Q1 FY27 from 1.91% in Q1 FY26 and 7.97% in Q4 FY26.
  • Impairment: Management said no further impairment is expected if satisfactory performance continues, subject to year-end review. Observed: no impairment was recorded in Q1 FY27, versus INR49 crores in Q4 FY26.
  • Capital return: In response to an investor request for a buyback given market cap below book value, management said no buyback plans currently exist and the Company is holding on to its cash with no further plans.
  • Expansion: Management said no new venture or horizontal/vertical expansion is currently in the pipeline.
  • Manpower: Management said manpower is at optimal level after roughly 20% reduction, with all energies focused on efficiencies and revenue growth.
  • Seasonality guidance: Management reiterated H1 ~45% / H2 ~55% split and said the Company will maximize share of spend as per seasonality, without providing specific revenue projections.
