ASALCBR: Recent Developments Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Ethanol oversupply pressure. In its Q1FY27 earnings presentation (quarter ended 30 June 2026), the company reported that both topline and margin from the ethanol segment declined due to oversupply, and stated this condition is expected to prevail for the rest of the financial year. The standalone segment results for Q1FY27 show the Ethanol Division posting a segment loss of Rs. 836.71 lakhs, compared with a profit of Rs. 107.21 lakhs in Q1FY26 and a loss of Rs. 647.07 lakhs in Q4FY26. For FY26, the Ethanol Division recorded a segment loss of Rs. 205.24 lakhs.

Overall profitability decline in Q1FY27. Net revenue from operations rose 5% YoY to Rs. 2,809 Mn, but EBITDA fell 20% YoY to Rs. 299 Mn (margin down 300 bps to 11%) and PAT fell 25% YoY to Rs. 178 Mn (margin down 300 bps to 6%). Total operating expenses grew 9% YoY, outpacing revenue growth.

IMFL licensed volume decline. IMFL licensed volumes declined due to the change in the business arrangement with Inbrew from licensing to contract manufacturing.

Regulatory/legal matters. A search was conducted by the office of the Director General of the Competition Commission of India on 27 October 2021 at the company’s registered office regarding the process of supply and sale of its IMIL products. The company received a CCI order dated 20 March 2024 referring back the investigation. The company filed a writ before the Hon’ble High Court, and the court has directed CCI not to take coercive action until the matter is pending before the Hon’ble High Court.

Working capital intensity. Working capital increased to Rs. 1,614 Mn as on FY26 from Rs. 1,319 Mn in FY25. The company notes that the primary emphasis on premium IMFL products carries an anticipated increase in working capital, expected to be offset by improved margins.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

IMFL proprietary segment momentum. IMFL proprietary revenue grew 58% YoY in Q1FY27, supported by increased market penetration and strong traction across the portfolio. The CP Series volume grew 260% YoY. Management targets 25%–30% YoY growth for the IMFL proprietary B2C business.

New product launches and category entry. The company launched Kultur, a hard seltzer/RTD, in Madhya Pradesh on 26 June 2026 in five flavours (Cranberry, Watermelon Mint, Mai Tai, Lemon, Juniper Triple Sec) in 330 ml cans, with registrations underway in Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Rajasthan, Delhi, Karnataka, West Bengal, and Goa. A Tequila launch is planned in Q2FY27 starting with Madhya Pradesh, and a premium brandy launch is planned in Kerala in Q2FY27. The company cites India’s hard seltzer market growing at over 10% CAGR and the broader RTD segment at nearly 18% CAGR.

Geographic expansion. The company entered the Odisha market, with plans to expand in Karnataka, and targets strategic entry into Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. It aims to become a pan-India player and secure a position among the top 10 IMFL companies in India.

Kerala scale-up and SDF Industries acquisition. The company targets 2 million cases in Kerala in FY27, where it is currently a top-3 private player with 1.5% market share gained in FY26. The NCLT Kochi approved the resolution plan for acquisition of SDF Industries Limited on 16 April 2026 for a total cash consideration of Rs. 3,085.45 lakhs under CIRP; SDF became a wholly owned subsidiary effective 13 May 2026. Upgraded automated machinery installation is in progress, with the plant expected to commence operations by December 2026.

Additional ethanol allocation. Pursuant to an Hon’ble Supreme Court order on allocation methodology for Q4 of Ethanol Supply Year 2025-26, the company received communication from BPCL dated 15 August 2026 for an additional allocation of 87.4 lakh litres of ethanol, in addition to existing allocation, expected to support ethanol operations and contribute positively.

Capacity and integration. The company commissioned a 6,000 LPD malt plant, with malt maturation underway and continued cask procurement for in-house single malt production. Capex of Rs. 550 Mn was incurred for the malt plant, with additional capex of Rs. 150 Mn expected on casks in FY27, Rs. 100 Mn towards bottling unit automation, and Rs. 400 Mn in relation to SDF Industries (including Rs. 308.5 Mn acquisition cost).

Industry tailwinds. The presentation cites India’s young population (67.45% aged 15–64), urbanisation, premiumisation trends, low per capita alcohol consumption (11.8 litres, rank 101 globally), and whisky dominating over 70% of the IMFL market.

3. Key Risks

  • Ethanol oversupply is expected to persist for the remainder of FY27, continuing to pressure the ethanol segment’s topline and margin.
  • The CCI/IMIL cartelisation matter remains pending before the Hon’ble High Court, with CCI directed not to take coercive action until resolution.
  • The SDF Industries acquisition is recorded at initial consideration only; purchase price allocation and fair value adjustments are pending, with formal valuation of identifiable assets and liabilities still ongoing.
  • The company’s forward-looking statements are subject to risks including regulatory changes, local political or economic developments, and other factors that could cause results to differ materially.
  • The company’s growth plans depend on new state registrations and market entries (Kultur rollout across eight states, Tequila and brandy launches, Karnataka/Andhra Pradesh entry) that are subject to regulatory approvals and execution.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Ethanol segment. Management stated in the Q1FY27 presentation that ethanol oversupply is expected to prevail for the rest of the financial year. Observed performance is consistent with this: the Ethanol Division recorded a segment loss of Rs. 836.71 lakhs in Q1FY27 versus a profit of Rs. 107.21 lakhs in Q1FY26. The 15 August 2026 additional allocation of 87.4 lakh litres from BPCL is expected to support ethanol operations, though the company notes this is subject to terms and conditions and the Supreme Court order.

IMFL proprietary growth. Management targets 25%–30% YoY growth for IMFL proprietary. Observed Q1FY27 performance shows 58% YoY revenue growth in this segment, above the stated target range.

Kerala target. Management targets 2 million cases in Kerala in FY27, having sold 1 million cases in FY23 and being a top-3 private player with 1.5% market share in FY26. The SDF Industries plant is expected to commence operations by December 2026, which is within FY27.

B2B segment. Management expects the B2B segment to progress steadily in congruence with inflation rate.

SDF Industries. Management expects to complete the purchase price allocation; the plant is expected to be operational by December 2026.

Dividend. The 37th AGM held on 16 September 2026 adopted standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY ended 31 March 2026 and declared a final equity dividend of Rs. 2/- per share (20%).
