NOIDATOLL: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Regulatory and revenue dispute with NOIDA Authority. NOIDA Authority issued a demand letter dated September 10, 2025 seeking to stop NTBCL’s advertisement displays and recover over ₹100 crore in alleged advertisement license fees. This directly targets the company’s primary income stream. The company maintains that advertisement revenue — an important operating income since the toll suspension in 2016 — is both lawful and essential for maintaining the DND Flyway and meeting financial obligations.

Dependence on advertising revenue. Advertising revenue continued to be the primary income stream during Q1FY27, supporting regular maintenance, security, and financial commitments including payments to NOIDA/MCD. With tolls suspended since 2016, the company’s operating income is concentrated in a single revenue source that is itself under legal challenge.

Maintenance obligations on ageing infrastructure. The DND Flyway was developed over 25 years ago. The Independent Technical Assessment recommended phased functional overlay and identified upkeep requirements, preventive maintenance, and routine asset preservation measures for maintaining ride quality over the next five years. A five-year preventive maintenance programme is now being initiated.

Governance and compliance workload. The company is holding its 30th AGM on September 21, 2026, with related requirements including closure of the Register of Members and Share Transfer Books from September 15–21, 2026, e-voting from September 18–20, 2026, and a cut-off date of September 11, 2026. SEBI mandates require security holders in physical mode to update KYC details and dematerialise physical securities.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Year-on-year financial improvement. On a consolidated basis, Q1FY27 revenue stood at ₹12.81 crore against ₹11.09 crore in Q1FY26, an increase of 15.51 per cent. Consolidated PAT rose to ₹5.33 crore from ₹4.14 crore. On a standalone basis, revenue was ₹12.81 crore against ₹11.08 crore (up 15.61 per cent), and PAT was ₹5.26 crore against ₹4.17 crore.

Independent technical validation of the asset. The Independent Technical Assessment, conducted by CRISIL Intelligence (Routine Operations and Major Maintenance) and Almondz Global Infra (Bridge Assessment), found that the DND Flyway remains structurally sound and does not require major structural strengthening or rehabilitation in the immediate future. The studies broadly validated the quality and durability of the underlying infrastructure developed over 25 years ago.

Completed carriageway upgradation. The second phase of the carriageway upgradation programme was completed during the quarter, and the company has made adequate provisioning towards undertaking the recommended changes and upgradation work.

Judicial protection of advertising operations. The Hon’ble Delhi High Court, by order dated September 25, 2025, granted an interim stay on NOIDA Authority’s demand letter and restrained NOIDA from taking coercive action against NTBCL or disrupting its advertisement operations. This protection was extended in the last hearing till October 14, 2026.

Critical infrastructure role. The DND Flyway serves over 2.5 lakh daily commuters and is described as an essential lifeline connecting Delhi and Noida, underpinning the strategic importance of the corridor.

Board strengthening. The Board approved the appointment of Mr. Balvinder Singh (DIN 03372237) as an Independent Director with immediate effect for a term of five years from August 3, 2026, subject to shareholder approval. He is a 1981 batch officer of the Indian Audit and Account Service, retired as Member (Technical) NCLAT and previously Deputy Comptroller and Auditor General, with 43 years of total experience.

3. Key Risks

  • Adverse outcome in the NOIDA advertisement fee dispute, which seeks over ₹100 crore and seeks to stop advertisement displays; the interim stay currently runs only until October 14, 2026.
  • Concentration risk in advertising revenue as the primary income stream since toll suspension in 2016.
  • Infrastructure ageing and maintenance execution risk, given the five-year preventive maintenance programme and recommended functional overlay.
  • Dependence on continued judicial protection to preserve advertisement operations.
  • Regulatory compliance risk arising from SEBI mandates on KYC updates and dematerialisation of physical securities.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance and forward-looking statements. Management states it will initiate a plan to undertake five-year preventive maintenance work in a scheduled manner, forming part of the voluntary Independent Technical Assessment previously undertaken; part of the identified activities has already been undertaken. The company has made adequate provisioning towards these changes and upgradation work. Management reaffirmed its commitment to ensuring safe, efficient, uninterrupted upkeep and operation of the DND Flyway, and to acting in the best interests of over 60,000 retail shareholders who collectively hold nearly 70 per cent of the company’s equity, while maintaining transparency, compliance, and accountability.

Observed performance against guidance. The second phase of the carriageway upgradation programme was completed during the quarter, consistent with the planned upgradation programme. The Independent Technical Assessment reported no major structural strengthening required for the DND Flyway while recommending functional overlay, and the company has begun acting on identified activities. Financially, Q1FY27 delivered year-on-year growth in both revenue and PAT on standalone and consolidated bases, with advertising revenue continuing as the primary income stream supporting maintenance, security, and financial commitments including payments to NOIDA/MCD.
