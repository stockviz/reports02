GAIL (India) Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

LNG supply disruption from West Asia. From March 2026, LNG supplies from the Middle East were disrupted due to the geopolitical situation in West Asia. Petronet LNG Limited (PLL) declared Force Majeure on 3 March 2026, which reduced RLNG allocation to GAIL under the contract to zero with effect from 4 March 2026. In addition, seven LNG cargoes during the quarter ended June 2026 under various other contracts were impacted. GAIL has responded with mitigation measures including procurement of LNG/Natural Gas from the spot market and alternative sources, to manage the impact and ensure supply to priority sectors in line with the Government of India’s Natural Gas (Supply Regulation) Order dated 9 March 2026.

Petrochemicals segment losses. The Petrochemicals segment recorded a loss at the segment-result level in each of the periods presented: a loss of ₹(122.53) crore in Q1 FY27, ₹(377.71) crore in Q4 FY26, ₹(248.63) crore in Q1 FY26, and ₹(1,408.22) crore for FY26 (standalone). Segment revenue from Petrochemicals fell to ₹646.08 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹1,870.76 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹1,681.18 crore in Q1 FY26.

Regulatory and tax disputes. - GAIL has filed appeals before the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (APTEL) against two provisional tariff orders issued by the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB) in respect of Petroleum and Petroleum Product Pipelines. Any adjustment will be recognised only when the matter is finally decided. - CESTAT, Delhi, by order dated 30 November 2018, confirmed a demand for differential Central Excise duty of ₹2,889 crore including penalty and interest (₹3,799 crore with interest up to 30 June 2026) relating to classification of ‘Naphtha’ manufactured by the Company. GAIL has appealed to the Supreme Court; the appeal has been admitted and stay granted on conditions of depositing ₹20 crore and furnishing security of ₹132 crore. Based on legal opinions, GAIL does not foresee any probable outflow and has treated the matter as a contingent liability.

Board and Audit Committee composition. The Board did not have any Independent Director with effect from 28 March 2026. Consequently, the Company re-constituted the Audit Committee comprising three Whole-time Directors. The auditors noted that the Company does not have the requisite number of Independent Directors as required under the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 18(1) of the Listing Regulations so as to validly constitute its Audit Committee with effect from 28 March 2026.

Rising leverage and finance costs. Standalone finance costs rose to ₹309.70 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹252.55 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹209.38 crore in Q1 FY26. The debt-equity ratio increased to 0.32 times in Q1 FY27 from 0.28 times in Q4 FY26 and 0.24 times in Q1 FY26. Total debts to total assets rose to 0.19 times from 0.18 times and 0.17 times respectively. Long-term loan outstanding stood at ₹13,823 crore as on 30 June 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong Q1 FY27 financial performance. Standalone revenue from operations rose to ₹38,981.63 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹34,797.03 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹34,792.45 crore in Q1 FY26. Standalone Profit Before Tax was ₹5,773.23 crore, up from ₹1,577.08 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹2,533.43 crore in Q1 FY26. Standalone Profit After Tax was ₹4,292.33 crore versus ₹1,262.18 crore and ₹1,886.34 crore respectively. Operating margin improved to 14.17% in Q1 FY27 from 2.33% in Q4 FY26 and 7.06% in Q1 FY26; net profit margin was 11.04% versus 3.65% and 5.43%.

Natural Gas Marketing segment turnaround. The segment posted a profit of ₹3,481.29 crore in Q1 FY27 (standalone), compared with a loss of ₹(151.32) crore in Q4 FY26 and a profit of ₹1,071.60 crore in Q1 FY26. Segment revenue was ₹34,437.58 crore in Q1 FY27.

LPG and Liquid Hydrocarbons strength. Segment results rose to ₹772.50 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹143.89 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹205.01 crore in Q1 FY26, with revenue of ₹2,039.21 crore.

Transmission and City Gas contributions. Natural Gas transmission segment results were ₹1,782.65 crore in Q1 FY27. On a consolidated basis, the City Gas segment contributed revenue of ₹2,256.11 crore and segment results of ₹225.62 crore in Q1 FY27.

Capital expenditure programme. Q1 FY27 capex was ₹6,176 crore against an annual planned capex of approximately ₹11,500 crore for FY2026-27. The breakup comprised operational capex (including ship chartering) ₹4,548 crore (74%), pipelines ₹332 crore (5%), petrochemicals ₹385 crore (6%), Net Zero/Renewables ₹309 crore (5%), equity contributions to JVs/subsidiaries ₹332 crore (5%), exploration & production ₹113 crore (2%), and CGD projects ₹157 crore (3%).

Consolidated performance. Consolidated revenue from operations was ₹41,350.18 crore in Q1 FY27; Profit Before Tax was ₹6,267.56 crore; Profit After Tax was ₹4,670.99 crore; and total comprehensive income was ₹4,344.21 crore. Share of profit of associates and joint ventures was ₹342.72 crore.

Balance sheet strength. Standalone net worth was ₹70,657.66 crore as on 30 June 2026, with capital employed of ₹105,910 crore and total assets of ₹1,30,731 crore. Current ratio improved to 1.02 times from 0.83 times in Q4 FY26. Interest service coverage ratio was 17.70 times versus 6.44 times; debt service coverage ratio was 4.88 times versus 1.46 times.

No defaults and clean fund utilisation. Total financial indebtedness including short-term and long-term debt was ₹16,793 crore as on 30 June 2026, with nil default. For the private placement of ₹1,575.00 crore raised on 20 December 2022 (outstanding as on 30 June 2026), funds were fully utilised for the stated objects, with no deviation or variation.

Innovation and start-up programme. GAIL, Petronet LNG Limited, and MC² Foundation, in association with iTIC Incubator of IIT Hyderabad, organised the ‘MC² Plus IGNITE Hyderabad Connect’ at IIT Hyderabad to accelerate innovation and entrepreneurship in the energy sector. Selected start-ups will be eligible for convertible funding of up to ₹50 lakh and milestone-based funding of up to ₹1.5 crore, with the programme aiming to support 30 high-potential early-stage start-ups through mentorship, technical expertise, pilot opportunities, funding and industry networks.

Shareholder returns. The 42nd AGM was held on 27 August 2026 through Video Conferencing/OAVM, where all 11 resolutions were approved with the requisite majority, including a final dividend of 5% (Re 0.50 per equity share) for FY 2025-26. The record date for the final dividend was Wednesday, 2 September 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Geopolitical supply risk: The West Asia situation disrupted Middle East LNG supplies from March 2026, with PLL’s Force Majeure reducing RLNG allocation to zero from 4 March 2026 and seven cargoes impacted in Q1 FY27. Continued reliance on spot market and alternative sources exposes GAIL to price and availability risk.
  • Regulatory tariff risk: Two PNGRB provisional tariff orders for Petroleum and Petroleum Product Pipelines are contested at APTEL; adjustments will be recognised only on final decision.
  • Tax litigation risk: The CESTAT order confirming ₹2,889 crore (₹3,799 crore with interest up to 30 June 2026) in the Naphtha classification matter remains before the Supreme Court, albeit with stay granted and no probable outflow foreseen based on legal opinion.
  • Governance/compliance risk: Absence of the requisite number of Independent Directors from 28 March 2026 and the resulting Audit Committee composition of three Whole-time Directors.
  • Segment concentration risk: Petrochemicals continued to report losses across all periods presented.
  • Leverage risk: Rising debt-equity (0.32 times), finance costs, and long-term loan outstanding of ₹13,823 crore.
  • Unreviewed components in consolidated results: Certain subsidiaries, joint ventures, associates, and E&P operations were not reviewed by their auditors; E&P operations reflected total revenues of ₹303.86 crore and net profit before tax of ₹166.77 crore for Q1 FY27, based on statements from Operators.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Capex guidance. Management indicated an annual planned capex of approximately ₹11,500 crore for FY2026-27. Observed Q1 FY27 capex was ₹6,176 crore, with operational capex (including ship chartering) forming the largest component at ₹4,548 crore (74%).

Supply mitigation guidance. Management stated it has taken mitigation measures including spot market and alternative source procurement to ensure supply to priority sectors in line with the Government of India’s Natural Gas (Supply Regulation) Order dated 9 March 2026. Observed performance shows Natural Gas Marketing segment revenue of ₹34,437.58 crore and segment profit of ₹3,481.29 crore in Q1 FY27, alongside Natural Gas transmission revenue of ₹3,042.09 crore.

Litigation outlook. Management stated, based on favourable legal opinions, that it is confident of a favourable outcome in the Naphtha matter and does not foresee any probable outflow, treating it as a contingent liability. The Supreme Court has admitted the appeal and granted stay on deposit of ₹20 crore and security of ₹132 crore.

Dividend. A final dividend of 5% (Re 0.50 per equity share) for FY 2025-26 was approved by shareholders at the 42nd AGM on 27 August 2026, with the record date fixed as 2 September 2026.

Investor engagement. GAIL officials are scheduled to attend the Asia Energy Security Field Trip on 21 September 2026 at 11:45 AM onwards, a physical Group Meeting with an Investor Group at the GAIL Corporate Office in New Delhi, with no Unpublished Price Sensitive Information proposed to be shared.

Start-up programme targets. The MC² Plus IGNITE programme aims to support 30 high-potential early-stage start-ups through mentorship, technical expertise, pilot opportunities, funding and industry networks, with convertible funding up to ₹50 lakh and milestone-based funding up to ₹1.5 crore for selected start-ups.
