Rajnandini Metal Limited (RAJMET) — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Tax demands and ongoing disputes - GST Authorities conducted a search of the company’s business premises during the financial year ended March 31, 2025, alleging fraudulent availment of ineligible input tax credit in earlier years. - An order under Section 74 of the CGST Act, 2017 dated January 16, 2025 directed the company to deposit ineligible input credit of Rs. 96.14 crores, interest of Rs. 98.42 crores, and penalty of Rs. 96.14 crores — a total demand of Rs. 290.70 crores. - The company’s rectification application before the concerned authorities was rejected, and management stated it would file an appeal. - Separately, during the financial year ended March 31, 2026, Income Tax Authorities raised a demand of Rs. 10.68 crores (referenced as Rs. 16.98 crores in one disclosure), against which management filed an appeal.

Qualified auditor opinion - The statutory auditors (Krishna & Associates) issued a qualified review conclusion on the unaudited standalone financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, citing inability to comment on the ultimate outcome of the GST proceedings and the consequential impact, if any, on the financial results. - The auditors noted that management is of the view that no provision is required in the accompanying financial results.

Weak quarterly performance - Revenue from operations declined to Rs. 5,195 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), compared with Rs. 5,645 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and Rs. 7,388 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. - Total income fell to Rs. 5,261 lakhs from Rs. 5,657 lakhs (Q4 FY2025-26) and Rs. 7,442 lakhs (Q1 FY2025-26). - Profit before tax was Rs. 4 lakhs, down from Rs. 166 lakhs in the prior quarter and Rs. (246) lakhs in the prior-year quarter. - Profit for the period was Rs. 39 lakhs, compared with Rs. 113 lakhs in the preceding quarter and Rs. (199) lakhs in the prior-year quarter. - Basic and diluted EPS stood at Rs. 0.01, versus Rs. 0.04 in the preceding quarter and Rs. (0.06) in the prior-year quarter.

Exceptional item - An incremental liability of Rs. 4 lakhs was disclosed as an exceptional item in FY2025-26, following reassessment of employee benefit obligations under the New Labour Codes notified in November 2025.

3. Key Risks

  • Contingent tax liability risk: The GST demand of Rs. 290.70 crores (including interest and penalty) and the Income Tax demand remain unresolved. Management has made no provision, asserting the liability will not crystallize based on documentary evidence and legal advice.
  • Audit qualification risk: The qualified review conclusion signals that the auditors could not ascertain the ultimate outcome or consequential impact of the GST proceedings on the financial results.
  • Revenue and profitability pressure: The sharp year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter decline in revenue and profitability indicates deteriorating operating performance.
  • Legal and regulatory proceedings: Both the GST and Income Tax matters are subject to appeal, with outcomes uncertain.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management’s stated position is that the company legitimately availed GST input credit, that the GST Authorities’ allegation is not tenable, and that the liability will not crystallize; accordingly, no provision has been made. Management has filed an appeal after its rectification application was rejected.
  • For the Income Tax demand, management is seeking legal recourse and has filed an appeal for deletion of the entire demand.
  • Observed performance shows a decline in revenue from operations (Rs. 5,195 lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 versus Rs. 7,388 lakhs in Q1 FY2025-26) and a fall in profit for the period (Rs. 39 lakhs versus Rs. 113 lakhs in the preceding quarter), alongside the unresolved tax demands and the qualified auditor conclusion.
  • The company has not provided any forward-looking financial guidance in the supplied material; the only forward-looking elements are management’s view that the tax liabilities will not crystallize and its intention to pursue appeals.
