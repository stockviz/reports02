Nazara Technologies — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin pressure from user acquisition. In Q1 FY27, gaming segment revenue grew 14% year-on-year to INR 275 crores with a 19.5% EBITDA margin, but EBITDA declined meaningfully because user acquisition spend was scaled up. Within Mobile Gaming, Bluetile and BestPlay grew revenue 54% year-on-year while EBITDA held flat as UA spend rose from 78% to 85% of revenue. Bluetile’s EBITDA margin fell to around 11% in Q1 FY27 from 17.6% in the prior-year period. Management described the margin decline as deferred rather than lost, since UA costs are absorbed upfront, and said margins should normalize into higher EBITDA over time.

Curve Games margin dilution during the investment phase. Curve’s Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin of 27% was below both the prior-year quarter (which benefited from the first-party Badlands Crew release) and the roughly 40% margin delivered last financial year. Newly signed titles have a 12–18 month development cycle, so benefits have been delayed. Badlands Crew has sold around 50,000–60,000 copies, in line with expectations but far below the 60 million units shifted for Human: Fall Flat.

Sportskeeda and search-driven traffic weakness. Sportskeeda has not seen a recovery, with challenges related to Google persisting. Management believes the business has hit its bottom, with cost actions expected to take full effect from Q2 FY27. Pro Football Network delivered its best Q1 yet at a 19% EBITDA margin versus break-even in Q1 FY26.

AdTech margin softness. Datawrkz reported Q1 FY27 revenue of INR 126 crores but EBITDA of only INR 3 crores. A significant portion of reported revenue (60% to nearly 80%) is pass-through, so revenue growth does not translate proportionally to EBITDA. Space & Time faced a softer demand environment, with one or two large-margin clients cutting budgets for a few months.

Losses and write-offs. Nazara reported a PAT loss of INR 82 crores, largely attributable to share of loss from associates and impairment loss. The share of loss of associates was about INR 62 crores, primarily related to write-offs on remnant values connected to the Moonshine transaction rather than NODWIN. Exposure to real-money gaming subsidiaries that received tax notices is limited to the extent of Nazara’s investments, which have been written off completely.

Broader risk factors. Platform dependence on third parties such as Apple and Google, algorithm and traffic risks, regulatory and policy uncertainty including impacts from the real-money gaming ban, geopolitical and economic instability in international markets, stagnation in Western esports, integration and M&A execution risk, hit-driven performance variability, seasonal volatility tied to TV IP calendars, currency and fiscal risks, and demographic pressures such as aging populations in Europe.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Bluetile and BestPlay acquisition and consolidation. The Board approved an amendment to acquire 100% of Bluetile and BestPlay for a fixed all-cash consideration of $303 million, replacing the earlier performance-based structure. $89 million is payable at closing, with the remaining $214 million payable in agreed tranches by 1 April 2027 (about $75 million due 90 days out, about $35 million in December 2026, and the balance before 1 April 2027). The business reported INR 518 crores of revenue and INR 55 crores of EBITDA in Q1 FY27, and is expected to be consolidated from Q2 FY27. Management cited certainty of ownership and price, full economics on closing, single-owner governance, immediate operating integration, strategic flexibility, and access to the business’s cash generation as benefits. Bluetile and BestPlay delivered 54% revenue growth year-on-year, with revenue net of UA spend rising from INR 73 crores to INR 80 crores.

Leadership transition. Raymond Stauffer, Bluetile’s founder and CEO, was appointed CEO of Nazara Technologies effective 1 September 2026, subject to regulatory approvals. Nitish Mittersain continues as Founder and Managing Director, focusing on long-term strategy, portfolio direction, and key relationships. Management expects Stauffer’s experience in AI-enabled game development, operating discipline, and capital-efficient growth to benefit other studios in the group.

Kiddopia inflection. Kiddopia revenue grew 19% year-on-year in Q1 FY27, with higher UA spend deployed against improving unit economics. The 24-month LTV/CAC ratio continues to trend upwards, though the step-up in UA moderated near-term EBITDA.

Fusebox pipeline. Fusebox grew 12% year-on-year to INR 82 crores, with Love Island ranking #1 in free apps and #5 in top-grossing apps in the U.S. Management expects a step change from the scale-up of Big Brother (already live, approaching the content threshold for scaling) and the upcoming launch of The Traitors, for which Fusebox holds global rights. Management expressed optimism about a significant runway for Fusebox.

Curve release slate. Releases are expected within the next three quarters of FY27, starting with Sovereign Tower later in the week of the call, Dragon Shelter in September, and further unannounced titles before year-end. Dragon Shelter has around 130,000 Steam Wishlists, exceeding expectations, and Sovereign Tower is tracking ahead of expected August revenue. First-party studio IronOak Games (For The King II) is working on new titles expected to deliver better margins.

Offline gaming expansion. Offline gaming delivered a 33% EBITDA margin on Q1 FY27 revenue of INR 34 crores and EBITDA of INR 11 crores. Funky Monkeys revenue grew 58% year-on-year on same-store sales growth and store expansion. SMAAASH 2.0’s first reinvention is planned at the Lower Parel, Mumbai outlet, with launch targeted for Q4 FY27 (January–March 2027). Total commitment across the two offline businesses, including a stake increase in Funky Monkeys, is approximately INR 50 crores, funded through internal accruals, some debt, and capital infusion by Nazara.

NODWIN turnaround and IPO path. NODWIN’s Q1, traditionally its slowest quarter, saw stable revenue growth with losses substantially lower year-on-year. Comic Con Mumbai sold out, and the IP is expanding to 14 events in India this year, alongside registration of Comic Con World as a global IP. NODWIN is targeting organic growth of 30%+ for FY27 and progressing toward IPO readiness, with strong traction for pre-IPO participation.

AdTech product scaling. The next focus for AdTech is scaling higher-margin products such as Vizibl, a DSP for mid-size businesses gaining traction in Western markets, and expanding into newer markets. Senthil Govindan has relocated to the U.K. and hired additional salespeople.

Fortnite and UEFN expansion. Nazara and Virstate launched Bodycam UEFN in Fortnite on September 11, 2026, under an agreement with Reissad Studio. The title features three multiplayer modes (Free For All, Team Deathmatch, and Gun Game), the Russian Building map, and long-term progression systems. Management intends to use UEFN as a template and serious platform for franchise expansion of major gaming IPs, with future updates planned including additional maps inspired by the original Bodycam, new weapons and equipment, rewards and unlockables, seasonal updates, community events, and additional gameplay modes.

Structural tailwinds. Rapid expansion of digital infrastructure, rising smartphone and internet penetration, growing mobile gaming markets in India and emerging regions, 5G-enabled cloud gaming, increasing monetisation through in-app purchases and ad-supported models, AI-native development tools, and an expanding international footprint in high-ARPU markets such as the U.S. and U.K.

3. Key Risks

  • Platform and algorithm dependence: Reliance on third-party platforms such as Apple and Google, with algorithm and traffic risks including organic traffic declines following search engine updates, as reflected in Sportskeeda’s ongoing challenges.
  • Regulatory and policy uncertainty: Including impacts from the real-money gaming ban and tax notices received by real-money gaming subsidiaries, where the Supreme Court upheld the notices as legitimate.
  • Integration and M&A execution: The accelerated Bluetile and BestPlay transaction requires funding of $214 million by 1 April 2027, with management citing multiple options including Bluetile’s balance sheet cash (approximately $20 million), additional cash flow, debt, equity, and potential stake sales. The NODWIN secondary sale was raised as a possible funding component.
  • Hit-driven performance variability: Inherent to gaming, illustrated by the gap between Human: Fall Flat’s 60 million units and Badlands Crew’s 50,000–60,000 copies.
  • Seasonal and calendar volatility: Tied to TV IP calendars and U.S. sports seasons, with NODWIN’s Q1 traditionally its slowest quarter and Sportskeeda/Pro Football Network revenue weighted to Q3 and Q4.
  • Geopolitical and economic instability: In international markets, with over 90% of gaming revenue generated internationally (primarily the U.S. and U.K.).
  • Currency and fiscal risks, and demographic pressures such as aging populations in Europe.
  • Margin timing risk: UA-driven revenue growth defers EBITDA, and management has not provided guidance on when normalization will occur.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - No formal revenue, EBITDA, or margin guidance was given at the Nazara level for FY27. - NODWIN is targeting organic growth of 30%+ for FY27. - Curve expects new titles to contribute significantly to increasing margins in subsequent quarters of FY27, with releases across the next three quarters. - Bluetile management declined to confirm a specific full-year margin or revenue trajectory, citing dependence on new game performance and seasonality, but stated the goal is increasing revenue now with flexibility to adjust EBITDA later. - SMAAASH 2.0 launch targeted for Q4 FY27. - Sportskeeda revenue expected to rise in Q2 and more so in Q3 and Q4 with the U.S. sports season. - AdTech margins expected to improve as Vizibl scales and large clients return. - FY 2026-27 strategic priorities: full integration of Bluetile Games, maturation of the existing mobile gaming portfolio, expansion of offline gaming (SMAAASH 2.0 and Funky Monkeys), deeper integration of AI-native development, cross-portfolio synergies through Nazara ID and shared UA infrastructure, and disciplined capital allocation.

Observed performance against prior expectations: - Curve’s Q1 FY27 margin of 27% came in below management’s full-year expectation and below the prior year’s roughly 40%, reflecting the investment phase; management attributed this to the 12–18 month development cycle for new signings. - Bluetile’s Q1 FY27 margin of around 11% was below the prior-year 17.6%, with management framing this as a deliberate choice to build a higher revenue and user base. - Sportskeeda’s Q1 FY27 revenue of INR 28 crores and EBITDA of INR 1 crore were in line with internal expectations, per management, with recovery expected from Q2 onward. - Kiddopia’s 19% year-on-year growth marked a change from what management described as long-standing stagnation. - NODWIN’s Q1 losses were substantially lower year-on-year, consistent with the turnaround narrative.

Capital structure actions: On August 06, 2026, the Board approved a preferential issue of up to 2,39,70,676 equity shares at ₹306 per share (including a premium of ₹304), aggregating up to ₹7,33,50,26,856, to six identified investors. Raymond Stauffer is proposed to receive 1,90,67,969 shares (4.67% post-issue), with the remainder allocated to Marc Sylvester Schutze, Maxime Loppin, Alexandre Paul Jean Noirot Cosson, Alexander Osou, and Hugo Rémy Gaston Blavin. The Board also approved increasing authorised share capital from ₹80,00,00,000 to ₹90,00,00,000 and convening an EGM on August 30, 2026. The relevant date for the preferential issue pricing was July 31, 2026.

Broker Narrative

Early reports framed Nazara as a Buy on eSports momentum (52.9% YoY growth), Kiddopia margin recovery and a Rs6.3bn cash pile, with RMG delays and M&A integration as key risks. By the 2026-08-06 report, the narrative shifted to managing an acquired portfolio: revenue fell 14% YoY after Nodwin deconsolidation, e-sports revenue dropped 82%, and attention moved to Curve Games/Fusebox launches and NODWIN IPO readiness. The persistent theme was growth via gaming/IP acquisitions, but the emphasis changed from near-term FY24 scaling to FY26-28 CAGR and balance-sheet/debt concerns.

Fears that came true

  • M&A integration risk materialized: the accelerated BT & BP acquisition consideration created expected debt, while associate losses of INR624mn and a INR218mn impairment hit earnings.
  • Sequential decline and segment margin pressure materialized: consolidated revenue declined 14% YoY after Nodwin deconsolidation, e-sports revenue fell 82% YoY, and EBITDA margin missed estimates (10.8% vs 13.4% PLe), contributing to DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes such as the 2025-06-27 Add call with -8.2% actual return.

Optimism that failed

  • Strong eSports expansion failed to persist: the earlier 52.9% YoY e-sports growth and NODWIN margin improvement reversed into an 82% YoY e-sports revenue decline after deconsolidation.
  • The Rs6.3bn cash-reserve tailwind failed to keep the balance sheet strong: accelerated acquisition payments left the company with expected debt rather than the envisaged opportunistic-M&A flexibility.
