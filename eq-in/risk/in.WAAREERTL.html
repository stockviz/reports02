Headwinds and Challenges

  • The global business environment continues to face multiple challenges, including geopolitical tensions, tariff uncertainties, supply chain adjustments, and regional conflicts, as described by CFO Manmohan Sharma in the Q1 FY27 press release dated July 22, 2026.
  • Q1 FY27 performance moderated on a sequential basis: revenue from operations declined 16.16% QoQ (from Rs. 1,102.40 crores in Q4 FY26 to Rs. 924.25 crores), EBITDA declined 16.12% QoQ, and PAT declined 23.60% QoQ.
  • Margins compressed year-on-year in Q1 FY27: EBITDA margin fell to 18.77% from 19.49% in Q1 FY26, and PAT margin fell to 12.87% from 14.32%.
  • Finance costs rose sharply year-on-year in Q1 FY27 (Rs. 9.59 crores versus Rs. 3.56 crores in Q1 FY26), and depreciation also increased (Rs. 5.78 crores versus Rs. 2.23 crores).
  • The 210MWp (150MWac) EPC order announced August 5, 2026 was awarded by Solaris Horizon Energy Private Limited, a step-down subsidiary of Waaree Energies Limited, and falls within related party transactions; the disclosure states it is done at arm’s length.
  • The AGM notice for the 27th AGM (September 23, 2026) includes approval of material related party transactions with Waaree Energies Limited, Waaree Forever Energies Private Limited, Waaneep Solar One Private Limited, and Lumina Greentech Private Limited, indicating a continuing volume of group-related transactions.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • India’s installed renewable energy capacity stood at over 288 GW as of June 2026, with solar capacity crossing 162 GW and remaining the leading driver of the clean energy transition; India targets 500 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030.
  • Government policies cited in the Q1 FY27 earnings presentation include PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (₹75,000 crores; 1 crore rooftop homes by March 2027, with 33 lakh done), 55 solar parks with ~40 GW sanctioned capacity across 13 states, PLI Tranches I & II (₹52,942 crores committed for 48 GW of integrated solar PV manufacturing), a 100 GW wind target by 2030, BESS VGF schemes (~43 GWh), ISTS waiver for co-located BESS projects, and National Electricity Plan (Transmission) capex of ~₹4.9 lakh crores through FY2027-32.
  • Crisil projects solar capacity additions in India of ~156–164 GW over fiscals 2027–2030.
  • Consolidated unexecuted order book stands at ~₹5,300 crores (EPC solar + BESS of ~₹2,600 crores and T&D of ~₹2,700 crores), with a bidding pipeline of 37 GWp+ solar, 10 GWh+ BESS, and ₹20k crores+ T&D.
  • Recent order wins include a 1,520 MWh BESS order and a 450 MWp ground-mounted solar project (both Q1 FY27), a 291 MWp solar PV project with 280 MWh BESS from an Indian thermal power generating company (scheduled for completion in FY2027-28), and the 210MWp (150MWac) order from Solaris Horizon Energy Private Limited (announced August 5, 2026, scheduled for completion in FY2027-28).
  • The company acquired a 55% equity stake in Associated Power Structures Pvt. Ltd. (APSPL), strengthening T&D capabilities; APSPL has manufacturing locations at Bamangam and Manglej, Gujarat.
  • The company is pursuing EPC opportunities in Battery Energy Storage Systems and data centre infrastructure. It incorporated Aurovault Data Centers Private Limited on September 14, 2026 as a wholly-owned step-down subsidiary (under Waaree Green Data Centers Private Limited) for data centers, hyperscale data centers, AI-ready data centers, and digital infrastructure.
  • O&M portfolio stands at ~1,154 MWp, with plans to expand third-party O&M through organic and inorganic routes and differentiated services such as drone thermography and analytics.
  • IPP assets: 82.82 MWp of solar generating assets developed, with an additional 198.6 MWp IPP plant being set up.
  • Credit rating upgraded to A+, Stable (CARE).

Key Risks

  • Related party concentration: the 210MWp order is from a Waaree Energies step-down subsidiary, and the AGM includes approval of material related party transactions with multiple group entities.
  • Group restructuring activity: the company approved transfer of its entire shareholding in Waaree Green Data Centers Private Limited (formerly Sunsantional Energy Private Limited) to holding company Waaree Energies Limited for Rs. 1,00,000 (face value Rs. 10 per share), with the agreement and completion expected on or before October 31, 2026. The subsidiary contributed Nil or negligible turnover, revenue, income, and net worth in FY2025-26.
  • The new data center subsidiary, Aurovault Data Centers Private Limited, is newly incorporated with Nil turnover.
  • Execution timelines: the 210MWp order and the 291 MWp solar + 280 MWh BESS order are both scheduled for completion in FY2027-28, leaving execution risk over the intervening period.
  • Macro and external factors cited by management: geopolitical tensions, tariff uncertainties, supply chain adjustments, and regional conflicts.
  • The AGM notice includes an enabling resolution for conversion of loan into equity under Section 62(3); management clarifies there are no proposals of conversion pending or envisaged currently.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Management stated in the Q1 FY27 press release that the company began FY27 on a strong note, with revenue growth of 53.23% YoY, driven by steady execution across the EPC portfolio, efficient resource deployment, and disciplined project delivery. Observed Q1 FY27 results confirm YoY revenue growth of 53.23% (Rs. 924.25 crores vs Rs. 603.19 crores), EBITDA growth of 47.58%, and PAT growth of 37.70%. However, on a QoQ basis, revenue, EBITDA, and PAT all declined.
  • Management cited a healthy unexecuted order book of Rs. 5,300+ crores as underpinning sustained revenue visibility and stated confidence in long-term growth strategy, with focus on disciplined execution, operational excellence, and sustainable value delivery. The order book figure is corroborated in the earnings presentation, which also lists a bidding pipeline of 37 GWp+ solar, 10 GWh+ BESS, and Rs. 20k crores+ T&D.
  • Management stated the acquisition of a 55% stake in APSPL strengthens T&D capabilities and enables integrated solutions while expanding renewable EPC in a disciplined and scalable manner. The earnings presentation lists APSPL manufacturing locations and combined offering plans.
  • Management stated it is actively pursuing EPC opportunities in BESS and data centre infrastructure. Subsequent developments include the 291 MWp solar + 280 MWh BESS Letter of Award (scheduled for FY2027-28 completion), the 210MWp order (scheduled for FY2027-28 completion), and the incorporation of Aurovault Data Centers Private Limited on September 14, 2026.
  • Management clarified that the resolution for conversion of loan into equity is merely an enabling resolution with no pending or envisaged proposals currently.
  • Management stated the agreement for sale of shares in Waaree Green Data Centers Private Limited shall be entered into on or before October 31, 2026, with expected completion on October 31, 2026.
  • Management stated the 210MWp order is scheduled to be completed during FY2027-28, and the 291 MWp solar + 280 MWh BESS project is scheduled to be completed during FY2027-28.
