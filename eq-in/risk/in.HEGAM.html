Headwinds and Challenges

  • The hydro and wind power operations are highly dependent on weather, wind, and hydraulic conditions, making them seasonal; the majority of hydro generation occurs between April and September.
  • Subsidiary NHPL’s project is on hold due to suspension of environmental clearance and wildlife reports, resulting in arbitration with the Government of Andhra Pradesh (GoAP). Subsidiary CYHPL surrendered its project due to circumstances beyond its control.
  • On a standalone basis, the company’s operations are limited to a 14 MW wind project, and it relies primarily on dividend income from subsidiaries; it has experienced periods of loss or inadequate profit.
  • Management cautions that forward-looking statements are subject to known and unknown risks and uncertainties, that actual results may differ materially, and that capacities and projects indicated as under development or construction are subject to change.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • TACC Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary, signed a non-binding MoU with a global lithium-ion cell manufacturer for proposed long-term supply of up to 40,000 tonnes of synthetic graphite-based Anode Active Material (AAM), with collaboration on R&D and custom development of next-generation fast-charging, high-range battery materials.
  • TACC’s first phase envisages production of 20,000 MT of AAM annually, with a committed expansion of 44,000 MT; commercial production of graphene is targeted at 500 MT per annum.
  • TACC received an approval letter for an incentive of Rs. 609.50 Crore under the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS) of MeitY, Government of India, toward the Anode Material project at the Sirsoda Industrial Area, Dewas, Madhya Pradesh.
  • Growth prospects include scaling the anode platform from 20K to 44K MT, an upcoming anode expansion, graphene and crucible projects, and expanding battery manufacturing and integration capabilities at REPLUS with a planned 5 GWh expansion facility to reach 6 GWh total capacity.
  • Additional avenues include entering the hard carbon cell platform, developing silicon-doped and graphene-enhanced anodes, a graphene plant capacity of 500 MT by September 2028, and a 200 MWh BESS IPP project under development in Gujarat targeted for commissioning in Q3 FY27.
  • Replus Engitech Private Limited, a subsidiary, received domestic orders from Indus Towers Limited for supply of Lithium-Ion Battery Banks worth Rs. 127.35 Crore (inclusive of GST), to be executed on or before May 31, 2027.
  • The company operates within advanced battery materials, renewable energy, and green power sectors, leveraging 50+ years of graphitization expertise; it holds operational hydro (278 MW) and wind (14 MW) power assets, which are debt-free and generate stable cash flows of over 300 crores annually.
  • A new Crucible manufacturing plant with 80,000 units P.A. capacity is planned, and the HEG Advanced Materials Limited Employee Stock Option Plan 2026 has been implemented.
  • Global lithium-ion battery production is projected to reach ~3 TWh in 2026 and ~8 TWh by 2035, with global battery graphite anode demand expected to reach 8.0 Mn MT by 2035.

Key Risks

  • Weather, wind, and hydraulic dependence of hydro and wind operations, with seasonal concentration of generation between April and September.
  • Project execution and clearance risk evidenced by NHPL’s project being on hold amid environmental clearance suspension and wildlife reports, with arbitration against GoAP, and CYHPL’s surrender of its project.
  • The MoU with the global lithium-ion cell manufacturer is non-binding and subject to further discussions and finalisation of technical and commercial requirements.
  • Capacities and projects under development or construction are subject to change, and forward-looking statements may differ materially from actual results.
  • Standalone dependence on dividend income from subsidiaries, with a history of loss or inadequate profit at the standalone level.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management guides a commercial operation date (COD) of June 2027 for the 20 KMT anode plant, with Phase I anode COD of June FY28 and Phase II (44K MT) COD of March FY29; the 20 KMT anode plant is currently under construction. The 44,000 MT anode expansion carries a target date of March 2029.
  • Management targets the crucible plant (80,000 units P.A.) and the graphene manufacturing plant (500 MT) for September 2028; graphene capacity of 500 MT is projected by September 2028.
  • The BESS IPP project in Gujarat (100 MWh/200 MWh, awarded by Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited) has a target commissioning date of Q3 FY27, with management expecting commissioning in the later part of FY 2026-27.
  • Management anticipates TACC Limited’s new-generation graphite anode platform will commence operations in the next financial year, and projects that dividend income from subsidiaries will increase in coming years.
  • The Indus Towers orders worth Rs. 127.35 Crore (inclusive of GST) are to be executed on or before May 31, 2027, or such extended date as mutually agreed.
  • Management stated that the two companies will work towards a progressive supply arrangement subject to further discussions and finalisation of technical and commercial requirements, with built-in scope for further scale-up as the partner’s cell manufacturing capacity expands.
