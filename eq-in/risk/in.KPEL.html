2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Rising power demand, described as reaching a record 271 GW and headed toward approximately 345 GW by 2030.
  • Policy and pipeline support for 100+ GW of wind by 2030, backed by a dedicated wind-tender trajectory of 10 GW per year.
  • Prioritization of hybrid and round-the-clock (RTC) power.
  • Offshore wind targets of 30 GW by 2030, supported by a central Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme.
  • Repowering potential of approximately 25 GW of ageing fleets.
  • A shift toward higher-capacity turbines of 4–5 MW and above.
  • A reduced GST rate on wind equipment, from 12% to 5%.
  • A next wave of growth driven by urbanisation, data centres, EV adoption, and green hydrogen.
  • Management states it has chalked out ambitious growth plans to scale up operations and profitability, with focused business strategies across all spheres to improve sales and profitability.
  • Management expresses confidence in achieving revenue growth in the future and continues to undertake measures to improve productivity and profitability.
  • The company targets a group capacity of 10 GW+ by 2030.
