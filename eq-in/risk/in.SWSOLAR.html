SWSOLAR: Recent Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue recognition timing. Q1 FY27 revenue of INR 1,590 crore was impacted by lower revenue recognition from the EPC segment. Six turnkey projects (three in India, three international) are yet to commence execution and form part of the unexecuted order value. These are likely to skew higher revenue recognition towards 2H FY27.

Margin compression. Q1 FY27 gross margin stood at 9.9%, versus FY26 gross margin of 10.5% and Q1 FY26 gross margin of 11.7%. EBITDA margin was 6.0% in Q1 FY27, compared with 9.6% in Q4 FY26.

Working capital stretch. Receivable days rose to 161 in June 2026 from 138 in March 2026, while payable days rose to 157 from 135. Net working capital remained negative at INR (260) crore as of June 2026, though less negative than INR (329) crore in March 2026.

Cash decline. Cash and bank balance (including fixed deposits) fell to INR 400 crore as of June 2026 from INR 575 crore as of March 2026.

GST demand. The Assistant Commissioner, CGST, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, issued an order dated June 18, 2026 (received by the Company on June 26, 2026) revising a demand relating to shortfall in tax payment and mismatch of input tax credit for April 2018 to March 2023. The revised demand aggregates to approximately INR 2.97 crore (excluding applicable interest and penalty), compared with the earlier demand of approximately INR 2.96 crore. The Company stated there is no material impact and is evaluating the order and deciding its future course of action.

International execution lag. Execution is yet to commence on two projects in South Africa won in FY26 and on the Egypt order won in Q1 FY27.

FY26 full-year loss. Consolidated FY2026 figures show PBT of INR (270) crore and PAT of INR (296) crore, with PBT margin marked “NM.”

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Record order book. Unexecuted Order Value reached a record INR 13,024 crore as of June 2026, described as the highest UOV post-Covid. Domestic UOV of approximately INR 7,900 crore provides revenue and growth visibility.

Marquee Egypt win. SWREL won a turnkey project in Egypt worth approximately USD 560 million — a 1,000 MW-AC solar PV plant integrated with a 600 MWh battery energy storage system at West Minya Solar Power Project, Minya Governorate. It is being executed through a 50-50 JV, with SWREL’s share of project value at approximately USD 280 million (approximately INR 2,641 crore). Once complete, it is expected to rank among Egypt’s largest utility-scale renewable developments.

O&M growth. The O&M portfolio grew to approximately 18.3 GW as of June 2026, compared with 13.5 GW in FY26. Q1 FY27 O&M revenue grew approximately 40% year-on-year. Management anticipates a significant improvement in O&M revenue/EBITDA contribution this fiscal due to the increased portfolio size, describing O&M as a stable annuity business at a key inflection point.

Bid pipeline. A bid pipeline of over 27.7 GW is cited, alongside a business diversified across solar, wind, battery storage, and third-party O&M. The FY27 bid pipeline (ex-BESS) is also presented.

Domestic EPC as growth engine. Domestic EPC is described as the key growth engine with a strong pipeline for Domestic EPC and BESS.

Debt reduction. Bank borrowings decreased by approximately INR 129 crore versus the prior quarter due to scheduled repayments. Term debt decreased sequentially by approximately INR 160 crore. Net D/E stood at 0.9x as of June 2026, with net worth of INR 709 crore and a BBB+ credit rating.

Execution track record. Commissioned 18.3 GW and under-construction 10.4 GW; over 50 projects under execution; global manpower of 4,138. In FY26, the company commissioned 4.5 GW AC capacities and achieved its highest annual turnover post IPO. In 2025, it ventured into Wind EPC and its solar O&M portfolio crossed 10 GW.

PAT growth. Q1 FY27 reported PAT of INR 53 crore was up 36% year-on-year.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution timing risk: Six turnkey projects in the UOV have not commenced execution, concentrating expected revenue recognition in 2H FY27.
  • Margin risk: Gross margins for EPC businesses are guided to stabilize around 8–10% (depending on turnkey/BOS), below prior-period levels.
  • Working capital and receivable risk: Receivable days increased to 161 in June 2026 from 138 in March 2026.
  • Regulatory/tax risk: The GST order for April 2018 to March 2023 remains under evaluation, with future course of action undecided.
  • International execution risk: South Africa and Egypt projects are yet to commence execution.
  • Related party exposure: The 9th AGM approved a Material Related Party Transaction with Shapoorji Pallonji and Company Private Limited, with the promoter/promoter group interested in the resolution.
  • Remuneration governance item: A special resolution approved waiver of recovery of excess remuneration paid to Mr. Chandra Kishore Thakur, Manager, during FY2025-26.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The company’s own disclosure notes risks including competition, economic growth in India and abroad, time and cost overruns on contracts, international operations management, and government policies and actions.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Gross margin guidance. Management expects EPC gross margins to stabilize around 8–10% (depending on turnkey/BOS) and O&M at approximately 20%. Observed: Q1 FY27 gross margin was 9.9%, within the guided EPC band; FY26 gross margin was 10.5%.

Revenue timing guidance. Management stated the six unexecuted turnkey projects are likely to skew higher revenue recognition towards 2H FY27. Observed: Q1 FY27 revenue of INR 1,590 crore was lower than Q1 FY26 revenue of INR 1,762 crore and Q4 FY26 revenue of INR 1,946 crore.

O&M contribution guidance. Management anticipates a significant improvement in O&M revenue/EBITDA contribution this fiscal due to the increased portfolio size. Observed: Q1 FY27 O&M revenue grew approximately 40% year-on-year.

Order book to financials. The Global CEO stated the company expects growth to reflect on the topline and bottom-line in the months ahead, citing a robust UOV. Observed: UOV reached a record INR 13,024 crore as of June 2026, while Q1 FY27 revenue declined year-on-year.

Debt trajectory. Term debt continues to decrease with repayments. Observed: bank borrowings fell by approximately INR 129 crore versus the prior quarter, and term debt decreased sequentially by approximately INR 160 crore.

Domestic EPC margins. Management cites steady gross margins at approximately 9–10% for domestic EPC. Observed: consolidated Q1 FY27 gross margin was 9.9%.

PAT growth. Q1 FY27 reported PAT of INR 53 crore was up 36% year-on-year, against Q1 FY26 PAT of INR 39 crore.
