RELTD: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Restatement of FY2025-26 financials. On August 14, 2026, the Board approved re-stated consolidated audited financial results for Q4FY2025-26 and re-stated consolidated audited financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026. The restatement follows revised accounting policies and supersedes the financial statements filed on April 29, 2026. The statutory auditor’s report notes prior period errors and restatement of comparative information for the year ended March 31, 2025 and the opening balance sheet as at April 1, 2024, with impacts on revenue recognition/tax obligations, property, plant and equipment and depreciation, intangible assets and depreciation, net income, and opening retained earnings.

Change in accounting policy for solar assets. The Group moved from capitalising solar plant expenditure as Property, Plant and Equipment (Ind AS 16) to accounting for Power Purchase Agreements with DISCOMs as Service Concession Arrangements under Appendix D to Ind AS 115, using the intangible asset model. The change was applied retrospectively under Ind AS 8. The restated numbers are lower than previously reported: FY2025-26 net profit after tax restated at ₹713.56 Mn versus ₹807.77 Mn previously reported, and basic/diluted EPS restated at ₹4.00 versus ₹4.53 previously reported. Q4FY2025-26 net profit restated at ₹110.50 Mn versus ₹125.64 Mn previously reported.

Loss-making trading segment. The trading segment recorded a loss before tax and interest of ₹50.02 Mn for FY2025-26, compared with a loss of ₹6.90 Mn in FY2024-25. In Q1FY2026-27 the trading segment returned a profit of ₹13.04 Mn, but the FY2025-26 full-year position remains negative.

Associate losses. Share of loss from the associate company was ₹77.79 Mn for FY2025-26 and ₹117.26 Mn in Q1FY2026-27, weighing on consolidated profit after tax.

Rising finance costs and borrowings. Consolidated finance costs rose to ₹347.41 Mn in FY2025-26 from ₹98.73 Mn in FY2024-25. Non-current borrowings increased to ₹4,745.59 Mn as at March 31, 2026 from ₹1,718.28 Mn a year earlier. In Q1FY2026-27, consolidated finance costs were ₹122.81 Mn.

Monitoring agency observations on preferential issue proceeds. India Ratings & Research, in its report for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, noted that the company utilised preferential issue proceeds directly from its SBI current account, and that a clear trail of utilisation could not be fully established because of other debits and credits in that account; the agency relied on management declarations and the statutory certificate. The agency also noted that the amount utilised for the renewable energy business exceeded the amount proposed in the offer document (₹96.00 crore utilised against ₹90.00 crore proposed), within the +/-10% deviation permitted under the NSE and BSE circulars of December 13, 2022.

Q1FY2026-27 consolidated profitability decline. Consolidated profit for the period fell to ₹16.35 Mn in Q1FY2026-27 from ₹222.07 Mn in Q1FY2025-26 (restated), and total comprehensive income fell to ₹15.26 Mn from ₹222.07 Mn. Basic and diluted EPS declined to ₹0.09 from ₹1.30.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Sharp year-on-year growth in FY2025-26. Restated consolidated revenue from operations rose to ₹5,637.36 Mn for FY2025-26 from ₹2,843.08 Mn in FY2024-25. Restated consolidated profit for the year was ₹714.10 Mn versus ₹215.21 Mn. Solar segment revenue was ₹5,417.61 Mn against ₹2,585.19 Mn, and solar segment results before tax and interest were ₹1,218.42 Mn against ₹498.02 Mn.

Solar asset base expansion. Other intangible assets rose to ₹7,361.40 Mn as at March 31, 2026 from ₹2,259.56 Mn a year earlier, reflecting the recognition of concession rights. Solar segment assets increased to ₹11,106.73 Mn from ₹6,311.94 Mn. In Q1FY2026-27, solar segment assets rose further to ₹13,619.27 Mn.

Rights issue and ESOP inflows. During Q1FY2026-27, the company raised funds through a rights issue of 1,98,54,940 equity shares at a premium of ₹91 per share, plus 70,000 equity shares issued on exercise of ESOPs, with total receipts of ₹2,014.84 million. Paid-up equity share capital rose to ₹1,987.99 Mn as at June 30, 2026 from ₹1,788.74 Mn as at March 31, 2026.

Operating cash generation. Net cash flow from operating activities was ₹1,663.74 Mn in FY2025-26, up from ₹707.71 Mn in FY2024-25. Cash and cash equivalents rose to ₹915.41 Mn from ₹840.97 Mn.

Q1FY2026-27 solar segment performance. Solar segment revenue was ₹1,101.91 Mn and solar segment results before tax and interest were ₹318.57 Mn, higher than the ₹303.32 Mn recorded in Q4FY2025-26. Consolidated profit before exceptional items and tax was ₹177.06 Mn in Q1FY2026-27, up from ₹170.71 Mn in Q4FY2025-26.

Leadership continuity and employee alignment. The Board re-appointed Mr. Shantanu Lath as Whole Time Director designated as Chief Executive Officer, subject to member approval at the ensuing 46th Annual General Meeting, and approved the Ravindra Energy Employee Stock Option Scheme, 2026, also subject to member approval.

No deviation in preferential issue utilisation. The monitoring agency report for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 recorded no deviation from the objects of the preferential issue, with the full ₹180.00 crore utilised as at the end of the quarter.

3. Key Risks

Accounting restatement and prior period errors. The restatement of FY2025-26 financials and comparative periods, arising from prior period errors and the change in accounting policy, indicates weaknesses in prior financial reporting. The auditor’s report on the previously issued financial results for the year ended March 31, 2026 is superseded.

Reliance on other auditors. The auditor did not audit the restated financial statements of thirty-seven subsidiaries, eighteen LLPs and one associate company, which reflect total assets of ₹15,850.40 Mn, total revenues of ₹7,377.53 Mn, total comprehensive income of ₹(191.54) Mn and net cash flows of ₹(7.21) Mn for the year ended March 31, 2026. The audit opinion on these entities is based solely on the reports of other auditors.

Concentration in solar and DISCOM counterparties. The business is substantially concentrated in solar, with long-term PPAs with state/central DISCOMs. Solar segment revenue was ₹5,417.61 Mn of total FY2025-26 revenue of ₹5,637.36 Mn.

Leverage and finance cost sensitivity. Non-current borrowings of ₹4,745.59 Mn and finance costs of ₹347.41 Mn in FY2025-26, with finance costs of ₹122.81 Mn in Q1FY2026-27, expose the company to interest rate and refinancing risk.

Associate company losses. Continued share of losses from the associate company (₹77.79 Mn in FY2025-26 and ₹117.26 Mn in Q1FY2026-27) may persist.

Trading segment volatility. The trading segment swung from a FY2025-26 loss of ₹50.02 Mn to a Q1FY2026-27 profit of ₹13.04 Mn, indicating volatility.

Monitoring agency trail limitations. The monitoring agency could not fully establish a clear trail of preferential issue proceeds utilisation from the SBI current account and relied on management declarations and the statutory certificate.

Dependence on member approvals. The CEO re-appointment and the Ravindra Energy Employee Stock Option Scheme, 2026 are subject to approval of members at the ensuing 46th Annual General Meeting.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The supplied material does not contain explicit forward-looking management guidance on revenue, profitability or capital expenditure. The available evidence of management’s stated positions and observed performance is as follows:

Preferential issue objects versus utilisation. The offer document set out objects of ₹90.00 crore for investment in renewable energy business, ₹60.00 crore for investment in electric vehicle business, and ₹30.00 crore for general corporate purpose, totalling ₹180.00 crore. As at the end of the quarter ended June 30, 2026, ₹96.00 crore had been utilised for renewable energy (against ₹90.00 crore proposed, within the permitted +/-10% deviation), ₹54.01 crore for electric vehicle business (against ₹60.00 crore proposed, with ₹5.99 crore unutilised), and ₹29.99 crore for general corporate purpose (against ₹30.00 crore proposed, with ₹0.01 crore unutilised). Total utilisation was ₹180.00 crore. The monitoring agency reported no deviation from the objects and no unfavourable events affecting viability.

Accounting policy rationale versus reported outcome. Management’s revised policy rationale is that the Group does not control the underlying infrastructure in the manner contemplated for PPE, and that the right conferred is essentially a right to sell power to a DISCOM under a regulated PPA, so classifying the asset as an intangible right better reflects the substance of the arrangement. The observed outcome of applying this policy retrospectively is lower reported net profit and EPS for FY2025-26 and Q4FY2025-26 than previously reported, with FY2025-26 net profit restated at ₹713.56 Mn (versus ₹807.77 Mn previously reported) and EPS at ₹4.00 (versus ₹4.53).

ESOP scheme implementation versus stated terms. The REL ESOP Scheme 2022 provided that 25% of stocks would vest after one year and the balance 75% after two years, with vested options exercisable within 10 years from the date of respective vesting. Observed performance: during the year, 25% of the stocks vested at completion of one year, of which 70,000 shares were exercised and issued. The Board subsequently approved the Ravindra Energy Employee Stock Option Scheme, 2026, subject to member approval.

Q1FY2026-27 versus prior periods. Consolidated revenue from operations was ₹1,199.69 Mn in Q1FY2026-27, compared with ₹1,401.64 Mn in Q4FY2025-26 and ₹1,816.46 Mn in Q1FY2025-26 (restated). Consolidated profit for the period was ₹16.35 Mn, compared with ₹111.04 Mn in Q4FY2025-26 and ₹222.07 Mn in Q1FY2025-26 (restated). The decline was driven by a share of loss from the associate company of ₹117.26 Mn and higher finance costs of ₹122.81 Mn.
