Headwinds and Challenges

NSEL-related litigation and regulatory exposure. The company continues to face civil suits, First Information Reports (FIRs), complaints, charge-sheets, and investigations connected to the NSEL payment default of July 2013. These involve the Economic Offences Wing (EOW), CBI, Enforcement Directorate, and SFIO. Assets have been attached by the State Government under the MPID Act and by the Enforcement Directorate under the PMLA. Statutory auditors have issued qualified conclusions because the outcomes of these legal and regulatory matters remain uncertain, and the matters are disclosed as contingent liabilities and audit qualifications.

Yes Bank AT-1 Bonds. Ongoing legal disputes regarding Yes Bank AT-1 Bonds persist, and the company expects impairment on these instruments, having adopted a conservative approach.

Macroeconomic headwinds. Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, the ongoing Russia-Ukraine war, high US tariffs, and potential inflationary pressures are cited as risks to overall economic conditions and business operations.

NCLAT appeal risk on the Ticker Limited scheme. Legal challenges and potential appeals before the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) remain open regarding the NCLT order and the Scheme of Arrangement involving Ticker Limited. The NCLT Hyderabad Bench dismissed the Interim Application filed by Resolution Applicant Mr. Vivek Kumar Ratakonda and approved the Resolution Plan dated April 11, 2026, under section 31 of the IBC in the matter of Avantine Software Pvt Ltd. vs. Baron Infotech Limited, where Ticker Limited is proposed as the Transferor Company. Ticker Limited will determine its future course of action after evaluating the order and its implications, including issues under Regulation 39(1A) and Section 29A.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

NSEL One-Time Settlement (OTS) Scheme. The Board approved participation in and support for a Scheme of Arrangement between NSEL and Specified Creditors aimed at a One-Time Full and Final Settlement to end litigations. The scheme received approval from 92.81% of Specified Creditors (91.35% in value) and was sanctioned by the NCLT on November 28, 2025. It involves ₹1,950 crores to settle claims of 5,682 traders and received overwhelming approval from traders. Management expresses confidence that the scheme will proceed with support from central and state governments, aiming to bring closure to legacy litigations and create sustainable value for shareholders.

Cybersecurity expansion. Growth prospects include expansion into the consumer cybersecurity domain through CYBX, a cybersecurity super app with embedded insurance, and strengthening of the 63SATS ecosystem covering critical infrastructure and enterprises. The strategic focus is described as “Securing Digital Life,” expanding capabilities across cybersecurity (63SATS, Cyberdome, CSF, and CYBX), Exchange Technology, Risk Solutions, and LegalTech.

Other growth drivers. Enhancements in Exchange Technology Solutions, Risk Solutions (such as DataCollector and Riskalculator), and QiLegal are cited as growth drivers.

ITNL recovery. The company expects the remaining carrying amount of ₹870.06 lakhs from IL&FS Transportation Networks Ltd (ITNL) to be recovered fully in future interim or final distributions.

Dividend. The Board recommended a dividend of ₹2/- per share (100%) for FY 2025-26, subject to shareholder approval and appropriate judicial orders.

Key Risks

  • Uncertain outcomes of ongoing legal and regulatory matters related to NSEL, reflected in qualified auditor conclusions and contingent liabilities.
  • Asset attachments under the MPID Act and PMLA.
  • Potential appeals before NCLAT concerning the NCLT order and the Scheme of Arrangement involving Ticker Limited.
  • Impairment expected on Yes Bank AT-1 Bonds.
  • Macroeconomic and geopolitical risks, including Middle East tensions, the Russia-Ukraine war, high US tariffs, and inflationary pressures.
  • Management’s stated belief that parties who filed civil suits against the company and the Group will not be able to sustain any claims against them is a forward-looking position rather than a resolved outcome.
