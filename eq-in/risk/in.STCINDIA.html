  • Consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, were not submitted because the subsidiary STCL did not submit its results, and consequently the board did not consider or approve them.
  • The company has numerous pending legal cases and disputes involving trade receivables and payables.
  • Title deeds are not available for several properties.
  • There has been non-compliance with various SEBI regulations, leading to fines.
  • The company lacks independent directors and full-time working directors.

  • Technical issues on the NSE website caused a delay in uploading the Outcome of the Board Meeting.

  • The company successfully uploaded the Integrated Filing (XBRL) for the Annual Audited Financial Results for the year ended March 31, 2026, and Un-audited Financial Results (Standalone) for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, on both BSE and NSE within the time period.
  • Management is hopeful of getting waivers for regulatory penalties imposed by stock exchanges.
