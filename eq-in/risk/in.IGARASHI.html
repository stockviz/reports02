Igarashi Motors India — Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Dividend distribution now carries a tax-withholding compliance burden. Effective 1 April 2026, under the Income-tax Act, 2025, the company must withhold tax on dividends. Resident shareholders face 10% TDS under Section 393(1), rising to 20% where PAN is unavailable, invalid, inoperative, or not linked with Aadhaar under Section 397(2). Non-resident shareholders face 20% plus surcharge and cess (or treaty rate, if beneficial), and 30% for residents of Notified Jurisdictional Areas. The company issued detailed documentation requirements on 20 June 2026, with a submission deadline of 5:00 PM IST on 24 July 2026. The company explicitly reserved the right not to apply beneficial DTAA rates where documentation was incomplete, and noted that shareholders subject to higher deduction would need to claim refunds via income-tax returns.
  • Dividend payment mechanics are constrained. Dividend to shareholders holding securities in physical form must be paid only through electronic mode, conditional on furnishing PAN, nomination choice, contact details, bank account details, and specimen signature — a SEBI mandate effective 1 April 2024 and reiterated in the 20 June 2026 communication.
  • Related-party resolution attracted the only meaningful dissent. Resolution 4 (Material Related Party Transactions with Igarashi Electric Works Limited, Japan — operational transaction) saw 6,076 votes against from public non-institutions (1.9412% of that category’s polled votes) and 0.4249% against on total votes polled. Promoter and promoter group holdings (23,606,288 shares) were excluded from voting on this resolution, reducing total votes polled to 1,430,069 (4.5435% of outstanding).
  • Low public non-institution participation. Public non-institution voting turnout was 4.7455% across most resolutions, indicating limited engagement from that shareholder class.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • All six resolutions passed with requisite majority at the 34th AGM held 7 August 2026 (10:00 AM to 11:25 AM, via VC/OAVM), covering adoption of audited FY2025-26 financial statements, final dividend approval, re-appointment of Mr. Haruo Igarashi (DIN 08587832), the related-party transaction with Igarashi Electric Works Limited, remuneration to a related party holding office or place of profit, and ratification of Cost Auditors’ remuneration for FY2026-27.
  • Dividend continuity. The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹1.30 per equity share (face value ₹10) for FY2025-26 at its 21 May 2026 meeting, approved by shareholders on 7 August 2026. Payment is to be made on or after 7 August 2026, within 30 days of the AGM. The record date was 31 July 2026.
  • Continued operational relationship with the Japanese parent ecosystem. Shareholder approval of material related-party transactions with Igarashi Electric Works Limited, Japan, supports ongoing operational transactions.
  • Q1 FY2026-27 results tabled on schedule. The Board took on record unaudited IndAS financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 at its meeting on 6 August 2026 (11:00 AM to 12:32 PM), with a limited review report from statutory auditors B S R & Co. LLP.
  • Broad institutional support. Public institutions voted 100% in favour on five of six resolutions; on the related-party resolution, institutions also voted 100% in favour (1,117,069 votes).

3. Key Risks

  • Concentration of promoter holding and related-party dependence. Promoter and promoter group held 23,606,288 shares, and the related-party transaction with Igarashi Electric Works Limited required a resolution where promoters were interested and excluded from voting. The company’s operations include material transactions with this related party.
  • Tax documentation risk for shareholders. Failure to submit required documents by the 24 July 2026 deadline could result in higher withholding (20% or 30%), with recourse only through income-tax refund claims. The company stated it is not obligated to apply beneficial DTAA rates.
  • Governance scrutiny signal. The related-party resolution was the only one to draw material against-votes (6,076), and it also recorded 23,606,288 abstained/invalid votes (promoter shares excluded from the poll).
  • Dependence on electronic payment infrastructure. Physical shareholders must have updated PAN, bank, nomination, and signature records to receive dividends.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Dividend guidance met. The Board recommended ₹1.30 per equity share on 21 May 2026; shareholders approved it on 7 August 2026. Payment was guided to occur on or after 7 August 2026 and within 30 days of the AGM.
  • Financial reporting timeline met. The company committed to submitting Q1 FY2026-27 results and did so on 6 August 2026, with the Board meeting concluding at 12:32 PM and the limited review report enclosed.
  • AGM commitments fulfilled. The company stated all six resolutions passed with requisite majority, and the scrutinizer’s report (dated 8 August 2026) confirmed the results, with e-voting open from 9:00 AM on 4 August 2026 to 5:00 PM on 6 August 2026, and the cut-off date of 31 July 2026.
  • No forward financial guidance on revenue, margins, or earnings is present in the supplied material; accordingly, no comparison of such guidance to performance can be made.
