KIOCL: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Governance and board composition gaps. The statutory auditor’s report on the audited standalone results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 (board meeting held May 27, 2026) records, as an Emphasis of Matter, that the company does not have Independent Directors and has therefore not constituted an Audit Committee as required under the Companies Act, 2013 and the Listing Regulations. In the absence of an Audit Committee, the Board itself reviewed and approved the audited results. The company also does not have a Woman Director, does not have the requisite number of required Directors, and has not constituted a Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The notes to the results state that, as a government entity, appointment and reappointment of Directors falls under the purview of the Government of India and is “in process.”

Suspended mining operations. Note 6 records that Kudremukh Mining Operations were suspended in 2006 by order of the Hon’ble Supreme Court, with the value of assets reduced to NIL; township assets were depreciated in full during 2014-15, excluding freehold land of 114.31 hectares with a book value of Rs. 29.05 lakhs, which remains on the books.

Non-operational Blast Furnace Unit. Note 7 states the BFU plant has not been in operation since 2009. An impairment test based on an independent valuer’s report found the recoverable amount of each class of BFU assets exceeds carrying amount, so no impairment loss was recognised. Forward and backward integration projects at the BFU plant remain in Capital Work in Progress, with no impairment envisaged as the recoverable amount exceeds carrying value. The company states it is working on various options to make the BFU operational along with the Coke Oven, whose installation and commissioning has been entrusted to M/s Tuaman Engineering Limited, Kolkata as an EPC contractor on a lumpsum turnkey basis, with technology from M/s CIMFR, GoI, Dhanbad.

Devadari mining rights not yet operational. Note 9 records capital expenditure of Rs. 54,549.55 lakhs up to March 31, 2026 (previous year Rs. 53,015.01 lakhs) for development of Devadari Iron ore mines, including NPV, CA, stamp duty, cess, registration charges and other incidental charges, classified as mining right under other intangible assets. The company has not obtained possession of the forest land or working permission from the Government of Karnataka to commence mining activities, and amortisation of the mining asset will be charged only on start of mining operations. The auditor notes the intangible asset is not available for use and commercial production has not commenced.

Lease-linked construction obligations. Note 8 records ROU leased land obtained from KIADB in Mangalore/Doddaballapura for which the company must construct a Railway siding/R&D Centre respectively; these are yet to be implemented. This includes 52.87 acres valued at Rs. 657.25 lakhs taken under a 20-year lease-cum-sale from KIADB for constructing a railway siding, valid till December 29, 2028, after which a sale deed is to be executed in favour of the company.

Segment performance. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the Pellet Plant segment recorded a loss of Rs. 4,342 lakhs (previous year loss of Rs. 22,942 lakhs) and the Pig Iron Plant segment recorded a loss of Rs. 1,095 lakhs (previous year loss of Rs. 2,237 lakhs). Pellet Plant segment revenue for the year was Rs. 3,722 lakhs against Rs. 48,370 lakhs in the previous year, while service contract revenue rose to Rs. 57,342 lakhs from Rs. 10,671 lakhs.

Statutory review. The audited accounts are subject to review by the Comptroller and Auditor General of India under Section 143(6) of the Companies Act, 2013.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Return to full-year profitability. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the company reported profit before tax of Rs. 1,189 lakhs and profit after tax of Rs. 1,657 lakhs, compared with a loss before tax of Rs. 20,507 lakhs and loss after tax of Rs. 20,458 lakhs in the previous year. Total comprehensive income was Rs. 2,472 lakhs against a loss of Rs. 20,700 lakhs previously. For the quarter ended March 31, 2026, profit after tax was Rs. 5,339 lakhs against a loss of Rs. 3,688 lakhs in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

Service contract contribution. Other Operating Revenue – Service Contract contributed Rs. 57,342 lakhs to net sales/income from operations for the year, up from Rs. 10,671 lakhs in the previous year. Contribution from the service contract (net of expenses) was Rs. 1,024 lakhs for the year and Rs. 3,052 lakhs for the March 2026 quarter.

Treasury and other income. Interest income from treasury operations was Rs. 5,428 lakhs for the year (previous year Rs. 4,255 lakhs), with net gain on financial assets designated at fair value through profit or loss of Rs. 174 lakhs (previous year Rs. 70 lakhs). Other income for the year was Rs. 9,480 lakhs against Rs. 5,010 lakhs previously.

Balance sheet position. Total equity rose to Rs. 173,622.77 lakhs as at March 31, 2026 from Rs. 171,150.36 lakhs a year earlier. Cash and cash equivalents increased to Rs. 7,464.45 lakhs from Rs. 6,882.97 lakhs, and bank balances other than cash and cash equivalents rose to Rs. 75,118.69 lakhs from Rs. 66,099.36 lakhs. Net cash flow from operating activities was positive at Rs. 901.65 lakhs for the year, compared with Rs. 34,334.95 lakhs in the previous year.

BFU integration projects. The forward and backward integration projects at the BFU Plant remain capitalised as Capital Work in Progress, with the recoverable amount assessed as exceeding carrying value, and the company is evaluating options to make the BFU operational along with the Coke Oven.

Devadari mining asset. Capital expenditure on the Devadari Iron ore mines continues to be capitalised as a mining right, with amortisation deferred until mining operations commence.

3. Key Risks

  • Governance non-compliance risk: Absence of Independent Directors, Audit Committee, Woman Director, requisite number of Directors, and Nomination and Remuneration Committee, as flagged in the auditor’s Emphasis of Matter; the company attributes this to the Government of India’s appointment process being in progress.
  • Asset recovery and impairment risk: The BFU plant has been non-operational since 2009 and the Kudremukh mining operations suspended since 2006; carrying values of BFU assets and capital work in progress depend on valuation reports and the recoverable amount exceeding carrying value.
  • Project execution risk: Devadari mining cannot commence without possession of forest land and working permission from the Government of Karnataka; the mining right remains unamortised and unavailable for use.
  • Lease obligation risk: Railway siding and R&D Centre construction on KIADB-leased land in Mangalore/Doddaballapura remain unimplemented, with the railway siding lease valid only until December 29, 2028.
  • Segment concentration and volatility: Pellet Plant revenue fell sharply year-on-year while service contract revenue rose, and both Pellet Plant and Pig Iron Plant segments recorded losses for the year.
  • Statutory review risk: The audited accounts remain subject to CAG review under Section 143(6) of the Companies Act, 2013.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The announcements do not contain explicit quantitative guidance or forward-looking financial projections. The forward-looking elements disclosed are:

  • The company states it is “working on various options to make BFU operational along with Coke Oven,” with the Coke Oven installation and commissioning entrusted to M/s Tuaman Engineering Limited as an EPC contractor.
  • The company states that amortisation for the Devadari mining asset “shall be charged as on start of mining operation,” indicating mining has not commenced.
  • The company states that the appointment and reappointment of Directors falls under the purview of the Government of India and is “in process,” indicating the governance gaps are expected to be addressed through that process.
  • The 50th Annual General Meeting of KIOCL Limited will be held on Tuesday, September 29, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. through Video Conferencing (VC) / Other Audio-visual Means (OAVM).

Observed performance against these positions: the BFU plant remains non-operational since 2009 and the integration projects remain in Capital Work in Progress; Devadari mining activities have not commenced and the mining right remains unamortised; the governance positions (no Independent Directors, no Audit Committee, no Woman Director, no Nomination and Remuneration Committee) remained in place as at the May 27, 2026 board meeting and the August 13, 2026 board meeting, with the August 13, 2026 intimation noting that the unaudited results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 were approved by the Board.
