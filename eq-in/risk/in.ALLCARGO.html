Allcargo Logistics — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Income-tax search and demand: A search by Income-tax Authorities was conducted at the Company’s office premises and residences of certain key managerial personnel during the year ended March 31, 2025, with disclosures made to the stock exchanges on February 12, 2025. An assessment order dated June 30, 2026, under section 143(3) read with section 115BC(1)(c) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, for the block period April 1, 2018 to April 5, 2025, raised a demand of ₹5.61 crores. The Company states the demand pertains to the International Supply Chain business demerged to Allcargo Global Limited, and that under the NCLT-approved scheme all liabilities and obligations, whether arising before or after the appointed date of October 1, 2023, belong to Allcargo Global Limited. The Company paid ₹1.21 crore in the subsequent month on behalf of Allcargo Global Limited and filed an appeal for the remaining ₹4.40 crore with a 20% deposit. The Company states there is no further financial impact on it depending on the final outcome.
  • Nature of the tax demand: The demand relates to notional additions — transfer pricing adjustments of ₹7.05 crore for AY 2023-24 and AY 2024-25 on corporate guarantees given for international subsidiaries, and disallowance of salary expenditure at 10% of total expenditure attributable to services rendered for foreign subsidiaries. The Company states these adjustments have no impact on operations, business continuity, or underlying financial position, and that it is examining the order and evaluating legal remedies including appeal.
  • Discontinued fuel stations business: The management decided during the year ended March 31, 2025, to discontinue the Diesel, Petrol, and Lubricants business, which met the criteria for classification as a discontinued operation under Ind AS 105. Results have been restated accordingly. In Q1FY27, revenue from discontinued operations was ₹18 crore with total expenses of ₹18 crore and profit before tax of nil, versus revenue of ₹46 crore and profit before tax of ₹1 crore in Q1FY26. The sale of the Bangalore fuel station for ₹2.52 crore was completed after March 31, 2026, with a loss of ₹0.1 crore recognised during the quarter.
  • Restatement and accounting override: The demerger of the International Supply Chain business has been given effect from the appointed date of October 1, 2023, overriding the requirements of applicable Ind AS, and comparative information for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 has been restated. The auditors drew attention to this in their review reports without modifying their conclusions. The merger of Allcargo Gati Limited into the Company was accounted for using the pooling of interests method with effect from April 1, 2024, with comparatives restated.
  • Leadership transition: Mr. Shashi Kiran Shetty, Founder and Chairman, resigned as Director and Chairman with effect from August 5, 2026, upon closure of business hours, due to other commitments. Mr. Dinesh Kumar Lal was appointed Chairman with effect from August 5, 2026, and the Board committees were reconstituted the same day.
  • Sequential profitability decline: While profit after tax rose 237.1% year-on-year to ₹14 crore in Q1FY27, it declined 33.0% quarter-on-quarter from ₹20 crore in Q4FY26. Pre-exceptional PBT of ₹19 crore in Q1FY27 compared with ₹4 crore in Q4FY26 (up 347.7% QoQ) and negative ₹12 crore in Q1FY26.
  • Space under management: Consultative Logistics space under management was 7.5 million sq ft in Q1FY27, up 0.2% year-on-year but down 6.4% quarter-on-quarter from 8.1 million sq ft in Q4FY26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record quarterly revenue: Q1FY27 revenue from operations was ₹546 crore, up 11.2% year-on-year from ₹491 crore and up 6.2% quarter-on-quarter from ₹514 crore, with highest-ever quarterly revenue recorded across both express distribution and contract logistics businesses.
  • Profitability expansion: EBITDA grew 39.2% year-on-year to ₹71 crore (from ₹51 crore) and 18.9% quarter-on-quarter (from ₹60 crore). Pre-exceptional PBT rose 258.3% year-on-year to ₹19 crore from negative ₹12 crore. Gross profit rose 11.6% year-on-year to ₹163 crore.
  • Express Distribution: Revenue grew 13.5% year-on-year to ₹385 crore, driven by a 6.7% year-on-year volume increase (to 312 thousand tons) and a 6.4% year-on-year increase in yield. Realisation per ton rose from an index base of 100.0 in Q1FY26 to 106.4 in Q1FY27.
  • Contract Logistics: Revenue grew 6.1% year-on-year to ₹161 crore, with 3% increase in revenue per sq ft for Q1, over 99% service quality adherence, a 98% customer retention rate, and 15 new business opportunities added in Q1 (3 in auto and engineering, 5 in chemical, 7 in e-com and others).
  • Operating leverage: Consolidated SG&A and opex costs as a percentage of sales declined to 70.1% in Q1FY27 from 70.3% in Q1FY26, while gross margin improved to 29.9% from 29.7%. EBITDA margin improved to 13% in Q1FY27 from 10% in Q1FY26.
  • Digital and AI initiatives: The Company is deploying AI-driven analytics and data-led decision-making for demand forecasting, network planning, shipment visibility, and operational decisions. Technology deployments include Hub Eye, Gate Scan, WMS, Control Tower (24x7), Route Optimization, an AI-enabled Booking App, a new booking app, consignee app, customer portal, and finance ERP transformation.
  • Network and reach: The Company operates a nationwide network covering 99% of India’s districts, with 100% serviceable pin codes, 700+ facilities, 90+ hubs, 80+ logistics parks, more than 1,500 distribution center departures a day, over 9,000 trucks in the fleet, and presence at 24 airports. It is a market leader in chemical warehousing with ~2,800 air logistics centres and 12M+ sq ft Grade-A warehousing space.
  • Seasonal outlook: Management expects business activity to strengthen further in the second and third quarters, especially during the upcoming festive season.
  • ESG positioning: The group has set a goal to become carbon neutral by 2040, with over 400 alternate fuel vehicles including 125+ electric vehicles, solar panels at more than 10 facilities generating 3,79,848 kWh annually and reducing CO2 by 2.77 lakh MTs, and ISO 27001 certification for data security.

3. Key Risks

  • Tax litigation: The Company has filed an appeal against the ₹4.40 crore portion of the tax demand with a 20% deposit. The outcome depends on the appeal, though the Company states any liability pertains to the demerged International Supply Chain business transferred to Allcargo Global Limited.
  • Demerger-related liabilities: The NCLT-approved scheme specifies that all liabilities and obligations of the International Supply Chain business, whether arising before or after October 1, 2023, belong to Allcargo Global Limited. The Company has paid amounts on behalf of Allcargo Global Limited, indicating potential cash flow timing considerations.
  • Accounting restatements: The demerger treatment overrides applicable Ind AS requirements, and comparative figures have been restated, which affects comparability of reported results.
  • Discontinued operations: The fuel stations business has been classified as discontinued, with the Bangalore fuel station sale completed at a loss of ₹0.1 crore in Q1FY27.
  • Forward-looking statement risks: The Company’s presentation notes that forward-looking statements are subject to known and unknown risks, uncertainties, and assumptions including the performance of the Indian and international economies, industry performance, competition, strategy implementation, growth and expansion levels, technological implementation, changes in revenue, income or cash flows, market preferences, and market risks.
  • Associate losses not consolidated: The value of investment in an associate (Gati Ship Limited) had been fully provided in an earlier period, and therefore the share of loss in the associate has not been considered in consolidated financial results.
  • Unaudited subsidiary results: The consolidated statement includes interim financial results of five subsidiaries not reviewed by their auditors, reflecting total revenue of nil, total net profit after tax of ₹0.37 crore, and total comprehensive income of ₹0.37 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, furnished by management and stated to be not material to the Group.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management stated it expects business activity to strengthen further in the second and third quarters, especially during the upcoming festive season, and remains focused on sustaining profitable growth through disciplined pricing, superior customer experience, operational excellence, and deeper market penetration across both express distribution and contract logistics, while maintaining a prudent growth strategy.
  • Observed performance: Q1FY27 delivered record revenue in both express distribution and contract logistics, with EBITDA growth of 39.2% year-on-year and pre-exceptional PBT growth of 258.3% year-on-year. Express revenue grew 13.5% year-on-year on 6.7% volume growth and 6.4% yield improvement. Contract Logistics revenue grew 6.1% year-on-year with 3% higher revenue per sq ft. The Company reported over 99% service quality adherence and a 98% customer retention rate in Contract Logistics.
  • Management commentary: Mr. Ketan Kulkarni, Managing Director and CEO, stated that Q1FY27 performance reflects disciplined execution, with growth driven by higher shipment volumes, stronger customer relationships, and sustained operational improvements. He noted that a service equation-led pricing approach improved yields, over 99% service quality adherence resulted in strong customer retention, and the Company is progressively leveraging AI-driven analytics and data-led decision-making. He stated the Company will continue to accelerate the use of AI, digital technologies, and data intelligence, and remains focused on delivering consistent service quality, expanding customer partnerships, and driving profitable growth through disciplined execution while driving long-term value creation.
  • Strategic approach: The Company describes a “4 P Approach” — Process and Power of EDGE (Everyday Great Execution), Paradigm Technology, People, and Performance — with sharp performance management driven by the ONE TEAM – ONE GOAL philosophy focused on unit level EDGE, and deployment of technology across the value chain.
  • Subsequent events: The 33rd Annual General Meeting was held on September 16, 2026, where all four resolutions (adoption of financial statements, appointment of Mr. Kaiwan Kalyaniwalla, payment of commission to non-executive directors, and continuation of directorship of Mr. Dinesh Kumar Lal) were passed with the requisite majority. A virtual group meeting with analysts and investors is scheduled for September 21, 2026, from 4 PM to 5 PM, based on publicly available information and the Q1FY27 Investor Presentation from August 2026.
