EIHAHOTELS: Recent Corporate Developments Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The company identifies several external pressures affecting its operating environment:

  • Geopolitical and aviation-related headwinds, including airline capacity constraints, which can affect both domestic and inbound travel.
  • Risks and uncertainties beyond the Company’s control, specifically cited as market conditions, regulatory changes, economic developments, competitive pressures, operational challenges, and development challenges for pipeline projects.
  • Renovation-related disruption: Trident Jaipur is noted as being under renovation, which affects the property’s contribution to the operating portfolio during the period.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management points to a supportive demand environment and an active development pipeline:

  • Domestic demand remains a core driver, supported by limited supply additions in the market.
  • MICE momentum, including marquee MICE events, is expected to support demand.
  • Domestic air traffic is described as resilient, growing approximately 1.4% YoY in Q1 FY27.
  • An expected revival in foreign tourists is anticipated for the rest of FY27.
  • Expansion and new hotel projects in the pipeline: Trident Udaipur (Expansion), Trident Jaipur (Renovation), Trident Vishakhapatnam (New Hotel), and a Banquet at Trident Agra (New).
  • Management continues to observe year-on-year increases in average rates across all regions.

3. Key Risks

  • Exposure to market conditions, regulatory changes, and economic developments that are outside the company’s control.
  • Competitive pressures in the hospitality sector.
  • Operational challenges across the existing portfolio.
  • Development challenges relating to pipeline projects (expansions, renovations, and new hotels), which could affect timelines or execution.
  • Aviation and geopolitical factors that could constrain travel demand, particularly inbound foreign tourist flows.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

Guidance provided: - Demand momentum is expected to remain strong through Q2 and FY27, supported by marquee MICE events and an expected revival in foreign tourists for the rest of FY27. - Management continues to observe year-on-year increases in average rates across all regions.

Observed performance (Q1 FY27, quarter ended 30 June 2026): - Revenue from Operations: Rs 65.9 Crores - Total Income: Rs 71.8 Crores - EBITDA: Rs 12.9 Crores - PAT: Rs 6.9 Crores, a 10% YoY increase - Total keys in India: 784 across properties including The Oberoi Rajvilas, Jaipur; The Oberoi Cecil, Shimla; Trident Agra; Trident Jaipur (under renovation); Trident Udaipur; Trident Bhubaneshwar; and Trident Chennai. - Net worth as of 30 June 2026: Rs 609 Crores - Fund Position: Rs 280 Crores

The Q1 FY27 results were approved by the Board at its meeting on 04 August 2026, with the meeting held from 11:30 A.M. to approximately 12:40 P.M.

5. Governance and Shareholder Matters

At the 43rd Annual General Meeting held on 04 August 2026 (4:30 P.M. to 5:10 P.M.), six resolutions were passed, all with overwhelming support:

  • Adoption of Audited Financial Statements for FY ended 31 March 2026
  • Declaration of dividend on equity shares for FY 2025-26
  • Re-appointment of Mr. Arjun Singh Oberoi (DIN: 00052106) as director retiring by rotation
  • Appointment of Mr. Atul Hiralal Shah (DIN: 02538052) as Non-Executive Independent Director (Special Resolution)
  • Approval of Material Related Party Transactions with EIH Limited (promoter/promoter group interested; promoter votes excluded, with 99.9993% of votes polled in favour)
  • Payment of Commission to Non-Executive Independent Directors

The record date for the AGM was 28 July 2026, with 26,274 shareholders on record. Total votes polled on ordinary resolutions were approximately 66.06% of outstanding shares, while the related party transaction resolution saw 13.50% of outstanding shares polled (reflecting promoter exclusion).
