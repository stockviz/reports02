STYLEBAAZA — Q1FY27 Announcements Review

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin pressure. Gross profit margin declined to 34.1% in Q1FY27 from 35.8% in Q1FY26, a fall of 173 bps. EBITDA margin also contracted, from 15.3% to 14.8% on an IND-AS consolidated basis (a decline of 53 bps), and from 6.5% to 5.6% on a pre-INDAS basis (a decline of 88 bps). On a pre-INDAS basis, Profit Before Tax fell 29% year-on-year to Rs 91 Mn, and PAT margin slipped from 2.4% to 1.9%.

Profitability at the bottom line remains thin. IND-AS consolidated PAT was Rs 22 Mn in Q1FY27, a PAT margin of just 0.5%. Profit Before Tax of Rs 33 Mn was down 8% year-on-year, despite a 29% rise in revenue, reflecting the weight of finance costs (Rs 203 Mn) and depreciation and amortisation (Rs 518 Mn).

Cost escalation. Employee expenses rose to Rs 414 Mn from Rs 337 Mn, and other expenses rose to Rs 524 Mn from Rs 436 Mn on an IND-AS basis. On a pre-INDAS basis, other expenses rose to Rs 968 Mn from Rs 768 Mn.

Balance sheet strain. Borrowings increased sharply, with non-current borrowings rising from Rs 177 Mn in March 2025 to Rs 326 Mn in March 2026, and current borrowings rising from Rs 1,488 Mn to Rs 3,151 Mn over the same period. Inventories grew from Rs 5,215 Mn to Rs 6,825 Mn. Cash and bank balances fell from Rs 221 Mn to Rs 129 Mn, and net cash flow turned negative by Rs 56 Mn in FY26.

Prior-quarter volatility. Q4FY26 showed a consolidated IND-AS loss: PAT of negative Rs 256 Mn, with an exceptional item of negative Rs 206 Mn, and EBITDA margin of only 10.3%. FY26 exceptional items of Rs 325 Mn also affected reported profitability.

Governance and organisational change. On August 13, 2026, the Board approved the re-designation of Mr. Siddhant Khemani from Chief Marketing Officer and Senior Management Personnel to Head E-commerce, with him ceasing to be identified as Senior Management Personnel with immediate effect.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Revenue momentum. Q1FY27 revenue from operations was Rs 4,864 Mn, up 29% year-on-year and 4% quarter-on-quarter. FY26 revenue was Rs 18,409 Mn, compared with Rs 13,431 Mn in FY25 and Rs 9,729 Mn in FY24.

Store network expansion. The company operated 276 stores as of June 30, 2026, spanning 2.6 Mn rental sq. ft across 10 states and 198 cities, with an average store size of 9,347 sq. ft. Management expresses an ambition to reach 500 stores and to continue driving sustainable growth while strengthening its position as a trusted fashion destination across real Bharat.

Private label strength. Eleven private labels contributed 62% of revenue, or Rs 3,006 Mn, in Q1FY27, with a private label revenue CAGR of approximately 64% over the past four years. The Square Up brand achieved its highest revenue of Rs 1,207 Mn in Q1FY27. Private labels give greater control over sourcing and support customer loyalty through repeat purchases.

Operational metrics. Same store sales growth was 7%, with 5.53 Mn bills, an average transaction value of Rs 933, and sales per sq. ft of Rs 679 per month. Apparel accounted for 87% of the mix and general merchandise 13%.

Cluster-based, underpenetrated market focus. The company follows a cluster-based approach with a presence across Tier 1, 2 and 3 cities and underpenetrated Tier 3 and 4 markets, with a dominant presence in West Bengal and a footprint across Eastern India.

Brand recognition. The company was awarded a Guinness World Record title for creating the World’s Largest Fashion Wardrobe, a 50 feet x 25 feet installation at City Centre 1, Salt Lake, Bidhannagar, Kolkata. It was also recognised at the Asian Brand & Leadership Conclave 2025 and the Kolkata Retail Summit 2025.

Balance sheet strengthening. Total equity rose from Rs 2,151 Mn in March 2024 to Rs 4,502 Mn in March 2026. Net cash flow from operating activities improved from Rs 527 Mn in FY25 to Rs 1,255 Mn in FY26.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued gross and EBITDA margin compression, as seen in the 173 bps and 53 bps year-on-year declines in Q1FY27.
  • Thin bottom-line profitability, with PAT margin at 0.5% in Q1FY27 and 2.5% for FY26.
  • Rising borrowings and lease liabilities, alongside growing inventories and declining cash balances.
  • Exposure to prior-quarter losses and exceptional items, as evidenced by the Q4FY26 loss and FY26 exceptional items.
  • Dependence on private labels for a majority of revenue (62%), and on apparel (87% of the mix).
  • Concentration in Eastern India and West Bengal, despite expansion across 10 states.
  • Sensitivity to the performance of the Indian economy, competition, and the company’s ability to execute its expansion strategy, as noted in the safe harbour statement.
  • Organisational changes in senior management, including the re-designation of the Chief Marketing Officer.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Store expansion. Management expresses an ambition to reach 500 stores. Observed store count was 276 as of June 30, 2026, against 282 stores referenced in a separate announcement. The company has grown its footprint across 10 states and 198 cities.

Growth and profitability. Management states it continues to drive sustainable growth. Observed performance shows 29% year-on-year revenue growth in Q1FY27, but with margin contraction at the gross profit and EBITDA levels, a 0.5% PAT margin, and a decline in pre-INDAS Profit Before Tax of 29% year-on-year.

Private label strategy. Management describes a strategic focus on building brand visibility through attractive pricing, with plans to shift to fair pricing once brand recall is established. Observed private label contribution was 62% of revenue in Q1FY27, with a four-year revenue CAGR of approximately 64%.

Brand positioning. Management describes strengthening its position as a trusted fashion destination across real Bharat. Observed recognition includes a Guinness World Record title and industry awards in 2025.

Corporate actions. The 13th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Friday, September 25, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. IST via video conferencing, with a cut-off date of Friday, September 18, 2026. Agenda items include adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements, re-appointment of Mr. Shreyans Surana and Mr. Rohit Kedia as directors, and appointment of Mr. Harish Chander Khurana as an Independent Director for a first term of five consecutive years. The Board also reconstituted the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, appointed Mr. Harish Chander Khurana as an Additional Director (Non-Executive Independent Director) for five years, designated Mr. Shreyans Surana additionally as Chairman while Mr. Rohit Kedia continues as Whole-Time Director, and resolved to withdraw pending legal cases (T.S. 1020 of 2026 and T.S. 1015 of 2026) against Cupid Limited & Ors. to pursue an amicable resolution.
