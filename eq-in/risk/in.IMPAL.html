IMPAL: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Sequential earnings pressure. Comparing the quarter ended 30 June 2026 with the quarter ended 31 March 2026, revenue from operations rose from Rs 210.72 crores to Rs 234.29 crores, but profit before tax fell from Rs 34.46 crores to Rs 27.44 crores and profit after tax fell from Rs 33.46 crores to Rs 21.70 crores. Total expenses rose from Rs 193.98 crores to Rs 216.52 crores over the same comparison.

Tax charge volatility. The tax expense for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was Rs 5.74 crores, including a deferred tax charge of Rs 3.83 crores, against a total tax expense of Rs 1.00 crore in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. The year ended 31 March 2026 also included a deferred tax charge of Rs 3.83 crores and a tax provision relating to earlier years of Rs 3.83 crores.

Other comprehensive income drag. Other comprehensive income was negative Rs 26.48 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, driven by a change in fair value of equity instruments of negative Rs 376.95 crores and a re-measurement loss on defined benefit plans of Rs 0.20 crores, partly offset by an income tax effect of Rs 53.85 crores. This compares with positive other comprehensive income of Rs 292.40 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and negative Rs 322.89 crores in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Total comprehensive income was Rs 157.43 crores for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, versus Rs 312.81 crores a year earlier.

Reserves movement. Reserves (excluding revaluation reserves) fell from Rs 2,295.69 crores as at 31 March 2026 to Rs 1,791.13 crores as at 30 June 2026.

Shareholder dissent on the related-party resolution. At the 72nd AGM, the resolution approving material related party transactions with M/s. Brakes India Private Limited up to an aggregate value of Rs 500 crores recorded 49 members abstaining on 74,83,880 shares and 9 members whose votes were declared invalid on 2,22,875 shares, alongside 2 votes against. The other three resolutions saw no abstentions and no invalid votes.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Year-on-year revenue growth. Revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was Rs 234.29 crores, up from Rs 196.69 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Total income rose from Rs 208.66 crores to Rs 243.96 crores over the same period.

Year-on-year profitability. Profit before tax improved to Rs 27.44 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from Rs 25.67 crores a year earlier, and profit after tax improved to Rs 21.70 crores from Rs 20.41 crores. Earnings per share rose to Rs 17.38 from Rs 16.35.

Full-year scale. For the year ended 31 March 2026, revenue from operations was Rs 837.11 crores, total income Rs 889.54 crores, profit before tax Rs 114.45 crores, profit after tax Rs 96.55 crores, and earnings per share Rs 77.36.

Approved related-party transaction envelope. Shareholders passed the ordinary resolution permitting material related party transactions with M/s. Brakes India Private Limited up to an aggregate value of Rs 500 crores, with 4,03,252 shares voted in favour (99.9995%) and 2 shares against.

Dividend. The 72nd AGM passed the ordinary resolution for declaration of a final dividend for the financial year 2025-26, with 81,10,007 shares voted in favour and 2 against.

Governance continuity. All four resolutions set out in the 72nd AGM Notice dated 8 May 2026 were passed with the requisite majority, including adoption of the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, re-appointment of Sri Srivats Ram (DIN: 00063415) as a director liable to retire by rotation, the dividend declaration, and the related-party transaction approval.

3. Key Risks

Concentration in a single segment. The company operates in only one segment, “Sale and Distribution of Automotive spares,” as stated in the notes to the financial results.

Exposure to fair value movements in equity instruments. The change in fair value of equity instruments swung from positive Rs 342.19 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 to negative Rs 376.95 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and was negative Rs 49.48 crores in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. These movements flow through other comprehensive income and drove the decline in reserves between 31 March 2026 and 30 June 2026.

Related-party dependence. The Brakes India Private Limited transaction approval covers an aggregate value of up to Rs 500 crores, a significant amount relative to the company’s annual revenue of Rs 837.11 crores for the year ended 31 March 2026. The abstentions and invalid votes on this resolution indicate shareholder scrutiny of the arrangement.

Inventory and working capital sensitivity. Changes in inventories of finished goods and work-in-progress were negative Rs 0.67 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, negative Rs 0.77 crores in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, and negative Rs 2.71 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2025, while purchase of stock-in-trade was Rs 197.63 crores, Rs 176.33 crores, and Rs 168.92 crores respectively.

Tax and deferred tax variability. Deferred tax was a charge of Rs 3.83 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and in the year ended 31 March 2026, and a charge of Rs 0.20 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2025, contributing to volatility in the effective tax rate.
