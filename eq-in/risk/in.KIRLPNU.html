Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

On August 3, 2026, KIRLPNU completed the acquisition of 99.49% of the total voting power in Kirloskar South East Asia Co. Limited (KSEA), making KSEA a subsidiary with effect from that date. The total consideration was 1,70,52,750 THB paid in cash, equivalent to Rs. 4,94,52,975, pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement. KSEA, incorporated on March 31, 2016, is engaged in trading of compressors across the South-East Asian market and has its registered office in Bangkok, Thailand. Its turnover for the calendar year ended December 31, 2025 was THB 30.91 million (Rs. 9.24 crores) and net worth was THB 22.65 million (Rs. 6.77 crores), against share capital of THB 20,00,000.

The company stated that establishing operations in Thailand through this subsidiary aims to strengthen its presence in the South-East Asian region, enhance customer engagement, and provide faster sales and after-sales support. A local presence is expected to enable more effective response to customer requirements, improve market coverage, and build stronger relationships with channel partners and end users. The acquisition followed earlier disclosures dated July 21, 2026 and July 31, 2026.
