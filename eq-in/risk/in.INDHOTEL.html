Broker Narrative

Early reports emphasized structural growth tailwinds—strong demand outpacing supply, G-20/Cricket World Cup events, FTA revival, upgraded AHVAAN targets, and an asset-light expansion pipeline. Over time, the narrative shifted toward near-term cyclical headwinds (geopolitical disruptions and domestic demand slowdown) while still citing pipeline and RevPAR growth as offsets, but realized returns turned sharply negative as enthusiasm faded.

Fears that came true

  • Geopolitical demand disruptions materialized as the key headwind flagged in the last report, correlating with multiple DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes from late 2024 onward.
  • Domestic demand slowdown flagged in the last report aligns with the persistent negative actual returns seen across 2025–2026 reports.

Optimism that failed

  • The AHVAAN 2025 target of 500+ AMA properties did not materialize, as management itself flagged the target miss, indicating growth fell short of early optimism.
  • High single-digit industry RevPAR growth and strong demand tailwinds from G-20 events, Cricket World Cup, and FTA revival failed to sustain, as actual returns turned negative and the last report cited only high single-digit RevPAR growth rather than the earlier double-digit demand outperformance.
  • The robust 7,094-room expansion pipeline targeting ~Rs150bn enterprise revenue and asset-light margin improvement did not translate into positive returns, with many Buy-rated reports post-2024 yielding DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes.
