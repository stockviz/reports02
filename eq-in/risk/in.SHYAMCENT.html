Shyam Century Ferrous Limited — Summary of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Cessation of manufacturing operations: The company ceased production at its manufacturing facility at EPIP, Rajabagan, Byrnihat, District Ri-Bhoi, Meghalaya, with effect from 7th May 2025, following a strategic decision to discontinue its manufacturing business, which was its sole operating segment. The closure is attributed to a significant increase in power tariffs that made operations economically unviable.
  • No active business operations: The company is currently not engaged in active business operations, resulting in limited revenue generation, asset maintenance concerns, regulatory compliance issues, and the need to identify suitable future business opportunities.
  • Industry-wide pressures: High power costs, volatile raw material and freight prices, and fierce competition from subsidized or low-cost international producers such as those in Bhutan and China.
  • Legal and regulatory claims: A demand notice of Rs 1,739 lakhs from the Director of Mineral Resources, Meghalaya, for alleged illegal coal procurement pursuant to an NGT order.
  • Losses: The company incurred losses in the quarter ended 30th June 2026 and in the previous financial year 2025-26, primarily due to the discontinuation of core manufacturing operations. For the quarter ended 30th June 2026, the net loss from discontinued operations was Rs 80.95 lakhs; for the year ended 31st March 2026, the net loss from discontinued operations was Rs 916.59 lakhs.
  • No dividend: The Board has not recommended any dividend for the 2025-26 financial year due to suspension of operations and net losses.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Asset disposal proceeds and redeployment: Following shareholder approval on 9th February 2026 for disposal of the whole or substantially the whole of the plant, machinery, and undertakings, the company is exploring strategic alternatives for optimal utilization of proceeds, including business restructuring, diversification into new business opportunities, and other initiatives to enhance shareholder value.
  • Investment portfolio income: The company maintains a substantial investment portfolio (current and non-current investments) that generates interest income, classified as “Other Income.” Other Income was Rs 246.58 lakhs in the quarter ended 30th June 2026, Rs 162.41 lakhs in the quarter ended 31st March 2026, Rs 198.67 lakhs in the quarter ended 30th June 2025, and Rs 837.58 lakhs for the year ended 31st March 2026.
  • Positive net worth and liquidity: The company has a positive net worth and sufficient liquid assets (investments) to meet its liabilities as and when they fall due.
  • Industry outlook: The medium-term outlook for the Ferro Silicon industry is considered cautiously optimistic, supported by planned expansion in steel-making capacities, infrastructure development initiatives, and continued industrial growth.
  • Diversification intent: Management states the company is actively evaluating opportunities across businesses that complement strategic capabilities to build a diversified and resilient platform, while assessing strategic alternatives for optimizing its asset portfolio.

3. Key Risks

  • Going concern and operational continuity: Despite discontinuation of manufacturing operations, the company continues to exist as a going concern. Management confirms no material uncertainty exists regarding the ability to continue as a going concern for at least twelve months from the balance sheet date, based on cash flow projections, the value of the investment portfolio, and expected proceeds from the sale of assets held for sale.
  • Asset disposal execution: The company is in the process of disposing of assets classified as Assets Held for Sale and has continued to sell such assets in a phased manner. The manufacturing assets were classified as “Non-current assets held for sale” under Ind AS 105 with effect from 9th February 2026.
  • Legal exposure: The Rs 1,739 lakhs demand notice from the Director of Mineral Resources, Meghalaya, remains a significant claim.
  • Dependence on investment income: With no active operations, the company’s income is currently reliant on interest income from its investment portfolio and proceeds from asset sales.
  • No separate reportable segments: As per Ind AS 108, there are no separate reportable segments; revenue from operations consists only of sale of raw materials, classified under discontinued operations.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on going concern: Management confirms that despite losses primarily due to discontinuation of core manufacturing operations, no material uncertainty exists regarding the company’s ability to continue as a going concern for at least twelve months from the balance sheet date, citing a sufficient investment portfolio and liquid assets. The auditors’ review report states their conclusion is not modified in respect of this matter.
  • Guidance on future business: Management states the company is looking for new business opportunities and has no intention to liquidate as a business entity. It is actively evaluating opportunities across businesses that complement strategic capabilities to build a diversified and resilient platform, while assessing strategic alternatives for optimizing its asset portfolio.
  • Observed performance versus guidance: For the quarter ended 30th June 2026, revenue from operations was Rs 32.88 lakhs (versus Rs 1,419.46 lakhs in the quarter ended 30th June 2025), total income was Rs 279.46 lakhs (versus Rs 1,618.13 lakhs), and net loss from discontinued operations was Rs 80.95 lakhs (versus Rs 402.80 lakhs). For the year ended 31st March 2026, revenue from operations was Rs 2,240.21 lakhs, total income was Rs 3,077.79 lakhs, and net loss from discontinued operations was Rs 916.59 lakhs. Total comprehensive income for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 was Rs 95.44 lakhs, supported by other comprehensive income of Rs 176.39 lakhs.
  • Forward-looking statement caveat: The company undertakes no obligation to publicly update or revise any forward-looking statements.
