J.G. Chemicals Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material price volatility and sourcing: Sharp volatility in LME zinc prices is a material headwind. Procurement of zinc scrap is challenging due to limited domestic availability from major steel companies, requiring sourcing from across the globe.
  • Supply chain and geopolitical disruption: Geopolitical and supply chain disruptions affect raw material availability and logistics costs.
  • Competitive pressure: Competition from low-cost unorganised manufacturers remains a challenge.
  • Demand and trade environment: Potential weakness in global industrial demand and global tariff and trade policy uncertainty affecting export-oriented customers.
  • Regulatory and compliance burden: Increasing environmental and compliance requirements. Customer approvals involve a long-drawn process averaging 4 to 5 years minimum, and securing stringent regulatory approvals such as IATF, WHO GMP, and various pharmacopoeia licenses requires significant capital expenditure and adherence to strict manufacturing norms.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Capacity expansion: The Greenfield expansion in Dahej, Gujarat expands the company’s western India presence, with a 40,000+ MTPA capacity addition expected to be commissioned in Q3 FY27. Brownfield debottlenecking at Naidupeta is aimed at increasing output and is expected to be completed by December 2026.
  • Portfolio mix shift: Expansion into high-value zinc oxide applications (electronics, healthcare, energy storage) and higher-margin non-rubber applications, with increasing contributions from pharmaceuticals, agriculture, and electronics. Ramp-up of the Zinc Sulphate business and specialized zinc derivatives.
  • Demand drivers: Growing demand for zinc sulphate and agricultural micronutrients.
  • Structural opportunities: China+1 and import substitution opportunities, and rising quality standards favoring organized and compliant producers.

3. Key Risks

  • Volatility in LME zinc prices.
  • Geopolitical and supply chain disruptions impacting raw material availability and logistics costs.
  • Competition from low-cost unorganised manufacturers.
  • Weakness in global industrial demand.
  • Global tariff and trade policy uncertainty affecting export-oriented customers.
  • Increasing environmental and compliance requirements.
  • Limited domestic availability of zinc scrap, necessitating global sourcing.
  • Long customer approval cycles (averaging 4 to 5 years minimum) and stringent regulatory approval requirements (IATF, WHO GMP, pharmacopoeia licenses) requiring significant capital expenditure.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

Guidance: - Installed capacity to scale from approximately 77,000 MTPA to over 122,000 MTPA by FY29 through the Dahej greenfield facility, Naidupeta debottlenecking, and portfolio expansion. - Phase 1 at Dahej targeted for commissioning in Q3 FY27, with estimated revenue potential of ₹9,000 mn at full utilization and break-even at 30–35% utilization. - Naidupeta debottlenecking expected to be completed by December 2026. - Non-rubber revenue targeted to climb from roughly 15% toward 30% over the next three to four years. - EBITDA margins expected to rise toward the mid-teens. - Management expects to commission the Dahej greenfield facility and the Naidupeta brownfield project in Q3 FY27 and to continue the strong performance momentum going forward.

Observed performance: - FY26: highest-ever financial performance with revenue of ₹9,729 million, EBITDA of ₹979 million, and profit after tax of ₹686 million, backed by a zero-debt balance sheet and net cash of ₹1,538.67 million. - Q1-FY27: revenue from operations of ₹3,157 Mn with a PAT of ₹261 Mn. - The company is India’s largest zinc oxide manufacturer with an approximate 31% market share and ranks among the top five global manufacturers, operating three plants with a combined capacity of 77,000 MTPA. It serves over 200 domestic and 50 global customers, counting 9 out of the top 10 global and top 11 domestic tyre manufacturers as customers.
