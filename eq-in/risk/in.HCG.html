HCG — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • North Bangalore start-up losses: The new North Bangalore (Hebbal) hospital, commissioned in May 2026, generated a loss of INR 70 Mn in Q1 FY27. This loss was excluded from Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter. Management notes the hospital’s contribution depends on insurance empanelment progressing and newly onboarded doctors ramping up their practices.
  • Case-mix pressure: Revenue quality was partly offset by changes in case mix, including a lower contribution from high-value, low-margin therapies. ARPP growth of 2% YoY was driven by a favourable payor mix but partly offset by these case-mix changes.
  • Regional moderation: Growth moderated in certain regional markets such as Gujarat.
  • One-time/provision items: An EPCG-related provision of INR 45.9 Mn was recorded.
  • Impairments and accounting impacts: Impairment losses on investments and carrying values of subsidiaries (such as BACC and HCG Kolkata Cancer Care LLP) were noted. Changes in wage definitions under new Labour Codes are expected to lead to incremental impacts on employee benefits and gratuity obligations. Potential risks related to recoverability of trade receivables or tax losses were also flagged.
  • Sequential PAT decline: Adjusted PAT fell 59.6% QoQ (INR 341 Mn in Q4 FY26 to INR 138 Mn in Q1 FY27), though the Q4 FY26 figure was normalized for a one-time goodwill impairment in the fertility business.
  • General risk factors: Macroeconomic conditions, changes in government policies and pricing regulations, evolving competitive dynamics, delays in project execution, challenges in obtaining regulatory approvals, availability of skilled human resources and capital, and potential disruptions in partnership or acquisition arrangements.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong Q1 FY27 performance: Revenue grew 13% YoY to INR 6,951 Mn; Adjusted EBITDA grew 20% YoY to INR 1,339 Mn with margins improving to 19.4% (up 121 bps YoY). Volume growth was 11% YoY.
  • Broad-based network growth: Excluding North Bangalore, 16 of 25 centres recorded their highest-ever quarterly revenues.
  • Revenue mix improvement: Non-institutional revenue grew 17% YoY, with its contribution rising from 67% to 69%.
  • New North Bangalore facility: Early traction with 550+ new patient registrations and 300+ admissions since launch; the MR Linac was commissioned in July 2026, making the centre home to one of India’s most advanced adaptive radiation therapy platforms.
  • Capacity expansion: 121 operational beds added during the quarter (56 at North Bangalore, 26 at Ranchi, 19 at Borivali, 8 at Nashik, 5 at Hubli, 7 at Kenya). Rajkot hospital became a Comprehensive Cancer Care Centre following commissioning of a new LINAC. Robotic surgery capabilities expanded with two high-end surgical robotic systems (new installation at Nashik, replacement robot at Bangalore CoE).
  • International business: Revenue of INR 208 Mn, up ~9% YoY, driven by stronger patient inflows for radiation oncology and PET cases.
  • Structural demand drivers: Rising cancer incidence, improving diagnosis and awareness, wider access to advanced treatments, aging demographics, and increased health insurance penetration.
  • Strategic initiatives: Scaling advanced precision oncology and genomics capabilities; increasing cash and TPA business mix; new greenfield projects in the pipeline; further investment of up to INR 16 Crore in HCG Rajkot Hospitals LLP; acquisition of additional 34% equity in VHCRPL for INR 15,450 lakhs; divestment of BACC Healthcare; and implementation of the HCG Employee Stock Option Scheme (HCG ESOS 2026).
  • Balance sheet strengthening: Completion of a rights issue of approximately INR 425 crore to support expansion.

3. Key Risks

  • Start-up losses at new facilities, particularly North Bangalore, until empanelment and doctor ramp-up materialize.
  • Execution risk on the large bed expansion program (delays in project execution, regulatory approvals).
  • Impairment risk on investments and carrying values of subsidiaries.
  • Incremental employee benefit and gratuity obligations from new Labour Code wage definitions.
  • Recoverability risk on trade receivables and tax losses.
  • Policy and pricing regulation changes, competitive dynamics, and availability of skilled human resources and capital.
  • Potential disruptions in partnership or acquisition arrangements.
  • Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties that are difficult to predict.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - Target of adding 1,000 beds by FY30, reaching an estimated total of ~3,670 operational beds by FY30, through approximately 188 bed additions in FY27, ~520 in FY28 and FY29, and ~230 in FY30, backed by capital expenditures across those periods. - Expansion led by 60% brownfield expansion. - Focus on strengthening quality of growth by expanding patient access, increasing adoption of advanced therapies, improving network utilization, and pursuing disciplined brownfield and greenfield expansion. - North Bangalore expected to contribute meaningfully as insurance empanelment progresses and onboarded doctors ramp up.

Observed performance against guidance: - Q1 FY27 bed additions of 121 are consistent with the FY27 target of approximately 188 beds, with the majority of the annual target yet to be delivered in the remaining three quarters. - North Bangalore contributed INR 67 Mn in Q1 FY27 (from May 2026 commencement), with the guided ramp-up still dependent on empanelment and doctor onboarding. - FY 2025-26 delivered consolidated revenue from operations of INR 25,384.29 million, a 14.44% YoY increase, with an improved EBITDA margin of 18.30%; Q1 FY27 revenue growth of 13% YoY and margin of 19.4% are broadly in line with this trajectory. - The rights issue of approximately INR 425 crore was completed, supporting the guided expansion program. - The fertility business was divested in June 2026 (Q1 FY27 revenue of INR 169 Mn and EBITDA of INR 23 Mn), and the divestment of BACC Healthcare Private Limited was completed, consistent with portfolio rationalization.

Additional investor-relevant facts: - For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, standalone total income was Rs. 38,622 lakhs with net profit of Rs. 558 lakhs; consolidated total income was Rs. 70,723 lakhs with net profit of Rs. 1,646 lakhs. Statutory auditor reports were unqualified. - The 28th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Friday, September 18, 2026, at 05:00 P.M. IST via VC/OAVM; the cut-off date for e-voting and participation is September 11, 2026; remote e-voting runs from September 15, 2026 (9:00 A.M. IST) to September 17, 2026 (5:00 P.M. Broker Narrative

The narrative moved from a cautious Add, focused on near-term hospital/diagnostic headwinds (muted occupancy, tender/contract losses, margin pressure from expansion), to a high-conviction Buy centred on HCG’s core oncology franchise, large capex, KKR’s 54% stake and a 25% long-term EBITDA margin aspiration. Persistent themes were volume-led hospital growth, network expansion and margin improvement from payer mix/ancillaries. What changed was the removal of diagnostic/other businesses from the story (fertility divested) and the shift from short-term concerns to a multi-year capacity/ownership/margin narrative.
