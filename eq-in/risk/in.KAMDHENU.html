Kamdhenu Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • West Asia conflict cost pressure: Q1 FY27 PBT included an additional ~₹1.76 crore of legal expenses and ~₹3 crore of fuel costs attributed to the impact of the West Asia war. Excluding these, PBT growth would have been 44% YoY instead of the reported 27%. Management noted fuel prices are subsequently normalizing.
  • Rising cost lines: Employee expenses rose to ₹15.7 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹13.0 crore in Q1 FY26; other expenses rose to ₹34.6 crore from ₹29.5 crore; finance cost rose to ₹0.2 crore from ₹0.0 crore; depreciation rose to ₹1.8 crore from ₹1.3 crore.
  • Working capital swing: Changes in working capital were negative ₹9.9 crore in FY26 versus a positive ₹10.7 crore in FY25, contributing to cash generated from operations falling to ₹92.8 crore from ₹87.0 crore and net cash from operating activities declining to ₹66.0 crore from ₹68.9 crore.
  • Cash decline: Cash and cash equivalents fell to ₹27.8 crore at end-FY26 from ₹35.2 crore at end-FY25, with net decrease in cash of ₹7.4 crore for FY26.
  • Southern India gap: Management identified Southern India as a key strategic focus area, indicating the company’s branded market share there offers significant room for expansion.
  • Legal expenses: The ~₹1.76 crore of legal expenses in Q1 FY27 indicates ongoing legal costs, though no specific matter is described.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record Q1 FY27 performance: Highest ever royalty income, franchisee volumes, PBT and PAT in Q1 FY27. Revenue rose 9% YoY to ₹213.3 crore; PBT grew 27% YoY to ₹36.4 crore; PAT grew 34% YoY to ₹28.7 crore.
  • Royalty-led franchise model: Royalty income grew 16% YoY to ₹48.1 crore; franchisee volumes rose to 10.3 lakh MT from 9.4 lakh MT. FY26 royalty income was ~₹175 crore, with FY26 volume sold of 39 lakh MT.
  • Own facilities growth: Revenue from own facilities rose 7% to ₹165.2 crore; volumes rose 6% to 31.9 thousand MT.
  • Margin expansion: Gross profit margin improved 80 bps to 33.4%; PBT margin expanded 250 bps to 17.1%; PAT margin expanded 250 bps to 13.4%.
  • Solar power plant: Commissioned a 5 MWp captive solar plant in Phalodi District, Rajasthan in June 2026, expected to generate annual savings of ₹4–5 crore and improve energy reliability.
  • Government infrastructure push: Management cited continued government focus on infrastructure, urbanisation and manufacturing, plus sustained investments in roads, railways and urban transit, as supporting healthy steel demand.
  • Construction demand: Management expects rising investments in commercial, residential and industrial construction to support steel industry growth in FY27.
  • Asset-light scalability: The franchisee model (100+ franchisees, 12,500+ dealers, 500+ distributors, 4 Mn MT franchisee capacity) is described as capital-efficient and scalable, with benefits including lower transport cost, quick delivery, and pan-India availability.
  • Return ratios: Core steel business ROCE improved to 34% (Q1 FY27 annualized) from 27% (FY26); ROE improved to 27% from 20%. Debt is Nil.
  • Dividend: Board recommended ₹0.40 per share dividend (40% of face value) for FY26, subject to shareholder approval.
  • Brand strength: Brand sales turnover of ~₹23,000 crore in FY26; company described as market leader in branded TMT bars in the retail segment.

3. Key Risks

  • Commodity and input cost exposure: Raw material cost was ₹142.0 crore in Q1 FY27 versus ₹132.0 crore in Q1 FY26, the largest expense line, exposing margins to steel/input price volatility.
  • Franchisee model dependence: Revenue and royalty income depend on franchisee volumes and the franchise network’s performance; any disruption to franchisee operations or compliance could affect royalty income.
  • Geopolitical and fuel risk: The West Asia war impact on fuel costs demonstrates exposure to external geopolitical events; management noted prices are subsequently normalizing but such events can recur.
  • Legal and regulatory costs: Legal expenses of ~₹1.76 crore in Q1 FY27 indicate potential ongoing legal exposure.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The safe harbor notes actual results could differ materially due to risks including Indian and international economic performance, industry performance, competition, strategy implementation, growth and expansion, technological changes, revenue/income/cash flow changes, and market risk exposure.
  • Investment valuation volatility: Other income included unrealized and realized gain/(loss) on investments measured at fair value through profit or loss: ₹1,590.66 lakh in Q1 FY27, negative ₹1,137.81 lakh in Q4 FY26, ₹698.9 lakh in Q1 FY26, and ₹90.02 lakh in FY26 — indicating earnings sensitivity to mark-to-market movements.
  • Other comprehensive income volatility: OCI swung from positive ₹63.02 lakh in Q1 FY26 to negative ₹496.97 lakh in Q4 FY26, driven largely by fair value changes in equity instruments.
  • Single segment concentration: The company operates in one reportable segment (manufacturing and marketing of steel products), offering no diversification across business lines.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Management commentary (Q1 FY27): Mr. Satish Kumar Agarwal stated the company began FY27 on a healthy note with strong execution and resilience of the branded business model; noted Southern India as a key strategic focus; highlighted the solar plant’s expected ₹4–5 crore annual savings; and expressed optimism about steel industry growth in FY27 on the back of rising construction investments.
  • Observed performance versus commentary: Reported Q1 FY27 results align with the stated healthy start — revenue +9% YoY, PBT +27% YoY, PAT +34% YoY, royalty income +16% YoY, franchisee volumes +9% YoY, own facility volumes +6% YoY, and margin expansion of 250 bps at both PBT and PAT levels. The solar plant was commissioned in June 2026 as stated, with savings described as expected (not yet realized in reported figures). The Southern India expansion is described as a strategic focus and opportunity, with no specific targets or timelines provided.
  • Forward-looking statements: Management provided qualitative guidance on FY27 steel demand supported by government infrastructure focus, urbanisation, manufacturing, and construction investments; expected annual solar savings of ₹4–5 crore; and described Kamdhenu as well positioned to capitalize on opportunities. No specific numerical revenue, volume, or earnings guidance was provided for FY27.
  • Dividend guidance: Board recommended ₹0.40 per share final dividend for FY26, subject to shareholder approval, with record date fixed as 18th September, 2026.
  • AGM: 32nd AGM scheduled for Friday, 25th September, 2026, at 12:00 Noon IST through Video Conferencing/Other Audio Visual Means, with proposed resolutions including re-appointment of Shri Satish Kumar Agarwal as Director, Smt. Pravin Tripathi as Independent Director, and approval of related party remuneration for Smt. Vipil Agarwal.
  • Share transfer requests: For the period 01.08.2026 to 31.08.2026, the company received Nil requests for re-lodgment of physical share transfer requests under the special window provided by the SEBI circular dated January 30, 2026.
