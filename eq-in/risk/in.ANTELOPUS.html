Antelopus Selan Energy Limited — Q1 FY’27 Update

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Custody transfer timing at IOCL: Custody transfer to IOCL ran for only ~86–87 days in the quarter, resulting in a build-up of c. 6,500 bopd of crude inventory. This is described as a once-a-year timing issue expected to normalize next quarter.
  • Flat reported sales: Average sales stayed flat at c. 1,705 boepd in Q1 FY’27 versus 1,758 boepd in Q4 FY’26, once the inventory build-up is taken into account.
  • Karjisan production decline: Total sales at Karjisan fell to 834 boepd in Q1 FY’27 from 991 boepd in Q4 FY’26.
  • Cambay reservoir uncertainty: The C-78 well in the eastern flank flowed sub-commercial hydrocarbons at surface, potentially induced by depletion from nearby wells. Western flank wells are delivering incremental oil production, pointing to further upside potential in that area.
  • Duarmara injectivity issue: An injectivity test at DMR#4z points to minimal communication between the well bore and the TS-1 reservoir, potentially due to near well bore damage. TS-3 and TS-2 showed gas influx, but lower than discovery wells.
  • Regulatory and approval dependencies: The FDP for 7 new wells at Karjisan is in the final stages of approval. The 2 onshore licenses won in DSF Bid Round IV (Cambay and KG basin) are awaiting formal award. Cambay drilling is subject to joint venture approvals.
  • Mature field and infrastructure constraints: Karjisan surface facilities are being scaled up, with crude oil storage and produced water handling being augmented to manage higher production and water expected in the field.
  • External risk factors: Exposure to global crude oil and natural gas price volatility, regulatory and approval complexities, mature field operational challenges such as declining reservoir pressure and water handling, pipeline and storage infrastructure constraints, environmental, health and safety expectations, community engagement and land access challenges, and customer concentration risk with IOCL as the anchor buyer.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Drilling ahead of schedule at Bakrol: 9 of 10 planned FDP wells have been drilled so far, with strong execution pace and efficiency. A continuous frac campaign is set to commence early August, with full productivity expected to flow through in Q2. Material production uplift is anticipated post-frac as new wells reach full potential. New wells are performing as per expectations, with production settling in line with plan post Q4 flash production.
  • Bakrol resource potential: The block is divided into 9 aerial segments. Phase 1 (2023 campaign) drilled 6 wells in the Eastern areas, increasing mapped in-place volumes and de-risking Eastern segments. Phase 2 (current 10-well campaign) is maximizing infill drilling potential identified in Phase 1 and extending into Northern segments, partially de-risked by BK-40. Even post Phase 2, a large portion of the block remains undrilled, implying potential to increase well density across the acreage.
  • Karjisan development: Phase 1 (6-well campaign in 2023) targeted the south eastern fault block. Phase 2 (4-well campaign in 2025) targeted 2 new infill locations and opened 2 more fault blocks towards the south western side. A 7-well FDP has been submitted to the regulator for drilling infill wells across the south western fault blocks.
  • Cambay work program: Committed work program under the extension FDP and farm-in agreement covers drilling of 3 wells and reviving sick wells where feasible. One new well (C-78) was drilled in the eastern flank and multiple wells were revived in the Western flank and central high. The next well is planned for H2 FY’27, targeting the deeper Eocene reservoir in the Western/Central part of the block.
  • Duarmara forward plan: Re-perforation and reservoir potential testing at TS-1 and TS-2 zones in all accessible wells (including DMR#1 and 2) through workovers, post monsoon. TS-1 reservoir zone flowed light oil (up to 37° API).
  • New license wins: Won 2 highly contested onshore licenses in DSF Bid Round IV, in Cambay and KG basin, awaiting formal award.
  • Credit rating: In June 2026, obtained a credit rating of IND A / Stable / IND A1 from India Ratings.
  • Cess refund: Commissioner (Appeals) allowed the Company’s refund claim for excess Cess paid in FY21–FY23, of c. ₹6.56 Cr., which was earlier expensed. To be recognized upon receipt of final order.
  • Financial performance: EBITDA at c. ₹93.2 Cr. (c. +57% q-o-q) with EBITDA margin at c. 70%; Profit After Tax at ₹53.8 Cr, up c. +42% q-o-q. Incremental revenue is described as a clean EBITDA flow-through. Oil and gas product mix is c. 80% oil and c. 20% gas.
  • Broader tailwinds: India’s growing energy demand and rapid urbanization, government policy support for domestic hydrocarbon production and regulatory reforms like the Oilfields (Regulation and Development) Amendment Act, an expanded asset portfolio following the merger spanning nine contract areas across five hydrocarbon basins, opportunities for operational synergies, infrastructure optimization, and enhanced recovery across mature fields like Bakrol and Karjisan, and strong growth in domestic natural gas consumption supported by expanding pipeline infrastructure.

3. Key Risks

  • Exposure to global crude oil and natural gas price volatility.
  • Regulatory and approval complexities for exploration and drilling activities.
  • Mature field operational challenges such as declining reservoir pressure and water handling.
  • Infrastructure constraints related to pipelines and storage capacity.
  • Increasingly stringent environmental, health and safety expectations.
  • Community engagement and land access challenges around operating areas.
  • Customer concentration risk with Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) as the anchor buyer.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Production guidance: Management’s guidance of reaching 2,500 boepd in FY’27 remains intact, with the impact from frac expected to be seen from Q2 onwards. Observed Q1 FY’27 average sales were c. 1,705 boepd, flat versus Q4 FY’26 at 1,758 boepd after accounting for the c. 6,500 boepd inventory build-up.
  • Drilling guidance: The FY 2027 plan includes drilling the remaining eight wells under the approved 10-well FDP at Bakrol, executing a seven-well drilling campaign at Karjisan, drilling remaining committed wells at Cambay subject to joint venture approvals, bringing required gas evacuation capacity online, addressing evacuation constraints at Dangeru for a frac-led production ramp-up, and progressing re-perforation and oil commerciality at Duarmara. Observed progress: 9 of 10 planned FDP wells at Bakrol have been drilled so far, ahead of schedule; the frac campaign is set to commence early August; the 7-well FDP at Karjisan is in final stages of approval; and the next Cambay well is planned for H2 FY’27.
  • Financial performance: Q1 FY’27 revenue from operations was ₹133.1 Cr. (net ₹131.0 Cr. after profit petroleum paid to GOI of ₹2.1 Cr.), total income ₹132.2 Cr., EBITDA ₹93.2 Cr., and net profit ₹54.3 Cr. (total comprehensive income ₹53.8 Cr.). Q4 FY’26 comparatives: revenue from operations ₹103.9 Cr. (net ₹102.0 Cr.), total income ₹103.7 Cr., EBITDA ₹59.4 Cr., and net profit ₹38.1 Cr.
  • Long-term objective: To build a leading independent upstream energy company in India through operational excellence, responsible growth, and disciplined execution.
  • Corporate action: The Board on July 27, 2026 approved a request for reclassification of Ms. Payal Upadhyay from ‘Promoter Group Shareholder’ to ‘Public Shareholder’ under Regulation 31A of SEBI LODR, subject to requisite approvals from stock exchanges and shareholders.
