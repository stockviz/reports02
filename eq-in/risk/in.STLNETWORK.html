  • The incorporation was approved by the Authorisation and Allotment Committee with a paid-up capital of Rs. 1,00,000 comprising 50,000 Equity Shares of face value INR 2 per share, funded entirely through cash consideration for 100% shareholding control.

The objects include repayment/servicing of financial facilities (original allocation ₹81 Crores, with ₹25 Crores utilized till June 30, 2026) and general corporate purposes (original allocation ₹27 Crores, nil utilized).
  • The company confirmed no deviation or variation in utilization of preferential issue proceeds and nil deviation for non-convertible debenture proceeds for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • The 5th Annual General Meeting was held on September 8, 2026, where all five resolutions were passed, including appointment of Mr. Chandrasekhara Rao Battula as Whole-Time Director and Interim CEO, and re-appointment of Mr. Pravin Agarwal as director.
