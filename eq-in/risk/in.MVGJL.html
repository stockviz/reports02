Headwinds and Challenges

  • GST order on IPO-related input tax credit: On June 22, 2026, the company received an order from the Office of the Principal Commissioner of Central Tax, Visakhapatnam, Central GST Commissionerate, concerning the availment and utilization of Input Tax Credit accrued on Initial Public Offer expenses for the period 2021-22 to 2024-25.
  • The order confirms tax demands and equivalent penalties:
    • For FY 2021-22 to 2023-24 under Section 74(1) of the CGST Act, 2017: tax demand of Rs. 2,84,97,628 (IGST: Rs. 2,20,50,300; CGST: Rs. 32,23,664; SGST: Rs. 32,23,664) and an equivalent penalty of Rs. 2,84,97,628.
    • For FY 2024-25 under Section 74A of the CGST Act, 2017: tax demand of Rs. 7,12,832 (IGST: Rs. 7,10,694; CGST: Rs. 1,069; SGST: Rs. 1,069) and an equivalent penalty of Rs. 7,12,832.
  • The alleged violation is irregular availment and utilization of ITC amounting to Rs. 2,92,10,460 accrued on IPO expenses from December 2021 to March 2025, treated as an exempt supply transaction in securities under Section 17(3) of the CGST Act, 2017.
  • The total monetary impact confirmed in the order is Rs. 5,84,20,920, comprising a cumulative tax demand of Rs. 2,92,10,460 and a cumulative penalty of Rs. 2,92,10,460, along with applicable interest.
  • Potential losses on uncovered metal-based jewellery purchase plan obligations: The company has identified potential losses on the uncovered portion of its metal-based jewellery purchase plan obligations as a material risk.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The company reported un-audited financial results for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026, with total income of Rs. 715.40 crores and profit for the period of Rs. 27.57 crores.

  • The board approved the re-appointment of key managerial personnel, including Mrs. Bharata Mallika Ratna Kumari Grandhi, Mrs. Sai Keerthana Grandhi, and Mrs. Sai Sindhuri Grandhi.
  • The company’s Annual Report for the financial year 2025-26, including the Notice of the 37th AGM, is available online, and shareholders can request physical or electronic copies through the company or its RTA, Bigshare Services Private Limited.

  • The company states there is no impact on its operational activities from the GST order.
  • Potential losses on the uncovered portion of metal-based jewellery purchase plan obligations remain a risk.
