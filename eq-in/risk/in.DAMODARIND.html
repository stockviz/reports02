Headwinds and Challenges

  • Weak sequential profitability despite higher revenue. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, total income from operations was Rs. 15,278.60 lacs, up sharply from Rs. 11,457.21 lacs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 10,633.34 lacs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. However, total expenses rose to Rs. 15,009.54 lacs, leaving profit from operations before exceptional items at Rs. 269.06 lacs, only modestly above the Rs. 143.13 lacs reported in the March 2026 quarter and below the Rs. 232.19 lacs net profit for the June 2025 quarter on a much smaller revenue base.
  • Cost pressure from raw material and inventory build. Raw material consumed rose to Rs. 10,986.69 lacs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 8,517.38 lacs in March 2026 and Rs. 7,493.10 lacs in June 2025. There was also an increase in stock in process and finished goods of Rs. 1,518.60 lacs, compared with Rs. 191.75 lacs in March 2026 and Rs. 626.40 lacs in June 2025, indicating a build-up of inventory alongside higher input costs.
  • Finance costs remain a burden. Finance costs were Rs. 408.19 lacs in the June 2026 quarter, higher than Rs. 384.21 lacs in March 2026, though lower than Rs. 464.08 lacs in June 2025.
  • Tax expense volatility. Current tax was Rs. 47.01 lacs in June 2026, with a matching mat credit entitlement of Rs. 47.01 lacs. In the March 2026 quarter, current tax was Rs. 25.01 lacs with deferred tax of Rs. 51.63 lacs and a mat credit entitlement of Rs. 25.01 lacs. In June 2025, there was a deferred tax credit of Rs. 64.76 lacs. This variability affects reported net profit.
  • Low shareholder participation in the AGM. Of 8,093 shareholders on the record date, only 38 were present in person or by proxy, and only 48 shareholders voted in favour of the financial statements resolution. Resolution 2 on the re-appointment of Mr. Aditya Biyani saw only 39 members voting in favour, with one promoter abstaining on 8,06,45,00 shares (99.40% of votes cast on that resolution), reflecting limited broader shareholder engagement.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong year-on-year revenue growth. Net sales/income from operations for the June 2026 quarter was Rs. 15,285.36 lacs, compared with Rs. 10,335.24 lacs in June 2025, an increase of nearly 48%. Total income from operations for the full year ended March 31, 2026 was Rs. 43,954.92 lacs.
  • Sequential improvement in operating profit. Profit from operations before exceptional items rose from Rs. 143.13 lacs in the March 2026 quarter to Rs. 269.06 lacs in the June 2026 quarter, and net profit rose from Rs. 91.50 lacs to Rs. 269.06 lacs over the same period.
  • Full-year profitability. For the year ended March 31, 2026, net profit was Rs. 537.24 lacs, with basic and diluted earnings per share of Rs. 2.31. Total comprehensive income for the year was Rs. 684.85 lacs, including a Rs. 147.61 lacs change in fair value of cash flow hedge.
  • Resolution of all AGM agenda items. All three resolutions placed before the 38th AGM on August 8, 2026 were passed with the requisite majority: adoption of the standalone financial statements for FY2025-26, re-appointment of Mr. Aditya Biyani as a director retiring by rotation, and fixing remuneration of the cost auditors for FY2026-27.
  • Business concentration in textiles. The company operates in a single primary business segment, textile goods, spanning spinning, fancy yarns, weaving, and processing, with a factory at MIDC Amravati, Additional Amravati Industrial Area, Textile Park, Nandgaon Peth, Maharashtra.

Key Risks

  • Inventory and working capital risk. The sharp increase in stock in process and finished goods of Rs. 1,518.60 lacs in the June 2026 quarter, versus Rs. 191.75 lacs in March 2026, exposes the company to inventory holding and valuation risks if demand or prices weaken.
  • Raw material price risk. Raw material consumed of Rs. 10,986.69 lacs in June 2026 was the largest cost item, and volatility in textile raw material prices could compress margins.
  • Interest rate and leverage risk. Finance costs of Rs. 408.19 lacs in the June 2026 quarter remain a significant fixed charge against operating profit of Rs. 269.06 lacs.
  • Single-segment concentration. The entire business falls within textile goods, with no other reportable segment, so the company is exposed to sector-specific cyclicality.
  • Governance and shareholder engagement risk. The low turnout at the AGM and the abstention by a promoter on Resolution 2, representing 99.40% of votes cast on that resolution, indicate limited participation in key decisions.
  • Tax and deferred tax uncertainty. The company stated that deferred tax assets/liabilities will be determined and accounted at the end of the financial year, and prior periods show swings between deferred tax charges and credits.

The board approved the unaudited financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 at its meeting on August 8, 2026, and the statutory auditors, Devpura Navlakha & Co., issued a limited review report on the same date stating that nothing came to their attention causing them to believe the statement contains any material misstatement.
