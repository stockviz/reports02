Le Merite Exports Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material price volatility across cotton, synthetic fibres, and dyes.
  • Global demand uncertainties and geopolitical tensions affecting export-oriented operations.
  • Currency fluctuations and logistics/freight cost risks.
  • Rising environmental, sustainability, and product-compliance requirements.
  • Intense market competition requiring continuous technological investment.
  • Market-linked accounting volatility: In Q1 FY27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), an unrealised fair-value loss of ₹1,106.41 lakh on FVTPL investments drove a reported Ind AS loss. Standalone reported loss before tax was ₹510.19 lakh and loss after tax was ₹425.76 lakh; consolidated loss after tax was ₹457.53 lakh. Management characterised this as a non-cash, market-driven impact that may reverse in future periods.
  • Revenue softness: Standalone revenue from operations declined to ₹8,975.44 lakh in Q1 FY27 from ₹11,046.15 lakh in Q1 FY26, though it rose sequentially from ₹7,357.90 lakh in Q4 FY26.
  • Governance transition: Independent Director Mr. Rohit Agarwal resigned effective close of business on August 14, 2026, citing personal and professional engagements, with no material reasons other than those stated. Ms. Kusum Naruka was appointed as an Additional Non-Executive Independent Director for five years from August 14, 2026, subject to shareholder approval.
  • Disclosure limitation: The company has not received complete information from all suppliers regarding their MSMED Act registration status, so related disclosures are made only to the extent available.
  • Regulatory filing correction: The company filed a revised XBRL for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 on July 10, 2026, correcting an inadvertent typographical error in Diluted EPS (correct figure: 4.37).

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Growing demand for technical and specialised textiles, including defence sector indigenisation.
  • Government initiatives such as Make in India, Atmanirbhar Bharat, and PLI schemes.
  • Expanding international trade agreements and global supply chain diversification.
  • Increasing market focus on sustainability and R&D.
  • Underlying profitability: Q1 FY27 PBT before unrealised FVTPL movement was ₹596.22 lakh, with adjusted PAT of ₹496.50 lakh before the unrealised FVTPL fair-value movement.
  • Strategic expansion into technical textiles: On July 8, 2026, the company incorporated an associate, Le Merite Torna Technical Textile Park Private Limited, holding 3,500 equity shares (35%) at face value of ₹10 each. The entity is yet to commence commercial operations and is intended to develop an integrated technical textile park.
  • Subsidiary structure: The company has three subsidiaries — Le Merite Laxmi Spinning Private Limited, Le Merite Filament Private Limited, and Le Merite Tactical & Defence Solutions Private Limited.
  • Capital structure actions: Equity shares were sub-divided 1:5 (face value ₹10 to ₹2), with record date May 29, 2026. Separately, 5,26,000 convertible equity warrants allotted September 17, 2025 at ₹320 per security remain outstanding as of June 30, 2026, representing entitlement to 26,30,000 equity shares of ₹2 each upon conversion within the stipulated 18-month period.
  • Leadership continuity: The Board approved re-appointments of Mr. Abhishek Lath (MD & CFO), Mr. Umashankar Lath (MD & Chairman), Mrs. Sweta Lath (Executive Director), Mr. Jaydeep Purujit Mehta and Mr. Narendra Kumar Srivastava (Non-Executive Independent Directors), each for five-year terms commencing between October and December 2026. Mr. Narendra Kumar Sharma was appointed Internal Auditor for FY 2026-27.

3. Key Risks

  • Market valuation risk on FVTPL investments, which can produce material unrealised gains or losses in reported results depending on market movements at each reporting date.
  • Export-linked risks from global demand uncertainty, geopolitical tensions, and currency fluctuations.
  • Compliance and sustainability risk from tightening environmental and product-compliance requirements.
  • Competitive pressure necessitating ongoing technological investment.
  • Execution risk on the technical textile park venture, which is yet to commence commercial operations.
  • Dilution risk from outstanding warrants convertible into 26,30,000 equity shares.
  • Supplier information gaps relating to MSMED registration status.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management stated it remains optimistic about opportunities in the Indian textile industry, particularly specialised and technical textile segments, and is focused on strengthening its core business, enhancing operational efficiency, expanding its international footprint, and pursuing sustainable long-term growth.
  • Management also cited business expansion and growth driven by increasing product portfolio, global footprint, and customer base, supported by experienced leadership.
  • Observed performance: Q1 FY27 showed positive underlying profitability (PBT of ₹596.22 lakh before unrealised FVTPL movement; adjusted PAT of ₹496.50 lakh), but reported results were a loss due to the ₹1,106.41 lakh unrealised FVTPL fair-value loss. Revenue declined year-on-year (₹8,975.44 lakh vs ₹11,046.15 lakh in Q1 FY26) while rising sequentially from Q4 FY26 (₹7,357.90 lakh).
  • For FY 2025-26, consolidated revenue from operations was ₹35,747.09 lakh and consolidated PAT was ₹1,159.87 lakh. The company decided not to recommend a dividend for FY 2025-26 to conserve resources.
  • The company migrated from the NSE SME platform to the NSE Main Board during FY 2025-26.
  • The incorporation of the technical textile park associate and the ongoing warrant programme are consistent with stated expansion intentions, though the park entity has yet to commence commercial operations.
