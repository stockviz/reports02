Broker Narrative

The broker stayed Buy in both reports, but the emphasis shifted from the MMR redevelopment boom, record GDV additions and a FY28E legacy-phase-out margin story to a more defensive case built on strong Q1FY27 financials, July 2026 launches, retained guidance, rating upgrade and cash reserves. Persistent themes were MMR demand resilience, margin improvement from new high-margin projects, and the twin risks of project delays and failing to add new projects. The key change was acknowledging Q1FY27 had zero launches and that legacy projects still dragged FY26 margins before phasing out in FY27.

Fears that came true

  • The early risk of inability to add new projects materialized as zero project launches in Q1FY27, forcing pre-sales to depend entirely on sustenance sales and contributing to the disappointing negative actual returns.

Optimism that failed

  • The FY27 launch-pipeline optimism failed in Q1FY27, as no projects were launched until after the quarter ended in July 2026.
  • The margin-tailwind expectation from phasing out legacy projects was not met in FY26, with legacy projects at ~5% margins still contributing 62% of FY26 revenues and dragging overall margins.
