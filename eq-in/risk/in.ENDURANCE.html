Endurance Technologies — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Raw material and energy cost pressure (Q1FY27). The Managing Director described Q1 FY27 as “one of the most challenging quarters,” citing raw material shortages and large increases in raw material and energy prices. Consolidated EBITDA margin fell to 13.1% from 14.3% in Q1FY26, and standalone EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 13.0%, attributed to higher commodity prices in the standalone entity. The company notes that despite pass-through of major elements, such cost increases adversely impact percentage margins. Standalone EBITDA grew 17.1% while standalone Total Income grew 35.9%, and consolidated PAT growth (8.0%) lagged consolidated Total Income growth (29.6%).

European demand mix and ICE exposure. EU new car registrations grew 7.4% YoY in Q1FY27, but a significant part of that growth came from Chinese OEMs that Endurance does not currently serve. European revenue in Euro terms grew only 1%, largely in line with the production trend of its existing European OEM customer base. The company expects a reduction in future revenues from currently serviced ICE orders owing to the growing market share of Chinese OEMs and the transition to EV/Hybrid in Europe. ICE end-use, currently ~30% of Endurance Europe revenues, is expected to reduce to 25% in FY28.

European profitability. Europe PAT declined 22.4% YoY in Q1FY27 (Rs 48 Cr vs Rs 62 Cr), impacted by accelerated depreciation of assets pertaining to OEM programs being phased out. Europe EBITDA margin was 18.2% in Q1FY27 versus 17.4% in Q1FY26, while Europe Total Income grew 19.4% YoY.

European energy costs. Electricity prices (PUN, Italy) rose 22% YoY and gas prices rose 23% YoY in Q1FY27; versus FY21, electricity is up 183% and gas up 257%.

European regulatory uncertainty. European lawmakers are in the process of formulating rules for localised content to create a level playing field for local manufacturers; management states it will continue to monitor these developments and diversify its customer portfolio.

Order book caveat. Projected peak values from new orders assume certain OEM projections may not be met, or programs may peak prior to FY29.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Indian industry volume growth (Q1FY27). Total 2W sales rose 23.6% YoY (motorcycles +19.3%, scooters +32.7%), 3W rose 40.0%, total 4W rose 22.7%, and total vehicles rose 24.0%. Standalone Total Income grew 35.9% YoY, reflecting market growth, higher raw material costs passed through, and ramp-up of sales against new orders.

Order wins. In Q1FY27, business won in India was Rs 405 Cr (including Rs 13 Cr in Maxwell), plus Euro 13.9 million in Europe. The order book is expected to be boosted further by Rs 4,526 Cr of RFQs under discussion. Specific wins include Rs 336 Cr from HMSI (including Rs 220 Cr of HMSI suspension facelift) and new brakes business from TVS taking their annual brakes business to Rs 250+ Cr. New business from a leading US EV OEM takes annual business potential for Shendra to Rs 223 Cr/annum.

EV order book. Cumulative non-Bajaj EV orders total Rs 1,496 Cr (Castings 599, Suspension 123, Brakes 395, Battery Packs 300, Alloy Wheels 31), plus Rs 310 Cr from Bajaj Auto, totaling Rs 1,806 Cr. This includes Rs 968 Cr for 2W/3W OEMs and Rs 528 Cr for 4W OEMs, and >Rs 500 Cr for e-4W applications from four international customers including Valeo and Yazaki.

European EV/hybrid mix. Of Euro 185 Mn cumulative orders won in the last 5 years, Euro 87 Mn (47%) are for EV applications and Euro 73 Mn (39%) for Hybrid applications.

Capacity expansion and new programs (FY27). AURIC Shendra project (machined castings for 4W and non-automotive) with SOP expected Q2FY27; expansion of 4W machined aluminium casting capacity across Chennai and Chakan; battery pack plant in Pune commenced production in Q1FY27 with 4W battery pack capacity expansion SOP expected Q4FY27; dual channel ABS SOP expected Q2FY27 plus additional ABS and Chennai disc brake capacity SOP expected Q2FY27; second SMT line for electronics (in-house ABS ECU and higher BMS volumes) SOP Q2FY27; Sanand plant infrastructure for solar dampers (SOP Q2FY27) and actuators (SOP expected Q4FY27); new aluminium forging plant SOP expected Q3FY27.

Embedded electronics and new energy. Continuing large-volume AVA BMS business; Rs 13 Cr orders won in Q1FY27 including new BMS variants (CT Safe GT, CT-Lite, LT+PRI); cumulative orders at peak Rs 238 Cr per annum; leads worth Rs 300 Cr+ being pursued for BMS, TPMS and DC-DC converter. 2W battery pack dispatches commenced and are ramping up; the company is engaging with OEMs for further orders.

India OEM capacity expansions. Management states that continuing demand strength has prompted significant capacity expansion announcements among major Indian 2W and 4W OEMs, representing a strong runway for profitable growth given Endurance’s established 2W content, deepening 4W presence, and growing share of business with multiple OEMs.

Aftermarket. Aftermarket sales from Indian operations rose 11.1% to Rs 125 Cr in Q1FY27 from Rs 112 Cr a year earlier.

Portfolio actions. Stake in Stöferle entities (Germany) raised to 68% via acquisition of an additional 8% for €6.24 million on 29 June 2026, with the remaining stake to be acquired over the next 4 years. Veicoli Srl, Italy was fully divested. The company holds 100 total patents and 110 design registrations.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material shortages and sharp increases in raw material and energy prices, with partial pass-through limiting margin protection.
  • Dependence on European OEM production trends and exposure to ICE programs being phased out; accelerated depreciation on such programs.
  • Loss of European growth to Chinese OEMs not currently served by the company.
  • Potential impact of European localised-content rules on the company’s positioning.
  • Order book projections are peak per-annum estimates that may not be realized if OEM projections are not met or programs peak earlier than assumed.
  • Elevated European energy prices relative to FY21 levels.
  • Maxwell Energy Systems recorded a full-year FY26 PAT loss of Rs 10 Cr (quarterly losses in Q1FY26 through Q4FY26), though it turned PAT-positive in Q1FY27 (Rs 1 Cr).

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Q1FY27 capex guidance. FY27 capex is stated as Rs 196 Cr for standalone and Euro 3.6M for Europe, with expansion capex and dies accounting for >80% of total capex, including capacity addition in brake assemblies, machined aluminium die casting and battery packs, and production capacity expansion for new orders including from the VW group. Separately, the European business invested Euro 6.24 million in Q1FY27 to acquire an additional 8% stake in Stöferle entities.

SOP timelines. Management guidance on start-of-production includes: AURIC Shendra (Q2FY27), 4W battery packs (Q4FY27), dual channel ABS (Q2FY27), additional disc brake capacity in Chennai (Q2FY27), second SMT line (Q2FY27), solar damper (Q2FY27, already commenced), actuators (Q4FY27), and new aluminium forging plant (Q3FY27). Observed performance against these: production commenced at the Pune battery pack plant in Q1FY27; solar damper SOP took place in Q2FY27; TVS conventional motorcycle suspension SOP took place in Q2FY27, with scooter suspension SOP expected Q3FY27.

European revenue trajectory. Management expects ICE end-use, currently ~30% of Endurance Europe revenues, to reduce to 25% in FY28. Observed Q1FY27 Europe revenue in Euro terms grew 1%, in line with the production trend of the existing European OEM customer base.

Order book conversion. Management presents Rs 4,526 Cr of RFQs under discussion as potential further order book boost, and describes the Indian OEM capacity expansion announcements as a strong runway for profitable growth. Observed Q1FY27 standalone Total Income growth of 35.9% reflects market growth, raw material pass-through, and ramp-up against new orders.

AGM voting outcomes (13 August 2026). All five resolutions passed with requisite majority as ordinary resolutions: adoption of standalone financial statements (99.9999% in favour), adoption of consolidated financial statements (99.9999% in favour), dividend of Rs 11.50 per equity share on 14,06,62,848 shares (99.9999% in favour), re-appointment of Mr. Massimo Venuti as director (99.9634% in favour, 0.0366% against), and ratification of Cost Auditor remuneration for FY2026-27 (99.9999% in favour).

Broker Narrative

The narrative shifted from near-term concerns about Indian 2W export weakness and EV/CVT traction to a broader focus on Europe’s structural challenges (Chinese OEM competition, ICE phase-out, energy costs) and standalone margin pressure. Persistent themes included margin dilution, European operational headwinds, and reliance on order wins/backlog for growth; Maxwell losses were expected to be a drag but instead turned into a PAT-positive story. The later narrative became more confident about India-led revenue growth and 4W die-casting/braking as margin drivers, while still flagging input costs and depreciation.

Fears that came true

  • Standalone margin dilution materialized and worsened, with standalone EBITDA margin falling 150bp YoY to 10.9% due to higher input costs, causing an adjusted PAT miss and muted stock return (+4.1% vs +15.6% predicted).
  • EU government subsidy cuts and European auto weakness contributed to Europe’s EUR revenue growth of only 1% despite 7.4% industry registrations, as Chinese OEMs captured growth and European production declined 5%.

Optimism that failed

  • The 4QFY23 consolidated EBITDA margin expansion to 12.8% did not persist; last report showed a ~100bp YoY contraction to 12.4% below estimates.
  • The expected recovery in European auto production reversed, as European vehicle production declined 5% and most industry growth came from Chinese OEMs rather than benefiting ENDURANCE.
  • Falling EU energy costs proved temporary, with the last report citing high energy costs as a key margin and European-profit headwind.
