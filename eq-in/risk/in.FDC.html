Headwinds and Challenges

  • Domestic Formulations weakness: Domestic Formulations revenue declined 1.9% year-on-year to Rs. 569 crores in Q1 FY27. The company attributed this to subdued performance in select key brands, specifically Zifi, Enerzal and Simyl MCT. Domestic Formulations made up approximately 85% of consolidated sales in the quarter, so this segment’s softness weighed on overall growth.
  • Muted API business: API sales fell 2.9% year-on-year to Rs. 24 crores, contributing approximately 4% of consolidated sales.
  • Margin pressure: EBITDA margin slipped to 21.4% in Q1 FY27 from 21.6% in Q1 FY26, even as EBITDA grew 1.9% year-on-year to Rs. 143 crores.
  • Regulatory action on product labeling: FSSAI Maharashtra seized stock and raised a contravention related to mislabeling of a product. Separately, FSSAI New Delhi issued a notice that was challenged, with the High Court of Delhi granting an interim stay.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • International Formulations momentum: International Formulations sales rose 74.2% year-on-year to Rs. 73 crores, contributing approximately 11% of consolidated sales. The US business grew 118.4% to Rs. 34 crores, and Non-US Export Formulations grew 47.7% to Rs. 39 crores.
  • US FDA approval: During the quarter, the company received US FDA approval for Cefixime for Oral Suspension USP 100mg/5mL and 200mg/5mL.
  • Secondary sales growth: As per IQVIA secondary sales data for the quarter and MAT ended June 30, 2026, the company recorded growth of 9.0% and 1.9% respectively.
  • Group captive solar investment: The Board granted in-principle approval to invest for a minimum 26% equity stake in Netra Green Energy Private Limited, in connection with power procurement under a Group Captive Solar Project for the API manufacturing plant at Roha.
  • Overall profitability growth: PBT rose 9.4% year-on-year to Rs. 175 crores and PAT rose 9.2% to Rs. 132 crores, with EPS at Rs. 8.14 versus Rs. 7.45 in Q1 FY26.

Key Risks

  • Continued softness in key domestic brands (Zifi, Enerzal, Simyl MCT) could keep Domestic Formulations under pressure, given the segment’s roughly 85% share of consolidated sales.
  • The API segment remains small at approximately 4% of consolidated sales and declined year-on-year.
  • Regulatory and labeling compliance risk is evident from the FSSAI Maharashtra seizure and mislabeling contravention, and the separate FSSAI New Delhi notice. The Delhi High Court interim stay allows eight months to exhaust existing stock bearing the descriptors ‘electrolyte’/‘electrolyte drink’ and to make required label changes, with no material adverse impact on financials or operations indicated.
  • The company’s forward-looking statement disclaimer cites risks and uncertainties including ability to implement strategy, growth and expansion plans, ability to obtain regulatory approvals, technological changes, fluctuation in earnings, foreign exchange rates, ability to manage international operations and exports, and exposure to market risks.
