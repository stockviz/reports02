CORONA Remedies: Recent Developments Summary

Headwinds and Challenges

  • Labour Code impact: The Government of India notified four Labour Codes on 21 November 2025, consolidating 29 existing labour laws. CORONA assessed an incremental, non-recurring statutory impact of ₹19.10 crores (post-tax ₹14.29 crores) for FY26, comprising gratuity of ₹13.60 crores and long-term compensated absences of ₹5.50 crores, primarily due to a change in wage definition. The company continues to monitor finalisation of Central/State Rules and clarifications, and would provide appropriate accounting effect as developments occur.
  • Rising cost base: Employee benefits expense rose to ₹122.09 crores in Q1FY27 from ₹97.44 crores in Q1FY26, and other expenses rose to ₹129.69 crores from ₹113.54 crores. Depreciation and amortisation increased to ₹13.24 crores from ₹8.92 crores, and finance costs rose to ₹2.69 crores from ₹1.99 crores.
  • Working capital and cash position: Net working capital days are shown as a tracked ratio. Cash and cash equivalents stood at ₹1.8 crores as of March 2026, down from ₹3.2 crores a year earlier. Net cash from investing activities was an outflow of ₹256.1 crores in FY26, and net increase/(decrease) in cash and cash equivalents was negative ₹1.4 crores.
  • Borrowings increase: Current borrowings rose to ₹142.9 crores as of March 2026 from ₹46.7 crores as of March 2025, while non-current borrowings fell to nil from ₹16.0 crores.
  • Governance-related dissent: At the 22nd AGM held on 9 July 2026, certain resolutions saw notable against-votes. The re-appointment of Mr. Nirav Mehta as Director received 93.03% in favour and 6.97% against. The appointment of Walker Chandiok & Co LLP as Statutory Auditors received 94.6% in favour and 5.4% against. The special resolutions on payment of commission to Non-Executive Directors (94.13% in favour), remuneration to Dr. Kirtikumar Mehta (91.5% in favour), and his re-appointment as Non-Executive Director and Chairman (91.54% in favour) also saw against-votes of 5.87%, 8.5%, and 8.46% respectively.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Outpacing IPM growth: CORONA is described as the fastest growing company among the top 30 pharma companies in IPM for MAT June-26 and the quarter ended June-26, outperforming the market by 1.9 times. Revenue MAT growth is stated at 18.4%, with the company climbing 6 ranks over MAT Jun-23 to MAT Jun-26.
  • Rank progression: Rank improved to 26th in MAT June-26 from 27th in MAT March 2026, and from 29th in MAT June-25. The company stands at 5th rank in Gynaecology, 5th in Pain Management, 9th in Urology, and 20th in Cardio-Diabeto.
  • New EU-GMP hormone facility: The company inaugurated and initiated commercialisation of an EU-GMP approved Female Hormone Manufacturing Facility at Ahmedabad on 30 June 2026. The facility spans one lakh sq. ft., with annual capacity of 194 million tablets/capsules and 1.5 million ointments/gels, covering tablets, soft gel capsules, ointments and gels within a single ecosystem. EU-GMP certification for the Oral Solid Dosage facility at the Bhayla plant was renewed in May 2026.
  • Chronic and sub-chronic focus: Chronic and sub-chronic therapies contributed ~73.4% in Q1FY27. The portfolio mix shifted from 70:30 to 28:72 (Acute:Chronic) from FY13 to FY23.
  • Diversified brand portfolio: Top 5 brands account for ~35% and Top 10 brands for ~50% of revenue. 79 brands with 33 engine brands. 97% of revenue came from the India business in Q1FY27.
  • Acquisitions and integrations: The company has acquired brands from GSK, Abbott, Sanofi, Bayer Zydus, and Dr. Reddy’s (Wokadine). Integration of Wokadine has been initiated from supply and distribution perspectives. In-licensing arrangement with Ferring Pharmaceuticals for women’s healthcare and urology, and investment in La Chandra Pharmalabs for backward integration into hormone API manufacturing.
  • Growth strategy: Plans include improving MR productivity, expanding prescriber base, growing women’s health via infertility specialists and IVF-focused chain hospitals, expanding into nephrology, CNS, rheumatology, dermatology and gastroenterology, and leveraging the hormone portfolio for overseas and regulated markets.
  • IPM outlook: IPM is projected to grow at a CAGR of 8–9% till FY30, with chronic segment growth expected to outpace acute (chronic share rising from 55% in FY25 to 57–59% by FY30P).
  • Dividend: A final dividend of ₹10 per equity share (100%) for FY2025-26 was declared and passed at the AGM.

Key Risks

  • Regulatory and compliance risk: The company operates in a regulated pharmaceutical environment with EU-GMP, EAEU GMP, WHO-GMP, Uzbekistan GMP, Uganda NDA, and DSIR certifications. Renewals and inspections are ongoing requirements.
  • Labour Code uncertainty: Finalisation of Central/State Rules and clarifications on other aspects of the Labour Code remain pending, with potential for further accounting effects.
  • Concentration and competition: The company operates in a competitive IPM landscape among top 30 players, with growth dependent on brand building, new launches, and field force expansion.
  • Working capital and cash flow: Negative net cash increase in FY26 and significant investing outflows indicate dependence on financing and operating cash generation.
  • Governance dissent: Notable against-votes on director re-appointment, auditor appointment, and remuneration resolutions at the AGM indicate shareholder dissent on certain governance matters.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The company’s own disclosures note risks including performance of the Indian and international economies, industry performance, competition, strategy implementation, growth and expansion, technological changes, revenue/income/cash flow changes, market preferences, and market risk exposure.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Q1FY27 performance versus stated focus: Management stated Q1FY27 shows commitment to consistent growth with operational excellence and brand building, with robust revenue growth and market rank improvement reflecting positive outcomes of strategies in place. Observed: Revenue from operations grew 21.9% Y-o-Y to ₹422.4 crores; EBITDA grew 33.5% to ₹93.1 crores with margin expansion of 190 bps to 22.0%; PAT grew 30.1% to ₹60.1 crores with margin expansion of 90 bps to 14.2%.
  • Therapy focus: Management stated aim to move steadfast with aligned focus on core therapies such as Women’s Healthcare, Cardio-diabetes, Pain Management and Urology, and to strengthen in Infertility and Biosimilar segments. Observed: The company holds 5th rank in Gynaecology, 5th in Pain Management, 9th in Urology, and 20th in Cardio-Diabeto. Chronic and sub-chronic therapies contributed ~73.4% in Q1FY27.
  • Hormone facility: Management inaugurated and initiated commercialisation of the EU-GMP approved Female Hormone Manufacturing Facility on 30 June 2026. Observed: The facility is described as India’s most advanced EU-GMP approved women’s hormone manufacturing facility, with capacity of 194 million tablets/capsules and 1.5 million ointments/gels annually.
  • Wokadine integration: Management stated it has initiated smooth integration of Wokadine from supply and distribution perspectives. Observed: Wokadine is listed among acquired brands from Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Limited.
  • Historical performance versus guidance: FY26 revenue from operations was ₹1,403.2 crores with EBITDA of ₹293.4 crores (20.9% margin) and reported PAT of ₹185.1 crores (adjusted PAT ₹199.4 crores, 14.2% margin). FY25 revenue was ₹1,196.4 crores with EBITDA of ₹240.0 crores (20.1% margin) and PAT of ₹149.4 crores. FY24 revenue was ₹1,014.5 crores with EBITDA of ₹154.7 crores (15.3% margin) and PAT of ₹90.5 crores. FY23 revenue was ₹884.1 crores with EBITDA of ₹128.0 crores (14.5% margin) and PAT of ₹84.9 crores. The trend shows improving margins and profitability over the period.
  • Return ratios: ROCE is shown at 50.2% and ROE at 34.0%, with EPS of ₹9.8 for Q1FY27 (annualised). For FY26, ROE and ROCE are annualised and exclude the one-time statutory impact of the New Labour Code.
  • Investor engagement: The company intimated participation in an Analyst/Investor Conference organised by Phillip Capital PCG on 26 August 2026 in Ahmedabad, with discussions based on publicly available information and no unpublished price sensitive information to be discussed.
