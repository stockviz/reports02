Key Risks

  • Governance concentration. Promoter and promoter group voted 100% in favour on all resolutions, and the resolutions on remuneration revisions for promoter-family office holders (Resolutions 5–8) were carried with promoter support; Resolution 5 was noted as having promoter/promoter group interest.
