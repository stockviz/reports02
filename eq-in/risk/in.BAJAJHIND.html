BAJAJHIND: Recent Corporate Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

Weak Q1 FY2026-27 operating performance. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, standalone revenue from operations was ₹1,116.30 crore, down from ₹1,242.10 crore in the corresponding quarter of the prior year and sharply lower than the ₹1,656.76 crore recorded in the preceding quarter (Q4 FY2025-26). The company reported a standalone net loss of ₹177.05 crore for the quarter, compared with a loss of ₹168.57 crore a year earlier and a profit of ₹391.06 crore in the preceding quarter. On a consolidated basis, the net loss after tax was ₹184.70 crore, versus a loss of ₹174.00 crore in Q1 FY2025-26 and a profit of ₹390.68 crore in Q4 FY2025-26.

Segment-level weakness. Standalone segment results for Q1 FY2026-27 showed a loss before tax and interest of ₹145.75 crore in Sugar, a loss of ₹23.94 crore in Power, and a profit of ₹7.58 crore in Distillery. This compares with a Sugar segment loss of ₹61.06 crore in Q1 FY2025-26 and a Sugar segment profit of ₹193.06 crore in Q4 FY2025-26. The Distillery segment’s contribution fell to ₹7.58 crore from ₹63.88 crore in the preceding quarter and ₹15.80 crore a year earlier.

Finance costs and leverage. Standalone finance costs were ₹89.92 crore in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with ₹88.01 crore a year earlier and ₹95.37 crore in the preceding quarter. Total standalone expenses of ₹1,295.59 crore exceeded total income of ₹1,118.46 crore.

Unrecognised interest income and auditor emphasis. The company has not recognised interest income of ₹28.03 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (same as the prior-year quarter) on an inter-corporate loan, applying prudence and conservatism; the income will be recognised upon realisation. The auditors included an Emphasis of Matter in their limited review report on this matter. The auditors also noted that the company holds investments aggregating ₹2,593.33 crore in its subsidiaries, comprising equity investments, inter-corporate loans, and accrued interest, with management actively undertaking measures for recovery and settlement.

Going-concern assessment and auditor attention. The auditors drew attention to the management’s assessment of the company’s ability to continue as a going concern, considering operating performance, liquidity position, and the impact of the approved debt restructuring. The financial results have been prepared on a going concern basis.

Subsidiary results not reviewed by auditors. The consolidated results include interim financial results of five subsidiaries not reviewed by their auditors, reflecting total revenue of ₹10.76 crore, net loss after tax of ₹4.72 crore, and total comprehensive loss of ₹3.92 crore for the quarter. One subsidiary’s interim results, reflecting nil revenue and a net loss after tax of ₹13.14 crore, were reviewed by other auditors.

Procedural compliance lapse. The company inadvertently did not submit the declaration regarding an unmodified audit opinion along with its audited financial results for FY2025-26, as required under Regulation 33(3)(d) of the SEBI LODR Regulations. The company clarified on June 29, 2026, that its statutory auditors, Siddharth N. Jain & Co., issued an unmodified audit opinion for FY2025-26, and that the omission was purely inadvertent and procedural. A confirmation certificate from the CFO was attached.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Completion of resolution plan allotments. Pursuant to a Framework Agreement executed in March 2026 under an approved Resolution Plan, the company during the quarter allotted 1,67,23,565 equity shares of face value Re. 1 each at an issue price of ₹5.12 per equity share and 44,56,67,369 Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) of face value Re. 1 each to one lender. Equity shares and CCPS had already been allotted to other participating lenders in March 2026. With these allotments, the issuance of equity shares and CCPS to participating lenders under the approved Resolution Plan and Framework Agreement stands fully complied.

Debt repayment moratorium. Following the restructuring, repayment of outstanding Optionally Convertible Debentures (OCDs) is under moratorium, with repayments scheduled to commence from March 31, 2031. Management expects this to support the company’s liquidity and operations.

Sugar Industry Promotion Policy claim. The company is pursuing a claim aggregating approximately ₹1,978 crore (including interest up to June 30, 2026) under the Sugar Industry Promotion Policy, 2004, which is presently sub judice. Management expects a favourable outcome, which, upon realisation, would further strengthen the company’s financial position and liquidity.

Disposal of non-core assets. Pursuant to restructuring agreements, certain plant and machinery, furniture and fixtures, and other equipment pertaining to the erstwhile Board Division (Ecotec) have been identified by the consortium of lenders as non-core assets for disposal. Some identified assets were disposed of during the quarter. The Board Division has been classified as a discontinued operation under Ind AS 105, with its net result presented separately. The standalone results show a profit of ₹0.06 crore from discontinued operations for the quarter.

Management’s going-concern view. Management assessed the company’s ability to continue as a going concern after considering current and expected operating performance, liquidity position, cash flow projections, positive net worth, prevailing firm sugar prices, and the impact of the approved debt restructuring. Management is of the view that the company will be able to continue operations and meet obligations in the normal course of business for the foreseeable future.

Shareholder approval of resolutions. The company held its 94th Annual General Meeting on August 27, 2026, where all proposed resolutions set out in the notice dated July 29, 2026, were passed by shareholders with the requisite majority.

Key Risks

  • Operating losses and margin pressure: The company reported a standalone net loss of ₹177.05 crore and a consolidated net loss of ₹184.70 crore for Q1 FY2026-27, with negative segment results in Sugar and Power.
  • Liquidity and going-concern dependence: The going-concern basis depends on expected operating performance, liquidity position, cash flow projections, positive net worth, firm sugar prices, and the impact of the approved debt restructuring.
  • Recovery of subsidiary investments: Investments aggregating ₹2,593.33 crore in subsidiaries, comprising equity investments, inter-corporate loans, and accrued interest, are subject to recovery and settlement measures.
  • Unrecognised income: Interest income of ₹28.03 crore on an inter-corporate loan has not been recognised and will be recognised only upon realisation.
  • Litigation uncertainty: The Sugar Industry Promotion Policy, 2004 claim of approximately ₹1,978 crore is sub judice, with no certainty of outcome.
  • Reliance on moratorium: OCD repayments commence only from March 31, 2031; liquidity support depends on the restructuring terms remaining in effect.
  • Discontinued operations: The erstwhile Board Division (Ecotec) has been classified as a discontinued operation, with asset disposals ongoing.
  • Regulatory compliance: The company inadvertently omitted the Regulation 33(3)(d) declaration regarding unmodified audit opinion for FY2025-26, which it subsequently clarified.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance on going concern: Management stated that, after considering current and expected operating performance, liquidity position, cash flow projections, positive net worth, prevailing firm sugar prices, and the impact of the approved debt restructuring, the company will be able to continue its operations and meet its obligations in the normal course of business for the foreseeable future. Observed performance for Q1 FY2026-27 shows a standalone net loss of ₹177.05 crore and a consolidated net loss of ₹184.70 crore, with negative Sugar and Power segment results, indicating that near-term profitability remains under pressure even as the company maintains a going-concern basis.

Guidance on liquidity support: Management stated that the OCD repayment moratorium, with repayments commencing from March 31, 2031, is expected to support the company’s liquidity and operations. Observed finance costs remained elevated at ₹89.92 crore standalone for the quarter, and total expenses exceeded total income.

Guidance on the Sugar Industry Promotion Policy claim: Management stated it expects a favourable outcome on the approximately ₹1,978 crore claim, which upon realisation would strengthen the company’s financial position and liquidity. The matter remains sub judice, and no realisation has been recorded in the Q1 FY2026-27 results.

Guidance on subsidiary investment recovery: Management stated it is actively undertaking appropriate measures for recovery and settlement of investments aggregating ₹2,593.33 crore in subsidiaries. No recovery has been reflected in the Q1 FY2026-27 results, and interest income of ₹28.03 crore on an inter-corporate loan remains unrecognised pending realisation.

Guidance on resolution plan completion: The company stated that, with the allotments made during the quarter, the issuance of equity shares and CCPS to participating lenders under the approved Resolution Plan and Framework Agreement stands fully complied. This is supported by the observed allotment of 1,67,23,565 equity shares and 44,56,67,369 CCPS during the quarter.

Guidance on non-core asset disposal: The company stated that some identified non-core assets of the erstwhile Board Division were disposed of during the quarter, and the division has been classified as a discontinued operation. The standalone results show a profit of ₹0.06 crore from discontinued operations for the quarter.
