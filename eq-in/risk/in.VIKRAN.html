Vikran Engineering: Recent Developments and Outlook

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Order cancellation – Ellume Energy. Vikran had accepted a Letter of Award on 24 October 2025 from Ellume Energy MH Solar One Private Limited (SPV) for a turnkey EPC contract for a 100 MW AC solar project in Maharashtra, valued at approximately ₹354.21 crore. On 27 June 2026, the company disclosed that it had decided not to proceed with the order. The stated reasons were prolonged delays at the client’s end in achieving project readiness milestones — the client could not provide the required Power Purchase Agreement, work commencement approvals, design clearances, scope finalization, and mobilization permissions within expected timelines, making execution commercially unviable. The cancellation was by mutual understanding, and the company stated it does not expect a material adverse impact. The Q1 FY27 press release (11 August 2026) confirmed the Ellume Energy EPC contract was concluded mutually due to prolonged project readiness delays at the client’s end.

Receivables litigation. The statutory auditor’s report (22 May 2026) includes an Emphasis of Matter on recoverability of trade receivables. Note 9 describes uncertainty over the outcome of ongoing litigation with a customer concerning a balance of INR 2,929 lakhs (standalone) / INR 2,926 lakhs (consolidated) due from that customer, pending in the Commercial Court, Jaipur. The customer had invoked performance and advance bank guarantees and short-closed the project in April 2023. The next hearing was scheduled for 23 June 2026. Management, based on contractual tenability of the claim and legal opinion obtained, considers the amount good and recoverable, and no adjustment was made. The auditor’s opinion is not modified in respect of this matter.

GST penalty. On 22 August 2026, the company received a Show Cause Notice cum Order in Form GST DRC-01 under Section 129(3) of the CGST Act, 2017, from the State Taxes Officer, Kashmir, Jammu and Kashmir, for detention of goods in transit without a valid E-Way Bill and valid tax invoice. A penalty of ₹4,01,400 was levied. The company voluntarily paid the penalty on 22 August 2026 and is processing an appeal. Management stated it does not foresee any material impact on financial, operational, or other activities.

Working capital intensity. The standalone cash flow statement for FY26 shows net cash used in operating activities of INR 43,699 lakhs, driven by increases in trade receivables (INR 42,384 lakhs), contract assets (INR 40,129 lakhs), and other assets (INR 10,394 lakhs), partly offset by higher trade payables (INR 31,352 lakhs). Trade receivables rose from INR 60,503 lakhs (31 March 2025) to INR 1,01,307 lakhs (31 March 2026), and contract assets rose from INR 46,637 lakhs to INR 86,394 lakhs.

Quarterly variability. Note 11 states that due to the nature of the business, revenue, costs, and profits can vary widely between reported quarters, largely driven by progress of individual projects, which do not follow a uniform timeline; quarterly results may not be directly comparable.

Regulatory disclosure lapse. NSE queried the company (email dated 19 June 2026) regarding non-submission of the declaration for unmodified opinion on FY26 audited results. The company clarified on 30 June 2026 that the declaration under Regulation 33(3)(d) was inadvertently omitted from the 22 May 2026 filing and was being submitted separately. The statutory auditors (Walker Chandiok & Co LLP) had issued an unmodified opinion on both standalone and consolidated FY26 results.

Forward-looking statement risk factors. The company’s cautionary statement cites regulatory changes, local political or economic developments, and other factors that could cause actual results to differ materially from forward-looking statements; past performance is not necessarily indicative of future results.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Q1 FY27 performance. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone revenue from operations was ₹204.0 crore, up 28.2% YoY from ₹159.2 crore. EBITDA (excluding other income) was ₹28.0 crore versus ₹22.7 crore, up 23.7%. EBITDA margin was 13.7% versus 14.2%. PAT was ₹17.5 crore versus ₹5.7 crore, up 209.9%. PAT margin was 8.6% versus 3.5%.

Order book. Total order book as on 11 August 2026 stood at ₹6,496.2 crore. The mix was Solar 62%, Power T&D 28%, Water Infrastructure 10%, and Railway Infrastructure 1%.

Solar EPC. The company secured a 969 MW AC Solar EPC project from wholly-owned subsidiary NOPL Solar Projects, with a contract value of ₹3,517.98 crore including GST, strengthening the renewable energy mix across the order book. The acquisition of NOPL Solar Projects adds a 969 MW PM-KUSUM solar portfolio with 25-year PPA visibility and expected annual revenue potential of ~₹525+ crore upon commissioning.

Power T&D. The company secured a ₹120.7 crore order from POWERGRID for a 400 kV GIS Substation Extension Package across Magarwada, Vadodara, and Rajgarh, strengthening its presence in high-voltage transmission infrastructure.

Execution track record. The company successfully commissioned the 132 kV Miao-Namsai Transmission Line in Arunachal Pradesh, covering 40.6 km and 138 tower foundations, reinforcing execution capabilities in challenging terrains.

Emerging opportunities. Management is selectively evaluating emerging opportunities such as Battery Energy Storage Systems and Data Centre infrastructure. The company describes structural growth opportunities across power and renewable energy infrastructure.

Corporate development. The company completed its IPO of 79,587,627 equity shares at ₹97 per share, listed on NSE and BSE on 3 September 2025, receiving net proceeds of INR 67,097 lakhs (gross INR 72,100 lakhs less estimated offer-related expenses of INR 5,003 lakhs). The net proceeds were fully utilized: INR 54,100 lakhs for funding working capital requirements and INR 12,997 lakhs for general corporate purposes.

Subsidiaries and acquisitions. The company acquired 100% equity stake in NOPL Solar Projects Private Limited for aggregate consideration of INR 1,000 lakhs, pursuant to Share Purchase Agreements dated 27 April 2026 and 20 May 2026 (for acquiring 49% and 51% equity stake respectively). It subscribed to 100% of paid-up equity share capital of INR 1 lakh in Vikran Renewables Private Limited on its incorporation date of 4 May 2026, and in Vikran For Good Foundation on 4 May 2026 (incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 for charitable and social impact initiatives). Vikran MP Solar Private Limited was incorporated on 22 January 2026 as a wholly-owned subsidiary.

Dividend. The Board recommended a final dividend of 18% of face value, i.e., ₹0.18 per equity share of face value ₹1 each for FY26, subject to shareholder approval.

Capital raising. The Board approved additional fund raising through secured, rated, listed and/or unlisted non-convertible debentures or other debt securities, on private placement and/or public issue basis, in one or more tranches, for an aggregate amount not exceeding ₹400 crore, delegating powers to the Corporate Affairs Committee. The Board also approved enhancement of overall borrowing limits from ₹1,000 crore (fund based) to ₹1,500 crore (fund based), subject to shareholder approval. The Board proposed issuing rated/unlisted/listed non-convertible debentures and commercial papers up to INR 1,000 crores and corporate guarantees up to INR 400 crores for subsidiaries.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management commentary (11 August 2026, Q1 FY27 press release). Chairman & Managing Director Mr. Rakesh Markhedkar stated: “We have commenced FY27 on a positive note, delivering approximately 28% year-on-year revenue growth, while EBITDA grew by around 24% year-on-year, with margins remaining broadly stable. The performance reflects continued execution momentum across our businesses, alongside our focus on maintaining a healthy balance between growth, profitability and financial discipline.” He noted the integration of NOPL Solar Projects marks an important milestone, with immediate focus on timely and disciplined execution while enhancing capabilities in large-scale renewable infrastructure. He stated the core Power T&D business continues to maintain strong momentum, citing the POWERGRID order and the Miao-Namsai commissioning. He stated: “Looking ahead, we remain confident in the structural growth opportunities across power and renewable energy infrastructure. While Solar EPC will continue to be an important growth driver, we are also selectively evaluating emerging opportunities such as Battery Energy Storage Systems and Data Centre infrastructure. Our focus will remain on disciplined growth, timely execution, prudent capital allocation and sustainable long-term value creation for our stakeholders.”

Observed performance versus guidance. The Q1 FY27 reported figures align with the management’s stated revenue growth (~28.2% YoY) and EBITDA growth (~23.7% YoY), with EBITDA margin at 13.7% versus 14.2% in Q1 FY26 — consistent with the description of “margins remaining broadly stable.” The order book of ₹6,496.2 crore as on 11 August 2026 and the order mix (Solar 62%, Power T&D 28%, Water 10%, Railway 1%) reflect the stated strategic emphasis on Solar EPC and Power T&D. The NOPL Solar acquisition and the POWERGRID order are consistent with the stated focus areas. The Ellume Energy cancellation was disclosed as not expected to have material adverse impact, and management reiterated focus on “opportunities that are strategically aligned, execution-ready, and accretive to long-term shareholder value.”
