KHAICHEM: AGM Voting Outcome and Related Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

The 44th Annual General Meeting, held on 21 July 2026 through video conferencing, saw resolutions passed with the requisite majority, but the voting pattern on the special resolution concerning the re-appointment of Shri Utsav Khaitan (DIN: 03021454) as Joint Managing Director (Key Managerial Personnel) stands out as the most contested item. While 92.90% of remote e-voting shares were cast in favour, 7.03% of shares (4,955,469 shares across 4 members) were recorded as invalid votes, and a further 0.07% (52,677 shares across 7 members) were cast against. This level of dissent and invalid voting is materially higher than on any other resolution, where against-votes were generally 0.01%–0.08% and invalid votes were nil.

The resolution to appoint Shri Praveen Uniyal (DIN: 08714038) as a director retiring by rotation also attracted measurable opposition: 56,877 shares (0.08%) voted against across 9 members, though 99.92% were in favour.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The AGM approved an increase in the borrowing limits under Section 180(1)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, with 99.99% of votes in favour (70,532,625 shares) and only 0.01% against (6,069 shares). A parallel special resolution authorised the creation of charges on the Company’s assets to secure borrowings up to Rs. 800 Crores under Section 180(1)(a), also passed with 99.99% support. These approvals provide the Company with enhanced financial flexibility to fund future requirements.

The Company also ratified the remuneration of Cost Auditors for the financial year ending 31 March 2027, with 99.92% of votes in favour (70,481,048 shares) and 0.08% against (56,945 shares).

Key Risks

The concentration of invalid and against votes on the Joint Managing Director re-appointment resolution points to shareholder scrutiny of key managerial personnel decisions. The presence of 4,955,469 invalid shares (7.03%) on this resolution—against zero invalid votes on all other resolutions—indicates either procedural or substantive shareholder concerns specific to this item.

The adoption of audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, including the Balance Sheet, profit and loss statement, cash flow statement, and the Board’s and Auditors’ reports, was approved with 100% of votes in favour (70,538,468 shares) and only 226 shares against across 3 members. The declaration of a dividend of Re. 0.05 per equity share (5% on face value of Re. 1 each) for FY2025-26 was approved with 99.99% support (70,534,512 shares in favour, 4,182 shares against).

Governance and Shareholder Participation

The Company engaged Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL) as the authorised e-voting agency. Remote e-voting was open from 09:00 A.M. on Saturday, 18 July 2026 to 5:00 P.M. on Monday, 20 July 2026. The cut-off date for determining eligible voters was Tuesday, 14 July 2026. Votes were unblocked in the presence of two witnesses, Ms. Shreya Singh and Ms. Pragya Singh, at 12:18 P.M. after the conclusion of the AGM on 21 July 2026.

The Company had 54,289 shareholders on record. Six promoters/promoter group members and 56 public shareholders attended the meeting through video conferencing. No physical meeting or proxy arrangement was made, consistent with the meeting being held through video conferencing/other audio-visual means. All seven resolutions proposed were approved by shareholders with the requisite majority.
