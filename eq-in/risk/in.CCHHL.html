Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates against a backdrop of macroeconomic uncertainty, including global inflation, tightening monetary policies, and geopolitical instability. The West Asia conflict and related disruptions to energy flows and aviation are specifically noted as pressures. Climate risks, in the form of extreme weather affecting operations and supply chains, add further operational uncertainty. Foreign exchange risk arises from the company’s overseas subsidiaries, and there is potential credit risk from deferred vacation ownership payment schemes.

At the segment level, the standalone results show the Hotel & Membership segment recording a loss (PBT) of Rs. 41.10 Lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, following a loss of Rs. 70.40 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and Rs. 343.93 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. On a consolidated basis, the Hotel & Membership segment loss was Rs. 41.10 Lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 1,906.01 Lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 343.93 Lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. For the year ended 31 March 2026, the consolidated Hotel & Membership segment loss was Rs. 2,741.99 Lakhs.

The auditors’ review reports include an Emphasis of Matter noting that investments in subsidiary companies continue at historical cost and not at fair value, in both the standalone and consolidated review reports dated 13 August 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company points to a resurgent MICE tourism sector, growing domestic leisure demand, rising disposable incomes, expansion of the middle class, favourable government policy support such as GST rate reductions on hotel rooms and tourism destination development initiatives, and strong demand for branded hotel rooms outpacing supply growth.

Within the results, the Real Estate segment provides positive momentum. Standalone Real Estate segment revenue was Rs. 521.78 Lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with segment PBT of Rs. 260.00 Lakhs. Consolidated Real Estate segment revenue was also Rs. 521.78 Lakhs with PBT of Rs. 260.00 Lakhs for the same quarter. For the year ended 31 March 2026, standalone Real Estate segment revenue was Rs. 3,910.53 Lakhs with PBT of Rs. 1,733.70 Lakhs. During the June 2026 quarter, the company recognised revenue from the sale of ten flats pertaining to its real estate project “Osadia Realty,” presented separately under Real Estate in segment reporting, distinct from hospitality and other operations.

Key Risks

The identified risks include macroeconomic and geopolitical instability, climate-related operational and supply chain disruption, foreign exchange exposure from overseas subsidiaries, and credit risk from deferred vacation ownership payment schemes. The auditor’s Emphasis of Matter regarding subsidiary investments carried at historical cost rather than fair value is a further point of note. The consolidated results also reflect an exceptional and extraordinary item of Rs. 2,561.54 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, which drove the consolidated loss before tax of Rs. 1,719.40 Lakhs for that quarter and the consolidated net loss of Rs. 1,684.06 Lakhs.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management anticipates the hospitality sector will remain stable, supported by domestic leisure travel and MICE demand, with industry revenues projected to grow 7–9% year-over-year. The company aims to focus on opportunistic growth avenues, fast turnaround deals, optimal financing, and expanding room inventory while maintaining liquidity and strict cost controls.

Observed performance shows a mixed picture. Standalone revenue from operations was Rs. 1,536.42 Lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, down from Rs. 2,402.58 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and from Rs. 1,832.60 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Standalone net profit was Rs. 32.14 Lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 16.09 Lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 164.11 Lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. For the year ended 31 March 2026, standalone net profit was Rs. 42.96 Lakhs.

On a consolidated basis, revenue from operations was Rs. 1,536.42 Lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, against Rs. 2,402.58 Lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 1,832.60 Lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. Consolidated net profit was Rs. 41.89 Lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 25.83 Lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, while the March 2026 quarter recorded a consolidated net loss of Rs. 1,684.06 Lakhs. For the year ended 31 March 2026, the consolidated net loss was Rs. 1,763.41 Lakhs.
