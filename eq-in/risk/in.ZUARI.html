Headwinds and Challenges

  • Derecognition of MCFL and loss of comparability. Following the Composite Scheme of Arrangement between Mangalore Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited (MCFL), Paradeep Phosphates Limited (PPL) and their respective shareholders and creditors, approved by the NCLT Bengaluru and Cuttack Benches by orders dated 24 September 2025 and 25 September 2025, Zuari Agro Chemicals Limited (ZACL) lost control over MCFL effective 26 September 2025. MCFL was amalgamated with PPL, and ZACL received 6,54,33,846 equity shares of PPL in exchange for its MCFL investment. The company states that, as a consequence, the figures for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 are not comparable with the corresponding quarter ended 30 June 2025 for the consolidated financial results.

  • Discontinued operations from the Mahad plant sale. Under a Business Transfer Agreement dated 29 August 2025 with MCFL, ZACL sold and transferred its granulated single super phosphate plant at Mahad, Maharashtra on a slump sale basis for consideration aggregating INR 72.75 crore. The transaction was consummated on 30 September 2025, with a gain of INR 9.32 crore recognised as an exceptional item. The impact of the BTA is disclosed as discontinued operations, and the company has regrouped its financial results for previous comparative quarters.

  • Water/sewerage demand notice. During the quarter, the company received a demand notice of INR 29,645.99 lakh (letter dated 21 April 2026) from the Office of the Executive Engineer WD w DDW Fatorda, Goa, towards arrears of water/sewerage charges. The company independently computed the differential liability and recognised INR 75,81,000 during the year ended 31 March 2026. For the remaining amount, the company disputes the demand and has approached the High Court of Bombay at Goa; the plea was allowed on 14 July 2025 to appoint an Arbitrator to settle the dispute. No provision has been created for the remaining amount.

  • Labour Codes impact. The Government of India notified the four labour codes on 21 November 2025, consolidating 29 existing labour laws. The company assessed the incremental impact based on the best available information and, considering its materiality and regulatory-driven, non-recurring nature, presented it as an Exceptional Item during the year ended 31 March 2026 — a net charge of INR 0.05 crore standalone and INR 4.93 crore consolidated. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, employee benefit expenses have been recognised on an ongoing basis considering the provisions of the Code.

  • Regulatory penalty. An adjudication order from the Registrar of Companies, Goa dated 25 August 2026 imposed a penalty of Rs. 3,00,000 on the Company and Rs. 50,000 each on four present and former KMPs for alleged non-compliance with Section 134(3)(c) read with Section 129(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 regarding the Directors’ Responsibility Statement for the financial year ended 31 March 2021. The disclosure states there is no material financial or operational impact on the Company.

  • Related-party ICD extension. The Board approved seeking extension of time for repayment of Inter Corporate Deposits availed from related party Zuari Industries Limited amounting to Rs. 95 crore for a further period of six months from their respective maturity dates.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strategic business evaluation. The company states it is currently evaluating new strategic business opportunities to strengthen its operational and financial position and create sustainable revenue streams.

  • Joint venture contribution. The consolidated results include the Group’s share of net profit after tax of Rs. 91.50 crore and share of total comprehensive income of Rs. 91.66 crore for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from one joint venture, as considered in the Statement and reviewed by the respective independent auditor.

  • Subsidiary performance. One subsidiary’s financial results reflect total revenues of Rs. 615.81 crore, total net profit after tax of Rs. 44.28 crore and total comprehensive income of Rs. 44.28 crore for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

  • Related party transaction approval. The 17th AGM approved material related party transactions with Paradeep Phosphates Limited up to Rs. 550 crore.

  • Governance continuity. The 17th AGM, held on 16 September 2026, transacted adoption of financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, re-appointment of Mr. Saroj Kumar Poddar and Statutory Auditors M/s. K.P. Rao & Co., and continuation of Mr. Saroj Kumar Poddar as Chairman. The Board also approved the appointment of M/s. SNSK & Associates as Internal Auditors for FY 2026-27.

Key Risks

  • Dispute risk on the water/sewerage demand. The remaining disputed amount has not been provided for; the outcome depends on arbitration.

  • Regulatory compliance risk. The RoC adjudication order relates to the Directors’ Responsibility Statement for FY 2021, indicating exposure to penalty for alleged non-compliance.

  • Related-party funding dependence. The ICD extension from Zuari Industries Limited indicates reliance on related-party borrowings for repayment timelines.

  • Comparability and structural change risk. The MCFL derecognition, amalgamation into PPL, and the Mahad slump sale materially change the Group’s composition and reported figures, limiting comparability with prior periods.

  • Labour code transition risk. The notified labour codes required assessment of incremental financial impact, with ongoing recognition of employee benefit expenses under the Code.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • The company assessed the incremental impact of the labour codes based on the best information available, consistent with ICAI guidance, and treated it as a non-recurring exceptional item for FY 2025-26; for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, employee benefit expenses are recognised on an ongoing basis under the Code.

  • The company recognised only INR 75,81,000 of the water/sewerage demand and created no provision for the remaining amount, reflecting its position that the demand is disputed and subject to arbitration.

  • The Board approved seeking a six-month extension for repayment of Rs. 95 crore of ICDs from Zuari Industries Limited from their respective maturity dates.

  • The statutory auditors issued an unmodified limited review report on both the standalone and consolidated unaudited results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
