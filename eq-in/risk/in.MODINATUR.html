MODINATUR: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Ethanol Division revenue pressure. Ethanol segment revenue declined 6% year-on-year in Q1FY27 (₹74.8 crore versus ₹79.4 crore in Q1FY26). Management attributed this to a planned maintenance shutdown of approximately 20 days during the quarter and to lower FCI rice prices. The shutdown also affected the division’s ability to produce and dispatch during the period.

Bulk Division commodity weakness. Commodity prices in the Bulk segment remained lower. Revenue in the division was ₹31.2 crore in Q1FY26 rising to ₹38.3 crore in Q1FY27, but the business continues to operate in a low-price commodity environment. Management described a conscious shift to a leaner inventory model to improve procurement discipline and reduce inventory intensity.

Consumer Division revenue softness. Branded/Consumer revenue declined 4% year-on-year to ₹42.5 crore in Q1FY27 from ₹44.4 crore in Q1FY26, despite margin improvement. The division continues to face lower commodity prices.

Sequential decline from Q4FY26. Consolidated revenue fell 36.0% quarter-on-quarter to ₹155.6 crore in Q1FY27 from ₹243.1 crore in Q4FY26. EBITDA declined 9.3% QoQ to ₹22.2 crore, and PAT declined 36.5% QoQ to ₹12.5 crore. Q4FY26 included an exceptional item of ₹4.9 crore (insurance claim for loss of profit from a FY24 machinery breakdown at the Ethanol plant).

Compliance lapse. The company received an email from BSE’s Listing Compliance Monitoring Team dated August 05, 2026 seeking clarification on delayed submission of the proceedings of the 52nd AGM held on July 31, 2026. The company acknowledged the error as unintentional, apologized, and resubmitted the proceedings.

Governance/attendance note. At the 52nd AGM, Chairman Anil Modi was unable to attend due to ill health; Akshay Modi chaired the meeting. Director Ankita Singal and CFO Pradeep Kapoor could not join due to unavoidable circumstances.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Additional ethanol order. On August 17, 2026, Modi Naturals announced that its wholly owned subsidiary Modi Biotech Private Limited received an additional allocation of 1.98 crore litres of ethanol for ESY 2025-26, with an estimated order value of ₹140 crore, to be supplied until October 2026. This is in addition to the existing order book of approximately ₹400 crore for ~47.9k KL from various OMCs.

Ethanol capacity expansion. Phase I capacity of 130 KLPD commenced in Q3FY24 (capex ₹125 crore). Phase II of 152 KLPD commenced in Q4FY26 (capex ₹91 crore), taking total capacity to 282 KLPD — an increase of approximately 117% over FY26. Both existing and expanded plants are expected to be fully operational by end-August 2026, supporting execution of the additional order book and progressive ramp-up over the balance ESY period.

Ethanol margin expansion. Despite lower revenue, Ethanol EBITDA margin expanded 500 bps to 22.2% in Q1FY27 from 17.1% in Q1FY26, driven by better plant performance and improved efficiencies. Ethanol EBITDA grew 22% to ₹16.6 crore from ₹13.6 crore.

Consumer Division momentum. Management reported encouraging response to newer categories: pasta performing well on quick commerce platforms, and the launch of Hing receiving strong market response. Consumer EBITDA margin improved to 9.3% in Q1FY27 from 8.8% in Q1FY26. Distribution expansion continues across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities.

Bulk Division operational improvement. Bulk EBITDA grew 71% to ₹1.3 crore in Q1FY27 from ₹0.8 crore in Q1FY26, with EBITDA margin improving to 3.4% from 2.4%. The leaner inventory model is yielding benefits in procurement planning and inventory turnover.

New product pipeline. The company is extending its pasta range (macaroni, spaghetti, multi-grain pasta), launching Oleev Junior Peanut Butter targeted at children aged 4–13, extending the PIPO brand into ready-to-eat flavoured roasted peanuts, and has soft-launched Jynx ready-to-mix beverages.

Ethanol policy tailwind. The presentation cites rising ethanol blending targets (E20 and beyond), with grain-based ethanol capacity required to treble over the next three years as molasses-based ethanol is relatively limited. Modi Biotech is described as one of the first greenfield grain-based ethanol distilleries in Chhattisgarh.

FY27 guidance. Management has provided FY27E guidance of revenue ₹925–965 crore, EBITDA ₹100–105 crore, and PAT ₹66–70 crore, compared to FY26 actuals of ₹719 crore revenue, ₹74 crore EBITDA, and ₹50 crore PAT (including the ₹4.9 crore insurance claim).

3. Key Risks

Commodity price exposure. The Bulk and Consumer divisions operate in edible oil commodity markets where prices have remained lower. The Ethanol division’s revenue was impacted by lower FCI rice prices. Continued commodity volatility could affect realisations and margins.

Execution risk on expanded ethanol capacity. The additional ₹140 crore order book and the ramp-up to 282 KLPD depend on both existing and expanded plants being fully operational by end-August 2026. Any delay in commissioning or stabilisation could affect the ability to execute the order book within the ESY period ending October 2026.

Concentration in ethanol order book. The ethanol business relies on allocations from various OMCs under the EBPP. The additional allocation is for the balance ESY period through October 2026, creating a defined execution window.

Working capital and leverage. Consolidated borrowings increased to ₹120.4 crore (non-current) and ₹40.9 crore (current) as of March 2026, compared to ₹70.8 crore and ₹78.0 crore respectively as of March 2025. Finance costs rose to ₹3.0 crore in Q1FY27 from ₹2.3 crore in Q1FY26. The company continues to focus on lower working capital intensity.

Regulatory and compliance risk. The delayed AGM proceedings submission and the BSE query highlight compliance monitoring. The company stated it has been regular in adhering to Listing Regulations and will be careful in future.

Segment asset concentration. Ethanol segment assets stood at ₹295.2 crore as of June 2026, representing the largest share of consolidated assets of ₹446.7 crore. This concentration exposes the company to ethanol-specific operational and market risks.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

FY27 guidance context. Management provided FY27E guidance of revenue ₹925–965 crore, EBITDA ₹100–105 crore, and PAT ₹66–70 crore. FY26 actuals were ₹719.2 crore revenue, ₹73.5 crore EBITDA, and ₹50.3 crore PAT (including ₹4.9 crore exceptional insurance claim). The guidance implies significant growth, particularly in EBITDA and PAT.

Q1FY27 performance against guidance trajectory. Q1FY27 consolidated revenue was ₹155.6 crore, broadly stable year-on-year (up 0.4%). EBITDA grew 25.6% to ₹22.2 crore, and PAT grew 19.1% to ₹12.5 crore. Management stated it has “started FY27 on a positive note” and remains “confident of delivering on the guidance we have provided for FY27.”

Ethanol division. Management stated the underlying operating performance has improved significantly, with EBITDA margin expanding 500 bps to 22.2%. The division is expected to benefit from full capacity operation from end-August 2026 and execution of the additional ₹140 crore order book. Management expects higher utilisation to drive operating leverage and profitability over the medium term.

Consumer division. Management stated the division “continues to show good traction” with momentum improving. EBITDA margin improved to 9.3% from 8.8%. Growth is expected from increasing reach, strengthening the existing portfolio, and scaling newer categories.

Bulk division. Management stated it remains “focused on further improving the quality of the Bulk business” through the leaner inventory model. The division showed EBITDA growth of 71% year-on-year in Q1FY27.

Capacity expansion timeline. Management stated both existing and expanded plants are expected to be fully operational by end-August 2026. The additional ethanol order is to be supplied until October 2026, providing a clear execution window for the expanded capacity.

Historical performance context. Consolidated revenue grew from ₹399.8 crore in FY24 to ₹662.9 crore in FY25 to ₹719.2 crore in FY26. EBITDA grew from ₹9.1 crore in FY24 to ₹56.0 crore in FY25 to ₹73.5 crore in FY26. PAT moved from a loss of ₹1.4 crore in FY24 to ₹31.0 crore in FY25 to ₹50.3 crore in FY26. The FY27 guidance of ₹925–965 crore revenue would represent growth of approximately 29–34% over FY26.
