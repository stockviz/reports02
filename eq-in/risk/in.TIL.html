TIL Limited: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Losses at the PBT and PAT level persist. On a consolidated basis, Q1 FY27 PBT was ₹(7.31) Cr and PAT was ₹(5.45) Cr, compared with PBT of ₹(10.29) Cr and PAT of ₹(6.22) Cr in Q1 FY26 and PBT of ₹(11.74) Cr and PAT of ₹(9.97) Cr in Q4 FY26. Standalone loss for the quarter was ₹(716) lakhs, against ₹(622) lakhs in Q1 FY26 and ₹(1,008) lakhs in Q4 FY26.
  • Finance costs remain a material drag. Standalone finance costs were ₹1,050 lakhs in Q1 FY27, higher than ₹956 lakhs in Q1 FY26. The Chairman and Managing Director explicitly referenced the need for a “step change” in the cost structure “including material costs and the incidence of finance cost.”
  • Material handling segment loss. The consolidated segment result for Material Handling Solution was ₹(996) lakhs in Q1 FY27, versus ₹(1,174) lakhs in Q4 FY26 and ₹(1,809) lakhs in Q1 FY26.
  • Deferred tax asset dependence. Deferred tax assets (net) of ₹10,919 lakhs, including ₹249 lakhs recognised for the period, are carried primarily against unused tax losses. The auditors drew attention to this as an Emphasis of Matter, noting management’s confidence, based on projected book and taxable profits, that sufficient future taxable profits will be available to adjust them.
  • Legacy tax and regulatory matters. A SEBI adjudicating officer imposed a ₹100 lakh fine and penalty by order dated 30 May 2024 relating to earlier years under sections 15HA and 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992. The Securities Appellate Tribunal, Mumbai has stayed the operation of the order pending the next hearing, and no provision has been made. Separately, the company paid ₹42 lakhs in the quarter towards earlier-year West Bengal Entry Tax and VAT/Sales tax demands under the West Bengal Sales Tax (Settlement of Dispute) Act, 2025, disclosed as an exceptional item; ₹474 lakhs and ₹84 lakhs were paid in FY2025-26.
  • Prior-period audit qualification. The review report notes that the audit of standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026 was carried out by M/s. Singhi & Co., who expressed a qualified opinion in their audit report dated 28 May 2026.
  • Partly paid rights shares and pending call money. Rights shares allotted on 9 April 2026 were partly paid (₹7.5 paid up per ₹10 share). The first and final call money period closed on 3 July 2026, and the Rights Issue Committee on 9 July 2026 approved allotment of fully paid shares to eligible shareholders who had paid, while approving reminders to shareholders who had not. A second reminder was sent on 19 August 2026, with acceptance from 21 August 2026 to 4 September 2026 through Non-ASBA mode only. Listing and trading of these shares will be applied for only after closure of acceptance on 4 September 2026.
  • Reported challenges from management commentary: geopolitical and logistics challenges impacting turnover materialisation, a stressed working capital cycle with high debtor days at the end of the year, dominant historical dependence on overseas supply chains, and the need to optimise material costs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong Q1 FY27 growth. Consolidated total revenue was ₹117 Cr (₹11,713 lakhs), up from ₹71 Cr in Q1 FY26 and ₹109 Cr in Q4 FY26. Consolidated EBITDA was ₹7.32 Cr at a 6.2% margin, versus ₹1.05 Cr at 1.5% in Q1 FY26 and ₹10.49 Cr at 9.6% in Q4 FY26.
  • Standalone performance. Standalone revenue from operations rose from ₹63 Cr to ₹79 Cr (₹7,861 lakhs), up ~25% YoY. Standalone EBITDA rose from ₹1.05 Cr to ₹3.39 Cr, up ~223% YoY, with margin expanding from 1.5% to 4.3% (~280 bps). Total operating expenses rose only ~8% against ~25% revenue growth.
  • TCPL acquisition and consolidation. Shareholders approved the acquisition at an EGM on 14 March 2026; TIL acquired 37,90,250 equity shares (60% of TCPL’s equity) for ₹11,901.38 lakhs from Gainwell Commosales Private Limited, with TCPL becoming a subsidiary effective 8 May 2026. TCPL standalone revenue was ₹39 Cr with EBITDA of ₹3.96 Cr for the part quarter. TCPL adds segments in packaging, erection, commissioning and O&M of gas compressors, with offerings including Composite CNG Dispensing Units, hydrogen compression systems, and the Freespin® Inline Turboexpander.
  • Order book and pipeline. TIL standalone order book is ₹211 Cr (prime products ₹187 Cr; aftermarket ₹24 Cr), with an enquiry pipeline of ₹373 Cr (prime products ₹356 Cr; aftermarket ₹17 Cr). Consolidated order book is ₹539 Cr (prime products ₹406 Cr; aftermarket ₹133 Cr), with a consolidated enquiry pipeline of ₹490 Cr (prime products ₹451 Cr; aftermarket ₹39 Cr).
  • Reach Stacker execution and market share ambition. Nine Reach Stacker units were completed and delivered in Q1 FY27 — the highest single-quarter delivery since new management took over. The company is targeting a 38%–40% market share in the segment in the coming year.
  • Aftermarket focus. Both companies are placing increasing strategic emphasis on aftermarket as a stable, high-margin revenue stream.
  • Certifications at TCPL. TCPL holds PESO certification for the Indian market and has secured SONCAP certification and formal compliance to ISO 16923 across its compression product portfolio, enabling work in Nigeria and strengthening standing in India’s City Gas Distribution sector and international markets.
  • Diversification positioning. Management describes TIL as transforming into a diversified indigenous capital goods engineering company spanning defence, material handling, and clean energy, aligned with India’s infrastructure, defence, and clean energy priorities under Atmanirbhar Bharat.
  • Balance sheet and funding. Consolidated net worth stood at ₹272 Cr as of 30 June 2026. The rights issue allotted on 9 April 2026 increased paid-up equity share capital from ₹7,035 lakhs to ₹7,941.88 lakhs. A warrant issuance raised ₹60 Cr on 28 January 2026, with no deviation or variation in use of funds reported for the quarter ended 30 June 2026; of the capital expenditure allocation of ₹3,600 lakhs, ₹188 lakhs had been utilised, leaving ₹3,321.96 lakhs to be utilised in a phased manner.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued PBT/PAT losses and reliance on future taxable profits to support the carrying value of deferred tax assets.
  • Finance costs and material costs remain elevated relative to the profitability turnaround sought.
  • The SEBI penalty matter remains unresolved, with a stay in place and no provision made.
  • Execution and integration risk associated with the TCPL acquisition, which has been consolidated only from 8 May 2026 and has not completed a full quarter within TIL.
  • Working capital stress, including high debtor days noted at the end of the year, and geopolitical/logistics challenges affecting turnover materialisation.
  • Dependence on overseas supply chains and the need to optimise material costs.
  • Rights issue call money remains outstanding from some shareholders; listing and trading of the relevant shares is contingent on completion of the acceptance period ending 4 September 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management expects order book conversion, deeper TCPL integration, synergistic growth, and continued aftermarket momentum to support sustained revenue and margin growth through FY27. Observed: Q1 FY27 showed consolidated revenue of ₹117 Cr and EBITDA margin of 6.2%, with standalone revenue up ~25% and standalone EBITDA up ~223% YoY, alongside nine Reach Stacker deliveries.
  • Guidance: Management targets tripling turnover over five to seven years and increasing aftermarket revenue contribution to 40%–50%. Observed: Aftermarket currently contributes ₹24 Cr of TIL’s ₹211 Cr standalone order book and ₹133 Cr of the ₹539 Cr consolidated order book.
  • Guidance: Medium-to-long-term EBITDA margin range of 15%–16% for TIL standalone and 14%–15% for Tulip. Observed: Q1 FY27 standalone EBITDA margin was 4.3% and consolidated margin was 6.2%.
  • Guidance: Plans to increase equity stake in Tulip up to 74%, supported by a board-approved ₹50 Cr equity infusion. Observed: TIL currently holds 60% of TCPL following the 8 May 2026 acquisition.
  • Guidance: Management remains focused on attaining scale with strong profitability and a step change in cost structure, including material costs and finance cost incidence, and on building a more profitable, cash-generative and financially resilient business. Observed: Total operating expenses rose ~8% against ~25% standalone revenue growth, while finance costs rose to ₹1,050 lakhs from ₹956 lakhs YoY.
  • Guidance: The company will apply for further listing and trading of the rights shares after closure of the call money acceptance period on 4 September 2026. Observed: A second reminder was sent on 19 August 2026, with acceptance from 21 August 2026 to 4 September 2026 through Non-ASBA mode only.
  • Guidance: Management is confident that sufficient taxable profits will be available in future against which deferred tax assets can be adjusted. Observed: The company reported a consolidated loss before tax of ₹(7.31) Cr and PAT of ₹(5.45) Cr in Q1 FY27.
