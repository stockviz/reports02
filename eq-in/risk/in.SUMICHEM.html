Analysis of Sumitomo Chemical India Ltd. (SUMICHEM) - Q2 & H1 FY26

Headwinds (Challenges Observed in Period): - Weather Disruptions: Prolonged heavy rains from mid-July to September severely curtailed farmer field access and spray cycles in key Kharif crops (rice, cotton, soybean), causing ~6% YoY revenue decline in Q2 (Rs 930 Cr vs Rs 988 Cr). Volumes contracted; industry-wide inventory buildup and channel pressures noted. - Export Softness: 4% YoY decline in H1 exports (15% of revenue), driven by shipment deferrals in Brazil/LatAm (-33% YoY) and muted Africa demand (Kenya, Ethiopia). Global agrochem destocking lingers. - Regulatory Hurdles: Biostimulant approvals delayed sales (PGR share fell from 11% to 9%); Barrix subsidiary profitability hit by input costs and regs. Glyphosate usage restrictions pending court resolution. - Margin Pressure: Q2 EBITDA margin dipped to 23.4% (-140 bps YoY) due to lower operating leverage; H1 stable at 22%.

Tailwinds (Positive Supports): - Resilient H1 Performance: Revenue +9% YoY (Rs 1,987 Cr), PAT +11% (Rs 356 Cr); domestic +11% (85% of revenue). No sales returns/pricing cuts despite weather; gross margins steady at 40.4%. - Strong Balance Sheet: Rs 2,089 Cr cash (no debt); NWC days improved to 55 (-7 YoY, -34 QoQ). Collections robust (Rs 2,277 Cr in H1 vs Rs 1,999 Cr prior). - Product Traction: New launches (Lentigo herbicide, Excalia Max fungicide) exceeded targets despite weather; core brands (e.g., Mera 71 at record 3,000 MT in H1) resilient. Specialties ~30% of mix. - Rabi Setup: Full reservoirs, moist soil; IMD forecasts non-disruptive rains.

Growth Prospects: - Domestic Leadership: Scale EDFD (61k+ farmer/channel connects, +35% YoY); 4-5 annual launches from SCC pipeline (e.g., Top Grain in H2). Specialties/basic segments balanced; aim >industry growth via farmer engagement/digital/phygital tools. - Manufacturing Expansion: Rs 65 Cr capex (Bhavnagar doubling, Tarapur for new SCC molecule; online Q4FY27). Dahej greenfield (Rs 250-600 Cr over 5 yrs, revenue from CY28) for SCC global molecules/intermediates. - Exports/Global: Revival in H2 (Africa Dec-Jan, LatAm positive); leverage SCC network for US/Europe/Asia. Semiconductor chemicals monitored (linked to India fabs like Tata Dholera). - Long-Term: 14x revenue growth (FY11-25); SCC positioning India as #1 test market for new molecules. Cash deployment for capex/other opportunities.

Key Risks: - Climatic/Seasonal: High monsoon dependency (70% Kharif consumption); Rabi rains could disrupt. - Regulatory/Policy: Biostimulant/Glyphosate delays; animal nutrition discontinuation (low-margin, non-material). - Execution: Capex delays (Japan approvals, Dahej 2028); export tariffs/channel pressures. - Competition/Geopolitical: Chinese pricing in generics/exports; LatAm/Africa volatility. - Macro: Input costs, forex; Barrix subsidiary drag (steady topline, profitability hit). - Subsidiary Exposure: Unreviewed financials immaterial but add consolidation risks.

Overall Summary: Despite Q2 weather headwinds, H1 resilience underscores strong fundamentals (cash-rich, margin discipline). Tailwinds from new products/Rabi setup/manufacturing scale position for H2 recovery and mid-teens growth. Risks remain weather/regulatory-tied, but de-risked portfolio and SCC backing mitigate. FY26 guidance intact: maintain/improve margins, responsible growth.

Broker Narrative

The narrative shifted from an initial broad demand slump spanning domestic and export markets, China-reopening pricing pressure, and weak regional exports to a more selective recovery in which exports became a growth engine while domestic demand remained constrained by monsoon delays and channel inventory. Weather, channel overhang, and margin repair were recurring themes from the first to the last report, but optimism increasingly centered on product launches, margin expansion, parent SCC support, and new semiconductor-chemicals optionality. Broker calls stayed Buy/Accumulate throughout, with the last report still flagging domestic softness even as profitability and exports improved.

Fears that came true

  • The adverse/delayed monsoon risk flagged in the first report recurred: the last report still cited a delayed South-West monsoon, 40% rainfall deficit, lower reservoir levels and ~20% lower kharif sowing, a backdrop that accompanied the -14% actual return on the Aug-2025 Buy calls.
  • The early warning on high carryover/channel inventory materialized: the last report again blamed elevated channel inventory from advance purchases and price corrections for weighing on domestic demand and herbicide revenue.
  • The subdued domestic demand theme persisted: the first report cited domestic revenue down 10% YoY, and the last report showed domestic revenue down ~3% YoY with softness across herbicides, fungicides and PGRs.
