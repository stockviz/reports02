Headwinds and Challenges

  • Withdrawal of the AIF business transfer. The proposal to transfer the fund/asset management services and advisory functions, and the investment manager role for the AIFs, to ITI Asset Management Limited (a wholly owned subsidiary) has been withdrawn. The Board had originally approved the transfer on May 9, 2025 as part of an internal restructuring to consolidate similar asset/fund management activities under ITI AMC. The approvals obtained for the AIF businesses have since lapsed, and the company decided not to proceed at this stage. Consequently, the Agreement to Transfer Business and related arrangements will not be proceeded with. The Investment Trust of India Limited will continue as Sponsor and Investment Manager for ITI Long Short Fund (a SEBI-registered Category III Alternative Investment Fund). If ITI AMC proposes to undertake the AIF business in future, a fresh SEBI application would be required.
  • Financing activities segment pressure. Segment results for financing activities fell to a loss of Rs. 64.80 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a profit of Rs. 442.07 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 1,345.23 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Broking segment moderation. Broking and related services segment revenue declined to Rs. 3,588.96 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 4,027.03 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, with segment profit before interest and tax at Rs. 659.77 lakhs versus Rs. 1,023.62 lakhs.
  • Lower consolidated revenue and profit year-on-year. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 5,936.00 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, against Rs. 7,010.07 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Net profit after tax was Rs. 242.44 lakhs versus Rs. 291.89 lakhs, and total comprehensive income was Rs. 1,251.87 lakhs versus Rs. 1,016.39 lakhs.
  • Standalone loss history. On a standalone basis, the company reported a loss before tax of Rs. 221.16 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026, and a loss after tax of Rs. 161.89 lakhs, although the quarter ended June 30, 2026 returned to a profit after tax of Rs. 20.39 lakhs.
  • Scheme execution dependency. The Scheme of Arrangement for amalgamation of ITI Gilts Limited, ITI Wealth Management Limited, ITI Alternate Funds Management Limited and Fortune Management Advisors Limited into the company (appointed date April 1, 2026) remains subject to regulatory approvals; its effect will be given only when all approvals are received and the Scheme becomes effective.
  • Comparability issues. ITI Gold Loans Limited became an associate with effect from November 30, 2025, so figures for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 are not comparable with corresponding previous periods.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Asset management segment recovery. Asset management activities posted a segment profit before interest and tax of Rs. 409.32 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a loss of Rs. 986.46 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and a profit of Rs. 94.89 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Segment revenue rose to Rs. 1,754.69 lakhs from Rs. 649.29 lakhs and Rs. 1,313.03 lakhs respectively.
  • Investment and advisory services improvement. This segment returned to profit at Rs. 197.74 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, against a loss of Rs. 21.08 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, with revenue of Rs. 1,101.08 lakhs versus Rs. 593.93 lakhs.
  • Continued AIF franchise. The company retains its position as Sponsor and Investment Manager for ITI Long Short Fund, a SEBI-registered Category III Alternative Investment Fund.
  • Related party funding and expansion support. The company proposes entering into material related party transactions, inter-corporate deposits, corporate guarantees, direct assignments, and equity infusions with subsidiaries and associates to support funding requirements, business expansion, capital deployment, and efficient treasury management.
  • Capital deployment capacity. Shareholder approval is sought to advance loans, give guarantees, or provide securities up to Rs. 3.15 Crores under Section 185 of the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Group consolidation. The proposed amalgamation of four wholly owned subsidiaries into the company is intended to consolidate similar activities, with an appointed date of April 1, 2026.

Key Risks

  • Lapsed AIF transfer approvals and the decision not to proceed mean the intended consolidation of asset/fund management activities under ITI AMC has not been achieved at this stage.
  • The Scheme of Arrangement is subject to regulatory approvals, and its accounting effect depends on the Scheme becoming effective.
  • Segment performance is uneven: financing activities swung to a loss in the June 2026 quarter, and broking revenue and profit moderated sequentially.
  • Consolidated total expenses of Rs. 5,643.99 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter included finance costs of Rs. 288.09 lakhs and other expenses of Rs. 2,325.65 lakhs.
  • The limited review relied on other auditors for eleven subsidiaries and three step-down subsidiaries, whose interim financial information reflected Group’s share of total assets of Rs. 89,182.48 lakhs as at June 30, 2026, Group’s share of total revenues of Rs. 4,625.70 lakhs, and Group’s share of total net profit after tax of Rs. 1,277.06 lakhs for the quarter; one associate’s share of net profit after tax of Rs. 496.40 lakhs was also not reviewed by the statutory auditors.
  • The figures for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 are balancing figures between audited full-year results and reviewed but unaudited nine-month figures.
