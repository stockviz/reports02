Broker Narrative

The narrative moved from a cautious Hold worried about profit moderation, distribution losses, gas-trading weakness and PSP regulatory ambiguity to a Buy celebrating margin expansion, adjusted PAT growth, regulatory ROE upside and fuel security. The recurring thread was distribution and execution: early distribution losses morphed into distribution margin expansion, while PSP/regulatory clarity concerns became renewable/transmission execution monitorables. Merchant/volume weakness remained a worry, but the final report framed it as an offset within a broader cost-led earnings recovery.

Fears that came true

  • The early warning that profit would moderate as merchant/volume contribution declined materialized: FY26 revenue fell 0.7% and reported PAT fell 19.3%, and the stock returned -28.5% after the first Hold call.
  • The early risk around project execution and timelines persisted, as the final report still flags timely renewable commissioning and transmission connectivity as key operational monitorables.

Optimism that failed

  • The optimism on strong merchant sales and volume growth did not sustain: FY26 was hit by lower power generation and subdued DISCOM offtake, driving a decline in consolidated revenue.
  • The early belief that locked-in renewable capacity and further additions would drive growth did not protect profitability, as the final report cites lower profitability in the generation and renewable businesses.
