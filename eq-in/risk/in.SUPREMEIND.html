SUPREMEIND: Recent Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Polymer price volatility: The first quarter of FY27 was impacted by extraordinary volatility in polymer prices, particularly an abnormal downward price movement during April 2026, which triggered inventory correction across the value chain. This affected industry volumes during the quarter.
  • Volume decline: Plastic goods sold fell to 157,536 MT in Q1 FY27 from 183,793 MT in Q1 FY26, a decline of 14.29%.
  • Rising costs: Employee benefit expenses rose 21.13% year-on-year (₹134.57 crores to ₹163.00 crores), depreciation increased 31.50% (₹93.03 crores to ₹122.33 crores), and finance costs rose 50.72% (₹2.76 crores to ₹4.16 crores).
  • Lower other income: Other income declined 46.01% year-on-year to ₹9.13 crores in Q1 FY27.
  • Cash surplus reduction: Total cash surplus stood at ₹542 crores as on 30th June 2026, down from ₹648 crores as on 31st March 2026.
  • Segment pressure: The “Others” segment reported a loss at the segment level, with segment results of negative ₹7.74 crores in Q1 FY27, compared with negative ₹0.16 crores in Q1 FY26.
  • Consumer Products weakness: Segment revenue for Consumer Products declined to ₹87.36 crores in Q1 FY27 from ₹98.39 crores in Q1 FY26, with segment results falling to ₹9.86 crores from ₹13.81 crores.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Value-added products growth: Turnover of value-added products increased to ₹1,142 crores in Q1 FY27 from ₹933 crores in the corresponding quarter of the previous year, a growth of 22%.
  • Profitability improvement: Operating profit rose 24.73% year-on-year to ₹398.04 crores (standalone), with operating margin expanding to 14.60% from 12.15%. Consolidated operating profit rose 36.89% to ₹471.00 crores, with margin at 17.27%.
  • Debt-free balance sheet: The company is debt free with a cash surplus of ₹542 crores as at 30th June 2026.
  • Polymer price stabilisation and policy changes: With polymer prices returning to more stable levels, removal of customs duty exemption with effect from 16th July 2026, and implementation of Minimum Import Prices (MIP) for suspension grade PVC resin, the company expects improved business momentum in coming quarters as channel inventories normalise and demand recovers.
  • Structural demand drivers: India’s infrastructure development, urbanisation, water management projects, and expansion of gas distribution networks are expected to support demand over the medium and long term.
  • Export opportunities via FTAs: India’s expanding network of FTAs covering UAE, Australia, EFTA nations, the UK, the EU and other strategic markets provides an opportunity to enhance export footprint. The company is investing in product development, certifications and market access initiatives.
  • Plastic Piping Systems expansion: Total installed capacity in this division has reached one million MT per annum. New products introduced include PERT Pipe Systems, Electrofusion Olefin Fittings, Industrial Valves and PP Silent Pipe Systems. The integration of the Wavin business enhances technology and product capabilities.
  • Composite Cylinders momentum: With the addition of BPCL and participation in the HPCL bid, all three Oil Marketing Companies have started procuring composite LPG cylinders for domestic requirements, paving the way for better capacity utilisation. The division has also commenced supplying cascades for CNG applications and continues expanding in international markets.
  • New business vertical: The company launched its new uPVC Windows & Doors business vertical, incorporating advanced European technology, with encouraging market response.
  • Material Handling: The business continued to perform steadily, and the company commenced production of PP Bubble Guard Sheets.
  • Packaging segment: The packaging segment (Cross Laminated film, Protective Packaging Products, Performance Packaging Film) is faring well and is on a path of moderate growth.
  • Capacity expansion: The company is progressing with plans to establish manufacturing facilities at new locations including Bihar, Jammu and Malanpur (near Gwalior), and is acquiring additional land at Puducherry and Erode.
  • Sustainability credentials: Renewable energy mix was 29% in FY25-26, with a target to increase to 35%. Solar capacity stands at 42.32 MWp with RE investment of ₹140 crores. The company is the first Indian company in the building product category with an SBTi Net Zero Target validated. 88,829 TCo2e emission was reduced during FY25-26.
  • Strong credit rating: AA+ CRISIL Stable Rating.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material price risk: Volatility in polymer prices, as witnessed in April 2026, can trigger inventory corrections across the value chain and affect volumes.
  • Demand recovery uncertainty: The company’s expectation of improved business momentum depends on channel inventories normalising and demand conditions recovering.
  • Margin sensitivity: Profitability is sensitive to raw material cost movements, as evidenced by the impact of polymer price volatility on Q1 FY27 volumes.
  • Segment concentration: Plastic Piping Products remains the dominant segment, contributing ₹1,790.88 crores of the ₹2,717.66 crores consolidated revenue in Q1 FY27.
  • Loss-making “Others” segment: The Others segment continued to report negative segment results.
  • Dependence on associate performance: Consolidated results include share of profit of an associate (Supreme Petrochem Limited, 30.78% equity), contributing ₹73.05 crores in Q1 FY27.
  • Execution risk on expansion: The company’s growth plans involve capacity expansion across multiple new locations, which carry execution and timing risks.
  • Export market access: Realisation of FTA-related export opportunities depends on product development, certifications and market access initiatives.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management commentary on Q1 FY27: The Chairman stated that the first quarter was impacted by extraordinary polymer price volatility and the abnormal downward price movement in April 2026, which triggered inventory correction. Management described this as a temporary phenomenon and expected improved business momentum in coming quarters as channel inventories normalise and demand conditions recover.
  • Observed performance: Q1 FY27 standalone revenue from operations grew 4.16% year-on-year to ₹2,717.66 crores, while volumes declined 14.29% to 157,536 MT. Operating profit grew 24.73% to ₹398.04 crores, with margin expanding to 14.65%. PAT grew 17.14% to ₹207.76 crores. Consolidated PAT grew 38.76% to ₹280.72 crores.
  • Value-added products: Management’s focus on value-added products is reflected in the 22% growth in value-added product turnover to ₹1,142 crores in Q1 FY27.
  • Composite cylinders: Management’s statement that all three Oil Marketing Companies have started procuring composite LPG cylinders aligns with the reported addition of BPCL and participation in the HPCL bid.
  • Plastic Piping capacity: Management’s statement that installed capacity has reached one million MT per annum is consistent with the segment’s revenue of ₹1,790.88 crores in Q1 FY27.
  • Cash position: Management’s statement on maintaining a robust financial position is reflected in the debt-free status and cash surplus of ₹542 crores as at 30th June 2026, though this is lower than the ₹648 crores as at 31st March 2026.
  • Dividend: The 84th AGM held on 3rd July 2026 passed an ordinary resolution to declare final dividend on equity shares for FY2025-26 and confirm payment of interim dividend, with 99.99% votes in favour.
  • Auditor appointment: MSKA & Associates LLP was appointed as Statutory Auditors for a term of five years from the conclusion of the 84th AGM till the conclusion of the 89th AGM, with 99.99% votes in favour.
  • Director reappointment: The special resolution to reappoint Shri V.K. Taparia as Director received 91.09% votes in favour and 8.91% against, with public institutions voting 77.77% in favour and 22.23% against.
  • Investor engagement: The company scheduled analyst/institutional investor meets on 13th August 2026 with SBI MF, UTI MF, Bajaj Life, Kotak Group, ICICI Pru MF, SBI Life Insurance, DSP MF, SBI Pension, IndusInd, Nippon Life Insurance, Star Union Dai-ichi Life Insurance, Sage One Investment Advisors LLP, Raay Investments, Canara Robeco Mutual Fund, 3P Investment Managers, and Likus Asset Managers.
