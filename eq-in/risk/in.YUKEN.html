Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential moderation in profitability: Consolidated profit before tax declined from ₹808.41 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 to ₹724.60 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and consolidated profit after tax fell from ₹580.01 lakhs to ₹520.74 lakhs over the same period. Standalone profit before tax similarly declined from ₹594.52 lakhs to ₹484.28 lakhs, and standalone profit after tax from ₹437.38 lakhs to ₹366.53 lakhs.
  • Associate losses: The Group’s share of loss from associates was ₹30.34 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a share of profit of ₹8.70 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and a loss of ₹5.48 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, the share of loss from associates was ₹30.96 lakhs.
  • Rising finance costs: Consolidated finance costs increased to ₹300.31 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹277.77 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹261.02 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone finance costs rose to ₹183.08 lakhs from ₹173.49 lakhs and ₹145.26 lakhs respectively.
  • Rising depreciation and employee costs: Consolidated depreciation and amortisation rose to ₹591.32 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹560.60 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹505.54 lakhs a year earlier. Consolidated employee benefits expense rose to ₹1,846.90 lakhs from ₹1,631.29 lakhs and ₹1,615.30 lakhs respectively.
  • New Labour Codes impact: Effective 21 November 2025, the Government of India consolidated multiple existing labour legislations into four Labour Codes. The Group recognised an incremental expense of ₹62.09 lakhs during the year ended 31 March 2026 and continues to monitor developments, with estimates subject to review.
  • Regulatory and compliance burden on shareholders: The company has been reminding shareholders holding securities in physical mode to update PAN, address with PIN code, mobile number, bank account details, specimen signature and nomination choice under the SEBI Master Circular dated 30 January 2026, and to dematerialise physical securities. Physical-mode folios without these details are eligible for payments including dividend only through electronic mode from 1 April 2024.
  • Unreviewed associate financials: The Group’s share of loss from two associates for the June 2026 quarter is based on interim financial results that have not been reviewed by their auditors and were furnished by management, though management states these are not material to the Group.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year revenue growth: Consolidated revenue from operations rose to ₹13,432.68 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹10,455.26 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone revenue rose to ₹11,453.37 lakhs from ₹8,714.58 lakhs.
  • Year-on-year profit growth: Consolidated profit after tax increased to ₹520.74 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹404.85 lakhs a year earlier, and standalone profit after tax rose to ₹366.53 lakhs from ₹309.44 lakhs. Consolidated basic earnings per share improved to ₹3.84 from ₹3.12, and standalone to ₹2.70 from ₹2.38.
  • Foundry segment expansion: Foundry segment revenue grew to ₹2,730.31 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹2,239.76 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, with segment results rising to ₹363.11 lakhs from ₹249.18 lakhs. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, foundry revenue was ₹8,858.34 lakhs.
  • Hydraulic segment scale-up: Hydraulic segment revenue rose to ₹11,705.09 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹8,944.26 lakhs a year earlier, with segment results of ₹1,541.88 lakhs versus ₹1,327.25 lakhs.
  • Subsidiary contribution: Three subsidiaries not reviewed by the principal auditor contributed total revenues of ₹4,707.53 lakhs, net profit after tax of ₹180.08 lakhs and total comprehensive income of ₹180.08 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter.
  • Dividend proposal: The Board proposed a final dividend of 15% (₹1.50 per equity share) for the financial year ended 31 March 2026, subject to shareholder approval, which would result in a cash outflow of ₹203.76 lakhs.
  • Group structure: The Group includes subsidiaries Coretec Engineering India Private Limited, Grotek Enterprises Private Limited and Kolben Hydraulics Limited, and associates Sai India Limited and AEPL Grotek Renewable Energy Private Limited.

Key Risks

  • Associate performance risk: Continued losses at the associate level, including the ₹30.34 lakhs share of loss in the June 2026 quarter, weigh on consolidated profitability.
  • Cost inflation risk: Increases in employee benefits, finance costs and depreciation could continue to pressure margins if revenue growth does not keep pace.
  • Regulatory transition risk: The financial implications of the New Labour Codes have been estimated and recognised, but the Group states it will review the estimates as implementation developments continue.
  • Reliance on other auditors and unreviewed information: The consolidated conclusion relies on review reports of other auditors for three subsidiaries and on unreviewed interim financial results for two associates.
  • Dividend approval risk: The proposed final dividend of ₹1.50 per equity share is subject to approval at the Annual General Meeting.
  • Shareholder compliance risk: Physical shareholders who do not update KYC details face restrictions on receiving payments through non-electronic modes.
