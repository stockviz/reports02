DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited — Q1 FY26–27 Update

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Margin compression despite revenue growth. Standalone revenue from operations rose 40.08% year on year in Q1 FY26–27, but EBITDA grew only 21.01% and profit before tax 14.76%. Standalone PAT margin fell to 6.29% in Q1 FY27 from 7.75% in Q1 FY26. On a consolidated basis, PAT margin declined to 6.08% from 7.15%.
  • Rising finance costs. Standalone finance cost increased to Rs. 105.96 lakh in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 68.77 lakh in Q1 FY26, and consolidated finance cost rose to Rs. 135.86 lakh from Rs. 78.11 lakh. Borrowings increased from Rs. 346.73 lakh (non-current) and Rs. 1,292.57 lakh (current) in FY25 to Rs. 1,598.91 lakh and Rs. 1,044.40 lakh respectively in FY26.
  • Working capital pressure. Trade receivables rose sharply from Rs. 2,565.66 lakh in FY25 to Rs. 5,108.62 lakh in FY26, and inventories increased from Rs. 1,978.78 lakh to Rs. 2,673.46 lakh over the same period.
  • Rising depreciation and employee costs. Standalone depreciation and amortisation increased to Rs. 167.70 lakh in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 146.93 lakh in Q1 FY26, and employee benefit expenses rose to Rs. 151.10 lakh from Rs. 91.12 lakh.
  • Cost of materials consumed remains the largest cost line. At Rs. 2,012.39 lakh in Q1 FY27 standalone, it continues to weigh on operating profitability even as revenue scales.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong top-line momentum. Standalone revenue grew 40.08% year on year in Q1 FY27, and consolidated revenue grew 44.12% to Rs. 3,648.78 lakh. FY26 standalone revenue of Rs. 11,636.49 lakh was up 49.00% over FY25.
  • Diversified integrated platform. DJML operates across printing, media, logistics, digitisation, record management and business communication, serving banking, financial services, government institutions and corporates through a single-window model.
  • Infrastructure scale. The company cites 5,00,000+ sq. ft. of secure storage infrastructure, 18+ locations across India, 1,000+ active business clients, 40+ years of industry experience and 24x7 operational monitoring.
  • Growth levers outlined for FY27. Management identifies increasing penetration in existing customer segments, improving wallet share through cross-selling, expanding digitisation services, strengthening communication platforms, enhancing logistics capabilities, growing project-based solutions and increasing utilisation of existing assets.
  • Sector positioning. DJML is an IBA-approved security printer and offers secure multi-channel communication (SMS, WhatsApp, email), record management, digitisation, print-to-post, logistics, global logistics and premium mobility services.
  • Shareholder returns. A final dividend of Rs. 0.15 per equity share (1.5%) for FY26 was approved at the 17th AGM held on July 13, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Concentration and execution risk in diversified operations. The breadth of services — from security printing to global logistics and premium mobility — exposes the company to operational and execution complexity across multiple verticals.
  • Receivables and liquidity risk. The sharp increase in trade receivables and inventories in FY26 relative to FY25 indicates elevated working capital intensity.
  • Leverage risk. Total borrowings increased materially in FY26, with finance costs rising in Q1 FY27 on both standalone and consolidated bases.
  • Margin sustainability. Despite revenue growth, profitability growth lagged, with PAT margins declining year on year in Q1 FY27.
  • Governance and compliance continuity. The AGM approved continuation of Mr. Devadas Alva as Non-Executive Director beyond age 84 and Mr. Navinchandra Rama Sanil as Independent Director beyond age 75, requiring special resolutions — indicating reliance on senior leadership continuity.
  • Dependence on institutional and government clients. The business model is oriented toward banking, financial services, government institutions and corporates, which may carry payment cycle and concentration risks.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management states DJML is “positioned to capture emerging opportunities across printing, logistics and digital transformation,” with FY27 growth levers including cross-selling, digitisation expansion, logistics capability enhancement and improved asset utilisation.
  • Observed performance: Q1 FY27 standalone revenue of Rs. 3,014.88 lakh (up 40.08% YoY) and consolidated revenue of Rs. 3,636.17 lakh (up 44.12% YoY) are consistent with the stated growth momentum. However, EBITDA growth (21.01% standalone, 29.66% consolidated) and PAT growth (12.98% standalone, 21.83% consolidated) lagged revenue growth, and PAT margins declined year on year — indicating that profitability improvement has not kept pace with top-line expansion.
  • Guidance: Management cites “better operational efficiency, optimised resource utilisation and higher contribution from value-added services” as profitability drivers.
  • Observed performance: Finance costs, depreciation and employee expenses all rose in Q1 FY27, and standalone PAT margin fell to 6.29% from 7.75%, suggesting these efficiency gains are not yet fully reflected in reported margins.
  • Guidance: Management emphasises “sustainable and scalable growth” and “creating sustainable shareholder value.”
  • Observed performance: The FY26 dividend of Rs. 0.15 per share (1.5%) was approved, and FY26 standalone revenue grew 49.00% with PAT up 53.27% — supporting the growth narrative at the annual level, even as quarterly margins softened.
