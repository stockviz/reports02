Chembond Material Technologies — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Weak sequential profitability in the Specialty Chemical segment. Consolidated segment results for Specialty Chemical fell sharply to ₹100.88 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹385.36 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and ₹189.29 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Total consolidated segment results before interest and tax declined to ₹206.44 lakh from ₹552.77 lakh sequentially and ₹271.38 lakh year-on-year.
  • Margin pressure despite revenue growth. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to ₹7,241.56 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹5,767.50 lakh a year earlier, but total expenses rose faster, to ₹7,035.12 lakh from ₹5,496.12 lakh, compressing the profit before tax to ₹582.99 lakh from ₹536.53 lakh on a much larger revenue base.
  • Cost of materials consumed increased to ₹4,686.66 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹3,312.31 lakh in the June 2025 quarter, and employee benefit expenses rose to ₹1,101.80 lakh from ₹923.83 lakh.
  • Animal Health segment revenue declined sequentially, to ₹1,036.08 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹1,142.14 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26, though it was higher than ₹961.51 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26.
  • Volatile other income. Consolidated other income swung from ₹265.14 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26 to a negative ₹166.30 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26, then to ₹376.55 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27, indicating a material non-operating swing between periods.
  • Gratuity-related exceptional items affected prior periods: an incremental gratuity effect of ₹47.13 lakh (consolidated) in Q4 FY2025-26 and a negative ₹132.48 lakh for the year ended March 31, 2026; standalone showed ₹53.10 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26 and negative ₹117.00 lakh for FY2025-26.
  • Low shareholder participation. Only 68 shareholders attended the 51st AGM through video conferencing (19 promoter/promoter group and 49 public), against 11,582 shareholders on the record date of July 10, 2026. Total votes polled represented 68.8997% of outstanding shares, with public institutions polling only 27.3589% of their holdings and public non-institutions only 3.5763%.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year revenue growth. Consolidated revenue from operations for Q1 FY2026-27 was ₹7,241.56 lakh versus ₹5,767.50 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26. Standalone revenue rose to ₹6,296.89 lakh from ₹4,892.03 lakh.
  • Year-on-year profit growth. Consolidated profit for the period rose to ₹572.62 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹399.02 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26; standalone profit rose to ₹450.33 lakh from ₹316.95 lakh. Consolidated basic and diluted EPS improved to ₹4.26 from ₹2.97; standalone EPS improved to ₹3.35 from ₹2.36.
  • Sequential improvement. Consolidated profit before tax increased to ₹582.99 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹433.61 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26, and consolidated profit for the period rose to ₹572.62 lakh from ₹348.48 lakh.
  • Animal Health growth year-on-year. Segment revenue rose to ₹1,036.08 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 from ₹961.51 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26, with segment results improving to ₹105.57 lakh from ₹82.09 lakh.
  • Dividend declared. The Board proposed and shareholders approved a final dividend on equity shares for the financial year ended March 31, 2026 (Resolution No. 2 at the 51st AGM).
  • Employee alignment through ESOP. On July 17, 2026, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee/Compensation Committee approved the grant of 50,738 stock options to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiary under the Chembond Material Technologies Limited – Employee Stock Option Plan 2025, at an exercise price of ₹171 per share, with vesting over a maximum of five years from the grant date and a minimum one-year gap between grant and vesting.
  • Clean audit review. The statutory auditors, M/s. Kastury & Talati, issued limited review reports on both the standalone and consolidated unaudited results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with no material misstatement identified.
  • Subsidiary consolidation. Chembond Biosciences Limited remains the sole subsidiary consolidated into the Group results as at June 30, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Segment concentration and cyclicality. The Group operates two reportable segments — Specialty Chemical and Animal Health. The Specialty Chemical segment dominates revenue (₹6,205.48 lakh of ₹7,241.56 lakh consolidated in Q1 FY2026-27) and its results are volatile, having fallen to ₹100.88 lakh in the latest quarter from ₹385.36 lakh sequentially.
  • Input cost exposure. Cost of materials consumed is the largest expense line and rose year-on-year in both consolidated and standalone results, exposing profitability to raw material price movements.
  • Dependence on non-operating income. Other income of ₹376.55 lakh (consolidated) in Q1 FY2026-27 was a significant contributor relative to profit before tax of ₹582.99 lakh, and this line has been volatile across periods.
  • Governance and shareholder engagement. Very low public participation in voting (public non-institutions polled 3.5763% of their shares; public institutions 27.3589%) and only 68 shareholders attending the AGM indicate limited broader shareholder engagement.
  • Dilution from ESOPs. The grant of 50,738 options convertible into equity shares of face value ₹5 each at ₹171 per share could result in future equity dilution; the company notes diluted EPS impact is not applicable at this stage.
  • Tax structure decision. The Company has decided not to opt for the new tax structure under Section 200 of the Income Tax Act, 2025, which may affect its future tax position.
  • Related-party interest in resolutions. Resolution No. 3 (reappointment of Mr. Jaywant Tawade as a rotating director) was noted as one in which the Promoter/Promoter Group were interested.

  • Dividend recommendation versus performance. The Board recommended a final dividend for FY2025-26, and shareholders approved it on July 17, 2026. This followed a full year in which consolidated revenue was ₹25,007.40 lakh, profit for the year was ₹1,289.07 lakh, and basic EPS was ₹9.59, with standalone profit of ₹775.88 lakh and EPS of ₹5.77.
  • ESOP grant versus performance. The NRC/Compensation Committee approved 50,738 options at ₹171 per share on July 17, 2026, the same day the Board approved Q1 FY2026-27 results showing year-on-year improvement in consolidated revenue (₹7,241.56 lakh vs ₹5,767.50 lakh), profit (₹572.62 lakh vs ₹399.02 lakh), and EPS (₹4.26 vs ₹2.97), though sequential segment profitability weakened.
  • Auditor review versus reported numbers. The limited review reports for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 state that nothing came to the auditors’ attention indicating material misstatement, and the comparative figures for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 were reviewed by the predecessor auditor with an unmodified conclusion (report dated July 25, 2025).
  • AGM outcomes versus Board proposals. All resolutions placed before the 51st AGM — adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY2025-26, declaration of final dividend, reappointment of Mr. Jaywant Tawade, and approval of cost auditor remuneration — were passed with the requisite majority. Resolution No. 3 saw 5 votes against (99.9999% in favour) and Resolution No. 4 saw 4 votes against (100% in favour on votes polled), indicating near-unanimous approval.
