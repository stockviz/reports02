Kshitij Polyline Limited — Summary of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory scrutiny over financial results filing. The company received queries from the National Stock Exchange regarding the financial results submitted on 26 June 2026, covering three issues: results not signed by the authorized signatory, results not submitted in the SEBI-prescribed format, and results not submitted within the stipulated 30-minute or 3-hour window from the conclusion of the Board Meeting.
  • Delayed submission of results. The company acknowledged that the delay in submitting financial results after the conclusion of the Board Meeting was inadvertent and unintentional, and confirmed it had already paid the penalty for the delay.
  • Format deficiency. The exchange observed non-compliance with the prescribed SEBI format due to the absence of a balancing figure note. The company stated this has now been incorporated in revised financial results.
  • Weak financial performance. The audited standalone results for the year ended 31 March 2025 show a loss before tax of ₹(936.70) lakh, compared with a loss before tax of ₹(51.13) lakh in the prior year. Total income declined to ₹3,238.00 lakh from ₹3,998.32 lakh. On a consolidated basis, the loss before tax was ₹(886.58) lakh against a profit before tax of ₹234.75 lakh in the prior year, with total income falling to ₹4,381.83 lakh from ₹6,619.29 lakh.
  • Cost pressure. Standalone cost of material consumed rose to ₹2,959.19 lakh for FY2024-25 from ₹2,850.66 lakh in the prior year, even as revenue from operations declined to ₹3,058.02 lakh from ₹3,421.98 lakh. Finance costs increased to ₹224.74 lakh from ₹178.18 lakh.
  • Negative reserves. Standalone reserves and surplus excluding revaluation reserves stood at ₹(322.45) lakh as at 31 March 2025, compared with ₹798.89 lakh a year earlier. Consolidated reserves and surplus were ₹1,178.40 lakh against ₹962.34 lakh.
  • Key managerial changes. Ms. Nikita Dhaval Mehta resigned as Company Secretary & Compliance Officer effective from closing business hours of 30 May 2025, citing preoccupation in other business. Mrs. Sarita Khamwani was appointed as Company Secretary & Compliance Officer the same day.
  • Rights Issue risk disclosure. The rights issue documentation states that investment in securities involves a high degree of risk.
  • Rights Issue closure extension. The closure period of the Rights Issue of 6,16,97,950 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹2 each, aggregating up to ₹1,955.83 lakh, was extended from Friday, 4 September 2026 to Wednesday, 9 September 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Rights Issue oversubscription. The Board finalized the allotment of 5,98,86,887 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹2.00 each at ₹3.17 per right equity share (including a share premium of ₹1.17). The Rights Issue was subscribed to 104.85% of the issue size. Post-issue paid-up equity share capital stands at 21,41,31,761 shares of face value ₹2.00 each.
  • Fresh capital infusion. The standalone cash flow statement records proceeds from fresh issue of share capital of ₹2,454.28 lakh for the year ended 31 March 2025, and the consolidated statement records the same amount.
  • Unmodified audit opinion. M/s. VRCA & Associates, Statutory Auditors, issued audit reports with unmodified opinion on the audited annual financial results for the year ended 31 March 2025.
  • Strengthened governance infrastructure. The Board appointed M/s. Ronak Jhuthawat & Co. as Secretarial Auditor for five years starting FY2025-26, and M/s. Valawat & Associates as Internal Auditor for FY2025-26.
  • Experienced compliance leadership. The incoming Company Secretary, CS Sarita Khamwani, is described as a qualified Company Secretary and law graduate with over 15 years of experience in secretarial and compliance functions, including IPO-related compliances and listed company regulations.
  • Subsidiary and associate contributions. The consolidated results include one subsidiary (Kshitij E-Store Ventures Private Limited) and one associate/joint venture (Sparion Infrastructure Private Limited). The group’s share of profit of associate and joint ventures was ₹185.29 lakh for the period from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025.
  • Continued leadership continuity. The 18th AGM agenda includes the retirement by rotation and re-appointment of Mrs. Vineeta Jain as Whole-time Director for three years from 30 January 2027 to 29 January 2030.

3. Key Risks

  • Going concern considerations. The auditor’s report outlines the Board’s responsibility to assess the company’s ability to continue as a going concern and to disclose matters related to going concern where applicable.
  • Material uncertainty language. The auditor’s responsibilities section notes that if a material uncertainty exists related to events or conditions that may cast significant doubt on the company’s ability to continue as a going concern, the auditor must draw attention to related disclosures or modify the opinion.
  • Internal financial controls. The auditor’s report references the auditor’s responsibility to express an opinion on whether the company has adequate internal financial controls with reference to standalone financial statements and the operating effectiveness of such controls.
  • Fraud and misstatement risk. The audit report notes that the risk of not detecting a material misstatement resulting from fraud is higher than for one resulting from error, as fraud may involve collusion, forgery, intentional omissions, misrepresentations, or override of internal control.
  • Compliance and disclosure risk. The exchange queries regarding signature authorization, format compliance, and timely submission highlight regulatory compliance risk. The company has stated it is taking appropriate steps to ensure such instances do not recur.
  • Investment risk. The rights issue material explicitly states that investment in securities involves a high degree of risk.
  • Dependence on subsidiaries and associates. The consolidated financial results include the audited financial results of one subsidiary and one associate/joint venture, whose interim financial statements reflect the group’s share of total assets of ₹68.12 lakh as at 31 March 2025, group’s share of total revenues of ₹11.20 lakh, total comprehensive loss of ₹0.53 lakh, and share of profit of ₹185.29 lakh for the period from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • On timely submission of results. Management stated that the delay in submission of financial results within the stipulated time after the conclusion of the Board Meeting was purely inadvertent and unintentional, expressed regret for the oversight, assured that the delay was not deliberate, and stated that appropriate steps are being taken to ensure such an instance does not recur. The company also confirmed it had already paid the penalty for the delay. Observed performance: the exchange had raised queries regarding the delay, and the company acknowledged the delay and paid the penalty.
  • On compliance with signature requirements. Management stated that the financial results for the year ended 31 March 2025 were signed by Mr. Mahendra Kumar Jain, Executive Director and Chairperson, and Ms. Vineeta Jain, Executive Director, and that they were authorized by the Board of Directors to sign the results. Certified true copies evidencing the authorization were attached. Observed performance: the Board resolution dated 30 May 2025 authorized Mr. Mahendra Kumar Jain and Ms. Vineeta Jain to sign the audited standalone and consolidated financial results.
  • On format compliance. Management stated that the observation regarding non-compliance with the prescribed SEBI format due to absence of the balancing figure note has been addressed, and the required disclosure/note has now been incorporated in the revised financial results. Observed performance: the company submitted revised financial results incorporating the note.
  • On the balancing figure explanation. Management clarified that the company submitted audited financial results for the fourth quarter along with results for the entire financial year, and that the figures for the last quarter are derived as balancing figures between the audited results for the full financial year and the published year-to-date figures up to the third quarter of the current financial year.
  • On the rights issue. The company disclosed that the Rights Issue was subscribed to 104.85% of the issue size, and the Board finalized the allotment of 5,98,86,887 equity shares at ₹3.17 per share. The post-issue paid-up equity share capital is 21,41,31,761 shares of face value ₹2.00 each. The closure period was extended from 4 September 2026 to 9 September 2026.
  • On auditor opinion. Management declared that M/s. VRCA & Associates issued audit reports with unmodified opinion on the audited annual financial results for the year ended 31 March 2025. Observed performance: the auditor’s report states that the standalone and consolidated financial results give a true and fair view in conformity with Ind AS and Regulation 33 of the Listing Regulations.
  • On AGM and director re-appointment. Management scheduled the 18th AGM for Tuesday, 29 September 2026, at 11:30 A.M. IST through video conferencing or other audio-visual means, with remote e-voting and e-voting during the AGM provided through KFin Technologies Limited. Agenda items include adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026, and re-appointment of Mrs. Vineeta Jain as Whole-time Director for three years from 30 January 2027 to 29 January 2030, along with fixation of her remuneration.
