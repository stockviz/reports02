FINCABLES — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Fuel availability constraints: Very limited availability of fuel (LPG/PNG) due to the war in the Middle East caused the copper rod plant not to be operated during the quarter. Limited fuel availability also impacted volumes in new product categories.
  • West Asia disturbances: Volumes were impacted across the board in the Others segment following disturbances in West Asia — supply chain disruptions as well as lower fuel availability.
  • Price erosion in Lighting: Price erosion in Lighting continues to impact the business, resulting in marginal degrowth for the year.
  • Others segment performance: The Others segment (mainly trading of electrical and other goods) saw revenue decline 2% YoY and 16% QoQ in Q1FY27, with EBITDA down 14% YoY and 15% QoQ.
  • Communication Cables EBIT decline: Despite strong revenue growth, Communication Cables EBIT declined 1% YoY in Q1FY27 (though up 438% QoQ).
  • Margin pressure at annual level: FY26 consolidated EBITDA margins were 9.8% versus 10.2% in FY25; PAT margins declined to 13.9% from 16.3%. Gross profit margins declined to 18.5% in FY26 from 19.3% in FY25.
  • Working capital: Inventory days increased to 73 in Q1FY27 from 61 in Q1FY26; receivable days rose to 41 from 21.
  • Certification timelines: Switch/switchgear variants require 6–8 month certification timelines.
  • Fiber market dynamics: A significant reversal in fiber market dynamics is described as a crucial enabler for margin recovery in the Communication Cables segment, indicating current market conditions remain a constraint.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Q1FY27 performance: Revenue grew 44% YoY to Rs. 2,013 Cr, partly due to pass-through of higher copper prices and partly due to volume growth. PAT stood at Rs. 221 Cr, a 59% YoY improvement. EBITDA margins improved 90% YoY, reflecting operating leverage benefits. Sequentially, margins improved 22% QoQ.
  • Electrical Cables volume growth: Volume growth was significant in Auto, Industrial, Agriculture and Solar applications. In volume terms, Electrical Wires were higher by 7%, with stellar contributions from agricultural, industrial, and solar applications. The increase in raw material prices was passed on to customers during the period.
  • Communication Cables: Optic Fiber Cable volumes were significantly higher than the previous year and with higher realizations, margins were also high. Revenue grew 62% YoY in Q1FY27.
  • Pillar I — Electrical Cables expansion: Stepped up exposure to utility-side projects; active tender participation; project-driven growth. E-Beam curing plant is a key product differentiation lever — E-beam cured cables offer 40–50-year life cycles, ideal for high-rise buildings with 100-year structural lifetimes; opens new application segments including high-rise buildings, industrial, defence, and specialty markets. Certifications being pursued for additional applications. Greenfield planning would begin once utilisation crosses 70%. Solar Cables expected to be the major revenue stream, with planning already underway for the next capacity increase. Auto Cables segment is “geared for growth” with a strong pipeline; Industrial Cables are growing at a healthy rate.
  • Pillar II — Communication Cables backward integration: Preform plant has been commissioned, making Finolex only the second Indian producer. This eliminates import duty (5% on non-Japan imports) and supply chain dependency. Excess preform capacity can be sold externally, creating a new revenue stream. Fiber Draw capacity expansion from 4 to 8 Mn Km remains on track to operationalise full 8 mn km capacity by end of Q2 FY27, with Phase 1 equipment (4 mn to 6 mn km) already at site pending cleanroom completion and Phase 2 equipment (6 mn to 8 mn km) expected by March-end 2026. Demand catalysts include fiber price recovery and global shortage, driven by defence applications globally, Data Center fiber requirements (US and India), and BharatNet Phase 3. A global shortage is emerging which would create a massive opportunity.
  • Pillar III — FMEG scaling: FMEG remains a focus area; current categories include fans and water heaters expected within 6 months; new models under design across all categories; new launches guided for near-term quarters.
  • Pillar IV — Channel & retail expansion: Pan-India network comprising over 800 distributors, 5,000 channel partners, and 2,15,000 retailers, aiming to extend retail reach to 2,50,000 retailers. Focus on Tier 2 & 3 cities and rural areas; strengthening partnerships with real estate developers; strategy to retain brand premium positioning and educate channel on life-cycle cost advantage.
  • Financial strength: Net zero debt balance sheet; Crisil Long Term Rating of AA+/Stable (Reaffirmed); five world-class manufacturing facilities with ongoing capacity expansion.
  • Dividend: Board recommended a dividend of 450% (Rs. 9/- per equity share) for FY2025-26.

3. Key Risks

  • Changes in economic conditions, industry demand, competitive intensity, commodity prices, foreign exchange rates, regulatory developments, supply chain disruptions, project execution and other factors beyond the Company’s control (as stated in the presentation disclaimer).
  • Fuel availability constraints (LPG/PNG) affecting plant operations and volumes.
  • Geopolitical disturbances in West Asia impacting supply chains and fuel availability.
  • Price erosion in Lighting continuing to impact the business.
  • Fiber market dynamics — margin recovery in Communication Cables depends on a significant reversal in fiber market dynamics.
  • Certification timelines for switch/switchgear variants (6–8 months).
  • Working capital intensity — inventory days and receivable days increased in Q1FY27.
  • Annual margin compression — FY26 consolidated EBITDA margins declined to 9.8% from 10.2% in FY25; PAT margins declined to 13.9% from 16.3%.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Fiber Draw Facility expansion: Management guided that expansion plans on the Fiber Draw Facility are on track, and full capacity from the new plant (4 million fiber kilometers) is now expected to be on stream by Q3 of this fiscal. The presentation states the 8 mn km capacity is expected to be operationalised by end of Q2 FY27, with Phase 1 equipment (4 mn to 6 mn km) already at site pending cleanroom completion and Phase 2 equipment (6 mn to 8 mn km) expected by March-end 2026. Observed performance: Optic Fiber Cable volumes were significantly higher than the previous year with higher realizations and high margins in Q1FY27; Communication Cables revenue grew 62% YoY.
  • Greenfield planning: Management guided that greenfield planning would begin once utilisation crosses 70%. No observed utilisation figure is provided in the source material.
  • FMEG launches: Management guided that fans and water heaters are expected within 6 months, with new models under design across all categories and new launches guided for near-term quarters. No observed FMEG launch outcomes are provided in the source material.
  • Retail reach: Management aims to extend retail reach to 2,50,000 retailers from the current 2,15,000. No observed progress figure is provided in the source material.
  • Q1FY27 performance vs. guidance: Revenue grew 44% YoY to Rs. 2,013 Cr; PAT stood at Rs. 221 Cr, a 59% YoY improvement; EBITDA margins improved 90% YoY. Management stated it remains focused on sustaining this momentum through operational efficiencies and market expansion.
  • Dividend guidance: Board recommended a dividend of 450% (Rs. 9/- per equity share) for FY2025-26, subject to member approval. Record date is Friday, 4th September, 2026, and dividend will be paid on or before 27th October, 2026 if declared.
  • AGM: The 58th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Monday, 28th September 2026, to be held through Video Conference / Other Audio Visual Means. Key agenda items include adoption of financial statements, declaration of dividend, re-appointment of directors, and ratification of cost auditor remuneration.
  • Plant visit: Finolex Cables Limited is hosting a physical group plant visit for analysts and institutional investors at its Urse plant in Pune on Wednesday, 9th September 2026 at 9 A.M., and no unpublished price sensitive information will be shared.
  • Board approvals: The Board approved the re-appointment of Mrs. Vanessa Singh, Mr. Zubin Billimoria, and Mr. Sriraman Raghuraman as Independent Directors, Mr. Ratnakar Barve as Whole-time Director, and the appointment of Mr. Nirnoy Sur as Company Secretary, Compliance Officer, and General Manager - Legal.

Broker Narrative

The narrative evolved from a cautious stance on consumer discretionary segments (ECD, cooling, lighting) to a more positive outlook driven by infrastructure, real estate, and data-centre demand. Early concerns about weak consumer sentiment and pricing pressure gave way to optimism about fibre optics, capacity expansion, and premium product ramp-ups, while the risk focus shifted from demand-side issues to input price volatility and operational disruptions.

Fears that came true

  • Inflation-led weak consumer sentiment continued to hurt demand for ECD products, contributing to disappointing stock returns in 2024.
  • High channel inventory of non-star rated fans and aggressive pricing pressured margins, correlating with the May and August 2024 DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes.
  • Significant price correction in LED products posed a risk to revenue growth, a factor in the November 2024 stock decline.

Optimism that failed

  • Copper price decline aiding C&W growth did not persist, as input price volatility later became a key headwind.
  • Low base expected to help margins normalise did not materialise, given sustained competitive pressure and weak consumer demand.
