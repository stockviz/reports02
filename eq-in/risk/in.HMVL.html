HMVL — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Newsprint cost pressure: Elevated newsprint prices, a weaker rupee, and global supply-chain uncertainties are flagged as concerns going forward. Raw material expense rose 21% year-on-year in Q1 FY2026-27 (from INR 54 crore to INR 65 crore) and 17% sequentially.
  • Digital moderation: Digital revenue fell 28% year-on-year (from INR 38 crore to INR 27 crore) and 29% sequentially, as the portfolio was deliberately reset around leaner, more focused offerings. The segment remained loss-making at the operating EBITDA level.
  • Radio footprint reduction: Radio revenue grew only 3% year-on-year, and the business surrendered licenses for certain non-viable FM stations to operate on a leaner footprint. Radio operating EBITDA remained negative.
  • Sequential softness: Consolidated total revenue declined 11% quarter-on-quarter (INR 558 crore to INR 497 crore), with EBITDA down 31% and PAT down 51% versus Q4 FY2025-26.
  • Structural industry pressures: Shifting consumer preferences toward digital media, circulation declines, geopolitical developments affecting newsprint availability and pricing, supply chain disruptions, and the rapid adoption of GenAI alongside evolving cyber security and data privacy requirements.
  • No dividend: No dividend was recommended for FY 2025-26.
  • Business discontinuation: The OTTplay business was discontinued during FY 2025-26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Print advertising momentum: Advertising revenue grew 15% year-on-year at the Print segment level (INR 256 crore to INR 295 crore), with English print ad revenue up 12% and Hindi print ad revenue up 20%.
  • Circulation resilience: Print circulation revenue was steady annually and sequentially (INR 51 crore to INR 52 crore), with English circulation up 14% year-on-year.
  • Margin expansion: Consolidated EBITDA margin expanded to 18% in Q1 FY2026-27 from 6% a year earlier, aided by disciplined cost management; Print segment margin rose to 13% from 4%.
  • Government advertising revision: A revival in advertising and a long-overdue revision in government advertising rates are cited as growth drivers for print advertising revenues.
  • Events economy and election cycles: The expanding events economy and state election cycles are identified as opportunities.
  • Technology leverage: Opportunities to deploy emerging technologies such as GenAI across editorial, operational, and audience engagement workflows.
  • Capital structure strengthening: The Board approved a preferential issue in the prior month (subject to regulatory and shareholder approval), intended to strengthen the capital structure, streamline the debt profile, and provide capital for general business requirements.
  • Robust cash position: Net cash stood at INR 1,001 crore at the end of the period.
  • Regional print confidence: Management remains confident in the structural strengths of the regional Print market and intends to continue investing in quality journalism, hyperlocal coverage, and reader-facing initiatives.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory changes, local political and economic developments, technological risks, and geo-political macro changes that could cause actual results to differ from forward-looking statements.
  • Newsprint price volatility and currency weakness affecting input costs.
  • Continued digital revenue moderation and segment losses.
  • Radio segment losses and the impact of surrendering non-viable frequencies.
  • Execution risk around the proposed preferential issue, which remains subject to regulatory and shareholder approval.
  • Cyber security and data privacy requirements accompanying GenAI adoption.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Cost discipline and margins: Management stated that profitability growth was achieved on the back of steady advertising revenue and disciplined cost management. Observed: employee cost declined 8% year-on-year (INR 38 crore to INR 35 crore) and other expenses rose 8%, while consolidated EBITDA margin expanded to 18% from 6%.
  • Print as anchor: Management described Print as the anchor of the business with advertising growing year-on-year and circulation resilient. Observed: Print ad revenue +15% and circulation +1% year-on-year, with segment margin at 13%.
  • Radio footprint: Management said Radio is now operating on a leaner, more sustainable footprint following license surrenders. Observed: revenue +3% year-on-year with operating EBITDA improving to negative INR 3 crore from negative INR 7 crore, though still loss-making.
  • Digital reset: Management said digital revenue moderated as the portfolio was deliberately reset around leaner, more focused offerings to drive sustainable and profitable growth. Observed: revenue down 28% year-on-year with operating EBITDA broadly unchanged at negative INR 3 crore.
  • Capital structure: Management characterized the preferential issue as a proactive step to strengthen the capital structure and streamline debt. Observed: net cash of INR 1,001 crore at period end; the issue remains subject to approvals.
  • FY 2025-26 full-year context: Revenue from continuing operations increased 9.9% to INR 740 crore, PAT was INR 157 crore, and basic/diluted EPS was INR 19.1, with no dividend recommended.
