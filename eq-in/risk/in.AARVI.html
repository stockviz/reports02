AARVI ENCON — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • New Labour Codes: Effective November 21, 2025, the Government of India consolidated multiple labour legislations into four Labour Codes. The Company assessed the incremental impact based on the best information available, resulting in an increase in gratuity of Rs. 147.75 lakhs, recognised as an exceptional item during the year ended March 31, 2026. For billable employees, the Company views these costs as contractually recoverable from customers, and therefore did not have any incremental impact on the statement of profit and loss. The Company continues to monitor developments on implementation of the New Labour Codes, including related rules, and would continue to assess accounting implications as guidance becomes available.
  • Forward-looking risk factors: Forward-looking statements involve known and unknown risks, uncertainties and other factors, which may cause actual results, financial condition, performance or achievements to differ materially.
  • Subsidiary and associate performance drag: For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, one subsidiary (not reviewed by the auditor) recorded total revenues of Rs. Nil and a net loss after tax of Rs. 5.74 lakhs, with a total comprehensive loss of Rs. 1.89 lakhs. One associate entity recorded revenues of Rs. 271.23 lakhs but a net loss after tax of Rs. 16.18 lakhs and a total comprehensive loss of Rs. 2.18 lakhs for the same quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Industry demand drivers: Growing adoption of compliant and organised flexi-staffing solutions; infrastructure, manufacturing and industrial investments driving demand for skilled technical manpower; GCC growth and digital transformation increasing demand for specialised talent; renewable, power, oil and gas and industrial projects expanding opportunities; and a shift in demand towards broader workforce management solutions.
  • New work orders: On August 27, 2026, the Company received four work orders from Certification Engineers International Limited for hiring manpower resources, with an aggregate value of INR 174,73,04,148 excluding applicable taxes, covering a period of three years from September 1, 2026, to August 31, 2029. These work orders strengthen the Company’s presence in the technical manpower services segment, reinforce its relationship with leading organisations in the energy and infrastructure sectors, and support its focus on expanding its presence across key industrial sectors.
  • Government payroll support: During the year under review, the Company received the first instalment of a subsidy amounting to Rs. 186.15 lakhs under the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana, a recurring and periodic government payroll support scheme, classified and included under “Other Income.” The subsidy is recognised as revenue only when there is reasonable assurance that the Company will comply with the conditions and the grant becomes receivable, in accordance with Ind AS 20.
  • Financial position and ratings: The Company has a 3-year revenue CAGR of 14% and a low gearing ratio of 0.10x, with operational revenue of INR 6,499 Mn in FY26 and INR 1,727 Mn in Q1-FY27. Long-term ratings are reaffirmed at CRISIL BBB/Stable and short-term ratings at CRISIL A3+.
  • Track record: Incorporated in 1987, Aarvi Encon is a leading Indian technical manpower outsourcing company with proven management experience of 38 years, having deputed more than 50,000 personnel and currently maintaining a deployed team strength of over 8,000 professionals.

  • Subsidiary and associate losses: Certain subsidiaries and an associate entity recorded net losses and comprehensive losses for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, as detailed above.
  • Audit scope limitations: One subsidiary with total revenues of Rs. 1,566.81 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 52.92 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was not reviewed by the auditor; two subsidiaries with a net loss after tax of Rs. 5.74 lakhs were not reviewed by their auditors; and one associate entity was not reviewed by the auditor. The auditor’s conclusion on these entities is solely based on management observations and procedures performed.
