Headwinds and Challenges

GTL’s financial position reflects substantial accumulated stress. The company’s net worth has been substantially eroded over the past years, and its current liabilities exceed its current assets. Balance confirmations for bank loans, escrow accounts, bank guarantees, bank current accounts, and fixed deposits aggregating to Rs. 79,987.41 Lakh had not been received as at June 30, 2026.

For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, total expenses of Rs. 7,247.63 lakhs exceeded total income of Rs. 6,035.51 lakhs, with revenue from operations at Rs. 6,002.18 lakhs. The reported profit for the period of Rs. 32,217.92 lakhs was driven by exceptional items of Rs. 33,430.04 lakhs, which included Rs. 31,585.04 lakhs from the accounting impact of the One Time Settlement and Rs. 1,845.00 lakhs from one-time income out of settled disputed dues.

On the governance front, Mrs. Siddhi M. Thakur, a Non-Executive & Non-Independent Director liable to retire by rotation, has decided not to seek re-appointment, and the Board proposes that the resulting vacancy not be filled up.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company has received in-principle approvals from all original secured lenders for the one-time settlement proposal. It has settled twelve lenders and deposited settlement amounts for the remaining two lenders in a designated escrow account. Management believes the company is in a position to revive its business and continue operations upon completion of the ongoing settlement.

Key Risks

The unresolved balance confirmations covering bank loans, escrow accounts, bank guarantees, bank current accounts, and fixed deposits aggregating to Rs. 79,987.41 Lakh as at June 30, 2026 represent an open item in the financial reporting process. The company’s continuation of operations is tied to completion of the ongoing settlement with lenders, and its accounts continue to be prepared on a going concern basis contingent on that outcome. The erosion of net worth and the excess of current liabilities over current assets remain features of the balance sheet.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management states that the company is in a position to revive its business and continue its operations upon completion of the ongoing settlement with the lenders, and continues to prepare its financial results on a going concern basis. The observed performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 shows operating revenue of Rs. 6,002.18 lakhs against total expenses of Rs. 7,247.63 lakhs, with the reported profit of Rs. 32,217.92 lakhs attributable to exceptional items rather than operating results. The settlement process itself is at an advanced stage, with in-principle approvals from all original secured lenders, twelve lenders settled, and amounts for the remaining two lenders deposited in escrow.
