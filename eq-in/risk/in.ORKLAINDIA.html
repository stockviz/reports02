  • GST show cause notice: On July 20, 2026, the Company received a Show Cause Notice (Form GST DRC-01) from the Office of the Assistant Commissioner (ST), Ayyappanthangal Assessment Circle, Chennai, proposing a demand of Rs. 8,40,82,951 for FY 2023-24. This comprises a tax demand of Rs. 5,23,57,426, proposed interest of Rs. 2,64,39,783, and penalty of Rs. 52,85,742. The SCN raises issues relating to Input Tax Credit, reversal of common ITC under Section 17 read with Rules 42 and 43, reconciliation of exempt supplies, credit notes, and transactions with related/distinct persons. The Company is in the process of filing detailed objections and evaluating legal options.
  • Institutional shareholder dissent on stock option resolutions: At the 30th AGM held on August 19, 2026, Public Institutions voted 75.3751% against Resolutions 3, 4, 5, and 6, all of which related to the ratification of the Employee Stock Option Plan 2025 and Management Stock Option Plan 2025, and the extension of their benefits to subsidiary/holding company employees. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • FMCG demand tailwind and robust growth in the domestic market, including strong digital commerce and modern trade growth.
  • Strong momentum in convenience foods and spices.
  • Expansive total addressable market in South India.
  • Regional product expansion: On August 17, 2026, MTR announced the launch of North Karnataka offerings — Groundnut Chutney Podi (Shenga), Nigerseed Chutney Podi (Gurellu), Flax Seed Chutney Podi (Agasi), and Masala Khara. These products are inspired by North Karnataka’s culinary traditions, where chutney podis are consumed in nearly 8 out of 10 households. The products will be available across General Trade, Modern Trade, Namma MTR stores, and leading e-commerce platforms, supported by a hyperlocal approach including digital content, retail branding, outdoor activations, influencer-led initiatives, and on-ground consumer engagement. This follows earlier launches of MTR Byadagi Chilli and MTR Dharwad Peda.
