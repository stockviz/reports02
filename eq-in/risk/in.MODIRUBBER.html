MODIRUBBER: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Industrial rehabilitation stalled. The industrial rehabilitation of the Modinagar Tyre Factory (MTF) has not been achieved. Possession of the property remains pending with the Allahabad High Court and the Official Liquidator.

Litigation and occupation issues. The company continues to face litigation with creditors, as well as illegal occupants and ex-employees occupying company properties.

Subsidiary wind-down. Business operations of subsidiary Modi Marco Aldany Private Limited (MMAPL) were adversely affected by the COVID-19 pandemic and the subsequent closure of overseas operations by Alma Beauty Corporation S.L. This led to plans to wind up MMAPL. The deterioration dates from around the financial year 2019-20 onwards, with Alma Beauty Corporation S.L. expressing its intention to discontinue the joint venture and wind up MMAPL.

Disputed tax matters. Pending disputed tax, excise, and sales tax matters remain across various forums.

Standalone losses. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, standalone loss before tax was ₹264.94 lacs and net loss after tax was ₹288.72 lacs, with basic and diluted EPS of ₹(1.15). For the year ended March 31, 2026, standalone net loss after tax was ₹1,941.32 lacs and total comprehensive loss was ₹2,614.50 lacs.

No dividend. The board did not recommend any dividend due to insufficient adequate profit.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Real estate monetisation. The company is taking active steps to utilise its real estate resources and generate revenue, and is vigorously pursuing re-possession of company properties.

Salon and wellness business. A proposed amendment to the Object Clause of the Memorandum of Association will enable the company to undertake salon, beauty, grooming, and wellness businesses. This is intended to facilitate the takeover and continuation of the existing salon presently operated by MMAPL, situated in the corporate building of the Company.

Joint ventures and associates. The company holds significant investments in joint ventures and associates, including Gujarat Guardian Ltd. and Asahi Modi Materials Pvt. Ltd., which contribute to the group’s overall performance. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the consolidated results include the Group’s share of net profit (including other comprehensive income) of ₹520.07 lacs in respect of two joint ventures and one associate company.

Consolidated profitability. On a consolidated basis, the company returned to profit in the quarter ended June 30, 2026: profit before tax of ₹453.99 lacs, net profit after tax of ₹424.68 lacs, and basic and diluted EPS of ₹1.70. Total comprehensive income was ₹431.10 lacs. For the year ended March 31, 2026, consolidated net profit after tax was ₹63.97 lacs, though total comprehensive loss was ₹1,167.01 lacs.

Segment performance. Consolidated segment revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 comprised real estate services ₹122.03 lacs, travel services ₹672.91 lacs, and others ₹nil, with gross revenue of ₹781.44 lacs after inter-segment elimination of ₹13.50 lacs. Segment results were ₹(264.92) lacs for real estate services, ₹136.20 lacs for travel services, and ₹62.63 lacs for others.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued inability to secure possession of the Modinagar Tyre Factory, with the matter pending before the Allahabad High Court and the Official Liquidator.
  • Ongoing litigation with creditors and occupation of company properties by illegal occupants and ex-employees.
  • Wind-up of MMAPL following Alma Beauty Corporation S.L.’s decision to discontinue the joint venture and its overseas operations.
  • Pending disputed tax, excise, and sales tax matters across various forums.
  • Standalone operations remain loss-making, with the standalone segment result for real estate services at ₹(264.92) lacs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • Consolidated other comprehensive income was negative for the year ended March 31, 2026 at ₹(1,230.98) lacs, and total comprehensive loss for that year was ₹1,167.01 lacs.
  • The consolidated financial results include the Group’s share of net profit of joint ventures and associates whose financial statements were not audited by the statutory auditor; the conclusion on those amounts is based solely on reviewed financial information furnished by management.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

BIFR scheme implementation. Management states it continues to implement the unimplemented provisions of the BIFR scheme sanctioned on April 8, 2008, and is following up with government agencies and creditors to secure relief and concessions. The results note that, in compliance with directions issued by the BIFR vide its order dated February 23, 2010, the unimplemented portion of the SS-08 is under implementation by the Company. Observed performance shows the standalone business remains loss-making in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with loss before tax of ₹264.94 lacs.

Salon business takeover. Management intends to take over and operate the salon business currently operated by MMAPL, subject to necessary corporate, legal, and regulatory formalities. The proposed amendment to the Object Clause of the Memorandum of Association is the enabling step, and the 53rd AGM scheduled for September 30, 2026 includes approval of amendments to the Object Clause. No revenue from this activity is reflected in the quarter ended June 30, 2026 results.

Real estate revenue generation. Management states it is taking active steps to utilise real estate resources and generate revenue. Observed real estate services segment revenue was ₹122.03 lacs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with ₹105.53 lacs for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 and ₹128.04 lacs for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, while the real estate services segment result remained negative at ₹(264.92) lacs for the quarter.

Re-possession efforts. Management states it is vigorously pursuing re-possession of company properties. Possession of the Modinagar Tyre Factory remains pending with the Allahabad High Court and the Official Liquidator.

Managing Director re-appointment. The 53rd AGM agenda includes re-appointment of Mr. Alok Modi as Managing Director for a term of five years commencing October 1, 2026, without remuneration.

Reporting and governance. The standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at their meeting held on August 14, 2026, and the statutory auditors issued an unmodified review opinion. The company’s equity shares are listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Public shareholding stood at 93,41,580 shares (37.31%), and promoters and promoter group held 1,56,98,952 shares (62.69%), all non-encumbered. No investor complaints were pending, received, or unresolved during the quarter.
