LLOYDSENGG: Recent Corporate Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

The company is undertaking a significant rights issue object variation, with INR 660.52 crore utilized and INR 326.73 crore unutilized as of June 30, 2025, indicating that a substantial portion of previously raised capital remains to be deployed as intended. The board has also approved a corporate guarantee not exceeding INR 130 crore on behalf of its associate company, Lloyds Infrastructure & Construction Limited, which introduces contingent exposure to that entity’s obligations.

The proposed amalgamation of Lloyds Infrastructure & Construction Limited, Metalfab Hightech Private Limited, and Techno Industries Private Limited into Lloyds Engineering Works Limited requires approval from unsecured creditors, with a meeting scheduled for October 16, 2026. The completion of this scheme remains subject to creditor and regulatory approvals, creating execution uncertainty.

The ESOP pool expansion from 4,40,00,000 to 7,35,00,000 options under the Lloyds Steels Industries Limited Employee Stock Option Plan 2021, subject to shareholder approval, represents potential equity dilution.

The SISCOL acquisition involved a share swap component, with 7,00,42,458 equity shares of the company issued on a preferential basis at INR 71.25 per share, aggregating to INR 499.05 crore. This increased the paid-up equity capital from INR 147,87,84,399.50 (148,02,96,454 shares) to INR 154,95,26,857.50 (155,10,38,912 shares), diluting existing shareholders.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company completed the acquisition of a 51.13% stake in Steel Infra Solutions Company Limited (SISCOL) on August 17, 2026, making SISCOL a subsidiary. SISCOL is engaged in heavy steel fabrication and infrastructure solutions, serving energy, infrastructure, and industrial segments. SISCOL reported turnover of INR 816.87 crore and net profit of INR 43.42 crore for April 2025–March 2026, with turnover growing from INR 573.49 crore in FY 2023–24 to INR 636.10 crore in FY 2024–25 and further to INR 816.87 crore in FY 2025–26. SISCOL operates six production facilities with a newly operational Hyderabad facility, bringing total capacity to 100,000 MT per annum, and has contributed to projects including Terminal 1 at Delhi Airport, ITPL Bangalore, and Noida International Airport.

The acquisition is expected to expand capabilities and product portfolio, generate operating synergies through consolidated procurement and shared engineering resources, strengthen the order book, and position the combined business to bid for larger turnkey or EPC projects. The company will endeavour to file a Draft Red Herring Prospectus for SISCOL’s listing within thirty months from completion of Stage 1.

The proposed amalgamation of LICL, MHPL, and TIPL into LEWL is expected to create a unified engineering and infrastructure company, leveraging LICL’s order book of over INR 4,500 crore, expanding operational scale, enhancing competitiveness in multidisciplinary contracts, achieving cost synergies, and improving financial strength.

Consolidated revenue grew approximately 142.92% to INR 527.15 crore, EBITDA grew approximately 124.38% to INR 79.23 crore, and PBT grew approximately 132.06% to INR 68.99 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared to the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Standalone revenue grew approximately 104.55% to INR 355.82 crore, EBITDA by approximately 82.19% to INR 60.03 crore, and PBT by approximately 76.69% to INR 52.41 crore. The order book position on July 1, 2026, is INR 2,817.42 crore (standalone INR 2,432.35 crore), representing an increase of approximately 81.19% compared to July 1, 2025. The associate company LICL has an order book position of INR 4,830.23 crore.

Key Risks

The SISCOL acquisition consideration included a share swap component, resulting in preferential allotment of 7,00,42,458 equity shares at INR 71.25 per share to 26 non-promoter allottees, plus 7,00,000 equity shares for cash consideration to Prime Securities Limited. This significantly increased the total share count and diluted existing shareholders.

The corporate guarantee of up to INR 130 crore on behalf of LICL exposes the company to potential financial obligations if LICL defaults.

The rights issue object variation indicates that the company has not fully deployed the proceeds as originally planned, which may signal delays or changes in project execution.

The amalgamation scheme requires approval from unsecured creditors, with voting cut-off date of March 31, 2026, and remote e-voting from October 12–15, 2026. Failure to obtain requisite approvals could delay or prevent the scheme.

The SISCOL listing timeline of thirty months from Stage 1 completion is an endeavour, not a binding commitment, and is subject to market conditions and regulatory approvals.

LADS shareholding diluted from 100% to 85% on June 29, 2026, making it a subsidiary rather than a wholly owned subsidiary, which may reduce control over that entity.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management has provided forward-looking statements regarding the SISCOL acquisition, stating it is expected to provide benefits including expansion of capabilities, operating synergies, strengthening of order book, and a pathway to future listing of SISCOL. The company will endeavour to file the Draft Red Herring Prospectus for SISCOL’s listing within thirty months from completion of Stage 1. These are expectations and endeavours, not guarantees.

Regarding the amalgamation, management states the merger is expected to result in better leveraging of combined assets and capital base, enhanced technical and operational capabilities, strengthened organizational leadership, greater operational efficiency through elimination of redundant functions, cost synergies, enhanced financial strengths with large net worth and strong asset base, and a simplified shareholding and group structure. These are forward-looking expectations subject to completion of the scheme.

Observed business performance shows strong growth: consolidated revenue grew approximately 142.92% to INR 527.15 crore, EBITDA grew approximately 124.38% to INR 79.23 crore, and PBT grew approximately 132.06% to INR 68.99 crore for Q1 FY 2026–27 compared to Q1 FY 2025–26. Standalone revenue grew approximately 104.55% to INR 355.82 crore, EBITDA by approximately 82.19% to INR 60.03 crore, and PBT by approximately 76.69% to INR 52.41 crore. The order book position on July 1, 2026, is INR 2,817.42 crore (standalone INR 2,432.35 crore), up approximately 81.19% from July 1, 2025. LICL’s order book is INR 4,830.23 crore.

The company is scheduled to participate in the Anand Rathi Annual Flagship Conference G-200 Summit 2026 on September 21–22, 2026, in Mumbai.
