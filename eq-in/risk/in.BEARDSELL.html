Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential moderation in consolidated profitability. Consolidated profit before tax fell to Rs. 229 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from Rs. 478 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, even as total income rose slightly to Rs. 7,272 lakhs from Rs. 7,252 lakhs. The decline was driven mainly by the Insulation segment, where segment results dropped to Rs. 408 lakhs from Rs. 729 lakhs, and by a sharp fall in Trading segment results to Rs. 15 lakhs from Rs. 49 lakhs.
  • Higher cost of materials consumed. Consolidated cost of materials consumed rose to Rs. 4,870 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 4,295 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, an increase of about 13.4%, outpacing revenue growth over the same period.
  • Compressed trading profitability. Trading segment revenue declined to Rs. 439 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 536 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 501 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, while segment results fell to Rs. 15 lakhs from Rs. 49 lakhs and Rs. 8 lakhs respectively.
  • Tax charge swings. Consolidated tax expense moved from a net credit of Rs. 65 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter to a charge of Rs. 109 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, contributing to the fall in profit for the period to Rs. 238 lakhs from Rs. 382 lakhs.
  • Standalone performance also softened sequentially. Standalone profit before tax declined to Rs. 216 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 472 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, with standalone Insulation segment results falling to Rs. 388 lakhs from Rs. 709 lakhs.
  • Controlled entity contribution declined. The standalone results include a profit of Rs. 16.74 lakhs from the controlled entity Saideep Polytherm for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with Rs. 35.74 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and Rs. 28.71 lakhs for the quarter ended 31 March 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year consolidated growth. Consolidated total income increased to Rs. 7,272 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 6,606 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, with revenue from operations rising to Rs. 7,220 lakhs from Rs. 6,566 lakhs.
  • Insulation segment scale-up. Consolidated Insulation segment revenue rose to Rs. 6,781 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 6,065 lakhs a year earlier and Rs. 6,642 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, Insulation segment revenue was Rs. 25,179 lakhs.
  • Improved year-on-year profitability. Consolidated profit before tax for the June 2026 quarter was Rs. 229 lakhs against Rs. 343 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, while profit for the period was Rs. 238 lakhs against Rs. 290 lakhs. Standalone profit before tax was Rs. 216 lakhs against Rs. 333 lakhs, and standalone profit after tax was Rs. 216 lakhs against Rs. 274 lakhs.
  • Stronger balance sheet capacity. Consolidated segment assets rose to Rs. 20,114 lakhs as at 30 June 2026 from Rs. 17,702 lakhs as at 31 March 2026, while segment liabilities fell to Rs. 8,504 lakhs from Rs. 10,168 lakhs. Standalone segment assets increased to Rs. 17,995 lakhs from Rs. 16,490 lakhs, with standalone segment liabilities declining to Rs. 7,326 lakhs from Rs. 8,703 lakhs.
  • Subsidiary and controlled entity contributions. Sarovar Insulation Private Limited reported total revenue of Rs. 371.52 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 10.24 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter. Saideep Polytherm reported total revenue of Rs. 1,008.03 lakhs and net profit after tax of Rs. 16.74 lakhs for the same quarter.
  • Dividend recommendation. A final dividend of Re. 0.10 per equity share has been recommended for the financial year ended 31 March 2026, with a record date of 21 September 2026.

Key Risks

  • Segment concentration. The Insulation segment accounted for Rs. 6,781 lakhs of the Rs. 7,220 lakhs consolidated revenue from contracts with customers in the June 2026 quarter, making overall performance highly sensitive to this segment.
  • Volatility in segment results. Consolidated Insulation segment results fell from Rs. 729 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter to Rs. 408 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, while Trading segment results fell from Rs. 49 lakhs to Rs. 15 lakhs over the same period.
  • Dependence on reviewed interim information from other auditors. The consolidated statement includes subsidiary and controlled entity results reviewed by their respective auditors, with the group auditor relying on those reports.
  • Finance costs. Consolidated finance costs were Rs. 59 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, Rs. 57 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 77 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Standalone finance costs were Rs. 52 lakhs, Rs. 43 lakhs and Rs. 69 lakhs respectively.
  • Unallocable expenditure. Consolidated other un-allocable expenditure net of un-allocable income was Rs. 135 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, Rs. 243 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 151 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Inventory movements. Consolidated changes in inventories of finished goods, work-in-progress and stock-in-trade moved from a positive Rs. 36 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter to a negative Rs. 209 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, and were negative Rs. 119 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter.
