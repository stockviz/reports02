Future Market Networks Limited — Summary of Recent Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Legacy lender disputes and enforcement action. The company received a demand notice of INR 18,448.96 lakhs from Yes Bank Limited dated April 19, 2022, primarily directed at Basuti Sales & Trading Private Limited, with FMNL having pledged 3,830 equity shares of Riddhi Siddhi Mall Management Private Limited and a secondary charge on immovable property. Yes Bank issued a notice under Section 13(4) of the SARFAESI Act on November 10, 2022 for the 10-acre mall property at Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Yes Bank subsequently assigned the debt to JC Flower, which obtained an order for physical possession from the Chief Metropolitan Magistrate, Ahmedabad, though JC Flower later withdrew its notice for taking physical possession of the 10 Acre Mall. FMNL and Basuti filed a SARFAESI Section 17 application challenging JC Flower’s Section 13(4) symbolic possession notice; the stay applications were dismissed, and the material notes that the matter is understood to be settled between the parties with JC Flower having taken possession of the premises, requiring amendments to the securitisation application. The next date in that matter is August 18, 2026.

DRT proceedings. Yes Bank filed an Original Application before the Debt Recovery Tribunal, New Delhi (TA/96/2022) against Basuti Sales & Trading Private Limited and Brattle Foods Private Limited, with FMNL as a party. Arguments concluded and the matter was listed for pronouncement on April 24, 2026, but the Presiding Officer sought clarification before passing a final order, so the matter is to be listed for clarification.

Future Retail insolvency and rental claims. FMNL filed IA 3861 of 2023 before the NCLT, Mumbai Bench against the Resolution Professional of Future Retail Limited under Section 60(5) of the IBC, seeking peaceful possession of “Akashganga Enclave” at the 10 Acre Mall, Ahmedabad, and payment of outstanding lease rental from the date of initiation of FRL’s CIRP. FRL was admitted into liquidation by order dated July 29, 2024, and FMNL sought to substitute the RP and implead the Liquidator. The Liquidator submitted that lenders had passed a resolution that rents would not be paid as CIRP cost. By order dated March 18, 2025, the NCLT directed removal of goods and handover of peaceful possession, and the RP undertook to hand over rental dues by May 31, 2025. FMNL filed a claim in FORM-C for Rs. 16,51,41,173 before the official liquidator of FRL on April 18, 2025. The notes also record that IA 3861 of 2023 was dismissed on March 18, 2025.

Arbitral award exposure. An Arbitral Award dated May 19, 2016 in favour of Mr. Surana awarded INR 12.90 crores relating to disputes over a commercial premises at Kharghpur, West Bengal, secured by two bank guarantees of INR 12.00 crores. The bank guarantees were liquidated and Rs. 10.25 crores paid to the claimant. FMNL filed a supplementary affidavit placing on record material from DRT proceedings demonstrating that Mr. Surana received an additional aggregate amount of Rs. 7.75 crores post termination of the arrangement by the company. Following the demise of Mr. Laxmi Pat Surana, applications were filed to bring legal heirs on record. The matter remains pending, with further hearing listed for September 2026.

Subsidiary collateral and recovery proceedings. Suhani Mall Management Company Private Limited, a subsidiary, provided its Visakhapatnam leasehold property as collateral for a loan availed by Future Corporate Resources Private Limited from RBL Bank. RBL Bank filed OA/3/2023 before DRT-3, New Delhi for recovery of INR 13,24,196,228.56, with SMMPL’s liability limited to the realisable value of the property subject to a maximum of INR 80 crores. The DRT dismissed the OA on November 12, 2025 (and a related order dated December 12, 2025 is also referenced), and RBL Bank has challenged the order before the DRAT. A Section 13(2) SARFAESI notice dated September 16, 2022 demanding INR 12,962.11 lakhs was issued, with the subsidiary’s liability limited to the marketable value of the property. An FIR No. 33/2026 dated June 24, 2026 was registered at CIDPS, A.P., Mangalagiri alleging that the security interest created in favour of RBL Bank was without prior approval and intimation; a quashing petition has been filed before the High Court, with a hearing date of August 17, 2026.

FLFL liquidation and warehousing dispute. Jeremia Real Estate Private Limited filed IA Nos. 1998 of 2025 and IA 1865 of 2025 in Company Petition (IB) No. 959 of 2022 filed by Bank of India against FLFL, concerning removal of the corporate debtor’s goods and reimbursement of rent. By order dated February 17, 2026, the Tribunal directed the RP of FLFL to vacate goods from the Bhiwandi premises within four weeks; on April 2, 2026 the RP submitted it could not comply due to liquidity issues, and the Tribunal gave one week to comply failing which contempt proceedings would lie. On April 17, 2026 the RP stated non-compliance and the Tribunal directed notice to the COC. FLFL was admitted into liquidation on July 9, 2026.

Warrants lapsed and forfeiture. 1,99,00,000 warrants issued to Jurox Enterprises Private Limited and 33,81,426 warrants issued to Surplus Finvest Private Limited lapsed, with forfeiture of amounts received.

Scheme withdrawal. The NCLT Mumbai Bench Court-IV approved the withdrawal of the scheme of arrangement with Metawear Limited by order dated June 18, 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Warrant conversion. Surplus Finvest Private Limited converted 31,18,574 warrants into 31,18,574 equity shares on June 13, 2026, which is reflected in the increase in paid-up equity share capital to Rs. 6,676.30 lakhs as at June 30, 2026 from Rs. 6,364.44 lakhs as at March 31, 2026.

Improved quarterly profitability. On a standalone basis, income from operations for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was Rs. 2,191.82 lakhs, with total income of Rs. 2,429.91 lakhs and net profit of Rs. 254.89 lakhs, compared with net profit of Rs. 151.88 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 120.58 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Standalone basic EPS was Rs. 0.40 for the June 2026 quarter versus Rs. 0.25 and Rs. 0.20 in the comparative periods.

Consolidated profitability. Consolidated profit after tax for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was Rs. 180.32 lakhs, compared with Rs. 195.34 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 146.83 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Consolidated profit before exceptional items, share of net profits of investments accounted for using the equity method and tax was Rs. 99.32 lakhs, with share of net profit of investments accounted for using the equity method of Rs. 45.30 lakhs.

Single operating segment. The group operates in only one segment, “Property and Related Services.”

Resolution of certain legacy matters. JC Flower withdrew its notice for taking physical possession of the 10 Acre Mall, and the DRT dismissed RBL Bank’s OA against SMMPL, with related appeals becoming infructuous in light of that dismissal.

3. Key Risks

  • Contingent liabilities arising from demand notices against the company relating to corporate guarantees and assets pledged as security, and disputes related to shopping malls, as flagged in the auditors’ Emphasis of Matter for both standalone and consolidated results.
  • Contingent liabilities of subsidiary Suhani Mall Management Company Private Limited relating to demand notices and assets pledged as security, also flagged in the auditors’ Emphasis of Matter.
  • If the borrower fails to repay outstanding dues to the lender, the lender may exercise all rights available under the mortgage or pledge.
  • Pending litigation before the DRT, DRAT, NCLT, High Court and arbitral proceedings, including the Surana award matter and the FLFL liquidation-related proceedings.
  • The FIR registered on June 24, 2026 alleging the security interest in favour of RBL Bank was created without prior approval and intimation.
  • The auditors’ review reports note that four subsidiaries and one joint venture were not reviewed by the principal auditor; those results reflect total revenues of Rs. 281.17 lakhs, total net loss after tax of Rs. (95.84) lakhs and total comprehensive loss of Rs. (95.84) lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and the group’s share of net profit after tax of Rs. 45.30 lakhs for the joint venture.
