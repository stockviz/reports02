Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals — Recent Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Price erosion and competitive intensity: Management cites price erosion in certain product segments and heightened price competition from reverse auctions as material headwinds.
  • Commodity cost pressure: The baseline commodity basket has significantly run up, pressuring margins.
  • Margin trajectory: Adjusted EBITDA margin stood at 15.0% in FY26, while the FY29 checkpoint targets an exit EBITDA margin of 11%–12% — indicating expected margin compression over the plan period.
  • Regulatory/tax compliance matters: A GST interest demand of INR 8,37,680 (CGST INR 1,02,449 and SGST INR 7,35,231) for July 2017–March 2018, arising from delayed filing of GSTR-3B, was upheld by the Office of the Deputy Commissioner of Appeals, Chennai (Central), Tamil Nadu, via order dated September 09, 2026.
  • Consumer litigation: A District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Rohtak, Haryana order (dated 29.05.2026, received 14.07.2026) requires replacement of a 1.5 H.P. motor, INR 5,000 compensation, INR 5,000 litigation costs, and INR 100 per week from 29.06.2026 until realization; disclosed financial impact of INR 10,300.
  • Dividend servicing obligations: TDS/withholding requirements on the FY26 final dividend of Rs. 3 per equity share, including a 20% rate for inoperative PAN cases and 20% (plus surcharge and cess) for non-resident shareholders, with treaty rates contingent on document verification.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • TAM expansion: Management sees the addressable market doubling from Rs 80k Cr to Rs 160k Cr+.
  • New categories: High growth expected in solar rooftop and smart & connected categories; the company is foraying into solar rooftops and renewable energy.
  • Outdoor/home extension theme: Growth opportunity seen in outdoor spaces becoming an extension of the home, with expansion of the WallSmile Outdoor Deco Wall Light Range (Halo, Prism, Nexo, Linea models; MRPs Rs. 1,000–2,000).
  • Geographic expansion: Opportunity in under-penetrated markets such as North and East.
  • Government schemes: Jal Jeevan Mission and PM Kusum cited as supportive initiatives.
  • Order wins: Letter of empanelment and rate contract from MSEDCL for 3,000 off-grid DC Solar Photovoltaic Water Pumping Systems under MTSKPY/PM-KUSUM-B across Maharashtra, total order value ~Rs. 64.99 Cr (excluding GST), to be executed within 60 days from Notice to Proceed; an additional allocation was received under this contract (intimated July 08, 2026, following the July 2, 2026 intimation).
  • Financial strength: Record revenue of Rs. 8,096 Cr, compounded at ~11% since FY22; net cash of Rs. 1,155 Cr as of March 31, 2026; negative operating working capital at ~4.0% of sales; self-funded growth model.
  • Brand and market position: Over 85 years of brand legacy; India’s market leader in fans and residential pumps.

3. Key Risks

  • Margin risk: The gap between FY26 adjusted EBITDA margin (15.0%) and the FY29 exit margin target (11%–12%) implies earnings sensitivity to commodity and pricing pressures.
  • Competitive risk: Reverse-auction-driven price competition could persist across segments.
  • Execution risk on new businesses: Targets require scaling smart & connected to ~15% mix and new businesses to ~14%–15% of revenue by FY29, and to ~20% each by FY31.
  • Legal/regulatory risk: The GST interest demand is being appealed under Section 112 of the SGST Act, 2017; management expects a favourable outcome but the matter remains open. The consumer commission order carries ongoing weekly payments until realization.
  • Tax withholding risk for shareholders: Higher TDS may apply where documentation is incomplete; the company reserves the right not to apply treaty rates, and shareholders bear responsibility for misrepresentation.
  • Dividend payment constraints: Physical shareholders must update PAN, KYC, bank details, and specimen signature to receive dividend electronically; inoperative PAN attracts 20% TDS.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - FY29 checkpoint: Revenue growth ~13–14% CAGR; smart & connected mix ~15%; share of new businesses ~14%–15%; exit EBITDA margin 11%–12%. - FY31 ambition: 2X revenue; ~20% smart & connected mix; ~20% share of new businesses; 12%+ exit EBITDA margin; ~35% cash payout ratio via dividend and/or buyback; annual capex run rate ~Rs. 120 Cr excluding greenfield capex. - GST matter: Plans to appeal the order and reasonably expects a favourable outcome from appellate authorities; states no material impact on financials, operations, or other activities. - Investor engagement: Virtual one-to-one analyst/institutional investor meeting with Schonfeld Strategic Advisors scheduled September 17, 2026, 12:00 pm–01:00 pm; no unpublished price sensitive information to be shared.

Observed performance against guidance: - Revenue of Rs. 8,096 Cr with ~11% CAGR since FY22 is below the ~13–14% CAGR trajectory targeted for FY29, indicating the growth guidance requires acceleration from the historical run rate. - FY26 adjusted EBITDA margin of 15.0% is above the FY29 exit margin guidance of 11%–12%, consistent with management’s expectation of margin compression as the mix shifts toward new businesses. - Net cash of Rs. 1,155 Cr and negative operating working capital (~4.0% of sales) support the stated self-funded growth model and the ~35% cash payout ambition. - The MSEDCL order (~Rs. 64.99 Cr) and the additional allocation provide early evidence of the solar/renewable foray referenced in the growth strategy. - The GST interest demand and consumer commission order represent the legal/regulatory risks flagged in disclosures; the GST matter remains pending appeal as of the September 09, 2026 order.
