Headwinds and Challenges

  • The company reported that geopolitical challenges persisted through the quarter, with the Chairman noting that “the sharp increase in raw material costs and higher international logistics expenses due to the current macro-environment impacted our margins.”
  • Profit before tax declined to INR 42.72 crores in Q1 FY 2026-27 from INR 89.18 crores in the corresponding quarter of the previous year, even as total income rose to INR 850.29 crores from INR 798.15 crores. Cost of materials consumed rose to INR 709.79 crores from INR 581.64 crores a year earlier, and other expenses rose to INR 95.62 crores from INR 88.78 crores.
  • Other income fell to INR 1.48 crores in Q1 FY 2026-27 from INR 17.52 crores in the prior-year quarter.
  • Subsidy for April to June 2026 of INR 714.83 crores was accounted based on a provisional Retention Price, as the final retention price had not been announced by the Department of Fertilizers. Any necessary adjustments and their consequential impact will be assessed when the final retention price is notified.
  • The lease relating to the land on which one of the manufacturing units operates expired on June 12, 2020. A renewal request has been filed with the Government of Tamil Nadu, and Right of Use Asset and corresponding lease liability have been accounted for based on an indicative increase in lease rent per the previous agreement.
  • The company’s profit as reworked pursuant to Section 198 remains negative in view of huge losses in earlier years.
  • The company continues to monitor finalization of State Rules and government clarifications related to the four Labour Codes notified on November 21, 2025, to recognize any financial impact as appropriate.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Total income for Q1 FY 2026-27 was INR 850.29 crores, higher than INR 798.15 crores in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The Chairman stated that “our Q1 turnover remained higher than the corresponding period of the previous year.”
  • The company achieved a Profit After Tax of INR 186.16 crores for the Financial Year 2025-26, has wiped out accumulated losses, and aims to drive strategic growth plans across Singapore, India, and the UK to accelerate future-readiness.
  • The Board recommended a dividend of 20% on equity shares of face value of Rs. 10 each, i.e., Rs. 2 per equity share, for FY 2025-26, subject to shareholder approval at the 55th AGM.
  • The Urea plant operated for 91 days during the quarter, the same as the prior-year corresponding quarter. Production and sale of Urea were 1.87 lac MT and 1.82 lac MT respectively.
  • The Government of India undertook strategic measures during Q1 FY26-27 to strengthen domestic fertilizer production, diversify sources of raw materials and fertilizer imports, and ensure uninterrupted availability of fertilizers to farmers amid global supply disruptions and volatility in international markets.
  • India produced 293.30 LMT of urea during FY 2025-26. In July 2026, the CCEA approved the National Investment Policy for Urea-2026 for Aatmanirbhar Bharat (NIPU-2026), envisaging up to nine new natural gas-based urea plants, expected to increase annual production, involving private, public, and cooperative entities.
  • The Chairman stated the company is focused on strengthening internal capabilities through operational efficiency, sustainable manufacturing practices, and domestic sourcing, such as the use of natural gas as a key raw material, to ready the business for sustainable growth over the longer term and navigate external shocks.
  • The company exercised the option of a lower tax rate under Section 200 of the Income Tax Act 2025 (erstwhile Section 115BAA of the Income Tax Act 1961), and re-measured the opening balance of Deferred Tax Liabilities (net) as at April 1, 2026, accounting for a tax credit of INR 12.92 crores in the current quarter.
  • Consolidated results include share of profit of joint ventures and associates of INR 19.90 crores for Q1 FY 2026-27, compared with INR 11.76 crores in the prior-year quarter. Consolidated net profit after tax was INR 60.18 crores, and consolidated total comprehensive income was INR 69.32 crores.

Key Risks

  • Sharp increases in raw material costs and higher international logistics expenses due to the macro-environment impacted margins.
  • The final retention price for subsidy has not been announced; adjustments and their consequential impact will be assessed when notified.
  • The land lease for one manufacturing unit expired on June 12, 2020; renewal is pending with the Government of Tamil Nadu, and accounting is based on an indicative increase in lease rent per the previous agreement.
  • Profit as reworked pursuant to Section 198 remains negative in view of huge losses in earlier years.
  • The impact of the four Labour Codes notified on November 21, 2025, is assessed as not material and has been accounted for in the previous year ended March 31, 2026; the company continues to monitor finalization of State Rules and government clarifications.
  • The company operates in a single reportable segment (manufacture and sale of fertilizers) and majorly operates in India, with no revenue or non-current operating assets located outside India.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • The Chairman stated: “Despite the ongoing geopolitical challenges, our Q1 turnover remained higher than the corresponding period of the previous year. However, the sharp increase in raw material costs and higher international logistics expenses due to the current macro-environment impacted our margins.” This is consistent with observed performance: total income rose to INR 850.29 crores from INR 798.15 crores, while profit before tax fell to INR 42.72 crores from INR 89.18 crores.

These measures will help us ready the business for sustainable growth over the longer term and navigate any external shocks.”
