Headwinds and Challenges

  • Overseas subsidiary losses and wind-down. Centum T&S Group Société Anonyme (S.A.) and its underlying overseas subsidiaries incurred losses that eroded net worth. The Company had provided for the carrying value of its investment in Centum T&S Group S.A. amounting to Rs. 1,537.83 million, disclosed as an exceptional item for the year ended March 31, 2026. The Court of Lyon approved transfer of substantially all of the operating business and employees of Centum T&S Group S.A. and certain underlying overseas subsidiaries to successful bidders by judgement dated June 04, 2026; following completion, those entities no longer retained operating assets or business activity. Subsequent to the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the court converted the proceedings into liquidation judiciaire procedure and appointed a judicial liquidator on July 02, 2026.
  • Discontinued Canadian operations. The Board decided on December 19, 2025 to discontinue business operations of Centum E&S (Centum Equipment’s ET Systems), Canada, and Centum T&S (Centum Technologies ET Solutions), Canada, step-down subsidiaries. The Company is making necessary regulatory filings and intimations with relevant regulatory authorities. Management had provided for the carrying value of trade receivables amounting to Rs. 396.00 million, inventory amounting to Rs. 100.78 million, and written back liabilities amounting to Rs. 1.54 million, disclosed as an exceptional item for the year ended March 31, 2026. In the consolidated results, management provided for the carrying value of assets amounting to Rs. 289.38 million and wrote back liabilities amounting to Rs. 45.34 million for the year ended March 31, 2026. During the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Company recovered Rs. 24.17 million out of such trade receivables provided for and wrote back liabilities amounting to Rs. 3.15 million, disclosed as an exceptional item.

The Group provided for goodwill arising on consolidation of Rs. 376.23 million, intangible assets (including under development) amounting to Rs. 178.33 million, and inventory amounting to Rs. 100.78 million, disclosed as profit/(loss) from discontinued operations for the year ended March 31, 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Export order win. The Company secured an export order from a Global OEM customer for the Design, Development and Delivery of Electronic Systems, valued at approximately USD 3.22M (approximately ₹305 Million), to be executed over the next 7 months. This was described as a significant milestone and a major strategic win for Centum’s Build to Specification (BTS) export business, strengthening the Company’s position as a trusted partner for advanced electronic systems in the global defence and aerospace ecosystem.
  • Industry tailwinds. China+1 diversification, government incentives (PLI, EMC 2.0, ISM 2.0), semiconductor expansion, high-value vertical expansion, value-chain upgrade, rising defence budgets and indigenisation mandates, global OEMs seeking Indian defence partners, modernisation across radar, EW & missile platforms, and space sector expansion.

Patil, Hon’ble Minister for Large and Medium Industries and Infrastructure Development, Government of Karnataka, and discussions were held regarding the Company’s proposed new manufacturing facility in Karnataka and related matters. This disclosure followed media reports published on August 7, 2026 relating to the proposed facility.
  • Business profile. Centum operates in two business segments (BTS & EMS) with ~1,650 employees, an order book of INR 18 Bn as on 30th Jun 26, 54% exports revenue, 17 patents granted, 350,000 sq ft manufacturing footprint, and key certifications including SAMAR L5, ISO 13485, IATF 16949, and ISO 27001.
