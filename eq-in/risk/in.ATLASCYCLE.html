Headwinds and Challenges

  • Continued losses. The unaudited standalone results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 show a net loss of Rs. 256.30 lakh, following a loss of Rs. 158.38 lakh in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and a loss of Rs. 941.26 lakh for the year ended 31 March 2026. Total income for the June 2026 quarter was Rs. 153.57 lakh against total expenses of Rs. 1,518.92 lakh.
  • Weak revenue base. Revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was Rs. 498.27 lakh, compared with Rs. 690.44 lakh in the preceding quarter and Rs. 1,165.83 lakh for the year ended 31 March 2026.
  • Overdue interest and MSME litigation. The auditors’ review report notes that the company has not provided for interest on overdue outstanding creditors, including creditors who have filed suits in different courts and MSME creditors. The exact quantum of liability is not ascertainable in the absence of reconciliations and balance confirmations from suppliers.
  • Default on inter-corporate loan repayment. The auditors note that the company has defaulted on repayment of an inter-corporate loan as per the agreed terms and has not provided interest of Rs. 24,75,000 for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, understating losses.
  • Land sale delayed again. The sale of 20 acres of land in Sonepat, Haryana — under an Agreement to Sell entered into on 7 December 2024 — has been extended further. The buyer sought an extension up to 31 December 2026 for execution of the Sale Deed, which the Board approved on 12 August 2026. Prior intimations on this matter were dated 7 December 2024, 29 May 2025, 31 October 2025, 16 January 2026 and 14 April 2026.
  • Governance structure gap. The company does not have a chairperson, managing director, or whole-time director. The June 2026 results were signed by a Director duly authorized by the Board, with the CEO’s signature included in addition to regulatory requirements.
  • Shareholder dissent at the AGM. At the 75th AGM held on 12 August 2026, Resolution 2 (re-appointment of Mr. Sanjiv Kavaljit Singh as a Director retiring by rotation) passed with 94.1682% votes in favour and 5.8318% against. Within the promoter and promoter group category, 64.5473% of e-votes cast were against the resolution. Resolution 1 (adoption of audited financial statements for FY ended 31 March 2026) passed with 90.9678% in favour and 9.0322% against. Total invalid votes cast were 48,317 across Resolutions 1 and 2.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Land sale advance received and further payment undertaken. Pursuant to the Agreement to Sell dated 7 December 2024 for the 20-acre Sonepat land, the company has received an advance of Rs. 45.00 crores to date, and the buyer has undertaken to pay another Rs. 15.00 crores by 30 October 2026.
  • Extension provides a pathway to completion. The Board approved the buyer’s request for extension up to 31 December 2026 for execution of the Sale Deed, keeping the transaction alive.
  • Single-segment focus. The company operates only in one reportable segment, i.e. Cycles.
  • No subsidiary complexity. The company did not have any subsidiary, associate or joint venture entity during the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and the financial year ended 31 March 2026.

Key Risks

  • Going-concern pressure from persistent losses. Losses at the net level in each reported period, with total expenses far exceeding total income in the June 2026 quarter.
  • Unquantified liabilities. The auditors state that the exact quantum of liability relating to overdue creditor interest is not ascertainable due to absence of reconciliations and supplier balance confirmations.
  • Unprovided interest. Interest of Rs. 24,75,000 for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 on the defaulted inter-corporate loan has not been provided, understating losses.
  • Execution risk on land sale. The Sale Deed has not yet been executed; the timeline has already been extended multiple times, and the buyer’s obligation to pay a further Rs. 15.00 crores by 30 October 2026 remains outstanding.
  • Dependence on a single asset transaction. The land sale advance and the promised further payment are material cash inflows referenced in the disclosures.
  • Leadership structure. Absence of a chairperson, managing director, or whole-time director, with results signed by an authorized Director and the CEO.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on results signing: Management stated that going forward, if required, the company shall submit results only with the signature of the director duly authorized by the board of directors. This follows the practice adopted for the June 2026 results, where the authorized Director signed and the CEO’s signature was included in addition to regulatory requirements.
  • Guidance on land sale: Management has continued to intimate material developments on the Sonepat land sale. The buyer sought extension up to 31 December 2026, which the Board approved on 12 August 2026. The company stated it will further intimate as and when any other material development takes place. Observed performance: the Sale Deed remains unexecuted as of 12 August 2026, and the advance received stands at Rs. 45.00 crores, with a further Rs. 15.00 crores undertaken to be paid by 30 October 2026.
  • Observed performance against prior intimations: The land sale process, first disclosed on 7 December 2024, has seen repeated extensions (29 May 2025, 31 October 2025, 16 January 2026, 14 April 2026, and now 12 August 2026), indicating that completion has consistently slipped beyond earlier timelines.
  • Financial performance trend: The company reported a net loss of Rs. 256.30 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), a net loss of Rs. 158.38 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26 (quarter ended 31 March 2026), and a net loss of Rs. 941.26 lakh for the full year ended 31 March 2026. Basic and diluted EPS were negative Rs. 3.94 for the June 2026 quarter, negative Rs. 2.44 for the March 2026 quarter, and negative Rs. 12.36 for the year ended 31 March 2026.
