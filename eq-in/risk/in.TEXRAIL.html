Headwinds and Challenges

  • Continued disruptions in the supply of key input materials are constraining wagon production volumes.
  • The geopolitical situation in Bangladesh is adversely affecting the pace of execution on existing contracts in the Infra Rail & Green Energy Division.
  • The freight wagon industry is cyclical in nature.
  • The engineering complexity of specialised and custom-engineered products is rising.
  • Risk management policies identify credit risk, liquidity risk, market risk, and foreign currency risk.
  • In the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the Infra-Rail & Green Energy segment recorded a loss at the profit-before-interest-and-tax level, both standalone and consolidated.
  • The planned capital expenditure for expanding capacity by adding new manufacturing facilities for rolling stock and rolling stock components (including castings) at Paradip, Odisha, and Kolkata/Howrah, West Bengal, has been put on hold. The balance of the object towards capital expenditure up to Rs 103.43 crore was revised to funding working capital requirements, based on shareholder approval dated 15 April 2026.
  • The original capital expenditure object was revised down from Rs 115.00 crore to Rs 4.34 crore, and the total revised object cost is Rs 142.77 crore against the original Rs 150.00 crore.
  • The general corporate purposes object saw a delay of 5 days for the 25% expenditure milestone, though the balance was completed within timeline.
  • The monitoring agency noted that, with respect to expenditure incurred towards working capital requirements (supplier payments), expenditure was incurred throughout Q1FY27 but reimbursement was made only after obtaining shareholders’ approval; it also noted numerous other debits and credits in the cash credit account and that it was not directly able to ascertain utilisation of funds for the defined objects.
  • A one-time provision for contingencies of Rs 700 crore was created from free reserves in FY2025-26.
  • The consolidated unaudited financial results include interim financial results of seven Indian subsidiaries not reviewed by their auditors, plus share of profit from two joint ventures and one associate based on unreviewed interim results, and interim financial information of one subsidiary located outside India that has not been subjected to review. Management stated these are not material to the Group.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record capital expenditure allocation of Rs 2.93 lakh crore to Indian Railways in the Union Budget 2026-27, with nearly Rs 1.20 lakh crore for safety-related works.
  • Target to increase freight traffic to nearly 3.3 billion tonnes by 2030.
  • Planned development of seven new intercity high-speed rail corridors and Dedicated Freight Corridors.
  • Strategic joint ventures, including the JV with RVNL and the Touax-Texmaco-TrinityRail platform.
  • Growing export opportunities in Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
  • Expansion into passenger mobility, defence, and digital engineering services.
  • Order book as of 31 March 2026 stood at Rs 5,408 crore.
  • Hindalco Industries Limited awarded an order of Rs 27.82 crore (including taxes) to Texmaco Rail & Engineering Limited for the supply of 1 BTAP rake along with 1 Brake Van for the Aditya Expansion Project, to be executed within 8 months from the date of the purchase order.
  • The Company continues to pursue its Vision 2030 roadmap to build a more diversified, technology-driven, and resilient portfolio across higher-value segments.
  • The Company obtained shareholder approval through Postal Ballot dated 24 May 2026 for the “Texmaco Long Term Incentive Plan (LTIP) Scheme 2026” for Key Executives, envisaging grant of options not exceeding 24,00,000 options in one or more tranches; in-principle approval was received from BSE and NSE on 9 July 2026 and 10 July 2026 respectively.
  • Shri Shibu Mathews has been appointed as the Chief Financial Officer effective 5 September 2026; he is a Chartered Accountant with 26 years of experience in finance leadership roles.
  • The Company scheduled physical one-to-one interactions with analysts/institutional investors in Mumbai on 26 and 27 August 2026.

Key Risks

  • Credit risk, liquidity risk, market risk, and foreign currency risk as identified in the Company’s risk management policies.
  • Cyclicality of the freight wagon industry.
  • Supply-chain disruption in key input materials affecting production volumes.
  • Geopolitical conditions in Bangladesh affecting execution pace on existing Infra Rail & Green Energy contracts.
  • Rising engineering complexity of specialised and custom-engineered products.
  • Execution risk on the Hindalco order, which is to be executed within 8 months from the date of the purchase order.
  • The capital expenditure expansion plan being on hold, with funds redirected to working capital, changes the means of finance for the disclosed objects.
  • Monitoring agency observations regarding the inability to directly ascertain utilisation of funds for defined objects from the cash credit account, and reimbursement of working capital expenditure only after shareholder approval.
  • Reliance on unreviewed interim financial information for seven Indian subsidiaries, two joint ventures, one associate, and one overseas subsidiary in the consolidated results, though management states these are not material to the Group.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management states that the Company remains well positioned to sustain its growth momentum and create long-term value, supported by a robust and diversified order backlog, expanding international presence, strategic partnerships, and record budgetary allocations for Indian Railways.
  • Management continues to pursue its Vision 2030 roadmap to build a more diversified, technology-driven, and resilient portfolio across higher-value segments.
  • For FY2025-26, the Company delivered Revenue from Operations of Rs 4,377 crore, EBITDA of Rs 450 crore (10.3% margin), and Profit After Tax of Rs 194 crore (4.4% margin). Total wagon deliveries stood at 8,372 wagons aggregating to approximately Rs 3,413.38 crore. The Board recommended a dividend of 75% (Rs 0.75 per equity share).
  • In the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone total segment revenue was Rs 75,292.34 lakh and consolidated total segment revenue was Rs 1,16,659.93 lakh. Standalone profit before tax was Rs 4,413.60 lakh and consolidated profit before tax was Rs 7,243.95 lakh.
  • The Freight Car Division remained the largest contributor to segment results, while the Infra-Rail & Green Energy segment recorded a loss at the profit-before-interest-and-tax level in the quarter.
  • The Company’s statutory auditors issued an unmodified review opinion on both the standalone and consolidated unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
  • The monitoring agency reported nil deviation from the objects of the preferential issue, with utilisation as per revised objects, and no material deviation observed over the earlier monitoring agency report dated 12 May 2026.
  • The Company has received Rs 37.50 crore (25% of the warrant issue price of Rs 193 per warrant) at subscription, and on 8 October 2025 received the balance 75% of the consideration, i.e. Rs 56.25 crore from Adventz Finance Pvt Ltd and Rs 50.83 crore from Samena Green Ltd, totalling Rs 107.08 crore, in exchange for allotment of 38,86,010 equity shares and 35,11,260 equity shares respectively. Remaining 3,74,750 warrants lapsed on 12 October 2025.
  • As of 30 June 2026, the Company has 7 subsidiaries, 2 joint ventures, and 1 associate.
