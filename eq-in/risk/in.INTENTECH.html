Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sharp sequential and year-on-year decline in profitability. Consolidated Total Income for Q1 FY27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) was ₹3,103.52 lakhs, marginally below ₹3,147.93 lakhs in Q1 FY26 and above ₹2,900.66 lakhs in Q4 FY26. However, consolidated EBITDA fell to ₹152.60 lakhs (4.92% margin) from ₹308.43 lakhs (9.80% margin) a year earlier. Consolidated Net Profit dropped to ₹87.16 lakhs from ₹125.27 lakhs, and consolidated EPS fell to ₹0.36 from ₹0.52.
  • Cost pressure across the board. Consolidated IT Infrastructure Cost rose to ₹590.09 lakhs from ₹380.90 lakhs year-on-year, Employee Benefit Expenses rose to ₹1,789.80 lakhs from ₹1,759.16 lakhs, and Operating Expenses rose to ₹102.23 lakhs from ₹61.36 lakhs. Total Expenses increased to ₹3,053.10 lakhs from ₹3,008.35 lakhs, compressing margins despite broadly flat income.
  • Standalone business slipped into a loss. Standalone Total Income fell to ₹2,068.19 lakhs from ₹2,391.25 lakhs year-on-year, and standalone Profit before Tax was negative ₹221.10 lakhs versus negative ₹54.41 lakhs a year earlier. Standalone loss for the period was ₹168.09 lakhs versus ₹52.91 lakhs.
  • Exceptional items and impairment weighed on the prior year. FY26 (year ended March 31, 2026) consolidated results included one-time non-recurring provisions as exceptional items towards intangible asset impairment and provision for doubtful debts. The consolidated statement shows a Provision for Impairment of ₹1,324.56 lakhs and a Provision for Doubtful Debts of ₹1,840.07 lakhs in the year-ended figures. FY26 consolidated Profit After Tax was negative ₹1,565.46 lakhs (as stated in the press release: ₹(15.65) Cr), with FY26 Consolidated Total Income of ₹129.91 Cr and EBITDA of ₹16.66 Cr.
  • Q4 FY26 was a particularly weak quarter. Consolidated Profit before Tax for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 was negative ₹2,951.12 lakhs, and Profit for the period was negative ₹2,239.46 lakhs, reflecting the exceptional provisions.
  • Deferred tax charge. A deferred tax charge of ₹53.01 lakhs was recorded in Q1 FY27 (versus ₹1.50 lakhs in Q1 FY26), contributing to a total tax liability of negative ₹36.75 lakhs.
  • Subsidiaries not reviewed by the auditor. The auditor did not review the interim financial results of four subsidiaries included in the consolidated statement, whose financial information reflects total revenue (including other income) before consolidation adjustments of ₹1,257.78 lakhs, total net profit after tax of ₹211.63 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of ₹212.43 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. For subsidiaries located outside India, results were converted from local GAAP to Indian GAAP by management, and the auditor’s conclusion on those amounts is based on those conversion adjustments.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Two new client wins. The company secured two new clients in the quarter — one in the domestic market and one internationally — reinforcing growth across key geographies.
  • 100% client renewal rate. The company maintained a 100% client renewal rate, reflecting customer satisfaction and long-term partnerships.
  • Government digital transformation opportunity. The company is pursuing government-led digital transformation initiatives to capitalize on emerging opportunities in the public sector.
  • DPDPA Governance Platform. Management describes DPDPA-compliant communications governance as a natural extension of its customer communications expertise. The market-ready DPDPA Governance Platform is positioned to address compliance needs of organisations across all B2C industries, expanding growth opportunities beyond BFSI.
  • AI and LLM embedding. The company continues to strengthen AI capabilities by embedding Large Language Models across its platforms, enabling contextual and hyper-personalised customer experiences while driving operational efficiency.
  • Analyst recognition. The company was recognised in the QKS Group–SPARK Matrix™ 2026 report for Customer Communications Management (CCM), which management says validates the strength of its AI-powered Centralised Communications Hub, IP-led technology platforms, and domain expertise in regulated industries.
  • International expansion focus. Management remains focused on driving growth across BFSI and government sectors, strengthening presence in India, and expanding footprint in key international markets including the UK and the UAE.
  • Scale of operations. The company states it manages delivery of 1 billion notifications annually, generates 50 million statements each month, and has onboarded over 1 billion subscribers to date. It operates globally across four continents and impacts over a billion lives daily.
  • Subsidiary profitability. The four subsidiaries not reviewed by the auditor contributed total net profit after tax (before consolidation adjustments) of ₹211.63 lakhs in Q1 FY27, indicating profitable overseas and domestic subsidiary operations.
  • Board and governance actions. The Board approved the re-appointment of Ms. Anisha Shastri as Whole-Time Director for a one-year term effective October 1, 2026 (subject to shareholder approval), and approved the appointment of M/s. KRYR & Associates, Chartered Accountants, as Statutory Auditors for five consecutive years from the conclusion of the 36th AGM until the conclusion of the 41st AGM (subject to shareholder approval). The 36th AGM is scheduled for Friday, September 25, 2026, at 12:00 P.M. through video conferencing. Shareholders passed three resolutions via remote e-voting on Saturday, September 12, 2026: the appointment of Mr. Amit Kumar Garg as a Non-executive Non-Independent Director (Ordinary Resolution), Mr. Premananda Panda as a Non-executive Non-Independent Director (Ordinary Resolution), and Ms. Ayushi Bhutada as a Non-executive Independent Director (Special Resolution).

Key Risks

  • Margin compression risk. The decline in EBITDA margin from 9.80% to 4.92% year-on-year, driven by rising IT infrastructure, employee, and operating costs against broadly flat income, indicates pressure on profitability if cost growth continues to outpace revenue growth.
  • Revenue concentration and flat growth. Consolidated Total Income was essentially flat year-on-year (₹3,103.52 lakhs vs ₹3,147.93 lakhs), and standalone income declined, suggesting limited near-term revenue momentum despite new client wins.
  • Exceptional items and impairment risk. The FY26 results included significant one-time provisions for intangible asset impairment (₹1,324.56 lakhs) and doubtful debts (₹1,840.07 lakhs), which materially affected reported profitability. The risk of further impairments or provisions cannot be ruled out.
  • Subsidiary review scope. The auditor did not review the interim financial results of four subsidiaries, and for overseas subsidiaries, the auditor relied on management’s conversion adjustments from local GAAP to Indian GAAP. This limits the scope of independent assurance over a portion of consolidated results.
  • Forward-looking statement risk. The company’s disclaimer notes that forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties including government actions, local, political, or economic developments, technological risks, and other factors that could cause actual results to differ materially.
  • Dependence on BFSI, Telecom, and Government sectors. The company’s focus on these sectors exposes it to sector-specific regulatory, budgetary, and economic cycles.
  • Foreign exchange and translation risk. Exchange differences on translation of foreign operations and remeasurement of foreign transactions were negative ₹5.90 lakhs in Q1 FY27 (versus negative ₹5.73 lakhs in Q1 FY26), indicating exposure to currency fluctuations given global operations.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management stated focus on growth across BFSI and government sectors, and expansion in the UK and UAE. Observed performance: The company added two new clients (one domestic, one international) and maintained a 100% renewal rate. However, consolidated Total Income was flat year-on-year and standalone income declined, indicating that new client wins have not yet translated into top-line growth at the consolidated level.
  • Management highlighted AI-led innovation and DPDPA platform as growth drivers. Observed performance: The company was recognised in the QKS Group–SPARK Matrix™ 2026 report for CCM, and management states it continues to embed LLMs across platforms. No separate revenue contribution from the DPDPA platform or AI initiatives is disclosed in the financial results.
  • Management stated it is “well positioned to create sustainable long-term value” backed by a strong innovation roadmap, differentiated platform strategy, and customer-centric approach. Observed performance: Q1 FY27 consolidated Net Profit was ₹87.16 lakhs (down from ₹125.27 lakhs year-on-year), EBITDA margin was 4.92% (down from 9.80%), and standalone results showed a loss of ₹168.09 lakhs. The prior year (FY26) included significant exceptional provisions. The company’s stated positioning is not yet reflected in improved profitability metrics in the reported quarter.
  • Management stated it remains focused on delivering technology solutions that improve operational efficiency, strengthen customer engagement, and generate sustainable long-term business value. Observed performance: Operating expenses, IT infrastructure costs, and employee benefit expenses all increased year-on-year, while income remained broadly flat, suggesting that operational efficiency gains have not yet offset cost inflation in the reported quarter.
  • Management stated it looks forward to strengthening its platforms and services portfolio, expanding its customer base, and deepening its presence across high-growth sectors. Observed performance: Two new clients were added and a 100% renewal rate was maintained, consistent with customer base expansion efforts. However, the financial impact of these engagements is not yet evident in Q1 FY27 revenue, which was flat year-on-year.
