Western Carriers (India) Limited — Recent Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak profitability trend in FY26 and Q1FY27. Consolidated revenue grew 6.0% year-on-year in FY26 to ₹1,829.2 crore, but EBITDA fell 29.1% to ₹85.0 crore and PAT fell 40.4% to ₹38.8 crore. EBITDA margin compressed from 6.9% in FY25 to 4.6% in FY26, and PAT margin from 3.8% to 2.1%. Gross profit declined 9.2% in FY26 even as revenue rose, with gross margin falling from 13.7% to 11.7%.

Q1FY27 continued the pressure. Revenue rose 11.8% year-on-year to ₹464.9 crore, but EBITDA declined 10.1% to ₹18.7 crore, EBIT fell 18.9% to ₹15.6 crore, and PAT fell 19.4% to ₹8.7 crore. EBITDA margin was 4.0% versus 5.0% in Q1FY26. Sequentially, revenue declined 6.2% from Q4FY26.

Operating cash flow deterioration. Net cash from operating activities was negative in FY25 (−₹2.7 crore) and worsened to −₹21.8 crore in FY26, despite positive operating profit before working capital changes. Trade receivables rose from ₹620.4 crore in FY25 to ₹695.2 crore in FY26.

Cost pressures. Employee expenses rose from ₹53.1 crore in FY25 to ₹61.4 crore in FY26; depreciation rose from ₹23.8 crore to ₹28.3 crore; other expenses rose from ₹62.9 crore to ₹68.0 crore.

Risk factors cited by the company. A downtrend in the industry (global, domestic, or both); significant changes in the political and economic environment in India or key markets abroad; tax laws; litigation; labour relations; exchange rate fluctuations; technological changes; investment and business income; cash flow projections; interest and other costs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal at Devaliya, Morbi (Gujarat). The 42-acre terminal is moving to full commercial activation. On September 15, 2026, the Hon’ble Railway Minister Shri Ashwini Vaishnaw formally flagged off the Bulk Tiles Train from this terminal. The terminal aligns with the Government of India’s PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan and serves industrial clusters including salt, ceramics, chemicals, agriculture, fertiliser, and MSMEs.

General Cargo Terminal at Kolkata Dock System. Inaugurated on September 11, 2026, strengthening rail-linked presence across the East-West freight corridor. The Board approved development and operation of a General Cargo Terminal for handling containers and other cargo at the GCD Yard of Kolkata Dock System, Shamaprasad Mookherjee Port, Kolkata.

Future expansion plans. An inland container depot and warehousing infrastructure are cited as future expansion plans.

Industry growth drivers. Supply-side enablers include DFC, Gati Shakti, Bharatmala, multimodal logistics parks, GST, e-way bills, infrastructure status for logistics, FDI inflows, public capex, logistics skilling, and adoption of AI, IoT, and automation. Demand-side drivers include exports rising 40% from FY20 to FY23 to ₹30.5 trillion, ‘Make in India’, e-commerce and MSME growth, Tier-II and Tier-III city demand, and digital adoption. B2B drivers include MSME expansion (~31% of GDP, ~46% of exports), manufacturing FDI up 76% with PLI incentives, and a shift to outsourced, tech-enabled end-to-end supply chain models.

Company growth engines. Deepening customer relationships, margin expansion and efficiency, inorganic growth, customer acquisition and sector expansion, technology capability building, project logistics, geographic expansion, infrastructure and asset investment, and improved integration and utilization.

Franchise strengths. 1,600+ customers as of March 31, 2026; 80% of FY26 revenue from customers with >3-year relationships; 100% retention rate for top 10 clients; 50+ branches, 4 zonal offices, 16 leased warehouses across 12 states, 55+ major rake handling points, 500+ GPS-enabled trucks, 100+ equipment, 850+ shipping containers, and service across 23 states. WCIL is the largest platinum business associate of Concor and the only associate partner of Concor to provide substantial EXIM business volume.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The company states that forward-looking statements about expected future events and financial and operating results are based on certain assumptions which it does not guarantee the fulfillment of, and that actual results might differ substantially or materially.

Observed performance against the stated growth engines shows mixed results. Revenue growth continued in FY26 (+6.0%) and Q1FY27 (+11.8% year-on-year), consistent with customer acquisition and deepening relationships. However, the stated focus on margin expansion and efficiency is not yet reflected in reported results: gross margin fell from 13.7% (FY25) to 11.7% (FY26) and was 11.2% in Q1FY27; EBITDA margin fell from 6.9% to 4.6% in FY26 and was 4.0% in Q1FY27; PAT margin fell from 3.8% to 2.1% in FY26 and was 1.9% in Q1FY27. The stated focus on reducing third-party dependencies and improving asset utilization has not yet translated into improved profitability or operating cash flow, with net cash from operating activities negative in both FY25 and FY26.

The company’s stated infrastructure and asset investment plans are evidenced by property, plant and equipment rising from ₹119.1 crore (FY25) to ₹167.1 crore (FY26), capital work-in-progress rising from ₹15.0 crore to ₹31.4 crore, and right-of-use assets rising from ₹3.5 crore to ₹7.9 crore. The Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal at Devaliya and the General Cargo Terminal at Kolkata Dock System represent tangible progress on the stated growth engines of project logistics, geographic expansion, and infrastructure investment.
