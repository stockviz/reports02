Broker Narrative

The narrative shifted from a structural long-term bull case in December 2025 to a more near-term tactical Buy in July 2026 that acknowledged demand softness and external uncertainties. Persisting themes included the asset-right expansion plan, strong cash balance, margin expansion, and favorable industry supply-demand dynamics. What changed was the emphasis: early reports focused on lower RoCE/valuation discount and launch ramp-up risks, while later reports highlighted April 2026 demand weakness and Q1FY27’s high MICE/wedding base, yet still expected improvement in H2FY27. All seven reports were Buy calls, but six of seven actual returns were negative.

Fears that came true

  • The flagged slowdown in occupancies/ARRs materialized: April 2026 saw lower foreign travel, air-travel uncertainty, and demand softness across key markets, and most subsequent actual returns were negative.
  • The short-term seasonal/event disruption risk materialized: Q1FY27 faced a high base of MICE and weddings from Q1FY26, contributing to disappointment versus the predicted Buy returns.

Optimism that failed

  • The favorable supply-demand dynamics expected to drive ARR growth and occupancy gains failed to translate into positive stock returns, as actual returns were negative after most Buy calls.
  • The strong travel and tourism macro outlook did not prevent April 2026 demand softness and lower foreign travel, so the predicted upside from tourism growth did not materialize.
