Yatra Online — Q1-FY27 Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Geopolitical disruption: The Middle East conflict significantly impacted the MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences & Exhibitions) business, particularly international corporate group travel. Several Q1 bookings were deferred to subsequent quarters in FY27.
  • Profitability pressure: EBITDA fell 45.6% YoY to INR 132 Mn, and Net Profit fell 97.9% YoY to INR 3 Mn. Adjusted EBITDA declined 39.4% YoY to INR 151 Mn.
  • Revenue decline: Revenue of INR 1,879 Mn declined 10.4% YoY.
  • Airline-related income weakness: Lower airline-related income resulted from reduced international air traffic and airline capacity. Regional airlines delayed finalization of their incentives for the current fiscal year due to an uncertain business environment.
  • Competitive intensity: Elevated competitive intensity in Air was cited as a factor impacting profitability.
  • Muted corporate travel spends: Corporate travel spends were muted YoY on account of higher ticket prices.
  • Challenging environment: The company described a challenging macroeconomic and geopolitical environment for the travel industry, alongside a volatile macroeconomic environment.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Gross bookings: Grew 16.5% YoY to INR 21,007 Mn, with market share gains despite the challenging environment.
  • Transactions and passengers: Total transactions across Air and H&P increased 12.2% YoY; Air passengers grew 4.8% YoY, outpacing broader industry growth.
  • Hotels: Room nights grew nearly 30% YoY, benefiting from continued expansion and strengthening of hotel supply. Standalone Hotels businesses maintained strong growth momentum.
  • Gross Margin (RLSC): Grew 6.1% YoY to INR 1,227 Mn, reflecting resilient demand.
  • Corporate business: Added 53 new corporate customers during the quarter with annual billable potential of INR 2,223 Mn. The newly established Go-to-Market team focused on the MSME segment added over 30 new logos, demonstrating early traction in a large and underpenetrated market.
  • RECAP: The AI-powered Expense Management solution, launched less than a year ago, reached a cumulative customer base of 20.
  • MICE recovery: The Q2 MICE pipeline is more than 50% higher than Q1 levels, with an improved margin profile, indicating a potential revival in activity.
  • International expansion: A seven-year strategic partnership with Kanoo Travel marks the first significant international expansion of Yatra’s enterprise travel technology platform, combining Yatra’s integrated travel and expense management technology with Kanoo’s regional presence and network of over 100 offices across the Middle East.
  • Market opportunity: Significant headroom for increased online penetration in the corporate travel market. The company serves over 1,300 large and medium corporates and over 60,000 SME clients.

3. Key Risks

  • Geopolitical and macroeconomic risk: International and domestic events, including the Middle East conflict, can disrupt travel volumes and margins, as seen in international MICE.
  • Competitive risk: Heightened competitive intensity in Air pressured profitability.
  • Airline incentive risk: Delays in finalization of airline incentives due to an uncertain business environment reduced airline-related income.
  • Demand sensitivity: Muted corporate travel spends on account of higher ticket prices.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The company’s safe harbor statement notes that actual future performance may differ materially from forward-looking statements due to risks, uncertainties, and assumptions, including changes in demand, competition, and technology, and factors such as India’s political and economic status, government policies, applicable laws, and international and domestic events beyond the company’s control.
  • Reporting correction: An Errata to the Annual Report for FY 2025-26 corrected typographical errors on page 141 concerning undisputed statutory dues under the Goods and Services Tax Act, revising amounts from 69.25, 69.25, 51.37, and 56.71 to 6.92, 6.92, 5.14, and 5.67 (In Millions), excluding interest aggregating Rs. 31.60 Million. The company stated these changes are purely correctional and do not impact the core Financial Report, Profit and Loss Accounts, or the integrity of the Audited Financial Statements.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - Management expects commercial discussions with regional airlines regarding incentives to yield a positive outcome in most cases in Q2. - Management anticipates corporate travel spends to bounce back in Q2 and further improve as the year progresses. - Management notes the Q2 MICE pipeline is 50%+ higher than Q1 with an improved margin profile, providing greater visibility. - CEO Siddhartha Gupta stated confidence in Yatra’s growth and profitability trajectory through FY27, citing sustained momentum across core businesses, continued expansion of the corporate customer base, and emerging opportunities from new products and international markets.

Observed performance against prior context: - Q1-FY27 profitability was impacted by the headwinds described above, with EBITDA down 45.6% YoY and PAT down 97.9% YoY, even as gross bookings, transactions, room nights, and gross margin grew. - The MICE pipeline improvement and airline incentive discussions are forward-looking expectations for Q2, with no Q2 results yet reported in the supplied material. - The Kanoo Travel partnership and RECAP adoption are cited as emerging growth drivers, with RECAP at 20 cumulative customers and the partnership described as an important step in taking the enterprise platform to international markets.

  • The company held its 20th Annual General Meeting on September 15, 2026, where all resolutions, including adoption of financial statements and re-appointment of Director Mr. Murlidhara Kadaba, were passed with the requisite majority through remote e-voting and venue e-voting.
  • A physical group meeting with investors and analysts was scheduled in Gurugram on September 15, 2026, at 11:00 AM, with no unpublished price-sensitive information to be shared.
  • The earnings conference call for Q1-FY27 was held on Thursday, August 13, 2026, at 11:00 AM IST.
