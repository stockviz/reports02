Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates against a backdrop of coal supply chain disruptions and the risk of lower-than-anticipated power offtake, which could weigh on plant load factors. The financial health of power distribution companies (Discoms) remains a structural concern for realising revenues. Ongoing litigation and regulatory matters, including disputes over change in law claims and arbitration proceedings with vendors such as BHEL and L&T, add further uncertainty.

A separate compliance matter arose around the financial results submitted on 07 May 2025. The exchange observed on 20 April 2026 that the company had not submitted a Statement of Impact of Audit Qualifications (SOI) signed by an MD/CEO. The company responded on 22 April 2026 that, as of 07 May 2025, its Board comprised three Wholetime Directors — Mr. Rajiv Rattan, Mr. Himanshu Mathur, and Mr. Baliram Ratna Jadhav — who were duly designated as Key Managerial Personnel under Section 203 of the Companies Act, 2013. On that basis, no separate MD or CEO appointment was required, and the SOI was signed by Mr. Himanshu Mathur, Whole-time Director.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

India’s electricity demand offers strong long-term structural growth, and there is potential for capacity additions in the thermal power sector. The Amravati Plant has demonstrated high operational availability and PLF. The company also benefits from uncontracted power sales on the Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) and favourable regulatory progress on long-pending Change in Law claims.

Key Risks

Principal risks include coal supply chain disruptions, lower-than-anticipated power offtake, reduced plant load factors, the financial health of Discoms, and ongoing litigation and regulatory matters such as change in law claims and arbitration proceedings with vendors like BHEL and L&T.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management remains focused on strengthening operational performance, pursuing resolution of outstanding regulatory and Change in Law claims, maintaining high plant availability, and creating sustained long-term value amid India’s growing energy needs.

For FY 2025-26, the company reported consolidated total income of Rs. 3,346 crore, revenue from operations of Rs. 2,991 crore, and a consolidated net profit of Rs. 52.44 crore. The Amravati Plant achieved a PLF of 82.10% and a Plant Availability Factor of 87.88%. No dividend was recommended due to the absence of distributable profits.

On 27 July 2026, the company announced that an aggregate of 209,75,98,310 equity shares — 88.65% of promoters’ shareholding — placed under a Non Disposal Undertaking in favour of Vistra ITCL (India) Limited as Security Trustee for Kotak Mahindra Bank, securing a Rs. 550 crore working capital facility, had been released. Consequently, the entire promoters’ shareholding of 236,61,03,603 equity shares, representing 44.06% of equity share capital, is now free from all encumbrances.

The 19th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 24, 2026, at 03:00 P.M. (IST) via video conferencing or other audio-visual means.
