BASML: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue contraction and margin pressure. Revenue from operations declined across the comparable periods presented. Standalone revenue fell from Rs. 22,071.06 lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026 to Rs. 21,650.93 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026, and was also below the Rs. 21,739.27 lakhs recorded in the quarter ended 30.06.2025. Consolidated revenue followed the same pattern (Rs. 22,071.06 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 to Rs. 21,650.93 lakhs in Q1 FY2027). Cost of materials consumed rose sharply to Rs. 14,585.52 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 10,812.06 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, compressing profitability.

Profitability decline. Standalone profit before tax fell to Rs. 246.03 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 from Rs. 364.85 lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026. Profit for the period from continuing operations dropped to Rs. 186.81 lakhs from Rs. 780.07 lakhs in the prior quarter. Consolidated profit before tax fell to Rs. 233.85 lakhs from Rs. 315.55 lakhs, and consolidated profit for the period declined to Rs. 1,369.42 lakhs from Rs. 1,407.53 lakhs in the year-ago quarter.

Warrant forfeiture. The Tranche I conversion option (42,25,806 warrants at Rs. 62, allotted 04 November 2024) was forfeited during the quarter ended 30.06.2026 due to non-payment of the balance subscription on 04 May 2025 (standalone note) / 04 May 2026 (consolidated note). The forfeited application money of Rs. 655 lakhs was transferred to capital reserve as at 30.06.2026. This represents a failed capital-raising tranche.

Discontinued operations drag. The land at SIPCOT, Perundurai remains classified as assets held for sale / discontinued operations under Ind AS 105 as at 30.06.2026. The Garment unit at Palladam, previously classified as held for sale, was sold during the year ended 31.03.2026. Discontinued operations contributed a loss after tax of Rs. 124.54 lakhs for the year ended 31.03.2026 and Rs. 24.85 lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026.

Regulatory filing lapse. By letter dated 26.6.2026 (Ref: BASML/SEC/362/NSE/2026-27), the company acknowledged a discrepancy in XBRL financial results filings, where yearly figures were inadvertently submitted instead of quarterly figures. The company attributed this to a clerical oversight, stated the error was rectified, and requested the Exchange to close the matter without further proceedings.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Capital raise via Tranche II warrants. The company issued Convertible Equity Warrants in two tranches: Tranche I (42,25,806 warrants at Rs. 62, allotted 04 November 2024) and Tranche II (41,66,660 warrants at Rs. 36, allotted 22 August 2025), convertible into equity shares within 18 months from allotment. The 25% upfront subscription of Rs. 1,030 lakhs (Tranche I: Rs. 655 lakhs; Tranche II: Rs. 375 lakhs) was classified as “Money Received Against Share Warrants” under Other Equity as at 31 March 2026. Tranche II remains live, with the balance subscription potentially bringing in further capital.

Capital reserve accretion. The forfeiture of Tranche I application money resulted in Rs. 655 lakhs being transferred to capital reserve as at 30.06.2026, strengthening the balance sheet.

Tax regime transition. The company proposed to adopt the new tax regime under section 200 of the Income Tax Act, 2025 (corresponding to section 115BAA of the Income Tax Act, 1961). Deferred tax for the quarter ended 30.06.2026 was computed under the new regime, and opening deferred tax liability was recomputed accordingly. This may lower the effective tax rate going forward.

Dividend declaration. Shareholders approved a dividend at the 36th Annual General Meeting held on August 10, 2026, alongside adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements, re-appointment of directors, and ratification of the cost auditor’s remuneration.

Subsidiary contribution. The consolidated results include Bannari Infotech Private Limited, which contributed to consolidated other income (Rs. 146.67 lakhs consolidated vs. Rs. 159.05 lakhs standalone in Q1 FY2027) and consolidated profit.

3. Key Risks

  • Demand and pricing risk: Revenue declined year-on-year and sequentially, with cost of materials consumed rising sharply, indicating margin vulnerability in the textile business.
  • Capital raise execution risk: The Tranche I warrant forfeiture demonstrates risk that Tranche II conversion may also not complete, potentially leaving the company without expected equity infusion.
  • Discontinued operations overhang: The SIPCOT, Perundurai land remains classified as held for sale; delays in monetisation could prolong drag on reported results.
  • Regulatory compliance risk: The XBRL filing discrepancy and the Exchange’s query indicate scrutiny of the company’s disclosure processes.
  • Single-segment concentration: The company operates primarily in textile and related products, which is considered the only significant business segment under Ind AS 108, leaving it exposed to sector-specific cycles.
  • Tax regime transition risk: Recomputing opening deferred tax liability under the new regime may create volatility in reported tax expenses.

  • The Tranche II warrants (41,66,660 warrants at Rs. 36, allotted 22 August 2025) remain convertible into equity shares within 18 months from allotment, implying a potential future equity infusion.
  • The company’s assurance in its 26.6.2026 letter that greater care will be exercised in future filings with the Exchanges.
