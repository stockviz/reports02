Shivam Autotech Limited — Summary of Recent Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates against a difficult operating and financial backdrop. The disclosed headwinds include constrained liquidity and limited working capital availability, geopolitical conflicts, evolving trade policies, supply chain disruptions, commodity price volatility, and inflationary pressures on power and manpower costs. The implementation of the revised wage code adds a further cost burden. The business is also exposed to dependence on a limited customer base, stringent environmental, safety, and regulatory requirements, increasing competition, margin pressures, and economic downturns.

These pressures are visible in the reported financials. For FY2025-26, the company reported revenue from operations of ₹409.58 crore, EBITDA of ₹31.80 crore, and a loss after tax of ₹81.33 crore. As of March 31, 2026, current liabilities exceeded current assets by ₹5,060.08 lakhs, and net worth was negative at ₹4,068.21 lakhs. The weakness persisted into the following quarter: for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the company incurred a net loss of ₹2,098.94 lakhs, and negative net worth deepened to ₹6,167.13 lakhs as at June 30, 2026. Deferred tax assets have not been recognized as a matter of prudence.

Governance and compliance matters have also arisen. On July 13, 2026, the company submitted a revised XBRL filing after the Exchange flagged deficiencies, including incorrect quarterly and yearly figures and a mismatch in the Cash Flow Statement between the XBRL filing and the PDF financial results. The company attributed these to an inadvertent error while preparing and uploading the XBRL instance document, stating that the PDF version contained the correct financial information. Separately, Mr. Shakti Kant Mahana resigned as Company Secretary, Compliance Officer, and Nodal Officer, with his resignation dated June 23, 2026, and cessation effective at the close of business hours on the same date, on account of personal reasons.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company points to healthy domestic demand in the Indian automotive industry, increasing localisation, and long-term opportunities across both conventional and emerging mobility segments. Growing demand for precision-engineered components and expanding export opportunities are also cited. Beyond automotive, potential is seen in non-automotive sectors such as industrial components, renewable energy equipment, power sector components, construction tools, and railways.

Operationally, the quarter ended June 30, 2026 showed positive EBITDA of ₹792.05 lakhs, and the company met all its debt obligations, including repayment of principal and interest to lenders, banks, and financial institutions as and when they fell due. A Rights Issue has been initiated subsequent to the close of the financial year to augment working capital, reduce leverage, and convert promoter unsecured loans into equity. The Board also approved increasing the Authorised Share Capital from ₹44,00,00,000 to ₹55,00,00,000, and approved the convening of the 21st Annual General Meeting on September 25, 2026, along with the re-appointment of Mr. Yogesh Chander Munjal as a Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director.

3. Key Risks

The principal risks are financial and operational. Negative net worth and the excess of current liabilities over current assets raise questions about liquidity and capital adequacy, even as the company states it has adequate resources to realize its assets and discharge its liabilities in the normal course of business. Continued losses, as seen in both FY2025-26 and the quarter ended June 30, 2026, pressure the balance sheet. The non-recognition of deferred tax assets reflects prudence amid uncertainty over future taxable profits.

Business risks include customer concentration, competitive intensity, margin pressure, commodity and input cost volatility, wage code implementation costs, supply chain disruptions, and regulatory and environmental compliance requirements. Macro risks include geopolitical conflicts, shifting trade policies, and economic downturns. Compliance and disclosure risks are also relevant, given the XBRL filing deficiencies and the resulting revised submission, as well as the resignation of the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management’s priorities for FY2026-27 include restoring operating scale and capacity utilisation, improving manufacturing productivity, increasing customer and product participation, strengthening cash generation, reducing leverage, expanding presence in high-growth product categories, and selectively expanding across automotive and non-automotive applications. Management also expects improvement in the financial position through strategic refinancing of existing borrowings and better operational performance in the coming years.

Observed performance shows mixed signals relative to this guidance. The quarter ended June 30, 2026 produced EBITDA of ₹792.05 lakhs and timely servicing of debt obligations, consistent with the emphasis on cash generation and financial stability. However, the same quarter recorded a net loss of ₹2,098.94 lakhs, and net worth turned more negative at ₹6,167.13 lakhs, indicating that profitability and balance sheet repair remain works in progress. The Rights Issue and the proposed increase in Authorised Share Capital are steps aligned with the stated goals of augmenting working capital, reducing leverage, and converting promoter unsecured loans into equity. The FY2025-26 results — revenue from operations of ₹409.58 crore, EBITDA of ₹31.80 crore, and a loss after tax of ₹81.33 crore — provide the baseline against which the FY2026-27 priorities will be measured.
