Headwinds and Challenges

Lexus Granito (India) Limited reported a sharp deterioration in its standalone financial performance for the quarter ended 30th June, 2026. Revenue from operations fell to Rs. 356.23 Lakh, compared with Rs. 1,153.59 Lakh in the quarter ended 31st March, 2026 and Rs. 1,803.53 Lakh in the quarter ended 30th June, 2025. Total expenses of Rs. 949.65 Lakh exceeded total income from operations of Rs. 685.45 Lakh, producing a loss before tax of Rs. 264.20 Lakh and a net loss after tax of Rs. 267.21 Lakh for the quarter. This compares with a net profit after tax of Rs. 62.17 Lakh in the quarter ended 30th June, 2025 and a net loss of Rs. 65.47 Lakh in the quarter ended 31st March, 2026.

The segment disclosure shows that segment results before finance costs, exceptional items and tax were negative Rs. 161.68 Lakh for the quarter ended 30th June, 2026, against positive Rs. 87.74 Lakh in the March 2026 quarter and positive Rs. 145.66 Lakh in the June 2025 quarter. Finance costs of Rs. 102.52 Lakh in the June 2026 quarter remained a significant burden relative to the revenue base.

The company’s own risk disclosures identify intense competition and pricing pressure, export and geopolitical risks, volatility in natural gas and energy costs, and dependence on real estate and construction activity as material headwinds.

The company’s revenue during the June 2026 quarter was generated entirely from domestic operations in India, with no reportable revenue from overseas or geographical markets during the period. This contrasts with the year ended 31st March, 2026, when overseas segment revenue was Rs. 130.91 Lakh against India segment revenue of Rs. 6,056.65 Lakh.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company identifies growth in domestic real estate and housing, increasing demand for premium products, expansion in the renovation and replacement market, commercial and institutional construction growth, and export market diversification as material tailwinds and opportunities.

For the year ended 31st March, 2026, total revenue from operations was Rs. 6,187.56 Lakh and net profit after tax was Rs. 104.17 Lakh, indicating that the full-year performance remained positive despite the weak June 2026 quarter. The company maintains a domestic demand base and holds 100% of its shares in dematerialized form.

The company has been pursuing a preferential issue of convertible warrants. It received 75% of the total consideration in respect of the allotment of 3,00,000 equity shares pursuant to the conversion of warrants at an issue price of Rs. 45.20 per warrant, aggregating to Rs. 1,01,70,000, as approved by the Board at its meeting held on 27th February, 2026. As of the quarter and year ended 30th June, 2026, the company has received total consideration amounting to Rs. 12,77,40,000. The objects for which funds were raised include repayment of unsecured loans (original allocation Rs. 3,200.00 Lakh, modified allocation Rs. 2,976.00 Lakh, utilised Rs. 1,074.00 Lakh), working capital requirements (original and modified allocation Rs. 1,000.00 Lakh, utilised Rs. 200.5 Lakh), issue-related expenses (original and modified allocation Rs. 10.00 Lakh, utilised Rs. 2.90 Lakh), and general corporate purposes (original and modified allocation Rs. 310.00 Lakh, utilised Rs. 0.00 Lakh). No deviation or variation in the use of funds raised was reported.
