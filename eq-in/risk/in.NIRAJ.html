Headwinds and Challenges

GST search and seizure proceedings. The office of the Director General of GST Intelligence carried out search and seizure operations at the company’s Mumbai office on 6th January 2021 under Section 67(1) and (2) of the CGST Act. The company has challenged the entire proceedings and filed a writ petition with the Gujarat High Court; the matter remains sub-judice. The company has deposited Rs. 108.40 Lacs under protest without any defined tax liability, and no provision has been made in the books. In the year ended 31st March 2021, the company reversed contractual revenue and corresponding sub-contracting costs and other direct expenses due to prudent accounting practices and contractual reasons. The related disputed receipts and payments remain unsettled and are reflected under “Other Current Liabilities” and “Other Current Assets.” The company states it is in the process of resolving the disputes.

Unreconciled balances. Trade payables, trade receivables, advances received, advances given, GST liabilities/input credits, and income tax assets (net of liabilities) are subject to reconciliation. Management is in the process of confirmation and reconciliation of unreconciled figures.

Uncertified and unbilled revenue. Revenue from operations and cost of material consumed include unbilled revenue and costs for uncertified work on ongoing projects across India. Sales include uncertified sales in respect of ongoing projects.

Joint venture accounting. The company books turnover and costs related to joint venture entities in its own books. Under the JV agreements/MOUs, projects are executed by JV partners under the company’s supervision and control; bank accounts receiving project proceeds are controlled by the company, and JV partners are authorised to raise/sign invoices and documents on behalf of the JV.

Expected credit loss provision. Per Ind AS 109, during the previous year the company provided an Expected Credit Loss provision of Rs. 2,606.43 lakh (net) against amounts where litigation/arbitration processes were in progress, pertaining to earlier years, adjusted against the balance of General Reserves. There was no change in the ECL provision during the quarter ended 30th June 2026.

Subsidiary losses. For the year, Niraj Consulting Group Limited (subsidiary) recorded total revenue of Rs. 8.80 lakhs and a loss of Rs. 9.07 lakhs; Niraj Build India Limited (subsidiary) recorded total revenue of Rs. 14.78 lakhs and a loss of Rs. 39.55 lakhs. This subsidiary financial information was not audited by the company’s auditors.

Sequential decline in quarterly profitability. Standalone net profit for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 was Rs. 405.64 lakhs, compared with Rs. 522.59 lakhs in the quarter ended 31st March 2026. Consolidated net profit was Rs. 356.64 lakhs versus Rs. 565.12 lakhs in the prior quarter. Standalone revenue from operations was Rs. 10,429.95 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter versus Rs. 13,463.42 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

New work order. On 5th August 2026, the company announced that M/s. Niraj Cement Structurals Limited JV received a work order from the Water Resource Department, Birpur, Bihar, for “Raising, Strengthening and Construction of Flexible Pavement (Total Length – 31.985 Km) with Restoration of 7 nos. stud and construction of 13 nos. new stud from Km 14.00 to Km 26.275 of ESML under Bihar Water Security and Irrigation Modernization Project (BWSIMP),” valued at Rs. 256.89 Crores including GST. The order is domestic, awarded by a domestic entity, with an execution period of 32 months. The promoter/promoter group/group companies have no interest in the awarding entity, and the order does not fall within related party transactions.

Year-on-year growth. Standalone revenue from operations rose to Rs. 10,429.95 lakhs for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 from Rs. 9,443.60 lakhs for the quarter ended 30th June 2025. Standalone net profit rose to Rs. 405.64 lakhs from Rs. 109.53 lakhs over the same comparison. Standalone profit before tax was Rs. 545.88 lakhs versus Rs. 139.53 lakhs. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 10,453.53 lakhs versus Rs. 9,454.16 lakhs, and consolidated net profit was Rs. 356.64 lakhs versus Rs. 99.87 lakhs.

Income tax appeal position. Income Tax Assets (Net) of Rs. 2,068.82 lakhs are shown under “Other Non-Current Assets.” The company has received appeal orders for most years in its favour, with the appeal order giving effect pending from the income tax department. Management believes the company should receive Income Tax Appeal/Tribunal orders in its favour for pending assessment years as well.

CSR foundation incorporation. On 20th August 2026, Niraj Group Foundation was incorporated in India as a Section 8 company limited by guarantee, with the objective of undertaking social welfare and CSR activities under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013. Niraj Cement Structurals Limited is the holding company and shall have control over the management and affairs of the foundation. No governmental or regulatory approvals were required for the incorporation; there is no cash consideration or share swap, and the foundation has no share capital.

Open offer. An open offer was made by Acquirer Gulshankumar Vijaykumar Chopra to acquire up to 1,55,20,529 equity shares of Rs. 10 each at Rs. 29 per fully paid-up equity share, representing 26.00% of the fully paid-up and voting share capital, pursuant to a Public Announcement dated 16th June 2026, a Detailed Public Statement dated 22nd June 2026 published on 23rd June 2026, and a Letter of Offer dated 18th July 2026. On 28th July 2026, the Committee of Independent Directors concluded that the open offer is fair and reasonable.
