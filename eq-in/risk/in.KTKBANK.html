KTKBANK: Q1 FY27 Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin and yield pressure: Yield on advances declined 10 bps QoQ to 8.68% and 60 bps YoY from 9.28%. Other income fell 10.89% QoQ to ₹355.42 crore and was marginally lower YoY (-0.82%). Operating profit declined 5.64% QoQ to ₹580.34 crore despite a 24.19% YoY rise.

Cost escalation: Employee expenses rose 46.13% QoQ to ₹408.56 crore and 19.77% YoY. Total operating expenses increased 13.82% QoQ to ₹713.37 crore, with the cost-to-income ratio under pressure.

CASA contraction: CASA ratio fell 119 bps QoQ to 32.42%, and CASA balances declined 2% QoQ to ₹35,787 crore, even as total deposits grew 1% QoQ to ₹1,10,396 crore.

Return on equity dip: ROE declined 21 bps QoQ to 12.48%, though it improved 290 bps YoY from 9.58%.

Asset quality overhang in specific sectors: MSME GNPA remained elevated at 6.65% (though down from 7.22% in Mar-26), and Agriculture GNPA stood at 3.08%. Standard restructured portfolio was ₹763 crore, with approximately 53% comprising loans requiring 30% recovery for upgradation.

Slippage additions: Gross NPA additions were ₹113.71 crore in Q1 FY27, with a slippage ratio of 0.14%. Post quarter close, ~76% of SMA-2 additions were regularized.

Cash and RBI balances drawdown: Cash & balances with RBI fell 35.55% QoQ to ₹4,552.46 crore, while borrowings declined 17.35% QoQ to ₹4,404.63 crore.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Credit growth: Gross advances grew 4% QoQ from ₹83,340 crore to ₹86,610 crore, with retail, agri and MSME segments contributing to the expansion. Retail advances rose 3% QoQ to ₹43,714 crore.

Deposit mobilization: Total deposits grew 7% YoY to ₹1,10,396 crore. Retail term deposits increased 3% QoQ to ₹69,410 crore and 6% YoY.

Profitability improvement: Net interest income rose 11.31% QoQ and 24.18% YoY to ₹938.29 crore. NIM expanded 13 bps QoQ to 3.20% and 38 bps YoY. PAT grew 2.64% QoQ and 43.28% YoY to ₹418.95 crore. PBT rose 54.74% YoY.

Asset quality improvement: GNPA improved 20 bps QoQ to 2.58% and 88 bps YoY from 3.46%. NNPA improved 11 bps QoQ to 0.87% and 57 bps YoY. PCR (excl. TWO) strengthened 164 bps QoQ to 67.03%. Credit cost declined to 0.03% from 0.10% QoQ and 0.16% YoY.

Capital strength: CRAR improved 103 bps QoQ to 21.10% and 64 bps YoY. The Bank transferred ₹127.00 crore from Investment Fluctuation Reserve to Revenue Reserve during the quarter.

Cost of funds relief: Cost of deposits declined 23 bps QoQ to 5.14% and 59 bps YoY. Cost of funds fell 22 bps QoQ to 5.16%.

Digital and product initiatives: Mobile app installations stood at 32,53,307; net banking users at 7,11,538; debit cards at 55,50,472; QR metrics at 1,48,697; ATM network at 1,469. The Bank launched KBL Finsurance insurance platform (press release dated August 03, 2026, correcting an earlier typographical error in the date reference). Flexi Deposit for HNI customers launched. PoS facility with M/s Pinelabs launched April 21, 2026. Products in pipeline include surrogate-based lending for housing and mortgage loans, digital document execution for vehicle loans, end-to-end portal for channel partners, digitalization of gold loans, virtual account facility, and capital gain account scheme.

Awards: The Bank won two awards at ‘The Great Indian BFSI & Fintech Awards 2026’ for Credit Line on UPI and Dairy Loan on ULI, plus Cyber Resilience of the Year and runner-up for Cyber Security Team of the Year at the 5th IBA CISO Summit & Citations 2026. It also won Best Product Customization in Finacle for Credit Line on UPI.

Government business: Empanelled as Agency Bank for Direct Tax, GST and Customs Duty collection. Live on EPFO platform, Khajane-II (Karnataka), KPPP e-BG, and RBI’s Digital Rupee pilot. Onboarding of Karnataka Housing Board and Karnataka State Beverages Corporations Ltd is under progress.

Geographic footprint: 976 branches, with 626 in Karnataka, 59 in Maharashtra, 55 in Tamil Nadu, 45 in Andhra Pradesh, and 30 in Telangana. 46.62% of branches are in rural and semi-urban areas.

3. Key Risks

Sectoral concentration in NPAs: MSME at 6.65% GNPA and Agriculture at 3.08% remain the highest NPA-contributing sectors, though both improved QoQ. Housing GNPA was 1.92%, Infra 0.34%, Large enterprises 1.90%.

Restructured portfolio recovery risk: Approximately 53% of the ₹763 crore standard restructured portfolio requires 30% recovery for upgradation; failure to recover could delay movement out of the restructured book.

Co-lending asset quality: Under co-lending arrangements (3 partners, 3,860 outstanding cases, ₹66.63 crore gross outstanding), non-performing advances stood at ₹18.46 crore against performing advances of ₹48.17 crore, indicating elevated stress in this unsecured business loan portfolio.

Project finance exposure: Projects under implementation at quarter end were 201 accounts totaling ₹983.52 crore. Of these, 20 accounts (₹84.86 crore) had resolution processes invoked for DCCO extension; 18 accounts (₹82.43 crore) had resolution plans implemented, and 2 accounts (₹2.43 crore) had plans under implementation.

CASA dilution: The 119 bps QoQ decline in CASA ratio raises the cost of funds trajectory risk if the trend continues.

Employee cost trajectory: The 46.13% QoQ jump in employee expenses, if sustained, could pressure operating leverage.

Unsecured NCD structure: The Bank’s Tier 2 Subordinated Unsecured Non-Convertible Bonds (ISIN INE614B08054, ₹300 crore outstanding) carry Nil security cover by the nature of the instrument, as confirmed in the Regulation 54(3) disclosure.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Targeted performance (as stated in the investor presentation): - Cost to Income %: 52.0% - 55.0% - ROA %: 1.1% - 1.35% - Consolidation of financial position to build stakeholder value and enhanced return on equity

Observed Q1 FY27 performance: - ROA (annualised) stood at 1.29%, within the guided 1.1% - 1.35% band. - ROE was 12.48%, down 21 bps QoQ but up 290 bps YoY. - Cost to income ratio is not explicitly disclosed as a standalone figure in the Q1 FY27 presentation, but total operating expenses rose 13.82% QoQ while operating profit declined 5.64% QoQ, indicating cost pressure relative to the guided range. - The Bank describes its approach as “Managing transitions to achieve stability and sustainable growth” and “Consolidation of financial position to build stakeholder value and enhanced return on equity.”

Guidance on asset quality trajectory: The Bank’s focus on recovering the restructured portfolio (post which it would move out of the restructured book) and the regularization of ~76% of SMA-2 additions post quarter close are consistent with the observed improvement in GNPA (2.58%) and NNPA (0.87%).

Capital adequacy: CRAR of 21.10% provides headroom relative to regulatory requirements, supporting the stated objective of effective capital allocation.

Dividend/distribution: No dividend announcement or capital raising guidance is included in the supplied material. The Bank allotted 21,773 equity shares during the quarter pursuant to the Employee Stock Options Scheme.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative stayed bullish (Buy throughout), but shifted from a confident story of geographic expansion, digital cross-selling, and stable 3.5-3.7% margins to a more guarded note acknowledging cost-income inflation to 55.1%, agri/MSME slippages, and muted fees. Persistent themes were credit growth, margins, opex/cost-income, asset quality, and management guidance; the key changes were margin expectations (3.7% faded to 3.2%), the cost-income tolerance (49-50% to 55.1%), and the growth driver narrative (expansion/collaborations to higher-yielding assets and ECL manageability).

Fears that came true

  • The early fear of a credit-growth slowdown materialized: Q4FY26 loan growth fell to 6.9% y/y, well below the +15% analysts had cautiously assumed, and the May 2024 Anand Rathi Buy call was the DISAPPOINTMENT with -8.2% actual return.
  • The early fear of rising operating expenses and cost-income pressure materialized: employee costs from retirement-benefit provisioning pushed cost-to-income to 55.1% in Q1FY27, above the 49-50% level initially flagged, keeping PPOP under pressure.

Optimism that failed

  • The expectation that margins would remain stable at 3.5-3.7% failed, as NIM declined to 3.2% by Q1FY27 even after a 13 bps q/q improvement.
  • The optimism around digital/collaboration-led cross-selling and third-party products did not translate into sustained fee income, as non-interest income fell sequentially due to muted fees in Q1FY27.
