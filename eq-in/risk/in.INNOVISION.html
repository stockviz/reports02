Headwinds and Challenges

NHAI performance security forfeiture and penalty. NHAI approved the forfeiture of part Performance Security amounting to ₹1,60,00,000 and imposed a penalty of ₹3,60,000 following a Forensic Audit report by M/s Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu India Ltd. that alleged excess collection of User Fee through Electronic Toll Collection at Diengpasoh Fee Plaza. The Company intends to file a Writ Petition before the appropriate Hon’ble Court to challenge the order of forfeiture and seek appropriate relief.

NHAI debarment order. The Company previously received a debarment order from NHAI dated 25.07.2025. The Hon’ble High Court had stayed the operation of the impugned NHAI debarment order at the interim stage, and in connected matters the Delhi High Court has ordered in favour of petitioners setting aside the impugned debarment orders. NHAI has candidly admitted that the court has quashed such debarment orders in batch matters, and the Company expects to obtain the same ruling.

Enforcement Directorate search. A search was conducted by the Directorate of Enforcement, Lucknow Zonal Office, at the Company’s office premises and the residential premises of the Chairman and Managing Director under Section 17 of the PMLA on 02 September 2026. No incriminating documents were found and seized at the residential premises, no final finding of violation or contravention has been communicated, and the search proceedings did not disrupt normal business operations. The Company stated it shall make appropriate disclosures to the Stock Exchanges regarding any further material developments as required under applicable laws.

Quarterly loss. The Company reported a loss before exceptional items and tax of Rs. (98.57) million for the standalone quarter and Rs. (105.06) million for the consolidated quarter ended June 30, 2026.

GST demand orders. The Company previously received demand orders from the GST Department for FY 2019-20 and FY 2023-24.

Regulatory filing deficiencies. The Exchange raised deficiencies in the Outcome of Board Meeting relating to the Audited Financial Results submitted on 28 May 2026, including discrepancies in the XBRL filing (standalone XBRL and PDF figures not matching; mismatch in standalone and consolidated Earnings Per Equity Share for discontinued operations; mismatch in Consolidated Cash Flow Statement figures) and non-submission of the declaration in case of unmodified audit opinion. The Company attributed the XBRL discrepancies to decimals and inadvertent data mapping errors, stated the PDF financial results approved by the Board represent the correct financial figures, and submitted corrected XBRL filings and the requisite declaration. The same query had earlier been raised by the Exchange, to which the Company had submitted a detailed clarification vide its letter dated 01 July 2026.

Forward-looking risk factors. Potential risks and uncertainties include government actions, local developments, technological risks, and other factors beyond the Company’s control as outlined in forward-looking statements.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

New NHAI Letters of Award. Innovision Limited received two separate Letters of Award from NHAI on September 7, 2026, for the IDTL A and IDTL B Fee Plazas (consideration of ₹219,07,29,635) and the Pachwara Fee Plaza (consideration of ₹24,52,79,635), each for a term of one year. The underlying model is asset-light, and commercial considerations reflect gross engagement values rather than direct net revenue additions. Separately, the Company received a Letter of Award / Work Order for engagement as a user fee collection agency for the Pachwara fee plaza of NH-39 and upkeep/maintenance of adjacent toilet blocks, valued at ₹24,52,79,635 with a time period of 1 year, to be executed within 1 year.

NHAI pipeline and digital shift. Opportunities include NHAI’s continuing pipeline of toll asset tenders, a sector-wide shift toward fully digital fee collection, and the potential for participation in future tenders subject to competitive bidding. Management intends to remain disciplined in participation across the NHAI toll asset pipeline while maintaining execution standards across toll, manpower, and skill development businesses.

IPO completion. The Company completed its Initial Public Offering during the year ended March 31, 2026, comprising a fresh issue of 49,13,294 equity shares and an offer for sale of 12,38,000 equity shares at an issue price of Rs. 519 per equity share.

Medium-term growth targets. Management is targeting a medium-term growth trajectory of 45-50% CAGR. The growth pathway indicates Revenue CAGR of 50-60% FY27-FY29, EBITDA CAGR of 50-60% FY27-FY29, and PAT CAGR of 60-70% FY27-FY29. For 2027, the Company is targeting 45+ NHAI toll plazas, with every ₹10 Cr toll working capital capable of unlocking ~₹120 Cr annual toll revenue, targeting 20-30% EBITDA margins in Drone operations, an ₹800 Cr toll order book already secured, and drone revenue commencement. For 2028, expansion across aerial survey, infrastructure inspection and smart-city drone operations, scaling corridor mapping and monitoring across NHAI-linked infrastructure, and indigenous drone manufacturing expansion under PLI and Atmanirbhar ecosystem. For 2029, scaling recurring government-led surveillance, inspection and aerial intelligence contracts, and scaling the trained pilot ecosystem through RPTO and skill development platform.

Emerging verticals. The Company expanded its technology-led offerings through AI-enabled Smart Surveillance solutions and laid the foundation for its Drone Manufacturing and aerial intelligence platform. Aerodrone Robotics operates a DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training Organisation and is among fewer than 50 licensed RPTOs in India, with 200+ trained pilots, 8+ active UAV variants, and 4+ drone products across dual-use defence and civil applications. The AI-enabled surveillance platform offers AI video analytics, facial recognition, ANPR, fire and smoke detection, worker safety and PPE compliance, and crowd analytics, deployable over existing analog and IP camera infrastructure.
