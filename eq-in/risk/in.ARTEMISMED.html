Artemis Medicare Services (ARTEMISMED) — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Other income declined across the standalone and consolidated results, which the company attributes to utilisation of IFC funds towards expansion projects. On a consolidated basis, other income fell 22.4% YoY to Rs 537 Lacs in Q1 FY27 and 31.0% QoQ.
  • Sequential growth was modest relative to the YoY comparison. Consolidated revenue from operations rose 2.9% QoQ, EBITDA 4.1% QoQ, PAT 3.8% QoQ and diluted EPS 4.2% QoQ in Q1 FY27 versus Q4 FY26.
  • Overseas patient revenue growth lagged overall growth. At the Gurgaon hospital, revenue from overseas patients increased 8.6% YoY to Rs 7,651 Lacs, slower than the 15.4% YoY growth in Gurgaon revenue from operations, although it still contributed 27.5% of revenue from operations.
  • Ramp-up execution risk at new facilities. The Raipur hospital commenced operations only in July 2026, and management states that as the facility ramps up, focus will be on strengthening clinical capabilities and expanding specialised services. The South Delhi hospital is not expected to be operational until Q4 FY2029.
  • Capital commitments and financing. The Tower IV expansion at Gurugram is estimated at ~Rs 160–180 Crore, to be funded through internal accruals and debt financing. A proposed ~INR 700 crore fund raise was approved by shareholders to fund growth initiatives and capital requirements.
  • Capacity addition timelines. Tower IV beds are proposed to be added over ~2 years, and the broader roadmap targets ~2,000 beds over the next 3–5 years, indicating a multi-year execution period before returns materialise.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong Q1 FY27 performance. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, consolidated revenue from operations rose 12.7% YoY to Rs 28,732 Lacs, EBITDA rose 27.9% YoY to Rs 6,182 Lacs, PAT rose 48.3% YoY to Rs 3,144 Lacs, and diluted EPS rose 46.7% YoY to Rs 1.98.
  • Gurgaon flagship operating momentum. Gurgaon revenue from operations rose 15.4% YoY to Rs 27,849 Lacs; EBITDA rose 24.4% YoY to Rs 6,163 Lacs; PAT rose 32.7% YoY to Rs 3,306 Lacs. Bed capacity utilisation improved to 65.7% from 61.2%, ARPOB rose to Rs 85,690 from Rs 79,752, and IP volume rose 11.4% YoY to 9,181.
  • Margin expansion. Consolidated EBITDA margin expanded 256 bps YoY to 21.5%, and PAT margin improved to 10.9% from 8.3%. Gurgaon EBITDA margin expanded 160 bps to 22.1%, with PAT margin at 11.9% versus 10.3%.
  • Raipur hospital commencement. The 300+ bed super speciality hospital in Raipur, Chhattisgarh commenced operations in July 2026, described by the Chairman as a milestone expanding Artemis into high-growth markets and extending access beyond the flagship centre.
  • South Delhi expansion. A 30-year operating agreement has been executed for a 650+ bed super-speciality hospital in South Delhi across ~7 lakh sq. ft. on ~3.5 acres of leased land, described as a capital-efficient, asset-light expansion with an expected operational date of Q4 FY2029.
  • Tower IV capacity addition. The Board approved expansion of Tower IV at the Gurugram flagship, adding 200+ beds dedicated to quaternary pediatric care and advanced gynecology & women’s health services, with investment of ~Rs 160–180 Crore over ~2 years.
  • Longer-term capacity roadmap. The company targets expansion to ~2,000 beds over the next 3–5 years. The Gurgaon facility received a Platinum Green Building Certification entitling a 15% increase in FAR, enabling addition of ~100 beds long term, and purchase of additional FAR to 2.15 could add another ~200 beds, taking capacity to 950–1,000 beds.
  • International platform. ~27–30% of revenue is derived from international patients, with a patient base spanning over 150+ countries and partnerships across Africa, CIS, the Middle East and SAARC nations.
  • Mauritius expansion. The company operates an 80-bed Artemis Curepipe Hospital (started FY24) and announced a 110-bed facility, “Artemis Cascavelle Hospital,” in FY27.
  • Clinical partnerships. A strategic partnership with KIMS to establish a Heart & Lung Transplant programme is noted as strengthening high-acuity clinical capabilities.
  • Track record cited in the presentation. Three-year revenue, EBITDA and PAT CAGRs of 14%, 29% and 40% respectively; EBITDA margin expanded from 16% to 20%+ over the last 5 quarters; ARPOB 3-year CAGR of 9%+; and EPS CAGR of 32% over the last 3 years at the Gurgaon facility.
  • Shareholder returns. The 22nd AGM held on July 31, 2026 passed all items (Item 1 to 8), including a final dividend of Re. 0.45 per equity share for FY2025-26.

3. Key Risks

  • Execution and ramp-up risk on the Raipur hospital, the planned South Delhi hospital (expected Q4 FY2029), and the Tower IV expansion (~2 years), each of which requires clinical capability build-out and patient volume ramp-up.
  • Financing risk associated with the ~Rs 160–180 Crore Tower IV investment funded through internal accruals and debt, and the proposed ~INR 700 crore fund raise.
  • Dependence on the flagship Gurugram hospital, which contributed Rs 27,849 Lacs of the Rs 28,732 Lacs consolidated revenue from operations in Q1 FY27.
  • Concentration in international patient revenue, which contributed 27.5% of Gurgaon revenue from operations and is exposed to the factors listed in the company’s disclosure, including general market, macro-economic, governmental and regulatory trends, currency exchange and interest rate movements, and competitive pressures.
  • Forward-looking statement risk. The company’s disclosure notes that a number of risks, uncertainties and other unknown factors could cause actual developments and results to differ materially from expectations, including competitive pressures, technological developments, changes in financial conditions of third parties, and legislative developments.
  • Other income variability tied to utilisation of IFC funds towards expansion projects, which reduced other income YoY and QoQ.
  • Governance-related dilution consideration. International Finance Corporation holds 12.0% as an FII as on June 30, 2026, and the proposed fund raise would be a capital-raising event.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Chairman’s statement on FY27 start. Mr. Onkar Kanwar stated the company “commenced FY27 on a strong note, delivering another quarter of consistent growth across our financial and operating metrics,” supported by improved occupancy, higher ARPOB, and sustained demand across specialised clinical services. Observed Q1 FY27 results are consistent with this: consolidated revenue +12.7% YoY, EBITDA +27.9% YoY, PAT +48.3% YoY; Gurgaon occupancy 65.7% (up 443 bps YoY) and ARPOB Rs 85,690 (up 7.4% YoY).
  • Raipur commencement guidance. The Chairman stated the Raipur hospital commenced operations in July 2026, and Dr. Devlina Chakravarty stated the focus will be on strengthening clinical capabilities, expanding specialised services, and establishing it as a trusted destination for advanced healthcare in Central India. The Board outcome document confirms ~300 beds in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, and the presentation states “Commenced Operations from July 2026.” No revenue contribution from Raipur is reported in the Q1 FY27 figures, consistent with a July 2026 start after the June 30, 2026 quarter end.
  • South Delhi timeline. The presentation states an expected operational date of Q4 FY2029 for the 650+ bed South Delhi hospital, and the Board outcome document states the Vimhans Artemis Hospital in Nehru Nagar, New Delhi will have 650+ beds in a phased manner and is expected to commence operations by FY29.
  • Tower IV timeline and cost. The Board approved 200+ beds at the Gurugram flagship with a period of ~2 years for addition and investment of ~Rs 160–180 Crore, financed through internal accruals and debt financing.
  • Capacity roadmap. The presentation states a roadmap to expand capacity to ~2,000 beds over the next 3–5 years, against ~800 operational beds predominantly in Delhi NCR plus ~300 beds in Raipur.
  • Margin trajectory. The presentation cites EBITDA margin expansion from 16% to 20%+ over the last 5 quarters. Observed consolidated EBITDA margin was 21.5% in Q1 FY27 versus 19.0% in Q1 FY26 and 21.3% in Q4 FY26, and Gurgaon EBITDA margin was 22.1% versus 20.5% YoY and 21.7% QoQ.
  • ARPOB trajectory. The presentation cites an ARPOB 3-year CAGR of 9%+ at the Gurgaon facility. Observed Gurgaon ARPOB was Rs 85,690 in Q1 FY27 versus Rs 79,752 in Q1 FY26 (+7.4% YoY) and Rs 84,571 in Q4 FY26 (+1.3% QoQ).
  • EPS trajectory. The presentation cites an EPS CAGR of 32% over the last 3 years at the Gurgaon facility. Observed consolidated diluted EPS was Rs 1.98 in Q1 FY27 versus Rs 1.35 in Q1 FY26 (+46.7% YoY) and Rs 1.90 in Q4 FY26 (+4.2% QoQ).
  • Operational efficiency. The presentation states ALOS reduced to 3.54 days from 3.67 days YoY, described as operational efficiency improving despite higher patient volumes. Observed IP volume rose 11.4% YoY to 9,181 over the same period.
  • Dividend. The AGM on July 31, 2026 passed the resolution to declare a final dividend of Re. 0.45 per equity share for FY2025-26, with 99.9986% of votes polled in favour.
  • Board approvals. The Board meeting held August 3, 2026 approved the unaudited standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and the Tower IV expansion plan.
