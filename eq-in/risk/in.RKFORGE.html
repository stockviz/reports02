Ramkrishna Forgings Limited — Q1 FY27 Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential PAT decline: Consolidated PAT fell 16.2% QoQ to ₹47 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹56 crore in Q4 FY26, with PAT margin contracting 74 bps to 3.85%.
  • Margin pressure versus historical levels: While EBITDA margin improved to 17.96% in Q1 FY27, this remains well below the 20.86%–22.28% range seen in FY22–FY24, reflecting the impact of the sharp drop to 13.87% in FY25 and 15.17% in FY26.
  • Elevated finance costs: Finance costs rose to ₹57 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹48.59 crore in Q1 FY26, with FY26 full-year finance costs at ₹210 crore versus ₹166 crore in FY25.
  • Rising borrowings: Total borrowings increased to ₹1,616 crore (non-current) plus ₹719 crore (current) in FY26, up from ₹1,083 crore and ₹930 crore respectively in FY25.
  • Joint venture losses: Share of loss of joint venture was ₹0.61 crore in Q1 FY27, following ₹17.85 crore for FY26.
  • Prior exceptional items: FY26 included a ₹42.05 crore provision for expected credit loss on trade receivables due to disruptions from the West Asia conflict and US tariffs, and a ₹9.41 crore impact from new labour codes effective November 21, 2025.
  • Export mix shift: Export share of total revenue declined to 32.3% in Q1 FY27 from 45.2% in FY22, with North America’s share of exports falling to 58.0% in FY26 from 71.3% in FY25.
  • Capacity underutilization: Total capacity utilization was 68% in Q1 FY27, down from 74% in Q4 FY26 and 70% in Q1 FY26, with the Press segment at 60% and Forgings at 64%.
  • Regulatory observations: The company received observations from NSE regarding financial results format and timing of submission related to the FY25 results filed on June 1, 2025.
  • Managerial remuneration approval pending: Proposed managerial remuneration of ₹5.10 crore for FY26 exceeded permissible limits under Section 197 of the Companies Act and requires shareholder approval.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong revenue growth: Consolidated revenue rose 19.8% YoY to ₹1,217 crore in Q1 FY27, with domestic revenue up 20.3% and exports up 11.0%.
  • Profitability improvement: EBITDA grew 47.0% YoY to ₹218 crore, with margin expanding 332 bps YoY to 17.96%. PAT rose 297.6% YoY to ₹47 crore.
  • PBT progression: Profit Before Tax improved to ₹65 crore in Q1 FY27 versus ₹64 crore in Q4 FY26 and ₹24 crore in Q1 FY26.
  • Diversification momentum: Newer verticals — Railways, Oil & Gas, Mining, and industrial segments — are scaling. Railways rose to 6.0% of revenue in Q1 FY27 from 1.7% in FY22.
  • Order wins: Q1 FY27 secured new orders worth ₹278 crore (4-year program life) from the automobile segment, with 82% in Passenger Vehicles and 18% in 2-Wheelers, plus ₹15 crore from the metro segment of Indian Railways.
  • Capex announcement: ₹170.52 crore capex for a 4,000 MT press line and passenger vehicle component manufacturing, with 8,800 MT capacity addition targeted by September 2027, financed by a mix of equity and debt.
  • Casting ramp-up: Casting production rose to 12,357 MT in Q1 FY27 from 6,625 MT in Q1 FY26, with capacity of 28,800 MT added on March 31, 2026.
  • New capacity additions: Press line capacity of 3,000 MT added January 20, 2026; 40,000 MT added March 6, 2026; and 18,000 MT added at Ramkrishna Casting Solutions Limited on December 31, 2025.
  • Non-ferrous expansion: The company is building its portfolio in non-ferrous forgings for Aerospace and Semiconductor industries.
  • Export recovery: Export markets showed revival, with Europe exports at ₹128.41 crore in Q1 FY27 versus ₹117.12 crore in Q1 FY26.
  • ESG progress: 39% increase in renewable energy consumption QoQ; 99.9% permanent employees trained on ESG; 99.8% trained on Human Rights; placed among top 5 suppliers at a leading OEM in ESG Champions Competition.
  • ESG rating: ESG Risk Assessments & Insights Limited assigned an ESG score of 60 based on publicly available information.
  • Leadership transition: Mr. Chaitanya Jalan re-designated as Joint Managing Director effective July 24, 2026, through November 8, 2029.

3. Key Risks

  • Customer concentration in exports: North America accounted for 58.0% of exports in FY26, exposing the company to US tariff and demand risks.
  • Geopolitical exposure: The West Asia conflict and US tariffs previously necessitated a ₹42.05 crore ECL provision on trade receivables.
  • Joint venture performance: Ramkrishna Titagarh Rail Wheel Limited continues to report losses, with FY26 share of loss at ₹17.85 crore.
  • Leverage increase: Borrowings have risen significantly, with FY26 non-current borrowings at ₹1,616 crore versus ₹1,083 crore in FY25.
  • Working capital intensity: Inventories remained elevated at ₹1,246 crore in FY26, and trade payables rose to ₹1,145 crore.
  • Capacity utilization risk: Total utilization declined to 68% in Q1 FY27, which could pressure returns if demand does not absorb new capacity.
  • Regulatory compliance: NSE observations regarding financial results format and submission timing indicate disclosure process risks.
  • Forward-looking statement caveats: The company’s safe harbour notes risks including general economic conditions, government regulation changes, tax laws, and factors beyond its control.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Management commentary (Q1 FY27): Mr. Naresh Jalan stated the company “commenced FY27 on a strong note” with “continued improvement in business momentum across both domestic and international markets,” citing “firm demand in the domestic market coupled with healthy volume growth in exports.”
  • Observed performance: Domestic revenue grew 20.3% YoY and exports grew 11.0% YoY, consistent with the stated momentum. EBITDA margin expanded 332 bps YoY, aligning with the claim of “improved operating leverage and a better business mix.”
  • Guidance on growth drivers: Management cited “sustained demand in domestic market, revival in export markets, expanded capacity and product portfolio, scaling of newer verticals such as casting and aluminum forging” as levers for “sustainable growth.”
  • Observed evidence: Casting production increased 86.5% YoY (12,357 MT vs. 6,625 MT), and new orders of ₹278 crore were secured, supporting the scaling narrative. However, total capacity utilization declined to 68% from 70% YoY, indicating that capacity expansion is outpacing near-term volume absorption.
  • Guidance on non-ferrous portfolio: Management stated the company is “actively working towards building its portfolio in non-ferrous forgings for Aerospace and Semiconductor Industry.” No financial contribution from this initiative has been reported in the supplied material.
  • Capex guidance: The board approved ₹170.52 crore capex with capacity addition targeted by September 2027. Existing capacity utilization was cited as Forging 68% and Casting 78% in the capex disclosure.
  • Investor engagement: The company scheduled group meetings with analysts and institutional investors on September 21, 22, and 23, 2026, in Mumbai, with no unpublished price sensitive information to be shared and no presentation to be made.
  • AGM outcome: All ordinary and special businesses contained in the Notice of the 44th Annual General Meeting held on August 29, 2026, were passed with the requisite majority.

Broker Narrative

The narrative moved from a bullish Buy (Dolat Capital) driven by aggressive capacity additions, the 20-year railway forged-wheel contract, and inorganic/EV expansion, to an Accumulate, and finally to a Not Rated stance (Yes Securities) focused on execution risk, rich valuations, and segment-specific delays such as JMT refurbishment and oil & gas gestation. Persistent themes were railway/forged wheels, revenue diversification, India’s supply-chain shift, and inorganic growth; what changed was the confidence level, with the final report tempering optimism and stressing monitorables.
