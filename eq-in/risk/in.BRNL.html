BRNL: Recent Developments, Headwinds, and Growth Prospects

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Financial distress and going-concern uncertainty. The statutory auditors’ limited review reports on both the standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 flag a material uncertainty related to going concern. The reports note that the Company has defaulted in repayment of its dues and has incurred significant losses as at June 30, 2026. Note 5 of the results records that the Company availed financial assistance of Rs. 19,357.72 lakhs, repayable on March 31, 2025, and defaulted on repayment of principal and interest due on that date. A partial payment of Rs. 8,000 lakhs was made in December 2025 towards interest. The Company states it is in discussions for potential restructuring or resolution of this financial support.

Qualified audit conclusions on interest recognition. The auditors issued qualified conclusions because the Company has not recognised interest on financial assistance availed from July 1, 2024 onwards, which the auditors state is not in compliance with Ind AS 34 read with Ind AS 109. This understates loss before tax for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 by Rs. 921.28 lakhs and understates current liabilities as at June 30, 2026 by Rs. 7,948.54 lakhs. A parallel qualification applies at subsidiary Guruvayoor Infrastructure Private Limited (GIPL), which has not recognised interest from October 1, 2024 onwards, understating loss before tax by Rs. 365.22 lakhs and current liabilities by Rs. 2,560.58 lakhs.

Sharp standalone loss in the June 2026 quarter. Standalone results show a loss before tax of Rs. 15,259.38 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a profit before tax of Rs. 164.35 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. This swing is driven largely by other expenses of Rs. 11,386.91 lakhs and an impairment on investment in associate of Rs. 4,000.15 lakhs. Standalone loss after tax was Rs. 15,251.49 lakhs, against a profit after tax of Rs. 122.30 lakhs a year earlier.

Consolidated losses. On a consolidated basis, the Group reported a loss before tax of Rs. 8,734.65 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, versus a profit before tax of Rs. 1,377.06 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Consolidated loss after tax was Rs. 8,726.76 lakhs, against a profit after tax of Rs. 1,335.01 lakhs a year earlier. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the consolidated loss after tax was Rs. 11,018.96 lakhs.

Legal, regulatory, and investigative proceedings. - SFIO investigation: On July 14, 2026, the Company and its subsidiary Orissa Steel Expressway Private Limited received an email from the Serious Fraud Investigation Office stating that the Ministry of Corporate Affairs had ordered an investigation into the affairs of SREI Infrastructure Finance Limited and others, calling for information under Section 217(1) of the Companies Act, 2013. The Company stated there was no material impact on financial, operational, or other activities at that time and that it would cooperate. - PMLA search proceedings at GIPL: Note 8 records search proceedings under Section 17(1) of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 at the office premises of subsidiary GIPL, pursuant to a CBI investigation and FIR alleging criminal conspiracy leading to unlawful gain. A chargesheet was filed on March 27, 2024, quantifying alleged undue gain at around Rs. 2,447 lakhs. The ED passed an order freezing GIPL’s movable properties (including bank balances and fixed deposits) to the tune of Rs. 12,521.42 lakhs. GIPL has appealed before the Appellate Tribunal (PMLA), and the ED has also filed a prosecution complaint before the Additional Special Sessions Court at Ernakulam. The matter is sub judice. - Kurukshetra Expressway Private Limited (KEPL): An associate was awarded Rs. 86,087 lakhs by an Arbitral Tribunal on August 16, 2024 towards losses including termination payment (net of counter claims), which was set aside by the Delhi High Court. Consequently, the Company has impaired/provided against investments in and receivables from KEPL. - Mahakaleshwar Tollways Private Limited (MTPL): An associate pursuing claims of Rs. 214,916 lakhs including termination payment and other damages filed an arbitration petition before the Madhya Pradesh Arbitration Tribunal, Bhopal, on October 30, 2021. The matter is sub judice. Management considers the carrying amount of investments and receivables from MTPL reasonable and appropriate and holds good for recovery. - IL&FS exposure: The Company received Rs. 7,000 lakhs from IL&FS Financial Services Ltd. in FY 2016-17 and has receivables of Rs. 11,419 lakhs from IL&FS Transportation Networks Ltd. IL&FS filed a Section-7 application before NCLT, Kolkata, which remains sine die. IL&FS filed a Restoration Application under Rule 11 on July 10, 2026 to seek revival of Company Application Number 226 of 2025 in NCLT Mumbai. The case remains sub judice. - GIPL arbitration: Claims and counterclaims are being pursued before an Arbitral Tribunal by both GIPL and NHAI, still pending adjudication. - Solapur Tollways Private Limited (STPL): Pursuant to initiation of CIRP via NCLT-Kolkata order dated December 20, 2024, the Company ceased control over STPL.

Broader sector and business risks. The company faces growth risk (challenges in executing business plans and securing attractive IRR or favourable valuations), business risks including competition, capital intensity, input cost escalation, and traffic growth for BOT projects, and regulatory risks from policy changes by the National Highways Authority of India, such as limitations on arbitration-related claims and awards.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Macro and policy support. Growth prospects are supported by India’s economic resilience, robust domestic demand, and the government’s strong capital expenditure focus on infrastructure, specifically the roads and highways sector. Opportunities arise from integrated corridor-based planning, Public-Private Partnership project pipelines (including BOT, HAM, TOT, and InvIT models), and asset monetization strategies by the government.

Operational and digital initiatives. The company aims to leverage its domain expertise in project management consultancy, operations, and maintenance, alongside digital highway management initiatives and tolling efficiencies to improve performance.

Leadership strengthening. On August 14, 2026, the Board approved the appointment of Mr. Amitabh Kumar Jha as Group Chief Executive Officer, designated as Whole Time Key Managerial Personnel, with effect from that date. His profile includes over 28 years of experience in roads, highways, and transportation, having managed over 50 national and state highway projects across India under BOT and annuity models, with prior roles as CEO and COO at Reliance Infrastructure and L&T IDPL Invit.

Governance and assurance measures. The Board approved the appointment of M/s. VMSM & Co., Chartered Accountants, as Internal Auditor for FY 2026-27, a firm with risk advisory capabilities covering internal audit, risk control matrix, and internal control reporting.

3. Key Risks

  • Going-concern risk: Defaults in repayment of dues and significant losses create material uncertainty over the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern, as flagged by the auditors.
  • Accounting and disclosure risk: Non-recognition of interest on financial assistance at both the Company and GIPL level has led to qualified audit conclusions and understatement of losses and current liabilities.
  • Litigation and investigation risk: Pending SFIO investigation, PMLA proceedings and asset freeze at GIPL, arbitration matters at GIPL and MTPL, the KEPL award being set aside, and the IL&FS matter all carry uncertain outcomes.
  • Asset recovery risk: Management’s view that investments and receivables from MTPL hold good for recovery is stated as reasonable and appropriate, but the underlying claims remain sub judice.
  • Sector and regulatory risk: Competition, capital intensity, input cost escalation, traffic growth assumptions for BOT projects, and NHAI policy changes limiting arbitration-related claims and awards.
  • Subsidiary-level stress: Negative net worth in subsidiaries and CIRP action against STPL indicate financial strain within the group.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Guidance. Management expects that ongoing strategic initiatives aimed at strengthening its financial position, improving operational performance through technology, optimizing operating costs, and strengthening its claims and litigation management framework (including exploring alternative dispute resolution mechanisms) will improve operational efficiencies and financial performance over the medium to long term. Management also states it is hopeful of a positive outcome on restructuring/resolution discussions for the financial assistance at both the Company and GIPL, and that accounts have been prepared on a going-concern basis keeping in view the positive net worth of the Company as at June 30, 2026 and expected realisation from its financial assets and potential restructuring/resolution.

Observed performance. The June 2026 quarter shows a substantial deterioration rather than improvement: standalone loss after tax of Rs. 15,251.49 lakhs (versus a profit of Rs. 122.30 lakhs in the prior-year quarter) and consolidated loss after tax of Rs. 8,726.76 lakhs (versus a profit of Rs. 1,335.01 lakhs). Standalone other expenses rose to Rs. 11,386.91 lakhs from Rs. 112.74 lakhs a year earlier, and an impairment on investment in associate of Rs. 4,000.15 lakhs was recognised. Consolidated revenue from operations for the quarter was Rs. 4,739.46 lakhs, compared with Rs. 4,624.58 lakhs in the prior-year quarter. The auditors’ going-concern material uncertainty and qualified conclusions indicate that the anticipated improvement in financial performance has not yet materialised in the reported period.

Governance actions. The appointment of a new Group CEO with deep highways experience and the appointment of an internal auditor for FY 2026-27 are concrete steps aligned with the stated focus on operational performance and financial strengthening, though their impact on reported results is not yet reflected in the June 2026 quarter.
