R Systems International — Q2 & H1 2026 Announcements Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Currency and hedging volatility. The company adopted hedge accounting for its forward covers effective January 1, 2026. In Q1 2026, a fair value loss of Rs. 18.04 crores was recognized in other comprehensive income as the rupee weakened from Rs. 89.88/USD at December 31, 2025 to Rs. 94.84/USD at March 31, 2026. In Q2, exchange rates ranged between roughly Rs. 92.6 and Rs. 96.5 per USD, and certain forward contracts matured, causing previously accumulated losses to be reclassified from OCI to the profit and loss account, plus a realized loss of Rs. 9 crores on settlement. Other income fell to negative Rs. 87 lakh in Q2 from Rs. 13.1 crores in Q1, and the company recorded an overall exchange loss of Rs. 2.1 crores in Q2 versus Rs. 11.3 crores in Q1.

Profit decline on a sequential basis. Adjusted net profit fell 17.1% quarter-on-quarter (Rs. 62.9 crores in Q2 versus Rs. 75.8 crores in Q1), which management attributed largely to the one-time currency hedging benefit of about Rs. 18 crores realized in Q1. Net profit was Rs. 55.6 crores in Q2 versus Rs. 65.4 crores in Q1, and adjusted EPS declined to Rs. 5.3 from Rs. 6.4.

Rising SG&A and deliberate investment spend. SG&A rose by Rs. 23.8 crores sequentially, from Rs. 91.4 crores to Rs. 115.3 crores, driven by higher sales and marketing spend, the addition of a Chief Revenue Officer, expanded sales bandwidth, AI and domain experts in the sales process, and the launch of the new brand identity and EXIQO AI studio. Q1 also included one-time AR reversals. Management stated these investments will continue.

Elevated effective tax rate. The effective tax rate was around 31%, higher due to non-deductibility of amortization of intangibles acquired through acquisition. The normalized effective tax rate is stated as between 28% and 29%.

Muted organic growth concerns raised by analysts. An analyst noted that despite organizational changes, senior hires, a new incentive structure, and partnership-led improvements in deal quality, organic growth remains muted. Management countered that the company has consistently shown organic quarter-over-quarter growth and grew about 18% year-on-year without real headcount change.

Flat ACV bookings sequentially. Trailing-twelve-month ACV bookings were $82.9 million in Q2 versus $82.3 million in Q1. An analyst characterized Q2 ACV as “almost flattish” relative to the strong Q1 increase. Management attributed this to the prior year’s Q2 having had a few large deal wins, which this year’s Q2 large wins effectively offset.

Geopolitical challenge at Novigo. Management stated Novigo has an ongoing geopolitical challenge due to the situation in the Middle East, though it continues to show strong deal wins across markets including the Middle East.

APAC revenue decline. APAC revenue share fell to 15.3% from 17.5%, attributed to the completion of some larger projects run in the previous quarter.

Project-based, discretionary revenue model. Management noted that data, AI, and cloud work remains largely project-based and discretionary, transformation- or innovation-led rather than maintain-or-sustain, and therefore subject to project life cycles. Annuity revenue remains “still not very high.”

AI deflation risk acknowledged in the sector. An analyst asked about contract value deflation from AI productivity pass-through, noting other IT companies have commented on renewal contract effects. Management responded that because R Systems does not do multi-year contracts or year-on-year renewals to the same extent, it avoids that deflationary effect, and that AI-based productivity is built into its AI-first delivery methodology.

Forward-looking statement risk. The company cautions that its presentation contains forward-looking statements involving risks and uncertainties, that it undertakes no obligation to update or revise them, and that actual results could differ.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong reported growth. Q2 2026 revenue was Rs. 601.7 crores ($63.6 million), up 17.7% year-on-year in dollar terms and 30.2% in rupee terms, and up 1.2% in dollars and 4.7% in rupees quarter-on-quarter. Adjusted EBITDA was a record Rs. 120.7 crores ($12.8 million) at 20.1% margin, up 51.4% year-on-year and 4.4% quarter-on-quarter. Adjusted net profit was Rs. 62.9 crores ($6.6 million) at 10.5% of revenues, up 35.4% year-on-year, with adjusted EPS of Rs. 5.3, up 35.3%.

H1 2026 performance. Revenue was Rs. 1,176.5 crores ($126.4 million), up 30.1% year-on-year; adjusted EBITDA was Rs. 236.4 crores ($25.4 million) at 20.1%, up 51% year-on-year; adjusted net profit was Rs. 138.7 crores ($14.9 million) at 11.8%, up 54.4%; adjusted EPS was Rs. 11.7, up 54.3%.

AI-native strategy and EXIQO. Management attributed growth to sustained demand for AI-first engineering services enabled by EXIQO, the AI studio, leveraging the OptimaAI platform. The company cites over 1,400 AI experts, 150+ reusable AI agents, and 18 industry blueprints. EXIQO is described as delivering almost 2x productivity and 55% gains in turnaround time.

Key deal wins. Five wins were highlighted: a global telecom and media player for advanced analytics and data science; a U.S. small business lender for a GCC covering AI product engineering, software engineering, and digital operations; a global insurance and financial services provider for AI-powered quality engineering on its High Net Worth Reimagine initiative; a global financial services and market access provider for a Microsoft Dynamics 365 retail transformation; and a U.S. ad tech company for AI-powered modernization of its core advertising platform.

GCC services recognition. R Systems was recognized as a Horizon 2 GCC Accelerator in the HFS Horizons: GCC Services 2026 report, described as validation of its AI-first GCC model and platform-led delivery, with proprietary accelerators including GCC Copilot, OptimaAI, EXIQO, and C-Osmosis.

Agentic business operations traction. Management cited deep domain understanding and investment in agentic AI ecosystems, with Novigo’s enterprise landscape and business flow expertise accelerating agentic business AI operations offerings, seen as green shoots in pipeline buildup and deal conversions.

Legacy modernization TAM. Legacy code bases, data estates, and reporting landscapes are described as a large total addressable market, with AI-led modernization a recurring theme across wins.

Engineering velocity as differentiator. Management positions engineering velocity as the key differentiator between where AI delivers ROI and where it fails, described as receiving positive traction from customers and analysts.

Revenue mix shift and productivity. Data, AI, and cloud services now exceed 50% of revenue and continue to rise. Revenue per employee and margin per employee are improving, with revenue up about 18% year-on-year without headcount increase. Utilization rose to 81.13% blended, within the stated 80%–81% operating band.

Client concentration and mining. Top client share rose to 6% from 5.8%, and top 10 clients to 24.4% from 24%. Management stated there is no significant client concentration risk and that mining and growing existing accounts continues to create recurring revenues.

Channel expansion. Management cited increased channel play through private equity firms, hyperscalers, and channel partners, alongside a rising AI component in wins.

Balance sheet strength. Cash and bank balances net of short-term borrowing were Rs. 3,351 million at June 30, 2026 versus Rs. 2,726 million at December 31, 2025. Total equity attributable to shareholders was Rs. 10,983 million versus Rs. 10,323 million. DSO was 55 days billed and 75 days billed plus unbilled. The CFO stated cash generation remains robust, providing flexibility to invest while sustaining shareholder returns.

Inorganic ambition. Management reaffirmed an organic-plus-inorganic growth thesis, stating it continues to evaluate inbound opportunities but will only acquire where capability is differentiated and accretive, while focusing on fully leveraging the Novigo acquisition.

ESG rating. CRISIL ESG Ratings & Analytics assigned the company an ESG rating on July 13, 2026, based on FY2025 disclosures and publicly available data. The company noted it did not engage CRISIL ESG for this assessment.

3. Key Risks

  • Currency and hedging risk: Rupee depreciation boosted results, but management explicitly stated this is beyond its control and cannot be taken for granted. Hedge accounting has introduced OCI volatility and reclassification of losses into profit and loss.
  • Decision cycle and macro uncertainty: Management cited delayed deal decisions and a market dealing with uncertainty as factors affecting constant currency growth reaching the 3%–4% range.
  • Project-based revenue model: Transformation and innovation work carries project life cycles; the company must win more than what closes to sustain growth.
  • Tax rate risk: Non-deductibility of acquisition-related intangible amortization keeps the effective tax rate around 31% versus a normalized 28%–29%.
  • Geopolitical risk: Novigo faces a Middle East geopolitical challenge.
  • Execution risk on pipeline conversion: Management repeatedly emphasized that pipeline quality has improved but conversion to revenue is the determining factor, and declined to provide forward-looking guidance.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: As stated in the company’s safe harbor, actual results could differ from statements made.
  • Governance transition: A postal ballot sought shareholder approval for three new Independent Directors (Mr. Shailesh Sharad Kekre, Ms. Sangeeta Kapil Jit Singh, Mr. Srikanth Balachandran), a Non-Executive Non-Independent Director (Mr. Pranav Damani), and commission remuneration to Non-Executive Independent Directors. The shareholders approved all five resolutions via postal ballot and remote e-voting, which concluded on September 10, 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Margin guidance. Management stated it continues to focus on staying in the 18%–19% adjusted EBITDA range on a sustainable basis, beyond which it will invest in AI initiatives and sales and marketing. Observed: adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.1% in Q2 2026 and 20.1% in H1 2026, above the stated sustainable band, aided by rupee depreciation, improved utilization, higher billable days, and revenue mix. EBITDA net of RSU expense was 19%, almost the same as the previous quarter.

No formal guidance. Management explicitly stated it does not provide forward-looking guidance. When asked about full-year organic growth outlook, the CEO said that if trailing-twelve-month numbers are an indication, the company is stepping into a zone of continued traction, and that bookings of the last half become revenues for this half.

Constant currency growth. An analyst asked when constant currency growth would return to 3%–4%. Management said it tracks this and that the biggest factor is decision cycles, expressing confidence that with reusable assets and repeat AI transformation stories, it is “only a matter of time” before this is seen consistently.

ACV outlook. Management declined to give quantitative guidance on deal wins for upcoming quarters, stating only that pipeline buildup is happening, pipeline quality has improved, and market positioning has improved, which should lead to better conversion.

Wage hike. Management stated it implemented a wage hike in Q2 as part of biannual cycles, and the impact is already reflected in the quarterly results. Observed: employee benefits expense rose to Rs. 3,661.55 million in Q2 from Rs. 3,597.49 million in Q1, while cost of revenues in dollar terms declined from $40.19 million to $38.59 million.

Productivity claims. Management stated EXIQO delivers almost 2x productivity and 55% turnaround time gains, and that revenue rose about 18% year-on-year without headcount increase. Observed: total headcount was 5,270 at June 30, 2026 versus 5,303 at March 31, 2026 and 4,561 at June 30, 2025; technical software services headcount was 4,728 versus 4,741 and 4,040 respectively.

Annuity revenue. Management stated it has started seeing some annuity but that this portion remains not very high, and hopes to report annuity revenue at some point as agentic business operations build.

AI monetization versus deflation. Management stated it focuses on AI monetization rather than AI deflation, and that its AI-first bids already contain productivity gains so won project revenue does not face deflationary pressure. Observed: gross margin improved to 39.23% in Q2 from 36.03% in Q1 and 36.00% in Q2 2025.

Novigo contribution. Management stated Novigo added a boost visible between Q3 and Q4 last year and Q4 to Q1 this year, and that both organizations are now growing in tandem, with Novigo accelerating agentic business operations offerings.

Broker Narrative

The first report was Not Rated and valuation-driven, flagging a 42x-43x forward P/E and below-peer margins while acknowledging structural tailwinds such as the outsourced engineering services market, Blackstone access, and an internal margin-improvement plan. By the Jan 2026 Buy report, the valuation caveat disappeared and the narrative shifted to operational/transactional tailwinds — cost takeout, AI productivity, INR depreciation, deal ramp-ups and acquisitions — while margin pressure resurfaced via wage hikes and lower utilisation. The persistent theme was margin/operating efficiency: first as management’s expected EBITDA improvement, later as an ongoing headwind.

Fears that came true

  • The high 42x-43x forward multiple was not sustainable: the stock fell 51.8% after the first report, unwinding the premium.
  • The margin disadvantage flagged against peers persisted as a risk: the later report still cited wage hikes and lower utilisation, and the eventual Buy call produced a -38.6% disappointment.

Optimism that failed

  • Management’s expected EBITDA margin improvement to high-15s/early-16s did not materialise in a way that protected returns, as the last report still carried wage-hike and utilisation headwinds.
  • The bullish growth narrative around Blackstone client access and global engineering-services market expansion failed to translate into positive share price performance, with actual returns of -51.8% and -38.6% across the two reports.
