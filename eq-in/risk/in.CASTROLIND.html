Castrol India — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Supply disruptions and severe commodity inflation: The 2Q 2026 press release (4 August 2026) describes the operating environment as marked by supply disruptions and severe commodity inflation, with continued challenges around raw material availability and pricing.
  • Inflationary pressures and uneven monsoon: Management stated it remains cautious considering inflationary pressures and uneven monsoon conditions that could influence demand in the second half of the year.
  • Transition period: The CFO described the company as progressing through a period of transition, with the interim dividend framed as accelerating cash returns to shareholders during this transition year.
  • Regulatory/labour cost changes: The company recognised an exceptional item of ₹22.35 crore for the year ended 31 December 2025, aggregating past employee service, following the Government of India’s notification of four Labour Codes effective 21 November 2025. The company assessed the financial impact of these changes and the New Labour Codes, and also referenced Central/State Rules and clarifications, noting it continues to monitor the situation.
  • Event scheduling risk: The Anand Rathi G-200 Summit 2026 investor conference (scheduled for 22 September 2026) is subject to change due to unavoidable circumstances and/or exigencies.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong reported growth: For 2Q 2026, revenue from operations was ₹1,871 crore (up 25% YoY), EBITDA ₹494 crore (up 41% YoY), and PAT ₹348 crore (up 43% YoY). For 1H 2026, revenue was ₹3,417 crore (up 17% YoY), EBITDA ₹823 crore (up 25% YoY), and PAT ₹590 crore (up 24% YoY).
  • Broad-based business performance: Management stated industrial, institutional and consumer businesses all performed well with strong volume growth; power brands in personal mobility grew ahead of the rest of the business, supporting a healthier portfolio mix.
  • Urban cluster strategy: Continued focus on urban clusters, where personal mobility remains a significant growth opportunity.
  • Distribution and reach expansion: National distribution footprint of approximately 160,000 outlets; Auto Care portfolio expanded to around 40,000 physical outlets; 34,000+ independent bike workshops, 16,000+ multi-brand workshops, 850+ Castrol Auto Service centres; rural distribution expanded to ~45,000 outlets with the Rural Service Express network exceeding 950 centres, delivering sustained double-digit growth.
  • Portfolio innovation and localisation: Launches of Castrol Activ Full Synthetic 10W-30 and 5W-30, upgrade of Castrol GTX 5W-30 to GTX Full Synthetic 5W-30, introduction of GTX Full Synthetic 0W-20, and localisation of Alusol SL 61 XBB coolant.
  • Brand engagement: Castrol Activ Full Synthetic TVC reached over 150 million consumers; brand and trade initiatives reached over 22 million consumers and trade audiences; Road Trip United engaged more than 10,000 riders.
  • Supply chain agility: Leveraged global supply chain and diversified vendor base to ensure uninterrupted supply amid volatility.
  • Shareholder returns: Interim dividend of ₹6.25 per equity share declared on 4 August 2026, payable on or before 2 September 2026, with record date 11 August 2026.
  • Leadership continuity: Mr. Saugata Basuray appointed Managing Director with effect from 1 June 2026 for five consecutive years, approved by shareholders via postal ballot concluded 22 August 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material availability and pricing volatility.
  • Inflationary pressures and uneven monsoon conditions affecting demand in the second half of the year.
  • Operational and safety risks inherent in manufacturing, addressed through safety performance (Paharpur nine years and Silvassa three years without significant incident; Silvassa NAMC Gold Award).
  • Regulatory transition risk from the New Labour Codes and related rules, with financial impact assessed and an exceptional charge recognised.
  • Changes to scheduled investor engagements due to unavoidable circumstances or exigencies.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance on shareholder payout: The CFO stated the Board’s interim dividend accelerates a greater portion of cash returns during this transition year, while the company continues to expect overall shareholder payout for the full year to remain broadly in line with established practice. Observed: an interim dividend of ₹6.25 per share was declared on 4 August 2026, payable within 30 days (on or before 2 September 2026).
  • Guidance on demand outlook: Management remains cautious on inflationary pressures and uneven monsoon affecting second-half demand, while committing to invest behind brands and expand distribution. Observed: 1H 2026 delivered double-digit revenue, EBITDA and PAT growth, with rural operations delivering sustained double-digit growth.
  • Guidance on supply continuity: Management cited leveraging global supply chain and diversified vendors to ensure uninterrupted supply. Observed: 2Q and 1H revenue growth of 25% and 17% respectively, alongside the stated volatile raw material environment.
  • Guidance on transition: The CFO expressed confidence in business resilience and ability to continue generating healthy cash flows during the transition period. Observed: PAT growth of 43% YoY in 2Q 2026 and 24% YoY in 1H 2026.
