Headwinds and Challenges

  • Geopolitical and macro volatility: Management attributed a “temporary moderation in business activity” in Q1FY27 to the overhang of geopolitical uncertainties and global economic volatility.
  • Delayed order acceptance: These factors resulted in delayed delivery acceptance of certain key orders, especially in the Heat Transfer segment.
  • Sharp margin compression: Consolidated EBITDA fell to ₹22.6 Cr (7.5% margin) in Q1FY27 from ₹39.8 Cr (14.0%) in Q1FY26 and ₹43.9 Cr (11.2%) in Q4FY26. PAT dropped to ₹2.1 Cr (0.7% margin) from ₹17.9 Cr (6.3%) a year earlier and ₹20.1 Cr (5.1%) in the prior quarter.
  • Segment weakness: Heat Transfer Equipment revenue fell 72.6% Y-o-Y to ₹10.6 Cr and posted a segment loss (EBIT of negative ₹4.1 Cr) versus positive ₹5.8 Cr in Q1FY26. Filtration, Drying and Other Equipment revenue declined 15.9% Y-o-Y to ₹96.8 Cr, with EBIT down 27.8%. Glass Lined Products EBIT fell 40.9% Y-o-Y to ₹6.2 Cr despite revenue growth of 49.0%.
  • Sequential slowdown: Consolidated revenue declined 23.2% Q-o-Q from ₹391.7 Cr in Q4FY26 to ₹300.8 Cr in Q1FY27.
  • Full-year FY26 profitability dip: FY26 PAT of ₹56.6 Cr was down 8.4% versus FY25’s ₹61.8 Cr, with PAT margin slipping to 4.2% from 6.0%.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Healthy and improving order book: Order book stood at ₹662.1 Cr as on June 30, 2026, and grew further to ₹713.8 Cr as at July 31, 2026, providing visibility for enhanced future performance. Enquiries continue across all business segments.
  • Early signs of recovery: The latter half of Q1FY27 showed demand re-emerging from end-user industries, improving customer enquiries, and several previously deferred projects moving toward finalisation.
  • Structural demand drivers: Increasing adoption of process automation, capacity expansion in pharmaceutical and specialty chemical industries, and growing demand for advanced process solutions.
  • International contract win: HLE Surface Technologies GmbH (a step-down subsidiary) received a contract valued at approximately Euro 20.56 million from FLC Portals Group I/S, Denmark, for vitreous enamel cladding panels for a tunnel between the German island of Fehmarn and the Danish island of Lolland, to be executed on or before April 2030.
  • Glass Lined Products momentum: Segment revenue grew 49.0% Y-o-Y to ₹193.1 Cr in Q1FY27, and FY26 segment revenue rose 16.2% to ₹676.4 Cr.
  • Filtration segment scale-up: FY26 revenue for Filtration, Drying and Other Equipment rose 50.9% to ₹474.3 Cr, with EBIT up 55.6%.
  • Heat Transfer full-year growth: Despite the weak quarter, FY26 Heat Transfer Equipment revenue grew 64.6% to ₹200.3 Cr and EBIT rose 19.5%.
  • Leadership position: Management cited leadership across key segments, technological capabilities, a diversified product portfolio, and long-standing customer relationships.
  • Governance continuity: The Board approved continuation of Mr. Yatish Parekh as Independent Director (subject to shareholder approval at the AGM), with his skills, diversified knowledge, and experience seen as beneficial to sustainable growth.

Key Risks

  • Continued geopolitical uncertainty and global economic volatility could further delay order acceptance and deliveries.
  • Concentration of weakness in the Heat Transfer segment, which swung to a segment loss in Q1FY27.
  • Margin pressure: the steep Y-o-Y and Q-o-Q declines in EBITDA and PAT margins indicate cost and pricing stress.
  • Revenue volatility, as shown by the 23.2% Q-o-Q revenue decline.
  • Execution risk on the Euro 20.56 million international contract, which runs to April 2030.
  • The company’s own safe harbor statement flags risks including industry downtrends (global or domestic), political and economic changes in India or key markets abroad, tax laws, litigation, labour relations, exchange rate fluctuations, technological changes, and interest and other cost movements.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Management stated the latter half of the quarter showed early signs of improvement, with demand re-emerging, enquiries improving, and deferred projects moving toward finalisation, giving confidence that the order book will gain momentum in coming quarters. It described the coming quarters as promising and expects to capitalise on emerging opportunities for sustainable long-term growth.
  • Observed performance: The order book did grow from ₹662.1 Cr (June 30, 2026) to ₹713.8 Cr (July 31, 2026), consistent with the improving-demand narrative. However, Q1FY27 reported results themselves were weak: revenue grew only 5.9% Y-o-Y, EBITDA fell 43.2%, and PAT fell 88.5%, with the Heat Transfer segment down 72.6% in revenue and in loss. The recovery management describes is thus indicated by order book and enquiry trends rather than by the reported quarterly financials.
  • Guidance: The international contract is scheduled to be executed on or before April 2030, providing a long-dated revenue visibility window.
  • Guidance: The Board authorised Executive Directors to decide the day, date, time, and venue/mode of the 35th AGM, with the AGM scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. IST via video conferencing/OAVM.
