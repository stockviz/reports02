Headwinds and Challenges

The global technology services environment remained measured, with businesses assessing technology spending against a backdrop of macroeconomic uncertainty, geopolitical developments, and changing priorities around technology investments. The IT services industry reflected a cautious demand environment, with clients remaining selective on discretionary technology spending and decision-making cycles for certain projects taking longer.

On the operational side, the company’s Annual General Meeting arrangements carry inherent limitations of transmission and coordination, with potential audio/video loss due to fluctuations in network connection or mobile hotspots, and risks of postal transit loss or interception.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Enterprises continue to prioritize initiatives that improve productivity, optimize costs, and deliver measurable business outcomes, with artificial intelligence increasingly moving from experimentation toward practical deployment. Growth opportunities include expanding client relationships, strengthening service offerings, developing international markets such as Australia, the United States, and Ireland, entering the public sector and U.S. government projects, and leveraging recurring revenue products like the Circulus automation product and SaaS AP products.

During Q1 FY27, the FAST Practice added seven new clients across Australia, the United States and Ireland, and the IT Services business added one client each in India and the US. XTGlobal commenced its first Finance & Accounting Outsourcing engagement in Ireland, involving an offshore finance support function from its India delivery centres covering management accounts support, accounts payable supervision, employee onboarding, HR administration and ongoing finance-function support. The company also secured an engagement from a leading U.S.-based State Transportation Agency for an Internal eForms Modernization Program leveraging Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), with a 14-month work order and a maximum payable amount of USD $1.59 Million (approximately ₹14.8 Crores).

Separately, XTGlobal was selected as a qualified technology partner under a multi-year Cloud Engineering and Technical Services program for a U.S. State Government technology authority, carrying an initial two-year term with three additional one-year renewal options. Management estimates the aggregate addressable opportunity across the qualified vendor pool could be in the range of USD $50 million to $100 million over the potential five-year period.

The Zoho transformation is 90% complete, covering approximately 13 products/modules, with focus on final go-live, stabilization and realization of expected operational benefits. The company engages in various software development services, IT and ITES services, consulting services, and accounting support services, leveraging inter-company arrangements with wholly owned subsidiaries and subsidiaries to drive operational performance.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management stated that Q1 FY27 marked a steady start to the year, with consolidated revenue rising 1.1% YoY to ₹93.3 crore and operating profitability improving meaningfully. Consolidated EBITDA increased 7.8% YoY to ₹7.1 crore, with EBITDA margin improving to 7.6%, and consolidated PAT for the quarter reached ₹3.9 crore. Management indicated that focus on Australia and Ireland is expected to be high next year, projecting them to reach into the million dollars (local currencies) over the next year, while PaaS is anticipated to represent less than 10% for the next couple of years given the current total revenue. Management also noted that dividend consideration for the financial year might be looked into next quarter upon declaring results.

Observed performance for Q1 FY27: on a standalone basis, revenue from operations stood at ₹19.19 crore (up 8% YoY and 1.8% QoQ), EBITDA reached ₹2.82 crore (up 76.3% YoY) with a margin of 14.7%, EBIT was ₹3.08 crore (up 75.6% YoY) with a 16% margin, and profit after tax was ₹1.82 crore (up 19.8% YoY) with a 9.5% margin. On a consolidated basis, revenue increased 1.1% YoY to ₹93.30 crore, EBITDA reached ₹7.06 crore (up 7.8% YoY), earnings before interest stood at ₹6.15 crore (up 25.2% YoY), and consolidated profit after tax was ₹3.89 crore (up 4.3% YoY).

The Board declared an interim dividend of ₹0.05 per equity share with a record date of 23 September 2026, scheduled the 38th AGM for 29 September 2026, and approved the grant of 734,250 Employee Stock Options and 790,000 Restricted Stock Units under the XTGlobal Infotech Limited Employees Stock Benefit Scheme - 2020. The 38th AGM will be held via Video Conferencing/OAVM, with proposed re-appointment of Ms. Vuppuluri Sreedevi and appointment of new directors, statutory auditor re-appointment, and approval of material related party transactions.
