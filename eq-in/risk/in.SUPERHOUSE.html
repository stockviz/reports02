Headwinds and Challenges

Superhouse Limited faced a compliance setback in 2025 when it could not submit its Audited Consolidated Financial Results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2025 within the deadline of May 30, 2025, as required under Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. The company informed the exchanges on May 30, 2025 that while the Audited Standalone Financial Results had been submitted on time, the consolidated results were delayed because audited financial results had not been received from some of its subsidiary companies. The Board of Directors considered the delayed compliance and advised the management of the subsidiaries that had not submitted their audited results to do so at the earliest, and decided to adjourn the Board meeting convened to consider and approve the consolidated audited results. On June 24, 2025, the company responded to a National Stock Exchange email dated June 23, 2025 regarding the non-submission, referring to its earlier acknowledgement (number 2025/May/114776/27264 dated May 30, 2025) and stating that it would shortly inform the exchange of the date of the forthcoming Board meeting at which the Audited Consolidated Results would be considered and approved.

The company also recognized an exceptional item of Rs. 402.50 Lacs representing impairment in the value of investment in one of its wholly-owned subsidiaries, Linea De Seguridad SLU, Spain. Its subsidiary M/s LA Compagnie Francaise De Protection SARL has ceased or substantially ceased operations in France, leading to a proposal for its disposal, liquidation, and dissolution.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management is streamlining the Group structure and optimizing administrative and operational costs by disposing of or liquidating the non-performing French subsidiary M/s LA Compagnie Francaise De Protection SARL, and utilizing residual funds for repatriation or distribution.

Key Risks

The delayed submission of Audited Consolidated Financial Results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2025 exposed the company to regulatory compliance risk under Regulation 33 of the Listing Regulations, arising from its inability to obtain audited financial results from some subsidiaries in time. The impairment of investment in Linea De Seguridad SLU, Spain, and the cessation of operations at M/s LA Compagnie Francaise De Protection SARL in France represent risks related to underperforming overseas subsidiaries. The proposed disposal, liquidation, and dissolution of the French subsidiary is subject to applicable statutory, regulatory, and liquidation timelines in France, which could affect the timing and outcome of the process.
