TVS Holdings Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Standalone profitability declined sharply in the June 2026 quarter. Standalone profit for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 was Rs. 9.45 crores, compared with Rs. 12.90 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 and Rs. 268.90 crores in the quarter ended 31 March 2026. The March 2026 quarter included dividend income of Rs. 286.54 crores, which the June 2026 quarter did not.
  • Standalone finance costs rose. Finance costs increased to Rs. 39.46 crores in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 21.07 crores a year earlier and Rs. 21.97 crores in the March 2026 quarter.
  • Standalone other expenses increased to Rs. 38.43 crores in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 13.94 crores a year earlier.
  • Leverage increased on a standalone basis. Net debt to equity rose to 1.18 times as at 30 June 2026 from 0.90 times as at 31 March 2026 and 0.57 times as at 30 June 2025. Total debts to total assets rose to 0.53 from 0.44 and 0.36 over the same comparison periods.
  • Standalone net profit margin fell to 10.14% for the June 2026 quarter from 77.62% in the March 2026 quarter and 23.34% in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Loss-making and unreviewed components within the consolidated group. The consolidated results include twenty-three subsidiaries whose interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors and is based solely on management-certified accounts; these reflected total revenue of Rs. 877.32 crores, a total net loss after tax of Rs. 306.88 crores and total comprehensive loss of Rs. 336.70 crores for the quarter ended 30 June 2026. The group’s share of losses of associates was Rs. 14.06 crores for the quarter.
  • Shareholder dissent on the re-appointment and continuation of directorship of Mr Venu Srinivasan. On Resolution 2 (re-appointment as Director liable to retire by rotation), 376,328 votes were cast against, representing 2.1356% of votes polled; public institutions voted 18.0089% against. On Resolution 3 (continuation of directorship as Non-Executive Director under Regulation 17(1A)), 368,629 votes were cast against, representing 2.0919% of votes polled; public institutions voted 17.6405% against.
  • Certain ratios are not applicable following receipt of CIC registration from RBI, including current ratio, debtors turnover, inventory turnover and operating margin, limiting comparability of some disclosed metrics.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Consolidated revenue growth. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to Rs. 17,076.18 crores in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from Rs. 12,742.19 crores a year earlier and Rs. 15,587.53 crores in the March 2026 quarter.
  • Consolidated profit growth. Consolidated profit for the period rose to Rs. 1,173.58 crores from Rs. 675.36 crores a year earlier and Rs. 865.36 crores in the March 2026 quarter. Profit attributable to owners of the Company rose to Rs. 610.25 crores from Rs. 335.55 crores a year earlier.
  • Both reporting segments grew. Automotive Vehicles & Parts segment revenue rose to Rs. 14,399.17 crores from Rs. 10,526.73 crores a year earlier; Financial Services segment revenue rose to Rs. 2,771.68 crores from Rs. 2,267.96 crores. Segment results before interest and tax rose in both segments.
  • Consolidated net worth increased to Rs. 5,666.66 crores as at 30 June 2026 from Rs. 5,070.00 crores as at 31 March 2026 and Rs. 4,030.22 crores as at 30 June 2025.
  • Consolidated leverage improved. Net debt to equity declined to 4.80 times as at 30 June 2026 from 4.88 times as at 31 March 2026 and 5.62 times as at 30 June 2025. Total debts to total assets declined to 0.56 from 0.60 a year earlier.
  • Consolidated net profit margin improved to 6.87% for the June 2026 quarter from 5.54% in the March 2026 quarter and 5.30% in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Investment in Home Credit India Finance Private Limited. During the quarter, the Company invested Rs. 176.38 crores in Home Credit India Finance Private Limited, which appears in the list of subsidiaries.
  • Bonus preference share issuance. The National Company Law Tribunal, Chennai Bench, approved a Scheme of Arrangement between TVS Holdings Limited and its shareholders under Sections 230 to 232 of the Companies Act, 2013, involving the issuance of 46 cumulative non-convertible redeemable preference shares of INR 10 each by way of bonus for every 1 equity share of INR 5 each held on the Record Date. The Company has accumulated substantial surplus reserves from retained profits over the years in excess of current and foreseeable future business requirements, allowing it to distribute surplus funds to shareholders through this bonus issue.
  • Credit ratings affirmed. The NCD credit rating was ‘CRISIL AA+/ STABLE’ and ‘CARE AA+/ STABLE’ across all periods presented.
  • All AGM resolutions passed. All three resolutions set out in the Notice of the 64th AGM held on 22 July 2026 were approved by shareholders with the requisite majority.

3. Key Risks

  • Dependence on dividend income at the standalone level. The sharp swing in standalone profitability between quarters reflects the receipt of dividend income of Rs. 286.54 crores in the March 2026 quarter and none in the June 2026 quarter, indicating standalone results are sensitive to the timing of dividend receipts from investments.
  • Rising standalone leverage and finance costs, with net debt to equity at 1.18 times and finance costs of Rs. 39.46 crores in the June 2026 quarter.
  • Unreviewed subsidiary accounts. Twenty-three subsidiaries’ interim financial information was not reviewed by their auditors and is based solely on management-certified accounts, and three associates’ information was likewise not reviewed. Fifteen subsidiaries and two associates are located outside India, with financial information converted to Ind AS by the Parent Company’s Management.
  • Losses in parts of the group. The unreviewed subsidiaries reflected a total net loss after tax of Rs. 306.88 crores and total comprehensive loss of Rs. 336.70 crores for the quarter ended 30 June 2026; the group’s share of associate losses was Rs. 14.06 crores for the quarter and Rs. 40.81 crores for the year ended 31 March 2026.
  • Interest payment obligations on NCDs. The next due date for payment of interest for NCDs is 22 January 2027, with the previous due date being 24 June 2026.
  • Investor dissent on director-related resolutions, with approximately 2.1% of votes polled against Resolutions 2 and 3 and public institutions voting approximately 17.6–18.0% against.
  • Regulatory and disclosure changes. Following receipt of CIC registration from RBI, classification of assets and liabilities into current/non-current and certain ratios are not applicable to the Company, and ratios for the reporting period and the respective previous period may not be comparable consequent to the winding up of the Trading business.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on free cash flow. Management believes the company will generate strong free cash flow from the redemption of instruments held by it, and that such cash flow will be in excess of its cash requirements in the near future. Observed performance in the June 2026 quarter shows standalone profit of Rs. 9.45 crores and consolidated profit of Rs. 1,173.58 crores, with consolidated net worth rising to Rs. 5,666.66 crores and consolidated net debt to equity improving to 4.80 times, consistent with a strengthening consolidated financial position, while standalone finance costs rose to Rs. 39.46 crores and standalone net debt to equity rose to 1.18 times.
  • Guidance on surplus reserves and bonus distribution. The Company states it has accumulated substantial surplus reserves from retained profits in excess of current and foreseeable future business requirements, allowing distribution of surplus funds to shareholders via the bonus preference share issue approved by the NCLT, Chennai Bench. Standalone reserves excluding revaluation reserve stood at Rs. 1,744.31 crores as at 31 March 2026, and standalone net worth was Rs. 1,759.90 crores as at 30 June 2026.
  • Observed segment performance versus growth prospects. Consolidated segment revenue and results grew across both the Automotive Vehicles & Parts and Financial Services segments in the June 2026 quarter compared with the June 2025 quarter, with consolidated revenue from operations rising to Rs. 17,076.18 crores and consolidated profit for the period rising to Rs. 1,173.58 crores.
  • Observed shareholder approval versus dissent. All resolutions at the 64th AGM on 22 July 2026 were passed with the requisite majority, with Resolution 1 (adoption of audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026) receiving 99.9999% of votes in favour, Resolution 2 receiving 97.8644% in favour, and Resolution 3 receiving 97.9081% in favour.
