The matter involved multiple rescheduled hearings over more than a decade (29.01.2025, 10.03.2025, 21.05.2025, 05.06.2025, 23.07.2025, 15.09.2025, 08.12.2025, 12.03.2026, and 15.06.2026) before resolution.

  • Gross margin expansion: Gross margin reached 7.9%, up 40 bps versus March-26 and up 130 bps versus June-25.

The appeal was allowed with consequential relief, with no material financial impact on the company.
