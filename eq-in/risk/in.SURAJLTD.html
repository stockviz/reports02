Suraj Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements: Key Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential revenue decline: Revenue from operations fell from Rs. 4,343.47 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to Rs. 3,902.77 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • Year-on-year revenue decline: Revenue for the June 2026 quarter (Rs. 3,902.77 lakhs) was lower than the June 2025 quarter (Rs. 5,034.33 lakhs).
  • Sequential profitability decline: Profit before tax fell from Rs. 520.79 lakhs (Q4 FY26) to Rs. 478.67 lakhs (Q1 FY27), and PAT fell from Rs. 379.93 lakhs to Rs. 324.22 lakhs on a standalone basis.
  • Higher effective tax burden: Tax expense rose to Rs. 154.45 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 140.86 lakhs in Q4 FY26, despite lower pre-tax profit, driven by higher current tax (Rs. 114.68 lakhs versus Rs. 29.60 lakhs).
  • Volatile depreciation line: Depreciation, amortization, impairment and obsolescence moved from a negative Rs. 116.36 lakhs in Q4 FY26 to Rs. 138.67 lakhs in Q1 FY27, indicating a swing in this expense item.
  • Dissent from public non-institution shareholders: On Resolutions 2 and 3 at the 33rd AGM (June 26, 2026), 311,254 votes (27.75% of public non-institution votes polled) were cast against, with 2 members voting against each resolution.
  • Related party resolutions saw significant invalid votes: For Resolutions 4 and 5 (Material Related Party Transactions with TBS Metal Private Limited and Suraj Enterprise Private Limited), 13,218,763 votes were declared invalid across 12 members, with only 809,287 valid votes in favour (72.22%) and 311,254 against (2.17%).

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year profit growth: Standalone PAT for Q1 FY27 (Rs. 324.22 lakhs) was more than double the Rs. 154.84 lakhs reported in Q1 FY25, and EPS rose from Rs. 0.84 to Rs. 1.77.
  • Consolidated profit uplift from associate: The consolidated results include a share of profit from associate Suraj Enterprise Private Limited of Rs. 34.74 lakhs in Q1 FY27, compared to a loss of Rs. 128.08 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and a profit of Rs. 119.54 lakhs in Q1 FY25.
  • Consolidated PAT growth: Consolidated PAT rose to Rs. 358.96 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 251.85 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 274.38 lakhs in Q1 FY25.
  • Consolidated EPS improvement: Basic and diluted EPS increased to Rs. 1.95 in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 1.37 in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 1.49 in Q1 FY25.
  • Material related party transactions approved: Shareholders approved Material Related Party Transactions with TBS Metal Private Limited and Suraj Enterprise Private Limited at the AGM, indicating ongoing related-party business relationships.
  • No investor complaints: The company reported no investor complaints received or pending as on June 30, 2026.
  • Cost of materials consumed declined sequentially: From Rs. 4,175.01 lakhs in Q4 FY26 to Rs. 2,410.06 lakhs in Q1 FY27, though this was partly offset by a smaller inventory drawdown (negative Rs. 534.05 lakhs versus negative Rs. 1,829.21 lakhs).

3. Key Risks

  • Revenue concentration in a single segment: The company operates in only one reportable segment — manufacturing stainless and various steel, seamless pipes, tubes, flanges and fittings — as per Ind AS 108.
  • Related party dependence: Two of the five AGM resolutions concerned material related party transactions, and the associate entity Suraj Enterprise Private Limited contributes to consolidated profits.
  • Associate results not independently reviewed: The consolidated financial results include unaudited interim financial results of associate Suraj Enterprise Private Limited, which have not been reviewed by the auditor; the auditor’s conclusion relies solely on management reports for this portion.
  • Governance dissent: The 27.75% against votes on director reappointment and cost auditor remuneration from public non-institution shareholders, plus the large block of invalid votes on related party resolutions, point to shareholder scrutiny.
  • Earnings volatility: The swing in depreciation from negative to positive and the sharp sequential decline in other income (Rs. 283.29 lakhs to Rs. 88.69 lakhs) indicate earnings variability.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • No explicit forward guidance disclosed: The source material does not contain management guidance on future revenue, margins, or profitability.
  • Observed performance versus prior periods: The company reported standalone Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs. 3,902.77 lakhs, down from both the immediately preceding quarter (Rs. 4,343.47 lakhs) and the prior-year quarter (Rs. 5,034.33 lakhs). Standalone PAT of Rs. 324.22 lakhs was higher year-on-year but lower sequentially.
  • Full-year FY26 context: Audited standalone revenue for the year ended March 31, 2026 was Rs. 20,585.08 lakhs, with PAT of Rs. 725.97 lakhs and EPS of Rs. 3.95. Q1 FY27 standalone EPS of Rs. 1.77 represents a pace above the prior full-year average, though the sequential revenue decline tempers this.
  • Consolidated versus standalone: Consolidated PAT (Rs. 358.96 lakhs) exceeded standalone PAT (Rs. 324.22 lakhs) in Q1 FY27 due to the associate’s contribution, whereas in Q4 FY26 consolidated PAT (Rs. 251.85 lakhs) was below standalone PAT (Rs. 379.93 lakhs) because of the associate’s loss.
  • AGM outcomes: All five resolutions were passed with requisite majority on June 26, 2026, including adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY26, reappointment of Mr. Kunal Tarachand Shah as director, ratification of cost auditor remuneration for FY27, and approval of material related party transactions with TBS Metal Private Limited and Suraj Enterprise Private Limited.
