Headwinds and Challenges

  • GST demand under appeal: The company received an order-in-original dated 11 February 2025 from the Commissioner, Central GST & Central Excise Commissionerate, Chandigarh, for an aggregate amount of ₹11.51 crore (including ₹5.75 crore tax demand and ₹5.76 crore penalty). An order-in-appeal upholding the demand was received from the Central Goods & Services Tax Appeals Commissionerate, Chandigarh, dated 20 February 2026. The company filed an appeal before the GST Appellate Tribunal, Chandigarh, on 23 June 2026. The auditor notes uncertainty over the outcome, which will be ascertained on disposal of the matter.
  • Margin pressure visible in the quarterly sequence: Revenue from operations was ₹1,115.27 crore in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 (audited), ₹553.60 crore in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 (unaudited), and ₹902.35 crore in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (unaudited). Profit before tax moved from ₹53.05 crore (Q4 FY26) to ₹15.05 crore (Q1 FY26) to ₹50.28 crore (Q1 FY27). The Q1 FY27 profit recovery came on lower revenue than the March 2026 quarter.
  • Working capital intensity: Changes in inventories of finished goods, work-in-progress and stock-in-trade were negative ₹101.16 crore in Q1 FY27, compared with negative ₹1.41 crore in Q4 FY26 and negative ₹5.55 crore in Q1 FY26, indicating inventory build or absorption dynamics that affect cash.
  • Finance costs: Finance costs were ₹10.66 crore in Q1 FY27, ₹10.57 crore in Q4 FY26, and ₹5.55 crore in Q1 FY26, roughly doubling year-on-year.
  • Governance concentration: The re-appointments involve Mr. Sanjay Gupta (Chairman and Managing Director), his wife Mrs. Sonia Gupta (Whole Time Director), and their sons Mr. Abhishek Gupta and Mr. Aditya Gupta (Whole Time Directors), all for five-year terms from 10 March 2027. The company is promoter-family led across key executive positions.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Profit growth year-on-year: Profit for the period from continuing operations rose to ₹44.98 crore in Q1 FY27 from ₹10.80 crore in Q1 FY26. Profit before tax rose to ₹50.28 crore from ₹15.05 crore over the same comparison.
  • Full-year FY26 profitability: For the year ended 31 March 2026 (audited), revenue from operations was ₹3,180.58 crore, profit before tax was ₹99.52 crore, and profit for the period was ₹73.03 crore, with basic and diluted EPS of ₹1.67.
  • Earnings per share improvement: Basic and diluted EPS were ₹1.03 in Q1 FY27, compared with ₹0.89 in Q4 FY26 and ₹0.25 in Q1 FY26.
  • Operational focus areas: Management states the company is focusing on execution and completion of existing projects to meet delivery timelines, monetization of finished inventory, near-term liquidity and cash flows, and maintaining a prudent capital structure and governance.
  • Business scope: The company is engaged in manufacturing and trading of electrical wires and cables and allied products, treated as a single operating segment under Ind AS 108.
  • Leadership continuity: The Board approved re-appointments of the Chairman and Managing Director, three Whole Time Directors, and five Non-Executive Independent Directors (Mr. Ish Sadana, Ms. Swati Jain, Ms. Monam Kapoor, Ms. Chetna) for second five-year terms from 11 March 2027, subject to shareholder approval. This provides continuity in executive and board oversight.

Key Risks

  • GST litigation outcome: The ₹11.51 crore aggregate demand (tax and penalty) remains contested before the GST Appellate Tribunal. The company, supported by external expert advice, believes it has a good case on merits, but the ultimate outcome is uncertain.
  • Related-party executive concentration: Four of the key executive roles are held by members of the same promoter family, which concentrates decision-making.
  • Revenue volatility: Quarterly revenue has fluctuated significantly (₹553.60 crore, ₹1,115.27 crore, ₹902.35 crore across the three reported quarters), which affects predictability.
  • Inventory and liquidity exposure: The large negative inventory change in Q1 FY27 and management’s stated focus on monetization of finished inventory and near-term liquidity point to working capital risk.
  • Dependence on shareholder approvals: All director re-appointments are subject to approval by ordinary or special resolution at the ensuing 20th Annual General Meeting.
  • Auditor scope note: The figures for the three months ended 31 March 2026 are balancing figures between audited full-year figures and published year-to-date figures up to the third quarter of the previous financial year; the figures up to the end of the third quarter of the previous financial year had only been reviewed and not audited.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management states the company is focusing on execution and completion of existing projects to meet delivery timelines, monetization of finished inventory, near-term liquidity and cash flows, and maintaining a prudent capital structure and governance.
  • Observed performance: Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹902.35 crore was higher than Q1 FY26 revenue of ₹553.60 crore, and Q1 FY27 profit before tax of ₹50.28 crore was higher than Q1 FY26 profit before tax of ₹15.05 crore. However, Q1 FY27 revenue was lower than Q4 FY26 revenue of ₹1,115.27 crore, while Q1 FY27 profit before tax of ₹50.28 crore was close to Q4 FY26 profit before tax of ₹53.05 crore. The negative inventory change of ₹101.16 crore in Q1 FY27, alongside the stated focus on monetization of finished inventory and near-term liquidity, indicates inventory and cash flow remain areas management is actively working on. Finance costs of ₹10.66 crore in Q1 FY27 were nearly double the ₹5.55 crore in Q1 FY26, which is relevant to the stated aim of maintaining a prudent capital structure.
