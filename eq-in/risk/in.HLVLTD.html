HLV Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Lease disputes with the Airports Authority of India (AAI) dominate the risk landscape.

  • AAI unilaterally increased lease rent for 18,000 sq. mtrs. of land used for the Mumbai hotel, effective 1 October 2014. The increased rentals work out to Rs. 584 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and Rs. 18,135 lakhs cumulatively up to that date. The Company has objected and has not provided for the amount.
  • AAI unilaterally terminated the lease and commenced eviction proceedings. The Bombay High Court, by order dated 9 June 2025, directed the Eviction Officer (EO) to proceed on a day-to-day basis and referred the matter to arbitration (other than the EO matter). On appeal, the Supreme Court held that both the Company and AAI may put forward submissions before the “Adjudicating Authority” under the AAI Act, with any adverse order being appealable. The Company has challenged the EO’s jurisdiction in the Bombay High Court, which directed the EO to decide the issue of settlement between the Company and AAI; the EO hearing is in process. Arbitration proceedings are also pending.
  • AAI has claimed Rs. 80,705 lakhs as on 31 January 2019 towards rent and minimum guarantee amount on projected turnover, plus interest, in respect of the lease of 11,000 sq. mtrs. of land in Mumbai on which the proposed hotel was not constructed. The Company disputes the claim on several grounds and, based on legal opinion, treats the liability as contingent, making no provision.
  • Depreciation on the Mumbai hotel building is provided on the assumption that the lease will be renewed.

Litigation involving a minority shareholder.

  • An appeal filed by ITC Ltd. (claiming to be a minority shareholder) with the Supreme Court against the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order — in the matter of transfer of the Business Undertaking to the Brookfield Group — is pending hearing.
  • The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai passed an order dated 24 January 2024 on ITC Limited’s and its subsidiary’s petition alleging oppression and mismanagement, waiving the minimum 10% shareholding threshold for filing under section 241 of the Companies Act, 2013. The Company has appealed to the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT); the matter is at the hearing stage.

Financial performance.

  • For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, total income was Rs. 4,995 lakhs against total expenses of Rs. 5,073 lakhs, producing a loss from operations before exceptional items and tax of Rs. 78 lakhs, and a net loss of Rs. 78 lakhs (basic and diluted EPS: Rs. (0.01)).
  • The Company has accumulated losses of earlier years; considering the same, no provision for taxes has been made.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Year-on-year improvement in the June quarter. Total income rose to Rs. 4,995 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 from Rs. 4,397 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2025. The net loss narrowed to Rs. 78 lakhs from Rs. 347 lakhs, and total comprehensive income improved to Rs. (51) lakhs from Rs. (389) lakhs. EPS improved to Rs. (0.01) from Rs. (0.05).
  • Sequential comparison. The quarter ended 31 March 2026 showed net profit of Rs. 860 lakhs and total income of Rs. 6,766 lakhs, indicating the June quarter was weaker than the immediately preceding quarter.
  • Full-year FY2025-26. Total income was Rs. 21,427 lakhs, profit before tax Rs. 208 lakhs, net profit Rs. 208 lakhs, total comprehensive income Rs. 317 lakhs, and EPS Rs. 0.03.
  • Lease renewal process underway. The disputes regarding rent and royalty payable to AAI were referred to a Settlement Advisory Committee (SAC) constituted by the AAI Board. The Company received an offer letter dated 1 December 2023 from AAI, based on the SAC report, for renewal of the lease of 18,000 sq. mtrs. of land, subject to terms and conditions. The Company has made a representation, which AAI is reviewing; the Company is following up and awaiting a response.
  • Going concern assumption. The financial results have been prepared on a going concern basis on the assumption that the Company will obtain favourable judgments/settlements in respect of the AAI matters, including renewal of the lease, and continue the business.
  • Corporate actions completed. The 45th Annual General Meeting was held on 27 August 2026, and all resolutions contained in the notice were approved by the requisite majority of shareholders. The Board approved the AGM notice and the unaudited Q1 FY2026-27 results at its meeting on 28 July 2026. The Annual Report 2025-26 was dispatched with web-links to members who had not registered e-mail addresses.

3. Key Risks

  • Adverse outcome in AAI disputes. If the Adjudicating Authority or courts rule against the Company, the disputed amounts — Rs. 18,135 lakhs (cumulative rent increase up to 30 June 2026) and Rs. 80,705 lakhs (AAI claim as on 31 January 2019) — could crystallise, along with eviction from the Mumbai hotel land. The auditor’s review report draws attention to these notes without modifying its conclusion.
  • Lease non-renewal. Depreciation on the Mumbai hotel building assumes lease renewal; failure to renew would affect the carrying value and continued operation of the hotel.
  • Going concern dependence. The going concern basis rests on favourable judgments/settlements in the AAI matters, including lease renewal.
  • Minority shareholder litigation. The ITC Ltd. appeal before the Supreme Court and the NCLT/NCLAT proceedings could affect the transfer of the Business Undertaking to the Brookfield Group and the Company’s governance.
  • No tax provision. Due to accumulated losses, no provision for taxes has been made; the tax position depends on future profitability.
  • Quarterly volatility. The June 2026 quarter swung to a loss from a profit in the March 2026 quarter.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance/forward-looking elements. The financial results are prepared on a going concern basis on the assumption that the Company will get favourable judgments and settlements in respect of the AAI matters, including renewal of the lease, and continue the business. The Company states it is following up with AAI on its representation against the 1 December 2023 offer letter and awaiting a response. The Company states it is legally contesting the enhanced rentals, the lease termination, and the eviction proceedings, and has challenged the EO’s jurisdiction.
  • Observed performance. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the Company reported a net loss of Rs. 78 lakhs and negative EPS of Rs. (0.01), compared with a net loss of Rs. 347 lakhs and EPS of Rs. (0.05) in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. For the year ended 31 March 2026, net profit was Rs. 208 lakhs and EPS Rs. 0.03. The AAI disputes remain unresolved as of the announcements: the EO hearing is in process, arbitration is pending, and AAI is still reviewing the Company’s representation on lease renewal. No provision has been made for the disputed amounts, consistent with the stated position that the liabilities are disputed and contingent.
