Sundrop Brands: Recent Developments, Risks and Performance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Channel pressure in spreads. The business continues to face headwinds across both Modern Trade and E-commerce channels in the spreads segment, even as overall consolidated revenue grew.

Workplace safety incident at Del Monte Foods. A fatal accident occurred at the factory unit of material wholly owned subsidiary Del Monte Foods Private Limited in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, resulting in the death of one workman. Operations on the pizza sauce manufacturing line were stopped under Section 40(2) of the Factories Act, 1948, per an order issued September 03, 2026. Other business activities and manufacturing operations remain unaffected, and the impact is currently deemed immaterial.

Provident fund compliance issue. The Company received a Notice-cum-Order dated 30 March 2026 from the Office of the Regional P.F. Commissioner-II (Exemption), Kukatpally, Hyderabad, levying a surcharge of Rs. 2,93,727 on account of minor inadvertent deviations from the prescribed investment pattern by the Company’s PF Trust (Agro Tech Foods Provident Fund) for FY 2023-24. The Company’s representations seeking waiver were rejected by letter dated 24 June 2026, received 25 June 2026, with a direction to remit the surcharge within seven days. The Company states there will be no material impact on operations or other activities.

ESOP allotment deferral. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee could not consider allotment of equity shares pursuant to exercise of vested options under the ESOP Scheme 2024, as the option grantee was restricted from exercising the vested options. No allotment proposal was therefore placed before the NRC on 06 August 2026; the matter will be revisited when the grantee exercises the vested options.

Labour code transition. Following the Government of India’s notification of the four Labour Codes on 21 November 2025, the Group restructured employee compensation with effect from 1 June 2026 and recognised an incremental impact of Rs. 1.55 crores under Employee Benefits expenses for the year ended 31 March 2026. The Group continues to monitor further FAQs and clarifications.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Q1 FY27 growth. Consolidated revenue grew 15%, B2B revenue grew 18%, and e-commerce grew 32%. Gross margin expanded by 110bps in Q1 FY27.

Financial position. Net worth stands at INR 1,496 Cr, with a free cash balance of INR 40 Cr and borrowings of INR 16 Cr as on 30 June 2026.

Marketing focus. The business will continue to prioritise focused, high-ROI marketing investments to strengthen brand equity and accelerate sales growth.

Investor engagement. The Company scheduled meetings with analysts, investors and AMCs — including ICICI Prudential AMC, Kotak AMC, UTI MF, Tata AMC and 360 One MF — on 31 August 2026 in Mumbai, referencing updates and presentations from the Q1 FY27 post-earnings call.

ESOP grants. The NRC approved a grant of 24,000 options to eligible employees of material subsidiary Del Monte Foods Private Limited, and re-approved a grant of 5,500 options to an eligible employee of the Company on 06 August 2026 (superseding the earlier 07 May 2026 approval). Total options granted on 06 August 2026 were 29,500, with exercise prices of INR 636 (tenure-based) and INR 515 (performance-based). During Q1 FY27, the Parent granted 130,367 options, in addition to 14,80,411 granted during FY26.

3. Key Risks

  • Operational disruption from the Hosur accident: the pizza sauce line remains stopped under the Factories Act order; the extent of damage or loss is still being evaluated.
  • Regulatory/compliance exposure: the PF Trust investment pattern deviation resulted in a surcharge and rejection of the waiver request, with a directed remittance timeline.
  • Channel concentration risk in spreads: continued weakness in Modern Trade and E-commerce.
  • Compensation cost inflation: incremental labour code impact recognised, with further clarifications pending.
  • Governance and disclosure obligations: the Board approved amendments to the Code of Conduct for regulating, monitoring and reporting insider trading and fair disclosure of UPSI, aligned with SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

4. Management Guidance vs Observed Performance

Guidance/forward-looking statements:

  • On the Hosur accident, management stated it is evaluating the extent of damage or loss, will intimate further upon completion of final assessment if identified to be material, and will make further disclosures upon occurrence or receipt of any material developments.
  • On investor meetings, management noted that no UPSI or forward-looking statements would be disclosed or discussed.
  • The business will continue to prioritise focused, high-ROI marketing investments to strengthen brand equity and accelerate sales growth.

Observed performance (Q1 FY27, quarter ended 30 June 2026):

  • Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs. 428.08 crores, versus Rs. 372.12 crores in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 386.55 crores in Q4 FY26.
  • Consolidated profit before tax: Rs. 17.15 crores, versus Rs. 5.66 crores in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 13.24 crores in Q4 FY26.
  • Consolidated profit after tax: Rs. 12.13 crores, versus Rs. 4.31 crores in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 9.85 crores in Q4 FY26.
  • Standalone revenue from operations: Rs. 241.75 crores, versus Rs. 208.40 crores in Q1 FY26.
  • Standalone profit after tax: Rs. 9.78 crores, versus Rs. 4.94 crores in Q1 FY26.
  • Consolidated basic EPS: Rs. 3.22 for Q1 FY27, versus Rs. 1.15 for Q1 FY26.
  • Share-based payment expense: Rs. 4.74 crores for Q1 FY27, versus Rs. 4.88 crores for Q4 FY26 and Rs. 19.25 crores for FY26.
  • Promotional expenses netted against revenue: Rs. 0.67 crores standalone and Rs. 1.19 crores consolidated for Q1 FY27, following reassessment of classification during Q4 FY26.
  • The statutory auditors issued an unmodified limited review report on both standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
  • The 39th Annual General Meeting held on 26 August 2026 passed all four resolutions — adoption of financial statements, reappointment of Mr. Ramit Bharti Mittal and Mr. Nitish Bajaj as directors, and ratification of Cost Auditors’ remuneration — with the requisite majority.
