Shemaroo Entertainment — Recent Developments Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Advertising and traditional media weakness. The company states that the overall advertising outlook for its traditional business is expected to remain subdued in the near term, citing the ongoing BARC blackout, macroeconomic pressures, and geopolitical tensions. Traditional media revenue for FY26 fell 28.8% year-on-year to INR 3,084 Mn, even though Q1-FY27 traditional revenue grew 5.2% year-on-year to INR 760 Mn on the back of select B2B licensing deals.

Digital revenue decline. Digital media revenue in Q1-FY27 was INR 557 Mn, down 17.3% year-on-year. Management attributed this to select B2B syndication deals being deferred on account of geopolitical uncertainty and the inherently lumpy nature of that business, partially offset by growth in consumer businesses.

Overall revenue contraction. Q1-FY27 revenue from operations was INR 1,317 Mn, down 5.6% year-on-year. On a full-year basis, revenue declined from INR 7,072 Mn in FY24 to INR 6,851 Mn in FY25 and INR 5,831 Mn in FY26.

Losses and margin pressure. Q1-FY27 EBITDA was negative INR 18 Mn (margin of negative 1.38%), with net loss of INR 81 Mn and EPS of negative INR 2.79. FY26 EBITDA was negative INR 2,648 Mn (negative 45.42% margin) and FY26 PAT was negative INR 2,182 Mn. Management described the last few years as the toughest the industry has witnessed and noted that traditional media monetization has been steadily declining over two to three years.

GST dispute. A GST Department order sought recovery of allegedly inadmissible Input Tax Credit of INR 7,025.61 lakhs plus alleged interest and penalty under Section 74(1) of the CGST Act, 2017, with a further alleged penalty of INR 6,334.98 lakhs. The company’s appeal before the Commissioner (Central Goods & Service Tax and Central Excise Appeals II) was disposed of in the department’s favour. A separate writ petition before the Bombay High Court has been stayed, with no further action to be taken by the department on the impugned orders, and the matter has been referred to a Larger Bench.

FAST channel ecosystem. Management noted that the FAST ecosystem globally has been degrowing over roughly the last 12–18 months, that the number of FAST channels globally has shrunk significantly, and that Shemaroo itself reduced its FAST presence to two channels. FAST was described as still at an early, experimental stage of monetization.

YouTube Shorts monetization. Management stated that monetization of Shorts has not moved to the extent assumed, and that YouTube’s focus appears to be on connected TV and long-form content, which carries better monetization.

Subdued channel profitability. On the free-to-air television channels (Shemaroo TV, Shemaroo Umang), management said monetization is steady but on some channels not good enough to break even, given the ad-led model and a subdued advertising market.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Sharp reduction in losses. Q1-FY27 EBITDA improved 96.7% year-on-year (from negative INR 555 Mn to negative INR 18 Mn), and PAT improved 82.4% (from negative INR 458 Mn to negative INR 81 Mn). Total expenses fell 31.5% year-on-year to INR 1,335 Mn. Management described the quarter as following the successful completion of the inventory charge-off initiative begun nearly ten quarters earlier.

New initiatives investment. Q1-FY27 results included INR 196 Mn of expenses on new initiatives (net of revenue). Excluding these, EBITDA for existing operations would have been approximately INR 180 Mn.

Digital engagement. Shemaroo Filmi Gaane surpassed 74.7 million YouTube subscribers and Shemaroo Entertainment crossed 61.9 million. The portfolio garnered approximately 9.0 billion views during the quarter. Management said the content library has ported well to digital, with titles such as Welcome, Jab We Met, and Phir Hera Pheri performing across platforms, and that legacy, fully charged-off content carries very high margins (cited as potentially 80–90%).

ShemarooMe Gujarati. The platform acquired the OHO Gujarati catalogue in April 2026, adding over 22 Gujarati original web series, and released 10 new titles during the quarter across movies, web series, and plays, including original web series Kajodu and the world digital premiere of Jalebi Rocks, plus Vitthal Teedi S1, Kadak Mitthi S1 & S2, and Cutting S1. Management said ShemarooMe offers a two-year plan with decent uptake, described it as a default Gujarati entertainment platform, and said it has a strong double-digit revenue growth trajectory.

Syndication and recognition. The company became worldwide digital and satellite distribution partner for the Malayalam action thriller Kattalan. Its first AI-powered campaign, “Kindness Badhaye Goodness,” won the Baby Blue Elephant at the Kyoorius Creative Awards 2026 and a Bronze at the Good Ads Matter Awards.

Cost and mix shift. Management said investment focus has pivoted toward digital media and away from traditional media, with new content creation in traditional scaled down and content acquisition dialed up in digital. Operational efficiency efforts over recent quarters are expected to show quarter-on-quarter improvement.

Micro drama optionality. Management said the company is technically ready to accommodate micro dramas on its OTT app and is investing experimentally, but has not gone full throttle pending clarity on monetization.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued advertising weakness in traditional media tied to the BARC blackout, macroeconomic pressures, and geopolitical tensions.
  • Lumpy and deferrable B2B syndication revenue, as evidenced by the Q1-FY27 digital decline.
  • Margin variability driven by content mix — newly acquired content can carry zero or negative margins depending on acquisition pricing, versus high margins on legacy charged-off content.
  • Competitive pricing for new content acquisition, which management noted limits margins on newly acquired titles across both YouTube and syndication.
  • The GST demand order and related litigation, including the adverse appellate outcome and the matter now before a Larger Bench of the Bombay High Court.
  • OTT profitability uncertainty: management said ShemarooMe is at least about two years away from the point where renewing customers reduce acquisition cost pressure, and that no consumer-facing OTT player is currently making money.
  • FAST channel monetization remains unproven and the global ecosystem has been shrinking.
  • Short-form/Shorts monetization remains limited.
  • Free-to-air channel profitability depends on an advertising recovery.
  • Debt levels: borrowings were stated at INR 311 crores as of the Q1-FY27 close, with inventory at INR 348 crores. Short-term borrowings stood at INR 2,949 Mn at FY26, and finance cost was INR 78 Mn in Q1-FY27.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Profitability. Management said the aim is for FY27 to be at least EBITDA positive, and expressed confidence that next year (FY28) will be bottom-line positive. It also said improvement should be visible quarter-on-quarter. Observed: Q1-FY27 EBITDA was negative INR 18 Mn and PAT was negative INR 81 Mn, though both improved sharply year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter (Q4-FY26 EBITDA was negative INR 872 Mn; PAT negative INR 720 Mn).

Revenue growth. Management said it aims for healthy double-digit blended growth over two to three years, with digital growing at double digits and traditional flat to flattish. Observed: Q1-FY27 revenue declined 5.6% year-on-year, with digital down 17.3% and traditional up 5.2%. FY26 revenue declined 14.9% year-on-year.

Margin aspiration. Management said it aspires to upwards of 20% EBITDA margin on a reported basis over a two-to-three-year perspective, while declining to specify timing. Observed: Q1-FY27 reported EBITDA margin was negative 1.38%; FY26 was negative 45.42%.

New initiative spend reduction. Management said the plan to reduce new initiative spending by more than 50% in FY27 versus FY26 is “well on track.” Observed: Q1-FY27 new initiative expenses (net of revenue) were INR 196 Mn; the presentation does not provide a directly comparable FY26 figure for verification.

Debt reduction. Management said there is a debt reduction plan for the year but declined to give a target, citing geopolitical uncertainty and the BARC blackout, while expecting a healthy second half. Observed: borrowings were stated at INR 311 crores at the Q1-FY27 close; the presentation shows short-term borrowings of INR 2,949 Mn at FY26 versus INR 2,962 Mn at FY25.

Inventory write-downs. Management said the company is now in a normal charge-off cycle with no further write-down risk beyond policy-based consumption. Observed: inventory declined from INR 6,823 Mn in FY24 to INR 5,683 Mn in FY25 and INR 3,391 Mn in FY26, and was stated at INR 348 crores at the Q1-FY27 close.

ShemarooMe disclosure. Management declined to disclose subscriber, ARPU, DAU/MAU, or platform revenue figures, saying it is too early to speculate on when separate disclosure might begin.
