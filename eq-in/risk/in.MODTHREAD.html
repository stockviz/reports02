MODTHREAD — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Unprovided preference share dividend. The company has not provided for interest (dividend) on cumulative redeemable preference shares for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 amounting to Rs. 9.01 lakhs, with cumulative unprovided interest (dividend) of Rs. 1092.88 lakhs up to 30 June 2026. Management states this is because the company is in the process of settling the remaining redeemable preference share capital. This matter was also qualified in the audit report on the standalone financial results for the quarter/year ended 31 March 2026 and in the limited review report for the corresponding quarter ended 30 June 2025, making it a repetitive qualification.

Unconfirmed trade balances. Balances of trade payables and trade receivables are subject to confirmation and consequential adjustments, if any. Management states the impact is not ascertainable and that the company is in process of identifying liability of trade payables and trade receivables. This is also a repetitive qualification.

Deferred tax assets not recognised. Deferred tax expense of Rs. 2.77 lakhs (net of OCI expense of Rs. 3.59 lakhs) for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and a deferred tax asset of Rs. 154.40 lakhs as at 30 June 2026, have not been recognised as it is not probable that future taxable profits will be available against which deferred tax assets can be utilised. The same treatment applied to the quarter and year ended 31 March 2026.

Refundable share application money. Share application money of Rs. 1450 lakhs was raised pursuant to a restructuring/settlement scheme submitted to BIFR. Following the enactment of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provision) Repeal Act, 2003 with effect from 1 December 2016, this amount became refundable.

Finance costs rising sharply. Finance costs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 were Rs. 212.62 lakhs, compared with Rs. 93.78 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and Rs. 44.60 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025.

Subsidiary results not audited. The consolidated results include the interim financial results of wholly owned foreign subsidiary Modern Woollens UK Limited, which have not been reviewed by any auditor. These were approved and furnished by management, and the auditor’s conclusion on the statement, insofar as it relates to the subsidiary, is based solely on such unaudited interim financial results.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Revenue growth. Standalone revenue from operations rose to Rs. 8,942.70 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, from Rs. 7,813.96 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and Rs. 6,986.41 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 8,661.69 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with Rs. 7,450.34 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026.

Profitability improvement. Standalone net profit after tax was Rs. 572.95 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, against Rs. 440.91 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 and Rs. 0.63 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Standalone earnings per share (basic and diluted) was Rs. 1.65, compared with Rs. 1.27 and Rs. 0.0018 in the respective comparative periods.

New subsidiary operations. Wholly owned subsidiary Modern Woollens UK Limited started operations in the quarter ended 31 December 2025. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the subsidiary contributed total revenue of Rs. 512.24 lakhs, profit after tax of Rs. 46.19 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 67.71 lakhs. Management states these interim financial results are not material to the Group.

Consolidated performance. Consolidated net profit after tax was Rs. 588.44 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with consolidated basic and diluted EPS of Rs. 1.69.

3. Key Risks

  • Repetitive qualified audit conclusions relating to non-provision of cumulative redeemable preference share dividend and unconfirmed trade payable/receivable balances.
  • Inability to recognise deferred tax assets due to lack of probable future taxable profits.
  • Refundability of Rs. 1450 lakhs share application money following the SICA Repeal Act.
  • Rising finance costs.
  • Reliance on unaudited subsidiary financial information in consolidated results.
  • The company operates as a single business segment (textile manufacturing), as recognised by management and the CODM, so segment-wise information is not disclosed.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

The announcements do not contain explicit forward-looking financial guidance. Management’s stated positions are:

  • On the preference share dividend: the company is in process of settlement with respective preference shareholders, and the cumulative unprovided amount relates to settlement of remaining redeemable preference share capital.
  • On trade balances: management is unable to estimate the impact and states the company is in process of identifying liability of trade payables and trade receivables.
  • On the subsidiary: management states the subsidiary’s interim financial information is not material to the Group.

Observed performance against these positions: the preference share dividend non-provision is repetitive across the quarter/year ended 31 March 2026 and the quarter ended 30 June 2025, indicating the settlement process remains ongoing. The trade balance confirmation qualification is likewise repetitive. Meanwhile, the subsidiary began operations in the quarter ended 31 December 2025 and contributed revenue and profit in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, consistent with management’s description of a newly operating entity.
