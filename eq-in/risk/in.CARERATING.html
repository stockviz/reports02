CARE Ratings Limited — Q1 FY27 Announcements Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty. The global economic landscape remains highly uncertain amid evolving geopolitical dynamics. The West Asia crisis has added volatility to global energy prices. Persistent and potential El Niño conditions, along with a poor monsoon, are flagged as key risks on the domestic front.

Sharp decline in corporate bond issuances. Corporate bond issuances fell 29.3% year-on-year in Q1 FY27 to Rs 2.5 trillion, amid lingering global market uncertainties and elevated yields on corporate bonds. The company notes these conditions likely incentivised large industries to prefer bank credit over bond market funding.

Shift in funding mix away from rated instruments. Bank credit growth accelerated to 18.6% y-o-y as of end-June 2026 (versus 9.5% a year earlier), with industrial bank credit growth at 19.2% (versus 6.3%) and credit to large industries at 16.6% (versus 2.0%). Services sector credit growth strengthened to 21.4% (versus 8.8%). This migration toward bank credit coincides with weaker bond issuance activity.

Competitive and operational risks (as disclosed in the safe harbour). The company identifies risks including competition (domestic and international), economic growth in India and abroad, ability to attract and retain highly skilled professionals, time and cost overruns on contracts, ability to manage international operations, government policies and regulatory actions, and interest and other fiscal costs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Resilient domestic economy. Several high-frequency indicators — domestic auto sales, IIP and core sector growth — point to healthy economic momentum. Merchandise exports have improved in recent months, while services exports and remittances have remained resilient. CareEdge projects India’s GDP growth at 6.7% in FY27.

Commercial paper issuances rose 18.5% y-o-y in Q1 FY27 to Rs 5.4 trillion.

Strong Q1 FY27 financial performance. Standalone operating income grew 16.5% y-o-y to Rs 88.15 crore, with EBITDA of Rs 32.09 crore (36.5% margin) and PAT of Rs 33.88 crore (32.7% margin). Consolidated operating income grew 18.9% y-o-y to Rs 111.68 crore, with EBITDA of Rs 34.63 crore (31.0% margin) and PAT of Rs 32.99 crore (26.1% margin). Consolidated EPS was Rs 11.27 (standalone Rs 10.73).

Segment performance. The ratings business contributed Rs 98.71 crore, up 19.1% y-o-y; the non-ratings business contributed Rs 12.97 crore, up 17.9% y-o-y. Management attributes performance primarily to a healthy uptick in the ratings business across segments, with subsidiaries contributing improved performance.

International and new business expansion. CareEdge Global, headquartered at GIFT City, has rated 45 sovereigns — ranking fourth globally by country coverage — and over USD 20 billion of debt. It has been ahead of the curve in sovereign credit assessment, with other global CRA rating actions aligning for 22 countries over the past 20 months. SBI and NSE announced they will take ~10% stake each in CareEdge Global IFSC Limited.

ESG franchise. CARE ESG Ratings is described as India’s premier ESG rating provider, with a continued market leader position among Category 1 ERPs at 58% market share (issuer-pays model), ratings across 18 sectors, ICMA enlistment for third-party review services, and World Bank registration.

Analytics and advisory build-out. CareEdge Analytics is developing AI-embedded products (CredEdge, IntelEdge, Kalypto, EdgeAvira.AI, ReInvitEdge) and CareEdge Advisory offers sustainability, research, grading and consultancy services.

Outreach activity. The quarter included the “CareEdge Conversation – Debt Market Summit, 2026” in Mumbai (May 26, 2026) graced by the SEBI Chairman; the third edition of the CareEdge Global Sovereign Rating Outlook covering 45 economies, launched in London on June 26, 2026 in the presence of the Minister of Commerce and Industry; and a report launched with ORF in Dehradun on May 29, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Global market uncertainties and elevated corporate bond yields suppressing bond issuance volumes.
  • Poor monsoon and potential El Niño conditions posing risks to domestic growth and bond issuances.
  • Continued preference for bank credit over bond market funding by large industries.
  • Competition (domestic and international), regulatory and government policy actions, and interest/fiscal cost conditions.
  • Execution risks in international operations and in attracting/retaining skilled professionals.
  • Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties, and actual results may differ materially.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance and forward-looking commentary. Management projects India’s GDP growth at 6.7% in FY27. Managing Director & Group CEO Mehul Pandya stated that FY27 commenced on a positive note for the CareEdge Group despite the challenging geopolitical environment, and that the company remains committed to providing superior analytics services to stakeholders across geographies. Management explicitly reiterated that the company’s financial performance should be evaluated on an annual basis, rather than on a QoQ or sequential basis.

Observed performance against commentary. The Q1 FY27 results show standalone revenue growth of 16.5% y-o-y and consolidated revenue growth of 18.9% y-o-y, which management describes as supported by improved subsidiary performance and a healthy uptick in the ratings business across segments. Profitability at both standalone and consolidated levels also reflected healthy growth. The non-ratings businesses continued their momentum of providing a good contribution.

Governance and capital return actions. The 33rd AGM held on July 3, 2026 (notice dated May 13, 2026) transacted adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY2025-26, confirmation of an interim dividend of Rs 8 per equity share and declaration of a final dividend of Rs 14 per equity share for FY2025-26, re-appointment of Mehul Pandya as a director retiring by rotation, and re-appointment of M/s B S R & Co. LLP as Statutory Auditors. All resolutions were passed with the requisite majority. The Statutory Auditors’ Report and Secretarial Auditors’ Report contained no qualification or adverse comment.

Board approval of results. The Board met on August 7, 2026 (11:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.) and approved the unaudited standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, along with the Limited Review Report issued by B S R & Co. LLP.

Investor engagement. CARE Ratings Limited is holding analyst/institutional investor meetings with Quest Investment Managers on 21st September 2026 at 2:30 PM and Bandhan Mutual Fund on 21st September 2026 at 4:30 PM, both in person, with discussions based on publicly available information.

Broker Narrative

The broker moved from an Add in May 2024, focused on Q4 FY24 earnings miss and sluggish domestic ratings growth, to a Buy in Sep 2025 on sector-leading growth and margin expansion. Persistent themes included bank-credit slowdown risk to NBFCs/large industry, Analytics losses, and non-ratings revenue traction. The later narrative added international growth and operating-leverage-driven EBITDA expansion, while de-emphasizing ESG as a catalyst.
