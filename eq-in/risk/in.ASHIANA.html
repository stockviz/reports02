Ashiana Housing — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Regulatory and tax matters. The Income Tax Department, Delhi, levied penalties of ₹2,08,286 under section 271C and ₹20,000 under section 271H of the Income Tax Act, 1961, per orders dated June 29, 2026, received July 1, 2026 and intimated July 2, 2026. The section 271C penalty relates to non-deduction and non-deposit of TDS under section 194-I on annual lease rentals paid to UIT (Bhiwadi) for FY 2013-14; the section 271H penalty relates to delay in filing TDS statements (Forms 27Q and 24Q) for FY 2013-14. The company states it has already deposited the TDS with applicable interest on December 23, 2025 and is exploring legal avenues to contest the orders. It reports no significant impact on financial or operational performance and says it is reviewing and reconciling its records on the monetary implications.

Operating environment and execution risks. The company identifies economic risks including GDP growth fluctuations, inflation, commodity price volatility, and geopolitical tensions affecting sales and costs. It also cites the capital-intensive nature of the business requiring continuous funding, tight regulatory restrictions on customer collections under RERA, extensive statutory and regulatory approval processes that can delay project launches, project execution risks including labour availability, raw material price fluctuations and utility access, and liquidity risks related to sales velocity and unsold inventory.

Quarterly performance moderation. On a standalone basis, total income fell to ₹9,469 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹31,259 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and ₹28,284 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Standalone net profit was ₹1,316 lakh for Q1 FY27, against ₹2,173 lakh in Q4 FY26 and ₹1,319 lakh in Q1 FY26. On a consolidated basis, total income was ₹11,976 lakh for Q1 FY27 versus ₹33,518 lakh in Q4 FY26 and ₹30,272 lakh in Q1 FY26, with net profit of ₹1,311 lakh compared with ₹2,098 lakh and ₹1,272 lakh respectively. Standalone earnings per share (basic, before extraordinary items) was ₹1.34 for Q1 FY27, against ₹2.24 in Q4 FY26 and ₹1.30 in Q1 FY26.

Leverage and coverage metrics. Standalone debt service coverage ratio declined to 0.62 in Q1 FY27 from 0.78 in Q4 FY26 and 2.75 in Q1 FY26; interest service coverage ratio was 2.66 versus 3.29 and 3.03. Standalone debt-equity ratio was 0.33 as at June 30, 2026, against 0.35 as at March 31, 2026 and 0.34 as at June 30, 2025. Inventory turnover ratio was 0.02 in Q1 FY27 compared with 0.31 in Q4 FY26 and 0.40 in Q1 FY26.

Investor grievances. 69 investor complaints were received during the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (including 42 complaints pending resolution at the beginning of the quarter), of which 52 were disposed of and 19 were pending as at June 30, 2026; all of the pending complaints were subsequently resolved as on date.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Macro and demographic demand. The company points to favourable macroeconomic conditions in India with steady GDP growth and urbanisation, and a structural growth opportunity in organised Senior Living driven by an aging population and changing family structures. It describes senior living as highly underpenetrated with need-based, resilient, lifestyle-led demand, and cites robust demand for differentiated segments such as Kid Centric Homes and Premium/Elite Homes.

Revenue visibility and pipeline. The company reports locked-in revenue of ₹7,681.77 crores over the next 3–5 years from ongoing projects, with an unsold value of ₹1,497.94 crores expected to contribute to revenue as units are sold and delivered. It cites a substantial ongoing project pipeline and land bank, strong cash generation supporting asset-light expansion and strategic land acquisitions, and a robust launch pipeline including new projects such as Ashiana Oma, Tattvam, and Aaranya.

FY26 performance base. For FY 2025-26, the company reported its highest-ever value of area booked at ₹2,421.13 crores and record revenue from operations of ₹1,14,301 lakh, driven by deliveries of 20.42 lakh sq. ft. Consolidated profit after tax was ₹11,788.64 lakh. It reports a low debt-equity ratio and pre-tax operating cash flows of ₹576.58 crores. The company describes itself as India’s No. 1 Senior Living brand with over 20 years of experience, over 45 years of overall experience, operations across 8 cities, and 36 ongoing projects.

Capital raising and funding actions. The Board approved the issue of unsecured non-convertible debentures/bonds on a private placement basis up to ₹50 crores. The company issued and allotted 4,325 non-convertible debentures at a nominal value of ₹1,00,000 each, aggregating ₹43.25 crores, on a private placement basis on April 24, 2026, in respect of a new Senior Living Project at Vadgaon (Pune). During the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the company redeemed principal of ₹31.25 crores of secured, rated, listed, redeemable NCDs (ISIN INE365D07085) allotted to ICICI Prudential Credit Risk Fund in May 2024. Outstanding listed debt securities stood at ₹287.50 crores as at June 30, 2026, against a sanctioned amount of ₹397.25 crores. The statutory auditor certified that the company maintained the security cover required under the Debenture Trust Deed and complied with all covenants as at June 30, 2026.

Dividend. The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹1.50 per equity share (75% on face value of ₹2 per share) for FY 2025-26, subject to shareholder approval at the AGM to be held in September 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Economic risks: GDP growth fluctuations, inflation, commodity price volatility, and geopolitical tensions affecting sales and costs.
  • Capital intensity and funding: continuous funding requirements, with tight regulatory restrictions on customer collections under RERA.
  • Regulatory approval risk: extensive statutory and regulatory approval processes that can delay project launches.
  • Execution risk: labour availability, raw material price fluctuations, and utility access.
  • Liquidity risk: dependence on sales velocity and exposure to unsold inventory.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: known and unknown risks, uncertainties and other factors that could cause actual results to differ materially from forward-looking statements, including risks related to implementing strategy, growth and expansion plans, obtaining regulatory approvals, provisioning policies, technological changes, investment and business income, cash flow projections, and market risks.
  • Tax penalty exposure: penalties under sections 271C and 271H of the Income Tax Act, 1961, which the company is contesting, with monetary implications still being reviewed and reconciled.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance. Management is targeting a reported net profit of around ₹2,000 crores for the period from FY 2025-26 to FY 2029-30. For FY 2026-27, the pre-sales target is around ₹2,200 crores, with Senior Living targeted to cross ₹700 crores, backed by a launch pipeline including Ashiana Oma, Tattvam, and Aaranya.

Observed performance. For FY 2025-26, the company reported its highest-ever value of area booked at ₹2,421.13 crores, record revenue from operations of ₹1,14,301 lakh, deliveries of 20.42 lakh sq. ft., and consolidated profit after tax of ₹11,788.64 lakh. In the first quarter of FY 2026-27, standalone total income was ₹9,469 lakh with net profit of ₹1,316 lakh, and consolidated total income was ₹11,976 lakh with net profit of ₹1,311 lakh. The company reports locked-in revenue of ₹7,681.77 crores over the next 3–5 years from ongoing projects and unsold value of ₹1,497.94 crores. The company also reports pre-tax operating cash flows of ₹576.58 crores for FY 2025-26 and a low debt-equity ratio, alongside the capital-raising and redemption actions described above.

Governance and disclosure items. The Board re-appointed Mr. Krishna Suraj Moraje (DIN: 08594844) as Independent Director for five years, subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing AGM, with no change in the constitution of statutory committees. The Board approved an increase in remuneration of Mr. Vishal Gupta (Managing Director), Mr. Ankur Gupta (Joint Managing Director), and Mr. Varun Gupta (Whole Time Director), subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing AGM. The company scheduled a non-deal roadshow (investor group conference, in-person) in Mumbai on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, from 10:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M., subject to change due to business exigencies. The statutory auditors expressed an unmodified opinion on the standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and reported no material deviation or variation in the use of issue proceeds for Q1 FY27.
