HITECHGEAR: Recent Corporate Announcements and Q1 FY27 Performance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Cost inflation and input pressures: Conversion costs increased due to inflationary pressures on PNG, LPG, industrial diesel, and cutting tool consumables, driven by crude and derivatives inflation.

Production disruption from gas shortages: Gas shortages caused intermittent production disruption, resulting in frequent changeovers and additional working hours.

Rising manpower costs: Government-mandated minimum wage revisions added to manpower costs. Employee costs also increased as the organisation strengthened its workforce in critical areas.

Margin pressure: The bottom line remained under pressure on account of continued cost absorption as the transformation programme progresses.

Profitability decline in Q1 FY27: Despite revenue growth, profitability declined across key measures. Consolidated PBT was INR 64 Mn (-39.0% YoY) and PAT was INR 48 Mn (-20.0% YoY). Standalone operational EBITDA was INR 183 Mn (-7.1% YoY), PBT was INR 85 Mn (-34.1% YoY), and PAT was INR 62 Mn (-34.0% YoY).

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Revenue stabilisation and growth: Revenues are stabilising and marginally higher both sequentially and year-on-year. Consolidated operational income for Q1 FY27 was INR 2,377 Mn (10.3% YoY); standalone operational income was INR 1,700 Mn (6.9% YoY).

Domestic demand intact: Domestic demand remained intact through Q1 FY27 with healthy premium motorcycle and scooter demand across both EV and ICE segments.

Export recovery: Export demand has started seeing improvement with consistent orders from North America customers. North American business revenues increased both sequentially and year-on-year, with market demand stabilising and customer ordering patterns returning to normal.

New business wins and pipeline: A new business LOI was received from a new market leader in the premium motorcycle segment in India. The RFQ pipeline includes active RFQs in the two-wheeler (TW) and Passenger Car segments from both new and existing customers. The company won new business in Q2 FY26 (additional business with two existing customers), and multiple other RFQs are being responded to with existing customers in North America.

Operational transformation: The company is progressing through a transformation programme, and has appointed Mr. Manoj Kumar Saxena as Vice President–Operations Transformation, Strategy & Special Projects and Senior Management Personnel effective August 06, 2026. He brings over three decades of leadership experience across the automotive and industrial sectors, with expertise in business transformation, manufacturing excellence, operational turnarounds, P&L management, global operations, and large-scale project leadership, including prior service as Senior Director – East Asia & India Operations at Gates Corporation.

3. Key Risks

Continued cost absorption: The transformation programme continues to absorb costs, keeping the bottom line under pressure.

Input cost volatility: Exposure to crude and derivatives inflation affecting PNG, LPG, industrial diesel, and cutting tool consumables.

Energy availability risk: Gas shortages that cause intermittent production disruption, frequent changeovers, and additional working hours.

Regulatory cost risk: Government-mandated minimum wage revisions adding to manpower costs.

Profitability risk: Declines in PBT and PAT at both consolidated and standalone levels despite revenue growth.

Governance and succession matters requiring shareholder approval: The re-appointment of Mr. Rajiv Batra as Non-Executive Independent Director for a second five-year term effective November 02, 2026, and his continuation beyond age 75 (attained September 30, 2027), as well as the continuation of Mr. Bidadi Anjani Kumar as Non-Executive Non-Independent Director beyond age 75 (attained March 25, 2027), are subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing AGM.
