Everest Industries: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Weak Steel Buildings Performance The steel buildings segment has been a persistent drag. In the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the segment reported a loss before tax and finance costs of Rs. 310.17 lakhs (standalone) and Rs. 314.64 lakhs (consolidated). This follows a much larger loss of Rs. 2,956.31 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the segment loss stood at Rs. 5,504.97 lakhs (standalone). Segment revenue for steel buildings fell to Rs. 6,225.80 lakhs in Q1 FY2027 from Rs. 12,992.48 lakhs in Q1 FY2026.

Withdrawal of CAPEX Plans On July 3, 2026, the Board of Everest Steel Building Private Limited withdrew its CAPEX plan for a new Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEB) manufacturing facility, including surrender of allocated land. Separately, the Board of Everest Buildpro Private Limited withdrew its CAPEX plan for a new Fibre Cement Boards plant, also surrendering allocated land. These decisions triggered impairment and write-off charges.

GST Show Cause Notice The company received a show cause notice (DRC 01) from the Deputy Commissioner of State Tax, CT & GST Circle, Balasore, Odisha, raising a total demand of Rs. 3,89,21,097 (Tax Rs. 1,30,83,256 + Interest Rs. 1,27,54,585 + Penalty Rs. 1,30,83,256) under Section 74 of the CGST Act, 2017 and OGST Act, 2017 for financial year 2020-21. The concerns raised include discharge of liabilities without due interest, issuance of credit notes beyond the time limit, credit notes for inward supplies and reduction of ITC, availed blocked credit, disallowance of input tax credit, and liability under RCM transactions.

Labour Codes Impact Following the Government of India’s notification of the Labour Codes on November 21, 2025, the company recognised an additional charge of Rs. 1,634.14 lakhs pertaining to gratuity as an exceptional item for the year ended March 31, 2026 (Rs. 1,668.84 lakhs for the Group). The company continues to monitor finalisation of Central/State Rules and clarifications on other aspects of the Labour Codes.

Year-End Losses For the year ended March 31, 2026, the company reported a standalone loss before tax of Rs. 11,105.18 lakhs and a consolidated loss before tax of Rs. 11,151.62 lakhs. The standalone loss for the period was Rs. 10,017.42 lakhs, with a consolidated loss of Rs. 10,168.70 lakhs.

Subsidiary Impairments During the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, the company recognised an impairment loss of Rs. 2,870.00 lakhs related to Everest Buildpro Pvt Ltd, comprising Rs. 1,500.88 lakhs on investment in equity shares and Rs. 1,369.12 lakhs on an inter-corporate deposit. In Q1 FY2027, an additional impairment of Rs. 489.00 lakhs on an inter-corporate deposit and Rs. 1.00 lakh on investment in Everest Steel Building Private Limited was recognised.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Building Products Segment Strength The building products segment delivered a profit before tax and finance costs of Rs. 5,671.69 lakhs (standalone) in Q1 FY2027, up from Rs. 2,927.76 lakhs in Q1 FY2026. Segment revenue stood at Rs. 35,643.91 lakhs (standalone) and Rs. 37,362.41 lakhs (consolidated). For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the segment generated a profit of Rs. 6,824.03 lakhs (standalone).

Property Sale Gain During Q1 FY2027, the company sold its property at Podanur, resulting in a profit of Rs. 9,615.42 lakhs, disclosed as an exceptional item. This property had been classified as ‘Asset Held for Sale’ in the audited balance sheet as of March 31, 2026.

Return to Profitability in Q1 FY2027 The company reported a standalone profit before tax of Rs. 12,836.03 lakhs in Q1 FY2027, compared to a loss of Rs. 6,314.61 lakhs in Q4 FY2026 and a profit of Rs. 999.10 lakhs in Q1 FY2026. Consolidated profit before tax was Rs. 12,275.22 lakhs. Standalone profit for the period was Rs. 10,771.99 lakhs, and consolidated profit was Rs. 10,209.34 lakhs.

Dividend Declaration The company declared a final dividend of Re. 1 per equity share of face value Rs. 10 each for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, approved at the 93rd AGM held on August 3, 2026.

Auditor Appointment M/s. Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP was appointed as Statutory Auditors for a first term of five consecutive years, from the conclusion of the 93rd AGM until the conclusion of the 98th AGM.

3. Key Risks

Regulatory and Tax Risks The GST show cause notice for FY2020-21 presents a contingent liability. The outcome depends on adjudication, and an adverse ruling could result in a demand of Rs. 3,89,21,097.

Operational Concentration Risk The building products segment contributes the majority of revenue and profitability, while the steel buildings segment continues to report losses. Any downturn in building products demand could materially affect overall financial performance.

Subsidiary Performance Risk Everest Buildpro Private Limited and Everest Steel Building Private Limited have both faced CAPEX withdrawals and impairments. The consolidated results include losses from subsidiaries, with one subsidiary reporting a total net loss after tax of Rs. 676.58 lakhs and four others reporting a combined net loss after tax of Rs. 385.88 lakhs for Q1 FY2027.

Labour Code Implementation Risk The finalisation of Central/State Rules and clarifications on the Labour Codes could result in further employee benefit obligations beyond the Rs. 1,634.14 lakhs already recognised.

Market and Demand Risk Revenue from operations declined to Rs. 41,869.71 lakhs (standalone) in Q1 FY2027 from Rs. 48,679.59 lakhs in Q1 FY2026, indicating a year-on-year contraction in topline.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

GST Show Cause Notice Management stated that since the matter is only at the Show Cause Notice stage and the company believes it maintains strong legal and factual grounds to defend its case, there is no expected material financial impact, and the potential demand is not considered likely to materialise as a liability. No provision has been recognised in the financial results for this demand.

CAPEX Withdrawal Management’s decision to withdraw CAPEX plans for new PEB and Fibre Cement Boards facilities was attributed to a re-assessment of feasibility and business considerations. The financial impact of these decisions has been recognised through impairments and write-offs in Q1 FY2027.

Labour Codes Management stated it continues to monitor the finalisation of Central/State Rules and clarifications from the Government on other aspects of the Labour Codes and would provide appropriate accounting effect as and when such clarifications are issued or rules are notified. The initial impact of Rs. 1,634.14 lakhs (standalone) and Rs. 1,668.84 lakhs (consolidated) has already been recognised.

Dividend The Board recommended a final dividend of Re. 1 per equity share for FY2026, which was approved by shareholders at the 93rd AGM on August 3, 2026. All 12 resolutions put before the AGM were passed with the requisite majority.

Auditor Transition The company transitioned from M/s. S R B C & Co. LLP, who issued limited review reports for Q1 FY2027, to M/s. Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP, appointed at the 93rd AGM for a five-year term.
