SAREGAMA: Recent Corporate Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

The most clearly identified headwind is in the Video business, where overall Video Revenue registered a degrowth. This is attributed to the company’s deliberate pivot away from own film production toward investment in Bhansali Production. This transition creates a revenue drag in the video segment even as the company repositions its film strategy.

On the operational and compliance side, the company has flagged that security holders in physical mode must update their PAN, KYC details, nomination, and bank details to receive payments electronically — a reminder of administrative requirements tied to the upcoming AGM.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The Q1 FY27 performance reflects strong momentum across the core business. Revenue from Operations reached INR 2,636 Mn, representing 27% YoY growth. Adjusted EBITDA stood at INR 1,124 Mn (69% YoY growth), and PBT was INR 705 Mn (38% YoY growth).

The Music segment was the principal growth engine: Music Revenue reached INR 2,306 Mn (39% YoY growth), Music EBITDA was INR 1,398 Mn (36% YoY growth), and Music Net Margin was INR 996 Mn (31% YoY growth). The company released over 750 films and non-film tracks, generating more than 250 Mn streams and views across Spotify and YouTube.

The Live Events business registered 214% YoY growth, driven by a multi-format strategy spanning Concerts, Devotional, Experiential, and Stand-up Comedy events.

The artiste management business expanded with 33 artistes added in Q1 FY27, bringing the total roster to 309 artistes with an aggregate digital reach exceeding 440 million. The company’s digital footprint across owned and controlled channels improved beyond 680 Mn+ followers and subscribers.

Structural tailwinds include the compounding proprietary IP catalog — over 1,80,000 songs owned in perpetuity globally — the scaling of digital footprint and audience reach, and capitalization on the vinyl revival. The company also notes future opportunities in continuing to work with Sun TV across languages and developing shows for Doordarshan.

Key Risks

The clearest risk evident in the material is the Video segment’s revenue degrowth during the transition away from own film production toward investment in Bhansali Production. This pivot introduces execution and timing uncertainty around when the video business will stabilize or return to growth.

The company’s participation in investor engagements is subject to change — the Non-Deal Roadshow in the UAE scheduled for 8th–9th September 2026 was noted as subject to changes, if any.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Management stated that the company has “started the new financial year on a strong note, delivering healthy financial performance while continuing to strengthen our Entertainment Flywheel.” This is supported by the observed Q1 FY27 results: 27% YoY revenue growth, 69% YoY Adjusted EBITDA growth, and 38% YoY PBT growth.

Management’s claim that the integrated ecosystem across music, video, live events, and artiste management “enables us to maximise the value of our intellectual property and positions us well for sustainable long-term growth” is consistent with the observed diversification — Music driving profitability, Live Events growing 214% YoY, and the artiste roster expanding to 309. However, the Video segment’s degrowth shows the ecosystem is not uniformly contributing to growth during the current transition.

Management’s forward-looking outlook includes continuing to work with Sun TV across languages and developing shows for Doordarshan, indicating a pipeline of content partnerships intended to support future growth.

Governance and Investor Engagement

The 79th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, 15th September 2026, at 12:30 P.M. IST through video conferencing. All four ordinary and special resolutions were successfully passed with the requisite majority, including adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY ended March 31, 2026, confirmation of interim dividend, re-appointment of Mr. Vikram Mehra by rotation, and ratification of Cost Auditors’ remuneration for FY ending March 31, 2027.

The Board approved unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 on 4th August 2026, with consolidated revenue from operations of Rs. 26,360 Lakhs and net profit of Rs. 5,188 Lakhs.

Management participated in a Non-Deal Roadshow in the United Arab Emirates from 8th to 9th September 2026.

Broker Narrative

The broker narrative shifted from Hold to Buy, but the central tension remained: the film/Bhansali pivot was seen as both an exclusive content/asset-light opportunity and a source of volatile earnings/multiple-compression risk. Early concerns about film-led lumpiness and licensing growth persisted into the final report, yet the upgrade rested on Q1FY27 revenue/EBITDA momentum, music licensing and artist-management growth, and diversification wins. The final Buy was followed by a -7.8% actual return, a sharp reversal after the two Hold calls were followed by large positive returns.

Fears that came true

  • The early film-concentration/lumpy-P&L fear materialized: the last report cited high losses in the films foray and a 52.4% YoY decline in TV/Events/Film revenue.
  • The multiple-compression/volatile-growth concern was consistent with the post-Buy disappointment, as the stock returned -7.8% after the upgrade.
