Headwinds and Challenges

Integrated sugar, co-generation and ethanol segment under pressure. In Q1 FY27, this segment’s revenue was INR 377.9 crore compared to INR 382.8 crore in Q1 FY26, while EBITDA loss widened to INR 14.6 crore from INR 4.5 crore. Management attributed this to challenging feedstock conditions, higher manufacturing costs and ethanol tender allotments, with improved sugar realizations providing only partial support.

Consolidated profitability weakened. EBITDA fell to INR 2.6 crore in Q1 FY27 from INR 6.5 crore in Q1 FY26, with margin at 0.5% versus 1.2%. Profitability was impacted by elevated inventory carrying cost and higher raw material prices. Net loss was INR 19.3 crore compared to INR 16 crore in Q1 FY26, also reflecting higher depreciation.

Ethanol pricing dynamics. Management noted that sugar prices have increased in recent weeks, while ethanol prices from sugarcane juice have remained the same over the past few years, requiring continued evaluation of the economics of sugar versus ethanol diversion.

Climate and water risk. Management remains focused on climate effects, including a prediction of El Niño, and monitors rainfall and dam levels in Maharashtra and Karnataka, since dams provide water through the year and rainfall supports the soil. The investor presentation notes sugarcane cultivation is highly water-intensive and vulnerable to uneven rainfall patterns and long-term climate shifts, and that the industry remains watchful of potential El Niño-related risks.

Volatile external environment. Management described geopolitical developments, volatility in energy and raw material markets, and evolving trade flows as creating challenges across industry, and noted the world is in “complete volatility.”

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Bio-based chemicals momentum. The bio-based chemicals business delivered revenue growth of 19.4% year-on-year to INR 168.7 crore in Q1 FY27, with EBITDA up 53% to INR 19.2 crore and margin expanding to 11.4% from 8.9%. Management attributed this to debottlenecking initiatives completed in FY26, favorable product mix and improved operating efficiencies. Management guided to approximately INR 190 crore of bio-based chemicals sales in the current quarter and approximately INR 240 crore per quarter once further debottlenecking is complete, with an incremental capex of about INR 25 crore expected to be completed by early FY28. Management stated the growth would come from bio-based specialty chemicals.

Ethanol capacity expansion and feedstock flexibility. The 200 KLPD grain-based distillery at Sameerwadi was commissioned in Q1 FY27, adding 60 million litres of annual ethanol capacity and taking total distillery capacity to 800 KLPD. Management is exploring adding 160 KLPD of fungible capacity within the existing 800 KLPD limit, which would take total fungible capacity to 360 KLPD, enabling optimization between sugar and ethanol and use of maize/grain feedstock.

India’s ethanol blending programme. Management cited the successful E20 rollout, continued policy focus on higher blending, and new draft guidelines for E85 and E100, along with flex-fuel vehicle launches by auto companies, as supporting long-term ethanol demand. Management expects higher ethanol demand going forward given fuel consumption growth and blending.

Fossil energy disruption as a tailwind. The investor presentation describes fossil fuel supply chain stress as pushing industries toward bio-based alternatives, with price parity with fossil fuels within reach and customers prioritizing dependable, integrated bio-based partners. Management noted the gap between fossil and green has narrowed, driving greater customer interest in bio-based specialty chemicals in India and export markets.

Drug discovery pipeline. A Japanese patent was granted for a novel anti-cancer molecule, and an Indian patent was secured for a cost-effective process to manufacture branched alcohols. The company filed an application with CDSCO for preliminary efficacy trials for its lead anti-cancer molecule targeting triple-negative breast cancer, with trials expected to commence from Q3 FY27 subject to regulatory approvals. Management estimated about INR 20 crore over 2 to 3 years to take the molecule to preliminary efficacy, funded from internal resources. The presentation lists milestones of trial start by Q3 FY27, preliminary results by Q2 FY28, and full completion by Q2 2030 (33 months).

Bio-butanol and DME platforms. An MoU with Synthomer covers use of bio-butanol as a chemical intermediate, and the company is seeking additional partners. The DME project with ICT is in pilot trials, with results expected by March 2027 (Q4 FY27). Management also noted a government call to explore bio-butanol for fuel energy security and CBG policy interest, with pressmud available as feedstock.

Jivana consumer brand. The presentation notes the Jivana brand recorded 19% year-on-year revenue growth in Q1 FY27.

Key Risks

  • Climate effects, including potential El Niño, and dependence on rainfall and dam levels in Maharashtra and Karnataka.
  • Volatility in energy and raw material markets and geopolitical developments.
  • Feedstock conditions and rising manufacturing costs affecting the sugar, co-generation and ethanol segment.
  • Ethanol tender allotments and the divergence between sugar and ethanol pricing.
  • Execution risk on the proposed 160 KLPD fungible capacity addition and the INR 25 crore bio-based chemicals debottlenecking, both of which are still being explored or implemented.
  • Regulatory risk on drug discovery trials, which remain subject to government approvals.
  • Leverage and finance costs: management indicated it expects to broadly keep debt levels similar, with no major changes to the overall debt-equity structure, while noting finance costs were INR 13.9 crore in Q1 FY27 versus INR 15.3 crore in Q1 FY26.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Bio-based chemicals. Management guided to approximately INR 190 crore of sales in the current quarter (Q2 FY27) and approximately INR 240 crore per quarter once further debottlenecking is complete in early FY28. Observed performance: revenue rose from INR 141.2 crore in Q1 FY26 to INR 168.7 crore in Q1 FY27, with EBITDA margin expanding to 11.4% from 8.9%. Management stated the debottlenecking benefits are now translating into improved operating efficiencies and profitability and expects the trend to continue.

Integrated sugar, co-generation and ethanol. Management stated improved sugar realizations provide an opportunity to benefit from better sugar economics going forward and that increased sugar realization is creating a more favorable environment for the integrated sugar and ethanol platform. Observed performance in Q1 FY27: segment revenue declined to INR 377.9 crore from INR 382.8 crore, and EBITDA loss widened to INR 14.6 crore from INR 4.5 crore.

Consolidated profitability. Management stated it remains focused on improving operating efficiency, strengthening margins and maintaining financial discipline, and believes initiatives across the business will support improvement in operating performance as the year progresses. Observed performance in Q1 FY27: EBITDA of INR 2.6 crore (0.5% margin) versus INR 6.5 crore (1.2% margin) in Q1 FY26, and net loss of INR 19.3 crore versus INR 16 crore.

Ethanol capacity. Management guided that the 200 KLPD grain-based distillery was commissioned in Q1 FY27, taking total capacity to 800 KLPD, and is exploring an additional 160 KLPD of fungible capacity. The commissioning is confirmed in the Q1 FY27 results materials; the additional 160 KLPD remains under exploration.

Drug discovery. Management guided to filing the CDSCO application in July, with trials expected to commence from Q3 FY27 subject to regulatory approvals, and preliminary results expected by Q2 FY28. The CDSCO application filing is confirmed in the Q1 FY27 materials.

DME. Management guided that pilot trials are progressing as planned and results are expected by March 2027, with commercial possibilities to be discussed only after successful pilot trials. No commercial contribution has been reported to date.

Debt. Management indicated it expects to broadly keep debt levels similar or with only a slight change, and not major changes in the overall debt-equity structure.
