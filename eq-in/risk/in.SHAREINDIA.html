Share India Securities — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory transition in derivatives and funding. Management describes the quarter as one shaped by a rapidly evolving regulatory landscape, including SEBI measures in the derivative segment and tighter RBI funding norms for proprietary desk trading. These changes were cited as industry-wide headwinds and as a source of investor apprehension regarding stock-holding entities.
  • Market volatility and geopolitical uncertainty. Continued volatility in global financial markets and heightened geopolitical uncertainty were noted, with mid-cap and small-cap stocks described as extremely volatile during the quarter.
  • Industry consolidation. Management characterises the last two years as a consolidation period for the industry, with growth challenged and participants taking more time to adjust.
  • Penalty from NSE Clearing Limited. A monetary penalty of Rs. 1,00,000 (plus GST of Rs. 18,000) was levied by NSE Clearing Limited for a Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) violation in the ordinary course of stock broking operations. The invoice was made available on the NSE portal on August 06, 2026. The company states there is no material impact on financials, operations, or other business activities.
  • Asset quality in the NBFC book. GNPA stood at 4.05% and NNPA at 2.26% in Q1-FY27, though both improved quarter-on-quarter (down 25 bps and 40 bps respectively).
  • Branch economics risk. Management acknowledges that branches not profitable after 12 months face a hard stop, indicating execution risk in the Tier-3 expansion.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record quarterly performance. Q1-FY27 was the company’s highest-ever quarterly consolidated net profit. Consolidated revenue from operations rose 31% year-on-year to Rs. 448 crores, with PAT up 48% to Rs. 124.41 crore; sequentially, consolidated PAT grew 114%. Standalone revenue rose 28% year-on-year to Rs. 349 crores, with PAT up 32% to Rs. 90.85 crore (up 21% sequentially).
  • Diversified business model. Presence across broking, market-making, merchant banking, wealth management, treasury, technology-driven businesses, and other financial services reduces dependence on any single revenue stream.
  • Retail and MTF expansion. MTF AUM grew 47.8% year-on-year to INR 4,655 Mn (around Rs. 470 crores), with a target of a Rs. 1,000 crore book in two years. Seven new branches were opened (Banaras, Indore, Bhopal, Raipur, Agra, Nagpur, and Hyderabad), part of a plan for 30 branches over two years, focused on Tier-3 cities.
  • New and scaling verticals. PMS launched in Q1 with AUM and advisory at Rs. 150 crores, targeting around Rs. 250 crores by year-end; AIF launch targeted for Q3. Share India Cred (debt trading) began operations in Q1 with Rs. 74 crores of sales/underwriting across six issues, EBITDA of Rs. 1.08 crores, and PAT of Rs. 40 lakhs. GIFT City operations turned positive at around Rs. 2 crores in Q1.
  • Institutional and algo growth. Institutional active clients rose to 212 from 186 (about 15% growth), with the institutional team growing 15–20% per quarter. uTrade algo subscriptions grew 20% between Q4 and Q1.
  • Merchant banking momentum. An SME IPO of around Rs. 200 crores was completed in Q1, and the company’s first main board IPO raised Rs. 167 crores with subscription exceeding 12x.
  • Funding diversification. A commercial paper program backed by a Crisil A1+ short-term rating has been initiated, alongside progress on an NCD issuance, intended to lower borrowing costs, diversify funding, and counter RBI restrictions on top-desk funding.
  • Strong capital base. Consolidated net worth stood at approximately Rs. 2,760 crores as of June 30, 2026.
  • Technology investments. Continued investment in AI, automation, and digital capabilities, plus the Silverleaf acquisition (an HFT firm led by IIT Bombay graduates), strengthening the technology stack and supporting global expansion plans.
  • Capital-raising capacity. The 32nd AGM (September 29, 2026) includes proposals to enhance borrowing limits under Section 180(1)(a) up to Rs. 6,000 crores and investment/loan limits under Section 186 up to Rs. 3,000 crores, alongside authority to provide financial assistance to subsidiaries, joint ventures, associates, and other entities.
  • Leadership continuity. Proposals to re-appoint Mr. Parveen Gupta as Managing Director and Mr. Sachin Gupta as Whole-time Director for five-year terms from July 21, 2027, and to continue Mr. Yogesh Lohiya as Independent Director post-75.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory risk. Ongoing SEBI derivative measures and RBI funding norms could continue to affect business mix and growth trajectory.
  • Market and volatility risk. Geopolitical uncertainty and volatility in mid-cap and small-cap stocks could affect retail participation and MTF book stability.
  • Concentration in proprietary trading. Proprietary desk contributed roughly 50–52% of profitability and around 58–60% of revenue, though management notes this dependence has fallen from 85–90% of revenue and 70–75% of profitability 8–9 years ago.
  • Execution risk in expansion. Branch profitability targets (8-month breakeven, 12-month hard stop) and new product launches (AIF in Q3, wealth distribution operations by Q3) carry execution uncertainty.
  • Asset quality risk. NBFC GNPA of 4.05% and NNPA of 2.26% remain areas to monitor despite sequential improvement.
  • Compliance and operational risk. The NCL penalty for MWPL violation illustrates exposure to operational and compliance lapses in broking operations.
  • Redemption and refinancing considerations. Early redemption of Series A NCDs (ISIN INE932X07023, 5,000 NCDs, face value ₹37,500) and Series B NCDs (ISIN INE932X07015, 4,990 NCDs, face value ₹1,00,000) is scheduled for September 30, 2026, ahead of the June 10, 2027 maturity. Record date is September 15, 2026; last trading date on BSE is September 11, 2026; the early redemption was approved by NCD holders on September 1, 2026.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Growth guidance. Management states it remains confident of delivering around 20% growth during the current financial year, subject to overall market conditions. Observed Q1-FY27 performance showed consolidated revenue growth of 31.3% year-on-year and net profit growth of 47.2% year-on-year, above the stated full-year aspiration on a year-on-year basis.
  • MTF book target. Management targets a Rs. 1,000 crore MTF book in two years; the Q1-FY27 MTF AUM stood at INR 4,655 Mn (around Rs. 470 crores), representing 47.8% year-on-year growth.
  • PMS target. Management targets around Rs. 250 crores in PMS AUM by end of financial year; Q1-FY27 PMS AUM and advisory stood at Rs. 150 crores following a Q1 launch.
  • AIF timeline. Management expects to launch AIF in Q3, with the application under process.
  • Wealth distribution. Management expects wealth distribution operations to begin by Q3 of the current financial year, with team hiring ongoing.
  • Branch expansion. Management targets 25–30 branches over 24 months, with seven already operational and an 8-month breakeven expectation per branch.
  • Institutional growth. Management indicates the institutional team grows around 15–20% per quarter; observed growth from 186 to 212 active clients between Q4 and Q1 is consistent with that range.
  • Dividend. A first interim dividend of INR 0.50 per share (face value INR 2 each) was declared for FY27. A final dividend of Rs 0.50 per equity share for FY2025-26 is proposed at the AGM, with a record date of September 09, 2026, and payment on or before October 20, 2026.
