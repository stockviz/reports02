SVLL: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Margin pressure in the core logistics segment. In the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the 3PL business generated revenue of ₹5,992.13 lakhs but segment results of ₹509.86 lakhs, down from ₹597.73 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, despite revenue rising from ₹5,831.40 lakhs. The retail trading segment contributed only ₹22.02 lakhs of segment results on ₹482.76 lakhs of revenue in the June 2026 quarter, and posted a negative result of ₹2.47 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter.

Rising operating cost base. Operating expenses rose to ₹3,289.30 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹2,974.66 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹2,623.90 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Employee benefits expense also increased to ₹894.45 lakhs from ₹877.36 lakhs and ₹616.22 lakhs over the same comparison periods.

Finance costs and depreciation burden. Finance costs were ₹453.54 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, and depreciation and amortisation was ₹953.93 lakhs. On a full-year basis (FY ended March 31, 2026), finance costs were ₹1,773.24 lakhs and depreciation ₹3,577.62 lakhs against profit before tax of ₹699.47 lakhs, indicating a capital-intensive cost structure.

Sequential earnings decline. Standalone profit before tax fell to ₹419.08 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹397.13 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter on a standalone basis, while consolidated profit before tax declined to ₹419.08 lakhs from ₹460.20 lakhs over the same period. Consolidated profit for the period fell to ₹324.94 lakhs from ₹400.34 lakhs sequentially.

Related-party exposure through lending. The board approved an unsecured loan of ₹9,05,00,000 to Nava Raipur Business Hub Private Limited, an associate company in which SVLL holds 33% and where promoter and Managing Director Mr. Atul Garg is also a director. The loan carries a 12% interest rate, has a term exceeding five years maturing on September 30, 2031, and is unsecured with no security provided. The transaction is disclosed as a related party transaction at arm’s length.

Associate drag. The consolidated results include a share of loss of associates and joint ventures of ₹0.01 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter and ₹0.12 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026, relating to Nava Raipur Business Hub Private Limited, incorporated on July 24, 2025.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Revenue growth. Standalone revenue from operations rose to ₹6,474.89 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹6,245.16 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹4,974.67 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Total income followed the same trajectory at ₹6,631.61 lakhs, ₹6,346.34 lakhs, and ₹5,115.04 lakhs respectively.

Year-on-year earnings improvement. Standalone profit before tax for the June 2026 quarter was ₹419.08 lakhs versus ₹240.90 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, and profit for the period rose to ₹324.94 lakhs from ₹186.67 lakhs. Basic earnings per share improved to ₹2.83 from ₹1.62.

Segment scale in 3PL. The 3PL business remains the dominant segment, with revenue of ₹5,992.13 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter and ₹20,639.00 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026. Segment assets for 3PL stood at ₹26,753.36 lakhs as of June 30, 2026.

Financial assistance to associate for expansion. The ₹9,05,00,000 loan to Nava Raipur Business Hub Private Limited is intended to provide financial assistance for business operations, capital expenditure, working capital requirements, expansion activities, and other lawful business purposes, with interest at 12% per annum and prepayment permitted without penalty.

Board-approved financing capacity. The AGM notice proposes ratification of a loan of ₹10,15,00,000 granted to Nava Raipur Business Hub Private Limited, approval of transactions under Section 185 up to ₹25 crores, and approval of loans and investments under Section 186 up to ₹25 crores or statutory limits, which would expand the company’s capacity to extend financial assistance.

Auditor continuity. The board re-appointed M/s APAS & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants, for a second term of five consecutive years from FY 2026-27 to 2030-31, subject to member approval. The firm has been in practice since 1971 with presence in Raipur and New Delhi.

3. Key Risks

Unsecured related-party lending. The ₹9,05,00,000 loan to Nava Raipur Business Hub Private Limited is unsecured, with no security provided, and the borrower is an associate company with common directorship through Mr. Atul Garg. The loan matures on September 30, 2031, unless repaid earlier.

Concentration in a single segment. The 3PL business accounts for the overwhelming majority of revenue and segment assets, with the retail trading segment contributing a small share and, in the March 2026 quarter, a negative segment result.

High leverage-related costs. Finance costs of ₹453.54 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter and ₹1,773.24 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026 represent a significant fixed charge against operating profits.

Dependence on capital expenditure. Depreciation and amortisation of ₹953.93 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter and ₹3,577.62 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026 reflect the asset-heavy nature of the logistics and warehousing business.

Associate performance risk. The associate Nava Raipur Business Hub Private Limited was incorporated on July 24, 2025, and the consolidated results include a share of its loss for the June 2026 quarter and the year ended March 31, 2026.

Related-party transaction governance. The loan to the associate company is a related party transaction, and the AGM proposals seek ratification and enhanced limits under Sections 185 and 186, which increases the scale of potential related-party exposure.
