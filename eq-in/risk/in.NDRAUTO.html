NDR Auto Components — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Income tax search and ongoing litigation: In FY2023-24, the Income Tax Department conducted a search under Section 132 of the Income Tax Act, 1961 at certain premises, including manufacturing locations and residences of some employees/key managerial personnel. Demand orders of Rs. 502.20 lakhs (excluding penalties) were received for Assessment Years 2020-21 to 2024-25, along with penalty demand orders of Rs. 142.20 lakhs for AY 2021-22 and 2022-23. Penalty demand orders for AY 2023-24 and AY 2024-25 were dropped. The Company filed appeals and a rectification application of Rs. 389.98 lakhs. The Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) reduced the demand to Rs. 366.89 lakhs for AY 2020-21 to 2024-25, except AY 2022-23, where the appellate order (including penalty) is still awaited; total demand for AY 2022-23 is Rs. 134 lakhs (including penalties). Appeals have been filed before higher authorities. The auditors included an Emphasis of Matter on this uncertainty in both standalone and consolidated review reports.
  • Sequential moderation in profitability: Standalone net profit declined from Rs. 1,555.32 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to Rs. 1,472.18 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Consolidated net profit declined from Rs. 1,845.23 lakhs to Rs. 1,640.34 lakhs over the same period. Standalone basic EPS moved from Rs. 6.54 to Rs. 6.19; consolidated basic EPS from Rs. 7.76 to Rs. 6.90.
  • Rising cost lines: Standalone employee benefits expense rose from Rs. 655.75 lakhs (Q1 FY26) to Rs. 786.87 lakhs (Q1 FY27); depreciation rose from Rs. 475.65 lakhs to Rs. 633.13 lakhs; other expenses rose from Rs. 2,302.94 lakhs to Rs. 2,566.87 lakhs.
  • Joint venture losses: The Group’s share of net loss after tax from 2 joint ventures was Rs. 57.74 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The Group discontinued recognizing its share of losses of Rs. 12.27 lakhs from Toyota Boshoku Relan India Private Limited (joint venture) after accumulated losses exceeded the carrying amount of the investment of Rs. 0.50 lakhs; recognition of share of profits will resume only after profits equal previously unrecognized losses.
  • Price escalation uncertainty: Sales/purchases are recognized on contract price subject to escalations and de-escalations under annual price revision, estimated on a best-estimate basis and formalized at year end.
  • General operating risks cited by management: Changes in industry structure, significant changes in the political and economic environment in India and overseas, tax laws, import duties, litigation, and labour relations.
  • Subsidiary with no turnover: NDR Auto Components South Private Limited, incorporated November 7, 2025, had Nil turnover as on March 31, 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong year-on-year revenue growth: Standalone revenue from operations rose to Rs. 22,359.85 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 18,521.95 lakhs in Q1 FY26. Consolidated revenue rose to Rs. 22,145.27 lakhs from Rs. 18,521.95 lakhs. Management cited a 19.56% YOY increase in revenues to Rs. 221.45 crore for Q1 FY27.
  • Demand drivers cited by management: Strong customer demand, increasing vehicle content, new model launches, premiumization, OEM capacity expansion, stable production schedules across major OEMs, and moderating commodity prices.
  • Zero-debt position with internal accrual funding: The company is described as a zero debt company with all capex funded through internal accruals.
  • Order book: Rs. 650 crore as on June 30, 2026.
  • Return on capital: ROCE of 35.34% as on June 30, 2026.
  • Capacity and portfolio expansion: Plans to expand capacities over the next 5 years and expand the portfolio with disruptive and innovative auto ancillaries.
  • Subsidiary investment for new project: The Board approved investing Rs. 8 crore in 80 lakh equity shares of NDR Auto Components South Private Limited via a rights issue, to fund that subsidiary’s project. The subsidiary is being set up for manufacturing frame structures, seat covers (trims), and other plastic/metal/polyurethane-based automotive components.
  • Associate and JV contribution: Share of profit of associate and joint ventures (net of tax) was Rs. 178.32 lakhs in Q1 FY27, versus Rs. 241.22 lakhs in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 312.94 lakhs in Q4 FY26.
  • Employee alignment: Grant of 66,641 stock options to eligible employees of the Company and Bharat Seats Limited (associate) under the ESOP Plan 2024, at an exercise price of Rs. 500.50 (a 35% discount to the NSE closing price of Rs. 770 on May 14, 2026), vesting not earlier than 2 years from grant and exercisable within 5 years from vesting.
  • Shareholder returns: Dividend of Rs. 4 per equity share of Rs. 10 each declared for FY2025-26 at the 7th AGM held July 27, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Tax litigation outcome risk: The final outcome of the income tax search-related appeals, including the pending AY 2022-23 appellate and penalty order, remains uncertain. Management, based on its own assessment and legal advice, is confident of a favorable outcome, and no adjustment has been made to the financial results pending the outcome.
  • Dependence on OEM production cycles: The business is tied to customer demand, OEM capacity expansion, and stable production schedules across major OEMs.
  • Commodity price and input cost exposure: Cost of materials and components consumed was Rs. 16,157.49 lakhs standalone in Q1 FY27, the largest expense line; moderating commodity prices are cited as a tailwind, implying sensitivity to reversal.
  • Regulatory and policy risk: Changes in tax laws, import duties, and the political and economic environment in India and overseas.
  • Joint venture performance risk: Continued losses at joint ventures, including the joint venture where loss recognition was discontinued.
  • Execution risk on expansion: The Rs. 8 crore investment in NDR Auto Components South Private Limited is for a project at an entity with Nil turnover as on March 31, 2026.
  • Review scope limitations: The auditors noted that the unaudited interim financial results of 2 subsidiaries and 2 joint ventures were not reviewed by their auditors and were furnished by management; these were stated to be not material to the Group.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • FY2030 vision: Management’s forward-looking guidance includes reaching revenues of Rs. 3,000 crore by FY2030, a gradually improving margin profile, ROCE of ~35%+, expanding and deepening OEM partnerships, portfolio expansion with disruptive and innovative auto ancillaries, and expanding capacities over the next 5 years.
  • Observed performance against guidance:
    • ROCE: The stated ROCE of 35.34% as on June 30, 2026 is consistent with the ~35%+ ROCE element of the FY2030 vision.
    • Revenue trajectory: Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs. 221.45 crore (consolidated Rs. 22,145.27 lakhs) represents 19.56% YOY growth, but reaching Rs. 3,000 crore by FY2030 would require a substantially higher growth trajectory than the recent YOY rate.
    • Margin profile: Standalone profit before tax declined sequentially from Rs. 2,066.64 lakhs (Q4 FY26) to Rs. 1,956.92 lakhs (Q1 FY27), and consolidated profit before tax declined from Rs. 2,367.87 lakhs to Rs. 2,110.11 lakhs, while employee, depreciation, and other expenses rose YOY. This is directionally at odds with a gradually improving margin profile in the near term.
    • Order book: Rs. 650 crore as on June 30, 2026 provides visibility relative to the FY2030 revenue ambition.
    • Capacity expansion: The Rs. 8 crore rights issue subscription in NDR Auto Components South Private Limited is an initial step toward the stated capacity and portfolio expansion plans.
  • Other guidance-related items: The company held an Analyst/Investor Meeting with Choice Institutional Equities on August 24, 2026, via a virtual one-on-one meeting under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
