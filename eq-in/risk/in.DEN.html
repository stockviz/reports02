DEN Networks: Recent Corporate Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

Weak operating performance in the core cable business. The consolidated segment results show the cable distribution network reporting a loss of Rs. 63.49 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, widening from a loss of Rs. 42.71 million in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 21.01 million in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. The broadband segment also remained loss-making at Rs. 9.67 million for the June 2026 quarter, though narrower than the Rs. 12.70 million loss a year earlier.

Rising content costs. Consolidated content cost rose to Rs. 1,651.64 million in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 1,549.15 million in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 1,500.73 million in the June 2025 quarter. On a standalone basis, content cost increased to Rs. 1,639.64 million from Rs. 1,536.76 million sequentially and Rs. 1,487.89 million year-on-year.

Decline in other income. Standalone other income fell to Rs. 537.82 million in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 694.78 million a year earlier. Consolidated other income declined to Rs. 553.98 million from Rs. 706.74 million over the same comparison.

Lower profitability year-on-year. Standalone profit after tax declined to Rs. 329.18 million in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 508.16 million in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated profit after tax fell to Rs. 345.89 million from Rs. 536.40 million. Consolidated profit before tax dropped to Rs. 464.92 million from Rs. 649.56 million.

Losses at associates and subsidiaries. The consolidated results include a share of loss of associates of Rs. 11.78 million for the June 2026 quarter, compared with a share of profit of Rs. 19.76 million in the March 2026 quarter and a loss of Rs. 17.96 million in the June 2025 quarter. The auditor’s review report notes that 21 subsidiaries not reviewed by the principal auditor reflected total revenue of Rs. 725.76 million and a net loss after tax of Rs. 42.83 million for the quarter.

Rising segment liabilities. Total consolidated segment liabilities increased to Rs. 5,725.15 million as of June 30, 2026 from Rs. 4,572.27 million as of March 31, 2026, driven mainly by the cable distribution network, where liabilities rose to Rs. 5,466.18 million from Rs. 4,327.63 million.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Sequential revenue growth. Standalone revenue from operations increased to Rs. 2,569.63 million in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 2,496.85 million in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 2,456.06 million in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to Rs. 2,427.74 million from Rs. 2,405.71 million sequentially and Rs. 2,412.73 million year-on-year.

Sequential improvement in standalone profitability. Standalone profit after tax improved to Rs. 329.18 million in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 289.06 million in the March 2026 quarter, with basic EPS rising to Rs. 0.69 from Rs. 0.61.

Lower employee and depreciation costs. Standalone employee benefits expense declined to Rs. 112.60 million from Rs. 157.90 million a year earlier, and depreciation and amortisation fell to Rs. 150.79 million from Rs. 171.09 million. On a consolidated basis, employee benefits expense fell to Rs. 147.61 million from Rs. 193.18 million, and depreciation and amortisation declined to Rs. 200.00 million from Rs. 243.57 million.

Substantial unallocated assets and investment corpus. Consolidated unallocated assets stood at Rs. 34,476.00 million as of June 30, 2026. The notes state that proceeds of Rs. 20,450.00 million from a preferential allotment made during the year ended March 31, 2019 remain invested in mutual funds and fixed deposits, pending utilisation.

Broadband segment revenue stability. Broadband segment revenue was Rs. 95.77 million in the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 96.81 million in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 104.64 million in the June 2025 quarter.

Key Risks

  • Continued losses at the operating segment level in both cable distribution and broadband, with the cable segment loss widening sequentially and year-on-year.
  • Dependence on other income, which exceeded segment operating results and was the principal contributor to consolidated profit before tax; a decline in other income materially reduced profitability year-on-year.
  • Exposure to losses at associates and at subsidiaries not reviewed by the principal auditor.
  • Growth in segment liabilities, particularly in the cable distribution network.
  • The preferential allotment proceeds of Rs. 20,450.00 million remain unutilised and invested in mutual funds and fixed deposits, exposing the corpus to market and deployment-timing considerations.
  • The company operates in a single reportable segment on a standalone basis (cable business), with no separate segment reporting under Ind AS 108.
