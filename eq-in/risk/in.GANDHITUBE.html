For example, on the financial statements resolution, 72 members cast 91,83,088 valid votes, with 65 members in remote e-voting casting 90,75,434 votes in favour. This suggests that a few large shareholders can significantly influence outcomes.
  • Low participation: Only 72 members participated in the combined voting on most resolutions, which is a small proportion of the total shareholder base, indicating limited retail participation.
  • Regulatory compliance risk: The company operates under a framework requiring compliance with the Companies Act, 2013, SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015, and MCA/SEBI circulars on virtual AGMs. Non-compliance could attract regulatory action.
  • Governance-related dissent: A small number of votes were cast against several resolutions (5 votes against the financial statements and dividend resolutions; 5 votes against the director re-appointment; 105 votes against the appointment of Mr. Manoj Gandhi; 5 votes against cost auditor remuneration; and 10 votes against the buyback).
