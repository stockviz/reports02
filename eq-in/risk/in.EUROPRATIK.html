Euro Pratik Sales Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Raw material costs and geopolitical uncertainty: Management stated that elevated raw material costs and geopolitical uncertainties continued to impact global supply chains during Q1FY27. Despite this, the company reported that value-added products, an improved product mix, and operating efficiencies enabled healthy profitability.
  • Margin compression: While revenue grew 60% year-on-year in Q1FY27, EBITDA margin declined to 27.5% from 35.6% in Q1FY26. For FY26, the EBITDA margin was 35.3%. PAT margin, however, improved to 18.9% in Q1FY27 from 14.1% in Q1FY26.
  • Fire incident at Bhiwandi godown: On April 26, 2025, one of the company’s godowns at Swagat Complex, Phase-2, Rahanal Village, Bhiwandi, Maharashtra, was severely damaged by fire. The company charged a net loss on account of fire and reversal of input tax credit under GST on inventory loss amounting to ₹788.79 lakh as an exceptional item. An insurance claim of ₹3,188.10 lakh has been lodged and recognised as a receivable, subject to settlement by the insurer.
  • Forward-looking risk factors: The company’s own disclosures cite risks and uncertainties including the performance of the Indian economy and international markets, industry competition, the ability to successfully implement strategy, growth and expansion levels, technological changes, and fluctuations in revenue, income, cashflows, and market preferences.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record quarterly performance: Q1FY27 revenue from operations rose to ₹103.3 crore from ₹64.5 crore in Q1FY26, a 60% increase. EBITDA rose 25% year-on-year to ₹29.2 crore, and PAT more than doubled to ₹20.0 crore, a 116% increase.
  • Demand drivers: Management attributed the quarter’s performance to healthy demand across residential and commercial segments, a growing portfolio of differentiated decorative surface solutions, and deeper engagement with architects, designers, and channel partners.
  • Industry tailwinds: Increasing urbanization and real estate development, rising disposable income, increasing focus on home aesthetics with quick installation, demand for sustainability and eco-friendly materials, technology advancement, and versatility and customization.
  • Market size projections: The Indian wall decorative market is projected to reach ₹62,890 crore by FY29E; the Indian wall panel market is expected to reach ₹5,507 crore by FY29E (with the organised market expected to reach 75% by FY2029); and the Indian wall laminates market is expected to reach ₹3,261 crore by FY29E (with the organised market expected to reach 70% by FY2029).
  • Product innovation: The company is scheduled to launch two new product design collections in August 2026 — Pastelite Volume 2 (following the success of Pastelite Volume 1, with new moulds, colour palettes, and designs) and Prism (a premium collection for direct wall installation featuring fabric, wood, stone, textured, metallic, and leather-inspired finishes). Management also notes a pipeline of 6 new products with over 270+ designs under development.
  • Strategic acquisitions and integration: On April 1, 2026, the company acquired a controlling 51% stake in Chawla Brothers (Jalandhar, Punjab) for ₹3,220 lakh, accounted under Ind AS 103. The integration of Uro Veneer World and Chawla Brothers is described as progressing well and strengthening market reach across South and North India. Projected financials cited for the acquisitions include Uro Veneer World (~₹115 crore revenue and ~₹20 crore PAT in FY27) and Chawla Brothers (~₹80 crore revenue in FY27).
  • International expansion: The company has subsidiaries in the U.S., the UAE, and Europe. On April 1, 2026, its shareholding in subsidiary Euro Pratik Trade FZCO was diluted from 100% to 80% pursuant to a fresh issue of equity shares by the subsidiary.
  • Financial strength: Management describes a debt-free balance sheet and healthy internal accruals, positioning the company to capitalize on growth opportunities, unlock integration synergies, and deepen market penetration.

3. Key Risks

  • Supply chain and input cost volatility: Elevated raw material costs and geopolitical uncertainties affecting global supply chains.
  • Margin sustainability: The decline in EBITDA margin in Q1FY27 relative to prior periods highlights sensitivity to product mix, input costs, and operating leverage.
  • Insurance claim settlement: The ₹3,188.10 lakh insurance claim receivable for the Bhiwandi fire loss remains subject to settlement by the insurer.
  • Acquisition integration: The company’s growth strategy relies partly on integrating acquisitions (Uro Veneer World, Chawla Brothers) and realizing projected synergies and financials.
  • Macro and market risks: Performance of the Indian economy and international markets, industry competition, strategy execution, technological changes, and fluctuations in revenue, income, cashflows, and market preferences.
  • Consolidation scope limitations: The consolidated results include seven subsidiaries whose financial results were not reviewed or audited by their auditors and were certified by management; these are stated to be not material to the group. One step-down subsidiary (Euro Pratik EU d.o.o.) was not consolidated as it had no operations or transactions through June 30, 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Revenue growth: Management highlighted Q1FY27 revenue growth of 60% year-on-year to ₹103 crore, consistent with the reported consolidated revenue from operations of ₹10,333.27 lakh (₹103.3 crore) versus ₹6,452.95 lakh (₹64.5 crore) in Q1FY26.
  • Profitability: Management cited EBITDA growth of 25% year-on-year to ₹29 crore and PAT growth of 116% year-on-year to ₹20 crore. Reported consolidated figures show EBITDA of ₹2,625.63 lakh (₹26.3 crore) before exceptional items and PAT of ₹2,004.86 lakh (₹20.0 crore), with the prior-year quarter PAT of ₹929.69 lakh (₹9.3 crore) reflecting the fire-related exceptional item.
  • Margin strategy: Management stated it remains focused on sustaining margins through premiumisation, scale benefits, and disciplined execution. Observed Q1FY27 EBITDA margin of 27.5% was below the FY26 level of 35.3%, while PAT margin improved to 18.9% from 14.1% in Q1FY26.
  • Integration progress: Management stated the integration of Uro Veneer World and Chawla Brothers is progressing well and has significantly strengthened market reach across South and North India. The Chawla Brothers acquisition was completed on April 1, 2026, and is included in the consolidated results.
  • Acquisition projections: Management highlighted projected financials for Uro Veneer World (~₹115 crore revenue, ~₹20 crore PAT in FY27) and Chawla Brothers (~₹80 crore revenue in FY27). These are forward-looking projections; the Q1FY27 consolidated results include the subsidiaries but do not separately disclose their actual performance against these projections.
  • Product pipeline: Management noted a pipeline of 6 new products with over 270+ designs under development, alongside the announced August 2026 launches of Pastelite Volume 2 and Prism.
  • Investor communications: The company is organizing an Analyst and Investor Meet on September 18, 2026, at the Glorio Experience Centre, Mumbai, with no unpublished price sensitive information intended to be discussed. The 17th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for September 22, 2026, at 3:00 P.M. IST through video conferencing, with agenda items including adoption of financial statements, noting an interim dividend of Re. 0.20 per equity share, re-appointment of Mr. Jai Gunvantraj Singhvi, approval of material related party transactions with subsidiaries and entities up to specified sums, appointment of M/s. M Baldeva Associates as Secretarial Auditors, and appointment of Mr. Manish Sacheti as an Independent Director.
