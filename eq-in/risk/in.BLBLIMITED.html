1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential moderation in profitability: Profit before tax declined from ₹678 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to ₹739 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026 on a standalone basis, while profit for the period rose from ₹434 lakh to ₹554 lakh. On a consolidated basis, profit before tax was ₹738 lakh and profit for the period ₹553 lakh, compared with ₹674 lakh and ₹431 lakh respectively in the preceding quarter.
  • Year-on-year earnings decline: Standalone profit before tax for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was ₹739 lakh against ₹1,484 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025, and profit for the period was ₹554 lakh against ₹1,149 lakh. Consolidated figures show a similar pattern (₹738 lakh PBT and ₹553 lakh PAT versus ₹1,484 lakh and ₹1,149 lakh).
  • Negative other income: Other income was negative ₹212 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, compared with negative ₹21 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and positive ₹39 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.

  • Dependence on a single business line: The company reports that its operations fall under the Shares & Securities business, which is the only reportable segment under Ind AS-108.
  • Concentrated shareholding and voting: Promoter and Promoter Group held 24,195,548 shares, and public non-institutions held 28,669,710 shares. At the 45th AGM, 63.8873% of outstanding shares were voted in aggregate, with public institutions holding nil shares and casting no votes.

  • Inventory movement: Changes in inventories of stock-in-trade were negative ₹1,000 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, compared with negative ₹215 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and negative ₹888 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.

  • Cost and inventory exposure: Purchase of stock-in-trade and changes in inventories are large relative to revenue, and negative inventory movements of ₹1,000 lakh in the June 2026 quarter reflect exposure to stock-in-trade valuation and turnover.
  • Single-segment concentration: Operations are confined to the Shares & Securities business, with no diversification reported.
  • Negative other income: Other income was negative in both the June 2026 and March 2026 quarters, which weighed on total revenue.
