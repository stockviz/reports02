Adroit Infotech Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Unpaid rights issue calls and forfeiture: Out of 30,91,222 rights shares on which calls remained unpaid, only 4,51,312 shares were allotted during the quarter ended 30th June 2026. The remaining 26,39,910 unpaid shares are to be forfeited after obtaining necessary permissions. This indicates that a substantial majority of the rights issue shares did not receive the first/final call payment from shareholders.
  • XBRL filing mismatch: The company had to clarify and revise its XBRL filing for the period ended 31st March 2026 to resolve a mismatch in Profit Before Tax (PBT) and Profit After Tax (PAT) figures compared to the PDF version. The mismatch arose from the treatment of Other Comprehensive Income/Expenses (OCI). The revised filing aligns with the PDF version, reporting Standalone Other Comprehensive Income/(Expenses) Net of Taxes at Rs. (3.63) lakhs and Consolidated at Rs. (50.00) lakhs.
  • Negative other comprehensive income: The revised OCI figures for the year ended 31st March 2026 are negative at both the standalone and consolidated levels.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Board-approved borrowing and investment powers: The 36th AGM transacted special business seeking approval for borrowing powers under Section 180(1)(c), creation of charge/mortgage on assets under Section 180(1)(a), and approval under Section 186 for making loans, giving guarantees, providing securities, and making investments. These approvals position the company to undertake financing and investment activities.
  • Related party and ESOP framework: Shareholders considered approval of material related party transactions under Regulation 23 of SEBI LODR and modification of the ‘AIL ESOP-2023’ with implementation through the ‘Adroit Infotech Employees Welfare Trust’, indicating ongoing employee incentive and related-party frameworks.
  • Clean audit reports: The Auditors’ Report contained no qualifications, observations, comments, or other remarks, and the Secretarial Audit Report contained no observation, qualification, or adverse remark.
  • Subsidiary remuneration revision: The AGM took note of a revision in remuneration of the Group Chief Executive Officer and Group Chief Operating Officer of the Material Subsidiary.

3. Key Risks

  • Forfeiture of shares: The forfeiture of 26,39,910 unpaid rights shares could affect the company’s share capital structure and the extent of funds raised through the rights issue.
  • Financial reporting accuracy: The need to revise the XBRL filing for the year ended 31st March 2026 due to a PBT/PAT mismatch arising from OCI treatment highlights a reporting discrepancy that required correction.
  • Governance and approval dependencies: Several special resolutions involve related party transactions, loans/guarantees to entities in which directors are interested, and borrowing/charge creation powers, all of which require shareholder approval and carry inherent related-party risk.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance/forward-looking elements: The Board approved a revision in the remuneration payable to the statutory auditors, M/s. Rao & Shyam Chartered Accountants, for the financial year 2026-2027. The company also stated that the remaining 26,39,910 unpaid rights shares will be forfeited after obtaining necessary permissions.
  • Observed performance: The Board approved the Un-Audited Financial Results (Standalone and Consolidated) for the quarter ended 30th June 2026. During the quarter, 4,51,312 shares were allotted out of the 30,91,222 rights shares where shareholders could not pay the first/final call. The 36th AGM was held on 14th August 2026, where the audited financial statements for the year ended 31st March 2026 were adopted, and all resolutions were passed with the requisite majority. The Chairman addressed members on the company’s performance against industry standards and future growth plans.
