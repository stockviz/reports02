PAKKA: Recent Corporate Announcements — Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Overseas subsidiary project suspension and audit qualification. The most significant challenge disclosed is the temporary suspension of project activities at an overseas subsidiary (Pakka Inc., USA / Pakka Guatemala) during the last quarter of the preceding year due to non-arrangement of necessary financing. As at the date of the auditor’s report (14 August 2026), the required funding had not been obtained. This resulted in a qualified opinion on the consolidated audited results for FY2025-26, relating to uncertainty over resumption of the project and recoverability of Capital Work in Progress amounting to Rs. 3,197.81 lakh. The same matter produced a qualified conclusion on the unaudited consolidated results for Q1 FY2026-27, with CWIP of Rs. 3,178.62 lakh. The standalone review report contained an Emphasis of Matter on the temporary pause in construction of the Guatemala manufacturing facility, though the standalone conclusion was not modified.

Working capital deficit at an overseas subsidiary. The auditor’s Emphasis of Matter noted that one overseas subsidiary faces a working capital deficit, and that its operations and ability to meet obligations depend on continued financial support from related parties and shareholders.

Decline in annual profitability. Consolidated revenue from operations fell from Rs. 40,604.08 lakh in FY2024-25 to Rs. 35,629.31 lakh in FY2025-26. Consolidated profit before tax declined from Rs. 4,797.29 lakh to Rs. 1,355.91 lakh, and profit for the year fell from Rs. 3,751.98 lakh to Rs. 650.31 lakh. Basic and diluted EPS declined from Rs. 8.96 / Rs. 8.95 to Rs. 1.45.

Moulded Products segment losses. The Moulded Products segment recorded a loss before tax of Rs. 1,084.24 lakh for FY2025-26, compared with a loss of Rs. 458.68 lakh in FY2024-25. In Q1 FY2026-27 the segment loss was Rs. 162.39 lakh, an improvement over the Rs. 690.82 lakh loss in Q4 FY2025-26 but still negative.

Rising finance costs and borrowings. Consolidated finance costs rose from Rs. 1,048.63 lakh in FY2024-25 to Rs. 1,177.33 lakh in FY2025-26, and further to Rs. 486.76 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27 alone. Non-current borrowings increased from Rs. 9,254.48 lakh to Rs. 30,991.29 lakh, and current borrowings from Rs. 11,189.52 lakh to Rs. 14,923.05 lakh. Capital work in progress rose sharply from Rs. 15,890.07 lakh to Rs. 58,668.78 lakh, reflecting heavy ongoing capital expenditure.

Cash position erosion. Cash and cash equivalents declined from Rs. 6,710.26 lakh at 31 March 2025 to Rs. 1,794.12 lakh at 31 March 2026. Net cash used in investing activities was Rs. 36,721.67 lakh in FY2025-26.

Operational disruptions and market pressures. The plant was shut from 16 June 2025 to 26 July 2025 on account of expansion of the production facility as part of Project Jagriti. Management commentary cited risks including PM4 start-up, market acceptance of products manufactured on PM4, the continuing Middle East situation affecting exports, high financing cost associated with Project Jagriti, and cost pressures or lower net sales realisation in certain segments.

Warrant lapse. 36,00,000 convertible warrants allotted on 14 October 2024 lapsed upon expiry of the extended exercise period on 13 April 2026, as warrant holders did not exercise conversion rights. The upfront consideration of Rs. 2,448 lakh stands forfeited and no equity shares were issued against those warrants.

Monitoring agency transition. CARE Ratings Limited, previously appointed as Monitoring Agency, communicated its inability to issue the Monitoring Agency Report for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and the engagement agreement remained unexecuted. The Board approved closure of the proposed engagement with CARE and appointed Brickwork Ratings India Private Limited as Monitoring Agency with effect from 14 August 2026. The report due could not be finalised and will be submitted upon receipt from the newly appointed agency.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Highest-ever quarterly revenue and strong year-on-year growth. Q1 FY2026-27 total revenue reached Rs. 119.60 crore, up 42% year-on-year. EBITDA was Rs. 17.85 crore, up 31% year-on-year, and PBT was Rs. 8.77 crore, up 34% year-on-year. Consolidated Q1 FY2026-27 revenue from operations was Rs. 11,718.68 lakh, compared with Rs. 8,196.73 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26.

Project Jagriti and PM4 capacity expansion. Project Jagriti is an ongoing capital expenditure programme at the existing Ayodhya manufacturing facility, comprising installation of a new paper machine (PM-4), enhancement of installed capacity of PM-3, augmentation of pulp mill capacity, and installation of an additional 15 MW captive power plant. Total project cost for PM4 is approximately ₹753 crore, adding over 30,000 tonnes of annual capacity. Funding for Project Jagriti was completed via Neo Asset Management, with promoter shares pledged temporarily. PM4 start-up is scheduled for November 2026, with commercial production and stabilisation anticipated around January.

Segment growth. The Food Services segment saw revenue growth of 34% year-on-year to Rs. 18.45 crore, and B2B growth of 46% to Rs. 16.5 crore. The company reported solid movement in Food Services with 9 new retail chains live, delivery ware customer success with ordering of the first facility, and final pilot trials for flexC base material. Growth was also reported in Wrap & Carry and Food Services business segments.

Expansion into flexible packaging. The company is expanding into flexible packaging with flexC base paper, with final pilot trials underway.

Scaling of asset-light models and new product launches. The company is scaling outsourced manufacturing models and launching new delivery containers.

Break-even trajectory in Food Services. The company is on track to break even this year after nearly a decade of losses in that business.

Capital raising completed. During Q1 FY2026-27, the company raised Rs. 425 crores through the issue and allotment of secured Non-Convertible Debentures on a private placement basis, with proceeds utilised towards repayment of existing borrowings, ongoing Jagriti project capitalisation, and other purposes as defined in the Debenture Trust Deed. The company also raised Rs. 51.10 crores through a preferential issue and allotment of 27,20,000 equity shares at Rs. 110 per share (face value Rs. 10, premium of Rs. 100) to non-promoter group, and 77,00,000 warrants of Rs. 110 each convertible into equity shares to the Promoters’ Group, with upfront money of Rs. 21.18 crores (25%) received on allotment. Proceeds have been / will be utilised for repayment of existing borrowings or the Jagriti Project and other purposes as defined in the notice of the Extra-Ordinary General Meeting.

Trading approval for preferential shares. Pakka Limited received trading approvals from BSE and NSE for 27,20,000 equity shares allotted to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis, with these shares listed and admitted to dealings effective from September 9, 2026. The shares have a face value of INR 10 each, were issued at a premium of INR 100 per share (issue price of INR 110), bear distinctive numbers from 4,49,48,101 to 4,76,68,100, are under ISIN INE551D01018, and remain under lock-in up to March 30, 2027 (lock-in release date March 31, 2027).

Restart of Pakka Inc., USA. The Board approved the restart of operations of Pakka Inc., USA, the company’s wholly owned subsidiary, and an aggregate financial outlay of up to USD 1 million. The approved amount is inclusive of the investment and working capital required for restarting Pakka Inc., applicable statutory severance obligations, and legal, professional, accounting, tax, compliance, storage, administrative and other necessary or unavoidable costs relating to Pakka Inc. and Pakka Guatemala. Any financial commitment exceeding the approved aggregate limit shall require prior approval of the Board. The investment is expected to be completed in one or more tranches on or before 31st December, 2026, subject to applicable approvals and compliances. Pakka Inc. operates in the sustainable and compostable food-packaging sector for the North American market, with turnover of Rs. 1.88 crores for FY2025-26 (NIL for FY2024-25 and FY2023-24).

Merger of Pakka Impact Limited. The scheme of merger of Pakka Impact Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary, into Pakka Limited effective 1st April 2025 was approved by the Board on 30th May 2025 under Sections 230-232 of the Companies Act, 2013, subject to regulatory approvals. In view of pending approvals, no effects of PIL financials are given in the results.

Closure of Pakka Pte Limited. As per Board approval, Pakka Pte Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary, shall be closed in this financial year. Requisite provision for impairment has already been considered in the audited financials of FY2023-24.

Annual General Meeting and governance items. The 46th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, 29 September 2026 at 10:30 a.m. (IST) via Video Conferencing or Other Audio-Visual Means. Key agenda items include adopting financial statements, re-appointing directors Mr. Gautam Ghosh and Mr. Himanshu Kapoor, altering the Articles of Association to include provisions for an Observer and Nominee Director pursuant to debenture trust arrangements, and clarifying the utilisation of preferential issue proceeds for Project Jagriti and term loan repayments. Separately, the Board approved the appointment of Kapoor Tandon & Co., Chartered Accountants, as Internal Auditor for FY2026-27.

3. Key Risks

  • Funding risk at overseas subsidiary: Uncertainty regarding resumption of the suspended project at Pakka Inc. / Pakka Guatemala and recoverability of CWIP of Rs. 3,197.81 lakh (audited FY2025-26) / Rs. 3,178.62 lakh (Q1 FY2026-27), with no adjustment quantified. Management stated it does not envisage impairment at this stage as most CWIP is for detailed engineering expected to be utilised in the near future, and is actively exploring financing options.
  • Working capital risk: One overseas subsidiary faces a working capital deficit and depends on continued financial support from related parties and shareholders.
  • Execution risk on PM4: Start-up scheduled for November 2026, with commercial production and stabilisation anticipated around January. Risks include start-up delays, market acceptance of products manufactured on PM4, and product adoptability.
  • Export market risk: The continuing Middle East situation is affecting exports.
  • Financing cost risk: High financing cost associated with Project Jagriti, with finance costs rising and borrowings increasing substantially.
  • Segment profitability risk: Moulded Products segment continues to record losses.
  • Regulatory and compliance risk: Merger of Pakka Impact Limited pending regulatory approvals; monitoring agency transition from CARE to Brickwork Ratings; investment in Pakka Inc. subject to FEMA, Overseas Investment Rules, and RBI reporting through the Authorised Dealer Bank.
  • Cash flow risk: Significant cash outflow for capital expenditure (Rs. 37,325.39 lakh in FY2025-26 for purchase of property, plant and equipment, intangible assets including CWIP and capital advances), with cash and cash equivalents declining to Rs. 1,794.12 lakh.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Revenue guidance. Management provided FY2026-27 revenue guidance of approximately ₹500 crore. Observed Q1 FY2026-27 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 11,718.68 lakh (Rs. 117.19 crore), with total income of Rs. 11,939.57 lakh. Standalone Q1 FY2026-27 revenue from operations was Rs. 11,718.68 lakh, with total income of Rs. 11,960.09 lakh. Management stated it expects the growth trend to continue through the year.

EBITDA margin guidance. Management expected FY2026-27 EBITDA margin of around 18% to 19%. Observed Q1 FY2026-27 EBITDA was Rs. 17.85 crore on total revenue of Rs. 119.60 crore, which equates to approximately 14.9%. Management stated it expects fixed costs to hold.

Tax rate guidance. Management expected an effective tax rate of approximately 26%. Observed Q1 FY2026-27 tax expense was Rs. 250.75 lakh on profit before tax of Rs. 839.86 lakh (consolidated), equating to approximately 29.9%. For Q1 FY2026-27 standalone, tax expense was Rs. 250.75 lakh on PBT of Rs. 876.82 lakh, approximately 28.6%.

PM4 commissioning guidance. Management anticipated commercial production and stabilisation of PM4 around January, with start-up scheduled for November 2026. No observed performance data on PM4 commissioning is available in the supplied material.

Food Services break-even guidance. Management expects to break even in Food Services after years of losses. Observed Q1 FY2026-27 Food Services revenue growth was 34% year-on-year to Rs. 18.45 crore, and B2B growth was 46% to Rs. 16.5 crore. The Moulded Products segment (which includes Food Services) recorded a loss before tax of Rs. 162.39 lakh in Q1 FY2026-27, compared with a loss of Rs. 690.82 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26 and a loss of Rs. 60.75 lakh in Q1 FY2025-26.

Growth strategy guidance. Management guidance includes growing B2B by adding cities and accounts, scaling B2C retail approximately 3x, optimising COGS with an asset-light capacity, and developing the delivery range with large-scale trials. Observed Q1 FY2026-27 performance shows revenue growth of 42% year-on-year and EBITDA growth of 31% year-on-year, with 9 new retail chains live in Food Services and final pilot trials for flexC base material.

Prior year performance context. FY2025-26 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 35,629.31 lakh, with profit before tax of Rs. 1,355.91 lakh and profit for the year of Rs. 650.31 lakh. Q4 FY2025-26 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 10,142.66 lakh, with profit before tax of Rs. 473.93 lakh. Q1 FY2026-27 consolidated revenue from operations of Rs. 11,718.68 lakh represents a sequential increase over Q4 FY2025-26 and a year-on-year increase over Q1 FY2025-26 (Rs. 8,196.73 lakh).
