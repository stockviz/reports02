KRN Heat Exchanger and Refrigeration — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Margin compression in the standalone business. While standalone revenue rose 59.07% year-on-year in Q1 FY27 (₹181.97 Cr vs ₹114.40 Cr), standalone EBITDA margin fell to 13.56% from 17.30% (down 374 bps) and net profit margin fell to 10.26% from 13.71% (down 345 bps). Standalone net profit grew only 19.02% against revenue growth of 59.07%.
  • Rising cost base. Standalone raw material rose to ₹147.25 Cr (from ₹84.88 Cr) and finance costs rose to ₹1.33 Cr (from ₹0.63 Cr). On a consolidated basis, employee costs rose sharply to ₹18.19 Cr (from ₹6.65 Cr), other expenses to ₹14.04 Cr (from ₹5.31 Cr), finance costs to ₹3.04 Cr (from ₹0.70 Cr), and depreciation to ₹6.47 Cr (from ₹2.19 Cr).
  • GST demand order. The Deputy Commissioner of State Tax, Circle Shahjahanpur, Zone Bhiwadi, Rajasthan passed an order dated 13 May 2026 (received 19 June 2026) for recovery of tax of ₹4,94,66,278 (IGST+CGST+SGST) under Section 74A of the RGST Act 2017, interest of ₹9,75,773 under Section 50, and penalty of ₹49,46,627 under Section 74A(5), relating to alleged ineligible input tax credit for FY2025-26. The company states it availed the ITC following due process and is taking appropriate legal action challenging the recovery, interest and penalty, and that there will be no material financial impact except to the extent mentioned.
  • Working capital absorption. Consolidated inventories rose to ₹272.91 Cr (FY26) from ₹95.85 Cr (FY25), and trade receivables to ₹174.71 Cr from ₹92.96 Cr. Consolidated cash flow from operating activities was negative at ₹-113.80 Cr in FY26, compared with ₹21.44 Cr in FY25.
  • Balance sheet gearing shift. Current borrowings increased to ₹187.10 Cr in FY26 from ₹32.04 Cr in FY25, and trade payables to ₹158.23 Cr from ₹55.32 Cr.
  • Export concentration. In Q1 FY27, the USA was the largest export market at 32.59% of overseas revenue, followed by France at 19.81%, Vietnam at 12.20%, UAE at 11.34% and Italy at 10.78%. Overseas revenue was 20.76% of total segment revenue in Q1 FY27.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong consolidated growth in Q1 FY27. Consolidated revenue from operations was ₹252.32 Cr versus ₹115.28 Cr in Q1 FY26, up 118.87%. Consolidated EBITDA was ₹49.06 Cr versus ₹17.59 Cr, up 178.85%, with EBITDA margin expanding 418 bps to 19.44%. Consolidated net profit was ₹32.90 Cr versus ₹12.42 Cr, up 164.84%, with net profit margin up 226 bps to 13.04%. Consolidated diluted EPS was ₹5.20 versus ₹2.00.
  • Export momentum. Overseas revenue grew 177.31% year-on-year to ₹52.38 Cr, with exports spread across 14 countries during the quarter. Export share of total segment revenue rose to 20.76% from 16.38% in Q1 FY26.
  • Domestic demand depth. Domestic revenue was ₹199.94 Cr, contributing 79.24% of total segment revenue, growing 107.42% over Q1 FY26.
  • Capital raise completed. A qualified institutions placement opened 26 May 2026 and closed 1 June 2026; 33,01,886 equity shares were allotted at ₹1,060 per share, aggregating ₹350.00 Cr, with net proceeds of ₹341.79 Cr. Funds have been allocated to stated objectives, with the unutilised portion parked in fixed deposits and bonds.
  • State incentive. The Government of Rajasthan sanctioned an incentive of ₹182.95 Cr for the Neemrana facility, supporting expansion, localisation and backward integration.
  • IPO proceeds fully utilised. IPO proceeds of ₹311.12 Cr were fully utilised as at 30 June 2026, with no unutilised balance — ₹235.76 Cr invested as equity in KRN HVAC Products Pvt. Ltd. for the Neemrana manufacturing facility and ₹75.36 Cr towards general corporate purposes.
  • Order win. Subsequent to the quarter, KRN HVAC Products Pvt. Ltd. received a domestic supply order of ₹50.87 Cr for heat exchanger coils on 23 July 2026.
  • Portfolio and capacity expansion. The company established KRN HVAC Product Pvt. Ltd. and acquired the Bus AC Division of Sphere Refrigeration Systems (2025-2026). Its product range spans Fin & Tube, Bar & Plate, Complete HVAC Systems, Bus AC, Refrigeration Components, Technical Tubes, Sheet Metal Parts and Tubings, serving HVAC, refrigeration, data centers, automotive, railways, industrial cooling, process, power and marine end-uses. Manufacturing footprint includes Unit-I at Neemrana (total land area 840,127.97 sq ft) and Unit-II under KRN HVAC Products Pvt. Ltd. (total land area 104,948.13 sq ft).
  • Multi-year growth. Consolidated revenue from operations rose from ₹308.28 Cr (FY24) to ₹429.85 Cr (FY25) to ₹600.06 Cr (FY26). Consolidated EBITDA rose from ₹58.45 Cr to ₹70.51 Cr to ₹112.48 Cr; consolidated net profit rose from ₹39.39 Cr to ₹52.88 Cr to ₹76.47 Cr.
  • Governance and compliance actions. The Board approved the appointment of M/s. R S Chauhan & Associates as Cost Auditor and M/s. Sharma Shankar & Co. as Internal Auditor for FY2026-27 on 12 August 2026. Mr. Pawan Nawal was appointed Chief Financial Officer with effect from 15 May 2026. The company will meet investors/analysts (Kirin Advisors) on 21 August 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Margin risk in the standalone entity, where EBITDA margin declined 374 bps and PAT margin declined 345 bps year-on-year in Q1 FY27 despite strong revenue growth.
  • Tax litigation risk from the Rajasthan GST order of 13 May 2026 (received 19 June 2026) covering tax, interest and penalty, which the company is challenging.
  • Working capital and cash flow risk, evidenced by negative consolidated operating cash flow of ₹-113.80 Cr in FY26, higher inventories and receivables, and increased current borrowings.
  • Export market concentration risk, with the USA, France, Vietnam, UAE and Italy together accounting for the bulk of overseas revenue in Q1 FY27.
  • Execution risk on the capacity, localisation and backward integration programme funded by the QIP and supported by the Rajasthan incentive.
  • Forward-looking statement risk, as noted in the company’s own disclaimers: statements are subject to risks and uncertainties including economic changes in India and abroad, government actions, political or economic developments, and technological risks, and actual results may differ materially.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management commentary (Q1 FY27). The Chairman & Managing Director stated that FY27 began on a strong note with consolidated revenue growing 119% year-on-year; that export revenue increased 177% year-on-year with products reaching customers across 14 countries; that domestic growth demonstrates depth of demand in the core market; and that focus remains on building scale, strengthening manufacturing capabilities, deepening presence in high-potential markets, and investments in capacity, localisation and backward integration.
  • Observed performance versus that commentary. Consolidated revenue grew 118.87% (₹252.32 Cr vs ₹115.28 Cr), consistent with the stated ~119%. Overseas revenue grew 177.31% to ₹52.38 Cr, consistent with the stated 177%. Exports reached 14 countries, as stated. Domestic revenue grew 107.42% to ₹199.94 Cr, supporting the stated domestic demand depth. Consolidated EBITDA margin expanded 418 bps to 19.44% and consolidated PAT margin expanded 226 bps to 13.04%.
  • Where performance diverged from the growth narrative. At the standalone level, margins contracted (EBITDA margin down 374 bps to 13.56%; PAT margin down 345 bps to 10.26%) and standalone net profit grew only 19.02% against 59.07% revenue growth. Consolidated growth was also accompanied by sharply higher employee costs, other expenses, finance costs and depreciation, and by negative operating cash flow for FY26.
  • Capital and incentive commitments. The QIP raised ₹350.00 Cr (net ₹341.79 Cr) and IPO proceeds of ₹311.12 Cr were fully utilised as at 30 June 2026, with ₹235.76 Cr invested as equity in KRN HVAC Products Pvt. Ltd. for the Neemrana facility. The Rajasthan government sanctioned a ₹182.95 Cr incentive for the Neemrana facility. These support the stated expansion, localisation and backward integration plans.
  • Post-quarter order. The ₹50.87 Cr domestic heat exchanger coil order received on 23 July 2026 by KRN HVAC Products Pvt. Ltd. is a subsequent event supporting the growth outlook.
  • AGM proposals. The 9th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Friday, 25 September 2026 at 3:00 p.m. IST through video conferencing/other audio-visual means. Proposals include ratifying cost auditor remuneration for M/s. R S Chauhan & Associates at ₹1,50,000, approving revised remuneration for Chairman and Managing Director Mr. Santosh Kumar Yadav at ₹1,26,00,000 per annum, and approving revised remuneration for Whole-time Director Mrs. Anju Devi at ₹84,00,000 per annum, both effective from 1 April 2026.
