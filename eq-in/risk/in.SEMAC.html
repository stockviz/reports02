Headwinds and Challenges

  • Middle East geopolitical uncertainty. The Group states it is closely monitoring ongoing geopolitical developments in the Middle East and their effects on operations and financial position. Regional uncertainties may affect client relationships, project execution timelines, and supply chain activities, potentially impacting revenue forecasts and margin strength.
  • Sequential revenue decline. Consolidated revenue from operations fell from Rs. 7,486.83 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to Rs. 3,042.06 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Standalone revenue fell from Rs. 7,056.13 lakhs to Rs. 2,638.89 lakhs over the same period.
  • Profit compression. Consolidated profit before tax declined from Rs. 352.78 lakhs (quarter ended March 31, 2026) to Rs. 101.53 lakhs (quarter ended June 30, 2026). Standalone profit before tax declined from Rs. 457.11 lakhs to Rs. 133.22 lakhs.
  • Loss-making overseas subsidiary. One subsidiary incorporated outside India (Semac and Partner LLC – Muscat, Oman, 65% held) recorded total revenue of Rs. 403.16 lakhs and a total net loss of Rs. 42.40 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with total comprehensive loss of Rs. 38.77 lakhs. This subsidiary’s interim financial information was not reviewed by the parent’s auditor.
  • Non-controlling interest loss. Consolidated profit attributable to non-controlling interest was negative Rs. 12.03 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • Pending merger. The Scheme of Amalgamation of Semac Construction Technologies India Private Limited into Semac Construction Limited, approved by the Board on July 30, 2025 and filed with the NCLT Chennai on September 29, 2025, had only received first motion approval as on March 31, 2026. Final NCLT approval is still awaited, and no effect of the merger has been given in the financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Diversified project portfolio. The Group describes itself as a multi-disciplinary engineering consultancy with a diversified project portfolio, which it states provides a level of resilience and helps mitigate short-term volatility arising from external events.
  • Wholly owned subsidiary consolidation. On June 26, 2025, the Company acquired 50% equity shares from the shareholders of its Associate Company, Semac Construction Technologies India Private Limited, making it a wholly owned subsidiary. The consolidated results now include this entity.
  • Overseas subsidiary. The Group’s consolidated results include one subsidiary incorporated outside India (Semac and Partner LLC – Muscat, Oman), providing an international presence.
  • Year-on-year standalone improvement. Standalone revenue from operations rose from Rs. 4,723.32 lakhs (quarter ended June 30, 2025) to Rs. 2,638.89 lakhs (quarter ended June 30, 2026). Standalone profit before tax rose from Rs. 128.87 lakhs to Rs. 133.22 lakhs over the same period.
  • Unmodified auditor conclusions. The statutory auditor issued an unqualified review report on both standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The auditor also confirmed an unmodified opinion on the standalone and consolidated audited financial results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Leadership continuity. The Board approved the re-appointment of Mr. Harivansh Dalmia as Whole Time Director for a further term of five years effective August 29, 2026, subject to member approval at the forthcoming AGM. He is a qualified Chartered Accountant with experience in statutory audit across consumer electronics, refractories, and IT services.

Key Risks

  • Geopolitical risk. The Group explicitly identifies ongoing geopolitical developments in the Middle East as a risk that may affect client relationships, project execution timelines, and supply chain activities, potentially impacting revenue forecasts and margin strength.
  • Merger execution risk. The Scheme of Amalgamation remains subject to final NCLT approval. Until the Scheme becomes effective, the accounting for the merger will not be carried out, and the financial results do not reflect its effects.
  • Overseas subsidiary review gap. The auditor did not review the interim financial results of the subsidiary incorporated outside India; the conclusion on the consolidated statement relies solely on the report of another auditor for that entity.
  • Sequential volatility. The sharp quarter-on-quarter decline in revenue and profit indicates susceptibility to volatility between periods.
  • Non-controlling interest exposure. The Group holds 65% of Semac and Partner LLC, exposing it to losses attributable to non-controlling interests, as observed in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Middle East impact guidance. Management states that, based on its initial assessment as at the reporting date, the Group does not expect any material impact on the financial statements for the period ended June 30, 2026, and is proactively implementing risk management strategies to safeguard stakeholder interests. Observed performance shows the overseas subsidiary recorded a net loss of Rs. 42.40 lakhs for the quarter, and consolidated profit attributable to non-controlling interest was negative Rs. 12.03 lakhs.
  • Merger accounting guidance. Management states that upon receipt of final NCLT approval and the Scheme becoming effective, accounting for the merger will be carried out in accordance with Ind AS 103 – Business Combinations and other relevant regulatory requirements. Observed status: only first motion approval had been received as on March 31, 2026, and no effect of the merger has been given in the financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • Resilience guidance. Management states that the Group’s diversified project portfolio provides a level of resilience and helps mitigate short-term volatility arising from external events. Observed performance shows a sequential decline in both revenue and profit before tax at standalone and consolidated levels.
  • Conservative estimates guidance. Management states that the Group’s conservative approach to estimates and revenue recognition seeks to factor in uncertainties appropriately. Observed performance shows the standalone and consolidated results were reviewed by the statutory auditor, which issued unqualified review reports.
