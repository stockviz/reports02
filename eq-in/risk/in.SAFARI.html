Broker Narrative

The broker narrative evolved from unbridled optimism in 2023 — with zero headwinds flagged and expectations of 20%+ sales/EBITDA/PAT CAGR and 100-150bps margin expansion — to a cautious 2026 stance acknowledging RM inflation, manpower shortages, and declining EBITDA/PAT. The capacity expansion thesis persisted across both reports, but execution challenges at Jaipur undermined expected returns. Margin improvement expectations completely reversed, with the 19.3% Q4FY23 EBITDA margin giving way to YoY compression under raw material cost pressure.

Fears that came true

  • Sharp raw material inflation pressured margins, underpinning a sustained streak of DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes across nearly all 2023–2026 calls.
  • Manpower shortage at the expanded Jaipur facility constrained production ramp-up, preventing the expected utilization benefits from the 6.5 lakh pieces/month capacity.
  • EBITDA and PAT declined year-on-year due to RM inflation and higher costs, directly contradicting the projected 20/24/23% sales/EBITDA/PAT CAGR and driving negative actual returns.

Optimism that failed

  • The gross margin improvement to 47.9% was not sustained, as RM inflation reversed the favorable product mix, soft raw material prices, and vendor price rationalization that had driven the margin peak.
  • The 100-150bps margin expansion thesis via product mix, branding, and retail expansion failed, as EBITDA margin compressed under cost pressures instead.
  • The projected 20/24/23% sales/EBITDA/PAT CAGR over FY23-25E failed entirely, with EBITDA and PAT ultimately declining YoY rather than compounding as expected.
