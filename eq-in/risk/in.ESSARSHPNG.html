Essar Shipping Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Erosion of net worth and accumulated losses. As of June 30, 2026, the standalone net worth of the Company is eroded, with accumulated losses of ₹5,972.55 crore against share capital and reserves of ₹5,218.23 crore. The Company has been incurring operating losses for several years. On a consolidated basis, the Group’s accumulated losses stand at approximately ₹4,816.25 crore against share capital and reserves of approximately ₹2,975.72 crore. The statutory auditors have flagged a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt on the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern, while noting that the financials have been prepared on a going concern basis.

Weak operating performance in the June 2026 quarter. Standalone income from operations for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was ₹0.04 crore, down sharply from ₹4.25 crore in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. The standalone loss after tax for the quarter was ₹4.52 crore. On a consolidated basis, the Group reported a loss before exceptional items of ₹27.61 crore for the quarter, with finance costs of ₹24.69 crore.

SFIO investigation. During the preceding financial year, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs initiated an investigation against the Company under the Serious Fraud Investigation Office (SFIO). The Company has stated it is fully complying with all requirements, including submission of requisite documents and information, and has intimated the matter to the stock exchanges.

Subsidiaries under liquidation. Certain entities have not been consolidated because they are under liquidation: OGD Services Limited (subsidiary), Seros Oilfields Services India Limited (associate), and Seros Drilling Private Limited (joint venture). The Company has stated that these entities are not considered material for consolidation.

Regulatory pendency on set-off. The Company has netted off ₹331.26 crore payable to a wholly owned overseas subsidiary against amounts receivable from the same subsidiary. This is subject to pending application and approval from regulatory authorities.

Reporting and disclosure lapses. The Company resubmitted its June 30, 2026 unaudited financial results on August 22, 2026, after identifying inadvertent clerical errors in Excel in the presentation of Earnings Per Share after exceptional items — originally reported as negative (₹11.13) and corrected to positive ₹11.13. Separately, a discrepancy between PAT figures in the consolidated PDF (₹12.78 crore) and the XBRL utility (₹12.76 crore) was attributed to a casting error and corrected.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Liquidity measures. During the preceding financial year, management took several measures to improve liquidity and address the mismatch between current assets and current liabilities. These included generating cash flow from TUG operations and obtaining comfort letters from Group Companies confirming deferment of payment of accrued interest for at least two years. As a result, on a standalone basis, current assets exceeded current liabilities by ₹8.09 crore as of June 30, 2026.

Exceptional gains. Standalone exceptional income of ₹0.93 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 includes reversal of impairment on a loan to a subsidiary of ₹0.75 crore on account of amounts realised, and a foreign exchange gain of ₹0.18 crore. On a consolidated basis, an exceptional gain of ₹258.02 crore was recognised, on account of reversal of impairment on receivables in the case of one foreign subsidiary.

Asset monetisation and portfolio actions. The board approved the sale of the Mobile Offshore Semi-Submersible Drilling Rig “Essar WildCat,” owned by wholly-owned subsidiary Essar Shipping FZCO, Dubai, with an expected completion date of March 31, 2027. The 16th Annual General Meeting scheduled for September 30, 2026 includes special resolutions for the disinvestment of overseas wholly-owned subsidiaries (Essar Shipping DMCC and OGD Services Holdings Limited) and the sale of assets including the Semi-Submersible Rig “Essar Wildcat” and “Essar Tug III.”

Market outlook. Management notes that the medium-term outlook for the global offshore drilling market is positive from 2027 onward, and the Company continues to monitor the market for opportune times to acquire ships and deploy assets.

3. Key Risks

  • Going concern risk: Material uncertainty related to the Company’s and the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern, given persistent operating losses and eroded net worth.
  • Regulatory and investigative risk: The SFIO investigation and the pending regulatory approval for the ₹331.26 crore set-off between payable and receivable with an overseas subsidiary.
  • Liquidation risk in group entities: Certain subsidiaries, associates, and joint ventures are under liquidation and have not been consolidated.
  • Execution risk on asset sales: The sale of the Essar WildCat rig and other disinvestment actions are subject to completion by expected dates and to shareholder/regulatory approvals.
  • Financial reporting risk: Identified clerical and casting errors in financial results submissions required resubmission and correction.
  • Low operating income: Standalone operating income has fallen to minimal levels, with losses continuing at both standalone and consolidated levels.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance on profitability and liquidity. Management has stated it is optimistic about future performance and will endeavor all efforts to keep the organization a profitable concern by actively pursuing measures to improve the Company’s financial position and liquidity. Observed performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 shows a standalone loss after tax of ₹4.52 crore and a consolidated loss after exceptional items and tax of ₹230.41 crore profit (driven by the ₹258.02 crore exceptional gain), with operating income at ₹0.04 crore standalone. The liquidity measures cited by management — TUG cash flows and comfort letters on interest deferment — are reflected in current assets exceeding current liabilities by ₹8.09 crore standalone as of June 30, 2026.

Guidance on asset sale completion. Management has indicated an expected completion date of March 31, 2027 for the sale of the Mobile Offshore Semi-Submersible Drilling Rig Essar WildCat. This remains a forward-looking expectation subject to completion.

Guidance on market outlook. Management notes a positive medium-term outlook for the global offshore drilling market from 2027 onward and continues to monitor the market for acquisition opportunities. Observed segment performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 shows fleet operating and chartering segment results of negative ₹1.67 crore and rig operating and chartering segment results of negative ₹1.24 crore, with total segment results of negative ₹2.91 crore before unallocated interest and finance costs.

Prior-year context. For the financial year ended March 31, 2026, standalone net profit was ₹553.12 crore (largely driven by exceptional items/reversals of impairment), compared to ₹370.95 crore in the previous year, while consolidated net profit/loss showed a loss of ₹112.06 crore. No dividend was recommended for the year due to accumulated losses.
