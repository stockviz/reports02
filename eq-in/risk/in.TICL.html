TICL — Recent Corporate Announcements: Headwinds, Tailwinds, Risks, and Guidance vs. Performance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Legacy resolution plan and NPA upgrade pending. The company is required to pay ₹221 crore in full and final settlement of its resolution plan, to be distributed among creditors. Payment is contingent on banks upgrading the company’s account status from NPA to Standard. The company states it has complied with all stipulations of the approved resolution plan and is awaiting the upgrade. NCLT Kolkata directed the banks to complete the upgrade and report by 27 January 2026; banks submitted a report confirming the account status has been made “Standard,” but this is still not reflected in the company’s CIBIL report. Under the NCLT order, the company will make the full and final payment within 90 days after the final upgrade.

Subsidiary and step-down subsidiary issues. Tantia Infrastructure (P) Ltd (TIPL), a subsidiary, has a loan of ₹543.83 lakh given to a Non-Banking Finance Company on which no interest income has been considered, leading to overstatement of loss and understatement of Other Current Assets. Tantia Raxaultollway Private Limited (TRPL), a step-down subsidiary, abandoned its only project after issuing a termination notice to NHAI; NHAI has taken over control of the project assets. TRPL’s facilities from banks and institutions became NPA with no provisions made for interest from 1 April 2018 onward, and the lead bank filed a case before DRT for recovery. The auditor notes that since project assets were taken over by NHAI, TRPL may not be able to realize its assets in the normal course of business at the disclosed value and may not be able to discharge its liabilities in full.

Associate company in insolvency. Tantia Sanjauliparkings Private Limited (TSPL) was admitted to CIR process by order dated 23 March 2023, and its financial statements as on 30 June 2026 are not available for consolidation.

Arbitration and suspended project. During the quarter ended 31 March 2025, the company initiated arbitration proceedings before the Hon’ble Commercial Court, Cuttack, against a customer regarding a suspended project. No provision for bad and doubtful debts has been made for the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Trade Receivable includes ₹2,877 lakh and Other Financial Assets includes ₹7,794 lakh receivable pursuant to an arbitration; management, supported by external legal opinion, views the amount as recoverable with no significant uncertainty as to ultimate collection.

Accounting gaps noted by auditors. The company has not made any provision for Gratuity and Leave Encashment in line with Ind-AS 19, resulting in understatement of profit. The company has not accounted for ₹8 lakh (April 2026 to June 2026) as rental income for the RMC Plant at Guwahati, resulting in understatement of profit by ₹8 lakh. The company initiated a reconciliation process with Trade Receivable, Advances to Vendor and Creditors to determine continuation of contracts, work-in-progress details, progress billing, and disputed/undisputed dues, and made a provision of ₹Nil for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 as provision for estimated credit loss based on its own assessment.

Revenue quality. Revenue from operations of ₹1,104 lakh for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 includes ₹302 lakh of unbilled revenue, of which ₹216 lakh has been billed subsequently.

Promoter shareholding reduction. During the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Mr. Upendra Singh and Mr. Shrish Tapuriah, promoters, sold 32,37,060 equity shares (2.09% of paid-up equity share capital) on the stock exchange. Promoter shareholding reduced from 13,02,98,301 shares (84.06%) to 12,70,61,241 shares (81.97%). The transactions do not result in any change in control or management.

Auditor qualifications. The consolidated review report includes a qualified opinion relating to TIPL and TRPL matters, and the auditor was unable to carry out procedures under the SEBI circular under Regulation 33(8) since related information/records were not available. The auditor did not review the financial results of 1 subsidiary and 1 step-down subsidiary (total revenue Nil, net loss after tax ₹23 lakh for the quarter ended 30 June 2026), relying solely on unaudited interim financial results furnished by management.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Infrastructure-led economic growth. The company operates in the infrastructure sector, which is supported by India’s infrastructure-led economic growth under initiatives such as Viksit Bharat @2047, PM Gati Shakti, and the National Infrastructure Pipeline. Opportunities span transportation, urban infrastructure, industrial manufacturing, renewable energy, and logistics, supported by sustained public capital expenditure and increasing private sector participation.

Resolution plan progress. Banks have submitted a report to NCLT Kolkata confirming the account status has been made “Standard,” which is a step toward completing the resolution plan and the final payment of ₹221 crore within 90 days after the final upgrade.

Single reportable segment. The company is engaged in infrastructure activities and has only one reportable operating segment as per Ind AS 108.

Leadership appointment. Mr. Biswajit Chakraborty was appointed as Chief Executive Officer (CEO) effective 11 August 2026, with core competencies in project management, co-ordination, contract administration and management, and site and construction management/resource planning.

3. Key Risks

  • Project execution risks, including regulatory approvals, client-related delays, and supply chain disruptions.
  • Commodity price fluctuations in steel, cement, fuel, and bitumen.
  • Regulatory and legal compliance changes.
  • Competition in the EPC sector.
  • Human resource availability.
  • Legacy matters and debt resolution constraints, with a material uncertainty noted by auditors regarding the company’s financial position and pending litigations.
  • Recoverability risk on arbitration receivables (₹2,877 lakh in Trade Receivable and ₹7,794 lakh in Other Financial Assets), though management views these as recoverable.
  • Subsidiary/step-down subsidiary financial exposure, including TRPL’s inability to realize assets at disclosed values and discharge liabilities in full, and TIPL’s non-recognition of interest income on a ₹543.83 lakh loan to an NBFC.
  • Non-provision for gratuity and leave encashment, resulting in understatement of profit.
  • Non-provision for bad and doubtful debts related to the Cuttack arbitration matter.
  • Impairment risk on investment in subsidiary preference shares; no change in value recognized during the quarter ended 30 June 2026 based on the earlier impairment study.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

Resolution plan payment guidance. Management states the company has complied with all stipulations of the approved resolution plan and is awaiting the NPA-to-Standard upgrade. Banks have confirmed to NCLT that the account status has been made “Standard,” but this is not yet reflected in the company’s CIBIL report. Per the NCLT order, the company will make the full and final payment of ₹221 crore within 90 days after the final upgrade. Observed status: the upgrade is confirmed by banks to NCLT but not yet reflected in CIBIL, so the payment trigger has not been fully satisfied.

Arbitration recoverability guidance. Management, supported by external legal opinion, views the arbitration receivables (₹2,877 lakh in Trade Receivable and ₹7,794 lakh in Other Financial Assets) as recoverable with no significant uncertainty. Observed status: the matter is currently under hearing before the Hon’ble Commercial Court, Cuttack, and no provision for bad and doubtful debts has been made for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

Impairment guidance. The company conducted an earlier impairment study for its investment in preference shares of its subsidiary and restated the investment at the value stated in the study, making consequent provisions. Management states there has been no significant development in the subsidiary subsequent to the study, so the preference shares continue to be accounted at the same value with no change recognized during the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Observed status: provision for impairment loss of ₹Nil on account of fair value of investment in subsidiary based on independent impairment study by the company.

Reconciliation guidance. The company initiated a reconciliation process with Trade Receivable, Advances to Vendor and Creditors to determine continuation of contracts, work-in-progress details with age, stage of completion, progress billing, and disputed/undisputed dues. Observed status: provision of ₹Nil for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 as provision for estimated credit loss based on the company’s own assessment, with the auditor relying on management representation on carrying amount and provisions for expected credit loss as at 30 June 2026.

Long-term sector outlook. Management remains optimistic about the long-term growth prospects of the Indian infrastructure sector and intends to maintain a disciplined approach toward project acquisition, focus on operational excellence, prudent financial management, strong governance, and selectively pursue sustainable business opportunities to generate long-term stakeholder value. Observed status: for FY 2025-26, the company reported standalone revenue from operations and other income of ₹6,855 lakh, a net profit of ₹910 lakh, and basic EPS of Re. 0.60, with no dividend recommended for the financial year. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, revenue from operations was ₹1,104 lakh (including ₹302 lakh unbilled), total expenditure ₹1,076 lakh, net profit before tax and exceptional items ₹51 lakh, net profit for the period ₹51 lakh, and EPS ₹0.03. Total assets were ₹76,640 lakh, total liabilities ₹45,447 lakh, and net worth ₹31,193 lakh.
