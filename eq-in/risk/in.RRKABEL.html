R R Kabel — Recent Corporate Announcements: Headwinds, Tailwinds, Risks, and Guidance vs. Performance

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • New labour codes: On 21 November 2025, the Government of India notified four Labour Codes (Code on Wages, 2019; Industrial Relations Code, 2020; Code on Social Security, 2020; and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020), consolidating 29 existing labour laws. The company has presented the impact as an exceptional item in its profit and loss statement. In Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), this was a negative exceptional item of Rs. 138 million, which reduced profit before tax. The company states it will continue to evaluate and account for any further impact as and when clarifications are issued.
  • Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) uncertainty: Under the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Amendment Rules, 2025, applicable from 1 April 2026, producers of cables and wires, including R R Kabel, must discharge EPR obligations for recycling of non-ferrous metal scrap by procuring EPR certificates from registered recyclers through a Centralized Online Portal to be operated by the CPCB. As of the date of the financial results (27 July 2026), the portal had not been made operational, and operational guidelines and pricing mechanisms had not been prescribed. The company states it is not in a position to reasonably determine the financial impact, if any, and will monitor developments.
  • FMEG segment still loss-making at the PBT level: While the FMEG business achieved operational breakeven in Q1 FY27, segment results show a small loss before tax of Rs. (0) million for the quarter, compared with a loss of Rs. (71) million in Q1 FY26 and Rs. (93) million in Q4 FY26. The segment remains marginally below the PBT breakeven line.
  • Working capital absorption: For FY26, changes in working capital were negative at Rs. (3,900) million, compared with a positive Rs. 656 million in FY25. Net cash from operating activities declined to Rs. 2,952 million in FY26 from Rs. 4,944 million in FY25. Inventories rose to Rs. 17,705 million in FY26 from Rs. 10,110 million in FY25, and trade payables (other than micro and small enterprises) increased to Rs. 13,490 million from Rs. 7,448 million.
  • Rising finance costs: Finance costs increased to Rs. 263 million in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 151 million in Q1 FY26, and to Rs. 753 million in FY26 from Rs. 589 million in FY25.
  • Cash and cash equivalents declined: Cash and cash equivalents fell to Rs. 855 million as at 31 March 2026 from Rs. 2,157 million as at 31 March 2025, with a net decrease in cash and cash equivalents of Rs. (1,302) million in FY26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Record quarterly revenue: Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was the highest ever quarterly revenue at Rs. 31,682 million, up 54% year-on-year from Rs. 20,586 million and up 7% quarter-on-quarter from Rs. 29,641 million. The company attributes this to healthy domestic demand and export growth.
  • Wires & Cables outperformance: The W&C segment delivered 57% year-on-year revenue growth in Q1 FY27, with segment revenue of Rs. 28,800 million versus Rs. 18,335 million in Q1 FY26. Segment profit increased 105% year-on-year to Rs. 2,854 million from Rs. 1,391 million, and segment margin expanded by 232 basis points year-on-year. The company cites impressive volume growth, strong execution, favourable industry dynamics, improved product mix, disciplined commodity management, and operating efficiencies.
  • FMEG operational breakeven: The FMEG business achieved operational breakeven in Q1 FY27, described as a significant milestone in the business transformation. Revenue grew to Rs. 2,882 million from Rs. 2,251 million in Q1 FY26, supported by demand for premium and new products and ongoing distribution expansion. Segment profitability improved substantially year-on-year, driven by premium products and operating leverage.
  • Profitability expansion: Operating EBITDA nearly doubled, rising 99% year-on-year to Rs. 2,853 million in Q1 FY27, with EBITDA margin expanding 205 basis points to 9.0%. Profit after tax grew 129% year-on-year to Rs. 2,052 million, with PAT margin expanding 212 basis points to 6.5%.
  • Export leadership: The company describes itself as India’s No.1 exporter of wires and cables, and cites export growth as a contributor to Q1 FY27 revenue.
  • Strategic focus areas: The company highlights expanding the cables portfolio, strengthening the distribution network, enhancing execution capabilities, premiumisation in FMEG, and cost discipline as ongoing drivers.
  • Shareholder returns: The 32nd AGM on 15 July 2026 approved a final dividend of INR 5.50 per equity share for FY26, in addition to an interim dividend of INR 4 per equity share already paid during FY25-26.
  • Leadership transition and scale milestone: The Chairman’s AGM address highlighted achievement of the USD 1 billion revenue milestone, a successful leadership transition, enhanced shareholder value, and a strategic roadmap for sustainable growth.
  • Capital expansion: Property, plant and equipment increased to Rs. 10,720 million in FY26 from Rs. 7,076 million in FY25, with capital work-in-progress of Rs. 722 million, indicating ongoing capacity investment.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory uncertainty on EPR: The company cannot currently determine the financial impact of EPR obligations because the CPCB portal is not operational and pricing/operational guidelines are not prescribed. Future costs could be material.
  • Labour code impact: The company has already recognised a Rs. 138 million negative exceptional item in Q1 FY27 and states further impact will be evaluated as clarifications are issued.
  • Working capital and cash flow pressure: FY26 saw negative working capital changes of Rs. 3,900 million, a decline in operating cash flow to Rs. 2,952 million, and a reduction in cash and cash equivalents to Rs. 855 million. Inventories and trade payables rose sharply.
  • Rising borrowings and finance costs: Current borrowings increased to Rs. 2,313 million in FY26 from Rs. 2,220 million in FY25, and finance costs rose to Rs. 753 million in FY26 from Rs. 589 million in FY25.
  • FMEG segment still marginally loss-making at PBT level: Despite operational breakeven, the segment reported a small PBT loss of Rs. (0) million in Q1 FY27.
  • Dependence on Wires & Cables: The W&C segment contributed Rs. 28,800 million of Rs. 31,682 million in Q1 FY27 revenue (approximately 91%), concentrating performance in one segment.
  • Forward-looking statement caveats: The company’s safe harbour notes that actual results may differ materially due to factors including industry downtrends, political and economic changes in India or key markets abroad, tax laws, litigation, labour relations, exchange rate fluctuations, technological changes, and interest and other costs.
  • Commodity and execution risks: The company cites disciplined commodity management as a driver of margin expansion, implying exposure to commodity price movements.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management commentary on Q1 FY27: Mr. Mahendrakumar Kabra, MD, stated that the company started FY27 on a strong note with another quarter of record performance, reflecting the strength of the business model and disciplined execution. He cited robust growth across Wires & Cables, healthy profitability, ability to capitalise on the strong demand environment, and operational excellence.
  • Observed performance versus commentary: The Q1 FY27 results are consistent with this commentary. Revenue grew 54% year-on-year to a record Rs. 31,682 million; operating EBITDA grew 99% to Rs. 2,853 million; PAT grew 129% to Rs. 2,052 million. The W&C segment grew 57% year-on-year with segment profit up 105%. The FMEG segment achieved operational breakeven, which the MD described as an important milestone reflecting the premiumisation strategy and focus on operating efficiencies.
  • Strategic focus versus observed results: The MD cited strategic focus on expanding the cables portfolio, strengthening distribution, and enhancing execution capabilities as yielding encouraging results. Observed results show W&C revenue growth of 57% year-on-year and FMEG revenue growth to Rs. 2,882 million from Rs. 2,251 million, with FMEG operational breakeven.
  • Confidence statement: The MD expressed confidence in the company’s ability to strengthen market position, drive profitability growth, and create long-term value. The Q1 FY27 results show year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter improvements in revenue, EBITDA, PBT, and PAT, which are consistent with this confidence, though the company’s safe harbour notes that forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties and actual results may differ materially.
  • AGM commentary versus observed performance: The Chairman’s AGM address on 15 July 2026 highlighted the USD 1 billion revenue milestone, successful leadership transition, enhanced shareholder value, and a strategic roadmap for sustainable growth. Observed FY26 revenue was Rs. 97,224 million, and the company declared a final dividend of INR 5.50 per equity share plus an interim dividend of INR 4 per equity share for FY25-26. The AGM approved all 10 resolutions with requisite majority, including adoption of FY26 financial statements, dividend declaration, director reappointments, remuneration revisions, and enhancement of borrowing limits under Sections 180(1)(c) and 180(1)(a) of the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Investor engagement: The company scheduled analyst/investor meetings on 12 August 2026 (Emkay Global Conference 2026), 13 August 2026 (Equirus Annual India Conference), and 18 August 2026 (Motilal Oswal Conference 2026) in Mumbai, stating that no unpublished price sensitive information would be shared.
