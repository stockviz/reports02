SAMBHV — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • The company’s Q1FY27 sales volume update (July 02, 2026) notes that overall volume stability was maintained “despite global disruption arising from ongoing war, which adversely impacted the broader industry.”
  • The company’s own risk disclosure identifies fiscal policy, competition, inflationary pressures, and general economic conditions as factors affecting demand, supply, and price conditions in both domestic and international markets.
  • Intermediate product volumes declined year-on-year in Q1FY27 (6,580 tonnes versus 12,989 tonnes in Q1FY26), indicating a shift away from intermediate product sales.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Q1FY27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) was the company’s highest-ever sales volume quarter for Value Added Products, with strong growth from Pre-Galvanised (GP) Coils & Pipes and the Stainless Steel Coils segment.
  • Value Added Products sub-total rose to 1,01,191 tonnes in Q1FY27 from 79,717 tonnes in Q1FY26, driven by Structural Pipes and Tubes (56,617 tonnes), GP Coils and Pipes (29,814 tonnes), and Stainless Steel Coils (14,760 tonnes).
  • The Board approved setting up an 8MW Captive Behind-The-Meter Solar Power Plant at the Kuthrel manufacturing units on August 03, 2026, at an estimated cost of up to ₹250 million, with Phase I (up to 3.2 MW) targeted for FY 2028 and Phase II (up to 4.8 MW) for FY 2029. The stated rationale is to meet power requirements of manufacturing facilities and optimise power cost.
  • Management targets expanding finished products capacity from 0.68 Million MTPA to more than 2 Million MTPA over the next 4–5 years, including greenfield expansions at Kesda and Kuthrel Unit-II, new power plants, and brownfield capacity for ERW Pipes & Tubes.
  • The company cites increasing per capita steel consumption and rapid government infrastructure boosts in India as supportive demand drivers.
  • The company operates two manufacturing facilities (Sarora and Kuthrel, Chhattisgarh) with installed capacity of 350,000 MTPA ERW pipes and tubes, 116,000 MTPA GP coils, 100,000 MTPA GP pipes, and 116,000 MTPA stainless steel coils, supported by 47 distributors across 15 states and one union territory, reaching over 1,000 dealers.

3. Key Risks

  • Fiscal policy, competition, inflationary pressures, and general economic conditions affecting demand, supply, and price conditions in domestic and international markets.
  • Global geopolitical disruption (referenced as “ongoing war”) adversely impacting the broader industry.
  • Execution risk on the announced capacity expansion programme, which is subject to project implementation schedules; the solar plant timelines are explicitly noted as “subject to Project implementation schedule.”
  • The solar project’s mode of finance is undecided (“Internal Accruals / Debt / lease — as may be deemed fit”), leaving funding structure open.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Capacity guidance: Management targets increasing finished products capacity from 0.68 Million MTPA to over 2 Million MTPA over 4–5 years, with Phase-I of the greenfield expansion at Kesda & Kuthrel Unit-II (including 0.36 MMTPA stainless-steel coils capacity and a 25 MW power plant) targeted for commissioning by Q4FY27. No commissioning has yet been reported in the supplied material.
  • Solar plant guidance: Phase I (up to 3.2 MW) targeted for FY 2028 and Phase II (up to 4.8 MW) for FY 2029; no capacity has been added as of the August 03, 2026 disclosure (existing capacity shown as Nil).
  • Observed Q1FY27 performance: Revenue of INR 7,322 million, total EBITDA of INR 1,001 million, operating EBITDA of INR 952 million, and PAT of INR 566 million. Consolidated revenue from operations for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was INR 7,321.73 million, with profit for the period of INR 565.23 million and basic/diluted EPS of INR 1.92/1.62.
  • IPO proceeds utilisation: Of the INR 4,400 million raised, INR 3,900.00 million was used to prepay/repay borrowings, INR 224.55 million for general corporate purposes, and INR 275.45 million for offer issue expenses — fully utilised as of June 30, 2026, with no unutilised amount.
  • Volume performance vs. stated strategy: The Q1FY27 volume update shows the company scaling high-value products (Value Added Products up year-on-year) while intermediate product volumes fell, consistent with the stated focus on high-value product portfolio expansion.
  • Governance/board developments: Mr. Bikash Agrawal was appointed as an Additional Director effective May 09, 2026, and consequently ceased to be Senior Management Personnel; the company stated the cessation was consequential and simultaneous with his appointment, with a separate disclosure submitted later to maintain complete disclosure records. The 09th AGM was held on September 10, 2026, where all resolutions — including adoption of standalone and consolidated financial statements, re-appointment of Director Mr. Suresh Kumar Goyal, and ratification of cost auditor remuneration — were passed with overwhelming majority.
  • Investor engagement: A plant visit for analysts/institutional investors (Chrys Capital) was scheduled at the Raipur facilities on September 17, 2026, with discussions limited to publicly available documents and no UPSI to be shared.
