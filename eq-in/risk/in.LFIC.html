LFIC: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Loss-making quarter and year. For the quarter ended March 31, 2026, LFIC reported a loss from ordinary activities of Rs. (470.14) lakhs and a net loss of Rs. (277.14) lakhs, compared with a net loss of Rs. (263.77) lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2025. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the company reported a net loss of Rs. (71.46) lakhs, against a net profit of Rs. 357.98 lakhs in the year ended March 31, 2025.

Fair value losses on investments. The quarterly loss was driven by a net loss on fair value changes of Rs. 593.66 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 565.72 lakhs in the March 2025 quarter. For the full year, the net loss on fair value changes was Rs. 593.66 lakhs, against Rs. 279.01 lakhs in the prior year.

Lower revenue from operations. Total revenue from operations declined to Rs. 697.09 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026, from Rs. 769.36 lakhs in the prior year. Net gain on sale of financial instruments fell to Rs. 395.73 lakhs from Rs. 476.16 lakhs.

Rising employee costs. Employee benefits expense for the March 2026 quarter rose to Rs. 68.64 lakhs from Rs. 66.10 lakhs a year earlier, and the quarterly total expenses increased to Rs. 673.82 lakhs from Rs. 649.23 lakhs.

Unrecognised MAT credit. Minimum Alternative Tax credit amounting to Rs. 139.30 lakhs was not recognised as at March 31, 2026, in the absence of convincing evidence that the company will pay normal income tax in the near future. Management stated the situation will be reviewed at every year-end.

Tax expense despite losses. Current tax of Rs. 20.00 lakhs was provided in the March 2026 quarter and Rs. 78.00 lakhs for the full year, alongside earlier years’ tax of Rs. 1.39 lakhs.

Legal proceedings over land. The company has initiated legal proceedings before the District Court, Adoni (AP) to regain possession of lands costing Rs. 11.59 lakhs from trespassers.

XBRL filing error. The XBRL filing for the financial results submitted on May 28, 2026 contained an inadvertent typographical error, with an incorrect XML/XBRL file uploaded, resulting in discrepancies in reported figures. On June 27, 2026, the company requested permission from NSE to upload a rectified XBRL and submitted the machine-readable financial results.

Declining equity and cash. Other equity decreased to Rs. 5,423.39 lakhs as at March 31, 2026, from Rs. 5,584.98 lakhs a year earlier. Cash and cash equivalents fell to Rs. 180.53 lakhs from Rs. 226.03 lakhs. Net cash flow from operating activities was negative at Rs. (161.46) lakhs for the year, though improved from Rs. (328.96) lakhs in the prior year.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Dividend recommendation. The Board recommended an annual dividend of Rs. 2.50 per equity share (25%) of Rs. 10 each for FY 2025-26, payable after shareholder approval at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The Register of Members and Share Transfer Books will remain closed from September 23, 2026 to September 29, 2026 for the purpose of declaring the dividend and for the 102nd Annual General Meeting scheduled on September 29, 2026, with a record date of September 22, 2026.

Dividend income growth. Dividend income rose to Rs. 178.66 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026, from Rs. 170.83 lakhs in the prior year. In the March 2026 quarter, dividend income was Rs. 102.65 lakhs, up from Rs. 97.96 lakhs a year earlier.

Interest income growth. Interest income increased to Rs. 70.40 lakhs for the year from Rs. 69.55 lakhs, and to Rs. 17.40 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter from Rs. 17.32 lakhs.

Rental income stability. Rental income from investment property was Rs. 52.30 lakhs for the year, compared with Rs. 52.82 lakhs in the prior year, and rose to Rs. 13.70 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter from Rs. 12.65 lakhs.

Positive investing cash flows. Net cash flow from investing activities was Rs. 223.40 lakhs for the year, up from Rs. 216.29 lakhs in the prior year.

No borrowings. Outstanding qualified borrowings as on April 1, 2025 and March 31, 2026 were both NIL. No incremental qualified borrowing was done during 2025-26, and no debt securities were issued.

Subsequent quarter profitability. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the company reported total revenue of Rs. 613.16 lakhs and a net profit of Rs. 404.35 lakhs.

Governance and leadership changes. The Board appointed Dr. D. Nageswara Rao, Independent Director (DIN: 02009886), as non-executive Chairman. Smt. M. Madhavi Latha was appointed Chief Financial Officer (KMP) with effect from May 28, 2026. M/s. M. Bhaskara Rao & Co, Chartered Accountants, was appointed Internal Auditor for FY 2026-27.

Unmodified audit opinion. M/s. Brahmayya & Co., Statutory Auditors, issued an Audit Report with Unmodified Opinion on the Standalone Audited Financial Results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Market risk on portfolio investments. The company is engaged mainly in portfolio investments. Investments stood at Rs. 5,271.02 lakhs as at March 31, 2026, down from Rs. 5,437.13 lakhs. Fair value changes produced a net loss of Rs. 593.66 lakhs for the year, demonstrating sensitivity to market movements.
  • Concentration in financial activities. All activities relate to the main activity of portfolio investments, with no reportable segments as per Ind AS-108, concentrating risk in a single business line.
  • Tax position uncertainty. The non-recognition of MAT credit of Rs. 139.30 lakhs reflects uncertainty about future taxable profits.
  • Asset recovery risk. Legal proceedings are pending before the District Court, Adoni (AP) to regain possession of lands costing Rs. 11.59 lakhs from trespassers.
  • Regulatory filing risk. The incorrect XBRL upload for the financial results submitted on May 28, 2026 required a rectification request to NSE dated June 27, 2026.
  • Declining asset base. Total assets decreased to Rs. 5,981.79 lakhs as at March 31, 2026, from Rs. 6,143.19 lakhs a year earlier.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Dividend. The Board recommended a dividend of Rs. 2.50 per equity share (25%) for FY 2025-26, subject to shareholder approval at the AGM on September 29, 2026. This follows a year in which the company reported a net loss of Rs. (71.46) lakhs, though the subsequent June 2026 quarter showed a net profit of Rs. 404.35 lakhs.

MAT credit. Management stated that the situation regarding the unrecognised MAT credit of Rs. 139.30 lakhs will be reviewed at every year-end. No recognition was made as at March 31, 2026.

Auditor opinion. The statutory auditors issued an unmodified opinion on the standalone audited financial results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, and the Managing Director declared this under Regulation 33(3)(d) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

XBRL rectification. Management assured NSE that the revised XBRL filing will accurately reflect the financial results already submitted to the Exchange, and requested approval for correction and re-submission.

Quarterly figures basis. The figures for the fourth quarter of the current and previous financial years are balancing figures between audited full-year figures and published year-to-date figures up to the end of the third quarter.
