MEDICO Remedies Ltd. — Summary of Recent Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • External and operational risks flagged by management: unforeseen circumstances such as natural calamities, keeping pace with technology, easy availability of counterfeit drugs, a challenging US generics pricing environment, significant volatility in the forex market, interest rate fluctuations, high rates of inflation, intellectual property infringement and leakage, delay in government spending on infrastructure, and talent risk.
  • Cost pressure visible in results: cost of raw materials consumed rose to Rs. 4,719.53 Lakhs in Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026) from Rs. 2,870.91 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025, alongside a sharp increase in purchases of traded goods to Rs. 1,204.3 Lakhs (from Rs. 62.46 Lakhs a year earlier).
  • Revenue mix concentration: in Q1 FY2026-27, domestic sales were Rs. 2,633.62 Lakhs against export sales of Rs. 4,269.74 Lakhs, leaving the business significantly exposed to export-market and forex dynamics.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Order win: on 5 August 2026, the company announced a government supply order from PROMESE/CAL, Dominican Republic, valued at approximately USD 1,406,450 (about INR 13.38 Crores), for tablets, capsules and dry syrups, to be supplied in full within 3 months. PROMESE/CAL is the programme responsible for centralised purchase and distribution of medicines for the Dominican Republic’s National Public Health System.
  • Industry growth projections cited by management: global spending on medicines projected to grow at a CAGR of 3–6% to over US$ 3.8 Trillion by 2028; the Indian pharmaceutical industry expected to grow at a CAGR of 12.3% to reach USD 130 billion by 2030; CARE Ratings projects India’s pharma industry to grow about 11% over the next two years to over $60 billion.
  • Demand drivers cited: increasing healthcare awareness, an ageing population, and rising incidence of chronic ailments.
  • Policy support cited: government initiatives such as PLI schemes and RODTEP incentives.
  • Stated strategic actions: plans to increase manufacturing capacities, take over existing units, and expand the product range.

3. Key Risks

  • The risk list above (natural calamities, technology obsolescence, counterfeit drugs, US generics pricing, forex volatility, interest rate and inflation movements, IP infringement/leakage, delays in government infrastructure spending, and talent risk).
  • Governance and key-person concentration: the board on 13 August 2026 approved re-appointment of Mr. Rishit Mehta as Whole-Time Director and Mr. Haresh Mehta as Chairman & Whole-Time Director, both effective 18 September 2026 for three years, and an increase in remuneration payable to Mr. Harshit Mehta, Managing Director. The disclosures record that the directors are immediate relatives of one another (Mrs. Rita Mehta, Mr. Harshit Mehta, Mr. Haresh Mehta, Mr. Rishit Mehta), indicating a closely held leadership structure.
  • Single-segment, no-group structure: the company operates as a single operating segment with no subsidiary, associate or joint venture as on 30 June 2026.
  • No dividend: no dividend has been recommended for FY 2025-26.
  • Credit profile: Crisil BBB-/Stable for long-term bank facilities and Crisil A3 for short-term bank facilities.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance/forward-looking statements: the industry growth projections noted above (global medicines spending CAGR of 3–6% to over US$ 3.8 Trillion by 2028; Indian pharma CAGR of 12.3% to USD 130 billion by 2030; CARE Ratings’ ~11% growth to over $60 billion in two years), plus stated intentions to expand capacities, take over existing units, and broaden the product range. The PROMESE/CAL order carries a commitment to complete supply within 3 months.
  • Observed performance: for FY 2025-26, total income was Rs. 21,231 Lakhs, turnover Rs. 20,637 Lakhs, and profit after tax Rs. 1,311 Lakhs. In Q1 FY2026-27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), revenue from operations was Rs. 6,940.06 Lakhs, total income Rs. 7,056.92 Lakhs, and profit for the period Rs. 251.80 Lakhs, compared with revenue from operations of Rs. 3,825.18 Lakhs and profit of Rs. 151.83 Lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Sequentially, revenue from operations rose from Rs. 5,680.77 Lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026, while profit for the period declined from Rs. 585.66 Lakhs. Basic and diluted EPS for Q1 FY2026-27 was Rs. 0.30 (not annualised), against Rs. 0.22 a year earlier and Rs. 0.71 in the preceding quarter.
  • Other key facts: the Register of Members and Share Transfer Book will remain closed from Wednesday, 16 September 2026 to Tuesday, 22 September 2026 (both days inclusive) for the 32nd Annual General Meeting scheduled on Tuesday, 22 September 2026 at 4:00 P.M. The Q1 FY2026-27 standalone results were reviewed by the audit committee and approved by the board on 13 August 2026, carry an unmodified limited review report from Shah Shroff & Associates, and the review for the period 1 April 2025 to 30 June 2025 was performed by the previous auditor, M/s V J Shah & Co.
