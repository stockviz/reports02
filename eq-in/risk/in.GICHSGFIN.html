Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory penalty for KYC non-compliance: On July 03, 2026, the Reserve Bank of India imposed a monetary penalty of Rs. 3,10,000 on the company for non-compliance with certain provisions of RBI’s KYC directions. The penalty was imposed under Section 52A of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987. The violation related to “absence of periodic review of risk categorization of customer accounts” under Paragraph 12 of Chapter IV and 37(a) of Chapter VI of the RBI (KYC) Directions, 2016. The violation was observed during a statutory inspection by the National Housing Bank with reference to the company’s position as on March 31, 2025. The company stated it is in the process of implementing corrective measures to ensure compliance with applicable RBI directions.

  • Elevated impairment charge in Q1 FY2026-27: For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, impairment of financial instruments (including write-off) was Rs. 3,249 lakh, compared to a negative figure of Rs. (482) lakh in the preceding quarter ended March 31, 2026, and Rs. 7,797 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. This contributed to total expenses of Rs. 24,963 lakh against total income of Rs. 26,732 lakh, compressing profit before tax to Rs. 1,769 lakh.

  • Sequential decline in profit: Net profit for Q1 FY2026-27 was Rs. 1,004 lakh, down from Rs. 5,358 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, though higher than Rs. 735 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Earnings per share for the quarter was Rs. 1.86, compared to Rs. 9.95 in the preceding quarter and Rs. 1.36 in the year-ago quarter.

  • Unpredictable related party transactions: Secondary market transactions and dynamic market conditions make it impossible to predict whether any Promoter Group Company may acquire, hold, or dispose of the company’s listed NCDs, which can result in unpredictable related party transactions that may exceed materiality thresholds.
