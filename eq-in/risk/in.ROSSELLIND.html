Headwinds and Challenges

  • Wage cost increases. The company attributes lower quarterly profit to notified increases in wage rates: a 12% revision for daily-rated workers at the tea estates effective 1 April 2026, and arrears from a retrospective salary revision for sub-staff at the tea estates effective 1 June 2025.
  • Orthodox subsidy uncertainty. Due to uncertainty regarding receipt of the Orthodox Subsidy, it has not been recognised under Other Operating Revenue during the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
  • Seasonality. Cultivation, manufacture and sale of tea is seasonal, with cropping patterns varying each quarter; quarterly results are therefore not indicative of full-year results.
  • Leadership transition. Mr. Nirmal Kumar Khurana, Whole Time Director, CFO and Company Secretary, retires from service on 1 September 2026 after 32 years, ceasing to hold the CFO and Company Secretary/Compliance Officer positions. Mr. Raunak Rathi takes over as CFO and Mr. Manish Shaw as Company Secretary and Compliance Officer, both effective 1 September 2026. The Board also re-constituted the Stakeholders Relationship Committee and Corporate Social Responsibility Committee effective 26 August 2026 in view of Mr. Khurana’s cessation.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Experienced incoming CFO. Mr. Raunak Rathi is a qualified Chartered Accountant with over 28 years in the tea industry, having held senior roles at Asian Tea Group (COO), Apeejay Tea Limited (CFO), Skipper Limited (GM-MRD) and McLeod Russel India Limited (Deputy GM), with experience in strategic finance, treasury operations, mergers and acquisitions, budgeting and cost control, commercial operations, process controls, enterprise risk management, compliance and audit, performance analytics and MIS.
  • Experienced incoming Company Secretary. Mr. Manish Shaw is a member of ICSI with over 10 years of experience in corporate compliance, corporate restructuring, corporate governance, due diligence and secretarial management, including compliance with the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI regulations.
  • Dividend continuity. The 32nd AGM on 25 August 2026 passed a dividend of Rs. 0.40 per equity share, alongside adoption of audited financial statements, appointment/re-appointment of directors and ratification of cost auditors’ remuneration, all with the requisite majority through remote e-voting and e-voting at the AGM.

Key Risks

  • Earnings volatility from input and wage costs. The quarter’s profit decline is linked to wage revisions and arrears, indicating sensitivity of margins to labour cost changes.
  • Dependence on subsidy recognition. Non-recognition of the Orthodox Subsidy in the quarter introduces uncertainty over future other operating income.
  • Seasonal cropping pattern. Quarterly performance may not be representative of the full year.
  • Single-segment concentration. The company operates only one business segment—cultivation, manufacture and sale of tea—and has no subsidiary, associate or joint venture as on 30 June 2026, so consolidated results are not applicable.
  • Key personnel transition risk. The retirement of a 32-year veteran holding both CFO and Company Secretary roles necessitates transition to two new appointees effective 1 September 2026.
