Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The principal growth driver disclosed is a major capacity expansion across two divisions:

  • Denim (Fabric) division: Orders have been placed for an advanced complete set-up of machinery for the RFD (Ready for Dyeing) line for bottom-weight fabrics and chinos. This is expected to increase fabric manufacturing capacity by 10 million metres per year.
  • Spinning (Yarn) division: New spindles are being installed for coarse-count yarn, enhancing yarn production capacity by approximately 1,080 MT per year.

Management states these expansions are aimed at meeting evolving market demand, enhancing revenue and profitability growth, improving production capabilities and product range, strengthening operational efficiency and market position, and creating opportunities for higher and stable revenues with improved profitability.
