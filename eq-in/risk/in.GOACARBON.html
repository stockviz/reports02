GOA CARBON — Summary of Recent Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Plant shutdowns dominated the June 2026 quarter. Due to scheduled maintenance activities and to optimize operations, all three plants remained under shutdown during the quarter ended June 30, 2026: Goa Plant for 91 days, Bilaspur Plant for 91 days, and Paradeep Plant for 76 days. This is reflected in the sharp sequential decline in revenue from operations, from ₹20,112.18 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 to ₹6,569.75 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Return to losses. The company reported a net loss after tax of ₹658.36 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with a profit after tax of ₹449.31 lakh in the preceding quarter (March 31, 2026). The loss was narrower than the same quarter a year earlier (₹795.25 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2025). Basic and diluted loss per share for the quarter was ₹7.19, against earnings per share of ₹4.91 in the March 2026 quarter and a loss per share of ₹8.69 in the June 2025 quarter.

Full-year FY2025-26 was loss-making. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the company reported a net loss after tax of ₹4,823.46 lakh and a loss per share of ₹52.71, with a loss before tax of ₹4,211.61 lakh.

Finance costs remain elevated relative to the reduced scale of operations. Finance costs were ₹582.90 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, higher than ₹557.53 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹493.52 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.

Goa Green Cess dispute. Following the dismissal of the company’s writ petition by the Hon’ble High Court of Bombay at Goa on September 14, 2023, the stay on the applicability of Goa Green Cess was removed. On September 22, 2023, the company received Show Cause Notices for unpaid cess covering FY 2014-15 to FY 2022-23. The company filed a Special Leave Petition before the Hon’ble Supreme Court on November 11, 2023, challenging the constitutional validity of the levy; per the Supreme Court’s direction, it deposited 50% of the demand under protest while the remaining 50% was stayed. Notices for FY 2023-24 and FY 2024-25 were subsequently received. The company has deposited ₹349 lakh under protest from FY 2014-15 to FY 2024-25, representing 50% of the total demand raised for that period, and is voluntarily filing monthly returns and depositing 50% of self-assessed cess under protest. The company states it does not expect any material impact of this matter on the financial results.

Income-tax interest dispute (AY 2010-11). Statutory interest under Section 244A of the Income-tax Act for Assessment Year 2010-11 was initially refused, requiring the company to file a grievance and subsequently a Writ Petition (L) No. 485 of 2026 before the Hon’ble Bombay High Court, Goa Bench. The company is evaluating whether the interest granted in the Rectification Order has been correctly computed.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Rectification Order yields a further refund. A Rectification Order dated July 10, 2026 (received August 27, 2026) allowed interest under Section 244A of the Income-tax Act, determining a further refund of ₹1,20,44,555 payable to the company in respect of Assessment Year 2010-11.

Other income provided support in the June 2026 quarter. Other income (net) rose to ₹435.09 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with ₹149.33 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹171.84 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.

Cost of materials consumed fell sharply with the shutdowns. Cost of materials consumed was ₹4,656.65 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, down from ₹16,707.12 lakh in the March 2026 quarter and ₹22,639.23 lakh in the June 2025 quarter, alongside reductions in other expenses (₹877.48 lakh versus ₹1,282.24 lakh and ₹1,952.66 lakh respectively).

Other comprehensive income turned positive. Total other comprehensive income was ₹111.34 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, including ₹125.71 lakh from equity instruments through other comprehensive income, compared with a loss of ₹48.67 lakh in the March 2026 quarter.

AGM resolutions passed. The 58th Annual General Meeting was held on September 9, 2026 via video conferencing/other audio-visual means. All three ordinary resolutions — adopting the audited financial statements, re-appointing Mr. Shrinivas Dempo as Director, and ratifying the remuneration payable to M/s. Joshi Apte and Associates as Cost Accountants — were passed with overwhelming majorities, as detailed in the Scrutinizer’s Report dated September 10, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Operational concentration and shutdown exposure: With a single reportable segment (“Sale of Calcined Petroleum Coke”) and all three plants subject to extended maintenance shutdowns in the June 2026 quarter, revenue is highly sensitive to plant availability.
  • Sustained losses: The company recorded a full-year net loss of ₹4,823.46 lakh for FY2025-26 and a further quarterly net loss of ₹658.36 lakh in June 2026.
  • Finance cost burden: Finance costs have remained in the ₹490–590 lakh range per quarter across the periods presented, weighing on profitability at reduced operating volumes.
  • Contingent statutory liabilities: The Goa Green Cess matter remains before the Hon’ble Supreme Court, with ₹349 lakh deposited under protest and ongoing monthly deposits of 50% of self-assessed cess. The Income-tax interest matter for AY 2010-11 remains subject to the pending Writ Petition (L) No. 485 of 2026, with the company evaluating the correctness of the computed interest and whether additional interest under Section 244A is payable.
  • Inventory movements: Changes in inventories of finished goods were a positive ₹932.93 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a negative ₹4,483.53 lakh in the June 2025 quarter, indicating build-up of finished goods during the shutdown period.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Goa Green Cess: Management states it “does not expect any material impact of this matter on the financial results.” Observed position: the company has deposited ₹349 lakh under protest from FY 2014-15 to FY 2024-25 (50% of the total demand), continues to file monthly returns and deposit 50% of self-assessed cess under protest, and the matter remains pending before the Hon’ble Supreme Court.

Income-tax interest (AY 2010-11): Management provides no definitive forward guidance; it states the company is “presently evaluating the legal propriety of the Rectification Order dated 10.07.2026, including the correctness of the computed interest, whether additional interest under Section 244A is payable, and the appropriate course of action regarding the pending Writ Petition (L) No. 485 of 2026.” Observed position: a further refund of ₹1,20,44,555 has been determined in the company’s favour, but the matter is not concluded.

Auditor review: The statutory auditors’ limited review report on the June 30, 2026 results is unmodified, with nothing coming to their attention indicating material misstatement or non-disclosure under Regulation 33 of the Listing Regulations.

Segment reporting: The company has only one reportable segment, “Sale of Calcined Petroleum Coke,” under Ind AS 108, and has no subsidiary, associate, or joint venture as on June 30, 2026, so consolidated results are not required.
