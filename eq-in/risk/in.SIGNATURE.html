Headwinds and Challenges

Geopolitical disruption in Q1 FY27. Management described the first quarter as being marked by “macro headwinds” tied to the Middle East conflict, including daily negative news flow and currency devaluation concerns. The central government responded with an advisory directing state RERA authorities to grant a four-month extension for eligible projects impacted by the conflict.

Revenue recognition lag versus current pricing. Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue from operations was Rs 5,519.89 million, down 37% QoQ from Rs 11,072.66 million and 50% YoY from Rs 8,656.69 million. Management attributed this to project completion timelines: inventory completed in the quarter carried an average realization of under Rs 6,000 per sq ft, against current selling prices of roughly Rs 17,000 per sq ft. As management put it, “the P&L is not fully reflective of the current position.”

Collections below trend. Q1 FY27 collections were Rs 6.7 billion, which management called “a bit of an aberration” against a recent run-rate of roughly Rs 1,100 crore per quarter. The stated cause was certain lumpy collection milestones slipping into the following quarter.

Profitability swing. Consolidated Q1 FY27 recorded a loss after tax of Rs 165.29 million, compared with profit of Rs 344.35 million in Q1 FY26 and Rs 11,524.08 million in Q4 FY26 (the latter inflated by an exceptional gain of Rs 12,672.19 million from the RMZ joint venture transaction). Adjusted gross margin was 24% in Q1 FY27 versus 27% in Q4 FY26 and 28% in Q1 FY26; adjusted EBITDA margin was 6% versus 12% and 16% respectively. Standalone Q1 FY27 loss after tax was Rs 254.67 million.

Weak coverage and return ratios. On a standalone basis at 30 June 2026, DSCR was 0.13, ISCR 0.60, operating margin (11.68%) and net profit margin (11.34%). Consolidated DSCR was 0.05 and ISCR 0.52. Consolidated debt-equity was 1.56; standalone debt-equity was 3.47.

Cost inflation. Management noted that construction costs have historically risen in late single digits, with roughly 7–8% blended escalation (materials plus labour) over the past decade, and that this should be budgeted going forward. No inordinate spike was observed in the quarter.

Weather disruption. Heavy rains in the days around the call were noted, though management said working days were largely unaffected.

Statutory auditor changes at material subsidiaries. Walker Chandiok & Co LLP resigned as statutory auditor of three material subsidiaries — Sternal Buildcon Limited, Signature Infrabuild Limited and Signatureglobal Homes Limited — via letters dated 12 August 2026, disclosed to exchanges on 13 August 2026. The stated reason was alignment with the auditors of the holding company for consistency across the group. The auditor confirmed no concerns, no management-imposed limitations, and no other material reason. Notably, the resignations occurred shortly after the firm issued audit reports dated 11 May 2026 on FY26 financial statements and limited review reports dated 4 August 2026 on the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and well before the expiry of the respective terms (2027 for Sternal Buildcon; 2030 for Signature Infrabuild and Signatureglobal Homes).

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Supportive market backdrop. Management cited a Cushman & Wakefield report noting 18,470 residential units launched in Delhi NCR in H1 2026, a 10% YoY increase, with Gurugram accounting for 74% of luxury housing launches. Average launch prices in Delhi NCR rose 24% YoY in Q2 2026. Management also cited the approved Phase 2 metro development as a positive for Gurugram.

Structural supply constraint. Management argued that Gurgaon saw very little supply creation between 2014 and 2022 at industry level, creating a vacuum, and that demand for new homes significantly exceeds supply — supporting both pricing power and the strategy of consistent launches.

Branded residences entry. In Q1 FY27 the company launched Tonino Lamborghini Residences on Southern Peripheral Road, Sector 71, Gurugram — over 12 acres, more than 2 million sq ft of super built-up area, priced above Rs 22,000 per sq ft (the highest the company has achieved), with GDV in excess of Rs 4,000 crore (about Rs 44 billion). Of roughly 800 units, about 400 were opened in Phase 1 and more than 300 were sold. Management said sales from this launch crossed Rs 1,500 crore.

Pre-sales momentum. Q1 FY27 pre-sales were Rs 19.7 billion, up 25% QoQ. Average sales realization rose to Rs 17,093 per sq ft from Rs 15,250 per sq ft in FY26, attributed primarily to the Tonino Lamborghini launch.

Launch pipeline. Management reaffirmed guidance of Rs 150 billion of new launches for the year, with launches planned around Diwali (Q3) and in Q4, largely in Sector 71/SPR. Since February 2024, the company has launched approximately 23 million sq ft with GDV of Rs 334 billion.

Delivery and completion pipeline. 19.2 million sq ft delivered to date. A further 9.2 million sq ft is in ongoing construction (5.8 million under construction, 3.4 million with OC received), with average completion timelines of 2–3 quarters. Management expects to complete projects worth over Rs 5,000 crore and recognize revenue of about Rs 50 billion during the year.

Forthcoming portfolio. 17.8 million sq ft forthcoming, including 5 million sq ft of commercial development in tie-up with the RMZ group (announced March 2026), plus 12 million sq ft to be launched this year and next.

Balance sheet strength. Cash and bank balances (including FDs) of Rs 25.22 billion at 30 June 2026; net debt of Rs 3.9 billion. Management described net debt levels as very low, though it may rise modestly as business development proceeds.

Business development ambitions. Management is actively evaluating land additions in core markets and outside Delhi NCR, with land capex potentially in the range of Rs 1,500–1,800 crore for the year. The preferred format outside NCR is large-format, low-rise, mid-income development (100–150 acres) to establish relevance and demonstrate delivery capability in new markets.

Commercial joint venture. The RMZ transaction consummated on 30 March 2026: the company transferred 3,569,731 GCL equity shares to RMZ for Rs 567.03 million, and GCL allotted 77,860,538 fresh shares at Rs 158.84 each to RMZ for Rs 12,367.71 million, diluting the company to 50%. GCL was deconsolidated and reclassified as a joint venture, generating an exceptional gain of Rs 12,672.19 million in Q4 FY26 (including a Rs 12,139.99 million remeasurement gain and Rs 532.20 million realised gain), with a related deferred tax liability of Rs 1,736.02 million.

Financing. 87,500 listed, secured, redeemable NCDs of Rs 100,000 face value each (Rs 8,750 million aggregate) were issued to IFC at 11% per annum, listed on BSE on 17 October 2025, redeemable in twelve equal quarterly instalments from 15 April 2026 to 15 January 2029. Security cover requirement is 1.50x; the registered valuer assessed the Sohna project land at Rs 36,155.00 million as at 30 June 2026 (Rs 36,595.00 million as at 31 March 2026). Credit rating is CARE A+ (Stable).

Governance and board continuity. The board approved re-appointment of three independent directors for second five-year terms: Mr. Chandra Wadhwa (from 15 February 2027), Ms. Lata Pillai and Mr. Venkatesan Narayanan (both from 15 March 2027), subject to member approval. M/s. Goyal, Goyal & Associates were appointed cost auditor for FY 2026-27.

Key Risks

  • Execution and timing risk. Revenue recognition, collections and profitability depend on project completion milestones; slippage directly affects reported results, as seen in Q1 FY27.
  • Geopolitical and macroeconomic risk. The Middle East conflict and related currency concerns weighed on sentiment in Q1 FY27.
  • Cost inflation risk. Historical 7–8% cost escalation must be absorbed into project budgets.
  • Concentration risk. A good portion of FY27 launches is planned in Sector 71/SPR, Gurugram; management acknowledged the question of bringing substantial inventory into a single micro-market.
  • Business development risk. Expansion outside Delhi NCR into new markets carries execution and brand-recognition risk, which management intends to mitigate via low-rise formats.
  • Leverage trajectory. Net debt could rise as business development proceeds, though management expects no significant increase.
  • Coverage metrics. Low DSCR and ISCR readings at 30 June 2026 (standalone DSCR 0.13, ISCR 0.60) indicate limited near-term debt-service coverage from EBITDA.
  • Forward-looking statement risk. The company’s own disclosure notes risks including cash flow projections, industry conditions, competition, regulatory approvals, growth management, interest rate and currency fluctuations, political/economic/legal conditions, raw material availability, and market acceptance.
  • Auditor transition risk at material subsidiaries. Three material subsidiaries changed statutory auditors mid-term in August 2026, requiring transition and continuity of audit processes across the group.

Broker Narrative

The narrative evolved from early FY25 confidence in strong pre-sales, debt reduction, and expansion into new NCR micro-markets to a FY27 reality of declining presales/collections, operating losses, and rising net debt. The reliance on fresh launches and concentration in Gurgaon/NCR remained persistent concerns, while diversification was still only ‘being evaluated.’ Despite the deterioration, both reports stayed Buy-rated, with the latest leaning on the Tonino Lamborghini launch and a large pipeline for recovery.

Fears that came true

  • The flagged launch-dependent pre-sales risk materialized as 1QFY27 presales declined 25% YoY and collections hit the lowest level since 2QFY24.
  • The Gurgaon/NCR concentration risk persisted, with the last report still flagging concentrated NCR exposure and diversification only under evaluation, coinciding with the -36.3% disappointment from the first call.

Optimism that failed

  • The strong operational performance narrative failed as 1QFY27 revenue fell 36% YoY and the company posted an EBITDA loss of INR445m and a PAT loss of INR165m.
  • The continued debt-reduction goal reversed, with net debt increasing QoQ to INR3.9b and guided to rise further in FY27.
  • The planned expansion into new Delhi/NCR micro-markets did not materialize, as the last report said diversification was still being evaluated.
