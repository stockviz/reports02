Broker Narrative

The narrative evolved from a confident recovery story—cheaper gas, margin rebound, and volume-driven rerating—to a defensive cost/margin story centred on price hikes, APM protection, and a lower growth CAGR. Gas costs and margins remained the dominant themes throughout, while early optimism about volume growth and multiple rerating faded.

Fears that came true

  • Higher gas costs persisted: gas cost/scm rose to INR36.8/scm by Q4FY26, keeping input costs ~25% above pre-war levels and weighing on EBITDA/scm.
  • Margin pressure materialised: Q4FY26 EBITDA/scm declined to INR4.8/scm, far below the Rs7.5–8.0/scm recovery expected for FY24E.
  • Rising opex materialised: opex/scm increased 25.8% YoY and 7.5% QoQ in Q4FY26.
  • EV/bus fleet transition hurt CNG volumes: Delhi CNG volumes were broadly flat due to DTC-related volume loss.

Optimism that failed

  • Margin recovery from lower gas costs failed to materialise: Q4FY26 EBITDA/scm was Rs4.8/scm, well below the Rs7.5–8.0/scm FY24E target.
  • The strong volume growth outlook faded: FY25–FY28E volume CAGR was cut to 5.1% from 6.1%, and Delhi CNG volumes were flat.
  • The expected P/E rerating to 18x FY25E did not happen; most subsequent Buy calls ended in DISAPPOINTMENT with negative actual returns.
