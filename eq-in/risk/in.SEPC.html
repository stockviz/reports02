SEPC: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

TCIL banning order (August 2026). On August 7, 2026, SEPC disclosed that it had received a banning order from Telecommunications Consultants India Limited (TCIL), conveyed via TCIL letter reference TCIL/DT/DCCS/PSPCL/2026/8 dated August 5, 2026. This follows earlier intimations dated February 7, 2026 and March 3, 2026 concerning a Letter of Intent from TCIL for the Smart Prepaid Metering Project in Punjab and its subsequent cancellation. SEPC states it strongly contests the order, rejects all contentions and allegations, has challenged it before the appropriate authorities, and is pursuing legal remedies seeking an immediate stay and quashing.

Disclosure and filing lapses. In a July 11, 2026 response to a stock exchange query, SEPC addressed two issues: the timing of its financial results disclosure and discrepancies in its consolidated XBRL filing. The Board meeting of May 25, 2026 concluded at 3:30 PM, and the company applied the three-hour disclosure timeline on the basis that the meeting ended after trading hours; results were submitted at 4:43 PM, 73 minutes after closure. Separately, the company acknowledged an EPS mismatch in its initial XBRL filing, describing it as an inadvertent clerical error during data entry, and resubmitted the XBRL with the corrected figure to align with the PDF disclosure. The company stated it has strengthened internal review processes to prevent recurrence.

Audit qualifications. The statutory auditors issued qualified opinions regarding Deferred Tax Assets and overdue non-current contract assets and trade receivables.

Sector and operating risks. The company identifies project execution and cost risks; working capital and counterparty risks including delayed customer payments and extended receivable cycles; intense competition in the EPC industry; regulatory and policy risks; macroeconomic and commodity price volatility (interest rates, foreign exchange rates, and prices of materials such as steel and cement); project cancellations; and contractual matters.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Major domestic order win. On August 5, 2026, SEPC disclosed receipt of a Letter of Acceptance from Steel Authority of India Limited – IISCO Steel Plant (ISP), Burnpur, for “Pellet Plant BOP including Civil & Structural (Pellet Package-2) for 4.08 Mtpa CS Expansion of SAIL-ISP Burnpur,” dated August 4, 2026. The total contract value is ₹854,56,71,234 (net of input tax credit), with execution over 32 months from the effective date of contract (the date of signing or 30 days from the LoA, whichever is earlier). The order was awarded through a domestic tender (Tender No. PROJECTS/EXPN-C/PP/BOP/1220 dated October 10, 2025, including corrigendum dated November 25, 2025).

Proposed UAE acquisition. On July 8, 2026, SEPC announced Board-approved proposals, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals, to acquire up to 90% of Avenir International Engineers and Consultants LLC, Abu Dhabi, through a share swap effected by preferential allotment of 153 crore equity shares at ₹10 per share, aggregating ₹1,530 crore, to Avenir’s shareholders. The transaction involves no cash outflow and is expected to conclude by December 2026. Avenir, established in 2011, is an engineering and design company serving the oil & gas and civil infrastructure sectors in the UAE, holds established qualifications with ADNOC, and reported turnover of approximately AED 75.01 million during 2025. The acquisition is intended to strengthen SEPC’s oil & gas capabilities and Middle East presence.

Capital and borrowing capacity expansion. The Board approved increasing authorised share capital from ₹2,250 crore (225 crore equity shares of ₹10 each) to ₹6,000 crore (600 crore equity shares of ₹10 each), and increasing borrowing limits to ₹7,500 crore. Shareholders’ approval is being sought via postal ballot with remote e-voting, scrutinised by Alagar & Associates LLP.

UAE petroleum trading entry. SEPC received in-principle approval to enter UAE petroleum trading through 100% acquisition of Wintality Petroleum FZE, via non-cash consideration by restructuring the equity and capitalising reserves of its wholly owned UAE subsidiary, SEPC FZE, Sharjah. Wintality Petroleum FZE, a UAE entity specialising in refined petroleum products, will become a step-down subsidiary. SEPC will retain a 95.75% post-issue stake in SEPC FZE. The transaction does not attract Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013 or Regulation 23 of SEBI LODR. The company stated it will keep exchanges updated on material developments, final valuations, and definitive agreements until completion.

Order book and demand environment. The company cites a substantial consolidated order book of approximately ₹10,000 crore. Management views the opportunity landscape as encouraging, citing India’s focus on infrastructure development, water management, industrial capacity expansion, steel and metals, mining, urban infrastructure, and energy transition. Growth prospects cited include government infrastructure spending (water, wastewater, roads, urban infrastructure, railways), industrial and private capital expenditure (steel, mining, manufacturing capacity expansion and modernisation), water and environmental infrastructure, energy transition and green infrastructure, and international expansion in the GCC region.

Governance addition. Ms. K B K Vasuki was appointed as Additional Director (Non-Executive, Independent) for a five-year term starting August 25, 2026, subject to shareholder approval at the AGM proposed for Monday, September 28, 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Legal and contractual risk: The TCIL banning order and the underlying cancelled Punjab smart metering LoI, with SEPC contesting the order and seeking a stay and quashing; outcome remains pending.
  • Execution and cost risk: Project execution and cost overruns, particularly on large contracts such as the 32-month SAIL Burnpur pellet plant package.
  • Working capital and counterparty risk: Delayed customer payments and extended receivable cycles; auditors’ qualified opinion on overdue non-current contract assets and trade receivables.
  • Tax-related risk: Auditors’ qualified opinion on Deferred Tax Assets.
  • Competitive risk: Intense competition in the EPC industry.
  • Regulatory and policy risk: Changes in government actions and policy.
  • Macroeconomic and commodity risk: Fluctuations in interest rates, foreign exchange rates, and prices of steel and cement.
  • Project cancellation risk: Demonstrated by the TCIL Punjab LoI cancellation.
  • Disclosure and compliance risk: The XBRL EPS discrepancy and the timing-of-disclosure query, both of which the company has addressed with corrective actions and process strengthening.
  • Transaction completion risk: The Avenir acquisition and Wintality Petroleum FZE acquisition remain subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals, final valuations, and definitive agreements.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance. Management states the opportunity landscape remains encouraging due to India’s focus on infrastructure development, water management, industrial capacity expansion, steel and metals, mining, urban infrastructure, and energy transition. The stated strategy centres on selective growth, disciplined bidding, efficient execution, prudent working-capital management, and strengthening the balance sheet. Management describes the Avenir acquisition as intended to complement engineering capabilities and provide access to the UAE oil & gas ecosystem, and the capital and borrowing enhancements as designed to support evolving business requirements. The company commits to keeping exchanges updated on material developments, final valuations, and definitive agreements regarding the Wintality transaction until completion.

Observed performance. For FY26, the company reported Total Income of ₹1,085.8 crore, EBITDA of ₹108.9 crore, and Net Profit of ₹53.5 crore, against Total Income of ₹646.0 crore in FY25, with Net Profit more than doubling over the previous year. Consolidated revenue from operations was ₹1,054.50 crore and profit after tax was ₹53.84 crore for FY 2025-26. The company completed a ₹350 crore Rights Issue, and shareholders approved an enhanced borrowing limit of ₹7,500 crore. The SAIL Burnpur LoA (₹854.57 crore) provides visible order inflow consistent with the stated focus on industrial and steel sector opportunities. Against the stated emphasis on prudent working-capital management, the auditors’ qualified opinion on overdue non-current contract assets and trade receivables indicates receivables stress. The TCIL banning order and the earlier Punjab LoI cancellation represent execution and counterparty challenges relative to the stated selective growth and disciplined bidding approach.
