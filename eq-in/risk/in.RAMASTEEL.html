RAMASTEEL: Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue contraction across periods. Standalone revenue from operations declined to ₹17,407.87 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹18,849.26 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and from ₹24,266.56 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Consolidated revenue followed the same trajectory, falling to ₹22,333.64 lakh from ₹24,615.27 lakh quarter-on-quarter and from ₹26,813.45 lakh year-on-year.

Sharp year-on-year profit compression. Standalone profit before tax fell to ₹227.79 lakh in Q1 FY27 from ₹848.60 lakh in Q1 FY26. Consolidated profit before tax declined to ₹380.43 lakh from ₹719.26 lakh over the same comparison. Standalone profit after tax for the quarter was ₹419.52 lakh, against ₹644.57 lakh a year earlier.

Weak trading segment performance. In the standalone segment results, trading of steel pipe and steel products contributed only ₹2.89 lakh to segment results in Q1 FY27, down from ₹10.47 lakh in Q1 FY26. On a consolidated basis, the trading segment recorded a segment loss of ₹64.08 lakh in Q1 FY26 and a loss of ₹109.19 lakh in Q4 FY26, before turning to a ₹74.05 lakh profit in Q1 FY27.

Manufacturing segment profitability remains thin relative to scale. Standalone manufacturing segment results were ₹246.32 lakh in Q1 FY27 against segment revenue of ₹10,141.75 lakh. In Q1 FY26, the manufacturing segment recorded a loss of ₹78.22 lakh.

Finance costs and depreciation. Standalone finance cost was ₹165.33 lakh in Q1 FY27, and depreciation and amortisation was ₹121.60 lakh. Consolidated finance cost was ₹170.30 lakh, with depreciation and amortisation of ₹162.86 lakh.

New Labour Codes evaluation pending. The Government of India consolidated 29 existing labour legislations into four Labour Codes on November 21, 2025. The company states it is already compliant with basic wages criteria for its own employees, with no material impact on the group, but is still evaluating possible impacts for its contract workforce. The company notes that once Central/State Rules are notified, it will evaluate any impact on measurement of employee benefits and provide appropriate accounting effect as needed.

Foreign subsidiary losses. The step-down foreign subsidiary, RST Industries Limited, recorded total revenue including other income of ₹NIL and a net loss after tax (including OCI) of ₹15.62 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with net assets of ₹31.35 lakh.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Sequential improvement in standalone profitability. Standalone profit before tax rose to ₹227.79 lakh in Q1 FY27 from ₹203.23 lakh in Q4 FY26, and standalone profit after tax rose to ₹419.52 lakh from ₹147.67 lakh over the same comparison. Total comprehensive income increased to ₹421.14 lakh from ₹163.49 lakh.

Consolidated profit growth. Consolidated profit for the period rose to ₹544.50 lakh in Q1 FY27 from ₹379.46 lakh in Q4 FY26. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was ₹511.40 lakh, up from ₹428.23 lakh sequentially.

Manufacturing segment recovery. Standalone manufacturing segment results improved to ₹246.32 lakh in Q1 FY27 from ₹0.07 lakh in Q4 FY26 and from a loss of ₹78.22 lakh in Q1 FY26. On a consolidated basis, manufacturing segment results were ₹372.85 lakh in Q1 FY27, compared with ₹73.83 lakh in Q1 FY26.

Trading segment return to profit on consolidated basis. The consolidated trading segment moved from a loss of ₹109.19 lakh in Q4 FY26 to a profit of ₹74.05 lakh in Q1 FY27.

Disposal of non-core unit. On July 17, 2026, the Board approved the sale of a unit (land and shed structure only) at B-5, Site IV, Sahibabad Industrial Area, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, for an aggregate consideration of INR 26,75,00,000 (Indian Rupees Twenty Six Crore Seventy Five Lakh). The buyer is Paras Buildwell LLP, which does not belong to the promoter/promoter group/group companies. The unit is described as a non-core business unit that did not contribute any turnover/revenue/income during the last financial year and has no net worth. The transaction is not a related party transaction and is not part of any Scheme of Arrangement.

Expanded group structure. The consolidated results include wholly owned subsidiaries RST International Trading FZE (foreign), Lepakshi Tubes Private Limited (Indian), and Rama Defence Private Limited (Indian); a subsidiary Ashoka Infrasteel (partnership firm, 51% held); step-down subsidiary RST Industries Limited (Nigeria, 51% foreign subsidiary of RST International Trading FZE); and associate Oram Green Energy Limited (40% held).

Subsidiary contribution. Four subsidiaries (including one domestic subsidiary with 51% stake) reflected total assets of ₹7,582.99 lakh and net assets of ₹6,169.91 lakh as at June 30, 2026, with total revenue including other income of ₹4,925.76 lakh and total net profit after tax (including OCI) of ₹160.37 lakh for the quarter.

Governance additions. The Board approved the appointment of Mrs. Anju Kumari (DIN: 10729753) as Additional Non-Executive Independent Woman Director for a term of five years effective from October 01, 2026, subject to shareholder approval. She is a practicing Advocate before the Supreme Court of India and the Delhi High Court with over 10 years of experience. The Board also appointed M/s Dhawan & Co., Cost Accountants, as Cost Auditor for the financial year 2026-27.

3. Key Risks

Revenue and profitability volatility. The company’s results show significant quarter-on-quarter and year-on-year fluctuations in revenue and profitability, particularly in the trading segment, which swung between losses and profits across recent quarters.

Dependence on manufacturing segment. The manufacturing segment carries the bulk of revenue and profitability, while the trading segment has shown inconsistent results. Standalone manufacturing revenue was ₹10,141.75 lakh in Q1 FY27, while trading revenue was ₹7,266.12 lakh, but trading contributed only ₹2.89 lakh to segment results.

Foreign operations exposure. The group includes foreign subsidiaries in Dubai (RST International Trading FZE) and Nigeria (RST Industries Limited). Exchange differences on translation of foreign operations were negative ₹14.71 lakh in Q1 FY27, compared with positive ₹368.18 lakh in Q4 FY26 and positive ₹25.86 lakh in Q1 FY26. The Nigeria step-down subsidiary recorded no revenue and a net loss for the quarter.

Regulatory transition risk. The New Labour Codes, consolidated on November 21, 2025, introduce a unified framework. While the company states there is no material impact for its own employees, the impact on contract workforce remains under evaluation, and the company notes it will provide appropriate accounting effect once Central/State Rules are notified.

Tax expense volatility. Standalone tax expense was negative ₹191.73 lakh in Q1 FY27, reflecting deferred tax credit and income tax of earlier years, compared with positive ₹55.56 lakh in Q4 FY26 and positive ₹204.04 lakh in Q1 FY26. Consolidated tax expense was negative ₹164.07 lakh in Q1 FY27.

Non-controlling interest volatility. Non-controlling interest in consolidated results was positive ₹33.10 lakh in Q1 FY27, compared with negative ₹48.76 lakh in Q4 FY26 and negative ₹36.85 lakh in Q1 FY26.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Labour Codes. Management states the company is already in compliance with basic wages criteria under the New Labour Codes for its own employees, with no material impact on the group. Management is evaluating possible impacts for contract workforce and is of the view that impact, if any, is unlikely to be material. Management states it will evaluate impact on measurement of employee benefits and provide appropriate accounting effect once Central/State Rules are notified. No financial impact has been quantified or recorded in the current results.

Sale of non-core unit. Management states the unit proposed for disposal is a non-core business unit that did not contribute any turnover/revenue/income during the last financial year and has no net worth. The agreement to sell was executed on July 17, 2026, and the transaction is proposed to be completed upon fulfilment of conditions stipulated in the agreement. The consideration is INR 26,75,00,000. No completion date is specified.

Auditor review. The statutory auditor, Rawat & Associates, issued an unqualified limited review report on both the standalone and consolidated unaudited financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The auditor notes that the consolidated results include unaudited financial information of four subsidiaries reviewed by their respective independent auditors, one step-down foreign subsidiary whose financial information is unaudited and furnished by management, and one associate reviewed by the auditor. The auditor states these are not material to the group.

Segment reporting. The company states it is carrying out manufacturing of steel tubes and pipes and trading of building material and steel related products, while its wholly owned subsidiary in Dubai (RST International Trading FZE) and step-down subsidiary in Nigeria (RST Industries Limited) are pursuing trading of steel products and non-ferrous metal products, and subsidiary Ashoka Infrasteel is also dealing in trading of steel related products.

AGM and governance. The 52nd Annual General Meeting is convened for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 12:30 P.M. (IST) via Video Conferencing/Other Audio Visual Means. Key business items include adoption of audited financial statements, re-appointment of director Mr. Rakesh Chaturvedi, appointment of Ms. Anju Kumari as Non-Executive Independent Woman Director for a five-year term, and ratification of remuneration of M/s Dhawan & Co. as Cost Auditors for the financial year ending March 31, 2027.
