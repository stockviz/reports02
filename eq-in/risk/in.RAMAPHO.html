RAMAPHO: Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

The Soya/Agri segment is the clearest drag on performance. In the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the segment reported a loss at the result level of ₹(28.18) lakhs, compared with a profit of ₹268.23 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and a loss of ₹(27.85) lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Segment revenue for Soya/Agri fell sharply to ₹356.88 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹1,410.76 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹1,391.05 lakhs a year earlier. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the segment contributed only ₹260.39 lakhs at the result level on revenue of ₹4,802.05 lakhs.

Finance costs rose to ₹405.23 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹191.60 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹351.79 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, even as total borrowings-related capital employed expanded. Total expenses in the June 2026 quarter were ₹20,273.61 lakhs, up from ₹16,948.42 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter, with cost of materials consumed rising to ₹17,135.55 lakhs from ₹12,935.62 lakhs.

The quarter ended March 31, 2026 was markedly weaker than the surrounding periods: revenue from operations was ₹21,907.50 lakhs, profit before tax was ₹684.50 lakhs, and profit for the period was ₹536.67 lakhs, with basic and diluted EPS of ₹1.52, against ₹2,281.23 lakhs PBT and ₹4.82 EPS in the June 2026 quarter and ₹2,146.32 lakhs PBT and ₹4.53 EPS in the June 2025 quarter.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The Fertilizers, Micro Nutrients & Chemicals segment is the principal growth engine. Segment revenue rose to ₹22,122.71 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter from ₹20,496.74 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹17,640.30 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter. Segment result (profit after depreciation but before interest and taxation) was ₹2,867.46 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, versus ₹846.78 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter and ₹2,753.67 lakhs a year earlier. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, the segment delivered ₹8,719.63 lakhs at the result level on revenue of ₹84,502.29 lakhs.

The company has received Environmental Clearance from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change for a new Greenfield project at Dhule covering 216,000 MTPA Single Super Phosphate (SSP) fertilizer and 90,000 MTPA Sulphuric Acid, along with consent to establish various micro nutrients and PDM/PROM/bio-fertiliser facilities. Phase-I on Plot-2, for SSP fertilizer manufacturing, is at the final stage of completion, with trial production expected to commence by the end of the quarter ending September 2026. Setup of the MgSO4 and PDM/PROM manufacturing plant on Plot-1 has also commenced.

At the Udaipur unit, the company is expanding the SSP plant by 65,000 MTPA, which will raise the unit’s SSP capacity to 315,000 MTPA and overall company SSP capacity to 979,000 MTPA. New Greenfield granulation, PDM, and PROM plants with aggregate capacity of 49,000 MTPA are also being set up at Udaipur.

The company holds a long-term credit rating of “A-” from ICRA.

3. Key Risks

Segment concentration is significant: the Fertilizers, Micro Nutrients & Chemicals segment accounted for ₹22,122.71 lakhs of ₹22,479.59 lakhs in June 2026 quarter revenue, and its segment assets were ₹72,746.19 lakhs of ₹77,369.92 lakhs total segment assets. Segment liabilities for the same segment were ₹29,287.79 lakhs of ₹33,421.89 lakhs total.

Working capital intensity is visible in the balance sheet movement: total segment assets rose to ₹77,369.92 lakhs as of June 30, 2026 from ₹71,922.11 lakhs as of March 31, 2026 and ₹64,502.48 lakhs as of June 30, 2025, while total capital employed rose to ₹43,948.03 lakhs from ₹42,246.72 lakhs and ₹38,715.16 lakhs respectively. Finance costs increased in step.

The Soya/Agri segment’s recurring losses at the result level in the June 2026 and June 2025 quarters, and its sharply reduced revenue, indicate volatility in that business.

Execution risk attaches to the Dhule Greenfield project and the Udaipur expansions, where trial production at Dhule is guided for the end of the September 2026 quarter.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management’s stated guidance in the June 2026 results notes is that trial production at the Dhule Phase-I SSP facility is expected to commence by the end of the current quarter (September 2026). The results also disclose that the Phase-I SSP facility is at the final stage of completion and that the MgSO4 and PDM/PROM plant setup on Plot-1 has commenced. No prior-period guidance on Dhule timelines is available in the supplied material for comparison, so the guidance can only be tracked against the disclosed project status.

On observed performance, the June 2026 quarter showed sequential improvement over the March 2026 quarter across key metrics: revenue from operations rose to ₹22,479.59 lakhs from ₹21,907.50 lakhs, profit before tax rose to ₹2,281.23 lakhs from ₹684.50 lakhs, profit for the period rose to ₹1,706.44 lakhs from ₹536.67 lakhs, and basic and diluted EPS rose to ₹4.82 from ₹1.52. Year-on-year, June 2026 revenue of ₹22,479.59 lakhs exceeded June 2025 revenue of ₹19,031.35 lakhs, and profit for the period of ₹1,706.44 lakhs exceeded ₹1,603.32 lakhs, though total expenses also rose to ₹20,273.61 lakhs from ₹16,948.42 lakhs.

For the full year ended March 31, 2026, revenue from operations was ₹89,304.34 lakhs, profit before tax was ₹7,030.58 lakhs, profit for the period was ₹5,270.89 lakhs, and basic and diluted EPS was ₹14.90.

At the 41st Annual General Meeting held on August 13, 2026, shareholders approved the audited financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, a final dividend of Re. 0.25 per equity share of face value ₹5 each for FY2025-26, the re-appointment of Mrs. Nilanjana Ramsinghani as a director retiring by rotation, the appointment of M/s Dayal & Lohia, Chartered Accountants (Registration Number 102200W) as Statutory Auditors, and ratification of Cost Auditor remuneration for the year ending March 31, 2027. All resolutions passed with the requisite majority; the dividend resolution recorded 99.9997% in favour and 0.0003% against, and the remaining resolutions recorded 100.0000% in favour.
