Archies Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

External environment. Tariff measures introduced by the Trump administration are significantly impacting international trade and export orders. The ongoing conflict in the Middle East and broader geopolitical uncertainties add further pressure. Domestically, rising prices of essential items are adversely affecting consumer sentiment and sales, while stress in the realty sector, high rental costs, and shrinking store size weigh on the retail footprint. Competition in the gifting segment is intensifying, and macroeconomic factors such as economic downturns and exchange rate fluctuations remain live concerns.

Financial performance. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, revenue from operations fell to Rs. 790.79 lakhs from Rs. 1,374.65 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. Total income declined to Rs. 821.19 lakhs from Rs. 1,798.70 lakhs. The company recorded a loss before tax of Rs. (196.21) lakhs and a loss for the period of Rs. (138.25) lakhs, compared with a profit of Rs. 30.01 lakhs in the same quarter a year earlier. For the year ended 31 March 2026, revenue from operations was Rs. 5,609.70 lakhs (down from Rs. 6,970.54 lakhs) and net loss after tax was Rs. (429.70) lakhs.

Segment performance. In the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the Stationery and Paper Bag segment posted a segment loss of Rs. (59.86) lakhs and Gifts Rs. (8.93) lakhs, while Greeting Cards contributed a profit of Rs. 28.12 lakhs and Others Rs. 2.14 lakhs. Total segment result before tax and finance costs was Rs. (38.53) lakhs.

Auditor observations. The Limited Review Report notes that the auditors were unable to comment whether the value of inventory is at cost or net realisable value, whichever is less, and that the book value of inventory is relied upon. It also notes that the company has not deposited Employees Provident Fund and Tax Deducted at Source for the period from April 2026 to June 2026. The auditors state that their opinion is not modified in respect of these matters.

Dividend. No dividend has been declared for the financial year 2025-26 due to losses and the need to conserve resources.

Regulatory/compliance matter. The company submitted its financial results for the quarter ended 30 September 2025 within due time on 14 November 2025, but the exchange raised a query that the XBRL submission contained discrepancies, with quarterly figures not provided. The company clarified that it had inadvertently selected the wrong reporting type as “Half Yearly” instead of “Quarterly” in XBRL format, and requested permission to resubmit, stating it would be more careful going forward (letter dated 23 January 2026, referencing an NSE email dated 19 January 2026).

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Expansion into the e-commerce and digital space through Archies Global and Archies Online.
  • Recent improvements in the export business, with new export orders.
  • Exploration of new markets.
  • Deeper penetration through physical stores across malls and high streets.
  • Tie-ups with quick commerce platforms allowing product delivery within 10 minutes.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued impact of tariff measures on international trade and export orders.
  • Geopolitical uncertainty, including the ongoing Middle East conflict.
  • Consumer sentiment and sales pressure from rising prices of essential items.
  • Realty sector stress, high rental costs, and shrinking store size.
  • Intensifying competition in the gifting segment.
  • Macroeconomic risks including economic downturns and exchange rate fluctuations.
  • Liquidity/compliance risk evidenced by non-deposit of Employees Provident Fund and Tax Deducted at Source for April–June 2026.
  • Inventory valuation uncertainty, with the auditors unable to comment on whether inventory is at cost or net realisable value.
  • Loss-making trajectory: loss before tax of Rs. (196.21) lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and net loss after tax of Rs. (429.70) lakhs for FY2025-26.
  • Borrowing burden: total borrowings of Rs. 3,215.41 lakhs, with finance costs of Rs. 89.47 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management has expressed confidence that continued focus on new markets, business initiatives, revamping the e-commerce platform, and expanding quick commerce and retail presence will help achieve higher revenue and improved profitability in the coming years, enabling Archies to emerge as a stronger, more resilient, and faster-growing company.

Observed performance in the most recent reported period runs counter to that trajectory. In the quarter ended 30 June 2026, revenue from operations declined year-on-year (Rs. 790.79 lakhs versus Rs. 1,374.65 lakhs), and the company moved from a profit of Rs. 30.01 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025 to a loss of Rs. (138.25) lakhs. For FY2025-26, revenue from operations declined to Rs. 5,609.70 lakhs from Rs. 6,970.54 lakhs, with a net loss after tax of Rs. (429.70) lakhs. The quarter ended 31 March 2026 was also loss-making, with loss before tax of Rs. (547.77) lakhs and a loss for the period of Rs. (427.03) lakhs. Segment results for the June 2026 quarter show losses in Stationery and Paper Bag and Gifts, partly offset by Greeting Cards and Others.

Governance and Board Developments

At its meeting held on 14 August 2026 (commencing 12:30 P.M., concluding 4:00 P.M.), the Board approved the unaudited standalone financial results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, along with the Limited Review Report. The Board also approved:

  • Re-appointment of Mr. Anil Moolchandani as Chairman and Managing Director with effect from 28 August 2026 for a further two years, subject to shareholder approval. He is 73 years old, has over 50 years of management experience in the social expression industry, holds 7,061,607 shares as on 31 March 2026, is the father of Mr. Varun Moolchandani, and attended 6 Board meetings during FY2025-26.
  • Appointment of Mr. Anil Kumar Verma as an Additional (Non-Executive and Independent) Director with effect from 14 August 2026 for five years, subject to shareholder approval. He is 69 years old, holds M.Com and CAIIB, has over 38 years of banking experience, and holds no shares in the company.
  • Reconstitution of the Audit Committee and the Nomination & Remuneration Committee with effect from 14 August 2026, following the resignation of Mr. Faizan Rashid Bhat and the appointment of Mr. Anil Kumar Verma. Both committees are chaired by Mr. Charan Jeet Singh Kochhar, with Mr. Anil Kumar Verma as member.
  • The 36th Annual General Meeting to be held on Thursday, 24 September 2026 at 11:30 A.M. through Video Conferencing (VC) or Other Audio-Visual Means (OAVM).
  • Appointment of Mr. Shailesh Dayal, Partner of M/s Dayal & Maur (Company Secretaries), New Delhi, as Scrutinizer to scrutinize the poll process and e-voting process at the ensuing AGM.

Operational Snapshot

The company operates 54 company-owned/managed stores and has total borrowings of Rs. 3,215.41 lakhs. Capital employed stood at Rs. 12,378.90 lakhs as at 30 June 2026, down from Rs. 12,516.81 lakhs as at 31 March 2026 and Rs. 13,052.49 lakhs as at 30 June 2025. Segment assets were Rs. 18,473.57 lakhs and segment liabilities Rs. 6,094.67 lakhs as at 30 June 2026. The company notes that, due to the nature of the business and common manufacturing facilities across segments, a reasonable allocation of capital employed to various segments is currently not practicable.
