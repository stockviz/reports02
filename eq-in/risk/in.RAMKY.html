Headwinds and Challenges

Ramky Infrastructure faces a set of execution and financial risks tied to its project portfolio. Tender risks arise from fixed-price contracts that lack adequate provisions for cost or time overruns. The tendering stage itself requires commitments that create cash flow pressures. Material cost fluctuations are a concern where contracts do not include adequate escalation provisions. The company also cites a shortage of skilled labor, compliance risks from complex statutory and regulatory requirements, unforeseen site and geological conditions, and delays in securing necessary statutory clearances.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Several demand-side factors support the business. Urban water demand is growing, alongside wastewater reuse requirements. The industrial parks sector is expanding, supported by government initiatives such as the BHAVYA scheme. Public infrastructure spending is increasing across urban infrastructure, transport, and water sectors. Growth in Opex contracts is providing long-duration revenue visibility.

Key Risks

The risks identified center on contract structuring and execution: fixed-price contracts without adequate cost or time overrun provisions, cash flow pressure from tendering-stage commitments, material cost fluctuations without escalation provisions, skilled labor shortages, complex statutory and regulatory compliance, unforeseen site and geological conditions, and delays in statutory clearances.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management expects to maintain a selective approach to bidding for EPC projects, focusing on commercial viability and execution risks. It also intends to continue pursuing strategic partnerships and acquisitions, expand R&D and compliance capabilities, and prioritize cash conversion and working capital discipline.

Observed performance provides context for these priorities. For FY 2025-26, the company reported consolidated revenue from operations of INR 18,464.78 million and a Profit After Tax of INR 2,827.77 million. The total order book stands at approximately INR 13,000 crores. The company successfully executed its exit from the restructuring agreement in July 2025. A dividend of ₹1.00 per equity share has been recommended for FY 2025-26.

On governance, Ms. Pooja Kachalia (Membership No. F9862) has been appointed as the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of Ramky Infrastructure Limited, effective 16th September 2026.
