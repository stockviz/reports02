Broker Narrative

The narrative moved from a micro Morbi/propane price war—where GGAS was sacrificing margins to regain ceramic-tile volumes—to a macro supply-security and portfolio-volume story. Morbi, propane and LNG sourcing remained recurring themes, but the later report shifted from cheap spot LNG and strong exports to West Asia supply disruptions, contract expiries, and a 9.6% FY26 CGD volume decline, while introducing gas trading profitability and tax-loss shields as new positives.

Fears that came true

  • The propane/dual-fuel threat materialized: dual-fuel ceramic units could switch on price differentials, and industrial PNG volumes fell 23.3% YoY in FY26 with PNG industrial/commercial volumes down 16.0% YoY in Q4FY26.
  • The margin-sacrifice-for-volume strategy did not produce durable growth: total CGD volumes fell 9.6% YoY in FY26 despite pricing actions.
  • Muted domestic ceramic/industrial demand persisted, reflected in weak industrial PNG offtake and overall CGD volume decline.

Optimism that failed

  • The hoped-for benefit from declining spot LNG prices was overtaken by the West Asia conflict, which disrupted LNG supply and caused the loss of two cargoes in May/June 2026.
  • The expected support from healthy ceramic export demand did not prevent a 23.3% YoY fall in industrial PNG volumes in FY26.
