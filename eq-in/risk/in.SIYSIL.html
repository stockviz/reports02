Headwinds and Challenges

  • Demand moderation in wedding and occasion-led consumption during Q1 FY27 due to the Adhik Maas period, as stated by Mr. Gaurav Poddar.
  • Value-conscious consumer spending with consumers adopting a selective approach to purchases.
  • Inflationary pressures on input costs persisted during the quarter.
  • Cadini S.R.L. (wholly owned foreign subsidiary) has incurred recurring losses, resulting in reduction of its net worth. Its continued operations depend on management’s business plans, profitability improvement measures, and financial support/recapitalisation arrangements. The auditor drew attention to this as an Emphasis of Matter in the consolidated review report.
  • Working capital pressure: Changes in working capital were negative ₹1,952 Mn in FY26, contributing to a decline in net cash from operating activities to ₹933 Mn from ₹2,561 Mn in FY25.
  • Rising finance costs: Finance costs increased from ₹203 Mn in FY24 to ₹238 Mn in FY25 to ₹342 Mn in FY26.
  • Rising borrowings: Current borrowings increased from ₹1,631 Mn in FY24 to ₹2,023 Mn in FY25 to ₹2,979 Mn in FY26.
  • Trade receivables increased from ₹4,677 Mn in FY24 to ₹5,204 Mn in FY25 to ₹6,469 Mn in FY26.
  • PAT margin compression at annual level: PAT margin declined from 8.7% in FY24 and FY25 to 8.6% in FY26; EBIT margin declined from 12.8% in FY25 to 12.6% in FY26.
  • Quarterly PAT margin remains thin at 2.4% in Q1 FY27 despite improvement from 1.1% in Q1 FY26.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Total Income growth of 16.4% YoY in Q1 FY27 (₹466 crores vs ₹400 crores in Q1 FY26).
  • EBITDA growth of 22.3% YoY in Q1 FY27 (₹40 crores vs ₹33 crores), with margin expanding to 8.6% from 8.2%.
  • PAT growth of 144.4% YoY in Q1 FY27 (₹11 crores vs ₹5 crores), with PAT margin expanding to 2.4% from 1.1%.
  • Diversified revenue mix: Fabric 71%, Garments 19%, Yarn & Others 10% in Q1 FY27.
  • Retail expansion: Added 3 ZECODE and 2 DEVO stores in Q1 FY27, taking total to 30 ZECODE and 19 DEVO stores. Plan to reach approximately 70 stores across both brands by FY27, funded entirely through internally generated cash flows.
  • Festive season demand anticipation: Management expects improvement in consumer demand and spending sentiment as the company moves into the festive season.
  • Strong brand portfolio including Siyaram’s, Mistair, J. Hampstead, CADINI, Oxemberg, Mozzo, Tessio, Ethnair, DEN-KNIT, EVITA, BREEZY, ZECODE, and DEVO.
  • New retail brands (ZECODE and DEVO) targeting fast fashion and ethnic wear markets, placed in high-footfall areas with billboard, hoarding, and on-ground event marketing, plus social media and digital engagement.
  • Bonus preference share issuance: 9% CNCRPS by bonus in 2 series — Series I: 4 CNCRPS of ₹10 each for every 1 equity share (redeemable at end of 3rd year); Series II: 3 CNCRPS of ₹10 each for every 1 equity share (redeemable at end of 5th year). Issue size ₹318 Cr from general reserves/retained earnings. CNCRPS to be listed on NSE/BSE.
  • Renewable energy expansion: Total installed solar capacity of 6,792 kWp across manufacturing facilities, reducing carbon emissions by approximately 5,000–6,000 metric tons of CO2 annually. Target of 15% electricity consumption through renewable energy by 2030.
  • Consistent dividend track record: Final Dividend of ₹5 per share (250%) declared for FY26, plus interim dividends (including a special dividend) paid during the year.
  • Exports contributed 10% of revenues in FY26.
  • Revenue growth described as volume-driven, supported by market demand and operational execution.
  • Return on Capital Employed and Return on Equity trends shown in investor presentation (specific figures not legible in source).
  • Guinness World Record for world’s most viewed livestream; ranked amongst Top 100 Franchise Opportunities; awarded India’s Most Attractive Brands by Trust Research Advisory; Most Trusted Brand by ET & Nielsen Media Research; The Next 500 by Fortune India; Felicitated as Iconic Brands of India by ET.

Key Risks

  • Cadini S.R.L. subsidiary risk: Recurring losses, reduction in net worth, and dependence on management’s business plans and financial support for continued operations.
  • Consumer demand sensitivity: Demand influenced by cautious purchasing behaviour and selective spending patterns.
  • Input cost inflation: Inflationary pressures on input costs persisted during Q1 FY27.
  • Seasonal/occasion-led demand volatility: Wedding and occasion-led consumption moderated due to Adhik Maas period.
  • Working capital deterioration: Negative working capital changes in FY26 impacted operating cash flows.
  • Rising debt levels: Current borrowings increased significantly from FY24 to FY26.
  • Receivables risk: Trade receivables have grown consistently over FY24–FY26.
  • Competitive environment: The fashion industry is described as competitive.
  • Forward-looking statement risks: The company’s safe harbour statement notes risks including performance of Indian and international economies, industry performance, competition, ability to implement strategy, growth and expansion levels, technological changes, changes in revenue/income/cashflows, market preferences, and market risks.
