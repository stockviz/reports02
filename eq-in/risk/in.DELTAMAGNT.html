Headwinds and Challenges

The company operates against a backdrop of cost pressures arising from global geopolitical instability, supply chain disruptions, and inflationary trends in raw material and energy prices. These are compounded by increasing overheads and intense industry competition.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Management points to several growth levers: a strategic joint venture with Premo S.L, Spain for soft ferrites; capacity expansion at the Tirupur facility for the textile trim segment; enhanced operational efficiency; accelerated R&D and product innovation; and strengthened customer responsiveness.
