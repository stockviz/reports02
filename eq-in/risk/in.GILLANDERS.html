Gillanders Arbuthnot and Company Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Seasonality of the tea business. The company states that the tea industry is seasonal in nature, and results for part of the year (which include the Tea Division) should not be taken as indicative of results for the full year. This is flagged in both the standalone and consolidated notes to the 7 August 2026 results.
  • Weak prior-quarter comparatives in tea. For the quarter ended 31 March 2026, the Tea segment recorded a segment loss of Rs. (1,911.26) lakhs, against a profit of Rs. 174.67 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and a loss of Rs. (303.38) lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025. The year ended 31 March 2026 tea segment result was a modest profit of Rs. 346.42 lakhs.
  • Textiles losses in the prior-year comparative. The Textiles segment posted a loss of Rs. (180.71) lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2025, although it returned to profit in subsequent periods (Rs. 18.17 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 69.62 lakhs in Q1 FY27).
  • Engineering (MICCO) volatility. Segment revenue for Engineering (MICCO) fell from Rs. 4,034.65 lakhs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 to Rs. 2,239.50 lakhs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with segment result declining from Rs. 423.80 lakhs to Rs. 145.57 lakhs over the same period.
  • Finance costs. Finance cost of Rs. 200.17 lakhs in Q1 FY27 (against Rs. 195.17 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 222.78 lakhs in Q1 FY26) continues to weigh on profitability.
  • Low public participation in voting. At the 92nd AGM held on 3 July 2026, total votes polled represented 69.0699% of outstanding shares, but public institutions polled 0 votes (0.0000%) across all four resolutions. Public non-institutions polled only 3,870 votes (0.0593% of their holding), with a notable dissent proportion of about 44% of those votes cast on each resolution.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Return to overall profitability in Q1 FY27. The standalone total segment result was a profit of Rs. 541.51 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, compared with a loss of Rs. (1,319.22) lakhs in Q4 FY26 and a loss of Rs. (208.92) lakhs in Q1 FY26. Profit before tax was Rs. 340.15 lakhs in Q1 FY27, against a loss before tax of Rs. (1,515.05) lakhs in Q4 FY26 and a loss of Rs. (416.37) lakhs in Q1 FY26.
  • All four segments profitable in Q1 FY27. Tea Rs. 174.67 lakhs, Engineering (MICCO) Rs. 145.57 lakhs, Textiles Rs. 69.62 lakhs, and Property Rs. 151.65 lakhs.
  • Property segment stability. Property segment revenue rose to Rs. 256.80 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 252.89 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 237.69 lakhs in Q1 FY26, with segment result of Rs. 151.65 lakhs in Q1 FY27.
  • Engineering (MICCO) division performance. The division reported total revenues of Rs. 2,244.65 lakhs, net profit of Rs. 109.02 lakhs, total comprehensive income of Rs. 117.52 lakhs, and net cash inflow of Rs. 277.12 lakhs for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, with total assets of Rs. 14,312.39 lakhs as at that date.
  • Tax regime change. The Income Tax Act 2025 allows existing domestic companies to opt for the New Tax Regime at a lower rate of 22% (plus surcharge and cess) instead of 30% (plus surcharge and cess). The company reassessed and decided to opt for the New Tax Regime with effect from 1 April 2026.
  • Consolidated group structure. The consolidated results include Gillanders Holdings (Mauritius) Limited (subsidiary) and Naming’omba Tea Estates Limited (step-down subsidiary), alongside the holding company.
  • Shareholder approvals secured. All four resolutions at the 92nd AGM on 3 July 2026 passed with requisite majority, including adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for FY26, re-appointment of Smt. Prabhawati Devi Kothari as director, declaration of dividend on 7.75% CRPS for FY 2018-19 and 2019-20, and ratification of cost auditor remuneration.

3. Key Risks

  • Seasonality risk in tea. Explicitly flagged by the company; part-year results are not indicative of full-year performance.
  • Segment concentration and cyclicality. Tea, Engineering (MICCO), Textiles, and Property each show quarter-to-quarter swings in revenue and result, with tea and textiles having recorded losses in recent comparative periods.
  • Finance cost burden. Finance costs of Rs. 200.17 lakhs in Q1 FY27 remain a fixed drag on profitability.
  • Reliance on other auditor for MICCO. The statutory auditor’s conclusion on both standalone and consolidated statements relies solely on the report of another auditor for the Engineering (MICCO) division, which represents total assets of Rs. 14,312.39 lakhs as at 30 June 2026.
  • Limited review, not audit. The Q1 FY27 results were subject to limited review, which provides less assurance than an audit.
  • Governance/voting concentration. Promoter and promoter group holdings of 14,737,277 shares (100% of their holding voted) dominate voting outcomes, while public institution participation was nil and public non-institution participation was minimal, with roughly 44% dissent among those public non-institution votes cast.
  • Preference dividend arrears. The AGM approved payment of dividend on 7.75% CRPS for FY 2018-19 and 2019-20, indicating accumulated preference dividend obligations being addressed.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Tax regime guidance. Management decided to opt for the New Tax Regime under the Income Tax Act 2025 with effect from 1 April 2026, at a lower rate of 22% plus surcharge and cess. The Q1 FY27 results are the first reported period reflecting this election.
  • Seasonality guidance. Management stated that tea industry seasonality means part-year results should not be taken as indicative of full-year results. Observed performance is consistent with this: tea segment result swung from a loss of Rs. (1,911.26) lakhs in Q4 FY26 to a profit of Rs. 174.67 lakhs in Q1 FY27, while the full year FY26 tea segment result was a profit of Rs. 346.42 lakhs.
  • Observed performance versus prior periods. The company moved from a consolidated/standalone loss before tax of Rs. (416.37) lakhs in Q1 FY26 and Rs. (1,515.05) lakhs in Q4 FY26 to a profit before tax of Rs. 340.15 lakhs in Q1 FY27, with all four segments contributing positively.
  • AGM outcomes versus board recommendations. All resolutions placed before members (adoption of FY26 accounts, director re-appointment, CRPS dividend for FY 2018-19 and 2019-20, and cost auditor remuneration ratification) were passed with requisite majority, with over 99.98% of votes polled in favour on each resolution.
