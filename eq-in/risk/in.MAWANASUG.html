Headwinds and Challenges

Seasonal quarterly loss. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, Mawana Sugars reported a standalone net loss of Rs. 23.21 crore (consolidated: Rs. 23.09 crore), compared with a loss of Rs. 13.66 crore in the same quarter a year earlier. The company attributes this to the seasonal nature of its sugar and power segments, noting that performance in any quarter may not be representative of annual performance.

Weak sugar and power segment results. Segment results for the June 2026 quarter show losses in sugar (Rs. 17.38 crore standalone) and power (Rs. 4.56 crore standalone), with only the distillery segment contributing positively (Rs. 4.37 crore). This contrasts sharply with the March 2026 quarter, when sugar and power both posted strong profits.

Rising finance costs. Finance costs increased to Rs. 7.89 crore in the June 2026 quarter from Rs. 6.15 crore in the March 2026 quarter, though they were lower than the Rs. 11.04 crore in the June 2025 quarter.

Cost of materials and inventory build-up. Cost of materials consumed was Rs. 29.50 crore in the June 2026 quarter, while changes in inventories of finished goods, stock-in-trade and work-in-progress were a negative Rs. 348.35 crore (standalone), reflecting inventory build-up during the quarter.

Regulatory and labour code uncertainty. The company accounted for an incremental impact of Rs. 14.75 crore (standalone) and Rs. 14.95 crore (consolidated) in the December 2025 quarter as an exceptional item related to the Code on Wages, 2019. During the March 2026 quarter, it reversed Rs. 9.43 crore (standalone and consolidated) following reassessment. The company continues to monitor implementation of the Codes and rules notified by Central and State Governments and will account for any material impact arising from future notifications.

Pending amalgamation. The Scheme of Arrangement for amalgamation of Mawana Foods Private Limited with the company has been reserved for order by the NCLT, New Delhi Bench. The financial results for the June 2026 quarter have been prepared without giving effect to the proposed amalgamation, pending receipt of the final order and completion of statutory formalities.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Distillery segment growth. The distillery segment posted a profit of Rs. 4.37 crore in the June 2026 quarter, up from Rs. 2.85 crore in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 3.03 crore in the March 2026 quarter. Distillery revenue was Rs. 65.02 crore in the June 2026 quarter, compared with Rs. 52.44 crore in the June 2025 quarter.

Full-year profitability. For the year ended March 31, 2026, the company reported a standalone profit after tax of Rs. 36.72 crore (consolidated: Rs. 37.09 crore), with basic and diluted earnings per share of Rs. 9.39 (standalone) and Rs. 9.48 (consolidated).

Dividend declared. Shareholders at the Annual General Meeting held on July 03, 2026, declared a final dividend of 40% (Rs. 4.00 per equity share of face value Rs. 10 each) amounting to Rs. 15.65 crore for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The dividend was deposited with a scheduled bank within the prescribed timeline and paid subsequently.

Subsidiary performance. Mawana Foods Private Limited, the subsidiary, reported total revenues of Rs. 9.92 crore, net profit after tax of Rs. 0.09 crore, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 0.09 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Proposed amalgamation. The Scheme of Arrangement for amalgamation of Mawana Foods Private Limited with the company, approved by the Board on August 02, 2025, has completed final hearing at the NCLT, New Delhi Bench, which has reserved its order. The Scheme shall become effective upon receipt of final sanction and filing of the certified copy of the NCLT Order with the Registrar of Companies.

Leadership continuity. The Board approved revised remuneration for Mr. Rakesh Kumar Gangwar as Managing Director for a further one year (August 13, 2026 to August 12, 2027), subject to shareholder approval via postal ballot. Mr. Gangwar has been with the company since 2009 and has 34 years of experience in sugar and distillery operations. The Board also approved re-appointment of Mr. Satish Agrawal as Non-Executive Independent Director for a second term of five years (November 3, 2026 to November 2, 2031), subject to shareholder approval via postal ballot.

Key Risks

Seasonality risk. The company explicitly states that its sugar and power business segments are seasonal in nature and therefore performance in any quarter may not be representative of annual performance.

Regulatory risk. The company continues to monitor implementation of the Code on Wages, 2019, and rules notified by Central and State Governments, and will account for any material impact arising from future notifications in the relevant reporting period.

Amalgamation execution risk. The Scheme of Arrangement for amalgamation of Mawana Foods Private Limited with the company remains subject to receipt of final sanction from the NCLT and filing of the certified copy of the NCLT Order with the Registrar of Companies. The financial results for the June 2026 quarter have been prepared without giving effect to the proposed amalgamation.

Segment concentration risk. The sugar segment remains the largest contributor to revenue (Rs. 396.44 crore standalone in June 2026 quarter) but posted a loss at the segment level, while power segment results are volatile (profit of Rs. 30.90 crore in March 2026 quarter versus loss of Rs. 4.56 crore in June 2026 quarter).

Interest rate and finance cost risk. Finance costs remain a significant expense, at Rs. 7.89 crore in the June 2026 quarter and Rs. 22.14 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Seasonality guidance. Management states that the sugar and power business segments are seasonal and quarterly performance may not be representative of annual performance. This is consistent with observed results: the June 2026 quarter showed losses in sugar and power, while the March 2026 quarter showed strong profits in both segments.

Labour code impact. Management stated it would continue to monitor implementation of the Code on Wages, 2019, and account for any material impact arising from future notifications. In the December 2025 quarter, the company accounted for an incremental impact of Rs. 14.75 crore (standalone) and Rs. 14.95 crore (consolidated) as an exceptional item. In the March 2026 quarter, following reassessment considering restructured compensation effective April 1, 2026, the company reversed Rs. 9.43 crore (standalone and consolidated), resulting in a cumulative incremental impact of Rs. 5.32 crore (standalone) and Rs. 5.52 crore (consolidated) for the year ended March 31, 2026.

Amalgamation timeline. Management stated the Scheme shall become effective upon receipt of final sanction from the NCLT and upon filing of the certified true copy of the NCLT Order with the Registrar of Companies. The final hearing has been completed and the NCLT has reserved its order. The financial results for the June 2026 quarter have been prepared without giving effect to the proposed amalgamation, pending receipt of the final order and completion of requisite statutory formalities.

Dividend guidance. The Board recommended a final dividend of 40% (Rs. 4.00 per equity share) for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, which was declared by shareholders at the AGM held on July 03, 2026, and paid subsequently.
