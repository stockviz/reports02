Windsor Machines Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Middle East conflict disruption: The company identifies disruption arising from the Middle East conflict as a material headwind.
  • Elevated raw material costs: Raw material costs have been driven up by supply chain disruptions, and this has moderated EBITDA margins in Q1FY27 even as revenue grew.
  • Broader macro and policy pressures: Risks cited include changes in fiscal policy, competitive intensity, inflationary pressures, climatic factors, and broader economic conditions affecting demand, supply, and pricing.
  • Wintal Machines SRL liquidation: The company filed for voluntary judicial liquidation of Wintal Machines SRL, Italy (a 100% subsidiary). The administrator appointed by the Court of Brescia took control of all Wintal activities effective December 30, 2024. The company has already provided for its total investment in and receivables from Wintal in the standalone financial statements in the past and does not expect any proceeds from the liquidation.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong underlying demand: The company cites strong underlying demand for its products.
  • Import substitution: Focus areas include manufacturing high-tonnage machines entirely in India, supported by anti-dumping duty imposed on imported injection moulding machines.
  • Operational consolidation and capacity: Operations have been consolidated into a large integrated Rajkot facility (36+ acres, 11L+ sq ft built area) with a capacity of 3,600 machines per annum, with expansion potential up to 8,400 machines per annum.
  • Strategic acquisitions: The acquisition of Unitech Workholding is cited as a growth lever.
  • Order book: An order book of ₹217 Cr as of June 30, 2026.
  • Revenue growth: Q1FY27 revenue grew 31.4% YoY to ₹148.9 Cr.
  • Leadership addition: Mr. Mohan Ramachandran joined as Chief Executive Officer.
  • Multi-decadal growth journey: The company frames its prospects within a multi-decadal growth journey.

3. Key Risks

  • Raw material and supply chain risk: Elevated raw material costs and supply chain disruptions are the principal near-term risks, with margin impact already observed.
  • Geopolitical risk: The Middle East conflict is explicitly identified as a source of disruption.
  • Demand, supply, and pricing risk: Changes in fiscal policy, competitive intensity, inflation, climatic factors, and broader economic conditions can affect demand, supply, and pricing.
  • Subsidiary liquidation risk: The Wintal Machines SRL judicial liquidation has resulted in the company not expecting any proceeds; the investment and receivables were already provided for in the standalone financial statements.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The company states it does not undertake to update any forward-looking statement.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management expects the impact of elevated raw material costs and supply chain disruptions to be transitory, with margins expected to recover as the situation stabilizes and pricing adjusts.
  • Observed performance: In Q1FY27, revenue grew 31.4% YoY to ₹148.9 Cr, while EBITDA margins moderated due to raw material costs. This is consistent with the guidance that margin pressure is a temporary phenomenon tied to input costs rather than demand weakness.
  • Order book: ₹217 Cr as of June 30, 2026, provides visibility on near-term revenue.
  • Capacity: The integrated Rajkot facility operates at 3,600 machines per annum, with stated expansion potential up to 8,400 machines per annum, indicating room to scale as demand materializes.

5. Other Key Facts

  • Preferential allotment: Windsor Machines received trading approval from NSE and BSE for 17,32,405 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each, issued to promoters at a premium of Rs. 189.85/- upon conversion of warrants on a preferential basis. These shares were listed and admitted to dealings effective September 17, 2026, and are subject to a lock-in period ending March 31, 2028.
  • Scheme of Amalgamation: The Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad Bench, approved the Scheme of Amalgamation of Global CNC Private Limited (a wholly-owned subsidiary) with the company on March 19, 2026, with an appointed date of April 1, 2025. The scheme became effective upon filing the certified copy with the Registrar of Companies, Ahmedabad on March 31, 2026. The scheme has been given effect to by merging the carrying amount of Global’s assets and liabilities with the company from the appointed date. The company had acquired control over erstwhile Global CNC Private Limited effective February 14, 2025. Comparative information for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 has been restated, and provision for tax from April 2025 has been recomputed.
  • Consolidated subsidiary: The consolidated results include Unitech Workholding Systems Private Limited as a wholly-owned subsidiary. For Q1FY27, this subsidiary reflected total revenues of Rs. 407.52 Lakhs, net profit after tax of Rs. 27.10 Lakhs, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 27.10 Lakhs, reviewed by another auditor.
  • AGM: The 63rd Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, September 29, 2026, at 02:00 P.M. (IST) through video conferencing/other audio-visual means. Key items include receiving and adopting financial statements, reappointment of Mr. Hitendrabhai Hasmukhbhai Patel as Director, and ratification of the cost auditor’s remuneration of Rs. 90,000 to M/s. Ashish Bhavsar & Associates.
  • Auditor transition: The standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 were audited by another firm of Chartered Accountants, which issued an unmodified conclusion dated August 6, 2025.
