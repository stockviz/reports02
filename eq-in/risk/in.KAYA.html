Kaya Limited – Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Elevated losses: For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), Kaya reported a loss after tax of ₹(15.2) crores, compared with a loss after tax of ₹(14.2) crores in Q1 FY26. The company attributed the higher loss primarily to the impact of GST 2.0 changes effective September 2025, which restricted input tax credit availability.
  • GST-driven margin pressure: The GST 2.0 changes effective September 2025 are cited as the primary driver of the increased loss, indicating a cost/margin headwind that persisted into Q1 FY27.
  • Governance and reporting scrutiny: The company received an NSE query dated June 17, 2026 regarding its audited standalone and consolidated financial results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 (submitted May 20, 2026). A further NSE communication requested resubmission of revised standalone audited financial results, which the company subsequently resubmitted in XBRL form and updated on the NSE website (disclosed July 7, 2026).
  • Board and committee churn: Mr. Nikhil Khattau resigned with effect from the close of business hours on July 31, 2026, and Mr. Om Prakash Manchanda completed his first term as an Independent Director with effect from the close of business hours on August 2, 2026. This necessitated reconstitution of the Audit Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Stakeholders’ Relationship Committee, approved by circular resolution on July 31, 2026, effective August 1, 2026.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Clinic business growth: The clinic business registered 16% revenue growth in Q1 FY27 over Q1 FY26.
  • Category momentum: Services revenue grew 16% over Q1 FY26, driven mainly by Brightening & Pigmentation, Acne & Scars, and Anti-Aging. The skin business grew 19%, the hair business grew 17%, and the product business grew 16% over Q1 FY26, with products driven mainly by Nutraceutical, Lighter and Brighter, and Anti-Aging categories.
  • Overall revenue growth: Revenue from operations was ₹60.1 crores for Q1 FY27, a 14% growth over the corresponding quarter Q1 FY26.
  • Capital infusion: On August 10, 2026, the Board approved a preferential issue of 18,24,150 equity shares of face value ₹10 each at an issue price of ₹274.10 per share (higher than the SEBI ICDR floor price), for aggregate cash consideration of ₹49,99,99,515, to Axana Estates LLP and Plutus Investments India Private Limited. Post-issue, Axana Estates LLP’s shareholding would rise from 20,90,068 shares (13.76%) to 29,35,077 shares (17.25%), and Plutus Investments India Private Limited would hold 9,79,141 shares (5.76%) from nil.
  • Leadership transition: The EGM held September 5, 2026 passed all four resolutions, including appointment of Mr. Harsh Mariwala as Chairman and Non-Executive Director (effective November 1, 2026) and Mr. Rishabh Mariwala as Managing Director for a five-year period (effective November 1, 2026).
  • Employee alignment: The Board approved increasing the Kaya ESOP Plan 2021 pool by 4,00,000 options, from 8,03,204 to 12,03,204 options, subject to member approval.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued loss-making trend: The Q1 FY27 loss widened year-on-year, and the company identifies GST 2.0-related input tax credit restrictions as the cause; if this persists, profitability pressure may continue.
  • Regulatory/listing compliance risk: The NSE sought clarification and revised standalone audited results for FY26, requiring resubmission and website updates, highlighting reporting accuracy risk.
  • Key personnel and board stability: Resignation of an independent director and completion of another independent director’s term led to committee reconstitution, indicating reliance on a small pool of board members.
  • Dilution risk: The preferential issue will expand the share capital and materially increase the holdings of the two allottees, diluting existing shareholders.
  • Execution risk on strategic changes: The new Chairman and Managing Director take effect only from November 1, 2026, and the ESOP pool expansion and preferential issue remain subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Management’s Q1 FY27 media release (August 3, 2026) highlighted clinic business revenue growth of 16% and overall revenue growth of 14% to ₹60.1 crores, alongside category-level growth in services, skin, hair, and products. Observed performance matched these growth figures as reported.
  • However, the same release reported a widened loss after tax of ₹(15.2) crores for Q1 FY27 versus ₹(14.2) crores in Q1 FY26, with management attributing the higher loss to GST 2.0 input tax credit restrictions effective September 2025.
