Broker Narrative

The narrative began with a Buy on expectations of a spending rebound in BFSI, Manufacturing, and Hi-Tech from H2FY24, plus cloud and generative AI adoption, while flagging discretionary spending and client decision delays. Those macro/discretionary headwinds persisted across the research history and were later joined by wage-cost pressures and software-license softness. The final Sell report still acknowledged AI-led transformation, cloud modernization, and strong pipelines, but the emphasis had shifted to cost discipline, offshore leverage, and macro uncertainty.

Fears that came true

  • Softness in discretionary spending materialized as multiple 2024 Buy/Accumulate calls returned -35% to -45% instead of predicted gains.
  • Client delays in decision-making and extended BFSI cycles are consistent with repeated DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes through 2024 as actual returns stayed deeply negative.
  • Unfavorable macroeconomic conditions persisted, evidenced by even a Reduce call with +0.4% predicted return actually delivering -35.6%.
  • Project ramp-downs and lower conversion to TCV appear to have shown up later as software-segment softness and weak perpetual-license sales in the final report.

Optimism that failed

  • The expected H2FY24 rebound in BFSI, Manufacturing, and Hi-Tech spending did not translate into positive stock returns, as 2024 Accumulate calls were followed by declines of roughly 35-45%.
  • The strong project pipeline cited initially failed to offset macro and discretionary headwinds, with the 2024-07-08 Accumulate call predicting +4.5% but actual returning -35.4%.
  • Acceleration in cloud adoption and generative AI was repeatedly cited but did not prevent sustained negative actuals in 2024 and 2025, and the research ultimately ended with a Sell call.
