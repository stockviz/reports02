Headwinds and Challenges

  • The India fluorochemicals market faces high production costs due to complex synthesis and specialised raw materials, which restrains market growth.
  • The company operates in a market where global helium supply is subject to constraints and periodic shortages, a factor it explicitly seeks to mitigate through long-term sourcing partnerships.
  • The business is exposed to highly volatile market conditions, which it manages through efficient inventory planning.
  • The company is undertaking a significant capital expenditure programme across multiple sites (Khalapur helium, Mambattu, Bhilwara R-32, and a proposed HFO plant), which carries execution and timeline risk. The Bhilwara R-32 project has a stated target completion of approximately eight months and commissioning by October 2026, with construction described as being in accelerated mode.
  • The proposed HFO manufacturing plant at Bhilwara (Hurda) involves a proposed investment of approximately ₹200 crore, with construction only to commence in 2027 end, indicating a long gestation period.
  • The IPO proceeds utilisation plan was revised pursuant to a Special Resolution passed on May 30, 2026, to acquire approximately 2 acres of land at Khalapur in lieu of the originally proposed warehouse construction, resulting in a differential cost of ₹20.13 crore.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • The global fluorochemicals market was valued at USD 76.7 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 204.4 billion by 2035, a CAGR of 10.3%, driven by refrigeration, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and automotive demand.
  • The India fluorochemicals market was valued at approximately USD 750 million in 2025 and is estimated to grow at around 10.5% CAGR during 2026–34, supported by industrial expansion and adoption of low-GWP refrigerants such as R-32 and HFOs.
  • Regulatory and policy support: environmental regulations, sustainability initiatives, PLI, Make in India, and state incentives (RIPS) are boosting domestic production and import substitution.
  • The Khalapur facility for high-purity helium processing has been completed, with installed capacity of 1,200 MT per annum and commercial operations expected to commence in the upcoming quarter. End-use industries include semiconductors and electronics, healthcare (MRI), aerospace and defence, and fiber optics.
  • The company has entered a long-term strategic sourcing partnership with Sharjah Oxygen Company L.L.C. (SOC) enabling sourcing of liquid helium from RAS Gases & Oilfields, Qatar.
  • The Mambattu facility (Andhra Pradesh) is progressing as planned, with planned capacity of 7,200 MT per annum, expected to commence operations between November and December 2026 (end of Q3 FY27). It will handle refrigerant debulking, blending and storage of HFC, HFO and blends, hydrocarbon debulking, and a helium plus semiconductor gases facility similar to Khalapur.
  • The 10,000 MT per annum R-32 manufacturing facility at Bhilwara, Rajasthan, received Environmental Clearance from SEIAA, Rajasthan on 28 December 2025, valid for 10 years. It also includes blended refrigerants capacity of 7,500 MT per annum (aggregate) and a by-product of Hydrochloric Acid (HCl – 30%). Estimated topline potential is ₹250 crore in FY26-27 and ₹500–600 crore in FY27-28, with expected PAT margin of 22–24%.
  • An MoU has been signed with the Government of Rajasthan for a Hydrofluoroolefin (HFO) manufacturing plant, proposed investment approximately ₹200 crore, at Bhilwara (Hurda), with land already acquired.
  • The company is working with ISRO on long-term Helium Recovery and Liquefaction solutions for space applications and has participated in a large tender floated by ISRO for such systems.
  • The company has commenced commercial HFO-1234yf supplies to Indian auto OEMs, aiding compliance with European F-gas export norms, and began Isobutane supplies from the Khalapur plant to refrigerator OEMs.
  • The company has an agreement with Honeywell to jointly market and distribute HFCs, fluorines, and refrigerant specialties in India, and has introduced next-generation Solstice HFO refrigerants under an agreement with Honeywell.
  • The company partnered with Daikin Japan and Daikin India as sourcing and logistics partner for HFC-32 for India’s AC&R industry.
  • The aftermarket segment, which makes up 80% of the total market by share, delivers substantially higher profit margins than OEM customers, and the company focuses on aftermarket clients.
  • The company has plans to expand its presence in Eastern India.
  • Cross-industry opportunities cited include air conditioners (projected USD 21.59 billion by 2034, 14.98% CAGR 2026–2034), refrigerators (USD 3.27 billion by 2033, 8.80% CAGR 2025–2033), fire fighting equipment (USD 4.1 billion by 2034, 5.59% CAGR 2025–2030), semiconductor manufacturing (USD 180.2 billion by 2034, 11.95% CAGR 2026–2034), pharmaceuticals and medicals (USD 174.67 billion by 2034, 10.98% CAGR 2026–2034), glass bottle manufacturing (USD 3,631.6 million by 2033, 8.3% CAGR 2026–2033), aerosols and sprays (USD 12,636.9 million by 2033, 7.2% CAGR 2024–2033), and automobile manufacturing (USD 213.74 billion by 2031, 7.69% CAGR 2026–2031).

Key Risks

  • Forward-looking statements in the presentation are subject to risks and uncertainties including performance of Indian and international economies, industry dynamics, competition, successful execution of strategies, technological advancements, revenue fluctuations, market preferences, and exposure to various risks.
  • The company assumes no obligation to update forward-looking information.
  • The business falls within a single business segment of manufacture of industrial gases, so no separate segment disclosures are reported.
  • The company’s growth strategy depends on scaling higher-value products and strengthening backward integration, which are subject to execution risk.
  • The company is exposed to volatile market conditions, managed through inventory planning.
  • The company’s operations are subject to stringent quality control and safety standards across its facilities.
  • The company’s proposed HFO plant construction is only to commence in 2027 end, indicating a long timeline to potential revenue contribution.
  • The company’s R-32 facility is targeted for completion by end of December 2026, with estimated production mentioned in projection for Oct 26–March 27.

Management Guidance vs Observed Business Performance

  • Management guidance: Expecting to grow on 30-35% CAGR for next 3 years with maintaining sustainable margin across the period.
  • Observed performance: FY22-23 to FY23-24 total revenue growth was 0.38%; FY23-24 to FY24-25 was 60.64%; FY24-25 to FY25-26 was 14.40%. Q1 FY25-26 to Q1 FY26-27 total revenue growth was 12.78%. The 30-35% CAGR guidance is for the next three years, and recent annual growth (14.40% in FY25-26) and Q1 FY27 growth (12.78%) are below that range.
  • Management guidance: Backward integration and higher value products like semiconductor gases to enhance profitability by 3-4% (EBITDA margins) over the medium term.
  • Observed performance: EBITDA grew 75.85% YoY in Q1 FY27 while total revenue grew 12.78% YoY, indicating margin expansion in the quarter. FY25-26 EBITDA growth was 23.34% on total revenue growth of 14.40%. FY24-25 EBITDA growth was 85.25% on total revenue growth of 60.64%.
  • Management guidance: Khalapur high-purity helium processing (1,200 MT per annum) completed, commercial operations expected to commence in the upcoming quarter.
  • Observed performance: The press release dated 12 August 2026 states preparations for high-purity helium processing at Khalapur have been completed, with commercial operations expected to commence in the upcoming quarter. The investor presentation dated 14 August 2026 states the Khalapur facility has been completed and will commence commercial operations.
  • Management guidance: Mambattu facility expected to commence operations between November and December 2026 (end of Q3 FY27).
  • Observed performance: The press release states the Mambattu facility is progressing as planned and is expected to commence operations between November and December 2026. The investor presentation states it is expected to commence operations by end 2026.
  • Management guidance: 10,000 MT R-32 manufacturing facility at Bhilwara targeted for completion by end of December 2026.
  • Observed performance: The press release states the Bhilwara R-32 facility is progressing towards completion, targeted for completion by the end of December 2026. The investor presentation states expected commissioning October 2026 and target completion approximately 8 months, with estimated production mentioned in projection Oct 26–March 27.
