Tijaria Polypipes Limited — Recent Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Operations effectively halted. The company reported no revenue from operations for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and the auditor noted that there was no production of goods during the quarter. Both reporting segments — Pipes (HDPE, PVC, irrigation systems) and Textile (mink blankets) — recorded nil segment revenue. The textile segment has been closed for a long time, and the auditor stated it could not find sufficient evidence that production in the Yarn Division would resume.

Persistent losses. The company posted a net loss of Rs. 36.11 lakh for Q1 FY2026-27, wider than the Rs. 29.92 lakh loss in the preceding quarter (Q4 FY2025-26) and the Rs. 26.62 lakh loss in the same quarter a year earlier. For the full year ended 31 March 2026, the loss was Rs. 128.72 lakh. Losses were recorded in both segments: Pipes at Rs. 9.71 lakh and Textile at Rs. 12.00 lakh for the quarter.

Bank account classified as NPA and SARFAESI action. Bank of India declared the company’s account a Non-Performing Asset on 30 June 2022 (with effect from 27 November 2020), when the outstanding loan was Rs. 7,116.27 lakh plus a bank guarantee of Rs. 57 lakh, totalling Rs. 7,173.27 lakh. The bank exercised powers under Section 13(8) of the SARFAESI Act, 2002 and forfeited promoter/director equity shares and investments worth Rs. 540.27 lakh. The bank sold 71,69,116 forfeited shares in FY2023-24 for Rs. 4,65,27,031 (Rs. 6.49 per share) and 14,17,858 shares in FY2022-23 for Rs. 76,33,435 (Rs. 5.38 per share), crediting proceeds against the outstanding loan. During the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the bank forfeited an FD with interest amounting to Rs. 66,54,979 and credited it to the outstanding loan.

NCLT proceedings. Bank of India has filed a case against the company before the NCLT Jaipur Bench (CP No. (IB) - 54/7/JPR/2023). The bank’s counsel submitted that no One-Time Settlement proposal is currently under consideration. The company’s counsel stated there has been communication with the bank regarding valuation of the mortgaged property. The next hearing is scheduled for 2 September 2026.

No interest provision on NPA loan. Management decided not to make interest provisions on the NPA-declared bank loan account since the NPA declaration on 1 July 2022.

Audit qualifications and disclaimer of opinion. The auditor issued a disclaimer of opinion, citing: receivables and advances to suppliers of Rs. 2,515.59 lakh as on 30 June 2026 without independent balance confirmation and with no interest accrued; no independent confirmation of bank balances; trade receivable and payable balances subject to confirmation; land and building of Rs. 29.82 lakh held for sale since long ago without a future plan provided by management; a gross tax asset of Rs. 26.48 lakh without status of assessment/refund/appeal; and no physical verification of fixed assets by management.

Directors transacting from personal accounts. The auditor noted that directors continued to make payments/receipts on behalf of the company from their own bank accounts during Q1 FY2026-27, which the auditor described as a violation of Section 185 of the Income Tax Act, 2025. The bank had seized the company’s accounts, preventing payments from company accounts.

Income tax not determined. Income tax including deferred tax was not determined and provided for the financial year.

Related-party dependence. Plant and machinery was given on rent to M/s Vasa Industries, a partnership firm and related party, pursuant to a special resolution passed at the AGM dated 29 September 2021.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Other income provides some support. Other income was Rs. 5.29 lakh for Q1 FY2026-27, compared with Rs. 2.14 lakh in the same quarter a year earlier and Rs. 6.60 lakh in Q4 FY2025-26. Full-year other income for FY2025-26 was Rs. 22.29 lakh.

Asset base remains. Segment assets for the Pipes segment stood at Rs. 4,805.86 lakh as on 30 June 2026. The textile segment’s gross assets were Rs. 83.98 crore with a net block of Rs. 12.04 crore (after impairment of plant and machinery), including the Blanket and Yarn Divisions. Management intends that plant and machinery belonging to the Textile and Pipe Divisions are operable and will be used when production activities resume in due course.

Rental income from related party. The company has given certain plant and machinery on rent to M/s Vasa Industries, a related party, under a special resolution passed at the AGM dated 29 September 2021.

Shareholder approvals obtained. At the AGM held on 7 August 2026, shareholders passed five resolutions, including adoption of financial statements for FY2025-26, reappointment of Praveen Jain Tijaria as director liable to retire by rotation, appointment of M/s Pramod & Associates as statutory auditor, appointment of Ms. Vishakha Saini (DIN: 11800700) as non-executive independent director, and approval of related party transactions. All resolutions passed with 100% of votes polled in favour.

Board committee reconstitution. On 14 August 2026, the Board reconstituted all committees with effect from that date. The Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, and Stakeholders Relationship Committee retained Sonu Surana (Chairperson), Bharat Sharma, and Avinash Saini. The Corporate Social Responsibility Committee retained Alok Jain Tijaria (Chairperson), Praveen Jain Tijaria, and Sonu Surana. The Independent Directors Committee changed with Bhairu Singh replaced by Vishakha Saini as a member, alongside Sonu Surana (Chairperson), Bharat Sharma, and Avinash Saini.

3. Key Risks

Going concern and solvency risk. With no production, no revenue, and continuing losses, the company faces significant uncertainty. Segment liabilities for the Pipes segment were Rs. 8,208.93 lakh against segment assets of Rs. 4,805.86 lakh as on 30 June 2026, indicating a substantial negative net position.

NCLT insolvency risk. The pending NCLT case (CP No. (IB) - 54/7/JPR/2023) with the next hearing on 2 September 2026 poses a risk of insolvency proceedings. The bank’s counsel stated no OTS proposal is currently under consideration.

Recoverability risk on receivables and advances. Receivables and advances to suppliers of Rs. 2,515.59 lakh lack independent balance confirmation, and the auditor could not comment on their recoverability and existence.

Asset impairment and obsolescence risk. The textile segment has been closed for a long time, and the auditor could not find sufficient evidence that production would resume. The land and building held for sale (Rs. 29.82 lakh) lacks a management plan for execution, and physical verification of fixed assets was not conducted.

Tax asset recoverability risk. The gross tax asset of Rs. 26.48 lakh lacks status information on assessment/refund/appeal, and the auditor could not comment on its recoverability or the need for provision.

Compliance and governance risk. Directors transacting from personal accounts on behalf of the company was flagged as a violation of Section 185 of the Income Tax Act, 2025. The auditor also noted the absence of physical verification of fixed assets and lack of independent confirmations for bank balances and balances with parties.

Interest provision risk. Management’s decision not to provide interest on the NPA loan since 1 July 2022 means the financial statements do not reflect accrued interest on the outstanding loan.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Production resumption guidance versus actual output. Management intends that plant and machinery belonging to the Textile and Pipe Divisions are operable and will be used when production activities resume in due course. However, observed performance shows no production of goods during the quarter ended 30 June 2026, nil revenue from operations in both segments, and the textile segment closed for a long time. The auditor stated it could not find sufficient evidence that the company would be able to resume production under the Yarn Division.

Debtor confirmation guidance versus actual confirmations. Management has ensured that debtors outstanding at the end of the financial year will be confirmed. However, the auditor noted that independent balance confirmation for the majority of outstanding loans and advances was not received, and no interest was accrued on those loans and advances.

Physical verification guidance versus actual verification. Management has ensured that physical verification of closing stock of raw materials, stores, spares, and finished goods will be conducted at the end of the financial year. However, the auditor noted that physical verification of fixed assets was not conducted by management.

OTS resolution versus current status. A board resolution was passed on 12 February 2025 to obtain an additional loan of Rs. 5 crore from directors specifically to deposit with Bank of India for a One-Time Settlement of existing loans/dues. However, during the NCLT proceedings, the bank’s counsel submitted that no OTS proposal is currently under consideration by the bank. The company’s counsel stated there has been communication with the bank regarding valuation of the mortgaged property.

Land and building held for sale guidance versus actual plan. The company declared land and building of Rs. 29.82 lakh situated at Daulatpura, Jaipur as held for sale since long ago. However, management did not provide any future plan to execute the sale, and the auditor referenced Ind AS 105 requirements regarding availability for immediate sale and commitment to a plan.

Interest provision decision versus financial reporting. Management decided not to make interest provisions on the NPA-declared bank loan account since the NPA declaration on 1 July 2022. This decision is reflected in the financial results, where finance cost for Q1 FY2026-27 was Rs. 14.40 lakh, compared with Rs. 0.44 lakh in the same quarter a year earlier, and Rs. 48.85 lakh for the full year FY2025-26.
