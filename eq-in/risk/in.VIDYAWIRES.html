Headwinds and Challenges

  • Regulatory filing deficiencies. On May 22, 2026, the National Stock Exchange flagged deficiencies in the financial results Vidya Wires filed on May 12, 2026. The company acknowledged that the mandatory “balancing figures” note for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 was inadvertently omitted, that its notes incorrectly described the audited annual results as “unaudited” with a “Limited Review” (a typographical carryover from earlier quarterly templates), and that a single UDIN had been used for both the standalone and consolidated audit reports instead of separate UDINs as required by ICAI guidelines. The company stated it rectified all three issues in a revised filing.
  • Income tax demand. The Income Tax Department raised a total tax demand of Rs 247.26 million for the block period April 1, 2018 to April 23, 2025. The company filed an appeal before the Commissioner of Income-tax (Appeals) on June 26, 2026.
  • Working capital absorption. Trade receivables rose from Rs 1,423.15 million to Rs 2,027.75 million and inventories from Rs 906.43 million to Rs 1,298.33 million between March 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026 on a consolidated basis. Consolidated net cash from operating activities was negative at Rs (92.90) million for FY2026, despite positive profit before tax of Rs 779.45 million.
  • Rising finance costs. Consolidated finance costs increased to Rs 128.00 million in FY2026 from Rs 113.51 million in FY2025.
  • No dividend. The Board decided not to recommend any final dividend for FY2025-26.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong revenue and profit growth. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to Rs 18,396.39 million in FY2026 from Rs 14,807.72 million in FY2025. Consolidated profit for the year rose to Rs 576.55 million from Rs 405.58 million. On a standalone basis, revenue rose to Rs 18,297.09 million from Rs 14,791.19 million and profit to Rs 600.96 million from Rs 410.54 million.
  • Momentum continuing into FY2027. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, unaudited consolidated net profit was Rs 171.24 million, compared with Rs 120.74 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Net sales were Rs 5,474.95 million, EBITDA Rs 252.25 million, PBT Rs 231.53 million, and EPS Rs 0.81.
  • IPO and listing. The company listed on BSE and NSE on December 10, 2025 following an IPO of 57.69 million equity shares at Rs 52 per share, aggregating Rs 3,000.05 million, comprising an offer for sale of 5,001,000 shares (Rs 260.05 million) and a fresh issue of 52,692,307 shares (Rs 2,740.00 million). Paid-up equity capital rose from Rs 160.00 million to Rs 212.69 million.
  • Capital expenditure programme. Consolidated capital work in progress rose to Rs 723.39 million from Rs 34.72 million, and property, plant and equipment to Rs 705.87 million from Rs 410.10 million. IPO proceeds are earmarked for a new project in the subsidiary, repayment of borrowings, and general corporate purposes.
  • Subsidiary investment. The company approved an investment of INR 125,00,00,000 in ALCU Industries Private Limited through 1% Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares. ALCU Industries is the company’s direct subsidiary.
  • Balance sheet strengthening. Consolidated total equity rose to Rs 4,798.29 million from Rs 1,662.79 million, and cash and cash equivalents to Rs 726.07 million from Rs 7.48 million. Consolidated borrowings fell, with current borrowings down to Rs 702.74 million from Rs 1,272.19 million.

Key Risks

  • The income tax demand of Rs 247.26 million for the block period 2018–2025 remains under appeal; the outcome is unresolved.
  • Operating cash flows were negative in FY2026 even as profits grew, reflecting heavy investment in receivables and inventories.
  • The capital expenditure programme is at an early stage, with substantial capital work in progress and unutilised IPO proceeds, exposing the company to execution and deployment risk.
  • The company has only one reportable primary business segment as per IND AS 108, implying limited diversification.
  • The regulatory deficiencies in the May 12, 2026 filing indicate disclosure and compliance process weaknesses that required correction.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Management characterised the May 12, 2026 submission as audited standalone and consolidated results for the full year ended March 31, 2026, and stated the mathematical calculations were accurate despite the omitted balancing figures note. The auditors issued an unmodified opinion on both the standalone and consolidated results.
  • Management stated that separate UDINs were generated for the standalone and consolidated audit reports after the error was identified. The standalone report carries UDIN 26122137Q0WKGL6228 and the consolidated report UDIN 26122137WNATPY2998.
  • On IPO proceeds utilisation as at March 31, 2026, the consolidated disclosure showed Rs 1,400.00 million earmarked for the subsidiary’s capital expenditure project with Rs 939.60 million utilised and Rs 460.40 million unutilised; Rs 1,000.00 million earmarked for repayment/prepayment of borrowings fully utilised; and Rs 340.00 million for general corporate purposes/IPO expenses with Rs 331.00 million utilised and Rs 9.00 million unutilised.
  • The company scheduled its 44th Annual General Meeting for Friday, September 18, 2026, with e-voting information, and confirmed no final dividend for FY2025-26.
