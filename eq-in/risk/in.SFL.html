Sheela Foam Limited — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • The company’s investor communication notes that schedules for analyst/investor interactions may change due to unforeseen circumstances involving analysts, investors, or the company.
  • The Q1 FY27 results include a gain of ₹6.26 crore from the sale of land and building at Haridwar, Uttarakhand, classified under exceptional items, indicating that reported profitability benefited from a one-time asset disposal rather than purely operating performance.
  • Standalone performance lagged the consolidated picture: standalone revenue grew 20% to ₹761 crore and standalone EBITDA grew 13% to ₹68 crore, a slower pace than the consolidated 26% revenue and 45% EBITDA growth.
  • Standalone net profit margin for Q1 FY27 stood at 5.8%, down from 9.2% in the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and standalone operating margin declined to 34.0% from 39.9% in the prior quarter.
  • Consolidated net profit margin for Q1 FY27 was 6.0%, compared with 8.7% in the quarter ended March 31, 2026.
  • Consolidated finance costs of ₹33.88 crore and depreciation of ₹46.12 crore in Q1 FY27 reflect a continuing fixed-cost burden, though both were lower year-on-year.
  • The company carries outstanding unsecured non-convertible debentures of ₹181.25 crore as of June 30, 2026, down from ₹362.50 crore as of March 31, 2026 and ₹543.75 crore as of June 30, 2025.
  • Consolidated debt-equity ratio stood at 0.27 times as of June 30, 2026, and the long-term debt to working capital ratio was negative at (2.03) times.
  • The consolidated current ratio was 0.81 times as of June 30, 2026, below 1.0.
  • The company’s stake in House of Kieraya Limited (Furlenco) was reduced to 34.53% during FY26 following a fresh issue of shares by that entity, and Furlenco Global Pte. Ltd. ceased to be a subsidiary of the jointly controlled entity effective April 1, 2026.
  • The auditor’s review of consolidated results relied on other auditors for nine subsidiaries, whose financial results reflected total revenues of ₹284.14 crore and net profit after tax of ₹15.22 crore for the quarter, and on conversion adjustments for overseas subsidiaries.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Consolidated revenue for Q1 FY27 grew 26% year-on-year to ₹1,032 crore from ₹821 crore, crossing ₹1,000 crore in the first quarter of a year for the first time.
  • Consolidated EBITDA rose 45% year-on-year to ₹109 crore from ₹75 crore, crossing ₹100 crore in Q1 for the first time, with margin expanding 139 basis points to 10.6% from 9.2%.
  • Consolidated PAT was ₹62 crore, up from ₹7 crore in Q1 FY26, described as a 9.5x increase, with consolidated cash EPS of ₹10.3.
  • E-commerce sales grew 69% year-on-year on brand.com and 19% year-on-year on marketplace platforms.
  • The mattress segment registered 6% volume growth and 15% value growth year-on-year; the foam segment grew 4% by volume and 26% by value.
  • Revenue growth was attributed to higher value growth in both mattress and foam segments, with margin expansion supported by value-accretive growth and operating leverage.
  • The company holds approximately 30% market share in the Indian branded mattresses segment and 40% market share in Australia, with brands Sleepwell and Kurlon reaching all Indian states through a network of over 11,000 retail touchpoints.
  • The company operates across Asia, Australia, and Europe, with overseas revenue of ₹262.73 crore in Q1 FY27, up from ₹184.85 crore in Q1 FY26.
  • The Chairman and Managing Director stated that both mattress and foam businesses sustained momentum built through FY26, that e-commerce and U2O initiatives continue to gain scale, and that the company is well positioned to carry this momentum forward, remaining committed to sustained growth with adequate, consistent profitability while building resilience and long-term value.
  • The board recommended the appointment of Ms. Avantika Singh Gautam as Non-Executive Non-Independent Director for five years from August 4, 2026, with the board stating her appointment would strengthen the board through professional expertise, strategic insight, and a next-generation perspective, and support long-term leadership succession planning.
  • The company paid a final dividend of ₹1 per share (20% on a face value of ₹5 per share) for FY26, approved at the AGM on July 16, 2026 and paid on July 30, 2026.

  • Management states that all forward-looking statements are based on current expectations, estimates, and projections, and that SFL undertakes no obligation to update or revise any forward-looking statements.
  • The company’s results include exceptional items — a net gain of ₹7.93 crore in FY26 from sale of land and buildings at Roorkee, Dabaspet, and Jhagadia, and a ₹6.26 crore gain in Q1 FY27 from the Haridwar land and building sale — indicating reported figures include non-recurring items.
  • The consolidated current ratio of 0.81 times and negative long-term debt to working capital ratio of (2.03) times as of June 30, 2026 indicate working capital dynamics that warrant attention.
  • The company’s consolidated results depend on subsidiaries reviewed by other auditors, including nine subsidiaries not reviewed by the primary auditor, and on conversion of overseas subsidiaries’ financial results from local accounting principles to Indian accounting principles.
  • The company’s investment in House of Kieraya Limited (Furlenco) has seen its equity stake change from 17.70% (August 2023) to 43.89% (FY25) to 34.53% (FY26), reflecting dilution from fresh share issuance by that entity.
  • The company’s dividend communication notes that if withholding tax is deducted at a higher rate, shareholders may file a return of income and claim a refund, and that no claim shall lie against the company for any taxes deducted.
  • The company reserves the right to reject documents submitted by shareholders for beneficial tax rates in case of discrepancies or incomplete documentation.

  • Management’s forward-looking statements are qualified by the statement that they are based on current expectations, estimates, and projections, and that SFL undertakes no obligation to update or revise them.
  • The company scheduled a one-on-one virtual meeting with investors on September 17, 2026, with no unpublished price sensitive information intended to be discussed.
  • The postal ballot for approval of Ms. Avantika Singh Gautam’s appointment as Non-Executive Non-Independent Director commenced e-voting on August 21, 2026 and was to continue until September 19, 2026, with the resolution deemed passed on the last date of e-voting if approved by the requisite majority.

Broker Narrative

The broker stayed Buy through both reports, but the narrative shifted from acquisition-led market-share expansion and margin synergies in 2023 to defending organic declines and awaiting cost synergies in 2024. Persistent themes were branded mattress demand, Kurlon/Kurl-on integration benefits, and input-cost/competition risks. What changed was the acknowledgment of B2B price-led revenue erosion, international B2B weakness, and an EBITDA margin below the ~10% target.

Fears that came true

  • Input cost volatility materialized in the form of B2B price corrections: organic consolidated revenue declined 10% YoY, furniture foam/technical foam revenues fell 14%/9% YoY, and Australia/Spain B2B revenues fell 17%/22% YoY despite broadly flattish volumes.

Optimism that failed

  • The China+1 sourcing tailwind for US/Europe did not prevent international B2B revenue declines in Australia and Spain.
  • The expected synergy/efficiency margin uplift was insufficient to stop consolidated EBITDA margin from falling ~120bps YoY to 9.5%.
