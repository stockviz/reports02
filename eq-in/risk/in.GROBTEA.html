GROBTEA: Recent Corporate Announcements — Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Tax provisioning lapse: The company has not ascertained, and consequently not provided for, current tax and deferred tax for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. This is not in accordance with Ind AS 12.
  • Stalled LED lights business: The company entered the LED lights business in an earlier year, with total investments aggregating to Rs. 119.77 Lakhs, entirely comprising stock-in-trade carried at Net Realisable Value. There has been no sale of LED products during the last five years.
  • Regulatory filing defect: The Board approved making an application to the Registrar of Companies (ROC), West Bengal, to mark Form ADT-1 filed vide SRN AC5514425 as defective, and to file a revised Form ADT-1 with correct particulars. Mr. Mukesh Kumar Agarwal (Director) was authorized to handle related documentation before the ROC.
  • Board and committee churn: Independent Director Mr. Niraj Kumar Harodia resigned effective August 12, 2026. The Board approved the reconstitution of the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, and Independent Director’s Committee.
  • Related-party resolutions saw dissent: At the AGM held August 11, 2026, the resolutions on the re-appointment of Mr. Pradeep Kumar Agarwal as Managing Director and on increasing his remuneration limit recorded 204 votes against (0.0225% and 0.0606% of votes polled, respectively), alongside invalid promoter/promoter group votes (40,660 in each case). The appointments of Mr. Kishan Kumar Kejriwal and Ms. Nidhi Shah as Independent Directors, and the related-party transaction with M/s K L Support Private Limited, each recorded 549 votes against (0.0580% of votes polled). The K L Support resolution also recorded invalid promoter/promoter group votes of 4,300.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Acquisition of Bazaloni Group Limited: Subsequent to the reporting period, the company entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated July 1, 2026, to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of Bazaloni Group Limited for a total investment of Rs. 72.16 Cr. Bazaloni Group Limited became a Wholly Owned Subsidiary as of July 28, 2026. The stated purpose is to strengthen business operations and expand the company’s presence in the tea industry.
  • Quarterly profitability: The Board approved the unaudited financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, reporting revenue from operations of Rs. 2,285.59 Lakhs and total comprehensive income of Rs. 377.15 Lakhs.
  • Dividend continuity: The AGM approved a dividend of Rs. 2/- per equity share of face value Rs. 10/- each for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.
  • Board refresh: The AGM approved the appointments of Mr. Kishan Kumar Kejriwal (DIN: 00362377) and Ms. Nidhi Shah (DIN: 00842660) as Independent Directors, and the re-appointment of Mrs. Indra Agarwal (DIN: 00704025) as a director retiring by rotation.
  • Governance and business approvals: Shareholders approved the appointment of the Statutory Auditor, ratification of Cost Auditor remuneration for FY 2026-2027, and material related-party transactions with M/s Banka Enterprises Private Limited and M/s K L Support Private Limited.

3. Key Risks

  • Accounting compliance risk: The absence of current and deferred tax provisioning for the June 30, 2026 quarter is not in accordance with Ind AS 12, which may affect the reliability of the reported results and invite regulatory or audit scrutiny.
  • Inventory realization risk: The LED lights business holds Rs. 119.77 Lakhs of stock-in-trade carried at Net Realisable Value, with no sales in the last five years, exposing the company to potential write-downs or non-recovery.
  • Regulatory filing risk: The need to refile Form ADT-1 as defective indicates a compliance lapse in statutory auditor appointment filings with the ROC.
  • Related-party concentration risk: Shareholders approved material related-party transactions with M/s Banka Enterprises Private Limited and M/s K L Support Private Limited, and the K L Support resolution attracted invalid promoter votes and dissent.
  • Key personnel and board stability risk: The resignation of an Independent Director and the reconstitution of three committees shortly after the quarter-end, alongside resolutions on the Managing Director’s re-appointment and remuneration increase that drew minority dissent, point to governance and continuity considerations.
  • Acquisition integration risk: The Rs. 72.16 Cr acquisition of Bazaloni Group Limited is a significant deployment of capital, and its integration into the tea business carries execution risk.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: The company states that the acquisition of Bazaloni Group Limited is intended to strengthen business operations and expand its presence in the tea industry.
  • Observed performance: The June 30, 2026 quarter recorded revenue from operations of Rs. 2,285.59 Lakhs and total comprehensive income of Rs. 377.15 Lakhs. However, this performance was reported without current and deferred tax provisioning, which is not in accordance with Ind AS 12, so the reported income does not reflect the tax charge that would otherwise apply.
  • Guidance: The company entered the LED lights business in an earlier year with investments of Rs. 119.77 Lakhs.
  • Observed performance: There have been no sales of LED products during the last five years, and the entire amount remains as stock-in-trade carried at Net Realisable Value, indicating the venture has not delivered commercial traction.
  • Guidance: The Board moved to correct the Form ADT-1 filing by marking the earlier filing defective and filing a revised form.
  • Observed performance: The original filing vide SRN AC5514425 required correction, and Mr. Mukesh Kumar Agarwal was authorized to handle the related documentation before the ROC.
  • Guidance: The AGM Notice dated May 13, 2026, proposed the re-appointment of Mr. Pradeep Kumar Agarwal as Managing Director and an increase in his remuneration limit.
  • Observed performance: Both special resolutions passed with the requisite majority, but each recorded 204 votes against and 40,660 invalid promoter/promoter group votes, indicating partial shareholder dissent on these items.
