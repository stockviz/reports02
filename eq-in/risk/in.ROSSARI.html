Broker Narrative

The narrative began with a cautious Accumulate: near-term price declines, a planned shutdown/SAP disruption, TSC destocking, Tristar weakness in Europe, subdued AHN volumes and Unitop agrochemical challenges were acknowledged, but volume growth, HPPC, FY24 margin guidance, Unitop utilisation and Dahej/Tristar recovery drove optimism. Across 2023-2026 the broker repeatedly stayed Buy/Accumulate even as actual returns were mostly sharply negative, so the same growth/margin optimism persisted despite poor outcomes. By the last report the story had shifted to export momentum in HPPC/textile chemicals, domestic revival, sustainable EBITDA margin and value-added products, while risks rotated from pricing/destocking/Europe/agrochem to freight, raw-material volatility, EO availability and an extended textile-chemicals slowdown.

Fears that came true

  • Early price declines across the portfolio and the ~6% YoY revenue fall materialised into persistent weak performance, with the first Accumulate call showing -48.1% actual and later Buy calls repeatedly delivering large negative returns.
  • The Textile Specialty Chemicals destocking flagged in 1QFY24 persisted into the last report’s ‘extended slowdown in textile chemicals’, coinciding with numerous DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes.
  • Global agrochemical challenges affecting Unitop were flagged early and did not fade as a drag; subsequent 2024-25 Buy calls continued to disappoint badly.
  • Tristar’s muted performance from weak European demand was an early concern, and the expected sequential improvement did not prevent later DISAPPOINTMENTs.

Optimism that failed

  • Management’s FY24 guidance for low-double-digit revenue growth and ~14% EBITDA margin, implying ~16% EBITDA growth, failed to translate into positive stock outcomes; actual returns after the first report were deeply negative.
  • The ~20% volume-growth optimism did not protect investors, as almost every Buy/Accumulate call from 2023 onward ended in DISAPPOINTMENT.
  • Unitop’s ~85-90% utilisation and expected momentum failed to offset agrochemical weakness, with later Buy calls still disappointing.
  • Tristar’s expected sequential improvement and the Dahej ramp-up/mix-improvement triggers did not produce the predicted upside; actual returns remained negative.
  • The early tailwind that key raw-material prices had returned to pre-Covid levels failed, with the last report flagging volatility in raw-material prices as a headwind.
