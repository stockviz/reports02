Headwinds and Challenges

  • Exceptional charges and impairments. The standalone results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 include an impairment provision of ₹2,847 lakhs relating to the carrying value of one subsidiary of Subex Assurance LLP, based on an external expert valuation. The standalone statement also shows a share of loss from Subex Assurance LLP before exceptional items of ₹110 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, ₹13 lakhs for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, ₹150 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2025, and ₹787 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  • New labour codes. Effective November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified four labour codes replacing 29 existing labour laws. The incremental impact of gratuity and compensated absences was presented as an exceptional item: ₹20 lakhs for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and ₹448 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026 on a standalone basis; ₹16 lakhs and ₹466 lakhs respectively on a consolidated basis.
  • Impairment allowances on trade receivables. The consolidated results show impairment allowances of ₹187 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, ₹142 lakhs for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, and ₹35 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Board and management changes. Mr. Anil Chandanmal Singhvi ceased to hold office effective September 29, 2025 after his reappointment resolution at the 31st AGM was not approved by shareholders. Ms. Poornima Kamalaksh Prabhu and Ms. Bottolanda Archana Muthappa ceased to be Independent Directors on the same date following their resignations. Mr. Venkata Erinti Narayana and Mr. Alok Ohrie were approved as Independent Directors via postal ballot dated February 10, 2026, and Mr. Stephane Raymond Marie Le Letty was appointed as an Additional Non-Executive Non-Independent Director on March 25, 2026.
  • Risks cited in the investor presentation. Fluctuations in earnings, ability to successfully integrate acquisitions, competition in business areas, client concentration, liability for damages in contracts, withdrawal of tax incentives, political instability, unauthorized use of intellectual property, and general economic conditions affecting the industry.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Q1 FY27 performance. Consolidated revenue was ₹7,945 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with ₹7,296 lakhs in the prior quarter and ₹6,640 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Normalized EBITDA was ₹1,687 lakhs versus ₹1,058 lakhs sequentially and ₹428 lakhs year-on-year. Profit before tax was ₹1,646 lakhs and profit after tax was ₹1,422 lakhs.
  • Management commentary. MD & CEO Nisha Dutt stated that the company delivered 8.9% sequential topline growth and 19.6% year-on-year growth, with EBITDA margin at 21.2%, attributed to disciplined execution, operational efficiencies, and accelerated conversion of order backlog. She stated the company remains committed to investing in core strengths, deepening customer value, and delivering sustainable, profitable growth through FY27 and beyond.
  • New contract win. Subex secured a three-year contract worth USD 0.6 million with a leading African mobile operator to deploy its HyperSense Fraud Management solutions, expanding its fraud management footprint across Africa and strengthening its position at the intersection of telecom and mobile financial services.
  • Industry opportunity. The investor presentation cites the telco industry being reshaped with $30–40B in new spending every year in a $1.7 trillion industry, telco revenue losses growing at almost 6x industry growth, and a $4.3B TAM where most of it did not exist three years ago.
  • Business scale and retention. The presentation cites 30+ years of domain expertise, 150+ installations in 100+ countries, 92% customer retention, ~70% recurring revenue, 700+ employees globally, and 500B+ records processed per day.
  • Profitability trajectory. The presentation cites a return to profitability from FY23 to FY27 YTD (TTM), with EBITDA improving from (3.9)% to 14.9% and PAT from (18.4)% to 10.2%.
  • ESOP 2026. The Board approved the Subex Employee Stock Option Plan 2026 covering up to 5% of total paid-up equity shares, implemented through the existing Subex Employees Benefit ESOP Trust via secondary acquisition from the open market, subject to shareholder approval. No grants had been made as of the announcement. Vesting is between 12 and 48 months from grant, based solely on achievement of performance parameters, with an exercise period of two years from each vesting.
  • Postal ballot outcome. Shareholders passed four resolutions by September 12, 2026, including implementation of the Subex Employees Stock Option Scheme 2026, granting options to subsidiary employees, authorizing the Subex Employee Welfare and ESOP Benefit Trust for secondary market share acquisition, and granting a loan to the trust.

Key Risks

  • Carrying values of intangible assets, investments in subsidiaries, and goodwill depend on achievement of future growth and profitability. As at March 31, 2026, the company and group concluded, based on external expert valuation, that carrying values were appropriate, with no change in management’s assessment as at June 30, 2026.
  • The standalone results present share of profit and loss from LLPs on a net basis, with exceptional items of the LLPs disclosed separately in the audited standalone results.
  • The group operates as a single business segment, “Software product and related services,” based on the management approach under Ind AS 108.
  • The quarter ended June 30, 2025 consolidated statement was reviewed by another auditor whose report dated August 11, 2025 expressed an unmodified conclusion.
  • The investor presentation states that certain statements concerning future growth prospects are forward-looking statements involving risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management stated the company enters FY27 with the foundation built over the last three years firmly in place and that Q1 reflects continued progress. Observed Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue of ₹7,945 lakhs, normalized EBITDA of ₹1,687 lakhs, profit before tax of ₹1,646 lakhs, and profit after tax of ₹1,422 lakhs are consistent with the stated 8.9% sequential and 19.6% year-on-year topline growth and 21.2% EBITDA margin.
  • Management attributed the improvement to disciplined execution, operational efficiencies, and accelerated conversion of order backlog. The sequential increase in revenue from ₹7,296 lakhs to ₹7,945 lakhs and in normalized EBITDA from ₹1,058 lakhs to ₹1,687 lakhs is consistent with that description.
  • Management stated it remains committed to investing in core strengths, deepening customer value, and delivering sustainable, profitable growth through FY27 and beyond. The three-year USD 0.6 million HyperSense Fraud Management contract with an African mobile operator is an observed order win following that statement.
  • Management stated the engagement will strengthen the operator’s ability to detect and combat emerging fraud across mobile networks and mobile money operations, leveraging HyperSense to identify evolving fraud patterns and enable faster, more effective investigations.
  • The investor presentation cites a return to profitability from FY23 to FY27 YTD (TTM), with EBITDA improving from (3.9)% to 14.9% and PAT from (18.4)% to 10.2%, alongside 92% customer retention and ~70% recurring revenue.
