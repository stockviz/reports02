Natural Capsules Limited (NATCAPSUQ) — Recent Developments Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • US HPMC segment weakness: Erratic demand in the US market for the HPMC segment, compounded by continued uncertainty around duty structures. There were no material US export volumes during the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
  • Currency cross-currents: Concurrent appreciation of the Chinese yuan and depreciation of the Indian rupee created headwinds for the African export business routed through merchant exporters.
  • Geopolitical uncertainty: Ongoing geopolitical uncertainty affecting API business operations.
  • ERP transition disruption: A brief disruption to billing and dispatch cycles due to implementation of a new ERP system towards the close of the quarter.
  • Sequential revenue decline: Q1FY27 revenue of ₹48.71 Cr was down 17% Q-o-Q, and EBITDA of ₹1.12 Cr was down 16% Q-o-Q.
  • Thin margins: EBITDA margin of 2.30% in Q1FY27 indicates limited cushion at the operating level.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Capsule demand and realisations: Sustained demand across capsule products with improved realisations and timely pass-throughs of rising input costs supporting margin preservation; firm demand trends into Q2FY27.
  • Export realisation tailwind: Depreciation of the rupee providing a meaningful tailwind to export realisations.
  • CMO pipeline expansion: Commencement of the first batch under the contract manufacturing agreement with Fermbox Bio in August, and a new contract manufacturing agreement executed with a Mumbai-based client.
  • API backward integration: Successful completion of prednisolone synthesis at gram-scale batches, with the first fully backward-integrated batch expected by the end of September.
  • Process improvement: Successful transition of a fermentation step to an enzymatic route, improving yields and cost optimisation.
  • Regulatory milestone: WHO GMP application process initiated, with an expected audit by the end of Q2FY27.
  • Capacity optimisation: One HPMC line temporarily converted to a gelatine line to optimise capacity utilisation; a second HPMC line expected to be converted in the coming month.
  • Leadership addition: A new Chief Operating Officer appointed for the API business with over 30 years of industry experience.
  • Structural opportunities: China Plus One strategy tailwinds and government incentives under the PLI scheme expected to amount to approximately ₹67 Crores over 6 years, with three PLI scheme approvals in hand.
  • Business profile: NCL is a hard capsule shell manufacturer pioneering vegetarian capsules in India and the second largest Indian manufacturer for gelatin capsules, with installed capacity of 20.25 BCPA as of FY26 across Bengaluru and Pondicherry units; it exports to 28 countries across 5 continents and derives 80% of turnover from repeat business. The company is foraying into API manufacturing through subsidiary Natural Biogenex Private Limited (NBPL), focused on steroidal APIs including Prednisolone, Betamethasone, Dexamethasone, and Hydrocortisone.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued uncertainty around US duty structures and erratic HPMC demand, with no material US export volumes in the quarter.
  • Currency movements (Chinese yuan appreciation alongside rupee depreciation) pressuring the African export business routed through merchant exporters.
  • Geopolitical uncertainty affecting API operations.
  • Execution risk around the API scale-up and backward-integration timeline, including the first fully backward-integrated prednisolone batch targeted for end-September.
  • Dependence on regulatory outcomes, notably the WHO GMP audit expected by end of Q2FY27.
  • Margin vulnerability given Q1FY27 EBITDA margin of 2.30% and a Q-o-Q revenue decline of 17%.
  • Operational disruption risk from systems transitions, as seen with the new ERP implementation.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Gelatine capsule profitability: Management remains confident of sustaining gelatine capsule profitability levels through the remainder of the year and achieving sustained performance in the capsules business through the financial year. Observed Q1FY27 performance showed EBITDA margin of 2.30% (up 2 BPS Q-o-Q, up 159 BPS Y-o-Y) and EBITDA up 251% Y-o-Y, though down 16% Q-o-Q.
  • Demand trends: Management notes firm demand trends into Q2FY27 across the capsules business and improved visibility across both segments entering Q2FY27. Q1FY27 revenue was ₹48.71 Cr, down 17% Q-o-Q but up 8% Y-o-Y.
  • API backward integration: Management expects to produce the first fully backward-integrated prednisolone batch by the end of September, following successful gram-scale prednisolone synthesis.
  • Regulatory: WHO GMP audit expected by the end of Q2FY27, with the application process already initiated.
  • Capacity actions: One HPMC line was temporarily converted to a gelatine line due to US market conditions; a second conversion is expected in the coming month.
  • Profitability trajectory: Q1FY27 PAT was ₹5.74 Cr (up 15% Q-o-Q, up 3% Y-o-Y) with EPS of ₹5.55 (up 15% Q-o-Q, up 3% Y-o-Y), even as revenue declined sequentially.
  • Capital actions: The Board approved a preferential issue of up to 1,12,500 equity shares and 4,50,000 convertible warrants at ₹178/- each to Mr. Sunil L Mundra (promoter/promoter group), with an Extraordinary General Meeting scheduled for September 9, 2026. At the 1st Extraordinary General Meeting held September 09, 2026, two special resolutions regarding issuance of equity shares and warrants convertible into equity shares on a preferential basis were passed with the requisite majority. Separately, on July 10, 2026, material unlisted subsidiary Natural Biogenex Private Limited approved a rights issue of 11,16,766 equity shares of ₹10/- each at par, aggregating to ₹1,11,67,660.
