Greenply Industries — Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Joint venture losses. The furniture hardware JV, Greenply Samet, continued to drag on consolidated profitability. In Q1 FY27, the JV recorded revenue of Rs 13.61 crores (100% basis) but a share of PAT loss of Rs 5.7 crores (Greenply’s 50% share). This compares with a loss share of Rs 5.4 crores in Q1 FY26 and Rs 6.5 crores in Q4 FY26. Equity investment by Greenply in the JV stood at Rs 105 crores as of the quarter. The consolidated share of loss from equity-accounted investees was Rs 5.7 crores in Q1 FY27, versus Rs 9.1 crores in Q1 FY26 (which included GMEL Dubai and Greenply Holdings Singapore losses) and Rs 6.6 crores in Q4 FY26.

Sequential decline in consolidated performance. Although consolidated revenue grew 20.7% year-on-year to Rs 724.9 crores in Q1 FY27, it declined 6.6% quarter-on-quarter from Rs 776.2 crores in Q4 FY26. Core EBITDA fell 16.0% QoQ to Rs 78.3 crores, and core EBITDA margin contracted 120 bps QoQ to 10.8% from 12.0%.

Plywood segment sequential softness. India Plywood revenue declined 10.5% QoQ to Rs 526.6 crores, with sales volume down 14.9% QoQ to 19.4 MSM. Core EBITDA fell 27.3% QoQ to Rs 44.5 crores, and core EBITDA margin contracted 200 bps QoQ to 8.4% from 10.4%.

Rising debt. Consolidated gross debt increased to Rs 554 crores in June 2026 from Rs 489 crores in March 2026, with net debt rising to Rs 533 crores from Rs 461 crores. Net debt-to-equity rose to 0.57x from 0.52x. Ply business debt increased to Rs 180 crores from Rs 120 crores.

Lower other income and forex volatility. Other income fell 86.2% YoY to Rs 1.8 crores in Q1 FY27 from Rs 13.2 crores in Q1 FY26. Foreign exchange movements created swings: a gain of Rs 0.7 crores in Q1 FY27 versus a loss of Rs 8.9 crores in Q1 FY26 and a loss of Rs 3.2 crores in Q4 FY26.

Exceptional items. Q1 FY27 consolidated results included an exceptional loss of Rs 49.5 crores, compared with a gain of Rs 4.43 crores in Q1 FY26 and a loss of Rs 15.2 crores in Q4 FY26. The India Plywood business reported no exceptional item in Q1 FY27, versus a loss of Rs 1.2 crores in Q1 FY26 and Rs 15.2 crores in Q4 FY26.

MDF PAT pressure excluding forex. MDF PAT without forex declined 5.0% YoY to Rs 16.1 crores in Q1 FY27 from Rs 16.9 crores, and fell 3.9% QoQ from Rs 16.7 crores.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Strong year-on-year consolidated growth. Consolidated revenue rose 20.7% YoY to Rs 724.9 crores in Q1 FY27. Core EBITDA grew 27.1% YoY to Rs 78.3 crores, with margin expanding 50 bps YoY to 10.8%. Profit before tax and exceptional items rose 55.8% YoY to Rs 49.5 crores. Profit after tax rose 32.2% YoY to Rs 37.6 crores.

Plywood volume and realisation gains. India Plywood sales volume grew 13.8% YoY to 19.4 MSM, and realisation improved 3.8% YoY to Rs 265 per sqm. Revenue grew 16.0% YoY to Rs 526.6 crores. Core EBITDA rose 23.5% YoY to Rs 44.5 crores, with margin up 50 bps YoY to 8.4%. PAT before exceptional items rose 22.7% YoY to Rs 26.6 crores.

MDF business momentum. MDF sales volume grew 24.7% YoY to 57,805 CBM, and realisation rose 5.5% YoY to Rs 33,525 per CBM. Revenue grew 32.8% YoY to Rs 195.7 crores. Core EBITDA rose 32.2% YoY to Rs 33.9 crores. Reported PAT more than doubled YoY to Rs 16.8 crores, aided by a forex gain versus a loss in the prior year. Pre-lam boards revenue grew 61.3% YoY to Rs 42.9 crores, and MDF boards revenue grew 25.1% YoY to Rs 150.9 crores.

Capacity expansion. Greenply’s plywood and allied own manufacturing capacity totals 66.3 Mn SQMpa across Bamanbore (20.2), Sandila (13.5), Kriparampur (11.0), Tizit (8.1), and Odisha (13.5, work in progress). MDF capacity includes Vadodara at 3,00,000 CBM per annum, with a second Vadodara line of 2,10,000 CBM per annum under construction. The company approved a corporate guarantee of up to INR 200 crores for its wholly-owned subsidiary Greenply Speciality Panels Pvt. Ltd. to support expansion of that subsidiary’s MDF plant.

JV restructuring to free up bandwidth. Greenply executed a binding Head of Terms with Samet B.V. and Greenply Samet Private Limited for Samet to invest USD 30 million to USD 40 million over two to three years. Samet’s voting interest will rise from 50% to approximately 81%, while Greenply’s voting interest will dilute from 50% to approximately 19% (approximately 18.98% per the corporate guarantee disclosure). Greenply’s economic interest will initially be approximately 43% and reduce over time. The JV will cease to be an associate company, and Greenply will no longer consolidate its financial results or provide further equity funding. This is intended to deploy financial bandwidth into core Plywood and MDF portfolios. The transaction is expected to be completed by January 2027.

Working capital improvement. Consolidated working capital days improved to 42 in June 2026 from 58 in June 2025, though marginally higher than 43 in March 2026.

Brand and marketing initiatives. Campaigns target premium consumers (Green), unbranded-to-branded conversion (Ecotec), and OEM/institutional segments (MDF). The company has associations with the IPL, contractor community engagement programmes, architect outreach, and sustainability initiatives. Awards cited include Great Place to Work (2020, 2021, 2022), India’s Most Trusted Brand (2022), Best Green Manufacturing Company (2022), Economic Times Sustainable Organisations Award (2023), and a Guinness World Record (2026).

JV dealer expansion. New dealer appointments in Q1 FY27 were 127, bringing total active dealers to 686 at the end of June 2026.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued losses at the Greenply Samet JV, with a share of PAT loss of Rs 5.7 crores in Q1 FY27 and cumulative equity investment of Rs 105 crores.
  • Rising consolidated debt (gross debt Rs 554 crores, net debt Rs 533 crores as of June 2026) and net D/E of 0.57x.
  • Forex exposure on borrowings, which caused significant swings in reported PAT (a gain of Rs 0.7 crores in Q1 FY27 versus a loss of Rs 8.8 crores in Q1 FY26 for MDF).
  • Exceptional items of Rs 49.5 crores loss in Q1 FY27 at the consolidated level.
  • Sequential margin compression in both consolidated and plywood segments.
  • Execution risk on capacity expansion projects, including the Odisha plywood facility (work in progress) and the Vadodara MDF Line 2 (work in progress).
  • The corporate guarantee of up to INR 200 crores for Greenply Speciality Panels Pvt. Ltd. creates contingent liability.
  • The JV restructuring is subject to completion, expected by January 2027, and involves dilution of Greenply’s voting and economic interest.
  • Forward-looking statements in the analyst presentation are subject to risks including changes in industry structure, political and economic environment in India and overseas, tax laws, import duties, litigation, and labour relations.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

JV restructuring. Management executed a binding Head of Terms with Samet B.V. for Samet to invest USD 30 million to USD 40 million over two to three years, with completion expected by January 2027. Observed performance: the JV continued to report losses in Q1 FY27 (Rs 5.7 crores share of PAT loss), and Greenply’s equity investment reached Rs 105 crores. The JV will cease to be an associate company and will no longer be consolidated.

Capacity expansion. Management is expanding MDF capacity through Greenply Speciality Panels Pvt. Ltd. (Vadodara Line 2, 2,10,000 CBM per annum, work in progress) and plywood capacity at Odisha (13.5 Mn SQMpa, work in progress). A corporate guarantee of up to INR 200 crores was approved for the MDF expansion. Observed performance: MDF volumes grew 24.7% YoY and revenue grew 32.8% YoY in Q1 FY27, indicating strong demand for existing capacity.

Financial bandwidth reallocation. Management stated the JV restructuring will allow deployment of financial bandwidth into core Plywood and MDF portfolios, and that Greenply will cease loss funding and capital expenditure obligations toward the JV. Observed performance: Plywood revenue grew 16.0% YoY and MDF revenue grew 32.8% YoY in Q1 FY27, while consolidated debt rose QoQ.

Investor engagement. Management will interact with investors and analysts at the Anand Rathi Annual Flagship Conference G-200 Summit 2026 on 21st September 2026, from 10:30 A.M. to 6:00 P.M. IST at Taj Santacruz, Mumbai. No Unpublished Price Sensitive Information will be shared, and the schedule is subject to change.

AGM outcomes. The 36th AGM was held on August 25, 2026, where shareholders approved financial statements, declared a final dividend of Re. 0.50 per share (50%), re-appointed Mr. Sanidhya Mittal and Ms. Vinita Bajoria, and appointed Mr. Girish Kulkarni as an Independent Director.

Broker Narrative

The narrative remained structurally bullish throughout, consistently centred on volume growth, MDF expansion and improving margins while repeatedly acknowledging demand softness and input-cost pressure. Early confidence that the capex cycle was complete and debt would decline gave way to a fresh ₹500 crore capex programme and leverage expected to peak at 0.7x D/E. Raw-material worries shifted from elevated timber prices and Gabon/Myanmar uncertainty to resin/imported chemical costs and the loss-making Samet JV. The focus on margin expansion persisted, but actual EBITDA margin in Q1FY27 (10.8%) only matched the FY24E target rather than improving to the FY25E forecast of 11.5%.

Fears that came true

  • The early warning about premium-segment demand slowdown recurred as later reports cited continued demand softness, and several post-2025 Buy calls ended as DISAPPOINTMENT outcomes.
  • Raw material cost pressure materialised: early elevated timber prices were followed by spikes in resin and imported chemical costs, keeping margins under pressure.
  • High debt levels did not reduce as anticipated; instead, leverage was expected to peak at 0.7x D/E to fund another major capex programme.

Optimism that failed

  • The expectation that major capex was completed and debt repayment would begin proved wrong, as a fresh ₹500 crore capex cycle and peak leverage were announced.
  • The projected margin/return expansion underdelivered: consolidated EBITDA margin in Q1FY27 was 10.8%, unchanged from the FY24E level and below the 11.5% targeted for FY25E.
