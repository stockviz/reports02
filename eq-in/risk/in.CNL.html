Creative Newtech Limited – Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Transition execution risk in surveillance: The proposed acquisition of Infinova (India) Private Limited (approved by the Board on 13 July 2026) is intended to shift the company from a “primarily distribution-led model to a more integrated, technology-led surveillance business platform.” This transition involves integrating manufacturing, brand rights, an experienced team, and service networks — a materially different operating model from distribution.
  • Acquisition not yet completed: The transaction remains subject to satisfactory due diligence, necessary approvals, execution of definitive agreements, and fulfilment of closing conditions. The exact completion timeline and final transaction numbers are not yet disclosed.
  • Turnover and financial details of the target undisclosed: Infinova India’s verified turnover for the preceding three financial years and other financial details will only be disclosed after due diligence and finalisation of definitive agreements, leaving limited visibility on the target’s current financial scale.
  • Quarter-on-quarter moderation in profitability: On a standalone basis, profit before tax declined from Rs. 1,633.23 lacs in the quarter ended 31 March 2026 to Rs. 1,217.47 lacs in the quarter ended 30 June 2026. Consolidated profit before tax similarly declined from Rs. 2,172.13 lacs to Rs. 1,670.33 lacs over the same period. Standalone profit for the quarter fell from Rs. 1,240.04 lacs to Rs. 900.67 lacs.
  • Finance costs rising: Standalone finance costs increased to Rs. 747.70 lacs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 713.00 lacs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 295.81 lacs in Q1 FY26. Consolidated finance costs rose to Rs. 762.51 lacs from Rs. 718.47 lacs and Rs. 300.75 lacs respectively.
  • Subsidiaries not reviewed: The consolidated limited review report notes that the results of Creative Peripherals and Distribution Limited (Hong Kong) and Secure Connection Limited (Hong Kong) were not reviewed by the auditors for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
  • Associate losses: Share of loss in associate was Rs. 7.08 lacs in Q1 FY27, compared with Rs. 3.52 lacs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 0.08 lacs in Q1 FY26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Surveillance market growth: The media release cites India’s video surveillance and security solutions market at approximately USD 4.8 billion in 2025, projected to grow to around USD 12.25 billion by 2030, a CAGR of approximately 20–21%, supported by Smart Cities Mission, Safe City Projects, and infrastructure upgrades.
  • Policy tailwinds: Increasing emphasis on trusted, compliant, and domestically manufactured surveillance systems, mandatory certifications, and the Government of India’s Make in India focus are described as accelerating the shift toward locally manufactured and certified solutions.
  • Infinova India acquisition rationale: The proposed acquisition is expected to provide local assembly and manufacturing capability (Pune facility), exclusive Infinova brand rights for India, product ownership, an experienced team with more than two decades of industry experience, established customer and service networks, supplier and OEM relationships, and significant project references (including Mumbai Safe City, Nagpur Smart City, more than 61 airports, Bangalore Metro, Dhaka Metro, IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, ONGC, NTPC, RCF, Bihar and Gujarat Jails and District Courts, Navy installations, and SHAR).
  • Existing surveillance foundation: The company has built partnerships with surveillance brands including Sparsh and Matrix, and is developing a bouquet of surveillance products intended to be BIS and STQC compliant, aligned with Make in India and Sell Global.
  • New brand partnerships: The company is actively engaged in ongoing discussions with significant international brands to establish enduring partnerships and secure distribution rights for additional brands in the upcoming fiscal year, anticipated to enhance revenue streams and contribute to a substantial increase in profitability.
  • Segment performance: Standalone Brand Business revenue rose to Rs. 4,171.64 lacs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 2,701.16 lacs in Q1 FY26. Standalone Market Entry Specialist revenue rose to Rs. 40,579.54 lacs from Rs. 32,570.76 lacs over the same period. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to Rs. 47,607.02 lacs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 39,296.40 lacs in Q1 FY26.
  • Employee alignment: The Board approved the CREATIVE ESOP Scheme – 2026 on 4 August 2026, covering grant of up to 2,00,000 options (not exceeding 1.5% of paid-up capital as on 30 June 2026), exercisable into equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each, subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing AGM.

3. Key Risks

  • Acquisition completion risk: The Infinova India acquisition is subject to due diligence, regulatory and contractual approvals, execution of definitive agreements, and closing conditions. The consideration is to be paid in cash, with the final amount subject to valuation reports and working capital closing adjustments.
  • Integration and execution risk: Building an integrated, technology-led surveillance platform with manufacturing, brand ownership, and after-sales service represents a shift from the company’s distribution-led model.
  • Working capital and balance sheet exposure: Standalone total assets were Rs. 56,923.52 lacs as on 30 June 2026, with total liabilities of Rs. 15,160.31 lacs. Consolidated total assets were Rs. 65,308.15 lacs with total liabilities of Rs. 12,779.53 lacs. Market Entry Specialist segment liabilities rose to Rs. 13,963.80 lacs as on 30 June 2026 from Rs. 8,985.18 lacs as on 31 March 2026.
  • Related party transaction scale: The AGM proposals include related party transactions with Secure Connection Limited up to Rs. 750 Crores.
  • Regulatory and compliance requirements: The acquisition is subject to applicable corporate, statutory, regulatory, and contractual approvals. The surveillance product portfolio is intended to be BIS and STQC compliant.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: The media release notes that certain statements are forward-looking and subject to risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on new brand partnerships: Management anticipates that securing distribution rights for additional international brands in the upcoming fiscal year will enhance revenue streams and contribute to a substantial increase in profitability. Observed performance shows consolidated revenue from operations of Rs. 47,607.02 lacs in Q1 FY27 versus Rs. 39,296.40 lacs in Q1 FY26, and consolidated profit for the period of Rs. 1,353.53 lacs versus Rs. 1,018.49 lacs in Q1 FY26. However, on a sequential basis, consolidated revenue declined from Rs. 74,000.59 lacs in Q4 FY26 to Rs. 47,607.02 lacs in Q1 FY27, and consolidated profit declined from Rs. 1,778.94 lacs to Rs. 1,353.53 lacs.
  • Guidance on surveillance platform transition: Management states the Infinova India acquisition will provide a foundation to progressively build a Make in India surveillance technology platform and move toward technology ownership and manufacturing capability. The transaction had not closed as of the latest disclosures; completion is subject to due diligence, approvals, and definitive agreements.
  • Guidance on ESOP implementation: The ESOP Scheme 2026 is subject to approval of shareholders at the ensuing AGM. The exercise price will be decided by the Committee at the time of grant, linked to market price, with vesting and exercise periods of not earlier than one year and up to five years. No options have vested or been exercised at this stage.
  • Dividend proposal: The Board fixed 23 September 2026 as the record date for determining entitlement to a final dividend for FY ended 31 March 2026, subject to shareholder approval at the AGM on 30 September 2026. The AGM notice proposes a final dividend of Re. 0.50 per share (5% on face value).
  • Investor engagement: The company scheduled an analyst/institutional investor meeting with Equirus India Growth Summit on 13 August 2026, 09:00 AM to 12:00 PM at Sofitel BKC, Mumbai, noting that no unpublished price sensitive information would be shared.
