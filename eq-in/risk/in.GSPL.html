Broker Narrative

The broker’s initial bullish narrative, driven by expected LNG demand growth and capacity expansion, gradually shifted to a cautious Hold as persistent volume declines and higher operating expenses eroded financials. While early reports highlighted the Mehsana-Bhatinda pipeline and Gujarat LNG capacity as growth catalysts, the final report reflects a reality of contracting volumes and downgraded estimates, leading to a reduced target price.

Fears that came true

  • Higher-than-estimated other operating expenses materialized, as Q3FY26 EBITDA fell 14.0% YoY due to higher other expenses.
  • Volume declines flagged early continued, with total transmission volume down 9.9% YoY in 9MFY26 and almost all segments recording YoY declines in Q3FY26.

Optimism that failed

  • The expected CGD demand growth from falling LNG prices did not materialize, as volumes consistently declined year-over-year.
  • The projected expansion of LNG capacity in Gujarat failed to boost transmission volumes, which remained well below initial estimates.
  • The commissioning of the Mehsana-Bhatinda pipeline did not revive volume growth, as transmission volumes dropped 3.5% QoQ in Q3FY26.
