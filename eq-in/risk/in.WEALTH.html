Wealth First Portfolio Managers — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue optics and base effects. Revenue from operations in Q1 FY27 was Rs. 14.3 crore, lower on both a YoY and QoQ basis. The YoY comparison was affected by a higher Q1 FY26 base, which benefited from strong insurance business traction in Q4 FY25 that spilled over into Q1 FY26; in Q1 FY27 all relevant revenue was recognised within the preceding quarter, with no spillover. Q1 FY26 also included Rs. 9 crore of revenue from trading activity, whereas the trading book has been consciously reduced to nil, resulting in no trading income in Q1 FY27.

Margin and cost pressure. Total operating costs rose 51.2% YoY to Rs. 5.0 crore in Q1 FY27. Employee expenses increased on both a YoY and QoQ basis due to continued investment in talent across Wealth First and its group companies, including the AMC and Insurance Broking businesses; on a standalone basis the increase was 24% YoY. Other expenses rose on a consolidated basis due to setup and establishment costs for the AMC and Insurance Broking businesses; standalone other expenses rose 26% YoY. PBT after exceptional items fell 36.1% YoY to Rs. 13.7 crore and PAT fell 34.7% YoY to Rs. 10.4 crore. The cost-to-income ratio increased, including AMC cost, to 36.6% from 21.5% YoY.

Build-out phase. Management describes the company as moving from an investment and setup phase towards execution and scale, with teams, platforms and technology still being built across businesses. The AMC (Lakshya) is described as fully established and poised to commence operations; the insurance broking technology foundation is described as in progress; and the PMS platform is described as fully operational with initial investors onboarded.

Integration and execution. The WFA acquisition is to be completed in two phases, with Phase I (51%) on or before December 2026 and Phase II (49%) in FY30 based on a pre-agreed valuation formula. Key talent and relationship managers are to be retained for a seamless client transition.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Acquisition of Wealth First Advisors (WFA). Announced 1 July 2026, the acquisition of controlling interest in Mumbai-based WFA at an equity valuation of Rs. 102.15 crore takes the combined AUM of the Wealth First and WFA platforms to close to Rs. 9,000 crore. Phase I covers a 51% stake for Rs. 52.1 crore, to be completed on or before December 2026, funded through internal accruals and a share swap; Phase II covers the remaining 49%, based on WFA’s valuation as on 31 March 2029, proposed to be settled entirely through a share swap. WFA was established in 2001, has a 25-year track record, is based in Mumbai with a strong Maharashtra presence, and serves HNI and UHNI clients. For FY26, WFA reported revenue of Rs. 17.9 crore, PAT of Rs. 7.8 crore, assets under advisory of Rs. 3,746 crore, networth of Rs. 22.0 crore, team size of 25, total clients of 2,800+ and 4,400+ client families.

Five-year ambition. Management aims to grow the combined platform to around Rs. 20,000 crore over the next five years, more than double current scale, and to rank among India’s leading independent wealth and asset management firms.

Lakshya Asset Management. A strategic joint venture with 69.7% held by Wealth First and 30.3% by JV partners — Mr. Sanjiv Shah, Mr. Rajan Mehta and Mr. Sanjay Gaitonde, the founding team behind Benchmark AMC. The AMC is positioned around retirement and income solutions, with SEBI’s formal license granted and the entity described as fully established and poised to commence operations.

Wealthshield Insurance Brokers. IRDAI licence received as a Direct Insurance Broker (Life & General), enabling entry into the General Insurance segment. Early traction is described as strong; 30 POSPs have been onboarded against a target of 1,000; the website is live; and core IT infrastructure is in progress.

NRI PMS for US and Canada. A Nifty 100 Index PMS addressing PFIC-related tax and compliance hurdles faced by US and Canadian tax residents. The platform is fully operational, initial investors have been onboarded, and early traction and positive market response are noted.

Operating metrics. Trail-based revenue grew 4.4% YoY to Rs. 12.3 crore in Q1 FY27. Total AUA grew 8.6% YoY and 12.3% QoQ to Rs. 13,647 crore as of June 2026, with the QoQ growth primarily supported by MTM gains in equities. Client families grew 5% YoY to 6,967, with 341 families added over the past year; the overall client base rose 5% YoY to 21,986, with 240 clients added in Q1 FY27. RM count increased 22% YoY, with 53% of RMs associated for more than 5 years and 29% for more than 10 years; 80% of clients have been with the company for more than 5 years.

Industry backdrop. The presentation cites a $2.7 trillion serviceable asset pool in Indian wealth management, specialised wealth managers projected to grow AUM from $300 billion to $1.6 trillion by FY35 at approximately 18% CAGR, and India’s serviceable assets growing from $3 trillion to $9 trillion at ~13% CAGR. A PwC report cited in the 1 July 2026 press release projects India’s asset and wealth management industry nearly doubling to US$1.7 trillion by 2030. Maharashtra accounts for nearly 40% of India’s mutual fund industry.

3. Key Risks

  • Revenue concentration and base-effect sensitivity, as illustrated by the insurance spillover and trading income dynamics affecting the Q1 FY27 YoY comparison.
  • Continued cost build-out in the AMC and Insurance Broking businesses weighing on near-term profitability, with the cost-to-income ratio already elevated.
  • Execution risk in completing the WFA acquisition in two phases, with Phase II contingent on a valuation as on 31 March 2029 and settled through a share swap.
  • Dependence on retention of key talent and relationship managers at WFA for a seamless client transition.
  • Market-linked variability in AUA, with QoQ growth primarily supported by MTM gains in equities.
  • Regulatory and compliance complexity in the NRI PMS offering, given PFIC regulations affecting US and Canadian tax residents.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance provided: - Phase I acquisition of 51% equity shares in WFA expected to be completed on or before 31 December 2026; Phase II of 49% expected to be completed on or before 31 March 2030. - Five-year objective to grow the combined platform to around Rs. 20,000 crore in AUM. - POSP target of 1,000, against 30 onboarded. - Intimations for the 24th AGM, along with the Notice, Record Date and E-voting period, to be given separately in due course.

Observed performance against prior commitments: - Management states it delivered on key commitments set out eighteen months earlier, including reducing the trading book to nil, expanding footprint to Mumbai, launching the index-based PMS for US and Canada-based NRI investors, and establishing Lakshya Asset Management and Wealthshield Insurance Brokers. - The trading book reduction to nil is reflected in Q1 FY27 having no trading income, versus Rs. 9 crore of trading revenue in Q1 FY26. - The Mumbai expansion is reflected in the WFA acquisition, which takes combined AUM close to Rs. 9,000 crore. - The NRI PMS is described as fully operational with initial investors onboarded. - Lakshya is described as fully established with SEBI’s formal license granted, and Wealthshield has received its IRDAI licence with 30 POSPs onboarded. - Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of Rs. 14.3 crore and PAT of Rs. 10.4 crore reflect the transition period, with management attributing the lower YoY figures to the higher Q1 FY26 base and the elimination of trading income rather than to a deterioration in the core trail-based business, which grew 4.4% YoY.
