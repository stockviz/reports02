AVT Natural Products Limited — Analysis of Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Seasonality of the sector: The company explicitly notes that, in view of the seasonality of the sector, the financial results for a quarter are not indicative of the full year’s expected performance. This means quarterly results can fluctuate significantly and should not be read as a run-rate.
  • Inventory build-up: Changes in inventories of finished goods, work-in-progress and stock-in-trade rose sharply to Rs. 3,605.80 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 (standalone), compared with Rs. 1,446.20 lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026 and Rs. 3,216.65 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2025. On a consolidated basis, the figure was Rs. 3,934.78 lakhs for 30.06.2026. This represents a working-capital absorption during the quarter.
  • Rising finance costs: Standalone finance cost increased to Rs. 210.65 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 from Rs. 162.47 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2025. Consolidated finance cost was Rs. 223.84 lakhs versus Rs. 196.10 lakhs a year earlier.
  • Employee cost inflation: Standalone employee benefits expense rose to Rs. 2,037.44 lakhs from Rs. 1,857.00 lakhs year-on-year; consolidated employee benefits expense rose to Rs. 2,311.03 lakhs from Rs. 2,075.79 lakhs.
  • Tax outgo increase: Standalone current tax rose to Rs. 762.00 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 from Rs. 276.00 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2025; consolidated current tax rose to Rs. 781.57 lakhs from Rs. 309.95 lakhs.
  • Subsidiary loss-making unit: One subsidiary included in the consolidated results recorded total revenue of Rs. 87.13 lakhs and a total loss after tax of Rs. (22.58) lakhs for the quarter ended 30.06.2026 (before consolidation adjustments). Its interim financial information was not reviewed by its auditor and was furnished by management, which stated it is not material to the Group.
  • Unreviewed subsidiary information: The auditors note that the interim financial results of one subsidiary were not reviewed by its auditor, and the conclusion on the consolidated statement relies on management-furnished information for that entity.
  • Negative other comprehensive income items in prior periods: Deferred losses on cash flow hedges were Rs. (617.13) lakhs for the year ended 31.03.2026 (standalone), reflecting hedging-related volatility outside the profit and loss account.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong year-on-year revenue growth: Standalone revenue from operations rose to Rs. 23,081.04 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 from Rs. 12,491.05 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2025. Consolidated revenue from operations rose to Rs. 24,121.46 lakhs from Rs. 13,242.00 lakhs over the same comparison.
  • Sequential revenue improvement: Standalone revenue increased from Rs. 21,641.62 lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026 to Rs. 23,081.04 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026; consolidated revenue rose from Rs. 22,648.97 lakhs to Rs. 24,121.46 lakhs.
  • Profit growth: Standalone profit before tax rose to Rs. 3,161.92 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 from Rs. 1,175.22 lakhs a year earlier and Rs. 2,574.75 lakhs in the preceding quarter. Consolidated profit before tax rose to Rs. 3,904.89 lakhs from Rs. 1,485.10 lakhs year-on-year and Rs. 3,040.75 lakhs sequentially.
  • Net profit and EPS expansion: Standalone net profit after tax was Rs. 2,386.65 lakhs for the quarter ended 30.06.2026 versus Rs. 935.62 lakhs a year earlier; diluted EPS was Rs. 1.57 versus Rs. 0.61. Consolidated net profit after tax was Rs. 3,110.05 lakhs versus Rs. 1,211.55 lakhs; diluted EPS was Rs. 2.04 versus Rs. 0.80.
  • Positive other comprehensive income: Standalone other comprehensive income (net of taxes) was Rs. 256.32 lakhs for the quarter ended 30.06.2026, including deferred gains on cash flow hedges of Rs. 347.19 lakhs, compared with Rs. 53.03 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2025.
  • International footprint: The consolidated results include AVT Natural Europe Limited (UK), AVT Natural S.A. DE C.V (Mexico), AVT Natural North America Inc. (USA, step-down subsidiary), and AVT Natural FZCO (UAE), indicating a multi-geography operating presence.
  • Dividend continuity: The 40th Annual General Meeting held on August 17, 2026 passed the resolution for declaration of the final dividend for 2025-26.
  • Leadership appointments: The AGM approved the re-appointment of Mr. Rahul Thomas, the appointment of Mr. Siddharth Thomas as Non-Executive Director, and the appointment of Mr. K. Nandakumar as Manager & Chief Executive Officer, all with the requisite majority.

3. Key Risks

  • Reliance on management-furnished subsidiary data: For one subsidiary, the interim financial information was not reviewed by its auditor; the auditors’ conclusion on the consolidated statement is not modified in respect of this matter, but the information source is management.
  • Reliance on other auditors: For another subsidiary, the auditors did not review the interim financial results and relied on the report of another auditor. That subsidiary reflected total revenue of Rs. 2,531.88 lakhs, total profit after tax of Rs. 57.26 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 57.26 lakhs for the quarter ended 30.06.2026 (before consolidation adjustments).
  • Single reportable segment concentration: The company operates in solvent extracted products as its primary reportable segment, and segment reporting is not applicable. This concentrates exposure in one business line.
  • Derivative and hedging exposure: Cash flow hedge movements produced significant swings in other comprehensive income — deferred gains of Rs. 347.19 lakhs in the quarter ended 30.06.2026 versus deferred losses of Rs. (320.55) lakhs in the quarter ended 31.03.2026 (standalone).
  • Quarterly results not indicative of annual performance: The seasonality note means investors cannot extrapolate quarterly performance to the full year.
  • Foreign operations exposure: The group’s operations across the UK, Mexico, USA, and UAE expose it to exchange differences on translating foreign operations, which are recognized in other comprehensive income.
  • Governance continuity: The AGM involved re-appointment and new appointment resolutions for key leadership positions (Mr. Rahul Thomas, Mr. Siddharth Thomas, Mr. K. Nandakumar), which are relevant to management continuity.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on seasonality: Management states that, in view of the seasonality of the sector, the financial results for the quarter are not indicative of the full year’s expected performance. Observed performance shows a sharp year-on-year increase in standalone revenue (Rs. 12,491.05 lakhs to Rs. 23,081.04 lakhs) and consolidated revenue (Rs. 13,242.00 lakhs to Rs. 24,121.46 lakhs) for the quarter ended 30.06.2026, consistent with a seasonal business where a single quarter does not represent the full year.
  • Guidance on segment reporting: Management states that the company operates in solvent extracted products as the primary reportable segment and therefore segment reporting is not applicable. The results are presented as a single operating segment, consistent with this position.
  • Guidance on subsidiary materiality: Management informed the auditors that the interim financial results of one subsidiary (revenue Rs. 87.13 lakhs, loss after tax Rs. (22.58) lakhs) are not material to the Group. The auditors’ conclusion on the consolidated statement is not modified in respect of this matter.
  • Observed performance versus prior periods: The quarter ended 30.06.2026 shows higher revenue, profit before tax, net profit, and EPS compared with both the quarter ended 30.06.2025 and the quarter ended 31.03.2026 on both standalone and consolidated bases, alongside higher inventory build-up and finance costs.
  • No explicit forward-looking financial guidance: The announcements do not contain specific forward-looking financial targets, revenue guidance, or margin guidance beyond the seasonality note and the segment reporting note.
