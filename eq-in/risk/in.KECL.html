KECL: 79th AGM Voting Results and Related Developments

Headwinds and Challenges

The 79th Annual General Meeting materials point to a shareholder base that is heavily concentrated in the promoter group, with limited participation from public institutions. Of the total 66,414,071 shares, promoter and promoter group entities held 33,034,787 shares, while public institutions held 3,434,506 shares and public non-institutions held 29,944,778 shares. Across all resolutions, public institutions polled only 9,825 votes, representing 0.2861% of their outstanding shares. This low institutional participation suggests limited engagement from institutional investors in the company’s governance decisions.

The special resolution for raising funds through a preferential issue and allotment of equity shares to Kirloskar Power Equipments Limited, a promoter group entity, saw the lowest overall participation among the eight resolutions. Total votes polled were 32,126,770, representing 48.3734% of outstanding shares, compared to approximately 50.0995% for the other resolutions. Within the promoter and promoter group category, only 31,887,622 votes were polled out of 33,034,787 shares held, representing 96.5274% of their holdings, indicating that a portion of the promoter group did not participate in this resolution.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

The company successfully conducted its 79th AGM on August 13, 2026, through video conferencing and other audio-visual means, with all eight resolutions passed with requisite majorities. The resolutions covered routine and strategic matters including adoption of audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, reappointment of directors, and approval of a preferential issue of equity shares to a promoter group entity.

The preferential issue of equity shares to Kirloskar Power Equipments Limited, a promoter group entity, for cash was approved as a special resolution. This resolution received 32,125,798 votes in favour and 972 votes against, representing 99.9970% approval on votes polled. The promoter group voted unanimously in favour with 31,887,622 votes, while public non-institutions cast 228,351 votes in favour and 972 votes against. This capital raising initiative indicates a potential infusion of funds into the company.

The reappointment of Mr. Vijay R Kirloskar as Executive Chairman (Whole-time Director) was approved as a special resolution with 33,272,113 votes in favour and 972 votes against, representing 99.9971% approval. The appointment of Ms. Janaki Kirloskar as Joint Managing Director was also approved as a special resolution with identical voting numbers, suggesting continuity in leadership.

Key Risks

The voting pattern reveals that a small number of public non-institutional shareholders voted against several resolutions. For resolutions 2 through 8, the number of votes against ranged from 972 to 1,128. While these represent a tiny fraction of total votes polled (0.0029% to 0.0034%), they indicate some dissent among minority shareholders.

The appointment of M/s. BMS Auditing, Chartered Accountants, Dubai, UAE, as auditor of the branch office situated at Ajman, UAE, was approved as an ordinary resolution. This suggests the company maintains international operations requiring separate branch audits.

The ratification of remuneration of Cost Auditors for the financial year ending March 31, 2026, was approved, indicating ongoing compliance requirements under cost audit regulations.
