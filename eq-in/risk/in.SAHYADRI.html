Headwinds and Challenges

  • Management states that the business faced “all challenges due to Geo political situations impacting our exports adversely” during Q1FY27.
  • The growth outlook for the roofing sector in FY27 is described as positive but “subject to Geo-political situation, forex volatility which may impact export markets and imported raw material costs.”
  • The company’s forward-looking risk disclosures cite fluctuations in earnings, competition (domestic and international), economic growth in India and abroad, ability to attract and retain skilled professionals, time and cost overruns on contracts, ability to manage international operations, government policies and actions, regulations, and interest and other fiscal costs.
  • Historically, Q1 is noted as always being a good quarter for the company and the industry, indicating some seasonality in performance.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Q1FY27 performance: Total income of ₹261.3 crore, up 32.9% QoQ and 20.9% YoY; EBITDA of ₹41.7 crore, up 100.5% QoQ and 92.2% YoY; PAT of ₹26.5 crore, up 150.0% QoQ and 145.4% YoY.
  • EBITDA margin improved to 16.0% in Q1FY27 from 10.1% in Q1FY26, an improvement of 58.4% YoY as stated by management.
  • Capacity utilization improved to 103% in Q1FY27 from 93% in Q1FY26.
  • The growth outlook for the roofing sector in FY27 remains positive, subject to the geopolitical and forex conditions noted above.
  • Capex plans: a new unit in Orissa for Asbestos Corrugated Sheet with capacity of 1,20,000 MTPA and CAPEX of ₹95 crore, to enter unrepresented markets of Orissa, Jharkhand, West Bengal and Bihar; and a new unit in Maharashtra for Non-Asbestos Cement Boards with capacity of 72,000 MTPA and CAPEX of ₹95 crore, to expand in Western India, exports, and enter unrepresented North India markets. Land has been identified and acquisition is in process for the Maharashtra unit.
  • Strategic priorities: increase share of margin-rich Value-Added Products (VAP) in the product mix, add clients across business segments, strengthen market share in existing geographies and establish presence in North and East India, and continue capacity expansion.
  • Distribution network of 3,000+ dealers, 5 plants across 4 states, 45+ products, presence in 5+ countries, and 1 million+ customers served since inception.
  • Dividend: final dividend of ₹1.5 per equity share for FY26 approved at the AGM; interim dividend of 25% (₹2.5 per share of face value ₹10) recommended for FY27.

Key Risks

  • Geopolitical situations adversely impacting exports.
  • Forex volatility affecting export markets and imported raw material costs.
  • Competition from domestic and international players.
  • Dependence on economic growth in India and abroad.
  • Execution risks on capacity expansion, including time and cost overruns on contracts.
  • Regulatory and government policy changes.
  • Interest and other fiscal costs prevailing in the economy.
  • Ability to attract and retain skilled professionals.
  • Risks associated with managing international operations.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management describes Q1 as historically a good quarter for the company and the industry; Q1FY27 total income of ₹261.3 crore and EBITDA margin of 16.0% are consistent with that characterization.
  • Management states the FY27 roofing sector growth outlook remains positive, subject to geopolitical and forex conditions; no specific numeric guidance is provided for FY27.
  • Management cites capacity utilization improvement to 103% in Q1FY27 from 93% in Q1FY26, which is supported by the reported operational figures.
  • Management’s stated strategic intent to expand capacity is evidenced by the Orissa and Maharashtra capex projects, with the Orissa unit in process and the Maharashtra land acquisition in process.
  • Management’s focus on margin-rich value-added products is reflected in the product portfolio (EcoPro cement boards, Cemply flat sheets, Swastik Sil Gold coloured designer roofing sheets) and the stated “Way Ahead” priorities.
  • The board recommended an interim dividend of ₹2.5 per share for FY27 in recognition of Q1 performance, following a final dividend of ₹1.5 per share for FY26.
