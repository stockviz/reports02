Hikal Limited — Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Regulatory remediation and its drag on Pharma sales. Hikal is in the “penultimate stage” of its US FDA remediation plan. Management states that the remediation plan has slowed down sales in the Pharmaceutical business because additional time was taken on plant shutdowns, based on recommendations from global regulatory authorities and consultants. A US FDA reinspection is expected during the current financial year.

Crop Protection CDMO weakness. CDMO demand in Crop Protection remained subdued due to ongoing inventory adjustments at the customer’s end. Global demand improved only gradually and continued pricing pressure persisted.

Input cost inflation. Geopolitical developments led to a significant rise across all input costs, including raw materials, resulting in margin pressure in Crop Protection.

FY 2025-26 loss and exceptional items. The material headwinds cited include the impact of the US FDA regulatory action, impairment of certain assets at the Panoli facility, and exceptional charges arising from implementation of the new Labour Code. These contributed to inadequate profits and a loss of ₹487 million in FY 2025-26.

Macro backdrop. The Q1FY27 revenue growth of 6.2% was achieved despite geopolitical and macroeconomic headwinds.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Pharmaceutical recovery. The Pharmaceutical business continued its recovery with improving customer offtake, contributing 58% of the revenue mix across Own Products and CDMO, supported by strengthening regulated market demand. Pharma revenue was ₹233 crore in Q1FY27, up 15.2% year-on-year.

Portfolio and geographic expansion. Continued portfolio expansion across differentiated APIs and specialty therapies including Oncology, CNS, Gastroenterology and Anti-diabetics. Presence is strengthening across regulated and emerging markets, with increasing focus on Japan, LATAM, MENA and other strategic geographies. The molecule mix continues to shift toward higher-value final APIs.

DMF filing trajectory. DMF filings are increasing to 5–6 filings annually versus 2–3 historically.

New cGMP pilot plant. A new cGMP pilot plant was commissioned in Pune, enhancing pharmaceutical development and scale-up capabilities.

Crop Protection Own Products. Own Products delivered sequential growth driven by higher domestic volumes. Four CDMO molecules are in development.

Personal Care commercialization. The Personal Care business was commercialized in July 2026, with a few products on track to contribute revenues in FY27, in line with the portfolio diversification strategy. A dedicated Panoli manufacturing facility has been commissioned.

Animal Health resilience. The Animal Health business continued to deliver resilient performance, supported by stronger customer partnerships, commercialization of new programs, and an expanding development pipeline comprising multiple advanced intermediates and NCEs.

Customer audits and ESG recognition. 100+ customer audits were passed at the Bangalore facility. Hikal received a Gold rating from EcoVadis, placing it in the top 5% of companies globally.

Order book and new customers. Growth prospects cited include a growing customer order book, onboarding of new customers in CDMO, development of several new products for launch, and repurposing of assets at the Panoli facility with new production lines.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued US FDA regulatory exposure, with reinspection timing and outcome uncertain.
  • Customer inventory adjustments suppressing CDMO demand in Crop Protection.
  • Geopolitical developments driving significant increases across all input costs, including raw materials.
  • Pricing pressure in global Crop Protection markets.
  • Execution risk around the remediation plan, which has already slowed Pharma sales through extended plant shutdowns.
  • Asset impairment and exceptional charges (Panoli impairment, Labour Code implementation) affecting reported profitability.
  • Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties and are not necessarily predictive of future results.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

Guidance. Management expects business momentum to strengthen progressively through FY27, supported by improving demand visibility, expanding CDMO opportunities, and continued focus on operational excellence. Management remains confident in a stepwise recovery of revenues and profitability during the year. For Crop Protection, management expects sequential improvement going forward. Management expects growth in both Revenue and EBITDA for the current year, supported by positive momentum from a growing customer order book, new CDMO customers, and new product launches that will improve profitability and ROCE in coming years.

Observed performance. Q1FY27 revenue was ₹403 crore, up 6.2% year-on-year, with EBITDA of ₹37 crore (up 47.4% YoY) and margins of 9.2%, expanding 260 bps YoY. PAT was negative ₹7 crore, an improvement from negative ₹22 crore in Q1FY26. Sequentially, revenue declined from ₹519 crore in Q4FY26 to ₹403 crore in Q1FY27, and EBITDA declined from ₹105 crore to ₹37 crore. Segment-wise, Pharmaceuticals rose to ₹233 crore (58% of mix) from ₹203 crore in Q1FY26, while Crop Protection fell to ₹170 crore (42% of mix) from ₹178 crore in Q1FY26. Management characterized Q1FY27 as a slower start to the year during a transition from regulatory-led disruption to growth.
