DNAMEDIA — Recent Corporate Announcements: Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Revenue contraction and losses. Revenue from operations fell to Rs. 650.88 lakhs for the year ended March 31, 2026, down from Rs. 1,319.19 lakhs in FY 2024-25 — a decline of roughly 51%. The company reported a net loss of Rs. 887.54 lakhs for FY 2025-26. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, revenue from operations was Rs. 171.83 lakhs, with a profit after tax of Rs. 39.99 lakhs, compared with a loss after tax of Rs. 188.60 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

Negative net worth and going-concern uncertainty. The company had a negative net worth of Rs. 25,259.50 lakhs as of March 31, 2026, and negative net worth as at June 30, 2026. Note 5 to the June 30, 2026 results states that the company has negative net worth and that current liabilities exceed current assets. The statutory auditors’ limited review report includes a “Material Uncertainty Relating to Going Concern” section, stating that the company’s ability to continue as a going concern is significantly dependent on improved profitability and sufficient cash flows from planned cost rationalization measures and initiatives, and that these conditions, along with the outcome of the arbitration matter, indicate a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt on the going-concern assumption.

Modified audit opinion on ICDs and NCRPS. The auditors issued a qualified conclusion. The basis relates to unsecured inter-corporate deposits (ICDs) granted to Veena Investments Private Limited (VIPL), outstanding at Rs. 17,340.27 lakhs as at June 30, 2026 (including accrued interest up to December 31, 2024 of Rs. 1,385.27 lakhs). VIPL holds 6% Non-cumulative Non-convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (NCRPS) of the company of Rs. 43,626.56 lakhs, redeemable on November 1, 2036, for which VIPL sought early redemption. The company declined early redemption and called upon VIPL to repay the ICDs. VIPL invoked arbitration. The company has not accrued interest from January 1, 2025 and has not performed an impairment assessment on the outstanding balance under Ind AS 109; the auditors state they are unable to determine whether any adjustment is required to the carrying amount of the outstanding balance as at June 30, 2026. The auditors note that their conclusion on the quarter ended June 30, 2025 and their opinion on the audited results for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 were also modified on this matter.

Deferred tax asset reversal. During the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, the company reassessed the carrying value of deferred tax assets and, considering uncertainty over sufficient future taxable income, reversed deferred tax assets of Rs. 645.28 lakhs and Rs. 631.44 lakhs respectively, charging these to the statement of profit and loss.

Regulatory matter. The company received a Show Cause Notice (as a co-noticee) dated February 12, 2026 from SEBI for alleged violations of certain SEBI Regulations. The company believes it is in compliance, has filed its response, and has filed an “Application for Settlement” under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018, which is under consideration. No material adverse impact is expected and no adjustments were made to the results.

Exchange query on audit qualification statement. By communication dated June 25, 2026, NSE sought clarification on the Statement of Impact of Audit Qualifications submitted with the May 28, 2026 results, observing that it was not in the SEBI-prescribed format and not signed by the MD. The company replied on June 26, 2026 that as on May 28, 2026 it had no CEO or Managing Director; the Board comprised Mr. Nagendra Bhandari (Whole-Time Director, Executive Director – Finance), Mr. Amit Singhal, Mrs. Garima Bharadwaj, Mr. Prakash Vaghela (Independent Directors), and Mr. Ronak Jatwala and Mr. Mukesh Jindal (Non-Executive, Non-Independent Directors). Key Managerial Personnel were Mr. Nagendra Bhandari (Chief Executive Officer) and Ms. Jyoti Upadhyay (Company Secretary). The company stated the MD/CEO signature requirement was therefore not applicable and that the statement was signed by all applicable officials.

Customer concentration and sector risks. The company is highly dependent on a small group of customers, with major revenue concentrated in fewer than 10 customers, and operates amid ongoing regulatory uncertainties and potential cybersecurity risks affecting the digital media sector.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Digital media market expansion. The company benefits from the rapid expansion of India’s digital media and entertainment market, which crossed the ₹1 trillion mark in 2025 and commands 63% of total advertising revenues.

Content monetization and revenue share agreement. The company re-entered a content monetization and revenue share agreement with Indiadotcom Digital Private Limited (IDPL) effective September 1, 2025, under which the company retains 85% of total revenue generated from its digital properties, aiming to optimize distribution and advertising operations.

Liquidity position and cost measures. Current assets exceed current liabilities, and management’s business plan focuses on cost rationalization, improved collections, and strengthening revenue streams. Note 5 to the June 30, 2026 results states that the management continues to focus on strengthening revenue streams and implementing cost rationalization measures, including optimization of employee and other operating expenses, to improve profitability and cash flows.

NCRPS/ICD resolution path. The arbitration process has produced a framework to resolve the ICD–NCRPS matter. The Board took note of the procedural Arbitration Order dated June 16, 2025 and accorded in-principle approval to VIPL’s set-off proposal. The company received the Arbitration Award dated August 20, 2025 directing that the company take necessary steps to redeem/cancel the NCRPS at a value of Rs. 17,340.27 lakhs, with VIPL setting off the entire ICDs outstanding against the NCRPS value; upon cancellation, neither party would have further claims. On September 18, 2025, the Board approved a Scheme of Reduction and cancellation of NCRPS Capital for Rs. 17,340.27 lakhs (adjusted against the ICDs outstanding), with the remaining paid-up NCRPS capital of Rs. 26,286.29 lakhs to be reduced in its entirety and accounted for under Ind AS. The Scheme was filed with BSE on October 7, 2025 and NSE on October 8, 2025, and is pending approval.

3. Key Risks

  • Going-concern risk: Negative net worth and current liabilities exceeding current assets, with continuation dependent on improved profitability, cost rationalization, collections, and projected cash flows from the Board-approved business plan.
  • Audit qualification risk: The qualified conclusion on the ICD carrying value and the absence of an Ind AS 109 impairment assessment, with the auditors unable to determine whether adjustment is required; the same matter modified prior-period conclusions and opinions.
  • Arbitration/scheme execution risk: The NCRPS reduction and cancellation scheme is pending approval, and the resolution of the ICD–NCRPS set-off depends on that approval and the arbitration outcome.
  • Regulatory risk: The SEBI Show Cause Notice dated February 12, 2026 and the pending settlement application; the NSE query regarding the format and signing of the Statement of Impact of Audit Qualifications.
  • Customer concentration risk: Major revenue concentrated in fewer than 10 customers.
  • Sector risks: Regulatory uncertainties and potential cybersecurity risks in the digital media sector.
  • Tax asset risk: Reversal of deferred tax assets reflecting uncertainty over sufficient future taxable income.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Guidance. Management states it continues to concentrate on expanding digital platforms, maintaining a sharp focus on cost optimization, and maximizing revenue. Based on the net working capital position, planned initiatives to improve collections, cost rationalization measures, and projected positive cash flows from the Board-approved business plan, management believes the company will be able to meet its obligations as they fall due and has prepared the financial statements on a going-concern basis. Management also believes no impairment adjustment is required on the ICD carrying value and expects no material adverse impact from the SEBI matter.

Observed performance. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, revenue from operations was Rs. 171.83 lakhs and profit after tax was Rs. 39.99 lakhs, compared with revenue of Rs. 78.67 lakhs and a loss after tax of Rs. 188.60 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Total expenses for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 were Rs. 137.36 lakhs, against Rs. 270.87 lakhs in the prior-year quarter, with employee benefit expense at Rs. 79.90 lakhs versus Rs. 134.55 lakhs and other expenses at Rs. 53.35 lakhs versus Rs. 129.64 lakhs. For the year ended March 31, 2026, revenue from operations was Rs. 650.88 lakhs and the net loss was Rs. 887.54 lakhs. The auditors’ going-concern material uncertainty statement and qualified conclusion indicate that the outcomes management projects remain dependent on execution of the cost rationalization, collection, and business-plan initiatives, and on the pending NCRPS scheme approval and arbitration outcome.
