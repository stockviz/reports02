Adani Enterprises (ADANIENT): Recent Corporate Announcements

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Profitability pressure despite strong topline growth. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), total income rose 50% Y-o-Y to Rs. 33,546 cr and EBITDA rose 49% Y-o-Y to Rs. 5,642 cr, but Profit Before Tax declined 12% Y-o-Y to Rs. 1,295 cr. The company attributes this to higher depreciation following the capitalisation of Navi Mumbai Airport in Q4 FY26. After an exceptional item — an OFAC settlement of USD 275 mn (Rs. 2,644 cr) — Profit After Tax was negative at Rs. (1,160) cr.

Segment-level declines. The New Energy Ecosystem saw Total Income fall 2% Y-o-Y to Rs. 3,937 cr, EBITDA fall 20% to Rs. 972 cr, and PBT fall 35% to Rs. 628 cr. Module sales were broadly flat (1,350 MW to 1,340 MW, down 1%). Airports PBT turned negative at Rs. (194) cr from Rs. 204 cr, attributed to the Navi Mumbai Airport capitalisation impact on depreciation. IRM sales volume fell 35% Y-o-Y to 8.3 MMT, with revenue down 8% on low volume. Mining Services dispatch fell 2% to 11.8 MMT, with EBITDA down 13% due to increased fuel cost. The presentation notes results were impacted by higher operating cost from increased fuel prices due to global volatility.

Elevated and rising debt. Gross debt rose from Rs. 86,702 cr (March 2026) to Rs. 97,622 cr (June 2026); net external debt rose from Rs. 64,051 cr to Rs. 73,998 cr. Working capital debt increased from Rs. 8,107 cr to Rs. 11,131 cr, with notable increases in the Copper and ANIL Ecosystem segments.

Governance/related-party dissent. At the 34th AGM (June 24, 2026), resolutions on material related-party transactions passed but with meaningful opposition from public institutions — e.g., the Adani Infra (India) resolution drew 3.29% of public institution votes against, and the Carmichael Rail Network Trust resolution drew 3.29% against. The special resolution on raising capital passed with 99.9999% in favour.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Record quarterly EBITDA and scale-up of incubated platforms. Management describes Q1 FY27 as beginning the year with “exceptionally strong performance,” with the highest-ever quarterly EBITDA of Rs. 5,642 cr, driven by maturing infrastructure and incubation platforms. Chairman Gautam Adani cites commencement of international operations at Navi Mumbai International Airport, toll collections on the Ganga Expressway, solar module capacity expansion, and a major new hyperscale data center order as key milestones.

Airports. Navi Mumbai Airport began international flight operations on July 15, 2026. Aero and non-aero revenue grew 16% and 53% Y-o-Y respectively in Q1 FY27. AAHL has entered binding agreements to raise Rs. 9,825 cr (~USD 1 bn) of primary equity from Alpha Wave Global, Premji Invest, Temasek, and BlackRock managed funds, at a pre-money equity valuation of ~USD 18 bn; investors will hold ~5.54% after three tranches, with the final tranche expected by July 2027. Proceeds will fund modernization, capacity expansion to serve ~200 mn passengers annually, ~22 mn sq. ft. of first-phase mixed-use Airport City development, and scaling of ground handling and non-aeronautical businesses. Management states it will continue to invest ahead of growth.

New Energy Ecosystem. Module line capacity expanded to 5.7 GW with commissioning of 1.7 GW in June 2026; the roadmap targets module capacity of 10 GW and cell capacity of 10 GW (from 4 GW). WTG sales rose 83% Y-o-Y to 64 sets. Domestic demand fully absorbs export offtake. The business received a Gold Medal (NAMC 2025-26) for manufacturing competitiveness.

Data Centers. A new hyperscale order of 400 MW in Vizag takes cumulative tied-up capacity to 960+ MW. Pune Phase II (9.6 MW) was handed over, taking operational capacity to 65.4 MW. 100% uptime availability was maintained across all data centers.

Roads. Ganga Expressway toll collection commenced May 15, 2026. A new operational BOT project of 620.6 lane kms (NH-59, Indore–MP/Gujarat border) was added.

Copper and Mining Services. Copper capacity utilisation rose to 52%, adding EBITDA of Rs. 749 cr for the quarter, with sales up 4.6x to 64.7 KT. The Dhirauli Mine service contract in Madhya Pradesh was operationalised, taking operational service contracts to 8 with peak capacity of 93.1 MMTPA; total peak capacity across 18 contracts is 145.4 MMTPA, with 41% growth available from operational contracts.

Capital markets. India’s largest QIP by a non-financial corporate raised Rs. 15,000 cr in July 2026, with bids of 3.8x the base issue size and participation from domestic mutual funds and global investors. Promoter shareholding stands at 71.97% post-QIP. Consolidated Net Debt/Equity was 0.85x in Q1 FY27.

ESG recognition. S&P DJSI 2025 score of 62, ranked among the top 5 companies in its sector globally; CDP Climate Change 2025 achieved A Leadership Category.

3. Key Risks

  • Regulatory/settlement risk: The OFAC settlement of USD 275 mn (Rs. 2,644 cr) in Q1 FY27 materially affected reported PAT.
  • Commodity and fuel price volatility: Higher fuel prices due to global volatility raised operating costs; Mining Services EBITDA fell 13% on increased fuel cost. IRM and Mining results were boosted by positive global price movements, indicating sensitivity to commodity prices.
  • Execution and ramp-up risk: Multiple projects are in ramp-up or under development — Navi Mumbai Airport, Ganga Expressway, Copper (52% utilisation), and balance module/cell capacity. Several road HAM projects (MSRL, SSRL, Sree Vishwa Varadhi, CTL) and the Kedarnath ropeway are under development with financial closure “TBD.”
  • Leverage risk: Rising gross and net debt, and increased working capital debt, particularly in Copper and ANIL.
  • Related-party governance scrutiny: Material RPT resolutions saw notable public institutional opposition at the AGM.
  • Volume sensitivity: IRM sales volume fell 35% and Mining Services dispatch fell 2%, showing exposure to offtake and volume variability.

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Capacity expansion: Management targets module line expansion from 5.7 GW to 10 GW and cell line from 4 GW to 10 GW. Observed: 1.7 GW module line commissioned June 12, 2026, taking module capacity to 5.7 GW; balance module and cell capacity is in progress.
  • Airport ramp-up: Management states Navi Mumbai Airport ramp-up is in progress and international services launched July 15, 2026. Observed: Navi Mumbai contributed 1.7 mn passengers and 12.6 thousand ATMs in Q1 FY27, while group passenger movement rose only 3% Y-o-Y to 24.2 mn (Mumbai passengers declined from 13.6 mn to 12.8 mn).
  • Data center scaling: Tied-up capacity reached 960+ MW against operational capacity of 65.4 MW, indicating a substantial gap between contracted and operational capacity.
  • Copper ramp-up: Management states ramp-up is in progress with utilisation at 52%; observed sales of 64.7 KT (4.6x Y-o-Y) and EBITDA of Rs. 749 cr.
  • Roads: Toll collection on Ganga Expressway commenced May 15, 2026, as stated.
  • Capital raise: The Rs. 15,000 cr QIP was completed in July 2026 with 3.8x bids, consistent with the special resolution approved at the June 24, 2026 AGM.
  • AAHL equity raise: Binding agreements for Rs. 9,825 cr are disclosed, with the final tranche expected by July 2027 — a forward commitment not yet completed.

Additional Corporate Developments

  • Board appointment: Ms. Anju Abrol (DIN: 11841715) was appointed as an Additional Director (Non-executive, Independent) effective July 29, 2026, for a first term of 3 years, subject to shareholder approval. She is a former CEO, Asia Pacific at ING Bank, with over 30 years of global banking experience.
  • AGM outcomes (June 24, 2026): All 12 resolutions passed with requisite majority, including adoption of standalone and consolidated FY26 financial statements, declaration of dividend for FY25-26, re-appointment of Dr. Vinay Prakash as rotating director, ratification of Cost Auditors’ remuneration, approval to raise capital, and multiple material related-party transaction approvals.
  • Investor interaction: The date for the Jefferies 5th India Forum interaction was revised from September 16, 2026 to September 17, 2026, to be held physically at Gurgaon.
