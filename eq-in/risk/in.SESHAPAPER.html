Headwinds and Challenges

  • Sequential revenue decline: Standalone revenue from operations fell to ₹492.00 crores in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹591.78 crores in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Consolidated revenue followed the same pattern (₹492.08 crores versus ₹591.84 crores).
  • Sharp rise in material costs: Cost of materials consumed rose to ₹315.72 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹281.14 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹286.10 crores in the June 2025 quarter, even as revenue declined sequentially.
  • Higher employee costs: Employee benefits expense increased to ₹29.78 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹25.79 crores in the March 2026 quarter and ₹27.38 crores in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Rising other expenses: Other expenses increased to ₹50.50 crores (standalone) in the June 2026 quarter from ₹34.53 crores in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Volatile other comprehensive income: Net fair value gain on equity instruments through OCI swung from a gain of ₹55.34 crores in the June 2025 quarter to a gain of ₹11.51 crores in the June 2026 quarter, and from a loss of ₹36.81 crores in the March 2026 quarter. This volatility flows into total comprehensive income, which fell to ₹43.49 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹64.55 crores in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Associate drag on consolidated profit: Share of loss of associate was ₹0.34 crores in the June 2026 quarter, compared with a share of profit of ₹8.61 crores in the March 2026 quarter and a loss of ₹0.82 crores in the June 2025 quarter.
  • Servalakshmi Paper Limited asset revival pending: The NCLT Chennai bench order dated May 12, 2023 approved the e-auction sale of assets of Servalakshmi Paper Limited (Corporate Debtor in Liquidation) as a going concern in favour of the company, and the sales certificate was issued and assets handed over. Of three appeals filed in NCLAT, two were dismissed as withdrawn and the remaining appeal has been heard and reserved for orders. The company continues to take steps for revival, refurbishment and recommencement of operations, indicating the acquired assets are not yet contributing to operations.

  • Year-on-year profit improvement: Standalone net profit after tax increased to ₹33.59 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹17.06 crores in the June 2025 quarter. Consolidated net profit after tax increased to ₹31.91 crores from ₹15.43 crores.
  • Sequential profit improvement: Standalone net profit after tax rose to ₹33.59 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹17.39 crores in the March 2026 quarter. Standalone profit before tax rose to ₹45.10 crores from ₹23.37 crores.
  • Lower finance costs: Finance costs declined to ₹0.41 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹1.07 crores in the June 2025 quarter and ₹0.71 crores in the March 2026 quarter.
  • Lower power and fuel costs: Cost of power and fuel declined to ₹50.91 crores in the June 2026 quarter from ₹53.93 crores in the June 2025 quarter and ₹52.21 crores in the March 2026 quarter.
  • Earnings per share growth: Standalone basic and diluted EPS rose to ₹5.58 in the June 2026 quarter from ₹2.83 in the June 2025 quarter and ₹2.89 in the March 2026 quarter. Consolidated EPS rose to ₹5.30 from ₹2.56 and ₹4.32 respectively.
  • Servalakshmi asset acquisition: The acquisition of Servalakshmi Paper Limited’s assets as a going concern, with sales certificate issued and assets handed over, positions the company for potential capacity or operational expansion once revival, refurbishment and recommencement steps are completed.
  • Board reconstitution: Sri R.J. Key Risks

  • Input cost inflation: The sharp increase in cost of materials consumed (₹315.72 crores in June 2026 versus ₹281.14 crores in March 2026) represents a margin risk if selling prices do not keep pace.
  • Revenue concentration and cyclicality: Paper is the only reportable segment of operation, exposing the company to sector-specific demand and pricing cycles.
  • Legal and regulatory uncertainty: The remaining NCLAT appeal concerning the Servalakshmi Paper Limited asset acquisition has been heard and reserved for orders. An adverse outcome could affect the company’s plans for those assets.
  • OCI volatility: Fair value movements on equity instrument investments are volatile and can significantly affect total comprehensive income, as seen in the swing from ₹55.34 crores gain in June 2025 to ₹11.51 crores gain in June 2026.
  • Associate performance risk: The associate (Ponni Sugars (Erode) Limited) contributed a loss of ₹0.34 crores in the June 2026 quarter, and its interim financial statements were not reviewed by the company’s auditors, relying instead on other auditors’ reports.
  • Dependence on subsidiary and associate results: Consolidated results include a wholly owned subsidiary (Esvi International (Engineers & Exporters) Limited) with total revenues of ₹0.10 crores and net profit after tax of ₹0.05 crores for the June 2026 quarter, and an associate whose results are based solely on other auditors’ reports.
