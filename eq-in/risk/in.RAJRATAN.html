asof: 2026-09-17

Headwinds and Challenges

  • Global geopolitical uncertainty, including conflicts in West Asia, shaped the operating environment in Q1 FY27.
  • Pricing pressure across the industry persisted.
  • Competition deepened in both domestic and export markets.
  • Supply chain disruptions affected operations.
  • The rupee weakened.
  • Management noted that the external environment is expected to remain challenging.
  • The company chose to prioritise sales growth over short-term margin increase, maintaining market share even in a competitive environment.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Chennai ramp-up: The greenfield Chennai facility crossed breakeven in the previous quarter and began contributing positively in Q1 FY27. Installed capacity of around 30,000 TPA is to increase to 60,000 TPA, and the unit is expected to emerge as a key growth driver.
  • Thailand deepening: As the only bead wire manufacturer in Thailand, Rajratan is embedded with global OEMs producing tyres there. New land in Ratchaburi has been secured to support the next chapter.
  • Beyond bead wire: A steel cord for conveyor belt project at Pithampur marks the company’s first product diversification into an adjacent industrial market.
  • Export tailwind: A weakening rupee, a global search for China+1 supply, and India’s push to become a tyre export base all point in the same direction; the three-plant, two-country footprint is built to serve this shift.
  • Approval moat: Multi-year qualification cycles, high switching costs, and consistency requirements mean capacity alone does not win business; Rajratan is a recognised name in the shortlist of qualified global bead wire suppliers.
  • Capacity expansions are planned across Pithampur, Thailand, and Chennai.
  • Sustainability initiatives: Solar power installations commissioned at the Thailand facility and Chennai plant; metal scrap recovery and recycling, wastewater reuse, and cleaner fuel transitions.

Key Risks

  • Sustained pricing pressure and deepening competition could continue to weigh on realisations.
  • Geopolitical uncertainty and supply chain disruptions could affect operations and demand.
  • The company’s volume-led strategy prioritises market share over short-term margins, which may pressure profitability if pricing remains weak.
  • Execution risk on capacity expansions across Pithampur, Thailand, and Chennai.
  • The steel cord for conveyor belt project is a first diversification into an adjacent market with its own qualification barriers.
  • Currency movements affect results: the weakening rupee was cited as a macro reality, and exchange differences on translation of foreign operations were negative Rs. 213 lakhs in Q1 FY27 (versus positive Rs. 215 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and positive Rs. 1,033 lakhs in Q1 FY26).
  • The auditor did not review the interim financial results of Rajratan Thai Wire Company Limited (reviewed by its own independent auditors) or Rajratan Wire USA Inc. (not reviewed by any auditors); the auditor’s conclusion relies on management-certified information for these subsidiaries.

Management Guidance vs. Observed Performance

  • Guidance: Sustained focus on market share growth; higher utilisation across manufacturing facilities; capacity expansions planned across Pithampur, Thailand, and Chennai; disciplined capital allocation for sustainable growth.
  • Observed Q1 FY27 performance: Highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs. 31,835 lakhs, up 29% year-on-year. EBITDA rose 35% to Rs. 4,171 lakhs. Profit after tax grew 70% to Rs. 2,296 lakhs. Total sales volume was 33,300 MT, up 16% year-on-year (India 19,710 MT, Thailand 13,590 MT). EBITDA margin improved 55 bps year-on-year to 13.10%; PAT margin improved 173 bps to 7.21%. Chennai began contributing to overall profits, consistent with the stated ramp-up.
  • Management commentary: The Chairman stated that despite geopolitical uncertainty, supply chain disruptions, and sustained pricing pressure, the company delivered profitable growth; a richer customer mix led by premium customers improved realisations; higher capacity utilisation improved manufacturing efficiencies and reduced unit costs; and the volume-led approach was validated through stronger margins, profitable growth, and improved interest coverage.
  • Prior-period context: Q4 FY26 revenue was Rs. 31,429 lakhs with EBITDA of Rs. 2,860 lakhs and PAT of Rs. 1,543 lakhs; Q1 FY26 revenue was Rs. 24,651 lakhs with EBITDA of Rs. 3,094 lakhs and PAT of Rs. 1,352 lakhs. Full-year FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs. 1,15,650 lakhs with PAT of Rs. 7,011 lakhs.
