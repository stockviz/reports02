Saksoft — Recent Corporate Announcements: Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Demand softness that began towards the third quarter of the last financial year, with near-term pressures faced by some top clients.
  • Consolidated revenue was broadly flat: net sales/income from operations of Rs. 24,861.86 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, against Rs. 24,884.50 lakhs in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 and Rs. 24,907.45 lakhs in the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
  • Consolidated profit before tax declined to Rs. 4,257.56 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 4,731.57 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 4,303.45 lakhs in Q1 FY26. Net profit for the period fell to Rs. 2,929.20 lakhs from Rs. 3,593.09 lakhs and Rs. 3,234.79 lakhs in the same comparative periods.
  • Consolidated basic EPS declined to Rs. 2.28 in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 2.81 in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 2.54 in Q1 FY26.
  • Segment revenue in BFS declined to Rs. 7,347.58 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 7,387.55 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 7,736.68 lakhs in Q1 FY26. Segment result in BFS fell to Rs. 1,040.25 lakhs from Rs. 1,229.89 lakhs and Rs. 1,401.07 lakhs respectively.
  • The New Labour Codes, effective November 21, 2025, resulted in an estimated one-time increase in provision for employee benefits of Rs. 486.45 lakhs at the consolidated level and Rs. 374.67 lakhs at the standalone level, recognised as an exceptional item in the year ended March 31, 2026. The Group continues to monitor rules to be notified by regulatory authorities and will assess accounting implications based on such developments.
  • Consolidated tax expense rose to Rs. 1,328.36 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 1,138.48 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 1,068.66 lakhs in Q1 FY26.
  • Other income at the consolidated level fell sharply to Rs. 175.75 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 738.62 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 330.30 lakhs in Q1 FY26.
  • Standalone net profit declined to Rs. 2,525.79 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 2,456.07 lakhs in Q1 FY26 on a lower base, but was down from Rs. 1,592.19 lakhs in Q4 FY26; standalone basic EPS was Rs. 1.91 versus Rs. 1.42 in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 1.20 in Q4 FY26.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Vision 2030 targeting $500 Mn revenues.
  • Leveraged strategic AI investments and accelerators.
  • Strengthened sales engine and delivery capabilities with strategic leadership hires.
  • A diversified growth strategy demonstrating resilience.
  • Segment performance in Emerging vertical grew: revenue of Rs. 11,177.68 lakhs in Q1 FY27 versus Rs. 11,540.89 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 11,803.62 lakhs in Q1 FY26, with segment result rising to Rs. 2,400.71 lakhs from Rs. 2,402.23 lakhs and Rs. 1,909.28 lakhs respectively.
  • Logistics segment revenue rose to Rs. 3,982.88 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 3,701.16 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 3,407.70 lakhs in Q1 FY26, with segment result improving to Rs. 858.50 lakhs from Rs. 711.42 lakhs and Rs. 1,077.87 lakhs.
  • Commerce segment revenue rose to Rs. 2,353.72 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 2,254.90 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 1,959.45 lakhs in Q1 FY26, with segment result improving to Rs. 240.16 lakhs from Rs. 182.00 lakhs and Rs. 194.76 lakhs.
  • The Board approved a composite Scheme of Amalgamation on August 8, 2025, whereby wholly owned subsidiary Augmento Labs Private Limited is to be merged with Saksoft Limited, with an appointed date of April 1, 2026, subject to necessary approvals. In the event the merger becomes effective, standalone revenue and profit after tax for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 would be INR 13,976.96 lakhs and INR 3,264.22 lakhs respectively.
  • All six resolutions at the 27th Annual General Meeting held on August 7, 2026 — including adoption of financial statements, declaration of final dividend, re-appointment of directors, and appointments of whole-time and independent directors — were passed with the requisite majority, with V Suresh Associates as Scrutinizer.

3. Key Risks

  • Continued demand softness and near-term pressures from some top clients.
  • Concentration and performance variability in the BFS segment, which saw both revenue and segment result decline year-on-year and sequentially.
  • Dependence on regulatory clarity regarding the New Labour Codes; the Group continues to monitor rules to be notified and will assess accounting implications based on such developments or guidance.
  • The proposed merger of Augmento Labs Private Limited with Saksoft Limited remains subject to necessary approvals; there is no impact of the proposed merger in the reported financial results.
  • Consolidated results include six subsidiaries and a Trust that were not reviewed by the statutory auditor; their interim financial information reflects total revenues of Rs. 3,678.93 lakhs, total net profit after tax of Rs. 390.73 lakhs, and total comprehensive income of Rs. 392.09 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and was reviewed by other auditors.
  • Other comprehensive income declined to Rs. 639.82 lakhs in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 924.60 lakhs in Q4 FY26 and Rs. 1,427.82 lakhs in Q1 FY26, with the reclassification component falling to Rs. 620.69 lakhs from Rs. 771.28 lakhs and Rs. 1,433.67 lakhs respectively.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Management states that focused investments and trusted client relationships position the company well to improve its growth trajectory, building a more diversified and resilient business well-positioned to deliver consistent, profitable growth over the long term.
  • Management’s Vision 2030 targets $500 Mn revenues.
  • Observed performance for Q1 FY27: Operating Income was INR 2,486.19 Mn, Operating EBITDA was INR 453.96 Mn with 18.26% Operating EBITDA Margins, Net Profit was INR 292.92 Mn with 11.78% PAT Margins, and Basic EPS was INR 2.28 per share. Market Cap was approximately INR 27,399.67 Mn as of June 30, 2026.
  • Against the stated ambition to improve the growth trajectory, consolidated revenue in Q1 FY27 was broadly flat year-on-year and sequentially, and consolidated profit before tax, net profit, and basic EPS all declined both year-on-year and sequentially.
  • The company will reiterate information from the earnings call held on August 10, 2026, for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, at a Group Virtual Investor Meet hosted by Valorem Advisors on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, at 2 PM IST. No unpublished price sensitive information is intended to be discussed.
