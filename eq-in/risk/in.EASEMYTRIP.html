EASEMYTRIP: Recent Corporate Announcements Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Weak profitability in Q1 FY27 and FY26. For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, the company reported a PBT of ₹(114.1) Mn and a PAT of ₹(116.9) Mn, compared with a PBT of ₹20.3 Mn and PAT of ₹4.4 Mn in Q1 FY26. For FY2026, PAT was ₹(476.0) Mn, with an exceptional item of ₹(509.6) Mn and a related tax benefit of ₹(99.3) Mn.
  • Declining EBITDA trajectory. Historical consolidated figures show EBITDA falling from ₹2,281.9 Mn in FY24 to ₹1,612.2 Mn in FY25 and ₹228.6 Mn in FY26, with EBITDA margin declining from 37.5% to 26.7% to 4.0% over the same periods.
  • Rising cost base. Employee benefits expense rose from ₹1,028.3 Mn in FY25 to ₹1,298.0 Mn in FY26, and other expenses rose from ₹3,392.0 Mn to ₹4,208.0 Mn. In Q1 FY27, service cost increased to ₹(391.8) Mn from ₹(180.4) Mn in Q1 FY26.
  • Weak operating cash flow. Net cash from operating activities was ₹(951.0) Mn in FY26, compared with ₹1,118.2 Mn in FY25, with cash and cash equivalents declining from ₹1,361.5 Mn to ₹802.2 Mn.
  • Decline in the Trains, Buses, and Others segment. In Q1 FY27, this segment recorded a 45.8% year-on-year decline, with bookings falling from 4.3 lacs to 2.4 lacs.
  • Revenue from operations declined year-on-year in FY26. Revenue from operations fell from ₹5,873.2 Mn in FY25 to ₹5,357.0 Mn in FY26.
  • External risks cited. The company identifies global macroeconomic headwinds, geopolitical uncertainties, supply chain disruptions, pricing pressures, changing consumer preferences, and intense competition. Additional risks include regulatory complexity, supply-side constraints in the hotel sector, cybersecurity and data privacy concerns, and reliance on air ticketing as a primary revenue source.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Dubai operations growing strongly. In Q1 FY27, Dubai operations recorded GBR of ₹4,618.1 Mn versus ₹3,180.6 Mn in Q1 FY26, a 45.2% year-on-year increase, described as a sustained quarter-on-quarter growth trajectory. For FY2025-26, Dubai operations grew 118.24% year-on-year in GBR.
  • Non-air business expansion. In Q1 FY27, hotel and holiday package bookings grew 95% year-on-year, from 3.3 lacs to 6.5 lacs. For FY2025-26, the hotels and holidays segment grew 88.9% year-on-year in room-night bookings. Management states a focus on expanding hotels and holiday packages, which offer higher profit margins.
  • EaseMyTrip 2.0 and Vision 2030. Management’s FY2026-27 outlook is anchored in advancing Vision 2030, transforming from an online travel platform into a diversified, technology-driven travel ecosystem, building multiple growth engines, and scaling hotels, holidays, international operations, and mobility.
  • International expansion. The company is expanding its international presence through subsidiaries in the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, the UAE, the UK, the USA, New Zealand, Saudi Arabia, and Brazil.
  • Mobility and EV initiatives. Easy Green Mobility will manufacture EV buses, with YoloBus as the operating arm. The plan includes a ₹200 Cr investment over 2–3 years for R&D and a manufacturing plant, and a target to operate 2,000+ electric buses by FY2028. YoloBus covers over 250 routes and has served over 300,000 travelers; it maintains an annual occupancy rate of over 88%.
  • Spree Hospitality acquisition. Spree operates 56 properties with over 2,200 keys, with approximately 30 more properties signed. It aims to expand to 200 properties over five years and recorded an NPS of 93 YTD with an average channel rating of 4.8.
  • Market opportunity. The India online travel market was valued at USD 22.8 billion in 2024 and is anticipated to reach USD 36.3 billion by 2027, a CAGR of 17%. India’s EV bus market is projected to grow at a 24% CAGR during 2024–2030.
  • Digital and demographic tailwinds. Approximately 1,030 million internet users with 70% penetration; India’s e-retail GMV reached $65–$66 billion in 2025 and is poised to grow at over 20% CAGR; by 2030 India is expected to gain 140 million middle-income and 21 million high-income households; Gen Z is expected to command 45% of online spend.
  • Customer loyalty. The company reports a 90%+ customer repeat booking rate and a ~95% repeat transaction rate, serving over 33 million premium users.
  • AI-led innovation. Initiatives include WhatsApp bookings, an AI chatbot, Book at INR 0, Lock Price, ONDC integration, EMTDESK, and EMTMATE.

3. Key Risks

  • Reliance on air ticketing as a primary revenue source.
  • Intense competition in the travel products and services industry.
  • Global macroeconomic headwinds and geopolitical uncertainties.
  • Supply chain disruptions and pricing pressures.
  • Changing consumer preferences.
  • Regulatory complexity.
  • Supply-side constraints in the hotel sector.
  • Cybersecurity and data privacy concerns.
  • Continued losses at the PAT level, with FY26 PAT of ₹(476.0) Mn and Q1 FY27 PAT of ₹(116.9) Mn.
  • Negative operating cash flow in FY26 of ₹(951.0) Mn.
  • Decline in the Trains, Buses, and Others segment bookings in Q1 FY27.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management’s FY2026-27 outlook is anchored in advancing Vision 2030 aspirations, using the EaseMyTrip 2.0 strategy to transform from an online travel platform into a diversified, technology-driven travel ecosystem, with priorities including multiple growth engines, scaling hotels, holidays, international operations, and mobility (including Easy Green Mobility and electric buses), and leveraging AI and technology.
    • Observed: Q1 FY27 GBR was ₹23,709.6 Mn versus ₹20,658.0 Mn in Q1 FY26, and Dubai GBR grew 45.2% year-on-year. Hotel and holiday package bookings grew 95% year-on-year. However, Q1 FY27 PAT was ₹(116.9) Mn and PBT was ₹(114.1) Mn, indicating continued losses alongside top-line growth.
  • Guidance: The company targets operating 2,000+ electric buses by FY2028 through YoloBus, with a ₹200 Cr investment over 2–3 years.
    • Observed: YoloBus currently covers over 250 routes and has served over 300,000 travelers; Easy Green Mobility has been announced as the manufacturing subsidiary.
  • Guidance: Spree Hospitality aims to expand to 200 properties over five years.
    • Observed: Spree currently operates 56 properties with over 2,200 keys, with approximately 30 more properties signed.
  • Guidance: Manmeet Ahluwalia, Chief Marketing Officer, noted the company is bringing together a broad range of travel options to support the shift in consumer behavior where travelers increasingly look beyond peak periods and plan trips around value and experiences.
    • Observed: The ‘Big Travel Days Sale’ was announced for September 9 to September 12, 2026.
  • Guidance: Management recommended increasing limits under Section 186 of the Companies Act and approved the Board’s Report (including Management Discussion and Analysis, Corporate Governance, and BRSR reports) for FY2025-26.
    • Observed: The 18th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, 29th September 2026 at 04:00 P.M. (IST) through video conference or other audio-visual means.
