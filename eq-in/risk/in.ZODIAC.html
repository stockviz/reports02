Zodiac Energy Limited – Recent Corporate Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Competitive intensity in the solar EPC market is cited as a source of pricing pressure.
  • Regulatory and policy uncertainty around subsidies, incentives, renewable purchase obligations (RPO), net-metering, and open-access rules.
  • Supply-chain and price volatility for solar modules, inverters, and electrical equipment.
  • Financing and interest-rate risk affecting project feasibility.
  • Land acquisition, permitting, and grid/transmission constraints.
  • Weather and climate-related risks affecting construction schedules and plant generation.
  • Potential working capital pressures.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • India’s renewable energy targets of 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030, with domestic solar capacity having reached approximately 162.15 GW.
  • Residential rooftop solar opportunities under PM Surya Ghar.
  • Decentralised solar and feeder-level solarisation under PM-KUSUM.
  • Commercial and industrial (C&I) solar adoption.
  • Expansion into the Independent Power Producer (IPP) segment, with a target of approximately 400 MW by FY2028-29.
  • Emerging technologies such as floating solar and battery energy storage systems (BESS).
  • PLI schemes supporting domestic manufacturing.
  • Order book momentum: a 2,000 Kwp ground-mounted solar PV order from a Gujarat-based domestic entity valued at approximately ₹7.12 crore (announced August 1, 2026), and a 2,500 Kwp ground-mounted solar PV order from a Uttar Pradesh-based domestic entity (announced August 21, 2026). Both are to be executed before December 31, 2026, subject to purchase order terms.
  • Portfolio expansion: acquisition of Umaputra Solar Projects LLP and Kripalu Solar Projects LLP at 98% ownership, and increase of stake in Govind Solar Projects LLP and Harikrishna Solar Projects LLP from 15% to 98%, effective August 13, 2026.
  • Industry recognition: three awards at Global Solar Expo 2026 – Ahmedabad (Solar Developer of the Year for KUSUM/DREEP Projects, Best Performing KUSUM Projects for Gujarat, and Tech & Business Model Innovation of the Year for RE + BESS – Onsite Power / Energy as a Service).

3. Key Risks

  • Pricing pressure from competition in the solar EPC market.
  • Policy and regulatory changes affecting project economics.
  • Volatility in module, inverter, and equipment prices.
  • Interest-rate and financing risks.
  • Execution risks related to land, permits, and grid connectivity.
  • Weather and climate impacts on construction and generation.
  • Working capital strain.
  • Concentration and execution risk tied to the timely completion of the two recently awarded orders (both due before December 31, 2026).

4. Management Guidance vs. Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance: Management targets an IPP portfolio of approximately 400 MW by the end of FY2028-29, built on four pillars — strengthening the core EPC business, expanding the IPP portfolio for recurring long-term revenues, establishing a presence in the BESS market, and developing integrated energy infrastructure and wind-solar hybrid parks. Management also emphasizes financial discipline, prudent capital allocation, and strong corporate governance.
  • Observed performance: For FY 2025-26, the company reported total revenue from operations of ₹543.52 crore (33.30% growth), EBITDA of ₹57.98 crore, and Profit After Tax of ₹21.07 crore, with basic EPS of ₹13.94. The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹0.75 per equity share (7.50% of face value), with a record date of September 16, 2026, and the 34th Annual General Meeting scheduled for September 23, 2026.
  • Portfolio status: Approximately 320 MW of IPP capacity is operational, with 60 MW in the pipeline, against the stated 400 MW target for FY2028-29.
  • Organisational scale: The company has a total workforce of 158 employees and maintains an internal financial control system.
  • Investor engagement: Senior management will participate in a virtual group meeting at the Bharat Connect Conference hosted by Arihant Capital on September 30, 2026, from 02:00 PM to 03:00 PM IST, with discussions limited to publicly available information and no unpublished price sensitive information (UPSI) to be shared.
