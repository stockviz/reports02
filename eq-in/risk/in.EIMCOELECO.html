1. Headwinds and Challenges

The company’s quarterly results for the period ended June 30, 2026 reveal several cost pressures. Total expenses rose to Rs. 6,566.40 lakhs in Q1 FY27, compared to Rs. 5,724.85 lakhs in the same quarter of the previous year (Q1 FY26). This increase was driven by higher costs across multiple line items:

  • Cost of materials consumed increased to Rs. 2,158.68 lakhs from Rs. 1,465.21 lakhs year-on-year.
  • Employee benefits expense rose to Rs. 579.45 lakhs from Rs. 560.44 lakhs.
  • Finance costs more than doubled to Rs. 28.05 lakhs from Rs. 11.94 lakhs.
  • Depreciation, impairment and amortisation expense increased to Rs. 210.75 lakhs from Rs. 186.95 lakhs.
  • Compensation to distributors rose to Rs. 870.66 lakhs from Rs. 777.66 lakhs.

The change in inventories of finished goods and work-in-progress was Rs. 1,113.13 lakhs in Q1 FY27, compared to Rs. 1,076.12 lakhs in Q1 FY26, indicating a build-up in inventory levels.

Other income for Q1 FY27 stood at Rs. 775.94 lakhs, a decline from Rs. 809.17 lakhs in Q1 FY26. In the immediately preceding quarter (Q4 FY26), other income was negative at Rs. (131.29) lakhs.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Revenue from operations for Q1 FY27 was Rs. 7,752.31 lakhs, representing growth over both Q1 FY26 (Rs. 6,756.99 lakhs) and Q4 FY26 (Rs. 6,688.28 lakhs). Total income for Q1 FY27 was Rs. 8,528.25 lakhs, compared to Rs. 7,566.16 lakhs in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 6,556.99 lakhs in Q4 FY26.

Profit before tax for Q1 FY27 was Rs. 1,961.85 lakhs, up from Rs. 1,841.31 lakhs in Q1 FY26 and significantly higher than Rs. 894.03 lakhs in Q4 FY26. Net profit after tax for Q1 FY27 was Rs. 1,537.85 lakhs, compared to Rs. 1,448.26 lakhs in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 636.42 lakhs in Q4 FY26.

Earnings per share (basic and diluted) for Q1 FY27 was Rs. 26.66, compared to Rs. 25.11 in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 11.03 in Q4 FY26. For the full year ended March 31, 2026, EPS was Rs. 67.11.

The company operates in a single reportable business segment—Machinery and Spares—and has no subsidiary, associate, or joint venture companies as of June 30, 2026.

At the 52nd Annual General Meeting held on June 25, 2026, shareholders approved the audited financial statements for FY26, declared a dividend for FY26, re-appointed Mr. Prashant Amin as a director liable to retire by rotation, and ratified the remuneration payable to the Cost Auditor for FY27. All resolutions were passed with the requisite majority.

The company’s finance costs more than doubled year-on-year, which could pose a risk to profitability if this trend continues. The build-up in inventories of finished goods and work-in-progress (Rs. 1,113.13 lakhs in Q1 FY27) may expose the company to inventory holding risks.
