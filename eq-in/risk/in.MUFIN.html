MUFIN Green Finance — Q1 FY27 Announcements Review

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Competitive intensity: increased competition from banks, established NBFCs, and digital lenders is compressing yields and raising customer acquisition costs.
  • Macro and funding volatility: geopolitical and trade uncertainties, plus energy-price, currency, and interest-rate volatility, affect both funding conditions and borrowers’ cash flows.
  • Regulatory change: regulatory changes can affect product economics and funding.
  • Demand sensitivity: potential slowdowns in economic activity, freight movement, MSME demand, or rural incomes could impact disbursement growth and repayment capacity.
  • Asset-quality pressure: cyclical or income-sensitive retail segments remain exposed to asset-quality pressures.
  • Cybersecurity risk: identified as a material risk.
  • Climate financing disbursement decline: quarterly disbursements in the climate vertical fell from ₹82.97 Cr in Q1 FY26 to ₹51.44 Cr in Q1 FY27, with a sequential decline through Q2 FY26 (₹101.19 Cr), Q3 FY26 (₹72.34 Cr), and Q4 FY26 (₹59.9 Cr).
  • MSME disbursement volatility: quarterly MSME disbursements moved from ₹80.49 Cr (Q1 FY26) to ₹112.25 Cr, ₹112.67 Cr, ₹83.14 Cr, and ₹51.44 Cr (Q1 FY27), showing a sharp sequential drop in the latest quarter.
  • Rajasthan softness in Salary Saathi: the mature anchor market declined 4.6% QoQ (₹25.41 Cr in Q4 FY26 to ₹24.24 Cr in Q1 FY27).
  • Rising provisions: provisions and write-offs rose 167.6% YoY to ₹2.89 Cr in Q1 FY27 (from ₹1.08 Cr), and 122% sequentially from ₹1.30 Cr in Q4 FY26.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: management noted that statements made during the earnings call are forward-looking and subject to various risks and uncertainties.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Economic and policy momentum: strong Indian economic momentum with projected real GDP growth; rising EV adoption supported by PM E-DRIVE; solar market expansion driven by PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana.
  • Low insurance penetration: creates a large opportunity for Mediclaim premium financing.
  • Mediclaim Financing scale-up: Q1 FY27 disbursements of ₹247.08 Cr (+56.2% YoY) across 48,214 loans (+48.4% YoY); June 2026 was the strongest month at 17,638 loans and ₹88.80 Cr (+74.6% YoY count growth). May and June 2026 disbursement counts grew 71.7% and 74.6% YoY respectively.
  • Salary Saathi expansion: Q1 FY27 disbursement of ₹38.20 Cr (+38.4% QoQ); Assam scaled +535% QoQ (₹2.20 Cr to ₹13.96 Cr) in its first full quarter after going live in March 2026; 6+ state government MoUs in progress versus two live states (Rajasthan, Assam).
  • Distribution and underwriting model: payroll-integrated, treasury-supported lending; underpenetrated Tier II and Tier III markets; digital transformation and data-led underwriting improving operating efficiency and customer reach.
  • Funding diversification and cost reduction: cost of borrowing fell from 13.80% in Q1 FY26 to 11.17% in Q1 FY27; total active lenders rose to 35+; new lender facilities of ₹125.00 Cr from Union Bank and Wint Wealth (June 2026) — a ₹25.00 Cr term loan and ₹100.00 Cr NCD facility.
  • Credit rating trajectory: Acuité upgraded to A– (Stable); CRISIL assigned A+ (SO) to securitised pools and an LGD of 1% ±0.25% on the Mediclaim Premium Financing Pool in Q1 FY27; management is in discussions for an ‘A’ rating.
  • Operating leverage: cumulative disbursements grew 6.9× while headcount fell 26.45% (499 in Q1 FY26 to approximately 367 in Q1 FY27); disbursement per employee of ₹6.04 Cr in Q1 FY27.
  • Capital headroom: CRAR of 30.55% (Q1 FY27) against a 15% regulatory minimum; debt/equity of 2.43× versus a typical 4–5× ceiling for rated NBFCs.
  • Leadership additions: new Head of Strategy, Group CISO, and CTO onboarded to drive growth planning and expansion.

3. Key Risks

  • Credit and asset-quality risk: GNPA of 1.91% and Net NPA of 1.65% (FY2026), with provisions rising sharply YoY and QoQ in Q1 FY27.
  • Concentration and execution risk in Salary Saathi: Rajasthan accounted for 63.4% of Q1 FY27 disbursement; the 6+ new state MoUs are in progress, not yet live, so expansion depends on execution.
  • Vertical concentration: Mediclaim Financing is 42% of AUM (₹676.61 Cr) and Climate Financing 26% (₹410.83 Cr); MSME 29% (₹458.84 Cr); Salary Saathi 3% (₹52.97 Cr).
  • Funding and refinancing risk: total borrowings of ₹1,550.99 Cr (Q1 FY27) versus ₹771 Cr in the same quarter last year; NCD issuances approved on 31 July 2026 (up to ₹50 Cr and up to ₹75 Cr, each including a ₹25 Cr green shoe option, 15-month tenure, coupons of 11% and 10.85%, secured by first and exclusive charge on identified receivables) and up to USD 6,000,000 of senior, rated, listed, USD-denominated non-convertible bonds under the ECB framework allotted on 11 September 2026 (600 bonds of USD 10,000 face value each).
  • Regulatory and compliance risk: changes affecting product economics and funding.
  • Cybersecurity risk.
  • Macro risk transmission: energy prices, currency, interest rates, and trade uncertainty affecting funding conditions and borrower cash flows.
  • Forward-looking statement risk: management’s guidance is subject to risks and uncertainties.

4. Management Guidance vs Observed Performance

  • Rating upgrade: management expects a one-notch upgrade to an ‘A’ rating in the next quarter. Observed: Acuité rating stands at A– (Stable) as of Q1 FY27, with CRISIL A+ (SO) on securitised pools; the upgrade to ‘A’ had not yet been reflected in the Q1 FY27 disclosures.
  • Headcount reduction: management aims to bring employee count to approximately 300 by the end of the year. Observed: headcount fell from 499 in Q1 FY26 to approximately 367 in Q1 FY27, already down 26.45%, with disbursement per employee at ₹6.04 Cr.
  • Salary Saathi volumes: management forecasts significant volumes going forward. Observed: Q1 FY27 disbursement of ₹38.20 Cr, +38.4% QoQ, with Assam contributing ₹13.96 Cr (36.6% of the quarter) after scaling +535% QoQ, while Rajasthan held broadly steady at ₹24.24 Cr.
  • Profitable growth and efficiency: management stated it will pursue profitable growth by improving operating efficiency, maintaining a balanced portfolio, and strengthening Mediclaim Premium Financing alongside expanding Salary Saathi through additional state partnerships. Observed: operating expenses fell 17.2% YoY to ₹13.55 Cr; PBT rose 345.6% YoY to ₹18.82 Cr; PAT rose 345.6% YoY to ₹14.01 Cr; NII rose 64.3% YoY to ₹35.16 Cr; interest income rose 61.8% YoY to ₹76.44 Cr.
  • Portfolio quality via data-driven underwriting and monitoring: management aims to enhance portfolio quality. Observed: GNPA improved from 1.94% (FY2026) to 1.91% (Q1 FY27); Net NPA at 1.65% (FY2026); CRISIL LGD of 1% ±0.25% on the Mediclaim pool.
  • Funding diversification and cost reduction: management aims to leverage the upgraded rating to diversify funding sources and reduce borrowing costs. Observed: cost of borrowing fell from 13.80% in Q1 FY26 to 11.17% in Q1 FY27; total active lenders 35+; new Union Bank and Wint Wealth facilities of ₹125.00 Cr in June 2026; NCD issuances approved 31 July 2026; USD 6,000,000 ECB bond allotment on 11 September 2026.
  • Tier II and Tier III expansion: management aims to deepen presence through technology-enabled customer assessment and loan processing. Observed: Salary Saathi expansion beyond Rajasthan and Assam is in progress via 6+ state MoUs; Mediclaim distribution is embedded in multiple insurer checkout flows.
  • AUM and disbursement trajectory: FY2026 AUM rose 83.81% to ₹1,541.17 Cr with annual disbursements of ₹1,767.59 Cr; Q1 FY27 AUM reached ₹1,599.23 Cr, with net AUM up 69.4% and quarterly disbursements up 37.3% from Q1 FY26 to Q1 FY27.
