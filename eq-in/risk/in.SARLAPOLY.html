SARLAPOLY: Recent Corporate Announcements Analysis

1. Headwinds and Challenges

Audit Qualification on Preference Share Sale The company’s Statutory Auditors, C N K & Associates LLP, issued a Qualified Opinion on both the Standalone and Consolidated Unaudited Financial Results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The qualification relates to the sale of 1% Non-Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares held by the company in its wholly owned subsidiary, SARLAFLEX INC. (USA). The necessary regulatory approvals, write-off of the associated loss, and receipt of balance sale consideration remain awaited as of the date of the Limited Review Reports (August 5, 2026).

Outstanding Receivable As at June 30, 2026, an amount of Rs. 111.59 lakhs representing consideration recoverable towards the sale is carried under Trade Receivables (standalone) / Other Receivables (consolidated). The auditors state they are unable to comment on the recoverability of this balance and the consequential impact pending receipt of regulatory approvals.

Repetitive Qualification The audit qualification is described as “Repetitive” in frequency, indicating this matter has persisted from prior periods. The regulatory approvals, write-off of loss, and receipt of balance sale proceeds were already awaited as of the audit report dated April 22, 2026, and continue to remain awaited.

Sarlaflex Inc. Going Concern Uncertainty The auditors drew attention via Emphasis of Matters to the fact that Sarlaflex Inc., a subsidiary, prepares its interim financial results on a ‘Going Concern’ basis despite suspension of manufacturing operations since December 2017 and net worth becoming negative.

Non-Consolidation of Joint Ventures The investment made by Sarla Overseas Holdings Limited (SOHL) in three Joint Ventures are not consolidated on account of non-resolution of disputes, or non-receipt of financial results and other financial information for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The auditors state they are unable to comment about the impact of the same on the Unaudited Consolidated Financial Results.

Regulatory Non-Compliance The management acknowledges that the auditor qualified the opinion as regulatory approvals are still awaited. The auditors comment that “This being a regulatory non-compliance we are unable to quantify the amount of penalty that may be imposed by the regulator to regularize this transaction pending clearance.”

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Dividend Declaration The Members of the Company, at the 33rd Annual General Meeting held on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, approved by way of an ordinary resolution the declaration of final dividend of Rs. 2/- per equity share of face value Rs. 1/- each for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The dividend was recommended by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on April 22, 2026.

Strong Voting Support All resolutions set out in the Notice convening the 33rd AGM were duly approved by the Members with the requisite majority. Resolution 1 (Adoption of audited financial statements) received 100% votes in favour. Resolution 2 (Declaration of final dividend) received 100% votes in favour.

Management’s Assessment of Regulatory Approvals The Management, based on its assessment of the facts and circumstances, believes that such approvals are procedural in nature and do not cast any uncertainty on the transaction imposed by the regulator to regularize this transaction pending clearance.

Subsidiary Performance The accompanying Statement includes unaudited interim financial results of two subsidiaries whose interim financial results reflect total assets (before consolidated adjustments) of Rs. 48,955.53 Lakhs as at June 30, 2026 and total revenues (before consolidated adjustments) of Rs. 12,462.04 lakhs, total comprehensive Profit (before consolidated adjustments) Rs. 3,708.62 lakhs and Rs. 3,728.25 lakhs for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 and for year to date from April 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026, respectively.

3. Key Risks

Regulatory Approval Risk The sale transaction of preference shares in Sarlaflex Inc. remains subject to receipt of necessary regulatory approvals. The company has applied for these approvals and is pursuing the matter with the Authorised Dealer. Pending receipt of such approvals, the sale proceeds continue to remain awaited.

Recoverability Risk The auditors are unable to comment on the recoverability of the balance consideration of Rs. 111.59 lakhs and the consequential impact, if any, on the financial statements pending receipt of regulatory approvals.

Penalty Risk The auditors note that this being a regulatory non-compliance, they are unable to quantify the amount of penalty that may be imposed by the regulator to regularize this transaction pending clearance.

Going Concern Risk Sarlaflex Inc. has suspended manufacturing operations since December 2017 and its net worth has become negative. The interim financial results are prepared on a ‘Going Concern’ basis despite these conditions.

Consolidation Risk Three Joint Ventures of Sarla Overseas Holdings Limited are not consolidated due to non-resolution of disputes or non-receipt of financial results. The auditors are unable to comment about the impact of the same on the Unaudited Consolidated Financial Results.

Loss Recognition The company recognized a loss of Rs. 7,713.26 lakhs (consolidated) / Rs. 5,433.16 lakhs (standalone, net of reversal of impairment provision of Rs. 2,280.10 lakhs carried out in earlier years) on sale of 1% Non-Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares held in Sarlaflex Inc. during the financial year 2024-25 (consolidated) / 2025-26 (standalone). This has been recognized as an Exceptional Item in the statement of Profit and Loss.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

Management’s Position on Regulatory Approvals Management believes that the pending regulatory approvals are procedural in nature and do not cast any uncertainty on the transaction imposed by the regulator to regularize this transaction pending clearance. Management is pursuing the matter with the Authorised Dealer to obtain the necessary approvals at the earliest.

Observed Status The regulatory approvals, write-off of the loss, and receipt of balance sale proceeds continue to remain awaited as on the date of the Limited Review Reports (August 5, 2026). The auditors state they are unable to comment on the recoverability of the said balance and the consequential impact pending receipt of such approvals.

Management’s Estimate on Impact Management’s estimate on the impact of audit qualification is stated as “Not Applicable” where the impact is not quantified by the auditor. Management states it is unable to estimate the impact because the auditor has qualified the opinion as the approvals from the regulators is still awaited.

Auditors’ Response to Management’s Assessment The auditors comment that this being a regulatory non-compliance, they are unable to quantify the amount of penalty that may be imposed by the regulator to regularize this transaction pending clearance.

Dividend Performance The Board of Directors recommended a final dividend of Rs. 2/- per equity share at its meeting held on April 22, 2026, which was subsequently approved by the Members at the 33rd AGM held on July 29, 2026. The dividend will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members / list of Beneficial Owners as on the record date fixed for this purpose, i.e., July 22, 2026.

AGM Resolutions All resolutions set out in the Notice convening the 33rd AGM were duly approved by the Members with the requisite majority. Resolution 3 (re-appointment of Ms. Neha Jhunjhunwala as Director) received 99.11% votes in favour and 0.89% against. Resolution 7 (approval of aggregate remuneration payable to Executive Directors who are members of the promoter and promoter group) received 99.11% votes in favour and 0.89% against.
