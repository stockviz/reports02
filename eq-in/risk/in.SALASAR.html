Salasar Techno Engineering — Recent Developments

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Enforcement Directorate attachment order. A Provisional Attachment Order dated September 12, 2026, issued by the Directorate of Enforcement under Section 5(1) of the PMLA, was received by the Company on September 15, 2026. It provisionally attaches immovable properties of the Company with an aggregate value of ₹98,31,25,000, for a period of 180 days.
  • Weak sequential profitability. Standalone profit before tax fell to ₹318.88 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from ₹1,084.44 lakh in the quarter ended June 30, 2025, and from ₹147.89 lakh in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Standalone net profit was ₹477.64 lakh for the June 2026 quarter, against ₹820.03 lakh a year earlier. Consolidated profit before tax was ₹364.64 lakh, versus ₹1,176.45 lakh in the June 2025 quarter.
  • EPC segment pressure. Standalone EPC segment revenue declined to ₹9,078.39 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹13,466.92 lakh a year earlier, with segment results of ₹95.48 lakh. On a consolidated basis, EPC revenue was ₹9,555.25 lakh against ₹13,893.99 lakh, with segment results of ₹196.87 lakh.
  • Rising finance costs and leverage. Standalone finance costs rose to ₹1,468.41 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹1,378.49 lakh a year earlier. Total standalone liabilities increased to ₹1,44,080.85 lakh as of June 30, 2026, from ₹96,597.82 lakh as of June 30, 2025; consolidated liabilities rose to ₹1,47,159.08 lakh from ₹98,561.03 lakh.
  • Working capital absorption. Total standalone assets grew to ₹2,27,211.86 lakh from ₹1,75,654.85 lakh a year earlier, with segment assets in both Steel Structures and EPC expanding, indicating a larger balance sheet supporting the enlarged business.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • EMC Limited merger completed. The National Company Law Tribunal, Kolkata Bench-I, sanctioned the Scheme of Amalgamation of EMC Limited with Salasar Techno Engineering Limited by order dated May 22, 2026, with an appointed date of October 23, 2024. The Allahabad Bench, Prayagraj, had earlier approved the scheme on October 29, 2025. The Company completed the formalities directed by the NCLT, and the effect of the merger is taken in the June 2026 quarter. Prior-period figures have been regrouped, reclassified and recast to give effect to the merger.
  • Steel Structures scale-up. Standalone Steel Structures revenue rose to ₹20,688.62 lakh in the June 2026 quarter from ₹17,282.78 lakh a year earlier, with segment results of ₹2,142.60 lakh. Consolidated Steel Structures revenue was ₹20,688.62 lakh against ₹17,035.41 lakh, with segment results of ₹2,142.60 lakh.
  • Management’s view on the attachment order. Management states that the Provisional Attachment Order is not likely to result in disruption to the Company’s business operations, and that the Company continues to conduct its business in the normal course.
  • Proposed business plans. Management is contemplating new and expansive business plans and is seeking members’ approval for smooth and efficient implementation of the Company’s proposed business plans, to be taken up at the 25th Annual General Meeting.
  • Return to consolidated profitability. After a consolidated loss before tax of ₹294.28 lakh in the March 2026 quarter, the June 2026 quarter returned to consolidated profit before tax of ₹364.64 lakh and net profit of ₹514.29 lakh, of which ₹499.20 lakh is attributable to owners of the Company.

3. Key Risks

  • The provisional attachment of immovable properties valued at ₹98,31,25,000 for 180 days, and any subsequent proceedings connected with the ED action.
  • Neither the Company nor its directors have been summoned or named as accused in the prosecution complaint filed by the ED under the PMLA, as disclosed.
  • Earnings volatility across quarters, as seen in the swing from consolidated loss before tax of ₹294.28 lakh in the March 2026 quarter to profit before tax of ₹364.64 lakh in the June 2026 quarter, and the year-on-year decline in profitability.
  • Continued dependence on the Steel Structures segment for profitability, given the weaker contribution from EPC Projects.
  • Balance sheet expansion and higher liabilities, with finance costs increasing year on year.
  • The consolidated results include unaudited interim financial results of five entities not reviewed or audited by their auditors, certified and furnished by management, reflecting total revenue of ₹476.86 lakh and total net profit after tax (including OCI) of ₹36.65 lakh for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

  • Management’s stated position is that the Provisional Attachment Order is not likely to disrupt business operations, that the Company continues to conduct business in the normal course, and that management remains committed to compliance and transparency. The June 2026 quarterly results, approved by the Board on August 13, 2026, show continued operations with standalone revenue from operations of ₹29,077.12 lakh and consolidated revenue from operations of ₹29,553.98 lakh, alongside a return to consolidated profit after the March 2026 quarter loss.
  • Management is contemplating new and expansive business plans and is seeking members’ approval for their implementation. The 25th Annual General Meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 11:30 A.M. IST through video conferencing or other audio-visual means, with a cut-off date of September 23, 2026, and remote e-voting from September 26, 2026, at 9:00 A.M. IST to September 29, 2026, at 5:00 P.M. IST.
  • The merger of EMC Limited has been given effect in the June 2026 quarter, with the appointed date of October 23, 2024, and prior-period figures recast accordingly. The observed performance for the quarter reflects this enlarged entity, with standalone total income of ₹29,427.44 lakh and consolidated total income of ₹29,904.30 lakh, but profit before tax of ₹318.88 lakh standalone and ₹364.64 lakh consolidated, indicating that the merged scale has not yet translated into proportionately higher profitability.
