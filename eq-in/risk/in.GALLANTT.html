Headwinds and Challenges

Seasonal and pricing pressure. The first quarter of FY27 was described as a seasonally softer period for the domestic steel industry due to the monsoon. Construction and infrastructure activity slowed, and long product prices — TMT and rebar in particular — corrected meaningfully through the quarter after opening the year on a firm note. Because Gallantt is predominantly in long products serving construction and housing markets in Uttar Pradesh and Gujarat, this correction weighed more directly on the company than it would on a more diversified player.

Input cost inflation. Coal and iron ore costs firmed industry-wide during the quarter. Raw material cost rose 9% year-on-year, outpacing revenue growth of 3%, and was also up modestly sequentially despite lower sales volumes. Management attributed roughly half of the raw material increase to the pellet plant shutdown, with the balance reflecting higher coal and iron ore pricing and logistics costs.

Pellet plant shutdown. The planned annual maintenance shutdown of the pellet plant reduced in-house pellet availability and required procurement of iron ore from the open market at higher cost than the normal captive route. Pellet production fell 36% year-on-year and 49% sequentially. This also moderated volumes across downstream DRI and steel operations.

Geopolitical and freight pressure. Ongoing geopolitical tensions, including the conflict in the Middle East, added pressure on global freight, shipping, and energy-linked costs. Management noted that fuel prices rise as oil prices and tensions increase.

Import competition. India turned a net importer of steel during the quarter, with imports rising as cargoes originally intended for other markets found their way to India and imports under some free trade routes increased. The industry and government have sought anti-dumping measures alongside existing safeguard duties.

Kutch rolling mill utilization. The Kutch facility’s rolling mill ran at around 66% capacity utilization, trailing Gorakhpur’s 93%. Improving this was flagged as a specific focus area for Q2.

Employee cost increase. Employee benefits expense rose 24% year-on-year, driven by the full-year impact of the DRI plant commissioned last year, strengthening of senior leadership and the board, and the annual salary revision effective April 2026.

China overhang. The global steel industry continues to be impacted by excess production in China and weak demand from its real estate sector, with record finished steel exports pressuring global prices and triggering trade protection measures.

Subsidiary with no business. Suryalaxmi Technologies Private Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary, has no revenue or profit to date and no business explored at present.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Capacity expansion. The expansion from 1 million to 1.23 million tonnes — part of the INR 3,000 crores capex program — remains on course for commissioning in the second half of FY27. Most of this capacity enhancement is at the Gorakhpur unit.

Renewable energy. About 85 megawatts of solar capacity is being installed: 18 MW at Gujarat targeted for commissioning in Q2 FY27 and 67 MW at Gorakhpur targeted for Q4 FY27. A 7 MW trial plant was already commissioned last year. Management described solar as a structural cost-side lever once operational.

Captive iron ore mines. Three virgin iron ore mines have been secured — two in UP and one in Rajasthan — with exploration ongoing. Exploration in UP is expected to complete over the next two to three months and in Rajasthan in about six months. FY28 remains the internal target for these to become operational. Management stated this would move Gallantt from a secondary to a quasi-primary producer, boosting EBITDA per tonne and removing dependence on the market for iron ore.

Coal block win. Gallantt was declared preferred bidder for commercial coal mining at the Dongrital-II, Bazori, Digwaha, Dongri, Bhaisanbuda, Singrouli, M.P. coal block by the Madhya Pradesh State Mining Corporation Limited on August 04, 2026, with a winning bid of 6.5% revenue share. The company stated it is evaluating modalities and next steps for development.

Demand outlook. Domestic steel demand is expected to grow in the range of 7% to 9% this year, with government infrastructure spending and the construction cycle remaining multi-year themes. India contributed 43% of incremental steel demand growth outside China. The National Steel Policy targets 300 MT crude steel capacity by FY30/FY31. Management expects pricing in construction steel to recover as the monsoon recedes and activity picks up from September and October onwards.

Integration and cost efficiency. The pellet-to-sponge iron-to-billet-to-TMT integration provides a structural cushion relative to non-integrated players. Debottlenecking work at the Gujarat unit continues and remains on track for completion within this financial year. Management stated that with solar kicking in and mines operational in FY28, the company is set to further improve margins.

Branding and market position. Gallantt holds more than 25% market share in UP from the addressable market. Ajay Devgn was engaged as brand celebrity four years ago, and Janhvi Kapoor has been roped in as a second celebrity. The company reported approximately 35% increase in brand awareness across core markets and a 2–3% realization premium versus unbranded peers. The dealer network comprises 3,000+ active dealers and 34 distributors, with 80% of sales via the dealer-distributor network.

Value-added products. Gallantt Advance offers premium grades (Fe 550D, Fe 600) with superior ductility, advanced thermal treatment, and corrosion-resistant solutions, supporting higher realizations.

Capital discipline. Gallantt remains a net cash surplus company with no term loans, and ongoing capex is funded entirely through internal accruals. INR 1,200 crores of capex was incurred without external borrowing over a five-year period. Capex of INR 137 crores was incurred in Q1 FY27, with INR 775 crores incurred in totality till June 30, 2026.

Related party synergies. The company engages in material related party transactions with entities like Gallantt Industry Private Limited and Gallantt Lifespace Private Limited up to an aggregate value of INR 900 crores each, providing business synergies, operational efficiencies, cost optimizations, and economies of scale.

Subsidiary plans. Management is planning to set up business units through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Suryalaxmi Technologies Private Limited, and necessary steps shall be taken up.

Key Risks

  • Geopolitical developments remain a key risk to iron ore and fuel prices, influencing supply chains, freight costs, and global demand sentiment.
  • China’s excess capacity and high exports continue to pressure global prices and trigger trade protection measures.
  • US Section 232 steel tariffs increased to 50%; EU CBAM implementation is accelerating the transition to low-carbon steel.
  • India’s safeguard duty (effective April 2025, for 200 days) supports domestic producers against import surges, but rising global trade remedies remain a feature — 64 measures initiated in first 9M CY2025, including 54 anti-dumping cases.
  • Q1 and Q2 are expected to see muted demand due to the monsoon, particularly in long products.
  • The mining projects carry aggressive timelines with government regulatory involvement; management acknowledged the targets are aggressive though currently on track.
  • The company’s forward-looking statements are subject to risks including fluctuations in earnings, competition, economic conditions, regulatory changes, time and cost overruns on projects, and interest rate movements.

Management Guidance Versus Observed Performance

Capacity expansion timeline. Management guided that the 1.23 MMTPA expansion would commission in H2 FY27. This was reiterated as on track with no change to the timeline. Observed: capex of INR 137 crores in Q1 FY27 and INR 775 crores incurred in totality till June 30, 2026, out of the INR 3,000 crores program.

Mine commissioning. Management guided FY28 as the internal target for captive iron ore mines to become operational. Exploration is ongoing at all three mines, with UP expected to complete in two to three months and Rajasthan in about six months. Management stated it is on track for FY28.

Solar commissioning. Management guided 18 MW at Gujarat for Q2 FY27 and 67 MW at Gorakhpur for Q4 FY27. The 7 MW trial plant was already commissioned last year. No slippage was reported.

Margin sustainability. Management stated margins are sustainable, citing end-to-end integration, no term loans, solar cost reduction, and FY28 mines. Observed: EBITDA margin of 18% in Q1 FY27, broadly in line with 17.3% in Q4 FY26 but below 23% in Q1 FY26. EBITDA per tonne of INR 8,787 was broadly in line with the previous quarter’s INR 8,882 but below INR 11,068 in Q1 FY26. Management noted FY26 average EBITDA per tonne was around INR 8,800 and Q1 FY27 was around INR 8,700 despite raw material pressure.

Pellet plant normalization. Management guided that with the shutdown complete, pressure would ease and plants would run to normal captive route with normalized utilization in the coming quarter. Observed: pellet production fell 36% year-on-year and 49% sequentially in Q1 FY27; external pellet sales were nil versus 7.5 KT in Q4 FY26.

Volume growth. Management guided that with capacity additions coming in H2 FY27, volumes would be much higher this year. Observed: TMT bar sales volumes were broadly flat year-on-year at around 192,000 tonnes, down about 8% sequentially. Billet volumes grew 13% year-on-year and 38% sequentially, running well ahead of internal plans.

Demand recovery. Management guided that Q2 FY27 would be muted due to full monsoon, with Q3 and Q4 expected to be good. This is consistent with the seasonal pattern described.

Kutch utilization. Management flagged improving Kutch rolling mill utilization as a specific focus area for Q2. Observed: Kutch TMT production fell 15% sequentially and Kutch TMT sales fell 22% sequentially.

Dividend. The Board recommended a final dividend of Rs. 2/- (20%) per equity share of face value Rs. 10/- for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The record date for the dividend and voting eligibility is September 23, 2026. The book closure period runs from September 24, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

AGM and auditor change. The 22nd Annual General Meeting will be held on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 12:30 P.M. through Video Conferencing/OAVM. Remote e-voting takes place from September 27, 2026 (9:00 A.M.) to September 29, 2026 (5:00 P.M.). M/s. Singhi & Co. is proposed to be appointed as Statutory Auditors, following the resignation of M/s. Maroti & Associates, who tendered their resignation on July 21, 2026, citing scale and complexity of the audit, resource allocation, professional commitments, priorities, and commercial considerations. The Audit Committee noted the Statutory Auditors had not raised any concern or issue. Mr. Amit Jalan was appointed as Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel effective July 27, 2026.
