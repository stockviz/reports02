  • Global tenders and institutional demand from governments and UN agencies.
  • Establishment of a manufacturing venture in South Africa, with development of local manufacturing capabilities, employment and skill development, technology transfer, supply-chain resilience, greater local value addition, expansion across the wider African market, and serving additional international markets including the Western Hemisphere.

The project may be undertaken through the company or a 100% wholly owned subsidiary to be incorporated, and would undertake manufacturing, processing, marketing, trading and distribution of medical devices, healthcare products and related products, plus allied, ancillary or diversified activities consistent with the company’s business objectives. Disclosures will be made upon finalization of the project and, where applicable, upon incorporation of the WOS and execution of definitive project-related arrangements.
