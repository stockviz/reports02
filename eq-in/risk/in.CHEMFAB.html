Headwinds and Challenges

Sharp sequential and year-on-year decline in profitability (standalone). For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, standalone revenue from operations was Rs. 7,302.14 lakhs, down from Rs. 8,758.10 lakhs in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. Profit before tax fell to Rs. 130.24 lakhs from Rs. 753.57 lakhs a year earlier, and from Rs. 229.81 lakhs in the preceding quarter ended 31 March 2026. Net profit after tax was Rs. 482.92 lakhs, supported by a deferred tax credit rather than operating performance.

Segment-level weakness in PVC-O Pipes. The PVC-O Pipes segment recorded revenue of Rs. 574.97 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, down sharply from Rs. 3,870.20 lakhs in the June 2025 quarter and Rs. 1,311.24 lakhs in the March 2026 quarter. The segment posted a loss before tax of Rs. 57.23 lakhs, following a loss of Rs. 87.08 lakhs in the preceding quarter and a profit of Rs. 863.16 lakhs in the prior-year quarter.

Rising finance and depreciation costs. Standalone finance cost rose to Rs. 226.05 lakhs from Rs. 156.40 lakhs year-on-year, and depreciation rose to Rs. 676.45 lakhs from Rs. 538.23 lakhs. On a consolidated basis, finance cost was Rs. 220.75 lakhs and depreciation Rs. 777.93 lakhs.

Consolidated profitability remains thin. Consolidated revenue from operations was Rs. 7,315.98 lakhs for the June 2026 quarter, against Rs. 9,154.09 lakhs a year earlier. Consolidated profit before tax was Rs. 222.37 lakhs, compared with Rs. 469.98 lakhs in the prior-year quarter. Consolidated net profit after tax of Rs. 573.04 lakhs again reflected the deferred tax credit.

Full-year consolidated loss in FY2025-26. The audited consolidated figures for the year ended 31 March 2026 show a loss before exceptional items and loss before tax of Rs. 20.00 lakhs, and a net loss after tax of Rs. 342.65 lakhs, with a basic loss per share of Rs. 2.38.

Statutory auditor transition. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP’s tenure as statutory auditor expired, and M/s. M S K A & Associates LLP was appointed for a first term of five consecutive years, subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing AGM. The change was subsequently approved by shareholders at the 17th AGM held on 9 September 2026.

Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

Chemicals and Related Products segment growth. Standalone segment revenue from Chemicals and Related Products rose to Rs. 6,727.17 lakhs in the June 2026 quarter, from Rs. 4,887.90 lakhs a year earlier and Rs. 5,563.23 lakhs in the preceding quarter. Segment result before tax improved to Rs. 232.24 lakhs from a loss of Rs. 58.85 lakhs in the prior-year quarter. On a consolidated basis, the segment contributed revenue of Rs. 6,741.01 lakhs and a result before tax of Rs. 292.03 lakhs.

Investment in captive hybrid power. During the quarter, the company invested Rs. 1,491 lakhs under the terms of a power purchase agreement for sourcing power from a hybrid power plant. This is expected to support power, fuel and other direct manufacturing costs over time.

Lower tax regime. The company exercised the option of the lower tax rate under section 200 of the Income Tax Act 2025 (erstwhile section 115BAA of the Income Tax Act 1961). It re-measured the opening deferred tax liability balance as at 1 April 2026 and recognised a tax credit of Rs. 380.27 lakhs in the quarter, which lifted reported net profit.

Shareholder returns. The 17th AGM approved a dividend of Rs. 1.25 per equity share, along with adoption of financial statements, retirement by rotation of director Mr. Suresh Krishnamurthi Rao, ratification of cost auditor remuneration, and payment of commission to non-executive directors.

Balance sheet expansion. Standalone total assets rose to Rs. 62,649.19 lakhs as at 30 June 2026 from Rs. 57,395.64 lakhs a year earlier. Consolidated total assets were Rs. 58,136.10 lakhs. Standalone reserves (other equity) stood at Rs. 39,975.52 lakhs.

Key Risks

  • Continued losses in the PVC-O Pipes segment would weigh on consolidated results.
  • The June 2026 net profit was materially aided by a one-time deferred tax credit of Rs. 380.27 lakhs; underlying pre-tax profitability was substantially lower than the prior-year quarter.
  • Finance costs and depreciation are on an upward trend, reflecting the hybrid power investment and related capital commitments.
  • Consolidated full-year FY2025-26 ended in a net loss, indicating earnings volatility.
  • The statutory auditor transition introduces a change in the audit firm for the first term of five years.
  • The company has a special window open for transfer and dematerialisation of physical shares, in line with SEBI circular dated 30 January 2026.
