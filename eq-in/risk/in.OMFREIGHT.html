OMFREIGHT: Q1FY27 Earnings Summary

1. Headwinds and Challenges

  • Flat EBITDA margin year-on-year: Despite 105% revenue growth, EBITDA margin was essentially unchanged at 5.84% in Q1FY27 versus 5.85% in Q1FY26 (a decline of 1 basis point), indicating that operating profitability did not expand in line with revenue on a year-on-year basis.
  • Rising finance costs: Finance costs increased to Rs. 0.83 crore in Q1FY27 from Rs. 0.58 crore in Q1FY26 and Rs. 0.71 crore in Q4FY26.
  • Working capital pressure: Trade receivables rose to Rs. 143.83 crore as at March 31, 2026 from Rs. 109.78 crore a year earlier, and the standalone cash flow statement for FY26 shows a cash outflow from operations of Rs. 7.08 crore, driven largely by a Rs. 38.86 crore increase in trade receivables.
  • Borrowings increased: Current borrowings rose to Rs. 42.44 crore as at March 31, 2026 from Rs. 19.21 crore a year earlier.
  • Impairment on Norway investment: The company’s investment in a Norway-based entity was measured at Net Realisable Value, with a resultant loss of Rs. 3.63 crore recognised in Other Comprehensive Income. The entity divested its assets and remitted only a first tranche of proceeds; the balance consideration is expected in the forthcoming months, and until receipt the investment remains classified as an “Asset held for sale.”
  • Regulatory filing lapses: On June 26, 2026, the company clarified to the NSE that financial results submitted on May 27, 2026 had inadvertently not been filed in the prescribed searchable and legible format, and that EPS in the Consolidated XBRL submission had been incorrectly reported due to an inadvertent error. Revised filings were made.
  • Deviation in IPO proceeds utilisation: The company stated it is in the process of filing a Statement of Deviation as the utilisation of IPO funds differs from the originally stated objects in the Prospectus, in terms of the amount allocated, the model, the quantity of assets to be procured, and the timeline of utilisation of funds.
  • Key management transition: The Company Secretary, Mr Hiren Bhanushali, vacated office with effect from February 14, 2026, and Ms. Manisha Kaur Saluja was appointed on May 13, 2026 as the new Company Secretary.

2. Tailwinds and Growth Prospects

  • Strong revenue growth: Revenue from operations for Q1FY27 stood at Rs. 193.35 crore, up 105% year-on-year from Rs. 94.43 crore and up 29% quarter-on-quarter from Rs. 149.54 crore.
  • Profit growth outpacing revenue: EBITDA rose 104% year-on-year to Rs. 11.29 crore, and PAT rose 126% year-on-year to Rs. 7.30 crore. Sequentially, EBITDA rose 59% and PAT rose 78%.
  • Sequential margin improvement: EBITDA margin improved to 5.84% in Q1FY27 from 4.74% in Q4FY26 (up 110 basis points), and PAT margin improved to 3.78% from 2.75% (up 103 basis points).
  • Project cargo as the growth driver: Management attributed the strong revenue growth during the quarter to project cargo and stated that the company remains focused on the project cargo business going forward.
  • Network and capabilities: The company describes itself as a third-generation logistics and freight forwarding company with over four decades of experience, with offices and associates across five continents, serving more than 700 unique national and international locations, and overseas offices in China, Hong Kong, the UK and Singapore. It holds MTO, IATA and FIATA accreditations.
  • IPO completed: The company completed an IPO of Rs. 24.44 crore through a fresh issue of 18,10,042 equity shares at Rs. 135 per share. Shares were allotted on October 6, 2025 and listed on NSE and BSE on October 8, 2025. Paid-up capital increased to Rs. 33.68 crore and securities premium to Rs. 19.87 crore.
  • IPO proceeds deployment: Of the gross issue proceeds of Rs. 24.44 crore, Rs. 22.16 crore had been utilised up to June 30, 2026, with Rs. 2.28 crore unutilised (towards capital expenditure requirements). General corporate purposes (Rs. 4.52 crore) and offer-related expenses (Rs. 2.77 crore) were fully utilised.
  • Governance measures: The Board approved a Dividend Distribution Policy voluntarily, in line with good corporate governance practices, although not mandatorily required. The Board also approved the re-appointment of M/s. Nitin R. Joshi & Co. as Secretarial Auditor for five consecutive financial years from FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31, subject to member approval.
  • Associate contribution: The consolidated results include the Group’s share of net profit after tax of Rs. 0.23 crore for Q1FY27 from associates (Arha Warehousing and Translift Private Limited, and Oscar Freight Private Limited).

3. Key Risks

  • Forward-looking statement risks: The company’s disclaimer lists risks and uncertainties including fluctuations in earnings, ability to manage growth, currency changes, intense industry competition, wage increases in India, ability to attract and retain skilled professionals, time and cost overruns on fixed-price/fixed-timeframe contracts, client concentration, industry segment concentration, ability to manage international operations, disruptions in networks or system failures, ability to complete and integrate acquisitions, liability for damages on service contracts, withdrawal of governmental fiscal incentives, political instability and regional conflicts, legal restrictions on raising capital or acquiring companies outside India, and unauthorised use of intellectual property.
  • Receivables and credit risk: The FY26 standalone cash flow statement shows an allowance for doubtful debts of Rs. 4.81 crore, up from Rs. 0.34 crore in FY25, alongside the significant increase in trade receivables.
  • Dependence on project cargo: Management’s stated focus on project cargo as the driver of growth and future focus concentrates exposure to that segment.
  • Uncertainty over Norway investment proceeds: The balance consideration from the Norway entity’s asset divestment is expected in forthcoming months; the transaction will only be recognised as a Sale of Investment upon receipt.
  • Compliance and reporting risk: The June 2026 exchange clarification and the pending Statement of Deviation indicate reporting and disclosure lapses that required rectification.

4. Management Guidance Versus Observed Business Performance

  • Guidance on project cargo focus: Management stated, “Going forward, we remain focused on project cargo business. We will continue to invest in strengthening our capabilities, expanding our reach and delivering reliable, efficient and customised logistics solutions to our clients.” Observed performance in Q1FY27 showed revenue growth of 105% year-on-year, which management attributed to project cargo, consistent with this stated focus.
  • Guidance on sustainable growth and operational efficiency: Management stated it believes its “experience, integrated service offering and international network provide us with a strong foundation to pursue sustainable growth while maintaining our focus on operational efficiency and value creation for all our stakeholders.” Observed performance showed year-on-year EBITDA margin flat at 5.84% versus 5.85%, while sequential EBITDA margin improved by 110 basis points from 4.74% in Q4FY26.
  • Guidance on profitability improvement: Management stated, “We also witnessed an improvement in profitability sequentially, with EBITDA margins increasing to 5.84% and PAT margins improving to 3.78%.” This is consistent with the reported sequential figures (Q4FY26 EBITDA margin 4.74%, PAT margin 2.75%).
  • Prior-year performance context: For the full year FY26, standalone revenue from operations was Rs. 476.92 crore (versus Rs. 490.14 crore in FY25), and standalone PAT was Rs. 15.38 crore (versus Rs. 21.65 crore in FY25), indicating a decline in full-year profitability even as Q1FY27 showed strong year-on-year growth.
  • Dividend policy: The Board voluntarily adopted a Dividend Distribution Policy; no dividend declaration or payout guidance was provided in the supplied material.
